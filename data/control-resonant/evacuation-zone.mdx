<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
From Central, take the southeast road into the **Evacuation Zone** and head for the building covered by the large red Hiss formation. Destroy the **three Hiss Orbs outside**, enter the Evac Building, then work upward through Floors 12 and 15 until you find Arish and clear the elevator route back out.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-to-reach-evacuation-zone">
  Take the southeast road out of Central
</h2>

Evacuation Procedures begins after Zoe's Watchtower briefing.

The FBC quarantined civilians inside the Evacuation Zone when the Pattern barrier made a normal evacuation impossible. Hiss then got into the site, Arish took a Ranger team inside, and contact with them stopped.

Your immediate assignment is not to clear the whole district.

Zoe wants the source of the **Hiss resonance tremors** coming from the evacuation site.

From the Central Supply Route side of the city, take the road running **southeast** into the Evacuation Zone.

Once the district opens in front of you, the destination is difficult to miss: look for the large building with a massive red Hiss formation rising above it.

That is the Evac Building.

Do not spend time trying every door at the entrance yet.

The building is being held shut by three Hiss sources spread around the surrounding streets.

<h2 id="three-hiss-orbs">
  Destroy the three Hiss Orbs before entering the Evac Building
</h2>

The easiest way to locate the Orbs is to watch the debris caught in the red Hiss trails around the building.

The floating objects move along the resonance lines toward the Evac Building. Follow those lines backward to their sources.

There are **three Orbs** outside.

1. The first is in the open area directly in front of the Evac Building. This one is the most straightforward: clear the nearby enemies, reach the Orb, and destroy it.
2. The second is southwest of the building, around the alley south of the street corner. Deal with the two healing Orbs in the enemy group first. The Hiss trail then leads toward a closed garage area. Use the nearby Power Core Station, take an electrical charge to the rooftop power box, and open the route into the garage where the Orb is located.
3. The third is north of the Evac Building near a group of shielded airborne enemies. Clear them, use the nearby rooftop for height, then jump across to the Orb.

After the third Orb breaks, return to the main Evac Building entrance.

The front door is now usable.

If the door is still sealed, one of the three exterior Orbs is still active. Do not search inside another building for a key.

<h2 id="enter-evac-building">
  Enter the Evac Building and take the elevator to Floor 12
</h2>

The lobby immediately shows how badly the evacuation failed.

FBC personnel are dead, the civilians are nowhere near the processing areas where they should have been, and the Hiss presence gets stronger as Dylan moves deeper inside.

Use the elevator on the **left side of the lobby**.

Choose the **12th Floor**.

Once you arrive, follow the available route into the large combat area and clear the Hiss.

When the room is secure, move toward the **south side** and look for the hallway with the **blue light above it**.

That is the progression route.

The rest of the room contains useful supplies, but the tremors are not solved here.

<h2 id="floor-12-route">
  Follow the blue-lit hallway and scaffolding
</h2>

The hallway leads Dylan out through a scaffolding route before looping back into another part of the building.

Stay with the constructed path rather than trying to drop back toward street level.

When you re-enter the building, there is a **Health Point Crate** on the left before the route continues.

Grab it while you are here.

Shortly afterward, Dylan is pulled into another disturbing memory sequence. Continue forward when control returns.

The next important room contains another **Hiss Orb**.

Destroy it.

This Orb is different from the three outside: its job is blocking progress deeper through the building rather than sealing the main entrance.

Once it is gone, another elevator becomes available.

Take that elevator to the **15th Floor**.

<h2 id="find-source-of-tremors">
  The civilians are the source of the tremors
</h2>

Floor 15 shifts the mission away from a normal Hiss-clearing operation.

Move through the long room filled with red resonance.

The building begins pushing Dylan into another sequence of memories and Hiss influence as he gets closer to the center of the disturbance.

Keep moving forward.

You eventually reach what remains of the evacuated civilians.

This is the point where the original objective effectively fails.

There is no switch to turn off and no Resonant boss to kill.

Dylan realizes the civilians themselves have become the source of the escalating resonance.

The tremors cannot simply be shut down from inside the room.

Do not remain here searching for an interaction with the mass.

The mission now changes to finding the one surviving FBC agent still inside the building.

<h2 id="find-arish">
  Arish is alive beyond the tremor source
</h2>

Continue through the sequence until Dylan reaches **Arish**.

He survived after the rest of his Ranger team died, but his Black Rock injector is almost empty and he is already struggling under the Hiss exposure.

Arish initially distrusts Dylan, but there is no dialogue choice here that changes the route.

He needs to leave immediately.

Turn around and retrace the path you used to reach him.

Arish follows behind.

You do not need to protect him like a normal escort NPC through every combat encounter, but the objective cannot finish until the elevator route is restored.

<h2 id="get-arish-out">
  Take Arish back to the elevator
</h2>

Follow the previous route toward the elevator.

Arish is already weak enough that taking the stairs is not a realistic fallback. When you reach the elevator controls, he opens the way and tells Dylan to use the lift.

The escape immediately fails.

Hiss material is blocking the elevator system.

Destroy the first Orb or obstruction stopping the elevator.

Once that section is cleared, talk to Arish again.

He gives Dylan a **Level 2 Keycard**.

This permanently upgrades your FBC access to **Security Clearance Level 2**, and it is also the key to finishing the escape route.

<h2 id="level-2-keycard">
  Use Arish's Level 2 Keycard to reach the second obstruction
</h2>

With Security Clearance Level 2, open the nearby restricted door.

Return into the adjacent section of the floor where Dylan fought the earlier Hiss.

There is another Orb tied to the elevator blockage.

Destroy it.

Do not return to Arish after every enemy unless the objective explicitly asks for him. Once the second Orb is gone, the building responds by throwing another Hiss wave into the room.

Clear that fight.

The elevator can now be used again.

Before leaving, the new Level 2 clearance also opens several nearby locked rooms containing material crates. They are worth checking while the floor is safe because returning later means repeating the trip through the building.

<h2 id="escape-building">
  Use the yellow-lit hallway and return to the Ground Floor
</h2>

When you are ready to leave, go back out through the hallway marked by the **yellow light**.

Call the elevator.

Choose the **Ground Floor**.

You do not need to escort Arish physically all the way through the lobby after the elevator is working. Once Dylan has cleared the route, Arish can get out.

Back in the lobby, the new Level 2 clearance also opens the room opposite the Customization closet.

The **SHUM Arcade** for this area is inside if you want to collect it before leaving.

Otherwise, walk out through the Evac Building's main entrance.

Evacuation Procedures ends as Dylan exits.

<h2 id="what-happens-next">
  The tremors are still active after the mission
</h2>

Saving Arish does not solve the actual crisis.

The civilians remain the source of the Hiss resonance, and Dylan reports that there is no way to stop the tremors from inside the evacuation building.

Arish survives and returns to the Field Office.

That is your next useful destination.

When Dylan gets back to Central, Arish and Pope begin turning what you learned at the Evac Site into a new plan involving the resonance below the city and the **Cancellation Field Device**.

The story then begins branching into several objectives instead of keeping Dylan on one straight mission.

For the overall order from this point, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main progression walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="rewards">
  What you get from Evacuation Procedures
</h2>

The most important practical reward is **Security Clearance Level 2** from Arish.

That access level opens doors that were previously unusable around Manhattan and remains useful beyond the Evacuation Zone.

Completing the mission also awards:

- **1 Living Shard**
- **Agent Uniform**
- **Rescue Mission** achievement / trophy

The story reward is just as important: Arish returns alive, and the FBC now knows that the evacuation-site tremors cannot be stopped with a normal strike team.

<h2 id="evac-building-problems">
  Evac Building doors, floors, and elevator problems
</h2>

**The Evac Building front door will not open.**  
One of the three exterior Hiss Orbs is still active. Follow the red trails away from the building to find the missing one.

**The southwest Orb looks sealed behind a garage door.**  
Use the nearby Power Core Station, carry the charge to the rooftop power box, then enter the opened garage.

**You are inside the building but do not know which floor to choose.**  
Use Floor **12** first. After clearing the internal Hiss Orb, the next elevator takes Dylan to Floor **15**.

**You found the civilians but the objective does not complete.**  
They are the source of the tremors, not an interaction puzzle. Continue forward until Dylan finds Arish.

**The elevator is blocked while escorting Arish.**  
The elevator is still the intended escape route. Destroy the Hiss obstruction, speak with Arish for the Level 2 Keycard, then clear the second Orb behind the newly accessible door.

**You escaped but the tremors are still happening.**  
That is the correct story state. The mission proves the FBC cannot stop the source directly; return to Central for the next plan.
