<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
When the story stops giving you one obvious destination, check the other active main objectives. The biggest mid-game gate requires **Power Lines, the Sinkhole operation, and the required Resonants** to converge before the story moves on. Later, follow the Underpass, Jesse Fault, and Hedron objectives by their active quest names rather than trying to finish Manhattan zone by zone.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-progression-works">
  How CONTROL Resonant progression actually works
</h2>

CONTROL Resonant begins like a linear story and gradually stops behaving like one.

The opening moves Dylan out of the Oldest House, into Manhattan, through the first FBC infrastructure objectives, and toward Zoe and the Central Field Office. After that, several quest chains can remain active at the same time.

A story objective may wait while Dylan restores power somewhere else. A Jesse Fault can unlock traversal needed in another region. A Resonant fight can be required before Pope and Arish are ready to continue their work.

That is why the most reliable question is not always "What is the top quest in my log?"

It is:

**Which unfinished objective is blocking the next progression gate?**

<h2 id="main-route">
  Main story route at a glance
</h2>

The overall progression is easier to follow when you treat it as a series of gates:

1. complete **Orientation** and reach Manhattan;
2. restore the **Central Checkpoint** and meet Zoe;
3. reach the **Central Field Office**;
4. complete **Evacuation Procedures** and recover Arish;
5. prepare the CFD through **Power Lines**, **Into the Sinkhole**, and the required Resonants;
6. clear the Central and West Incursion Resonants so the story can continue through **Enemy of My Enemy**;
7. survive the CFD failure and return below the city;
8. follow **Recursions and Iterations** through the Underpass;
9. advance the Jesse Fault chain when dimensional traversal becomes relevant again;
10. return to the Oldest House crisis and follow the Hedron links through the final sequence.

Several of these steps overlap. The game does not expect every item in that list to appear one after another without interruptions.

<h2 id="opening">
  Orientation and the first route into Manhattan
</h2>

The opening is the most straightforward part of the game.

Follow Jesse through the dimensional sequence, complete the first Fault interaction, and continue until Dylan reaches Manhattan and answers the FBC radio call. Zoe takes over as his main contact outside the Oldest House and directs him toward the hotel and the first pieces of FBC infrastructure.

At this stage, follow those infrastructure objectives instead of roaming deep into Manhattan.

Set up the mapping equipment when prompted, restore access through Central, and keep moving toward Zoe's position. The city becomes much easier to understand once the first checkpoint and watchtower briefing are complete.

<h2 id="central-checkpoint">
  Restore the Central Checkpoint before pushing farther into the city
</h2>

The Central Checkpoint is a genuine progression block.

Until it has power, the route through Central does not properly open.

Follow the maintenance objective, deal with the electrical charge sequence, and restore the checkpoint before trying to treat the surrounding districts as fully available.

The exact Power Core route and the failure states for carrying the charge are covered in the <Link href="/control-resonant/central-checkpoint">Central Checkpoint walkthrough</Link>.

Once the checkpoint is active, continue to Zoe.

Her briefing establishes the three areas that matter immediately:

- **Central**, where the FBC is operating;
- **Evacuation Zone** to the east;
- **West Incursion Zone** to the west.

West Incursion already shows heavy Patterning, but the Evacuation Zone has the more urgent story call.

<h2 id="central-field-office">
  Reach the Central Field Office before chasing distant objectives
</h2>

After the watchtower briefing, continue through the underground supply route to the **Central Field Office**.

This is where the story stops behaving like a single chain.

Pope begins gathering data about the Resonant phenomenon, Arish is tied to the containment effort, and later missions repeatedly return Dylan to this location for briefings.

Speak to the available FBC personnel and let the current story objectives populate before spending a long stretch on optional Manhattan activity.

West Incursion becomes important here, but you do not need to finish everything in that district immediately.

<h2 id="evacuation-procedures">
  Finish Evacuation Procedures and get Arish out
</h2>

The Evacuation Zone mission is more linear than the open streets around it suggest.

Once Dylan reaches the Evac Building, keep moving inward. The source of the tremors is inside the structure, and exploring the exterior does not advance the investigation.

Eventually Dylan discovers that the **civilians themselves are the source of the tremors**.

That discovery advances the objective. There is no separate machine in the room that needs to be shut down.

Arish becomes the next priority.

Find him, follow the escape sequence, and restore the blocked elevator route so he can leave the building. For the complete interior route, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/evacuation-zone">Evacuation Zone walkthrough</Link>.

Once Arish is safe, return toward the Field Office when the story calls Dylan back.

<h2 id="cfd-gate">
  The CFD creates the first major multi-objective gate
</h2>

The next major plan is the **Cancellation Field Device**, or CFD.

Arish and Pope cannot complete it from one mission alone. Dylan is given three different requirements:

- route enough electrical power into the project;
- gather the required resonance from the Sinkhole;
- provide more Resonant data.

This is the first point where finishing a long mission can produce no immediate story transition.

Completing Power Lines alone is not enough.

Returning from the Sinkhole alone is not enough.

Killing one Resonant alone may not be enough either.

When the story appears to pause here, check which part of the CFD preparation is still active.

<h2 id="power-lines">
  Complete all four Power Lines station routes
</h2>

The power branch sends Dylan through several electrical stations.

The first three are:

1. **Perimeter Station**
2. **East Park Station**
3. **Factory Station**

The last complication is the **Vanished Station**.

That final station uses two calibration areas, **Platform A** and **Platform B**. Completing only one side does not finish the network.

The full station order, Factory cooling problem, Vanished Station route, and both platform calibrations are in the <Link href="/control-resonant/power-lines">Power Lines guide</Link>.

Once both Vanished Station platforms are complete, the FBC can use the restored network for the CFD.

Return to the remaining CFD objectives rather than waiting at the station for another story event.

<h2 id="into-the-sinkhole">
  Install the Probe Relays, then escape by the new route
</h2>

The Sinkhole branch begins with Dylan descending by diving bell and escorting the Probe Relays toward Bedrock.

Install the relays where directed.

After that, the area changes.

The diving bell is no longer reachable by simply reversing the descent route, and following the same path back will not solve the escape. That change is intentional rather than a sign that you missed an interaction.

Continue through the altered Sinkhole until Zoe's guidance gives Dylan a workable way out.

The detailed relay placement and escape sequence are in <Link href="/control-resonant/into-the-sinkhole">Into the Sinkhole</Link>.

Once Dylan reaches the surface, return to the current FBC objective instead of immediately going back underground.

<h2 id="required-resonants">
  Defeat the Central and West Incursion Resonants when the story needs them
</h2>

The other major requirement is Resonant data.

The two important early checks are:

- **The Central Resonant — Deserter**
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant — Dancer**

The Deserter is inside the Patterned Factory. The Dancer is in the West Incursion Theater.

Their individual routes, mechanics, and ability rewards are handled separately in the <Link href="/control-resonant/patterned-factory">Patterned Factory and Deserter guide</Link> and <Link href="/control-resonant/dancer">Dancer guide</Link>.

The West Incursion route also depends on Jesse progression. When impossible gravity prevents Dylan from reaching the Theater, complete the Incursion Fault rather than trying to force the surrounding streets.

That sequence is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

Once both required Resonants are down, **Enemy of My Enemy** can move forward when the other story requirements are also satisfied.

For the remaining Resonants later in the game, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants list</Link>.

<h2 id="cfd-test">
  Return for the CFD test after all required branches converge
</h2>

When the power, Sinkhole, and Resonant requirements have come together, Pope and Arish can proceed with the CFD.

The plan begins cautiously.

The FBC wants controlled testing, but the situation escalates beyond the original experiment. Dylan eventually has to reach the device and stop the activation rather than allow the Patterning event to continue.

Destroying the CFD changes the direction of the campaign.

The device was supposed to contain the crisis. Instead, the Patterning is driven deeper into the Sinkhole, forcing Pope to investigate what is happening below the city rather than trying another immediate containment solution.

That is the transition into the Underpass portion of the story.

<h2 id="underpass">
  Return below the city for Recursions and Iterations
</h2>

Pope sends a four-person research team into the changed Sinkhole.

Their communications fail.

Dylan follows their trail into the **Underpass**, where the expedition has become tangled in overlapping versions of the same events.

Do not treat this as a repeat of Into the Sinkhole.

The objective has changed from transporting equipment to finding the researchers, restoring their systems, and recovering what they learned about the Patterning.

The research sequence has its own power puzzle, timeline shifts, data transmission, and relay work beneath the Oldest House. Follow the <Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass walkthrough</Link> once Dylan enters that region.

Two other objectives can also appear there, and their names matter:

- **Jesse Sightings: Underpass** continues the Jesse Fault chain;
- **The Underpass Resonant** leads toward the Spire, Astral Plane, and Physicist.

For the Fault, continue with <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>. For the Resonant, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist and Astral Keys guide</Link>.

Do not search the research team's yellow-tape routes for Astral Keys; those objectives share the region but not the same solution.

<h2 id="jesse-progression">
  Follow the Jesse Fault chain when traversal becomes the blocker
</h2>

Jesse's appearances are not only story scenes.

The early Faults change what Dylan can physically reach.

The **Incursion Fault** is what makes West Incursion's altered gravity route manageable. The **Subway Fault** later gives Dylan another way to cross dimensional gaps that normal movement cannot solve.

When a route ends at a gap or gravity state that feels physically impossible, check the active Jesse objective before spending time searching nearby rooms for a hidden switch.

The three Fault sequences and their order are all covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

Later, the Underpass investigation reconnects this dimensional problem with the wider Patterning crisis.

<h2 id="late-game">
  Follow active emergencies instead of clearing every district
</h2>

The later game moves Dylan between Manhattan, the Underpass, the Oldest House, and Unknown.

At this point, clearing a region completely before leaving it is usually unnecessary.

The Park, for example, contains the Collective and several optional activities, but the main story does not require Dylan to finish the entire district the moment it opens. The <Link href="/control-resonant/the-park">Park and Collective route</Link> can be completed when you want its Resonant reward or broader completion progress.

Unknown creates a similar distinction.

Some objectives there belong to the Hedron crisis, while **The Unknown Resonant** leads to the optional Co-Director encounter. Read the active quest title before assuming every Patterned Field Office area belongs to the same mission.

The Co-Director fight and Command reward are covered in the <Link href="/control-resonant/co-director">Co-Director guide</Link>.

<h2 id="hedron">
  Follow the Hedron links when the crisis reaches the Oldest House
</h2>

Late in the story, the Patterning reaches the Oldest House itself.

Pope identifies a **Hedron** as the source of the new event. The entity has linked itself to the House and is pushing the crisis far beyond the earlier Manhattan containment problem.

The Patterned Unknown spaces, research balloons, link locations, and late Oldest House route are covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">The Hedron Link</Link>.

This section of the story eventually carries Dylan through the final confrontation rather than returning him to another normal Manhattan progression gate.

<h2 id="ending">
  Continue after the final confrontation
</h2>

The game does not stop immediately when the last major fight ends.

Continue through the scripted aftermath and epilogue.

Dylan returns to FBC life, receives his belongings, and can reconnect with characters after the immediate crisis has passed.

If you left optional Resonants, Side Stories, taxis, Park activities, or other Manhattan objectives unfinished, this is the point where cleaning them up becomes easier because story progression is no longer competing for attention.

Use the <Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest checklist</Link> to see which mission chains are still incomplete.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Main story stuck fixes
</h2>

### Manhattan opened but I cannot get past Central

Restore the **Central Checkpoint** first. The early electrical objective is required before Central functions as the main route into the city.

### I finished Evacuation Procedures but the story split into several missions

That is expected. After Arish returns, CFD preparation requires multiple branches rather than one uninterrupted quest.

### I completed Power Lines but nothing major happened

Check **Into the Sinkhole** and the required Resonants. Power is only one part of the CFD preparation.

### I returned from the Sinkhole but the story still will not advance

Check whether **The Central Resonant** and **The West Incursion Zone Resonant** are complete.

### I can see the West Incursion Theater but cannot reach it

Complete the **Incursion Fault** first. The route relies on the gravity traversal gained during the Jesse sequence.

### I reached an impossible dimensional gap later in the story

Check the Jesse quest chain. A later Fault unlocks the traversal Dylan needs for gaps that normal movement cannot cross.

### I entered the Underpass and now have several unrelated objectives

Read the active quest name. The research expedition, Jesse Sightings: Underpass, and The Underpass Resonant all use the same broader region but lead to different routes.

### I finished the Underpass research but do not know where the story goes next

Follow the new FBC call and Jesse-related objective rather than remaining at the Research Site. The Underpass investigation is a transition back into the larger Patterning crisis.

### I am in Unknown and the Field Office route does not match the Co-Director objective

Check whether the active mission says **Sever the Hedron Links**. The Hedron story and The Unknown Resonant both use Patterned areas in Unknown.

### I reached the end of the final fight but the game has not finished

Keep playing through the aftermath. The epilogue continues after the main confrontation before Dylan returns to free exploration.
