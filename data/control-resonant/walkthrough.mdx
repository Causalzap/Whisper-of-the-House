<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
CONTROL Resonant is mostly linear until Dylan reaches Central. The first major
story gate comes later, when the **CFD** cannot proceed until **Power Lines**,
**Into the Sinkhole**, and the required **Central and West Incursion
Resonants** have all moved far enough forward.

After that, follow the active quest name rather than trying to clear one zone
at a time. If a route becomes physically impossible, check the **Search for
Jesse** chain before looking for another hidden path.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The opening hours make CONTROL Resonant look like a straightforward trip from
one objective marker to the next. That changes once Central becomes Dylan's
base of operations. Power restoration, Resonant investigations, Jesse Faults,
and the main containment plan begin running beside one another, and finishing
the objective directly in front of you does not always produce the next story
mission immediately.

The campaign becomes much easier to follow if you treat each major step as a
gate. Finish what the current gate actually requires, return to the FBC when
the story calls you back, and resist the urge to completely clear every region
the first time you enter it.

If you only need to check whether a named mission or Side Story is still
unfinished, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest list</Link>.
The route below follows the main campaign and the points where another quest
can stop that campaign from moving.

<h2 id="main-route">
  CONTROL Resonant main story route
</h2>

The broad progression is:

1. Complete **Orientation** and leave the Oldest House.
2. Reach Manhattan and restore the **Central Checkpoint**.
3. Meet Zoe and reach the **Central Field Office**.
4. Complete **Evacuation Procedures** and rescue Arish.
5. Prepare the **Cancellation Field Device** through Power Lines, the Sinkhole operation, and Resonant data.
6. Defeat the required **Central** and **West Incursion** Resonants.
7. Return for the CFD test and follow the failure beneath Manhattan.
8. Complete **Recursions and Iterations** in the Underpass.
9. Advance Jesse's Fault chain whenever dimensional traversal becomes the blocker.
10. Return to the Oldest House crisis and follow the **Hedron** objectives into the final sequence.

Several of those steps overlap. The middle of the game is not supposed to
produce one perfectly continuous chain of quest markers.

<h2 id="opening">
  Orientation: leave the Oldest House and reach Manhattan
</h2>

The opening is the simplest part of the campaign. Follow Jesse through the
dimensional sequence, complete the first Fault interaction, and continue until
Dylan reaches Manhattan and answers the FBC radio call.

Zoe becomes his main contact outside the Oldest House and starts pointing him
toward the infrastructure the FBC still has working in the city. At this stage
there is little benefit in wandering deep into side districts. Complete the
mapping step when it appears, restore access through Central, and keep moving
toward Zoe.

The mapping system itself is covered in the
<Link href="/control-resonant/map">CONTROL Resonant map guide</Link>. For the
opening story, the important result is simply getting enough of Central online
to continue.

<h2 id="central-checkpoint">
  Restore the Central Checkpoint before exploring farther
</h2>

The Central Checkpoint is not optional progression. Until it has power, the
route through Central does not properly open.

Follow the maintenance objective, complete the electrical sequence, and restore
the checkpoint before trying to push deeper into Manhattan. If carrying the
charge or the Power Core route is where you are stuck, continue with the
<Link href="/control-resonant/central-checkpoint"> Central Checkpoint walkthrough</Link>
rather than searching the surrounding streets for another entrance.

Once the checkpoint is active, continue to Zoe. Her briefing establishes the
three regions that matter immediately: Central around the FBC, the Evacuation
Zone to the east, and the heavily Patterned West Incursion Zone to the west.

The evacuation call is the more urgent story lead.

<h2 id="field-office-evacuation">
  Reach the Field Office, then complete Evacuation Procedures
</h2>

After the Watchtower, follow the underground supply route into the
**Central Field Office**. This becomes the place Dylan repeatedly returns to
for briefings as Pope studies the Resonants and Arish helps organize the
containment response.

Speak to the available FBC personnel and let the new objectives appear before
spending too much time on optional activities. West Incursion is already open
enough to matter, but the main story first needs Dylan in the Evacuation Zone.

Once inside the Evac Building, keep following the interior route. The tremors
are not caused by a separate machine hidden somewhere in the building; Dylan
eventually discovers that the **trapped civilians themselves are producing the
resonance**. That discovery moves the investigation forward.

Arish then becomes the immediate concern. Reach him, survive the escape, and
restore the blocked elevator route so he can get out. The interior floors,
exterior Hiss Orbs, and elevator sequence are covered in the
<Link href="/control-resonant/evacuation-zone">Evacuation Procedures walkthrough</Link>.

When Arish is safe, return toward the Field Office when the story calls Dylan
back. The campaign now begins its first major split.

<h2 id="cfd-gate">
  The CFD is the first major multi-objective story gate
</h2>

Arish and Pope's next plan is the **Cancellation Field Device**, or CFD. It
cannot be prepared through a single mission.

The FBC needs three things to come together: enough electrical power, the
resonance work from the Sinkhole, and additional Resonant data. This is why
finishing one long branch can end with no new story transition.

<h3 id="power-lines">
  Restore the power network
</h3>

**Power Lines** takes Dylan through the electrical stations needed to support
the CFD. The final complication is the Vanished Station, where both
**Platform A** and **Platform B** have to be calibrated before the branch is
actually complete.

Completing one platform is not enough, and restoring power does not immediately
finish CFD preparation because the other two branches still matter.

For the station order, Factory cooling problem, Vanished Station route, and
both calibrations, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/power-lines">Power Lines guide</Link>.

After the network is restored, leave the station and return to whatever CFD
requirement is still active.

<h3 id="into-the-sinkhole">
  Finish the Sinkhole operation
</h3>

**Into the Sinkhole** begins with Dylan descending by diving bell and escorting
Probe Relays toward Bedrock. Install the relays where the mission directs you.

The important change comes afterward: the route back is no longer the same
route Dylan used to descend. Trying to reverse the trip and reach the diving
bell again will not solve the escape.

Continue through the altered Sinkhole and follow Zoe's guidance until Dylan
finds the new way out. Relay placement and the changed return route are covered
in <Link href="/control-resonant/into-the-sinkhole">Into the Sinkhole</Link>.

Once Dylan reaches the surface, do not immediately go back underground looking
for another trigger. Check the remaining CFD requirements.

<h3 id="required-resonants">
  Defeat the Central and West Incursion Resonants
</h3>

The two early Resonants that matter for this story gate are the **Deserter** in
Central and the **Dancer** in West Incursion.

The Deserter route runs through the Patterned Factory. The Dancer waits in the
West Incursion Theater, but that approach depends on Jesse progression because
the altered gravity can block Dylan before he ever reaches the fight.

For the Central route, use
<Link href="/control-resonant/patterned-factory">Patterned Factory and Deserter</Link>.
For the Theater fight, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/dancer">Dancer guide</Link>.

If West Incursion ends at a gravity route Dylan clearly cannot traverse,
complete the **Incursion Fault** through
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link> rather
than trying to force the surrounding streets.

Once both required Resonants are defeated and the other CFD branches are done,
**Enemy of My Enemy** can move the campaign forward.

<h2 id="cfd-test">
  Return for the CFD test after all three branches are ready
</h2>

When the restored power network, Sinkhole work, and Resonant data have all
converged, Pope and Arish can finally proceed with the CFD.

The test does not end the Manhattan crisis. The situation escalates, and Dylan
eventually has to reach the device and stop its activation rather than allow the
Patterning event to continue.

Destroying the CFD changes the direction of the story. The device was intended
to contain the crisis, but the Patterning is instead driven deeper below the
city. Pope's attention shifts away from another immediate containment attempt
and toward what is happening beneath Manhattan.

That transition leads into **Recursions and Iterations**.

<h2 id="underpass">
  Recursions and Iterations: follow the research team into the Underpass
</h2>

Pope sends a research team below the city after the CFD failure. Their
communications stop, and Dylan follows their trail into the **Underpass**.

This is not another version of Into the Sinkhole. The goal is no longer to
transport equipment into Bedrock. Dylan is now looking for the researchers,
restoring their systems, and recovering what they learned about the changing
Patterning.

The Underpass is also where quest names become especially important. Three
different objectives can occupy the same broad region:

- **Recursions and Iterations** follows the research expedition.
- **Jesse Sightings: Underpass** continues Jesse's Fault story.
- **The Underpass Resonant** leads toward the Spire, Astral Plane, and Physicist.

Do not assume a marker belongs to the research-team route simply because it is
inside the Underpass. If the objective has changed to Astral Keys or the
Physicist, switch to the
<Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist and Astral Keys guide</Link>.
If Jesse is the active objective, continue with
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

For the research expedition itself, including its power work, timeline changes,
and transmission sequence, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="jesse-progression">
  Check Jesse's Faults when movement becomes impossible
</h2>

Jesse's appearances are tied to more than story scenes. The early Faults change
what Dylan can physically reach elsewhere in Manhattan.

The **Incursion Fault** solves the altered-gravity problem that becomes
important in West Incursion. The **Subway Fault** later gives Dylan another way
to cross dimensional gaps that normal movement cannot handle.

That gives you a useful rule for the middle and later game: if an objective
looks reachable on the map but the actual gap, gravity state, or dimensional
geometry makes the route physically impossible, check the active Jesse quest
before spending ten minutes searching the room for a hidden switch.

All three Fault sequences are covered in
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

<h2 id="late-game">
  Follow the active emergency instead of clearing every region
</h2>

The later campaign starts moving Dylan between Manhattan, the Underpass, the
Oldest House, and Unknown. Trying to completely finish one district before
leaving it becomes increasingly inefficient.

The Park is a good example. Its Collective fight and optional activities can be
finished when you want the Resonant reward or broader completion, but the main
story does not require you to clear the entire region the moment it becomes
available. If you decide to deal with it, the full route is in
<Link href="/control-resonant/the-park">The Park and Collective guide</Link>.

Unknown creates a similar overlap. Story objectives connected to the Hedron can
occupy the same broader region as **The Unknown Resonant**, but they are not the
same task. If the active quest says **Sever the Hedron Links**, follow that
objective rather than assuming the nearest Patterned route belongs to the
Co-Director.

The optional Co-Director encounter and its reward are covered in the
<Link href="/control-resonant/co-director">Co-Director guide</Link>.

<h2 id="hedron">
  Follow The Hedron Link when the crisis reaches the Oldest House
</h2>

The late story turns back toward the Oldest House when the Patterning reaches
the House itself.

Pope identifies a **Hedron** behind the new event, and the immediate problem
shifts away from the mid-game CFD work. From here, follow **The Hedron Link**
and then **Sever the Hedron Links** as those objectives appear.

another large Manhattan preparation gate.

<h2 id="ending">
  Keep playing after the final confrontation
</h2>

The campaign does not stop the instant the last major fight ends.

Continue through the scripted aftermath and epilogue. Dylan returns to FBC
life, receives his belongings, and can reconnect with characters after the
immediate crisis has passed.

This is also the cleanest point to return to optional work that never fit
comfortably into the main route. Unfinished Resonants, taxis, Park activities,
Side Stories, Mapping Pylons, and other Manhattan objectives can be handled
without another story gate competing for attention.

If you are not sure what remains, compare the unfinished entries against the
<Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest list</Link>.

<h2 id="stuck">
  If the main story stops moving
</h2>

Most apparent progression blocks come from one of a small number of missed
requirements.

**Central is still closed.**  
Restore the **Central Checkpoint** before trying to push farther into Manhattan.

**Power Lines is finished but the CFD story has not advanced.**  
Check **Into the Sinkhole**, the **Central Resonant**, and the **West Incursion
Zone Resonant**. Power is only one part of the preparation.

**The Theater is visible but Dylan cannot reach it.**  
Complete the **Incursion Fault**. The West Incursion route depends on the
gravity traversal gained through Jesse's sequence.

**A later dimensional gap cannot be crossed.**  
Check the Search for Jesse chain. A Fault may be supplying the movement ability
the route expects.

**Several objectives are active in the Underpass.**  
Read the quest title before following the nearest marker. Recursions and
Iterations, Jesse Sightings: Underpass, and The Underpass Resonant use the same
region but lead to different objectives.

**Unknown has a marker that does not match the Co-Director route.**  
Check whether the active story mission is **Sever the Hedron Links**. The
Hedron story and the Unknown Resonant overlap geographically without sharing
the same route.

**The final fight ended but free exploration has not returned.**  
Continue through the aftermath. The epilogue still has to play before Dylan
returns to unfinished Manhattan activities.
