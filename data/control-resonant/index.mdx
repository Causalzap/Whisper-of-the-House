The easiest way to get lost in **CONTROL Resonant** is to treat Manhattan like a normal open-world map.

That works for a while. Then Central starts feeding Dylan several jobs at once, Jesse's Faults begin changing what he can physically reach, Resonants start blocking story progress, and later regions reuse the same space for completely different objectives.

When I lose the route, I stop following the nearest marker and read the **exact quest name** first.

<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  Start with the quest name, then decide what kind of problem you have
</h2>

Once Dylan reaches Central, three things can move independently.

The **main crisis** pushes the story through Manhattan, the Sinkhole, the
Underpass, and eventually the Hedron. **Search for Jesse** unlocks traversal
that other routes begin to expect. **Regional Resonants** sit beside both of
those, and some of them must be defeated before the next story step appears.

That creates three different kinds of "stuck":

- you do not know which mission comes next;
- you know the mission, but the route is physically blocked;
- you know where to go, but the location itself is hard to place.

Those are different problems and they usually have different answers.

For story order, follow the
<Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link>. For active
missions and their relationships, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest list</Link>. For zones, Map Kits,
and region orientation, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/map">map guide</Link>.

<h2 id="early-manhattan">
  Central is the first place where the game starts to branch
</h2>

The opening stays fairly directed until the route through Central begins to
open.

If the city still feels closed, the first thing to check is the
**Central Checkpoint**. That electrical sequence is a real progression gate,
not optional infrastructure. The
<Link href="/control-resonant/central-checkpoint">Central Checkpoint route</Link>
covers the Power Core relay problem and the point where Central properly opens.

After Zoe's Watchtower briefing, the city stops behaving like a sequence of
self-contained chapters. The Evacuation Zone becomes the urgent story route,
West Incursion starts mattering for Resonant and Jesse progression, and the
Field Office becomes the place where several objectives reconnect.

At that point, finishing everything visible in one district before leaving it
usually creates more backtracking than progress.

<h2 id="evacuation-zone">
  Evacuation Procedures is the next major story handoff
</h2>

When the active mission sends Dylan east, follow it into the Evacuation Zone
rather than continuing to clear Central.

The Evac Building investigation leads to Arish and the wider containment plan.
If you are stuck inside the building, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/evacuation-zone">Evacuation Procedures walkthrough</Link>
for the Hiss Orbs, interior floors, Arish, and the escape.

Once that mission is finished, the story begins asking Dylan to solve several
different problems at the same time. This is the point where checking only the
top marker in the Quest Log becomes unreliable.

<h2 id="cfd-plan">
  When the CFD plan appears, expect several objectives to stay active together
</h2>

The **Cancellation Field Device** is the first large story gate built from
multiple branches.

The FBC needs restored power, work from the Sinkhole, and enough Resonant data.
That means completing one long objective does not guarantee an immediate story
transition.

If **Power Lines** is active, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/power-lines">Power Lines walkthrough</Link> for
the station network and Vanished Station.

If the mission changes to **Into the Sinkhole**, switch to the
<Link href="/control-resonant/into-the-sinkhole">Sinkhole route</Link> for the
diving bell, Bedrock, Probe Relays, and the changed escape route.

If both of those feel finished but the story still refuses to move, check the
required Resonants before repeating either mission.

<h2 id="required-resonants">
  Enemy of My Enemy depends on the Deserter and Dancer
</h2>

The two Resonants that matter most to the early story gate are:

- **Deserter** — the Central Resonant in the Patterned Factory;
- **Dancer** — the West Incursion Resonant in the Theater.

Both need to be dealt with before **Enemy of My Enemy** can move forward.

If the active objective is the Central Resonant, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/patterned-factory">Patterned Factory and Deserter route</Link>.
If Dylan is already headed toward the Theater, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/dancer">Dancer guide</Link> once the fight itself
becomes the problem.

The full group of six Resonants, their regions, requirements, and ability
rewards are listed in
<Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants</Link>.

<h2 id="west-incursion">
  If West Incursion looks impossible, the missing answer may be Shift
</h2>

West Incursion is where the city stops respecting normal street geometry.

Seeing the Theater does not mean Dylan can reach it by finding a better road.
The route eventually runs into altered gravity around the Parking Garage, and
that is where Jesse's story becomes part of the solution.

Follow the
<Link href="/control-resonant/west-incursion-zone">West Incursion route</Link>
until the problem becomes traversal rather than navigation. Once the active
objective moves into **The Incursion Fault**, continue with
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

Completing that Fault unlocks **Shift**. After that, walls, rotated roads, and
other gravity-altered surfaces become usable routes, so there is no reason to
keep looking for an ordinary street-level entrance to the Theater.

<h2 id="search-for-jesse">
  Jesse's Faults change where Dylan can physically go
</h2>

The Jesse missions are easy to misread as side material because they can appear
alongside larger story objectives. The first two Faults are much more important
than that.

The sequence is:

**Incursion Fault → Subway Fault → Underpass Fault**

The first two provide traversal that later routes expect Dylan to have. If an
objective marker is obviously beyond a gap or gravity state that normal
movement cannot solve, check the Jesse chain before searching the room for
another switch, door, or staircase.

The complete Fault sequence is in
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

<h2 id="underpass">
  In the Underpass, the region name is not enough
</h2>

The Underpass can hold several unrelated objectives at the same time.

If Dylan is following the missing research team, restoring systems, dealing
with time fluctuations, or moving beneath the Oldest House, continue with the
<Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass walkthrough</Link>.

If the quest name changes to a Jesse objective, return to
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

If the active mission is **The Underpass Resonant**, the route instead turns
toward the Spire and Astral Plane. The
<Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist guide</Link> covers the
Astral Keys, locked doors, boss, and the Spike / Astral Rebuke choice.

Those objectives can all exist in the same broad region without sharing the
same route. This is one of the clearest places where reading the quest title is
more useful than following the closest marker.

<h2 id="hedron">
  The Hedron objectives take over the late story
</h2>

Once the crisis reaches the Oldest House again, the main story stops revolving
around the earlier CFD preparation.

If the Quest Log names **The Hedron Link** or **Sever the Hedron Links**, follow
that thread. The route moves through increasingly distorted space, and
research balloons and Probe Relays become part of reaching the next connection.

The
<Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">Hedron Link route</Link> covers
those late objectives.

If your active quest still says Power Lines, Into the Sinkhole, a Jesse Fault,
or an earlier Resonant mission, stay with that unfinished work instead of
jumping ahead just because you can see a later area.

<h2 id="unknown">
  Unknown can point Dylan toward several different late-game objectives
</h2>

Unknown is easy to misread because the same distorted region can contain story
progression, Pope's Research, and the **Unknown Resonant**.

A visible island or FBC structure is not automatically reachable from the
platform Dylan is standing on. Some routes depend on progression gained
elsewhere, and others belong to a different active mission entirely.

If the current objective is the Unknown Resonant, continue to the
<Link href="/control-resonant/co-director">Co-Director guide</Link> when Dylan
reaches that encounter.

If the Quest Log still points toward Hedron work, follow the Hedron objective
instead of treating every structure in Unknown as part of the Resonant route.

<h2 id="park">
  The Park can wait until its own objectives matter
</h2>

The Park is dominated by Mold and has its own surface, sewer, parking, and
underground paths. It also contains the **Collective**, but the main story does
not require Dylan to clear the whole region the moment it becomes accessible.

When the Park Resonant is the objective you actually want to finish, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/the-park">Park and Collective route</Link>. That
sequence runs through the Mold Gateway, four unusual Mold strains, and the
Collective fight.

If another story mission is currently pulling Dylan elsewhere, there is no need
to force The Park into the route simply because the region has opened.

<h2 id="map">
  Use the map when the objective is clear but the location is not
</h2>

Sometimes the problem is much simpler: you know exactly what Dylan needs to do,
but you cannot work out which part of Manhattan contains it.

That is when the
<Link href="/control-resonant/map">CONTROL Resonant map guide</Link> is useful.

Mapped regions use a Map Kit with one central Pylon and three Sensors. Activate
the Sensors, return to the Pylon, and use it again to bring the zone data
online.

The map is useful for finding the right region and re-establishing orientation.
It is much less useful inside Faults, the deeper Sinkhole, the Astral Plane, or
other spaces where the current quest and local geometry matter more than the
street layout.

<h2 id="side-stories">
  Optional quests are easier to manage once you know whether they stay local
</h2>

Some Side Stories are compact enough to finish when Dylan is already nearby.
Others stretch across several regions and are better allowed to progress in the
background.

The <Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest list</Link> is the quickest way
to compare them against the current story objective.

A few are especially easy to recognize in the world.

### Crossing the Threshold

The Field Office investigation involving the Dig Site, Field Hospital, Loading
Docks, Wilson, and AVP-3 belongs to
<Link href="/control-resonant/crossing-the-threshold">Crossing the Threshold</Link>.

Once that investigation is active, follow its evidence rather than clearing
unrelated Central markers.

### The Party Won&apos;t Stop

If you find **Singing Reported?** in West Incursion, it leads into
<Link href="/control-resonant/party-wont-stop">The Party Won&apos;t Stop</Link>.

The karaoke sequence can still complete after a bad answer, but the outcome for
the people inside can change, so use the fixed sequence if saving everyone
matters to you.

### The Last Taxi

Entering an anomalous taxi begins
<Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">The Last Taxi</Link>.

The chain is spread across all seven major regions. There is no reason to stop
the campaign and chase every taxi as soon as the first one appears. Solve the
ones that are already convenient, then return for the remaining locations as
more of Manhattan becomes accessible.

<h2 id="lost-right-now">
  If the route suddenly stops making sense
</h2>

I use the same sequence whenever CONTROL Resonant appears to stop giving a clear
next step.

First, read the **quest name** and the objective directly beneath it. If the
route is physically impossible, check whether a Jesse Fault is unfinished. If
the story has stalled around the CFD, check Power Lines, Into the Sinkhole, and
the required Central and West Incursion Resonants. Only after that do I use the
region name to decide which path Dylan should follow.

That order matters because being inside **Central**, **West Incursion**,
**Underpass**, or **Unknown** does not identify the task by itself.

The Underpass can contain the research-team mission, Jesse's Fault, and the
Physicist route at the same time. Unknown can contain Hedron progression,
Pope's Research, and the Co-Director.

If you know the mission but not the order, return to the
<Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link>. If several
missions are competing for attention, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/quests">quest list</Link>. If the mission is
clear and only the geography is confusing, use the
<Link href="/control-resonant/map">map guide</Link>.
