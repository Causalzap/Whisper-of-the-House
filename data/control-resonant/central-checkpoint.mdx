<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Do not try to carry the electrical charge straight to the Central Checkpoint. Hit the charged **Power Core**, sprint to the next Core, transfer the charge into it to refresh the timer, then repeat through the relay route until you reach the Checkpoint.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="why-charge-disappears">
  The charge is supposed to run out before a direct trip
</h2>

The first time the game asks you to **Move the Charge to Central Checkpoint**, the obvious approach looks correct: take the electricity and run toward the objective.

It fails because the charge only stays on Aberrant for a short time.

That is intentional.

The objective is teaching you how Power Cores work. You are not carrying one charge all the way across Downtown; you are moving it through a chain of Power Cores, using each Core as a relay point before continuing.

The maintenance instructions near the starting area mark the **Power Core relay route** between the starting point and the Central Checkpoint.

Opening the <Link href="/control-resonant/map">map</Link> also helps because the Power Cores appear as electrical box icons around Downtown.

Before taking a charge, identify the next Core and the path to it. Some stops require vertical movement, and checking the map after the timer has already started wastes most of the useful window.

<h2 id="how-power-core-charge-works">
  Every Power Core refreshes the charge timer
</h2>

Hit the active, sparking Power Core with **Aberrant** to pull its electrical charge onto the weapon.

From that moment, keep moving.

You have roughly enough time for one short leg of the route, not the complete trip to Central Checkpoint.

When you reach the next Power Core, strike it while carrying the charge. The electricity transfers into that Core.

That transfer is the important part:

**putting the charge into another Core resets the route.**

You can then take the charge from that newly powered Core and start the next leg.

The basic loop is:

1. take the charge from the current Core;
2. sprint to the next Power Core;
3. hit that Core to deposit the charge;
4. take the refreshed charge;
5. repeat until the Checkpoint is powered.

Stopping to fight is usually a bad trade while carrying electricity. Unless an enemy physically blocks the route, keep running and deal with combat after the charge is safely stored in the next Core.

<h2 id="first-relay">
  First relay: move the charge to the Core by the central pillar
</h2>

Start at the charged Power Core near the maintenance instructions.

Take the charge, turn toward the Central Checkpoint area, and run to the Power Core positioned against the large central structure where you fought the nearby Hiss.

Transfer the electricity there.

Do not continue past it just because the Checkpoint looks close on the map. Skipping an intermediate Core is the most common reason the charge disappears.

Once the first relay is powered, take the charge again and prepare for the loading-dock leg.

<h2 id="loading-dock">
  Second relay: use the loading dock and check Baker's garage
</h2>

From the central relay, head along the road and turn into the loading-dock route.

The next Power Core sits down the ramp inside the lower service area.

Depositing the charge here opens access around the nearby garage.

This is also where you find **Baker**, the maintenance worker Zoe originally expected to repair the Central Checkpoint.

Walk close enough to Baker's body for Dylan to report what happened to Zoe.

There is also a loot container inside the garage. This is the best time to grab it because your charge is safely stored in the nearby Core.

Do not open the crate while electricity is still fading on Aberrant.

Once you are finished in the garage, return to the powered Core, take the charge again, and move on.

<h2 id="rooftop-core">
  Third relay: plan the rooftop climb before taking the charge
</h2>

The next Core is above street level.

This leg feels much tighter if you take the charge first and only then start looking for a way onto the roof.

Instead, scout the climb while the electricity is safely sitting in the loading-dock Core.

From the loading-dock area, head back toward the ramp and continue through the nearby alley. Use the low awnings and reachable structures to climb toward the rooftop.

Once you know the ascent, return for the charge and run the same route without stopping.

Transfer the charge into the rooftop Power Core as soon as you reach it.

If your charge repeatedly disappears during this leg, the solution is not another ability. This puzzle happens before Dylan has most of his later traversal tools.

You are simply losing too much time finding the climb.

<h2 id="fourth-core">
  Keep relaying through the concrete structure before the Checkpoint
</h2>

The rooftop Core is not the final destination.

The route continues toward the concrete structure containing the green industrial containers.

Take the charge from the rooftop relay and carry it to the next Power Core there.

This extra stop is easy to overlook because by now the Central Checkpoint feels physically close enough to reach in one run.

Use the Core anyway.

The puzzle is designed around repeated transfers, and taking the apparent shortcut can leave Aberrant unpowered a few seconds before you reach the final box.

Once the charge is safely deposited here, the last run is much shorter.

<h2 id="power-up-checkpoint">
  Take the final charge to the Central Checkpoint
</h2>

With the last intermediate Core powered, collect the charge one more time and sprint to the Power Core directly in front of the Central Checkpoint.

Transfer the electricity into it.

That completes the power-routing objective.

Do not relax immediately after the electricity comes back.

Powering the Checkpoint triggers a Hiss attack in the area. Clear that fight before trying to continue through the gate.

Once the enemies are down, the route through Central Checkpoint opens and Dylan can continue toward Zoe at the Watchtower.

<h2 id="charge-keeps-disappearing">
  If the charge keeps disappearing, change the route rather than restarting the mission
</h2>

A disappearing charge does not mean the objective is broken.

It means too much time passed since the last Power Core.

The cause is usually one of four things.

**You tried to run directly to Central Checkpoint.**  
Use every required relay Core instead.

**You stopped to fight while carrying the charge.**  
Run past nonessential enemies and fight after the electricity is stored.

**You got stuck looking for the rooftop route.**  
Scout the climb first, then go back and collect the charge.

**You skipped an intermediate Core because the next destination looked close.**  
Deposit the charge anyway. Each relay refreshes the timer and gives you a much safer next leg.

If an attempt fails, return to the last Core that successfully received the electricity. You do not need to restart Welcome to Manhattan or redo the Mapping Pylon.
