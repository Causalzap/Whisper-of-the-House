<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Reach the **Patterned Factory in Central**, enter through the upper factory route, and take the elevator down. Follow the glowing heat trail and destroy the **three heat tumors blocking the cooling pipes** to open the furnace route, then defeat the **Deserter**. In its final phase, destroy all **four magma sacs** immediately or they will keep restoring its health.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="enter-patterned-factory">
  How to get inside the Patterned Factory
</h2>

The Patterned Factory is the location of **The Central Resonant**.

When Dylan first reaches it, the building should not even be operating. It was shut down during the evacuation, yet the furnace is running hot enough to vent steam around the exterior.

The ground-level approach can make the building look sealed.

Instead of circling the doors, work upward along the factory exterior. Use the raised structures beside the building to reach the roof, cross toward the boarded entrance, and break through it with a heavy attack.

Once inside, clear the enemies and use the elevator to descend into the factory.

This is where the actual Resonant route begins.

<h2 id="follow-heat">
  Follow the heat trail away from the furnace
</h2>

At the bottom, the obvious destination is the furnace.

It is also the wrong first destination.

Heat and steam prevent Dylan from reaching it directly, while a glowing trail runs away from the furnace and deeper into the service tunnels.

Dylan describes the material almost like **lava**.

Follow it backward.

The trail keeps going even when the route moves farther away from the furnace.

Stay with it.

Trying to force the steam-covered passage only damages Dylan and does not skip the puzzle.

<h2 id="clear-heat-tumors">
  Destroy all three heat tumors on the cooling route
</h2>

The cooling system is being blocked by abnormal growths attached to the pipes.

There are **three** that matter for progression.

Destroying the first one immediately changes the environment: steam begins clearing from part of the factory.

That is the confirmation that you are doing the correct thing.

Do not return to the furnace after the first obstruction.

Continue through the tunnels and keep checking the pipes wherever the glowing trail leads.

The route is:

**heat trail → first obstruction → deeper tunnels → remaining obstructions → furnace access**

There is no valve combination, purchasable item, or consumable involved.

The progress state is simply how many of the three pipe growths Dylan has destroyed.

There is no pump-control solution hidden nearby.

The empty pool explains the condition of the factory, but the objective remains the same: follow the abnormal heat back to its source and remove the growths interfering with the system.

Bodies along the route show how severe the heat became before Dylan arrived.

Keep moving through the service tunnels.

<h2 id="service-tunnels">
  Use the heat trail when the factory layout stops making sense
</h2>

The lower factory has already been affected by the Patterning.

Rooms no longer feel completely consistent, and old industrial landmarks are less useful than they should be.

The glowing trail remains reliable.

Whenever the route branches, follow the direction where the residue continues. When steam cuts off a passage, inspect nearby piping instead of searching the opposite side of the factory for a key.

Information Dylan finds underground also points toward someone living beneath the factory before the Resonant appeared.

That person is **Oscar Brook**, a former FBC employee who had separated himself from the Bureau.

The trail eventually leads Dylan to what happened to him.

<h2 id="reach-furnace">
  Return to the furnace after all three obstructions are gone
</h2>

Once the three heat tumors have been destroyed, the route toward the furnace opens.

That is the point to stop searching the side tunnels.

Head back into the hotter central section.

The blocked passage that previously looked lethal is now the route forward.

You do not need to interact with another cooling console before continuing.

The pipe obstructions were the progression gate.

<h2 id="deserter">
  The Central Resonant is the Deserter
</h2>

Oscar Brook did not simply die below the factory.

He became the **Deserter**, the Resonant responsible for the extreme heat spreading through the building.

The Quest Log calls this objective **The Central Resonant**, but the entity Dylan fights is the Deserter.

Once the encounter begins, stop looking for another factory mechanism.

The furnace puzzle is over.

Now the heat is part of the boss fight.

<h2 id="beat-deserter">
  How to beat the Deserter
</h2>

The Deserter is dangerous when Dylan stays in front of it for too long, but several of its attacks also leave long recovery windows.

At the start, expect a **tracking lava-spew attack**.

Do not stand close and try to dodge through the entire stream.

Use Rush and keep moving around the arena until the attack finishes. Once the Deserter stops, circle behind it and take the free melee window.

Another useful opening comes after its forward body slam.

Move out of the landing zone, then punish while the Deserter is recovering on the ground.

The rhythm is less about maintaining permanent contact and more about:

**avoid the long fire attack → get behind it → spend a short damage window → disengage before the arena becomes unsafe again**

<h2 id="deserter-projectiles">
  Watch for the three-projectile attack later in the fight
</h2>

After the fight advances, the Deserter adds a ranged fire sequence.

It launches **three fiery projectiles**.

The first two are mostly there to force movement. Keep enough distance that Dylan is not cornered by the time the third arrives.

The Deserter can also stab forward with the heated objects protruding from its body.

That attack is much more dangerous when Dylan stays directly in front of the boss.

Attacking from behind after a missed Deserter attack reduces how often you have to deal with it.

<h2 id="four-magma-sacs">
  Destroy the four magma sacs immediately in the final phase
</h2>

The most important Deserter mechanic comes late in the fight.

Four **magma sacs** appear around the arena.

Each creates another fire hazard, but the larger problem is the boss itself:

**while the sacs remain active, the Deserter can recover health.**

Do not continue hitting the boss while ignoring them.

Switch targets immediately.

There are **four sacs**. Destroy all four as quickly as possible, then go back to the Deserter.

This is the main reason the final part of the encounter can feel as though your damage has stopped mattering. Dylan may still be hurting the boss, but the active sacs are undoing part of that progress.

Once they are gone, finish the remaining health normally.

<h2 id="deserter-rewards">
  Ignite or Inferno after beating the Deserter?
</h2>

Defeating the Deserter sends Dylan to the Gap and gives him a choice between two fire-based Combat Abilities:

**Ignite** or **Inferno**.

### Ignite

Ignite adds **Burn** to Dylan's weapon attacks while the ability is active.

It fits naturally into a melee-focused setup because you do not need to stop attacking and perform a separate offensive action. Turn it on, stay on the target, and let normal Aberrant hits build the fire pressure.

For a general melee run, this is the option I would take first.

### Inferno

Inferno produces a sequence of **short-range fire attacks**.

It is more direct than Ignite and gives you a separate way to apply fire damage instead of modifying the weapon attacks Dylan is already making.

Take it when you specifically want an independent close-range fire ability rather than another layer on top of your normal melee loop.

Neither choice changes traversal or blocks story progression.

<h2 id="lighter">
  You also unlock the Lighter Artifact recipe
</h2>

The Deserter also unlocks the **Lighter** Artifact recipe.

Its defining benefit is **Burn resistance**, which makes sense given the environment and status pressure surrounding the Central Resonant.

The ability choice is still the larger reward, but the Lighter is worth remembering if Burn-heavy enemies are becoming a recurring problem.

<h2 id="required">
  Do you have to beat the Deserter?
</h2>

Yes, the Central Resonant matters to main progression.

The Deserter is one of the required regional Resonants used to move the Contain the Crisis story forward.

If **Enemy of My Enemy** is not opening and the Central Resonant is still alive, this is one of the objectives to finish before searching the Field Office for another trigger.

The other early regional check is the West Incursion Resonant.

If you want to compare the remaining bosses, locations, and ability rewards before choosing your next fight, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants guide</Link>.

<h2 id="factory-complete">
  When the Patterned Factory is finished
</h2>

Once the Deserter is dead and Dylan completes the post-fight Gap sequence, The Central Resonant is finished.

There is no additional furnace shutdown puzzle.

The complete chain is:

**enter the factory → descend by elevator → follow the heat trail → destroy three heat tumors → reach the furnace → defeat the Deserter → choose Ignite or Inferno**

Any remaining exploration in the factory is optional unless another active objective sends Dylan back inside.

For the next story step, return to the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Patterned Factory and Deserter stuck fixes
</h2>

### I cannot find a normal entrance into the factory

Go upward instead of circling the ground-floor doors.

Reach the roof route and break through the boarded entrance, then clear the interior and take the elevator down.

### The furnace is blocked by heat and steam

Do not walk through it.

Follow the glowing trail away from the furnace and start clearing the abnormal growths attached to the cooling pipes.

### I destroyed one obstruction but the furnace is still blocked

There are **three heat tumors** in this route.

Keep following the glowing residue through the tunnels until all three are gone.

### The cooling pool is empty

Leave it alone.

There is no refill puzzle here. The empty pool explains the overheating but does not need to be repaired manually.

### I reached the furnace but cannot find another machine to activate

There is no additional machine puzzle.

Once the three obstructions are gone, the next objective is the **Deserter**.

### The Deserter keeps getting health back near the end

Stop attacking the boss.

Find and destroy the **four magma sacs** around the arena first. Leaving them active allows the Deserter to recover health.

### The Deserter keeps hitting me when I stay in melee range

Do not stay directly in front of it through the whole fight.

Move through the tracking lava attack, wait for its recovery window, then attack from behind and disengage before the next sequence.

### I beat the Deserter but The Central Resonant has not completed

Finish the Gap sequence and make the **Ignite / Inferno** choice.

The quest completion state does not need to resolve on the exact frame when the boss health reaches zero.
