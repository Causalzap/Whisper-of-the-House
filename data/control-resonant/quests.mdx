<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Follow the three main quest chains together rather than finishing one column at a time: **Contain the Crisis**, **Search for Jesse**, and **Defeating Resonants**. When the story stops giving you a useful next objective, check whether you still owe a Jesse Fault or a required Resonant.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

CONTROL Resonant does not use one uninterrupted mission list.

The campaign runs several quest chains at the same time, and that is the main reason the Quest Log can look as though the story has suddenly stopped.

The three chains I keep separate are:

- **Contain the Crisis** — the primary story;
- **Search for Jesse** — the Faults and traversal progression;
- **Defeating Resonants** — the regional boss chain.

A story mission can wait for progress in either of the other two.

That means an objective disappearing is not always a bug and does not necessarily mean you missed an NPC. Before I start searching the current zone for another trigger, I check the other two chains.

<h2 id="main-story">
  Main story quest checklist
</h2>

The main campaign still has a recognizable order, but several stages pause until another quest chain catches up.

`Plan of Attack` acts as the umbrella for **Power Lines** and **Into the Sinkhole**, so I track those two playable routes directly instead of treating the parent note as a completely separate adventure.

### Opening and Central

- **Orientation** — the opening escape, first Jesse contact, and Artist Resonant. <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">Follow the main walkthrough</Link> if you are still moving through the opening.
- **Welcome to Manhattan** — reach Central, restore the Checkpoint, and meet Zoe. <Link href="/control-resonant/central-checkpoint">The Central Checkpoint route</Link> covers the Power Core relay.
- **Evacuation Procedures** — reach the Evac Building, investigate the tremors, and rescue Arish. <Link href="/control-resonant/evacuation-zone">The Evacuation Procedures walkthrough</Link> covers the three exterior Hiss Orbs, Floors 12 and 15, and the escape with Arish.
- **Arish&apos;s Briefing** — return to the Field Office and open the next containment plan.

### CFD preparation

- **Power Lines** — restore the power network across the subway stations. <Link href="/control-resonant/power-lines">The Power Lines guide</Link> covers Factory, East Park, Perimeter, and Vanished Station.
- **Into the Sinkhole** — escort the Probe Relays to Bedrock and survive the collapsed return route. <Link href="/control-resonant/into-the-sinkhole">The Into the Sinkhole walkthrough</Link> covers the diving bell, Bedrock, relay activation, and escape.
- **Enemy of My Enemy** — continues after the required Central and West Incursion Resonants have been handled. <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">Check the Resonant list</Link> if this quest is not opening.

### Underpass and late story

- **Recursions and Iterations** — enter the Underpass, find the research team, and restore their transmission. <Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">The Underpass walkthrough</Link> follows that route.
- **The House in Distress** — returns the crisis to the Oldest House and intersects with Jesse&apos;s later Fault progression.
- **The Hedron Link** — investigate the Hedron connection and rescue Pope. <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">The Hedron Link guide</Link> covers the transition into this stage.
- **Sever the Hedron Links** — travel through Unknown and cut the remaining Hedron connections. <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">Continue with the Hedron Link route</Link> when this becomes the active objective.
- **Rebuild** — late-story sequence after the Links are severed.
- **Fight the Core** — the final approach toward the Hedron.
- **The End** — the final confrontation.
- **The Beginning** — the epilogue and return to unfinished Manhattan activities.

The two most important interruptions in that order are deliberate.

**Enemy of My Enemy** expects Dylan to have dealt with the required Resonants, while the later Underpass and Oldest House progression intersects with the Jesse Fault chain.

So when the next story mission appears unavailable, check the next two sections before assuming the campaign route is broken.

<h2 id="search-for-jesse">
  Search for Jesse quest checklist
</h2>

The Jesse chain is short, but mechanically it matters more than its size suggests.

The first two Faults unlock traversal that other missions expect Dylan to already have.

- **The Incursion Fault** — begins in the West Incursion Zone around the Parking Garage. It unlocks the gravity traversal needed for later West Incursion routes.
- **The Subway Fault** — begins from the Evacuation Zone subway route. It unlocks the traversal used on distant dimensional points.
- **The Underpass Fault** — appears during later Underpass progression. It continues Jesse&apos;s ritual and story but does not add another traversal unlock.

The complete route through all three is in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

I prioritize the Faults when they become available.

Leaving the Incursion Fault unfinished can block the route toward the Theater. Leaving the Subway Fault unfinished can later put Dylan in front of a gap he simply cannot cross yet.

That is different from an optional Side Story. No amount of searching for another ledge replaces a traversal ability you have not unlocked.

<h2 id="resonant-quests">
  Regional Resonant quest checklist
</h2>

The Artist appears during Orientation, so there is no separate regional Artist quest to add here.

After the opening, the Quest Log tracks five named regional Resonant entries:

- **The Central Resonant** — the **Deserter** in Central / Patterned Factory. Required for the early story gate.
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant** — the **Dancer** in the West Incursion Theater. Required for the same early story gate.
- **The Park Resonant** — the **Collective** in The Park. Optional when first available.
- **The Underpass Resonant** — the **Physicist** at The Spire / Astral Plane. Available once its Underpass route opens.
- **The Unknown Resonant** — the **Co-Director** in Unknown. This is a late regional Resonant.

The important pair is:

**Central Resonant + West Incursion Zone Resonant**

Both need to be handled before **Enemy of My Enemy** can progress.

If you have reached that part of the campaign and nothing useful is happening at the Field Office, check those two before clearing unrelated Side Stories.

For locations, prerequisites, ability rewards, and the individual boss routes, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants guide</Link>.

<h2 id="side-stories">
  Side Stories and Notes worth tracking
</h2>

Side content is less linear.

Some quests begin from NPC conversations, some trigger when Dylan approaches an activity, and several Notes chains continue across more than one region.

I keep them separate from the main-story order.

### Good to take when they appear

- **Freeze Frame** — Central. Pick it up when available; the longer cleanup can wait.
- **The Party Won&apos;t Stop** — West Incursion Zone. If you are already beside the apartment, this is a good time to finish it, especially if you want the perfect party outcome.
- **Into the Valley of Death** — Central / Field Office progression. Take it naturally when the Field Office opens the lead.
- **Zoe&apos;s Briefing** — Watchtower. Use it to collect later leads Zoe gives Dylan.
- **Crossing the Threshold** — Central. A good mid-game detour once the loading-dock investigation appears.
- **Urban Survival** — Evacuation Zone. Optional, but efficient when you are already clearing this part of the zone.
- **New Growth** — leads into The Park. Easier once Park traversal is comfortable.
- **Panopticon Service Request** — Central / Field Office. Worth accepting early because its targets can overlap normal exploration.
- **Carbon Copy** — starts from Heidi in The Park and continues beyond the first Park objective.

### Better treated as longer multi-zone chains

- **Every Dog Has Her Day** — begins from a Mysterious Dog encounter. Let the multi-zone cleanup happen naturally rather than forcing it early.
- **Unsupervised Science** — tied to FBC Testing Facilities across more than one area. It becomes easier after traversal opens.
- **The Complete Set** — starts around Downtown / Auction House and works better as later collection cleanup.
- **The Last Taxi** — starts with the first anomalous taxi you enter. All seven major zones are involved, so solve taxis as you naturally reach their regions.
- **Mapping the New New York** — begins with the first Mapping Pylon. Keep it active while opening new regions instead of saving every Pylon for one large cleanup run.

### Late-story Side Stories

- **Pope&apos;s Research** — tied to Unknown progression. Do not postpone this once Unknown opens because it directly affects how Dylan moves through the late region.
- **A New Directive** — Unknown. Follow the call while exploring the region.
- **Maintenance Protocol** — appears from later Zoe progression and continues into the Oldest House alarm route.

Some of these appear under **Notes** rather than behaving like a conventional one-location Side Story.

If something seems to be missing from the Side Story list, check Notes before assuming you never started it.

<h2 id="best-order">
  A practical order when several quests are active
</h2>

I use progression blockers first, nearby Side Stories second, and multi-zone cleanup last.

Early on, the route is still straightforward:

**Orientation → Welcome to Manhattan → Evacuation Procedures**

Once Manhattan opens further, I stop expecting a completely linear order.

The useful priority becomes:

1. take a **Jesse Fault** when it unlocks a movement ability;
2. finish any **Resonant specifically required by the next story gate**;
3. continue the active Contain the Crisis mission;
4. complete nearby Side Stories while already in that zone;
5. leave multi-zone collection chains for later unless their next target is already on the route.

That prevents a common waste of time: trying to clear every activity in a region before Dylan has the traversal needed to reach all of it.

<h2 id="enemy-of-my-enemy">
  Why Enemy of My Enemy is not starting
</h2>

This is the clearest example of the three quest chains crossing.

You can finish the surrounding story objectives and still not receive the next useful progression step.

Check:

- **The Central Resonant**
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**

If either remains unfinished, deal with that Resonant before searching the Field Office for another conversation trigger.

The Central route leads through the Patterned Factory.

The West Incursion route eventually leads to the Dancer in the Theater, but the Incursion Fault needs to be handled before that route fully opens.

<h2 id="underpass-block">
  Why the Underpass story can stop at a traversal blocker
</h2>

Recursions and Iterations takes Dylan deep enough into the Underpass that the Jesse chain can become relevant again.

When the route reaches a dimensional gap that normal movement cannot solve, check whether **The Subway Fault** is still unfinished.

Later, **The Underpass Fault** becomes part of Jesse&apos;s story while Dylan is already working through the same large region.

This is why I follow the active quest name rather than simply saying, "I am in the Underpass."

Three very different tasks can coexist there:

- the research-team route;
- Search for Jesse;
- the Physicist Resonant.

Use the <Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass route</Link> for the physical region, <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link> for the Fault, and the <Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist guide</Link> once the active objective is The Underpass Resonant.

<h2 id="unknown">
  Do Pope&apos;s Research before forcing the Unknown story route
</h2>

Unknown is another place where the main quest and Side Story interact directly.

**Sever the Hedron Links** sends Dylan into the region, while **Pope&apos;s Research** supplies progression that helps him actually move through it.

If the story marker appears to sit on an island or Patterned route you cannot sensibly reach, check Pope&apos;s Research before repeatedly attacking the same main-story objective.

The late region also contains the Unknown Resonant, so the Quest Log can have all three types of content active at once:

- story;
- Side Story;
- Resonant.

Read the active quest title before following the nearest marker.

<h2 id="side-quest-timing">
  Which Side Stories should I leave until later?
</h2>

The easiest quests to postpone are the ones deliberately spread across several zones.

I would not stop the campaign just to finish:

- The Complete Set;
- Every Dog Has Her Day;
- Unsupervised Science;
- The Last Taxi;
- full Mapping Pylon cleanup.

Take progress when it is nearby.

Finish the remaining locations after Shift, Reach, later region access, and fast travel have reduced the amount of forced backtracking.

A self-contained Side Story is different.

For example, once you are standing outside the karaoke apartment, completing <Link href="/control-resonant/party-wont-stop">The Party Won&apos;t Stop</Link> then and there is reasonable because most of the task is concentrated in that one location.

The same logic applies to <Link href="/control-resonant/crossing-the-threshold">Crossing the Threshold</Link> once its Central investigation is already on your route.

<h2 id="last-taxi">
  The Last Taxi is a seven-zone cleanup chain
</h2>

The Last Taxi behaves differently from a normal Side Story because its objectives are distributed across Manhattan.

There are **seven anomalous taxis**, one associated with each major region.

You do not need to find all seven as soon as the first one transports Dylan into its Threshold.

I complete each taxi when the surrounding region is already open, then use the <Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">Last Taxi locations and puzzle guide</Link> for the ones still missing later.

The puzzle attached to a particular taxi location can vary, so the useful checklist is the taxi location itself rather than assuming one specific puzzle always belongs to one specific region.

<h2 id="party-wont-stop">
  One Side Story where the result matters during the quest
</h2>

The Party Won&apos;t Stop can still finish after a bad karaoke selection, but the people inside do not necessarily get the same outcome.

A wrong timed answer can kill one of the partygoers.

If you care about saving everyone, use the fixed sequence in the <Link href="/control-resonant/party-wont-stop">Party Won&apos;t Stop guide</Link> before starting the karaoke machine.

Do not assume **Quest Completed** means every optional outcome was successful.

<h2 id="after-ending">
  What should I leave for after The Beginning?
</h2>

After the main story and epilogue, unfinished Manhattan activities are the best place to clean up the longer chains you deliberately skipped.

That is when I would finish:

- missing regional Side Stories;
- remaining taxi locations;
- unfinished dogs;
- Testing Facilities;
- Mapping Pylons;
- collection-based Side Stories;
- optional Resonants left alive during the main route.

At that point, traversal and region access are no longer competing with the next story mission.

The checklist becomes much simpler:

**unfinished quest name → correct region → finish its remaining objective.**

If you are not sure whether you have missed a quest or simply reached a story gate, compare the three main-chain checklists at the top of this page before starting a full-map cleanup.
