<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
CONTROL Resonant moves three quest lines forward at the same time:
**Contain the Crisis**, **Search for Jesse**, and **Defeating Resonants**.

If the next story objective does not appear, do not assume the Quest Log is
broken. First check whether a Jesse Fault is holding back a traversal ability,
then check whether the next story step requires a regional Resonant. The most
important early gate is **Enemy of My Enemy**, which waits for both the
**Central Resonant** and the **West Incursion Zone Resonant**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

CONTROL Resonant looks fairly linear during the opening, then the Quest Log
starts carrying several different kinds of progress at once. That is where the
campaign can become confusing: finishing the objective currently at the top of
the screen does not always create the next story marker immediately.

The easiest way to read the log is to separate what Dylan is doing into three
threads. **Contain the Crisis** moves the main story forward. **Search for
Jesse** handles the Faults, including traversal Dylan needs elsewhere.
**Defeating Resonants** tracks the regional bosses, some of which are required
before the story can continue.

Side Stories sit beside those three threads. Some are short detours in one
area, while others deliberately remain unfinished across several zones.

<h2 id="main-story">
  Contain the Crisis main story quest order
</h2>

The main campaign keeps a recognizable order even when another quest temporarily
interrupts it.

`Plan of Attack` is best read as a parent objective rather than a separate route:
it stays open while **Power Lines** and **Into the Sinkhole** are being dealt
with.

### Opening and Central

- **Orientation** — Dylan escapes the opening sequence, makes contact with Jesse, and encounters the Artist Resonant. If you are still working through the opening route, continue with the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link>.
- **Welcome to Manhattan** — moves Dylan into Central, restores the Checkpoint, and brings him to Zoe. The <Link href="/control-resonant/central-checkpoint">Central Checkpoint route</Link> covers the Power Core relay section.
- **Evacuation Procedures** — takes Dylan to the Evac Building, the resonance tremors, and Arish. The <Link href="/control-resonant/evacuation-zone">Evacuation Procedures route</Link> follows the exterior Hiss Orbs, Floors 12 and 15, and the escape.
- **Arish&apos;s Briefing** — sends Dylan back to the Field Office and opens the next stage of the containment plan.

At this point the game stops behaving like one uninterrupted corridor. Dylan can
have useful objectives in more than one region, and the next story step may
depend on work sitting under another quest heading.

### Power network and Sinkhole

- **Power Lines** — restores the subway power network through Factory, East Park, Perimeter, and Vanished Station. The route is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/power-lines">Power Lines</Link>.
- **Into the Sinkhole** — follows the Probe Relays into Bedrock and through the collapsed return route. Continue with <Link href="/control-resonant/into-the-sinkhole">Into the Sinkhole</Link> if the objective has moved below the surface.
- **Enemy of My Enemy** — will not advance until the required Central and West Incursion Resonants are dealt with.

That last condition is the first major point where players can finish the visible
story work and still wonder why nothing new is happening. The answer is usually
in the Resonant section of the Quest Log rather than at another conversation
marker in Central.

### Underpass and late story

- **Recursions and Iterations** — sends Dylan into the Underpass to find the research team and restore their transmission.
- **The House in Distress** — brings the crisis back toward the Oldest House and crosses into later Jesse progression.
- **The Hedron Link** — investigates Dylan&apos;s connection to Hedron and the route toward Pope. The transition into this stage is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">The Hedron Link</Link>.
- **Sever the Hedron Links** — moves the story through Unknown and the remaining Hedron connections.
- **Rebuild** — follows the severed Links and the late-story change in Manhattan.
- **Fight the Core** — begins the final approach to the Hedron.
- **The End** — the final confrontation.
- **The Beginning** — the epilogue, after which Dylan can return to unfinished activities around Manhattan.

The late campaign still crosses other quest lines. If an Underpass or Unknown
objective looks physically impossible to reach, check the active quest name
before trying to force the route. A Fault or Side Story may be supplying the
missing progression.

<h2 id="search-for-jesse">
  Search for Jesse quests
</h2>

There are only three major Fault entries here, but the first two matter far
beyond their own objectives because they change where Dylan can travel.

- **The Incursion Fault** — begins around the Parking Garage in the West Incursion Zone. Completing it gives Dylan the gravity traversal needed for later routes in that region.
- **The Subway Fault** — begins through the Evacuation Zone subway route. Its traversal unlock is required for distant dimensional points that appear later in the campaign.
- **The Underpass Fault** — arrives during later Underpass progression and continues the Jesse storyline without adding another traversal unlock.

I do not leave the first two sitting in the log once their routes become
relevant. If a West Incursion path ends at an impossible gravity gap, or a later
objective expects Dylan to cross a point he cannot reach, repeatedly searching
the room will not solve it. The missing movement ability is the real blocker.

The full sequence through the Faults is in
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse"> Search for Jesse</Link>.

<h2 id="resonant-quests">
  Regional Resonant quests
</h2>

The Artist is encountered during **Orientation**, so there is no separate
regional Artist entry to wait for after the opening.

The Quest Log later tracks five named regional Resonant quests:

- **The Central Resonant** — the Deserter in Central / Patterned Factory. This one is required for early story progression.
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant** — the Dancer in the Theater. This is also required, and the route depends on the Incursion Fault having opened the necessary traversal.
- **The Park Resonant** — the Collective in The Park. It does not have to be forced into the first visit to the region.
- **The Underpass Resonant** — the Physicist, reached after the relevant Underpass routes have opened.
- **The Unknown Resonant** — the Co-Director in Unknown during the late game.

The pair to remember is **Central Resonant + West Incursion Zone Resonant**.
Both have to be finished before **Enemy of My Enemy** can properly move the
story forward.

If that story step is waiting and the Field Office has nothing useful left to
trigger, look at those two quest entries instead of clearing unrelated Side
Stories.

Locations, prerequisites, rewards, and the individual fights are collected in
the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">Resonants list</Link>.

<h2 id="side-stories">
  Side Stories and Notes
</h2>

Optional quests do not all arrive the same way. Some begin from a conversation,
some appear after Dylan investigates a location, and several are spread across
enough of Manhattan that they make more sense as ongoing background objectives
than as something to finish immediately.

That difference matters more than trying to clear every Side Story as soon as
its name appears.

### Good detours when you are already nearby

- **Freeze Frame** — picked up in Central. Starting it early is useful, but the longer cleanup does not have to interrupt the campaign.
- **The Party Won&apos;t Stop** — West Incursion Zone. Once you are already beside the apartment, it is a compact detour rather than a cross-city commitment. The timed karaoke outcome is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/party-wont-stop">The Party Won&apos;t Stop</Link>.
- **Into the Valley of Death** — follows naturally from Central / Field Office progression when that lead appears.
- **Zoe&apos;s Briefing** — begins around the Watchtower and gathers later leads Zoe gives Dylan.
- **Crossing the Threshold** — a Central investigation that is worth following once the loading-dock lead is active. The evidence chain is in <Link href="/control-resonant/crossing-the-threshold">Crossing the Threshold</Link>.
- **Urban Survival** — an Evacuation Zone detour that fits well when Dylan is already working through that part of Manhattan.
- **New Growth** — leads toward The Park and is easier to follow once movement through the region is comfortable.
- **Panopticon Service Request** — starts from Central / Field Office progression and can overlap normal exploration if accepted early.
- **Carbon Copy** — begins with Heidi in The Park and continues beyond its first Park objective.

None of these needs to replace the next important movement unlock or required
Resonant. If a local Side Story is directly in front of you, finish it; if
continuing it would send Dylan across several unopened regions, leave it in the
log.

### Multi-zone chains

A different group is designed to stay active for much longer:

- **Every Dog Has Her Day** — begins from a Mysterious Dog encounter and continues across Manhattan.
- **Unsupervised Science** — follows FBC Testing Facilities in more than one area.
- **The Complete Set** — starts around Downtown / Auction House and works better once more of the city is accessible.
- **The Last Taxi** — spreads anomalous taxis across the major regions. There is no need to abandon the story and chase all of them after finding the first one; the remaining locations and puzzles are in <Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">The Last Taxi</Link>.
- **Mapping the New New York** — begins with the Mapping Pylons. Reveal each region as you naturally reach it rather than saving every Pylon for one late sweep. The mapping process is explained in the <Link href="/control-resonant/map">map guide</Link>.

These are the quests I let accumulate progress in the background. New traversal,
fast travel, and wider region access remove a lot of unnecessary backtracking
later.

### Later Side Stories

- **Pope&apos;s Research** — becomes especially important once Unknown opens because its progression affects Dylan&apos;s ability to move through the late region.
- **A New Directive** — appears during Unknown exploration.
- **Maintenance Protocol** — follows later Zoe progression and carries back toward the Oldest House alarm route.

Some optional chains can appear under **Notes** instead of looking like a simple
one-location Side Story. If a quest name seems absent from the Side Story list,
check Notes before deciding that it never started.

<h2 id="best-order">
  Which quest should you do next?
</h2>

Once Central opens the campaign up, I stop trying to finish quests in the order
they were added to the log.

The better priority is based on what each objective changes:

1. Take a **Jesse Fault** when the missing traversal is already blocking useful routes.
2. Defeat a **Resonant required by the next story gate**.
3. Continue the active **Contain the Crisis** objective.
4. Finish a nearby Side Story if most of it stays in the region you are already exploring.
5. Let multi-zone cleanup chains wait unless their next target happens to be on your route.

That order avoids one of the most common wastes of time in Manhattan: trying to
fully clear a region before Dylan has the movement abilities or story access
needed to reach everything visible there.

It also explains why two players can take slightly different routes through the
middle of the game without either of them being on the wrong quest.

<h2 id="story-stops">
  What to check when the story stops moving
</h2>

Several different blockers can look like the same problem from the Quest Log.
The quickest fix is to match the stalled objective to the system that controls
it.

**Enemy of My Enemy is not starting.**  
Check **The Central Resonant** and **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**. If
either remains unfinished, deal with that quest before searching Central for
another dialogue trigger.

**The West Incursion route ends at a movement problem.**  
Look at **The Incursion Fault**. The Dancer route eventually expects the gravity
traversal gained there.

**Recursions and Iterations reaches an impossible Underpass gap.**  
Check whether **The Subway Fault** is still unfinished. Later in the same broad
region, **The Underpass Fault** can also become active, so the district name
alone is not enough to tell you which objective Dylan should be following.

The physical Underpass route is in
<Link href="/control-resonant/underpass"> Underpass</Link>; the Jesse objective
stays with <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

**Sever the Hedron Links points into Unknown but the route makes no sense.**  
Check **Pope&apos;s Research** before repeatedly trying to force the nearest
Patterned path. The main story and that Side Story interact directly in the
late region.

**A Side Story seems to have disappeared.**  
Look under Notes and reconsider where the quest was started. Several optional
chains do not behave like a single marker that remains in one zone from start
to finish.

In every case, read the **quest name first** and the region second. Underpass can
hold the research-team story, a Jesse objective, and the Physicist route at the
same time; Unknown can similarly contain Hedron progression, Pope&apos;s Research,
and the Co-Director.

<h2 id="side-quest-timing">
  Which quests are better left until later?
</h2>

The easiest quests to postpone are the ones deliberately spread over several
regions.

I would not interrupt an important story or traversal objective just to finish
all remaining targets for:

- **The Complete Set**
- **Every Dog Has Her Day**
- **Unsupervised Science**
- **The Last Taxi**
- **Mapping the New New York**

Take the convenient targets as you pass them. Finish the leftovers after Shift,
Reach, later region access, and fast travel have reduced the amount of forced
backtracking.

A self-contained local quest is different. If Dylan is already at the karaoke
apartment for **The Party Won&apos;t Stop**, or already following the Central
evidence in **Crossing the Threshold**, leaving solely to preserve some imagined
perfect quest order usually creates more travel rather than less.

<h2 id="after-ending">
  What to finish after The Beginning
</h2>

The epilogue is a natural point to return to work that was deliberately left
unfinished. By then, region access and traversal are no longer competing with
the next major story objective.

That is when I clean up:

- regional Side Stories that never fit naturally into the main route;
- remaining anomalous taxis;
- unfinished dog locations;
- FBC Testing Facilities;
- Mapping Pylons;
- collection-based Side Stories;
- optional Resonants that were not required earlier.

At that stage the Quest Log becomes much easier to read. Pick the unfinished
quest, identify the region containing its next objective, and finish that chain
without worrying that a missing movement ability or unopened story gate is
about to send you somewhere else.
