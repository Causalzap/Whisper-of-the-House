<h2 id="enter-west-incursion-zone">
  Follow the abandoned cars into the West Incursion Zone
</h2>

The West Incursion Zone is the point where Manhattan stops behaving like a city with damaged streets and starts behaving like a place whose gravity has been rewritten.

From Central, follow the road where abandoned cars are all pointing in the same direction.

Near the checkpoint, clear the Hiss blocking the approach and continue upward through the FBC route into the zone.

An FBC survivor near the entrance gives a useful warning before Dylan goes much farther: almost everyone who entered this area disappeared, and the Patterning did not simply damage the buildings. Streets, walls, and gravity itself were shifted.

He specifically warns Dylan about the parking garage.

That warning matters a few minutes later.

Zoe points out the **spotlights in the sky**.

A dance performance had been underway when the AWE hit. The Patterning appears to have protected the audience from the initial Hiss outbreak, because civilians continued sending distress calls afterward.

The FBC sent a Ranger team to investigate.

Then both the Rangers' radios and the civilian calls stopped.

That theater is your main destination.

<h2 id="follow-theater-spotlights">
  Keep the theater in sight instead of following the normal street layout
</h2>

The theater becomes visible fairly early, but reaching it directly is another matter.

West Incursion contains:

- rotated streets;
- buildings functioning as walls;
- sections where "down" no longer matches the rest of Manhattan;
- long gaps Dylan cannot initially cross.

When a normal road bends into an impossible surface, do not assume you missed an alley below.

Keep advancing along the Patterned geometry that is actually walkable.

The theater spotlights remain the best orientation point.

You will eventually reach the area where the abandoned traffic converges on a parking garage.

Dylan recognizes why all of the cars were heading in the same direction.

The obvious entrance is blocked.

You need Jesse.

<h2 id="incursion-fault">
  Complete The Incursion Fault before trying to reach the theater
</h2>

Entering this part of West Incursion triggers the first major Jesse Fault sequence.

If the parking garage entrance is blocked and the theater still looks unreachable, do not spend time circling the same block looking for a conventional entrance.

Switch to the Jesse objective.

The full Parking Garage key route and Incursion Fault sequence are covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

The important result for this stage is **Shift**.

Shift changes how Dylan interacts with West Incursion's rotated architecture. Surfaces that previously looked like dead ends can now become valid traversal paths.

Once the ritual is complete, leave the Fault and resume the theater objective.

<h2 id="return-to-theater-route">
  Return to the long gap after unlocking Shift
</h2>

You do not need to find a completely different route after obtaining Shift.

Go back toward the theater.

The critical obstacle is the **long gap** separating Dylan from the next section of the district.

Stand toward the **left side of that gap**.

Look at the side of the nearby building and use Shift to align Dylan with the Patterned surface.

From there, follow the building-side route forward.

The geometry looks wrong from the original street orientation, but after Shift this is now the intended path.

Do not drop back to the road simply because the theater appears lower or sideways relative to Dylan.

Stay with the Shift route until the world transitions back into a more readable walking surface.

This is the progression gate the zone was built around.

If you have Shift and still cannot cross, the usual mistake is trying to jump the center of the gap rather than using the wall/building route on the left.

<h2 id="ranger-route">
  Follow the missing Rangers toward the theater
</h2>

Once Dylan is across, the route begins giving you evidence of the FBC team Zoe sent into the zone.

Keep moving in the direction of the theater rather than detouring back toward the parking garage.

You eventually pick up signs that the Rangers continued forward on foot.

Near the final approach, the situation becomes much worse.

Dylan finds the missing Ranger team caught in the Patterning.

They have not simply died in a normal Hiss attack.

Their bodies have been **Patterned and multiplied**.

There is nothing to activate on the Rangers.

Continue toward the theater.

<h2 id="enter-theater">
  Enter the theater after finding the Rangers
</h2>

The theater entrance is ahead of the Ranger scene.

Once inside, Dylan can hear something that does not sound like ordinary Hiss resonance.

He describes it almost like **singing or a choir**.

That is the final warning before the Resonant encounter.

At this point, the navigation part of the West Incursion route is effectively finished.

Use the nearby supplies if you need to recover, check your Artifact and Combat Ability loadout, then continue into the performance space.

The Resonant inside is **The Dancer**.

For the attack cues, damage windows, and the reward choice after the fight, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/dancer">Dancer guide</Link>.

Do not treat the boss arena as another navigation puzzle. Once you are inside the theater and the Resonant appears, you have reached the correct destination.

<h2 id="after-dancer">
  What changes after defeating the Dancer
</h2>

The audience Dylan came to investigate is already gone.

After the fight, Dylan identifies the Resonant as the performer — **the Dancer** — and Zoe confirms that destroying a Resonant is still valuable even though the rescue arrived too late.

The Dancer gives a combat-power choice, but that decision belongs to the boss itself rather than the West Incursion route.

More importantly for progression, defeating the West Incursion Resonant is required for the main Contain the Crisis storyline.

You also need the **Central Resonant** completed before the later story can move into **Enemy of My Enemy**.

If Central is still unfinished, return there rather than searching West Incursion for another mandatory story trigger.

For the overall order, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="before-leaving">
  Useful things to remember before leaving West Incursion
</h2>

Shift changes this region permanently from "mostly blocked" to "worth exploring."

Areas that looked unreachable on the first entry can now be revisited by using walls and rotated surfaces as movement paths.

That makes this a good time to:

- activate any nearby map or fast-travel point you passed on the way to the theater;
- revisit visible loot that was above or sideways from the original street;
- mark unexplored side streets for later cleanup rather than forcing them before Shift;
- listen for the anomalous taxi if you are working through <Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">The Last Taxi</Link>.

There is also an important side story in the southwest of the zone.

If you hear loud music coming from an apartment building, that begins **The Party Won't Stop**.

The karaoke sequence can affect an achievement, so do not rush through its choices just because it looks like optional dialogue.

<h2 id="theater-route-problems">
  Theater route, Shift, and progression checks
</h2>

**You can see the theater but cannot reach it.**  
Complete The Incursion Fault first. The theater route requires Shift.

**The parking garage entrance is blocked.**  
That is intentional. Stop trying to force the main entrance and follow the Jesse objective.

**You unlocked Shift but still cannot cross the large gap.**  
Move to the left side of the gap and use the side of the building as the traversal surface.

**The streets look vertical and the objective marker seems to be above you.**  
West Incursion deliberately changes orientation. Follow usable Patterned surfaces instead of trying to return to street-level "down."

**You found the missing Rangers but nothing updates when you inspect them.**  
There is no rescue action there. Continue forward into the theater.

**You entered the theater and do not know where to go next.**  
You have reached the Resonant encounter. Prepare your build and continue into the performance area.

**You defeated the Dancer but the main story is not advancing.**  
Make sure the Central Resonant is also complete. Both are required before the later Contain the Crisis progression opens.
