<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Find the anomalous taxi in each of Manhattan's **seven main zones**, enter it, and answer **Mila's phone** inside the Threshold. Solve three taxi rounds each time by choosing the vehicle that breaks the current pattern; after the seventh taxi, answer the phone one final time to finish The Last Taxi.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-last-taxi-works">
  How The Last Taxi actually works
</h2>

The first strange taxi does not look like the beginning of one of the longest side stories in Manhattan.

It flashes its lights, honks for Dylan's attention, and leaves a door open.

Getting inside pulls Dylan into a different version of the city.

The woman on the phone is **Mila**.

She is trapped somewhere inside the same Threshold, but Dylan and Mila are not standing in the same version of the city. They can hear each other while occupying different iterations of Manhattan.

That changes the objective from simply finding a way back out.

Dylan needs to keep entering the anomalous taxis scattered across Manhattan until the two versions of the Threshold line up closely enough to reach Mila.

There are **seven taxis in total**, one in each major zone:

- Downtown
- Central
- Evacuation Zone
- West Incursion Zone
- The Park
- Underpass
- Unknown

You do not need to find them in one fixed order.

Several are also gated by normal story progression, so trying to complete all seven the moment the first taxi appears only creates unnecessary backtracking.

<h2 id="mila-phone">
  Always answer Mila's phone before choosing a taxi
</h2>

Every taxi Threshold begins the same way.

Leave the main road and enter the side alley where the phone is ringing.

Answer it.

After the call, return to the main street and inspect the taxis.

Each visit gives you **three successful selections** to make.

A correct taxi produces a short sequence with another **green traffic light** appearing.

A wrong taxi sends Dylan back without advancing that round.

Nothing is permanently lost when you make a mistake. Return to the street, read the clue again, and pick another car.

<h2 id="puzzles-can-change">
  Do not memorize one puzzle for one zone
</h2>

The seven taxi locations are stable.

The puzzle variation attached to a particular taxi is less dependable.

Two players can reach the same regional taxi and encounter the puzzle types in a different order. The solution logic itself remains consistent.

So use the sections below by **what the taxis are doing**, not simply by the zone where you entered the Threshold.

This also explains why a solution you saw for "the Central taxi" may look completely different from the taxis standing in front of you.

Look at the actual clue.

<h2 id="all-locations">
  All seven Last Taxi locations
</h2>

- **Downtown** — northern end of the Deserted Area, among the traffic jam. Reach the separated Deserted Area first.
- **Central** — streets behind the warehouse on the Patterned Factory side of Central.
- **Evacuation Zone** — alley between the Sinkhole–Evac Building road and the unauthorized mines route. Approach from above if the Hiss Spikes and lasers block the street.
- **West Incursion Zone** — parking area behind the Empire theater/cinema route. **Shift** is required for the altered gravity.
- **The Park** — inside the central parking garage, reached from the Mold-covered route above.
- **Underpass** — near the later Research Site after crossing the chasm.
- **Unknown** — along the road beside the Patterned Evac Building on its floating island.

Your <Link href="/control-resonant/map">Manhattan map</Link> makes the cleanup much easier once several zones have already been revealed.

The taxi also helps you at close range: listen for repeated **horns** and watch for flashing lights when you are near the right location.

<h2 id="downtown-taxi">
  Downtown taxi
</h2>

The Downtown taxi sits inside the **Deserted Area**.

You first need to cross the barrier separating that section from the normal Downtown streets.

Once inside, follow the main road north.

Near the northern end, the road is packed with abandoned vehicles. The anomalous taxi is mixed into that traffic jam.

Do not spend too long trying to identify it visually from far away. Move through the cars and listen for the horn.

<h2 id="central-taxi">
  Central taxi
</h2>

Start from the Field Office side of Central.

Go through the building that opens access toward the **Patterned Factory** portion of the zone, then continue onto the surrounding streets.

The taxi is behind the warehouse / factory-side blocks rather than directly beside the Field Office.

If you already explored Central thoroughly for Power Lines, Crossing the Threshold, or the Patterned Factory, check your map before repeating the entire route. The taxi may already have been recorded when you passed nearby.

<h2 id="evacuation-taxi">
  Evacuation Zone taxi
</h2>

Look between:

- the road connecting the **Sinkhole and Evac Building**;
- and the route toward the unauthorized mines.

Near the Evac Building side, find the alley blocked by **Hiss Spikes**.

The taxi is beyond those hazards.

Trying to weave through the spikes and lasers at street level is possible, but it is unnecessarily awkward.

Climb upward and use the surrounding rooftops to cross the dangerous section from above, then drop back down on the far side.

<h2 id="west-incursion-taxi">
  West Incursion Zone taxi
</h2>

The West Incursion taxi is near the route leading toward the theater.

Look around the small parking area behind the Empire theater/cinema section.

The surrounding streets have been rotated by Patterning, so the taxi may appear to be above Dylan even when the map marker looks close.

Complete <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">The Incursion Fault</Link> first if gravity is stopping you from reaching it.

Shift is the intended traversal tool here.

Once you are close, the taxi horn is far more useful than trying to interpret the normal street grid.

<h2 id="park-taxi">
  The Park taxi
</h2>

The Park taxi is inside the central parking structure.

Walking into the garage from street level does not take Dylan directly to it.

Continue through the garage until you emerge on the opposite side, then look for the large Mold-covered wall nearby.

Climb onto that growth to reach the roof.

From above, locate the larger Mold formation inside the parking structure and drop onto the lower level.

Follow the internal road around until you reach the anomalous taxi.

Do not clear every piece of Mold in the garage first. Most of it has nothing to do with reaching the taxi.

<h2 id="underpass-taxi">
  Underpass taxi
</h2>

The Underpass taxi becomes practical to collect during the later part of **Recursions and Iterations**.

Progress until Dylan has crossed the chasm and reached the Research Site again.

From the later research camp, search around the **northeastern side**.

The taxi periodically flashes its lights and sounds its horn.

Because the Underpass has poor sight lines and repeated-looking concrete structures, I rely on the sound here more than the scenery.

You can enter it while the Underpass story objective is still active.

When the Threshold returns Dylan, he may not be standing at the exact point where he entered, but he remains in the same general section.

<h2 id="unknown-taxi">
  Unknown taxi
</h2>

The last naturally reachable taxi is in the **Unknown**.

You need enough late-game progression to move reliably between its floating islands.

Reach the island containing the **Patterned Evac Building**.

Stand facing the building and look down the road to the right.

The anomalous taxi is along that road.

Unlike the Underpass taxi, this one is not especially difficult to spot once Dylan is on the correct island. Getting to the island is the real progression gate.

<h2 id="streetlight-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: find the different streetlight
</h2>

One Threshold variation places taxis beneath streetlights.

Ignore the cars at first.

Watch the lamps.

### Round 1

Only **one streetlight is flashing**.

Enter the taxi underneath it.

### Round 2

The rule reverses.

Now the streetlights flash, but **one stays continuously lit**.

Use the taxi beneath the steady light.

### Round 3

All of the lights flash, but one is **out of sync**.

Wait through two or three cycles rather than guessing from the first blink.

Enter the taxi under the lamp that breaks the shared rhythm.

<h2 id="vehicle-lights-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: headlights, rear lights, and Taxi sign
</h2>

This variation uses the taxi itself rather than the streetlights.

### Round 1

Find the car where the full light set is active:

- headlights;
- rear lights;
- illuminated Taxi roof sign.

Enter that taxi.

### Round 2

This time, look specifically for the taxi with the **roof Taxi sign illuminated**.

Do not require the full headlight and rear-light combination from the previous round.

### Round 3

Return to the full-light rule.

Choose the taxi with the headlights, rear lights, and Taxi sign all active together.

<h2 id="roof-sign-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: the Taxi roof sign reacts when Dylan approaches
</h2>

This variation is easier if you walk directly toward each candidate rather than studying everything from the center of the street.

### Round 1

Choose the taxi with the illuminated **Taxi roof sign**.

Some versions of this opening can also give an audible indicator/ticking clue; in that case, use the only vehicle producing the odd signal.

### Round 2

Approach each taxi.

Most roof signs switch off when Dylan gets close.

One stays illuminated.

Enter that car.

### Round 3

Move close to one taxi, then turn the camera and scan the others.

Look for the roof sign that remains visibly illuminated after the proximity reaction.

That is the next correct taxi.

<h2 id="flashing-pattern-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: watch the taxis flash
</h2>

Do not choose during the first flash.

Stand still and watch several cycles.

### Round 1

Most taxis flash together.

One flashes **off-beat**.

Enter the out-of-sync taxi.

### Round 2

The cars now flash in sequence, but one taxi flashes **twice** while the others flash once.

Use the double-flashing car.

### Round 3

Almost everything flashes.

One taxi remains completely dark.

Enter the taxi that never turns its lights on.

<h2 id="blackout-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: compare taxi lights when the streetlights go dark
</h2>

For this variation, the answer only becomes obvious during the blackout.

Wait.

### Round 1

When the streetlights switch off, one taxi keeps its:

- headlights;
- rear lights;
- roof sign

illuminated.

Enter that car.

### Round 2

Wait for the next blackout.

This time, choose the taxi with **all of its lights off**.

### Round 3

Look for the car whose relationship with the streetlight is different from the rest.

The correct taxi stays dark during the normal state, then briefly reveals its lights around the streetlight transition.

Do not rush the final choice. Watch one full light cycle before entering.

<h2 id="mold-repair-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: repair the taxi in The Park-style Threshold
</h2>

This is the only major variation where spotting the odd car is not enough.

You have to physically rebuild the taxi.

### First repair

Destroy the grey Mold growth around the vehicle.

Recover and reinstall:

- the hood;
- the trunk cover.

Once both pieces are attached, enter the taxi.

### Second repair

This time find:

- **two wheels**;
- the **yellow hood**.

Attach all three.

Do not use another hood simply because it fits near the vehicle.

### Final Mold section

The main Mold mass regenerates until the connected growths are removed.

Follow the grey strands away from the taxi and destroy the smaller connected Mold clusters first.

Once those are gone, destroy the growth covering the car.

Now rebuild it with the correct pieces:

- yellow hood;
- Taxi roof light;
- yellow front-side body panel;
- dark-yellow trunk cover;
- rear grille.

There are decoy parts nearby.

In particular, leave the **police lights** and the incorrect light-yellow hood alone.

When the taxi looks complete and consistent, get in.

<h2 id="shadow-puzzle">
  Puzzle type: use the portable light to inspect shadows
</h2>

The Unknown-style variation gives Dylan a movable light.

Pick it up.

Do not compare the taxis themselves.

Shine the light across each one and watch the shadow.

Several vehicles cast the silhouette of something that is clearly not a taxi.

### Round 1

Find the only vehicle producing a proper taxi-shaped shadow.

### Round 2

Repeat the process with the larger group of candidates.

Some taxis can be farther across the street, so scan beyond the closest row.

### Round 3

The shadows become more convincing.

Look closely at the roof.

The correct shadow includes the shape of the **Taxi sign** on top of the vehicle.

Enter that car.

<h2 id="correct-or-wrong">
  How to tell whether you picked the correct taxi
</h2>

The Threshold gives immediate feedback.

A **green traffic light** means the selection was correct and the puzzle has advanced.

A red result means the choice was wrong.

You do not lose the entire quest or reset the previous taxis.

Return to the road and solve the current round again.

<h2 id="after-seven">
  What to do after solving all seven taxis
</h2>

The seventh successful Threshold does not necessarily finish the story the instant Dylan returns to Manhattan.

Go back into the alley one final time and answer the ringing phone.

That last conversation resolves Mila's attempt to escape the Threshold and closes **The Last Taxi**.

Finishing The Last Taxi does not add a separate combat power, Artifact, or equipment reward to Dylan's inventory.

The payoff is completing Mila's story and closing the side-story objective. If the Quest Log updates without another reward screen, the mission is finished.

The main payoff is completing Mila's storyline and clearing the side-story objective.

So if the Quest Log closes without a new weapon or ability appearing, you have not missed a reward screen.

<h2 id="taxi-problems">
  Why The Last Taxi stops progressing
</h2>

**You found a taxi but nothing happens when you inspect the other cars.**  
Answer Mila's ringing phone in the alley first. The puzzle starts after the conversation.

**The solution you found for this Zone does not match your taxis.**  
Do not force it. Puzzle variations can appear at different taxi locations. Identify the actual clue type in front of you.

**You chose a taxi and got sent back.**  
That was a wrong selection. Watch the lights, shadows, or proximity reaction again and retry the same round.

**The Mold covering the repair taxi keeps growing back.**  
Follow the connected strands and destroy the smaller linked Mold clusters before attacking the central growth.

**You cannot reach the West Incursion taxi.**  
Come back after unlocking Shift from The Incursion Fault.

**You cannot find the Underpass taxi.**  
Progress deeper into Recursions and Iterations until you have crossed the chasm and reached the later Research Site.

**You cannot reach the Unknown taxi.**  
Continue late-game Unknown progression until you can traverse to the Patterned Evac Building island.

**You solved seven taxi Thresholds but the side story is still active.**  
Return to the alley and answer Mila's phone one final time.
