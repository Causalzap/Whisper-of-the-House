<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Go to **Singing Reported?** in the western West Incursion Zone, enter the apartment through the rooftop, then use the karaoke choices in this order: **Right → Left → Right → Up → Left → Down → Right → Up → Right**. Do not miss a choice if you want to save every partygoer and unlock **Be What You Want to Be**.

<h2 id="where-to-start">
  Where to start The Party Won&apos;t Stop
</h2>

The party is inside an apartment building in the **western part of the West Incursion Zone**, south of the Parking Garage near the Broadway Testing Facility.

Look for the **Singing Reported?** activity marker.

When I reached the building, the first useful clue was not the music itself but the body outside. Report the situation to Zoe, then check the entrance.

The front door does not solve the route. It is locked from the inside.

You need to approach the apartment from above.

If you already completed the Jesse Fault sequence and have stronger traversal options, getting onto the surrounding rooftops is much easier. The gravity traversal you need is unlocked during the <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Incursion Fault</Link>.

<h2 id="how-to-enter">
  How to get inside the party building
</h2>

From the locked entrance, move toward the road beside the apartment and use the nearby gravity-shifted geometry to gain height.

The route I use is:

1. move away from the front door toward the roadside gravity transition;
2. follow the altered surface upward rather than trying to force the locked entrance;
3. cross toward the apartment rooftop;
4. look for the blue rooftop doors;
5. enter and follow the music downstairs.

You will pass more bodies before reaching the party.

That tells you what the failure condition actually is before Dylan ever touches the microphone: the people inside are not simply dancing for fun. Whatever is controlling the karaoke machine keeps them moving until their bodies give out.

Downstairs, enter the room with the dance floor.

The Altered karaoke machine is the object keeping everyone trapped.

<h2 id="karaoke-solution">
  The Party Won&apos;t Stop karaoke answers
</h2>

Interact with the machine to begin.

You do not fight it.

Instead, Dylan has to finish the song while the game repeatedly gives you a timed choice.

The useful rule is that the correct option completes the rhyme, but there is no reason to solve every prompt manually when the lives of the remaining dancers are tied to your answers.

For a perfect run, use this sequence:

1. **Right**
2. **Left**
3. **Right**
4. **Up**
5. **Left**
6. **Down**
7. **Right**
8. **Up**
9. **Right**

There are **nine choices total**.

The opening prompts are forgiving because the number of available options is limited. Later prompts give you more choices while still running a timer, so this is where it becomes easy to lose someone while trying to read every option.

I keep the nine-direction sequence visible before interacting with the machine rather than trying to remember it after the song starts.

<h2 id="wrong-answer">
  What happens if you choose the wrong karaoke answer?
</h2>

A wrong answer does **not** simply reset the current line.

One of the partygoers dies.

The same risk applies if you fail to make the required choice before the prompt expires.

The quest itself can continue after a mistake, so seeing the sequence move forward does not mean you still have a perfect result.

For the **Be What You Want to Be** trophy / achievement, every partygoer must survive.

That makes the important distinction:

- wrong answer → quest can continue;
- wrong answer → one civilian can die;
- any death → no **Be What You Want to Be** for that attempt.

If you only care about finishing the Side Story, you can continue.

If you are completing trophies, do not accept the failed result just because the objective advances. Reload an earlier save and repeat the karaoke sequence cleanly.

<h2 id="save-everyone">
  How to save everyone
</h2>

There is no extra combat condition or hidden NPC interaction after you reach the dance floor.

Saving everyone comes entirely from completing all nine karaoke selections correctly.

Do not leave the machine midway through the sequence, and do not spend the short selection windows rereading the entire screen.

Work through the directional sequence in order:

**Right → Left → Right → Up → Left → Down → Right → Up → Right**

After the ninth successful response, let the performance finish.

The civilians are released from the machine's influence instead of being forced to continue dancing.

<h2 id="rewards">
  The Party Won&apos;t Stop rewards
</h2>

Completing the encounter rewards Dylan with the **Varsity Jacket** and **Untapped Bar Blade**.

The Untapped Bar Blade is especially relevant if you use summons, because its effect can restore health while a summon is active.

A perfect karaoke run also unlocks:

**Be What You Want to Be**

That trophy / achievement is the reason I treat the karaoke section differently from most optional dialogue choices. The Side Story can survive a mistake; the perfect-survival result cannot.

Once the machine is dealt with and the survivors are safe, there is nothing else you need to activate inside the apartment to finish this sequence.

<h2 id="party-failures">
  Front door, missed answers, and trophy failures
</h2>

**The apartment front door will not open.**

That is expected. The intended entrance is on the rooftop. Move around the building and use the altered gravity route to gain height.

**You reached the roof but cannot find the party.**

Use the rooftop doors and move downward through the apartment. Follow the music rather than looking for another exterior entrance.

**You made one wrong choice but the quest did not fail.**

The quest and the perfect-survival result are separate. A wrong choice can kill a partygoer while still allowing you to finish the Side Story.

**The trophy did not unlock.**

All nine selections need to be correct and every partygoer needs to survive. Finishing the karaoke machine after losing someone is not enough.

**The later answers are disappearing too quickly.**

Do not try to solve each lyric after the prompt appears. Use the fixed directional order instead:

**Right, Left, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up, Right.**
