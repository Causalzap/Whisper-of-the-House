<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Investigate the **headless bodies at the Dig Site and Loading Docks**, use the Security Station evidence to locate **Wilson**, then hear him out. For the better tangible reward, **believe Wilson and carry AVP-3 back to the Threshold yourself** instead of choosing containment.

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How to start Crossing the Threshold
</h2>

Crossing the Threshold begins around the **Central Field Office** once Dylan has enough security access to investigate the nearby FBC facilities.

The first unmistakable clue is a dead FBC researcher whose head has violently ruptured.

One body can be found around the Field Office loading-dock area. Dylan identifies the victim as a **parabiologist assigned to a Threshold expedition team**, which points the investigation toward the Dig Site behind the Field Office.

This is not a normal Hiss casualty.

The expedition connection is what matters, so report the body when the radio prompt appears instead of simply looting the area and moving on.

Do not assume all of the clues are in one building. The investigation branches across the **Dig Site, Loading Docks, Field Hospital, and Security Station** before it finally points to Wilson.

<h2 id="dig-site">
  Investigate the Dig Site behind the Field Office
</h2>

The expedition's Dig Site is behind the Field Office.

Once you reach it, look for the large marked Threshold area and the dead researcher nearby.

Report the body first.

Then search beside it for the expedition access item and use the nearby elevated research room to inspect what the team left behind.

The site recording establishes two useful facts:

- something went badly wrong after the team interacted with the Threshold;
- surviving members moved away from the Dig Site rather than simply dying here.

The Threshold itself is also important later, so remember this location.

Zoe explains that the Threshold appeared only a couple of weeks before the crisis. The expedition was still studying its auditory and visual effects when everything collapsed.

That makes the deaths recent enough that following the surviving team is still useful.

<h2 id="field-hospital">
  Check the Field Hospital for the expedition's medical trail
</h2>

The Field Hospital contains another part of the story.

A medical recording describes one expedition member with symptoms that did not match normal Hiss possession: speech difficulty, impaired movement, delayed reactions, and worsening physical condition.

Another patient was already dead.

The staff began treating the situation as a possible **Threshold contagion**.

When you find the locked evidence cabinet in this area, report it rather than searching the hospital for a physical key.

The combination is stored in the Security Station.

The code is:

**0665**

Enter it at the cabinet after retrieving the information from the security terminal.

Inside is more expedition material that helps connect the Field Hospital deaths with the Dig Site and Loading Docks.

<h2 id="loading-docks">
  Report both the body and the security camera at the Loading Docks
</h2>

The Loading Docks contain another exploded-head victim.

Report the body, but do not leave immediately afterward.

Look around the room for the **security camera**.

That camera is the useful clue because the recording may show what happened before the victim died.

Once Dylan reports it, the investigation sends you back toward the Security Station to review the footage.

This step is easy to miss if you treat the Loading Docks as nothing more than another corpse location.

The body tells you another expedition member died.

The camera tells you there may have been a witness.

<h2 id="security-footage">
  Use the Security Station before searching Central for Wilson
</h2>

Go back to the Security Station and use the terminals.

One terminal provides the Field Hospital cabinet code.

The other lets Dylan review the Loading Docks recording.

That evidence shifts suspicion toward **Wilson**, a member of the Threshold expedition team who survived when several coworkers did not.

Do not start searching rooftops or the Field Office interior for him.

A Ranger report later places Wilson at a **warehouse behind the Field Office near the northern perimeter**.

That is your next destination.

<h2 id="find-wilson">
  The warehouse marker is not on the roof
</h2>

When you reach the warehouse, the objective marker can look as though it is pointing above you.

There is no required rooftop interaction.

Move around to the **northwestern side of the warehouse** and find the usable entrance at ground level.

Clear the enemies inside first.

Wilson is farther in with another badly injured researcher.

Do not make the decision based only on the bodies you found.

Let him explain what happened.

<h2 id="what-is-avp-3">
  What actually happened to Wilson and the expedition team?
</h2>

Wilson says the entity was not a disease and did not deliberately murder the team.

He calls it **AVP-3**.

According to Wilson, AVP-3 came through the Threshold and cannot survive normally in Dylan's dimension without occupying a human host.

The host it is using now is dying.

That creates the danger.

If the current body fails before AVP-3 reaches the Threshold, the entity may jump into someone nearby. Wilson believes it cannot properly control that reaction.

His goal is therefore to get AVP-3 back to the Threshold rather than let the FBC contain and study it.

This explains why the investigation looked like a series of bizarre murders: the expedition encountered something whose biology did not fit ordinary human survival, and the team interpreted the resulting deaths as contagion or deliberate violence.

<h2 id="choice">
  Should you contain AVP-3 or help Wilson?
</h2>

This is the real decision in Crossing the Threshold.

If you **believe Wilson and carry AVP-3 yourself**, Dylan takes the entity back through its Threshold and receives the **Sensory Restraint Hood** plus the **Untapped Practice Knot**.

If you **choose containment instead**, AVP-3 is treated as a containment threat and you do not receive the Hood or Practice Knot from this route.

For a normal playthrough, I would **believe Wilson and help AVP-3 return**.

It gives Dylan the stronger material outcome and also lets the quest resolve through the discovery you actually made rather than treating the entity as a conventional hostile Altered threat.

Once you commit to helping, AVP-3 needs a new host for the trip back.

Dylan can carry it.

<h2 id="return-avp3">
  Take AVP-3 back to the Dig Site Threshold
</h2>

After Dylan becomes the temporary host, return to the same Dig Site you investigated earlier.

You do not need to search for another Threshold somewhere else in Central.

The destination is the **large marked Threshold at the original expedition site**.

The warehouse roof can provide a quick way to regain elevation, but the important part is simply reaching the Dig Site again with AVP-3 still inside Dylan.

Walk into the Threshold area.

That returns the entity to its own dimension and resolves the dangerous host problem.

There is no boss fight with AVP-3 on this route.

<h2 id="wilson-choice">
  What should you say about Wilson afterward?
</h2>

After AVP-3 is returned, Dylan can still decide how much of Wilson's involvement to disclose.

That conversation changes how Dylan frames what happened, but the critical gameplay decision has already been made earlier: whether to trust Wilson and personally return AVP-3 or turn the situation into an FBC containment response.

If your priority is the equipment and Artifact reward, the important choice is the **help AVP-3 / carry it yourself** route.

You do not need to reload just because Wilson originally looked suspicious.

The investigation is intentionally structured so the early evidence points toward him before the full explanation becomes available.

<h2 id="investigation-problems">
  Why the Crossing the Threshold investigation stops progressing
</h2>

**You reported a headless body but have no clear next clue.**  
Check both the Dig Site and Loading Docks. This investigation expects evidence from more than one location.

**The Field Hospital cabinet is locked.**  
Use the Security Station terminal and enter **0665** at the cabinet.

**You finished the Loading Docks but no Wilson marker appeared.**  
Make sure you reported the security camera and reviewed its footage at the Security Station.

**The warehouse marker looks like it is on the roof.**  
Do not climb around looking for an interaction. Enter from the northwest side of the building.

**Wilson sounds guilty, but the quest still gives you dialogue choices.**  
Keep listening. The deaths are connected to AVP-3's inability to survive without a host, not a simple murder plot.

**You chose to help AVP-3 but do not know where to go.**  
Return to the original Dig Site behind the Field Office and enter the Threshold there.
