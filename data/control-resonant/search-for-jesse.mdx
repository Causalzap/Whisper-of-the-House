<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
You find Jesse by following the dimensional Faults rather than one permanent
map marker. Complete them in order: **Incursion Fault → Subway Fault →
Underpass Fault**. The first gives you the gravity traversal needed for West
Incursion, while the subway sequence gives you the ability needed to cross the
large distortions encountered later.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

After Dylan first makes contact with Jesse, she does not return to Manhattan as
a normal NPC you can walk up to.

She is somewhere inside the dimensional instability spreading through the
city. From this point forward, finding her means following the **Faults**.

That distinction matters because several moments can look like ordinary
navigation blockers. A locked Parking Garage, a street rotated onto another
gravity plane, or an impossible gap are often signs that Dylan has reached the
next stage too early rather than missed a nearby door.

The Jesse route progresses through three major encounters:

1. **The Incursion Fault** in the West Incursion Zone
2. **The Subway Fault** around Railyard Street Station
3. **The Underpass Fault** later in the story

Each one moves Dylan closer to Jesse and changes what routes he can use
afterward.

<h2 id="incursion-fault">
  Find Jesse in the Incursion Fault
</h2>

The first major search begins in the **West Incursion Zone**.

Follow the zone progression until Dylan reaches the Parking Garage. If you are
still trying to reach this part of the district, the main route is covered in
the <Link href="/control-resonant/west-incursion-zone">West Incursion Zone walkthrough</Link>.

All of the abandoned cars eventually point Dylan toward the same structure.

The Parking Garage.

When you reach it, the entrance is blocked.

The objective changes because Dylan cannot enter from here.

Do not spend time searching the garage doors for another interaction point.
Leave the entrance and move toward the nearby **laundromat**.

<h2 id="parking-garage-key">
  Where to find the Parking Garage key
</h2>

The laundromat is the route around the blockage.

Go around the building rather than staying at its front entrance and look for
access from the rear.

Once inside, search the back section for the **Parking Garage key**.

Take it, then return to the garage.

The same route that previously displayed the key requirement can now be opened,
allowing Dylan to move deeper below the building.

From this point onward, normal city navigation stops being reliable.

The Fault is already affecting the area. Cars, floors, walls, and gravity begin
to separate from the orientation Dylan entered with.

Keep following the accessible structure downward until the Jesse sequence
begins.

<h2 id="first-jesse-encounter">
  What happens when Dylan reaches Jesse
</h2>

Dylan eventually realizes Jesse is inside the Fault.

She is trying to stop something from breaking through the dimensional
boundaries and tells Dylan she needs to complete the ritual alone.

There is no dialogue choice that changes this section.

Keep moving through the sequence.

Jesse explains that every Fault must be closed before the walls between
dimensions fail completely.

Dylan refuses to simply leave her behind.

The encounter ends with Jesse still separated from him, but Dylan now
understands two important things:

- the Patterning is connected to the Faults;
- finding Jesse means following those Faults as they appear.

When the sequence releases Dylan back into Manhattan, do not keep searching the
Parking Garage for Jesse.

She is gone from this location.

<h2 id="gravity-traversal">
  What changes after the Incursion Fault
</h2>

The Incursion Fault unlocks **Shift**, which lets Dylan follow gravity-altered surfaces in West Incursion.

Walls, roads, and buildings that previously looked like hard dead ends can now become the route itself. When the street continues along the side of a building, follow the altered gravity instead of searching for a normal staircase.

When the street appears to continue along the side of a building, stop trying
to find a conventional staircase back to normal ground.

Follow the altered orientation.

This is especially important when returning toward the Theater in West
Incursion. Several routes that looked impossible before the Jesse encounter are
now intentional traversal paths.

If your current objective is the West Incursion Resonant, return to the <Link href="/control-resonant/west-incursion-zone">West Incursion route</Link> after finishing the Fault.

<h2 id="subway-fault">
  Find the Subway Fault at Railyard Street Station
</h2>

The next Jesse trail takes Dylan toward the affected subway around
**Railyard Street Station** in the Evacuation Zone.

This is not the same objective as simply rerouting a station during Power
Lines.

The subway itself has been distorted.

Reports from the area describe tunnels and trains being displaced by severe
spatial changes, and the route becomes increasingly unstable as Dylan follows
it.

Once inside, keep moving through the available tunnel route even when the
station no longer resembles a normal subway layout.

Do not leave because the path appears to repeat or bend back on itself.

That deformation is part of the Fault.

As Dylan pushes farther in, the search connects him with Jesse again and opens
the next stage of dimensional traversal.

<h2 id="reach-distortions">
  What the strange spherical distortions are for
</h2>

The Subway Fault unlocks **Reach**, which lets Dylan cross the large spherical distortions placed across gaps that normal movement cannot clear.

The Underpass gives one of the clearest examples.

Dylan reaches a gap and immediately recognizes that he cannot make the jump.

Across the gap is a visible distortion.

The game is telling you that ordinary movement is no longer the solution.

If you encounter one of these gaps before completing the subway Jesse sequence,
leave it and return later.

Once the required traversal is available, the distortion becomes the route
forward.

<h2 id="underpass-fault">
  Find Jesse again in the Underpass Fault
</h2>

The third major search happens much later in the **Underpass**.

By this point Dylan has already been following the research team deeper into
the area, and the environment is becoming increasingly unstable.

Continue down until the route begins using the large dimensional distortions
across broken spaces.

This is where the previous subway progression becomes necessary.

A platform can look close enough to jump to and still be deliberately out of
range. When that happens, look for the distortion rather than repeatedly
attempting the same jump.

As Dylan moves deeper, the Underpass gradually gives way to another Fault.

Do not turn around when the location stops resembling the physical Underpass.

You are entering the Jesse sequence.

<h2 id="underpass-ritual">
  How to progress through the Underpass ritual
</h2>

The Underpass Fault works differently from the first encounter.

Instead of navigating only through warped architecture, Dylan begins moving
through fragments of memory.

You will encounter glowing human figures along the route.

They matter to progression.

Move forward slowly enough for them to follow Dylan.

When one stops and the next part of the sequence refuses to trigger, go back
toward it.

Do not keep exploring farther ahead hoping to activate something else.

Once the figure starts moving again, lead it toward the next memory space.

This is not a combat escort objective. The important part is keeping the
required figure connected to Dylan's route.

<h2 id="dylan-forgets-jesse">
  What to do when Dylan forgets Jesse
</h2>

The most disorienting part of the Fault comes when Dylan begins losing his
memory.

He reaches for Jesse and suddenly cannot even identify who she is.

There is no key, collectible, or hidden switch to find here.

Keep following the memory route.

Jesse warns Dylan that this place takes pieces from people, especially their
memories.

The sequence then rebuilds parts of their relationship while Dylan struggles to
hold onto who Jesse is.

When a figure falls behind, return for it.

When Dylan temporarily forgets Jesse, continue forward.

When the environment changes again, follow the new path rather than trying to
return to the previous room.

Eventually Dylan remembers enough to reconnect with her.

<h2 id="one-fault-left">
  What Jesse means by the remaining Fault
</h2>

The Underpass encounter gives Dylan a clearer explanation of where Jesse has
been.

They are not simply standing in another room beneath Manhattan.

Jesse describes their dimension as one room in a house full of rooms, while the
space they occupy inside the Fault exists **between** those places.

She is trying to seal the openings before more can leak through.

The environment itself is dangerous because prolonged exposure begins taking
their memories.

After Dylan reconnects with Jesse, this stage of the search ends.

Do not remain in the Underpass looking for another nearby Jesse marker.

The progression now moves back into the larger story rather than immediately
opening another Fault beside the one you just completed.

<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  Where to go after finding Jesse in the Underpass
</h2>

Once the Underpass sequence is finished, follow the new story objective and
Zoe's radio instructions.

At this point, Jesse is no longer a normal "find the missing person" objective.
Dylan understands what she is doing and why she cannot simply walk back into
Manhattan with him.

If you are unsure where the main story goes from here, return to the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">CONTROL Resonant walkthrough</Link> and continue from your current objective.

<h2 id="jesse-route-problems">
  Why the Jesse route stops progressing
</h2>

### The Parking Garage says Key Required

Leave the garage.

Go to the nearby laundromat, enter from the rear, take the Parking Garage key,
then return.

### The Parking Garage entrance is blocked

That is the intended progression state.

You are not supposed to force the main vehicle entrance. Follow the alternate
route connected to the Incursion Fault.

### Jesse disappeared after the first Fault

She is supposed to.

Do not keep searching that room or garage. Continue the game until the next
Fault-related Jesse sequence appears.

### West Incursion has a route on a wall and I cannot progress normally

You have reached gravity-altered traversal.

Complete the Incursion Fault first, then treat the rotated surface as the route
rather than looking for stairs back to the original ground plane.

### I reached a gap in the Underpass that Dylan cannot jump

Look across the gap for the spherical dimensional distortion.

If you cannot use it yet, you are missing the traversal progression connected
to the subway Jesse sequence.

### A glowing person stopped moving during the Underpass Fault

Walk back toward the figure and let it catch up.

The ritual can stop progressing when the required figure is left behind.

### Dylan cannot remember Jesse's name

Keep going.

The Fault is taking Dylan's memories. You have not missed an item or dialogue
choice.

### I finished the Underpass sequence and cannot find Jesse again

Leave the area and follow the updated main story objective.

There is no additional Jesse interaction hidden in the same section of the
Underpass.
