<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Reroute the three normal subway stations by collecting their **Calibration Units** and placing them around each **Tuning Device**. After those lines are working, continue into the **Vanished Station**, rescue the survivors, collect four Calibration Units, then calibrate **Platform A and Platform B** to finish the power route.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-power-lines-works">
  Power Lines uses three normal stations before the Vanished Station
</h2>

Arish sends Dylan into the subway because the CFD needs a huge amount of electricity.

The FBC has already done most of the engineering. Three stations were prepared to redirect power through paranatural junctions, while a fourth station was supposed to funnel that combined electricity into the CFD.

Then the fourth station disappeared.

That distinction makes the mission easier to read. The first three stations are individual rerouting jobs:

- **Factory Station**
- **East Park Station**
- **Perimeter Station**

The exact order you visit those three is less important than completing all three. Different routes through Central can put you at them in a different sequence.

Each teaches another part of the same system: collect Calibration Units, identify the correct taped positions, then use the Tuning Device.

Once the three feeder stations are handled, the mission leads into the **Vanished Station**, where those mechanics become much more involved.

You do not need to buy or craft anything for these puzzles. Every Calibration Unit, electrical charge, and device required to finish Power Lines is found inside the stations themselves.

<h2 id="factory-station">
  Factory Station: line up three Calibration Units
</h2>

Factory Station is the cleanest introduction to the system.

You need **three Calibration Units**.

They are large movable units with prominent magnetic hardware on top. Search the station before trying to guess where they belong.

The three are located:

1. inside the small shop above the ticket barriers near the entrance;
2. on top of the train on the left side of the platform;
3. behind the entrance staircase, reached by dropping onto the tracks and moving around the back.

Once all three are together, placement matters.

Two units go into the taped squares **between the two benches**. The third goes into the taped square **in front of the staircase**.

The three units should form a straight alignment in front of the Tuning Device.

Use the device only after they are positioned.

A useful failure check is the Tuning Device itself. If activating it causes the device to move instead of completing the reroute, one or more Calibration Units are in the wrong positions.

Interact with it again to restore the device, correct the alignment, and try again.

After the station reroutes successfully, use the intercom before moving on.

<h2 id="east-park-station">
  East Park Station: clear the Mold and use the Tuning Device to reach the last unit
</h2>

East Park introduces **Mold** into the station puzzle.

Start by going down to the platform and destroying the Mold covering the three taped Calibration Unit squares. Until those spaces are clear, there is no reason to start arranging the units.

Again, you need **three Calibration Units**.

The first is inside the small shop just past the ticket barriers.

The second is already on the platform.

The third is the one that can make this station look incomplete. It is trapped inside the other small shop.

Use the **Tuning Device** repeatedly until the spatial shift puts Dylan inside that locked shop. Once inside, destroy the electrical panel to raise the gate and carry the Calibration Unit out.

There are exactly three valid squares here, so unlike Factory Station, you do not need to solve a specific ordering puzzle. Put one unit in each cleared square and activate the Tuning Device.

When all three are positioned correctly, the device reroutes the station instead of moving Dylan somewhere else.

Use the intercom before leaving.

<h2 id="perimeter-station">
  Perimeter Station: the extra yellow squares are decoys
</h2>

Perimeter Station is where I stop treating every taped square as a valid answer.

There are still only **three Calibration Units**, but the station gives you more possible yellow positions than you actually need.

Two units are straightforward to collect.

One is at the top of the staircase opposite the stairs you used to enter the platform.

Another is down on the tracks behind the entrance staircase, near the Mold.

The third requires electricity.

Before picking up the electrical charge, locate the **Power Core on the tracks beyond the platform**. Memorizing that route first saves a failed run because the charge does not last indefinitely.

Then:

1. go to the small shop and take the electrical charge;
2. run directly to the Power Core;
3. transfer the charge before it dissipates;
4. enter the room that opens;
5. take the third Calibration Unit.

If the charge disappears before you reach the Core, nothing is permanently lost. Go back, take it again, and make a cleaner run without stopping to fight or explore.

Now place the three Calibration Units in the correct squares:

- one at the top of the opposite staircase near the Mold;
- one inside the shop opened by the powered Core;
- one in the square nearest the Tuning Device, beside the trash can.

Do not fill the other taped spaces just because they are visible.

Activate the Tuning Device after those three positions are occupied.

When the configuration is correct, the station sequence carries Dylan toward the final part of Power Lines.

<h2 id="vanished-station">
  Vanished Station: do not waste time trying to climb back to the surface
</h2>

The Vanished Station immediately breaks the pattern established by the first three stations.

Your first instinct may be to head upstairs.

Do it once to confirm the route, but the stairs lead into a **brick wall**. There is no hidden opening to find there.

Report the situation, then use the intercom.

From this point the mission is doing two things at once:

- rerouting the last station;
- getting the trapped survivors out.

Drop onto the tracks and continue through the station until you reach the other platform area.

The Tuning Device becomes part of the route between **Platform A and Platform B**.

Once you reach Platform B, go upstairs and destroy the electrical panel beside the large gate. That opens the physical connection between the two platforms, so you are no longer dependent on the Tuning Device every time you need to cross.

There is an intercom near the vending-machine area where you can contact the survivors.

<h2 id="rescue-survivors">
  Cut the lights before clearing the Mold around the survivors
</h2>

The survivors are blocked by Mold, but simply destroying it is not enough while the environment keeps supporting its growth.

Look at the nearby Power Cores.

Move the electrical charge away from the Core feeding the lights and into the Core associated with the shop.

The room becomes darker, but that is intentional.

With the lights no longer feeding the Mold, destroy the growth blocking the survivor area. It will stop immediately rebuilding itself.

Go inside and speak with **Dr. Florez**.

This conversation also puts you in the right area to begin gathering the Vanished Station's Calibration Units.

<h2 id="vanished-calibration-units">
  Collect all four Calibration Units before finishing either platform
</h2>

Vanished Station needs **four Calibration Units** in total.

Platform A uses two.

Platform B uses two.

The individual units are interchangeable, so there is no special "A unit" or "B unit." What matters is ending with **two on each platform**.

The four units are:

1. **one inside the shop** opened through the nearby power routing;
2. **two inside the survivors' room**;
3. **one behind the raised gate on Platform A**.

For the Platform A unit behind the gate, send electrical power into the appropriate Power Core so the gate at the top of the stairs opens.

Because two units are already sitting together inside the survivor room, I carry those toward Platform B and avoid unnecessary trips back across the station.

Collecting all four before committing to the final calibrations also makes it much easier to keep track of what is still missing.

<h2 id="platform-a">
  Platform A only needs two units in the two yellow squares
</h2>

Platform A is the simpler half of the Vanished Station.

Bring **two Calibration Units** to the platform.

Place one unit in each of the two marked yellow squares, then activate the Tuning Device.

There is no additional ordering requirement between those two units.

Once Platform A registers correctly, move on to Platform B.

Do not assume the same solution works there. Platform B's wiring is damaged, and its Mold needs to be manipulated rather than simply removed.

<h2 id="platform-b">
  Platform B uses Mold to reconnect the broken wiring
</h2>

Platform B is the real final puzzle.

The important detail is counterintuitive:

**you need some of the Mold.**

The Mold conducts electricity and can bridge the damaged wiring around the platform.

Start with the electrical charge already available in this section.

Move it into the Power Core closer to the Tuning Device. This encourages the Mold to spread into the wiring.

Then move the charge back to the original Core so the growth extends farther.

You are deliberately allowing the useful Mold to connect the broken electrical path.

Once enough has grown, remove the electrical charge from Platform B entirely. A nearby Core around the survivor area works as a place to leave it.

This stops the Mold from continuously regrowing while you clean up the platform.

Now destroy the **unwanted Mold** covering the metal placement plates.

Leave the Mold that is actually bridging the damaged wires.

The easiest way to read the puzzle is:

- Mold attached to the broken wiring = useful;
- Mold covering the Calibration Unit positions = remove it.

Once the two placement points are clear, put the remaining **two Calibration Units** into them and activate the Tuning Device.

That completes the second platform.

If the Mold immediately grows back over the placement areas, power is still feeding the wrong part of the platform. Move the charge away first, then clear the excess growth again.

<h2 id="finish-power-lines">
  Finish the station and get the survivors back to the surface
</h2>

After both platforms are calibrated, clear the remaining hostile Mold creatures and return toward Dr. Florez.

The station geometry has changed again by this point, and the exit route becomes usable.

Get the survivors out and finish the final combat on the way back to safety.

Then speak with Dr. Florez when the objective calls for it.

Zoe later confirms that the **power lines to the CFD are up and running**.

That does not necessarily trigger the next major story event immediately.

Power is only one requirement for the CFD. The FBC also needs the Sinkhole resonance work and additional Resonant data completed during the same broader stage of the story.

If Power Lines is finished but the main story appears to pause, continue with the remaining active objective instead of returning to these stations.

<h2 id="power-lines-stuck">
  Use the station state to tell what is still wrong
</h2>

Power Lines gives several different failure states, and each points to a different fix.

**Factory Station's Tuning Device moves instead of rerouting power.**  
The three Calibration Units are not aligned in the correct squares. Put two between the benches and the third in front of the stairs.

**East Park still has one missing Calibration Unit.**  
Use the Tuning Device until it shifts Dylan into the locked shop, then break the electrical panel and carry the unit out.

**Perimeter's electrical charge disappears before reaching the Core.**  
Find the destination Core first, then repeat the run without stopping.

**Perimeter has all three units but will not complete.**  
There are extra taped squares. Use the three specific positions near the opposite staircase, inside the powered shop, and beside the Tuning Device.

**The Vanished Station exit is a brick wall.**  
That is normal. Continue through the platforms.

**Mold keeps returning on Platform B.**  
Move the electrical charge away from Platform B before destroying the excess growth.

**Both platforms are calibrated but Power Lines has not closed.**  
Return to the survivors, clear the remaining enemies, and follow the objective back to Dr. Florez.
