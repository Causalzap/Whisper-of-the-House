<h2 id="how-to-reach-underpass">
  Go back through the Sinkhole route below the laundromat
</h2>

The Underpass does not open as another street-level Manhattan zone.

After the CFD incident, Pope sends a four-person research team below the city
to study how the Patterning has changed the Sinkhole. Their communications
fail, no research data comes back, and Dylan is sent after them.

Use the **escape route below the laundromat** to head down again. This is the
same broader route Dylan used to get out of the earlier Sinkhole sequence, but
the area beneath it has changed enough that the FBC treats what lies below as a
new section.

Keep descending until Zoe identifies the new depth as the **Underpass**.

The first useful trail is physical rather than supernatural.

You find **climbing ropes** left by the research team, then abandoned equipment
farther below. Neither marks the end of the search. When Dylan finds their gear
without the researchers, Zoe correctly assumes they continued deeper.

FBC lights and beacons farther ahead are the next landmarks.

<h2 id="research-site">
  Follow the research trail until time starts repeating
</h2>

The Underpass becomes much harder to read once Dylan reaches the expedition
area.

This is not just architecture shifting around him. Different versions of the
same research expedition begin overlapping.

Dr. Melanie Grist explains that she has been observing **variant timelines** of
the expedition. The people and camps Dylan encounters can belong to different
iterations of the same events, and time has passed differently for them.

That explains several otherwise contradictory moments:

- somebody can speak as though Dylan has already been here;
- a researcher may have experienced weeks or months while very little time
  passed for Dylan;
- equipment can be intact in one iteration and ruined in another;
- people who appear alive in one state can already be dead in another.

Do not try to solve those contradictions by returning to the previous camp and
checking whether you missed a conversation.

They are part of the route.

Grist's research changes the objective from simply finding the missing team to
recovering what all of those iterations have learned.

Pope wants the **data from every iteration**, because some versions of the
expedition have effectively had far longer to study the Patterning.

That makes the Research Site's transmission equipment your next real
progression gate.

<h2 id="restore-power">
  Restore the Research Site power: one Power Core is active, three need charges
</h2>

The transmission equipment cannot send the accumulated research while the site
is underpowered.

At the main power area, there are **four Power Cores** in the circuit.

Only **one** starts with an active charge.

So you are looking for **three more charges**, not four.

This section looks more complicated than it is because the surrounding space
keeps changing.

The useful navigation clue is the **yellow tape on the ground**.

The researchers placed the tape so they could find their way through the camp,
and although the Patterning has started distorting the route, those markings
still lead toward the remote Power Cores.

Work through them one route at a time.

The reliable loop is:

1. start at the room with the four Power Cores;
2. choose one yellow-tape route;
3. follow it to an active remote Power Core;
4. take its electrical charge;
5. immediately retrace the same markings;
6. place that charge into one of the dead Cores;
7. repeat until all three missing Cores are powered.

The electrical charge does not remain available indefinitely, so this is not a
good section for exploring side rooms while carrying one.

Once you pick up a charge, go straight back.

<h2 id="yellow-tape">
  Use the yellow tape in both directions
</h2>

The easiest way to get turned around here is to navigate by scenery.

The Underpass is already difficult to read before the timeline effects begin,
and the research camp itself is being altered. A corridor or piece of equipment
that looked distinctive on the way out is less dependable than the markings
under Dylan's feet.

Use the tape as a two-way route.

When you reach an active Power Core, turn around and follow the same trail back
instead of looking for a shorter return path.

A failed trip is easy to recognize: the charge disappears before you reach the
dead Power Core.

When that happens, return to the active Core and make the run again. You have
not consumed the solution permanently.

The important state is the receiving room.

After each successful delivery, another of the four Cores should be powered.
When all four are active, stop searching the taped branches and continue with
the transmission objective.

<h2 id="transmit-research-data">
  Four powered Cores are not the end of the objective
</h2>

Restoring power only brings the Research Site back online.

The reason Pope sent Dylan down here is the research data.

As the overlapping timelines become clearer, the FBC begins receiving short
transmission bursts, but those partial blips are not enough. Dylan has to keep
moving through the time fluctuations and the different versions of the camp
until the system finally transmits the complete dataset.

A brief message from Pope can make this section feel finished too early.

It is not complete when she receives **a blip**.

Keep going until the FBC confirms that **all data is transmitting in full**.

That is the reliable completion state for the Research Site transmission.

<h2 id="follow-time-fluctuations">
  Keep following the current iteration even when the camp changes
</h2>

Once power is restored, Dylan keeps encountering other versions of Grist and
the expedition.

Some have been underground for far longer. Others have already reached
conclusions that the version you met earlier had not.

There is no single camp state you are supposed to restore.

Follow whichever time fluctuation the objective is currently using.

Eventually the complete research archive reaches Pope.

The result is disappointing: even after combining decades of research spread
across multiple timelines, the data still does not provide a way to stop the
Patterning.

That failed conclusion opens the next route rather than ending the Underpass
mission.

<h2 id="cross-chasm">
  Cross the chasm and place the relays beneath the Oldest House
</h2>

A later version of Grist realizes the expedition has ignored one important
variable:

**the Oldest House itself is resisting the Patterning.**

She wants to study that resistance directly.

To do that, Dylan has to cross the chasm and help place relays **beneath the
Oldest House**.

Do not return to the surface just because Pope has already received the first
dataset.

Follow the new objective across the chasm and continue toward the relay
positions.

Once the final relay work is complete, Pope begins receiving data from directly
beneath the Oldest House.

That is the real end of this Research Site investigation.

<h2 id="underpass-resonant">
  The Underpass Resonant is a separate route
</h2>

The Underpass also contains a regional Resonant, but it is **not part of the
four-Power-Core research sequence above**.

If your Quest Log changes to **The Underpass Resonant**, or you reach **The
Spire** and the objective begins sending Dylan toward the Astral Plane, switch
to the{" "}
<Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">
  Physicist and Astral Keys guide
</Link>

That route requires **three Astral Keys** to open three consecutive locked
doors before Dylan reaches the Physicist.

Do not keep searching the Research Site's yellow-tape branches for those keys.
The Power Core route and the Astral Plane route are two different objectives
sharing the same broader region.

</h2>

A Jesse Fault also appears in the Underpass.

When the Quest Log says **Jesse Sightings: Underpass**, the objective is no
longer about Grist's research, Power Cores, or relays.

The Fault uses dimensional traversal and a memory sequence instead.

Continue with{" "}
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">

This distinction matters because three completely different progression chains
can occupy the Underpass:

- **Recursions and Iterations** — research team, power, timelines, relays;
- **Jesse Sightings: Underpass** — dimensional Fault and Jesse;
- **The Underpass Resonant** — The Spire, Astral Keys, Physicist.

Read the yellow quest name before trying to solve the next obstacle.

<h2 id="underpass-stuck">
  If the Underpass looks stuck, check which objective is active
</h2>

### You found the research team's gear but nobody is there

Keep descending.

Their abandoned equipment confirms you are on the correct route; it does not
mark the team's final position.

### The same research camp suddenly looks different

Follow the current time fluctuation.

Different timeline iterations are supposed to overlap here.

### The Research Site needs four charges but you can only find three

One Power Core is already active.

You only need to bring back **three additional charges**.

### A charge disappears while you are carrying it

Return to the remote active Core and make the run again.

Follow the same yellow tape back and do not stop to explore while transporting
the charge.

### Pope received a data transmission but the mission did not finish

A partial burst is not enough.

Keep following the time fluctuations until the FBC confirms that **all data is
transmitting in full**.

### All four Power Cores are active but nothing ended

Power restoration is only one part of the objective.

Continue through the timeline sequence until the complete research dataset is
transmitted.

### The research data transmitted but the Underpass objective continues

Follow Grist across the chasm and complete the relay work beneath the Oldest
House.

### The objective says Jesse Sightings: Underpass

Switch to{" "}
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">
  Search for Jesse
</Link>

The Fault is a different progression chain from the Research Site.

### The objective says The Underpass Resonant

Go to{" "}
<Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">
  the Physicist guide
</Link>

The three Astral Keys and the boss belong to The Spire / Astral Plane route,
not the Research Site.

### I finished the research mission and cannot find the next objective here

Leave the Underpass.

The next story destination is the **Prime Candidate training grounds near
Dylan's old cell**.
