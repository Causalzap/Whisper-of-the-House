<h2 id="rescue-pope">
  Rescue Pope, then stop treating the Oldest House as an evacuation objective
</h2>

The Hedron section begins while the Oldest House is already failing under a large Patterning event.

Once Dylan reaches Pope, the immediate question changes from getting personnel out to identifying what is causing the transformation. Pope confirms that the source is a **Hedron** and that it has formed a direct link with the Oldest House.

That Link is the next target.

Pope makes the scale of the threat clear: this Hedron is not behaving like the weakened entity Dylan and Jesse knew in the past. It has entered this dimension through the Oldest House and is actively reshaping the space around it.

From here, follow the route toward the **Hedron Link** rather than returning to unfinished mid-game objectives.

<h2 id="first-link">
  Reach the first Hedron Link and break the connection
</h2>

The first Link is the proof-of-concept for the rest of this stage.

Fight through the active Patterning route until Dylan reaches the connection point. The goal is not to damage a conventional boss health bar here. You need to disrupt the Link itself.

Once the first Link breaks, two things happen that confirm you did it correctly:

- the Oldest House begins stabilizing;
- Dylan realizes there are **more Links** still active.

The Hiss inside Dylan also flares when he gets close to the Hedron's influence.

That flare is not an automatic failure.

Dylan suppresses it and confirms that each Link gives him another chance to resist the reaction. So if the screen or dialogue makes it look like he is losing control immediately after cutting a Link, keep going until the sequence resolves instead of reloading.

<h2 id="why-links-matter">
  Cutting the Links is how you damage the Hedron
</h2>

After the first successful disruption, Pope has enough data to confirm the mechanic.

The Hedron has **tethered itself to this world**, and breaking those tethers hurts it.

That means the next part of the route is not about searching for a new weapon or returning to the CFD plan. The Links themselves are now the damage condition.

Dylan's job becomes:

1. locate the next reachable Link;
2. create a route through the Patterning;
3. reach the Link;
4. disrupt it;
5. repeat as the Hedron loses more connections.

Pope also sends research balloons upward to help Dylan cross sections of Patterning that do not have a normal path.

<h2 id="research-balloon">
  Use the research balloons to create the next route
</h2>

When Pope tells you a **research balloon** is nearby, look upward and around the current traversal area rather than searching the ground for another doorway.

The balloon carries the equipment needed to manipulate the Pattern.

At each balloon:

1. **release the payload**;
2. collect or move to the Probe Relays;
3. place the relays in the indicated positions;
4. wait for the Pattern around the nearby knots to react;
5. use the new geometry to continue toward the next Link.

Pope describes the nearby Patterning as a series of **knots**. The Probe Relays release those knots in controlled positions so the surrounding structure shifts in a more predictable way.

The result is effectively a temporary bridge.

It may not look like a normal walkway when it forms. That is expected. Once the relays finish altering the Pattern, look for the newly reachable geometry rather than waiting for a conventional platform to appear.

<h2 id="bridge-route">
  The Pattern bridge is the route — do not wait for the world to return to normal
</h2>

This is the easiest place to hesitate.

The world does not reset into ordinary Manhattan architecture after you place the relays. The Pattern itself becomes the path.

When the geometry shifts:

- watch the newly formed surfaces;
- look above and across the gap;
- commit to the reachable structure;
- keep moving in the direction Pope is tracking you.

The next balloon is placed farther along this route.

Pope explicitly tells Dylan there will be **more balloons**, so finding and using one does not complete the whole traversal sequence.

When another balloon comes into range, repeat the same process instead of searching for a permanent elevator or stairwell.

<h2 id="next-link">
  Keep following balloons until Pope warns that another Link is close
</h2>

The clearest progress check comes from Pope and Zoe.

As Dylan nears another Hedron Link, the FBC begins reading the Pattern barrier expanding rapidly. At one point the measured expansion is reported as roughly **1 mm in two hours**, while people at the field hospital begin showing unusual symptoms including memory problems and repetitive behavior.

That message tells you the route is still advancing toward an active Link.

Soon afterward, the next research balloon is already in position.

Use it the same way:

**payload → Probe Relays → Pattern shift → cross → continue toward Link.**

You do not need to solve a new mechanical puzzle for every balloon. The challenge is reading the altered route the relays create.

<h2 id="hiss-reaction">
  Dylan's Hiss reaction gets worse near the Hedron, but it does not change the route
</h2>

The Hedron affects Dylan differently from normal Patterning because the Hiss is still inside him.

Pope offers to remove his containment device earlier in this sequence, but Dylan refuses. He knows getting close to the Hedron can trigger the Hiss again and wants the device left in place as insurance.

That matters when another flare happens near a Link.

The visual and dialogue can make the situation look like Dylan has lost control permanently. Wait for the sequence to finish.

After the first Link, he proves that he can suppress the reaction. The same risk remains as he moves closer to the Hedron, but the objective is still to continue severing the dimensional connections.

There is no alternate safe route that avoids Hedron exposure entirely.

<h2 id="hedron-link-blocked">
  If the Hedron Link route looks blocked, check what kind of gate you are facing
</h2>

There are three different kinds of apparent dead ends in this section.

**You reached Pope, but there is no obvious next evacuation marker.**  
The objective has changed. Go toward the Hedron Link rather than trying to lead more personnel out.

**You cut one Link and the story did not end.**  
That is expected. The first disruption proves that the mechanic works, but Dylan immediately confirms there are more Links.

**You reached a gap with no normal bridge.**  
Look for the current research balloon. Release its payload and place the Probe Relays in their marked positions. The Pattern shift is what creates the crossing.

Once Pope or Zoe says you are approaching another Link, stop searching previous platforms and continue forward through the new geometry.

<h2 id="before-final-approach">
  Treat every severed Link as progress toward the Hedron, not as a separate mission
</h2>

The Links all serve one purpose: weaken the Hedron's hold on this dimension.

The Oldest House stabilizing after the first disruption proves that the plan is working, but the Hedron itself remains active until Dylan cuts the remaining connections and pushes farther toward the entity.

By this point, there is no benefit to returning to the Field Office between every Link unless the active objective sends you there.

Continue using the FBC's balloons and Pattern data to cross the broken space, sever each connection you can reach, and keep advancing toward the Hedron itself.
