<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Enter **The Park** from Central through the Park Checkpoint and head for the
large pit near the middle of the region. Eat the unusual Mold beside the sealed
Gateway first, then collect the three remaining strains from the **Minimart,
Pizzeria, and Gym**. Return to the Gateway after all four have been consumed.

Inside, the **Collective** does not take meaningful damage while its active
tentacles are still controlling the arena. Remove the tentacles, break the Mold
weak points that appear on its body, and repeat until the final Execution
becomes available. Defeating the Collective gives the choice between
**Spore Burst** and **Growth**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Park is one of the stranger detours in Manhattan because the route starts
like an exploration objective and gradually turns into a boss encounter built
around Mold exposure. It is also possible to arrive here before you actually
want to finish the region: the Collective does not have to be defeated the
moment The Park opens, and leaving it for later does not fail the quest.

The important distinction is between activities that happen to be located in
The Park and the sequence that actually opens the Mold Gateway. If your goal is
the Park Resonant, the route is always the same: **Gateway sample → Minimart,
Pizzeria, and Gym → Gateway → Collective**.

<h2 id="how-to-enter-the-park">
  How to reach The Park
</h2>

The Park opens from the northeastern side of Central through the **Park
Checkpoint**. Follow the FBC-controlled approach into the district and continue
until the region opens around the large depression near its center.

The warnings at the entrance are unusually serious about Mold exposure. FBC
personnel are expected to use heavy protective equipment because the growth can
spread quickly and affect anyone exposed to it. Zoe reduces that warning to a
much simpler instruction for Dylan: stay away from the Mold.

That stops being an option once the Park Resonant sequence begins.

<h2 id="start-park-resonant">
  Start The Park Resonant at the Mold Gateway
</h2>

Drop into the large central pit and look for the sealed Gateway covered in Mold
and Patterning. A different-looking Mold sample is growing beside it.

Dylan immediately notices that this one is not the same as the ordinary growth
covering the district. He describes its smell as an impossible mixture of food
— bread, butter, jam, candy, popcorn, and honey — while Zoe is very clear that
he should not touch it.

Consume the sample anyway.

the Gateway, followed by the Mold in the Minimart, Pizzeria, and Gym.

The last three can be collected in any order.

<h2 id="mold-locations">
  All four Mold locations in The Park
</h2>

<h3 id="gateway-mold">
  1. Mold Gateway sample
</h3>

The first sample is at the bottom of the central pit beside the sealed Gateway.
This one starts the sequence.

If the Park Resonant appears to have no usable objective after you enter the
region, go here first. Merely crossing the Park Checkpoint is not enough to
reveal the other Mold locations.

<h3 id="minimart-mold">
  2. Minimart Mold
</h3>

The **Minimart** is in the southwestern part of The Park.

When you reach the store, clear the enemies around the objective area before
trying to enter. The building can remain unavailable while the local encounter
is still active, so checking the door repeatedly will not help if an enemy has
been left alive nearby.

Once combat ends, enter the Minimart and consume the unusual Mold inside.

Nothing else has to be activated afterward. The sample itself is the objective,
and Dylan can leave as soon as it has registered.

<h3 id="pizzeria-mold">
  3. Pizzeria Mold
</h3>

The **Pizzeria** is toward the northwestern side of The Park, but the obvious
street approach does not take Dylan directly to the sample.

Look for the nearby sewer entrance and use the underground route to reach the
Pizzeria area. Clear the enemies before going inside.

This location has an additional failure state that can make the quest look
broken. Sometimes the Mold is clearly visible but no interaction prompt appears.
If yellow contamination is still hanging inside the restaurant, go back
outside and smash the **Pizzeria windows**. Clearing that obstruction can make
the sample usable again.

Return inside and consume it.

There is no reason to abandon the quest or immediately reload just because the
prompt is missing on the first attempt.

<h3 id="gym-mold">
  4. Gym Mold
</h3>

The **Gym** is also in the northwestern portion of The Park.

Reach the marked building and finish the combat encounter around it first. As
with the Minimart, the entrance can remain unavailable until the area is clear.

The unusual Mold is inside the Gym. Consume it to complete the set if this is
your fourth sample.

By the end of the sequence, the exposure is affecting Dylan much more strongly.
He begins to understand that the infected growth, Patterning, and human hosts
are not changing independently; they are being pulled toward the same source.

That source is beyond the Gateway.

<h2 id="open-mold-gateway">
  How to open the Mold Gateway
</h2>

Return to the central pit after consuming all four strains.

There is no code, key, switch puzzle, or additional item required. The Gateway
opens only after the first sample beside it and all three regional samples have
been completed.

If it is still sealed, one of these is missing:

- the first sample beside the Gateway;
- the Minimart Mold;
- the Pizzeria Mold;
- the Gym Mold.

Once the four samples are complete, approach the Gateway again and continue
through.

The route beyond it leads directly toward the Resonant encounter, and Dylan's
connection with Zoe becomes less reliable after crossing. This is a good point
to spend unused Talent Points and check the current Combat Ability, Aberrant,
and Artifacts rather than walking into the fight with an unfinished setup.

<h2 id="collective-boss">
  How to beat the Collective
</h2>

The entity behind the Mold outbreak is **the Collective**.

The central mass is not the part of the fight you should attack first. The
encounter advances by destroying the tentacles controlling the arena, surviving
the Mold enemies between phases, and then breaking the exposed weak points on
the Collective itself.

If its main body seems to take almost no useful damage, stop attacking it and
look for an active tentacle.

<h3 id="collective-opening">
  Remove the first two tentacles
</h3>

At the start of the fight, choose one side of the arena and move across the
altered gravity surfaces toward the large tentacle attached to the wall.

Stay on that target until it is destroyed. Damaging both sides without
finishing either one only leaves more attacks active at the same time.

The tentacle pressures Dylan with direct attacks and Mold projectiles. Moving
laterally and using the arena geometry is safer than standing in front of it
and trading hits.

When the first tentacle dies, deal with the smaller Mold enemies before
crossing to the opposite side. Bringing a full enemy group into the traversal
between surfaces makes the next approach much harder than it needs to be.

Destroy the second tentacle the same way.

If moving across the walls and altered surfaces still feels awkward,
<Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse"> Search for Jesse</Link> can be
finished first so Dylan has a more comfortable set of traversal options before
returning.

<h3 id="collective-weak-points">
  Attack the Mold weak points after the tentacles die
</h3>

Once the active tentacles are gone, move back toward the Collective and look at
the lower surfaces of its body.

Swollen, discolored **Mold orbs** become exposed. These are the points that
actually move the fight forward.

Focus one orb until it bursts, then get away from the central body. The arena
becomes dangerous again as contamination spreads and another wave of Mold
enemies enters.

Do not stay beside the boss trying to force extra damage into an inactive
target. Clear the wave, regain a safe position, and wait for the next set of
tentacles.

<h3 id="collective-wall-ceiling-phase">
  Clear the wall and ceiling tentacles one by one
</h3>

Later phases spread the fight onto walls and the ceiling. Dylan has to change
gravity surfaces while Mold contamination continues to build around the room.

The safest way to reduce pressure is to completely remove one source of attacks
before moving to the next. A practical sequence is to finish one wall
tentacle, move to the ceiling target, then cross to the opposite wall.

The exact side you start on matters less than committing to a target until it
is dead.

Standing on contaminated surfaces can also build **Infected**, so abandon a
bad position rather than staying for one more melee string. Once the active
tentacles are gone, return to the Collective and break the next exposed Mold
orb.

<h3 id="collective-final-phase">
  Destroy the last two tentacles and use the Execution
</h3>

Near the end of the fight, two additional tentacles appear close to the
Collective.

Take one down completely before switching to the other. Splitting damage keeps
both sources of pressure active and stretches out the most crowded part of the
fight.

When both are dead, the last weak point becomes available. Destroy it and watch
for the **Execution** prompt.

Use the Execution to finish the Collective.

<h2 id="spore-burst-or-growth">
  Spore Burst or Growth after the Collective?
</h2>

Defeating the Collective sends Dylan to the Gap and offers two Mold-based
Combat Abilities: **Spore Burst** and **Growth**.

Neither choice is needed for traversal or for continuing the main story, so the
decision comes down to how you want the Mold ability to behave in combat.

### Spore Burst

**Spore Burst** is the more direct option. Dylan launches the Mold pressure
himself and can apply **Infected** to the enemy he is actively targeting.

It fits a setup where you want control over timing and target selection. When a
specific enemy needs the status immediately, Spore Burst puts the application
in Dylan's hands instead of leaving it to another source.

### Growth

**Growth** creates a Mold growth that attacks independently.

It also builds Infected, but Dylan can turn his attention back to movement,
melee pressure, or another enemy while the growth continues attacking on its
own.

That makes Growth more useful when the fight benefits from pressure in two
places at once.

The simplest distinction is:

- choose **Spore Burst** when you want direct control over ranged Infection;
- choose **Growth** when you want an autonomous Mold source working while Dylan
  does something else.

<h2 id="is-park-required">
  Is The Park required for the main story?
</h2>

The **Collective does not have to be defeated when The Park first becomes
available**.

The main Contain the Crisis progression can continue while the Park Resonant is
still unfinished, so there is no penalty for leaving if the region or boss is
taking too long with your current setup.

That can be the better decision when normal Park enemies already feel
overly durable, traversal through the area is uncomfortable, or Dylan is
sitting on upgrades that would make the return trip easier.

Come back with more Talent Points, stronger Artifacts, a better Aberrant setup,
or additional movement options.

The Collective still counts toward clearing the regional Resonants. For the
other entities, their locations, prerequisites, and ability rewards are listed
in <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants</Link>.

<h2 id="other-park-objectives">
  What else can you find in The Park?
</h2>

Several unrelated activities overlap the same streets, underground routes, and
buildings used during the Collective sequence.

You may encounter Heidi and her missing twin, FBC Mold research connected to
Dr. Underhill, a lost dog, an anomalous taxi, Secret Hideouts, and the Park
Vendor while moving through the region.

None of those activities opens the Mold Gateway.

If the objective in your log is **The Park Resonant**, only the four unusual
Mold strains matter. The other activities can be completed when they naturally
cross your route or left for another visit.

The anomalous taxi is part of a chain spread across all seven major regions, so
there is no need to finish the rest of that activity while you are here. Its
remaining locations and puzzles are in
<Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">The Last Taxi</Link>.

Likewise, you do not need to clear every Side Story or exploration activity in
The Park before entering the Gateway. Once the four Mold strains are complete,
you can go straight back to the central pit and continue to the Collective.
