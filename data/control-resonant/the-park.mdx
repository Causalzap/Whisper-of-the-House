<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Enter **The Park** from the northeast side of Central, drop into the large central pit, and eat the unusual Mold beside the sealed Gateway. Collect the other three strains from the **Minimart, Pizzeria, and Gym**, return to the Gateway, then destroy the Collective's tentacles and exposed weak points until you can execute it.

<h2 id="how-to-enter-the-park">
  How to reach The Park
</h2>

The Park is an optional region on the northeast side of Central. The main story can continue without clearing its Resonant, so it is possible to spend a long time elsewhere in Manhattan before coming back here.

Enter through the **Park Checkpoint** and follow the FBC-controlled approach into the district. The warnings around the entrance are unusually direct: personnel are supposed to wear heavy protective equipment around the Mold because exposure can cause rapid fungal growth and severe mental effects.

Zoe gives Dylan a simpler version of the rule: stay away from the Mold.

That advice becomes impossible to follow once the Park Resonant objective begins.

<h2 id="start-park-resonant">
  Drop into the central pit and eat the first Mold sample
</h2>

At the bottom of the central pit, Dylan finds a sealed Gateway covered in Mold and Patterning. Beside it is a Mold sample that looks and smells different from the ordinary growth spreading through the rest of the Park.

Dylan describes the sample as smelling like an impossible mixture of food — bread, butter, jam, candy, popcorn, and honey. Zoe tells him not to touch it.

Eat it.

The reaction is immediate, but once Dylan recovers he can sense three more unusual Mold strains elsewhere in The Park.

There are **four required samples total**:

1. the first sample beside the Mold Gateway;
2. the Minimart sample;
3. the Pizzeria sample;
4. the Gym sample.

The last three do not have to be collected in a fixed order.

<h2 id="minimart-mold">
  Minimart Mold location
</h2>

The Minimart sample is in the **southwestern part of The Park**.

Reach the marked store and clear the enemies around it first. The building remains tied to the active combat state, so repeatedly checking the entrance while enemies are still alive will not advance anything.

Once the encounter is over, enter the Minimart and find the unusual Mold inside.

Eat it.

By the second dose, Dylan is already reacting differently. His thoughts shift away from simple hunger and toward ideas of change, improvement, and testing. There is nothing else to activate in the store afterward, so move on to the next strain.

<h2 id="pizzeria-mold">
  Pizzeria Mold location and missing interaction fix
</h2>

The Pizzeria is in the **northwestern side of The Park**, but approaching it directly from the street is misleading.

Look for the sewer entrance nearby and follow the underground route until it brings Dylan into the Pizzeria area. Clear the enemies there before trying to collect the Mold.

Once combat is over, enter the restaurant and approach the unusual sample.

A useful failure state can occur here: the Mold may be visible while the interaction prompt refuses to appear.

When that happens, go back outside and break the **Pizzeria windows**. If yellow contamination is still filling the interior, smashing the windows can clear the remaining obstruction and restore the interaction.

Return inside and eat the sample.

There is no need to abandon the entire quest or reload immediately just because the first interaction prompt is missing.

<h2 id="gym-mold">
  Gym Mold location
</h2>

The Gym contains the remaining strain and is also in the northwestern portion of The Park.

Reach the objective area, clear the enemies around the building, then enter once the combat state releases the route.

The unusual Mold is inside.

Eat it.

By this point the repeated exposure is affecting Dylan much more strongly. The connection between the Mold and whatever is behind the Gateway becomes harder for Zoe to interrupt.

After all four samples, Dylan realizes the Mold is not changing in isolation. The Patterning, the infected growth, and human hosts are being drawn together around the same source.

Return to the central pit.

<h2 id="open-mold-gateway">
  Return to the Mold Gateway after eating all four samples
</h2>

The Gateway that was sealed earlier can now be opened.

There is no code, key, or separate environmental puzzle at this stage. If the Gateway remains closed, one of the three marked Park samples has not been consumed.

Check that you have finished:

- Minimart;
- Pizzeria;
- Gym.

Together with the first Gateway sample, that makes **four Mold strains**.

Once all four are complete, approach the Gateway again and continue through.

The connection with Zoe becomes less reliable after Dylan crosses, and the route commits you to the Resonant encounter. Spend any unused Talent Points and make sure the current Combat Ability and Aberrant setup can handle both a large target and smaller Mold enemies before moving deeper.

<h2 id="collective-boss">
  How to beat the Collective
</h2>

The entity behind the Park's Mold outbreak is **the Collective**.

At the beginning of the fight, attacking the central mass is inefficient. The encounter progresses by removing the tentacles connected to the arena and then damaging the weak points that become exposed afterward.

The basic rhythm is:

1. destroy the active tentacles;
2. clear the Mold enemies that appear between phases;
3. return to the Collective when a weak point opens;
4. break the exposed Mold orb;
5. move away before the next contamination and enemy wave;
6. repeat until the final Execution becomes available.

Trying to treat the Collective like a normal single-target boss makes the fight much longer.

<h3 id="collective-first-tentacles">
  Destroy the first wall tentacles
</h3>

At the opening, move toward one side of the arena and use the altered gravity surfaces to reach the large tentacle attached to the wall.

Commit to that tentacle until it dies.

Its attacks include heavy physical pressure and Mold projectiles, so lateral movement is safer than standing directly in front of it and trading damage.

After the first tentacle falls, clear the smaller Mold enemies before crossing the arena. Carrying several enemies into the next traversal section makes it much harder to reposition safely.

Then move to the opposite side and remove the second tentacle.

If the wall and ceiling movement still feels awkward, completing <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link> first makes this traversal much easier.

<h3 id="collective-weak-points">
  Break the exposed Mold orbs
</h3>

Once the current tentacles are gone, return toward the Collective.

Watch the lower surfaces of its body for swollen, discolored **Mold orbs**.

Those are the weak points.

Attack one until it bursts. This is where the meaningful boss damage happens.

Do not stay beside the Collective after breaking a weak point. Back away as the arena becomes dangerous again and another Mold wave begins.

Clear the new enemies, restore a safe position, then wait for the next opening instead of forcing damage into the inactive body.

<h3 id="collective-wall-ceiling-phase">
  Clear the wall and ceiling tentacles one at a time
</h3>

Later phases spread the encounter across more of the room.

Tentacles begin appearing on walls and the ceiling, forcing Dylan to move between different gravity surfaces while Mold contamination continues building around the arena.

Finish one tentacle before changing targets.

A reliable sequence is:

1. one wall tentacle;
2. the ceiling tentacle;
3. the opposite wall tentacle.

Removing one source of projectiles completely is more useful than leaving three damaged tentacles active at the same time.

The contaminated surfaces can also build **Infected**, so do not remain in a bad position just to finish one extra melee string.

Once the active tentacles are gone, return to the Collective and break the next exposed weak point.

<h3 id="collective-final-phase">
  Destroy the final two tentacles before attacking the last weak point
</h3>

Late in the encounter, two more tentacles appear close to the Collective.

Pick one and destroy it completely before moving to the other.

Splitting damage between both keeps two sources of pressure active for longer.

When both are dead, the final weak point opens.

Destroy it.

The Collective then becomes vulnerable to the finishing **Execution**. Take the prompt to end the fight.

<h2 id="spore-burst-or-growth">
  Spore Burst or Growth after beating the Collective?
</h2>

Defeating the Collective sends Dylan to the Gap and gives him a choice between two Mold-based Combat Abilities:

**Spore Burst** or **Growth**.

### Spore Burst

Spore Burst is the direct option.

Dylan fires Mold projectiles himself and rapidly applies **Infected** to enemies in front of him.

It suits a build where you want to choose exactly which enemy receives the status and when. You activate it, aim the pressure yourself, and immediately know what target is being affected.

### Growth

Growth creates a Mold growth that attacks enemies independently.

It also builds Infected, but Dylan does not have to keep directing every hit once the growth is active.

That makes it more useful when you want extra pressure elsewhere in the arena while concentrating on melee, movement, or another target.

The practical difference is simple:

- **Spore Burst** — controlled ranged Infection;
- **Growth** — autonomous Mold pressure.

Neither ability is required for traversal or for continuing the story.

<h2 id="is-park-required">
  Is The Park Resonant required for the main story?
</h2>

No.

You can continue the main Contain the Crisis progression without defeating the Collective when The Park first becomes available.

That makes the Collective a reasonable fight to postpone if ordinary Park enemies are already taking too long to kill or your current Aberrant setup feels underpowered.

Leaving does not fail the quest.

Come back after gaining stronger upgrades, more Talent Points, better Artifacts, or traversal options that make the region easier to move through.

Defeating the Collective still matters for broader Resonant completion. If you are deciding which remaining entity to fight next, the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants guide</Link> shows their locations, requirements, and ability rewards.

<h2 id="other-park-objectives">
  Other activities in The Park do not replace the four Mold samples
</h2>

The Park contains several objectives that can overlap the Resonant route geographically.

You may encounter:

- Heidi and her missing twin;
- FBC Mold research connected to Dr. Underhill;
- a lost dog searching for its toy;
- an anomalous taxi;
- Secret Hideouts;
- the Park Vendor.

None of them opens the Mold Gateway.

For the Collective, the required sequence remains the first Gateway sample plus the **Minimart, Pizzeria, and Gym** strains.

The anomalous taxi can also appear while you are moving through the region. You can solve it when you find it or leave it for later; the complete seven-region chain is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/last-taxi">The Last Taxi</Link>.

There is no need to clear every optional Park activity before fighting the Collective.

<h2 id="stuck">
  The Park Resonant stuck fixes
</h2>

### The Park Resonant did not start

Go to the large central pit and find the sealed Mold Gateway. Eat the unusual Mold beside it; entering The Park alone does not start the four-sample sequence.

### The Mold Gateway is still closed

You need all **four** samples: the first one beside the Gateway, then the **Minimart, Pizzeria, and Gym** strains. If all four are complete, return to the same central pit.

### The Pizzeria Mold has no interaction prompt

Clear every enemy in the objective area first. If the sample still cannot be used and yellow contamination remains inside, go outside, break the Pizzeria windows, then return to the Mold.

### I cleared the Minimart or Gym but cannot enter

Check for remaining enemies around the building. The entrance can stay locked while the local combat encounter is still active.

### The Collective barely takes damage

Stop attacking the central body while active tentacles remain. Destroy the tentacles first; that exposes the Mold weak points that actually advance the fight.

### The wall and ceiling phase keeps overwhelming me

Finish one tentacle before switching targets. Leaving several damaged tentacles alive keeps more projectile pressure active at the same time. Move off Mold-covered surfaces when Infected starts building too quickly.

### Spore Burst or Growth?

Choose **Spore Burst** for direct ranged Infection application. Choose **Growth** when you want an independent Mold source applying pressure while Dylan attacks or repositions elsewhere.
