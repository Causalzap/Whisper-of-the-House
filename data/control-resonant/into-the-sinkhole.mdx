<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Ride the **diving bell** down, clear the Hiss disruption, then keep escorting the Probe Relays until you reach **Bedrock**. Place and activate the relays there. When the Sinkhole shifts and you can no longer reach the bell, stop trying to retrace the descent route and keep moving through the changed path until Zoe's PA music leads you back out.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="enter-sinkhole">
  Ride the diving bell down and keep the Probe Relays moving
</h2>

**Into the Sinkhole** starts as a simple delivery job: the FBC needs the stronger pattern resonance at Bedrock, and Dylan is being sent down with the Probe Relays needed to collect it.

Once you are in position over the Sinkhole, get onto the diving bell and stay with it during the descent. Zoe switches away from normal radio communication because the signal will not reach the deeper sections reliably.

The objective at this point is not to explore every side passage. The relays are what determine your progress.

A Hiss disruption interrupts the first descent. Leave the bell when the game forces the stop, clear the enemies holding the area, then return to the relay route once Zoe confirms the disruption has been removed.

After that first fight, continue downward. This is also the point where you are moving beyond the depth Pope's earlier team managed to reach, so the route becomes less like an established FBC worksite and more like an unstable path through the Sinkhole.

<h2 id="reach-bedrock">
  Keep descending until the game confirms you have reached Bedrock
</h2>

The clearest progression cue is Zoe confirming that Dylan has reached **Bedrock**.

Do not start placing the relays early just because you see an open chamber or another temporary stopping point. The main probe is released from the diving bell only when you are at the correct depth.

Once you reach Bedrock:

1. move the Probe Relays into their required positions;
2. place each relay where the objective allows it;
3. **activate the relays after placing them**;
4. wait for the objective to update before trying to leave.

The activation step matters. Placing a relay in position is not the same thing as completing it, and Zoe specifically reminds Dylan to activate the relays after they are set.

When the last required relay is active, the environment reacts immediately. That reaction is the transition into the part of the mission where most of the confusion starts.

<h2 id="escape-sinkhole">
  The diving bell is supposed to become unreachable after the relays activate
</h2>

As soon as the relay work is complete, Zoe tells Dylan to get back onto the diving bell so the FBC can pull him up.

You cannot do that normally.

The Sinkhole shifts before Dylan reaches it, and the bell is no longer accessible from his position. This is not a missed jump, a failed relay, or a reason to reload the previous checkpoint.

Zoe later confirms what happened: the Sinkhole shifted and closed up around the route above, leaving the bell on the other side of the changed structure.

That is the important state check.

If your objective says **Escape the Sinkhole** and you can see or hear the bell but cannot get back onto it, your mission is progressing correctly.

Do not spend time looking for a switch that brings the bell back down.

<h2 id="follow-cable">
  Follow the escape route until the cable stops helping
</h2>

With the normal extraction route gone, continue through the opening the altered Sinkhole gives you.

For a while, the cable provides a useful sense of direction. Keep pushing along that route rather than returning toward Bedrock.

Eventually Dylan reports that he cannot follow the cable any farther.

That line marks another intentional change in the escape, not another indication that you have taken a wrong turn. From there, continue looking for the route that carries you deeper through the shifted space.

Zoe attempts another solution using explosives. When that fails, you still do not need to return to the relay chamber or search for a way to reset the Sinkhole.

The next useful navigation cue is audio.

<h2 id="follow-music">
  Use Zoe's PA music as the cue for the final way out
</h2>

Zoe eventually reaches the PA system and starts playing music into the Sinkhole.

Dylan confirms that he can hear it.

From that point, treat the music as the sign that you are finally reconnecting with a route toward the surface. Continue through the available path instead of doubling back toward the cable or the lost diving bell.

The escape sequence deliberately removes the dependable landmarks you used during the descent:

- the diving bell is gone;
- the original path has shifted;
- the cable eventually stops being useful;
- the explosive attempt does not open the exit.

The PA audio replaces those landmarks late in the sequence.

Keep advancing until Dylan begins registering on Zoe's radar again. Once communication becomes clear and Dylan confirms he is outside, the mission has successfully moved back to the surface.

<h2 id="after-sinkhole">
  Return to FBC contact after you make it outside
</h2>

Getting out is the end of this Sinkhole run. There is no reason to immediately go back down and search Bedrock for another relay interaction.

Zoe contacts Dylan again after the escape, and the FBC continues working on the larger CFD plan.

The important thing to remember is that **Into the Sinkhole is only one requirement in that stage of the story**. Power routing and Resonant research are also connected to the same larger operation.

So if escaping the Sinkhole does not immediately trigger the next major story event, check the other active main objectives rather than repeating this mission.

The <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link> follows those overlapping progression requirements in order and shows which unfinished branch can hold the next story trigger.

<h2 id="sinkhole-problems">
  Why Into the Sinkhole stops progressing
</h2>

There are three points where the mission can look broken even when it is behaving normally.

**The diving bell stops during the descent.**  
Clear the Hiss disruption nearby. The bell route continues after the fight.

**The relays are placed but the mission has not advanced.**  
Make sure the relay objective has actually been activated rather than only positioned at Bedrock.

**You cannot reach the diving bell after installing the relays.**  
That is the intended escape sequence. The Sinkhole has shifted. Continue forward through the altered route, let the cable guide you as far as it can, then use Zoe's PA music as the final navigation cue.

Once Dylan reaches the outside and radio contact stabilizes, you are finished with the mission.
