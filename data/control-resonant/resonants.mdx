<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
The practical Resonant checklist is **The Artist**, then the five regional Resonants: **Deserter, Dancer, Collective, Physicist, and Co-Director**. If the main story has stopped around **Enemy of My Enemy**, prioritize the **Deserter in Central** and **Dancer in West Incursion** first.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Resonants are more than optional bosses in CONTROL Resonant.

Each one gives Dylan a new Combat Ability or ability choice, but defeating one also pushes Manhattan into another **Resonant Cycle**. Killing the next available boss is therefore not automatically the best move the moment its quest appears.

The first Resonant, **The Artist**, is fought during Orientation.

After that, the Quest Log tracks five regional Resonant quests:

- **The Central Resonant**
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**
- **The Park Resonant**
- **The Underpass Resonant**
- **The Unknown Resonant**

Those names describe the **region**, not the actual entity Dylan eventually fights.

The bosses themselves are the **Deserter, Dancer, Collective, Physicist, and Co-Director**.

<h2 id="all-resonants">
  All Resonants, locations, and ability rewards
</h2>

There are six Resonants in the full checklist:

- **Artist — Downtown:** fought during Orientation. Rewards **Barrage / Shield / Volatile Anomaly**.
- **Deserter — Central, Patterned Factory:** clear the Patterned Factory route. Rewards **Ignite / Inferno**.
- **Dancer — West Incursion Zone, Theater:** complete the Incursion Fault and unlock **Shift** first. Rewards **Ground Slam / Push**.
- **Collective — The Park, Mold Gateway:** consume all four required Mold samples. Rewards **Spore Burst / Growth**.
- **Physicist — Underpass, The Spire / Astral Plane:** open three locked doors with three Astral Keys. Rewards **Spike / Astral Rebuke**.
- **Co-Director — Unknown:** follow The Unknown Resonant route. Unlocks **Command**.

This is useful for seeing where each Resonant belongs, but it is not a strict boss order.

After the opening, Manhattan gives you room to pursue several objectives in parallel. Some Resonants matter immediately to story progression, while others can wait until you want their Combat Ability or are ready to advance the world into another Resonant Cycle.

<h2 id="artist">
  The Artist — Downtown
</h2>

The Artist is different from the other entries because you do not pick up a separate regional Resonant quest and then go hunting for it.

You meet it during **Orientation**, while Dylan is still moving through the opening Downtown progression.

Defeating the Artist sends Dylan to the Gap and introduces the first major Combat Ability decision:

- **Barrage**
- **Shield**
- **Volatile Anomaly**

This is the first time the game establishes that defeating a Resonant does not simply hand Dylan one fixed attack. The reward changes depending on what you choose in the Gap.

If you are still in Orientation and have not reached the Artist, stay with the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link> rather than searching Manhattan for a separate Artist marker.

<h2 id="deserter">
  The Deserter — Central Resonant
</h2>

The **Central Resonant** is the Deserter.

Its route starts at the **Patterned Factory** in Central. The factory itself is part of the challenge, with overheating machinery, blocked pipes, steam, and Patterned growth appearing before Dylan ever reaches the entity.

That entire approach and the boss encounter stay together in the <Link href="/control-resonant/patterned-factory">Patterned Factory and Deserter route</Link>.

After defeating the Deserter, Dylan chooses between:

**Ignite** and **Inferno**.

Ignite modifies Dylan's weapon attacks around Burn, while Inferno gives him a more direct fire-based Combat Ability.

More importantly for progression, the Central Resonant is one of the specific bosses the campaign expects Dylan to clear before the mid-game story can move forward.

If the main quest appears to have stopped after the early Central objectives, check whether the Deserter is still alive before looking for another Field Office trigger.

<h2 id="dancer">
  The Dancer — West Incursion Zone Resonant
</h2>

The Dancer is inside the **Theater** in the West Incursion Zone.

Seeing the Theater does not mean Dylan can immediately reach it. The region first pushes him into **The Incursion Fault**, and completing that Jesse sequence changes how he traverses the gravity-broken streets.

If the Theater is visible but the route ends at an impossible wall or gap, finish the Jesse progression first.

The full Fault chain is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

Once the Theater route is open, the <Link href="/control-resonant/dancer">Dancer guide</Link> covers the Resonant itself, including the fight and the reward choice.

The Dancer gives Dylan:

**Ground Slam** or **Push**.

Like the Deserter, the Dancer is tied to the early story gate.

When **Enemy of My Enemy** refuses to advance, the first two regional Resonants I check are:

**Central + West Incursion.**

<h2 id="collective">
  The Collective — The Park Resonant
</h2>

The Park handles its Resonant differently.

There is no normal door leading directly to the Collective. Dylan first finds a **Mold Gateway** and an unusual Mold sample beside it. Eating that first sample reveals the rest of the route.

The full sequence requires **four Mold samples total**:

1. the first sample beside the Gateway;
2. the Minimart Mold;
3. the Pizzeria Mold;
4. the Gym Mold.

After all four are consumed, return to the same Gateway.

That opens the route to the Collective.

The complete sample locations, Mold problems, and boss encounter are in the <Link href="/control-resonant/the-park">Park and Collective guide</Link>.

The reward choice is:

**Spore Burst** or **Growth**.

The Park is also a good example of why I do not treat every Resonant marker as an instruction to kill the boss immediately. If the current build is struggling and the Park Resonant is not blocking the story step you are pursuing, you can leave and return later.

<h2 id="physicist">
  The Physicist — Underpass Resonant
</h2>

The Underpass Resonant is the **Physicist**.

Its route branches upward from **The Spire** and then leaves the physical Underpass for the Astral Plane.

Once Dylan reaches the locked Astral route, the progression is:

**3 Astral Keys → 3 locked doors → Physicist**

The keys are the main navigation problem. They sit on separate floating branches rather than beside the doors they unlock, and they do not provide a useful minimap shortcut.

For all three key routes and the boss encounter, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist and Astral Keys guide</Link>.

Defeating the Physicist gives the choice between:

**Spike** and **Astral Rebuke**.

The Underpass contains several unrelated objectives at the same time, so check the active quest title before climbing toward The Spire.

If you are still working with the research team, Power Cores, timeline iterations, or the route beneath the Oldest House, stay on the <Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass walkthrough</Link> instead.

Only switch to the Physicist route when the active objective is **The Underpass Resonant**.

<h2 id="co-director">
  The Co-Director — Unknown Resonant
</h2>

The Co-Director belongs to **The Unknown Resonant** quest.

This is a late regional encounter in Unknown.

Because several late objectives can overlap in the same region, check the Quest Log before assuming every Patterned FBC route is leading toward this boss.

Once **The Unknown Resonant** is your active objective and the boss becomes the actual problem, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/co-director">Co-Director guide</Link> for the encounter.

The reward is **Command**.

Unlike the earlier two-choice regional rewards, Command is the defining Combat Ability associated with this encounter.

It lets Dylan temporarily turn affected enemies against their own side.

<h2 id="evacuation-zone">
  Is there an Evacuation Zone Resonant?
</h2>

There is no separate regional Resonant quest for the Evacuation Zone.

That is an easy assumption to make because several other major Manhattan regions eventually send Dylan toward a named Resonant entity.

Evacuation carries different progression instead:

- the Evac Building and resonance tremors;
- Arish;
- the Sinkhole;
- power-related story requirements;
- the Subway Fault connected to Jesse.

So if you have finished Evacuation Procedures and still cannot find an **Evacuation Zone Resonant**, you are not missing a hidden regional boss.

Continue the active story or Jesse objective instead.

<h2 id="which-resonant-first">
  Which Resonant should you fight first?
</h2>

The Artist is fixed by the opening.

After that, I use the current story gate as the first filter rather than choosing a Resonant only because its quest has appeared.

During the early and middle game, the important pair is:

**Deserter + Dancer**

The Central Resonant and West Incursion Zone Resonant are the bosses that matter when the campaign is trying to move into **Enemy of My Enemy**.

Once those requirements are satisfied, the decision becomes more dependent on Dylan's current build and the state of Manhattan.

The Collective, Physicist, and later Co-Director all add new Combat Abilities, but another Resonant kill also advances the world into a new Cycle.

That tradeoff matters more than simply asking which boss icon appeared first.

<h2 id="resonant-cycle">
  Why you may want to wait before killing the next Resonant
</h2>

Every Resonant kill starts another **Resonant Cycle**.

Manhattan pushes back.

Previously cleared encounters can return at a higher level, new threats can appear, and World Feats refresh. The upside is that the refreshed world also supports stronger rewards.

A Resonant kill therefore does two things at once:

**Dylan gets stronger → Manhattan gets more dangerous.**

Before starting an optional Resonant, I check whether there is anything easy I still want to finish in the current Cycle.

Waiting can make sense when:

- ordinary enemies are already taking too long to kill;
- Dylan is short on useful Aberrant upgrades;
- several unfinished World Feats are still comfortable at the current level;
- the Resonant is not required for the story step I am currently pursuing.

There is little reason to create a harder Manhattan just because another Resonant happens to be available.

The opposite can also be true.

When the current Cycle has become comfortable and Dylan needs another Combat Ability, defeating a Resonant moves both the build and the world forward together.

<h2 id="ability-choice">
  Do you get every Resonant ability?
</h2>

Not during the initial reward choice.

The Gap lets Dylan choose from the Combat Abilities tied to the Resonant he just defeated.

The regional choices are:

- Deserter → **Ignite / Inferno**
- Dancer → **Ground Slam / Push**
- Collective → **Spore Burst / Growth**
- Physicist → **Spike / Astral Rebuke**

The Artist gives the larger opening choice:

**Barrage / Shield / Volatile Anomaly**

The Co-Director unlocks:

**Command**

I would not choose only by whichever name sounds strongest.

The more useful question is what Dylan's current Aberrant setup is missing: ranged pressure, crowd control, status application, disruption, survivability, or autonomous pressure.

The individual boss pages go deeper into those choices where the differences actually matter.

<h2 id="all-six">
  Do you need to defeat every Resonant?
</h2>

You do not need every regional Resonant at the exact moment it first becomes available.

The story only blocks progression at specific points when particular Resonant objectives are required.

For full completion, however, all six matter.

The full Resonant checklist is:

- **Artist**
- **Deserter**
- **Dancer**
- **Collective**
- **Physicist**
- **Co-Director**

**Hedron is separate.**

It is a major story boss, but it is not another entry in this six-Resonant set.

Completing the Mural in the Gap requires finishing the Resonant set as well as the story's Hedron fight.

<h2 id="quest-log-mismatches">
  Quest Log mismatches around Resonants
</h2>

### Enemy of My Enemy is not starting

Check **The Central Resonant** and **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**.

The Deserter and Dancer are the two important boss checks for that story gate.

### I can see the Dancer's Theater but cannot reach it

Complete **The Incursion Fault** first.

The route requires Shift from the Jesse sequence before the gravity-broken path into the Theater becomes usable.

### The Park Gateway will not open

You need all four Mold samples.

The first sample beside the Gateway counts toward the total. The other three come from the Minimart, Pizzeria, and Gym.

### The Underpass boss route keeps asking for another Astral Key

There are **three Astral Keys and three locked doors**.

Opening the first or second door only advances you to the next layer of the route.

### I completed The Underpass Resonant but never saw a boss with that name

That is expected.

**The Underpass Resonant** is the Quest Log name. The actual entity Dylan defeats is the **Physicist**.

The same regional naming pattern is used elsewhere in the Resonant chain.

### I finished the Evacuation Zone but cannot find its Resonant

There is no separate Evacuation Zone regional Resonant quest in this chain.

Continue the active story or Jesse objective instead.

### Manhattan became harder after I defeated a Resonant

You triggered the next **Resonant Cycle**.

Previously cleared combat encounters can return at a higher level, and new threats can appear. That is part of the progression system rather than a failed quest or broken save.

### I defeated a Resonant but the completion result has not appeared yet

Finish the post-boss sequence in the Gap.

Several Resonant completion states resolve after Dylan leaves the Gap rather than at the exact moment the boss health bar reaches zero.
