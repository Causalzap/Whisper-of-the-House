<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
There are **six Resonant bosses** in CONTROL Resonant: **The Artist, Deserter, Dancer, Collective, Physicist, and Co-Director**. The Artist is part of Orientation, while the other five belong to regional Resonant quests. If the main story has stopped around **Enemy of My Enemy**, check the **Deserter in Central** and **Dancer in West Incursion** first. **Hedron is a separate major story encounter, not a seventh member of this six-Resonant set.**
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Resonants are more than a boss checklist. Each one belongs to a specific part of Manhattan, gives Dylan a Combat Ability or ability choice, and pushes the world into another **Resonant Cycle** when defeated.

The useful questions are not only which bosses exist, but which one is blocking the current route and whether defeating another Resonant is worth advancing the world right now. This page covers that complete collection. When one specific encounter becomes the actual problem, the dedicated route or boss guide takes over instead of repeating the full fight here.

<h2 id="all-resonants">
  All 6 Resonant bosses, locations, requirements, and rewards
</h2>

The first Resonant, **The Artist**, appears during Orientation. After that, the Quest Log tracks five regional objectives:

- **The Central Resonant**
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**
- **The Park Resonant**
- **The Underpass Resonant**
- **The Unknown Resonant**

Those quest names describe the region rather than the actual entity Dylan eventually fights.

This is the complete Resonant checklist, but it is **not a strict six-boss order**. After the opening, several objectives can exist at the same time. Some Resonants matter to the next story gate, while others can wait until you want their power or are comfortable advancing Manhattan into another Resonant Cycle.

<h2 id="how-many-bosses">
  How many Resonant bosses are there?
</h2>

There are **six Resonants** in this set:

**Artist → Deserter → Dancer → Collective → Physicist → Co-Director**

That does not mean CONTROL Resonant only contains six major boss encounters. **Hedron is separate.**

The distinction becomes especially clear late in the game. Defeating the Co-Director completes **The Unknown Resonant**, while Dylan can already have a separate main objective telling him to fight the Hedron.

<h2 id="which-resonant-first">
  Which Resonant should you fight first?
</h2>

The **Artist** is fixed by the opening, so there is no real order decision there.

Once Manhattan opens up, I use the current story gate as the first filter rather than chasing whichever Resonant quest appeared most recently. The most important early pair is **Deserter + Dancer**.

The Deserter belongs to the **Central Resonant**, while the Dancer belongs to the **West Incursion Zone Resonant**. These are the two regional bosses I check first when the campaign appears to stop around **Enemy of My Enemy**.

The Collective and Physicist can be approached as their local routes open, while the Co-Director comes much later in the Unknown. A practical progression therefore looks more like:

**Artist → Deserter / Dancer → Collective / Physicist → Co-Director**

The middle pair is not a rigid order. Which one makes sense first depends on the region you are already working through, the Combat Ability your current setup needs, and whether another Resonant Cycle would make Manhattan harder before you are ready.

<h2 id="artist">
  The Artist — Downtown
</h2>

The Artist is the opening Resonant and works differently from the five regional bosses. You encounter it during **Orientation**, before Dylan has the freedom to move around Manhattan choosing between several active Resonant objectives.

Defeating the Artist sends Dylan to the Gap and introduces the first major Combat Ability choice:

**Barrage / Shield / Volatile Anomaly**

This encounter establishes the basic Resonant rule: defeating an entity does not simply hand Dylan one fixed power. The Gap determines how that kill changes the build.

If you are still in Orientation and have not reached this encounter, follow the <Link href="/control-resonant/walkthrough">main walkthrough</Link> rather than searching Manhattan for a separate Artist quest marker.

<h2 id="deserter">
  The Deserter — Central Resonant
</h2>

The **Central Resonant** is the Deserter. Its route runs through the **Patterned Factory** in Central, and reaching the entity is part of the challenge because overheating machinery, blocked pipes, steam, and Patterned growth all appear before the boss.

The complete factory approach and Deserter encounter are covered in the <Link href="/control-resonant/patterned-factory">Patterned Factory and Deserter guide</Link>.

Defeating the Deserter gives the choice between **Ignite** and **Inferno**.

More importantly for progression, this is one of the regional Resonants that matters to the mid-game story. If Central objectives look finished but the campaign still refuses to move, check whether the Deserter is still alive before wandering back through old Field Office triggers.

<h2 id="dancer">
  The Dancer — West Incursion Zone Resonant
</h2>

The **West Incursion Zone Resonant** is the Dancer, found inside the Theater.

Seeing the Theater does not mean Dylan can immediately reach the boss. The region first pushes him into **The Incursion Fault**, and completing that Jesse sequence changes how Dylan moves through the gravity-broken streets.

If the Theater is visible but the route seems physically impossible, finish the Jesse progression first. The full route is covered in <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link>.

<h2 id="collective">
  The Collective — The Park Resonant
</h2>

The Park handles its Resonant differently. There is no ordinary boss door leading directly to the Collective. Dylan first finds a **Mold Gateway** and a Mold sample beside it, and that first sample counts toward the requirement.

The complete route needs **four Mold samples**:

1. the sample beside the Gateway;
2. the Minimart Mold;
3. the Pizzeria Mold;
4. the Gym Mold.

After consuming all four, return to the same Gateway to open the route to the Collective.

The full Mold sample route and the Collective encounter are covered in the <Link href="/control-resonant/the-park">Park and Collective guide</Link>.

Defeating the Collective gives **Spore Burst** or **Growth**.

The Park is also a good example of why I do not kill every Resonant simply because its objective becomes available. If the Collective is not blocking the current story step and the build already feels weak, leaving it for later can be the better progression decision.

<h2 id="physicist">
  The Physicist — Underpass Resonant
</h2>

The **Underpass Resonant** is the Physicist. Its route branches upward from **The Spire** and eventually leaves the physical Underpass for the Astral Plane.

The important progression chain is:

**3 Astral Keys → 3 locked doors → Physicist**

The keys are the real navigation problem. They sit on separate floating branches rather than beside the doors they unlock, so opening one door does not mean the route is finished.

For all three Astral Key routes and the boss encounter, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/physicist">Physicist and Astral Keys guide</Link>.

<h2 id="co-director">
  The Co-Director — Unknown Resonant
</h2>

The **Unknown Resonant** is the Co-Director. This is a late regional encounter after Dylan reaches the Unknown and begins working through its Patterned FBC spaces.

Several late objectives overlap in this region, so I do not assume every copied Field Office or Patterned route is leading toward the Resonant.

When **The Unknown Resonant** becomes the active objective and the entity itself becomes the obstacle, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/co-director">Co-Director guide</Link> for the complete route and encounter.

Defeating the Co-Director unlocks **Command**.

Unlike the earlier two-choice regional rewards, Command is the defining Combat Ability tied to this encounter. It lets Dylan temporarily turn affected enemies against their own side.

<h2 id="resonant-cycle">
  Why you may want to wait before killing the next Resonant
</h2>

Every Resonant kill starts another **Resonant Cycle**, which makes boss progression different from a normal checklist.

Dylan gains another Combat Ability or ability option, but Manhattan also pushes back. Previously cleared combat encounters can return at a higher level, new threats can appear, and World Feats refresh.

The tradeoff is simple:

**Dylan gets stronger → Manhattan gets more dangerous.**

Before starting a Resonant that is not currently blocking the story, I check whether there is anything easier I still want to finish in the current Cycle.

Waiting can make sense when:

- ordinary enemies are already taking too long to kill;
- Dylan still needs better Aberrant upgrades;
- comfortable World Feats remain unfinished;
- the Resonant is not required for the objective I am currently pursuing.

There is little benefit in advancing the world just because another boss marker is available.

The opposite is also true. If the current Cycle has become comfortable and the build needs another Combat Ability, defeating a Resonant is exactly what moves both Dylan and Manhattan forward.

<h2 id="evacuation-zone">
  Is there an Evacuation Zone Resonant?
</h2>

No. The Evacuation Zone does not have another hidden regional Resonant in this six-boss chain.

Its major progression instead revolves around the Evac Building, Arish, resonance tremors, the Sinkhole, power-related objectives, and the Subway Fault connected to Jesse.

If you finish **Evacuation Procedures** and keep searching for an “Evacuation Zone Resonant,” you are not missing a seventh regional boss. Continue the active story or Jesse objective instead.

<h2 id="resonant-progression">
  If your Resonant progression still looks wrong
</h2>

A few situations can look like missing bosses even when the real problem is a route or quest gate.

### Enemy of My Enemy is not starting

Check whether both the **Central Resonant** and **West Incursion Zone Resonant** are complete. Those are the Deserter and Dancer.

### I can see the Theater but cannot reach the Dancer

Complete **The Incursion Fault** first. The West Incursion route needs the traversal change from the Jesse sequence before the path into the Theater becomes usable.

### The Park Gateway still will not open

Make sure all **four Mold samples** have been consumed. The first sample beside the Gateway is part of that total.

### The Underpass route keeps asking for another Astral Key

There are **three Astral Keys and three locked doors** before the Physicist. Opening the first or second door only moves Dylan deeper into the same route.

### I completed The Underpass Resonant but never saw a boss with that name

That is expected. **The Underpass Resonant** is the Quest Log name, while the actual entity Dylan defeats is the **Physicist**.

### Manhattan became harder after I defeated a Resonant

You advanced to another **Resonant Cycle**. Previously cleared encounters can return at a higher level and new threats can appear, so the difficulty increase is part of world progression rather than a broken quest state.

### I defeated a Resonant but the completion result has not appeared yet

Finish the post-boss sequence in the **Gap**. Some Resonant completion states resolve after Dylan leaves the Gap rather than at the exact moment the boss health bar reaches zero.
