<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Complete **Pope's Research** so Dylan can move between the Unknown islands, then follow **The Unknown Resonant** objective to the Patterned Field Office and onward to the Co-Director. During the fight, keep the **Command meter below 100%**, punish the boss after its beam and spinning-ring attacks, and kill controlled Rangers before they overwhelm the arena.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How to reach The Unknown Resonant
</h2>

The Co-Director is the Resonant tied to the **Unknown**.

This encounter becomes available late, after the story has opened the region around the Hedron investigation. Reaching the Unknown itself is not enough, because Dylan still needs a way to move between its separated floating islands.

Complete **Pope's Research** first.

The research balloons in the Unknown drop antenna equipment. Place the antennas in the marked circles around each balloon to create a **Gravity Well**, then use those wells to move farther across the region.

Once you can travel between the islands, work toward the **Field Office** side of the map.

The Unknown copies familiar Manhattan and FBC spaces, so seeing Central-looking architecture here does not mean Dylan has returned to the real Central district. Stay with The Unknown Resonant objective and continue through the Patterned version of the area.

<h2 id="field-office-route">
  Follow The Unknown Resonant through the Patterned Field Office
</h2>

The route toward the Co-Director passes through a Patterned copy of the Field Office.

Rangers are trapped inside the distorted area, and Dylan has to push through the local combat before the path continues. Familiar rooms can repeat, shift, or appear in places that do not match the real Field Office.

The important distinction is the active quest.

If your objective still says **Sever the Hedron Links**, continue the <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">Hedron Link route</Link> rather than treating every Patterned room as part of the Co-Director encounter.

When **The Unknown Resonant** is active, stay with its marker through the Field Office section until the route carries Dylan beyond the repeating interior and toward the boss arena.

Do not leave Unknown simply because the geometry stops matching the original building.

<h2 id="before-fight">
  Prepare for a fight with a permanent timer
</h2>

The Co-Director is unusual because time pressure matters even when Dylan is not taking damage.

The arena applies **Command**.

That meter rises during the fight and does not steadily drain back to zero while you wait for safer openings.

At **100% Command**, Dylan loses the encounter.

That changes the preparation priorities.

A defensive setup that survives forever but deals poor damage can still fail because the meter eventually reaches its limit. Direct damage, short punish windows, and reasonable Power efficiency matter more here than they do against a boss where waiting carries no cost.

Around **110 Aberrant Power** is a comfortable point for this fight, although it is not a hard entry requirement.

Before entering, I would favor:

- reliable direct damage;
- enough Health to survive one mistake without panicking;
- a Combat Ability that can be used without draining all Power at once;
- Artifacts that improve damage or shorten the fight;
- fewer summons if the build depends on keeping them alive.

The last point matters because the Co-Director can turn allies against Dylan.

<h2 id="command-meter">
  Finish the fight before Command reaches 100%
</h2>

Keep the Command meter visible in your decision-making throughout the encounter.

A low-health boss with 80% Command on Dylan is a very different situation from the same boss at 20%.

The fight rewards short, immediate retaliation:

**avoid the attack → punish the recovery → reset only when the next dangerous tell begins**

Long periods of circling the arena are safe for Health but bad for Command.

Getting hit can make the situation worse, so this is not an invitation to trade damage blindly. The goal is to remove unnecessary downtime, not to ignore attack tells.

When Command is already high, spend Power during a clean recovery window rather than saving everything for a perfect moment that may never come.

<h2 id="beam">
  Move sideways when the beam locks onto Dylan
</h2>

The Co-Director spends much of the fight above the arena.

One of its simpler ranged attacks is a beam aimed directly at Dylan.

Moving straight backward wastes space and can keep Dylan inside the line of fire longer than necessary.

Dash sideways.

That breaks the firing line while keeping Dylan close enough to return damage once the beam stops.

The end of the beam is one of the cleaner moments to attack, especially when Command is already climbing and you cannot afford another long reset.

<h2 id="spinning-ring">
  Leave melee range when the outer ring begins spinning
</h2>

The clearest danger tell is the Co-Director's outer ring.

When the ring contracts around the boss and begins rotating, get away from the body.

The immediate area becomes unsafe, and trying to finish one more melee string is rarely worth the damage.

Wait until the rotation has fully ended.

The recovery afterward is valuable because the boss stays vulnerable long enough for Dylan to close distance and spend a stronger combo or Combat Ability.

Later versions of the spin last longer and can follow Dylan around the arena.

During that extended sequence, stop forcing damage. Keep moving until the attack has completely finished, then turn back toward the boss.

This is one of the few moments where losing several seconds is better than taking a hit that both removes Health and makes the Command situation worse.

<h2 id="bombs">
  Move again after dodging the bomb
</h2>

The Co-Director eventually adds small spherical projectiles.

The projectile itself is only the first half of the attack.

After landing, it explodes.

A dodge that clears the incoming orb can still leave Dylan standing directly beside the detonation point.

Move diagonally away from where the bomb is landing and keep going until the explosion resolves. One continuous movement is safer than dodging the projectile and immediately stopping to attack.

<h2 id="ground-slam">
  Raised arms signal the ground slam
</h2>

When the Co-Director raises its arms overhead, leave the nearby area.

That animation leads into a heavy ground slam.

The damage is high enough that squeezing in one extra attack before dodging is usually a poor trade, especially because every mistake costs time as well as Health.

Back away, let the slam resolve, then use the recovery to close the gap again.

The boss fight becomes much easier once these recovery windows become your main damage opportunities rather than trying to remain underneath the Co-Director continuously.

<h2 id="controlled-rangers">
  Kill Rangers after the Co-Director takes control of them
</h2>

Additional soldiers become part of the encounter as the fight develops.

At first they look like help.

That can change quickly.

The Co-Director can take control of Rangers and turn them against Dylan. Once a soldier is visibly hostile under that effect, treat the Ranger as an enemy and remove them.

Leaving controlled soldiers alive creates pressure from two directions: Dylan has to dodge their attacks while still reading the boss, and every second spent recovering control of the arena pushes the Command meter higher.

Do not waste time trying to preserve a Ranger that has already been taken over.

Remove the immediate threat, then return to the Co-Director.

Summoned allies can create the same problem if the boss is able to take control of them, which is why summon-heavy setups are less comfortable here.

<h2 id="rocket-bursts">
  Expect three rockets once the ranged soldiers begin firing
</h2>

The later fight adds more pressure around the outside of the arena.

Ranged soldiers can fire rockets in **bursts of three**.

Do not treat the first projectile as the whole attack.

Once the first rocket appears, keep moving through the following two before planting Dylan for another combo.

This is especially important late in the fight, when tunnel vision on the Co-Director can make the outer enemies easy to forget.

Clearing a controlled Ranger and avoiding the entire three-rocket burst is usually more valuable than forcing a few extra hits on the boss while standing still.

<h2 id="damage-check">
  What to change when Command keeps reaching 80–100%
</h2>

Repeatedly losing to Command while the Co-Director still has substantial Health left is usually a damage problem rather than a dodging problem.

Look at where the fight is slowing down.

If Dylan spends most of the encounter waiting for full safety, begin attacking during beam, slam, and spinning-ring recovery instead.

If the attacks are already being punished but the boss health barely moves, change the build before repeating the same attempt.

The priorities I use are:

1. direct damage that lands during short windows;
2. enough survivability to recover from one mistake;
3. Power efficiency;
4. crowd control for taken-over soldiers.

A small defensive gain is useful.

A large damage loss is not, because Command continues rising while Dylan plays cautiously.

<h2 id="reward">
  What do you get for beating the Co-Director?
</h2>

Defeating the Co-Director unlocks **Command**.

There is no two-ability choice after this Resonant.

Command allows Dylan to take control of hostile enemies and temporarily turn them into allies, giving him another way to change the balance of larger encounters instead of only dealing direct damage.

The fight also rewards the **Untapped 1996 Commemorative Coin** Artifact recipe.

Its developed effect improves the Health Dylan receives from Health Elements, which suits aggressive setups that expect to restore Health while staying involved in combat.

<h2 id="honorable-discharge">
  When Honorable Discharge unlocks
</h2>

**Honorable Discharge** is tied to killing the Resonant in the Unknown.

The achievement or trophy does not need to appear at the exact moment the Co-Director dies.

Complete the reward sequence and leave **The Gap**.

The unlock occurs after that transition.

If the boss is dead and The Unknown Resonant has completed, do not reload the fight just because the notification was not visible inside the arena.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Co-Director stuck fixes
</h2>

### I reached Unknown but cannot get to the Field Office island

Complete **Pope's Research** and use the research balloons to create Gravity Wells between the floating islands. The later Field Office section is not reachable by ordinary traversal alone.

### The objective in the Patterned Field Office says Sever the Hedron Links

Follow the <Link href="/control-resonant/hedron-link">Hedron Link objective</Link> first. The Unknown contains overlapping late-game routes, so the active quest name matters more than the familiar location.

### Command keeps reaching 100% before I can kill the boss

Shorten the fight. Attack during the recovery after the beam, ground slam, and spinning ring instead of waiting for long periods of complete safety. If those windows are already being used, increase direct damage before trying again.

### The spinning ring keeps hitting me

Leave melee range as soon as the outer ring contracts and starts rotating. Later in the fight, keep running until the extended spin has completely ended before returning.

### A bomb hits me even after I dodge it

The projectile explodes after landing. Continue moving away from the landing point instead of stopping after the first dodge.

### Rangers suddenly started attacking Dylan

The Co-Director can take control of soldiers. Kill the controlled Ranger quickly so the arena does not become crowded while Command continues to rise.

### Rockets keep catching me after the first dodge

They arrive in **bursts of three**. Keep moving through the complete burst before committing to another stationary attack.

### The boss is dead but Honorable Discharge has not unlocked

Finish the Gap sequence and leave. The achievement or trophy triggers after the transition rather than at the instant the Co-Director dies.
