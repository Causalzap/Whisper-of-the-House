<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Go to **The Spire** in the Underpass, climb into the Astral Plane, then open the
three locked doors with **three separate Astral Keys**. The keys are not marked
on the minimap. After the third door, follow the route to the Physicist and
defeat it to choose **Spike** or **Astral Rebuke**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Physicist is easy to miss even after the Underpass itself is already open.

That is because reaching the boss is not part of the normal route through the
research site. The **Underpass Resonant** branches upward from **The Spire**,
then leaves Manhattan entirely for an Astral Plane section built around three
locked doors.

The boss is not the part that made me stop and check the route.

The three **Astral Keys** did.

None of them gets a useful minimap marker, and every door makes it look as
though the next key should be somewhere immediately nearby. In practice, each
one sends Dylan through a separate branch of the Astral Plane before looping
back to the same central progression route.

<h2 id="start-underpass-resonant">
  How to start the Underpass Resonant at The Spire
</h2>

Reach **The Spire** in the Underpass.

If you are still working through the research team, Power Cores, or the route beneath the Oldest House, stay with the <Link href="/control-resonant/underpass">Underpass walkthrough</Link> until The Spire becomes accessible.

Once there, look toward the Spire from the surrounding platform and interact
with its Resonant prompt.

The objective changes to investigating where the Spire leads.

You need **Shift** for this route.

The climb repeatedly uses differently oriented surfaces, so a section that
looks like a vertical wall from the current platform can become the next floor
once Dylan changes orientation.

Do not search around the base for a conventional staircase to the top.

Climb the Spire using the Patterned surfaces until Dylan crosses into the
Astral Plane.

Dylan comments on how strange this is even by FBC standards. He has experienced
the Astral Plane before through projection, but here he is effectively walking
into it through a breach.

Keep following the floating structures until you reach the **first locked
door**.

This is where the real puzzle begins.

<h2 id="astral-keys">
  You need three Astral Keys, one for each door
</h2>

There are **three consecutive locked doors**.

Each door consumes one Astral Key.

Opening one does not mean you have finished the key section; it simply exposes
the next locked door.

The important rule is:

**do not expect the minimap to show the keys.**

Use each locked door as the center of that key's search route, collect the key,
then come back to the same door before progressing to the next layer.

<h2 id="first-astral-key">
  First Astral Key location
</h2>

Stand with your **back to the first locked door**.

Take the route on the **right**.

Follow it through the first enemy encounters until you reach the sections with
boxes tied to Astral spike traps.

Continue until the **second spike-trap box**.

From there, take the path on the **right** again and keep going straight.

The first Astral Key is at the end of this branch.

Pick it up.

Do not continue searching beyond it for another door. The next step is to
retrace the route to the locked door you started from.

Use the key there.

The door opens, but almost immediately reveals a second locked barrier.

That is normal.

<h2 id="second-astral-key">
  Second Astral Key location
</h2>

At the **second door**, turn around so the locked door is behind Dylan.

This time, go **straight ahead**.

Follow the route all the way to the end and jump across the gap.

There are more enemies beyond it, followed by another section of spike hazards.
Clear the combat rather than trying to sprint past everything; the return trip
is easier when the branch is already safe.

Continue climbing until you reach a small area with a **chair and television**.

That is the landmark I use instead of trying to remember which floating island
I came from.

From the chair and TV:

1. use **Shift** to descend;
2. use Shift a second time;
3. jump across to the opposite side.

The second Astral Key is there.

Take it and return through the same branch.

Use the key on the second locked door.

Behind it is the third and final locked door.

<h2 id="third-astral-key">
  Third Astral Key location
</h2>

Put the **third locked door behind Dylan** and move forward.

Watch the **left side** for a series of floating platforms.

The first platform is not your destination.

Move toward the **second platform** instead, using Dylan's aerial movement to
close the gap.

Soon afterward you encounter a strange box that moves away whenever Dylan gets
close.

Follow it.

This is not an optional random event. The moving box is effectively leading
you along the route toward the final key.

Enemies appear farther along the branch.

Clear them, continue after the moving object, and keep following the path until
you reach the **third Astral Key**.

Take the key, collect any nearby resources you still need, then return to the
third door.

This is the last time you have to leave the central route looking for a key.

Open the door.

<h2 id="before-physicist">
  Prepare before going through the third door
</h2>

Once the third Astral Key has opened the final barrier, stop treating the area
as an exploration puzzle.

The remaining route leads to the Resonant.

I use this point to check three things:

- health is not already depleted from the key-route fights;
- the Aberrant form I actually use against a single tough target is equipped;
- my Combat Ability loadout has something I can use without relying on the
  arena giving me a very specific setup.

There is no benefit to arriving at the boss with half a health bar simply
because the Astral Key route technically let you continue.

The key section is long enough that several small combat mistakes can add up.

If the route has consumed most of your healing or normal enemies are taking too
long to kill, leaving and improving Dylan is a better use of time than forcing
the boss immediately.

<h2 id="physicist-boss">
  How to beat the Physicist
</h2>

The person at the center of the breach has been consumed by the same phenomenon
he was trying to understand.

His recordings repeatedly return to the same idea: the math suggested that
another space existed, and he wanted to find a point of connection that would
let him reach it.

He succeeded.

Just not in the way he expected.

This is not another Astral Key puzzle once the fight starts.

The useful approach is to preserve room to move rather than staying committed
to one long melee string.

The Astral arena gives Dylan plenty of unusual geometry, and the safest damage
comes from short attack windows followed by repositioning.

Build Falter when you can, take the Execution opportunities you earn, and use
your Combat Ability when it creates a clean opening instead of dumping every
resource at the beginning of the encounter.

The biggest mistake I would avoid is chasing damage after Dylan's positioning
has already broken down.

A longer fight with health remaining is better than finishing one extra combo
and then being trapped in a bad part of the arena.

If your damage is extremely low rather than merely slow, the boss is a good
signal to stop and upgrade the Aberrant or spend unused Talent Points. You can
leave the Resonant route and return later; the three-key section is not a reason
to lock yourself into an underpowered attempt.

Keep pressure on the Physicist whenever the arena is stable, disengage when the
space around Dylan becomes unsafe, then re-enter instead of attacking through
everything.

When its health is gone, the Astral breach sequence ends and Dylan is taken to
the Gap for the Resonant reward.

<h2 id="spike-or-astral-rebuke">
  Should you choose Spike or Astral Rebuke?
</h2>

The Physicist gives you a choice between **Spike** and **Astral Rebuke**.

Both revolve around the **Astralized** status, which makes affected enemies
more vulnerable to other status effects, but they solve different combat
problems.

### Spike

**Spike** gives you an additional controlled Astral presence that pressures
enemies for you.

I would take Spike when the rest of the build already benefits from having
another source of autonomous pressure.

It is more attractive if you like:

- summons;
- splitting enemy attention;
- continuing to apply pressure while Dylan moves or attacks something else.

The tradeoff is that its value depends more on what the summoned pressure is
actually doing during the fight.

### Astral Rebuke

**Astral Rebuke** creates a spike from the ground and launches the target
upward.

It is the simpler choice when you want one button to immediately disrupt a
grounded enemy.

That makes it easier to fit into a direct melee loop:

**open enemy → launch / disrupt → follow with Aberrant pressure.**

The limitation matters, though.

Astral Rebuke needs a **grounded target**. It loses value when the enemies that
are giving you trouble spend most of their time in the air.

<h3 id="which-one">
  Which Physicist ability should you take?
</h3>

For a straightforward melee-oriented run, I would lean toward **Astral
Rebuke**.

Its result is immediate and easier to plan around.

Choose **Spike** instead when your current build already leans into summoned
allies or you want pressure happening somewhere Dylan is not personally
attacking.

Neither choice unlocks traversal.

They are combat rewards, so you do not need one specific option to keep moving
through the story.

<h2 id="physicist-rewards">
  Physicist rewards
</h2>

Defeating the Physicist gives Dylan:

- the **Spike / Astral Rebuke** Combat Ability choice;
- the **Untapped Golden Earrings Artifact Recipe**;
- completion of the **Underpass Resonant** objective.

After the fight, Dylan reports what actually happened to Zoe.

The Resonant had burrowed into the **Astral Plane** and opened a breach back
into the Underpass.

Dylan describes the person behind it as some kind of amateur physicist who
essentially managed to break space.

<h2 id="astral-key-checks">
  Astral Keys, locked doors, and Physicist fight checks
</h2>

### I reached The Spire but cannot find the boss

The Physicist is not at ground level.

Start the Underpass Resonant interaction at The Spire, then use Shift to climb
the altered surfaces into the Astral Plane.

### The first door is locked and the map shows no key

That is intentional.

Put the first door behind Dylan, go right, then take another right after the
second spike-trap box.

### I opened the first door and found another locked door

You did not use the wrong key.

There are **three locked doors and three different Astral Keys**.

### I cannot find the second key

From the second door, go straight ahead through the gap, fights, and spike
section until you find the **chair and TV**.

Shift downward twice there, then jump across.

### I cannot find the third key

From the third door, move forward and watch the left side.

Ignore the first floating platform, reach the second, then follow the box that
keeps moving away from Dylan.

### I collected a key but the objective did not progress

Return to the locked door for that layer and use the key.

Collecting it alone does not open the next section.

### I opened all three doors but am still searching for another key

Stop searching.

There are only **three Astral Keys**. After the third door, follow the route
forward to the Physicist.

### The Physicist feels much harder than the enemies on the key route

Do not let the time already spent finding the keys force you into repeated bad
attempts.

Leave, spend Talent Points, improve the Aberrant, refill what you need, then
return when normal enemies and the boss's openings are no longer consuming most
of your health.

### I chose the wrong ability after the boss

Spike and Astral Rebuke are mutually exclusive during the initial choice, but
the decision is not the same as permanently breaking progression.

Neither is required to unlock a traversal route, so continue the game even if
the choice does not perfectly fit the current build.
