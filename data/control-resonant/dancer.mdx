<h2 id="where-to-find-dancer">
  Where to find the Dancer
</h2>

The Dancer is the **West Incursion Zone Resonant** and waits inside the Theater.

You can see the Theater long before Dylan can actually reach it. Patterning has rotated streets and buildings through different gravity planes, so the direct-looking route is blocked until Dylan completes **The Incursion Fault**.

Finish <Link href="/control-resonant/search-for-jesse">Search for Jesse</Link> through that first Fault, then return to West Incursion with the new gravity traversal available.

From there, follow the Theater spotlights and the trail left by the missing FBC Rangers. The complete path through the district is covered in the <Link href="/control-resonant/west-incursion-zone">West Incursion Zone walkthrough</Link>; once Dylan reaches the Patterned Ranger team outside the Theater, the boss is close.

<h2 id="dancer-attacks">
  Read the Dancer's movement before committing to a combo
</h2>

The Dancer's first phase revolves around fast melee pressure rather than complicated arena mechanics. Its attacks become much easier to handle once you stop trying to trade through them.

A forward lunge is one of the most common openings. The Dancer pulls its body toward Dylan, commits forward, then chains into close-range strikes. Dash away from the initial contact and wait until the sequence finishes before moving back in.

The spinning attack is more dangerous because it can travel across the stage while the Dancer's limbs remain extended. One dodge is often not enough. Keep moving laterally until the spin has completely stopped, then use the recovery window for a short melee string.

The boss also has a broad pouncing strike with more reach than the first part of the animation suggests. Give it room to land, then close the distance while the Dancer is recovering.

A reliable first-phase rhythm is:

1. let the Dancer commit to its attack;
2. move clear of the full animation;
3. return during the recovery;
4. land a short combo;
5. reset your position before the next wind-up.

Long strings are much riskier than repeated short punish windows because the Dancer recovers quickly enough to catch Dylan before an extended combo finishes.

<h2 id="phase-two">
  Phase two adds airborne attacks and audience puppets
</h2>

Emptying the first health sequence does not end the encounter.

The Dancer returns with the same close-range pressure but begins using the Theater audience as part of its attacks.

When the boss rises into the air, it can send **two puppet-like figures** toward Dylan before coming back down.

Do not chase directly underneath the boss while it is airborne. That position hides the incoming puppets behind the camera and makes the Dancer's landing harder to read.

Back away far enough to keep both the airborne boss and the stage in view. Deal with the incoming figures first, then re-engage after the Dancer lands.

A ranged Combat Ability is especially useful during this part because melee uptime drops whenever the boss leaves the floor. Save enough Power to exploit those periods rather than spending everything during the easier first phase.

<h2 id="falter-window">
  Use the blue stage flash as the final damage window
</h2>

The lighting becomes more intense as the fight approaches its conclusion.

Watch for the stage to flash blue and the Dancer to enter a vulnerable state.

That is the important Falter window.

Move in immediately and spend damage while the boss is exposed. This is not the moment to back away and wait for another clean rotation; the vulnerability is the opportunity to push the fight toward its finish.

Once the final vulnerable state has been exhausted and the Dancer goes down, the encounter moves into the Gap reward sequence.

<h2 id="push-or-ground-slam">
  Push or Ground Slam: which Dancer ability should you choose?
</h2>

The Dancer offers **Push** and **Ground Slam**.

**Push** deals **50–100 damage**, **5–15 Falter**, and costs **200 Power**. It is the cheaper control option for creating space, knocking enemies away, and pushing targets toward hazards.

**Ground Slam** deals **230–460 damage**, **20–30 Falter**, and costs **630 Power**. It is the heavier offensive option, especially for aerial attacks and aggressive melee setups.

The decision comes down to whether the current build needs control or damage.

### Push

Push costs only **200 Power**, so it is much easier to fit into normal combat without emptying Dylan's resource bar.

Its value comes from displacement. Enemies crowding Dylan can be knocked away, dangerous melee pressure can be interrupted, and targets near ledges or environmental hazards can be forced into worse positions.

Choose Push when the build already deals enough damage but regularly loses control of crowded fights.

### Ground Slam

Ground Slam costs **630 Power**, more than three times as much as Push, but its damage and Falter are also dramatically higher.

Its listed damage reaches **230–460**, with **20–30 Falter**, compared with Push's 50–100 damage and 5–15 Falter.

Height also works naturally with it, making the ability a better match for aggressive aerial movement and melee setups that already spend a lot of time close to enemies.

For a damage-focused build that can manage the higher Power cost, **Ground Slam** is the stronger offensive choice.

For control and frequent use, **Push** is easier to weave into ordinary fights.

Neither choice is required for traversal or story progression.

<h2 id="is-dancer-required">
  Is the Dancer required for the main story?
</h2>

Yes.

The Dancer is one of the regional Resonants tied to the story's early progression gate.

If **Enemy of My Enemy** is not advancing, check whether both of these objectives are complete:

- **The Central Resonant**
- **The West Incursion Zone Resonant**

The Central encounter leads to the Deserter, while the West Incursion encounter ends with the Dancer.

Killing only one is not enough to satisfy that part of the progression.

If you need to compare the remaining Resonants, their locations, and their Combat Ability rewards, use the <Link href="/control-resonant/resonants">all Resonants list</Link>.

<h2 id="dancer-stuck">
  Dancer stuck fixes
</h2>

### I can see the Theater but cannot reach it

Complete **The Incursion Fault** first. The West Incursion approach requires the gravity traversal gained during that Jesse sequence.

### I found the missing Rangers but nothing happens

They cannot be rescued at this point. Continue toward the Theater and follow the singing toward the stage.

### The Dancer came back after I emptied its health

The encounter has a second phase. Expect airborne movement and two audience puppets before the boss returns to the floor.

### The spinning attack keeps catching me after I dodge

Do not attack immediately after the first dodge. Keep moving until the entire spinning animation finishes, then punish the recovery.

### I keep taking damage when the Dancer goes airborne

Move away from directly underneath it. Keeping more distance makes the incoming puppets and the boss's landing easier to see.

### I do not know whether to take Push or Ground Slam

Take **Ground Slam** for much higher burst and Falter if the Power cost fits your build. Take **Push** when you want a cheaper ability for knockback, spacing, and crowd control.
