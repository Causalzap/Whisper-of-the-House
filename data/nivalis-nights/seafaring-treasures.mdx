<section id="quick-answer" className="scroll-mt-24">
  <GuideQuickAnswer title="Where Are All 3 Seafaring Treasures in Nivalis Nights?">

    Catch the **Old Android Head** near the Oil Rig, return to **Salt Pete**,
    then find the **Android Body** off Calypso Island. After speaking to
    **Caleb Foamheart**, take the boat through the Sewers' open-water exit
    and search the nearby fallen ruins for the final **Logic Unit**.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>

Salt Pete's Seafaring treasure hunt begins with a riddle about fishing
near the Oil Rig. It eventually sends you across three different bodies
of water, but reaching the named location is only half the search.

The first treasure can be several hundred meters from the Oil Rig marker.
The second requires another conversation with Pete before the quest
advances. The third is particularly confusing: the clue points north,
while the useful approach leads west through the Sewers, where two
nearby fishing signals can overlap.

The three objects belong to an old android captain. You recover the head,
torso, and logic unit in that order, with additional conversations
connecting each part of the expedition.


<h2 id="start-seafaring" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Start the Seafaring Treasure Hunt
</h2>

Start at the **Docks**, where Salt Pete introduces you to the boat.
The opening Seafaring objectives are about getting onto the **Putter**,
taking the wheel, and discovering a new area by water.

Once you have made that trip, return to Pete. He begins talking about
an old treasure, then contacts you again through a message that Ava
translates from Morse code.

The message contains the first clue:

**"The oil is rigged in your favor."**

Ava interprets this as an instruction to fish near the **Oil Rig**.
Pete also leaves you an old fishing rod.

There is no need to buy a second rod if you already received one from
Boardwalk Addy. You can use the equipment you have to begin recovering
the treasure.

Before leaving, check that the Putter has enough fuel and that your
inventory contains a usable fishing rod. If you have not yet registered
the boat or learned to cast, the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">fishing and boat guide</Link> covers those controls.

Your journal should now direct you to find the first treasure near
the Oil Rig.


<h2 id="first-treasure-oil-rig" className="scroll-mt-24">
  First Treasure: Old Android Head Near the Oil Rig
</h2>

Sail toward the Oil Rig from the Docks. As you approach the area,
Ava explains how the boat scanner distinguishes fish from unusual
objects beneath the water.

**Watch for yellow pulses rather than ordinary fish activity.**
The scanner can pick up submerged objects that are not visible
from the surface.

The most common mistake here is stopping directly beside the main
Oil Rig structure and repeatedly casting into the surrounding water.
The actual treasure fishing spot is farther out, in the same general
waterway near a group of cranes.

Move around the surrounding water until the scanner leads you to
the treasure marker. Once you reach its fishing circle, stop the
boat within casting distance and aim your rod inside it.

Several things may come up before the correct object, including
Pike, Stickleback, and ordinary salvage. Those catches do not mean
the quest has progressed. Check the name of the recovered object,
then keep searching if it is unrelated to Salt Pete.

The first required catch is the **Old Android Head**.

The object is a badly damaged android head. Ava recognizes that
you have recovered the first piece and tells you to speak to Pete
again.

**Return to Salt Pete at the Docks.** There is no benefit to
continuing to fish around the Oil Rig after the head has been
recovered.

During the conversation, Pete explains more about the old captain
and gives you another clue. This one leads toward the golden shores
of **Calypso Island**.


<h2 id="second-treasure-calypso" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Second Treasure: Android Body at Calypso Island
</h2>

Calypso Island lies farther west across the Nivalis waterways.
Your next journal objective directs you to search the sea around
the island with the boat scanner.

Travel to Calypso Island, reach its boat dock, and begin checking
the nearby offshore water. The treasure spot is a short distance
from the island's dock and boat fuel station.

You are looking for another submerged-object signal. The treasure
is not inside the resort buildings and cannot be collected by
walking along the beach.

Search the water surrounding the dock before committing to a long
trip away from the island. When the scanner picks up the relevant
signal, approach the fishing circle and cast into it.

The second required object is the **Old Android Body**, described
by Ava as the android's torso.

A regular fish, lamp, or other recovered object does not advance
the treasure objective. Continue until Ava confirms that the
second piece has been found.

Keep enough fuel for the return trip. Calypso's fuel station is
a useful stop if you have spent a long time searching, but there
is no particular reason to buy a more expensive fishing rod for
this stage.

Once you have the torso, return to **Salt Pete at the Docks**.
His next conversation introduces someone who knows more about
the captain.

You must speak to that person before the final treasure objective
becomes active.


<h2 id="caleb-foamheart" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Find Caleb Foamheart at the Mountain Town Boat Hangar
</h2>

Salt Pete sends you to **Caleb Foamheart**, who works at the
**Mountain Town Boat Hangar**.

Travel to Mountain Town, find the hangar, and talk to Caleb about
the captain and the recovered android parts.

This conversation provides the next clue:

**The final treasure is near fallen ruins, toward the northern sea.**

The wording is easy to misinterpret. Searching the far northern
edge of the water is not the route you need. The relevant ruins
are accessible through the Sewers, and the useful water passage
heads west.

After Caleb's conversation, check that your objective has changed
to finding the third treasure. Then travel to the Sewers.


<h2 id="third-treasure-sewers" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Third Treasure: Old Android CPU / Logic Unit in the Sewers
</h2>

The third piece is the one most likely to waste your time.

The ruins do exist, and the boat scanner can detect the nearby
fishing spots. The difficulty comes from choosing the correct
Sewers exit and distinguishing the treasure signal from another
submerged-object location beside it.

Start by fast travelling to the **Sewers** and summoning your
Putter at an accessible dock.


<h3 id="sewers-water-exit" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Take the Water Tunnel Out of the Sewers
</h3>

From the Sewers, look for the large pipe or tunnel leading
directly into exterior water.

There is an important distinction between the two types of exits
you may encounter:

- **Correct exit:** an open water passage that you can sail through
  without changing maps.
- **Wrong exit:** a glowing district-transition passage that
  transports you to another area.

Stay in your boat and follow the open tunnel toward the outside.
You should see daylight or the open, fog-covered water ahead,
along with the silhouettes of ruined structures.

Once you leave the tunnel, the **fallen ruins** become visible
almost immediately. Look for damaged buildings, exposed structures,
and large walls rising out of the water.

The area is already close to the correct treasure location.
There is no need to continue sailing north for several districts.

Move out into the ruined waterway and begin sweeping the scanner.
The relevant signals appear near the ruins, with the search
concentrated to the left of the exit around the wall and
surrounding structures.


<h3 id="sewers-overlapping-signals" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Check Both Nearby Signals, Not Just the First One
</h3>

This is the distinction that makes the third treasure different
from the earlier two.

The scanner can reveal **two nearby submerged-object signals**
in the ruined waters. They may appear very close together or
overlap as you move the boat.

The first signal is not guaranteed to contain the Logic Unit.
One search recovered ordinary **Plasteel** from the first marked
location instead.

After catching unrelated salvage, return to the boat's scanner
and look a little farther along the same stretch of water.

The second signal is close enough that you do not need to start
the route over. Move beyond the first fishing circle, stop beside
the next detected location, and cast again.

The second fishing location produced the quest item after only
a few casts in one successful attempt.

That does not establish a fixed number of casts or mean that
every player must ignore the first signal. Both nearby locations
are worth checking. The reliable distinction is the item you
actually recover.

A stronger fishing rod is not an established requirement for
this objective. Spending more money on equipment should come
after checking the correct quest stage and fishing locations,
not before.

The object you want is the **Old Android CPU**, also described
as the captain's **Logic Unit**. Once Ava identifies it,
you have all three parts.

You can leave the Sewers and return to the Docks.


<h2 id="complete-the-captain" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What to Do After Finding All Three Treasures
</h2>

Recovering the Logic Unit completes the treasure collection,
but the Seafaring story still has several conversations left.

Your first destination is **Karel Crabface Jones** at the Docks.
Talk to him about the android captain and the recovered pieces.
He helps advance the expedition to its final stage.

After that, return to **Salt Pete** and tell him the truth
about the captain.

The recovered parts are connected to Pete's past, so the
conversation is no longer simply about finding valuable
objects at sea.

There is one more visit to make: **Caleb Foamheart at the
Mountain Town Boat Hangar**.

Follow the remaining conversations in this order:

1. **Karel Crabface Jones** — complete the captain.
2. **Salt Pete** — reveal the truth.
3. **Caleb Foamheart** — finish the Seafaring storyline.

Do not stop after speaking to Crabface or Pete if the quest
journal still shows another active Seafaring objective.

Completing the quest is worth **1 Achievement Point**.
Its conversations can also award additional recipes, so
finish the dialogue rather than leaving immediately after
the last fishing objective.


<h2 id="seafaring-treasure-not-working" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Seafaring Treasure Not Appearing or Quest Not Progressing
</h2>

Several different situations can look like a broken treasure
quest. The fastest solution depends on which objective is
currently active.

**The Oil Rig marker is visible, but nothing is directly
underneath it.**

The marker identifies the general location rather than an
exact place to cast. Search the nearby water toward the
cranes and follow the submerged-object signal. You are
looking for the Old Android Head, not ordinary fish.

**The second treasure is not showing up near Calypso.**

Confirm that you returned the head to Salt Pete and received
the Calypso clue. Then search the offshore water near the
island's boat dock and fuel station. Walking around the resort
will not reveal the submerged item.

**The third treasure is not available after returning the
android body.**

Speak to Salt Pete again, then visit Caleb Foamheart at the
Mountain Town Boat Hangar. The fallen-ruins objective follows
that conversation.

**The Sewers scanner shows a signal, but you keep getting junk.**

Make sure you left through the open-water tunnel rather than
a glowing transport portal. Search the ruined waterfront
outside the tunnel and investigate both nearby scanner
signals. Ordinary Plasteel from one location is not the
Logic Unit.

**The treasure fishing spot appears broken or no longer works.**

An earlier version of the game had a conflict between two
treasure fishing spots that could block quest progression.
**Update #3, released October 5, 2026, fixed that conflict.**

Update the game before repeating a long search on an older
build. After updating, reload your save, inspect the current
objective, and revisit the appropriate fishing area.

An update does not guarantee that every interrupted save will
recover in exactly the same way, so distinguish a missing
quest objective from a missing scanner signal before spending
more time casting.

Once Ava confirms the Logic Unit, there is no reason to keep
searching the fallen ruins. The remaining Seafaring progression
takes place through conversations at the Docks and Mountain Town.
