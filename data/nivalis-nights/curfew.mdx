Curfew changes travel rather than instantly ending the night.

At **2:00 AM**, CorpSec orders civilians indoors or into authorized shelters.
Security cameras and drones become the main obstacle, and normal travel can be
less convenient once the restriction is active.

The important decision is where the night will end. When home is close, return
to your apartment. When you are stranded in another district, use the nearest
shelter instead of trying to force a long route across the city.


<h2
  id="curfew-time"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Time Does Curfew Start?
</h2>

Curfew starts at **2:00 AM**.

The warning appears before the deadline and tells civilians to clear the
streets and be indoors or at an authorized shelter by 2:00.

Treat the warning as the point to finish local tasks rather than start another
long trip. A boat excursion, unfamiliar district, or distant quest can leave
you looking for a safe place after the restriction has already started.

When the clock is close to 2:00, decide where you are sleeping before taking
another objective.


<h2
  id="home-or-shelter"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Home if It Is Close; Use a Shelter if It Is Not
</h2>

Your apartment and a public shelter both work as places to end the night.

Going home is simplest when you are already nearby and know the entrance. In
another district, or when you no longer have an easy route back, an authorized
shelter is the practical fallback.

Ava explains that shelters exist for people who cannot make it back to their
apartment.

Shelter**. Follow that marker instead of continuing to search for an apartment
entrance you do not remember.

Entering the shelter lets you sleep and continue into the next day.


<h2
  id="stuck-outside"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What to Do if You Are Stuck Outside After 2:00 AM
</h2>

Once curfew has started, stop treating the night like normal exploration.

Use this route:

1. stop the activity that kept you outside;
2. find the current shelter objective or nearby shelter marker;
3. move around camera sightlines and patrolling drones;
4. enter the shelter and sleep.

Fast Travel is not something to rely on after curfew begins. During one
late-night fishing run away from Lowtown, the map could no longer be used to
simply fast travel home, so finding a shelter in the current district became
the solution.

That matters most during activities that make the clock easy to ignore.
Fishing, boat exploration, and long quest chains can all keep you occupied until
2:00 arrives.

The
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">fishing route</Link>
covers the fishing system itself; for curfew purposes, the important part is to
dock early enough to know where you are sleeping.


<h2
  id="cameras-and-drones"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Avoid CorpSec Cameras and Drones on the Way
</h2>

Curfew turns **security cameras and drones** into route hazards.

Cameras watch fixed areas, while drones can move through the streets and change
when a path is safe to cross. The first nighttime route gives you enough room
to move around both, so reaching a shelter does not require getting detected.

Use corners and nearby structures to stay outside obvious camera coverage, and
wait for moving surveillance to pass before crossing an exposed route. A direct
line toward the shelter marker is not always the safest one.


<h2
  id="detected"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens if CorpSec Detects You?
</h2>

Being spotted does not immediately end the night.

After a camera detects you, the surveillance message reports the behavior and
states that the bureau will keep an eye on you for the rest of the night.

Keep moving toward home or shelter rather than staying outside to test the
surveillance system further.

CorpSec also warns that non-compliance can lead to penalties. In one
next-morning report, **50 Lims** were deducted for trespassing on private
property.

That example should not be treated as a universal fixed fee for being seen by a
camera. The report specifically named the trespassing offense, so the safer
conclusion is that illegal nighttime activity can create financial penalties
depending on what you did.


<h2
  id="night-activities"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Staying Out After Curfew Can Still Be Intentional
</h2>

Not every nighttime trip is an accident.

Ava makes it clear that some people deliberately operate after curfew, and
later activities can give you reasons to remain outside once the streets are
restricted.

The difference is preparation. A planned nighttime trip has a known objective,
a nearby shelter or return route, and enough awareness of camera and drone
positions to move deliberately. An accidental late trip should usually end with
the nearest safe place rather than another optional objective.

There is also an achievement connected to CorpSec detection during curfew. The
exact completion requirement is listed in the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/achievements">achievement tracker</Link>.


<h2
  id="first-curfew"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The First Curfew Is Easier if You Use the Shelter Objective
</h2>

The first night can be confusing because Lowtown is still unfamiliar and the
apartment entrance is easy to lose among similar buildings.

When that happens, the shelter objective is more useful than trying to
reconstruct the route home while surveillance is already active. Follow the
marker, avoid the obvious camera and drone paths, sleep, and relearn the area
during daylight.

The
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/beginner-guide">opening route</Link>
covers the restaurant setup leading into this first night. Once curfew begins,
the immediate priority is simply getting indoors safely.


<h2
  id="curfew-priority"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Decide Where the Night Ends Before 2:00 AM
</h2>

Curfew becomes much easier once that decision is made before the deadline.

Return to your apartment when it is nearby. Use a shelter when you are far from
home or normal travel is no longer convenient. If you deliberately remain
outside, know where the next safe destination is before moving deeper into the
district.

When 2:00 AM is getting close, decide between home and the nearest shelter
before starting another long trip, quest step, or offshore activity.

If curfew has already begun, stop trying to finish one more objective and take
the safest available route indoors.
