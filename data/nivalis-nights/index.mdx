Nivalis Nights gives you a restaurant before it gives you much freedom to run it. During the opening, Banor needs ingredients, customers begin arriving, and your money has to cover the next day of service. At the same time, the city is full of people to meet, businesses to discover, and districts you have not yet visited.

The first major change comes when Ramen Noir can operate for a while without you standing inside it. You do not need to make the restaurant perfect or reach Level 3 before leaving Meridian Market. You need enough food, working staff, and cash to avoid having every trip interrupted by another emergency.

From there, progress depends on what you want to accomplish. Fishing opens a different way to earn money and explore the waterways. Farming gives you control over some of your ingredient supply. Higher venue levels unlock more restaurant options, while relationships, quests, and Achievement Points gradually make the rest of Nivalis accessible.


<h2 id="what-to-do-first" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Start in Meridian Market and Get Ramen Noir Running
</h2>

The opening journey takes you from your apartment in **Lowtown** to **Meridian Market** by HOVA taxi. Once you take over Ramen Noir, the immediate tasks are to arrange its starting furniture, select Chicken Noodle Soup, and obtain the ingredients Banor needs to keep cooking.

The first shopping trip is particularly important. Ramen Noir continues using ingredients while you visit the market, so the quantities on your shopping list can increase before you return. Purchased food must also be deposited in the correct restaurant storage before the kitchen can use it.

Work through those tasks, speak to the nearby restaurant owners for the first additional recipes, and assign your new waiter when the staffing objective appears. You can then begin making short trips away from Meridian Market without leaving Banor to handle everything alone.

If you are still setting up the first service, follow the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/beginner-guide">Day 1 and Day 2 walkthrough</Link> from Lowtown through the initial restock, hiring, and first night.


<h2 id="progression-systems" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Use the Journal to Keep Track of Your Progress
</h2>

The journal becomes increasingly useful once several activities are available at the same time. Restaurant objectives sit alongside conversations, new locations, recipes, properties, fish, and other discoveries.

It also tracks individual skills such as **Trading, Cooking, Farming, Fishing, Boat, Business, and Serving**. Progress in one activity does not mean you have completed everything needed in another.

Check the journal before setting out for a distant district. A conversation you have already started may lead to someone in the same area as another active objective, saving you an unnecessary return trip.

It also helps distinguish tasks that need a particular action from activities you can continue at your own pace. A request to harvest potatoes needs a completed potato crop, for example; repeatedly growing onions will not satisfy it just because both activities increase your Farming progress.

You can continue several skills alongside the story without concentrating on one until it reaches its maximum level. What matters most is whether your current task needs a specific person, location, item, or unlock.


<h2 id="business" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What If Ramen Noir Is Busy but Still Losing Money?
</h2>

Ramen Noir can serve plenty of customers and receive good reviews while spending more than it earns. Ingredients, wages, equipment, and debt payments all affect how much money remains after a service day.

When the venue balance is negative, adding more tables or employees is not necessarily the solution. The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir business walkthrough</Link> explains how to use Local Preferences, Menu Orders, prices, and daily financial results to decide what to change.

There is a different problem when the restaurant makes money but still demands too much of your time.

At **Venue Level 3**, Managers become available. They can take over routine ingredient ordering, although purchases still cost money and supplies take time to arrive. If you have reached that level but keep returning to shop or investigate empty storage, check the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/manager">Manager hiring and restocking instructions</Link>.

Until that unlock appears, you can still explore Nivalis. Just plan shorter trips around your restaurant's remaining ingredients and working hours.


<h2 id="where-to-go-next" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Should You Visit the Docks, Start Farming, or Stay With Ramen Noir?
</h2>

The Docks are a natural next destination once you can leave Meridian Market for a while.

**Boardwalk Addy** gives you a free fishing rod, so you can begin catching fish without purchasing equipment. **Salt Pete** introduces the Putter, which eventually lets you reach fishing areas and waterways that are inaccessible from the shore.

The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">fishing and boat walkthrough</Link> covers both starting points, along with the Fish Database and scanner signals used to locate catches.

Salt Pete also has a treasure hunt involving three missing android parts. It takes you through waters near the Oil Rig, Calypso Island, and the Sewers, with further conversations required between recoveries. Once that quest is active, follow the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/seafaring-treasures">Seafaring Treasures walkthrough</Link> rather than treating every unusual scanner signal as one of the missing parts.

Farming offers a different reason to visit the Docks. When your restaurant progression directs you toward growing vegetables, speak with **Clen** and use Thaddius's old greenhouse. The starter equipment already supports onions, and the next farming objective introduces potatoes. The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/farming-guide">farming walkthrough</Link> explains the crops, modules, greenhouse controls, and later expansion options.

You do not need to complete both activities immediately. If restaurant ingredients remain affordable but you want to explore the city, fishing and boat travel make sense. If repeated vegetable purchases are consuming your earnings, growing food may be more useful.

And when Ramen Noir cannot afford another normal service day, correct that problem before renting new properties or buying expensive equipment elsewhere.


<h2 id="achievement-points" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Why Is a District Locked Even Though You Can See It?
</h2>

Some districts require **Achievement Points** before you can travel to them. Their markers can appear on the map before you have earned the necessary total.

For example, **Helix Monumental** can display a requirement of **55 Achievement Points**. With only **53 points**, the district remains inaccessible. Once the missing points are earned, the location unlocks.

That distinction saves unnecessary travelling. If the destination itself is locked, finding another station entrance will not remove the requirement.

Achievement Points come from progress across Nivalis, including tracked discoveries and accomplishments. Meeting vendors and earning achievements can contribute, while the journal records many other activities such as discovering locations and apartments, fishing, farming, and expanding businesses.

If you are only a few points short, inspect your unfinished activities before taking on a long quest. You may have a nearby discovery or achievable objective that advances the total without requiring a new property or expensive upgrade.

The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/achievements">120-achievement checklist</Link> is useful when you want to see which completion objectives you have already finished and which ones remain.

A locked district can also interrupt another active task. If an objective sends you toward Calypso Island before you can enter the area, deal with the access requirement first rather than repeatedly searching for the destination from a different direction.


<h2 id="map-and-travel" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Travel Between Districts in Nivalis Nights
</h2>

The city map helps you understand where districts sit relative to one another, but it does not show every useful street-level detail.

Once you arrive somewhere, follow the local HUD and active objective markers to reach people, vendors, and nearby destinations.

The main ways to travel include **HOVA taxis, trains, and boats**. You will use different connections as more of Nivalis opens up.

Before making a long journey, determine whether your destination has been unlocked and which type of transport actually reaches it. Those are separate questions.

**Helix Monumental** provides a useful example. After you earn the required Achievement Points and receive the location unlock, travel to **Metro Hub** to find the train connection. The unlock message does not itself transport you to the district.

Boat travel introduces another distinction: a navigable water passage is not necessarily the same as a fast-travel connection. During Salt Pete's treasure hunt, for instance, reaching the correct Sewers water exit matters when searching beyond the main tunnel.

If the objective marker seems to point somewhere you cannot reach, check the district's access status and the transport connection before spending more time searching the surrounding streets.

Not every exchange requires choosing the most agreeable response. However, consider who you are speaking to and what you need from them before deliberately making the conversation hostile.

An NPC may be connected to more than the task currently displayed in your journal. Keeping track of those relationships can help you recognize familiar characters when another objective brings you back to them.


<h2 id="curfew" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What Changes at 2:00 AM?
</h2>

The city's **2:00 AM curfew** affects travel when you are still outside late at night.

This matters most during longer trips. It is easy to spend the evening following conversations or scanner signals, then discover that you are far from somewhere safe as the deadline approaches.

Before travelling to another district late at night, consider whether you can complete the objective and reach shelter in time. A short errand near your current location is different from starting a long trip that involves several transport connections.

If curfew begins while you are still outside, shelter locations become important. CorpSec surveillance can make continuing through the streets dangerous.

The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/curfew">curfew and shelter walkthrough</Link> covers the deadline, cameras, drones, and what to do when you cannot get home normally.


<h2 id="apartments-and-properties" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Should You Rent Every Apartment or Greenhouse You Discover?
</h2>

You can discover **apartments, restaurant venues, and greenhouses** while exploring Nivalis. Some properties can be rented when found, while others require additional progression or permits.

The important distinction is that discovering a property and paying to use it are not the same action.

A property that appears on the map may count toward exploration progress without requiring you to take on its daily rent. Several apartment achievements involve discovering locations rather than renting everything you encounter.

Another greenhouse is useful when the first one cannot support the crops you need or when separating production would solve a growing-condition problem. A second restaurant brings additional expenses for staff, equipment, and ingredients before it can generate reliable income.

Do not treat an available lease as an upgrade you are expected to purchase immediately. Inspect the property, note its location and equipment, and return when it provides something your current businesses cannot.

This approach also preserves cash for essential purchases and prevents newly discovered properties from becoming another daily expense before you are ready to use them.


<h2 id="side-jobs" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Combine Side Jobs That Take You to the Same District
</h2>

As you meet more people, the journal begins filling with objectives in different parts of the city. Completing them strictly in the order they appeared can send you back and forth between districts without accomplishing much during each journey.

A better approach is to check the destination before departing.

When several active tasks lead to the **Docks**, handle their conversations and nearby locations during the same visit. If a quest takes you toward Mountain Town or the Oil Rig, inspect whether another objective can be advanced there before returning to Meridian Market.

Some tasks also depend on earlier access or conversations. If you cannot enter the required district yet, leave that objective for later rather than repeatedly attempting the same journey.

You can use quieter periods at Ramen Noir for these trips, returning when the restaurant needs attention or when another objective brings you near Meridian Market.

That way, the time spent travelling contributes to several activities instead of being consumed by a single delivery or conversation.


<h2 id="achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Make Progress Toward All 120 Achievements
</h2>

Nivalis Nights has **120 achievements** covering activities throughout the city, including business management, fishing, farming, exploration, properties, dining, and character objectives.

Many of those activities overlap with ordinary progression. Catching unfamiliar fish expands your Fish Database, repeated harvests improve Farming, and discovering locations gives you more places to visit.

You do not have to pursue every achievement as soon as its activity becomes available.

When you want to work toward full completion, the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/achievements">achievement tracker</Link> lets you mark completed requirements and see what remains. It is especially useful once several districts and systems have been unlocked and you want to concentrate on unfinished objectives rather than repeat activities you have already completed.


<h2 id="when-stuck" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Find Your Next Objective When Progress Stops
</h2>

When you are unsure where to go, open the journal and identify what the active objective is actually asking you to complete. A task may require an item to be delivered, a particular person to be visited, a crop to be harvested, or access to a district you have not yet unlocked.

The next action depends on what is missing:

- **An item was purchased, but the restaurant still needs it:** Check the current shopping quantity and whether the food reached the correct storage.
- **A destination is visible but inaccessible:** Compare its Achievement Point requirement with your total. If it is already unlocked, check the taxi, train, or boat connection.
- **A character's quest is not advancing:** Look for the next conversation or objective in the journal before repeating the previous action.
- **You cannot afford the next purchase:** Keep existing business operations funded and postpone equipment or property expansion that is not immediately needed.
- **Several objectives are active:** Choose one district and complete the accessible tasks there before starting another long journey.

You do not need to finish every activity before moving to a different part of Nivalis. A restaurant can continue earning money while you explore, crops can grow while you meet people, and discoveries made during ordinary trips can contribute to later completion.

Choose the objective you can advance with your current access, equipment, and cash. When that changes, return to the journal and follow the next available opportunity.
