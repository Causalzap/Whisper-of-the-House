The opening has a clear route even though Nivalis starts putting vendors,
quests, relationships, properties, and other systems on the screen almost
immediately.

For the first day, keep the focus narrow:

**reach Ramen Noir, get the restaurant serving consistently, and finish the
objectives that teach you how food, staff, and restocking work.**

Once that loop is running, leaving Meridian Market becomes much less likely to
end with an empty kitchen and no money to fix it.


<h2
  id="go-to-meridian-market"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Take the Taxi From Lowtown to Meridian Market
</h2>

You begin in your apartment in **Lowtown** with a meeting waiting in Meridian
Market.

Leave the apartment, follow the navigation marker to the HOVA taxi, and select
**Meridian Market**.

The map already shows more of Nivalis than you need at this stage. Ignore the
temptation to investigate every visible district or marker before reaching the
restaurant.

Once you arrive in Meridian Market, follow the local objective marker to
**Ramen Noir** and complete the handover.


<h2
  id="set-up-ramen-noir"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Set Up Ramen Noir Before Spending Your Starting Cash
</h2>

The restaurant does not need much before the first service.

Take the furniture from storage and complete the initial setup:

1. place the **2 tables**;
2. place the **4 chairs**;
3. open the restaurant monitor;
4. put **Chicken Noodle Soup** on the menu.

At Level 1, Ramen Noir has **2 tables, 4 available seats, and 2 menu slots**.

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">

Chicken Noodle Soup uses:

- **Ramen Noodles**
- **Chicken**
- **Onions**

The starting stock is enough to begin serving, but not enough to keep the
restaurant running for long. Banor soon tells you that only a couple of meals
remain, which starts the first grocery run.

Keep the starting cash for that objective. Extra furniture and optional
equipment can wait until the restaurant has food to sell.


<h2
  id="first-shopping-list"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Check the Live Shopping List While You Are Buying Ingredients
</h2>

The first restock is one of the easiest opening objectives to misread because
the required quantities are not frozen when you leave Ramen Noir.

Customers can keep consuming ingredients while you are walking around Meridian
Market.

One opening run began around **5 onions, 5 chicken, and 5 ramen noodles**.
During active service, the requirement later showed **7 onions, 7 ramen
noodles, and 8 chicken**.

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The shopping list can increase while Ramen Noir is serving customers, so check the current amount again before leaving the market.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

If the objective does not clear after buying the amount you remembered, open
the shopping list again rather than assuming the quest is stuck.

The nearby Meridian Market vendors carry what you need for this first run. You
do not need to travel to another district.

Bring the ingredients back and place them in the correct storage:

- **Chicken → fridge**
- **Onions → cupboard**
- **Ramen Noodles → cupboard**

Perishable food can decay, so buying a huge surplus this early is not
automatically useful. With only four seats, the safer goal is enough stock to
keep the current service moving while preserving cash for the next required
purchase.


<h2
  id="get-first-recipes"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Talk to the Restaurant Owners for Your First New Recipes
</h2>

After the first grocery run, Ramen Noir's next weakness is menu variety.

You are sent around Meridian Market to speak with:

- **Marius Mitchell**
- **Nola Berg**
- **Dagmar Fitz**

These conversations also introduce the relationship system, so dialogue choices
are doing more than moving a tutorial objective forward.

Early rewards can include options such as **Beer, Onion Rings, and Tonkotsu
Ramen**.

For the opening route, getting access to those recipes is enough. You do not
need to immediately build every appliance associated with them.

Recipe profitability, equipment spending, pricing, and deciding how large the
active menu should become belong to the longer-term
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir business decisions</Link>.


<h2
  id="hire-first-worker"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Hire Your First Waiter When Service Starts Falling Behind
</h2>

Banor covers several jobs at the beginning, but customer service eventually
starts demanding more attention than one worker can comfortably provide.

When the staffing objective appears, use the restaurant's staff screen to hire
another employee.

After hiring them, assign the role rather than assuming a new worker
automatically starts serving customers.

For this opening step, **Waiter** is the important role. The goal is to take
pressure off Banor while meals continue moving through the kitchen.

Workers have skills, schedules, wages, and happiness, but there is no need to
turn the first hire into a full staffing overhaul. One useful employee solving
the current service bottleneck is enough to continue the opening progression.

Later venue levels allow a much larger operation. Staff composition, wages,
profitability, and expansion are covered in the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">business guide</Link>.


<h2
  id="first-day-cash"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Keep Enough Cash to Open Again Tomorrow
</h2>

The first day introduces far more things to buy than Ramen Noir can sensibly
support at once.

Furniture, appliances, additional food, more employees, and other upgrades all
draw from the same balance used to keep the restaurant serving.

A simple opening priority works well:

**required ingredients → required worker → required equipment → optional
purchases**

The first daily report also introduces Ramen Noir's inherited debt. That is a
long-term business obligation rather than something the opening route needs to
solve immediately.

Avoid leaving yourself without enough money to restock the next morning. The
exact debt choices, operating reserve, and repayment tradeoff are covered in
the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">business guide</Link>.


<h2
  id="first-curfew"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Watch the Clock on the First Night
</h2>

Ramen Noir's initial working hours run late enough that the city's nighttime
rules can become relevant before you are familiar with the route home.

When the curfew countdown is close, finish the current opening objective instead
of starting a long trip to another district.

If **2:00 AM** arrives before you are safely indoors, use the shelter objective
rather than wandering around trying to reconstruct the route to your apartment.

The
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/curfew">curfew guide</Link>
covers shelters, CorpSec cameras, drones, detection, and what to do when normal
travel is no longer convenient.


<h2
  id="day-two"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Check Ramen Noir Before Leaving Meridian Market on Day 2
</h2>

The second morning is when the city starts pulling your attention in several
directions.

Before committing to a longer trip, check the restaurant once:

- Is the active menu stocked?
- Is the waiter actually assigned and working?
- Is there enough cash left for another necessary purchase?

The shopping list can keep changing while customers are served, so leaving the
restaurant with almost no food can turn a simple side trip into another
emergency grocery run.

Once Ramen Noir can continue serving without you standing beside it, the
opening has done its job.


<h2
  id="opening-breakpoint"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  When Is the Opening Phase Finished?
</h2>

There is no need to wait until Ramen Noir is fully automated.

A useful opening breakpoint is when:

- Chicken Noodle Soup has been serving consistently;
- the first restock is complete;
- you have collected the early recipe options;
- at least one additional worker is helping service;
- the restaurant has enough stock and cash to survive while you leave Meridian
  Market.

At that point, venue progression can continue in the background while you
start spending time elsewhere.

If the restaurant itself is still the priority, continue improving
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir's staff, menu, pricing, and daily balance</Link>.

If you are ready to leave the market, the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">Docks fishing route</Link>
is one of the first substantial systems you can start exploring.
