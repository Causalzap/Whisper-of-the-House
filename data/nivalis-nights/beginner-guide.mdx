Nivalis Nights opens with an entire city waiting to be explored, but your first responsibility is much smaller: a noodle restaurant in Meridian Market that barely has enough ingredients to finish its opening service.

You can start conversations, visit vendors, and investigate other districts almost immediately. I would leave most of that until Ramen Noir has completed its first grocery run. The restaurant continues serving customers while you are away, which means a shopping list can change before you return and a small stock shortage can become a much larger interruption.

The first two days are easier when you follow the restaurant's objectives in order. Once the kitchen has food, a waiter is helping Banor, and there is enough money for the next restock, you can begin exploring without having to return after every short trip.


<h2 id="go-to-meridian-market" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Get From Lowtown to Ramen Noir
</h2>

You begin in your apartment in **Lowtown**, with a meeting waiting in **Meridian Market**.

Leave the apartment and follow the navigation marker toward the **HOVA taxi**. Interact with the taxi and select Meridian Market as your destination.

The map shows several other districts, but there is no advantage to making a lengthy detour before meeting the people involved in the restaurant handover.

After arriving, follow the nearby objective marker to **Ramen Noir**. Complete the conversation that puts you in charge of the restaurant, then enter and inspect the space.

You will soon meet **Banor**, an employee who remains at Ramen Noir after the change of ownership. He is important during the opening because he introduces the kitchen's immediate needs and helps you begin serving customers.

The restaurant is not ready for a full service yet. Your next task is to arrange the furniture and choose something the kitchen can cook.


<h2 id="set-up-ramen-noir" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Set Up the Tables, Chairs, and First Menu Item
</h2>

Ramen Noir begins with a small dining area and equipment already available for its opening service.

Take the starting furniture from storage and arrange it in the restaurant:

1. Place the **two tables** in the dining area.
2. Set out the **four chairs** so customers have somewhere to sit.
3. Open the restaurant monitor and inspect the available menu slots.
4. Add **Chicken Noodle Soup** to the active menu.

At **Venue Level 1**, Ramen Noir supports two tables, four seats, and two menu items. The early venue objective is to **Serve 10 Meals**.

Chicken Noodle Soup is the opening dish. Its ingredients are **Ramen Noodles, Chicken, and Onions**, all of which are available from vendors in Meridian Market.

Banor begins working once the restaurant is set up, but he quickly points out that the kitchen only has enough ingredients for a couple more meals.

This is your first reason to leave the restaurant: not to explore the city, but to replenish the food Banor needs to continue cooking.

Keep the money you have rather than buying more decorations or kitchen equipment. The first grocery objective introduces how purchasing and restaurant storage work, and you will need that knowledge before expanding the menu.


<h2 id="first-shopping-list" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Complete the First Shopping List Without Getting Stuck
</h2>

Banor provides a shopping list for the ingredients Ramen Noir is running out of. You can purchase everything needed for this opening objective from vendors around **Meridian Market**.

The confusing part is that the list is not necessarily fixed.

Customers continue consuming ingredients while you are shopping, so the required quantities can rise during the same trip. One opening session began with a requirement of roughly **five onions, five chicken, and five ramen noodles**. After service continued, the shopping list showed **seven onions, seven ramen noodles, and eight chicken**.

Use the shopping list that is currently displayed rather than relying on the quantities you remember from leaving the kitchen.

The opening purchases are straightforward:

- **Onions:** buy them from a produce vendor.
- **Chicken:** visit the meat vendor.
- **Ramen Noodles:** purchase them from the appropriate food vendor in Meridian Market.

Vendor prices can change, and relationships with sellers can influence deals. You do not need to optimize those differences during the first restock. The priority is getting the required ingredients back to Ramen Noir before its service stalls.

Once you have purchased the food, return to the restaurant and place everything in the proper storage.

- **Chicken → Fridge**
- **Onions → Cupboard**
- **Ramen Noodles → Cupboard**

Buying the ingredients is not the same as making them available to the kitchen. If Banor still cannot prepare the dish after you return, inspect the storage and confirm that the purchased items were deposited.

If the shopping objective remains incomplete, open the list again. A changed quantity is a more likely explanation than a broken quest when the restaurant has continued serving customers.

There is also no reason to buy a huge reserve at this stage. Some ingredients deteriorate with time, and four seats cannot consume an unlimited amount of food. Purchase enough to restore service without exhausting the money needed for the rest of the opening.


<h2 id="get-first-recipes" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Where to Get Beer, Onion Rings, and Tonkotsu Ramen
</h2>

After the initial grocery run, the restaurant progression introduces other business owners in Meridian Market.

You are directed to speak with:

- **Marius Mitchell**
- **Nola Berg**
- **Dagmar Fitz**

Follow the objective markers to find them and complete their conversations. These interactions introduce more of the city's relationships while giving you access to additional food and drink options.

Early recipes include **Beer, Onion Rings, and Tonkotsu Ramen**.

You do not need to activate every newly unlocked dish immediately. Chicken Noodle Soup is already enough to keep the first service moving, and expanding the menu changes which ingredients the kitchen needs.

For example, adding Beer can introduce **Wheat** to the shopping list. That creates another ingredient to purchase and keep available even though you have only just completed the original restock.

The important thing during this stage is to finish the conversations and collect the available recipes. You can decide which ones to serve after checking your kitchen equipment and available cash.

There is also no need to visit every restaurant owner in the city at once. Follow the current Meridian Market objectives, then return to Ramen Noir when the next restaurant task becomes available.

Once the venue is established, the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir business walkthrough</Link> explains how to compare menu sales, local customer preferences, ingredient costs, and prices before making larger changes.


<h2 id="hire-first-worker" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Hire Your First Waiter and Help Banor
</h2>

Banor takes on several responsibilities during the opening service. That works while only a few customers are ordering, but service becomes harder to maintain as more people arrive.

When the hiring objective appears, open the restaurant's staff controls and select an available worker.

**Hiring someone and assigning their role are separate steps.**

For this stage, assign the new employee as a **Waiter**. The additional serving coverage gives Banor more time to handle the kitchen instead of constantly switching between different duties.

Pay attention to the employee's shift after making the assignment. Someone who is not scheduled during the restaurant's active hours will not provide the assistance you expected.

You can inspect each candidate's skills, wages, and working preferences, but the opening objective does not require a large team. One additional waiter is enough to begin learning how staff assignments affect service.

Watch a few customer orders after the new employee starts working. Food should continue being prepared while the waiter helps move service forward.

If customers are still waiting, look at where the delay occurs. An unfinished meal is not the same problem as food that has been prepared but has not reached the table.

You will have more opportunities to improve staffing as Ramen Noir gains levels. For now, avoid hiring several additional employees when the opening cash reserve is still small.


<h2 id="first-day-cash" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How Much Money Should You Keep After the First Day?
</h2>

Your first operating day introduces several expenses before Ramen Noir has demonstrated that it can reliably earn enough to pay for them.

Ingredients and the first required employee have an immediate purpose. Extra furniture, optional kitchen appliances, and additional workers can wait until the restaurant needs them.

There is no universal minimum cash balance for the opening because your remaining money depends on what you purchased and how much food the restaurant consumed.

Instead, judge the balance against the next service period. You still need enough money for another grocery run and the wages associated with your employees.

The end-of-day report introduces **Ramen Noir's inherited debt** as another financial responsibility. You do not need to clear that debt before continuing the opening storyline, and committing too much cash to repayment can leave the kitchen without enough money to reopen comfortably.

When the daily report appears, look beyond the customer count. A restaurant can serve plenty of meals while its costs exceed the revenue earned from them.

Your immediate objective is to keep the current restaurant operating. The detailed repayment choices and daily-profit decisions are covered in the <Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir profit and debt walkthrough</Link>.

If the first day has been expensive, resist making another optional purchase before checking the restaurant's ingredient stock the next morning.


<h2 id="first-curfew" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What to Do When Curfew Approaches on the First Night
</h2>

The opening service can keep you busy long enough for Nivalis's nighttime restrictions to become relevant.

The city has a **2:00 AM curfew**, and you may still be travelling around Meridian Market when it approaches. This is especially easy to overlook if you spend the evening shopping, speaking to restaurant owners, or arranging employees.

Watch the clock while completing the first-day objectives. When curfew is close, avoid starting a journey to an unfamiliar district just to investigate another marker.

If you cannot reach somewhere safe before the restriction begins, follow the available shelter objective rather than continuing to wander through the streets.

The nighttime rules involve CorpSec surveillance and the risks of remaining outside. The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/curfew">curfew and shelter walkthrough</Link> covers how to handle that situation when you cannot simply finish your journey before 2:00 AM.

Once the restaurant's immediate work is finished, end the night somewhere safe and return to its remaining objectives the following morning.


<h2 id="day-two" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What to Check at Ramen Noir on Day 2
</h2>

On the second morning, Nivalis begins presenting more opportunities to explore. You may have new conversations waiting, another ingredient to purchase, or a reason to visit the Docks.

Before leaving Meridian Market, go back to Ramen Noir and see what actually happened during the previous service.

The active menu is the first thing to inspect. Chicken Noodle Soup still needs noodles, chicken, and onions, while any additional recipes you activated may require different supplies. The shopping list will show what the kitchen currently lacks.

Then check that your newly hired waiter is assigned and scheduled. Having another name on the staff list is not enough if nobody is covering the role during the hours customers arrive.

Finally, look at the available balance. Buying enough ingredients for the next service is more important than purchasing an optional upgrade immediately.

One mistake is assuming that the restaurant will now take care of its own grocery shopping. **Your first waiter does not automate ingredient purchasing.** The Manager role becomes available much later, at Venue Level 3.

Until then, you still need to pay attention to the shopping list and make restock trips when necessary.

You do not have to remain inside Ramen Noir all day. The restaurant can continue serving customers while you are elsewhere, provided the kitchen has supplies and the employees needed to do their jobs.

That makes the second morning a useful time to decide how far you can travel before needing to return.


<h2 id="opening-breakpoint" className="scroll-mt-24">
  When Should You Leave Meridian Market and Explore Nivalis?
</h2>

You do not need a high-level restaurant or a fully automated kitchen before visiting the rest of the city.

Once the first grocery objective is finished, the early recipe conversations are complete, and a waiter is helping Banor, most of the opening restaurant controls have been introduced.

The remaining question is whether Ramen Noir has enough supplies and money to handle a period without your attention.

There are two useful directions to take from here.

**Stay with Ramen Noir** when customer service is regularly falling behind, the ingredient list is constantly growing, or the daily balance is worsening. Continue serving customers and work through the venue progression while correcting the problem that is limiting the restaurant. The next important decisions involve staffing, recipe selection, pricing, and eventually automatic restocking.

**Visit the Docks** when Ramen Noir is serving normally and you have enough supplies for the immediate future. You can meet Boardwalk Addy to obtain a free fishing rod, start catching fish, and begin exploring the waterways once you have access to a boat. The <Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">fishing and boat walkthrough</Link> covers that progression.

There is no need to abandon restaurant advancement when you leave Meridian Market. Return periodically to check your supplies, complete the next service objective, and collect any available upgrades.

Nivalis opens up gradually through these activities. Getting Ramen Noir through its first two days gives you a dependable place to return to while the city's other opportunities begin taking shape.
