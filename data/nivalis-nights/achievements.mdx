system. Some arrive naturally during the opening, while others require broad
city exploration or finishing a long category such as fishing, farming,
restaurants, apartments, collectibles, and character stories.

The tracker is the complete list. The sections below focus on requirements that
are easy to misread or that benefit from being planned alongside normal
progression.

## Business Achievements With Easy-to-Confuse Requirements

Several of the **17 business achievements** look similar in the list but count
different things.

**500 Daily Revenue** requires **500 Lims of revenue during one day across your
venues**. **1000 Revenue** is cumulative and does not need to be earned in one
service period.

Reviews use the same distinction. **First 4* Review** and **First 5* Review**
only require one review at the named rating, while **4* Average Reviews**
requires one venue to hold at least a **4-star average after 100 reviews**.

The two debt achievements are also unrelated. **Debt Free** refers to the
inherited Ramen Noir debt; **Paid Back a Loan** requires repaying a loan shark
with interest.

Venue ownership has another specific gate: **Own 2 Venues** requires a permit
before the second venue can be leased. **Own 5 Venues** continues the same
ownership progression later.

Customer milestones count across all venues, so the **100** and **500**
requirements can advance while you are working on the restaurant normally. For
staff, pricing, debt, venue profitability, and expansion decisions, use the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/business-guide">Ramen Noir business guide</Link>.

</section>

## Fishing Has 24 Separate Species Achievements

Fishing is the largest single achievement category with **24 species**.

Each requirement is direct: find and catch the named fish. The tracker above
already contains the complete species list, so the useful distinction is not
the wording of the achievement but whether the fish has actually been caught.

Burbot, Pike, and Stickleback can appear relatively early once fishing opens.
Other species become easier to investigate as the Fish Database and broader
water access develop.

Catch unfamiliar species whenever they appear instead of forcing the entire
24-fish category at once. The
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/fishing-guide">fishing guide</Link>
covers the rod, boat, Fish Detector, Fish Database, scanner, and early fishing
route.

</section>

## Farming Achievements Count Different Crop Types

The five farming achievements are based on variety rather than repeatedly
harvesting one profitable crop.

The progression is:

- **First Harvest** — plant and harvest something
- **Harvester** — harvest **5 unique crops**
- **Farmer** — harvest **10 different crop types**
- **Serial Farmer** — grow **20 different crops** from seed to harvest
- **Farming Specialist** — cultivate all available crop types

Repeatedly growing onions can still improve production, but it does not advance
a requirement that specifically asks for another unique crop.

The
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/farming-guide">farming guide</Link>
covers seeds, modules, crop levels, environmental controls, and greenhouse
expansion; use the tracker here only to see which variety milestones remain.

</section>

## Location Achievements Require 18 District Visits

There are **18 location achievements**, each tied to reaching a named part of
Nivalis.

Lowtown and Meridian Market arrive during the opening, followed by places such
as the Docks as transport and quests take you farther across the city. The
remaining district names are all searchable in the tracker.

These are usually poor candidates for isolated trips early on. Let active
quests and transport progression open the city, then filter the tracker later
to find any district that never registered.

</section>

## Apartment Achievements Ask for Discovery, Not Ownership

The **11 apartment achievements** use the word **discover**.

That matters because finding a property and renting it are separate decisions.
You do not need to create eleven recurring housing costs simply to satisfy
these achievement descriptions.

When a route brings you near an unfamiliar apartment, discover it and let the
tracker record the milestone. Property rental can remain a separate decision
based on whether you actually want the space.

</section>

## Dining Progress Counts Distinct Food Spots

Dining achievements increase through **5, 10, 20, 30, and 50** different food
locations.

Repeated meals at the same restaurant do not replace a distinct-location
requirement. The efficient way to build this category is to eat somewhere new
when another quest, shopping trip, or district objective has already brought
you there.

By the time broader city progression is complete, the tracker can show whether
you need a handful of new restaurants or a much larger dining cleanup.

</section>

## Graffiti and Menu Cards Use Milestones Before the Full Set

Graffiti achievements trigger at the **first find, 5, 10, and all graffiti**.

Collectable menu cards use the **first card, 10 cards, and the complete set**.

The numbered milestones confirm that the collectible type is registering
properly. Once those counters are finished, the remaining requirement becomes
a full-set cleanup rather than another incremental threshold.

Use the tracker to keep the completed milestones out of the way while checking
what is still missing.

</section>

## Postcards Have 10 Individual Achievements

There are **10 postcard achievements**, each tied to one named destination
rather than a cumulative postcard counter.

They cover places including Meridian Market, Docks, Metro Hub, Central Canyon,
Calypso Island, Helix Monumental, Space Port, Skyhigh Gardens, Spire, and Outer
Space.

Because the achievements are destination-specific, collecting a postcard while
another objective already has you in the area can prevent an unnecessary
return trip later.

</section>

## Quest Achievements That Need Extra Attention

The remaining **23 achievements** are tied to character, story, or special
activity objectives. Some are explicit; others reveal only the final condition.

A few requirements are straightforward:

- **Good Game** — play a game of chess
- **A Knight in Nivalis** — win the chess tournament
- **Coffee Time** — complete **3** Coffee PoccoCup delivery runs
- **Home Makeover** — design an apartment for a client
- **Certified Decorator** — stage **5** client apartments
- **Project Grapevine** — spread seeds on the Oil Rig
- **Ma Gomba's Gift** — grow poisonous mushrooms for Ma Gomba

Several hidden entries are less useful as route instructions because the text
only tells you the outcome. **Aseptic**, **Seen a Ghost**, **Fauxter's Band**,
**Find Love**, **Find Salma's Brother**, **Lost Child**, **Mask for Iann**,
**Scorpion**, **Thaddeus Mystery**, **The Lost Captain**, **The Network**, and
**Where is Ava?** all belong to longer character or story progression.

Three hidden conditions are worth distinguishing early:

**Noodle Bar Introduction** is tied to becoming familiar with the Noodle Bar.

**Seafarer** requires becoming a boat captain and exploring the gridless sea
around Nivalis.

**After Curfew** specifically requires being **detected by a CorpSec security
camera**. Merely remaining outdoors after 2:00 AM does not satisfy the stated
condition. For the actual 2:00 AM rules, shelters, cameras, drones, and
detection behavior, use the
<Link href="/nivalis-nights/curfew">curfew guide</Link>.

For hidden story achievements, use the checklist as a record of unfinished
storylines rather than assuming the short requirement contains the complete
route.

</section>

## Use Categories to Plan the Final Cleanup

The categories overlap enough that many achievements can advance during normal
play.

Business milestones continue while Ramen Noir operates. New crops can be
rotated through the greenhouse while useful ingredients stay in production.
Unfamiliar fish can be caught during ordinary fishing trips, while district
visits can also advance dining, apartments, postcards, graffiti, and other
collectibles.

Near the end, filter the tracker to unfinished items instead of sweeping every
system again. The remaining category usually tells you what the next trip
should accomplish: catch a missing species, visit an unexplored district,
finish a collectible set, or return to an unresolved character storyline.
