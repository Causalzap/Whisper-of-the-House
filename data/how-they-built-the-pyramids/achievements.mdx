<section id="quick-answer" className="scroll-mt-24">
  <GuideQuickAnswer title="Complete the Building Milestones, Then Check the Special Conditions">
    How They Built the Pyramids has 18 Steam achievements, including two hidden achievements. Most come from placing bricks, finishing pyramid layers, hiring workers, and using buildings you unlock along the way.

    Keep an eye on the requirements that need a specific action: finish a monument, run 100 conveyor tiles, hold gold chips, trade with the Nile trader, reach 32 RPM on a shaft, and carve a perfect 100-quality engraving. Crowning the pyramid and leaving Earth are also two separate achievements.
  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>

You can earn much of the How They Built the Pyramids achievement list while completing a normal pyramid, but several requirements are easy to mistake for related actions.

Having gold chips collected in a machine is not the same as holding them. Building a monument is not the same as finishing one. A shaft that turns slowly is not enough for Spin Doctor, and a nearly perfect engraving still falls short of Steady Chisel.

The distinction matters most when your construction site is already producing thousands of bricks. Check the smaller requirements as their equipment becomes available instead of trying to remember every side activity after completing the pyramid.

<h2 id="all-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What are all 18 How They Built the Pyramids achievements?
</h2>

The full list contains 18 achievements. Now You Know and Too Much Sun are the two hidden entries; their requirements are revealed below.

The requirements fall into a few practical groups. The building milestones need ordinary pyramid progress. Trading, worker recruitment, and monuments require their respective systems. The more deliberate challenges involve engraving quality, shaft speed, gold-chip handling, and fainting from heat.

<h2 id="pyramid-progress" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Which achievements unlock as you build the pyramid?
</h2>

Six achievements are directly tied to placing bricks or finishing layers:

- **Brick One:** Place the first brick into the pyramid.
- **Foundations:** Complete the first layer.
- **Brick by Brick:** Place 1,000 bricks into the pyramid.
- **Ten Courses Up:** Complete ten layers.
- **Above the Palms:** Complete twenty layers.
- **Capstone:** Crown the finished pyramid.

The important distinction is between producing bricks and placing them. Brick by Brick requires 1,000 bricks to go into the pyramid, not simply 1,000 bricks sitting in molds, crates, or conveyors.

The layer achievements require completed layers rather than reaching a particular height with an unfinished row of bricks below.

You do not need to change your production methods specifically for these milestones. Keep supplying the construction site and watch the layer completion indicator as the pyramid rises.

Capstone happens later, when the completed pyramid is crowned. It does not replace the achievement for leaving Earth, which has another step afterward.

For the wider decisions about first upgrades, construction progress, and reaching the last layer, use the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids">beginner guide</Link>.

<h2 id="touched-by-ra" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you unlock Touched by Ra with a golden brick?
</h2>

Touched by Ra requires you to place a golden brick into the pyramid.

The brick must actually be placed. Finding a golden brick, collecting one, or keeping it in storage does not by itself satisfy the achievement condition.

Golden bricks can appear during brick production, while later gold-processing equipment provides another way to work toward a supply of them.

Once you have one, carry it to an available pyramid position and place it as you would another brick.

Do not confuse Touched by Ra with Gold Fever. Touched by Ra concerns a finished golden brick being placed into the pyramid. Gold Fever concerns gold chips held in your hands.

<h2 id="hired-help-worth-its-weight" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you unlock Hired Help and Worth Its Weight?
</h2>

These two achievements involve the Shabti Market but have different requirements.

**Hired Help** unlocks when you hire your first Shabti. The important action is completing the purchase, not building a Work Post or giving the new worker its first job.

**Worth Its Weight** requires a Gold Shabti in your crew.

Check the Shabti Market for a worker identified as `Gold Shabti`, then purchase it and confirm that it has joined your crew. A Gold Shabti appearing among the market offers is not enough to unlock Worth Its Weight.

For market prices, worker attributes, and traits, see the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids/shabti">Shabti guide</Link>.

<h2 id="blessed-monument" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you complete a monument for Blessed?
</h2>

Blessed requires you to complete a monument.

Placing a monument at the construction site is only the beginning. You must finish the building by supplying its remaining requirements.

The monument's construction progress is the useful check. If it still has unfinished spaces or remaining materials, continue completing it before expecting the achievement.

Completing the Obelisk of Ra displays `OBELISK OF RA COMPLETE`, confirming that the monument is finished. The completion of the monument is the condition that matters for Blessed.

<h2 id="reed-highway" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you get Reed Highway with 100 conveyor tiles?
</h2>

Reed Highway requires 100 running belt tiles in your world. Buying conveyor parts alone will not satisfy it; the tiles need to be placed and operating.

If you need to extend a conveyor network or fix disconnected, blocked, or invalid belt sections, use the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids/automation">automation guide</Link>.

<h2 id="gold-fever" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you hold gold chips for Gold Fever?
</h2>

Gold Fever requires you to hold gold chips in your hands.

Gold chips can be collected through gold-processing activities involving equipment such as the Sifting Tray and Rocker Cradle.

The key action is taking the chips from their collection point rather than leaving them inside the equipment.

For example, a Rocker Cradle can display an interaction such as `Take 85 chips` when gold chips have accumulated in its bowl. Use the available collection interaction so that you actually take the chips.

This is different from making a golden brick. You do not need to place a gold brick into the pyramid for Gold Fever.

If gold chips are visible in a machine but the achievement has not unlocked, first confirm that you have collected them into your hands. Merely having the machine produce them is not the stated requirement.

<h2 id="spin-doctor" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you reach 32 RPM for Spin Doctor?
</h2>

Spin Doctor requires a shaft to reach **32 RPM**. Check its running speed rather than judging the result from how quickly a nearby production machine operates. A shaft turning below the target will not satisfy the achievement.

If the shaft is stationary or cannot reach the required speed, inspect its drive and connections using the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids/automation">power and shaft instructions</Link>.

<h2 id="down-the-nile" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you trade with the Nile trader for Down the Nile?
</h2>

Down the Nile requires you to **seal a trade with the Nile trader**.

First, unlock and build the trader's dock at the riverbank. The dock allows the trader's boat to call and makes trading available to the team.

When the trader arrives, interact with the available trade options. Some offers may require goods or other resources, so check what the trader wants before choosing a deal.

The achievement requires an actual completed transaction.

In one multiplayer session, the players built the dock, encountered the Nile trader, and later completed a deal that triggered Down the Nile.

Do not stop after placing the dock or opening the trader's menu. Neither action alone fulfills the stated condition.

If the trader is present but you cannot complete an offer, check its requirements and return when the necessary goods are available.

<h2 id="scribe-and-steady-chisel" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you unlock Scribe and Steady Chisel?
</h2>

Both achievements use the Scribe's Bench, but they test different things.

- **Scribe:** Complete your first engraving at a Scribe's Bench.
- **Steady Chisel:** Complete an engraving with a perfect 100 quality.

You can work toward both while using the same building, but a finished engraving does not automatically qualify as perfect.

<h3 id="use-scribes-bench" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you carve your first engraving?
</h3>

Use the Scribe's Bench with suitable bricks available in its input tray.

The bench interaction includes `Carve the plain brick`, which starts an engraving rather than sending the brick directly to the pyramid.

<Image

Once you begin carving, the engraving screen displays the pattern, the current `Scribe's hand` percentage, a value multiplier, and the controls for making the engraving.

Use the left mouse button to carve, the mouse wheel for fine chiseling adjustments, and Enter to finish the current engraving.

For Scribe, complete an engraving rather than simply opening the bench and looking at its available patterns.

<h3 id="perfect-100-quality" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you get a perfect 100-quality engraving?
</h3>

Steady Chisel requires a perfect result. A high quality rating is useful, but it is not automatically enough.

The example below shows `Carve the Sun of Ra` at 99%, with a x2.9 value multiplier and the rating `masterful`.

<Image
  src="/images/how-they-built-the-pyramids/pyramids-sun-of-ra-99-percent-carving.webp"
  alt="Sun of Ra engraving at 99 percent Scribe's hand quality with a x2.9 masterful value multiplier"
  width={1206}
  height={954}
  className="my-8 rounded-xl"
/>

That is an excellent result for the value of the brick, but Steady Chisel asks for **100 quality**, not merely a Masterful rating.

A perfect engraving reaches the x3.0 value multiplier. Intermediate results can be lower, as shown by the 99% example, but they should not be treated as a fixed percentage-to-multiplier formula.

For the achievement, concentrate on the carving itself:

1. Start an engraving at the Scribe's Bench.
2. Use the carving controls to work through the selected pattern.
3. Make fine adjustments rather than finishing as soon as the rating becomes Masterful.
4. Reach a perfect 100-quality result and finish the engraving.

If you finish at 99%, you have made a valuable engraving, but you have not met the stated perfect-quality requirement.

Try another engraving rather than assuming that repeating the same pattern at the same quality will trigger the achievement.

<h2 id="too-much-sun" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How do you unlock the hidden Too Much Sun achievement?
</h2>

Too Much Sun is unlocked by **fainting from the desert heat**.

The important event is fainting. Becoming hot, losing comfort, or noticing signs of overheating does not necessarily mean the achievement condition has been completed.

Spend time exposed to the desert heat rather than cooling off in shade or using a water source to recover. Continue until the character actually faints from heat.

In a multiplayer session, a player collapsed from the heat and the Too Much Sun achievement was announced immediately afterward.

If you are only trying to finish this achievement, attempt it when a temporary interruption will not disrupt an important construction task.

Once the character recovers, you can return to the normal building operation.

<h2 id="capstone-and-now-you-know" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What happens after Capstone, and how do you unlock Now You Know?
</h2>

Capstone and Now You Know occur near the end of the game, but they are not the same achievement.

**Capstone** requires you to crown the finished pyramid. Once the final construction requirements are met, the game opens a naming prompt with a `CROWN IT!` button. Choose a name for your pyramid and confirm the crown.

<h2 id="missing-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Why didn't an achievement unlock?
</h2>

Check the final result rather than the equipment used to reach it. A missing achievement usually comes down to one of three differences: the required construction is unfinished, the final interaction has not been completed, or the displayed count, speed, or quality is still below the target.

Compare your current game state with the exact requirement in the achievement table above. If the relevant equipment is already working, focus on the last action needed for the unlock instead of rebuilding the entire setup.

For a specific requirement, return to its section above before changing the rest of your construction site.

<h2 id="achievement-order" className="scroll-mt-24">
  What is a practical order for unlocking all 18 achievements?
</h2>

You do not need to complete the achievements in the order they appear on Steam.

During the early layers, aim to place your first brick, complete the first layer, hire a Shabti, and place a golden brick when one becomes available. A completed monument can also be handled while the pyramid is still relatively small.

As the factory expands, check Reed Highway, Gold Fever, and the opportunities to trade with the Nile trader. Once the appropriate equipment is available, complete an engraving and work toward a perfect-quality result.

Spin Doctor is worth checking when you already have access to the necessary shaft and power equipment. Avoid rebuilding unrelated parts of the factory just to chase the speed target.

Keep the layer milestones progressing alongside those activities. You will earn the 1,000-brick, ten-layer, and twenty-layer achievements through continued construction.

Before crowning the pyramid, compare your remaining achievements with the list above. Anything tied to the existing construction site is easier to examine while the relevant equipment is still available.

After the crown, finish the departure sequence for Now You Know.

For labor and Gold Shabti decisions, use the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids/shabti">Shabti guide</Link>. For powered machinery, conveyors, and processing equipment, use the <Link href="/how-they-built-the-pyramids/automation">automation guide</Link>. Both can help you finish the construction site without turning every achievement into a separate factory project.
