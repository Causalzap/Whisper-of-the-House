<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To finish **Playrooms**, fill all eight ball pits, buy the six area keys as you progress, and walk through the final exit in the Playroom. The main route takes you through the Waiting Area, Backroom, Poolroom, School, Office, and Hotel. You do not need to remove every leftover ball or collect all 20 Sparrows and 14 office items to reach the ending.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The six keys take you from the Playroom into progressively more demanding areas. Watch for the School's running restriction, the Office telephone, and the Hotel's numbered doors; these can interrupt an otherwise straightforward cleanup. You can leave loose balls behind while opening the next area, but you still need to finish the required ball pits before taking the final exit.

<section id="room-order" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Is the Room Order in Playrooms?

A practical progression route uses these six area keys in order:

1. **Waiting Area — 10:** Second door on the right of the Playroom.
2. **Backroom — 25:** First door on the right.
3. **Poolroom — 50:** First door on the left.
4. **School — 60:** Second door on the left.
5. **Office — 90:** Third door on the right.
6. **Hotel — 100:** Third door on the left.

These are the area-key costs, not the amounts you must collect in the ball pits. The door board in the starting area shows the available keys and their prices.

The purchase order does not force you to finish cleaning every room before buying the next key. You can leave some balls behind, earn money somewhere else, and return later.

Use the key board whenever you have enough money to open the next area. Leftover loose balls can be collected later; the required ball pits still have to be completed before the final exit.

## What Should You Do on Your First Day?

Launch `light.exe`, collect balls close to the starting room, and unload them into the first pit. Return before the lights go out. Once you've earned enough to improve your pickups, finish the pit that unlocks Sprint and save toward the Waiting Area key. For how to spend your first earnings, see <Link href="/playrooms">which upgrades and tools solve your current bottleneck</Link>.

## How Do You Unlock Sprint and Crouch?

The opening Playroom contains multiple collection pits. The first important milestone is filling the pit that rewards **Sprint**.

Collect nearby balls, deposit them into that pit, and continue until its required total is reached. The ability becomes available when the pit is completed.

Sprint changes how comfortably you can reach distant rooms and return before dark. It is particularly useful once you begin working beyond the Playroom.

You can keep filling other pits while saving for keys. When deciding where to deposit your next load, check what reward the unfinished pit offers instead of distributing balls randomly among all of them.

Once Sprint is available and you have earned enough money, purchase the Waiting Area key.

## How Do You Clear the Waiting Area?

Purchase the **10-cost Waiting Area key** and enter through the second door on the right.

The Waiting Area has suction equipment that can handle groups of loose balls. Once the **Push Broom** is available in shop.exe, you can buy it for **€15** and use it to move larger piles toward the intake.

### How Do You Move Balls into the Waiting Area Intake?

Start with a nearby group of balls and push it toward the suction point. Pick up isolated balls by hand instead of spending the whole shift trying to sweep them together.

You can move on to the Backroom before this area is completely empty. Return when your equipment makes the remaining cleanup easier.

### Is There a Hidden Area in the Waiting Area?

Yes. A door off the Waiting Area's left hallway leads into the hidden maze. The Whale is optional for the normal ending. If you want to find it, follow the <Link href="/playrooms/green-office-map">Green Office route to the Whale</Link> instead of exploring its side corridors at random.

## What Should You Do After Unlocking the Backroom?

The next area key costs **25** and opens the Backroom through the first door on the right.

The Backroom contains larger quantities of balls, making collection efficiency increasingly important.

The Backroom has long aisles and large groups of balls. Clear one recognizable section at a time so you can tell which areas still need attention.

### How Should You Clear the Backroom's Larger Sections?

If collection equipment is available in shop.exe, place it near the densest group of balls and work outward. Move the equipment as you finish each aisle, then check the edges for isolated balls.

Avoid leaving a handful of balls in every corridor. Clearing sections completely makes the final cleanup easier.

When the Backroom becomes less profitable to work in, you can purchase the Poolroom key and return for the remaining cleanup later.

## How Do You Clear the Balls in the Poolroom?

Buy the **50-cost Poolroom key**, which opens the first door on the left.

The Poolroom changes how you approach collection because many balls are spread across water surfaces.

**Balls floating on the water are not automatically considered stored.** They still need to reach the designated collection system.

The room contains a mixture of areas where sweeping, manual collection, and suction equipment can be useful.

### What Is the Best Way to Collect Balls in Water?

Work through one section of the room before moving to the next.

Use the broom or collection equipment where the balls can be gathered efficiently. When a large group is cleared, inspect the pool edges and corners for smaller groups that were left behind.

Do not assume that a visually empty central pool means the entire area has been cleared. Check the room's remaining completion percentage and look for balls outside the main collection path.

### How Do You Find the Last Balls in the Poolroom?

If the remaining count stops decreasing, check shop.exe for the **€10 Scanner**. Once available, use it inside the Poolroom to mark unstored balls, including those hidden behind walls.

Follow the markers to the missed balls instead of repeatedly searching sections you have already cleared.

The next area key costs **60** and opens the School.

## How Do You Get Through the School Without Being Caught?

The School is one of the biggest changes in Playrooms because it adds an explicit movement restriction.

After buying the **60-cost School key**, the principal's message tells you:

**Running is prohibited in the corridors. The stairs are not counted.**

    The School rules prohibit sprinting in corridors, while stairs are explicitly exempt.
  </figcaption>

That rule is not a suggestion. Sprinting through a School corridor can trigger the principal and cause you to lose your current day's progress on Normal difficulty.

### Where Can You Sprint in the School?

Treat the corridors as walking-only areas.

You can use Sprint on the stairs under the stated rule, but release Sprint when you enter a corridor again. Do not hold the sprint button while turning off a staircase into a hallway.

This is particularly important when returning late in the day. A long route back can tempt you to sprint through the School, but that may end the entire shift.

If you are repeatedly being caught here, first check whether you are still sprinting in a corridor rather than assuming the problem is the regular darkness timer.

### How Do You Clear the School's Remaining Balls?

The School has multiple floors, corridors, lockers, and other areas that can conceal small groups of balls.

Clear the visible balls along each floor before opening the red lockers. Some lockers hold balls that are not visible from the corridor. Recheck classroom corners if the area's remaining count stays above zero, and allow extra time to return from the long staircase.

### Should You Buy the Hand Vacuum Before Finishing School?

The **€50 Hand Vacuum** becomes available after you unlock the School. It helps collect scattered balls around classrooms and lockers; check <Link href="/playrooms">the equipment choices</Link> before spending money you still need for the next key.

## What Changes When You Unlock the Office?

The **Office key costs 90** and opens the third door on the right.

When it activates, you receive a message warning you about the telephone and elevators.

The important instruction is to **answer the telephone promptly when it rings**.

### What Should You Do When the Telephone Rings?

Pay attention to the ringing while cleaning.

If you hear the telephone, locate it and answer it rather than continuing to collect balls without checking the source. The message specifically tells you not to delay answering.

The game also warns you to exercise caution when calling elevators. That warning is worth keeping in mind as you explore, but it does not establish that every elevator interaction is fatal.

### How Do You Clear the Office?

The Office contains desks, furniture, and separate sections where balls may be difficult to see among other objects.

Use the collection equipment for larger groups first, then inspect individual desk areas and corners.

The scanner becomes especially useful here. When the room is nearly empty, sweep the remaining sections systematically rather than searching the same group of desks over and over.

You may also find objects that can be taken back to your starting room. These are separate from the ordinary balls you must store.

If you are collecting office decorations, do not confuse the object's pickup with clearing the remaining balls in that area.

After the Office key, the final main area key opens the Hotel.

## How Do You Reach the Ending in Playrooms?

To reach the ending, complete the required progress for **all eight ball pits**, including the final pit.

Once the final pit is full, return to the Playroom's exit doors and walk through them to finish the game. This unlocks **Clock out**.
