White office walls and green carpeting make the maze's corridors look nearly identical. The challenge is recognizing the correct junctions and finding your way back after discovering the Whale.

## Where Is the Green Office Entrance in Playrooms?

Enter the **Waiting Area** from the Playroom. Take the hallway on the left, then go through the **first door on your right**.

White office walls, green carpeting, and branching corridors indicate that you have entered the maze. Use this entrance as the starting point for the A–D route shown below.

If you have not unlocked the Waiting Area, follow the <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">Playrooms walkthrough</Link>.

</section>

## What Is the Route Through the Green Office Maze?

Follow the highlighted route from the entrance marker to the Whale room. The letters A–D correspond to the four steps below.

1. **A — First T-junction:** follow the corridor out of the entrance room and turn **right** at the first T-shaped split.
2. **B — Second T-junction:** keep to the main corridor, then turn **left** at the next T. If you have taken other branches in between, return to the entrance before continuing.
3. **C — Long corridor:** follow the bend toward the far end, passing side rooms without entering them.
4. **D — Whale room:** take the final corner and enter the larger room containing the Whale. Approach it to unlock **Out of water**.

## Where Is the Whale Inside the Green Office?

On the map, the **Whale** is near the lower-left corner. Enter the larger green-carpeted room and approach it; the **Out of water** Steam notification confirms you found it.

## How Do You Exit the Green Office Maze?

After finding the Whale, look for an **EXIT** sign along the upper edge of a white wall.

The passage may look like an ordinary wall until you approach it. Walk toward the marked section rather than looking for a conventional open doorway.

### Where Is the False Wall Shown on the Map?

The map also marks a false-wall passage near its upper edge, labelled as an exit toward the Office.

That marking is a useful landmark, but do not assume that the top exit on the map and every EXIT sign you encounter lead to exactly the same destination.

If you are already beside a marked EXIT passage, use that visible landmark instead of wandering through the entire maze searching for another exit.

## What Should You Do If You Get Lost in the Green Office?

If you cannot identify the next T-junction, stop applying the A–D turns from an unknown position. Try to return to the entrance room; circling the same wall can lead you through a loop without getting any closer to the Whale.

Do not treat the maze as a safe place to wait through a blackout. On Normal or Hardcore, leave enough time to reach your starting room.
