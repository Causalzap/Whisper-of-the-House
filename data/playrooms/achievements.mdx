Playrooms has **30 Steam achievements**. Use the checklist below to track your progress, complete the eight ball pits and each room's cleanup, then finish the 20 Sparrows, 14 office items, hidden discoveries, and special challenges. Save **Hardcore mode** for a run where you already know the rooms.

Finishing Playrooms does not automatically unlock every achievement. Entering a room, filling its ball pit, and collecting every loose ball inside it are different goals. You can reach the ending while some rooms still need cleaning.

The longest remaining tasks usually involve collecting every hidden object, finishing equipment upgrades, and completing a separate Hardcore run. A single-colour ball pit also needs advance planning: once you have already mixed colours in a pit, collecting only one colour afterward does not undo those deliveries.

## Which Achievements Unlock When You Enter a New Room?

Seven achievements are tied to entering named areas:

- **Clocking in** — Enter the Playroom.
- **Please take a number** — Enter the Waiting Area.
- **Level 0** — Enter the Backrooms.
- **Splash splash** — Enter the Poolroom.
- **Back to School** — Enter the School.
- **Overtime** — Enter the Office.
- **Housekeeping** — Enter the Hotel.

Buying the key is not the same as entering the area. If an entry achievement remains locked, walk through the corresponding door before checking Steam again.

### What Counts for Door Operator?

**Door Operator** requires you to **discover every room**.

That is different from simply buying all the keys. If you have purchased the Hotel key but Door Operator is still locked, enter any rooms you have unlocked but not yet visited.

Check your progress before assuming that cleaning every ball pit is the missing condition. The achievement specifically concerns discovering rooms.

For the room order and key locations, follow the <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">Playrooms walkthrough</Link>.

## Why Didn't Clean Playroom or Another Room-Clear Achievement Unlock?

The seven room-cleaning achievements require collecting **every ball in their respective areas**:

- **Clean Playroom**
- **Clean Waitingroom**
- **Clean Backrooms**
- **Clean Poolroom**
- **Clean School**
- **Clean Office**
- **Clean Hotel**

A completed ball pit does not satisfy a **Clean** achievement by itself. Check the area's remaining-ball count and collect what is still unstored.

For room-specific obstacles and ways to find missed balls, follow the <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">Playrooms walkthrough</Link>.

## How Do You Unlock Hooray, Model Employee, and Clock Out?

These achievements reward ball-pit progress or completion of the main route, but they do not all check the same thing.

### Hooray — Complete the First Ball Pit

**Hooray** unlocks when you finish the first ball pit.

That pit also unlocks **Sprint**, making it easier to reach distant rooms and return before the lights go out.

Keep delivering balls into the first pit until its required amount is reached. Starting to fill it is not enough.

### 10.000 Reasons to Quit and And They Keep Coming

Two achievements track the total number of units delivered:

- **10.000 reasons to quit** — Deliver **10,000 units**.
- **And they keep coming** — Deliver **100,000 units**.

These are delivery totals, not requirements to hold 10,000 or 100,000 balls in your bag at once.

The units must reach the collection system. Simply picking up balls or leaving them in an equipment tank does not replace completing the delivery.

Continue filling pits and processing balls as you progress through the different rooms.

### Model Employee — Complete All Eight Ball Pits

**Model employee** has the description *Requirements met. The supervisor has no objections.*

Its completion condition corresponds to **all eight ball pits** being filled.

Check your unfinished pits before concentrating on the last handful of loose balls in every room. The pit objectives and the seven room-cleaning achievements are separate.

### Clock Out — Finish the Final Pit and Leave

**Clock out** requires filling the final ball pit and walking through the exit.

After completing the required pit progress, return to the exit and finish the final sequence. You do not need every Sparrow or office item to trigger the normal ending.

If you have already filled the last pit but Clock out remains locked, make sure you actually passed through the exit. Filling the pit alone is not the complete achievement condition.

The <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">full room-order walkthrough</Link> covers the route to the exit.

## Which Achievements Require Robots or Maximum Upgrades?

Three achievements focus on equipment purchases and skill progression.

### A New Colleague — Buy Your First Vacuum Robot

**A new colleague** unlocks when you purchase your first Vacuum Robot.

The achievement is about buying the robot, not clearing an entire room with one. You do not need to wait for the robot to finish a full cleaning assignment.

Once robots are available for purchase, buy your first one through the equipment system.

### Mighty Hand — Fully Upgrade Your Hand Skills

**Mighty Hand** requires you to fully upgrade all hand skills.

Open `skills.exe` and check the hand-related upgrade rows. Buying the first level of every skill is not the same as reaching each one's maximum level.

If Mighty Hand is still locked, check whether a hand skill remains below its maximum or another hand-related option has become available as you progress.

### Fully Equipped — Max Out Every Possible Upgrade

**Fully equipped** is broader than Mighty Hand. Its requirement is to **max out every possible upgrade**.

Continue purchasing available upgrade levels until the relevant upgrade options are fully completed.

As more equipment becomes available, check `skills.exe` again for additional upgrade rows. Finishing only the early hand upgrades does not satisfy a requirement covering every possible upgrade.

You can work toward these two achievements during normal progression rather than delaying every room key until you have exhausted your money on upgrades.

## How Do You Complete the Sparrow, Office Item, and Hidden Discovery Achievements?

Five achievements involve collectible objects or secrets rather than ordinary ball delivery.

### First Sparrow and All the Chirps

**First sparrow** unlocks when you rescue any Sparrow.

**All the chirps** requires finding **all 20 Sparrows**.

Check the bird total in `stats.exe`. If it is below 20, revisit the <Link href="/playrooms/sparrows">Sparrow hiding places</Link> and confirm each pickup before expecting **All the chirps**.

### Eagle Eye — Find All 14 Office Items

**Eagle Eye** requires finding all the objects for your office.

Finding all **14** unlocks **Eagle Eye**. If the item total is short, check the <Link href="/playrooms/office-items">office decorations in each area</Link>; finishing an area's loose-ball count does not collect them.

### Out of Water — Find the Whale

**Out of water** unlocks when you discover the hidden Whale in the green office-like area connected to the Waiting Area.

The Whale is found through the Waiting Area, not the Poolroom. Follow the <Link href="/playrooms/green-office-map">turns through the green-carpeted maze</Link> to reach it.

### Free Lexi — Meow Meow Meow

**Free Lexi** is a hidden achievement with the description *Meow meow meow*.

Do not mark **Free Lexi** complete based only on the Sparrow count, office items, or ending. Check its own Steam achievement status.

## How Do You Get Colour Is a Distraction and No Re-hires Needed?

These two achievements are worth planning separately from ordinary room cleanup because they change how you approach a run.

### Colour Is a Distraction — Fill a Pit With One Colour

**Requirement:** Fill one ball pit using only balls of a single colour.

You can complete this challenge on a fresh **Cozy** save. One successful approach uses orange balls and Ball Pit 1, which requires **1,500 balls**.

### How Do You Fill a Ball Pit With Only Orange Balls?

Choose an untouched pit and collect only orange balls. Avoid **Ball Magnet**, since it can pick up nearby balls of other colours without giving you precise control.

Manual pickups are safer than holding Vacuum Collect when different colours are mixed together.

Before depositing, check that your load contains only orange balls. If you accidentally collect another colour, deposit the mixed load into a different pit instead of contaminating your target pit.

The opening Playroom does not contain enough orange balls for this approach. Continue progressing to access more balls, but keep your chosen pit free of other colours.

Once Ball Pit 1 is full of orange balls, **Colour is a distraction** unlocks. The achievement notification confirms that no other colours were deposited into the pit.

Attempt Hardcore after learning your return route and the School's no-sprinting rule. Finish the required pits and exit first; optional collection trips can wait. If you are still choosing upgrades for longer shifts, compare them in the <Link href="/playrooms">first-day and equipment walkthrough</Link>.

## What Is a Practical Order for Getting All 30 Achievements?

Plan **Colour is a distraction** before mixing colours in every pit, because that mistake cannot be undone by delivering only orange balls afterward. During your main run, finish the entry and ball-pit achievements, then revisit rooms for their remaining loose balls and collectibles. Save Hardcore for when you know the return routes. Mark each item in the checklist only after Steam confirms it.
