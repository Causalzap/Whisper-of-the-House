## How Do You Unlock the Hotel in Playrooms?

From the Playroom entrance, buy the **€100 key for the third door on the left**. The Building Services message confirms Hotel access and introduces the Cleaning Schedule.

The key opens the Hotel even if the Office still contains loose balls.

If you are still unlocking the earlier areas, the <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">Playrooms walkthrough</Link> covers the main room order and the other keys.

## How Does the Hotel Cleaning Schedule Work?

The Hotel's introductory message gives you two important instructions:

- Check off each room that you finish cleaning.
- Remember the **room numbers**, not the way to reach them.

Find the Cleaning Schedule in the Hotel hallway. It acts as a list of numbered rooms that you can mark as you work.

### How Do You Mark a Room as Finished?

Read the number outside a room before entering it.

After clearing the balls inside, return to the Cleaning Schedule and mark that number. This prevents you from repeating work in rooms you have already visited.

A useful sequence is:

1. Select an unmarked room number from the schedule.
2. Find the matching number on a Hotel door.
3. Enter and collect its balls.
4. Check the room for anything left behind.
5. Mark the number on the schedule.
6. Move to the next unfinished room.

**Do not mark a room just because it looks empty.**

A room can contain a ball you overlooked. If you discover that you marked a room too early, correct your tracking and revisit it rather than assuming that the checkmark means the cleanup is complete.

The schedule is useful for organizing your search. It does not replace the actual requirement to collect the remaining balls.

## How Do You Find the Right Numbered Hotel Room?

The Hotel's corridors can be disorienting. Building Services tells you to remember room numbers rather than the way to reach each room, so use the numbers on doors to check where you are.

### Should You Memorize Every Corridor?

No. When searching for an unfinished room, compare the door number with the Cleaning Schedule. During that shift, remember enough of your route to return to safety before the lights go out.

The schedule shown here has **56 numbered entries**. It is a record of rooms to check, not a map of how the hallways connect. Do not assume a number's position on the board tells you where its door is located.

If you return on another day, read the door numbers again rather than following a remembered set of turns.

## What Is a Practical Way to Clean the Hotel?

Start with the balls blocking the main passages, so you can reach numbered doors without pushing through large piles. Once the hallways are manageable, move to the guest rooms on the Cleaning Schedule.

### Step 1 — Clear the Main Hallways

Begin near the Hotel entrance.

Collect the large groups of balls in the corridor and continue toward the next accessible section. Where you have suitable collection equipment, use it to process large amounts without carrying every load back by hand.

Do not spend the whole shift inspecting empty rooms while the main hallway is still covered in balls.

You can return for individual room numbers after the largest deposits have been cleared.

### Step 2 — Work Through Numbered Rooms

Once the main corridors are manageable, check the Cleaning Schedule and begin visiting unmarked room numbers.

In each room, focus on the balls that remain inside. Some rooms contain far more than others, so do not assume every door will require the same amount of work.

You may also encounter rooms with unusual orientations or openings. Check the accessible surfaces carefully before deciding that the room is empty.

Return to the schedule and mark the room only after you are satisfied that its collection work is finished.

### Step 3 — Use the Scanner for Missed Balls

When the Hotel's remaining count gets low, stop treating every room as equally likely to contain the missing balls.

Use the Scanner to mark balls that have not been stored. It can help identify objects that are difficult to see or located behind room geometry.

If a particular numbered room still seems suspicious, revisit it and scan again instead of clearing another already empty corridor.

Before the lights go out, stop opening new rooms and return to the starting room. Continue with the unchecked numbers on your next shift.

## Why Does the Hotel Still Have One or Two Balls Left?

The last few Hotel balls can be much harder to find than the thousands collected earlier.

There are two different problems to distinguish: balls you simply missed and balls that appear stuck or unreachable.

### The Scanner Shows a Ball You Cannot See

Check the immediate area around its marker.

Look around beds, corners, room edges, and unusual surfaces. If the marker remains in a location you cannot reach, it may be a ball-position problem rather than an overlooked pile.

Do not immediately assume the whole Hotel has to be cleaned again.

### Can Reset Room Balls Fix Missing Balls?

Open the pause menu and choose **Reset Room Balls** if a ball appears stuck outside the reachable area. This is separate from **Unstuck**, which is also shown in the menu.

After resetting, return to the Scanner marker and check whether the ball is reachable. If it remains inaccessible, try another method rather than resetting repeatedly.

### Can Vacuum Robots Collect Stuck Hotel Balls?

They can help in some situations.

If a ball is close to a reachable part of the room, you can try positioning a Vacuum Robot nearby and letting it work while the room is active.

There is also a completed run where two apparently stuck Hotel balls were collected after the player left Vacuum Robots in the affected areas and continued to the next working day.

That is a possible recovery method, not a guaranteed solution.

Try the Scanner and the game's ball-reset option first. If those fail, a Vacuum Robot near the affected position may be worth testing before abandoning the cleanup.

### What If the Room Looks Different Tomorrow?

Read the door number again instead of navigating by the furniture or corridor turns you remember. If you were tracking an inaccessible ball, scan the room again before trying another reset.

## How Do You Know When the Hotel Is Completely Clean?

Check the Hotel progress display. Once all loose balls in the area have been collected, the status shows **All clean!**

The **Clean Hotel** achievement checks the Hotel's complete cleanup, not simply whether a ball pit is full. Marking entries on the Cleaning Schedule does not replace collecting the balls.

### Do You Need to Clean Every Hotel Room to See the Ending?

No. You can reach the final exit without earning **Clean Hotel**. Return to finish the remaining rooms if you want that achievement; the <Link href="/playrooms/walkthrough">final-exit steps</Link> cover the normal ending.
