<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
There are **14 hidden office items** in Playrooms: one in the Playroom, two each in the Waiting Area, Backroom, Poolroom, and School, three in the Office, and two in the Hotel. Examine each collectible and take it to add it to your collection. Check the **Wall Clock beneath the School stairs**, the **Three Seat Couch at the end of the Backroom corridor**, and the **Tall Plant inside an Office elevator** if you're missing one. Finding all 14 unlocks **Eagle Eye**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Office items are different from the balls you collect and the Sparrows you rescue. They are furnishings and decorations you can take back to your own office.

Each item has an **Examine** interaction before you take it. The easiest ones to miss are the objects away from the usual ball-cleaning route—under stairs, inside lockers, in elevators, or at the end of a long corridor.

## Where Are All 14 Office Items in Playrooms?

<GuideTable columns={playroomsOfficeItemColumns} rows={playroomsOfficeItemRows} />

The easiest way to avoid missing an item is to check furniture and side rooms when you first enter an area. Collecting all the loose balls does not automatically collect the decorations.

When you approach a collectible, look for the interaction that allows you to examine and take the object. A piece of furniture that initially looks like part of the environment may be something you can add to your office.

## Where Is the Toy Block in the Playroom?

### Toy Block — On the Padded Play Equipment

The **Toy Block** is the first office item you can collect.

Find the small dark cube on top of a **pink padded block** beside the starting Playroom ball pits. Aim at the cube, examine it, and take it. You can do this before completing the ball pits.

## Where Are the Bookshelf and Gramophone in the Waiting Area?

### Bookshelf — Along the Waiting Area Chairs

Follow the Waiting Area corridor with rows of chairs into its **darker section**. Against the wall, look for a tall, narrow grey-framed **Bookshelf** with light wooden shelves. Walk up to it and take it.

Do not mistake this for a storage rack: the screen identifies it as **Bookshelf** when examined.

In the Waiting Area, enter the **first door on the right from the middle hallway**. Inside the green-carpeted room, take the Gramophone from the **white-topped table**.

## Where Are the Coffee Table and Three Seat Couch in the Backroom?

Both Backroom items are found around the yellow-walled corridors, but one requires a much longer trip.

### Coffee Table — In the Yellow Hallway

In the yellow-walled Backroom, look for a **small, light-coloured oval Coffee Table** surrounded by loose balls. Its legs and tabletop can be partly obscured by the balls, so examine the pale oval shape before moving on.

Follow the Backroom's long yellow corridor into the darker room at its far end. The **Three Seat Couch** is a light-coloured three-person sofa, visible against the dim surroundings.

## Where Are the Wall Clock and Copper Icosahedron in the School?

Remember that sprinting in School corridors can trigger the principal. The stairs are excluded from that restriction.

### Wall Clock — Beneath the Staircase

At the bottom of the School stairwell, turn beneath the stairs and look up at the wall. A **round white Wall Clock** hangs below the sloping staircase. Examine it and take it; merely walking up the steps will not bring it into view.

## Where Are the Television and Abstract Sculpture in the Hotel?

The Hotel contains two office items. Use the door numbers to keep track of your search, but identify each collectible by the **objects visible inside its room** rather than memorizing corridor turns.

### Television — Room With Stacked Suitcases

Look inside the numbered guest rooms for a dark flat-screen **Television** mounted on a white wall. If you find a room with stacked luggage, check its walls rather than looking for the screen among the balls. Examine the Television to take it.

## Why Am I Stuck at 13/14 Office Items?

Compare the decorations you've actually taken with the 14 names above. The small Copper Icosahedron inside a School locker and the Wall Clock beneath the stairs are easy to pass without examining. If you spent most of your shifts in the main corridors, revisit the Waiting Area's side room, the far end of the Backroom, and the open Office elevators.

Emptying a room's balls does not pick up its furniture. Look for **Examine** on the missing object before leaving.

## How Do You Unlock Eagle Eye in Playrooms?

Take all **14 office items** to unlock **Eagle Eye**. Check its Steam status after the last pickup. Sparrows and the Whale are separate objectives, tracked on the <Link href="/playrooms/achievements">achievement checklist</Link>.
