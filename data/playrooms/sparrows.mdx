<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
There are **20 Sparrows** in Playrooms. Check the light-blue round stool, pink padded equipment, and both hiding places at **Ball Pit 6** before leaving the starting room. The one on top of the black ball-count display is easy to miss. In the Hotel, search tables and other raised furniture, confirm each bird with **Collect**, and use door numbers only to track where you've already looked.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

You can clean every ball from an area and still leave a Sparrow behind. These birds are separate collectibles, and the game does not mark their exact positions on the same display used to find unstored balls.

The first bird you rescue unlocks **First sparrow**. Collecting all 20 unlocks **All the chirps**.

If you have already found 19, do not immediately search every corridor again. Start with the black ball-count display above Ball Pit 6, the School's locker and dark classroom, the Hotel's numbered rooms, and the Office furniture.

The numbers below group hiding places by area; they are not an in-game pickup order.

## Where Are the Sparrows in the Waiting Area?

The Waiting Area contains long corridors lined with wooden chairs. Check the seating carefully, including the less brightly lit sections.

### Sparrow 5 — Wooden Chairs in the Lit Hallway

Search the row of wooden chairs in the brighter part of the Waiting Area.

Check the **third chair** along the hallway. Walk beside the row and inspect the seats rather than checking only from the entrance.

### Sparrow 6 — Chair in the Dark Hallway

Continue into the darker seating area.

Use your flashlight and inspect the wooden chairs, beginning with the first chair in the dark section.

The Sparrow may be difficult to distinguish from the seat if you scan the corridor too quickly.

If both seats are empty and you cannot remember collecting the birds, check the remaining chair rows while you still have time to get back before darkness.

</section>

<section id="backroom-sparrows" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Are the Three Backroom Sparrows?

The Backroom contains yellow-walled corridors and rooms with recognizable furniture. Use those objects as landmarks.

### Sparrow 7 — Chair Beneath the Clock

Find the yellow room containing a wall clock.

Look at the wooden chair below it. Check the seat before leaving the room.

### Sparrow 8 — Wooden Counter Ledge

Search the yellow room with a wooden counter and loose balls scattered nearby.

Look on the raised counter ledge, above the balls.

This is easy to overlook if you use collection equipment while keeping your attention on the floor.

### Sparrow 9 — Chair Below the Cat Picture

Look for the room decorated with a cat picture.

Inspect the wooden chair beneath the picture. The bird is associated with the chair rather than the picture itself.

If you have already cleaned the Backroom, revisit these three furniture landmarks before retracing every corridor.

</section>

<section id="poolroom-sparrows" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Are the Two Poolroom Sparrows?

The Poolroom birds are associated with the pool structures, not the loose balls floating in the water.

### Sparrow 10 — Metal Handrail Near the Stairs

Find the tiled stairs and the nearby metal railing.

Check along the handrail from above and from the side. The bird is small enough to miss when the railing blends into the pool surroundings.

### Sparrow 11 — Top of the Green Slide

Locate the large green water slide.

Look toward the **upper edge of the slide**, well above the pool deck.

Do not limit your search to the base of the slide. You may need to change position to see the bird from below.

</section>

<section id="school-sparrows" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Are the Two School Sparrows?

The School contains lockers, classrooms, and darker areas where birds are easier to miss.

Remember the School rule: **do not sprint in the corridors**. The stairs are excluded from the restriction.

### Sparrow 12 — Inside a Red Locker

Inspect the red lockers in the School.

Open a red locker and look on its upper shelf for the Sparrow.

Look into the locker itself rather than checking only its exterior. If you pass through while collecting balls from the corridor, this bird can remain unnoticed.

### Sparrow 13 — Desk in the Dark Classroom

Find the dark classroom or projector room.

Look across the desks and benches. The Sparrow sits on the wooden furniture.

Use your flashlight to check the seating surfaces, especially if fog or darkness obscures the room.

Avoid sweeping collection equipment directly across a bird before attempting to pick it up. There have been player reports of difficulty retrieving a School Sparrow after an unusual interaction, although that does not establish a permanent failure rule.

If you cannot see the bird, check the room again before assuming it has disappeared.

## How Do You Find the Hotel Sparrows in Playrooms?

Four Sparrows are hidden in Hotel guest rooms. Check wooden tables and other raised furniture, then look for the Collect prompt.

Two useful hiding places are a table beside a bed and a table next to a chair. Continue inspecting the furniture in the other numbered rooms until all four Hotel birds have been collected.

### Sparrow — Table Beside a Bed

One Sparrow sits on a wooden table by a bed surrounded by coloured balls. Walk along the wall side of the room and look at the tabletop, not just the floor.

### Checking the Other Guest Rooms

Read each room number so you can avoid visiting the same rooms repeatedly, but do not rely on a fixed list of numbers from memory. Tables, desks, and other raised surfaces are the places to inspect. A room you have entered without finding a bird is **checked**, not **rescued**.

Keep bird pickups separate from the Hotel's Cleaning Schedule. Crossing out a room number records ball cleanup, not Sparrow collection.

## Where Are the Three Office Sparrows?

The Office birds are easier to miss if you concentrate entirely on the cubicle floors and desks.

### Sparrow 18 — Back of the Dark Leather Sofa

Near the Office entrance or adjoining hallway, find the dark leather sofa.

Look along the **top of its backrest**. The Sparrow is above the seating surface, not underneath the sofa.

### Sparrow 19 — Computer Monitor in a Cubicle

Search the cubicle desks containing computer equipment.

Look at the top of the monitors, not only the keyboards and desk surfaces.

If you have already cleared every ball in the Office, this is an especially useful spot to revisit.

### Sparrow 20 — Blue Rolling Chair

Look for the room containing multiple blue rolling office chairs.

Inspect the seats individually; a Sparrow may be sitting on one of these blue chairs.

There may also be an office collectible nearby, but finding that object is a separate goal. Make sure you actually rescue the Sparrow before marking this spot complete.

<section id="missing-sparrow" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Am I Stuck at 19/20 Sparrows in Playrooms?

If you have 19 Sparrows, distinguish between a bird you picked up and a place you merely visited. **Ball Pit 6 has two separate birds:** one on the pink padded rim and another above the pit on the black count display. Standing at the rim does not mean you have checked the display above it.

After that, revisit the surfaces you could have missed while collecting balls: the upper shelf of the School locker, the dark classroom desks, the Office monitor tops and rolling chairs, and the Hotel's wooden tables. In the Waiting Area, check both the bright and dark chair rows.

For each place, note whether you saw **Collect**, confirmed the pickup, or found nothing. Do not cross an empty room off as a rescued bird; the game counts the interaction, not your visit.

</section>

<section id="sparrow-achievement" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Happens After Finding All 20 Sparrows?

The first rescue unlocks **First sparrow**; the twentieth unlocks **All the chirps**. Neither is required for the normal ending. If the counter reaches 20 but Steam has not registered the achievement, check its status in the <Link href="/playrooms/achievements">achievement checklist</Link>.
