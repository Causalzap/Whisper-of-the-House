<h2 id="challenge-horn-materials">
  What do I need before fighting Velgar?
</h2>

Dragon Slayer sits across the early **Brynmoor and Ghornfell** stage of Ashenfall, with Velgar as the major boss at the end of that stretch. The quest does not send you straight from the marker into the fight: first you need to finish the **Challenge Horn**, including materials such as **Dragon Tooth** and **Bloodwood Sap**.
The Dragon Tooth does not come from a normal ore deposit. Look for the enormous dragon remains and inspect the exposed teeth in the skull. You only need **one Dragon Tooth** for this step.

If the tooth cannot be harvested, check the tool rather than searching for a different skull. My route had already moved into **Iron Pickaxe** progression before the tooth became collectible. Bloodwood Sap creates a similar tool check on the Woodcutting side, so both ingredients naturally push the character beyond the most basic starter tools.

Once the required materials are in the inventory, return home and finish the Challenge Horn. Continuing to explore while carrying the quest materials only adds another chance to arrive at Velgar with damaged equipment and half-used supplies.

My first attempt was made with fairly modest equipment rather than a heavily farmed intermediate set, and the fight still progressed far enough to show that raw armor tier was not the main problem. The bigger weakness was **poison**.

Before summoning Velgar, I would make sure the primary weapon is repaired, healing food is full, combat Runes are stocked, and **Anti-poison** is actually on the hotbar or ready to use. In co-op, friendly-fire positioning also matters: a melee player staying beside Velgar and a magic player firing through the same space can create damage that has nothing to do with the boss.

<h2 id="best-damage">
  What damage works best against Velgar?
</h2>

Among the spells I used, **Air produced the best immediate single-target damage**.

Water was noticeably weaker as direct damage. Fire still had value from its continuing pressure, but Air moved Velgar's health bar faster and reached **51-damage criticals** even without turning the attempt into a large gear grind.

Melee is still completely workable. The difference is positioning.

Backing too far away encouraged more ranged pressure from Velgar. Staying closer reduced some of that, but the melee player then had to read the bite and paw attacks correctly. For magic, a middle distance felt better: far enough to avoid standing inside every close-range attack, but not so far away that the fight became a constant ranged exchange.

<h2 id="poison-resistance">
  Do I need Anti-poison for Velgar?
</h2>

I would bring it even if you are confident in your dodging.

On the first attempt, poison became dangerous enough to drain most of a health bar before the next major boss attack was even the problem. Once that happens, food is being spent to recover from status damage instead of saving you from actual mistakes.

After the wipe, the next attempt did not involve a huge equipment rebuild. We repaired the gear, refilled healing, added poison resistance, and went back in.

That was enough to change the fight substantially.

The Anti-poison used during this progression provided a **120-second protection window**, so timing matters. I would not drink it several minutes before the pull while still reorganizing the inventory. Use it close enough to the fight that the protection covers the dangerous portion of the encounter.

<h2 id="liftoff">
  How do I survive Velgar's liftoff attacks?
</h2>

Velgar becomes much more dangerous when he takes off.

The airborne sequence can send several lines of ground damage across the arena in quick succession. One failed attempt became especially unstable because a player died just before this transition, leaving the remaining player alone when the hardest movement pattern began.

The resource I protect here is **stamina**.

Do not empty the bar chasing Velgar or rolling during a quiet moment. Once the ground lines begin arriving, you need enough stamina to move through several attacks rather than surviving the first and getting trapped by the next one.

Dodging **forward through the incoming line** worked better for me than trying to run sideways after the danger had already spread across the floor. The timing is cleaner because you pass through the attack instead of racing its width.

This is also the worst moment to finish one more spell or melee swing. Stop attacking, clear the sequence, then resume damage when Velgar comes back down.

<h2 id="successful-attempt">
  What changed between the failed attempt and the kill?
</h2>

The successful attempt did not come from replacing the whole equipment set.

The meaningful changes were much smaller:

- damaged equipment was repaired;
- healing supplies were restored;
- Anti-poison was carried and used;
- Air became the preferred direct-damage spell;
- stamina was saved for the airborne ground attacks instead of being spent on extra pressure.

That second fight moved faster immediately. Air was taking larger pieces from Velgar's health while the melee player held the closer position, and poison stopped consuming the healing supply by itself.

There were still deaths, so the kill was not a perfect execution. What changed was the recovery. A death no longer combined with uncontrolled poison damage and turned one mistake into an unrecoverable collapse.

By the final quarter of Velgar's health, experimentation was no longer useful. I stayed with the strongest reliable attacks and kept enough stamina available in case another liftoff started.

That gives a useful line for deciding whether to farm more gear. If ordinary Velgar attacks are deleting most of your health and your damage barely moves the bar, improve the setup first. If you are already reaching the second half consistently, repair, fix poison protection, and clean up the liftoff movement before spending hours on another full equipment tier.

<h2 id="after-velgar">
  What do I do after beating Velgar?
</h2>

The quest does not finish the moment Velgar falls.

Open the **quest-item tab** after the kill. **Velgar's head** is placed there as proof of the victory. Take it back to the quest giver and hand it over to complete Dragon Slayer.
