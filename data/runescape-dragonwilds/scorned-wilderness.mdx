<h2 id="how-to-reach-scorned-wilderness">
  How do I get into the Scorned Wilderness?
</h2>

The Scorned Wilderness is reached from the **Fractured Plains**, beyond the bridge route leading to the huge Dragon Gate. **Fuzan must already be defeated** before this endgame path properly opens.

Use the physical route to the gate rather than trying to force a Lodestone jump into unexplored terrain. An attempted teleport toward the still-locked region can leave you pressed against inaccessible terrain instead of giving you a shortcut.

The Dragon Gate itself confirms whether your earlier progression is complete. Its message refers to four seals tied to **earth, water, air, and flame**. Once those requirements have been satisfied, the gate opens and the route continues toward Commander Zilyana.

<h2 id="commander-zilyana">
  What should I do when I reach Commander Zilyana?
</h2>

Commander Zilyana is the last safe staging point before the raid begins. Her dialogue makes the situation clear: she can take you toward **Kuldra's lair**, but the route through the stronghold still has to be cleared by you.

The return loop becomes **base → Lodestone → Zilyana → latest extraction point**, rather than another long trip across the map after every death or restock.

<h2 id="what-to-bring">
  What should I bring into the Scorned Wilderness?
</h2>

The raid is long enough that missing a tool or resistance item hurts more than entering with one imperfect armor slot.

Strong **Tier 7 equipment from Umbral Sands** is enough to begin working through the region, and the Scorned Wilderness itself starts supplying materials for the next equipment tier. A practical first run can therefore enter with reliable Tier 7 combat gear rather than delaying until every new item has already been crafted.

The items I would not leave behind are **Antifire**, plenty of **Anti-poison**, strong healing food, an **Adamant pickaxe**, and an **Adamant logging axe**. The pickaxe and axe are not convenience tools here; breakable walls and thorny obstacles appear on the route itself. Leave several inventory slots free as well, because Runite, Luminite, Black Dragon Hide, Discordant Residue, chest supplies, and other new materials accumulate quickly.

Combat infusions and Whetstones are useful extras, but resistance, healing, and the two tools solve the failures that can actually stop a run.

<h2 id="raid-checkpoints">
  How do Scorned Wilderness extraction points work?
</h2>

My first blind clear took roughly **2.5 hours** once puzzle solving, deaths, gathering, and crafting trips were included. The extraction system is what keeps that from becoming one uninterrupted endurance run.

Whenever Zilyana appears at an **extraction point**, activate it before moving on. You can then leave the raid, unload new materials, repair equipment, rest, craft an upgrade, replenish food and potions, and return through Zilyana to that same point.

Death also returns you to the latest activated stage rather than forcing you to replay everything already cleared in that run.

The route moves through:

**Dragontail Vines → Fragments of Ghornfell → Shattered Stronghold → Cataclysm Keep → Kuldra's Sanctum**

I normally leave when either inventory pressure or equipment condition becomes more dangerous than the next combat section. Carrying a full stack of new ore through another boss room just to avoid one trip home creates risk without improving the clear.

<h2 id="anima-mechanics">
  How do Anima, pylons, and Anima Orbs work?
</h2>

Almost every major puzzle uses the same four elements: **Air, Water, Earth, and Fire**.

Crystallized Anima temporarily attunes you to its element, usually for about **30 seconds**. That charge can pass an elemental barrier, activate a matching pylon, or power an Anima device before the timer expires.

The other recurring object is the **Anima Orb Dispenser**. If the interaction reads **Charge Required**, it is not asking for an inventory item; the character needs the correct elemental attunement first.

After charging the dispenser, carry the generated orb to the required mechanism. Normal attacking is restricted while holding one, but movement abilities still work, so jumping, double jumping, dodging, and Wind Step matter more than trying to clear every enemy first.

Some rooms give Anima through enemies instead. Killing **three enemies carrying the same elemental attunement** can fill the charge and give you the element required by the nearby pylon.

Once that pattern is familiar, most rooms reduce to three practical questions: which element is missing, where the charge comes from, and where that charge or orb has to end up.

<h2 id="dragontail-vines">
  How do I get through Dragontail Vines?
</h2>

**Dragontail Vines** introduces the raid's systems before later sections begin combining them.

Runite and Luminite start appearing early, so collect them as you pass rather than assuming all Rune-tier materials have to be farmed before entering. Magic Trees can also appear before your logging tool is strong enough to harvest them; they do not block the route, so leave them until the appropriate upgrade is available.

The first Anima sequence is straightforward: collect the elemental charge and take it to the matching pylon.

Soon afterward, Kuldra charges a large attack while Zilyana creates a safe barrier. Move into the protection when it appears. Staying outside to finish an enemy or grab one last resource exposes you to heavy damage and Soul Scorch for very little gain.

The first substantial combat check mixes a **Black Dragon with several smaller enemies**. Clearing the adds first made the fight much more stable for me. The dragon has the largest health bar, but overlapping attacks from several smaller enemies are what quickly consume food and stamina in the confined arena.

Black Dragons also begin supplying **Black Dragon Hide**, while nearby chests can contain additional raid materials.

Continue through the early elemental sequence until the first extraction point appears, then activate it before entering the next section.

<h2 id="fragments-of-ghornfell">
  How do I open the Fire and Air gate in Fragments of Ghornfell?
</h2>

**Fragments of Ghornfell** is a later Scorned Wilderness raid section, not the early-game **Ghornfell region** you travel through before Velgar. This section is the first point in the raid where two different Anima routes feed one gate.

For **Fire**, cut through the thorny vine and take the Fire attunement. The timer is about **30 seconds**, so the climb is mostly a movement problem. Wind Step and double jump save more time than stopping to fight enemies on the way. Reach the top, use the Orb Dispenser, and bring the result back to complete the Fire side.

The **Air** side works differently. Air-attuned enemies appear behind the water-barrier area, and killing **three** grants the required charge.

The easiest way I found to preserve the timer was to pull those enemies closer to the pylon before killing the third one. Finishing all three at their original position wastes a large part of the 30-second window just running back.

Once both Fire and Air are active, the gate opens into the poison-maze section.

<h2 id="poison-maze">
  Which way do I go through the poison maze?
</h2>

The maze is confusing because poison vents, side loot, enemies, vertical paths, and breakable sections all compete for attention.

The useful rule is to keep looking for the route that gains elevation. Follow it until you reach an **elevator**, ride upward, clear the next enemies, then inspect the far end of the corridor for a wall that can be mined through.

The **Plague King** is behind it.

Anti-poison earns its inventory slot here. Air damage worked comfortably against the Plague King in my run, but staying inside poison to force a faster kill was a worse trade than moving out and extending the fight slightly.

After the Plague King dies, check the nearby route before leaving. A **Sanguine Urn** can be collected shortly after this encounter.

The next dangerous room contains an **Abyssal Demon** with additional enemies nearby. I prefer pulling the outer enemies with a ranged weapon before entering the center. Starting the full group at once turns a manageable demon fight into several simultaneous attack patterns.

After the Abyssal Demon, continue to the next extraction point. By this stage the inventory is often full of new ore, hide, consumables, and crafting materials, making this a natural place to bank and repair.

<h2 id="shattered-stronghold">
  Where do I go in the Shattered Stronghold?
</h2>

The main navigation problem in **Shattered Stronghold** is that the correct route repeatedly looks like solid scenery.

After the next Zilyana protection sequence, enter the area around the fire trap and inspect the damaged masonry instead of following only the obvious corridor. Later, when the elemental path appears to stop again, look behind the large fallen structure.

The wall you need is pale stone covered in **bright red-orange cracks**.

A **Rat King** waits beyond the wall. Kill it, collect the Earth attunement, and move immediately because the timer is already running.

Carry Earth through the trap section, open the next route, and clear the enemies in the following chamber. Garou fighters can turn the timed section into a bad fight if you arrive already damaged, so heal before committing rather than trying to save a few seconds.

The route eventually gives you the Fire charge needed by the original pylon. Take it back, activate the pylon, and the bridge forward appears.

Another strong Black Dragon waits farther ahead. Kill it, collect any useful hide and nearby materials, and keep moving until the next extraction point becomes available.

<h2 id="cataclysm-keep">
  How do I finish Cataclysm Keep?
</h2>

**Cataclysm Keep** shifts more of the difficulty into movement.

The route uses narrow surfaces, gaps, pipes, and timed platforms, so stamina matters even when no enemy is attacking. Going into a jump sequence with almost no stamina leaves very little room to recover after a bad landing.

The final major room contains **three Vault Guardians**. Kill them before working on the pylons; trying to solve the room while attacks are still coming from behind makes the sequence harder than necessary.

One of the pylons is missing an orb, and the remaining elements are reached through the surrounding traversal routes.

Start with Fire. Cross to the Fire source, clear the enemies, charge the pylon, and then return through the room. Some hazards become active after the Fire side is powered, so the trip back is not identical to the trip in.

The next obstacle is a set of platforms controlled by a valve.

You do not need to perfectly time the extending and retracting platforms. **Wait until they extend, then turn the valve off.** The platforms remain in place, turning the section into a normal climb instead of a timing challenge.

From there, reach the next element, pull the lever that opens the return route, and finish the remaining Orb Dispenser and pylon interactions.

<h2 id="air-water-earth-fire">
  What is the Air, Water, Earth, Fire puzzle solution?
</h2>

The final sequence comes from **Rasthin's Riddle of Faith**.

Its wording points to each element in order: life is **breathed** into creation, the gods shed **tears**, hope is buried in the **ground**, and bridges are **burned**.

That gives the solution:

**Air → Water → Earth → Fire**

The final Zilyana extraction point is immediately worth activating. Bank the materials you do not want to risk, repair anything close to breaking, restore consumables, and then return for the final encounter.

<h2 id="raid-materials">
  Which Scorned Wilderness materials should I keep?
</h2>

**Runite Ore and Luminite** are the most important new materials because they feed the Rune equipment tier. Multiple deposits appear along the raid route, including more in the later sections, so collect them when inventory space allows rather than turning every deposit into a separate farming trip.

Black Dragons provide **Black Dragon Hide**, while enemies can drop **Discordant Residue**. Supply chests also add food, potions, Magic Logs, hides, and other useful materials that reduce how much has to be carried in from outside.

I use extraction points to convert those resources into immediate upgrades when the return is worthwhile. If the backpack contains enough material for a weapon or tool that changes the next section, I leave and craft it. If the upgrade is only marginal and the current equipment is still handling the raid comfortably, I keep the material and continue.

<h2 id="before-kuldra">
  What should I do at the last checkpoint before Kuldra?
</h2>

Activate the final extraction point before entering Kuldra's Sanctum.

Use it to unload valuable raid materials, repair damaged equipment, refill food and resistance consumables, and craft any immediate upgrade that clearly improves the next fight. There is no reason to carry ore, hides, and other bankable resources into the boss arena.

The fight ahead uses the Anima systems you have already learned, but the Resonator, elemental phases, beam attack, Heart phase, and rewards all belong to the encounter itself.
