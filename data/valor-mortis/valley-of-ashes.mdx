<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Follow **Guidepost → The Climb → Demolished Path → The Other Side → Military Camp → Quarry**. Collect the **Pistol** on the broken bridge, shoot the red growths to unlock the lift, then follow the upper route toward the Abandoned Church.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

**Chapter 2: Regroup** takes William from the broken bridge in Valley of Ashes to the Abandoned Church. The main route passes through a lift shaft, an abandoned military camp, and the Quarry, but several paths branch away from the direction you need to travel.

At Guidepost, speak with the Adjutant in the suspended railway carriage to begin **An Unheard Farewell**. The missing scouts are inside a hidden cave halfway along the lift shaft. After investigating their fate, return to the Adjutant and choose whether to tell the truth or lie; both choices complete the quest, but their additional rewards differ.

You will also find unreachable blue Grapple targets in Quarry. Return to those platforms after unlocking Grapple in Chapter 3 to collect another Pure Blade Material and a Ferrofluid Flask upgrade.

<section id="guidepost" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Guidepost: Speak to the Adjutant Before Crossing the Bridge

From the **Guidepost Lantern**, follow the road past the overturned wagon until you reach a wooden platform overlooking a gap. The route forward uses the pole suspended over the drop.

Before crossing, break the boxes on the left side of the platform. A short passage leads to an enemy guarding a chest containing a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

This is a useful pickup before the bridge and lift section, where missed jumps and repeated enemy encounters can drain your healing.

Continue across the gap and defeat the first **Risen Grenadier**. Just beyond that fight, check the chest on the left before entering the next metal carriage. It contains **Indestructible Shell**.

The next important landmark is a railway carriage hanging over the destroyed bridge. A soldier inside calls for help.

Speak with the **Adjutant Sous-Officier** and agree to search for his missing scouting party. This begins **An Unheard Farewell**.

The soldiers are not inside the carriage, and there is no reason to search for a way to climb into it. Their last known route leads through the caves below the bridge.

Continue downward to reach them.

</section>

## Broken Bridge: Find the Pistol and Release the Lift

The bridge descent takes you across damaged train cars, wooden platforms, and narrow ledges.

Inside one of the railway carriages, collect the **Pistol**. It is required for opening several passages later in the chapter because the red Nephtoglobin growths controlling certain mechanisms must be shot.

Continue down the bridge, cross the remaining platforms, and follow the fallen log toward **The Climb Lantern**.

Before using the nearby lift, check the chest beside the Lantern for an **Empty Ferrofluid Flask**. This increases your number of healing charges and is available without a lengthy detour.

### How to activate the lift at The Climb

The lift is initially obstructed by red Nephtoglobin growths.

Shoot the exposed growths with your Pistol, then operate the lift.

At the first stop, enter the cave on the right. Three Carrion enemies occupy the passage, with a ladder farther inside.

Climb the ladder and follow the upper cliffside route until you reach another section of the lift mechanism. Shoot the two red growths controlling the upper portion, then use the lever to call the lift.

The lift can now travel through the shaft.

That gives you access to the missing scouts' location, but you must leave the lift **between its normal stops**.

<section id="missing-scouts" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Are the Missing Scouts in An Unheard Farewell?

Return to the lift after releasing its upper mechanism.

As the lift moves through the shaft, watch the **left wall**. About halfway along the journey, a side platform becomes reachable.

Jump from the moving lift onto that ledge.

Across from the path used during your first ascent, there is a cave entrance sealed by golden Nephtoglobin growths.

Burn the obstruction with **Fire Transmutation** and enter.

![The hidden cave entrance halfway along the lift shaft at The Climb.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-lift-hidden-cave.webp)

### Defeat the soldier guarding the orders

A heavily armed soldier blocks the cave.

He is considerably tougher than the Carrion enemies near the lift, and taking repeated melee hits here can consume most of your remaining healing.

Fire Transmutation lets you apply damage while moving away from his attacks. Grenades are another option when he is exposed, though they are not required to finish the encounter.

Once the soldier is defeated, search the area near the **tent and lamp at the back of the cave**.

Pick up the **Letter to all Commanding Officers of the Line Infantry**.

![The military orders found near the tent in the missing scouts' cave.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-scout-orders.webp)

The letter establishes what happened to the missing scouting party.

Before leaving, open the chest guarded by the soldier. It contains **Pure Blade Material**, which is needed for upgrading melee weapons beyond their early Crude Material levels.

This is a separate pickup from the Pure Blade Material accessible in Quarry after you obtain Grapple.

Continue through the cave and follow the upper exit. You will encounter the remaining evidence of the scouts' fate as you rejoin the main route.

The investigation is not complete until you return to the Adjutant.

</section>

## Demolished Path: Tell the Truth or Lie to the Adjutant?

The upper passage eventually reaches **Demolished Path**.

Follow the path toward the remaining red Nephtoglobin growths and shoot them to clear the route. Continue until you reach a ladder that can be kicked down.

This shortcut reconnects the upper path with the suspended carriage near Guidepost.

Open the ladder before heading back to the Adjutant. It provides a direct way to return without descending the bridge and riding the lift again.

Speak to the Adjutant to report what happened to his soldiers.

He asks whether they survived. You can tell him the truth or lie.

### Which answer gives the better reward?

Both answers complete **An Unheard Farewell**, award the **Sleight of Hand** Amber, and satisfy the **Aggressive Reconnaissance** achievement requirement.

The difference appears after you rest at a Lantern.

- **Tell the truth:** Rest, then return to the carriage area. You can obtain an **Epic Catalyst Clot**.
- **Lie:** Rest, then return to the scouts' cave near the lift. You will find the Adjutant's remains and a **Remnant's Return**, which allows you to recover lost Catalysts without traveling back to the place where you died.

Choose the truth when you prefer an immediate source of additional Catalysts. The lie provides an item that can be held until a future death makes recovery difficult.

The Sleight of Hand Amber is not exclusive to either choice. Its effect improves firearm reload speed and weapon switching.

### Optional Eternal Guardist After the Shortcut

Back on the upper route, look for the side path to the left behind a group of three enemies.

An **Eternal Guardist** waits along this path. Defeating this enemy begins **The Oathbound** and provides the **Mysterious Stone Pyramid** and **Lucien Lafontaine's Emblem**.

A chest behind him contains **Perfectionism**, which increases melee damage while your health is full.

This encounter does not block the main route. The enemy's longer combinations make him difficult with limited upgrades, so you can leave this challenge until your parries and weapon damage are stronger.

Continue toward **The Other Side**.

## The Other Side: Can You Get Through the Poison Gas Now?

Near **The Other Side Lantern**, a separate passage on the left contains thick yellow poison gas.

You can enter far enough to reach a carriage holding **Emissary's Seal**, which begins **The Emissary**.

The investigation cannot be finished during your first Chapter 2 visit. Progress through the main story until you obtain **Shield Transmutation**, then return to cross the poisoned section.

Collect the Seal now if you want the quest in your journal, and continue toward Military Camp.

When you have Shield, return to The Other Side and [investigate the missing Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary).

</section>

## Military Camp: Clear the Soldiers Before Climbing Uphill

Military Camp contains several groups of soldiers spread across raised platforms and ground-level positions.

Moving into the center immediately can draw attacks from multiple directions. The elevated cannon and explosive barrels give you ways to remove enemies before fighting at close range.

From the **Military Camp Lantern**, follow the route toward the stairs and reach the cannon overlooking the lower camp.

![The cannon overlooking enemy positions in Military Camp.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-military-camp-cannon.webp)

Use the cannon against the soldiers below, then take the right-hand approach into the camp.

The route through the tower offers firing angles toward enemies gathered near explosive barrels.

Clear the camp in this order:

1. Fire the cannon into the groups below.
2. Move along the right side and climb into the tower.
3. Shoot explosive barrels when enemies are close enough to be caught in the blast.
4. Fight the remaining shielded or heavily armored soldier after the surrounding enemies have been reduced.

Wait until multiple soldiers are near an explosive barrel before shooting it. The blast can damage several enemies at once, reducing the number you need to fight individually before reaching the armored soldier.

### Where to find Dominatus

Before leaving Military Camp, look for the tent in the corner to the **left of the route climbing uphill**.

A chest inside contains **Dominatus**.

![The Dominatus chest inside the Military Camp tent.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-dominatus-tent.webp)

Dominatus restores health when you land critical attacks.

This makes it useful for a player already relying on parries and posture breaks, particularly before the Chapter 2 encounter with General Lothaire.

Equip the Amber at an available slot if you intend to use its effect during combat.

### One more Flask upgrade uphill

Continue toward the higher part of the camp until you reach a Grenadier attacking from an elevated position.

A chest immediately to the right of that position contains a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

Collect it before leaving if your existing Flasks restore too little health to survive another heavy hit.

Then continue uphill toward Quarry.

## Quarry: Reach the Abandoned Church

The **Quarry Lantern** marks the final major stretch of Chapter 2.

Follow the wooden platforms upward, cross the raised walkways, and continue toward the zipline that leads to the higher Quarry route.

There are two optional Amber chests along this ascent:

- **Potent Concoction:** Near the earlier wooden platforms, look for the shortcut ladder after climbing and dropping onto the next ledge. Opposite it, burn the golden Nephtoglobin growths to open a passage toward a guarded chest.
- **Marksman's Bane:** After riding the first zipline, turn left instead of immediately going uphill. Break the boxes near the platform edge, follow the concealed path, and enter the enemy-occupied tower.

Both are accessible during Chapter 2.

Collect them now if you want their effects before continuing, but neither chest is required to reach the Church.

### What happens near the end of Quarry?

The route leads toward the second possible encounter with **General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer**.

What happens here depends on the result of Chapter 1:

- **If Lothaire defeated you in Chapter 1:** You must defeat him here to continue.
- **If you defeated Lothaire on the Battlefield:** The later fight does not trigger, and you can continue toward the Church.

During the required encounter, focus on parrying his regular combinations and dodging the red-glowing attacks in his second phase.

For the first-encounter consequences, combat patterns, and rewards, see [General Lothaire](/valor-mortis/general-lothaire).

After clearing the route, enter the **Abandoned Church**.

James and the facilities inside the Church provide the next stage of progression, along with access to fast travel between discovered Lanterns.

The main story then moves toward **Fallen Village**.

</section>

## Return to Quarry After Grapple: Pure Blade Material and Flask Upgrade

One part of Quarry remains inaccessible during your first Chapter 2 visit.

The elevated route requires **Grapple**, which you obtain after defeating Iron Marshall in Chapter 3.

Once you have that ability, fast travel back to **Valley of Ashes → Quarry**.

You can collect another Pure Blade Material and a Ferrofluid Flask upgrade without replaying the full region from Guidepost.

### How to reach the upper Quarry platforms

From the Quarry Lantern, turn around and use the elevator to return to the higher section.

Look for the blue Grapple targets that were unusable during your first visit.

Cross toward the opposite platform using Grapple, then interact with the movable platform to pull it down.

Jump onto the platform and let it carry you upward.

![The Grapple targets and movable platform used to reach the upper Quarry area.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-quarry-grapple-route.webp)

There are several enemies above, including Musketeers who can shoot you while you cross between platforms.

Once you reach the first upper landing, move toward the next raised section. You can bypass some enemies by continuing across the traversal route, but the Musketeers are best dealt with when you have secure footing.

Operate the next movable platform and climb toward the upper group of enemies.

### Where is the Pure Blade Material?

Near the upper Musketeer positions, reach the platform containing **Pure Blade Material**.

![The Pure Blade Material pickup on the upper Quarry return route.](/images/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes-quarry-pure-blade-material.webp)

This is a different pickup from the Pure Blade Material in the hidden scouts' cave near The Climb.

Use this material after deciding whether to upgrade the Saber or Rapier. You can obtain the Rapier shortly after leaving the Abandoned Church, so there is no need to commit your early upgrade materials before comparing both weapons.

### Continue for the Ferrofluid upgrade

The Pure Blade Material is not the end of this return route.

Continue across the nearby upper platforms, dealing with the Musketeers and the tougher enemy farther along.

A chest near the end contains a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

After collecting both upgrades, return to the Quarry Lantern and fast travel back to your current story area.

You can make this trip immediately after unlocking Grapple. There is no requirement to wait until the University or later chapters.

</section>
