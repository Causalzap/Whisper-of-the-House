Leave the **Abandoned Church** toward Fallen Village, collect the **Rapier** and **Blunderbuss**, then use the **Storage Key** beneath Marketplace to clear the blocked route. Defeat **Iron Marshall** at Orchard, unlock **Grapple**, find **Louis** beyond the boss area, and return to James at the Church.

**Chapter 3: Rendezvous** begins at the Abandoned Church and takes you through the Fields, Marketplace, and Orchard in search of Louis.

The main route is interrupted by several obstacles that look similar but require different solutions. One key opens a house across the river, another opens an underground passage, and a plague-covered barrier cannot be removed until you reach its weak point from the correct side.

The two side quests in this region, **The Scarecrow** and **Lullaby**, can be completed while passing through the areas that contain their objectives. You can also leave them unfinished and continue the rescue, since neither quest opens the path to Iron Marshall.

For the main story, your checkpoints are:

**Abandoned Church → Fields → Blunderbuss Carriage → Marketplace → Orchard → Iron Marshall → Grapple → Louis.**

<section id="abandoned-church" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where to Go From Abandoned Church

After clearing the [Valley of Ashes](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) route, William reaches the Abandoned Church.

Speak with James to begin the search for Louis.

The Church provides access to equipment upgrades and fast travel between discovered Lanterns. It also has more than one route leading away from it, so make sure you take the one toward Fallen Village.

The other direction leads toward Fortress, which is not your next main-story destination.

### Pick up the Rapier before entering Fields

Follow the path out of the Church toward the village.

A body obstructs the passage ahead. Interact with it to pull out the **Rapier**, clearing the way forward.

The Rapier changes your defensive options. It rewards accurate parries and can strike exposed weak points, but it lacks the Saber's normal blocking capability.

Before spending scarce weapon-upgrade materials, try its parry timing against the enemies in Fields. The Saber remains usable, especially when you want the option to block unfamiliar attacks.

The Rapier also has a chained heavy-attack sequence. Practice the [Rapier heavy-attack timing](/valor-mortis#rapier-heavy-attacks) before committing to the full combination against a boss.

Continue downhill until you reach the **Fields Lantern**.

</section>

<section id="fields" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Fields: Find the Scarecrow and Open the House Shortcut

From the Fields Lantern, follow the path into the farmland and take the route turning right.

A man is tied to a wooden scarecrow post in the field.

Speak with him to start **The Scarecrow**. He asks William to recover two belongings: the **Lover's Pendant** and a **Letter with a Drawing**.

![The man tied to the scarecrow post in Fallen Village Fields.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-scarecrow-post.webp)

Both items can be collected before leaving Fields, and the nearby house shortcut makes returning to the scarecrow much faster.

### Get the Lover's Pendant

From the scarecrow, head toward the barn and take the right-hand path leading to a large tree.

At the base of the tree, a body lies near a wooden cross.

Search it for the **Lover's Pendant**.

The pendant is lying outdoors, rather than inside a building or chest. Look for the tree and cross when searching the farmland.

### Open the gate near the large house

Continue toward the next group of buildings.

At a fork in the path, a wooden gate with a Grenadier is on the right. Take the opposite direction toward the large house and the nearby well.

A chest beside the well contains a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

Open the gate shortcut connecting this area to the earlier route. You can then return to the Fields Lantern or the scarecrow without crossing every enemy position again.

This shortcut also makes it easier to finish The Scarecrow after collecting the second item.

### Find the Letter with a Drawing

Proceed toward the tall haystacks.

A group of **four human enemies** attacks along this route. Once you pass them, climb the hill on the left and enter the large house.

Go upstairs and take the **Letter with a Drawing** from the table.

The lower exit reconnects with the area near the house shortcut.

With both items collected, return to the scarecrow and hand them over.

</section>

<section id="scarecrow" className="scroll-mt-24">

## The Scarecrow: Where to Go After Returning Both Items

Giving the Lover's Pendant and Letter with a Drawing to the scarecrow does not complete the investigation.

The final objective is inside a nearby barn where the lovers once met.

From the large-house route, return toward the barn near the Fields path. Look for the building with an accessible upper loft.

Enter and climb to the top level.

You will find **two pillows lying together**. Interact with them to complete The Scarecrow.

The reward is **Bountiful Harvest**, an Amber that restores health when collecting Nephtoglobin remnants.

Because those remnants appear regularly during combat, the effect can provide additional recovery without spending a Ferrofluid Flask charge.

### Should you collect Volatile End here?

The same general Fields route contains another barn with bodies that explode when disturbed.

A chest at the rear contains **Volatile End**.

Keep your distance from the bodies and trigger the dangerous ones with a firearm before moving toward the chest.

This is an optional pickup, separate from The Scarecrow reward.

After finishing the barn investigation, return to the road leading toward the next major enemy encounter.

</section>

<section id="blunderbuss" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Get the Blunderbuss in Fallen Village

The main Fields route eventually reaches a large enemy carrying a heavy sword.

This enemy is a **Volunteer** and stands in front of a carriage blocking the way forward.

Its attacks have enough reach to punish repeated melee swings. Leave room to evade its heavier strikes, then attack during recovery rather than trying to interrupt every windup.

Fire Transmutation and Pistol shots can help maintain pressure while staying outside its immediate reach.

After defeating the Volunteer, enter the carriage behind it.

The **Blunderbuss** is on a table inside.

![The Blunderbuss pickup inside the carriage beyond the Volunteer.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-blunderbuss-carriage.webp)

Collecting the weapon opens the route through the other side of the carriage.

The Blunderbuss is designed for short-range attacks. Its damage is useful against larger enemies, particularly when you can approach an exposed weak point.

Keep some ammunition available for the Chapter 3 boss rather than spending every shot on ordinary soldiers.

Continue along the path beyond the carriage until you reach the **Marketplace Lantern**.

</section>

## Weathered Key Location: How to Complete Lullaby

At Marketplace, there are two unrelated locked routes.

The **Weathered Key** belongs to the optional Lullaby investigation. The **Storage Key** opens the underground passage required for the main story.

To begin Lullaby, travel from the Marketplace Lantern toward the water, keeping the distant flames as a landmark.

Enter the nearby house and approach the empty crib.

You hear a baby's cry, and the **Lullaby** investigation begins.

### Find the Weathered Key behind the crib house

Leave the house through its rear door.

Several enemies occupy the area outside. Search the ground near them for the **Weathered Key**.

![The Weathered Key pickup outside the rear of the Marketplace crib house.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-weathered-key.webp)

The key does not open the nearby underground storage door.

Instead, it belongs to a locked house across the river, back toward the area you passed after fighting the Volunteer.

### Where to use the Weathered Key

Return toward the Marketplace Lantern and retrace the path into the previous area.

Once you pass the Volunteer encounter location, take the road to the right and follow it to the water.

The house you need is across a narrow river crossing.

Jump across to the entrance and use the Weathered Key on the locked door.

Inside, inspect the bed and collect the **Toy**.

Return to the original Marketplace house containing the empty crib. Interact with the crib to place the Toy and complete Lullaby.

Your reward is **Tomorrow's Promise**, an Amber that causes Ferrofluid Flask healing to continue regenerating health for a period after use.

Activating another Flask while that regeneration effect is active refreshes its duration rather than stacking a second simultaneous effect.

Once the Toy has been returned, there is no further locked door or item needed for Lullaby.

</section>

## Storage Key Location: How to Open the Marketplace Plague Wall

The route toward Orchard continues through the larger Marketplace village.

From the Marketplace Lantern, head into the streets occupied by patrolling soldiers.

A chest inside the first house on the left contains a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**. Collect it before entering the more heavily defended area if you need stronger healing for the upcoming boss.

Continue through the village until you reach a building being fired upon by nearby enemies.

That house is an important landmark for the main route.

A separate upper-floor detour leads to the **Warrior's Fury** Amber: climb through the building and use its balcony to reach the chest beyond the street-level path.

However, the route that clears the blocked passage lies **behind the house**, not on its upper floor.

### Go down the backyard ladder

Move through the house and exit into the backyard.

Find the ladder descending below ground and climb down.

Inside the underground passage, move around the corner near **two enemies**. The **Storage Key** lies on a box nearby.

![The Storage Key on a box in the underground Marketplace passage.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-storage-key.webp)

Collect it and approach the locked door farther along the passage.

Use the Storage Key to open the door.

### Destroy the plague-wall weak point

Beyond the locked door, follow the passage toward the exposed Nephtoglobin weak point.

Destroy it to remove the plague wall blocking your progress above.

The key distinction is:

- **Weathered Key:** Opens the house across the river and lets you retrieve the Toy for Lullaby.
- **Storage Key:** Opens the underground door leading to the weak point that clears the main-story obstruction.

If you have the Weathered Key but still cannot move through Marketplace, go back to the house with the backyard ladder. You need the Storage Key from below ground.

Once the wall is cleared, continue through the opened passage and the graveyard route until you reach the **Orchard Lantern**.

</section>

## How to Beat Iron Marshall in Orchard

The **Iron Marshall** is the major boss of Chapter 3.

Rest at the Orchard Lantern before entering the arena.

Its attacks deal heavy damage, particularly when you remain directly in front of its weapon. The arena also contains explosive barrels that you can use during the fight.

You do not need to defeat the boss entirely through close-range melee exchanges.

### Use the barrels and exposed weak point

At the beginning of the fight, identify the explosive barrels around the arena.

Move so the Iron Marshall approaches one of them, then shoot the barrel from a safe distance.

After the explosion, watch for a chance to move behind the boss.

The Iron Marshall has an exposed weak point on its back. The **Blunderbuss** is useful here because the large target gives you an opportunity to fire at close range.

A practical attack sequence is:

1. Draw the boss toward an explosive barrel.
2. Trigger the explosion while remaining outside its blast radius.
3. Dodge or parry the next heavy attack.
4. Move behind the boss during recovery.
5. Fire the Blunderbuss into its exposed weak point, then reposition.

The Marshall swings its cannon as a melee weapon. Watch for the wide sweeping attack, which can catch you even when you are standing beside it. When its body glows red before a forward cannon slam, dodge rather than attempting to block or parry.

When the boss raises its weapon for a heavy strike, wait for the actual attack before dodging. Its slower windups can catch players who evade as soon as the animation begins.

**Iron Marshall has 2,400 HP, 800 Posture, and one phase.** Defeating it grants **7,000 Catalysts** and completes the **Ultima Ratio Regum** achievement requirement.

### Collect Glass Soldier after the fight

Before leaving the boss area, check the building on the right when viewed from the direction you entered the arena.

Climb inside, break the wooden boxes, and drop through the rear opening to reach a chest containing **Glass Soldier**.

This Amber increases melee damage dealt but also increases damage received.

It can be useful for an aggressive combat setup, but collecting it does not mean you need to equip it immediately. Players still learning boss attacks may prefer an Amber without the incoming-damage penalty.

After defeating the Marshall, continue beyond the arena to obtain Grapple.

## How to Unlock Grapple and Continue After Iron Marshall

Defeating Iron Marshall does not finish Rendezvous.

Continue through the passage beyond the arena and acquire **Grapple**.

The ability lets you cross gaps using marked blue targets. Certain blue growths can also be used to move objects or pull structures into position.

![The blue Grapple target and traversal route after Iron Marshall.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-grapple-unlock.webp)

When a ledge seems unreachable, look for the glowing blue Grapple point and its interaction prompt.

Ordinary walls and roofs cannot be used as Grapple targets without the appropriate marker.

### Follow the blue targets toward Louis

Use Grapple to cross the water and climb into the higher part of the village.

Continue along the elevated route, moving between marked targets and platforms.

Some of the traversal points are attached to plague-covered pillars. After using the **second pillar**, check the area to the right.

A chest there contains an **Empty Ferrofluid Flask**, giving you another healing charge.

This is different from a Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade, which increases how much health each Flask restores.

As you continue, Musketeers and other ranged enemies can attack while you are approaching a Grapple landing. Deal with enemies covering the next platform before attempting a difficult crossing, or move through quickly when you have a clear route.

### Return to Quarry now or later?

Grapple also opens the previously unreachable upper route in Valley of Ashes.

You can return to **Quarry** to collect Pure Blade Material and a Ferrofluid Flask upgrade.

For the exact elevator, blue Grapple points, movable platform, and reward locations, follow the [Quarry return route](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes).

If you already have sufficient healing and weapon damage, finish the rescue before making that return trip. The Quarry rewards remain accessible afterward.

</section>

## Where to Find Louis and Finish Chapter 3

Beyond the Orchard, keep following the elevated Grapple route.

You will eventually approach a section of the village with a **burning roof and smoke rising above the buildings**.

Use the fire as a landmark while crossing the final platforms.

Continue toward the building's upper area to find **Louis**.

![Louis at the end of the elevated route beyond Orchard in Fallen Village.](/images/valor-mortis/fallen-village-louis-rescue.webp)

Speak with him to advance the rescue objective.

Louis does not require another key from Marketplace. The Storage Key has already served its purpose by opening the route through the plague wall, and the Weathered Key belongs to Lullaby.

Once you have reached Louis, return to the **Abandoned Church** and speak with James.

That conversation completes the Chapter 3 progression and sets up the investigation of the Salt Mine.

### What should you do before entering Chapter 4?

At the Church, you can spend Catalysts on weapon and healing upgrades.

If you want the additional materials in upper Quarry, you now have Grapple and can travel back there. The main story does not require that detour.

When you are ready to investigate the Salt Mine, speak with James to obtain the **Cave Grotto Key**, then follow the cliffside route into [Caverns](/valor-mortis/caverns).
