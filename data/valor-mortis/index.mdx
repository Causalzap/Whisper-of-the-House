**Valor Mortis has 11 main-story chapters**, starting on the Battlefield and ending in the Ancient Lair beneath the Salt Mine.

The route through these chapters is mostly linear, but the places you visit are not. A locked gate may become accessible after you obtain a key, a raised platform may require Grapple, and poisonous ground can remain impassable until much later. Clearing a boss also does not always advance the current objective: several chapters require a conversation or an item pickup before William can continue.

The **Abandoned Church** is the central point between many of these journeys. Once you have access to its facilities and the Lantern network, you can revisit unfinished investigations without walking through every earlier area again.

<section id="chapter-order" className="scroll-mt-24">

## All 11 Valor Mortis Chapters in Order

The main-story sequence is:

1. **Chapter 1 — Revival:** Battlefield and the first General Lothaire encounter.
2. **Chapter 2 — Regroup:** Valley of Ashes, Quarry, and Abandoned Church.
3. **Chapter 3 — Rendezvous:** Fallen Village, Iron Marshall, Grapple, and Louis.
4. **Chapter 4 — Dark Descent:** Caverns, Stasis, Salt Mine, and Sphinx.
5. **Chapter 5 — Crime:** Vale of Wrath, Musket, and Slaughtermill.
6. **Chapter 6 — Punishment:** Cursed Hollow, The Bride, and Claymore.
7. **Chapter 7 — Witch Hunt:** The Outskirts, City of Orphans, sewers, and Laboratory Key.
8. **Chapter 8 — Blasphemy:** University, Shield Transmutation, and The Orphan.
9. **Chapter 9 — In Ruins:** Fortress, signal fires, and Nephtis Superioris.
10. **Chapter 10 — Shackled:** Prison escape, Armoury Key, and equipment recovery.
11. **Chapter 11 — Reflection:** Ancient Lair and the final encounter with The Entity.

Reaching the last location in a chapter is not always enough to complete it. After Iron Marshall, for example, you still need to find Louis. After leaving the sewers, you need the Laboratory Key from the Shrike before entering the University.

Optional investigations and previously blocked paths can usually wait until the appropriate movement ability becomes available.

</section>

<section id="chapters-1-2" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 1–2: Battlefield, Valley of Ashes, and the Abandoned Church

### Chapter 1 — Revival

William begins on the **Battlefield**, where the opening encounters introduce melee attacks, firearms, parries, and Fire Transmutation.

The chapter ends with **General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer**.

This encounter has an unusual outcome: defeating Lothaire immediately grants additional Catalysts and changes what happens later in Valley of Ashes. Losing the first fight is also a valid progression route and does not require restarting the game.

For the different outcomes, rewards, and the later Quarry encounter, see [General Lothaire's boss fight](/valor-mortis/general-lothaire).

### Chapter 2 — Regroup

After the opening, follow **Valley of Ashes** from Guidepost through the ruined military positions toward Quarry.

The main route passes through **The Climb, Demolished Path, The Other Side, Military Camp, and Quarry**.

Several obstacles here serve different purposes. The damaged lift is part of normal progression, the missing scouts lead to an optional investigation, and the upper Quarry platforms require an ability you obtain in Chapter 3.

The poisoned passage near **The Other Side** is another later-return location. It belongs to **The Emissary** investigation and cannot be completed with the abilities available during your first visit.

Follow the [Valley of Ashes walkthrough](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) for the lift, scouts, Quarry route, and the return path after acquiring Grapple.

At Quarry, General Lothaire appears again if you did not defeat him during the opening encounter. Players who won that first fight do not need to repeat it.

Continue to the **Abandoned Church** afterward.

Speak with **James** to establish your next objective: locating Louis in Fallen Village.

</section>

<section id="chapters-3-4" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 3–4: Rescue Louis and Explore the Salt Mine

### Chapter 3 — Rendezvous

James sends William into **Fallen Village** to find Louis.

The route begins outside the Abandoned Church, where you acquire the **Rapier**, and continues through **Fields**, **Marketplace**, and **Orchard**.

You also gain access to the **Blunderbuss** during this chapter.

Marketplace is the area most likely to interrupt progression. The **Weathered Key** belongs to an optional house investigation, while the **Storage Key** helps you reach the weak point blocking the required Orchard route.

Once the obstruction is cleared, continue to **Iron Marshall**.

Defeating Iron Marshall leads to **Grapple**, which allows you to reach the elevated path beyond the boss arena. Follow it to find Louis, then return to James.

The [Fallen Village walkthrough](/valor-mortis/fallen-village) covers the key distinction, blocked Marketplace path, Grapple pickup, and Louis's location.

### Chapter 4 — Dark Descent

Louis's investigation takes William beneath the Salt Mine.

Speak with James at the Abandoned Church to obtain the **Cave Grotto Key**, then use the cliffside approach to reach **Caverns**.

The route runs through **Last Sunlight**, **Weeping Ledge**, and **Old Mine** before descending into the deeper mine.

You obtain **Stasis Transmutation** during this chapter. From that point onward, moving machinery can require timing, temporarily stopping a mechanism, or releasing it again at the correct moment.

The **Mining Shaft Key** is another important obstacle. It must be collected before you can open the corresponding lower route.

Follow the [Caverns walkthrough](/valor-mortis/caverns) if the moving platforms, minecart tracks, or Mining Shaft Key are preventing you from reaching the final chamber.

The chapter ends with **Sphinx, Devourer of the Unworthy**.

After the fight, return to the Abandoned Church and speak with Louis and James. Their next objective requires information from the University.

Obtain the **Outward Pass** before leaving for Vale of Wrath.

</section>

<section id="chapters-5-6" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 5–6: Vale of Wrath and Cursed Hollow

### Chapter 5 — Crime

The roads directly into the city are too heavily defended, so the next journey takes William through **Vale of Wrath**.

Use the Outward Pass to leave the Abandoned Church along the appropriate route.

The chapter proceeds through **Wolfsbane Field**, **Under the Bridge**, the military settlements, and **Ship Graveyard**.

You obtain the **Musket** during this journey. It provides a longer-range firearm option than the Pistol or Blunderbuss, which is useful when enemy positions are spread across open ground.

Your main objective is to reach and defeat **Slaughtermill**.

After the boss fight, continue through the exit passage near **Old Windmill**. You do not need to find another way out of the boss arena.

A clue connected to the missing imperial envoy can be collected along this exit route. If you began The Emissary earlier, take the **Emissary's Letter** with you. The investigation itself cannot be finished until Shield becomes available.

Continue into Cursed Hollow.

### Chapter 6 — Punishment

**Cursed Hollow** begins at **Eerie View** and leads through flooded villages, elevated walkways, and military positions toward **Village Church**.

The chapter's central boss is **The Bride**. Defeating her opens the next part of the route, but the wedding wreath near the arena is connected to an optional investigation that finishes later.

Three activities are worth recognizing before you leave:

- **Sleeping Beauty:** Find Headman's Key and resolve the spinning wheel choice.
- **The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory:** Bring blood or wine to the soldier hiding in his house.
- **Till Death:** Return a Red Ribbon found near Village Church to the Bride's wreath.

You also obtain the **Claymore** inside Village Church.

The [Cursed Hollow walkthrough](/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow) follows the route from Eerie View to the church and explains the three investigations, their choices, and the required return visits.

Completing those optional tasks is not necessary to begin Chapter 7.

Once you leave Village Church through the rear route, continue toward **The Outskirts**.

</section>

<section id="chapters-7-8" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 7–8: Reach the City, Find the Laboratory Key, and Unlock Shield

### Chapter 7 — Witch Hunt

The journey to the University begins in **The Outskirts**.

Two major encounters along this approach are **High Justicar Aurelius** and **Experiment 83**. Continue through the surrounding routes and toward the Lighthouse before entering the **City of Orphans**.

The city's rooftops, streets, buildings, and underground passages are interconnected. A locked gate on the surface may lead back to the same area through the sewers, rather than opening through a nearby switch.

The underground progression involves two different keys:

- **Sewers Key:** Opens the first required locked passage.
- **Sewers Master Key:** Opens the remaining gates marked with its name.

The second key is not an upgrade to the first one, and finding the ordinary key does not unlock every sewer door.

For both pickup locations, the platform crossing, locked gates, and useful shortcuts, follow the [Sewers Key and Master Key route](/valor-mortis/sewers-keys).

Your late-chapter objective is to encounter **the Shrike**.

You do not need to defeat her in a boss fight. Complete the conversation and obtain the **Laboratory Key**.

This is the item required for the next story destination. Having both sewer keys is not enough to enter the University.

### Chapter 8 — Blasphemy

The **University** contains several interconnected interior routes, including libraries, halls, and laboratory areas.

Follow the story objective through the building toward the laboratory. The most important new ability obtained during this chapter is **Shield Transmutation**.

Shield allows William to cross hazardous areas that were inaccessible earlier. It does not replace Grapple or Stasis, which remain necessary for their own traversal obstacles.

After obtaining Shield, continue into the University's underground sections and defeat **The Orphan**.

Recover the documents Louis needs, then return to the **Abandoned Church** and speak with him. His next objective leads toward Fortress.

Shield also allows you to return to earlier poison-blocked areas, including [The Emissary investigation](/valor-mortis/the-emissary). These optional returns do not have to be completed before Chapter 9.

</section>

## Chapters 9–11: Fortress, Prison Escape, and the Final Boss

### Chapter 9 — In Ruins

After speaking with Louis at the Abandoned Church, take the route toward **Fortress**. Shield allows you to cross the poisonous approach that was inaccessible earlier.

Inside the castle, the main obstacle is the gate controlled by **two signal fires**. Climb the outer towers and ignite both wooden pyres with Fire Transmutation to open the route forward.

You will also encounter two optional keys:

- **Captain's Key:** Opens the captain's quarters near Traverse.
- **Fortress Armory Key:** Opens the armory after a Stasis-based gear puzzle.

Neither key is required to reach the chapter boss, although both lead to useful healing upgrades.

The [Fortress walkthrough](/valor-mortis/fortress) covers the two signal fires, key locations, locked rooms, Stasis mechanism, and route through the castle's six Lanterns.

Continue through the upper fortifications toward **High Tower**, where you face **Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris**.

Defeating him completes Chapter 9 and leads into a story sequence in which William is captured. Consider spending Catalysts on needed upgrades before committing to the final Fortress encounter, since access to your normal equipment is temporarily restricted afterward.

### Chapter 10 — Shackled

William awakens in **Napoleon's Prison** without his weapons and equipment.

The immediate objective is to recover your possessions. Work through the prison corridors, climb to the upper floor, and cross the wooden beams to reach the kitchen containing **Armoury Key**.

Use the key on the locked Armoury downstairs, then open the red chest to retrieve your confiscated equipment.

Before leaving, there is one important decision: **Napoleon's Prison cannot be revisited after escaping**.

The optional **The Choir** investigation requires collecting the prison warden's journals, opening the chapel, and taking a list of deceased prisoners. The list is delivered later at a notice board in Burning City.

You can escape without completing this investigation, but you cannot return for its missing prison documents afterward.

The [Prison Escape walkthrough](/valor-mortis/prison-escape) explains the unarmed route, Armoury Key, equipment recovery, Chapel Key, The Choir, and the exit through the sewers.

After collecting what you need, follow the passage marked with the Shrike's symbol and continue through the sewers toward Burning City.

### Chapter 11 — Reflection

After escaping the prison, continue through the remaining story conversations until William receives the final objective.

The last chapter returns to the mystery beneath the Salt Mine.

Speak with **Lucjan** when you are ready to proceed. Choose the option to return to the cave, which takes you toward **Caverns → Ancient Lair**.

From the Ancient Lair Lantern, approach the large stone door at the end of the area.

Interacting with it begins the final encounter against **The Entity, Voice of the Void**.

Before starting the battle, finish any exploration or optional upgrades you still want to complete. Grapple, Stasis, and Shield can all be used to revisit earlier blocked routes.

For players pursuing all 39 achievements, check the [achievement roadmap and checklist](/valor-mortis/achievements) for unfinished collections, combat challenges, and Shield upgrades.

</section>

<section id="ability-unlocks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When Do You Unlock Grapple, Stasis, and Shield?

Each of the three major exploration abilities arrives during normal story progression.

- **Grapple — Chapter 3, Fallen Village:** Obtained after the Iron Marshall encounter. Used at marked blue Grapple points, including previously inaccessible elevated routes.
- **Stasis — Chapter 4, Caverns:** Obtained during the Weeping Ledge progression. Used to temporarily halt moving machinery and affect enemies.
- **Shield — Chapter 8, University:** Obtained through the University story route. Used to survive hazardous passages, including poison-filled areas that blocked earlier investigations.

The obstacle tells you which ability is missing. Blue Grapple targets need Grapple; moving machinery may need Stasis; dense poison often requires Shield.

You cannot bypass these requirements by repeatedly attacking a locked mechanism with Fire or your firearm.

</section>

<section id="return-later" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which Valor Mortis Areas Are Worth Returning to Later?

You do not need to revisit every discovered Lantern whenever a new ability unlocks. Two returns have particularly clear rewards or unfinished objectives.

### After Grapple: Valley of Ashes → Quarry

Once Grapple becomes available in Chapter 3, fast travel back to **Quarry**.

The upper platforms that were previously out of reach lead to additional weapon-upgrade materials and a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

You can make this return as soon as you have Grapple; there is no need to wait for the final chapters.

The [Valley of Ashes route](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) explains how to reach those upper platforms from the Quarry Lantern.

### After Shield: Valley of Ashes → The Other Side

After obtaining Shield in Chapter 8, return to **The Other Side**.

The poisoned passage there leads to the final investigation area for **The Emissary**, which you could not safely reach when first passing through Valley of Ashes.

You do not need to finish the main story before completing the investigation.

Follow [The Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary) for the required clues, blocked passage, and reward.

### Before your final cleanup

Keep track of unfinished optional investigations, Eternal Guardists, weapon upgrades, and combat challenges as you move through the last chapters.

Several achievements are easiest to complete while you are already near their required enemies or locations. The [39-achievement checklist](/valor-mortis/achievements) lets you track those goals without repeating the full campaign route.

</section>

## What Should You Save for Abyssal Eviction?

**Abyssal Eviction** requires knocking an enemy into an abyss with **Shield Bash**, which becomes available at **Shield Level 5**.

Do not spend all of your rare final-tier Transmutation materials on Fire and Stasis if you still need this achievement. The [achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) covers the material sources, upgrade priority, and suitable enemy location.

</section>

<section id="rapier-heavy-attacks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Can the Rapier Chain Three Heavy Attacks?

Yes. Holding the heavy-attack input allows the **Rapier** to chain **three consecutive heavy attacks**.

The sequence is useful during a long enemy recovery window, but committing to the entire chain leaves less time to dodge. You acquire the Rapier when leaving the Abandoned Church for [Fallen Village](/valor-mortis/fallen-village); it remains an option throughout later boss encounters.
