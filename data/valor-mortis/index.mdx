**Valor Mortis has 11 main-story chapters**, beginning on the Battlefield and ending with a return to the Ancient Lair beneath the Salt Mine.

The journey follows a mostly fixed order, but it frequently sends you back to earlier areas. Some paths are blocked until you acquire Grapple, Stasis, or Shield, while other apparent dead ends are optional investigations that can be finished later.

The Abandoned Church becomes your main base early in the game. Several important story transitions begin there, and fast travel through discovered Lanterns allows you to revisit locations without replaying every enemy encounter.

When you are unsure where to go, start with your current chapter. The next destination is usually determined by the last required boss, ability unlock, or conversation you completed.

<section id="chapter-order" className="scroll-mt-24">

## All 11 Valor Mortis Chapters in Order

The complete main-story order is:

1. **Chapter 1 — Revival:** The Battlefield and the first General Lothaire encounter.
2. **Chapter 2 — Regroup:** Valley of Ashes, Quarry, and the route to the Abandoned Church.
3. **Chapter 3 — Rendezvous:** Fallen Village, Iron Marshall, Grapple, and rescuing Louis.
4. **Chapter 4 — Dark Descent:** Caverns, Stasis, the Salt Mine, and Sphinx.
5. **Chapter 5 — Crime:** Vale of Wrath, the Musket, and Slaughtermill.
6. **Chapter 6 — Punishment:** Cursed Hollow, The Bride, and the Claymore.
7. **Chapter 7 — Witch Hunt:** The Outskirts, City of Orphans, and Laboratory Key.
8. **Chapter 8 — Blasphemy:** University, Shield Transmutation, and The Orphan.
9. **Chapter 9 — In Ruins:** Fortress, signal fires, and Nephtis Superioris.
10. **Chapter 10 — Shackled:** Prison escape, Armoury Key, and equipment recovery.
11. **Chapter 11 — Reflection:** Ancient Lair and the final encounter with The Entity.

Each chapter has a required destination, but not every locked door or side passage needs to be cleared before advancing.

The most useful progression check is your current objective. A newly acquired movement ability may let you revisit an earlier location, but it does not automatically replace the main mission you were following.

## Chapters 1–2: Survive the Battlefield and Reach the Church

### Chapter 1 — Revival

William begins on the Battlefield, learning the basics of melee combat, parrying, firearm attacks, and Fire Transmutation.

The opening ends with an encounter against **General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer**.

You can defeat him during this first encounter, but losing does not end the campaign. A normal defeat advances the story through Lucjan's intervention and into Chapter 2.

Winning immediately provides an unusually large Catalyst reward and changes the later encounter.

The complete consequences, including the early victory rewards and both combat phases, are covered in [General Lothaire's fight](/valor-mortis/general-lothaire).

### Chapter 2 — Regroup

The next area is **Valley of Ashes**.

Begin at Guidepost and follow the damaged bridge toward The Climb. After obtaining the Pistol, use it to destroy the red Nephtoglobin growths controlling the lift.

The main path continues through Demolished Path, The Other Side, Military Camp, and Quarry.


Before leaving Valley of Ashes, check which side routes you can finish now and which require returning later:

- **An Unheard Farewell:** The missing scouts are found through a cave entrance halfway along the lift shaft.
- **The Emissary:** You can collect the Seal at The Other Side, but the poisoned route requires a later ability.
- **Upper Quarry platforms:** These become reachable after acquiring Grapple in Chapter 3.

The [Valley of Ashes route](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) takes you through the lift, the scouts' investigation, and the Quarry approach.

Players defeated by Lothaire in Chapter 1 face him again near the end of Quarry. If you won the opening encounter, that later fight does not trigger.

After clearing the approach, continue into the **Abandoned Church**.

Speak with James, use the available upgrade facilities, and prepare to search for Louis in Fallen Village.

</section>

<section id="chapters-3-4" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 3–4: Find Louis and Investigate the Salt Mine

### Chapter 3 — Rendezvous

James sends you into **Fallen Village** to rescue Louis.

Leave the Abandoned Church by the village route. You collect the **Rapier** shortly after leaving, then continue through Fields toward Marketplace.

A Volunteer guards the carriage containing the **Blunderbuss**. This weapon provides strong close-range damage for the encounters ahead.

Marketplace contains two keys with different purposes.

The **Weathered Key** opens a riverside house for the optional Lullaby investigation. The **Storage Key**, found in an underground passage behind a house, opens the route to the weak point that removes the main-story plague wall.

Once the obstruction is cleared, continue to Orchard.

Defeat **Iron Marshall**, collect **Grapple**, and follow the blue traversal targets beyond the boss area to reach Louis.

The chapter is not finished simply because Iron Marshall is dead. You must find Louis and return to James at the Abandoned Church.

For the two keys, the Orchard fight, and the final rescue route, follow [Fallen Village](/valor-mortis/fallen-village).

### Chapter 4 — Dark Descent

Louis's investigation leads to a Salt Mine with a blocked main entrance.

Speak with James at the Church for the **Cave Grotto Key**, then use Grapple to reach the cliffside entrance into Caverns.

The underground route begins at **Last Sunlight**, passes through **Weeping Ledge**, and continues into **Old Mine**.

You acquire **Stasis Transmutation** during this chapter.

Moving platforms now require you to operate their controls, stop machinery at the correct position, and sometimes release Stasis so the mechanism can move again.

Deeper in the mine, a track switch redirects a minecart through an obstruction.

The **Mining Shaft Key** is hidden on a side platform partway along an elevator shaft. After collecting it, return to the locked door near the minecart junction and descend toward the final area.

The chapter ends with **Sphinx, Devourer of the Unworthy**, inside an ancient chamber beneath the Salt Mine.

If you are stuck at the moving machinery or cannot locate the Mining Shaft Key, follow the [Caverns route](/valor-mortis/caverns).

After the Sphinx encounter, William returns to the Abandoned Church.

Louis needs information from his old University laboratory. Speak with James to obtain the **Outward Pass** and begin the journey toward the city.

</section>

<section id="chapters-5-6" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 5–6: Cross Vale of Wrath and Cursed Hollow

### Chapter 5 — Crime

The main roads toward the city are heavily defended, so James directs William through **Vale of Wrath**.

Use the Outward Pass to leave the Abandoned Church along the appropriate route.

The journey continues through **Wolfsbane Field**, **Under the Bridge**, and the settlements beyond, eventually reaching **Ship Graveyard**.

The **Musket** is acquired during this chapter. It gives you a longer-range firearm option than the Pistol or Blunderbuss.

Progress through the military positions until you reach **Slaughtermill**, the required boss.

After defeating Slaughtermill, leave the boss platform through the exit passage near **Old Windmill**.

There is also a clue connected to the missing imperial envoy along this route. Check the right-hand side of the passage for **Emissary's Letter** if you collected the Seal earlier.

The letter adds to the investigation but does not unlock the poisoned route by itself. You will still need Shield later. For the complete investigation, see [The Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary).

Continue onward into Cursed Hollow.

### Chapter 6 — Punishment

**Cursed Hollow** takes you through another collection of ruined settlements, flooded approaches, and paths toward a village church.

One of the chapter's main encounters is **The Bride**.

The **Claymore** also becomes available through required progression, completing your access to the three main melee-weapon types.

The Claymore favors heavier attacks and wider swings, while Rapier and Saber offer different defensive trade-offs.

This is not a requirement to change weapons. Continue using whichever melee option matches your combat style and available upgrades.

Once you clear the required encounters and reach the exit from Cursed Hollow, continue toward **The Outskirts** for Chapter 7.

</section>

<section id="chapters-7-8" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 7–8: Find the Laboratory Key and Unlock Shield

### Chapter 7 — Witch Hunt

Chapter 7 starts in **The Outskirts**, the approach to the city.

Two major enemies stand along this progression: **High Justicar Aurelius** and **Experiment 83**.

Continue through the surrounding routes and toward the **Lighthouse**. After this section, the story moves deeper into the **City of Orphans**.

The city connects streets, rooftops, buildings, and underground passages. You will often reach an area from above or below rather than through the most obvious street-level entrance.

Your important late-chapter objective is the encounter with **the Shrike**.

The Shrike is part of the story progression, not another boss you need to defeat. Continue the required conversation and obtain the **Laboratory Key**.

That key is needed for the next destination: the University.

If you have reached the University entrance but cannot enter, finish the Shrike sequence in the City of Orphans before searching for another entrance.

### Chapter 8 — Blasphemy

The **University** contains interconnected libraries, large halls, elevators, and several routes that return to earlier rooms.

Follow the required route through the building toward the laboratory area.

The most important ability obtained here is **Shield Transmutation**, which becomes available during the University progression.

Shield lets William survive environmental hazards that previously blocked certain areas.

It does not replace Grapple or Stasis. Those abilities still have their own traversal and machinery uses.

After obtaining Shield, continue through the University until you reach **The Orphan**, the chapter's required boss.

Defeating The Orphan opens the way to the next major region, **Fortress**.

You can also return to older poison-filled routes at this point. One example is [The Emissary's investigation](/valor-mortis/the-emissary), which begins much earlier in Valley of Ashes.

</section>

<section id="chapters-9-11" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chapters 9–11: Fortress, Prison Escape, and the Final Encounter

### Chapter 9 — In Ruins

Chapter 9 takes place in **Fortress**.

The approaches through the fortifications eventually converge on the required route through the courtyards and higher defenses.

The **signal fires near Traverse** are part of opening the way forward.

After completing the necessary interactions, continue through the Chapel and Ramparts toward **Nephtis Superioris**.

The boss marks a major story transition.

Before entering the encounter, spend Catalysts that you intend to use for upgrades and check your equipment. After this section, William is taken captive, and the normal travel network is temporarily unavailable.

The next chapter begins with an escape rather than another expedition from the Abandoned Church.

### Chapter 10 — Shackled

William begins Chapter 10 imprisoned and without normal access to his equipment.

Your first priority is to recover your possessions.

Make your way toward the upper prison section and use the overhead beams to reach the **kitchen**.

The **Armoury Key** is located along this route.

After collecting it, return to the locked armoury door and retrieve your equipment.

Once your possessions are recovered, continue through the prison passages and sewer route toward the city.

There is also an optional investigation called **The Choir** in the prison. Complete it before leaving if you want to finish the activity while the prison route is available.

The main objective remains straightforward: recover your equipment, escape the prison, and return to the wider world.

### Chapter 11 — Reflection

After the prison escape, the story returns to the mystery beneath the Salt Mine.

Speak with **Lucjan** when you are ready to continue the final objective.

Choose the option to return to the cave, which takes you back toward **Caverns → Ancient Lair**.

From the Ancient Lair Lantern, approach the large stone door at the end of the area.

Interacting with it begins the final battle against **The Entity, Voice of the Void**.

Finish any exploration or optional investigations you want to complete before starting the encounter. By this stage, you have the abilities needed to return to earlier Grapple, Stasis, and Shield obstacles.

</section>

<section id="ability-unlocks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When Do You Unlock Grapple, Stasis, and Shield?

Three abilities are responsible for most of the routes that appear inaccessible during an earlier chapter.

- **Grapple — Chapter 3, Fallen Village:** Defeat Iron Marshall and continue beyond the arena to acquire it. Use the marked blue targets to cross gaps and reach elevated platforms.
- **Stasis — Chapter 4, Caverns:** Obtained during the Weeping Ledge progression. It temporarily stops moving machinery and affects enemies.
- **Shield — Chapter 8, University:** Obtained through the main-story library route. It provides protection needed to cross hazardous poison areas.

The required action depends on the obstruction in front of you.

A **blue Grapple point** needs Grapple. A rotating platform or moving mechanism may require Stasis. A passage filled with damaging poison may require Shield.

Trying to solve all three kinds of obstacles with Fire Transmutation or firearm attacks will not work.

</section>

## Which Areas Should You Return to Later?

Several rewards are accessible only after acquiring an ability in a later chapter. You can collect them when that ability becomes available without waiting until the end of the game.

### After Grapple: return to Quarry for weapon materials

Once you acquire Grapple in Chapter 3, fast travel to **Valley of Ashes → Quarry**.

Turn around from the Lantern and use the elevator to reach the higher section. The blue Grapple targets lead to movable platforms and the upper Quarry route.

There is another **Pure Blade Material** here, separate from the one inside the hidden scouts' cave at The Climb.

Continue along the upper route for a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

The [Quarry Grapple route](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) explains the platform order and both pickups.

This return is most useful after you have chosen a melee weapon to upgrade. You can obtain the material shortly after unlocking Grapple instead of waiting for a later chapter.

### After Stasis: revisit moving mechanisms when needed

Stasis opens routes involving moving platforms, gears, and other machinery.

Its first major use is in Caverns, where the crank and Stasis interactions are part of the required progression.

You may also encounter earlier mechanisms that behave differently once Stasis is available.

There is no need to revisit every previous Lantern immediately. Return to a particular mechanism when you have a reward or unfinished route you want to explore.

### After Shield: finish The Emissary

The clearest Shield return objective is **The Emissary**.

You can collect **Emissary's Seal** at The Other Side in Chapter 2, but the dense poison prevents you from completing the investigation during that first visit.

After obtaining Shield in Chapter 8, travel back to **Valley of Ashes → The Other Side**.

Cross the contaminated passage, investigate the hidden escort camp, and follow the clues to the bloodstained red fabric.

For the exact investigation points, blocked carriage weak point, and rewards, follow [The Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary).

Completing the main story is not required before returning. The important condition is having Shield available.


</section>

## Saving Transmutation Materials for Abyssal Eviction

If you are trying to earn **Abyssal Eviction**, do not spend every rare Transmutation upgrade material on Fire and Stasis.

The achievement requires you to knock an enemy into an abyss with **Shield Bash**, which becomes available when Shield reaches **Level 5**.

Keep a **Quintessential Transmutation Material** available for the Shield upgrade instead of using all of your limited materials on the other Transmutations.

Once you have Shield Bash, find an enemy near a ledge and use the ability to knock it off the edge.

</section>

<section id="rapier-heavy-attacks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Can You Chain Three Heavy Attacks With the Rapier?

Holding the attack input lets the **Rapier** chain **three consecutive heavy attacks**.

The full sequence is most useful when an enemy is staggered or has committed to an attack with a long recovery. Repeated thrusts can maintain pressure during that opening, but committing to all three attacks leaves less time to react if the enemy recovers.

Unlike the Saber, the Rapier cannot use a normal block. If an enemy begins another attack during your combination, you must rely on your dodge or parry timing.

Try the heavy-attack sequence against ordinary enemies first. Against stronger enemies, use the full chain only when you have enough time to finish it without taking a counterattack.

You can obtain the Rapier shortly after leaving the Abandoned Church for [Fallen Village](/valor-mortis/fallen-village).
