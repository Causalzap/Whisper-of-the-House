**Chapter 4: Dark Descent** takes you from Last Sunlight through Weeping Ledge and Old Mine, ending with the Sphinx at the bottom of the Salt Mine.

To reach the lower mine, unlock **Stasis**, operate the moving platforms, and redirect the minecart through the blocked passage. The **Mining Shaft Key** is on a ledge partway along an elevator shaft. After collecting it, return to the track junction to open the locked descent.

You can also collect additional Ferrofluid Flask upgrades along this route before fighting the Sphinx.

## How to Enter Caverns After Rescuing Louis

Chapter 4 begins after completing Rendezvous in Fallen Village.

Return to the **Abandoned Church** and speak with James. Louis has been investigating a Salt Mine, but its main entrance is inaccessible. James gives you the **Cave Grotto Key**, allowing you to reach the mine through the surrounding cave system.

Before leaving, you can visit Louis's station to purchase two healing upgrades:

- **Empty Ferrofluid Flask — 5,000 Catalysts:** Adds one healing charge.
- **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade — 3,000 Catalysts:** Increases the healing restored by a Flask.

The additional charge is useful before entering the mines. Several platforming sections contain moving machinery, and falling can consume resources you would otherwise need for enemies.

If you have only enough Catalysts for one upgrade, choose according to what has caused your recent deaths. Running out of Flasks favors an additional charge; surviving with several unused Flasks but taking large amounts of damage may make the power upgrade more attractive.

### Reach the cliffside entrance

Leave the Church and follow the cliffside route using the **Grapple** ability acquired in Fallen Village.

Cross to the cave-side path using the marked Grapple point. The locked cave door accepts the Cave Grotto Key.

You cannot open the main Salt Mine entrance by searching for another switch outside. The cliffside cave is the intended route into this chapter.

Once inside, rest at the **Last Sunlight Lantern** before descending.

## Last Sunlight: Find the Lower Tunnel and Open the Shortcut

From Last Sunlight, follow the main passage into a large cavern containing enemies at several different elevations.

Some passages loop around the chamber, while others descend to lower tunnels. The main route continues deeper, but there is a useful optional investigation near the beginning.

Close to a memory of a concerned mother, look for a note called **Longing Wife Letter**.

Picking it up begins **Homesick**.

### Where to find the Death Notice for Homesick

Continue into the larger enemy chamber and look for an opening in the floor leading to a lower tunnel.

Follow the descending passage toward a Grenadier. Beyond the enemy, cross the water to reach a boat.

The **Death Notice** is on the boat.

Read it, then return to the area where you originally found the Longing Wife Letter. William acknowledges what happened, completing the investigation.

The reward is another **Bottomless Well** Amber, increasing its rank if you already possess the earlier version.

Because the two letters are relatively close together, finishing the quest during your first exploration avoids another journey through the lower tunnels.

### Two optional pickups near the first cavern

The large chamber also contains **Dominatus** and **Lucky Shot**.

Dominatus comes from a tougher enemy near a campfire along the left side of the cavern. Players using critical attacks regularly may want the duplicate Amber, but the fight is optional.

Lucky Shot is in a chest near the Grenadier in the lower tunnel. It gives firearm attacks a chance not to consume ammunition.

The lower route also connects toward the Death Notice, so collecting Lucky Shot fits naturally into the Homesick detour.

After completing any exploration you want here, continue toward **Weeping Ledge**.

## Weeping Ledge: How to Unlock Stasis

Weeping Ledge contains narrow passages and a chamber crowded with Carrion enemies.

Watch for the opening that leads down into the enemy-filled room. The exit is obstructed by golden Nephtoglobin growths.

Use **Fire Transmutation** to burn the growths and continue.

A chest to the left of this obstruction contains **Lunatics Grasp**. It increases Ignite and Stasis buildup inflicted on enemies, but also lowers your resistance to those effects.

You can collect the Amber now without equipping it. The reduced resistance is worth considering before entering areas with frequent status attacks.

### Turn the crank to reach Stasis

Farther along the route, you reach a rotating walkway operated by a crank.

Activate the crank to move the walkway.

Continue through this progression sequence to obtain **Stasis Transmutation**.

Unlike Grapple, which works on designated blue traversal targets, Stasis can temporarily halt certain moving mechanisms. It also affects enemies during combat.

This ability changes the way the mine's platforms are crossed.

### Stop moving machinery at the right moment

The basic interaction has two parts:

1. **Operate the crank or control** to start moving the platform.
2. **Use Stasis on the mechanism** when the moving section reaches a position you can cross.

A stopped platform is only helpful when it is aligned with your destination.

Using Stasis too early may freeze the machinery before it reaches the required height or angle. Wait until you can see a viable crossing, then stop the motion.

![A glowing Stasis interaction target on machinery inside the Caverns.](/images/valor-mortis/caverns-stasis-control.webp)

Once the walkway is positioned correctly, cross and continue toward the next Lantern.

Open shortcut ladders whenever you reach the opposite side. A failed jump is much less expensive when a ladder provides a shorter return route.

## Old Mine: Use the Suspended Crate to Cross the Gap

At **Old Mine**, the machinery becomes the route itself.

Near the beginning of the area, a crane controls a suspended crate that moves between different heights.

Use the crate to cross the gap and reach the ladder on the opposite side.

The elevated platforms beyond the ladder also lead to an optional **Eternal Guardist** encounter.

### How to use Stasis on the suspended crate

The crate can be stopped while moving.

Operate its control, then watch the crate's height. Apply Stasis when the crate reaches a position you can safely access.

![The suspended cargo crate used to reach another level in Old Mine.](/images/valor-mortis/caverns-old-mine-suspended-crate.webp)

Some routes require more than freezing the crate in place.

Once you are standing on it, releasing Stasis lets the machinery continue moving. You can use that motion to reach the higher platform.

This is different from the earlier rotating walkway, where the main objective was to hold a crossing steady.

**The crate can serve as both a bridge and a lift**, depending on when you stop or release it.

Do not jump directly toward the highest ledge if the crate has not carried you close enough. Let the mechanism create a reachable landing.

### Optional Eternal Guardist

From the upper crane platforms, follow the side path toward a cave containing an **Eternal Guardist**.

Defeating it advances **The Oathbound** and rewards an emblem. A chest behind the enemy contains another rank of **Perfectionism**.

This encounter is not required to reach the Salt Mine.

You can return after improving your weapon or healing capacity rather than spending all your resources fighting it now.

### Get the Empty Ferrofluid Flask

Return toward the lower crane route and enter the tunnel to the left.

At the first left turn, you will find another moving platform. Stop it with Stasis in a position that allows you to cross.

A chest on the opposite side contains an **Empty Ferrofluid Flask**.

Collecting another healing charge before the minecart tunnels is useful because several upcoming mistakes result in falls or repeated environmental damage rather than ordinary combat exchanges.

With the lower shortcut opened, continue into the tunnels containing moving minecarts.

## Old Mine Minecarts: Avoid the Tracks and Change Their Direction

The next section contains narrow tunnels with minecarts passing through on rails.

A cart can kill you even when you are otherwise prepared for the enemies nearby. Before entering a track, watch the cart complete a pass and identify a safe place to stand.

The carts can also strike enemies.

When an opponent is positioned near the rails, you can draw its attention toward the track and let an approaching cart deal with it. This avoids spending ammunition and healing on an enemy that does not have to be fought directly.

The main progression eventually reaches a wooden control mechanism near a section of blocked track.

### Use the track switch to redirect the minecart

At the junction, look for the **wooden operating lever beside the rails**.

![The wooden minecart switch beside the tracks and blocked tunnel.](/images/valor-mortis/caverns-minecart-track-switch.webp)

Operate the control to change the cart's route.

The redirected minecart strikes the obstruction ahead, opening access to the passage beyond.

You do not need to destroy that barrier with repeated Pistol shots or grenades.

The important action is changing the cart's direction, then allowing the moving cart to clear the obstacle.

Beyond the newly opened route is an elevator that connects toward an earlier part of Old Mine.

Do not leave the elevator area without checking its intermediate landing. That is where the Mining Shaft Key is found.

## Mining Shaft Key Location: Stop at the Elevator's Middle Ledge

The Mining Shaft Key is not obtained from the track switch itself.

After following the minecart route through the broken obstruction, reach the elevator connecting back toward the Old Mine Lantern.

Ride the elevator and look along the shaft as it moves.

There is a **side ledge between its normal stopping points**, with a body holding the Mining Shaft Key.

Step off the elevator onto that intermediate platform and search the body.

![The Mining Shaft Key pickup on a small ledge beside the elevator route.](/images/valor-mortis/caverns-mining-shaft-key.webp)

If the elevator takes you all the way to its normal destination and you have not found the key, ride it again and look for the opening partway along the shaft.

### Where to Use the Mining Shaft Key

After collecting the key, return to the **minecart track junction** where you redirected the cart.

The locked door beside the control mechanism accepts the **Mining Shaft Key**. Open it, then take the elevator beyond the door down into the lower mine.

If you have already returned to the Old Mine Lantern, follow the shortcut back toward the minecart junction rather than searching for another locked door elsewhere.

## Lower Mine: Ferrofluid Flask Upgrade and Limit Break Location

After opening the Mining Shaft door, ride the elevator down and follow the wooden walkways along the shaft.

The vertical route includes several platforms at different heights. Look for ladders and marked Grapple points rather than attempting to drop directly to the lowest visible floor.

There are two useful optional rewards in this section.

### Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade

A **Volunteer** guards a chest containing a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

Defeat it and open the chest if you need stronger healing before the Sphinx. The Volunteer does not block the main route, so you can continue down the shaft without fighting it.

### Limit Break Amber

The multilevel cavern with wooden platforms and a rope bridge also hides **Limit Break**.

From the lower portion of that chamber, explore in the direction opposite the rope bridge.

A Grapple target beside a wall-running section leads toward a concealed corner beneath a ramp.

The chest contains Limit Break, which converts healing into temporary additional health.

This can be useful for preparing to enter a dangerous encounter with extra temporary health, although it depends on how you use your Flasks.

Neither reward is required to advance.

Once you have collected the items you want, open the available shortcut and continue toward the Lantern near the final machinery section.

## How to Get Through the Final Minecart Sequence

The last major traversal challenge is a series of moving machinery interactions leading toward the bottom of the Salt Mine.

This section combines the controls you have learned throughout Caverns.

You must operate mechanisms, freeze moving targets, and cross between safe platforms while the machinery is correctly positioned.

The important difference from the earlier walkway is that some actions happen while the cart or platform is already moving.

### Work through one mechanism at a time

As you approach each mechanical obstacle:

1. Identify the next usable control before committing to the crossing.
2. Operate the mechanism to move the platform or cart.
3. Apply Stasis when the next target reaches a usable position.
4. Cross to stable ground before preparing the following interaction.

Some controls display a **USE** prompt when you look toward the correct mechanical target.

A blue-glowing element can help distinguish the interactive mechanism from the surrounding framework, but the prompt and your current position still matter.

The mistake to avoid is activating the first mechanism successfully and immediately rushing toward the next without checking its movement.

During a moving-cart section, you may need to use a control while already traveling. Keep the next target in view instead of turning away to fight every nearby enemy.

### What happens if Stasis ends too soon?

If you are still on a safe platform, operate the mechanism again and wait for a better crossing position.

Trying to compensate for a badly aligned platform with a longer jump is unreliable in the narrow mine shafts.

The route also includes places where Stasis must be released so the machinery can continue moving.

Think about whether your next action requires the object to **remain still** or **resume movement**.

That decision is more useful than repeatedly activating Stasis whenever the control becomes available.

After clearing the consecutive machinery sections, follow the route to the bottom of the Salt Mine.

## Sphinx: How to Survive the Chapter 4 Boss

The industrial mine gives way to a sandy chamber with ancient Egyptian structures.

Here you encounter **Sphinx, Devourer of the Unworthy**.

![The Sphinx at the end of the Salt Mine, recognizable by its enormous golden head.](/images/valor-mortis/caverns-sphinx-arena.webp)

This is the required boss at the end of Dark Descent.

The Sphinx combines close-range attacks with Stasis effects. Its most dangerous patterns become easier to handle when you separate the ordinary melee combinations from the expanding ground attacks.

### The ground shock wave

When the Sphinx prepares a glowing Stasis attack and releases a pulse across the ground, watch the expanding wave.

**Jump over the approaching pulse.**

Dodging sideways along the ground does not reliably avoid an expanding wave that continues moving across the arena.

Later in the fight, the boss can release several pulses in succession.

Wait for the entire sequence to finish before moving back in for a long melee punish.

### Melee combinations and the second phase

The first phase includes fast claw combinations and other close-range strikes.

Parry the regular attacks when you recognize the timing, but watch for the unblockable jumping attack rather than attempting to parry every final hit.

After the phase change, a glowing weak point becomes exposed on the Sphinx's head.

That provides a useful firearm target. The Blunderbuss can deal substantial damage at close range, while the Pistol is more appropriate when you need distance.

The second phase also introduces more aggressive Stasis attacks and additional ranged pressure.

Preserve enough ammunition and healing to take advantage of the exposed weak point without remaining beside the boss during every attack.

Defeating the Sphinx awards **20,000 Catalysts** and the **Not a Cat Person** achievement.

## Where to Go After Defeating the Sphinx

After defeating the Sphinx, William returns to the **Abandoned Church**.

Speak with **Louis and James** about what you discovered beneath the Salt Mine. Louis needs information from his former laboratory, but the route ahead does not lead directly from Caverns into the University.

James explains that the main roads into the city are heavily guarded and directs you toward another route.

Receive the **Outward Pass**, then leave the Church to begin **Chapter 5: Crime**.

Your next destination is **Vale of Wrath**, where the journey continues toward the city.

Before leaving, use the Catalysts earned from the Sphinx if you have enough for a weapon or healing upgrade. You now have Stasis, but no additional ability is required to start the next chapter.
