The Fortress is built around several routes that cross over one another. An upper walkway may lead to a new Lantern, a ladder may return to a courtyard you visited earlier, and a locked door can turn out to contain an upgrade rather than the way forward.

There are two decisions worth making before spending an hour exploring every branch. First, the **two signal fires are required** to open the main progression gate. Second, **Captain's Key and Fortress Armory Key are optional**. Both unlock useful healing upgrades, but you can reach the chapter boss without collecting either key.

The route through Chapter 9 runs from the castle entrance toward **Main Gate, Traverse, Chapel, Ramparts, Courtyard, and High Tower**. Those six Lanterns are useful landmarks when trying to work out whether a passage is new or simply a shortcut back to an earlier section.

## Where to Enter the Fortress After the University

Chapter 9 begins after William finishes the University investigation and returns to the **Abandoned Church** with the information Louis needs.

Speak with Louis before leaving. The next objective takes you to the ruined castle, where the relic connected to his investigation is believed to be hidden.

From the church, head toward the route where you previously collected the Rapier before entering Fallen Village. Instead of following the village path, take the turn toward the castle ruins.

The approach winds between old stone walls and eventually leads into a poisoned passage. This is one of the routes that could not be explored safely before obtaining **Shield Transmutation**.

### Cross the poison and open the shortcut

Activate Shield before entering the dense poison and continue through the passage.

You need the protection granted by the ability itself. **Shield Level 5 is not required** to enter Fortress.

Where the path reaches a gate barred from the opposite side, continue through the accessible route until you can open it from behind. Opening these gates reduces the distance between later attempts and the earlier Lanterns.

Beyond the poisoned section, follow the stone road toward the castle.

![The stone road and ruined walls leading toward the Fortress entrance.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-ruined-castle-approach.webp)

The castle is visible ahead as the path narrows between the cliffs and ruined fortifications.

### From Main Gate to Traverse

The first defended area contains soldiers, mutated enemies, and several branches around the outer fortifications.

A powerful enemy near the early approach can award the **Fire Breathing** Amber. This is an optional encounter, so there is no need to spend every healing charge fighting it before reaching a useful checkpoint.

Continue over the damaged bridge and through the fortifications, activating the **Main Gate Lantern** along the way.

As you climb, look for blue Nephtoglobin activation points that extend platforms across gaps. Some nearby doors are barred from the far side and open only after you circle around through an elevated route.

Reach **Traverse** and activate its Lantern before beginning the signal-fire climb.

This checkpoint will become particularly useful when you return for the Captain's Key door or the upgrade chest behind the main gate.

## How to Light Both Fortress Signal Fires and Open the Gate

The main progression gate near Traverse does not require Captain's Key or Fortress Armory Key.

It opens after you ignite **two signal fires** on separate elevated routes.

Each signal fire is a wooden pyre covered in material that can be burned with Fire Transmutation. The hard part is reaching both towers through the ruined walls, wooden platforms, and Nephtoglobin crossings.

### Reach and light the first signal fire

From the outer Fortress route, climb the wooden structures attached to the ruined tower.

Activate the blue Nephtoglobin mechanisms wherever the next platform is out of reach, and use Grapple to cross the gaps.

The path eventually leads to an elevated platform holding a large wooden pyre.

Aim **Fire Transmutation** at the pyre until it catches fire.

The burning structure confirms that the first signal has been activated. There is no need to search the surrounding room for another switch.

### Reach and light the second signal fire

Continue along the exterior wall toward the next tower.

The route contains a glowing red weak point that obstructs access to another section of the wall. Shoot the weak point with a firearm, then use the newly accessible Grapple and Nephtoglobin interactions to continue around the structure.

Climb into the second defended tower and clear the enemy guarding the upper section.

At the top, ignite the second wooden pyre with Fire Transmutation.

![The second Fortress signal fire burning on its elevated stone tower.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-second-signal-fire.webp)

Once both fires are lit, the gate-opening sequence plays.

### Where is the gate that opens?

The opened gate is back on the main Fortress progression route near **Traverse**.

Return toward the junction connecting the tower climb and the larger stone passages. Use the previously opened shortcuts rather than repeating every elevated crossing.

Go through the newly opened gate and continue toward the inner castle.

If the gate is still closed, check both pyres. Merely reaching the top of a tower does not activate its signal; the wooden structure must actually be burning.

There is no third pyre to find.

One other distinction matters here: the Fortress also contains a hidden bonfire for **Frenzied Flame**. That is a separate optional interaction and does not replace either of the two signal fires.

## The Upgrade Chest Behind the Signal-Fire Gate

Before following the main corridor deeper into the castle, check the area immediately beyond the gate opened by the two signal fires.

From **Fortress → Traverse**, pass through that gate and look to the **left**.

A chest contains a **Quintessential Transmutation Material**, one of the rare materials used for the final Transmutation upgrade.

This is a valuable pickup because the known maximum-level Transmutation materials are limited. Another is awarded for completing Monster Hunt in the City of Orphans.

You can collect the Fortress material as soon as the two signals are active. Neither Fortress key is required.

If you have not obtained Shield Bash yet, preserve a Quintessential Transmutation Material for **Shield Level 5**. That upgrade is needed for the Abyssal Eviction achievement. The [39-achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) covers the full upgrade requirements and a suitable location for the final knockback challenge.

### Do you need to search every chest now?

Fortress also contains weapon materials, Amber pickups, and healing upgrades behind optional routes.

The signal-fire chest is particularly convenient because it lies beside the required progression path. The Captain's Key and Fortress Armory Key rewards take longer detours.

Collect the easy material now. Leave the more distant locked rooms until you have reached their nearby Lanterns, especially if you are carrying enough Catalysts that losing them would interrupt your next upgrade.

The castle's exposed walkways are also covered by artillery and ranged enemies. Cross quickly when the next landing is safe, and avoid stopping on a narrow wooden platform to fight a distant cannon crew.

## Captain's Key and Fortress Armory Key: Locations and Locked Doors

The two Fortress keys are associated with the passages around **Ramparts**.

They open different rooms, but both reward exploration with a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

The names are easy to confuse because the next chapter also contains an Armoury Key. That later item belongs to Napoleon's Prison and cannot open either Fortress door.

### Captain's Key: where to find it

Begin at the **Ramparts Lantern**.

Backtrack down the ladder into the large room containing a blue swing point above an open gap.

Use the swing point to cross toward the doorway on the left, then descend the stairs into the lower passages.

A golden web-like growth blocks one of the routes. Burn it with Fire Transmutation to open the way into a longer corridor.

Follow the corridor to its end and inspect the ground near the far corner to collect **Captain's Key**.

This key opens the captain's quarters, not the Fortress armory.

### Where to use Captain's Key

Fast travel or return to the **Traverse Lantern**.

Face the locked door across from the Lantern and use Captain's Key to enter the captain's quarters.

Search the chest inside for a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

A Flask Power Upgrade improves the amount of health restored by each charge. It is different from an Empty Ferrofluid Flask, which increases the number of healing charges.

The room is optional. Missing this upgrade will not prevent the main gate from opening or stop you from reaching Captain Raphael.

### Fortress Armory Key: the Stasis gear puzzle

The second key requires another trip through the passages below Ramparts.

Start at the **Ramparts Lantern** and descend to the large room with the blue swing point.

Cross to the adjoining area and continue downstairs into the interior passage system.

Follow the main corridor forward until it ends. Turn **right**, then **left**, to reach a chamber containing a lever-operated mechanism and rotating gears.

The nearby gate opens when the lever is operated, but the machinery can return to its starting position before you get through.

The intended sequence is:

1. **Pull the lever** to open the gate.
2. Look toward the rotating gears controlling the mechanism.
3. Apply **Stasis Transmutation** to stop the gears while the gate is open.
4. Cross through the passage before the mechanism resumes.
5. In the next room, watch for the large enemy and locate the ladder on the left.
6. Climb the ladder and collect **Fortress Armory Key**.

![The Fortress armory winch and the gate beyond it, marked after the opening mechanism is activated.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-armory-stasis-gate-open.webp)

The lever and Stasis have different jobs. The lever opens the passage; Stasis keeps the moving mechanism from immediately closing it.

If the gate shuts before you pass through, return to the lever and repeat the sequence. Apply Stasis while the gears are moving, rather than waiting for the gate to close.

The key is beyond the mechanism, up the ladder in the adjoining room. It is not lying beside the lever itself.

### Where to use Fortress Armory Key

Return to the large room with the blue swing point.

The locked Fortress armory is nearby. Open it using **Fortress Armory Key** and search the chest inside for another **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

There is no need to take the key outside Fortress or carry it into the prison chapter.

### Should you get both keys before Captain Raphael?

The two upgrades are useful if Raphael or the enemies leading to his arena are exhausting your healing supplies.

However, **neither key is mandatory**.

Once the signal-fire gate has opened, the castle has a route that continues upward without requiring you to clear the captain's quarters or Fortress armory.

If you already have strong Flask recovery, continuing through the main castle can be more efficient than collecting both optional keys immediately.

## Follow the Main Route Through Chapel, Ramparts, and Courtyard

After the signal-fire gate, the Fortress becomes a sequence of large interior halls, exterior battlements, and routes that reconnect at different elevations.

The important checkpoints are **Chapel**, **Ramparts**, and **Courtyard**.

Activate each Lantern as you reach it. The shortcuts between them make the Fortress Armory Key detour easier to revisit and reduce the distance back to the main route after a death.

### Recognize the route that actually advances the chapter

A side passage can end at a locked door, while a higher staircase or exterior crossing continues toward the next section of the castle.

When you return to a familiar room, check whether a ladder, gate, or previously inaccessible upper path has been opened. The Fortress frequently connects old and new routes this way.

You can complete the main chapter without collecting the optional keys.

Continue climbing through the fortifications toward the **Courtyard** and the large ruined hall beyond it.

### The elite officer in the ruined nave

A powerful Eternal Guardsman waits in the bloodstained hall beyond the Courtyard route.

His sword combinations and lunging attacks can punish long melee commitments. Stay clear of the end of his attack strings, then counter while he recovers.

Defeating him awards **20,000 Catalysts** and the **Glass Soldier** Amber.

The fight also leads to an important separate investigation.

Near the altar at the far end of the hall, examine the body to obtain:

- **Bastien Beaufort's Emblem**
- **Mysterious Stone Pyramid**

These items belong to **The Oathbound**.

The body must be investigated separately after the enemy is defeated. The Amber drop and the investigation items are not the same reward.

If you have already defeated the Eternal Guardists in earlier regions, this is an important step toward finishing their investigation. Keep the items and check the [achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) for the remaining overall completion conditions.

### Optional detours beneath Chapel

The underground passages beneath Chapel contain additional collectibles, including a **Quintessential Blade Material** in a trapped chest.

There is also a hidden bonfire associated with Frenzied Flame. It is not one of the two main signal fires, and lighting it does not advance the Fortress gate objective.

These are worthwhile completion pickups, but you do not need to clear the entire underground area before proceeding toward High Tower.

## Reach High Tower and the Final Fortress Lantern

Beyond the ruined hall, follow the upper walkways through the later Courtyard route.

You will encounter additional ranged enemies, damaged beams, and Nephtoglobin platforms that must be activated before crossing the gaps.

One of the crossings leads to a ladder that can be dropped from the far side. Open that shortcut so a failed attempt does not require repeating the entire exposed platform sequence.

Continue through the battlements and use the marked Grapple points to cross the larger gaps above the castle.

### The moving platform before High Tower

The final approach includes a large circular lifting platform.

Step onto the platform and activate its central mechanism to ride upward.

At the top, continue toward the **High Tower Lantern**.

This is your last useful checkpoint before Captain Raphael.

Rest to restore your healing charges and prepare your equipment. If you want to collect the remaining Fortress upgrades, you can return through the Lantern network before beginning the fight.

The next stairs lead directly toward the boss arena.

## How to Beat Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris

**Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris** is the final boss of Chapter 9.

He has **4,000 HP across two phases** and combines sword attacks with a firearm, defensive reactions, and Transmutation abilities.

Unlike many earlier enemies, Raphael can block or parry your attacks. He can also use a Ferrofluid Flask to restore his health, turning an otherwise successful exchange into lost progress if you give him too much room.

![Nephtis Superioris confronting William before the final Fortress boss fight.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-captain-raphael-boss.webp)

### Phase 1: punish the end of his sword combinations

Raphael's ordinary sword attacks are parryable, making controlled defense an effective way to wear down his posture.

His longer combinations are more dangerous than the opening swings suggest. Wait for the final committed strike before beginning a heavy attack.

Watch for three particularly important patterns:

- **Running thrust:** Raphael closes distance quickly. Dodge sideways when he commits rather than retreating straight backward.
- **Pistol attacks:** He can fire several shots. Do not assume the first shot ends the sequence.
- **Transmutation shockwave:** A slam produces an expanding wave that can be jumped over.

When he finishes a combination, attack during the recovery and then prepare for his next move.

Repeatedly attacking into his guard gives him opportunities to parry and counter.

### Interrupt his Ferrofluid Flask healing

Raphael can restore his own health.

Watch for him raising a glowing Flask. The drinking animation creates an opening because he briefly stops attacking.

Close the distance and strike, or use a firearm when you cannot safely reach him.

Interrupting the healing attempt prevents you from having to remove the restored health afterward.

This is one reason excessive retreating makes the fight more difficult. Keeping a manageable distance gives you a better chance to respond when he reaches for the Flask.

### Phase 2: prepare for the extended attacks

Raphael becomes more aggressive in the second phase.

His mutated attacks introduce wider coverage and faster follow-ups. The longer strings make it easier to mistake a temporary gap for a completed combination.

Keep enough Endurance available to dodge the attacks that cannot be blocked normally.

When he leaps or begins a heavily committed attack, evade the impact and punish his landing or recovery rather than trying to interrupt every movement.

His firearm and healing behavior remain relevant throughout the encounter, so the overall priority stays the same: survive the full attack sequence, take a short opening, and stop him from recovering health freely.

### Rewards and the Chapter 10 transition

Defeating Captain Raphael awards **50,000 Catalysts** and the **Behemoth** achievement.

The victory also completes **Chapter 9: In Ruins**.

Continue through the area beyond the arena to trigger the next story sequence.

William is subsequently captured, beginning **Chapter 10: Shackled**, where recovering his confiscated equipment becomes the immediate objective.

Before committing to the final Fortress encounter, consider spending any Catalysts you intend to use for upgrades. Normal equipment access is temporarily restricted during the opening of the prison chapter.

You can leave the remaining optional Fortress rooms for later if their rewards are not immediately needed.
