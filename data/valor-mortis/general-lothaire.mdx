General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer, first appears at the end of **Chapter 1: Revival**. If he defeats you there, you encounter him again near the end of **Chapter 2: Regroup**. If you win the opening fight, the later battle does not trigger.

An early victory awards **10,000 Catalysts** and **Commander's Catalyst Clot**, which can be consumed for another 10,000. Losing the opening fight does not prevent you from reaching the Abandoned Church or earning the Torn Banner achievement later.

In combat, Lothaire uses delayed sword combinations, cannon attacks, and exposed Nephtoglobin weak points. His second phase adds red-glowing unblockable attacks, ground waves, and summoned soldiers. Parry his regular strikes to break posture, but dodge when an unblockable attack begins.

<section id="first-fight" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Do You Have to Beat General Lothaire in Chapter 1?

No. Lothaire appears near the end of **Chapter 1: Revival**, when William has limited equipment, healing, and character upgrades.

If Lothaire kills you, the game continues into a story sequence involving Lucjan. William survives, and Chapter 2 begins without requiring you to retry that fight.

### Is the early victory worth attempting?

Winning early grants a substantial amount of Catalysts, but the encounter demands consistent parries and careful healing management. You also have to survive both phases with the equipment available at the end of Chapter 1.

The additional Catalysts can accelerate your initial character upgrades. For a normal first playthrough, however, continuing after the loss lets you gain more health, Endurance, and combat experience before the later encounter.

A normal defeat also closes the opening encounter rather than returning you to its entrance for another attempt.

</section>


<section id="early-victory" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Do You Get for Beating Lothaire Early?

An early victory grants **10,000 Catalysts** and **Commander's Catalyst Clot**.

The victory notification displays the immediate Catalyst reward.

![General Lothaire's victory notification showing 10,000 Catalysts.](/images/valor-mortis/general-lothaire-first-win-reward.webp)

The Clot is a separate consumable. Its item description confirms that using it grants another **10,000 Catalysts**.

![Commander's Catalyst Clot grants 10,000 Catalysts when used.](/images/valor-mortis/general-lothaire-commanders-catalyst-clot.webp)

Together, the immediate reward and the Clot provide **20,000 Catalysts**.

There is one useful distinction between them: the first 10,000 becomes carried currency immediately, while the other 10,000 remains stored in your inventory until you consume the Clot.

Spend carried Catalysts at the next opportunity if you need health or Endurance upgrades. Keep the Clot unused until you are ready to spend its value, particularly before entering an unfamiliar region where dying could put your carried currency at risk.

Winning the opening encounter also earns **Torn Banner** and removes the later Lothaire fight near the Abandoned Church. The main story continues into Chapter 2, but you can proceed through Quarry without fighting him again.

</section>


<section id="normal-rematch" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Is Lothaire if You Lost the Chapter 1 Fight?

After losing the opening encounter, you meet Lothaire again beyond **Quarry**, near the end of **Chapter 2: Regroup**.

The [Valley of Ashes route](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) takes you through Military Camp and up to the Quarry approach. Rest at the Quarry Lantern before entering the arena.

### What should you prepare?

The starting **Saber** is enough to defeat Lothaire. You do not need the Rapier, which becomes available after reaching the Abandoned Church.

Focus your available upgrades on surviving his combinations and having enough Endurance to retaliate after defending.

**Dominatus**, found in Military Camp, restores health from critical attacks. Equip it if you are comfortable breaking posture and following up with critical strikes.

Bring Pistol ammunition for the exposed Nephtoglobin growths and reserve some Fire Transmutation resources for safe damage openings.

You are preparing for two health bars, not a single short exchange. Consistent defence and enough remaining healing for phase two matter more than increasing your damage at the expense of survivability.

</section>


<section id="phase-one" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Phase 1: Parry the Delayed Swings, Then Punish

Lothaire's sword attacks have long windups. He raises the weapon, holds the attack, and then strikes after a noticeable delay.

Dodging or parrying as soon as his arm rises is likely to leave you exposed when the actual hit arrives.

His regular melee combinations can contain **two or three attacks**. Watch the weapon through each swing and wait until the combination finishes before committing to a long punish.

Successful parries build posture damage. Once his posture breaks, move in for a critical attack rather than spending the opening on ordinary light strikes.

### Ranged attacks and the jumping slam

Lothaire can fire a cannon volley from range. Move laterally and dodge the incoming shots instead of standing still to return fire.

His jumping slam requires a different response. Wait for the descent, dodge near the moment of impact, and attack during the recovery if you remain close enough.

![General Lothaire in the battlefield arena, showing the distance used to read his attacks.](/images/valor-mortis/general-lothaire-battlefield-arena.webp)

<section id="weak-points" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Shoot His Weak Points to Recover Nephtoglobin

The red Nephtoglobin growths on Lothaire's body are firearm targets.

Shooting an exposed growth deals damage and releases Nephtoglobin, providing more resources for Fire Transmutation.

Use the openings created by his completed attack combinations:

1. Parry or dodge the combination.
2. Shoot an exposed red growth while Lothaire recovers.
3. Collect the released Nephtoglobin.
4. Apply Fire during a safe opening, then return to defence.

This combines posture damage, firearm damage, and Fire buildup without requiring you to remain in melee range throughout the fight.

Avoid aiming at the growths during an active sword swing. A missed shot that leads to another hit is a poor exchange when you still have an entire second phase to survive.

</section>

<section id="phase-two" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Phase 2: Dodge Red Attacks and Control the Summons

When Lothaire's first health bar is depleted, his body mutates and a second health bar appears.

The familiar sword combinations now include **red-glowing unblockable attacks**. Continue parrying ordinary strikes, but dodge when the red cue appears.

![General Lothaire's mutated second phase and glowing attack effect.](/images/valor-mortis/general-lothaire-second-phase.webp)

### How to avoid the orb attacks

Lothaire can form a glowing orb in his mutated hand, followed by different area attacks.

When he drives the orb into the ground, an expanding shock wave moves across the arena. **Jump over the wave** instead of attempting to block it.

He can also release spikes that pursue your position. Keep moving until the attack finishes, then use the recovery window to heal or resume pressure.

A clear shot at the glowing orb can interrupt the attack before release. Only attempt this when you already have a suitable firing angle; an uncertain shot is not worth taking damage while aiming.

### What to do when soldiers appear

The second phase also introduces summoned soldiers.

Melee soldiers can obstruct your movement and are worth removing when they get close. Musketeers fire from a distance, then disappear after their attack.

Watch for the ranged attack cue and dodge the volley. Chasing the Musketeers wastes time and ammunition that could be used against Lothaire's weak points.

Keep the same basic damage cycle from phase one: defend against his melee attacks, shoot exposed growths, and use Fire when he is recovering.

The difference is that phase two requires you to recognize when a normal parry is no longer the correct defensive action.

</section>


<section id="rewards" className="scroll-mt-24">

## General Lothaire Rewards: Chapter 1 vs. Chapter 2

Defeating General Lothaire earns the **Torn Banner** achievement. The reward timing depends on when you win.

### If you defeat Lothaire in Chapter 1

You receive **10,000 Catalysts** immediately, along with **Commander's Catalyst Clot**, which grants another 10,000 when consumed.

The opening victory also skips the later Lothaire battle near the Abandoned Church.

### If you defeat Lothaire in Chapter 2

After losing the first encounter, you must defeat him near the end of Quarry to continue.

Winning the Chapter 2 battle awards **10,000 Catalysts**, satisfies the **Torn Banner** achievement requirement, and opens the route to the **Abandoned Church**.

### Where to go after the fight

Continue into the Abandoned Church and speak with **James**.

You can use the upgrade facilities there before starting Chapter 3.

The route toward Fallen Village also leads to the **Rapier**, which offers a different melee option from the starting Saber.

Continue into [Fallen Village](/valor-mortis/fallen-village) to begin **Chapter 3: Rendezvous**.

</section>
