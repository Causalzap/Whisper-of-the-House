The flooded streets of Cursed Hollow lead to three different kinds of dead ends. Some doors need to be opened from the other side, some passages are blocked by Nephtoglobin growths, and the green poisoned water is dangerous enough to kill you before you find a safe crossing.

Chapter 6 also contains several investigations that are easy to leave unfinished even after reaching the church. The Bride's wreath needs an item found much later in the area, the Headman's Key opens a house you pass during the village exploration, and Seamus Diggory will not accept just any blood sample.

The main route runs through **Eerie View, the Bride's flooded square, the village, Deep Water, Silent Cannons, and Village Church**. You can return to earlier Lanterns, so there is no need to force every optional pickup while carrying a large amount of Catalysts.

## From Eerie View to the Bride's Arena

Chapter 6 begins after the route through Vale of Wrath and the Slaughtermill encounter. Activate the **Eerie View Lantern** before entering the flooded settlement.

The first houses are connected by raised wooden ramps and walkways. Staying above the water is safer than cutting across the lower ground, especially where the green plague growths cover the street.

Several early obstructions require different interactions:

- **Golden Nephtoglobin growths:** Burn them with Fire to clear the passage.
- **Red weak points behind wooden walls:** Find a shooting angle through the nearby window or gaps between the boards.
- **Doors locked from the other side:** Continue around the building and open them after reaching the opposite entrance.

The red weak points are particularly easy to miss. One is visible through a narrow opening in a house; another requires changing your angle from the elevated walkway. Shooting the wooden barrier itself does nothing. Look for the glowing red target behind it.

The upper route also allows you to collect supplies without remaining in the poisoned lower streets. Open any shortcut you reach from behind before continuing toward the decorated square.

### Do you need Shield to cross Cursed Hollow?

No. **Shield Transmutation is obtained later, in Chapter 8**.

The poisoned sections you encounter here are not instructions to search for Shield immediately. Follow the dry walkways, use Grapple where available, and find the alternate paths around the contaminated ground.

A passage completely filled with poison can be left for a later visit. The required Chapter 6 route is accessible with the abilities you already have.

The elevated walkways eventually descend into a flooded square decorated for a wedding. This is the arena for the chapter's first major encounter.

## How to Defeat the Bride in Cursed Hollow

**The Bride** emerges from the flooded square and fights with sickle-like weapons. Her attacks include close-range strikes and lunges that can close the distance quickly.

The most important positioning decision is staying on ground where you can see her approach. The water and surrounding scenery make it easier to lose track of the boss when she submerges or moves away.

During the fight:

1. Keep enough space to recognize her next lunge rather than continuously attacking at close range.
2. Use your firearm when she has moved beyond comfortable melee distance.
3. Punish the recovery after her committed attacks, then reposition before the next sequence.
4. Clear summoned villagers when they begin interfering with your movement or recovery windows.

The Rapier's thrusts can be effective when the boss is exposed, but attempting a long attack sequence during an uncertain recovery leaves you vulnerable to the next strike.

The Blunderbuss is another option at close range, although spending all your ammunition early can make the later part of the encounter less forgiving.

Defeating The Bride awards **My Dying Bride**.

### Examine the wedding wreath before leaving

After the fight, walk to the altar at the end of the square.

Interact with the wilted wedding wreath to begin **Till Death**. The wreath is missing a ribbon, but the item is not lying somewhere in the boss arena.

A memory provides the connection to the church and graveyard farther along the route.

You will return here after obtaining the **Red Ribbon**. For now, continue into the village rather than repeatedly inspecting the empty wreath.

## Sleeping Beauty: Where to Find the Headman's Key

The **Sleeping Beauty** investigation takes place in the village beyond the Bride's arena.

Its central obstacle is a locked house. The door requires **Headman's Key**, which is carried by an enemy elsewhere in the same section of the village.

Start by finding the girl's prayer. This explains why the locked room and the spinning wheel matter.

### Step 1 — Find Headman's Daughter's Prayer

Continue through the flooded village until you reach the area with a large tree.

Instead of proceeding directly toward the tree, turn right and look for a low opening beneath a fence.

Crouch to pass underneath.

Follow the path through the nearby building and descend toward the small island with a shrine. On the ground, collect **Headman's Daughter's Prayer**.

The letter describes the girl's fear of her father and begins the Sleeping Beauty investigation.

You can now follow the trail toward the house where the Headman can be found.

### Step 2 — Defeat the Headman and collect his key

Progress into the adjoining area and enter the building along the route.

A hostile miniboss attacks inside, beyond the first room. This is the enemy you need to defeat for the key.

Its aggressive close-range attacks make dodging especially important. Give yourself room to move instead of trying to parry every strike in the confined interior.

After the fight, collect **Headman's Key**.

![Headman's Key added to the inventory inside a building in Cursed Hollow.](/images/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow-headmans-key.webp)

The key belongs to the locked house you encountered during the earlier village exploration. It does not open the church or a later story gate.

### Step 3 — Return to the locked house

Backtrack toward the village's main path and the building near the low fence opening.

Use **Headman's Key** on its locked door.

Go upstairs to the second floor. Before interacting with the spinning wheel, read the note on the table to the right.

The letters explain the connection between the girl's illness, her father's attempt to cure it, and the Nephtoglobin-infused spindle.

The girl is still trapped in the room, but the wheel gives you control over what happens next.

### Step 4 — Should you wake the girl or let her sleep?

Interacting with the spinning wheel presents two choices:

**Remove the spindle — Wake Her Up**

Removing the spindle disrupts the plague threads keeping the girl asleep. She awakens, but the transformation forces another hostile encounter.

Be ready to fight immediately after making the choice.

**Push the pedal — Let Her Sleep**

Operating the wheel strengthens the existing plague threads and leaves the girl in her slumber.

This avoids the additional confrontation.

![The spinning wheel and plague threads inside the Headman's daughter's room.](/images/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow-sleeping-beauty-wheel.webp)

Both outcomes complete **Sleeping Beauty** and award a **Distilled Transmutation Material**. The difference is the girl's fate and the additional fight, not access to the upgrade material.

Both choices finish the investigation, but removing the spindle adds a fight. Choose whether to wake the girl before interacting with the wheel, since the decision changes the final encounter.

Once the quest is complete, you can return to the main village route. There is no additional key or church entrance unlocked by repeating the spinning wheel interaction.

## Cross Deep Water and Open the Village Shortcuts

The next stretch of Cursed Hollow moves between flooded paths, raised buildings, and enemy positions overlooking the water.

The safest way through is to use roofs and wooden crossings as your main route. The direct ground-level path can appear shorter, but plague-covered water punishes small navigation mistakes.

**Deep Water** is an important Lantern to activate before exploring the nearby miniboss encounter.

A powerful enemy patrols a passage affected by poison. Fighting it before activating the next Lantern can lead to a long return run, especially when the poison buildup interferes with ordinary melee positioning.

Once the checkpoint is available, return and fight from a position that allows you to retreat out of the contaminated area.

Defeating this enemy provides **Wound That Heals**, an Amber that may already be familiar if you obtained an earlier copy. Check the chest behind the encounter for a **Distilled Transmutation Material**.

### The optional Eternal Guardist

Farther along, the route widens near a round clearing with a hay bale and a cannon-wielding enemy.

A branch to the right leads toward an Eternal Guardist beside a building. Look for the island and structures beyond the explosive barrels rather than staying on the central road.

Defeating this Guardist awards **Severin Susse's Emblem** and a **Mysterious Stone Pyramid** for The Oathbound.

A nearby chest contains **Life Diverted**.

This is an optional detour for players who want to complete the Eternal Guardist collection. It does not need to be repeated to advance the main story.

If you are tracking **We Wear the Same Colors**, the [39-achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) explains how the Guardist encounters fit into the overall completion route.

</section>

## The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory: Blood or Wine?

The **Silent Cannons** route eventually takes you across a flooded river and toward a forested area with a secluded house.

A soldier named **Seamus Diggory** is hiding inside. He speaks through the window but will not come outside.

His investigation is called **The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory**, and completing it earns **Blood and Wine** along with a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

The task becomes much simpler once you know that the game provides two possible items to hand over.

### Find Seamus's window

From the **Silent Cannons Lantern**, cross the river using the platforms and raised route.

Continue until you reach the enemy carrying a cannon. Near this position, look to the right for the wall-running route that leads toward the forest.

Follow it into the adjoining area and approach the house.

Speak with Seamus through the window. He asks for a blood sample and gives you an **Empty Bottle**.

There is no need to search the river for another bottle or buy one.

### Find the pig's blood

Continue into the nearby forest area.

An animal miniboss guards the section beyond the house. Defeat it to make exploration easier, then examine the pig lying on the ground.

Interact with the freshly dead pig to fill your bottle.

You receive the **Blood-Filled Bottle**.

![The freshly dead pig in Cursed Hollow that can be used to fill Seamus Diggory's bottle.](/images/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow-seamus-pig-blood.webp)

Not every dead animal here is suitable. William can recognize plague-contaminated remains, which are unsuitable for the sample Seamus requested.

Use the interaction that actually produces the Blood-Filled Bottle before returning to the window.

### Where to find the Bottle of Wine

The alternative item is in a nearby shack containing hanging animal carcasses.

Enter the shack and inspect the barrel holding the **Bottle of Wine**.

The wine belongs to the same investigation. You do not need to carry it out of Cursed Hollow or exchange it elsewhere.

Once you have both items, return to Seamus.

### What happens if you give him blood or wine?

Speak through the window again.

With both the Blood-Filled Bottle and Bottle of Wine available, you can choose which one to hand over.

Either choice finishes the investigation and grants the **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**. You also earn **Blood and Wine**.

The choice changes how the interaction plays out, but it does not block the Flask upgrade.

There is no need to reload a save to obtain the other reward after completing this quest.

## How to Enter Village Church and Get the Claymore

After the forest investigation, the route continues through another flooded settlement and the fields leading toward **Village Church**.

Along the way, activate the available Lanterns and open shortcuts back toward the village.

The fields contain additional infected enemies and a heavily armed opponent. You do not need to defeat every optional enemy to reach the church, but clearing those closest to the narrow crossings makes the approach safer.

### Open the church entrance

The church's front entrance is obstructed when you first reach it.

Look for **two red weak points**, one on each side of the building. Shoot them to clear the obstruction and open the way inside.

Do not spend time trying to break the door with melee attacks. The targets controlling the blockage are positioned around the structure.

You can also use the exterior traversal route to reach higher sections of the church. The nearby Grapple path provides access to additional pickups, including **Anointed Blade Material**.

Activate the **Village Church Lantern** when you reach it so you can return here without crossing the fields again.

### Take the Claymore from the body

Inside the church, approach the body in the main hall.

Interact with it to obtain the **Claymore**, the third melee weapon.

![Claymore weapon instructions showing its inability to parry and its Burning Wave and Wave of Stasis attacks.](/images/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow-church-claymore.webp)

The Claymore is a heavy weapon that cannot parry incoming attacks. With the controller inputs shown here, pressing LT releases a Burning Wave, while holding LT releases a Wave of Stasis.

Its wide attacks can catch several enemies together, but the slower swing leaves less room to dodge. Unlike the Saber, you cannot rely on a last-second parry when an enemy interrupts your attack.

Be prepared for enemies to become active after taking the weapon. Create room before experimenting with its heavier attacks inside the church.

The Claymore also completes the set of three melee weapons needed for **Arsenal Absolute**, although the full achievement requires collecting the other firearms and Transmutations as well.

Once the church encounter is clear, leave through the rear exit.

Before advancing into The Outskirts, turn right into the graveyard. There is still an important item to collect for the Bride's unfinished investigation.

## Till Death: Find the Red Ribbon and Complete the Bride's Wreath

The **Till Death** quest begins after The Bride's defeat, but its remaining item is located at the end of the chapter.

This means reaching Village Church is not the end of the investigation. You must return to the earlier boss arena after collecting the ribbon.

### Where to find the Red Ribbon

Leave Village Church through the rear doorway and turn right toward the graves.

A nearby memory reveals the connection to the wedding.

Inspect the relevant grave and collect the **Red Ribbon**, the Bride's missing wedding item.

![The Red Ribbon found at the grave behind Village Church for the Till Death quest.](/images/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow-red-ribbon-grave.webp)

The ribbon cannot be collected from the Bride's arena, and examining the wreath repeatedly before reaching the church will not advance the quest.

### Return to the wreath

Fast travel to the Lantern nearest the Bride's flooded square and return to the altar.

Interact with the wreath to place the Red Ribbon.

This step rewards **Tomorrow's Promise**, an Amber also obtainable through another earlier investigation. An additional copy can improve the Amber you already have.

However, placing the ribbon does not finish Till Death.

### Why is Till Death still unfinished after placing the ribbon?

The final change happens after you rest.

Follow this order:

1. Place the Red Ribbon on the wreath in the Bride's arena.
2. Travel to or rest at a Lantern.
3. Return to the altar and inspect the wreath again.

The wreath has changed after resting.

Interact with it once more to complete **Till Death** and obtain a **Revenant's Return** consumable.

The additional Lantern rest is part of the quest's completion sequence. It is not a missing item or another objective hidden in the graveyard.

## What to Do After Cursed Hollow

After collecting the Claymore and leaving Village Church through the graveyard, the route continues into **Chapter 7: Witch Hunt** and **The Outskirts**.

Before moving on, check the tasks that benefit from already being in Cursed Hollow:

- **Sleeping Beauty:** Headman's Key used, spinning wheel choice completed.
- **The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory:** Blood or wine delivered, Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade collected.
- **Till Death:** Red Ribbon returned, Lantern rested at, wreath inspected a final time.
- **The Oathbound:** Optional Eternal Guardist defeated and Severin Susse's Emblem collected.

You can return to Cursed Hollow later, so an unfinished investigation does not require restarting the chapter.

If you have just obtained the Claymore, returning to the Abandoned Church to upgrade your chosen weapon is reasonable before the longer encounters in The Outskirts.

You do not need to spend rare final-tier upgrade materials on the Claymore simply because it is the newest weapon. The Saber, Rapier, and Claymore serve different combat styles, and the maximum-level material is more valuable once you know which weapon you intend to keep.

</section>
