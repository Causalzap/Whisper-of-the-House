The fastest way to lose time on the Valor Mortis achievement list is to treat all 39 requirements as separate objectives. Several unlock through mandatory bosses, while others share the same enemy groups, weapons, or exploration routes.

You do not need to defeat General Lothaire during his first Chapter 1 encounter to earn Torn Banner. You also do not need to finish every side quest before advancing the story. The important exception is how you spend the materials used for maximum-level Transmutation upgrades.

## Valor Mortis 100% Roadmap: What to Do in Each Chapter

The main campaign has 11 chapters. You can finish the story before completing every collection, but some achievements become much easier if you handle their preparation while passing through the relevant region.

### Chapters 1–2: collect the early achievements and start The Oathbound

In **Chapter 1: Revival**, focus on learning parries and firearm weak-point attacks. Four of the simpler achievements can be earned without advancing far:

- **Rookie Mistake:** Attempt to use a Ferrofluid Flask after all charges are gone.
- **In the Nick of Time:** Interact with a Lantern at less than 20% health while carrying no remaining Flask charges.
- **Mind the Gap:** Die from repeated falls into an abyss.
- **Symphony of Steel:** Complete ten consecutive parries without taking damage.

For In the Nick of Time, prepare the empty-Flask condition near a Lantern rather than risking a long run. You must still be alive when you interact with it.

**General Lothaire** is the first major decision. Losing the Chapter 1 encounter advances the story, and you can earn **Torn Banner** by defeating him in Chapter 2. Winning the opening battle instead skips that later encounter.

The [General Lothaire guide](/valor-mortis/general-lothaire) covers both outcomes and the early victory rewards.

In **Chapter 2: Regroup**, the broken bridge provides the Pistol, and the route through The Climb leads to the missing scouts in **An Unheard Farewell**.

Complete the investigation by returning to the Adjutant. Both the truth and lie outcomes earn **Aggressive Reconnaissance**, so you can choose based on the additional reward you prefer.

The Valley also introduces **Risen Grenadiers**. Shoot the bucket at a Grenadier's waist for **Single Stroke**. Once you recognize their exposed weak points, you can also start practicing ten consecutive firearm weak-point or headshot hits for **God's Trigger**.

An optional Eternal Guardist appears beyond the Demolished Path shortcut. Defeat it and keep its quest-related rewards for **The Oathbound** rather than treating the encounter as unrelated to 100% completion.

Follow the [Valley of Ashes walkthrough](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) for the lift, hidden scouts' cave, Adjutant choices, and Quarry route.

### Chapters 3–4: unlock weapons and continue gathering Ambers

**Chapter 3: Rendezvous** takes you through Fallen Village.

Collect the Rapier and Blunderbuss, complete the nearby optional investigations, and defeat **The Iron Marshall** for **Ultima Ratio Regum**. Continue beyond the arena to unlock Grapple and rescue Louis.

Do not leave the village after defeating the boss without finishing Louis's rescue. The next chapter begins after you return to James at the Abandoned Church.

The Blunderbuss also makes **Scattershot** possible. It requires killing five enemies with one shot, although the challenge is easier after the weapon has been upgraded. You can return to suitable groups later instead of trying to force it with a weak starting Blunderbuss.

The [Fallen Village walkthrough](/valor-mortis/fallen-village) covers the two Marketplace keys, Iron Marshall, Grapple, and Louis.

After obtaining Grapple, consider returning to **Quarry** for another Pure Blade Material and a Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade. These pickups help your equipment progression even though neither independently awards an achievement.

In **Chapter 4: Dark Descent**, unlock **Stasis** in Caverns, operate the Old Mine machinery, obtain the Mining Shaft Key, and defeat **Sphinx** for **Not a Cat Person**.

The [Caverns walkthrough](/valor-mortis/caverns) covers the elevator's intermediate key ledge and the minecart track switch.

This is also a reasonable time to practice **Fight Fire With Fire**. The achievement requires igniting an enemy while you are already suffering Ignite. A campfire near ordinary enemies allows you to create the condition without relying on a boss.

### Chapters 5–6: finish the relevant quests and unlock Claymore

**Chapter 5: Crime** takes you through Vale of Wrath toward **Slaughtermill**, whose defeat awards **Quixotry**.

While exploring, complete the **Deserter** investigation for **Hand in the Jar**. This quest involves following the deserter's trail and finding items hidden in beehives.

Vale of Wrath also contains later encounters with enemies resembling the Iron Marshall. These are useful for **Statue of Insanity** once Stasis is available.

The achievement is not awarded for defeating Iron Marshall. You need to fully lock a **Remnant of the Iron Regiment** enemy in Stasis by filling its Stasis buildup.

During **Chapter 6: Punishment**, defeat **The Bride** for **My Dying Bride** and obtain the Claymore through the Cursed Hollow progression.

Complete **The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory** for **Blood and Wine**. You can finish the investigation using blood or wine; the full route and both choices are covered in the [Cursed Hollow walkthrough](/valor-mortis/cursed-hollow).

The Claymore unlock also gives you the weapon needed for **Hoodsnatcher**, which requires decapitating three enemies in one attack.

Do not worry about forcing the Claymore challenge during the area's boss fight. Groups of weaker enemies are more suitable for a single charged attack.

### Chapter 7: clear The Outskirts and City of Orphans

In **Chapter 7: Witch Hunt**, defeat **Justicar Aurelius** for **True Oath** and **Experiment 83** for **Lament of The Limbs**.

Two other achievement objectives deserve attention during this chapter.

**The Bitter Cure** requires completing the **Laudanum** investigation in The Outskirts.

**The Book of Beasts** requires finishing **Monster Hunt** in the City of Orphans by finding **17 drawings** spread across its streets and sewers.

Monster Hunt is especially valuable because its completion provides a **Quintessential Transmutation Material**. Keep that material for Shield rather than spending it immediately on Fire or Stasis.

The City of Orphans sewers contain two separately required keys. Collect the ordinary Sewers Key first, then the Master Key to reopen the remaining passages. Use the [Sewers Keys route](/valor-mortis/sewers-keys) when a locked gate interrupts your exploration.

At the end of the City of Orphans route, meet **the Shrike** and obtain the **Laboratory Key**. This encounter awards **Birdwatcher** and opens the next part of the University progression.

The Outskirts also provides a suitable setup for **Aerial Annihilation** and **Windhover** because its traversal routes contain wall jumps and enemies positioned beyond them.

### Chapter 8: unlock Shield and protect the maximum-level material

**Chapter 8: Blasphemy** takes you through the University.

During the library and Main Hall progression, obtain **Shield Transmutation**. This is the final Transmutation required for the full ability set.

By this point, your arsenal should include:

- **Melee:** Saber, Rapier, Claymore.
- **Firearms:** Pistol, Blunderbuss, Musket.
- **Transmutations:** Fire, Stasis, Shield.

With all nine unlocked, **Arsenal Absolute** becomes available through normal progression.

Now prioritize upgrading Shield to **Level 5** before spending your remaining maximum-level Transmutation materials. This simultaneously prepares **Abyssal Eviction** and fulfills the maximum-upgrade requirement for **Transmutation Artifex**.

Defeat **The Orphan** for **Coup de Grâce** before leaving the University.

After obtaining Shield, you can also return to the poisoned route at **Valley of Ashes → The Other Side** to finish [The Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary). Its nearby **Perfect Copy** Amber counts toward your Amber collection.

### Chapters 9–11: finish the story without abandoning cleanup

In **Chapter 9: In Ruins**, progress through Fortress, light the required signal fires, and defeat **Nephtis Superioris** for **Behemoth**.

The Fortress region is important for three remaining tasks: finding a maximum-level Transmutation material, lighting the hidden bonfire for **Frenzied Flame**, and finishing the later stages of **The Oathbound**.

For **Abyssal Eviction**, a wooden platform near **Fortress → Traverse** provides a place to lure enemies toward a drop and push them off with Shield Bash.

In **Chapter 10: Shackled**, recover your equipment using the Armoury Key and escape the prison. There is no need to force the unrelated combat achievements while you are temporarily separated from your weapons.

**Chapter 11: Reflection** returns you to the Ancient Lair beneath Caverns. Defeat **The Entity, Voice of the Void**, to earn **In His Own Image**.

After the story, earlier Lanterns and regions remain accessible. Use that opportunity to collect missing Ambers, finish remaining quests, and complete the combat challenges that need a controlled setup.

## Abyssal Eviction: The One Achievement You Can Lock Yourself Out Of

**Abyssal Eviction** requires killing an enemy by pushing it into an abyss using **Shield Bash**.

The problem is the ability requirement, not finding an enemy near a ledge. Shield Bash becomes available after upgrading **Shield Transmutation to Level 5**.

The launch version has two known **Quintessential Transmutation Materials**, the materials needed to reach the final Transmutation upgrade level.

**Monster Hunt reward:** Collect all 17 drawings in the City of Orphans and complete the Monster Hunt quest to receive one Quintessential Transmutation Material.

**Fortress material:** From Fortress → Traverse, open the gates after lighting both signal fires. Check the chest on the left for another Quintessential Transmutation Material.

You only need one of these materials for Shield, but spending both on Fire and Stasis can prevent you from unlocking Shield Bash on the same save.

### How to prepare Shield Bash

1. Unlock Shield during Chapter 8 at the University.
2. Upgrade Shield through the earlier levels with the appropriate Crude, Pure, and Distilled Transmutation Materials.
3. Use **one Quintessential Transmutation Material** to reach Level 5.
4. Confirm that **Shield Bash** is unlocked before looking for a suitable ledge.

This upgrade also counts toward **Transmutation Artifex**. You do not need to maximize Fire first.

### Where to push an enemy into the abyss

Travel to **Fortress → Traverse** and follow the uphill route through the gate toward the path around the castle.

Near the tower, two human enemies can approach you. Draw one toward the nearby wooden platform instead of fighting it on solid ground.

Keep the enemy between yourself and the unprotected edge. Activate Shield, then use **Shield Bash** to push it backward until it falls.

The enemy must die from the fall. Simply staggering an enemy near the edge or knocking it backward on safe ground will not satisfy the requirement.

If the enemy dies before falling, rest at the Traverse Lantern and attempt the setup again.

## Ambersmith, Monster Hunt, and Eternal Guardists

These achievements are best handled alongside exploration rather than saved as separate scavenger hunts without a route.

### Ambersmith: collect all 28 unique Ambers

**Ambersmith** requires obtaining all **28 unique Ambers**.

You do not need to collect every duplicate used to raise an Amber's rank. Once a unique Amber has been obtained, additional copies are only relevant if you want to improve its effect.

Several Ambers come from side quests or guarded chests rather than mandatory story pickups.

Examples worth checking before leaving a region include:

- **Sleight of Hand:** Earned through An Unheard Farewell in Valley of Ashes.
- **Perfectionism:** Found behind optional Eternal Guardist encounters.
- **Dominatus:** Available from Military Camp and other locations.
- **Perfect Copy:** An optional Amber collected while completing [The Emissary](/valor-mortis/the-emissary). It is separate from the quest completion reward.

Do not confuse the achievement's unique-Amber requirement with the total number of Amber pickups. One Amber can have several copies in different regions.

### The Book of Beasts: finish Monster Hunt

**The Book of Beasts** requires completing the Monster Hunt quest.

The important target is **17 drawings** scattered through the City of Orphans and its sewers.

Begin tracking the drawings while exploring Chapter 7. When a passage requires a sewer key you do not yet have, continue toward the next unlock rather than assuming you have reached the end of the collection route.

Complete the quest once all drawings have been collected. The reward is important for both this achievement and the Shield Level 5 material plan.

### We Wear the Same Colors: complete The Oathbound

**We Wear the Same Colors** requires defeating the Eternal Guardists and completing **The Oathbound** quest.

The encounters are scattered across the game, beginning in earlier regions and continuing into later ones.

The first useful opportunities include the optional Guardist beyond the Demolished Path shortcut in [Valley of Ashes](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes) and another on the upper crane route in [Caverns](/valor-mortis/caverns).

Each defeated Guardist contributes quest-related items. Keep collecting the associated emblems and other required objects as you progress.

Defeating a Guardist is not the same as completing the entire quest. Once you have gathered the required items, follow The Oathbound to its final Fortress objective instead of assuming the achievement will unlock from your last ordinary fight.

## Scattershot, Flame Feast, and Hoodsnatcher: Use One Enemy Group

Three achievements benefit from grouping enemies together rather than trying to complete them in boss arenas:

- **Scattershot:** Kill five enemies with one Blunderbuss shot.
- **Flame Feast:** Ignite four enemies with one continuous Flamethrower use.
- **Hoodsnatcher:** Decapitate three enemies with one Claymore attack.

A repeatable location is the barn near **Fallen Village → Marketplace**.

Entering the barn draws a group of five villagers. Move back toward the doorway so they approach through a narrower opening.

The enemies can be reset by resting at the Marketplace Lantern, allowing you to repeat the setup for each weapon.

### Scattershot: five kills with a single Blunderbuss shot

This is a kill requirement, not merely a requirement to hit five targets.

Upgrade the Blunderbuss before attempting it. A **Level 6 Blunderbuss** with the **Collateral Damage** spread upgrade is a workable setup.

Let the villagers gather near the entrance, reduce their health if necessary, and fire when all five are within the weapon's spread.

If even one survives, the shot does not meet the requirement. Rest, reposition, and try again.

### Flame Feast: ignite four enemies during one use

**Flame Feast** counts enemies that become Ignited during a single continuous Fire Transmutation use.

It does not require all four enemies to die.

Gather the villagers at the doorway, then hold the Flamethrower on the group until at least four have accumulated enough Ignite buildup to catch fire.

If you are interrupted before enough enemies are Ignited, reset the group and try again.

### Hoodsnatcher: three decapitations with one Claymore swing

Wait until Chapter 6 gives you the **Claymore**.

Return to the same barn, group the villagers, and use a charged Claymore attack aimed through the clustered enemies.

The attack must decapitate at least three enemies in a single swing. Ordinary kills without the required decapitations do not count.

Give yourself space to charge before the group reaches you. If you are interrupted, move farther back toward the doorway on the next attempt.

## How to Complete the Precision, Parry, and Stasis Achievements

Some combat requirements are easiest when you deliberately choose a weak enemy and repeatable checkpoint.

### Aerial Annihilation and Windhover

Both are easier after unlocking the **Airborne Aiming** skill.

From **The Outskirts → Broken Bridge**, the route includes wall jumps with enemies positioned beyond the traversal section.

For **Aerial Annihilation**, use a wall jump and kill an enemy with a headshot before landing. The final shot must be the headshot, even if the enemy was damaged earlier.

For **Windhover**, kill three enemies using the **Pistol** while aiming in mid-air. The three kills can happen across separate jumps; they do not have to come from one uninterrupted airborne sequence.

If you kill a target without satisfying the airborne requirement, rest to respawn enemies and try again.

### God's Trigger: ten shots without missing

**God's Trigger** requires ten consecutive headshots or weak-point hits.

Use the Pistol or Musket against predictable enemies. A firearm miss breaks the sequence, but ordinary melee actions between shots do not invalidate the requirement.

You can return to early Battlefield enemies to practice rather than attempting the streak while under pressure from a boss.

Aim at exposed head weak points or another reliable Nephtoglobin target. Fire only when you have a clear shot instead of rushing through ten attacks.

### Symphony of Steel: ten parries without damage

Find an ordinary human enemy with a readable melee combination and allow it to attack.

Parry ten consecutive strikes without taking damage. Blocking a hit is not equivalent to a successful parry if you still receive chip damage.

If the sequence fails, reset your attempt instead of continuing to count earlier parries.

### Statue of Insanity: fully apply Stasis

This achievement is tied to a **Remnant of the Iron Regiment** enemy, not to defeating the Chapter 3 Iron Marshall again.

One suitable enemy appears on the route from **Vale of Wrath → Wolfsbane Field**, after clearing the wall controlled by two weak points.

Use Stasis until the enemy's Stasis buildup is fully applied and all required buildup indicators are filled.

Avoid killing it with ordinary damage before the full Stasis effect takes hold. If it dies too early, return to the Lantern to reset the encounter.

### Jack-knife: all nine damage sources in one life

Wait until Chapter 8, after obtaining Shield, so all three melee weapons, three firearms, and three Transmutations are available.

Then damage enemies using:

1. Saber, Rapier, and Claymore.
2. Pistol, Blunderbuss, and Musket.
3. Fire, Stasis, and Shield.

The sequence must be completed **without dying**.

Travel to an early area with weak enemies and keep track of which weapon or ability has actually damaged a target. Merely equipping a weapon or activating an effect without causing damage is insufficient.

Shield requires special attention because basic protection is not a damage attack. A damage-dealing Shield action, such as Shield Bash after its upgrade, is the safer way to satisfy that part of the sequence.

If the achievement does not register after cycling through the arsenal, repeat the Transmutation attacks and ensure they actually produce damage rather than only a status or defensive effect.

## Bladesmith, Gun of Glory, and Transmutation Artifex

Three achievements require maximizing one piece of equipment or one ability. They do not require upgrading everything.

**Bladesmith — Melee Weapon Level 10:** Fully upgrade one Saber, Rapier, or Claymore.

**Gun of Glory — Firearm Level 10:** Fully upgrade one Pistol, Blunderbuss, or Musket.

**Transmutation Artifex — Transmutation Level 5:** Fully upgrade Fire, Stasis, or Shield. Prioritize Shield if you still need Abyssal Eviction.

### Which melee weapon should you upgrade?

The Saber is available from the beginning, Rapier becomes available in Chapter 3, and Claymore arrives in Chapter 6.

You can use any one of them for Bladesmith. Choose based on the weapon you actually intend to use through the later bosses.

The final level needs **Quintessential Blade Material**. Earlier levels require Crude, Pure, and Distilled Blade Materials.

You can collect early Pure Blade Materials during the [Valley of Ashes walkthrough](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes), including an upper Quarry return after unlocking Grapple. Collect upgrade materials as you explore, but choose your Level 10 melee weapon before committing the rare final-tier material.

### Which firearm should reach Level 10?

For **Gun of Glory**, bring one firearm to **Level 10**.

The final upgrade uses **Masterwork Firearm Parts**, which are much more restricted than ordinary Firearm Parts.

Do not assume that every firearm can receive the last upgrade from the same limited supply. Keep your final Masterwork part for the gun you want to finish.

The Pistol is useful for accurate weak-point hits, while the Blunderbuss supports close-range groups and the Musket gives you a longer-range option.

### Why Shield should be your Level 5 Transmutation

Fire, Stasis, and Shield can each fulfill **Transmutation Artifex** if upgraded to Level 5.

However, only Level 5 Shield also prepares **Abyssal Eviction**.

Upgrade Shield first. This earns the maximum-level achievement while preserving your ability to complete the missable Shield Bash challenge.

After Shield is secured, the remaining maximum-level material can be used on whichever Transmutation benefits your combat style.

## What to Check Before Your Last Achievement

After completing the campaign, use the tracker to identify which of these groups remains unfinished.

**Story and bosses:** The main-story achievement chain ends with In His Own Image. If one of the earlier boss achievements is missing, compare it against your actual chapter progression before assuming you need another playthrough.

**Side quests:** Aggressive Reconnaissance, Blood and Wine, Hand in the Jar, The Bitter Cure, The Book of Beasts, and We Wear the Same Colors all depend on completing specific investigations or extended quest objectives.

**Collectibles:** Ambersmith requires the 28 unique Ambers, while The Book of Beasts tracks its own 17-drawing collection.

**Upgrades:** Bladesmith, Gun of Glory, and Transmutation Artifex need only one maximum-level example in each category.

**Combat:** Return to predictable enemies for the precision shots, Stasis lock, multi-kill attacks, and Jack-knife sequence.

### Frenzied Flame: the hidden Fortress bonfire

One easy-to-misread achievement is **Frenzied Flame**.

You are not looking for a normal campfire. The target is a **sword stuck in the ground** inside the Fortress sewer passages.

Starting from **Fortress → Chapel**, follow the route into the underground area and find the side passage reached after the pole jump. Descend the stairs into the adjoining room and look for the sword.

Use **Fire Transmutation** on it to ignite the bonfire and earn the achievement.

### Nephtis Superioris: finish all 39

**Nephtis Superioris** is the achievement awarded for obtaining every other achievement. Do not confuse it with **Behemoth**, the separate story achievement for defeating the boss Nephtis Superioris.

If the final achievement is still missing, check the tracker for a remaining side quest, collection, or special combat condition before starting over.

The main campaign and most unfinished activities can be completed on the same save. The resource allocation for Shield Level 5 is the one decision to protect from the beginning.
