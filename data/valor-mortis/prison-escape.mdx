The castle becomes visible long before William is ready to enter it. Once Chapter 9 begins, however, getting inside is only the first obstacle. The Fortress has several overlapping routes, and two locked doors can draw you away from the climb toward the final boss.

The distinction that matters is between the **two signal fires**, which open the required route, and the **Captain's Key** and **Fortress Armory Key**, which unlock additional supplies. Both keys are worth collecting, particularly if your healing equipment still needs upgrades, but neither is a prerequisite for defeating Captain Raphael.

## How to Reach the Fortress From the Abandoned Church

After completing the University chapter and recovering the documents Louis needs, return to the **Abandoned Church**.

Speak with Louis at his workbench. His next objective is to retrieve a relic from the ruined castle, beginning **Chapter 9: In Ruins**.

The route leaves from the church grounds, near the earlier path where you acquired the Rapier before entering Fallen Village. Take the branch toward the castle ruins rather than following the ordinary village route again.

You can find this approach earlier in the campaign, but a dangerous enemy and a poison-filled passage prevent convenient access.

By Chapter 9 you should already have **Shield Transmutation** from the University.

### Getting through the poisoned entrance

Continue toward the castle and deal with the enemies guarding the approach. The passage beyond contains dense poison that builds up too quickly to cross safely without protection.

Activate **Shield** before entering the contaminated area and maintain its protection until you are clear of the hazard.

The base Shield ability is sufficient. You do not need Shield Level 5 or Shield Bash just to enter the Fortress.

If your Nephtoglobin supply is low, replenish it before attempting the crossing. Losing protection in the middle of the poison forces you to retreat or risk dying before reaching the far side.

Continue through the ruined passage and open any shortcut gate accessible from its opposite side.

![The ruined stone approach leading toward the Fortress castle entrance in Valor Mortis.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-ruined-castle-approach.webp)

### Reach Main Gate and Traverse

The first part of the castle contains heavily armed soldiers and mutated enemies among broken walls, stone bridges, and artillery positions.

One powerful enemy near the early archway carries a cannon-like weapon. Defeating it awards **Fire Breathing**, an Amber associated with burning damage during dodge attacks.

The encounter is optional for reaching the upper castle, so decide whether the reward is worth the immediate ammunition and healing cost.

Proceed over the damaged stone bridge and activate the next Lantern. Continue through the adjoining gate into the Fortress's upper defensive routes.

The upcoming climbing section leads toward **Traverse**, the most useful checkpoint for the two signal fires and several nearby locked rooms.

## How to Light Both Fortress Signal Fires

The first major progression obstacle is the large gate controlled by **two signal fires**.

These are elevated wooden pyres built into separate tower routes. Lighting only one does not open the passage. You must climb to both positions and ignite them with Fire Transmutation.

The surrounding platforms make the sequence appear more complicated because some ladders return to previously explored areas, while other paths lead into optional rooms.

### First signal fire: climb the ruined outer platforms

From the upper Fortress route, continue along the wooden structures and ruined stone walls.

Use the available Grapple points and activate the blue Nephtoglobin mechanisms that extend platforms across the gaps.

Several towers are connected by narrow landings. When an ordinary gate is barred from the far side, look for an elevated route rather than another key.

Climb until you reach a wooden signal pyre covered in yellow web-like material.

Use **Fire Transmutation** directly on the pyre to ignite it.

The flame is your confirmation that the first signal has been activated. You do not need to return to Traverse before looking for the second one.

### Second signal fire: reach the opposite tower

Continue around the castle's exterior and look for another climb toward the upper battlements.

A glowing orange-red weak point blocks part of this route. Shoot it with a firearm before attempting the next crossing.

Follow the exposed Grapple and Nephtoglobin activation points around the outside of the structure. The route eventually brings you into another defended tower.

Clear the soldier guarding the approach, then climb to the second signal pyre and ignite it with Fire.

Once both pyres are burning, a scene shows the previously sealed gate opening elsewhere in the Fortress.

### Where to go after lighting both fires

Return toward the gate shown opening when the second signal was activated.

You have already done the important part of the puzzle. There is no third signal fire to search for, and the Captain's Key is not required to activate the gate.

Use the opened route to continue into the deeper castle.

The distinction becomes useful here:

- **Two signal fires:** Open the required progression gate.
- **Captain's Key:** Opens the captain's quarters near Traverse.
- **Fortress Armory Key:** Opens the Fortress armory and its optional supplies.

If the gate remains closed, inspect the second tower again and confirm that its pyre was actually ignited. Reaching the tower without lighting the structure does not count.

## What to Collect Before Leaving the Signal-Fire Area

There are two rewards worth checking while you are moving through the outer castle, especially if you intend to continue upgrading equipment before the final encounters.

### Quintessential Transmutation Material

After the signal fires open the large gate, check the left-hand side of the newly accessible route and the adjoining arched stone hall.

A red chest in this section contains a **Quintessential Transmutation Material**, alongside a Catalyst item.

This is a maximum-tier Transmutation upgrade material.

It is especially valuable if you have not yet upgraded Shield to Level 5, but you do not need to spend it immediately. Keep it until you have decided which Transmutation needs its final upgrade.

The resource decision also matters for **Abyssal Eviction**, which requires Shield Bash. The [39-achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) covers the upgrade requirements and how to avoid spending the limited materials on the wrong ability.

### The artillery crossing

Some exterior walkways are exposed to cannon fire from distant positions.

A long jump or Grapple crossing can leave you vulnerable if you stop on the landing to fight every enemy. Move between solid pieces of cover, and eliminate exposed shooters from a protected position before committing to another long traversal.

There is no advantage to remaining on a wooden platform while artillery is striking it.

If you are carrying a large quantity of Catalysts, use the nearest Lantern shortcut before exploring the more dangerous side routes.

## Captain's Key and Fortress Armory Key Locations

Both Fortress keys are found around the deeper castle routes accessible near **Ramparts**.

Their doors lead to optional equipment rather than forming a mandatory two-key progression sequence. You can collect them while exploring, or come back after opening more Lanterns and shortcuts.

### Where to find Captain's Key

From the **Ramparts Lantern**, return down the ladder toward the large interior room where a blue swing point crosses an opening.

Swing across toward the doorway on the left.

Go through the door and descend the stairs. A golden web-like growth blocks a passage below.

Burn the obstruction with **Fire Transmutation** to reveal a long corridor.

Follow that corridor to its end and collect **Captain's Key**.

The key's purpose is to open the captain's quarters. It is not the same item as the Fortress Armory Key.

#### Which door does Captain's Key open?

Return to the **Traverse Lantern**.

The locked captain's door is directly across from this Lantern. Use Captain's Key to enter the quarters and search the chest inside.

The reward includes a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.

This improves the amount of health restored by a Flask charge; it does not add another charge to your maximum Flask count.

If you first encountered this locked door on the way into the Fortress, there was no need to search the surrounding wall for a hidden lever. Its key is farther along the Ramparts exploration route.

### Where to find Fortress Armory Key

Return to **Ramparts** and descend toward the same large room with the blue swing point.

Cross into the side area, descend the stairs, and enter the adjoining maze-like interior.

Continue straight until you can no longer proceed, then turn right and left to reach a room containing a lever and moving gears.

This mechanism is the important obstacle before the key.

1. Operate the lever to open the gate.
2. Use **Stasis Transmutation** on the moving gears while the mechanism is active.
3. Cross the gate while the machinery is held in the correct position.
4. In the room beyond, watch for the large enemy and locate the ladder on the left.
5. Climb the ladder and collect **Fortress Armory Key**.

A gate that closes again before you reach it usually means the machinery was not held long enough. Reset the lever, apply Stasis to the gears, and cross before the mechanism returns to its starting state.

Do not waste time searching for a second lever in the same room.

### What does Fortress Armory Key unlock?

Backtrack to the earlier large room containing the blue swing point.

The locked armory entrance is nearby, along the route you used before entering the interior maze.

Use **Fortress Armory Key** to open it.

Search the armory for a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade** and the supplies inside.

This door and Captain's Key's door are separate. Collecting one key will not open the other's room.

The name can also be confusing later: the **Armoury Key in Napoleon's Prison** belongs to Chapter 10 and is unrelated to either Fortress key.

### Should you collect both keys before the boss?

Neither key is required to reach Captain Raphael.

However, the two Flask Power Upgrades can be valuable if you are struggling with the later encounters and have not collected many healing improvements.

Both side routes become easier to revisit once their nearby Lanterns and shortcuts are open. You can continue the main story through the signal-fire gate first and return to the locked rooms when you want the upgrades.

## Through Chapel, Ramparts, and Courtyard

Past the opened progression route, the Fortress continues through ruined halls, defensive positions, and interconnected upper passages.

Activate the **Chapel**, **Ramparts**, and **Courtyard** Lanterns as you encounter them. They reduce the distance you need to repeat after deaths and make returning for the two optional keys much simpler.

The later climb involves additional Grapple points, moving Nephtoglobin structures, and exposed crossings above the castle.

### Don't mistake every side passage for the boss route

The Captain's Key and Fortress Armory Key detours can take you through long corridors that eventually connect back to areas you already know.

This is intentional. They are useful routes for equipment, shortcuts, and optional rewards, but following every branch is not necessary to finish Chapter 9.

Once the two signal fires have opened the main gate, your priority is to move through the higher castle toward the **Courtyard** and the passage leading to the final tower.

The route from Courtyard eventually leads through a long stone corridor into a ruined hall resembling a chapel nave.

### The powerful officer in the ruined hall

A dangerous officer awaits in the bloodstained interior.

His long sword combinations and lunging attacks make it risky to stand directly in front of him and repeatedly trade hits. Give his longer combinations room to finish, then attack during the recovery.

Fire Transmutation and firearm shots provide opportunities to deal damage without committing to an extended melee exchange.

Defeating this enemy awards **20,000 Catalysts** and the **Glass Soldier** Amber.

At the far end of the hall, check the body near the altar for **Bastien Beaufort's Emblem** and a **Mysterious Stone Pyramid** connected to **The Oathbound**.

The fight and the altar investigation are separate interactions. Killing the officer does not automatically collect the items at the altar.

### The hidden Fortress bonfire

There is also a concealed bonfire beneath the Chapel section, associated with **Frenzied Flame**.

It is different from the two tower signal fires that open the progression gate. Lighting those two pyres does not award Frenzied Flame.

If you are working toward all achievements, check the [achievement roadmap](/valor-mortis/achievements) before leaving the Fortress. The hidden bonfire is an optional objective, not a condition for entering Captain Raphael's arena.

## How to Reach the Final Tower and Captain Raphael

After clearing the ruined hall, continue through the upper castle passages.

The route passes additional damaged walkways, small interior rooms, and a crossing that uses an activated Nephtoglobin platform.

A wooden ladder can be dropped to create a shortcut back toward an earlier courtyard. Open it when you reach the far side rather than repeatedly attempting the exposed platform crossing from the beginning.

Farther up, use the available Grapple points to cross the ruined battlements and reach the structures suspended above the bay.

The final approach includes a circular lifting platform operated from its center.

Step onto the mechanism to rise toward the upper tower.

Activate the **High Tower Lantern** before continuing up the stairs.

This is the last useful checkpoint before Captain Raphael. Rest to replenish your Flask charges and ammunition, and check whether you want to return to the Abandoned Church for an upgrade before entering the arena.

The Fortress contains several rare materials and optional healing improvements, but you do not need to collect every chest before fighting the boss.

## How to Defeat Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris

At the top of the tower, enter the arena to begin the confrontation with **Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris**.

Raphael fights with a sabre and relies on aggressive melee pressure. His attacks can carry him forward through several strikes, so an early dodge or an attempt to punish the first swing may leave you exposed to the remainder of the combination.

The safest opportunities come after his committed attack sequences.

### Fight the complete combination, not just the first swing

Stay close enough to recognize the start of his sword attacks, but leave yourself space to retreat when a combination continues longer than expected.

Parrying is useful against ordinary sword strikes, particularly if you have spent much of the campaign using the Saber or Rapier. A successful sequence can create an opportunity to reduce his posture and deliver a counterattack.

Do not commit to a full heavy-attack chain merely because the first strike missed. Wait for an unmistakable recovery.

Fire Transmutation can help maintain pressure when you are unable to stay in melee range. Save firearm ammunition for windows where Raphael has finished moving and you can land a reliable shot.

![Nephtis Superioris confronting William before the final Fortress boss fight.](/images/valor-mortis/fortress-captain-raphael-boss.webp)

### What does defeating Captain Raphael reward?

Defeating Raphael awards **50,000 Catalysts** and completes **Chapter 9: In Ruins**.

The encounter also fulfills **Behemoth**, the story achievement associated with defeating Nephtis Superioris.

After the fight, follow the route beyond the arena and continue through the next story sequence.

This transition leads into **Chapter 10: Shackled**, where William is taken prisoner and temporarily loses access to his equipment.

You cannot begin that chapter by simply fast-travelling back to the Fortress. Recovering your possessions becomes the next main objective.

### Prepare before the prison transition

If you have unspent Catalysts or upgrades that would make later encounters easier, return to the Abandoned Church before committing to the final sequence.

The next chapter initially restricts access to your normal weapons and equipment.

You do not need to finish every optional Fortress chest or collect both keys to defeat Raphael. Those side routes are most valuable when you specifically need their healing upgrades or remaining collectibles.
