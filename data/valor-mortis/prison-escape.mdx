William begins **Chapter 10: Shackled** inside Napoleon's Prison, with his weapons and equipment confiscated. The first guards are especially dangerous because you cannot rely on your usual melee attacks, firearms, or upgraded weapon damage.

The prison is smaller than the preceding Fortress, but leaving it creates a more serious consequence: **you cannot return once you escape through the sewers**. The Armoury Key is required for normal progression, while the chapel investigation and a hidden lockpick lead to optional rewards that must be collected during this visit.

The safest route is to recover your weapons first, explore the chapel and remaining prison cells afterward, then take the passage marked with the Shrike's symbol.

<section id="starting-cell" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Escape the Starting Cell and Find the Hidden Silver Lockpick

The chapter begins after the confrontation at Fortress. William awakens in a prison cell without his normal equipment.

Once you regain control, inspect the nearby cells before heading toward the guarded corridors.

One prisoner complains about a man who hanged himself in the neighbouring cell. A **Prisoner's Note** explains that the dead man had been talking about a hidden treasure.

This begins the investigation known as **The Forgotten Fortune**.

### Burn the hanging prisoner

Look for the hanging corpse in the starting cell area.

Although your weapons have been confiscated, **Fire Transmutation** is still available. Use it to burn the hanging body.

The flames reveal a **Silver Lockpick** concealed inside the corpse.

Take the lockpick and return to the nearby cell door that was previously locked.

Use the Silver Lockpick to open the cell and collect the **Eternal Catalyst Clot** inside. The item is worth **5,000 Catalysts**.

This completes the treasure investigation. The lockpick does not open the Armoury, and collecting the Catalysts will not restore William's confiscated weapons.

Because the prison cannot be revisited, this is worth handling before moving far into the escape route. The first part of the chapter is also an opportunity to look through the cells without fighting the heavily armed guards farther ahead.

## Where to Find the Armoury Key in Napoleon's Prison

The **Armoury Key** is located in the kitchen area on the upper floor. You must reach it by moving past guards and using the wooden beams above the main prison hall.

From the starting cells, follow the corridor away from the prisoner's hanging body.

### Get past the first guards without your weapons

Move slowly through the connecting rooms and watch the soldiers' patrols.

There are places where the broken walls leave narrow openings. Crouch through these gaps instead of trying to cross a brightly lit corridor while a guard is facing you.

The route eventually reaches a larger room with a cage in the middle and guards patrolling around it.

You can see the locked Armoury during this section, but the key is not inside the cells or on the lower-floor guards.

Some of the mice you helped earlier may open a shortcut during the escape. If a door opens for you, use it to bypass part of the patrol route. Otherwise, continue through the adjoining rooms and approach the main hall from the available corridor.

Both routes lead toward the same upper-floor area.

### Climb onto the wooden rafters

From the large guarded room, find the staircase leading upward.

Near the upper passage, look for stacked wooden crates and scaffolding that allow you to climb above the soldiers.

Use the crates to reach the wooden beams spanning the hall.

The guards patrol below you, so crossing overhead is safer than dropping immediately into the centre of the room.

Move along the beams toward the middle, then continue toward the kitchen on the far side.

Wait until the guards below have turned away before making the final drop.

### Pick up the key from the kitchen table

Enter the kitchen area and examine the table.

Collect **Armoury Key**.

![The Armoury Key pickup in the upper-floor kitchen of Napoleon's Prison.](/images/valor-mortis/prison-escape-armoury-key.webp)

This is the key needed to recover your equipment.

It is not the **Fortress Armory Key** from Chapter 9. The Fortress key opens an optional equipment room in the castle; this prison key opens the Armoury containing William's confiscated possessions.

Before leaving the kitchen area, check the adjacent small room. It contains **Prison Warden's Journal – Part Two**, which belongs to The Choir investigation.

Collecting it now saves another trip through the rafters later.

<section id="recover-equipment" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Open the Armoury and Get Your Weapons Back

With Armoury Key collected, return to the lower floor.

Leave the kitchen area and take the passage leading back toward the stairway. Follow the corridors down until you reach the guarded section where you first encountered the locked Armoury.

A narrow opening in the wall allows you to crawl through part of this route while staying out of sight.

The red-caped guard patrols near the Armoury entrance. Let him move away before approaching the door.

### Unlock the Armoury

Use **Armoury Key** on the locked door.

Inside, the room contains weapon racks, military equipment, and a large red chest.

Open the chest to recover William's confiscated possessions.

![The red equipment chest inside Napoleon's Prison Armoury where William recovers his weapons.](/images/valor-mortis/prison-escape-equipment-chest.webp)

The **Shackled** objective updates when your equipment is restored.

Your weapons, armour and normal combat options are available again. This changes how you can approach the remaining guards: stealth remains useful, but you can now fight enemies instead of relying entirely on hiding and escape routes.

### What if you have the key but still cannot find the Armoury?

The Armoury is below the upper-floor kitchen where you collected the key.

Return to the larger prison hall with the cage and patrols. Follow the lower corridor past the guarded doorway rather than searching for another room beside the kitchen.

The chest inside the Armoury is the important interaction. Opening the door alone does not restore your equipment.

### Should you escape immediately after recovering your weapons?

You can continue toward the exit once your equipment has been restored, but there is still an investigation involving the prison chapel.

That investigation requires items found on both sides of the upper floor. Leaving through the sewer exit before collecting its final document permanently prevents you from completing the prison portion.

Use your restored equipment to clear troublesome patrols and finish exploring before committing to the escape.

## The Choir: Find Both Warden's Journals and the Chapel Key

**The Choir** investigates what happened to prisoners who were used in the warden's disturbing musical experiments.

The evidence is spread through the prison's offices, upper corridors and chapel. The investigation does not finish when you first enter the chapel: you must carry a list of the deceased prisoners back into the city and post it publicly.

The crucial restriction is that **all prison-side evidence must be collected before you escape**.

### Find Prison Warden's Journal – Part One

During the early escape route, look for a small office on the left containing a horn-like instrument and a desk.

Read **Prison Warden's Journal – Part One**.

The entry describes the warden's ambition to compose music in Napoleon's honour and begins the investigation.

You can collect this note while unarmed or return for it after recovering your weapons.

There is no need to defeat every guard before reading it.

### Investigate the door at the end of the upper hallway

Continue upstairs to the large room where multiple soldiers patrol beneath the wooden beams.

At the end of the long hallway, approach the door and interact with it.

This advances the investigation even though it does not immediately give you the chapel key.

If the task appears to stop here, continue collecting evidence in the neighbouring upper-floor rooms rather than repeatedly interacting with the same door.

### Find Prison Warden's Journal – Part Two

Return to the kitchen section where the Armoury Key was located.

A small room beside the key's pickup location contains **Prison Warden's Journal – Part Two** on a table.

Read it to learn more about the warden's treatment of the prisoners.

The two journal parts are separate objects. Reading only the first one does not complete the investigation.

### Where to find the Key to the Chapel

From the upper-floor kitchen area, cross toward the opposite side of the upper level.

Look for a side room containing a memory that can be investigated.

Inside that room, check the table and take **Key to the Chapel**.

![The Key to the Chapel pickup inside the upper-floor room in Napoleon's Prison.](/images/valor-mortis/prison-escape-chapel-key.webp)

This key opens the prison chapel, not the Armoury.

The chapel entrance is marked by a military notice titled **Headquarters' Orders – Sealing of the Chapel**. It explains that the room has been closed by order of the authorities.

Return to that locked entrance once you have the key.

### Open the chapel and collect the deceased-prisoners letter

Use **Key to the Chapel** to unlock the door.

Inside, investigate the room and the bodies associated with the warden's experiments.

Behind the bodies, look for a counter holding the document:

**Letter to Headquarters – A List of Deceased Prisoners**

Take the letter.

This document is the most important item to collect before leaving the prison. Without it, you cannot perform the final public notice-board interaction later.

If the journal still shows The Choir as unfinished, that is expected. You have obtained the evidence, but you have not yet delivered it.

### Where to post the letter after escaping

Finish the prison escape and continue through the sewers until William reaches the **Burning City**.

Near the Lantern along the boulevard, look for a notice board.

Approach the board and interact with it to post the deceased-prisoners letter.

![The notice board in the Burning City where William posts the deceased-prisoners list to complete The Choir.](/images/valor-mortis/prison-escape-choir-notice-board.webp)

Posting the letter completes **The Choir** and awards a **Remnant's Return** consumable.

The reward is separate from restoring your equipment and does not open another prison route.

If the quest remains unfinished after leaving the prison, check whether you have actually placed the document on the board. Merely reaching the city is not the final interaction.

## Optional Chests and Other Prison Pickups

Once your weapons are restored, the remaining prison rooms are easier to search. Several rewards are hidden behind red Nephtoglobin weak points rather than ordinary locked doors.

These are optional detours. None replaces the Armoury Key or Key to the Chapel.

### Distilled Blade Material near the upper patrol corridor

Return toward the corridors adjoining the Armoury Key area.

Look for a red Nephtoglobin growth concealing a passage or chest.

Destroy its glowing weak point with your firearm, then enter the newly accessible space.

A chest here contains **Distilled Blade Material**, which can be used for a later melee-weapon upgrade.

It is worth collecting if your chosen weapon has not yet reached its next upgrade threshold.


### Remnant's Return chest below the walkway

A second red Nephtoglobin weak point can be seen from the raised passage near the prison cells.

Look down from the ledge toward the blocked area below. Shoot the glowing weak point to open access to the hidden chest.

The chest contains a **Remnant's Return** consumable.

This pickup is separate from the Remnant's Return awarded for completing The Choir. You can collect the chest reward without finishing that investigation.

### The room behind the movable Nephtoglobin structure

Near the Armoury, another optional route uses a blue Nephtoglobin interaction to clear a wooden obstruction.

After opening the passage, continue upstairs and look for an opening that allows you to drop into the room below.

This detour leads to a chest containing **2 Distilled Transmutation Materials**.

Collect them before leaving the prison if you still need intermediate Transmutation upgrades.

It is an upgrade pickup, not part of the required escape route.

### Is it worth collecting everything before leaving?

The optional materials can save you an upgrade trip later, but the more important distinction is whether the item will remain accessible.

Once you leave Napoleon's Prison, you cannot return for a missed chest, the Silver Lockpick investigation, or The Choir's prison documents.

You can still finish the main story without these optional rewards. If you intend to clear the region completely, search the side rooms before following the Shrike-marked exit.

## Where to Find the Prison Exit and Finish Shackled

After recovering your equipment and collecting anything you want from the prison, return toward the passage connected to the Armoury.

The mice provide a useful clue about the exit: look for a door marked with **the Shrike's symbol**.

This route is not opened by the chapel key.

### Clear the Shrike-marked passage

Follow the corridor until you reach the obstruction associated with the Shrike sign.

A red Nephtoglobin weak point blocks part of the passage. Shoot the glowing target with a firearm.

Then use **Fire Transmutation** to burn the web-like growths covering the opening.

Pass through and descend into the sewers beneath the prison.

The lower passages contain more enemies, but you have your equipment back and can deal with them normally.

### Reach the Prison Lantern and climb the ladder

Continue through the underground passage until you reach the **Prison Lantern**.

Activate it to establish a checkpoint. Fast travel may still be unavailable during this story sequence, so do not mistake a disabled travel option for a missing key.

Nearby, a ladder leads upward from the sewer route.

Climb the ladder to advance the chapter.

The **Shackled** completion notification appears during the escape sequence. Keep moving through the connected passages toward the city.

### Can you return to Napoleon's Prison?

No. The prison is a one-way story area.

Once you have left through the sewers, normal travel does not provide a return route into its cells, upper hallways or chapel.

Before taking the final exit, make sure you have:

- Opened the Armoury chest and recovered your equipment.
- Completed The Forgotten Fortune and collected the Silver Lockpick treasure, if wanted.
- Read both parts of Prison Warden's Journal.
- Obtained Key to the Chapel and searched the chapel.
- Collected Letter to Headquarters – A List of Deceased Prisoners.

The Choir can still be completed after the escape, but only if you have already taken the prison letter with you.

For a full achievement run, The Choir is not required for a named achievement. Its reward and quest completion are optional, although the prison portion becomes inaccessible afterward.

After climbing out of the sewers, continue through **Burning City**. If you collected the deceased-prisoners list, post it on the notice board beside the Lantern.

The main story then returns William toward the Abandoned Church and the final chapter, **Reflection**.
