<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
You can begin **The Emissary** in Chapter 2 by collecting the **Emissary's Seal** from the abandoned carriage near **Valley of Ashes → The Other Side**.

Return after completing **Chapter 9**, once you have **Shield Transmutation**. Use Shield to cross the poisonous passage, investigate the dead escort in the hidden camp, defeat the enemy, and destroy the red weak point blocking the carriage.

Finally, investigate **Blood-Soaked Velvet** beside the blocked gate to complete the quest. The camp fight and nearby Perfect Copy Amber do not complete the investigation.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Emissary can sit unfinished in your quest journal for a long time, even after you have found its first clue. The abandoned carriage is accessible during Chapter 2, but the path beyond it is filled with poison that your starting abilities cannot overcome.

The investigation has three distinct turning points: the Seal starts it, examining the body in the hidden camp reveals what happened to the escort, and a final piece of bloodstained fabric completes it. Defeating the enemy in the camp or finding the nearby Amber is not the final objective.

<section id="first-visit" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Found the Emissary's Seal but can't get through the poison?

The first opportunity to begin The Emissary comes during **Chapter 2: Regroup**, while crossing Valley of Ashes.

Once you reach **The Other Side Lantern**, leave the normal road toward Military Camp and take the side route on the left, where yellow-green poison covers the ground.

An abandoned carriage stands partway into this dangerous area. Enter it and examine the table to collect the **Emissary's Seal**.

William recognizes the seal as something an important imperial envoy would be unlikely to leave behind. The quest is added to your journal, but finding the object provides no immediate explanation of where the emissary went.

You can reach the carriage during your first visit. The path beyond it is another matter.

![The abandoned carriage and poisonous route where The Emissary begins.](/images/valor-mortis/the-emissary-poison-gas.webp)


### Why Can't You Cross the Poison Yet?

The poison beyond the carriage cannot be cleared by shooting nearby objects or using Fire Transmutation. You need Shield to protect William while crossing the contaminated passage.

Collect the Seal, return to **The Other Side Lantern**, and continue through [Valley of Ashes](/valor-mortis/valley-of-ashes). You can return once Shield is unlocked.

</section>

<section id="letter" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What about the Emissary's Letter?

The investigation gains another piece of context in **Chapter 5: Crime**, after the Slaughtermill boss fight in Vale of Wrath.

Once Slaughtermill is defeated, continue from the boss platform into the exit tunnel near **Old Windmill**.

A short distance down the passage, look along the ground on the **right-hand side** for an item named **Emissary's Letter**.

The letter describes the emissary's movements and a meeting involving a French officer.

Collect the letter before leaving Old Windmill, but leave the poisoned passage for later. Return to **The Other Side** after Chapter 9, once Shield Transmutation is available.

</section>

<section id="retu   rn-with-shield" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When should you return to The Other Side?


The reliable time to return is **after completing Chapter 9**, with **Shield Transmutation** unlocked.

The Emissary can remain in your journal while you continue the main story. The abandoned carriage is accessible in Chapter 2, but the poisoned passage beyond it cannot be crossed using your starting abilities.

Once you have Shield, return to **Valley of Ashes → The Other Side**. You need the Shield ability to survive the contaminated passage, rather than Fire Transmutation or a separate poison-resistance item.

With the ability unlocked:

1. Travel to **Valley of Ashes → The Other Side**.
2. Take the left-hand route back toward the abandoned carriage.
3. Activate Shield before crossing the dense poison and continue through the area that previously stopped you.

The original carriage is your orientation point. You are not looking for a second Seal or another carriage elsewhere in the valley.

Keep Shield active while moving through the contaminated stretch. Once you reach the other side, follow the route downhill toward the concealed military camp.

### How to Recognize the Hidden Camp

After crossing the poison, follow the descending path into a ruined military camp.

Look for abandoned equipment, fallen soldiers, and red Nephtoglobin growths. Investigate the fallen escort here before continuing toward the blocked carriage farther ahead.

</section>

<section id="investigate-camp" className="scroll-mt-24">

## The hidden camp: investigate the note and the body

Start by checking the ground near the fallen escort for a written clue.

Pick up the note, then move farther into the camp toward the body caught among the red Nephtoglobin growths.

Look directly at the corpse until the **INVESTIGATE** prompt appears.

![The body inside the escort camp that must be investigated to advance The Emissary.](/images/valor-mortis/the-emissary-dead-escort.webp)

This interaction is more important than attacking the surrounding growths at random.

Examining the body changes the situation in the camp and triggers a hostile encounter.

### What happens after examining the corpse?

The enemy that appears is considerably more threatening than the ordinary soldiers encountered on the way to the original carriage.

One danger is the appearance of additional shooters. Their attacks can come together, making it risky to stand exposed while trying to finish a long melee combination.

Treat the open areas between the camp structures as places to cross, rather than positions to remain in while taking repeated shots.

When the enemy moves away, use the available obstacles to interrupt incoming fire and give yourself room to heal. Avoid committing to an extended attack sequence while several firearms are aimed at you.

Defeat the enemy before searching the remaining camp.

**The fight ending does not mean The Emissary is complete.** You still need to open the blocked route and investigate what lies beyond it.

</section>

<section id="blocked-route" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Can't open the blocked carriage after the camp fight?

Look for the carriage or train-car structure partly covered by red Nephtoglobin growths.

The obstruction has a **glowing red weak point** that must be destroyed with a firearm.

You are looking for the exposed core within the growth, not simply trying to break the wooden panels of the carriage.

From ground level, part of the structure can obscure your view of the target. Move to a higher position along the nearby cliffside and look down toward the obstruction when you cannot get a clear shot from directly in front.

Once you can see the core, fire at it to clear the blocked passage.

Do not confuse this obstruction with the poison that prevented entry during Chapter 2. Shield handles the environmental hazard; the firearm opens the physical barrier.

Continue along the newly accessible path.

</section>

<section id="final-clue" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where to Find Blood-Soaked Velvet and Finish The Emissary

After opening the carriage obstruction, move forward toward the blocked gate.

You may notice a blue spectral figure moving through this part of the area. It points the investigation toward the final location, rather than back toward the first carriage.

Near the blocked gate, look for **Blood-Soaked Velvet**, the bloodstained red fabric attached to a wooden post.

The investigation point is on the post, not the gate. Check the fabric itself rather than searching for another mechanism to open the passage.

![Blood-Soaked Velvet attached to the wooden post beside the blocked gate in The Emissary investigation.](/images/valor-mortis/the-emissary-final-investigation.webp)

Approach **Blood-Soaked Velvet** until the **INVESTIGATE** prompt appears, then interact with it.

The quest ends when **QUEST COMPLETE** appears and your journal updates.

![The Emissary completion notification after investigating the red fabric.](/images/valor-mortis/the-emissary-quest-complete.webp)

You do not need to return to the original carriage to hand in the investigation.

</section>

<section id="rewards" className="scroll-mt-24">

## The Emissary Reward and the Nearby Perfect Copy Amber

Completing The Emissary by investigating the bloodstained fabric grants **Sands of Time**, a consumable that returns you to the last Lantern where you rested without losing your carried Catalysts.

**Perfect Copy** comes from a separate chest inside the tent near the passage opened by destroying the carriage's red weak point.

This Amber increases the ranks of your other equipped **Tier 1 and Tier 2 Ambers**. The effect applies to qualifying equipped Ambers, not the ones sitting unused in your inventory.

Check the tent before leaving if Perfect Copy would improve your current setup. It is an optional pickup and is not automatically awarded when The Emissary is completed.

</section>
