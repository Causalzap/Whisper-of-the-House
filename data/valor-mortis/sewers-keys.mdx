Two different keys control the passages beneath the City of Orphans. Finding the first one opens the way deeper into the sewers, but several gates will still refuse to open until you collect the second.

The distinction matters because some of those locked doors appear before you even enter the underground area. You can spend a long time circling through the city's rooftops and courtyards without finding a way around them. Their solution is farther along the sewer route, not another switch hidden in the city.

The route is part of the journey toward the University. You already have **Grapple** from Fallen Village, and you do not need Shield Transmutation to obtain either key.

## How to Reach the Sewers Lantern in the City of Orphans

Continue through the City of Orphans until you reach the tower and the route leading down from the upper streets.

The approach involves climbing through buildings, crossing rooftops, and using the available zipline before reaching the sewer entrance. The children and their burrows provide clues as the route descends toward the underground passages.

Once inside, activate the **Sewers Lantern**. It is the most useful checkpoint for both keys and the nearby optional passages.

Your immediate destination is the circular chamber beyond the Lantern. The route through this chamber eventually loops back after you obtain the first key, so pay attention to its central Grapple point and the locked gate on the far side.

## Where to Find the Sewers Key

The **Sewers Key** is inside a chest in a lower room guarded by **two Risen Grenadiers**.

Start at the Sewers Lantern and follow the passage into the chamber with the circular pool. Use the blue Grapple point above the middle of the room to cross toward the other side.

You will see a locked gate along this route. It is the gate you need to return to after collecting the key, so remember its position and continue along the open passage for now.

The next stretch contains several different threats. Carrions and a Spewer occupy the bridge area, and a Risen Sapeur patrols farther along the walkway. The narrow ground makes it easy to lose room for dodging if you draw every enemy into the same fight.

The Sapeur is particularly dangerous when approached from the front. Its explosive equipment provides an opening for ranged damage, but clearing the enemies around it first gives you more space to move.

Continue until you encounter a **Volunteer** guarding the route near a ladder.

Defeat the Volunteer and look for the ladder descending into the room below.

### Open the chest below the ladder

Two Risen Grenadiers guard the cramped lower room.

Their explosives become more dangerous here because you cannot retreat very far without backing into the surrounding walls. Rather than fighting both at close range, draw their throws toward the opposite side of the room and punish the recovery.

A Grenadier's explosives can also damage the other Grenadier if they land close enough, allowing you to reduce the pressure without using as much ammunition.

Once the room is clear, search beside the body on the ground and open the chest.

![The Sewers Key pickup in the lower chamber guarded by two Risen Grenadiers.](/images/valor-mortis/sewers-key-chest.webp)

You receive the **Sewers Key**.

This is the ordinary key, not the Master Key. Its immediate purpose is to open the gate you passed near the circular chamber's Grapple point.

A nearby passage leads to the mice's **Mummy and Daddy** investigation. The two infected parents inside the cell must be left alive. Avoid shooting them while clearing the area; killing them can fail the request. This optional detour does not provide either sewer key.

## Which Door Does the Sewers Key Open?

Return to the **circular chamber with the overhead Grapple point**, the area you crossed shortly after leaving the Sewers Lantern.

Grapple across to the side with the locked gate and interact with it again.

The Sewers Key now opens this door.

Follow the stairs into the next stretch of tunnels. The route continues through enemies, confined walkways, and several branches that eventually connect back toward the Lantern.

This is the part of the sewers where the key distinction becomes important.

**The Sewers Key opens the first progression gate. It does not open doors marked as requiring the Sewers Master Key.**

![A locked passage requiring the Sewers Master Key, which cannot be opened with the ordinary Sewers Key.](/images/valor-mortis/sewers-master-key-locked-door.webp)

Some of those Master Key doors lead to exterior connections between the sewers and the City of Orphans. Others block access to optional pickups.

Finding a door that still says it requires the Master Key does not mean you missed a second ordinary key or need to return to Shamble Street. Keep moving through the newly opened underground route until you reach the platform section.

## Where to Find the Sewers Master Key

The **Sewers Master Key** is picked up from a body beside a platform over a flooded gap. The final obstacle is a movement sequence involving a swing point and a platform that can be pulled outward.

From the first gate opened with the Sewers Key, follow the tunnel route through the next enemy encounters.

Eventually the path divides. Take the **left-hand route** and continue toward the section with a blue Nephtoglobin mechanism.

You can activate this mechanism to open a shortcut back to the Sewers Lantern. It is worth doing before attempting the nearby jumps. Falling into the water is fatal, and the shortcut reduces the distance you must travel after a mistake.

Once the shortcut is available, turn back toward the junction and follow the route to the other side.

### How to cross the gap and reach the key

Look for the railing beside a gap with another Nephtoglobin platform on the opposite side.

The key is not reached by dropping into the water below. You need to use the higher route.

1. Climb or jump onto the railing to line up the crossing.
2. Use the available swing point to move across the gap.
3. During the crossing, interact with the pullable Nephtoglobin structure on the opposite wall to extend the platform.
4. Land on the extended platform and check the body just to its left.

![The Sewers Master Key pickup beside the extended platform above the flooded sewer gap.](/images/valor-mortis/sewers-master-key-pickup.webp)

Collect the **Sewers Master Key** from the body.

Unlike the first key, this one opens the remaining Master Key doors connecting the underground passages with the streets above.

### What to do if the platform is still out of reach

The important interaction is **pulling the platform outward**, not simply swinging as far as possible.

Watch for the blue Nephtoglobin structure on the far wall. It can blend into the surrounding growths when viewed from the starting ledge.

Trying to cross while the platform is still retracted leaves you without a proper landing surface. Move back to the safe side, line up the swing again, and use the pull interaction before committing to the landing.

You do not need to unlock Shield or spend an upgrade material to make this crossing.

## What Does the Sewers Master Key Unlock?

The Master Key opens the doors that remained locked during your first exploration of the City of Orphans and its underground passages.

There are two reasons to return to those doors:

- **Shortcuts:** Several gates connect the sewers to the city, reducing repeated travel through the same tunnels.
- **Optional equipment materials:** At least one Master Key route leads to another pickup that is useful for firearm upgrades.

After collecting the key, return toward the larger corridor and take the branch leading to the previously locked Master Key gate.

Open it and continue up the ramp toward the exterior section.

Look back toward the Nephtoglobin railing that can be activated to access a nearby platform. Clear the enemies beyond it, then check the crates for **Masterwork Firearm Parts**.

These are useful if you are upgrading the Pistol, Blunderbuss, or Musket toward their final levels.

The pickup is separate from the Sewers Master Key itself. Finding the key does not automatically give you the firearm material.

### Should you reopen every city door immediately?

The shortcut between the sewers and the city is worth opening as soon as you pass it, especially while you still remember which path it connects.

You can leave distant optional gates until later. The main progression does not require making a full circuit of every City of Orphans courtyard before proceeding.

If a locked door is standing between two locations you have already visited, unlocking it now is usually just a convenience. The more important question is whether the door leads to an unexplored room or an upgrade chest.

</section>

<section id="reach-shrike" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Leave the Sewers After Getting Both Keys

With the **Sewers Master Key**, open the remaining gates marked with its name and follow the passage that leads out of the underground network.

The next required encounter is **the Shrike**. Speak with her to receive the **Laboratory Key**, which is needed for the University.

Neither sewer key can replace the Laboratory Key. If you have collected both but still cannot enter the University, finish the conversation with the Shrike before searching for another entrance.

</section>
