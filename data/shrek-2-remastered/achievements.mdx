**Shrek 2 Remastered has 45 Steam and Xbox achievements**, worth **1,000 Gamerscore** on Xbox. The PS5 version has **46 trophies**, including the **Happily Ever After** Platinum for earning the other 45. Completing the story is only part of the list: several achievements require a particular mission outcome, a flawless Hero Time, or a return trip to an earlier chapter.

The Storybook's **Chapter Select** lets you revisit completed chapters, so a missed challenge does not mean restarting the whole campaign. Keep the harder no-hit runs for a separate attempt, but complete ordinary rescues and special-ability actions as you encounter them.

A useful completion order is:

1. **Play through the story:** Finish the 11 chapters and complete all of King Harold's tasks in Far Far Away.
2. **Handle actions tied to particular scenes:** Use newly introduced characters' special moves, meet Crazy Larry, free captives, and try Fiona's blackbird Hero Time.
3. **Build your collection:** Check the Scrapbook for incomplete Snapshots and Magic Bean sets; revisit chapters where something is missing.
4. **Spend coins and open Bonus Levels:** Work toward all nine upgrades and play each Bonus Level as it becomes available.
5. **Finish challenge runs:** Return for the no-damage achievements, Gingerbread Man's 45-second run, and four perfect Hero Times.
6. **Check remaining counters:** Finish enemy defeats, enemy types, Photo Mode, and any last Snapshot or rescue objectives.

The Steam completion percentages in the checklist are a snapshot. The requirements, not the changing percentages, determine what you still need to do.

## Which Achievements Unlock During the Story?

Every completed chapter awards its corresponding story achievement, starting with **Once upon A Time.. Again** in Shrek's Swamp and ending with **Victorious** after Final Fight. The nine chapters between them also award one achievement each when cleared.

These rewards follow story completion. You can leave unfinished Magic Beans, Snapshots, and flawless Hero Time challenges for later without losing your chapter-clear achievements.

If a chapter remains locked, finish its required objectives before returning to the Storybook. The [11-chapter walkthrough](/shrek-2-remastered#chapter-order) covers the encounters and tasks that control progression.

</section>

## At your Service and Grand Theft Carriage — Far Far Away

**At your Service** unlocks after you finish every assignment King Harold gives you in Far Far Away. If it remains locked, return to the [six royal tasks](/shrek-2-remastered#far-far-away) and complete whichever assignment is still unfinished before leaving the city.

**Grand Theft Carriage** has a different condition: obtain a **wanted rating** in Far Far Away. The ordinary mission fights are not themselves the achievement requirement. Look for the wanted indicator to register during city play rather than assuming a completed combat mission counts.

## Can't Touch Me, Ogre Killer, and Cinderella's No-Damage Challenge

Three achievements reward avoiding damage, but they do not test exactly the same situation.

### Can't Touch Me — Finish Any Level Without Getting Hurt

**Can't Touch Me** explicitly requires **single-player**. Pick a chapter you know well, start it alone, and complete the full level without taking a hit. A clean boss or Hero Time alone is not the same as finishing the entire chapter without damage.

**Ogre Killer** is worth considering because the level is a short, self-contained Puss in Boots encounter. That also gives you a chance to earn **Mightier Than the Sword** on the same successful run. If a mistake costs health, begin a fresh attempt rather than completing a damaged run and expecting either achievement.

### Mightier Than the Sword — Puss in Boots Without Damage

**Mightier Than the Sword** requires a damage-free victory in Ogre Killer. Winning the encounter after losing health still clears the chapter, but it will not complete this achievement.

Practice the three QTE rounds in [Ogre Killer](/shrek-2-remastered#ogre-killer), then make a clean attempt from the beginning. A single-player clear without damage can also satisfy **Can't Touch Me**.

### We're going shopping — Protect Cinderella

**We're going shopping** checks **Cinderella's health**, not your party's. Her health bar must remain full throughout Shopping Spree. If it drops, you need another attempt even if you successfully escort her to the final shop.

Keep an eye on Cinderella's portrait whenever enemies appear. The [Far Far Away walkthrough](/shrek-2-remastered#far-far-away) covers the normal Shopping Spree route.

## Black Bird Pie, Attack of The Giant Tomatoes, and Hero Needed

### Black Bird Pie — All 12 Birds in Shrek's Swamp

Fiona's Hero Time begins near Grandma's cottage at the end of Shrek's Swamp. Match the moving button prompts as they reach their target to pop the blackbirds. You need **all 12** for **Black Bird Pie**.

Completing the rhythm sequence does not by itself confirm that every bird was hit. If one gets away, replay the event rather than counting a partial result as success.

### Attack of The Giant Tomatoes — Under 45 Seconds

Gingerbread Man's Hero Time at Jack and Jill's Farm sends him uphill past rolling giant tomatoes. The requirement is to reach the end in **less than 45 seconds**.

Keep moving whenever a wave has passed your route. Stop briefly at safe positions to avoid being knocked back, then move as soon as the opening appears. Do not break your timed attempt to hunt for optional pickups; handle those in a separate replay.

### Hero Needed — Four Perfect Hero Times

**Hero Needed** needs four Hero Time completions with **no damage and no mistakes**. Winning an event after being hit or missing a prompt does not meet that condition.

Good events to prioritize for clean attempts are Fiona's blackbirds in Shrek's Swamp, the Dragon chase in Spooky Forest, Shrek's paddy-wagon challenge in Far Far Away, and Gingerbread Man's tomato run at the farm. They also fit naturally into the early story. This is a suggested practice order, not a requirement that these exact four events be used.

Watch each challenge's failure condition: avoid collisions in the chase, keep every rhythm input clean, protect yourself during the city fight, and avoid getting struck on the tomato slope. Replay an event if the run contains a mistake.

## Fee-Fi-Fo-Fum, Family Album, and Paparazzi — Beans and Snapshots

The two collections are related, but they are **not the same counter**:

- **In Vogue:** Earn your first Snapshot.
- **Family Album:** Find 50% of all Snapshots — **35 of 70**.
- **Paparazzi:** Obtain **all 70 Snapshots**.
- **Fee-Fi-Fo-Fum:** Find **all 108 Magic Beans**.

**Fee-Fi-Fo-Fum** tracks Magic Bean completion, while **Family Album** and **Paparazzi** track Snapshots. Collecting every bean does not automatically complete every Snapshot objective, and clearing the story is not enough to finish either collection.

If an achievement remains locked, check the unfinished entries in your **Scrapbook**, then return to the corresponding chapter through **Chapter Select**. Complete the missing objective and check whether the Snapshot or Magic Bean achievement unlocks.

## Five Bonus Level Achievements — What Actually Counts?

Five achievements ask you to **play** named Bonus Levels:

- **Ring around the Coins:** Ring Coliseum.
- **No Dunking:** Floating Floor.
- **Walking on Air:** Cloud Maze.
- **Wolf Trap:** Cage Drop.
- **Are You Not Entertained:** Crash Coliseum.

Once a Bonus Level becomes available in the **Storybook's Bonus menu**, enter it for the corresponding achievement. Each requirement specifies **playing** the named level, not earning a particular score or winning a round.

If a Bonus Level is locked, continue completing Snapshot objectives and return when it becomes available.

</section>

## Crazy Larry: Getting Stronger, Big Spender, Fully Upgraded, and Top o' The Morning

The upgrade achievements are straightforward purchases:

- **Getting Stronger:** Buy your first upgrade.
- **Big Spender:** Buy **four** upgrades.
- **Fully Upgraded:** Buy **all nine** upgrades from Crazy Larry.

**Fully Upgraded** requires all nine purchases, while **Top o' The Morning** requires meeting Crazy Larry at every appearance, including encounters outside his upgrade shops.

If you're missing an upgrade, return to the corresponding shop using the [Crazy Larry locations and prices](/shrek-2-remastered#crazy-larry-upgrades). If Top o' The Morning is still locked after all nine purchases, check the other encounters rather than spending more coins.

For **Everybody's Special**, use the special ability of each playable character at least once. Do not stop after the four characters from the opening swamp party. Other characters join or become playable later, including Puss, Wolf, Fairy, and the Shrek-and-Donkey pairing. Use each new character's special move while you can control them, then review the achievement after finishing the story.

## Coin Milestones, 1,000 Enemy Defeats, and Got them all!

The coin achievements require **250**, **500**, and **1,000** coins collected: **Pocket Change**, **Pot o' Gold**, and **Make It Rain**. Keep collecting coins from enemies, destructible objects, and chapter activities as you play. If the last milestone is unfinished after the story, a familiar replay or unlocked Bonus Level gives you another opportunity to collect coins.

Enemy-count achievements follow four milestones: **125** for Green Horn, **250** for Villains Beware, **500** for Bane Of Villains, and **1,000** for They See Me Coming. The Pied Piper rat encounter in Far Far Away is a useful place to fight many enemies during normal progression. You can return to combat-heavy chapters if the total is still short.

**Got them all!** tracks enemy variety. Defeat at least one of each type as you progress, paying particular attention to spiders, stone enemies in The Mines, stronger trolls, and different knight variants.

Even after reaching 1,000 total kills, this achievement can remain locked if you've missed a particular enemy type. Return to chapters containing unusual opponents instead of repeatedly defeating the same common enemies.

</section>

## For the People — Free Every Captured Character

**For the People** requires every captured character to be rescued. Reaching a chapter exit does not guarantee that you've freed everyone along the way.

The dwarves in [Shrek's Swamp](/shrek-2-remastered#shreks-swamp), the prisoners in [Prison Break](/shrek-2-remastered#prison-break), and the captives in [The Mines](/shrek-2-remastered#the-mines) are important groups to check. If the achievement remains locked, revisit unfinished rescue objectives before spending time repeating ordinary fights.

</section>

## Say Cheese, In Focus, and Giddy Up

**Say Cheese** unlocks when you enter **Photo Mode** for the first time. **In Focus** requires spending **more than 10 minutes** there. These are Photo Mode achievements, not Snapshot-collecting achievements; using the camera does not replace completing the Scrapbook's chapter objectives.

For **Giddy Up**, watch the Shrek-and-Donkey **idle animation**. Once the paired Shrek-and-Donkey character becomes available later in the story, choose a safe location, leave the game unpaused, and stop moving long enough for the idle animation to play. Simply riding or moving with Donkey is not the condition; the idle animation is what counts.

</section>

## What to Do After Final Fight — Achievement Cleanup

After earning **Victorious**, return to Chapter Select and finish the objectives you deliberately left for a replay.

Start with **Magic Beans and Snapshots**. They unlock Bonus content and feed directly into **Fee-Fi-Fo-Fum**, **Family Album**, and **Paparazzi**. Use the Scrapbook rather than searching every level again without knowing what you missed.

Next, finish **Crazy Larry** encounters and the **nine upgrades**. Check special abilities and captive rescues while visiting those chapters. Then run the five Bonus Levels and check Photo Mode and the Shrek-and-Donkey idle animation.

Save your concentrated challenge attempts for when you know the controls: a flawless **Ogre Killer** can address two no-hit conditions, while **Black Bird Pie** and the tomato run overlap with the broader **Hero Needed** objective. Finish by reviewing **Got them all!** and the cumulative coin and enemy totals.

On PS5, collecting the other 45 trophies completes **Happily Ever After**. Keep the checklist matched to your platform's unlocks and use the remaining requirements to decide your next replay.
