**Shrek 2 Remastered has 11 main story chapters**, beginning with Shrek's Swamp and ending in the ballroom battle against Fairy Godmother. The route follows the original console game's four-character party system: different obstacles require Shrek, Fiona, Donkey, Gingerbread Man, and the characters who join later.

## All 11 Chapters in Order

1. [Shrek's Swamp](#shreks-swamp) — Rescue the dwarves, collect eyeballs, help Lil' Red, and finish Fiona's Hero Time.
2. [Spooky Forest](#spooky-forest) — Guide the blind mice, open the cemetery path, and complete Canyon Run.
3. [Far Far Away](#far-far-away) — Complete King Harold's six city assignments.
4. [Ogre Killer](#ogre-killer) — Beat Puss in Boots.
5. [Walking the Path](#walking-the-path) — Escort the goats, cross the dangerous bridges, and finish Puss's Hero Time.
6. [Jack and Jill's Farm](#jack-and-jills-farm) — Help the Three Little Pigs finish their farm chores.
7. [Fairy Godmother's](#fairy-godmothers) — Break into the potion factory and escape.
8. [Prison Break](#prison-break) — Free the prisoners and recover the key.
9. [The Mines](#the-mines) — Rescue the trapped characters and chase Fairy Godmother.
10. [Cookie Cookie](#cookie-cookie) — Clear the burning city and help Mongo reach the castle.
11. [Final Fight](#final-fight) — Defeat Prince Charming and Fairy Godmother.

Each chapter changes the available party. If a character's normal attack cannot open a gate or move an object, inspect the obstacle for a symbol or nearby Magic Mirror hint before trying another attack.

## Which Character Should You Use?

Several obstacles cannot be cleared with ordinary attacks. The fastest way through them is to switch to the character whose special ability matches the object.

- **Shrek:** Lift and throw stunned enemies or movable objects. His belly flop can activate certain switches and knock down opponents.
- **Fiona:** Slow Time delays fast-moving hazards and enemies, giving the team a longer opening to pass.
- **Donkey:** Burro Blast breaks marked gates, knocks over trees, and moves heavy obstacles.
- **Gingerbread Man:** Throw candy canes at distant targets. His cookies attract enemies or other creatures to a chosen position.
- **Lil' Red:** Throw apples at distant switches, traps, and enemies.
- **Puss in Boots:** Cross ropes and tightropes that other characters cannot use.
- **Big Bad Wolf:** Huff 'n' Puff moves heavy objects, breaks weakened walls, and clears certain hazards.
- **Fairy:** Use her floating ability to get past obstacles that cannot be crossed from ground level.
- **Human Shrek and Steed Donkey:** Charge through marked obstacles during the later chapters.

Special abilities can require a charge. If an ability does not activate, check the available power before assuming you're using the wrong character.

Hero Time works differently from regular party play. It puts one character into a focused challenge, such as a chase, rhythm sequence, or timed obstacle course. Complete the current challenge to finish that story section.

## Chapter 1 — Shrek's Swamp Walkthrough

The opening chapter introduces the first four characters: Shrek, Fiona, Donkey, and Gingerbread Man.

### Collect the Eyeballs and Reach Robin Hood

Shrek needs **18 eyeballs** for the journey. Defeat the snails around his house and along the swamp paths, then collect the eyeballs they leave behind. Watch the objective counter and clear the nearby raised platforms and pond area if you're short.

The first rescued dwarf asks you to save his six brothers. Look along the side paths, raised platforms, and breakable obstacles until the rescue count reaches **7/7**. If you have only six, check the earlier paths before heading to Grandma's cottage.

Robin Hood and his Merry Men block an early section. Defeat the attackers and use Shrek's strength when a movable crate or seesaw blocks your way. At a seesaw, position another party member on one end and use Shrek's belly flop on the opposite end to launch that character across. Switch to the launched character and activate the gate.

### Catch the Fairies

A leprechaun blocks the next passage until you collect **12 fairies**.

Switch to Fiona and use Slow Time as you approach the moving fairies. Touch them while their movement is slowed, then check the counter before returning to the gatekeeper.

After the gate opens, use Gingerbread Man's ranged attacks for targets on the far side of water or other gaps. His cookies can also draw nearby creatures away from your route.

### Catch Eight Chickens for Lil' Red

Lil' Red asks for help gathering chickens for her grandmother.

1. Find a chicken in the clearing near the cauldron.
2. Attack it to stun it.
3. Switch to Shrek if necessary and pick up the stunned chicken.
4. Aim toward the cauldron and throw it inside.
5. Repeat until the objective reaches **8/8**.

A stunned chicken lying on the ground has not been delivered. Make sure it lands inside the cauldron before moving to the next one.

### Cross the Crocodiles and Reach Grandma

Approach the crocodile crossing and wait for their jaws to begin closing. Activate **Fiona's Slow Time** just before moving across, then keep going while the effect lasts.

In co-op, make sure both players are ready before activating Slow Time. Using it while the other player is still getting into position can waste the safe crossing window.

If you missed a Magic Bean earlier in Shrek's Swamp, you can return along the route before finishing the chapter. After the initial crossing, the crocodiles no longer block the return path.

Farther ahead, use Gingerbread Man to hit the distant target that lowers the crossing. Donkey can break the marked gate with Burro Blast. Where a raised switch is out of reach, place Shrek's movable crate beneath the ledge, jump up, and activate the switch.

Enter Grandma's cottage to begin **Fiona's Blackbird Symphony Hero Time**. Watch the button prompts beside the birds and press the matching input as it reaches the target marker. Clear the sequence to finish Shrek's Swamp.

A perfect result also contributes to **Black Bird Pie**. The additional conditions are covered in the [achievements checklist](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#hero-time-challenges).

## Chapter 2 — Spooky Forest Walkthrough

The carriage horses have been transformed into **three blind mice**. They follow the cheese trail through the forest, but gates, traps, and broken crossings prevent them from reaching the witch's house safely.

### Open the Gates and Protect the Mice

Ring the bell near the starting gate, then move ahead of the mice along the cheese trail.

When a talking tree blocks the crossing, switch to Donkey and use **Burro Blast**. The fallen tree becomes a bridge. If the mice stop at another crossing, check for an upright tree or switch on the far side before moving farther ahead.

Lil' Red is useful throughout the forest. Throw apples at mousetraps to disable them before the mice reach that part of the path. Her ranged attack can also reach bells or targets that are too far away for the other characters.

In areas with multiple gates, defeat the enemies guarding the path first. Some gates open after a mouse can reach the small passage beyond the fight.

### Visit Crazy Larry and Keep 100 Coins for the Toll

Crazy Larry runs his first upgrade shop near the cliff path leading toward the Poison Apple inn. You can buy upgrades there, but keep at least **100 coins** for the leprechaun who blocks the main gate farther ahead.

If you arrive at the toll without enough coins, look along the nearby bridge and surrounding paths before moving back through the forest.

### Cross Pest Cemetery

Beyond the toll, the route passes through a cemetery with more traps and broken paths.

Use Lil' Red to destroy the mousetraps and attack enemies behind the fence. At the broken crossings, switch to Donkey and use Burro Blast on the upright tombstones. The fallen stones cover the gaps so the mice can continue following the cheese.

Follow the path toward the Gingerbread House and break the marked gate with Donkey when it blocks the final approach.

### Complete Dragon's Canyon Run

At the end of the forest, Donkey rides Dragon during **Canyon Run**.

Watch the approaching obstacles rather than following every coin trail. Move around tree trunks, rise over low debris, and descend beneath overhead hazards. Begin adjusting your position before the obstacle reaches Dragon, especially through the narrower sections.

Finish the chase to clear Spooky Forest and proceed to Far Far Away.

## Chapter 3 — Far Far Away: All Six King Harold Tasks

Far Far Away is built around a city square and several surrounding assignments. King Harold's task markers indicate where help is needed.

You must complete **six tasks** before finishing the city's main progression. You can approach the assignments from their marked starting locations rather than following one fixed combat route.

### 1. Friar's Fat Boy — Catch the Chickens

Look for the overturned chicken truck close to the main road.

Stun each escaped chicken, pick it up, and put it into the marked pen. The task finishes when all **12 chickens** have been returned.

### 2. Family Jewels — Recover the Gems

At the museum, a giant troll scatters jewels across the floor as it moves. Follow from a safe distance and collect the dropped gems without drawing its attention. If the troll turns toward you and gives chase, move away until you can circle back to the remaining jewels.

Keep collecting until the objective counter is complete. Defeating the troll is unnecessary.

### 3. Street Brawl — Put the Hooligans in the Paddy Wagon

Find the city street where the hooligans are fighting.

This becomes **Shrek's Hero Time**. Stun an enemy with Shrek's attacks or belly flop, pick it up, and throw it into the police wagon. Continue until **18 hooligans** have been captured.

Defeating or stunning an enemy without putting it into the wagon does not finish that delivery.

### 4. Shopping Spree — Protect Cinderella

Cinderella moves between shops while thieves try to attack her.

Stay near Cinderella and stop enemies approaching her route. She has her **own health bar**, so protecting your party alone is not enough.

Clear the attackers at each shop and keep moving with Cinderella until the escort finishes. If she takes damage, you can still finish the mission normally, but you'll need a clean attempt for the [We're going shopping achievement](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#no-hit-challenges).

### 5. Humpty Dumpty — Find the Eggshell Pieces

Humpty Dumpty has broken into pieces around the city.

Follow the task area and look for eggshells around the street, raised surfaces, and nearby rooftops. Use springboards and accessible platforms where an item is above ground level. Return the missing pieces until the task counter is complete.

### 6. Pied Piper — Defeat the Rats

The Pied Piper challenge sends you through several rat encounters.

Clear the rats in the current arena before advancing. When a gate needs multiple switches held down, position party members on the floor plates to open the route.

Continue through the arenas and defeat the Pied Piper to finish the assignment.

### How to Leave Far Far Away

Once the six royal jobs are complete, return to King Harold and choose to continue to the next level.

Completing all six royal tasks is also required for [At your Service](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#far-far-away-tasks).

## Chapter 4 — How to Beat Puss in Boots in Ogre Killer

Ogre Killer plays out across **three rounds of timed button prompts**. Puss attacks one party member at a time, and a symbol moves toward that character's target. Press the matching button as the symbol reaches the marker.

The prompts become faster in later rounds. Watch for the next symbol as soon as the previous one registers; pressing too early can cost you the timing window. After the final round, Puss joins the party, bringing the rope-crossing ability you'll need in Walking the Path.

Players attempting **Mightier Than the Sword** need to complete the encounter without damage. See the [no-damage achievement requirements](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#no-hit-challenges).

</section>

## Chapter 5 — Walking the Path Walkthrough

The route toward Jack and Jill's Farm has pumpkins, difficult crossings, goat escorts, and a troll guarding the way forward. Puss in Boots is now part of the party.

### Clear the Pumpkins and Move the Logs

Defeat the hostile pumpkins, including the larger ones that control parts of the path. When machinery blocks the next crossing, look for a switch or cog that can operate the nearby crane or bridge.

Shrek can move heavy objects, while Puss can cross ropes to reach mechanisms the rest of the party cannot access. Use the character whose route leads to the switch rather than trying to force the entire party through the obstacle.

### Escort William Gruff and Cross the Sawblades

William Gruff asks the party to help him get across a dangerous bridge.

Clear nearby enemies before leading him through the crossing. At the sawblade machinery, watch the blades' movement and use the available switches or levers to create an opening.

Do not rush the escort through moving blades. Activate the mechanism, wait for the clear section, and cross before the hazard returns.

Another goat escort appears later in the chapter. Finish its route before continuing toward the bridge guarded by the troll.

### Defeat the Bridge Troll

The troll throws bombs and becomes vulnerable when one is returned.

Wait until a bomb is close enough to strike back, then send it toward the troll. Keep away from its club attacks while it recovers, and repeat until it is defeated.

If your attacks are doing little damage, focus on returning the bombs rather than repeatedly attacking the troll at close range.

Crazy Larry's second shop is on a narrow grassy path opposite one of the bridges. It is worth checking before committing to the later encounters.

### Finish Puss in Boots' Hero Time

Puss's Hero Time takes place along a steep cliffside and waterfall route.

Use his movement to traverse ropes and narrow platforms. Watch the landing point before jumping between moving sections, and use springboards to reach the next ledge.

Continue through the climbing sequence until the exit triggers. Finishing it completes Walking the Path and opens Jack and Jill's Farm.

## Chapter 6 — Jack and Jill's Farm Walkthrough

The Three Little Pigs are running the farm while Jack and Jill are away. They agree to help the party reach Fairy Godmother after their chores are finished.

### Enter the Farm

Clear the large vegetables and enemies along the opening path. Where the cow-print gate blocks the way, provoke the nearby cow so it charges through the obstacle.

Use Gingerbread Man's ranged attack on targets above or beyond the path. His cookies can distract the wasps near the farm entrance, allowing the party to pass.

Speak to the Three Little Pigs to begin the chores. They are spread around the farm, so return to the central area when looking for another assignment.

### Find the Needle in a Haystack

Jill's sewing needle is hidden among the hay piles. Break the stacks in the pig's search area while keeping nearby enemies away, then pick up the needle when it appears and bring it back to the pig to finish the chore.

### Scare the Chickens Out of the Cornfield

Another pig needs the harvest protected from chickens.

The objective is to **drive the chickens out**, not kill them. Use Shrek's belly flop to frighten the birds away from the crops and through the field.

If chickens remain, check the branches of the cornfield paths rather than assuming they have all left after the first pass.

### Protect Jack's Beanstalk

The pigs are trying to grow a replacement beanstalk, but rats keep attacking it.

Stay near the growing plant and defeat the rats before they reach it. Clear each approaching group and continue defending until the beanstalk objective finishes.

### Catch the Falling Eggs

Move beneath the falling eggs during the henhouse challenge. Follow the lane where the next egg is descending and reposition before it reaches the ground.

Continue catching eggs until the required total has been reached. Moving too far from the active drop lane can cause you to miss several in a row.

### Get Water From Jack's Hill

The final water task leads to Gingerbread Man's **Giant Tomatoes Hero Time**.

Giant tomatoes roll down the hill in waves. Use the gaps between the flower beds as safe positions, then move uphill after each wave passes. Avoid standing in the middle of a lane while a large tomato is approaching.

Reach the water pail at the top to finish the challenge. An under-45-second finish is required for [Attack of The Giant Tomatoes](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#hero-time-challenges).

When the pigs' tasks are finished, speak to them again and collect the delivery for Fairy Godmother.

</section>

## Chapter 7 — Fairy Godmother's Potion Factory Walkthrough

The party needs a potion from Fairy Godmother's factory. Shrek, Donkey, Puss in Boots, and Lil' Red must work together to pass the security systems.

### Redirect the Security Beams

The first factory rooms are guarded by enemies and bright security beams.

Defeat the guards and inspect the crystal-ball stands. Move the available crystal ball into the stand that redirects the beam, allowing the party to cross the blocked passage.

After crossing, you may need to move the same crystal to another stand farther along the route. In later rooms, break display cases to recover additional crystal balls.

Avoid walking through active security beams when you can redirect them first.

### Open the Gear Door and Face Fairy Godmother

Find the cog that activates the machinery near the next door and place it into the mechanism.

Lil' Red then faces Fairy Godmother during Hero Time. Throw apples toward her while avoiding the incoming spells. The moving platform changes speed and starts carrying obstacles, so watch the floor as carefully as the enemy.

Jump over crates and keep firing during safe openings until the encounter ends.

### Reach the Potion Room

The factory's potion room rises through several levels.

Use the floor switches and movable platforms to send party members upward. When one character reaches a higher level, look for the next mechanism that raises the remaining team.

Puss in Boots can cross the ropes near the upper part of the room. Follow those ropes to reach the potion that cannot be accessed from the floor.

### Escape the Factory

After the potion is taken, continue past the guards and machinery. Where a large potion vat blocks the route, knock it over to open the next passage.

Prince Charming and his guards appear farther along. Clear the attackers and continue through the factory.

The escape ends with a large rolling cauldron pursuing the party. Move forward, jump the low obstacles, and avoid stopping to fight enemies that are not blocking the exit.

Finish the escape to complete Fairy Godmother's.

## Chapter 8 — Prison Break Walkthrough

Shrek and Donkey have been imprisoned. The rescue team consists of **Big Bad Wolf, Lil' Red, Gingerbread Man, and Fairy**.

### Get Past the Searchlights

Follow the prison walls and avoid the moving searchlights where possible.

When a security section blocks the gate, position characters on the floor switches to expose the target. Use Gingerbread Man's ranged attack to hit it.

Some gates require more than one switch held at the same time, so leave party members standing on the correct plates before moving the others.

### Use Wolf to Move the Iron Ball

Several prison gates are connected to large iron balls and switches.

Switch to Big Bad Wolf and use Huff 'n' Puff to push a ball toward the required floor plate. When the path slopes upward, position Wolf behind the ball and continue pushing it toward the raised mechanism.

Other sections require sending the ball downhill to smash a reinforced gate.

If the ball cannot reach the target, inspect the nearby switch or barrier before pushing it again. The correct route usually depends on opening a passage first.

### Rescue the Captives

Search the cells and side rooms as you advance. Some captives are behind ordinary cell doors, while others are reached after moving a ball or raising a set of cages.

Use Gingerbread Man's ranged attacks for cell targets that cannot be struck from the floor. Big Bad Wolf can also break weakened walls concealing alternative paths.

The level includes prisoners and the blind mice, so check the nearby cells instead of immediately following the next exit marker.

### Complete Staircase of Doom

Near the end of the chapter, Big Bad Wolf must climb the **Staircase of Doom** during Hero Time.

Move upward across the steps and cages while avoiding falling bombs and shifting platforms. Wait for moving ledges to reach a safe position before jumping onto them.

Continue to the key at the top. Recovering it allows the rescue party to complete the prison escape.

## Chapter 9 — The Mines Walkthrough

The route through Mount Grim introduces **Human Shrek riding Steed Donkey**, alongside Puss in Boots, Gingerbread Man, and Big Bad Wolf.

### Break Through the Mine Gates

Use the mounted Shrek-and-Donkey charge to smash the marked obstacles. Fight the frogs and other enemies guarding the tunnels, then activate the nearby switches to open the next passage.

Gingerbread Man can hit enemies or targets on ledges with his candy cane. Puss can reach ropes and raised platforms that lead to switches for the rest of the party.

### Rescue the Trapped Dwarves

The mine contains several trapped dwarves and suspended cages.

Look for raised platforms, ropes, and switches near the prison areas. Use Puss to cross high ropes and operate mechanisms that lift or release the cages.

Some passages require clearing a spider nest or moving through an enemy-filled chamber before reaching the next rescue.

Keep checking the side cages as you advance. Reaching the exit without freeing the trapped characters can leave rescue objectives unfinished.

### Defeat the Stone Warriors

Animated stone enemies appear farther into the mine.

Avoid standing in front of their heavy swings. Attack after an enemy has committed to its strike, then move back before the next attack starts.

Continue through the switches and gates until you reach the encounter involving Snow White and Fairy Godmother.

### Finish the Minecart Chase

The Mines ends with a mounted chase.

Ride Shrek and Donkey along the track, jump over hazards that must be avoided, and charge through the marked obstacles. Watch the sections with moving blades and barriers.

Stay close enough to Fairy Godmother's cart to land attacks when the opening appears. Continue until the chase finishes and the route back to Far Far Away opens.

## Chapter 10 — Cookie Cookie Walkthrough

The party returns to Far Far Away to find the city burning while Mongo, the giant Gingerbread Man, is under attack.

### Put Out the Fires and Rescue the Citizens

Papa Bear asks the party to extinguish **19 fires** and rescue **26 citizens**.

Use Big Bad Wolf's breath to put out fires within reach. For flames that are too far away, look for the hose equipment near the city route.

Check the doors behind the fires after extinguishing them. Citizens may still need to be freed before the rescue objective is complete.

Return to Papa Bear when the task counter is finished.

### Reach the Rooftops

Fight through the knights and move toward the raised platforms. Use Puss in Boots to cross the ropes and reach the switches that raise or open the next route.

Look for TNT and destructible obstacles around the rooftops. Clearing the enemy positions allows Mongo to continue advancing toward the castle.

### Use Gingerbread Man's Cookies to Guide Mongo

Mongo can destroy the large obstacles blocking the street, but he needs to attack the correct location.

Switch to Gingerbread Man and aim a cookie toward the target you want Mongo to hit. Throw it where the path is blocked, then move out of the way as Mongo strikes.

Repeat this around the police wagons and other obstructions. A cookie thrown at an unrelated location may attract Mongo's attack without opening the passage.

### Protect Mongo From the Balloons

The final Hero Time puts you on a cannon defending Mongo.

Aim at the approaching balloons and fire before they reach him. Prioritize balloons that are already low or close to the target, then sweep back across the higher ones.

Mongo must survive the attack. Keep firing until the challenge ends, then continue to the Final Fight.

## Chapter 11 — How to Beat Fairy Godmother in Final Fight

The final chapter takes place in the ballroom. Shrek, Fiona, Donkey, and Puss in Boots fight a series of enemies before confronting Fairy Godmother.

### Clear the First Enemy Waves

The fight begins with knights, followed by enemies throwing potions.

Defeat the enemies in each wave to advance. Keep moving when potion attacks target the floor and avoid remaining in a crowded section of the arena.

Two trolls armed with bombs appear later.

Wait for a bomb to land within reach, then return it toward the troll. Move away before it explodes. Repeat the process until both trolls have been defeated.

### Fight Fairy Godmother

Fairy Godmother attacks from the center of the arena and uses a protective bubble.

Fairy Godmother's blue shockwaves can hit several party members in quick succession. Watch the floor as the attack travels toward you, then jump over the wave or move out of its path.

**Fiona's Slow Time** can make these dodges easier when the attack timing becomes difficult. Activate it before the wave reaches the party, then use the longer opening to reposition.

Wait until Fairy Godmother's protective bubble disappears before moving in to attack. If the entire party is defeated, you may have to repeat the encounter's earlier phases, so avoid wasting healing and defensive power-ups at the beginning.

### Defeat Prince Charming and the Remaining Trolls

Prince Charming arrives with another group of trolls.

Handle the trolls' bombs as before and avoid fighting several enemies at close range at once. After clearing them, attack Prince Charming when he is exposed.

Fairy Godmother returns for the last stage. Continue avoiding the blue attacks, strike during the unshielded windows, and repeat until her health is depleted.

The final scene completes **Victorious** and ends the main story.

## Where to Find Crazy Larry and Which Upgrades to Buy

Crazy Larry offers permanent improvements to the party. There are **three upgrade shops** with **nine purchases** in total.

### Spooky Forest — First Shop

Find Larry on the cliffside route before the bridge leading toward the Poison Apple inn.

Available upgrades:

- **Three Bears Porridge:** +3 Hit Points — **200 coins**
- **Ogre Ring:** +1 Attack — **400 coins**
- **Pot of Gold:** Double coins — **600 coins**

Pot of Gold is useful early because it increases the coins you collect afterward. Remember that the chapter also contains a **100-coin toll**, so keep enough money to open the story gate.

### Walking the Path — Second Shop

Find Larry on the grassy path near the bridges.

Available upgrades:

- **Green Clover:** +6 Hit Points — **400 coins**
- **Heart of Gold:** Regenerate +8 HP per minute — **1,200 coins**
- **Lucky Horseshoe:** +4 Attack — **1,300 coins**

Attack and health improvements help with the larger enemies and the later prison and ballroom fights.

### Prison Break — Third Shop

Larry's final shop is behind a breakable wall along an alternative prison route. Use the iron ball to open the gate and reach the area beyond it.

Available upgrades:

- **Nine Lives:** +9 Hit Points — **900 coins**
- **Cupid's Arrow:** +6 Attack — **1,800 coins**
- **Lucky Rabbit's Foot:** Regenerate +8 HP per minute — **2,200 coins**

All nine upgrades cost **9,000 coins**. The Storybook cannot sell upgrades, so return to Larry's shop in the relevant chapter if you skipped a purchase. Coins collected later in the story or from unlocked Bonus Levels can help pay for the more expensive upgrades.

A separate achievement requires purchasing all nine upgrades. Check [Getting Stronger, Big Spender, and Fully Upgraded](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements#larry-upgrades) if you're tracking completion.

## How to Replay Chapters From the Storybook

Open the **Storybook** and use **Chapter Select** to return to a level you've already completed. You can replay chapters to retry a difficult encounter, revisit a missed objective, or practice a Hero Time.

To continue the story, select the next unlocked chapter. If it is still unavailable, return to your current chapter and complete its required story objective.

You can finish all 11 chapters without collecting every Magic Bean or Snapshot. Check the **Scrapbook** to see whether a completed chapter still has unfinished collection objectives.

</section>

<section id="after-final-fight" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Do After Finishing Shrek 2 Remastered?

Final Fight ends the story, but the Storybook remains available. You can revisit earlier chapters, play unlocked Bonus Levels, or spend leftover coins at Crazy Larry's shops.

Magic Beans and Snapshots are tracked in the Scrapbook. For trophies and Steam achievements that require a special outcome, such as a flawless Hero Time or a no-damage clear, use the [45-achievement checklist](/shrek-2-remastered/achievements) to identify what remains.
