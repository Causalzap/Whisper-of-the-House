**Go-Go Town progression** usually feels stuck because several separate systems
are being treated as one progress bar.

Town Rank, Top Town Tournament goals, Claim Rewards, EGO, EGO Tech, Rebuy,
building construction, fit-outs, recipes, workers, and actual use all control
different steps.

I diagnose them in this order:

**current goal → completed reward → Town Rank → EGO Tech node → Build or Rebuy
location → finished building → fit-out or recipe → worker and supply**

When I follow that order, I can usually identify the missing step without
grinding another full day, buying unrelated Tech nodes, or rebuilding an entire
district.

<h2 id="identify-stuck-layer">
  First Identify Which Progression Layer Is Stuck
</h2>

Before gathering more Coins or EGO, I identify the exact symptom.

There are four common “locked” states:

### 1. The EGO Tech tier is visible, but the node is still unpurchased

Reaching the required Town Rank can make a tier available without purchasing
every node inside it.

I still need enough EGO and must select the node manually.

### 2. The node was purchased, but I am searching the wrong Build category

A building, fit-out, processor, decoration, service, or terrain option may
appear in a different Build menu category than I expected.

I reopen the node and check what type of content it actually unlocked before
assuming it is missing.

### 3. I already bought the first copy, so another copy is in Rebuy

EGO Tech handles the original unlock.

Rebuy handles additional copies of many objects and buildings after that first
purchase.

I do not search Rebuy for something I have never unlocked, and I do not
repurchase the original Tech node when I only need a duplicate.

### 4. The building exists, but it is still only a shell or has no setup

A placed foundation is not a finished building.

Even after construction, the building may still need:

- its ribbon cut;
- an interior fit-out;
- a selected recipe;
- an assigned worker;
- input materials;
- or free output space.

These four states can look almost identical from the outside. The fix depends
on the exact layer.

<h2 id="town-goals-not-progressing">
  Why My Town Goals Are Not Progressing
</h2>

I begin with the exact wording in the Town Rankings App.

A goal that says `Build` is not always complete when I place a foundation.

I normally need to:

1. place the building;
2. deliver every required construction material;
3. finish the shell;
4. cut the ribbon;
5. and wait for the goal to register.

Other goal wording can require a different final action:

- a sale goal requires a tourist to purchase the item;
- a recipe goal requires the correct fit-out and selected product;
- a worker goal requires a housed Townie to be assigned;
- a tourist goal may require actual use;
- a collection goal requires the requested item count to register;
- an invitation goal may require returning to the Townie after completing the
  request.

I do not build a second copy until I have checked whether the first copy is
finished and configured.

When a goal remains incomplete, I stand beside the relevant building and check
the objective again. That lets me compare what the game is asking for with what
I have actually completed.

I reopen Town Rankings and look for the reward claim.

I do not close the App immediately after pressing the claim button.

If the new goals still do not appear, I leave the area, complete one short task,
and reopen the App. I reload the save only after confirming the reward was
actually claimed.

<h2 id="town-rank-vs-ego-tech">
  Town Rank and EGO Tech Are Separate Progression Tracks
</h2>

One of my clearest progression mistakes happened after reaching **Town Rank 9**.

I opened EGO Tech and realized that I had never purchased **Tier 6**.

This is the rule I use:

- **Town Rankings** determine tournament goals and bracket progression.
- **Town Rank** opens access to later progression.
- **EGO Tech** contains purchases I must select manually.
- **EGO** is the currency used to purchase those Tech nodes.
- **Rebuy** supplies additional copies after the original unlock.

When a building appears to be missing at a late Town Rank, I do not only inspect
the newest tier.

I scroll backward through every earlier EGO Tech tier and look for:

- an unpurchased node;
- a node I skipped because it was not needed at the time;
- an earlier prerequisite;
- or an unlock I bought but forgot was moved to Rebuy afterward.

<h3 id="when-to-spend-ego">
  When I Spend EGO and When I Save It
</h3>

I purchase a Tech node when:

- the active Town Goal names its building or system;
- it unlocks a required production material;
- it provides housing for a Townie I already need;
- it fixes a measured tourist desire gap;
- or it removes a real transport or service bottleneck.

I wait when:

- the node is mainly cosmetic;
- the current goal needs a different unlock;
- I have not stabilized the existing version of the same service;
- or I am buying it only because the EGO meter is full.

A full EGO meter is a reason to inspect EGO Tech. It is not a reason to buy an
unrelated node.

<h2 id="not-enough-ego">
  How I Fix Not Enough EGO
</h2>

Happy tourists generate EGO.

The slower method is to walk around town and inspect individual tourist
reactions. I still use that when I want to see whether a specific shop entrance
or attraction works.

For the overall diagnosis, I open the **Tourist Tracker App** first.

The App helps me identify whether tourists are consistently missing:

- Shopping;
- Hunger;
- Exploration;
- Entertainment;
- Rest;
- or Health.

    I check the Tourist Tracker before buying another service. The lowest desire
    tells me which part of the tourist route needs attention.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

My EGO route is:

1. open the Phone;
2. open Tourist Tracker;
3. identify the weakest desire;
4. go to the district where tourists are failing that need;
5. inspect the nearest matching service;
6. fix access, staffing, stock, cleanliness, or capacity;
7. allow another group of tourists to use the district;
8. check the Tracker again.

I do not automatically build a new object when a category is low.

First I check whether the existing service is:

- open;
- staffed;
- stocked;
- connected to a usable path;
- close enough to the tourist route;
- surrounded by clean ground;
- and able to handle the current visitor count.

A restaurant does not satisfy Hunger when it has no selected recipe or
ingredients.

A health or entertainment building does not help when tourists cannot reach its
entrance.

A service can exist on the map while contributing almost nothing to actual
tourist satisfaction.

<h3 id="when-to-add-tourist-service">
  When I Add Another Tourist Service
</h3>

I add another object or service when:

- Tourist Tracker repeatedly shows the same weak desire;
- the existing service is functioning;
- tourists are actually reaching it;
- and its capacity is still insufficient.

I wait when:

- the current object is closed or unstaffed;
- its shop Storage is empty;
- garbage is surrounding the route;
- tourists cannot reach the entrance;
- or another desire is clearly the larger problem.

This prevents me from spending EGO on three versions of the wrong solution.

<h2 id="ego-full-tech-locked">
  Why EGO Tech Is Still Locked With a Full EGO Meter
</h2>

A full EGO meter does not automatically purchase Tech.

I still need to open EGO Tech, select the node, and confirm the purchase.

When I have enough EGO but the desired node cannot be selected, I check:

1. whether its Town Rank or tournament requirement is complete;
2. whether the previous bracket reward was claimed;
3. whether an earlier node in the tier is required;
4. whether I am looking at the correct tier;
5. whether the item was already unlocked and moved to Rebuy.

I do not keep farming EGO when the meter is already full.

At that point, the blocker is usually a requirement, an unclaimed reward, a
missed Tech node, or the wrong menu.

<h2 id="building-missing">
  A Building Is Unlocked but Missing From the Build Menu
</h2>

When a building appears unlocked but I cannot find it, I use the four-state
check from the opening section.

### Check the original node

I reopen EGO Tech and confirm that the node says purchased rather than merely
available.

### Check the content type

I verify whether the unlock is:

- a full building;
- a fit-out;
- a processor;
- a service object;
- an Industry Zone item;
- a terrain option;
- or an interior object.

A fit-out will not appear beside full building shells.

### Check Rebuy

When I already placed or used the first copy, I look in Rebuy for another one.

### Check the existing shell

I inspect the map for an unfinished foundation or completed shell I forgot to
configure.

I do not spend another full day earning EGO until all four checks are complete.

<h2 id="rebuy-vs-ego-tech">
  Rebuy Does Not Replace EGO Tech
</h2>

EGO Tech and Rebuy solve different problems.

I use EGO Tech when:

- the item has never been unlocked;
- the original node is still available for purchase;
- or a prerequisite node is missing.

I use Rebuy when:

- the original node has already been purchased;
- I need another copy;
- the first copy was placed elsewhere;
- or the game directs me to purchase the object again.

If Rebuy does not contain the item, I return to the original EGO Tech node.

If EGO Tech says the node was purchased, I check whether the first copy already
exists in town before assuming it disappeared.

`No Spot` message.

That is a placement problem, not an EGO or Town Rank problem.

When I see `No Spot`, I:

1. move to a visibly open area;
2. rotate the building;
3. clear small decorations and loose objects;
4. check for water, elevation, walls, or another hidden footprint conflict;
5. make sure the entrance can face a usable path;
6. place it temporarily before deciding on the final district layout.

I do not begin by flattening a large section of town.

A small decoration, narrow edge, uneven tile, or blocked entrance can invalidate
an otherwise empty-looking area.

I build the Tourism Hub when:

- the active Town Goal requires it;
- I am ready to support its tourist role;
- or I have a suitable central location.

I wait when the current goal is unrelated and placing it would force me to
destroy a working shop, road, or service route.

<h2 id="recipe-not-appearing">
  Why a Recipe Is Not Appearing
</h2>

A completed building does not guarantee that every recipe is immediately
available.

I check:

1. the building has the correct fit-out;
2. the required recipe or production Tech was purchased;
3. I am interacting with the production point rather than the outer shell;
4. the selected fit-out supports that recipe;
5. the recipe belongs to this processor or shop type;
6. an earlier EGO Tech node was not skipped.

I distinguish two different symptoms:

### The recipe is absent from the menu

This is usually an unlock, Tech, fit-out, or wrong-building problem.

### The recipe is visible but production does not start

This is usually an input, worker, demand, Storage, or output-space problem.

<h2 id="creative-mode">
  When Creative Mode Is the Better Choice
</h2>

Creative Mode is useful when my real goal is building and layout practice rather
than advancing the current Top Town Tournament save.

I use it to test:

- a large tourist district;
- road and Courier layouts;
- Industry Zone spacing;
- terrain changes;
- building footprints;
- and decorative plans without normal economic pressure.

I do not treat Creative Mode as a repair button for a blocked normal town.

When I want to continue the same progression save, I still need to fix the
missing goal, reward, Tech node, building setup, recipe, or worker.

I switch to Creative Mode when I am no longer interested in solving the current
economic bottleneck and only want to experiment with the town design.

That distinction prevents me from spending several hours rebuilding a layout
and then expecting it to complete a normal Town Rankings objective.

<h2 id="progression-achievements">
  EGO and Ranking Achievements as a Progress Check
</h2>

Some achievements are tied to the same systems covered on this page.

For example, **Ego Boost** tracks reaching **10,000 EGO**, while other
achievements are connected to EGO Tech progression and reaching the top of the
Town Rankings.

I treat these achievements as confirmation, not as the main route.

When EGO, Tech, or ranking progress is working correctly, the related
achievement progress follows naturally.

I do not grind toward 10,000 EGO while:

- the EGO meter is already capped;
- a required Tech node is waiting to be purchased;
- a tournament reward remains unclaimed;
- or tourists are repeatedly failing the same desire.

The achievement is the result of a functioning progression loop. It is not the
fix for a broken one.

<h2 id="when-to-go-forward">
  When I Move Forward and When I Go Back
</h2>

I move forward when:

- the next Town Goal set is visible;
- the required EGO Tech node is purchased;
- the building appears in Build or Rebuy;
- construction is complete;
- and the relevant fit-out or recipe is available.

I go back to Town Rankings when:

- completed goals have not produced the next bracket;
- Claim Rewards is still available;
- the rank requirement is missing;
- or the final TownCo. dialogue did not finish.

I go back to EGO Tech when:

- a building or recipe is absent;
- an old tier remains incomplete;
- or I have enough EGO but have not purchased the node.

I go back to the building when:

- the shell is still unfinished;
- the ribbon has not been cut;
- no fit-out is selected;
- or I have not interacted with the completed structure.

I go to automation troubleshooting when:

- the building and recipe exist;
- but workers, processors, Couriers, Storage, or Maintenance are not moving.

This keeps me from solving an unlock problem by adding workers or solving an
automation problem by buying unrelated Tech.

<h2 id="final-reset">
  My Final Progression Reset
</h2>

When the correct next step is still unclear, I use this reset:

1. Save the game.
2. Open Town Rankings and read the active goal word for word.
3. Claim any completed reward.
4. Let every TownCo. dialogue line finish.
5. Reopen Town Rankings.
6. Record the current Town Rank.
7. Open EGO Tech and scan every earlier tier.
8. Purchase the required original node.
9. Check Rebuy only after confirming the original purchase.
10. Find and finish any existing building shell.
11. Cut the ribbon and interact with the completed building.
12. Select the correct fit-out and recipe.
13. Use Tourist Tracker if EGO generation is the blocker.
14. Test one normal gameplay cycle before reloading.
15. Reload only after preserving the current goals, assignments, and recipes.

I do not delete buildings, spend all remaining EGO, or rebuild the town before
this check.

Those actions remove the evidence that tells me which progression layer was
actually missing.

<h2 id="faq">
  Go-Go Town Progression FAQ
</h2>

### Why does Tourist Tracker show a need is covered but EGO is still slow?

An object can exist without serving tourists effectively. I check whether it is
open, staffed, stocked, reachable, clean, and close to the route visitors
actually use. I also check whether another desire has become the new lowest
category.

### Can Creative Mode fix my stuck normal town?

No. I use Creative Mode to test layouts and terrain without normal economic
pressure. A blocked normal save still needs its Town Goal, reward, Tech node,
building, recipe, or worker requirement completed there.

### Can Rebuy unlock an item for the first time?

No. I purchase the original unlock through EGO Tech first. Rebuy is for another
copy after that original node has already been purchased.

### Why is a Tech tier available but its building still missing?

The tier may be visible while the individual node remains unpurchased. The
unlock may also be a fit-out or processor in another Build category, or the
first copy may already exist and additional copies may be in Rebuy.

### Why did building construction not complete the Town Goal?

The goal may also require the ribbon, a fit-out, recipe selection, worker
assignment, tourist use, sale, or another final interaction. I reread the exact
goal wording after finishing the shell.

### Why did my EGO achievement not unlock even though the meter is full?

A full current meter is not necessarily the same as reaching the achievement's
tracked total or completing related Tech progression. I continue using EGO and
earning it through satisfied tourists instead of leaving the meter capped.

### Should I build another Advanced Materials Factory when no recipe appears?

No. I first confirm the existing factory is fully constructed, its ribbon was
cut, and I am using the completed production interface. If the recipe is still
absent, I check EGO Tech and the required fit-out before buying another factory.

### Why can I reach Town Rank 9 with an earlier EGO Tech tier unfinished?

Town Rank and EGO Tech are separate tracks. Ranking can advance through Top Town
Tournament goals while older Tech nodes remain unpurchased. I scan backward
through every tier instead of checking only the newest one.
