The **Agent Peanut safe code** is part of Go-Go Town's final progression
route.

I found the purple Town Co. Safe much earlier, but interacting with it only
produced this message:

**“The Safe appears to be locked.”**

That is a normal progression lock. I do not get an early keypad puzzle, and I
do not need to search nearby rocks, trees, Phone apps, or TownCo. messages for a
hidden number.

The actual route begins after I finish the final Top Town Tournament challenge,
reach number one in Town Rankings, and speak to Agent Peanut at the Train
Station.

  Why the Town Co. Safe Is Locked

I reached the Town Co. Safe several Town Ranks before I could open it.

The entrance loaded normally, the interaction prompt worked, and the game
identified the location as the **Town Co. Safe**. It simply told me that the
Safe was locked.

<h2 id="safe-location">
  Where I Find the Town Co. Safe
</h2>

The Safe is in the **northwest or upper-left part of the Town Centre**.

In my route, I approached it from the buildable plot beside the pink forest
area. A shallow-water gap separated the normal walking area from the small Safe
clearing.

My route is:

1. travel toward the northwest edge of the Town Centre;
2. enter the unlocked plot beside the pink forest;
3. look for the isolated purple TownCo. entrance;
4. extend a narrow strip of grass across the shallow water;
5. clear a blocking rock with the Drill if necessary;
6. walk into the clearing and interact with the Safe.

I only create enough ground to cross the gap.

Covering the entire water area does not help the objective and removes terrain I
may want to use later.

After confirming the locked message, I place a recognizable path, lamp, or
decoration near the entrance. By the time I finish Challenge 10, the northwest
district may contain enough shops, housing, Storage, and tourist objects to hide
the original route.

<h3 id="when-to-open-safe-plot">
  When I Open the Safe-Side Plot
</h3>

I open the surrounding land when:

- my active Town Goals are already funded;
- I need more practical building space;
- I am already exploring the northwest side;
- or the purchase no longer delays an important EGO Tech unlock.

I wait when:

- a required challenge building still needs Coins;
- my existing shops are unstaffed or empty;
- I am short of EGO for an active objective;
- or my only reason is the expectation that the Safe will open immediately.

The land is useful before the ending. The Safe itself is not.

2. Every final Town Goal is complete.
3. I have claimed the completed bracket reward.

I do not rely only on the normal Town Rank number.

A full-looking progress bar can still leave Agent Peanut unavailable when the
reward is unclaimed or the final dialogue is still waiting to finish.

My final ranking check is:

1. open Town Rankings;
2. inspect every remaining goal;
3. claim the final reward;
4. let the complete conversation finish;

<h2 id="agent-toffee">
  Agent Toffee and Agent Peanut Are Separate
</h2>

Agent Toffee and Agent Peanut appear during the same final progression window,
but they do not share one objective.

Agent Toffee asks for **30,000 Coins**.

Agent Peanut sends me to the Town Co. Safe.

Paying Toffee does not provide the Safe code, and finding Peanut's bunker key
does not complete Toffee's invitation.

I still speak to both agents while I am at the Train Station so one unfinished
conversation does not leave the platform sequence waiting.

During Challenge 10, I protect the 30,000-Coin balance by delaying:

- decorative packs;
- duplicate shops;
- extra vehicles;
- optional terrain work;
- and another service building when the existing one has spare capacity.

I still spend Coins on anything required to complete the final Town Goals.

My decision is simple:

- **The final challenge requires it:** buy it.
- **It removes a real production bottleneck:** buy it.
- **It is optional or duplicated:** wait until the Train Station sequence is
  complete.

<h2 id="agent-peanut-not-appearing">
  Why Agent Peanut Is Not Appearing
</h2>

If Agent Peanut is missing from the Train Station, I check progression before
searching the rest of the map.

My order is:

1. confirm Challenge 10 is complete;
2. claim the final reward;
3. confirm the number-one leaderboard position;
4. finish any active TownCo. conversation;
5. finish any agent dialogue already running at the station;
6. leave the platform;
7. allow the next arrival sequence to complete;
8. return to the Train Station.

I do not search the Bunker Network for Agent Peanut. His invitation route begins
at the Train Station.

I also avoid repeated save reloads while a ranking or arrival conversation is
still pending. I first leave the station, finish another short task, and return
after the previous sequence has had time to clear.

<h2 id="get-safe-code">
  How I Get the Safe Code From Agent Peanut
</h2>

I speak to Agent Peanut and continue through his complete dialogue.

He gives me a numeric code connected to the Town Co. Safe.

I do not walk away as soon as the number appears. I let the complete
conversation finish so the quest state has time to update.

Afterward, I return to the physical Safe in the northwest Town Centre.

I do not enter the code through:

- the Phone;
- the Jailbreak App;
- the EGO Tech App;
- a bunker console;
- or another TownCo. keypad.

The code belongs to the Safe quest interaction.

If I forget the displayed number, I still return to the Safe first. The quest
state may be enough for the next interaction. If it remains locked, I go back to
the Train Station and confirm that every line of Peanut's dialogue was
completed.

<h2 id="open-safe">
  Open the Safe and Collect the TownCo. Bunker Door Key
</h2>

After speaking to Agent Peanut, I retrace my marked northwest route and interact
with the Safe again.

I collect the **TownCo. Bunker Door Key** inside.

Picking up the key and completing Agent Peanut's invitation are separate steps.

If his objective remains active, I return to the Train Station and report the
discovery before continuing deeper into the bunker route.

I separate the next actions like this:

- return to Peanut to finish his invitation;
- check the newly available terrain controls before redesigning the town;
- enter the Bunker Network to continue the Town Deed route.

<h2 id="terrain-controls">
  What I Check After Getting the Bunker Door Key
</h2>

After completing the Safe route, I reopen the Terraforming menu and check the
additional terrain controls available to my town.

These tools are most useful when I need to:

- shorten a Courier route;
- remove an awkward elevation break;
- reshape water around a district;
- create a cleaner road connection;
- or recover practical building space.

I make a manual save before changing a large part of the map.

I do not immediately flatten every hill or fill every section of water. Terrain
helps separate districts and makes routes easier to recognize. A completely
flat town can create more space while making navigation and visual organization
worse.

I change one small area first, then watch:

- Courier travel time;
- Maintenance walking distance;
- tourist access;
- building entrances;
- and road continuity.

If the terrain controls do not update immediately, I finish Agent Peanut's
return dialogue and clear any pending final TownCo. conversation before checking
the menu again.

<h2 id="bunker-network">
  How the TownCo. Bunker Network Unlocks
</h2>

The Bunker Network connects several surface regions, but discovering one
entrance does not open every underground door.

Brundle explains that the underground doors connect to different destinations.
I need to discover their matching **surface bunker entrances**.

When I discover a surface entrance, I do not merely mark it on the map.

I enter the underground wing and look for the nearby wall switch or activation
point that connects it to the main hallway.

My entrance routine is:

1. discover the surface entrance;
2. interact with it;
3. enter the local bunker wing;
4. locate the nearby switch;
5. activate the connected door;
6. confirm that I can reach the shared hallway.

Finding an entrance visually is not always enough. I enter and activate it
before returning to town.

<h3 id="own-bunker-entrance">
  When I Build My Own TownCo. Bunker Entrance
</h3>

Brundle gives me access to a player-built TownCo. Bunker entrance.

I use it as a shortcut, not as a replacement for surface exploration.

I build one when:

- my existing entrance is far from the Town Centre;
- I expect several return trips;
- the Agent Peanut route has started;
- and I have a practical location beside a road.

I wait when:

- I have discovered only one network entrance;
- the current Town Goals need the same Coins or materials;
- or I expect the constructed entrance to unlock destinations I have never
  visited.

The player-built entrance gets me into the network faster. It does not activate
every connected destination.

- and which corridor returns to the entrance I used.

1. enter through one known surface entrance;
2. activate its switch;

4. test the newly connected door;
5. return through the same route;
6. repeat after discovering another entrance.

This takes longer than running through every doorway once, but it gives me a
reliable mental map when the Bunker Door Key sends me back underground.

<h2 id="navigation-error">
  Fix “Unable to Pass to Destination”
</h2>

A bunker route can display:

**Unable to pass to destination**

The message may also refer to a missing **Townie Navigation Module**.

I do not treat this as a Courier, worker, or general automation error.

It means the connected bunker destination has not been fully activated.

I check:

1. whether I found the matching surface entrance;
2. whether I actually entered it;
3. whether I used its switch or navigation point;
4. whether the destination-side objective is complete;
5. whether I returned to the correct underground door.

Walking into the blocked doorway repeatedly does not activate the missing
connection.

I return to the relevant surface region or bunker wing and search for the
missing activation step.

<h2 id="red-bunker-door">
  Use the Key on the Red Bunker Door
</h2>

Once I have the TownCo. Bunker Door Key, I enter through the shortest reliable
Bunker Network entrance.

I prefer:

- my constructed TownCo. Bunker entrance;
- or the discovered surface entrance closest to the Town Centre.

From the shared hallway, I continue toward the deeper story corridor.

The target is visually different from the ordinary network doors. It belongs to
the red-lit bunker route rather than one of the normal transport connections.

I do not test the key on every locked door.

Ordinary bunker doors are opened through surface entrances, switches, and
destination activation. The Safe reward is for the later story lock.

When the key interaction does not appear, I check:

- the Safe was opened after Peanut's dialogue;
- the key was collected;
- Peanut's discovery step was registered;
- I am at the deeper red story door;
- and I am interacting with the correct side of the doorway.

<h2 id="town-deed">
  How I Find the Town Deed
</h2>

Opening the red door is not the final interaction.

After entering the deeper room, I continue all the way to the back and inspect
the desks.

The Town Deed objective registers there.

The final two steps are:

1. open the red story door with the Bunker Door Key;
2. interact with the desks at the back of the room.

I do not turn around as soon as the door opens.

Crossing into the room proves that the key worked, but it does not prove that I
found the Town Deed.

When the objective remains incomplete, I walk around the rear desks slowly and
check each available interaction prompt before leaving the network.

<h2 id="when-to-return">
  When I Continue and When I Return
</h2>

I continue the Agent Peanut route when:

- Challenge 10 is complete;
- the final reward has been claimed;
- the town is number one;
- Peanut is present at the Train Station;
- and I already know how to return to the Safe.

I return to Town Rankings when:

- Peanut does not appear;
- the Train Station does not update;
- or the number-one position is missing.

I return to Agent Peanut when:

- I collected the key;
- but his invitation objective remains active.

I return to surface exploration when:

- an ordinary bunker door remains locked;
- its connected entrance has not been found;
- or its switch has not been activated.

I return to the deeper bunker room when:

- the red door has opened;
- but the Town Deed objective is still incomplete.

Keeping those states separate prevents me from searching the wrong system for
the next trigger.

<h2 id="troubleshooting-checklist">

I verify Challenge 10, Claim Rewards, the number-one ranking, and any unfinished

### The Safe still shows the early locked message

I return to Agent Peanut and complete every line of his conversation before

### I collected the key but Peanut has not joined

I return to the Train Station and report the discovery. Finding the item and
finishing the invitation are separate steps.

### The new terrain controls are missing

I finish Peanut's return dialogue, clear pending final conversations, and reopen
Terraforming.

### A normal bunker door remains locked

I find its matching surface entrance, enter the local bunker wing, and activate
the nearby switch.

### The bunker reports a missing navigation module

I search the connected destination route instead of rebuilding Couriers,
Storage, or Industry Zones.

### The red door has no key interaction

I confirm that the Safe reward was collected and that I am standing at the
deeper story door rather than a normal transport door.

### The Town Deed objective does not complete

I walk to the rear desks and inspect the interaction prompts. Entering the room
alone is not enough.

<h2 id="faq">
  Agent Peanut, Safe Code, and Town Deed FAQ
</h2>

### Can I open the Town Co. Safe when I first find it?

No. The early interaction only displays the locked message. I return after
finishing Challenge 10, reaching number one, and speaking to Agent Peanut.

### Is the Safe code entered through the Phone?

No. I finish Peanut's dialogue, travel back to the physical Safe, and interact
with it there. The Phone and Jailbreak App are unrelated to this objective.

### Do I need to pay Agent Toffee before opening the Safe?

Toffee and Peanut have separate objectives. Toffee asks for 30,000 Coins, while
Peanut provides the Safe route. I finish both station conversations, but
Toffee's payment is not the Safe code.

### Why is Agent Peanut not appearing?

I confirm Challenge 10, claim the final reward, verify the number-one ranking,
finish pending dialogue, leave the platform, and return after the arrival
sequence updates.

### Do I need to return to Agent Peanut after opening the Safe?

Yes when his invitation objective is still active. Collecting the Bunker Door
Key and reporting the discovery are separate quest steps.

### Does one surface bunker entrance unlock every underground door?

No. Each surface entrance connects to a specific bunker route. I enter it,
activate its switch, and then return to the central network.

### Does my constructed bunker entrance unlock missing destinations?

No. It provides a convenient route into the shared network. I still need to
discover and activate the other surface entrances.

### Why does the bunker say it cannot pass to the destination?

The connected route is missing an entrance, switch, or navigation activation.
I return to the corresponding surface region or local bunker wing instead of
repeatedly testing the blocked door.

### Where is the Town Deed?

It is found at the desks in the back of the deeper bunker room. Opening the red
door or entering the room does not complete the objective by itself.
