progression route.

I found the purple Town Co. Safe much earlier, but interacting with it only
produced this message:

**“The Safe appears to be locked.”**

do not need to search nearby rocks, trees, Phone apps, bunker consoles, or
TownCo. messages for a hidden number.

The actual route begins after I finish Top Town Tournament Challenge 10, claim
the final reward, receive the Boss's follow-up call about the Town Deed, and
speak to Agent Peanut at the Train Station.

  Why the Town Co. Safe Is Locked
</h2>

I reached the Town Co. Safe several Town Ranks before I could open it.

The entrance loaded normally, the interaction prompt worked, and the game
identified the location as the **Town Co. Safe**. It simply told me that the
Safe was locked.

There is no separate keypad menu during this early visit.

I do not guess Town Rank numbers, reuse a code from another save, or enter
anything through the Phone. I mark the route and return to the current Town

<h2 id="safe-location">
  Where I Find the Town Co. Safe
</h2>

The Safe is in the **northwest or upper-left part of the Town Centre**.

In my route, I approached it from the buildable plot beside the pink forest
area. A shallow-water gap separated the normal walking area from the small Safe
clearing.

My route is:

1. travel toward the northwest edge of the Town Centre;
2. enter the unlocked plot beside the pink forest;
3. look for the isolated purple TownCo. entrance;
4. extend a narrow strip of grass across the shallow water;
5. clear a blocking rock with the Drill if necessary;
6. walk into the clearing and interact with the Safe.

I only create enough ground to cross the gap.

Covering the entire water area does not help the objective and removes terrain I
may want to use later.

After confirming the locked message, I place a recognizable path, lamp, or
decoration near the entrance. By the time I finish Challenge 10, the northwest
district may contain enough shops, housing, Storage, and tourist objects to hide
the original route.

<h3 id="when-to-open-safe-plot">
  When I Open the Safe-Side Plot
</h3>

The surrounding land can be useful long before the Safe itself opens.

I purchase or develop it when:

- my active Town Goals are already funded;
- I need practical building space;
- I am already expanding through the northwest side;
- the plot improves an existing road or tourist route;
- or the purchase no longer delays an important EGO Tech unlock.

I wait when:

- a required Challenge 10 building still needs Coins;
- my existing shops are unstaffed or empty;
- I am short of EGO for an active goal;
- my Courier and Maintenance routes are already too long;
- or my only reason is the expectation that buying the land will open the Safe.

The land is useful before the ending. The Safe itself is not.

Finding the Safe early gives me a route marker, not an immediate objective.

<h2 id="agent-peanut-not-appearing">
  Why Agent Peanut Is Not Appearing
</h2>

If Agent Peanut is missing from the Train Station, I check progression before
searching the rest of the map.

My order is:

1. confirm Challenge 10 is complete;
2. claim the final reward;
3. let the reward conversation finish;
4. wait for the Boss's Town Deed call;
5. confirm the number-one leaderboard position;
6. finish any active TownCo. conversation;
7. finish any other agent dialogue already running at the station;
8. leave the platform;
9. allow the next train or arrival sequence to complete;
10. return to the Train Station.

I do not search the Bunker Network for Agent Peanut. His invitation route begins
at the Train Station.

I also avoid repeated save reloads while a ranking, phone call, or arrival
conversation is still pending.

I first leave the station, finish another short task, and return after the
previous sequence has had time to clear.

If another final agent appears instead of Peanut, I speak to that character and
finish the visible dialogue. I do not assume that completing the other agent's
full objective is what supplies the Safe code.

<h2 id="get-safe-code">
  How I Get the Safe Code From Agent Peanut
</h2>

I speak to Agent Peanut at the Train Station and continue through his complete
dialogue.

He gives me a numeric code connected to the Town Co. Safe.

I record the number when it appears.

I do not walk away as soon as the code is displayed. I let the complete
conversation finish so the quest state has time to update.

Afterward, I return to the physical Safe in the northwest Town Centre.

I do not enter the code through:

- the Phone;
- the Jailbreak App;
- the EGO Tech App;
- a bunker console;
- or another TownCo. keypad.

The code belongs to the Safe quest interaction.

If I forget the displayed number, I still return to the Safe first and check
the updated interaction.

If it remains locked or asks for information I no longer have, I return to the
Train Station and confirm that every line of Peanut's dialogue was completed. I
do not rely on a number copied from another save.

<h2 id="open-safe">
  Open the Safe and Collect the TownCo. Bunker Door Key
</h2>

After speaking to Agent Peanut, I retrace my marked northwest route and interact
with the Safe again.

I collect the **TownCo. Bunker Door Key** inside.

Picking up the key and completing Agent Peanut's invitation can be separate
steps.

If his objective remains active, I return to the Train Station and report the
discovery before continuing deeper into the bunker route.

I separate the next actions like this:

- return to Peanut when his invitation marker remains active;
- enter the Bunker Network when Peanut's discovery step is complete;
- leave optional town redesign until the Town Deed route is finished.

If the Safe still displays the original locked message, I do not search the
bunker for another key.

I return to the Train Station and recheck Peanut's dialogue, the Boss's call,
the number-one ranking, and the final reward state.

<h2 id="bunker-network">
  How the TownCo. Bunker Network Unlocks
</h2>

The Bunker Network connects several surface regions, but discovering one
entrance does not open every underground door.

Brundle explains that the underground doors connect to different destinations.
I need to discover their matching **surface bunker entrances**.

    single entrance does not activate the entire underground network.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

When I discover a surface entrance, I do not merely mark it on the map.

I enter the underground wing and look for the nearby wall switch, navigation
point, or activation interaction that connects it to the shared hallway.

My entrance routine is:

1. discover the surface entrance;
2. interact with it;
3. enter the local bunker wing;
4. locate the nearby activation point;
5. activate the connected door;
6. reach the shared hallway;
7. return through the same route to confirm it works.

Finding an entrance visually is not always enough. I enter and activate it
before returning to town.

<h3 id="own-bunker-entrance">
  When I Build My Own TownCo. Bunker Entrance
</h3>

Brundle gives me access to a player-built TownCo. Bunker entrance.

I use it as a shortcut, not as a replacement for surface exploration.

I build one when:

- my existing entrance is far from the Town Centre;
- I expect several return trips;
- the Agent Peanut route has started;
- the shared network is already accessible;
- and I have a practical location beside a road.

I wait when:

- I have discovered only one network entrance;
- the current Town Goals still need the same Coins or materials;
- I do not yet understand which bunker routes are active;
- or I expect the constructed entrance to unlock destinations I have never
  visited.

The player-built entrance gets me into the network faster.

It does not automatically discover surface entrances, activate destination
switches, or open every connected door.

5. test the newly connected corridor;
6. return through the same route;
7. repeat after discovering another entrance.

This takes longer than running through every doorway once, but it gives me a
reliable mental map when the Bunker Door Key sends me back underground.

It also stops me from confusing an ordinary locked transport door with the
later red story door.

<h2 id="navigation-error">
  Fix “Unable to Pass to Destination”
</h2>

A bunker route can display:

**Unable to pass to destination**

The message may also refer to a missing **Townie Navigation Module**.

I do not treat this as a Courier, worker, or general automation error.

It means the connected bunker destination has not been fully activated.

I check:

1. whether I found the matching surface entrance;
2. whether I actually entered it;
3. whether I used its switch or navigation point;
4. whether the destination-side route is complete;
5. whether I returned to the correct underground door;
6. whether I confused the route with another bunker connection.

Walking into the blocked doorway repeatedly does not activate the missing
connection.

Building another TownCo. Bunker entrance does not activate it either.

I return to the relevant surface region or local bunker wing and search for the
missing interaction.

<h2 id="red-bunker-door">
  Use the Key on the Red Bunker Door
</h2>

Once I have the TownCo. Bunker Door Key, I enter through the shortest reliable
Bunker Network entrance.

I prefer:

- my constructed TownCo. Bunker entrance when it already reaches the shared
  network;
- or the discovered surface entrance closest to the Town Centre.

From the shared hallway, I continue toward the deeper story corridor.

The target is visually different from the ordinary network doors. It belongs to
the red-lit bunker route rather than one of the normal transport connections.

I do not test the key on every locked door.

Ordinary bunker doors are opened through surface entrances, switches, and
destination activation. The Safe reward is for the later story lock.

When the key interaction does not appear, I check:

- the Safe was opened after Peanut's dialogue;
- the Bunker Door Key was collected;
- Peanut's discovery step was registered;
- I am at the deeper red story door;
- I am interacting with the correct side of the doorway;
- and no active Peanut dialogue is still waiting at the Train Station.

If an ordinary gray or transport door remains locked, I return to the surface
entrance checks instead of blaming the Bunker Door Key.

<h2 id="town-deed">
  How I Find the Town Deed
</h2>

Opening the red door is not the final interaction.

After entering the deeper room, I continue all the way to the back and inspect
the desks.

The Town Deed objective registers there.

The final sequence is:

1. open the red story door with the Bunker Door Key;
2. enter the deeper room;
3. walk past the entrance area;
4. continue to the desks at the back;
5. inspect the available interaction prompts;
6. confirm that the Town Deed objective updates.

I do not turn around as soon as the door opens.

Crossing into the room proves that the key worked, but it does not prove that I
found the Town Deed.

When the objective remains incomplete, I walk around the rear desks slowly and
check each edge of the furniture for an interaction prompt before leaving the
network.

I also verify that I did not enter an ordinary destination room that merely
resembles the deeper TownCo. area.

I continue to the Train Station when:

- Challenge 10 is complete;
- the final reward was claimed;

- the number-one position is not displayed;

I return to Agent Peanut when:

- I collected the Bunker Door Key;
- but his invitation objective remains active.

I return to surface exploration when:

- an ordinary bunker door remains locked;
- its connected entrance has not been found;
- or its switch has not been activated.

I continue deeper into the bunker when:

- the Safe reward was collected;
- Peanut's discovery step is complete;
- and I have a working route into the shared network.

I return to the Town Deed room when:

- the red door has opened;
- but the objective still says the deed has not been found.

Keeping those states separate prevents me from searching the wrong system for
the next trigger.

<h2 id="troubleshooting-checklist">
  Agent Peanut and Safe Troubleshooting Checklist
</h2>

### I claimed the final reward but the story stopped

I let the complete reward conversation finish and wait for the Boss's follow-up
Town Deed call.

I also clear any active Phone or Train Station dialogue before reloading.

### Agent Peanut is missing

I verify Challenge 10, Claim Rewards, the number-one ranking, the Boss's Town
Deed call, and any unfinished TownCo. or Train Station dialogue.

I then leave the platform and return after another arrival sequence.

### The Safe still shows the early locked message

I return to Agent Peanut and complete every line of his conversation before
trying the Safe again.

I also confirm that the tournament reward and Boss call both registered.

### I forgot the Safe code

I return to the Safe and test the updated quest interaction first.

If it still needs the number, I return to Peanut instead of using a code copied
from another save.

### I collected the key but Peanut has not joined

I return to the Train Station and report the discovery.

Finding the item and finishing the invitation can be separate quest steps.

### A normal bunker door remains locked

I find its matching surface entrance, enter the local bunker wing, and activate
the nearby switch or navigation point.

### My constructed bunker entrance does not open a destination

The constructed entrance is only a shortcut into the shared network.

It does not replace discovering and activating the corresponding surface route.

### The bunker reports a missing navigation module

I search the connected destination route instead of rebuilding Couriers,
Storage, or Industry Zones.

### The red door has no key interaction

I confirm that the Safe reward was collected, Peanut's objective registered,
and I am standing at the deeper red story door rather than a normal transport
door.

### The Town Deed objective does not complete

I walk to the rear desks and inspect the interaction prompts around their edges.

Entering the room alone is not enough.

<h2 id="faq">
  Agent Peanut, Safe Code, and Town Deed FAQ
</h2>

### Does the Boss's Town Deed call happen before or after the final reward?

It happens after I claim the final Top Town Tournament reward and let the
tournament conversation finish. I wait through the apparent ending instead of
leaving immediately.

### Can I use a Safe code from another save?

I do not rely on another save's number. The Safe is controlled by the current
quest state, so I complete Peanut's dialogue and use the code connected to the
current town.

### Do Agent Toffee or the other final agents unlock Peanut's Safe?

No. Final agents can appear during the same Train Station window, but their
objectives are separate. I may need to clear their dialogue, but their rewards
do not provide Peanut's code.

### Do I need to unlock every Bunker Network entrance before finding the Town Deed?

I need a working route into the shared network and access to the deeper story
corridor. Unrelated surface destinations can remain locked, although activating
more entrances makes navigation easier.

### Can my constructed TownCo. Bunker entrance replace the surface entrances?

No. It can shorten repeated trips into an already accessible network, but each
destination still requires its own surface entrance and activation.

### Can I open the Town Co. Safe as soon as I find the northwest plot?

No. Buying the land and reaching the entrance only lets me confirm the early
locked state. The Safe remains progression-locked until the final tournament
sequence and Agent Peanut dialogue are complete.
