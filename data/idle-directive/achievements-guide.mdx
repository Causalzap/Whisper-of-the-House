I do not chase all 43 IDLE_DIRECTIVE achievements in one run.

The early achievements reward things I already need: stable Energy, more
capacity, the first item upgrades, Trader contact and a few repaired POIs. The
late achievements ask for a different machine entirely—50 million installed
Energy capacity, 300% Energy Efficiency, a level-250 item, maximum Reputation,
150 repaired POIs and every Augment unlocked.

Trying to build for all of them at once slows down both the current run and the
next Prestige.

I split the list into four stages:

> take cheap milestones during normal progression → automate cumulative goals →
> build temporary layouts for expensive stat checks → finish the multi-Prestige
> endgame achievements last.

This guide covers all 43 requirements and the decisions that determine when I
attempt them. It does not repeat the complete Auto Explore configuration,
Prestige point routes or current Terminal resource costs; those belong to the
guides dedicated to those systems.

I have not completed the hidden Ship launch in a current save, so I do not invent
a final recipe, location or prerequisite chain for it.

<h2 id="achievement-route">Plan the Achievement Route in Four Stages</h2>

I choose achievement targets based on what the current machine can finish
without abandoning its main purpose.

### Stage 1: opening and first Prestige

I take the milestones that naturally appear while restoring the machine:

* the first Energy Efficiency levels;
* 50,000 and 200,000 Energy Capacity;
* item levels 50 and 100;
* contact with any revealed Trader;
* early travel, exploration and repair progress.

I do not remain in the opening run to force a million-capacity build or a
level-200 item.

### Stage 2: exploration and automation

After unlocking Signal Decryption, Tasks and Auto Explore, I work on:

* contact with all four Traders;
* 30–90% world exploration;
* cumulative travel;
* the first maximum-Reputation target;
* the 10, 25 and 50 POI repair milestones.

This is where several achievement chains begin progressing together.

### Stage 3: dedicated stat builds

Once Energy production, Body Space and storage are much stronger, I make
temporary builds for:

* 10 million and 50 million Energy Capacity;
* 250% and 300% Energy Efficiency;
* item level 200 and 250;
* maximum Mobility.

These layouts exist to trigger a requirement. I dismantle them afterward if they
make the normal machine worse.

### Stage 4: multi-Prestige endgame

I leave these until the permanent progression supports them:

* 100 and 150 repaired POIs;
* maximum Reputation with all four Traders;
* all Terminal repairs;
* Omega;
* Launch.

<h2 id="early-achievements">Take Cheap Achievements Before Prestige</h2>

I check the achievement list before System Reset, but I do not let it become a
reason to delay forever.

Good pre-Prestige targets are requirements I can finish with something already
installed or visible on the map:

* one additional Battery crosses the next capacity threshold;
* a cheap Energy Cell reaches the next item level;
* a nearby Spire completes a repair milestone;
* a short route completes the next exploration percentage;
* an Overcharger immediately crosses an efficiency threshold;
* one revealed Trader is close enough to contact.

In the early progression, each completed achievement contributes a base
percentage bonus to the System Reset calculation. Later Augments can improve
that contribution.

That makes a nearby milestone valuable, but it does not make every milestone
worth chasing.

### My one-nearby-achievement rule

Before resetting, I ask:

1. Can I finish the requirement with an item already in Inventory?
2. Is the required POI already visible?
3. Can I finish it without rebuilding the main production chain?
4. Will it take minutes rather than a new production tier?
5. Does it still leave enough Energy and resources to confirm the reset safely?

I complete it when most answers are yes.

I reset when the next achievement requires:

* millions more Energy;
* a much larger Body Grid;
* a new Trader Reputation tier;
* Auto Explore or Auto Repair;
* several distant POIs;
* a machine I cannot yet craft or buy.

<h2 id="energy-capacity">Reach 50 Million Energy Capacity Without Charging It</h2>

Energy Capacity achievements check the amount of capacity currently installed.

The Batteries do **not** need to be fully charged.

That is the most important part of the route. I do not wait until the machine
contains 50 million stored Energy. I need the Body Space and Battery capacity to
reach 50 million at the same time.

My capacity route is:

1. keep large Batteries instead of filling the grid with weak 1×1 and 1×2
   versions;
2. replace several small Batteries when one larger model provides more capacity
   per occupied tile;
3. increase Body Space during a later run;
4. obtain stronger Battery models through POIs, Traders or crafting;
5. clear a temporary section of the Body Grid;
6. install the saved Batteries until the achievement triggers;
7. restore the normal production layout afterward.

A tested 3×2 Battery had **10,000 base capacity**.

A 4×2 Battery had **50,000 base capacity**, five times as much before further
upgrades. The current Databank lists the 4×2 model as:

* found in Crater B;
* found in a Lab;
* purchasable at Reservoir;
* craftable after the relevant Blueprint is available.

I attempt the thresholds differently.

### 50,000 and 200,000

I normally complete these while building the first functional machine.

I do not need a special layout unless the opening drops are unusually poor.

### 1,000,000

I complete this after Body Space and upgraded Batteries become affordable.

It can still fit into a normal progression run.

### 10,000,000 and 50,000,000

These are dedicated late-game builds.

I prepare by:

* saving large Batteries;
* improving their levels;
* expanding Body Space;
* moving temporary processors and storage out of the capacity section;
* making sure the achievement triggers before the next System Reset.

I do not delay an early Prestige simply because the next visible capacity
achievement is 10 million. The jump from one million to ten million is not one
more Battery—it is a different build.

<h2 id="energy-efficiency">Build Toward 300% Energy Efficiency in Stages</h2>

The Energy Efficiency achievements measure the efficiency stat, not the size of
the Energy generation number.

A Biogenerator producing thousands of Energy per second does not automatically
satisfy the 250% or 300% requirements.

I combine two types of progress:

* permanent base Energy Efficiency from Augments;
* temporary efficiency boosts such as Overchargers.

My route is:

1. reach 100% during the opening;
2. use a temporary boost when it immediately crosses 150% or 200%;
3. improve permanent efficiency between Prestiges;
4. save compatible boosts for the 250% attempt;
5. attempt 300% only after the permanent value is already close.

I use Overcharger I, II and III together only when their combined value reaches a
specific threshold.

For example:

* at 235%, a suitable boost may finish the 250% achievement;
* at 170%, consuming several rare boosts is unlikely to solve 300%.

I save the items in the second situation.

I also check whether the boost is applied to Energy Efficiency rather than
generation, capacity or manual collection. Similar Energy-related item names do
not always affect the achievement stat I need.

<h2 id="item-level-250">Upgrade One Cheap Item to Level 250</h2>

I use one relatively inexpensive item for the Item Upgrade chain.

I do not spread Energy across every machine and hope one eventually reaches
level 250.

Energy Cells are practical candidates because their upgrade path is often
cheaper than complex Mark II processors, amplifiers or large utility machines.

My route is:

1. choose one item that can remain installed;
2. reach level 50 during normal progression;
3. continue to 100 or 150 while the cost remains comfortable;
4. stop if the upgrades consume the Energy needed for Navigation, Mobility or
   repairs;
5. build a much larger Energy reserve before attempting 200;
6. reserve millions of Energy before attempting 250;
7. use an Upgrader when I want the same selected item to progress without
   repeated manual clicks.

In one earlier attempt, spending the available Energy could only raise the
selected item to around **level 177**.

A later level-250 attempt still required millions of Energy.

<h2 id="exploration-travel">Complete Exploration and Travel Together</h2>

I do not grind Exploration and Travel as separate projects.

Auto Explore advances both while the machine searches for resources, Traders,
Spires and repair targets.

The Travel chain is cumulative in the tested progression. I do not need to cover
5,000 kilometers during one uninterrupted run.

My achievement route is:

1. unlock Auto Explore;
2. build enough Mobility and positive Energy to sustain movement;
3. send the machine through unexplored regions;
4. collect useful resources instead of traveling through empty POIs without a
   purpose;
5. change direction when one section of the map remains dark;
6. finish the last gaps manually when the automatic pattern stops helping.

For 30%, 45% and 60%, normal progression usually supplies enough exploration.

The 75% and 90% milestones need more deliberate routing. A west-east sweep can
leave an unexplored strip above or below the current path. I switch patterns or
manually route through the remaining dark sections rather than waiting for the
same loop to discover them.

I use a fast “leave immediately” route only when:

* Travel distance is the sole remaining target;
* storage and production no longer matter during that run;
* the map has already been explored enough;
* the machine can afford continuous Mobility.

When resources, repairs or Trader Tasks still matter, I let the route stop long
enough to complete those jobs.

<h2 id="repair-achievements">Use Spires for the Repair Count Before Reactors</h2>

The Repair chain counts repaired POIs, not the amount of resources spent.

That makes Spires the practical early target.

I complete the first 10 manually while:

* revealing the world;
* decrypting Trader signals;
* building the first Augment Point total;
* searching for useful resource POIs.

For 25, 50, 100 and 150 repairs, I add Auto Repair after the supporting resource
chains are stable.

My order is:

1. unlock Auto Explore;
2. unlock Auto Repair on a later Prestige;
3. begin with Spires;
4. watch whether Metal and Energy remain positive;
5. improve automated production when repairs repeatedly fail to start;
6. add Reactors after Rare Metal and Biomass are no longer bottlenecks.

I do not use expensive Reactors merely to increase the raw achievement count.

Reactors can become excellent sources of Augment Points after repair-reward
upgrades, but that is a Prestige decision. For the achievement counter itself, a
faster and cheaper Spire still adds one repaired POI.

I return to the machine when:

* Auto Repair reaches a location without enough resources;
* the Metal buffer is falling;
* Mobility is consuming the Energy safety margin;
* the route repeatedly reaches already repaired locations;
* a Terminal has become the real progression target.

<h2 id="trader-achievements">Reach and Max All Four Traders</h2>

There are two Trader achievement groups.

The contact achievements are:

* Reach Habitat;
* Reach Reservoir;
* Reach Outpost;
* Reach Enclave.

The Reputation achievements are:

* Reputable with Habitat;
* Reputable with Reservoir;
* Reputable with Outpost;
* Reputable with Enclave.

### Contact all four Traders

I repair a Spire, decrypt its signals and travel to each revealed Trader.

Entering the Trader location is enough for the contact achievement. I do not
need maximum Reputation first.

### Unlock Tasks before grinding Reputation

Maximum Reputation requires the Tasks progression.

Buying an item does not replace completing Tasks. Selling Metal does not create
the same Reputation route either.

My order is:

1. unlock Signal Decryption;
2. unlock Tasks;
3. choose one Trader;
4. accept and complete its available Tasks;
5. use a Transponder only when a Task needs a marked location;
6. buy Reputation Gain after task speed becomes the bottleneck;
7. complete the maximum-Reputation achievement before resetting;
8. use Known Reputation later when preserving progress saves a meaningful amount
   of rebuilding.

I focus on one Trader at a time.

Trader Reputation normally resets during System Reset unless permanent Augments
preserve part of it. Splitting a short run across all four factions can leave
every bar unfinished.

<h2 id="maximum-movement">Reach Maximum Mobility During a Long Route</h2>

Maximum Movement requires the highest Mobility level.

The tested maximum is **level 30**.

I do not force level 30 during the first Prestige. Mobility upgrades compete
with Body Space, repairs and the production systems needed to support movement.

I finish it during a run already working on:

* 90% exploration;
* 5,000 kilometers of travel;
* 100 or 150 repaired POIs;
* all four Trader routes;
* later Terminal progression.

My upgrade rule is simple:

> buy Mobility when travel is the slowest part of the current route.

I continue upgrading when:

* the machine has positive Energy while moving;
* Metal and Rare Metal production can replace the cost;
* travel time is delaying repairs or Tasks;
* the run will continue long enough to benefit.

I pause when:

* the next level stops a required repair;
* Body Space is the current bottleneck;
* the machine already crosses the map faster than it can process resources;
* Prestige is about to erase the current Mobility level.

I make sure level 30 is reached and the achievement has triggered before
resetting.

<h2 id="omega-achievement">Unlock Every Augment for Omega</h2>

**Omega** requires every Augment to be unlocked.

It does **not** require every repeatable Augment to be fully maxed.

That distinction saves a large number of points.

When I am close to Omega, I stop buying another expensive level in my favorite
branch and check the entire tree for untouched nodes.

My route is:

1. unlock the cheap progression branches during normal Prestiges;
2. identify Augments still shown as locked;
3. buy their prerequisites;
4. purchase at least the first level of every missing Augment;
5. confirm Omega before returning to repeated repair, Replicator or Reputation
   levels.

<h2 id="when-to-stop">Know When an Achievement Belongs to the Next Run</h2>

The biggest time loss is not missing a hidden item. It is trying to complete a
late-game requirement with an early-game machine.

I continue the achievement attempt when:

* the next threshold is already close;
* the necessary item or POI is visible;
* the current production chain can pay the cost;
* the achievement improves the upcoming Prestige;
* finishing it does not require a total rebuild.

I stop and Prestige when:

* the requirement needs millions more Energy;
* a new permanent Augment would make it dramatically easier;
* the correct Trader stock is still locked;
* Auto Explore or Auto Repair is missing;
* the Body Grid cannot fit the required temporary layout;
* another hour would only improve equipment that System Reset will remove.

That keeps the 43-achievement route connected to the rest of the game:

> take the cheap milestones → use Prestige to remove the bottleneck → automate
> cumulative goals → build one temporary late-game layout → return to permanent
> progression before the current machine becomes the objective.
