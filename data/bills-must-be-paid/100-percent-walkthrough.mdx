A clean Bills Must Be Paid 100% route does not require me to buy every upgrade
or finish every collection in the first cycle.

My three-cycle route paid the main bill sequence in **Cycle 3**, used the
post-game to finish the Skill Tree, and entered **Cycle 4** only to buy the
remaining Rings and Bracelets.

That is a route target, not a reset requirement.

Another full completion took five cycles. A weak Piggy Shuffle result, an
unlucky bill, or a late Hammer purchase can add a cycle without damaging the
save.

The important part is assigning a clear purpose to each cycle:

- Cycle 1 establishes the first permanent upgrades;
- Cycle 2 handles most risky achievements and builds a stronger Prestige;
- Cycle 3 becomes the collection and main-ending cycle;
- post-game finishes the Skill Tree;
- the final bankruptcy buys the remaining jewelry.

<h2 id="route-overview">
  What Must Be Finished Before the Final Reset
</h2>

I separate 100% completion into current-cycle and permanent objectives.

Before the final bankruptcy, I complete:

- the full normal Skill Tree;
- the late King Piggy Skill Tree branches;
- every Shop purchase;
- every Piggy unlock;
- the Coin Collection;
- the main bill sequence;
- the King Piggy fight;
- the Taxes ending.

After that final bankruptcy, I finish:

- every remaining Ring;
- every remaining Bracelet;
- the Prestige Jewelry achievement.

This order matters because bankruptcy removes the current Hammer, cash,
gadgets, Skill Tree progress, and Piggy progression.

Rings and Coins survive.

I therefore do not reset immediately after the ending while `Maxed Out` is
still incomplete.

<h2 id="cycle-one">
  Cycle 1: Accuracy, Basic Bills, and the First Prestige
</h2>

Cycle 1 is not my winning cycle.

Its job is to unlock basic systems, pay several bills, and produce enough
Legacy Points for Cycle 2 to start stronger.

### Finish accuracy before increasing Hammer speed

I complete these achievements immediately:

- `Not a Single Miss`;
- `Twice as Precise`.

The starting Hammer is slow enough that I can place every swing deliberately.

I avoid Attack Speed until both achievements are complete.

When a Piggy reaches the edge of the desk, I wait for a safe target instead of
dragging the Hammer across empty space and recording a miss.

After both achievements unlock, I stop restarting profitable runs for imperfect
accuracy.

### Buy only enough combat power to reach the next bill

My early purchase order is:

1. Base Damage;
2. one Stamina upgrade;
3. Hit Radius;
4. Starting Piggies or Spawn when the desk becomes empty;
5. Loot after I can reliably destroy the available Piggies.

I do not chase expensive effects during this cycle.

The first cycle ends soon enough that several speculative purchases will
disappear before repaying their cost.

### Protect the bill reserve

With one day remaining, I stop treating the bill amount as spendable cash.

When I have $320 and tomorrow's bill costs $200, my real upgrade budget is
$120.

I only break that reserve for an upgrade that immediately changes the number
of hits required to destroy the current Piggy.

### Do not force Cycle 1 to survive

My first route ended after paying **five bills totaling $2,015**.

That produced **40 Legacy Points**, following the conversion of one Legacy
Point for every $50 paid in bills.

<h2 id="cycle-two">
  Cycle 2: Build Permanent Power and Clear Risky Achievements
</h2>

Cycle 2 starts with better opening Damage and more Piggies, so the first bills
rebuild much faster.

My goal is not necessarily to reach the ending.

I use this cycle to complete achievements that become awkward once the Hammer
is extremely fast.

### Complete Strong Start and Multismasher

For `Strong Start`, the first swing of the run must destroy two Piggies.

I wait until:

- at least two Piggies begin close together;
- both die to one normal hit;
- Hit Radius can cover the overlap.

For `Multismasher`, I use three weaker Piggies rather than three high-HP
targets.

If one survives, I stop repeating the same setup and return after another
Damage or Hammer breakpoint.

### Handle Piggy Shuffle with disposable cash

Piggy Shuffle has no reliable fixed ending position.

I track the selected Piggy during the slower part of the movement, but the
final acceleration can still become a guess.

I never wager the amount reserved for the next bill.

The objective is one successful attempt, not building the cycle around
repeated gambling.

### Complete both Big Toni achievements before the late-money grind

I take one small Big Toni loan during Cycle 2, use it only to close an
immediate bill gap, and repay it after the next profitable run.

This unlocks both loan-related achievements without carrying Toni's daily cut
into the much larger payouts of the winning cycle.

<h2 id="cycle-three">
  Cycle 3: Turn Permanent Power Into the Winning Build
</h2>

Cycle 3 is where the permanent upgrades begin compounding.

My opening setup now includes several of the following:

- flat Damage;
- percentage Damage;
- more Starting Piggies;
- permanent Loot;
- Crit Chance and Crit Damage;
- first-kill Stamina restoration;
- early-payment bonuses when bills become cheap.

I rebuild the inexpensive normal Skill Tree first.

I do not immediately save for the largest node while cheap Damage, Spawn,
Stamina, and Loot upgrades are still available.

### Rebuild the winning cycle around the current bottleneck

My Cycle 3 route is:

1. restore enough Damage to one-hit the early Piggies;
2. add targets only when the desk begins emptying;
3. increase Stamina when runs end with killable targets remaining;
4. buy Loot after the desk is clearing consistently;
5. replace the Hammer when a new Piggy tier creates an HP wall;
6. buy Super Jackpot only after its $2 million cost no longer threatens the
   next bill.

I do not follow one fixed Skill Tree order.

When a target survives for most of the run, I return to Damage. When the desk
empties, I return to Spawn. When Stamina ends before the available targets, I
return to Stamina.

### Pay early only after the cycle is ahead

I start using Early Speed Ring and Early Piggy Ring once one normal run can
cover the current bill.

The Early Speed effect grants 2% Attack Speed per early day, up to 25%.

The Early Piggy effect adds one desk Piggy for every four full days paid early.

I do not activate those effects while saving for an essential Hammer.

Skipping four upgrade days for one extra Piggy is poor value when the current
Piggies already survive the whole run.

<h2 id="finish-collections">
  Finish Piggies, Shop Items, and Coins Before the Ending
</h2>

Once Cycle 3 is clearly profitable, I stop treating collection purchases as
optional cleanup.

They are now part of the route.

### Unlock every Piggy Bank

Piggy progression is tied to both its progress bar and bill advancement.

A Piggy can appear ready but remain unavailable until I pay another required
bill.

I keep progressing the bills and checking the Piggies menu rather than farming
the same tier indefinitely.

`Piggy Bank Collector` unlocks after the final Piggy Bank becomes available
through continued bill progression.

<h2 id="king-piggy">
  Prepare for King Piggy Before Advancing the Final Bill
</h2>

King Piggy is the first point where a high-income build can still fail a direct
damage check.

Before advancing the final bill, I make sure:

- normal late-game Piggies die quickly enough not to occupy the desk;
- the Hammer has enough Damage to maintain progress against one large target;
- Stamina supports a long sequence of swings;
- Crit and Attack Speed are developed enough to maintain the combo;
- I have bought the large late Damage nodes rather than only Loot.

The final bill sequence reveals additional King Piggy upgrades, including
large Damage and Crit bonuses.

I buy the affordable nodes before assuming the visible pre-boss Skill Tree is
complete.

### The first attempt does not have to be the successful one

My first King Piggy attempt did not finish the fight.

I returned to the normal upgrade loop, bought more Damage, Crit, Attack Speed,
and Stamina, then entered the encounter again.

During the successful attempt, maintaining the combo made the swings faster
and increased my sustained damage.

Once I maintained that pressure long enough, King Piggy went down and the final
Taxes sequence began.

The Skill Tree can look complete before the final bill reveals all of its

<h2 id="faq">
  Bills Must Be Paid 100% Walkthrough FAQ
</h2>

### Do I have to finish the game in three cycles?

No.

Three cycles was the faster completed route, but another full run reached 100%
in five cycles. RNG, upgrade choices, and failed gambling attempts can add a
cycle without making any achievement missable.

### Do all Piggies need to reach maximum level?

No.

`Piggy Bank Collector` requires every Piggy Bank to be unlocked. A separate
complete run reached 100% while some Piggy levels were still unfinished.

### Can I continue after paying Taxes?

Yes.

Choose Keep Playing on the ending screen. This preserves the profitable
late-game cycle so I can finish the Skill Tree before declaring bankruptcy.

### Should I reset immediately after unlocking every Piggy?

Not when the current cycle is strong.

Piggy progress resets, while Shop and Skill Tree completion are easiest with
the same late-game income. I finish those objectives and the ending before the
final Prestige.

### What should I do when my cycle count is already higher than this route?

Continue from the equivalent stage rather than restarting the save.

A later cycle with stronger permanent jewelry is usually easier. The important
order is Skill Tree before final bankruptcy, followed by the remaining Rings
and Bracelets.
