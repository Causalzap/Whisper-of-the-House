**"A protester glued themselves to your business"** means that
the affected service has been blocked. The building cannot
continue serving customers until the protester is removed.

Arresting that protester may restore the immediate business,
but a larger outbreak can leave several services closed,
vehicles burning, and parking spaces occupied by wrecks.

The response depends on what is happening in the city.
An isolated protest requires a ready Riot Police unit.
Several simultaneous protests may require additional
personnel and patrol coverage. Damaged vehicles and
fires need separate recovery services after the
confrontations have ended.


<h2 id="arrest-protester" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Arrest a Protester Blocking Your Business
</h2>

The protest warning can appear directly in a business
or vehicle-service interface:

**"A protester glued themselves to your business.
Arrest the protester to continue service."**

This is an operational shutdown, not a shortage of
customers or an incorrectly configured selling price.

Open the **Riot Police and Emergency Services**
construction category and select the
**Riot Control Truck Garage**.

Its construction price is **$2,000** in the
October 2026 Early Access build.

Place the garage on owned land with access to the
city's road network.

The garage must have personnel available before
you can send anyone to confront a protester.

Follow these steps:

1. **Select the Riot Control Truck Garage.**
   Open its unit-management controls.
2. **Prepare Riot Police units.**
   Police officers and police dogs are available
   for the response.
3. **Right-click an active protester.**
   The Riot Police interface uses this command
   to start a fight.
4. **Allow the unit to reach the confrontation.**
   Wait for the protester to be dealt with before
   checking the affected business again.

A police vehicle arriving on the street does not
necessarily mean the protest has been stopped.
Return to the original business and check whether
the obstruction warning has disappeared.

For an isolated protest, a functioning garage
with enough ready personnel may resolve the
shutdown without requiring several additional
security buildings.


<h2 id="citywide-protests" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Stop Citywide Protests and Restore Riot Police Availability
</h2>

A citywide protest can interrupt multiple
businesses at once.

In Bald Town City, anti-car protesters can gather
around streets, parking areas, and other facilities.
During larger disturbances, vehicles may be
damaged or set on fire while the population's
Car Braininess and parking activity decline.

The Civil Unrest indicator helps identify how
severe the situation has become.

When unrest rises above **50%**, the game can
warn that smaller protest actions may occur.
At **100% Civil Unrest**, large protests
may occur.

Once several protests are active, prioritize
the ones directly preventing businesses
from operating or damaging parked vehicles.

Additional commands cannot produce an immediate
response when no units are available.

Prepare more personnel before sending the next
group into an active confrontation.

### Prioritize the Most Damaging Protests

Start where businesses are already closed.
A protester blocking a busy Car Shop Carousel,
Gas Station, or another essential service
may interfere with customers across the city.

Use direct Riot Police commands to address those
obstructions, then inspect neighboring streets
for additional protest groups.

A larger confrontation may need more personnel
than an isolated protest. Check the available
unit count before committing the remaining
officers and dogs to another fight.

Patrol routes can help cover areas where
protesters repeatedly gather. Concentrate
those patrols near operating businesses
rather than sending them through empty
parts of the map.

### Avoid Spending All Your Money on Security

Riot control has operating expenses.

When parking income falls during a large
disturbance, building additional garages and
maintaining more personnel can add substantial
daily costs.

Expand the response when the current units
cannot handle the number of active protests.
Once the immediate confrontations are over,
return available units to their garages
while keeping useful patrol coverage in
the affected districts.

Civil Unrest can also rise when the city
fails to satisfy newly introduced needs
or when occupied buildings are repeatedly
cleared.

Check the <Link href="/car-park-capital">Car Dependency and Car Braininess requirements</Link>
when unrest continues increasing after the
active protesters have been removed.

For relocation work, delay further unnecessary
evictions until the city's condition improves.


<h2 id="patrols-not-working" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Fix Riot Police and Tow Truck Patrols That Cannot Reach Their Targets
</h2>

Emergency and recovery vehicles need
reachable routes.

A patrol may stop working as intended
when its waypoints lead to disconnected
roads, inaccessible plots, or locations
the assigned vehicle cannot reach.

The warning **"Not all waypoints can be
reached"** indicates that the route
needs attention.

Check the route before spending money
on another vehicle.

Begin with the garage, follow the
assigned streets, and identify any
waypoint separated from the available
road network.

A practical correction is to:

1. Select the affected vehicle or garage.
2. Inspect its assigned patrol waypoints.
3. Move inaccessible points onto roads
   the vehicle can reach.
4. Confirm the route can be completed
   without a missing connection.
5. Allow the vehicle to resume its
   assigned operation.

Riot Police also have a direct confrontation
command. When an individual protester is
already blocking a business, select a
ready unit and right-click the protester
instead of relying only on a general
neighborhood patrol.

Check the unit panel before issuing the
command. **"No units ready to fight!"**
means the current response has no
immediately available personnel.

Tow Trucks use patrol waypoints as well.
Their route and wreck-collection behavior
become important during the cleanup
after a large protest.


<h2 id="fires-and-burned-cars" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Remove Fires and Wrecked Cars After a Riot
</h2>

Stopping the protesters does not
automatically restore every damaged
parking facility.

Burned or abandoned vehicles may
remain inside garages, preventing
those spaces from returning to
normal use.

A large Bald Town City disturbance
can leave approximately **70 wrecked
vehicles** to be cleared from a
parking garage.

A city may therefore have no active
protesters while its simultaneous
parking count remains much lower
than before the outbreak.

### Extinguish Active Fires

Inspect the affected roads, parking
lots, and garages for vehicles that
are still burning.

Deploy a **Fire Engine Garage** response
where fires remain active, and make
sure the emergency vehicle can reach
the damaged area.

The riot control response and the
fire response serve different purposes.
Police deal with protesters, while
fire engines respond to fires.

A fire can continue after the
protesters have left. Check the
affected parking area before
assuming that it is safe to
resume normal operations.

### Tow Wrecked Cars to a Junkyard

After dealing with the active
fires, inspect the parking bays
for wrecked vehicles.

Build a **Tow Truck Garage** and
provide a **Junkyard** where the
recovered wrecks can be taken.

The Junkyard is the destination
used for unwanted or damaged
vehicles collected by Tow Trucks.

Configure the Tow Truck patrol
to visit the affected parking
facilities.

For a parking garage with many
wrecked cars, a single truck may
need several trips before the
area is cleared.

Follow the truck to confirm that
it collects damaged vehicles and
can reach the Junkyard.

When the recovery operation is
not removing wrecks, inspect:

- Whether the Tow Truck is assigned
  to the affected parking area.
- Whether its patrol waypoints
  are reachable.
- Whether it has a usable route
  to the damaged vehicles.
- Whether the recovered wrecks
  have an accessible Junkyard
  destination.

Do not replace the garage simply
because cleanup is slow.
First confirm that the assigned
trucks are moving wrecked cars.

Parking activity should recover
as damaged vehicles are removed
and usable spaces become available
again.


<h2 id="plastic-trees" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Use Plastic Trees to Reduce Civil Unrest
</h2>

**Plastic Trees** can reduce Civil
Unrest after they have been produced
and placed in the city.

The **Plastic Tree Factory** is
available in the Oil and Fuel
construction category.

Its October 2026 Early Access
construction menu shows:

- **Construction cost:** $6,000
- **Input:** 3 Crude Oil
- **Output:** 1 Plastic Tree
- **Effect:** Reduces Civil Unrest

The factory needs a supply of Crude
Oil before it can produce trees.

Place the factory where it can be
served by the existing oil transport
network, then check whether Crude
Oil is arriving.

For extraction and delivery
problems, use the
<Link href="/car-park-capital/oil-gasoline">Pump Jack and Crude Oil Transportation instructions</Link>.

Once Plastic Trees are available,
place them where their unrest-reducing
effect is needed.

The production building itself
also reduces nearby parking demand.
Keep it away from your strongest
commercial parking locations when
suitable industrial land is available
elsewhere.

Plastic Trees are a preventive
measure for Civil Unrest.
They do not replace the direct
Riot Police response needed when
a protester has already blocked
a business.

Their production also consumes
Crude Oil that could otherwise
be transported to a refinery,
so inspect the refinery's incoming
supply when adding more
Plastic Tree Factories.


<h2 id="restore-businesses" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Check Why Businesses Are Still Closed After the Protests
</h2>

When the streets are no longer
occupied by protesters, inspect
the businesses and parking
facilities that were disrupted.

Start with the buildings that
previously displayed an active
protest warning.

**A business still shows the
protester warning:** Look for
another active protester nearby
and confirm that a ready Riot
Police unit can reach them.

**The protester is gone, but a
Gas Station has no customers:**
Check whether its drive-thru is
accessible and whether the
station has fuel available.

A station that remains empty
after the protest ends may
need its Gasoline supply restored.
Follow the <Link href="/car-park-capital/oil-gasoline">Gasoline delivery and depleted-supply checks</Link>
when the fuel warning remains active.

**A parking garage has many
empty or unusable spaces:**
Inspect the facility for
damaged cars that still need
to be removed. The Tow Truck
operation may require additional
time to clear a large backlog.

**A business has reopened, but
Civil Unrest is still increasing:**
Check the current unrest
indicator and the population's
needs before resuming
large-scale evictions.

Riot Police units can return
to their garages after the
immediate confrontations have
ended. Keep patrol routes active
around areas where protesters
have repeatedly interrupted
services.

Resume major construction when
the affected businesses are
operating, emergency vehicles
can reach their destinations,
and damaged parking spaces
are being cleared.
