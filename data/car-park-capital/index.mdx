<div className="my-4 text-sm text-slate-400">
  Car Park Capital — Early Access, October 2026.
</div>

In **Car Park Capital**, parking spaces bring customers and
revenue, but upgrading the city also requires residents to
own cars and satisfy an expanding list of needs.

The first parking lot should start earning money before
you commit to expensive land purchases. As Car Dependency
increases, the important decision changes: sometimes you
need more qualifying parking spaces, sometimes additional
car owners, and sometimes a service that has fallen behind
the population's expectations.

Check the relevant counter before starting construction.
A full garage, an empty Car Shop Carousel, and a sudden
drop in Car Braininess require different responses.


<h2 id="tutorial" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Build Your First Parking Lot
</h2>

The opening tutorial begins at an amusement park and
introduces land purchases, demolition, road surfaces,
parking entrances, exits, and individual parking bays.

The parking lot must provide a continuous route from
the public road to its parking spaces and back out again.

Follow the construction sequence:

1. **Buy the land.** Purchase the plot where the
   parking facility will operate.
2. **Clear obstacles.** Remove rocks and other objects
   occupying the construction area.
3. **Lay asphalt.** Create a drivable surface between
   the intended entrance, parking bays, and exit.
4. **Place the Parking Entrance and Parking Exit.**
   Check their directions and road connections.
5. **Add Parking Bays.** Rotate each bay so vehicles
   can enter it.
6. **Install barriers where needed.** Prevent cars
   from bypassing the intended parking route.

### Why Won't Cars Enter the Parking Bays?

The direction of each bay matters.

**Cars enter a Parking Bay from the side without
the yellow marking.** The tutorial explains this
while asking you to place parking spaces.

Use the rotation controls before confirming each
bay. If several finished spaces remain unused,
check their orientation and the asphalt route
leading to them.

The tutorial also allows incorrectly placed
objects to be removed while building. Correct
a misplaced entrance, barrier, or bay before
filling the remaining plot.

A functioning ground-level parking lot is enough
to establish the basic operation. Multistory
construction becomes more useful when you need
additional capacity on valuable land.


<h2 id="parking-demand" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Where Should You Build Parking Lots?
</h2>

The **Parking Demand Heatmap** shows where people
want to park.

Open it before buying land, especially when
choosing between an expensive city-center plot
and a larger area farther away.

Established destinations such as offices and
hotels can create strong local demand. Empty
land does not generate the same number of
customers simply because it is available
for development.

Compare three things before purchasing:

- **Demand:** Does the plot overlap an area
  where people already want to park?
- **Access:** Can vehicles reach the entrance
  from an existing road?
- **Construction cost:** Will enough money
  remain to finish the parking lot and keep
  other necessary services running?

The heatmap becomes important again when Car
Dependency requires **parking spaces in
high-demand areas**.

That counter is not necessarily equal to
the total number of bays you have built.
Spaces outside qualifying demand areas
may fail to advance the upgrade requirement.

### What Lowers or Increases Nearby Parking Demand?

Public transport can reduce the need for
parking in surrounding areas. Removing it
may create opportunities for car-focused
development, although buying the land can
be expensive.

Industrial buildings also affect nearby
parking demand.

**Pump Jacks, Car Factories, and other
industrial facilities can reduce the
attractiveness of their surroundings
for parking.**

Keep production facilities away from the
strongest office and commercial parking
districts when a practical alternative
exists.

The same principle applies when expanding
onto previously unused land. First establish
why people would visit the area, then build
the parking capacity to serve them.

When an existing lot is consistently busy,
expansion near that destination may be more
valuable than opening another business in
a low-demand district.

  How to Set Parking Fees and Make Money
</h2>

to open its pricing controls.

The Parking Entrance panel shows the
current rate per minute, vehicles passed,

### How High Should the Parking Fee Be?

The price slider should be adjusted against
the population's willingness to pay.

Residents with higher Car Braininess may
accept higher prices. When a fee exceeds
what customers are willing to pay, the
business can lose activity despite
being located in a high-demand area.

Start with an accessible price and observe
whether vehicles continue using the lot.
Increase the rate when willingness to pay
allows it, then monitor the response.

The pricing interface also includes
**Apply to all**. Review that option before
changing fees across multiple parking
facilities at once.

A price that works near a busy office
district may not be equally appropriate
for a less attractive location.

### Why Is the City Losing Money?

Daily operating costs continue even when
a facility is underused.

Inspect occupancy and upkeep before
constructing another parking structure.
More bays only improve revenue when
customers can and will use them.

The main expenses to check are:

- Land purchases that have not yet produced
  operating businesses.
- Parking facilities with low occupancy.
- Garages, service buildings, and transport
  vehicles with ongoing upkeep.
- Construction projects that still lack
  a required road or facility connection.
- Outstanding bank loans and interest costs.

Some large land purchases can be delayed
until parking revenue and vehicle sales
are supporting the city.

For example, buying land around a subway
station may help a scenario objective,
but that purchase need not take priority
over completing an unfinished business
that could begin earning income sooner.

### Should You Take a Bank Loan?

The bank can provide loans for additional
construction. An Early Access bank offer
shows a **10% interest rate**.

Check the rate displayed in your current
bank interface before borrowing.

A loan is most useful when a specific,
affordable construction step will
make an existing project operational.

Finishing a missing road connection
or completing a productive parking
facility gives the borrowed money
an immediate purpose.

Borrowing for several untested buildings
adds financial pressure without
establishing whether any of them
will attract customers.

Calculate the remaining land and
construction expenses first, then
borrow only what the project needs.


<h2 id="car-dependency" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Increase Car Dependency Levels
</h2>

The **Car Dependency** indicator at the
top of the screen shows the city's
progression level.

Each upgrade uses requirements displayed
in its own panel. The two recurring
progression counters are:

- **Parking spaces in high-demand areas**
- **Car ownership**

They measure different parts of the
car economy.

Parking capacity represents spaces built
in locations with sufficient demand.
Car ownership represents residents
who own vehicles.

Neither number is interchangeable with
the count of vehicles currently
parked in the city.

### Which Requirement Should You Work On?

When the qualifying parking counter is
below its target, use the heatmap to
find additional suitable space.

Expanding a busy parking lot can increase
that counter without requiring a separate
business in an undeveloped area.

When car ownership is below its target,
residents need access to vehicles they
can purchase.

A **Car Shop Carousel** must have cars
available for sale. If it displays
**"No Vehicles for Sale,"** inspect its
stock before constructing another
parking garage.

The <Link href="/car-park-capital/car-production">Car Factory, Car Carrier, and Car Shop Carousel instructions</Link>
cover direct car purchases, automated
manufacturing, deliveries, and sales
shortages.

Once the displayed requirements are met,
Car Dependency advances and the
newly available buildings or services
are shown.


### Car Dependency Level Requirements

Each Car Dependency level requires enough parking
spaces in high-demand areas and enough residents
who own cars.

The progression targets shown in the Early Access
build are:

- **Level 1 → 2:** 50 high-demand parking spaces
  and 50 car owners.
- **Level 2 → 3:** 100 high-demand parking spaces
  and 125 car owners.
- **Level 3 → 4:** 250 high-demand parking spaces
  and 340 car owners.
- **Level 4 → 5:** 450 high-demand parking spaces
  and 590 car owners.
- **Level 5 → 6:** 700 high-demand parking spaces
  and 990 car owners.
- **Level 6 → 7:** 1,000 high-demand parking spaces
  and 1,390 car owners in the Bald Town City
  progression shown here.

Check the targets in your current Car Dependency
panel, particularly when playing a different
scenario or game version.

separately. Completing one requirement does
not automatically advance Car Dependency when
the other is still below its target.

qualifying parking spaces but only 508/590
car owners. More parking bays will not resolve
the remaining ownership shortage.

The opposite situation is also possible:
288/450 parking spaces with 620/590 car
owners. In that case, additional car sales
are not the priority. Build qualifying
parking spaces in an area with sufficient
demand instead.

### Important Car Dependency Unlocks

- **Level 2 — Food:** Food becomes a Car Braininess
  need. Gulf of Freedom also unlocks the
  Amphibious Car Factory.

- **Level 3 — Fuel:** Residents develop a Fuel need.
  Pump Jacks, Oil Refineries, fuel transportation,
  and Gas Stations become available.

- **Level 5 — Drinks and Restroom:** New customer
  needs include Drinks and Restroom. Existing
  Fast Food Drive-Thrus can be expanded with
  drink-serving equipment.

Level 7 is a victory requirement in both
Bald Town City and Gulf of Freedom, but
their other scenario objectives differ.

Bald Town City also requires simultaneous
parked cars and subway demolitions.
Gulf of Freedom has different Car Braininess
and suburban population targets.

After advancing Car Dependency, check the
Scenario Objective panel and newly unlocked
customer needs before your next expansion.


<h2 id="car-braininess" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Raise Car Braininess and Fix Missing Needs
</h2>

**Car Braininess** reflects the
population's acceptance of the
city's car-focused lifestyle.

Hover over its indicator to inspect
the needs contributing to the
current percentage.

Parking and Propaganda are
important early categories.

Accessible parking facilities
serve the population's parking
needs, while propaganda buildings
and vehicles increase support
for car-focused development.

### Where Should You Place Propaganda?

Place billboards around populated
streets where they can affect
residents.

A billboard in an empty industrial
area is unlikely to be the best
use of money when the population
is concentrated around existing
homes and businesses.

The game also offers mobile
propaganda through vehicles
that follow assigned routes.

As the city grows, additional
propaganda options become
available. Check the current
coverage and population response
before buying several
facilities with overlapping
purposes.

### Why Did Car Braininess Drop After Leveling Up?

A dependency upgrade can introduce
a new need before the necessary
service has been established.

The most direct early example
occurs at **Car Dependency Level 2**,
which introduces **Food**.

A city that previously satisfied its
available needs may show a lower
percentage after this change.

The parking businesses have not
necessarily stopped functioning.
The population now expects another
service that may not yet be operating.

Open the needs indicator and identify
the weakest category before purchasing
additional land.

A missing Food need calls for food
service. Missing fuel availability
calls for working Gas Stations.
A parking expansion cannot replace
either of those operations.

As more services unlock, return to
existing businesses and check whether
they can be upgraded.

At Level 5, Drinks become relevant.
An existing Fast Food Drive-Thru
may have room for an additional
drink-serving component, avoiding
the need to construct an entirely
separate food business.

When Gas Stations exist but repeatedly
run out of fuel, follow the
<Link href="/car-park-capital/oil-gasoline">Gasoline production and delivery checks</Link>
to restore supply.

### Why Are Protests Appearing?

An unsatisfied population and
disruptive redevelopment can
contribute to Civil Unrest.

Evicting residents while the city's
needs are already falling behind
can increase the pressure.

Once a protester has blocked a
business, restoring satisfaction
alone does not remove that
immediate obstruction.

Use the <Link href="/car-park-capital/unrest-protests">Riot Police, Tow Truck, and Plastic Tree procedures</Link>
when protests, fires, or wrecked
vehicles have begun interrupting
normal operations.

After clearing active disruptions,
inspect the needs indicator again
before continuing with further
evictions or expensive construction.


<h2 id="drive-thrus" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Build Drive-Thru Services That Work
</h2>

Car Park Capital uses drive-thru
layouts for several services.

A facility needs a usable vehicle
route, including the required
entrance, queue, service objects,
and exit.

The exact objects depend on
the business being built.

A **Fast Food Drive-Thru**
can include ordering and
food-serving equipment,
with additional options
becoming available during
progression.

Complete the construction
steps shown by the facility
before expecting customers
to use it.

### Why Does the Drive-Thru Look Finished but Not Operate?

Inspect the construction panel.
A required object or route section
may still be incomplete.

Typical problems include:

- An entrance or exit that
  does not connect correctly
  to the street.
- A missing service object.
- A queue that does not reach
  the service point.
- An incomplete connection
  between the service area
  and the exit.
- Insufficient space for
  newly unlocked equipment.

When adding a drink service
to an existing food facility,
check whether the new object
can fit into its current route.

A short adjustment may be enough,
but a crowded layout can require
reworking turns or queue sections.

Long queues are a separate
problem from missing ingredients
or supplies.

When cars are lining up outside
a working facility, inspect its
traffic flow before purchasing
another production building.

For Gas Stations, fuel delivery
is handled separately from
the physical drive-thru layout.
The <Link href="/car-park-capital/oil-gasoline">Gas Station setup and fuel supply instructions</Link>
explain the required production
and transportation network.


<h2 id="multi-story-parking" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Build a Multi-Story Parking Garage
</h2>

A multistory garage lets you add
parking capacity above a plot
instead of repeatedly purchasing
adjacent land.

The construction tools include
**Platforms, Ramps, Pillars, and
Elevation** controls.

These allow a parking structure
to be raised above the original
ground-level driving area.

Begin with a working lower floor.
Then build a route that vehicles
can use to reach the additional
parking level.

A basic expansion involves:

1. **Build a platform** above
   the existing parking area
   or on the next intended level.
2. **Adjust its elevation** to
   the desired height.
3. **Add ramps** connecting
   the lower and upper routes.
4. **Use pillars** where the
   structure needs support.
5. **Place parking bays** on
   the additional usable surface.
6. **Test access** before
   constructing another floor.

Up and down ramps must connect
to the relevant driving areas.
An upper floor full of bays is
not useful when cars cannot
reach it or return to the exit.

### Use Blueprints or Build the Garage Yourself?

Blueprints provide an alternative
to assembling every platform,
ramp, and parking bay manually.

A premade multistory structure
can be placed into a suitable
construction area, including
within a larger parking facility
when space and connections allow.

Inspect its entrances, ramps,
and usable parking spaces after
placement.

A structure can look complete
while still needing adjustments
to connect with the surrounding
parking operation.

### Do You Need a Separate Parking Fee on Every Floor?

A multistory facility can
operate through an existing
paid parking entrance.

Drivers pay to enter the parking
operation and can use its
connected spaces without
requiring a separate payment
at every floor.

The main reason to expand
vertically is to increase
usable capacity on land that
already attracts customers.

Before adding another level,
check whether the current
garage is filling regularly
and whether the extra bays
will contribute to your
dependency requirements.

Unused upper floors add
construction expense without
solving an underlying lack
of parking demand.


<h2 id="suburban-homes" className="scroll-mt-24">
  How to Build Suburban Homes and Evict Tenants
</h2>

Suburban housing becomes important
when a scenario requires people
to move out of dense urban areas.

The relevant objective counts
**residents living in suburban
homes**, not houses purchased
or placed on the map.

Build the homes on land that
people can access from the
road network.

A house that exists but
has no usable route home
may fail to provide the
expected residential progress.

### Why Does Eviction Say Not Enough Space in Suburban Homes?

The warning **"Not enough space
in suburban homes"** means the
relocation cannot proceed with
the currently available
housing capacity.

Add suitable suburban housing
and check whether residents
have moved in before continuing
with the eviction.

Demolishing occupied city
buildings is not the first
step when the replacement
housing is still unavailable.

Once residential capacity exists,
use the eviction and demolition
tools on city plots you need
for another development.

Do not clear a large number
of occupied buildings merely
because land has become
purchasable.

Evictions can increase unrest,
and the purchased plot still
needs to support a worthwhile
business or objective.

Island housing introduces
additional navigation concerns.
A resident driving an ordinary
car may not be able to reach
a home separated from the
road network by water.

In that situation, check
road connectivity and
vehicle access before building
more houses.

For a suburban population
objective, monitor the
actual residents counter
after construction.


<h2 id="choose-scenario" className="scroll-mt-24">
  Bald Town City or Gulf of Freedom: What Should You Focus On?
</h2>

The same construction and
progression systems apply
across scenarios, but the
map layout changes which
investment is worthwhile.

### Bald Town City

Bald Town City's dense
office districts provide
strong existing parking demand.

Its challenge is coordinating
several victory conditions
under a **40-day deadline**.

Expanding parking capacity
is only part of the task.
Subway demolition, suburban
housing, car ownership,
Car Braininess, and
simultaneously parked vehicles
must also reach their
required targets.

The <Link href="/car-park-capital/bald-town-city-walkthrough">Bald Town City walkthrough</Link>
covers those conditions and
the late dependency upgrades.

### Gulf of Freedom

Gulf of Freedom distributes
parking demand around a
hotel, beaches, harbor
areas, and small islands.

Its major difference is
the **Amphibious Car Factory**
unlocked at Level 2.

Amphibious Cars can enter
and leave the water where
roads reach the shoreline.

The main construction risks
involve expensive coastal land,
disconnected island roads,
and residential areas that
ordinary cars cannot reach.

The <Link href="/car-park-capital/gulf-of-freedom">Gulf of Freedom instructions</Link>
cover the hotel and beach
opening, the amphibious
unlock, shoreline access,
and island navigation.
