To **repair the Bell Tower in Fields of Mistria**, I first finish the Saturday
Market Plaza, meet Zorel at her new stall, then wait for her later letter asking
me to meet at the tower.

The repair itself needs **20 Refined Stone, 30 Hardwood, 1 Dragon-Forged Core,
and 1 Dragon-Forged Fang**. The part that cost me the most time was not the
construction materials — it was collecting the rare Forge ingredients and then
realizing that a full Donation Box still does **not** start the repair
automatically. I have to find Adeline and tell her everything is ready.

</h2>

up to the sealed entrance.

Zorel explains why she wants the town bell restored, while Landen agrees to

I let the entire scene finish before leaving because the **Donation Box only
appears outside the tower after the repair project has been accepted**.

<h2 id="dragon-forged-core">
  How I craft the Dragon-Forged Core
</h2>

The **Dragon-Forged Core** requires:

- **5 Monster Cores**
- **5 Mistril Ore**
- **1 Perfect Diamond**
- **1 Ancient Firefly**
- **1 Medium Essence Stone**

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/fields-of-mistria/fields-of-mistria-dragon-forged-core-recipe.webp"
    alt="Dragon-Forged Core recipe with Monster Cores Mistril Ore Perfect Diamond Ancient Firefly and Medium Essence Stone"
    width={1280}
    height={720}
    className="h-auto w-full rounded-xl"
  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    I check this entire recipe before spending any rare Mine drops on optional
    crafting.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The **Ancient Firefly** was the ingredient most likely to send me in the wrong
direction.

I do not search the farm, Beach, Eastern Road, or another outdoor area for a
seasonal spawn. I look in the **Ancient Ruins section of the Mines**.

For Monster Cores, the most practical reset point in my run was around the
**Floor 15 checkpoint**.

I use this loop:

1. take the elevator to the Floor 15 area;
2. scan nearby rooms for lantern enemies;
3. defeat the lanterns;
4. collect their drops;
5. move floors or reset once the useful enemies are gone.

I stop when I have five usable Monster Cores instead of fully clearing every
rock on each floor.

The lantern enemies do **not** guarantee a Monster Core every time. I sometimes
get Glass or another drop, so Floor 15 is a convenient farming loop rather than
a fixed five-Core room.

I also protect the Perfect Diamond once this quest is active. If I already have
one in storage, I do not spend my last copy on an optional craft and then create
another Bell Tower bottleneck for myself.

<h2 id="dragon-forged-fang">
  How I craft the Dragon-Forged Fang
</h2>

The **Dragon-Forged Fang** requires:

- **5 Void Stones**
- **5 Void Herbs**
- **1 Medium Essence Stone**

The detail I make sure not to miss is the **Medium Essence Stone total**.

The Core consumes one and the Fang consumes another, so the Bell Tower route
needs **2 Medium Essence Stones total**.

For the Void materials, I use the Mine checkpoints around **Floors 65–69**.

I found Void Herbs on Floor 66 during my run, but I do not treat that as a
guaranteed five-Herb spawn. Room layouts change.

When the elevator checkpoints are open, I scan the room before committing time
to it:

- if I can see a Void Herb, I collect it;
- I check Void Stone sources on the same pass;
- if the room has neither target, I leave;
- I reset from the elevator instead of clearing a bad floor out of sunk-cost habit.

That last change saved me more time than trying to make every Mine floor
"worth" the trip.

<h2 id="material-priority">
  What I gather first when several materials are missing
</h2>

I prioritize the ingredients that can block the entire craft.

My order is:

1. confirm whether I already own the Perfect Diamond and Ancient Firefly;
2. count both Medium Essence Stones;
3. finish the Monster Cores for the Core;
4. finish the Void materials for the Fang;
5. fill any remaining Mistril Ore, Hardwood, or Refined Stone gap.

I craft the Core or Fang as soon as one full recipe is ready.

That turns several rare loose materials into one finished quest component and
makes the remaining requirement much easier to track.

I also stop carrying completed ingredients back into the Mines. Once something
is safely stored or deposited, I take only food, recovery items, the tools I
need, and enough empty inventory space for the materials still missing.

The two Dragon-Forged items have to be **crafted first**. Putting Monster Cores,

Once all four lines are complete, I do **not** wait beside the Bell Tower for
construction to start.

<h2 id="report-to-adeline">
  The Donation Box is full — now find Adeline
</h2>

This is the step I would check first if the Bell Tower still looks broken after
all materials have been deposited.

The fourth completed Donation Box requirement does not automatically trigger the
repair.

I open the map and find **Adeline**.

In my run she was near the Request Board, but I do not use that as a fixed
location because her daily schedule can move her elsewhere.

What matters is the conversation.

I tell her that all Bell Tower materials are ready. That is the trigger that
brings the repair group back together.

I do **not** need to wait for:

- another Saturday Market;
- another night of sleep;
- Zorel to return to the tower;
- or a specific time of day.

If the Journal still wants me to speak with Adeline, I follow that objective
instead of waiting at the completed Donation Box.

<h2 id="bell-tower-complete">
  What I unlock after repairing the Bell Tower
</h2>

After I report to Adeline, the group completes the construction and the sealed
tower opens.

The repair also satisfies the **Ringing Ovation** achievement requirement.

I do not need to buy every Song Crystal or test every Bell Sound for that
achievement. The important progression event is repairing the tower itself.

Once the interior is open, the repair quest is complete and the Crystal
Resonator becomes usable.

<h2 id="bell-sounds">
  How the Bell Sound menu works
</h2>

Inside the repaired tower, I interact with the Crystal Resonator to change the
sound of Mistria's town bell.

The visible options in my menu included:

- Default
- Gothic
- Meow
- Moonlight Through My Window
- Nostalgic
- School Bell
- Shaking Off a Storm

I do not treat that as the complete list because the menu continues beyond the
first visible group.

The selected sound is used when the town bell rings around **nightfall**.

I did not see the choice alter the tower exterior. It behaved as an audio
customization.

I also keep the Bell Tower Resonator separate from Zorel's placeable Crystal
Resonators:

- the **Bell Tower Resonator** changes the town bell;
- a **placed Crystal Resonator** plays Song Crystals in the room or area where I put it.

Buying a placeable Resonator does not unlock the Bell Tower.

<h2 id="bell-tower-problems">
  Bell Tower repair problems I would check first
</h2>

### Zorel has appeared, but I still have no Bell Tower letter

I make sure the Saturday Market Plaza is complete, speak to Zorel at her new
stall, then sleep and check the next mail.

Her Bell Tower letter belongs to a later step after the market introduction.

### I cannot find the Ancient Firefly

I stop searching outdoor insect locations and go to the **Ancient Ruins section
of the Mines**.

This is the Core ingredient that is easiest to misread as a normal seasonal bug.

### One Dragon-Forged recipe is still missing a Medium Essence Stone

I recount both recipes.

The Core uses one Medium Essence Stone and the Fang uses another, so I need
**two total** for the Bell Tower.

### The Donation Box is full but the Bell Tower is still sealed

I leave the tower and find **Adeline**.

The completed Donation Box is not the final trigger. Her hand-in conversation is.

### The tower is repaired but I cannot find the Bell Sound menu

I enter the newly opened Bell Tower and interact with the Crystal Resonator
inside.

I do not look for that menu at Zorel's market stall or on a Resonator placed at
home.
