The Fields of Mistria Bell Tower repair becomes available after I finish the
Saturday Market Plaza and meet Zorel at her new stall.

Zorel later sends a letter asking me to meet her, Adeline, and **Landen** at the
Bell Tower. The project requires two normal construction materials and two
Dragon-Forged components:

- 20 Refined Stone;
- 30 Hardwood;
- 1 Dragon-Forged Fang;
- 1 Dragon-Forged Core.

The real bottleneck is not the Hardwood or Refined Stone. It is checking both
Forge recipes before spending Monster Cores, Mistril Ore, Perfect gemstones,
Ancient Ruins insects, Void materials, or Medium Essence Stones elsewhere.

After I deposit everything, the repair still does not begin automatically. I
must find Adeline and tell her that the Bell Tower materials are ready.

<h2 id="bell-tower-meeting">

</h2>

During the meeting, Zorel explains that she remembers hearing the town bell when
her family visited Mistria.

The bell itself still functions, but the earthquake damaged the rest of the
tower. Because the building was less urgent than bridges, shops, and other
public repairs, the town sealed it instead of restoring it immediately.

Landen agrees to handle the construction work, while the player is responsible
for supplying the materials.

I check every storage chest before starting a Mine run.

On an established save, I may already have:

- Perfect Diamonds saved from mining;
- Mistril Ore left after equipment upgrades;
- Monster Cores from lantern enemies;
- Void Stones or Void Herbs from previous Mine trips;
- an Ancient Firefly stored for the Museum;
- and Medium Essence Stones left from another recipe.

I also deposit completed categories early.

Once the 30 Hardwood is inside the Donation Box, I cannot accidentally turn it
into furniture or another building component. The box also gives me a cleaner
view of which Dragon-Forged ingredient is still blocking the project.

<h2 id="dragon-forged-core">
  Craft the Dragon-Forged Core
</h2>

The Dragon-Forged Core recipe requires:

- **5 Monster Cores**
- **5 Mistril Ore**
- **1 Perfect Diamond**
- **1 Ancient Firefly**
- **1 Medium Essence Stone**

  <Image

    width={1280}
    height={720}
    className="h-auto w-full rounded-xl"
  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    I write down the full recipe before leaving the Forge because its
    ingredients come from several different Mine regions.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The Ancient Firefly was the ingredient I was most likely to confuse with a
seasonal outdoor insect.

It is not a normal seasonal farm or town spawn. I search the **Ancient Ruins**
section of the Mines.

That changes my preparation. I do not wait for a different season or spend a
day checking the Narrows, Eastern Road, Beach, and farm for it.

For Monster Cores, the lantern route around the Floor 15 checkpoint worked well
in my save. Several lantern enemies appeared on nearby Upper Mines floors, and
the checkpoint made the route easy to reset.

The drop is not guaranteed from every lantern. Some gave Glass or another item
instead, so I treat Floor 15 as a tested reset point rather than a fixed
five-Core location.

My route is:

1. take the elevator near Floor 15;
2. scan the room for lantern enemies;
3. defeat the visible lanterns;
4. collect the drops;
5. move to a nearby floor or leave and reset;
6. stop once I have five usable Monster Cores.

I do not keep clearing every rock after the enemy drops are collected. The
objective is the Core recipe, not a full Upper Mines resource run.

The Perfect Diamond and Mistril Ore are better checked in storage first. I avoid
using the last Perfect Diamond on an optional craft when the Bell Tower quest is
active.

I needed **two Medium Essence Stones in total**:

- one for the Dragon-Forged Core;
- one for the Dragon-Forged Fang.

That total is easy to miss when the recipes are checked separately. Crafting or
spending the first stone does not remove the requirement from the second
recipe.

The mid-60s Mine checkpoints gave me the most practical search loop for the
Void materials during this run.

I used Floors 65–69 as reset points and found Void Herbs on Floor 66. The exact
room layout changed, so I did not treat Floor 66 as a guaranteed five-Herb
floor.

I scan each room before breaking ordinary rocks:

- Void Herbs are visible gathering targets;
- Void Stone sources can be checked during the same room;
- the elevator lets me reset without starting from the top;
- and Sacred Light can help reveal a large room when visibility is poor.

If a floor has none of the target material, I leave quickly.

The first mistake I made was staying on an empty floor because I had already
spent time reaching it. Once the elevator checkpoints are active, moving on is
cheaper than clearing a bad layout.

<h2 id="material-priority">
  Finish the rare components before spending another Mine day
</h2>

Before starting another run, I compare the two recipes and count the remaining
items across both.

My combined checklist is:

- 5 Monster Cores;
- 5 Mistril Ore;
- 1 Perfect Diamond;
- 1 Ancient Firefly;
- 5 Void Stones;
- 5 Void Herbs;
- 2 Medium Essence Stones.

I do not carry every completed ingredient into the Mines.

Once a category is safely stored or already deposited, I take only:

- food;
- healing or Mana support;
- the tools needed for the target area;
- and enough empty inventory slots for the missing ingredients.

This matters most when only one rare item remains. A nearly full bag can force
me to discard a useful drop or leave the Mine before finishing the route.

I craft each Dragon-Forged component as soon as its full recipe is available.
That converts several loose rare materials into one protected quest item and
makes the remaining requirement easier to read.

<h2 id="report-to-adeline">
  Find Adeline after the Donation Box is full
</h2>

The Bell Tower does not repair itself when the fourth category reaches its
required total.

This was the main quest blocker in my route.

I initially waited because the Plaza project had also used a construction box,
and I expected the next event to trigger automatically. The Journal still
required me to speak to Adeline.

I open the map and look for her current location instead of waiting until the
next Saturday or returning to Zorel's stall.

In my run, Adeline was standing near the Request Board when I completed the
hand-in.

The exact daily location can change. The important condition is the
conversation, not where she happens to be standing.

When I tell her that all Bell Tower materials are ready, she immediately gathers
the repair group.

I do not need to:

- wait for Saturday;
- sleep another night;
- speak to Zorel first;
- remove the materials from the Donation Box;
- or stand beside the tower at nightfall.

The achievement is not tied to:

- previewing every Bell Sound;
- buying every Song Crystal;
- placing a Resonator in the farmhouse;
- or hearing the bell on a certain number of days.

Once the repair scene finishes and the interior becomes available, the main
Bell Tower quest is complete.

<h2 id="bell-sounds">
  Use the Crystal Resonator to change the daily bell
</h2>

The repaired tower contains a Crystal Resonator with a Bell Sound menu.

The visible choices in my menu included:

- Default;
- Gothic;
- Meow;
- Moonlight Through My Window;
- Nostalgic;
- School Bell;
- Shaking Off a Storm.

The menu continues beyond the first visible group, so I do not treat this as a
complete list of every sound that can appear.

I use Preview before confirming a choice because several names do not clearly
describe the actual sound.

The town bell rings around nightfall each day. Changing the selection changes
the audio used for that daily bell.

I did not observe the selected sound changing the tower's exterior appearance.
The feature behaved as an audio customization rather than a building-style
menu.

This Bell Tower Resonator is also separate from Zorel's placeable Crystal
Resonators.

- The tower menu changes the town bell.
- A placed Resonator plays Song Crystals in the room or area where I place it.

Buying a farmhouse Resonator does not unlock the Bell Tower, and repairing the
tower does not automatically place a music device in my home.

<h2 id="bell-tower-problems">
  Fix the Bell Tower step that has not progressed
</h2>

### Zorel has not sent the Bell Tower letter

I complete the Saturday Market Plaza, wait for Stillwell and Zorel to appear,
and speak to Zorel at her stall.

The Bell Tower letter belongs to her later town storyline rather than the first
market introduction.

### I cannot find the Ancient Firefly

I search the Ancient Ruins section of the Mines.

It is not treated as an ordinary seasonal outdoor insect, so changing seasons
does not solve this ingredient.

### I crafted one Medium Essence Stone but the second recipe is still blocked

The Dragon-Forged Core and Dragon-Forged Fang each consume one Medium Essence
Stone.

I need two in total.

### The Donation Box is full but the tower is still sealed

I find Adeline and tell her that all materials are ready.

The box does not automatically begin the construction scene.

### Do I need to wait until Saturday after filling the box?

No.

The final repair is triggered by Adeline's conversation, not by the Saturday
Market schedule.

### I repaired the tower but cannot find the Bell Sound menu

I enter the newly opened tower and interact with the Crystal Resonator inside.

The menu is not accessed from Zorel's market stall or a Resonator placed at
home.

### Does the Bell Sound change the appearance of the tower?

I did not see the exterior change after selecting a different sound.

The tested effect was the audio played by the daily bell.

### When does the selected bell play?

The bell rings around evening or nightfall. The chosen Bell Sound is used when
that daily ring occurs.
