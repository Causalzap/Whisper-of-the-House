The Fields of Mistria Water Seal is the first major seal puzzle inside the
Mines.

I find the Water Tablet on **Floor 20**, take a rubbing of its unknown writing,
show it to Eiland, and then follow the translation route to Juniper at the
Bathhouse.

The translated tablet asks for four Upper Mines offerings:

- 1 Ruby;
- 1 Stone Loach;
- 1 Lantern Moth;
- 1 Upper Mines Mushroom.

I return to Floor 20 with all four items physically inside my active inventory
and place them in the seal chamber.

Opening the Water Seal unlocks the **Opened The Water Seal** achievement,
restores part of Caldarus' power, and gives me the **Summon Rain** spell.

<h2 id="take-tablet-rubbing">
  Take the Water Tablet Rubbing before leaving
</h2>

The tablet uses an unknown language.

When I interact with it, the game suggests that Eiland may be able to help if I
take a rubbing.

I make sure the **Water Tablet Rubbing** is obtained before leaving the chamber.

Simply seeing the Water Seal or interacting with the sealed structure does not
advance the translation step. The quest should update to direct me toward
Eiland.

I do not continue deeper immediately when:

- my energy is already low;
- my inventory is full;
- I have not activated the Floor 20 checkpoint;
- or the Journal still does not mention Eiland.

The rubbing is the progression item. Once I have it, there is no benefit to
remaining in the room and repeatedly interacting with the untranslated tablet.

<h2 id="speak-to-eiland">
  Show the tablet rubbing to Eiland
</h2>

I leave the Mines and locate Eiland through the map.

Eiland examines the rubbing and identifies the writing as an old language
associated with communication between humans and dragons.

He cannot complete the translation himself.

Instead, he decides that Juniper is the person most likely to understand it and
directs the next part of the investigation toward the Bathhouse.

<h2 id="translate-water-tablet">
  Follow Eiland to Juniper at the Bathhouse
</h2>

The translation continues at the Bathhouse.

Juniper agrees to examine the tablet and explains that future tablet rubbings
should be brought to her.

Her translation describes four offerings:

- a Ruby;
- a swiftly flowing fish of stone;
- a moth drawn near flame;
- a brown mushroom from the Mines.

Those clues correspond to:

| Tablet clue | Required item |
|---|---|
| A red gemstone | Ruby |
| A swiftly flowing fish of stone | Stone Loach |
| A moth that gathers near flame | Lantern Moth |
| A brown mushroom from the Mines | Upper Mines Mushroom |

<h2 id="collect-offerings">
  Collect the four Water Seal offerings
</h2>

All four offerings come from the Upper Mines portion of the route.

Before farming anything, I check:

- farmhouse storage;
- Mine-material chests;
- fish storage;
- insect storage;
- Museum duplicates;
- and the active inventory.

By the time I reached Floor 20, I had already encountered several of the
required items during normal checkpoint runs.

The efficient route is therefore not to start four separate searches
immediately. I identify the one missing item and target only that category.

<h3 id="find-ruby">
  Find a Ruby in the Upper Mines
</h3>

Ruby is the most straightforward offering.

I mine visible gemstone nodes while progressing through the Upper Mines and
keep one normal Ruby in storage.

A Perfect Ruby is a separate item. When the altar asks for a Ruby, I bring the
standard item rather than assuming that any red gemstone will satisfy the slot.

I also avoid selling the last Ruby after receiving the translated quest.

<h3 id="find-stone-loach">
  Catch the Stone Loach in an Upper Mines fishing pool
</h3>

The Stone Loach was the offering most likely to delay my return.

It is caught from fishing water inside the Upper Mines.

I use the elevator to revisit the early checkpoints and scan the generated
rooms for a fishing pool. When a room has no water, I do not clear every rock
hoping that a pool will appear later.

My route is:

1. enter through an Upper Mines elevator checkpoint;
2. scan the room for accessible water;
3. fish the available shadows;
4. move to another floor when the pool is absent or exhausted;
5. return after another reset if the Stone Loach did not appear.

<h3 id="find-lantern-moth">
  Catch the Lantern Moth with the Bug Net
</h3>

The Lantern Moth is an insect, so I carry the Bug Net when searching for it.

It appears as an Upper Mines catch rather than an enemy drop or crafted item.

The clue mentioning flame does not mean I must:

- light a torch;
- craft a lantern;
- defeat every lantern enemy;
- or wait beside the Floor 20 seal.

I scan the floor for the insect during normal Upper Mines runs and use the Net
when it appears.

Because insects can be easy to miss against the Mine background, I avoid
sprinting through every room when the Lantern Moth is my final missing
offering.

<h3 id="find-upper-mines-mushroom">
  Pick up an Upper Mines Mushroom
</h3>

The Upper Mines Mushroom is a forage item found during the opening Mine region.

I check the ground while moving between ladders and elevator checkpoints.

I do not substitute:

- a Cave Mushroom;
- a Deep Woods mushroom;
- a cooked mushroom dish;
- or another brown forage item.

The altar requires the item specifically named **Upper Mines Mushroom**.

This offering is easy to sell or donate before learning the Water Seal
requirements, so I keep one spare copy after the Mines open.

<h2 id="prepare-return">
  Carry all four offerings before returning to Floor 20
</h2>

Once all four items are collected, I move them into my active inventory:

- Ruby;
- Stone Loach;
- Lantern Moth;
- Upper Mines Mushroom.

This is the check that prevents the most unnecessary travel.

An item inside a farmhouse chest may be owned and recorded in the Journal, but
it is not physically available to the Water Seal altar.

Before leaving the farm, I confirm that I can see all four item icons in the
inventory screen.

I then prepare for a short targeted run:

- enough food to reach and leave the chamber safely;
- open inventory slots for new drops;
- the Sword and Pickaxe;
- and no unrelated town-delivery items.

I do not need to repeat the translation dialogue after collecting the items.

The direct route is farmhouse storage → Mine elevator → Floor 20 → Water Seal
chamber.

- another Mine mushroom instead of Upper Mines Mushroom.

Once all four offerings are accepted, the seal opens and the route beyond Floor
20 becomes available.

The game also awards **Opened The Water Seal**.

I let the entire seal sequence finish before leaving the room.

<h2 id="five-seals">
  Treat the Water Seal as the first of five seals
</h2>

Caldarus explains that there are **five seals in total**.

The Water Seal is therefore the beginning of the larger Mine progression
rather than the end of the Mines.

> Activate each elevator checkpoint and finish each seal objective before

That keeps the next permanent shortcut ahead of optional mining, fishing,
artifacts, and enemy drops.

<h2 id="water-seal-problems">
  Fix a Water Seal quest that has stopped progressing
</h2>

### I reached Floor 20 but the Water Seal will not open

Reaching the room is only the first step.

I interact with the tablet, take the Water Tablet Rubbing, speak to Eiland, and
complete Juniper's translation before returning with the offerings.

### Eiland will not translate the tablet

I confirm that the Water Tablet Rubbing was collected from Floor 20 and that the
Journal directs me to Eiland.

Eiland begins the investigation, but Juniper provides the final offering list.

### What are the four Water Seal offerings?

The four items are:

- Ruby;
- Stone Loach;
- Lantern Moth;
- Upper Mines Mushroom.

### Where do I catch the Stone Loach?

I fish in pools generated inside the Upper Mines.

When the current floor has no water, I use another elevator checkpoint or reset
the Mine layout rather than clearing the entire room.

### Can I use a Perfect Ruby?

I bring the standard item named Ruby.

I do not rely on a Perfect Ruby or another red gemstone as a substitute.

### Why does the altar say I am missing an offering?

I move all four items from storage into the active inventory and check their
exact names.

Owning an item in a farmhouse chest does not make it available to the Floor 20
altar.

### Does the Lantern Moth drop from lantern enemies?

The Lantern Moth is caught with the Bug Net.

I search for the insect in the Upper Mines rather than farming lantern enemies
for it.

### Can I leave immediately after opening the Water Seal?

Yes.

The opened seal and story progress remain complete. When energy, health, time,
or inventory space is low, I return later before exploring the next Mine
region.

### What does opening the Water Seal unlock?

It opens the route beyond Floor 20, awards Opened The Water Seal, advances the
seal storyline, and unlocks Summon Rain through Caldarus.

### How many seals are in Fields of Mistria?

Caldarus says there are five seals in total.

The Water Seal is the first one completed in the Mine progression route.
