Fields of Mistria has **69 achievements** covering the opening story, Town
Ranks, Mine seals, town repairs, relationships, skills, ranching, festivals,
the Museum, marriage, children, and Stillwell's Mission Quests.

I do not complete them in the order shown on Steam.

The efficient route is to let the main story clear most of the early list,
begin the long calendar-dependent goals before I need them, and leave the
Museum, Tier 5 systems, Mistril Star Rank, Stillwell missions, and a child's
first birthday for the established-save cleanup.

This page is a complete checklist and completion roadmap. For achievements tied
to a larger quest, it explains the actual unlock point and the mistake that can
prevent it from registering without repeating an entire standalone
walkthrough.

<h2 id="old-save-achievements">
  Load an established save before starting over
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

Fields of Mistria can recognize achievement conditions completed before the
1.0 achievement list was added.

When I load an established save, I give the game time to process the current
Town Rank, repairs, skills, relationships, Museum progress, Mine progression,
animals, and other completed systems.

My first check is:

1. load the save completely;
2. remain in the game while the first notifications appear;
3. open the Journal, Skills, Collections, and relationship menus;
4. sleep once;
5. compare the remaining Steam list with the save's actual progress.

I do not restart solely because the Steam page initially shows zero
achievements.

A restart can make calendar achievements slower because it removes the
relationships, ranks, Museum sets, Mine access, breeding progress, and
resources that an established save has already built.

I also do not assume that every unregistered achievement is broken. Some
conditions may be checked only after I revisit the related system, finish a new
day, or perform the triggering interaction again.

<h2 id="plan-long-achievements">
  Begin the achievements that contain real waiting time
</h2>

Several achievements cannot be finished efficiently through one late-game
grind.

I begin these while following the normal story:

- **Happy Birthday** — track birthdays from the beginning of the year.
- **Master Rancher** — begin breeding before I need the Tier 5 generation.
- **Hero** — check Stillwell's board every available Saturday.
- **Fish Fanatic** — keep seasonal and weather-dependent fish.
- **Entomologist** — catch seasonal and weather-dependent insects.
- **Celebrated Curator** — donate the first unfamiliar copy of every item.
- **Parent** — complete marriage before expecting family progression.
- **Time Flies** — allow enough time for the child to reach the first birthday.

I can earn more money, Essence, or skill experience on demand. I cannot recover
a missed season, birthday, festival, Saturday Market, or family milestone
without advancing the calendar.

My storage rule is therefore:

> The first unfamiliar copy is a Museum or progression item until I prove
> otherwise.

Before selling something rare, I check the Museum, active quests, Mine seals,
town repairs, crafting recipes, Stillwell missions, and gift records.

<h2 id="story-town-achievements">
  Complete the 24 story, access, and town-development achievements
</h2>

Most achievements in this group follow the natural progression route. The main
risk is confusing an unlocked project with a completed project.

### Opening story and town access

- **Welcome to Mistria** — finish the prologue.
- **Don't You Love That Sense of Achievement?** — repair Caldarus' Shrine.
- **Patron Of The Arts** — complete the first Museum set.
- **Becoming a Familiar Face** — meet every neighbor.
- **Bridge Builder** — repair the Eastern Road Bridge.
- **Market Regular** — greet the four original Saturday Market vendors.
- **New Pet Owner** — welcome the first pet to the farm.
- **Novice Farmer** — complete Restocking Mistria's Food Reserves.
- **On That Grind** — repair the Mill.

For **Patron Of The Arts**, I use a small seasonal forage or flower set instead
of waiting for a difficult artifact, fish, or insect collection.

For **Becoming a Familiar Face**, I use the map and relationship menu to identify
the missing person. Searching every building without checking the list wastes
most of the day.

**Market Regular** belongs to the original Saturday Market vendors. It is
different from the two later achievements for expanding the vendor roster.

### Copper Star and early upgrades

- **Summit Scaler** — complete the Copper Star town event that repairs the
  Summit stairs.
- **New Friend** — adopt the first ranching animal.
- **For the General Good** — complete the General Store upgrade.
- **A Bridge For Terithia** — repair the Beach Bridge.
- **Barnyard Bounty** — upgrade Hayden's Barn and Coop.
- **Novice Rancher** — complete Restocking Mistria's Food Reserves 2.
- **Horse-Hearted** — befriend the Mistmare.

**Summit Scaler** is not a separate Donation Box project.

After Copper Star, I attend the Manor House town-cleanup event. The group clears
earthquake damage and uses the remaining material to reopen the Summit stairs.

For **New Pet Owner** and **New Friend**, I keep the systems separate:

- a pet satisfies New Pet Owner;
- a farm animal satisfies New Friend.

Buying or welcoming one does not necessarily complete the other.

### Later areas and buildings

- **The Found Woods** — unlock the Deep Woods.
- **Inn Good Company** — complete the Sleeping Dragon Inn upgrade.
- **Refined** — build the Stone Refinery.
- **Fine Grained** — upgrade the Carpenter's Shop.
- **Good Marketing** — add the earlier pair of new Saturday Market vendors.
- **Hold Your A Plaza** — finish the later Plaza expansion that adds Stillwell
  and Zorel.
- **From a House to a Home** — fully upgrade the farmhouse.
- **Ringing Ovation** — repair the Bell Tower.

For **The Found Woods**, I complete the Fire Seal route, obtain Dragon's Breath,
and burn the magical barrier on the northern Eastern Road. Reaching the barrier
with a stronger Axe does not unlock the achievement.

The three Saturday Market achievements track different stages:

- **Market Regular** — meet the four original vendors.
- **Good Marketing** — complete the earlier vendor expansion.
- **Hold Your A Plaza** — complete the late Plaza project that brings Stillwell
  and Zorel.

The Plaza achievement is tied to completing the construction route, not to
buying every item sold by the two vendors.

<h2 id="relationship-achievements">
  Separate friendship, dating, marriage, and family achievements
</h2>

The relationship group contains 13 achievements:

- **Acquaintance** — reach Two Hearts with a villager.
- **Friend** — reach Four Hearts with a villager.
- **Good Friend** — reach Six Hearts with a villager.
- **Romantic** — reach Eight Hearts with an eligible villager and choose
  Dating.
- **Best Friend** — reach Eight Hearts and choose Best Friend.
- **Gifted** — give 100 Liked or Loved gifts.
- **Love Is In The Air** — attend the Shooting Star Festival with a date.
- **Happy Birthday** — give every villager a Liked or Loved birthday gift.
- **Thoughtful Gifter** — give one villager every item in their Liked and Loved
  gift lists.
- **True Love** — reach 10 Hearts with a villager I am dating.
- **I do!** — get married.
- **Parent** — have a child.
- **Time Flies** — reach the child's first birthday.

### Use different villagers for Romantic and Best Friend

The Eight Heart choice creates two separate achievement routes.

I use one intended marriage candidate for **Romantic** and another eligible
villager for **Best Friend**. That avoids changing the relationship path I want
to continue toward 10 Hearts.

Reaching Eight Hearts alone is not enough if the event still requires me to
choose between the two relationship outcomes.

### Combine Gifted with birthday and romance progress

I do not give 100 random items after every other relationship achievement is
finished.

I build **Gifted** naturally by:

- giving birthday gifts;
- progressing the intended spouse;
- filling a villager's Liked and Loved gift record;
- and maintaining other friendships.

A neutral gift does not help when the achievement specifically requires Liked
or Loved gifts.

### Start Happy Birthday in the first year

**Happy Birthday** is one of the most expensive achievements to postpone.

I track each villager's birthday and prepare at least one confirmed Liked or
Loved item in advance. Missing one person near the beginning of a season can
force me to wait almost another full year.

I do not test unknown gifts on the birthday itself. I use a known positive item
from the Journal.

### Choose one practical villager for Thoughtful Gifter

For **Thoughtful Gifter**, I focus on one villager instead of partially filling
every person's gift list.

I choose someone whose remaining Liked and Loved items:

- are already revealed;
- can be grown, cooked, bought, or gathered repeatedly;
- and do not consume unique quest materials.

I check the Journal after every gift so I do not repeat an item that has
already registered.

### True Love and I do! are different milestones

**True Love** requires reaching 10 Hearts while Dating.

It does not require a wedding.

**I do!** requires completing the marriage route through the ceremony. Reaching
10 Hearts, receiving the Engagement Ring Recipe, or becoming engaged does not
unlock it by itself.

<h2 id="skill-economy-achievements">
  Combine skills, Essence, money, ranching, and festival goals
</h2>

This group contains 12 achievements:

- **Master Blacksmith** — reach Level 60 in Blacksmithing.
- **Master Farmer** — reach Level 60 in Farming.
- **Master Woodcrafter** — reach Level 60 in Woodcrafting.
- **Master Chef** — reach Level 60 in Cooking.
- **Jack Of All Trades** — reach the maximum level in every skill.
- **Essential Essence** — earn 10,000 cumulative Essence.
- **Bread Winner** — earn 1,000,000 cumulative Tesserae.
- **Master Rancher** — breed the first Tier 5 animal.
- **Perk Procurer** — acquire a Tier 5 perk in every skill.
- **First Place** — take first place in a festival.
- **Close Call** — go to sleep after 1:50 a.m. but before 2:00 a.m.
- **Go To Sleep Already** — remain awake until the character faints.

### Use useful recipes for the Level 60 grinds

I do not craft large piles of items with no purpose solely for experience.

For each skill, I prioritize actions that also produce:

- town-repair materials;
- Museum items;
- profitable shipping;
- gifts;
- food for Mine runs;
- equipment;
- animal infrastructure;
- or ingredients needed by another skill.

When only one skill remains below the maximum, I stop repeating my normal
Farming routine and work directly on the missing category.

**Jack Of All Trades** should naturally be later than the individual Level 60
milestones.

### Essence and Tesserae are cumulative goals

For **Essential Essence**, I continue earning and spending Essence normally.
Purchasing perks does not make the past earned total useless for a cumulative
achievement.

For **Bread Winner**, I do not need to keep 1,000,000 Tesserae in the wallet at
once. I continue earning through normal crops, animals, cooking, crafting, and
shipping while buying permanent upgrades when they are useful.

I avoid selling unique Museum or quest items simply to accelerate the money
counter.

### Start Tier 5 animal breeding early

**Master Rancher** depends on animal generations rather than one expensive
purchase.

I begin the breeding line early enough that each generation can mature and
produce the next one while I am still completing the story and Museum.

I do not fill every building with unrelated animals before establishing the
male and female line needed for the chosen species.

### Save Essence for Tier 5 perks

**Perk Procurer** requires a Tier 5 perk in every skill.

Reaching the skill requirement is only one part of the route. I also need
enough Essence to purchase the perks.

Once the lower-tier perks are no longer urgent, I stop spending all Essence
immediately and reserve enough for the final tier across every skill.

### Use a festival with repeatable progress for First Place

The Spring Festival is a natural early attempt because Breath of Spring can
appear through farming, fishing, mining, and other daily activities after the
collection period begins.

I do not claim that one fixed Breath of Spring number guarantees first place.
The first-year result can depend on how early the event started and how much
normal activity I completed.

If I do not win, I continue the save and target another festival instead of
restarting the year.

### Complete Close Call and Go To Sleep Already on different nights

For **Close Call**, I stand beside the farmhouse bed before 1:50 a.m. and wait
until the required final window.

I do not begin the attempt in the Mines or Deep Woods, where travel time can
turn it into an unintended faint.

For **Go To Sleep Already**, I use a separate low-risk night. I empty valuable
items into storage and allow the character to remain awake until the automatic
faint.

<h2 id="museum-stillwell-achievements">

The final collection and mission group contains four achievements:

- **Fish Fanatic** — complete the Legendary Fish Set.
- **Entomologist** — complete the Legendary Insect Set.
- **Celebrated Curator** — complete the entire Museum.
- **Hero** — complete all of Stillwell's Mission Quests.

### Finish small Museum gaps before blind farming

I begin Museum cleanup from the Museum interface rather than choosing an
activity at random.

My order is:

1. inspect all four wings;
2. identify sets missing only one item;
3. check every storage chest;
4. separate seasonal outdoor targets from permanent Mine targets;
5. finish ordinary sets before repeatedly hunting Legendary items;
6. donate the first required copy before shipping or crafting with duplicates.

The Deep Woods Forage Set is a useful example of this approach. It contains
five items, so collecting the first copy during normal Deep Woods resource runs
removes an entire set from the final Museum cleanup.

### Check Stillwell every Saturday

Stillwell appears at the expanded Saturday Market after the Plaza project is
complete.

His Mission Board updates over time, so I check it on Saturdays before starting
a Mine run.

I combine missions with other unfinished goals:

- enemy targets with Monster drops;
- Mine-region targets with artifacts and forage;
- combat missions with Essence;
- and travel with missing Museum items.

I read the actual target and region before entering the Mines. A mission for
Upper Mines enemies does not become faster by starting from the deepest
checkpoint.

**Hero** belongs to completing all of Stillwell's Mission Quests, not merely:

- meeting Stillwell;
- finishing his first mission;
- buying an item from the market;
- or reaching a certain Town Rank.

<h2 id="final-cleanup">
  Use the remaining Steam list as a targeted checklist
</h2>

When the main story, town repairs, Mine seals, and marriage are complete, I stop
following one general daily routine.

I compare each remaining achievement with the system that controls it.

If the remaining list contains:

- **Town Rank achievements**, I finish requests, Museum sets, repairs, missions,
  and useful shipping.
- **Skill achievements**, I work only on the skill categories below their
  target.
- **Master Rancher**, I protect the active breeding line and avoid filling its
  building.
- **Perk Procurer**, I earn and reserve Essence.
- **Happy Birthday**, I follow the calendar rather than skipping days.
- **Fish Fanatic or Entomologist**, I wait for the correct season, weather,
  place, and time.
- **Celebrated Curator**, I inspect every Museum wing for the exact missing
  set.
- **Hero**, I return to Stillwell each Saturday.
- **Parent or Time Flies**, I continue the family calendar while completing the
  other remaining systems.

I do not sleep through weeks solely for one future event while other unfinished
achievements could be advancing on the same days.

The best 100% route ends with several systems moving together, not with a long
single-purpose grind that could have started a year earlier.

<h2 id="achievement-faq">
  Fields of Mistria achievements FAQ
</h2>

### How many achievements are in Fields of Mistria?

The current list contains **69 achievements**.

### Do I need a new save to unlock the 1.0 achievements?

No. An established save can receive credit for previously completed
conditions.

I load the save fully, revisit the relevant menus or systems, and complete
another day before assuming an old achievement cannot register.

### Are all 69 achievements guaranteed to be non-missable?

I do not treat every achievement as safely delayable.

Birthdays, festivals, seasons, weekly Stillwell missions, animal generations,
and family progression can create long waits even when they are still
technically possible later.

I begin those systems early instead of relying on a non-missable label.

### Can Romantic and Best Friend unlock on the same save?

Yes, by using different eligible villagers.

I choose Dating with the person I intend to continue toward marriage and Best
Friend with another villager.

### Does reaching 10 Hearts unlock I do!?

No.

Reaching 10 Hearts while dating unlocks **True Love**. **I do!** requires the
completed wedding.

### Is Parent the same as Time Flies?

No.

**Parent** unlocks when the child joins the household. **Time Flies** requires
reaching the child's first birthday later.

### Do I need one million Tesserae in my wallet for Bread Winner?

The achievement tracks cumulative earnings, so I continue buying useful
upgrades while working toward the total rather than holding every Tesserae.

### Does finding the Magic Key unlock Used The Magic Key?

No.

I must take the key to the Priestess Quarters and complete the interaction that
uses it.

### Why did the Bell Tower Donation Box not unlock Ringing Ovation?

A full Donation Box is not the final step.

I speak to Adeline and complete the Bell Tower repair scene. Ringing Ovation
belongs to the restored tower, not the filled box.

### What should I do when only Museum achievements remain?

I inspect every Museum wing, identify the exact unfinished sets, check storage,
and then match each missing item to its season, weather, location, Mine biome,
or collection method.

Random fishing, bug catching, or mining is inefficient when the missing item
belongs to a different condition.
