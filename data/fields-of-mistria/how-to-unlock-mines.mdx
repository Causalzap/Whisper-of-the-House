<h2 id="start-mines-quest">
  Attend the Museum meeting before trying to raise the mine gate
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

The Mines storyline begins with a message asking me to meet Adeline, Eiland,
and Errol at the Museum. I go to the Museum west of the central town area and
let the full discussion play out.

Eiland wants the Mines reopened because Mistria needs ore and the Museum needs
new artifacts. Errol refuses because the earthquake damaged the structure, the
elevator no longer works, and strange noises are coming from below.

The useful part of this scene is the condition Errol gives me:

> Mistria must reach at least **Stone Star Town Rank**.

That is the objective I follow. I do not keep walking back to the mine entrance
after every ordinary town quest, and I do not search for a missing key,
pickaxe upgrade, or repair bundle. None of those replaces the Town Rank gate.

<h2 id="raise-renown">
  Raise Renown through requests, Museum sets, and shipping
</h2>

The same meeting gives me three reliable ways to move the Town Rank bar:

- turn in items that complete Museum sets;
- complete requests from the town Request Board;
- and ship items from the bin beside the farmhouse.

I combine all three instead of grinding one activity.

My normal early-day route is:

1. check the Request Board when I enter town;
2. collect easy forage and request items while moving between buildings;
3. donate new Museum items before selling them;
4. finish requests that already match what I have in storage;
5. and ship duplicate crops, fish, forage, or other items overnight.

This route works because one trip can advance several systems. A flower may
finish a request, another copy may complete a Museum set, and the remaining
duplicates can be shipped for Renown and Tesserae.

I avoid donating or storing every duplicate indefinitely. If the Museum already
has the item and no active request needs it, shipping it helps the Town Rank
instead of leaving it in a chest with no immediate purpose.

I also avoid buying Saturday Market cosmetics just because a vendor quest is
active. Greeting the vendors can complete the relevant objective without
forcing me to spend money. Before the Mines open, I value Tesserae for inventory
space, food, and essential progression more than an early cosmetic purchase.

<h2 id="museum-set">
  Use an almost-complete Museum set instead of selling its last item
</h2>

The Museum is especially useful when a set is already close to completion.

In my early route, the **Spring Flower Set** becomes a practical Renown target
because its items can be collected while I am already walking between the farm,
town, and request locations. Completing the set also gives a separate reward
bundle rather than only the value of one shipped flower.

My decision is simple:

- when an item has the Museum donation marker, I donate the first copy;
- when it completes a nearly finished set, I prioritize the Museum trip;
- after the Museum has it, I keep only what an active request needs;
- then I ship ordinary duplicates.

I do not delay the entire Mines route for one stubborn set item. If a missing
item is rare, unavailable in the current weather, or taking several days to
appear, I continue with requests and shipping instead.

The goal is Stone Star, not perfect early-game Museum completion.

<h2 id="complete-requests">
  Choose requests that fit the route I am already taking
</h2>

The Request Board is strongest when I stop treating every posting as a separate
expedition.

I first accept the requests, then group them by location:

- town conversations and deliveries are done while visiting shops;
- forage requests are handled on the farm-to-town route;
- Museum-compatible items are donated before extra copies are delivered;
- and requests requiring an item I do not yet have are left behind the easier
  Renown gains.

The common trap is spending an entire day searching for one low-priority item
while several simple deliveries remain unfinished. When the Mines are my
current goal, I take the Renown that is already close to completion first.

I also check storage before buying a requested item. Early requests often ask
for materials or forage I already placed in a chest. A short return to the farm
can be cheaper than purchasing something I collected the previous day.

<h2 id="reach-stone-star">
  Claim Stone Star and read the Mines message before leaving home
</h2>

When the Renown bar reaches Stone Star, the rank notification confirms that the
Town Rank gate is complete.

The important follow-up appears in the mail or active messages. Errol confirms
that Mistria has reached Stone Star and asks me to meet him and Eiland at the
**Mine entrance west of town**.

I read that message before running to the entrance. Without it, it is easy to
reach the correct area and wonder why nobody is waiting there.

<h2 id="find-mines-entrance">
  Follow the marker to the Mine entrance west of town
</h2>

I open the Journal map, select the active Mines objective, and follow its marker
west of town.

I rely on the quest marker rather than searching every western path manually.
The Western Ruins and Narrows are separate destinations and do not trigger the
Mine reopening scene.

When I reach the correct entrance, Eiland and Errol are already waiting and the
conversation begins automatically.

<h2 id="reopen-mines">
  Finish the reopening scene, then inspect what is still broken
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_middle" />

At the entrance, Errol finally agrees to reopen the Mines. The cutscene also
explains why this is not a fully repaired facility:

- the earthquake exposed unknown passages;
- the main mine shaft has collapsed;
- the original elevator is not working;
- and a new dragon statue has appeared near the entrance.

I let this scene finish before leaving to prepare. Once the meeting is
complete, the Mines are unlocked; I do not have to descend on the same day.

That distinction matters when it is already late or my inventory is full. I can
trigger the reopening, learn the entrance location, then return the next morning
with better preparation.

<h2 id="prepare-first-mine-run">
  Buy inventory space when ten extra slots solve the real problem
</h2>

My first mine trip produces several different item types quickly:

- Copper Ore;
- insects;
- forage;
- monster materials;
- artifacts;
- treasure;
- and ordinary stone or environmental drops.

That makes inventory space more valuable here than it was while completing
simple town errands.

The first Bag Upgrade costs **1,000 Tesserae** and adds **10 inventory slots**.
I buy it before the first serious descent when I can afford it without blocking
an essential tool or leaving myself unable to buy basic food.

I do not treat it as a hard requirement. The Mines unlock without it.

When I cannot comfortably spend 1,000 Tesserae yet, I make a shorter first run:

1. empty unnecessary tools and town-delivery items at home;
2. bring the Pickaxe and Sword;
3. carry a small amount of food;
4. aim for the first elevator platform;
5. return after reaching Floor 5.

The upgrade is worth buying when the extra ten slots prevent me from throwing
away ore, Museum items, and monster drops. It is not worth delaying Stone Star
or the reopening scene just to purchase it in advance.

<h2 id="enter-by-ladder">
  Use the ladder because the main shaft and elevator are still disabled
</h2>

The collapsed main shaft is not another quest blocker. After reopening, I use
the available ladder to begin the descent.

The elevator beside the entrance is also not supposed to work immediately. I
restore access gradually by reaching platforms below.

A common false alarm is:

> “I reached Stone Star and completed the cutscene, but the elevator still does
> nothing.”

That is expected. Stone Star opens the Mines; it does not unlock every elevator
destination.

<h2 id="reach-floor-five">
  Make Floor 5 the first safe stopping point
</h2>

The opening tutorial explains that elevator platforms unlock every **five
floors**, so Floor 5 is my first permanent target.

On my early attempts, I repeatedly ran out of energy on Floors 2–4 because I
kept breaking ordinary rocks after the ladder had already appeared. I was
treating each room as a resource floor when the real objective was the first
checkpoint.

I changed the goal from “clear the floor” to “reach Floor 5”:

- I stop breaking ordinary rocks once the ladder appears;
- I collect visible Copper Ore without searching the whole room;
- I avoid enemies that are not blocking the route;
- I bring food instead of relying on underground forage;
- and I leave several inventory slots open before entering.

My priority order is:

1. reveal or find the next ladder;
2. collect obvious Copper Ore and new Museum items;
3. fight only what prevents me from advancing;
4. preserve enough energy and health for the final transition;
5. activate the Floor 5 elevator checkpoint.

The Floor 5 checkpoint matters more than one additional Copper node. Once it is
active, I can return for missed ore, insects, artifacts, and monster drops
without repeating the entire entrance route.

<h2 id="mines-not-opening">
  Fix a closed Mines entrance without repeating unrelated quests
</h2>

### The Museum scene says the Mines must stay closed

That is the first version of the quest, not a failure. I leave the Museum and
raise Mistria to Stone Star through requests, Museum sets, and shipping.

### I reached Stone Star, but nobody is at the entrance

I check my mail and active quest list for the message asking me to meet at the
Mine entrance west of town. I also confirm that I am following the active Mines
marker rather than entering the Western Ruins or Narrows.

### The Mines are open, but the elevator does not work

I use the ladder. The entrance elevator starts without recovered platforms and
gains destinations every five floors.

### I entered once and immediately ran out of inventory space

I empty town-quest items before returning and consider the 1,000-Tesserae Bag
Upgrade for ten additional slots. I do not carry the Hoe, Watering Can, Net, and
unrelated delivery items unless I have a specific reason to use them.

### I cannot reach Floor 5 before losing all my energy

I stop breaking rocks after the ladder appears, bring food, and treat Floor 5
as the only progression goal. Ore and Museum cleanup can wait until the first
elevator checkpoint is active.

for the tablet and offering route around Floor 20.
