Getting married in Fields of Mistria starts after I reach **10 Hearts** with a
villager I am already dating.

Eight Hearts opens the Dating or Best Friend choice. It does not unlock the
Engagement Ring, and repeatedly checking the Forge at eight or nine hearts will
not make the recipe appear.

After the tenth heart appears, I finish the day and sleep. Elsie visits the
farmhouse the following morning and gives me the **Engagement Ring Recipe**.

I craft the ring at the Forge, use it on my dating partner, complete their final
relationship scene, and then decide whether to propose. Accepting the proposal
opens a calendar with **10 available wedding dates**.

The days before the ceremony are not empty waiting days. I speak to my engaged
partner each day because the flower, cake, and meal choices are delivered
through separate conversations. In my route, skipping one of those
conversations meant I never received the meal choice before the wedding.

<h2 id="marriage-requirements">
  Start dating and reach 10 Hearts
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

The marriage route has two separate relationship gates:

- at Eight Hearts, I choose whether to Date or become Best Friends;
- at 10 Hearts, the Engagement Ring route becomes available.

A villager at 10 Hearts on the Best Friend route is not the same as a villager
I am dating. Before waiting for Elsie, I open the relationship menu and confirm
that the intended spouse is still marked as a romantic partner.

I also complete the available heart events instead of trying to force the final
heart through gifts alone.

The last heart moved more slowly than the earlier ones in my save. I continued
using the normal daily actions:

1. find the character on the map;
2. speak to them;
3. give a confirmed Liked or Loved gift;
4. use the kiss interaction when available;
5. invite them on a date when the option is available;
6. check the relationship display after the next morning begins.

The final increase happened after a date in my route.

I do not spend the entire day repeatedly clicking the same character after the
useful interactions are finished. Once I have talked, gifted, kissed, and dated,
I return to my normal progression and allow the next day to process the
relationship change.

<h2 id="elsie-engagement-ring">
  Sleep after reaching 10 Hearts and wait for Elsie
</h2>

Reaching 10 Hearts does not immediately place a ring or recipe in my inventory.

I finish the current day and sleep.

The following morning, Elsie visits the farmhouse. She explains that she has
noticed how close my partner and I have become and gives me the
**Engagement Ring Recipe**.

She gives me the recipe, not a finished ring.

Elsie also explains the purpose of the item: using the Engagement Ring invites
someone I am dating to discuss the future. Starting that discussion does not
mean I must complete the proposal.

If Elsie does not arrive, I check the actual progression state before skipping
several more days:

- the relationship must show 10 Hearts;
- the character must be on the Dating route;
- the available heart events must not be waiting unfinished;
- and I must sleep after the tenth heart appears.

I do not search Elsie's house, the Manor House, or the Saturday Market for a
purchasable Engagement Ring.

<h2 id="craft-engagement-ring">
  Craft the Engagement Ring at the Forge
</h2>

The Engagement Ring appears in the Forge after Elsie gives me the recipe.

The recipe panel confirms:

- the ring is used after reaching 10 Hearts with someone I am dating;
- the craft takes **30 in-game minutes**;
- and the completed item has a displayed sale value of **3,000 Tesserae**.

I do not use the sale value as a money-making plan. The ring exists to advance
the relationship route, and its late-game components are more valuable as
progression materials.

The supplied recipe screenshot shows the required component icons, but it does
not display every ingredient name clearly enough for me to identify them safely
from the image alone.

I therefore use this preparation order:

1. open the Engagement Ring recipe at the Forge;
2. inspect the current ingredient list directly in the game;
3. check every storage chest;
4. craft or collect only the missing components;
5. return to the Forge with all required materials;
6. leave at least one inventory slot for the completed ring.

I do not guess a component from its icon and spend several days farming the
wrong item.

<h2 id="use-engagement-ring">
  Use the ring and start the final relationship discussion
</h2>

I carry the completed Engagement Ring to the person I am dating.

The normal interaction menu now includes a discussion option connected to the
ring.

I speak normally first when I still want the day's regular dialogue, gift, or
relationship interaction. I then choose the discussion when I am ready to enter
the longer scene.

The location and dialogue are character-specific. In my Caldarus route, we
walked into the woods.

The scene is not only a short proposal prompt. It continues the character's
personal relationship story and can award a character-specific item.

Caldarus gave me the **Dragon-Sworn Statue** during his scene. That is specific
to his route and should not be treated as the universal reward for every
marriage candidate.

Most importantly, the game gives me several opportunities to stop before the
proposal is confirmed.

Using the ring does not immediately:

- choose the spouse;
- schedule the wedding;
- end every other dating relationship;
- or remove the ability to back out during the scene.

This lets me finish the conversation and see the character's final event before
making the permanent relationship decision.

<h2 id="confirm-proposal">
  Confirm the proposal only when I am ready to choose a spouse
</h2>

Near the end of the discussion, I receive the final proposal choice.

This is the point I treat as the commitment step.

After I confirmed the proposal in my save:

- Caldarus became my engaged partner;
- the wedding calendar appeared;
- other villagers began using engagement dialogue;
- and my other Dating relationships began changing.

On the following day, the other characters I had been dating sent breakup
letters and moved toward Best Friend status.

Fields of Mistria includes gameplay settings related to:

- breakup letters;
- follow-up breakup dialogue;
- and the Dating-to-Best-Friend status change after engagement.

I check those settings **before** confirming the proposal. Changing them after
the game has already processed the engagement cannot undo letters or status
changes that have occurred.

I also clear mailbox space before sleeping when I have been dating several
characters. Multiple relationship letters can arrive after the engagement.

<h2 id="choose-wedding-date">
  Choose a wedding date from the 10-day window
</h2>

After the proposal is accepted, the calendar opens and displays **10 available
wedding dates**.

I do not automatically choose the earliest option.

The days between the engagement and ceremony are used for wedding-planning
conversations. Choosing a date with only one free day in between leaves almost
no room to find the engaged partner and complete the customization dialogue.

My safer approach is to leave several full days.

Before confirming, I check:

- the Saturday Market;
- festivals;
- birthdays;
- seasonal deadlines;
- active requests;
- and any Mine or town project I do not want interrupted.

A wedding can take over the normal beginning of the selected day. I avoid
choosing a date when I still need a limited weekly vendor or event.

<h2 id="wedding-planning">
  Speak to the engaged partner every day before the wedding
</h2>

The flower, cake, and meal options do not appear in one large menu.

They are delivered through separate planning conversations with the person I
am marrying.

That makes the route easy to miss. The Journal did not place three obvious
wedding objectives on the map in my save. I had to find Caldarus and speak to
him on the available days.

My post-engagement routine is:

1. wake up;
2. check how many days remain;
3. locate the engaged partner on the map;
4. speak to them before entering the Mines or starting a long farm task;
5. complete any new planning dialogue;
6. repeat the next morning.

I do not assume that skipping a conversation only delays it.

In my route, I failed to speak to Caldarus on the first available planning day.
The flower and cake choices appeared later, but I reached the wedding without
ever choosing the meal.

The ceremony still proceeded, which means missing a choice does not necessarily
cancel the wedding. It can instead leave the final detail outside my control.

<h3 id="choose-wedding-meal">
  Do not miss the meal conversation
</h3>

The third customizable element is the reception meal.

I did not receive that choice because I skipped one planning day. I could not
recover it by repeatedly speaking to Caldarus on the final day before the
wedding.

The safest judgment is therefore:

> Do not leave all wedding conversations until the day before the ceremony.

I schedule several preparation days and speak to my engaged partner once each
day until flowers, cake, and meal have all been discussed.

<h2 id="before-wedding">
  Finish important work before the final sleep
</h2>

I treat the evening before the wedding as a save-preparation point.

Before sleeping, I:

- complete expiring requests;
- store Mine and Museum items;
- clear unnecessary inventory;
- bring animals inside;
- finish any daily gift I do not want to miss;
- and save the game.

I do not need to purchase wedding clothing beforehand.

That was an important decision in my route because I was worried that choosing
an early wedding date had left no Saturday Market between the engagement and
ceremony.

The game provides the wardrobe during the wedding event.

I also do not spend the final night deep in the Mines. The wedding begins the
next morning, so a late run provides little benefit and increases the chance of
ending the day unprepared.

<h2 id="wedding-day">
  Let the wedding event handle the morning chores
</h2>

The wedding begins automatically on the selected date.

A villager arrives at the farmhouse and explains that the town is helping with
the normal morning work. In my ceremony, the dialogue specifically covered the
animals being fed and watered.

I do not need to leave the event to find my partner or prepare the venue.

The event takes me to the Sleeping Dragon Inn, where the east guest room has
been prepared for getting ready. My future spouse prepares separately, so I do
not see their finished outfit until the ceremony.

The Engagement Ring does not need to remain in my active inventory on the
wedding day.

Once the proposal and date are confirmed, the scheduled event controls the
transition.

- veils;
- a top hat;
- Wedding Flower Clips;

- and multiple color choices.

I do not have to buy these pieces from a shop.

They become available during the ceremony preparation sequence. I can use a
traditional wedding style, choose another color, combine accessories, or ignore
the dedicated outfit pieces and style the character differently.

<h2 id="complete-wedding">
  Finish the ceremony, reception, and return home
</h2>

After leaving the Inn room, the wedding moves to the decorated town gazebo.

The selected flower color and cake appear as part of the event and reception.
The meal continued without my input because I had missed its planning
conversation.

The ceremony includes character-specific dialogue from the chosen spouse and
the formal joining sequence.

I let the full event continue through:

1. the ceremony;
2. the vows;
3. the town celebration;
4. the reception;
5. and the return-home sequence.

Reaching 10 Hearts only satisfies the separate **True Love** relationship
milestone. Becoming engaged is also not enough for I do!; the ceremony must be
completed.

<h2 id="after-marriage">
  Treat family progression as a separate optional route
</h2>

The wedding completes the marriage route.

It does not immediately require me to begin the child system, and having a
child is not necessary for the marriage achievement.

I keep this guide focused on:

- Dating;
- 10 Hearts;
- the Engagement Ring;
- the final partner event;
- the proposal;
- wedding planning;
- outfits;
- and the completed ceremony.

The later family-planning route, child arrival, and first birthday are separate
progression goals. I do not add an assumed countdown or untested child steps to
the end of the wedding guide.

<h2 id="marriage-problems">
  Fix the marriage step that has not triggered
</h2>

### I reached Eight Hearts but cannot propose

Eight Hearts unlocks the Dating or Best Friend decision.

I must choose Dating and continue to 10 Hearts before the Engagement Ring route
begins.

### I reached 10 Hearts but Elsie did not visit

I confirm that the character is marked as Dating and finish the current day.

Elsie delivers the recipe after I sleep following the tenth-heart requirement.

### Elsie visited, but I do not have an Engagement Ring

She gives me the recipe, not a completed ring.

I open the Forge and craft the Engagement Ring using the current displayed
requirements.

### I cannot identify the Engagement Ring ingredients

I inspect the recipe directly at the Forge.

The component icons in a screenshot are not clear enough to name every
ingredient safely without the in-game labels.

### Does using the ring immediately lock the marriage?

No.

The ring starts a final relationship discussion, and the scene provides several
opportunities to stop before confirming the proposal.

### Why did my other dating relationships change?

The default engagement behavior can move other Dating relationships toward
Best Friend status and generate breakup letters.

I review the related gameplay settings before confirming the proposal.

### How many wedding dates can I choose from?

The calendar displayed 10 available dates in my route.

I choose one with enough preparation days to complete the planning
conversations.

### Why did I not get to choose the meal?

The flower, cake, and meal choices occur through separate partner
conversations.

Skipping an available planning day can leave one choice unfinished before the
ceremony.

### Do I need to buy a wedding dress or tuxedo?

No.

The wedding event opens a preparation room at the Inn with dedicated dresses,
tuxedos, shoes, veils, headwear, accessories, and color options.

### Does reaching 10 Hearts unlock I do!?

No.

Ten Hearts while dating is the True Love milestone. I do! requires completing
the scheduled wedding ceremony.
