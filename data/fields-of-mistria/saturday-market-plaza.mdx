The Saturday Market Plaza is a late Fields of Mistria town project that adds
two more vendors: **Stillwell** and **Zorel**.

The project begins after I reach **Gold Star Town Rank** and receive Nora's
letter asking me to attend a meeting at the Manor House.

During the meeting, the town chooses the raised ground west of the Blacksmith
for the new Plaza. Adeline then places a Donation Box at the construction site.

The complete material list is:

- 50 Voidite;
- 20 Refined Stone;
- 5 Mistril Ingots;
- 10 Monster Cores;
- 5 Monster Blocks.

Filling the Donation Box is not the final step. I must find Adeline and tell her
that the materials are ready. Completing that conversation finishes the Plaza
quest, and Stillwell and Zorel appear at the following Saturday Market.

<h2 id="unlock-plaza">
  Reach Gold Star and read Nora's Saturday Market letter
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

The Saturday Market Plaza is not the same project as reopening the original
Saturday Market.

The earlier Eastern Road Bridge repair restores the normal Saturday vendors.
The Plaza is a later expansion that creates room for two additional applicants.

After I reach Gold Star Town Rank, Nora sends a letter explaining that the
Saturday Market has attracted more interest and asking me to attend a meeting
at the Manor House.

I read the letter before visiting the Manor House. Simply reaching Gold Star or
walking to the future Plaza site does not place the Donation Box.

quest.

<h2 id="plaza-meeting">
  Attend the Manor House meeting and choose the Plaza location
</h2>

At the Manor House, Nora explains that two applicants stand out for the new
Saturday Market spaces:

- Stillwell, a fortune teller who offers Mine-related Mission Quests;
- Zorel, a musician who sells Crystal Resonators and Song Crystals.

The town needs more space before either vendor can join.

The group chooses the raised area west of the Blacksmith. March and the others
have already considered cleaning up that land and turning it into a small
Plaza.

Adeline handles the project organization and places the Donation Box at the
new construction site.

The Donation Box tracks each category separately.

<h2 id="check-storage">
  Check storage before starting another Mine run
</h2>

I check every storage chest immediately after receiving the project.

An established save may already contain:

- Voidite from late Mine progression;
- Refined Stone left from earlier repairs;
- Mistril Ingots saved after tool or equipment upgrades;
- Monster Cores used by crafting recipes;
- and Monster Blocks collected without realizing they would become a quest
  material.

In my route, the main mistake was spending or decorating with Monster Cores
before checking the new construction requirements.

My preparation order is:

1. record the amount already in storage;
2. deposit completed categories;
3. identify the exact missing material;
4. enter only the Mine region that contains that target;
5. stop once the remaining requirement is complete.

I do not carry every Plaza item into the Mines. Completed categories stay in
storage or inside the Donation Box, leaving more inventory space for the current
target.

<h2 id="voidite-route">
  Farm 50 Voidite with the Floor 90 and 95 reset route
</h2>

Voidite is the largest numerical requirement, but it becomes manageable once I
stop clearing every late-Mine floor completely.

The fastest loop in my save used the elevator checkpoints around Floors 90 and
95:

1. take the elevator to Floor 90;
2. scan the room for visible Voidite;
3. collect the exposed nodes;
4. move to Floor 95;
5. scan and collect again;
6. return between the checkpoints to generate another room layout.

I collect visible Voidite first and leave as soon as the useful nodes are gone.

I do not break every ordinary rock or defeat every enemy after the target
material has been collected. The elevator makes resetting a weak layout cheaper
than clearing it.

When a room has no visible target, I leave immediately instead of spending
Stamina to prove that the floor is unproductive.

<h2 id="monster-block-route">
  Farm Monster Blocks near the Floor 85 checkpoint
</h2>

Monster Blocks come from block-shaped enemies in the late Mines.

They are enemy drops, not a construction item that I craft from ordinary
blocks.

I use the elevator checkpoint around Floor 85 because it provides a quick enemy
search and reset route:

1. take the elevator near Floor 85;
2. scan the room for block-shaped enemies;
3. defeat the visible targets;
4. collect the drops;
5. reset when no useful enemies remain.

One block enemy does not always produce one Monster Block.

During my route:

- some enemies dropped no Monster Block;
- one could drop a Monster Core instead;
- and several room resets contained too few useful targets.

I therefore treat Floor 85 as a practical enemy-reset point, not a fixed
five-Block room.

Once I have five Monster Blocks, I stop farming them even if more block enemies
are visible. The remaining Plaza materials use different routes.

<h2 id="monster-core-route">
  Farm Monster Cores from lantern enemies near Floor 15
</h2>

Monster Cores can appear during the late-Mine Monster Block route, but Floor 15
was more efficient when Cores became the only missing material.

I use this loop:

1. take the elevator near Floor 15;
2. scan for lantern enemies;
3. defeat the visible lanterns;
4. collect the drops;
5. move to a nearby floor or reset;
6. repeat until I have 10 usable Monster Cores.

Some enemies gave:

- Glass;

I check the inventory total after each reset. It is easy to continue fighting
after already reaching 10 because the drop notifications appear quickly.

<h2 id="stone-and-ingots">
  Prepare the Refined Stone and Mistril Ingots separately
</h2>

The normal construction materials are easier than the Mine drops, but they
still require the correct processed form.

For the Plaza I need:

- **20 Refined Stone**, not ordinary Stone;
- **5 Mistril Ingots**, not loose Mistril Ore.

I check storage and the relevant processing stations before mining more raw
material.

When I already have the required ore or stone, processing the finished items is
faster than returning to a Mine biome without checking the farm first.

I also prepare all five Mistril Ingots before spending Mistril on optional
equipment or decorations.

The Plaza does not accept partial raw ingredients in place of the finished
items.

<h2 id="donation-box">
  Deposit completed categories in the Plaza Donation Box
</h2>

The Donation Box appears at the construction site west of the Blacksmith after
the Manor House meeting.

I deposit each completed category as soon as it is ready.

This prevents me from:

- spending Mistril Ingots on another craft;
- using Monster Cores for decoration;
- confusing Voidite with other Void materials;
- or losing track of what remains in several storage chests.

The box accepts partial quantities, so I do not need all 50 Voidite or all 10
Monster Cores in one inventory transfer.

After depositing a category, I check the remaining totals before beginning
another Mine run.

The box itself is only a material tracker. Filling its final slot does not
automatically summon the construction group.

<h2 id="report-to-adeline">
  Find Adeline after the Donation Box is full
</h2>

Once all five categories are complete, the Journal still directs me to speak
with Adeline.

I open the map and find her current location.

I do not need to:

- wait beside the Plaza;
- sleep until the next morning;
- wait for Saturday;
- speak to Nora first;
- or remove anything from the Donation Box.

Adeline's daily location can change. The trigger is the conversation, not a
specific building or time of day.

After I tell her that the materials are ready, the town completes the Plaza.

<h2 id="zorel-music">
  Buy one Crystal Resonator before collecting Song Crystals
</h2>

Zorel sells two connected item types:

- **Crystal Resonators** for 20,000 Tesserae;
- **Song Crystals** for 5,000 Tesserae each.

A Song Crystal unlocks music, but I still need a placed Crystal Resonator to
play it.

For that reason, my first purchase is one Resonator rather than several songs
that I cannot yet use.

<h2 id="plaza-problems">
  Fix a Saturday Market Plaza quest that has stopped progressing
</h2>

### I reached Gold Star but did not receive the Plaza quest

I finish the current day, open all new mail, and check for Nora's letter.

I also confirm that I am not confusing the Plaza with the earlier Saturday
Market bridge or first vendor expansion.

### Where is the Saturday Market Plaza construction site?

The site is on the raised ground west of the Blacksmith.

It is separate from the original vendor stalls in the central town market.

### Can I use Mistril Ore instead of Mistril Ingots?

No.

The Donation Box requires five finished Mistril Ingots.

### Can Monster Blocks be crafted?

The Plaza Monster Blocks come from block-shaped enemies in the late Mines.

I farm them near the Floor 85 checkpoint rather than trying to craft them from
ordinary blocks.

### Why am I not getting a Monster Block from every enemy?

The drop is random.

A block-shaped enemy can give another item, a Monster Core, or no Monster Block.
I reset the Floor 85 route until five have dropped.

### What is the best Monster Core route for the Plaza?

Lantern enemies near the Floor 15 checkpoint worked well in my save.

The drop is not guaranteed, so I reset the route when the visible lanterns are
gone.

### Why is the Plaza not complete after filling the Donation Box?

A full Donation Box is not the final trigger.

I find Adeline on the map and tell her that the materials are ready.

### Why are Stillwell and Zorel not at the completed Plaza?

They join the Plaza during the next Saturday Market.

Completing the construction on another weekday does not make their stalls open
immediately.

### Should I buy Song Crystals or a Crystal Resonator first?

I buy one Crystal Resonator first.

Song Crystals unlock music, but the music needs a placed Resonator before it can
play.

### Does completing the Plaza immediately unlock the Bell Tower?

No.

The Plaza allows Zorel to join the Saturday Market. I introduce myself to her
and then wait for her later Bell Tower letter.
