The first Faelindral Ranger assignment sends you to three locations around
Evershade Weald, but reaching each place is only part of the quest. Progress
comes from examining the correct landmark, following the clue text, and
recording what you find in the Ranger's Log Book.

This is a scouting assignment rather than a clearing quest. Several enemies
around the objectives are dangerous for a new Ranger, so there is no reason to
fight deeper into an area once the required evidence has been recorded.

<section id="start-ranger-quest" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where do you start the Faelindral Ranger quest?

Go to **Ranger's Rest** and speak with **Captain Relgen Greenblade**.

He gives you the **Ranger's Log Book** and sends you to investigate three
locations:

- **the ruined archway near the Keeper's Bight border**
- **Spore Haven Karst**
- **Whispering Pools**

The Ranger's Log Book is a **Unique, No Drop container with 4 capacity**.
Keep it with the character for the entire scouting route because the evidence
from the three locations eventually needs to be combined inside it.

Greenblade's instructions focus on observation. When the objective area
contains enemies that are too strong, look for the clue before committing to a
fight. The quest advances through environmental interactions, not by killing
everything around the landmark.

</section>

<section id="ruined-archway" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Ruined Archway: find the arch beneath the footbridge

The first major trap is confusing the required archway with the larger ruins
nearby.

From Faelindral, move **south away from the river** and follow the main road
toward **Keeper's Bight**. Before crossing the border, look for the ancient
archway directly beneath a footbridge.

![Captain Greenblade's journal directions point toward the ancient archway beneath a footbridge near the Keeper's Bight border.](/images/monsters-and-memories/ranger-quest-ruined-archway.webp)

Once positioned at the archway, use:

`look`

This reveals unusual footprints around the old structure. Follow with:

`inspect footprints`

The trail leads away from the archway toward another set of ruins and a large
**Elder Spire tree**. Continue following the track clues there rather than
wandering through the larger ruins looking for another trigger.

The trail reveals signs of undead activity, including bone fragments, before
leading to another set of heavier three-toed prints. Inspect those tracks as
well. The final observation identifies **Orc tracks** and records the evidence
needed for this part of the assignment.

### Why the archway objective does not advance

The usual failure is standing at the nearby ruins instead of the arch beneath
the footbridge. Commands such as `inspect ruins` do not replace the first
interaction.

Return to the footbridge, stand by the ancient archway, and begin with `look`.
The footprints that appear from that interaction start the actual clue chain.

</section>

<section id="spore-haven" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Spore Haven Karst: inspect the foam and collect a sample

Spore Haven Karst lies in the damp cave system below Faelindral. The objective
is close enough to the river that there is no need to push deep into the
fungal area.

Follow the water until you see the **multicolored foam collecting against the
stone along the riverbank**.

![Multicolored foam collecting along the riverbank marks the Ranger quest clue inside Spore Haven Karst.](/images/monsters-and-memories/ranger-quest-strange-foam.webp)

Use:

`inspect foam`

The description connects the strange substance with conditions farther along
the waterway and warns against touching it directly. After inspecting it, use:

`rub sample`

This places a sample onto a blank page of the Ranger's Log Book and completes
the evidence step for Spore Haven Karst.

Check the Log Book before leaving. Once the entry has changed, there is no
quest reason to continue deeper into the cave.

</section>

<section id="whispering-pools" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Whispering Pools: check the wet footprints behind the waterfall

Greenblade points toward **the falls of the great river** for the Whispering
Pools investigation.

Move through the waterfall, but stay near the entrance. The required clue is
on the damp stone shortly after passing through the water rather than deep
inside the cave.

![Small wet footprints just beyond the waterfall provide the Whispering Pools evidence.](/images/monsters-and-memories/ranger-quest-whispering-pools-footprints.webp)

When the text draws attention to **small wet footprints**, use:

`inspect footprints`

The tracks are smaller than those left by the creatures guarding the area and
show that a nymph has moved beyond the waterfall barrier. That observation is
then recorded for the Ranger's Log Book.

Once the evidence is registered, turn back. Continuing farther inside adds
risk without advancing this assignment.

</section>

<section id="full-log-book" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How do you make the Full Ranger's Log Book?

Collecting all three pieces of evidence is not enough by itself. Captain
Greenblade will not accept the separate entries as the finished report.

After completing the archway, Spore Haven Karst, and Whispering Pools
investigations, open the **Ranger's Log Book** and make sure the completed
evidence is inside it.

Then **combine the contents**.

The result should become:

**Full Ranger's Log Book**

Return to Ranger's Rest only after the container has changed into the Full
Ranger's Log Book.

When Greenblade refuses the turn-in despite all three locations being visited,
check the book first. Repeating the route is unnecessary if the missing step
was simply combining the completed evidence.

</section>

<section id="keepers-cloak" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Initiate Keeper's Cloak reward and stats

Hand the **Full Ranger's Log Book** to Captain Relgen Greenblade to complete
the scouting assignment.

The reward is the **Initiate Keeper's Cloak**, a Ranger back-slot item with:

- **AC: 1**
- **STA: +1**
- **DEX: +1**
- **Slot: Back**
- **Class: Ranger**
- **Unique, No Drop**

![The Initiate Keeper's Cloak has AC 1, +1 Stamina, and +1 Dexterity.](/images/monsters-and-memories/initiate-keepers-cloak-stats.webp)

This is early equipment, so the individual stat increases are small, but the
cloak fills the back slot while adding both durability and Ranger-relevant
attributes.

Greenblade then moves into a different assignment built around enemy drops.
The next collection requires:

- **2 shattered undead skulls**
- **2 samples of Dryad Sap**
- **2 Fellstone Warrior's Belts**

That collection begins after the reconnaissance report is finished. Complete
the Full Ranger's Log Book and claim the cloak before treating those six items
as the next objective.

</section>

<section id="quest-not-advancing" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What should you check if the Ranger quest is not advancing?

Each part fails for a different reason, so check the objective that is actually
stuck:

- **Ruined Archway:** make sure you are beneath the footbridge, not at the
  larger ruins. Use `look`, then follow the footprint interactions.
- **Spore Haven Karst:** `inspect foam` first, then use `rub sample`.
- **Whispering Pools:** pass through the waterfall only far enough to find the
  wet footprints, then use `inspect footprints`.
- **Captain Greenblade refuses the turn-in:** place the completed evidence in
  the Ranger's Log Book and combine it into the **Full Ranger's Log Book**.

The safest route is to confirm the Log Book changes before leaving each
location. That prevents a missed command from turning into a full return trip
after reaching Ranger's Rest.

</section>
