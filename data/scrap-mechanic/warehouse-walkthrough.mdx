The story Warehouse was the longest combat route I had completed in Scrap
Mechanic 1.0 up to that point.

My objective marker placed it about **2.2 miles** from my base. I entered with
roughly **1,000 Spud rounds**, several types of food, a working vehicle, and a
regular Warehouse Key.

That preparation mattered because the route includes several floors of Tape
Bots, the Warehouse **Trash Bot** boss, a Warehouse Master Key door, the
Mining Site 01 story reveal, and a timed self-destruct escape.

The most important sequence correction is:

**Defeating the Trash Bot does not start the self-destruct timer.**

The countdown begins later, after I rescue Lorenzo, open the Master Key door,
inspect the secret area, and destroy the factory control panel with the Hammer.

<h2 id="warehouse-route">
  How I Navigate the Warehouse Floors
</h2>

I navigate using progression landmarks rather than trying to memorize every
office, storage area, and side room.

My repeating route is:

1. clear the current corridor;
2. look for painted arrows and Warehouse logos;
3. move through the next office or storage section;
4. find a door marked **Utility**;
5. follow the Utility route to an elevator;
6. ride upward and repeat.

</figure>

The layouts contain loops and optional rooms, but the story route continues
upward.

I keep my health near full before opening a new doorway. Tape Bots can react
quickly, and two nearly consecutive hits can end an otherwise controlled run.

My normal-floor combat rhythm is:

- listen for the Tape Bot cutting sound;
- check balconies, shelves, stairwells, and door corners;
- expose only a small part of the next room;
- use ranged attacks before crossing open space;
- reload before entering another stairwell;
- keep healing food on an accessible hotbar slot.

### The Return Chute I Mark Before the Boss

During the ascent, I find vertical Return Chutes that lead quickly toward the
exterior.

I identify one useful chute and remember:

- which side of the corridor contains it;
- which Utility room or elevator reconnects with it;
- where it releases me outside.

The boss sends patterns across the arena that can block a direct route.

Some incoming effects create or reveal openings through those barriers. I move
toward the usable gap instead of staying committed to one side of the room.

The head is the important damage target.

When it lights up or becomes exposed, I attack and then reposition. Late in my
fight, the damage window allowed only one clearly safe hit, so I stopped trying
to force a second attack.

Greedy damage attempts caused more trouble than the boss's total health.

My priorities are:

1. preserve enough health to survive one positioning mistake;
2. locate the next opening before attacking;
3. keep the boss and nearest safe route in view;
4. attack the exposed head;
5. disengage when the window closes;
6. heal before the next pattern begins.

I do not assign a universal number of hits because weapon choice and upgrades
can change the result.

The stable mechanic is the same: survive the pattern, use the opening, and
damage the exposed head.

<h2 id="recover-after-death">
  How I Recover My Backpack After Dying to the Trash Bot
</h2>

My first attempt ended after I lost the movement rhythm and died in the boss
area.

The difficult part was not only restarting the fight. The Warehouse was a long
drive from my base, and my food, ammunition, and weapons were sitting beside
the arena.

My recovery route was:

1. take another working vehicle back to the marked Warehouse;
2. bring food and enough ammunition to remove one blocking enemy;
3. retrace the arrows, Utility rooms, and elevators;
4. move cautiously through previously cleared corridors;
5. stop before the backpack and inspect the surrounding room;
6. remove the Tape Bot threatening the recovery route;
7. collect the backpack, restore the hotbar, heal, and then return to the boss.

Most previously cleared rooms were safer on the return, but I still found an
enemy near the dropped items.

I reached the backpack without enough health to survive another clean hit.
Instead of forcing the pickup, I retreated and found food first.

That decision prevented a second death.

For later attempts, I keep a small reserve outside the building:

- healing food;
- spare ammunition;
- fuel;
- another usable vehicle when available.

Those supplies should remain outside the Anti-theft area and away from the
eventual explosion zone.

</h2>

Defeating the Trash Bot does **not** start the countdown.

After collecting the story information and Plasma Drill unlock, I follow
Lorenzo into the hidden bot-production area.

1. reach the factory-control area;
2. finish Lorenzo's dialogue;
3. equip the Hammer;
4. destroy the control panel;

<h2 id="escape-warehouse">
  How I Escape Before the Warehouse Explodes
</h2>

Once the countdown begins, I return to the chute identified during the ascent.

I no longer search optional rooms or try to rediscover the entrance elevator.

My escape order is:

1. leave the factory-control room;
2. follow the cleared route toward the known Return Chute;
3. enter the chute and exit the Warehouse;
4. move the nearest important vehicle away from the building;
5. confirm that it is outside the danger zone;
6. move a second vehicle only when enough time remains.

The timer starts at **five minutes**.

In my run, I was already outside with approximately **1 minute 30 seconds**
remaining.

That gave me enough time to think about the second vehicle, but the decision
still depended on distance.

I prioritize:

1. my character reaching safety;
2. the vehicle carrying important supplies;
3. another useful vehicle already close to the exit;
4. optional objects and cosmetic loot.

The explosion is not only a visual ending.

The story Warehouse is destroyed and its original entrance is no longer a
repeatable farming route.
