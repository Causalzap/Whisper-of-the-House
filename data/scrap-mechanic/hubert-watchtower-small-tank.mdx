The hardest part of **rebuilding Hubert's Watchtower** was not placing the
hundreds of Wood and Concrete Blocks. It was finding the single **Small Tank**
required by the blueprint.

I searched several Outposts, checked Ruins, searched the Craftbot for both
“small” and “tank,” and scanned dozens of Schematic Boxes without unlocking a
recipe.

What finally worked was entering another intact Warehouse, finding the physical
Small Tanks near the lower ramp and elevator route, completing the Warehouse
unlock sequence, and taking one during the loot window.

<h2 id="why-small-tank-blocks-watchtower">
  Why the Small Tank Blocks Hubert's Watchtower Quest
</h2>

Hubert's Watchtower rebuild uses a fixed hologram rather than a simple delivery
box.

I cannot hand Hubert a pile of materials and let the structure complete
automatically. I have to bring the required parts to the build area and place
each one into its matching outline.

Most of the blueprint uses materials I can craft or collect in bulk. The Small
Tank is different because it may not appear in my Craftbot list when the quest
begins.

In my save, I had already:

- visited multiple Outposts;
- checked several Ruins and roadside structures;
- searched the Craftbot for “small”;
- searched it again for “tank”;
- scanned a large batch of Schematic Boxes;
- unlocked many unrelated decorative parts.

The Small Tank still did not appear.

for a physical part that I could remove from a Warehouse.

<h2 id="where-to-find-small-tank">
  Where I Find the Small Tank
</h2>

I find usable Small Tanks inside an **intact Warehouse**.

The Tank is a small cylindrical industrial object rather than one of the large
yellow or red storage vessels. From a distance, it is easy to dismiss it as
ordinary decoration.

My route through the Warehouse was:

1. enter with a regular Warehouse Key;
2. clear the early Tape Bots;
3. continue until I reconnect with the elevator and escape-hatch side of the
   interior;
4. move into the lower section;
5. look around the bottom of the ramp;
6. check the floor and wall edges for several matching Small Tanks.

The important landmark is the lower ramp near the elevator route.

I first found the correct object at approximately the bottom of that ramp.
There were additional Tanks nearby, including some close to the next elevator
and upper route.

That preparation matters because seeing the Tank is not enough.

<h2 id="when-to-return-for-small-tank">
  When I Return for the Small Tank
</h2>

Once the Warehouse unlocks its removable objects, I retrace the route to the
lower ramp.

This is why I clear the corridors and identify the Tank before triggering the
final sequence. I do not want to spend the loot window fighting enemies or
checking every floor again.

I prioritize the Small Tank over decorative objects.

My pickup order is:

1. return to the marked ramp;
2. take one Small Tank immediately;
3. confirm it entered my inventory;
4. take extra Tanks only when they are directly beside the route;
5. ignore bulky furniture until the quest item is secured;
6. leave with enough time to find the exit and move away from the Warehouse.

In my run, I left the Warehouse with four Small Tanks available in storage, but

I still take extras when they are beside my exit path. Repeating an entire
Warehouse only because I later need another Tank costs far more time than
using one extra inventory slot now.

I do not stay for another room when:

- the Small Tank is already secured;
- the timer is below two minutes;
- I no longer know the shortest exit;
- my inventory is full;
- or the Seekerbot is moving along the road outside.

<h2 id="watchtower-materials">
  What I Prepare Before Returning to Hubert
</h2>

The Watchtower is a real construction project, not a short interaction.

The tracker in my run called for one Small Tank slot and the following confirmed
bulk or structural pieces:

- 4 Shack Lights;
- 90 Punched Steel Blocks;
- 4 Shack Awnings;
- 520 Concrete Block 1;
- 3 Pipe Corners;
- 800 Wood Block 2;
- 17 Pipe Long pieces;
- 1 Air Conditioner.

I prepare the materials in three groups.

### I automate the bulk blocks

The **800 Wood Block 2** and **520 Concrete Block 1** are the main crafting
bottlenecks.

I queue those before driving back to Hubert. Waiting beside the tower while a
Craftbot produces hundreds of blocks wastes time and may force another trip
home.

### I craft the small fixed pieces

Shack Lights, Pipe Corners, Pipe Long pieces, and the Air Conditioner use much
smaller quantities.

I craft these after the bulk queues are running, then place them in one vehicle
chest so they do not disappear among food, seeds, ammunition, and random
Warehouse loot.

### I reuse decorative parts when practical

I already had more Punched Steel than the required 90, and I reused or collected
Shack Awnings instead of creating an unnecessary new farming route.

I salvage these when:

- the part is already sitting unused at my base;
- it does not break another active machine;
- it saves a long material queue;
- and I can still identify it correctly at the Watchtower.

I craft a fresh replacement when removing the old part would damage a working
vehicle, farm, or storage system.

<h2 id="rebuild-watchtower">
  How I Rebuild Hubert's Watchtower
</h2>

At Hubert's build area, I interact with the Watchtower project and follow the
blue hologram.

I build from the lowest stable section upward.

My order is:

1. place the base and large block sections;
2. complete the Wood and Concrete outlines;
3. add the Punched Steel sections;
4. place the Pipe Corners and Pipe Long pieces;
5. fit the Shack Awnings;
6. install the Air Conditioner;
7. place the Small Tank in its dedicated outline;
8. finish the Shack Lights and remaining small fittings.

The Small Tank has a specific mount. I do not drop it beside Hubert or attach it
where it merely looks reasonable.

is covered by pipes, lamps, and awnings.

<h2 id="watchtower-not-completing">
  Why the Watchtower Is Not Completing
</h2>

The project did not complete the moment the tower looked finished.

The final blockers were small pieces that were easy to overlook after the main
walls and platform were already in place.

If Hubert does not react, I inspect the hologram in this order:

### Check the inner shaft

A remaining shaft or Pipe piece can sit inside the tower where it is hidden by
the outer blocks.

I walk around the structure and look upward from underneath rather than only
viewing it from the front.

### Check blocks placed inside the outline

One block can occupy the correct general area without being on the exact
hologram square.

When a section looks misaligned, I remove the most recent block and place it
again from a clearer angle.

### Check the lamps last

The lamps appear late in the build and are easy to miss after the larger
structure looks complete.

I count every Shack Light rather than assuming the remaining blue outline is
part of the finished decoration.

### Check the Tank orientation

The Small Tank must snap into the designated mount.

I rotate it before placement instead of forcing it against a nearby wall or
platform.

### Check the tracker, not the appearance

A finished-looking tower can still have one outstanding object.

I keep the project tracker visible until Hubert begins his completion dialogue.

<h2 id="faq">
  Hubert Watchtower and Small Tank FAQ
</h2>

### How many Small Tanks should I take from the Warehouse?

The Watchtower uses one visible Small Tank mount, but I take extra Tanks when
they are beside my exit route. I left my run with four available, which avoids
repeating a Warehouse if another blueprint needs the same physical part.

### Does Hubert's reward prove that the Fire Extinguisher recipe is unlocked?

The confirmed reward is one Fire Extinguisher handed over after the Watchtower
completes. I still check the Craftbot separately before assuming the item became
a permanent crafting recipe.

### Why is one blue Watchtower outline still visible inside the structure?

A Pipe, shaft, or block can be hidden behind the finished outer wall. I inspect
the tower from underneath and from the opposite side, then remove the nearest
solid block temporarily if the blue outline remains inaccessible.
