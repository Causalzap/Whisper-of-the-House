The regular **Warehouse Key** has two practical sources in Scrap Mechanic 1.0.

For the main-story Warehouse, I continue **Lost and Found** until Hubert
identifies the old Warehouse and gives me a spare key. For an optional intact
Warehouse, I hunt Farmbots until one drops the same regular entrance key.

My biggest mistake was assuming that a Warehouse Master Key was an upgraded
version of the normal key. I reached an exterior door with a Master Key already
in my possession and was still stopped by:

`Requires warehouse key`

<h2 id="warehouse-key-types">
  Warehouse Key vs. Warehouse Master Key
</h2>

Scrap Mechanic uses two similarly named progression items:

- the **Warehouse Key** opens an exterior Warehouse entrance;
- the **Warehouse Master Key** is used at a deeper locked door after I am
  already inside.

I treat them as separate checks rather than two tiers of the same item.

The distinction became clear when I reached an ordinary Warehouse while already
carrying a Master Key. The exterior entrance still refused to open because I
did not have a regular Warehouse Key.

My rule is:

- **outside the Warehouse:** carry a regular Warehouse Key;
- **at the deeper locked interior door:** use the Warehouse Master Key.

If the exterior message still says `Requires warehouse key`, I stop searching
for another button or repeatedly reloading the area. The wrong key type is not a
door bug.

<h2 id="hubert-story-key">
  How I Get the Story Warehouse Key From Hubert
</h2>

My reliable story route begins with **Lost and Found**.

I finish the recorder objective, listen to the messages, and return to Hubert.
Simply finding a Warehouse in the world does not trigger the key conversation.

Hubert first identifies the old Warehouse as the likely location of the missing
mechanic. He also warns that the building contains dangerous Tape Bots and that
a Hammer with a lightly supplied Spud Gun will not be enough.

I continue the full conversation.

Near the end, Hubert explains that one of the farmers had a spare Warehouse
Key and gives it to me.

If Hubert only discusses farming, deliveries, or another active task, I check
the story state in this order:

1. confirm that Lost and Found is active;
2. finish every required recorder interaction;
3. let the recorder dialogue complete;
4. wait for the objective to update;
5. return to Hubert;
6. continue his full conversation about the missing mechanic.

I do not switch immediately to random Farmbot hunting when the story key should
still be available through Hubert.

<h2 id="choose-warehouse-and-prepare">
  Which Warehouse I Enter and When I Return to Base
</h2>

The Warehouse closest to Hubert is not automatically the story destination.

After receiving the spare key, my active objective pointed to a different
Warehouse roughly **two miles away**. The nearby building was visible, but the
quest marker was sending me elsewhere.

  </figcaption>
</figure>

I make the route decision before driving:

- **Hubert gave me the spare key and a Warehouse is marked:** follow that
  marker;
- **I am exploring without a story Warehouse marker:** use a Farmbot key on
  another intact Warehouse;
- **the marked destination is far away and my supplies are incomplete:** return
  to base first;
- **a Farmbot fight consumed most of my ammunition:** restock before attempting
  the building.

Getting the key is not the same as being ready to enter.

Hubert specifically warns me to bring food and a large amount of Spuds. For my
long story run, I waited until I had about **1,000 rounds** and several food
options.

That was my safe loadout, not a universal door requirement.

Before leaving for the marked Warehouse, I check:

1. the regular Warehouse Key is in my carried inventory;
2. the active marker matches the destination;
3. I have ranged ammunition for the interior enemies;
4. I have more than one healing option;
5. the vehicle has enough fuel for the trip;
6. a Bed or another practical respawn point is available nearby;
7. emergency food and ammunition can remain outside the entrance.

A Warehouse is not a quick room where I can collect one item and immediately
leave. The story route contains several combat areas, a deeper lock, a Boss
sequence, and a timed escape.

<h2 id="farmbot-key-drop">
  How I Get Warehouse Keys From Farmbots
</h2>

Farmbots can drop regular Warehouse Keys, but I do not treat the first kill as
a guaranteed drop.

One Farmbot I defeated near a Warehouse gave me no key. A later Farmbot dropped
the actual entrance item.

I bring enough ammunition for more than one attempt.

A failed first drop can otherwise leave me with:

- no Warehouse Key;
- too few Spuds for another Farmbot;
- low healing supplies;
- a damaged vehicle;
- no realistic way to clear the Warehouse afterward.

I continue hunting while the vehicle, food, and ranged ammunition remain
healthy.

I stop after obtaining one key when another fight would consume the supplies
needed for the Warehouse itself. Collecting a second key is less valuable than
preserving the vehicle and loadout required to use the first one.

<h2 id="door-still-locked">
  Why the Warehouse Door Still Says “Requires Warehouse Key”
</h2>

I run four checks in order.

### 1. The correct key is in my carried inventory

The exterior entrance needs the regular Warehouse Key described above.

A key stored in a vehicle chest is not being carried when I interact with the
door. I move one regular key into my personal inventory and try again.

### 2. I am standing at the marked story Warehouse

For Lost and Found, I compare the building with the active quest marker.

The intended destination may be much farther away than the intact Warehouse
closest to Hubert.

### 3. The Farmbot actually dropped a key

I check the ground pickup and my inventory.

Killing a Farmbot does not prove that the key dropped. When no item appeared, I
need another Farmbot or the Hubert story reward.

### 4. The building is still intact

The story Warehouse does not return to its original reusable state after the
self-destruct sequence.

When the exterior has already exploded and the entrance is blocked, I use
another intact Warehouse for later exploration instead of trying to reopen the
destroyed story building.

These checks cover different failures. Repeatedly interacting with the same
door does not change the key type, inventory location, quest destination, or
state of the building.

<h2 id="faq">
  Warehouse Key Edge Cases
</h2>

### Can a Farmbot inside a Grow Lab drop a Warehouse Key?

Yes. My confirmed Farmbot key drop occurred during a Grow Lab run rather than
directly outside a Warehouse.

The enemy type matters more than one fixed Warehouse landmark, so I still check
the pickup whenever I defeat a Farmbot in another combat area.

### Can I re-enter the story Warehouse after it explodes?

Not through its original entrance.

After the self-destruct sequence, the story building is damaged and its former
entrance is blocked. For repeat exploration, another Small Tank, or optional
Warehouse materials, I use a different intact Warehouse.
