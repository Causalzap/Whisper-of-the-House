I do not chase them in Steam-list order. I finish the short Builder and
Challenge objectives first, begin every long counter during normal Survival
progression, and leave the Vault, Schematic, outfit, Mining Hub, and final
completion achievements for late-game cleanup.

trigger, location, interaction, or completion state. Achievements whose
descriptions name a hidden target without revealing its location remain in the
complete checklist until I have a repeatable route.

<h2 id="secret-achievements">
  Does Scrap Mechanic Have Secret Achievements?
</h2>

Steam currently exposes the names and basic requirements of all 34
achievements.

I therefore do not label an achievement as officially secret unless I can
verify that Steam actually hides it.

Some visible descriptions still conceal the practical route. Achievements such
as Huberts high friction mustache, New best friend, Friendly revival, and Still
in one piece name the target without giving me a location, starting landmark,
or completion route.

I include those official requirements in the complete checklist below. I do not
fill the missing route with an unverified compass direction or another player's
save-specific coordinate.

<h2 id="master-of-defence">
  How I Prepare for Master of Defence
</h2>

**Requirement:** Successfully defend crops against a Level 5 raid.

I do not build the largest possible farm immediately. I increase the crop value
in controlled steps and check the raid-level indicator before the wave locks
in.

My preparation checklist is:

- finish the outer wall before planting;
- keep repair blocks in the hotbar;
- leave a clear firing lane around the crops;
- keep the main vehicle away from the farm entrance;
- carry enough food and ranged ammunition for the complete wave;
- confirm that the UI specifically displays a Level 5 raid.

I stop adding crops as soon as Level 5 appears.

Going beyond the required level only increases the pressure without improving
the achievement condition.

During the defense, I prioritize enemies already attacking the outer wall
instead of chasing every Bot away from the farm. A successful defense matters
more than clearing the surrounding field quickly.

I wait for both the raid completion state and the Steam notification before
harvesting the protected crops.

### Bot splat

The creation must get the kill.

I use a low, stable vehicle against a weak Totebot in open terrain. A damaging
front attachment gives me a clearer result than clipping the target with an
ordinary wheel.

I leave enough space to turn around. A large vehicle that becomes stuck after
the first impact makes the setup harder than the achievement itself.

### Wet circuits

I throw water on a living Haybot before finishing it.

Using water after the Haybot has already been destroyed does not test the
required interaction.

I isolate one Haybot, apply the water, wait for the visible reaction, and then
finish the fight.

### Farmbot smackdown

I fight a Farmbot from open ground with ranged ammunition and a vehicle ready
for retreat.

A Farmbot can also drop a regular Warehouse Key, so the same fight can advance

### Surviving the impossible

I treat the Seekerbot scan as the warning phase.

When it begins tracking near a road, I move toward solid cover instead of
remaining beside an exposed vehicle. Food stays in an immediately usable slot,
and I continue moving until the attack sequence ends.

A permanent early base directly beside a major road makes repeated Seekerbot

### Lethal snack

The official condition is:

`Destroy two Blue Tapebots with a Cornade`

I take that wording literally.

My controlled setup is:

1. clear unrelated enemies;
2. keep two Blue Tapebots alive and visible;
3. weaken them only when necessary;
4. bring both into one Cornade blast area;
5. throw after they are close together;
6. confirm that both Blue Tapebots die.

I do not substitute a normal Tapebot for the second Blue Tapebot.

### Questionable behaviour

I move one Woc and an explosive canister away from my farm, fuel storage,
vehicle, and important machinery.

The achievement setup is inexpensive. Rebuilding damaged infrastructure is
not.

### Pizza Burger chow down

I equip the **Pizza Burger shirt first**, confirm that it is visible on my
character, and only then eat a **Pizza Burger**.

I keep the food in inventory until the outfit is definitely active. Eating the
food first and changing clothes afterward does not reproduce the simultaneous
condition.

### The Stuntman

Steam does not state the exact speed threshold.

I use:

- a lightweight chassis;
- a powerful engine;
- a normal Seat;
- stable steering;
- a long straight road;
- no heavy mining cargo.

I accelerate while seated rather than launching an empty creation while
standing outside it.

<h2 id="multiplayer-achievements">
  How I Handle Achievements in Multiplayer
</h2>

I do not assume that every action completed by the host automatically gives the
same achievement to every client.

My safest multiplayer rule is to let the player seeking the achievement perform
the final interaction.

That player should:

- deliver the final crop or farmer;
- throw the water or Cornade;
- wear the shirt and eat the Pizza Burger;
- get revived by the Baby Woc;
- open the final chest;
- activate the final Station;
- complete the decisive quest interaction.

For long counters, each player should track personal progress instead of
assuming that the host's kills, harvested crops, or crafted blocks are shared.

When the host unlocks an achievement and a client does not, I repeat the final
trigger with the client performing the action. If it still fails, I test that
trigger in the client's own save before restarting an entire counter.

This is a conservative troubleshooting method. I do not assume that every
achievement uses the same multiplayer ownership rule.

<h2 id="achievement-not-unlocking">
  Why an Achievement Is Not Unlocking
</h2>

### I completed a related action, but not the exact condition

I read the wording literally.

Examples:

- opening one Mining Hub floor is not activating all Stations;
- rescuing the Baby Woc is not being revived by it;
- building in the Garage is not sharing on the Steam Workshop;
- reaching 10,000 in the Vault is far below 2,000,000;
- killing one Blue Tapebot and one normal Tapebot is not two Blue Tapebots.

### The final state did not register

I wait for at least one visible confirmation:

- Steam achievement notification;
- quest completion text;
- objective update;
- collection slot changing;
- counter completion;
- final NPC dialogue.

Leaving when an activity merely looks complete makes later diagnosis harder.

### The wrong player performed the final action

In multiplayer, I let the player seeking the achievement perform the decisive
interaction.

This is especially important for deliveries, combination achievements,
revivals, final Stations, and quest-completion interactions.

### The estimated counter is inaccurate

For 100 kills, 500 crops, 50 defenses, and 10,000 crafted blocks, I count
conservatively.

A handwritten estimate may include the wrong enemy, a failed defense, crops
that were planted but not harvested, or blocks that were collected rather than
crafted.

### The game version is old

When a combination achievement repeatedly fails, I update the game before
rebuilding the entire setup.

I do not assume that every attempt made on an older version will register
retroactively after a patch.

<h2 id="faq">
  Scrap Mechanic Achievement FAQ
</h2>

### Does Scrap Mechanic have secret achievements?

Steam currently exposes the names and basic requirements of all 34
achievements. Some still hide their practical route or location, but I do not
label them officially secret without a verified Steam hidden flag.

### Do all players receive the same achievement in multiplayer?

I do not rely on automatic shared credit. The safest approach is for the player
seeking the achievement to perform the final interaction, delivery, kill,
revival, or Station activation.

### Are all 0.1% achievements extremely difficult?

No. The achievement set is still new, and many players have not reached the
later systems. Some 0.1% achievements are long grinds, while others are short
combinations or hidden-location tasks.

### Does Growlab champion require the Carrot Seed?

No. The achievement is tied to completing the first Grow Lab. The Carrot Seed
is a separate progression pickup that is easy to miss before reaching the final
Large Chest.

### Does Schematic collector club require every Craftbot recipe?

The official condition specifies all **block Schematics**. I track unknown
block recipes separately from tools, furniture, consumables, and recipes locked
only by Craftbot level.

### Can I spend Vault Credits while working on Bank mechanic?

I keep the Vault balance intact while working toward 2,000,000. The requirement
refers to reaching that Vault value, so withdrawing Credits creates additional
value I must deposit again.

### Does Spectacular Mechanic need a separate trigger?

No. It is the final achievement for obtaining every other achievement. When it
does not appear, I identify which of the other 33 achievements is still
missing.
