The first Grow Lab is an early Scrap Mechanic 1.0 story dungeon tied to
Hubert's quest line.

My clear was not difficult because of one complicated puzzle. The real problems
were entering without a vehicle Bed, carrying too little healing food, losing
track of the vertical route, and assuming that every recipe pickup meant the
Lab was already complete.

The actual completion sequence was:

1. follow **A Farmers Side Hustle**;
2. enter the marked Grow Lab;
3. clear the connected combat rooms;
4. collect the Net Block unlock and Schematic Boxes;
5. hit the red-marked target to open the linked door;
6. collect the Carrot Seeds before leaving the final combat area;
7. open the Large Chest;
8. enter the passage behind it;
9. drop into the plant-filled chamber;
10. wait for the Main Quest completion banner.

<h2 id="how-long">
  How Long the First Grow Lab Took Me
</h2>

My blind clear took roughly **50 minutes** from setting out for the Lab to
receiving the final quest-complete banner.

That total included:

- entering without a vehicle Bed;
- running short of healing food;
- dying inside;
- returning to recover my dropped bag;
- leaving again to collect more Corn;
- checking side paths for loot;
- learning the vertical route without a map.

For a prepared attempt, I would reserve at least **30–45 minutes**.

A player who already understands the room order and ignores optional loot may
finish faster, but the route becomes much slower when food or ammunition runs
out halfway through.

<h2 id="what-to-bring">
  What I Bring Before Entering
</h2>

My minimum loadout is:

- a vehicle with a Bed attached;
- a reliable Hammer;
- a ranged weapon and enough ammunition;
- several stacks of healing food;
- one empty inventory section for Schematic Boxes and other loot;
- enough fuel to make the return trip;
- a beacon on the vehicle if it is easy to lose among the terrain.

The Bed is the most important preparation item.

Without one, death sends me back to an older respawn point instead of the Grow
Lab entrance. That turns a short recovery into another full drive across the
map.

### How much healing food I needed

One piece of Corn restored only a small amount of health during this early
run.

Recovering from a nearly empty health bar could take about **20 pieces of
Corn**, which is too slow while ranged projectiles are still crossing the room.

I now separate healing into two groups:

- quick food for active combat;
- bulk Corn for healing between rooms.

I also avoid using crops that are reserved for Farmer deliveries unless the
alternative is losing the entire run.

My navigation rule is:

1. clear the current room;
2. collect visible loot before dropping to a lower level;
3. remember whether I entered the chamber from above or below;
4. check for newly opened doors before repeating an old corridor;
5. use the position of the central machinery as a height reference;
6. avoid jumping down until I know I can return or continue forward.

The route is easier when I treat each height as a separate floor rather than
trying to remember the Lab as one flat maze.

My combat priority is:

1. ranged Bots with a clear line of sight;
2. enemies already blocking the next doorway;
3. close-range Bots that have entered striking distance;
4. remaining enemies that can be pulled into a safer corridor.

I avoid healing in the middle of an open platform. Eating large amounts of
Corn is practical between rooms, but it is too slow when projectiles are still
incoming.

Narrow corridors are useful because they prevent several enemies from
surrounding me at once. I retreat through a cleared doorway instead of running
blindly into the next unexplored room.

<h2 id="recover-dropped-bag">
  How I Recovered My Dropped Bag
</h2>

My first run failed because I entered with too little food and no Bed on the
vehicle.

Rather than repeating that explanation in every preparation section, this is
the recovery route I used:

1. respawn at the previous available Bed;
2. follow the Grow Lab quest marker back to the structure;
3. find the parked vehicle before entering;
4. call the entrance elevator again;
5. follow only the rooms I had already cleared;
6. use the dropped-bag marker instead of searching every side room;
7. recover the bag before starting another fight;
8. check the restored food and ammunition immediately;
9. leave the Lab again if the recovered supply is still too low;
10. install a vehicle Bed before continuing the run.

The important decision is step nine.

Recovering the bag does not automatically mean I am ready to continue. If the
same shortage caused the death, returning immediately to the same room only
repeats the failure.

The last part of my route was:

1. collect the Carrot Seeds;
2. clear the nearby group of Bots;
3. continue to the Large Chest;
4. open the chest;
5. enter the passage behind it;

This distinction only applies to the first Grow Lab route documented here. It
does not prove that every later Grow Lab uses the same ending.

<h2 id="troubleshooting">
  First Grow Lab Troubleshooting
</h2>

### I reached the Grow Lab, but the entrance will not progress

Check the active quest.

For the first story Lab, my route came through **A Farmers Side Hustle** and
the Hubert objective. Reaching a similar structure before the correct quest
state may not reproduce the same entry sequence.

### Do I need a Grow Lab key for the first Lab?

I did not use a separately collected overworld key for this first story visit.
The active Hubert quest provided the progression state needed to enter.

Later Grow Labs use their own key progression.

### I keep returning to the central chamber

Check height, not only direction.

The Lab reconnects through upper and lower paths. A chamber that looks familiar
may be the same landmark viewed from a different floor.

Return to the central machinery, identify the level you entered from, and then
check for a newly opened door.

### The red-target door is still closed

Hit the red-marked target itself with the Hammer.

After the hit, return to the linked door on the correct level. Do not assume
every nearby door uses the same target.

### I collected a Schematic Box, but nothing unlocked

A Schematic Box is physical loot that must be processed later.

Picking it up does not automatically reveal every recipe, open a nearby door,
or complete the Grow Lab.

### I unlocked Net Block, but the quest is still active

That is normal.

Net Block is an intermediate progression unlock. Continue through the Lab
until you open the Large Chest, enter the passage behind it, drop into the
plant chamber, and receive the Main Quest completion banner.

### I opened the Large Chest, but the quest did not complete

Do not stop at the chest.

Enter the passage behind it and continue into the plant-filled section. My
quest completed after I dropped into that final area.

### I completed the Lab but did not get Carrot Seeds

The seeds are collected before the final Large Chest.

Backtrack through the late combat section and check the ground around the
defeated Bots. The chest itself did not supply my Carrot Seeds.

### I died and my bag is deep inside

Follow the dropped-bag marker through rooms you already cleared.

Recover the bag before starting new fights, then check whether the restored
food and ammunition are enough to continue. Leaving for more supplies is safer
than repeating the same death.

<h2 id="faq">
  First Grow Lab FAQ
</h2>

### What quest starts the first Grow Lab?

My route started through the in-game quest titled **A Farmers Side Hustle**.
The first displayed objective asked me to find out what Hubert was doing and
talk to him.

### How long does the first Grow Lab take?

My blind attempt took roughly 50 minutes because I died, recovered my bag, and
left to collect more food. I would reserve about 30–45 minutes for a prepared
first clear.

### Does the first Grow Lab have a Boss?

I did not encounter a named Boss or Boss health bar. The late section contained
several Bots, followed by the Large Chest and the final drop into the plant
chamber.

### Are the Carrot Seeds inside the Large Chest?

No. I collected the Carrot Seeds from the late combat section before reaching
the Large Chest.

### Does opening the Large Chest complete the quest?

Not immediately in my run. I opened the chest, entered the passage behind it,
dropped into the plant-filled chamber, and then received the Main Quest
completion banner.

### Should I bring a vehicle Bed?

Yes. It was the most important item I omitted. A Bed near the Grow Lab entrance
turns a death into a short recovery instead of another long drive from an older
respawn point.

### Do Schematic Boxes immediately unlock Craftbot recipes?

No. I collected them as physical loot and processed them later. A Schematic Box
pickup is separate from direct recipe notifications such as the Net Block
unlock.
