The first Grow Lab—often searched as **Growlab 1**—is an early Scrap
Mechanic 1.0 story dungeon tied to Hubert's quest line.

The main route is not difficult because of one complicated puzzle. The common
blockers are losing track of the vertical floors, missing the red-marked door
target, leaving the Carrot Seeds behind, and stopping at the Large Chest before
the quest actually completes.

The full completion sequence is:

1. follow **A Farmers Side Hustle**;
2. enter the marked Grow Lab;
3. clear the connected combat rooms;
4. collect the Net Block unlock and Schematic Boxes;
5. hit the red-marked target to open the linked door;
6. collect the Carrot Seeds before leaving the late combat section;
7. open the Large Chest;
8. enter the passage behind it;
9. drop into the plant-filled chamber;
10. wait for the Main Quest completion banner.

### Do I need a Grow Lab key for the first Lab?

I did not need to find a separate overworld key for this first story visit. I
followed the active Hubert objective and used the entrance provided by that
quest state.

Later Grow Labs are different. Hubert explains after the first clear that the
other keys were lost when the Bots took over, so finishing Grow Lab 1 does not
open every later Grow Lab immediately.

<h2 id="entering-the-lab">
  How to Enter Grow Lab 1
</h2>

I follow the story marker to the Grow Lab structure and park near the entrance
without blocking the elevator or doorway.

The entrance elevator is not instantaneous. I call it, wait for it to reach the
platform, and use it to enter the main interior route.

My navigation rule is:

1. clear the current room;
2. collect visible loot before dropping to a lower level;
3. remember whether I entered the chamber from above or below;
4. check for newly opened doors before repeating an old corridor;
5. use the position of the central machinery as a height reference;
6. avoid jumping down until I know I can return or continue forward.

The route is easier when I treat each height as a separate floor instead of
trying to remember the Lab as one flat maze.

The important progression pickups appear along this route, but neither the Net
Block notification nor a Schematic Box means the dungeon is finished. I keep
moving until I reach the red-target door, the Carrot Seed section, and the final
Large Chest.

3. whether I returned to the correct linked door on the correct level.

<h2 id="boss">
  Does the First Grow Lab Have a Boss?
</h2>

I did not encounter a named Boss, a Boss health bar, or a generator-triggered
multi-wave arena in this first story Grow Lab.

There was combat near the end, but my route finished with the Large Chest and
the drop into the plant-filled chamber rather than a separate named Boss fight.

This only describes the first Grow Lab documented here. It does not prove that
every later Grow Lab ends in the same way.

<h2 id="what-to-bring">
  What to Bring Before Entering Grow Lab 1
</h2>

My minimum loadout is:

- a vehicle with a Bed attached;
- a reliable Hammer;
- a ranged weapon and enough ammunition;
- several stacks of healing food;
- one empty inventory section for Schematic Boxes and other loot;
- enough fuel to make the return trip;
- a beacon on the vehicle if it is easy to lose among the terrain.

The Bed is the most important preparation item.

Without one, death sends me back to an older respawn point instead of the Grow
Lab entrance. That turns a short recovery into another full drive across the
map.

### How much healing food I needed

One piece of Corn restored only a small amount of health during this early run.
Recovering from a nearly empty health bar could take about **20 pieces of Corn**,
which is too slow while ranged projectiles are still crossing the room.

I separate healing into two groups:

- quick food for active combat;
- bulk Corn for healing between rooms.

I also avoid using crops reserved for Farmer deliveries unless the alternative
is losing the entire run.

My combat priority is:

1. ranged Bots with a clear line of sight;
2. enemies blocking the next doorway;
3. close-range Bots already within striking distance;
4. remaining enemies that can be pulled into a safer corridor.

I avoid healing in the middle of an open platform. Bulk Corn is practical
between rooms, but it is too slow while projectiles are still incoming.

Narrow corridors help prevent several enemies from surrounding me at once. I
retreat through a cleared doorway instead of running blindly into the next
unexplored room.

<h2 id="recover-dropped-bag">
  How to Recover a Dropped Bag Inside the Grow Lab
</h2>

My first run failed because I entered with too little food and no Bed on the
vehicle.

This is the recovery route I used:

1. respawn at the previous available Bed;
2. follow the Grow Lab quest marker back to the structure;
3. find the parked vehicle before entering;
4. call the entrance elevator again;
5. follow only the rooms I had already cleared;
6. use the dropped-bag marker instead of checking every side room;
7. recover the bag before starting another fight;
8. check the restored food and ammunition immediately;
9. leave the Lab again if the recovered supply is still too low;
10. install a vehicle Bed before continuing the run.

The important decision is step nine. Recovering the bag does not automatically
mean I am ready to continue. If the same shortage caused the death, returning
to the same room immediately only repeats the failure.

<h2 id="how-long">
  How Long the First Grow Lab Takes
</h2>

My blind clear took roughly **50 minutes** from setting out for the Lab to
receiving the final quest-complete banner.

That included:

- entering without a vehicle Bed;
- running short of healing food;
- dying inside;
- returning to recover my dropped bag;
- leaving again to collect more Corn;
- checking side paths for loot;
- learning the vertical route without a map.

For a prepared attempt, I would reserve at least **30–45 minutes**.

A player who already understands the room order and ignores optional loot may
finish faster, but the route becomes much slower when food or ammunition runs
out halfway through.

<h2 id="what-completion-unlocks">

After the quest completes, Hubert explains that other Grow Lab keys were lost

That makes the first Lab both:

- a self-contained early dungeon;

The first clear does not automatically give me access to every later Lab.
Future progression still depends on:

- finding or receiving later Grow Lab keys;
- completing the relevant Farmer supply tier;

<h2 id="troubleshooting">
  First Grow Lab Troubleshooting
</h2>

### The Grow Lab entrance will not progress

Check the active quest. My first story visit came through **A Farmers Side
Hustle** and the Hubert objective. Reaching a similar structure before the
correct quest state may not reproduce the same entry sequence.

### I keep returning to the central chamber

Check height, not only direction. The Lab reconnects through upper and lower
paths, so a familiar chamber may be the same landmark viewed from a different
floor.

### The red-target door is still closed

Hit the red-marked target itself with the Hammer, then return to the linked door
on the correct level.

### I collected a Schematic Box, but nothing unlocked

A Schematic Box is physical loot that must be processed later. Picking one up
does not automatically reveal every recipe, open a nearby door, or complete the
Grow Lab.

### I unlocked Net Block, but the quest is still active

That is normal. Continue until you open the Large Chest, enter the passage
behind it, drop into the plant chamber, and receive the Main Quest completion
banner.

### I opened the Large Chest, but the quest did not complete

Do not stop at the chest. Enter the passage behind it and continue into the
plant-filled section.

### I completed the Lab but did not get Carrot Seeds

Backtrack through the late combat section and check the ground around the
defeated Bots. The Large Chest did not supply my Carrot Seeds.

### I died and my bag is deep inside

Follow the dropped-bag marker through rooms you already cleared. Recover the
bag before starting new fights, then check whether the restored food and
ammunition are enough to continue.

<h2 id="faq">
  First Grow Lab FAQ
</h2>

### What quest starts the first Grow Lab?

**A Farmers Side Hustle**. Talk to Hubert, then follow the active objective to
the marked Grow Lab.

### Do I need a key for the first Grow Lab?

I did not need a separate overworld key for this first story visit. Later Grow
Labs use their own key progression.

### Where are the Carrot Seeds?

They are in the late combat section before the final Large Chest. Check the
ground near the defeated Bots.

### Does opening the Large Chest complete the quest?

No. Continue through the passage behind the chest and drop into the plant-filled
chamber. The completion banner appears after entering that final area.

### Does the first Grow Lab have a Boss?

I did not encounter a named Boss or visible Boss health bar. The route ended
with several Bots, the Large Chest, and the final drop.

### How long does the first Grow Lab take?

I would reserve about **30–45 minutes** for a prepared clear. My blind attempt
took roughly 50 minutes because it included a death, recovery, and extra supply
run.

### Should I bring a vehicle Bed?

Yes. A Bed near the entrance turns a death into a short recovery instead of
another long drive from an older respawn point.

### Do Schematic Boxes immediately unlock Craftbot recipes?

No. They are physical loot that must be processed later and are separate from
direct recipe notifications such as the Net Block unlock.
