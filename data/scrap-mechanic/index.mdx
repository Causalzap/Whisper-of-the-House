Scrap Mechanic 1.0 progression is easier to understand when I separate it into
three connected routes:

- the **story route**, which moves through Grow Labs, Hubert, and the marked
  Warehouse;
- the **underground route**, which begins with Mining Site 01 and the Plasma
  Drill;
- the **long-term route**, which includes Vault Quotas, Watchtower construction,
  optional Warehouses, machine upgrades, and achievements.

This hub is organized around the point where I am currently stuck.

It does not repeat the complete Warehouse, mining, ore-processing, or
achievement solutions. Each detailed blocker has its own guide.

<h2 id="progression-order">
  My Scrap Mechanic 1.0 Progression Order
</h2>

I use the following order as a route map, not as a complete walkthrough.

### 1. Establish the early crop and Grow Lab loop

The first Grow Lab teaches the basic pattern of bringing the correct key,
preparing for an enclosed combat area, completing the objective, checking the
reward, and leaving with enough supplies to continue.

I use the dedicated [First Grow Lab walkthrough](/scrap-mechanic/first-grow-lab-walkthrough) for the full route.

### 2. Continue Hubert's story until the Warehouse objective appears

Lost and Found eventually points me toward the old story Warehouse.

At this stage, the important blocker is usually not combat. It is obtaining the
regular Warehouse Key and following the correct objective marker.

The [Warehouse Key guide](/scrap-mechanic/warehouse-key) handles that access
step.

### 3. Complete the marked story Warehouse

The story building contains the Trash Bot encounter, Lorenzo, the Warehouse
Master Key route, the planetary survey map, and the factory sequence.

This is a one-time story run, so I use the [complete Warehouse walkthrough](/scrap-mechanic/warehouse-walkthrough) rather than treating it like an ordinary repeatable resource location.

### 4. Follow the Mining Site 01 objective

The Warehouse survey map reveals the underground Gyro Core route and provides
the Plasma Drill progression needed for the next stage.

The [Mining Site 01 guide](/scrap-mechanic/mining-guide) begins at that
transition.

### 5. Separate extraction from processing

The Plasma Drill produces physical Ore, but that object is not finished
progression value.

Extraction belongs to the mining vehicle page. The Crushbot, Prospector,
Mining Hub intake, Caster, Ingots, and Vault belong to the
[ore processing guide](/scrap-mechanic/ore-processing-guide).

Keeping those two jobs separate makes troubleshooting much easier.

### 6. Return to side projects and completion goals

Hubert's Watchtower and the achievement checklist become easier after I have
better transport, stronger material production, more ammunition, and access to
intact Warehouses.

I do not need to finish every optional build before beginning Mining Site 01.

When an interaction fails, I check three things before assuming the game is
bugged:

1. the item has the exact required name;
2. it is in my carried inventory rather than vehicle storage;
3. the correct quest state and destination are active.

The regular and Master Key details remain on the Warehouse Key page rather than
being repeated here.

### Reaching an object does not always mean it is usable

Scrap Mechanic often lets me see a destination, machine, or physical part
before the progression state allows me to use it.

Examples include:

- a Small Tank visible while Warehouse Anti-theft protection is still active;
- Mining Site 01 displayed on the survey map before I reach its access route;
- deeper mine floors visible before the required progression unlocks them;
- protected Warehouse objects visible before the final factory sequence.

My first check is therefore quest state, not tool choice.

Hammering, welding, scanning, reconnecting, or rebuilding an object will not
remove a deliberate progression lock.

### The active marker matters more than the nearest landmark

A nearby Warehouse or Grow Lab is not automatically the current story
destination.

When a quest provides a marker, I follow that marker first.

Unmarked intact Warehouses are still useful for optional materials, additional
Small Tanks, and later exploration, but they do not replace the marked story
sequence.

### I use different vehicles for different jobs

One vehicle does not need to solve every part of the update.

My roles are:

- **story travel vehicle:** fuel, storage, food, ammunition, and a safe return
  route;
- **mining vehicle:** Plasma Drill clearance, Battery supply, traction, ore
  collection, and recovery blocks;
- **Ingot hauler:** low loading height, stable suspension, balanced weight, and
  simple steering;
- **construction carrier:** organized chests for bulk blocks and fixed quest
  parts.

Trying to keep every drill, weapon, Ingot, building block, and decorative part
on one chassis creates unnecessary weight and makes failures harder to
diagnose.

### Later Grow Labs use the same preparation logic

The first Grow Lab has its own walkthrough because it establishes the basic
route and early progression pattern.

For later numbered Labs, I currently use a compact rule set:

1. confirm the active Lab number and matching key;
2. place the respawn point before the long trip;
3. carry food and ranged ammunition;
4. keep enough empty inventory space for the crop or story reward;
5. inspect enemy drops before leaving;
6. verify the next crop, key, or objective update at the end.

A later Grow Lab only needs a separate solution when it introduces a unique
route, interaction lock, boss mechanic, or reward that cannot be explained by
this shared pattern.

<h2 id="trip-check">
  My Universal Long-Trip Check
</h2>

Before a distant Grow Lab, Warehouse, mining floor, or construction delivery, I
check the same small set of failure points.

### Access

- the correct key or card is in my carried inventory;
- the active objective points to the destination;
- the location has not already entered a destroyed or completed state.

### Survival

- healing food is on the hotbar;
- ranged ammunition covers the route and return;
- fuel or Batteries include a reserve;
- a Bed or practical respawn point is available;
- a second vehicle or emergency supply cache exists for difficult recovery.

### Transport

- storage has enough free space for the expected reward;
- heavy physical objects have a stable hauling platform;
- the Lift and spare blocks are available for vehicle recovery;
- important supplies are not left inside a system that may become locked.

### Exit

- the vehicle is parked facing a practical return direction;
- I recognize the closest elevator, chute, or road;
- optional loot will not delay a timed or one-time objective;
- the most important quest item is collected before decorative parts.

This checklist stays broad on the Hub.

Exact quantities and route-specific preparation remain inside the relevant
Spoke.

<h2 id="faq">
  Cross-System Edge Cases
</h2>

### Can I continue the main story if I leave the Warehouse without a Small Tank?

Yes.

The Small Tank is needed for Hubert's Watchtower project, not for the Mining
Site 01 survey-map reveal or Plasma Drill progression.

I can continue the underground story route and return to the Small Tank problem
later. Because the marked story Warehouse is destroyed, I use another intact
Warehouse when I need to obtain the physical part afterward.

The complete recovery route is in the
[Watchtower and Small Tank guide](/scrap-mechanic/hubert-watchtower-small-tank).

### Can an optional intact Warehouse replace the destroyed story Warehouse?

Not for the Lorenzo and Mining Site 01 story sequence.

That progression belongs to the marked Warehouse reached through Hubert's
objective.

Other intact Warehouses can still be useful for optional exploration, protected
interior parts, additional Small Tanks, and resource runs. They do not replay
the original story reveal or restore the exploded building.
