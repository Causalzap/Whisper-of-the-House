Start with the **Turret** or **Autocannon** to establish automatic asteroid destruction. Prioritize the **Tractor Beam** once your guns release enough minerals to make ore recovery worthwhile. The Engineering Terminal in **No Asteroids** offers nine ship guns, two supporting systems, and four upgrade categories. Every configuration has to fit within your unlocked mounting slots, available budget, and the amount of time your ship can attack during a mining shift.

### How Should You Compare Ship Gun Stats?

Hover over a weapon in Engineering to inspect its Damage, Radius, and Rate.

- **Damage** indicates the strength of an attack.
- **Radius** indicates the area affected.
- **Rate** indicates how frequently the weapon attacks.

Do not compare weapons using Damage alone. A weapon with frequent small attacks serves a different purpose from one that creates a large blast at longer intervals.

For deep excavation, also consider whether the weapon penetrates rock or creates a shaft. For broad destruction, compare its affected area and attack frequency.

The displayed stats reflect your current Engineering upgrades, so compare weapons at the same upgrade levels.

## Which Ship Guns Should You Buy First?

The Turret is an inexpensive starting point when Engineering first becomes available.

You do not need to wait until you can afford advanced weapons before beginning ship-based destruction.

A useful early ship configuration is:

- One affordable Turret or Autocannon.
- Mineral recovery through the Tractor Beam when you can afford it.
- Shared gun upgrades that improve the equipment already installed.
- Additional weapons or mounting slots as your income grows.

### Should You Save for the Autocannon?

At $500, the Turret gives you an inexpensive way to start automatic excavation. The $8,000 Autocannon becomes a more practical target when another productive shift can cover the difference.

Several days of saving are harder to justify when your ship has no installed weapons. Compare the money you still need with your typical earnings per shift before delaying your first installation.

### Should You Skip Early Guns and Save for the Collapsar?

Saving directly for the Collapsar is a much larger commitment.

Unless your income is already substantial, spending many shifts without improving automatic destruction can make progression unnecessarily slow.

Establish productive ship equipment first. Advanced weapons become easier to afford when your existing mining operation is already generating a reliable income.

For improving the manual mining side of that income, see the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades">best mining tools and upgrades</Link>.

## Why Are Your Ship Guns Losing Ore?

Watch what happens to the minerals released by your ship's attacks. Without a **Tractor Beam**, that ore floats away instead of entering the Ship Hold. Your asteroid destruction counter can therefore increase substantially while automatic mining contributes little to your mineral income.

### How Do You Get the Tractor Beam?

Open the Systems tab in Engineering and select the Tractor Beam.

The Tractor Beam collects minerals released by your ship guns and transfers them into the Ship Hold. It also redirects overflow from your Lander when its Cargo Hold cannot accommodate additional minerals.

Check the current purchase price in the Engineering Terminal before buying it.

### Is the Tractor Beam Worth Buying?

Prioritize the Tractor Beam before another expensive gun if your existing weapons already release substantial amounts of ore.

It can also be valuable when your handheld mining equipment fills the Lander Cargo Hold early in a shift.

If you are still using inexpensive guns and rarely fill your Lander, improving your basic mining equipment may provide a better immediate return.

As both manual and automatic mineral production increase, the Tractor Beam becomes a more useful investment.

### Ship Hold vs Lander Cargo Hold

The Ship Hold and Lander Cargo Hold have separate capacity limits and upgrade paths.

Upgrade the Cargo Hold through Lander Service and the Ship Hold through Engineering.

With the Tractor Beam installed, watch both storage indicators during a shift. A full Lander does not necessarily mean that all available mineral storage has been exhausted.

If the Ship Hold repeatedly reaches its limit, improve your mineral storage before investing in equipment that releases even more ore.

The Compactor also applies to the Ship Hold. If recovered minerals regularly fill your storage, compare the next Ship Hold upgrade with your available <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#make-money">Compactor upgrades</Link>.

### Firepower, Caliber or Rate of Fire?

Start by inspecting your installed weapons in Engineering.

If most of your guns have small impact areas, consider Caliber. If their attacks lack sufficient strength against the remaining material, consider Firepower.

For weapons that attack at long intervals, Rate of Fire may help them perform more attacks during each shift.

After purchasing an upgrade, compare the affected weapon stats and your destruction progress over subsequent shifts.

Do not assume that the same attribute provides the best return for every weapon combination.

### Should You Upgrade Existing Guns or Buy Another One?

Shared upgrades become especially attractive when you already have several weapons installed.

One purchase can improve the performance of multiple guns. That can make a relatively affordable attribute upgrade more useful than saving for a single expensive replacement.

Buy another weapon when you want a different attack effect, have available mounting capacity, or can make a substantial improvement over the current configuration.

When comparing the options, inspect both the cost of the next shared upgrade and the cost of purchasing and installing another weapon.

## How Do You Unlock More Ship Gun Slots?

Your ship has a limited number of mounting slots for Engineering modules.

Additional slots require a separate purchase. Slot 2 costs $4,000, while Slot 4 costs $90,000 and Slot 5 costs $250,000.

Buying a new gun does not automatically provide another mounting location.

Open the Engineering Terminal and inspect the available slots before purchasing another weapon.

If you do not have a free slot, you can still purchase a weapon. It goes into storage instead of being installed immediately.

Unlock another mounting slot or remove an existing module, then install the stored weapon before starting your next shift.

### Is It Worth Unlocking Every Slot?

Not immediately.

An empty slot does not destroy rock or generate income on its own. Its value depends on what you can install afterward.

Consider opening another slot when:

- You can also afford a useful weapon or module.
- Removing existing equipment would significantly reduce your current performance.
- You want to add an Air Generator without replacing an important gun.
- The next available shared upgrade is less attractive than expanding your loadout.

The Engineering panel provides six numbered mounting slots. The later slots can require substantial investment, so account for the combined cost of unlocking the slot and purchasing its equipment.

Opening every ship slot is also required for **Armed to the teeth**, which you can track in the <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements">No Asteroids achievements checklist</Link>.

### Can You Remove and Reinstall Ship Guns?

You can remove installed weapons through Engineering and install equipment in available compatible slots.

This allows you to change your configuration as your priorities change.

For example, you may replace an early Turret with a stronger weapon, or remove an Air Generator when you want another gun contributing to asteroid destruction.

Unwanted ship modules can also be sold. Compare the available sale value with the cost of your planned replacement before removing useful equipment.

Always check the resulting configuration before starting your next shift.

## Is It Worth Buying the Same Ship Gun Twice?

You can install multiple copies of the same ship gun, but purchasing duplicates increases the cost.

For example, a second Laser Beam costs $625,000 instead of $250,000, while a second Collapsar costs $3,000,000 instead of $1,200,000.

Both second purchases cost 2.5 times their initial price. Check the displayed price before purchasing further copies.

Before buying another copy of an expensive weapon, compare three options:

1. The cost of purchasing the duplicate.
2. The cost of adding a different weapon you do not yet own.
3. The cost of upgrading all your existing guns.

A mixed loadout can be considerably easier to establish than repeatedly purchasing the same expensive gun.

That does not mean duplicates are a bad investment. Once you have substantial income and most shared upgrades are already completed, another copy of a powerful weapon may become a reasonable choice.

Check the current purchase price rather than assuming a fixed price increase applies to every weapon.

## Air Generator vs Another Ship Gun

The **Air Generator** trades one mounting slot for 30 additional seconds of air per shift. It removes no rock directly, but the extra operating time benefits both your handheld mining and every installed ship gun.

The important comparison is whether that additional time contributes more destruction than the weapon you would otherwise install in the same slot.

### When Is the Air Generator Worth Using?

Choose the Air Generator when oxygen consistently forces you to finish a shift while you still have productive mining available.

It can be particularly useful when you are trying to complete a demanding mineral order, excavate a distant deposit, or continue working with an advanced handheld tool.

An extra 30 seconds also gives your installed ship guns more time to operate during the shift.

However, the additional operating time must be weighed against the destruction lost if you remove a gun to make room for the Air Generator.

### When Should You Replace the Air Generator?

Consider replacing the Air Generator when your existing oxygen supply is already sufficient and another ship gun would contribute more destruction during each shift.

Check three things before removing it:

- Whether you regularly finish your mining objectives with oxygen remaining.
- Whether additional mining time still produces useful minerals or asteroid destruction.
- Whether another gun would compensate for the shorter operating time of your existing weapons.

A full Lander Cargo Hold alone is not a reason to remove the Air Generator, especially if the Tractor Beam can still recover overflow into the Ship Hold.

If additional oxygen remains useful, consider opening another slot instead.

For other ways to increase your available oxygen, check <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#suit-upgrades">suit upgrades</Link> and <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#drones">drone upgrades</Link>.

Instead of relying entirely on individual shells or separated strikes, it maintains a cutting attack against the asteroid.

### Laser Beam or Rockets?

Rockets cost $100,000 for the first purchase, while the Laser Beam costs $250,000. They also destroy material in different ways.

The Laser Beam is suited to sustained cutting, while Rockets deliver repeated explosive attacks.

If you already have several weapons that rely on individual impacts, adding a Laser Beam introduces a different attack pattern to your configuration.

If you have enough slots and income, you can use both rather than treating them as mutually exclusive choices.

There is also a considerable difference between the ship-mounted Laser Beam and the handheld Laser. Purchasing one does not replace the other.

<section id="collapsar" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Is the Collapsar Worth Buying in No Asteroids?

The Collapsar is the most expensive ship gun in the Engineering weapon selection.

It fires a singularity that consumes surrounding rock, making it particularly attractive when you need to remove large portions of the asteroid.

The initial listed purchase price is $1,200,000.

It is a major investment compared with earlier ship weapons, so it becomes easier to justify when your mining operation and automatic mineral recovery already generate substantial income.

### When Should You Buy the Collapsar?

Consider buying the Collapsar when you meet most of these conditions:

- Your current equipment produces a reliable income.
- You have an available mounting slot or a weaker module worth replacing.
- Your ship already contributes meaningful automated destruction.
- The purchase will not leave an essential part of your mining operation severely underdeveloped.
- Enough asteroid remains for the weapon to provide useful destruction.

The Collapsar can be particularly valuable when continuing into larger asteroid contracts, where expensive equipment has more remaining work to perform.

Purchasing an Orbital Strike or Collapsar also unlocks the Lance and Slicer in the Workshop. See the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#mining-tools">late-game mining tools</Link> before deciding how to spend your next equipment budget.

### Should You Buy the Collapsar Before Upgrading Your Other Guns?

Not necessarily.

The Collapsar provides a different destruction effect, while shared Engineering upgrades improve the weapons already installed.

If several of your guns have relatively low shared upgrade levels, improving them may be a more affordable immediate investment.

If your current guns already perform well and you want to expand their destructive capability, adding the Collapsar becomes more attractive.

Avoid judging the purchase entirely by its price or by the total asteroid destruction achieved during one shift. Handheld tools, drones, other ship guns, and existing Engineering upgrades also contribute to that result.

</section>

<section id="best-loadout" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Is the Best Ship Gun Loadout?

Choose a configuration that fits your unlocked slots and current budget. You can replace individual modules as your mining operation develops without rebuilding the entire ship.

### Early Ship Loadout

For a ship with only one or two available mounting slots, start with one Turret or Autocannon.

Once you have established automatic gunfire, prioritize ore recovery and open another slot when you can afford equipment to install in it.

If oxygen is still limiting your mining shifts, an Air Generator is another option to consider.

### Mid-Game Ship Loadout

A possible configuration for three occupied mounting slots is:

- Air Generator
- Autocannon
- Rockets

This configuration combines additional mining time with two different automatic attack patterns.

If your oxygen supply is already sufficient, consider replacing the Air Generator with another weapon instead.

Keep enough money available for ore recovery and shared Engineering upgrades.

### Late-Game Ship Loadout

A mixed high-end configuration can combine a Collapsar, Laser Beam, and Rockets without immediately purchasing expensive duplicate weapons.

For a fully developed ship with all six mounting slots open, another possible configuration is:

- Slot 1: Air Generator
- Slot 2: Laser Beam
- Slot 3: Collapsar
- Slot 4: Collapsar
- Slot 5: Rockets
- Slot 6: Orbital Strike

This configuration is particularly expensive because it includes a second Collapsar. Establish the single-Collapsar version first if you cannot comfortably afford the duplicate.

Keeping an Air Generator also extends the time available to your other installed weapons, provided you use the additional shift time.

Replace individual modules according to your remaining asteroid, available oxygen, and upgrade levels rather than treating this configuration as a mandatory final build.
