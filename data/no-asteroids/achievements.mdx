<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Unlock All 65 Achievements in No Asteroids?">
Aim for a successful contract without blacking out to unlock **Earth saved** and **Wide awake**. Collect all eight minerals and 36 Museum Finds, complete optional ship interactions, and accept another asteroid contract after an ending. Leave achievements requiring a blackout, asteroid impact, or self-destruct for separate attempts.

**No Asteroids** has 65 achievements. Mining and equipment purchases unlock many of them naturally, but a successful rescue mission can still leave achievements unfinished. The easiest ones to overlook involve optional ship interactions, drifting Museum Finds, and actions that conflict with a successful no-blackout attempt.

## Can You Unlock Every Achievement in One Contract?

Not every achievement can be earned during the same uninterrupted contract.

The most important conflict is between **Wide awake** and **Nap time**. Wide awake requires reaching an ending without ever blacking out, while Nap time requires you to experience a blackout.

Earth saved and Complaints accepted also require different outcomes. Completely destroying the asteroid prevents that asteroid from reaching Earth.

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementPriorityColumns}
  rows={achievementPriorityRows}
/>

A practical completion order is:

1. Start a contract with Wide awake in mind.
2. Collect equipment, minerals, Finds, and ordinary progression achievements.
3. Complete optional ship interactions as they become available.
4. Destroy the asteroid completely to earn Earth saved.
5. Accept a new asteroid contract for Overtime.
6. Arrange a separate attempt for achievements that require a blackout or an impact ending.

Leave the self-destruct achievement until you are prepared for its possible consequences rather than experimenting with it during a nearly completed contract.

You do not need to finish every Museum collection before completing the first asteroid.

## How Do You Unlock the Mining and Progression Achievements?

Most asteroid destruction achievements unlock as you reduce the remaining volume of rock.

The destruction milestones are:

- **The first percent:** Destroy 1%.
- **Tithe:** Destroy 10%.
- **Quarter gone:** Destroy 25%.
- **Halfway there:** Destroy 50%.
- **Apple core:** Destroy 75%.
- **Home stretch:** Destroy 90%.

The asteroid's remaining volume is displayed at the top of the mining interface. Continue improving your excavation equipment as the remaining rock becomes more difficult to remove.

First month and Two months, no days off unlock when you start Day 30 and Day 60 respectively. They require reaching those days, not simply owning advanced equipment.

### How Do You Unlock A Million by Hand?

A million by hand requires 1,000,000 m³ of excavation without the ship's help. Total asteroid destruction is not a reliable measure of this achievement because it includes material removed by ship guns.

Keep excavating personally as your equipment develops. Purchasing <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#mining-tools">stronger handheld mining tools</Link> can help you reach the required volume faster.

### How Do You Unlock Fifty Down and Straight to the Heart?

Fifty down requires reaching a depth of 50 metres below pad level.

Watch the Depth indicator during excavation and continue digging below your landing area.

Straight to the heart requires reaching the asteroid's center. It is a different objective from reaching a particular depth below a landing pad.

If harder rock prevents you from continuing inward, improve your mining equipment before attempting the remaining excavation.

### How Do You Unlock Government Property?

Government property requires hitting a landing pad.

Aim your mining tool at the landing pad and strike it directly.

The achievement does not require destroying the entire platform.

### How Do You Unlock Rock Rider and Low Orbit?

Rock rider requires riding a loose piece of rock while it flies.

Watch for pieces that have separated from the main asteroid during excavation and try to remain on one as it moves.

Low orbit requires staying in the air for 15 consecutive seconds. The Jetpack can make this easier when there is enough open space around the asteroid.

Keep enough oxygen and mobility to return safely afterward, particularly when attempting Wide awake during the same contract.

</section>

## How Do You Unlock the Tool, Drone and Ship Gun Achievements?

The Workshop contains most of the purchases needed for the equipment-related achievements.

You do not need to maximize every upgrade attribute to complete the tool and drone purchase achievements.

### Full Toolbox and Beehive

Purchasing the Missile Swarm unlocks Beehive. When you also own the preceding five <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#mining-tools">mining tools</Link>, that purchase completes Full toolbox.

### Little Helper and Squadron

Your first drone purchase unlocks Little helper. Squadron requires one of each drone type: Digger, Prospector, Oxygen Drone, and Sapper.

Neither the complete tool collection nor the complete drone collection requires maximizing equipment attributes.

### How Do You Unlock Demolition Expert?

Demolition expert requires using all five Charges available through the Workshop.

Buying the Charges without using them is not enough.

As additional Charge types become available, take them into a mining shift and use each one at least once.

Keep track of the types you have already used so you can identify any missing requirement after purchasing your final Charge.

### How Do You Unlock Rocketeer and On a Leash?

Rocketeer unlocks when you purchase the Jetpack.

On a leash requires using the Tether to pull yourself back toward the Lander.

Buying the Tether alone is not enough. After purchasing it, move away from the Lander and press R during a mining shift to pull yourself back toward it.

### Gun on the Roof and Armed to the Teeth

Gun on the roof requires mounting your first ship gun.

A purchased gun that remains in storage has not yet been mounted. Install it in an available Engineering slot.

Armed to the teeth requires unlocking all six ship mounting slots. You do not need to fill every slot with an expensive weapon just to satisfy the unlock requirement.

See <Link href="/no-asteroids/ship-guns#weapon-slots">how to unlock additional ship slots</Link> for the Engineering controls and costs.

</section>

<section id="money-orders" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Complete the Money and Daily Order Achievements?

The money achievements distinguish between total earnings and the amount of cash you currently possess.

**Millionaire** and **Multimillionaire** require earning $1,000,000 and $3,000,000 respectively in one game.

**Six figures**, however, requires having $100,000 at once.

If you immediately spend your earnings on upgrades, you can continue progressing toward the cumulative earnings achievements without satisfying Six figures.

Save enough money to reach the required balance before making your next major purchase.

First paycheck unlocks after selling ore for the first time.

### Quota Met and Employee of the Month

Quota met requires completing one Order of the Day.

Employee of the month requires completing ten.

Prioritize achievable Orders of the Day throughout your contract rather than leaving all ten completions until the final stages.

If an order stops progressing, follow the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#daily-orders">Daily Order troubleshooting steps</Link>.

## How Do You Unlock Wide Awake Without Blacking Out?

Wide awake requires reaching an ending without a single blackout. One failed return to the Lander invalidates that attempt, even if you subsequently destroy the entire asteroid.

Leave Nap time for a separate attempt. Follow the <Link href="/no-asteroids#hazards">oxygen and return-route precautions</Link> throughout your contract, especially when excavating deep deposits.

### How Do You Unlock Second Wind?

Second wind requires continuing your shift using the emergency ampoule.

Interact with the ship's First-Aid Box by pressing E before starting your mining shift.

The Oxygen Ampoule provides an additional 15 seconds of air if your normal oxygen runs out during that shift.

To satisfy Second wind, the emergency effect must actually be used. Preparing the item alone is not the achievement condition.

Attempt this separately from Wide awake if you are uncertain whether you can return without blacking out after the emergency oxygen is consumed.

### How Do You Unlock Nap Time?

Nap time requires blacking out on the asteroid.

Leave this achievement for an attempt in which you are not trying to finish without blackouts.

Avoid carrying valuable cargo if you deliberately allow your oxygen to run out.

### Boom, Gas! and Seen It All

Boom requires striking Antimatter.

Gas! requires breaching a gas pocket during excavation.

Seen it all requires encountering a meteor shower, a solar flare, and a vein.

These are separate achievement conditions. Continue mining and pay attention to event notifications rather than assuming that surviving one type of hazard completes all three requirements.

For Antimatter's mining behavior, see <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#minerals">the mineral guide</Link>.

## Which Ship Interaction Achievements Are Easy to Miss?

Several achievements can be unlocked without mining another section of the asteroid.

Explore the living areas, Bridge, dome, and other accessible rooms between mining shifts.

### Coffee Break and Hot Lunch

Coffee break requires drinking a coffee before a shift.

Hot lunch requires heating a ration in the microwave.

Complete these interactions while preparing for your next mining trip rather than leaving immediately after purchasing equipment.

### How Do You Unlock Five-Dollar Massage?

Go to the dome and sit in the massage chair.

Press **F** to activate the massage for $5.

### Can I Drive? and Lights Out

Can I drive? requires sitting in a pilot's seat.

Lights out requires sleeping in the bed.

### How Do You Unlock Zero-G?

Open the ship's control panel and select OFF under Gravity to disable the ship's artificial gravity.

### How Do You Unlock One More Game?

Find the computer in the canteen and play its available game.

One more game requires finishing a game, not merely opening the computer interface.

The achievement does not specify a required winning score.

### How Do You Unlock Not in the Plan?

Not in the plan is a hidden achievement that requires starting the ship's self-destruct.

The ship has a Self-destruct control among its interactive systems.

Attempt this only when you are prepared to risk the current contract. Do not assume that starting the sequence is harmless or that it can always be reversed.

The achievement requires starting the self-destruct rather than completing an asteroid destruction milestone.

## How Do You Unlock Fresh Off the Line?

Fresh off the line requires washing the Lander until it is completely clean.

Cleaning most of the visible exterior is not enough if small dirty areas remain.

### How to Wash the Lander to 100%

1. Return to the ship and use the Lander cleaning equipment.
2. Wash the larger exterior surfaces first.
3. Move around the Lander to reach the other sides.
4. Check the legs, steps, railings, and other smaller surfaces.
5. Follow any highlighted areas that still require cleaning.
6. Continue until the washing progress reaches 100%.

## How Do You Unlock Mineralogist?

Mineralogist unlocks when the Museum's Minerals counter reaches 8/8. Compare any undiscovered entries with the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#minerals">eight mineral types</Link> to identify what you still need to mine.

### How Do You Unlock On a Roll?

On a roll requires reaching a mining combo of ×1.5.

Watch the combo indicator while excavating mineral deposits.

Continue mining while the combo is active until the displayed multiplier reaches the required value.

Once the multiplier reaches ×1.5, check whether the achievement has unlocked before changing your mining target.

## How Do You Unlock the Ending Achievements?

The ending-related achievements require different actions from ordinary mining milestones.

### Earth Saved

Earth saved requires 100% asteroid destruction. Reaching 90% only satisfies Home stretch.

If the remaining asteroid volume refuses to reach zero, follow the <Link href="/no-asteroids#last-one-percent">final asteroid cleanup steps</Link>.

### Complaints Accepted

Complaints accepted requires allowing something to reach Earth.

Plan a separate impact-ending attempt if you have already destroyed the asteroid completely.

Do not sacrifice a successful contract that you are still using to pursue Earth saved or other completion goals.

### How Do You Unlock Overtime?

Accept another asteroid assignment after reaching an ending. Merely opening the Next Contract selection screen does not satisfy Overtime.

You can select your next assignment through the <Link href="/no-asteroids#next-contracts">post-game contract system</Link>.
