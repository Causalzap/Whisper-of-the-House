<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Beat No Asteroids in 90 Days?">
The opening contract gives you 90 days to destroy 5,000,000 m³ of asteroid material. Use the first mining shifts to fund better equipment, put the ship's guns to work when the **Engine Room** opens, and measure your later progress against the remaining impact countdown. Finish by clearing isolated rock fragments until the remaining volume reaches zero.

Your first shift in **No Asteroids** begins with a Pickaxe, limited air, and an asteroid containing five million cubic metres of material. The volume above the Lander barely moves during the opening days. That changes as mineral sales fund stronger tools and the ship gains access to automatic weapons.

The asteroid must reach zero remaining volume before impact for complete destruction.

### What Are the Best Tips for Winning in 90 Days?

- Upgrade the equipment that is limiting your current mining shift rather than following a fixed purchase order.
- Start automatic destruction as soon as the Engine Room becomes available.
- Recover ore released by ship guns through the Tractor Beam.
- Compare your daily destruction with the remaining asteroid volume and impact countdown.

<section id="first-shift" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Do on Your First Day?

Your first goal is to return from the asteroid with minerals you can sell.

Do not attempt to reach the asteroid's center with your starting equipment.

### How to Start Your First Mining Shift

1. Enter the Lander aboard your ship.
2. Launch to the asteroid.
3. Locate an accessible mineral deposit near your landing site.
4. Use the left mouse button to break the deposit with your Pickaxe.
5. Allow the released minerals to travel to the Lander.
6. Watch your Cargo and Air indicators.
7. Return to the Lander and press E to fly back to the ship.

You begin with limited oxygen and cargo capacity. Staying close to the landing area makes it easier to complete the first shift without losing your collected minerals.

### How Do You Use the Mineral Scanner?

Press C to use the mineral scanner. Radar icons show nearby mineral deposits, while downward indicators display their depth in metres.

A marker appears on the rock directly above the deposit, helping you choose where to begin digging.

Choose deposits that you can reach and excavate within your remaining oxygen supply.

A mineral buried deep beneath the surface may be valuable, but reaching it can consume most of an early mining shift.

### How Do You Sell Your First Minerals?

After returning to the ship, approach the Trade Terminal beside the Lander.

Press Enter to sell your collected minerals at the Trade Terminal.

For example, an opening shift that returns with 40 units of Meteoric Iron can provide $158 at the displayed market price.

<section id="early-game" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Progress Through the Early Game?

After your first mineral sale, return to the asteroid with a specific deposit in mind. The next few shifts should establish a repeatable cycle: excavate accessible ore, return safely, sell the cargo, and improve the equipment that limited the trip.

An unsuccessful attempt at a deep deposit can consume an entire day's air without producing much income. Return to easier material until the appropriate equipment becomes affordable.

Choose your next purchase using the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#best-first-upgrades">early upgrade priorities</Link>.

### When Are You Ready to Move Beyond Early Mining?

You should be bringing back useful cargo regularly, reaching deposits beyond the immediate landing area, and earning enough money to improve your operation between shifts. Achievable Orders of the Day can help fund that progress.

Continue mining until the Security Council releases the ship's weapons.

</section>

## What Should You Do Before Every Mining Shift?

Your ship contains optional interactions that improve the following shift.

You do not need to use every interactive object aboard the ship, but a short preparation routine can make repeated mining trips more productive.

### A Simple Pre-Shift Routine

1. Sell the minerals you intend to use for purchases.
2. Check your next important equipment upgrade.
3. Read the current Order of the Day and any useful notifications.
4. Prepare available air and tool-speed bonuses.
5. Choose your next landing site.
6. Launch with a clear objective.

Do not wait until your oxygen is nearly exhausted to discover that you have no reliable route back to the Lander.

For optional ship activities such as the massage chair, see the <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements#ship-interactions">ship interaction achievements</Link>. ## How Do You Change Landing Sites?

Use the Landing Navigator to select an available landing site before launching from the ship.

Choosing a different site gives you access to another part of the asteroid without travelling there from your original landing area.

### When Should You Change Landing Sites?

Change your landing site when:

- Your current area contains very few accessible mineral deposits.
- Reaching untouched rock requires travelling through a large excavation.
- A notification identifies a useful deposit or hollow beneath another landing site.
- You need to investigate another part of the asteroid.
- You are searching for the last remaining fragments.

The landing-site selection screen can include North Pole, East Slope, Hope Crater, West Slope, Far Side, and South Pole.

Choose from the sites currently available to you rather than following a fixed excavation order.

### How Do You Travel Between Landing Sites During a Shift?

The Hop upgrade allows the Lander to travel to another landing site without ending the current mining shift.

Once Hop is available, return to the Lander and select the next site through its navigation controls.

This becomes particularly useful when you are searching for remaining deposits or clearing small sections of rock around several landing areas.

Check your remaining oxygen before travelling. Reaching another landing site does not mean that you have unlimited time to excavate there.

For the purchase decisions involving these navigation upgrades, see the <Link href="/no-asteroids/upgrades#make-money">Lander upgrades</Link>.

<section id="hazards" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Survive Solar Flares and Other Mining Hazards?

The asteroid becomes more difficult to navigate as you create deeper tunnels, larger excavation areas, and detached sections of rock.

Pay attention to event notifications rather than relying entirely on your equipment upgrades.

### What Should You Do During a Solar Flare?

When the solar flare warning appears, move into a shaft or cave before the countdown expires.

A warning can give you 15 seconds to reach shelter, so stop exposed surface excavation and move underground.

If there is not enough time to reach shelter and finish your objective safely, return to the Lander instead.

Wait for the flare to end before resuming exposed surface excavation.

### What Should You Do During a Meteor Shower?

When a meteor shower begins, falling rocks can make exposed mining areas more dangerous.

Move toward sheltered excavation when practical, and avoid spending your remaining oxygen travelling through exposed areas unnecessarily.

If your return route becomes unsafe, stop pursuing the current deposit and focus on reaching the Lander.

### How Do You Avoid Running Out of Oxygen Underground?

Before descending into a deep excavation, make sure you have a reliable way to leave it.

Watch both the Lander distance marker and the Air indicator. A large remaining oxygen supply can disappear quickly if you spend too much time trying to escape a complicated tunnel.

Use your mobility equipment to make the return journey more reliable.

If your air runs out, you risk blacking out and losing collected cargo. Returning early is preferable to losing the proceeds of an entire productive shift.

The <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements#survival-achievements">Wide awake achievement</Link> also requires reaching an ending without any blackouts.

### Should You Follow Notifications About Veins and Hollows?

Some notifications identify deposits or unusual underground areas near particular landing sites.

Read the named location and depth before deciding whether to investigate.

If the target is accessible with your current equipment, change your next landing site and search that area.

These notifications provide useful exploration opportunities, but they do not replace the main objective of destroying the asteroid before impact.

</section>

<section id="midgame" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Speed Up Asteroid Destruction in the Midgame?

The first installed ship guns change what happens during a mining shift. While you concentrate on valuable deposits, automatic attacks remove material elsewhere on the asteroid. Additional excavation systems can increase that contribution as your equipment develops.

Check the destruction recorded at the end of each shift, particularly after major purchases. An expensive improvement should eventually translate into more useful excavation, not simply a higher equipment level.

Ore drifting away from automatic attacks is a separate problem. Install the <Link href="/no-asteroids/ship-guns#tractor-beam">Tractor Beam</Link> when mineral recovery becomes important.

### When Are You Ready for Large-Scale Demolition?

Once several consecutive shifts remove substantial volumes of rock, compare the remaining asteroid with the days left until impact. The next question is whether your operation can finish the contract at its current pace.

</section>

<section id="progress-check" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Are You on Track to Destroy the Asteroid Before Day 90?

Do not judge your progress using the destruction percentage alone.

Your first shifts remove relatively little material because your equipment is weak. Later shifts can become substantially more productive after major equipment purchases.

Use the remaining asteroid volume, remaining days, and recent destruction per shift to decide whether your current operation is sufficient.

### How to Estimate Your Required Daily Destruction

Divide the remaining asteroid volume by the number of days until impact.

**Required destruction per day = Remaining asteroid volume ÷ Days until impact**

Compare the result with the destruction recorded over your recent mining shifts.

If your current rate is below the required average, you need to improve it before the deadline. If it exceeds the required average, maintain that pace while allowing extra time for final cleanup.

### Is It Too Late If You Have Barely Destroyed Anything by Day 40?

Not necessarily.

One contract reached Day 40 with only 6.29% of the asteroid destroyed, leaving 4,685,368 m³ of rock and 51 days until impact.

With 51 days left, this contract required an average of approximately 91,870 m³ of destruction per day.

Further equipment investments brought the same asteroid to roughly 60% destruction with 25 days remaining. The remaining volume had fallen to about 2,000,000 m³, reducing the required average to approximately 80,000 m³ per day.

The contract was still unfinished. Nevertheless, its remaining destruction requirement was lower despite the shorter deadline. Compare your recent mining results with that requirement before abandoning a slow contract.

### Should You Continue or Restart a Slow Contract?

A low destruction percentage alone is not a reason to restart.

Continue if your recent destruction rate is improving and you can afford equipment that could substantially reduce the remaining asteroid volume.

Consider restarting if your current destruction rate remains far below the required average, very little time remains, and you have no realistic way to improve your equipment sufficiently.

If automatic destruction is your main limitation, compare the available <Link href="/no-asteroids/ship-guns#best-loadout">ship gun configurations</Link> before making that decision.

<section id="late-game" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When Should You Stop Focusing on Money and Start Destroying Everything?

The value of another mineral shipment changes as impact approaches. Early earnings fund equipment that can operate over dozens of future shifts. Near the deadline, money has less value unless you can spend it on something that helps remove the remaining asteroid.

Once your current equipment can sustain the required destruction rate, put that capacity to work. Continuing to maximize every economic or equipment attribute may leave less time for the actual excavation.

### When Should You Switch to Final Cleanup?

Large-scale demolition remains useful while substantial sections of solid rock are intact. Once those sections disappear, search for individual fragments and pieces that automatic attacks have missed.

</section>

<section id="last-one-percent" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Destroy the Last 1% of the Asteroid?

The final stage is different from the main excavation process.

Once most of the asteroid is gone, you may be left with separated fragments, small pockets of material, and pieces near landing platforms.

Continuing to attack the largest visible section is not always enough to finish.

### Step 1: Search for Detached Floating Rocks

Look around the remaining asteroid rather than concentrating entirely on the rock directly beneath your current landing site.

Small pieces can remain floating away from the main mass.

Use your mining equipment to remove these fragments while keeping track of your position relative to the Lander.

A small rock may contribute very little to the remaining volume, but every remaining piece matters when the objective is to reach zero.

### Step 2: Inspect Rock Around Landing Platforms

Visit landing areas that still have visible material nearby.

Check around and beneath the platforms for small sections of rock that may have been missed during larger excavation passes.

The landing platform is not the same thing as the surrounding asteroid material.

If attacking the platform does not reduce the remaining volume, change your angle and search for actual rock nearby.

### Step 3: Revisit Multiple Landing Sites

Use the Landing Navigator or Hop to inspect different parts of the remaining asteroid.

For example, you can revisit East Slope, Hope Crater, West Slope, and Far Side rather than spending the entire shift around one platform.

These locations are not a mandatory cleanup sequence. Visit whichever areas still contain material.

If you cannot find the remaining pieces from your current position, changing landing sites may provide a better viewing angle.

### Step 4: Watch the Remaining Volume

Check the m³ counter after clearing each isolated piece. A decreasing counter confirms that you have removed material contributing to the remaining asteroid volume.

### Why Is Your Asteroid Stuck at 99%?

The destruction percentage may remain unchanged while small fragments are being removed. As long as the remaining m³ counter continues to decrease, your cleanup is still progressing.

A completely stationary volume counter calls for a different target. Change your viewing angle or landing site instead of repeatedly attacking the same object. Continue until the game reports that the asteroid has been destroyed.

If a remaining fragment appears completely unreachable, make sure you are running version 1.0.8 or later, which includes a fix for unreachable final asteroid fragments.

Large detached pieces can also be excavated with drones, Shaker Charges, and drilling rigs.

</section>

## What Happens When You Destroy the Entire Asteroid?

After the remaining asteroid volume reaches zero, the game reports successful destruction.

The completion certificate summarizes your contract performance, including the amount of material destroyed, days taken, manual excavation, ship gun excavation, Finds, and earnings.

The required result is zero remaining asteroid volume, not a particular income, equipment level, or division of excavation work.

### Do You Need Every Collectible to Save Earth?

No. The asteroid can be destroyed without completing the Museum. Track any unfinished collections using the <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements#museum-collections">Museum completion requirements</Link>.

### What Happens If the Countdown Expires?

Your opening objective is to prevent remaining asteroid material from reaching Earth.

If material remains when impact occurs, the contract can produce a different outcome from complete destruction.

The outcome is not determined solely by reaching a particular destruction milestone such as 90%.

For achievements associated with successful destruction and asteroid impact, see the <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements#ending-achievements">ending achievement requirements</Link>.

Select Next Contract on the completion screen, then inspect the available asteroid assignments through the Bridge.

Inspect the available contracts before choosing your next asteroid.

This gives you new opportunities to use the equipment developed during earlier mining operations.

### What Should You Do on Larger Asteroids?

Later contracts can give your developed equipment substantially more material to excavate, including asteroids with volumes of 15,000,000 m³.

Check the new asteroid's properties and available landing sites before choosing your next mining objective.

Do not assume that every later assignment has the same conditions as the opening rescue mission.

### How Does New Game+ Work in No Asteroids?

When starting the next year, you can choose two of three permanent bonuses.

Later years offer additional progression rewards, giving you another way to strengthen your mining operation beyond the opening rescue mission.

Completed asteroid shapes are marked on the sector map, and uncompleted shapes are offered first. Check the available assignments before selecting your next target.

### Should You Accept Another Contract?

Accept another contract if you want to continue developing your equipment or complete a larger asteroid.

If your next objective is achievement completion, check the <Link href="/no-asteroids/achievements#ending-achievements">Overtime and ending achievement requirements</Link> before choosing how to proceed.

## Does No Asteroids Have Cheats or an Easy Mode?

If the 90-day countdown is making your contract too difficult, consider using **No Rush mode** instead of relying on external cheats or trainers.

In No Rush mode, days do not advance automatically. You can also enable the **No Blackouts** option to remove blackout-related pressure.

These options are built into the game. They are separate from external trainers and Cheat Engine tables.

### Can You Make a Backup of Your Campaign?

Open the Load Game menu and copy your campaign into a separate save slot before experimenting with different progression decisions.

Keeping a separate save is particularly useful before committing to an expensive equipment purchase or attempting an alternative contract outcome.
