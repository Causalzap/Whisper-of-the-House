<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
For a practical Main Sequence research order, unlock Logistics I and Mining Drones early, then work toward Computation Science, Logistics II, and the Enrichment technologies. Expand into Cobalt and Graphite for Electromagnetic Science, then produce Superconductor Coils to advance into Quantum Science. After unlocking interstellar travel, collect Iridium, Lonsdaleite, and Stellar Survey Data to make Encoded Simulation Matrices and start Astronomic Science. If research stops, check the required Science types and whether the corresponding labs are receiving materials.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

**Main Sequence research progression** becomes harder to follow as your factory expands. Early technologies require Material Science, but later research can require Computation Science, Electromagnetic Science, Quantum Science, and eventually Astronomic Science.

You may have enough materials to build another factory floor but still be unable to research the machine you need. Or you may have a long Research Queue that stops because one Science Lab is not producing anything.

The solution is to identify the next technology that changes what your station can do, then make sure every Science type it requires is available.

You do not need to finish every optional technology before moving forward. Prioritize the unlocks that solve the production, travel, or research problem you are facing.

<section id="research-order" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Research First in Main Sequence?

The Research Tree becomes difficult when a technology is visible but the Science needed to unlock it is not yet available. The next useful choice depends on which research type you can produce and what equipment will become available afterward.

Early unlocks lead through Logistics I and Mining Drones into Computation Science. Electromagnetic and Quantum Science then open more advanced technologies, while later interstellar exploration supplies materials required for Astronomic research.

These are progression milestones rather than one uninterrupted prerequisite chain. Check the connected technologies and required Science amounts before committing to the next unlock.

### Prioritize What Unlocks the Next Useful System

Before adding a technology to your Research Queue, ask what you intend to do immediately after unlocking it.

For example:

- If you are still moving materials manually, Logistics I and Mining Drones are useful early targets.
- If new research requires a second Science type, unlock Computation Science and build the corresponding lab.
- If your factory is running out of basic components, research the production equipment or upgrades that address that shortage.
- If the next required resource is in another sector, prepare the technology needed to reach it.
- If advanced research requires materials you cannot make, inspect the technology that unlocks those materials before queueing several later upgrades.

This avoids a common problem: finishing research that unlocks equipment your station cannot yet build or supply.

### Do You Have to Finish the Entire Tech Tree?

No. Progress through the branches that support your current goals.

Some technologies improve convenience, such as additional building space, ship components, or upgraded equipment. Others open an entirely new research type or make it possible to reach a different region.

Those major unlocks should generally take priority when your goal is to continue progressing.

You can return to optional technologies after your essential production and Science Labs are running.

<section id="early-research" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Progress From Material Science to Computation Science?

Material Science is the starting point for your first major technology unlocks.

Build a Material Science Lab at your station and supply it with supported research items.

The early lab can research items such as Hull Plates and Servomotors. These are converted into Material Science, which is then used to unlock technologies in the Research Tree.

### Unlock Logistics I and Mining Drones

**Logistics I** is one of the first important technologies because it makes basic Conveyor automation available.

It unlocks equipment including Conveyor Belts, Underground Belts, Splitters, and Belt Balancers.

You should then work toward **Mining Drones**, which reduce the need to collect every resource manually.

Early research costs are relatively small compared with later technologies.

In the Research Tree shown below, Logistics I costs 5 Material Science, Mining Drones costs 15, and Computation Science costs 50. Research costs can differ between game builds, so check the requirements displayed in your current Research Tree before planning the next unlock.

The precise order in which you obtain every supporting technology depends on your current Research Tree and immediate needs.

For a player who already has a functioning first station, these unlocks create the foundation for larger production systems.

If your Material Science Lab is not yet powered or supplied, [complete the first station's power and research setup](/main-sequence#first-power-and-research) before queueing more technologies.

### Why Is Computation Science Important?

Computation Science introduces another Science type required by more advanced research.

Unlocking the technology alone is not the same as continuously producing the new Science.

After researching Computation Science:

1. Locate the Computation Science Lab.
2. Check its construction requirements.
3. Build it at a powered station.
4. Inspect which materials it accepts for research.
5. Supply those materials and confirm that the Science amount increases.
6. Return to the Research Tree and continue toward the next technology.

If your next upgrade requires both Material Science and Computation Science, keeping only the Material Science Lab active is not sufficient.

Both required types must be available.

### When Should You Research Logistics II?

Logistics II becomes useful when you need the additional Conveyor equipment unlocked by that technology. Check its prerequisite nodes and Science cost before adding it to the Research Queue.

If your factory is producing too few plates or components, faster transport alone will not increase their output. In that case, [inspect the stalled production line](/main-sequence/factory-automation#production-bottlenecks) before spending Science on a logistics upgrade.

</section>

<section id="enrichment-and-electromagnetic" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock Electromagnetic Science?

Electromagnetic Science is the next major step after basic Material and Computation research.

By this stage, you need to expand beyond the materials available around your first station.

**Cobalt and Graphite become important as you develop the production chains used for more advanced technologies.**

### Research the Enrichment Technologies

Enrichment Chambers allow additional material processing that basic Refineries cannot provide.

As you progress, research the Enrichment Chamber and the ore-processing technologies needed for your next products.

These unlocks support materials such as enriched ores and Superalloy.

They become increasingly important when your new machinery and station modules require Reinforced Plates or other advanced components.

You do not need to construct a large Enrichment factory immediately.

First unlock the relevant machinery, produce the materials needed for your current research goal, and confirm that your Science Labs can continue operating.

If you have unlocked Enrichment but cannot produce Superalloy, [check whether all three enriched ores reach the final Enrichment Chamber](/main-sequence/factory-automation#superalloy-production).

### Find Cobalt and Graphite

Check the map for sectors containing the materials you need.

Cobalt and Graphite are important targets as you move beyond the earliest research and begin working toward Electromagnetic Science.

If your current station cannot reach a suitable resource source, you may need to improve your travel capabilities before building another outpost.

Once the necessary materials are accessible, continue through the appropriate refining and research unlocks.

### Why Is Electromagnetic Science Still Locked?

Inspect the technology requirements in the Research Tree.

Look for three possible problems:

1. A required earlier technology is unfinished.
2. The necessary production equipment is not unlocked.
3. You have not yet established the Science type or material supply needed to support the next stage.

After unlocking Electromagnetic Science, build and supply its corresponding Science Lab.

Check that its output actually increases before queueing multiple technologies that require it.

### Is Logistics III Worth Researching?

Logistics III is useful when your growing factory needs a higher-capacity material transportation system.

It unlocks Mk. 3 logistics equipment.

This becomes particularly relevant when several machines share the same input material and the existing Conveyor network cannot transport enough of it.

It is not a requirement to upgrade every belt immediately.

Prioritize the areas where increased transportation capacity can remove an actual bottleneck.

</section>

<section id="slipstream-drive" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Slipstream Drive?

The **Slipstream Drive** is an important travel upgrade that helps you move quickly around the solar system.

It is different from the Interstellar Warp Drive used to reach other star systems.

Research the Slipstream Drive when longer trips between resource sectors become a significant part of your progression.

### Slipstream Drive vs Interstellar Warp Drive

The distinction is straightforward:

- **Slipstream Drive:** Fast travel within the current solar system.
- **Interstellar Warp Drive:** Travel between separate star systems.

Unlocking the Slipstream Drive does not mean you have already unlocked interstellar travel.

If your immediate goal is reaching another asteroid field to collect Cobalt or Graphite, the earlier travel upgrade may be enough.

If you want to leave the current star system entirely, you need the later Interstellar technology.

### What Do You Need After Unlocking the Slipstream Drive?

The Slipstream Drive requires Warp Fuel to operate. Unlocking its technology does not automatically install or fuel the module on your ship.

For installation, fuel requirements, and ship configuration, follow the [Main Sequence ship design guide](/main-sequence/ship-design).

</section>

## How Do You Unlock Quantum Science?

Quantum Science is a major research milestone that opens the path toward advanced machinery, more capable ships, and interstellar development.

The key material to prepare is the **Superconductor Coil**.

### Research Superconductor Coils First

Find the Superconductor Coil technology in the Research Tree.

In the current research progression, this technology uses Material Science, Computation Science, and Electromagnetic Science.

After unlocking it, produce the coils needed for your next research equipment.

Superconductor Coils require Maglev Couplings, Containment Coils, and Coolant. If their production is preventing you from building the next research facility, check the [Superconductor Coil production instructions](/main-sequence/factory-automation#coolant-production).

### Build the Quantum Science Lab

Once Quantum Science has been researched, inspect the Quantum Science Lab's construction requirements.

A working setup needs the relevant advanced materials, including Superconductor Coils and Detector Arrays.

Build the lab at a powered station, supply its research material, and verify that Quantum Science is accumulating.

Do not immediately queue every new Quantum technology.

First confirm that your factory can maintain the material flow required by the lab.

### What Should You Research After Quantum Science?

The most useful next targets are generally:

- **Advanced Assembler:** Supports higher-tier manufacturing.
- **Superalloy Frame:** Important for advanced construction.
- **Advanced Circuits:** Used in more sophisticated equipment.
- **Interstellar Warp Drive:** Enables travel between star systems.
- **Astronomic Scanning:** Begins the path toward collecting Stellar Survey Data.
- **Supercomputer:** Required for the later Encoded Simulation Matrix chain.

Choose your immediate goal.

If you need better manufacturing, begin with the Advanced Assembler and the relevant components.

If your factory is ready and you want to reach new systems, focus on travel and scanning technologies.

The materials required to build and operate the machines may take more preparation than the research itself.

</section>

<section id="relic-technology" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Research Relic Technology?

Relic Technology is a separate research category based on alien materials rather than the regular Science types alone.

To begin using it, unlock the Relic Analyzer and collect Relic Shards.

### Use the Relic Analyzer

The Relic Analyzer converts Relic Shards into Relic Technology research.

This opens a separate selection of alien technology upgrades.

Some of these upgrades improve ship equipment, while later research includes the Relic Synthesizer and related production options.

Do not spend all your Relic Technology on optional equipment if you are preparing for a particular advanced unlock.

Check what the technology you want requires before choosing the next branch.

### When Should You Research the Relic Synthesizer?

The Relic Synthesizer is valuable when you want additional options for supplying materials.

Its later uses include resource synthesis, allowing you to produce certain basic resources through Protomatter and Enrichment.

This can become useful for remote factories with inconvenient natural resource access.

However, Relic Synthesis does not replace the need to unlock and power the relevant equipment.

Research it when you have the resources and a practical use for the additional production flexibility.

After unlocking the Relic Synthesizer, [connect Protomatter production to an Enrichment Chamber](/main-sequence/factory-automation#relic-synthesis) when you need another Silica supply.

</section>

<section id="relic-destroyer" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When Should You Fight the Relic Destroyer?

The **Relic Destroyer** is a major mission target near the edge of the starting solar system.

It is associated with the transition toward more advanced exploration and interstellar technology.

Do not approach it simply because you have discovered its location.

Prepare a ship that can survive a prolonged fight before committing to the encounter.

### Check Your Interstellar Warp Drive Progress

Open the Research Tree and the current mission objectives.

If Interstellar Warp Drive is still unavailable, check whether the Relic Destroyer objective remains part of your current progression.

If Interstellar Warp Drive is already researched and available in your save, you can prepare for interstellar travel without assuming you need to defeat another Destroyer first.

The important distinction is between:

- Seeing the Interstellar Warp Drive technology in the Research Tree.
- Having the technology researched and unlocked.
- Having a ship with the required module and fuel.
- Completing a separate Relic Destroyer mission objective.

These are not interchangeable progress states.

### How Should You Prepare Before Fighting the Relic Destroyer?

Before attempting the mission, make sure your ship has the weapons, protection, and fuel needed for the encounter. For combat modules and ship upgrades, use the [Main Sequence ship design guide](/main-sequence/ship-design).

### What Should You Do After the Relic Destroyer?

Check whether the mission is complete, collect any available rewards, and inspect the Research Tree again.

If the objective was preventing a required technology from becoming available, that technology should now be the next thing to investigate.

If your interstellar equipment was already unlocked, treat the battle as a completed progression objective and continue preparing for your next destination.

Your next major exploration milestone is reaching another star system and establishing access to advanced materials.

</section>

<section id="interstellar-progression" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Reach Another Star System in Main Sequence?

To travel beyond your starting solar system, you need the **Interstellar Warp Drive** and a ship prepared for that journey.

Researching the technology is only the first step. The ship also needs the module installed and enough Warp Fuel for the planned trip.

### Research Interstellar Warp Drive

The Interstellar Warp Drive is a Quantum-era technology.

Its research cost is:

- 200 Material Science
- 200 Computation Science
- 200 Electromagnetic Science
- 200 Quantum Science

Check whether all four required Science types are available.

If the technology remains unavailable, inspect its connected research path and the relevant mission objectives.

Once researched, the ship module becomes available for construction.

After completing the research, [fit the Interstellar Warp Drive in the Ship Designer](/main-sequence/ship-design#warp-drive-installation) and check the required construction materials and fuel before attempting the journey.

### What Should Be Unlocked Before You Leave the Starting System?

Confirm that Interstellar Warp Drive research is complete and that the intended destination is available on the Star Map. If either is missing, inspect the connected research requirements and your current mission objectives.

After reaching another system, locating Iridium and Lonsdaleite will help you progress toward the Supercomputer and Astronomic Science.

### How Do You Select a Star System?

Open the Star Map and inspect the available systems.

In one interstellar journey, the destination was selected from the map and added to the HUD before the ship departed.

### Why Should You Look for Iridium?

Iridium becomes important for later research equipment.

After reaching another star system, inspect the local asteroid fields for a useful Iridium source.

You can establish a small powered outpost near the resource before committing to a larger factory.

The goal is to obtain enough Iridium to support refining and the next advanced research facilities.

Do not automatically turn your first landing site into a permanent industrial base. Survey the local resources before deciding where further expansion makes sense.

### What Else Should You Collect?

**Lonsdaleite** is another important late-game material.

It is used in the Empty Simulation Matrix production chain, which eventually supports Astronomic Science.

You may need to visit a different sector or system to obtain a useful supply.

Once you have Iridium and Lonsdaleite available, you can begin preparing the Supercomputer and Astronomic Science Lab.

</section>

<section id="stellar-survey-data" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Get Stellar Survey Data?

**Stellar Survey Data comes from scanning stellar objects, including planets and stars.**

This material becomes important because it is needed to create Encoded Simulation Matrices.

Those matrices are used for Astronomic Science.

### Unlock and Use the Astronomic Scanner

Research **Astronomic Scanning**, then prepare a ship equipped with an Astronomic Scanner.

The scanner is a ship module, not the same thing as the Deep Space Observatory that becomes available later.

After installing the scanner:

1. Travel near a planet or another suitable stellar object.
2. Access the Astronomic Scanner.
3. Select a valid target.
4. Start the scan when the target is in range.
5. Collect the resulting Stellar Survey Data.
6. Repeat at additional valid targets when you need more data.

### Why Do You Need Stellar Survey Data?

The data is one of the two ingredients used by the Supercomputer to create Encoded Simulation Matrices.

You need those matrices to produce Astronomic Science.

Before you unlock the later automated data-producing equipment, collecting Stellar Survey Data manually is a practical way to begin the chain.

Keep the data available instead of using all of it without a specific research goal.

## How Do You Unlock Astronomic Science?

**Astronomic Science is produced from Encoded Simulation Matrices in an Astronomic Science Lab.**

This is one of the most important late-game research bottlenecks.

You can have advanced factories, Quantum Science, and an interstellar-capable ship while still being unable to progress because no Astronomic Science is being generated.

The required chain is:

**Advanced Circuits + Lonsdaleite → Empty Simulation Matrix**

**Empty Simulation Matrix + Stellar Survey Data → Encoded Simulation Matrix**

**Encoded Simulation Matrix → Astronomic Science Lab → Astronomic Science**

### Step 1: Unlock Supercomputer and Astronomic Science

Find the Supercomputer and Astronomic Science technologies in the Research Tree.

They require the earlier Science types, including Quantum Science.

Do not confuse researching the Astronomic Science technology with having a functioning Astronomic Science Lab.

The technology unlocks access to the relevant equipment. The lab must still be constructed and supplied.

### Step 2: Produce Empty Simulation Matrices

Use an Advanced Assembler to create Empty Simulation Matrices.

The recipe uses:

- 2 Advanced Circuits
- 1 Lonsdaleite

This produces one Empty Simulation Matrix.

If you cannot start the recipe, check whether the required technology is unlocked and both ingredients are available.

Lonsdaleite is especially important because you may need to travel beyond your original resource network to obtain it.

### Step 3: Produce Encoded Simulation Matrices

Build a Supercomputer and select the Encoded Simulation Matrix recipe.

Each matrix requires:

- 1 Empty Simulation Matrix
- 1 Stellar Survey Data

Make sure both inputs are supplied to the Supercomputer.

If you already collected Stellar Survey Data manually, you can use it to start production rather than waiting until the Deep Space Observatory is unlocked.

The Supercomputer cannot produce Encoded Simulation Matrices from survey data alone.

### Step 4: Build the Astronomic Science Lab

The Astronomic Science Lab requires:

- 5 Iridium Plates
- 3 Encoded Simulation Matrices

Gather those construction materials before placing the lab.

Once built, connect the Encoded Simulation Matrix supply to the lab.

Watch the Research Tree after the first matrix is processed.

If Astronomic Science begins increasing, the research bottleneck has been resolved.

### Step 5: Resume Your Research Queue

Return to the Research Tree.

The technologies requiring Astronomic Science should now be able to progress, provided the other required Science types are also available.

Do not immediately consume all of your Encoded Simulation Matrices on research if you still need some to construct another important machine.

Check upcoming construction requirements before allowing every matrix to enter the lab.

<section id="deep-space-observatory" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Deep Space Observatory?

The Deep Space Observatory is a later station module used to gather Stellar Survey Data.

It becomes especially useful after you have established Astronomic Science but still need more survey data to sustain advanced research.

### Research Requirements

The Deep Space Observatory requires:

- 300 Material Science
- 300 Computation Science
- 300 Electromagnetic Science
- 300 Quantum Science
- 300 Astronomic Science

The final requirement is what makes the Observatory difficult to obtain if you have not built an Astronomic Science Lab.

**You must establish Astronomic Science production before you can finish this research.**

Do not wait for the Observatory to provide the survey data needed to produce your first Encoded Simulation Matrices.

Collect the initial data with the Astronomic Scanner instead.

### What Does the Observatory Change?

Once unlocked and constructed, the Deep Space Observatory can produce Stellar Survey Data using Advanced Circuits.

That gives your station a way to supply the data without relying entirely on manual scanning trips.

The Observatory is therefore a way to expand and sustain the Astronomic Science system, rather than a substitute for completing its initial unlock chain.

After research is complete, check the Observatory's building requirements and power demand before adding it to the station.

If your Advanced Circuits production is already supporting other equipment, consider whether it can also meet the Observatory's needs.

For Advanced Circuits and related production shortages, use the [factory automation guide](/main-sequence/factory-automation#advanced-production).

</section>

<section id="xenobiologic-science" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock Xenobiologic Science?

Xenobiologic Science is a separate late-game research branch focused on biological materials.

You can investigate its technologies alongside Astronomic research once the required earlier Science types are available. You do not need to finish every Astronomic technology first.

### How Is Xenobiologic Science Different?

Unlike Astronomic Science, which uses Encoded Simulation Matrices, Xenobiologic Science uses **Biolattice** as a research item.

Biolattice is produced through biological processing using the Growth Chamber system.

The Xenobiologic Science Lab converts Biolattice into Xenobiologic Science.

This research branch supports later biotechnology and specialized equipment.

### How Do You Start the Biological Research Branch?

Once the relevant technologies are unlocked:

1. Unlock the Growth Chamber and the technologies required for Biolattice.
2. Make sure Biolattice is available for the Xenobiologic Science Lab.
3. Unlock and construct the Xenobiologic Science Lab.
4. Supply Biolattice and confirm that Xenobiologic Science begins increasing.

The Xenobiologic Science Lab requires Iridium Plates and Biolattice for construction.

Once the Xenobiologic Science Lab is operating, check which technologies require its output. The relevant Biolattice supply must continue running while those technologies are researched.

### Should You Prioritize Astronomic or Xenobiologic Science?

Choose based on the technology you need next.

If you are working toward the Deep Space Observatory, Strange Matter Crystal, or advanced ship and station equipment that requires Astronomic Science, develop the matrix and survey-data chain first.

If your target belongs to the biological technology branch, prepare Biolattice and the Xenobiologic Science Lab.

The best choice depends on the immediate unlock you want, not on finishing the entire research category before moving to another.

## Why Is Research Not Progressing in Main Sequence?

If a technology is queued but the progress bar remains unchanged, determine which Science type is missing.

**A queued technology does not create the Science required to complete it.**

Use the Research Tree to identify every Science type the selected technology needs, then inspect the corresponding labs.

### Check the Missing Science Type First

A technology may require Material Science, Computation Science, Electromagnetic Science, and Quantum Science at the same time.

If three of these are available but one is not being produced, the remaining requirement can prevent progress.

Open the Research Tree and identify the missing amount.

Then check the lab responsible for that Science type.

Do not rebuild unrelated production lines until you know which research requirement is actually unavailable.

### Is the Science Lab Powered?

If a Science Lab is not operating, confirm that the station has power.

Several labs can stop together when the station's energy supply fails.

Restore the required power first, then inspect whether each lab resumes processing its research material.

If the station itself has a fuel or production problem, follow the [factory power troubleshooting steps](/main-sequence/factory-automation#power-and-fuel).

### Does the Lab Have the Correct Research Item?

A lab may be powered but still have no research material.

Inspect the item it accepts and whether that item is arriving.

For example, a functioning Astronomic Science Lab requires Encoded Simulation Matrices.

Having Stellar Survey Data in a Container elsewhere does not mean the lab can use that data directly.

The data must first be converted into the correct research item.

### Is a Required Technology Still Locked?

If the problem is not Science production, inspect the technology's connected Research Tree path.

You may need another technology before the current one can become available.

A construction recipe can also remain unavailable until its associated technology is researched.

Do not confuse a locked recipe, insufficient construction materials, and a Science shortage.

Each problem has a different solution.

### Why Did Research Stop After Building a New Factory?

A new production line may consume materials that were previously supplying a Science Lab.

If the required research items stop arriving, check the [factory automation guide](/main-sequence/factory-automation#production-bottlenecks) to identify the missing supply before adding more labs.

## What Should You Research Next?

An available technology is not always the best one to research immediately. Look at what prevents you from using the equipment it would unlock.

When your station has only Material Science, the next major investment is the equipment that produces Computation Science. Once Electromagnetic and Quantum research are available, decide whether advanced manufacturing or interstellar travel will help you reach the missing materials for your next project.

Astronomic Science has a more specific requirement: Encoded Simulation Matrices. If Deep Space Observatory remains at 0/300 Astronomic Science, additional Material or Quantum Science will not clear that final requirement. Produce the matrices, supply the Astronomic Science Lab, and resume research after its output begins increasing.

For biological technologies, the equivalent milestone is a functioning Biolattice supply and Xenobiologic Science Lab. Keep the Research Queue focused on technologies whose construction materials and required Science types you can actually obtain.
