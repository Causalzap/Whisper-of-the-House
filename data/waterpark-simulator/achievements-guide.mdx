Waterpark Simulator has **71 achievements in version 1.0**, including
progression milestones, long maintenance and sales counters, Museum and Yogi
quests, Beach and City Map goals, Diamond Prestige, Security, Ziplines, Custom
Slides and character customization.

I do not treat them as 71 separate grinds. Most of the list can be combined
inside one long-running Hard Mode Beach Map save, while the City-specific
Prestige achievements need their own park.

For the achievements I can verify, I use one long-running **Hard Mode Beach Map**
save for almost everything that benefits from accumulated progress:

- Park Prestige;
- cleaning and repairs;
- visitor counters;
- food sales;
- revenue and spending;
- Research;
- rescues;
- staff;
- Museum progression.

That same save can carry the general five-star achievement, the Beach five-star
achievement, Hard five stars and Diamond without restarting.

The biggest mistake I would avoid is automating every manual job too early.

Janitors take puddles and trash, Maintenance Workers take repairs, Vendors take
sales and Security handles disruptive guests. That makes the park healthier,
but it can leave my personal achievement counters barely moving.

<h2 id="achievement-roadmap">
  My Waterpark Simulator 1.0 Achievement Route
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

I use the **Beach Map** as the main save.

That immediately lets one park work toward:

- **Water you are waiting for?**
- **Welcome to the Coast**
- tutorial and first-slide achievements;
- general Park Prestige achievements;
- Beach-specific three- and five-star achievements.

If I start that save on **Hard**, I can also work toward **Unstoppable Resort**
instead of building another five-star park later.

I still watch the notification at each milestone. Several Prestige conditions
overlap, but I do not assume that a generic, map-specific and
difficulty-specific achievement all triggered until I actually see them.

My route is:

1. tutorial and one star;
2. first employee and first expansion;
3. two stars and early maintenance counters;
4. three stars on Beach;
5. Museum and Yogi progression;
6. four stars;
7. five stars on Beach and Hard;
8. all Research and maximum expansion;
9. Diamond;
10. City Map to three and five stars;
11. long counters and remaining one-action cleanup.

<h2 id="version-1-achievements">
  Important 1.0 Achievement Additions
</h2>

The 1.0 achievement group added several goals that change how I plan the final
cleanup:

- **Welcome to the Coast** — open a park on Beach;
- **Seaside Prestige** — reach three stars on Beach;
- **King of the Coast** — reach five stars on Beach;
- **Downtown Delight** — reach three stars on City;
- **City Legend** — reach five stars on City;
- **Above the Stars** — reach Diamond Star Prestige;
- **Order Restored** — have Security remove 50 disruptive guests;
- **High Above the Park** — ride a Zipline;
- **Slide Architect** — make the first Custom Slide;
- **Fresh Look** — customize the character;
- **Trumpet Solo** — play a song on a trumpet.

I fold almost all of these into normal progression.

The only ones I deliberately leave for a short cleanup session are actions that
do not benefit from running the park for another ten days.

<h2 id="progression-achievements">
  Opening, Prestige and Map Achievements
</h2>

### Water you are waiting for?

**Condition:** Open the park for the first time.

I complete the opening construction, then use the park's Open control.

### Caution! Slippery when wet

**Condition:** Build the first slide.

The tutorial naturally provides the right moment. I do not delay it for
decoration.

### Lifeguard-ish

**Condition:** Finish the tutorial.

I complete the starting task chain before moving into my own layout.

### One-Star Wonder

**Condition:** Reach one-star Park Prestige.

I follow the current star objectives rather than expanding early just because
new land is available.

### Visionary

**Condition:** Expand the park once.

I buy the first expansion after the original section can survive a normal
operating day. Empty land adds workload before it adds revenue.

### Double Shine

**Condition:** Reach two-star Park Prestige.

At two stars I still keep the layout compact. I do not build a late-game park
inside an early-game budget.

### Making Waves

**Condition:** Have 20 visitors in the park.

I add usable pool capacity, then make sure the entrance can actually process the
extra guests.

### Three-Star Standard

**Condition:** Reach three-star Park Prestige.

This comes naturally in the main progression save.

### Seaside Prestige

**Condition:** Reach three stars on the Beach Map.

I combine this with Three-Star Standard by using Beach as the main save.

When my Beach park hit three stars, the new expansion made the park larger
immediately while the old area was still producing lemonade queues, dirty pools
and rescue calls.

I stopped expanding and stabilized the old route before filling the new parcel.

### High Tide

**Condition:** Have 50 visitors in the park.

I increase capacity only after ticketing, food stock and cleaning can handle the
existing crowd.

### Beyond Borders

**Condition:** Expand the park three times.

I do not buy three empty expansions back to back.

After each expansion I make sure guests, staff and maintenance can reach the
new section before opening another one.

### Almost a Legend

**Condition:** Reach four-star Park Prestige.

My Beach save reached four stars after the required Ocean progression and the
larger three-Prestige attraction setup.

<h2 id="maintenance-achievements">
  Cleaning, Repair and Safety Achievements
</h2>

These are the achievements I start working on **before** the park becomes fully
automated.

Once several Janitors and Maintenance Workers are active, they can remove the
same jobs I need for my personal counters.

### Bin There, Done That

**Condition:** Clean 100 pieces of trash.

I pick up path trash and empty bins myself during the first few park stages.

### Sweep Dreams

**Condition:** Clean 500 pieces of trash.

I continue the same counter while expanding.

Food stalls, vending routes, toilets, pools and loungers are the areas I check
first.

### Trash No More

**Condition:** Clean 1,000 pieces of trash.

I use the same late-day patrol instead of trying to manufacture 1,000 pieces of
trash in a short grind.

### Mop it like it's hot

**Condition:** Mop 100 puddles.

I start this before Janitors take every puddle.

### Mopocalypse

**Condition:** Mop 500 puddles.

Large operating days create more opportunities, but I stay close enough to new
puddles that employees do not remove them first.

### Thou shall not slip

**Condition:** Mop 1,000 puddles.

I continue mopping manually even after the park can afford more cleaners.

If this counter is almost finished, I would rather spend one cleanup session
mopping myself than fire a useful Janitor permanently.

### Slip Happens

**Condition:** Have 100 visitors slip on puddles.

I let this happen naturally.

I do not intentionally turn the park into a giant slip hazard because injuries,
refunds and Satisfaction losses create more problems than the achievement is
worth.

### Nailed it!

**Condition:** Clean 500 attractions.

This is not the same as loose trash.

I clean pools, slides, toilets, showers and service buildings as I move through
the closing route.

### Screw it!

**Condition:** Repair 500 attractions.

I inspect durability before opening and again after closing.

### Hot Fix

**Condition:** Repair an attraction within five seconds of it becoming out of
order.

I do this in a compact part of the park.

I watch an attraction that is already close to failure with the hammer ready.
Trying to sprint from the opposite side of a mature park wastes most of the
five-second window.

### Lifesaver Protocol

**Condition:** Save an injured visitor with the Injector.

I keep the Injector available once injury alerts start appearing.

If a staff member can solve the problem before I arrive, I stay close to
high-traffic attractions while attempting this one.

### Pool-itzer awardee

**Condition:** Save 100 drowning guests.

I let the counter grow in the long-term save.

I do not build remote pools solely to create rescue opportunities because every
extra rescue then takes longer to reach.

### Pushed to the limit

**Condition:** Push 100 panicking guests.

I watch diving boards and other attractions where panic interactions occur and
take the action whenever I see it.

This is another counter that is much easier to accumulate during normal
operation than in one final grind.

### Sniff happens

**Condition:** Use the water gun on 100 smelly guests.

I keep the water gun in my normal tool rotation and use it whenever the smell
state appears.

### Doo-Doo Duty

**Condition:** Clean 50 poop from pools.

This is the first milestone in the same pool-waste counter.

### Zookeeper

**Condition:** Clean 100 poop from pools.

I keep using the same active park rather than restarting after the 50 milestone.

### Pool-ution Solution

**Condition:** Clean 500 poop from pools.
