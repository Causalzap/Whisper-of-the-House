Waterpark Simulator has **71 achievements in version 1.0**, including new
goals for the Beach Map, City Map, Diamond Prestige, Security, Ziplines,
Custom Slides and character customization.

I use one long-running Hard Mode Beach Map save for most progression, cleaning,
repair, sales and visitor counters. That save has been tested through four
stars. Five-star, Diamond, City Map and exact hidden-achievement triggers are
marked separately whenever I have not completed them myself.

I would not approach the list as 71 unrelated chores. Most achievements can be
combined while I keep the park running, complete Museum quests and work toward
five stars.

The route becomes slower once employees remove every manual job. Janitors take
puddles and trash, Maintenance Workers take repairs, Vendors take sales, and
Security handles disruptive guests. That is good for the park, but it can leave
several personal counters moving very slowly.

My main rule is to keep one developed save for the long counters and use short
cleanup sessions only for one-off actions such as painting a guest, playing the
trumpet, riding a Zipline or building a Custom Slide.

<h2 id="achievement-roadmap">
  My Version 1.0 Route for All 71 Achievements
</h2>

I use the Beach Map as my main long-term save because it combines the general
Prestige route with the Beach-specific three- and five-star achievements.

Opening it immediately targets:

- **Water you are waiting for?**
- **Welcome to the Coast**
- the tutorial and first-slide achievements;
- the general Prestige achievements;
- the Beach-specific three- and five-star achievements.

If I choose Hard Mode, the same save also works toward **Unstoppable Resort**,
which requires five stars on Hard.

I still check each achievement notification at the milestone. The achievement
conditions strongly overlap, but I do not assume every generic, map-specific
and difficulty-specific achievement will stack correctly without confirming
the unlock.

My broad route is:

1. tutorial and one star;
2. first employee and first expansion;
3. two stars and early maintenance counters;
4. three stars on Beach;
5. Museum and Yogi progression;
6. four stars and a larger staff team;
7. five stars on Beach and Hard;
8. all research and maximum expansion;
9. Diamond Prestige;
10. City Map to three and five stars;
11. remaining long grinds and one-off actions.

This route minimises unnecessary restarts, but I still keep a separate City Map
save because its three- and five-star achievements cannot be completed on
Beach.

<h2 id="version-1-achievements">
  What Was Added in Waterpark Simulator 1.0
</h2>

The 1.0 achievement set adds new goals tied to the Beach and City Maps,
Diamond Prestige, Security, character customization, Ziplines and Custom
Slides.

The new achievement group includes:

- **Welcome to the Coast** — start the Beach Map;
- **Seaside Prestige** — reach three stars on the Beach Map;
- **King of the Coast** — reach five stars on the Beach Map;
- **Downtown Delight** — reach three stars on the City Map;
- **City Legend** — reach five stars on the City Map;
- **Above the Stars** — reach Diamond Star Prestige;
- **Order Restored** — have Security remove 50 disruptive guests;
- **High Above the Park** — ride a Zipline;
- **Slide Architect** — create the first Custom Slide;
- **Fresh Look** — customize the character;
- **Trumpet Solo** — play a song on the trumpet.

This is why I do not use an older achievement route built only around the
original Early Access list. A completion save now needs both maps, the new
late-game Prestige target and several 1.0 systems that did not exist in the
older guides.

I still combine most of these with the main Beach save. City Map achievements
need their own park, while Fresh Look, Trumpet Solo, High Above the Park and
Slide Architect can be completed during a short cleanup session.

### Water you are waiting for?

**Condition:** Open the park for the first time.

I unlock this naturally after completing the opening construction and using the
park's Open control.

### Caution! Slippery when wet

**Condition:** Build the first slide.

The tutorial route supplies a basic pool and slide. I complete this before
spending money on decorations or extra staff.

### Lifeguard-ish

**Condition:** Finish the tutorial.

I follow the starting task list rather than disabling or abandoning the opening
steps.

### One-Star Wonder

**Condition:** Reach one-star Park Prestige.

This is part of normal progression. I complete the current Road to the Star
tasks before starting an unrelated large build.

### Visionary

**Condition:** Expand the park once.

I buy the first expansion only after the starting area can operate through a
normal day. The expansion itself creates more cleaning distance and does not
automatically create revenue.

### Double Shine

**Condition:** Reach two-star Park Prestige.

I keep the park compact and complete the task list rather than trying to build
the final layout at two stars.

### Making Waves

**Condition:** Have 20 visitors in the park.

I increase capacity with usable pools and slides, then make sure the entrance
can process the extra demand.

### Three-Star Standard

**Condition:** Reach three-star Park Prestige.

On the Beach Map, I reached this milestone at the moment the next park expansion
was purchased.

### Seaside Prestige

**Condition:** Reach three stars on the Beach Map.

I combine this with Three-Star Standard by making Beach my main progression
save.

The three-star expansion created a new operational problem immediately: the
park became larger while guests were still requesting lemonade, drowning and
dirtying the existing pools. I do not treat the star notification as permission
to build the whole new area at once.

### High Tide

**Condition:** Have 50 visitors in the park.

I distinguish simultaneous park capacity from the total guests served in the
Daily Report. I raise pool and park capacity, then increase traffic only after
ticketing and stock are stable.

### Beyond Borders

**Condition:** Expand the park three times.

I do not purchase three empty parcels in a row. Each expansion receives at
least one complete service route before I open another.

### Almost a Legend

**Condition:** Reach four-star Park Prestige.

My four-star milestone came after building the required three-Prestige Ocean
slide and supporting pool.

<h2 id="maintenance-achievements">
  Cleaning, Repair and Safety Achievements
</h2>

The maintenance achievements should be worked on from the first day.

I do not leave all of them until a fully automated late-game park. By then,
Janitors and Maintenance Workers may complete most qualifying actions before I
reach them.

### Bin There, Done That

**Condition:** Clean 100 pieces of trash.

I pick up path trash and empty bins personally during the opening park.

### Sweep Dreams

**Condition:** Clean 500 pieces of trash.

I continue the same counter while expanding instead of expecting a short
cleanup session to finish it.

### Trash No More

**Condition:** Clean 1,000 pieces of trash.

I patrol food, vending, toilet, pool and lounger routes after closing. These are
the highest-yield areas because guests generate repeated waste there.

### Mop it like it's hot

**Condition:** Mop 100 puddles.

I start before hiring multiple Janitors.

### Mopocalypse

**Condition:** Mop 500 puddles.

Rain and large operating days create more opportunities, but I stay close to
new puddles because employees can remove them first.

### Thou shall not slip

**Condition:** Mop 1,000 puddles.

I keep mopping manually even after the park can afford a second Janitor. The
second cleaner is useful for Satisfaction, but it slows my personal counter.

### Slip Happens

**Condition:** Have 100 visitors slip on puddles.

This counter conflicts with safe park management.

I let it accumulate naturally during early and mid-game operation rather than
deliberately covering the park in puddles. Fines and injuries can cost more than
the achievement is worth.

### Nailed it!

**Condition:** Clean 500 attractions.

This is separate from cleaning loose trash. I clean pools, slides, toilets,
showers and service buildings during the closing circuit.

### Screw it!

**Condition:** Repair 500 attractions.

I inspect durability before opening and repair again after closing.
