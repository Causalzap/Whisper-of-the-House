  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Get All 7 Golden Feathers">

  Queen's Castle opens at **7/7 Golden Feathers**. The seven rewards come from
  **Lady of the Drains**, **Shrine of the Seven Sons**, **Starsinger**, rebuilding
  **Lutter**, **Squinton's birthday party**, the **Forest King**, and escaping
  **Webdrench Inn**. Their middle-game order can vary, so match your missing
  Feather to the event you have not actually completed.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>


<div className="my-4 text-sm text-slate-400">
  Last updated: September 24, 2026.
</div>


You can reach the base of **Queen's Castle** before the story is ready to let
you inside. The guard checks one thing: your Golden Feather total.

You need **7/7**.

These are not seven loose pickups hidden in ordinary rooms. Each Feather is the
reward for finishing a boss or larger event somewhere in the kingdom, which is
why running through old corridors rarely fixes a counter stuck at 5/7 or 6/7.

The seven sources are:

- **Lady of the Drains**
- **Shrine of the Seven Sons**
- **Starsinger**
- **Lutter's four missing pieces**
- **Squinton's birthday party**
- **Forest King**
- **Webdrench Inn**

Several of them can be finished in a different order. The event name is more
useful than calling one Feather “number three” or “number five.”


<h2
  id="lady-of-the-drains"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Lady of the Drains
</h2>

The Drains Feather comes from the boss at the bottom of the broken elevator.

Repair the lift with all **3 Elevator Parts**, ride it down, and defeat
**Lady of the Drains**. The Golden Feather is awarded immediately after the
encounter.

    Lady of the Drains awards a Golden Feather after the elevator boss fight.
  </figcaption>

If the elevator is still showing **1/3** or **2/3**, finish the repair before
looking for the Feather. <Link href="/well-dweller/elevator-parts-locations">The three Elevator Parts are spread across the upper and lower Drains routes, with Part 3 reached from the far-left lower branch</Link>.

For the route from Wind Ride through the boss,
<Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">continue through The Drains until Lady of the Drains is defeated</Link>.


<h2
  id="shrine-seven-sons"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Shrine of the Seven Sons
</h2>

The **Shrine of the Seven Sons** is reached while exploring the Midnight Drench
side of the kingdom.

On the approach, the surrounding route loops through water-assisted traversal
and reconnecting passages. When you reach the split where the path can continue
left or climb upward, take the **upper route**.

Keep going until you find the small shrine with the engraving:

**“My seven sons, where have you gone? I will wait inside until you return to me.”**

Entering the shrine starts the encounter.

Defeat the Shrine of the Seven Sons and the **Stormy Night** objective
completes. The Golden Feather appears immediately afterward.

  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-golden-feather-shrine-seven-sons.webp"
    alt="Golden Feather awarded after the Shrine of the Seven Sons encounter in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Stormy Night completes when the shrine encounter ends.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Once the Feather has appeared, that objective is finished. The surrounding
Midnight Drench rooms may still contain other paths and collectibles, but there
is not a second Feather hidden inside the shrine.


<h2
  id="starsinger"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Starsinger at the Lookout Tower
</h2>

The **Starsinger** Feather sits at the end of the later **Lookout Tower**
climb.

The tower can be entered before you have enough movement to finish every upper
gap. If one of the long aerial crossings still falls short, continue other
reachable parts of the tower first. The route contains upgrades that increase
your flight time, and the later gaps become possible once that movement has
been extended far enough.

One useful checkpoint is **Lookout Tower Page 4**. From that section, continue
through the newly reachable loops and shortcuts. When the route starts opening
above you, work **up and left** through the remaining climb.

A safe tent sits shortly before the final ascent.

That is the point where the different approaches through the tower converge.
Continue upward from the tent and you reach **Starsinger**.

Defeating Starsinger completes **Song of the Stars** and awards another Golden
Feather.

  <Image

    width={1600}

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Starsinger waits beyond the final safe tent on the upper tower route.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

If you can explore the tower but cannot make the last long flight, the problem
is not the boss door. Finish the reachable flight-time upgrades and return to
the upper route.


<h2
  id="lutter"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Rebuild Lutter From All 4 Pieces
</h2>

Lutter's Feather is the easiest one to mistake for an ordinary collectible
search because the objective sends you back across four different regions.

After learning what happened to him, speak with **Whisper**. Her spell reveals
that the Queen tore Lutter apart and left his body in four places:

- **Head:** below the Queen's Castle pipe in **The Bog**
- **Arms:** deep inside the **Desiccated Palace**
- **Legs:** high in **Graven Valley**
- **Torso:** deep within **The Drains**

Whisper also gives you the key that opens the **golden gates** on these routes.

You do not need to search an entire region from one edge to the other. Use Tent
Travel to approach each marked area from the nearest safe tent, open the golden
route, collect the body part, and move on to the next location.

Finding one, two, or three pieces does not change the Golden Feather count.

All **four** must be taken back to Whisper.

She stitches Lutter together, and Lutter reveals something he stole from the
Queen's carriage: a Golden Feather.

If Lutter is already standing back together, this Feather is done. Any other
golden-gate pickups left on the map belong to separate objectives.


<h2
  id="squinton-party"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Squinton's Birthday Party
</h2>

The Burial Vault route eventually points toward **Squinton's birthday party**.

Follow the passages marked toward the party until the path reaches the
**Gravedigger**. This boss blocks the final stretch, but defeating it does not
award the Feather.

After the Gravedigger falls, keep going.

The route continues upward to the party room. Enter the gathering and let the
birthday conversation finish. One of the guests has no extra present prepared
and hands Glimmer a Golden Feather instead.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-golden-feather-squinton-party.webp"
    alt="Golden Feather received during Squinton's birthday party in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}

  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The Feather is given at the party, not when the Gravedigger dies.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

This is one of the most useful checks when the counter looks wrong.

**Gravedigger defeated + party not reached = Feather still missing.**


<h2
  id="forest-king"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Forest King
</h2>

The **Autumn Forest** event centers on the Festival of the Sleeping King.

When the Forest King wakes, the visitors present their offerings. Glimmer
arrives without a proper gift and ends up offering old berries from the
Hunter's Cabin.

The king does not take it well.

That failed offering starts the fight.

Finish the encounter and stay for the sequence afterward. A better offering is
presented, the Forest King accepts it, and the event resolves with **Win Over
the Forest King**.

The Golden Feather is then handed to Glimmer.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-golden-feather-forest-king.webp"
    alt="Golden Feather received after completing the Forest King event in Autumn Forest"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Finish the fight and the following offering scene to receive the Feather.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

You do not need to leave the event and search for a special food item yourself.
The sequence continues after the fight and supplies what is needed.


<h2
  id="webdrench-inn"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Golden Feather — Escape Webdrench Inn
</h2>

The **Webdrench Inn** sequence begins when the innkeeper mistakes Glimmer for
the Queen.

Work through the inn until she asks for a signature in her **guest book**.
She tells you to meet her in the back, where the book is waiting.

When Glimmer cannot provide the expected royal signature, the disguise falls
apart. The innkeeper realizes she has the wrong bird and the objective changes
immediately:

**run.**

This is an escape sequence, not a fight you can win by stopping to trade
damage.

The chase is long enough that a late mistake matters. Failing before the exit
can send you back through the whole escape, so keep moving toward the route
ahead instead of turning around to attack.

Once you reach the end, **Late Night Guest** completes and the Golden Feather
appears.

Reaching the guest book does **not** count as completion. The Feather is tied
to surviving the escape.


<h2
  id="missing-feather"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stuck at 6/7 Golden Feathers?
</h2>

At **6/7**, compare the events you have actually finished rather than searching
for another loose pickup.

Use these completion states:

- **Lady of the Drains:** the elevator is repaired, Lady is dead, and the
  post-fight Feather was collected.
- **Shrine of the Seven Sons:** **Stormy Night** completed and the shrine
  Feather appeared.
- **Starsinger:** **Song of the Stars** completed after the Lookout Tower boss.
- **Lutter:** all four pieces were returned to Whisper and Lutter was rebuilt.
- **Squinton:** you continued past the Gravedigger and reached the birthday
  party.
- **Forest King:** the fight and the following offering sequence both finished.
- **Webdrench Inn:** you survived the chase and completed **Late Night Guest**.

Lutter, Squinton, and Webdrench Inn are the easiest to misread because each has
an intermediate state that looks more final than it really is.

A partial Lutter set is not enough. Killing the Gravedigger is not enough.
Reaching the Webdrench guest book is not enough.


<h2
  id="after-seven"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return to Queen's Castle at 7/7
</h2>

The seventh Golden Feather changes the objective to returning to the base of
**Queen's Castle**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-queens-castle-entry.webp"
    alt="Queen's Castle entrance after collecting all seven Golden Feathers in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The castle guard allows entry once the total reaches 7/7.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

There is no eighth Golden Feather and no additional collectible total attached
to the gate.

Return to the same castle entrance that blocked Glimmer earlier. With **7/7**
collected, the guard lets you through and Queen's Castle becomes the next
story destination.
