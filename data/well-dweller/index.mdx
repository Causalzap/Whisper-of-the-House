Well Dweller starts with a fairly direct trip for food, then gradually opens
into a kingdom where several family objectives, bosses, movement upgrades, and
Golden Feather events can be active at the same time.

The main progression runs roughly like this:

**Night Garden → Hunter's Cabin → The Bog → The Drains → family and Golden
Feather objectives → The Docks and upper regions → 7/7 Golden Feathers →
Queen's Castle → post-Queen route.**

Not every ledge or locked passage should be cleared when you first see it.
Slingshot, Climb, Wind Ride, later movement upgrades, and finally Soar all make
earlier routes accessible.


<h2
  id="walkthrough"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Do You Go Next?
</h2>

The opening regions have a clear order, while the middle of the game allows
more flexibility.

After The Drains, concentrate on major objectives that rescue family members,
unlock movement, open another region, or increase the Golden Feather counter.
If one route suddenly asks for movement that feels well beyond your current
abilities, another active objective will often provide what you need.

<Link href="/well-dweller/walkthrough">Follow the complete Well Dweller progression order when you are unsure which major region or objective should come next</Link>.


<h2
  id="night-garden"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Night Garden and Hunter's Cabin
</h2>

Mother first sends Glimmer out to bring food home.

Cross Night Garden, learn to heal with Oil Vials, defeat the
**Groundskeeper**, and enter the Hunter's Cabin. Inside, helping Looter gives
you the **Flaming Vessel**, while **Slingshot** becomes the first major
movement upgrade.

Take the berries from the Hunter's meal and return them to Mother. The Hunter
appears immediately afterward, turning the next sequence into an escape rather
than a fight.

<Link href="/well-dweller/night-garden-hunters-cabin-walkthrough">Use the Night Garden and Hunter's Cabin walkthrough for the Groundskeeper, Flaming Vessel, Hunter's Locket, Slingshot, berries, and Hunter escape</Link>.


<h2
  id="the-bog"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Bog
</h2>

The Bog is where the kingdom starts branching.

You meet **Ilda**, gain access to her map station, and can reach the entrance
to Queen's Castle much earlier than the story allows you inside. The guard
requires **7 Golden Feathers**, so the castle is not your destination yet.

Continue through The Bog until you defeat **The Swarm** and receive
**Climb**. The return path toward Night Garden then unlocks **Tent Travel**,
making later visits to old regions much faster.

<Link href="/well-dweller/the-bog-walkthrough">Follow The Bog from Ilda and the early castle gate through the Swarm, Climb, Tent Travel, and the route toward The Drains</Link>.


<h2
  id="the-drains"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Drains
</h2>

The Drains add **Wind Ride** and eventually lead to a broken elevator.

The lift will not move until you collect **3 Elevator Parts**. Once repaired,
it carries Glimmer directly to **Lady of the Drains**, who awards a Golden
Feather when defeated.

<Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">Use The Drains walkthrough for Wind Ride, Willow, the elevator sequence, Lady of the Drains, and the Golden Feather reward</Link>.

If the only problem is an incomplete elevator counter,
<Link href="/well-dweller/elevator-parts-locations">follow the three Elevator Part locations from the upper Drains routes to the final far-left lower path</Link>.


<h2
  id="golden-feathers"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Collect 7 Golden Feathers
</h2>

The seven Golden Feathers come from major events rather than ordinary room
pickups.

They are tied to:

- Lady of the Drains;
- Shrine of the Seven Sons;
- Starsinger;
- rebuilding Lutter;
- Squinton's birthday party;
- the Forest King;
- Webdrench Inn.

The middle-game order can vary, but Queen's Castle always checks the same
number:

**7/7.**

If the counter stops at 5/7 or 6/7, identify which event has not actually
reached its reward state instead of searching random rooms.

<Link href="/well-dweller/golden-feathers-locations">Check all seven Golden Feather events, including the conditions that count as completion, when the castle gate is still closed</Link>.


<h2
  id="queens-castle"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Enter Queen's Castle at 7/7
</h2>

Return to the castle entrance once all seven Feathers are collected.

The guard now lets Glimmer through. Continue across the castle, resolve the
remaining family objectives, defeat the **Silver Doll**, and reach the Queen.

There is no requirement to collect every Trinket, Spirit, Page, or upgrade
before this point.

Finish the Queen sequence and let the credits roll.


<h2
  id="after-the-queen"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Continue After the Queen
</h2>

Load the same save after the credits.

A new route opens inside Queen's Castle and gives Glimmer **Soar**. A map then
marks five hidden locations around the kingdom, each tied to a Spirit Fragment.

Complete all five marked objectives and return to Night Garden for
**Reunite the Flock**.

<Link href="/well-dweller/true-ending">Continue from the Queen through Soar, the five Spirit Fragments, and Reunite the Flock</Link>.


<h2
  id="completion"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Remaining 100% Requirements
</h2>

Once Soar is available, the kingdom is much easier to clean up.

The major completion totals include:

- **41 achievements**
- **32 Trinkets**
- **50 Trinket Slots**
- **20 ordinary Spirits**
- all three story-page collections
- every Trinket upgraded to its maximum level

Late-game Map Markers can reveal missed Trinkets, Slots, Spirits, Pages, and
Tokens in rooms that have already been visited. Unexplored rooms still need to
be checked separately.

<Link href="/well-dweller/100-percent-completion">Use the 41-achievement tracker and final cleanup order when the remaining work is collectibles, Spirits, Pages, upgrades, or one-off achievement conditions</Link>.
