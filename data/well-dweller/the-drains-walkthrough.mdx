The Drains starts as a vertical exploration area, but its required progression
has a clear sequence:

**Wind Ride → 3 Elevator Parts → repaired elevator → Lady of the Drains →
Golden Feather.**

Willow's missing-frog story, Trinkets, locked doors, and unreachable upper
rooms sit around that route. Some are worth picking up while they are directly
on your path, but the elevator is what eventually opens the boss.


<h2
  id="enter-the-drains"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Enter The Drains After Getting Climb
</h2>

After finishing The Bog with **Climb** and **Tent Travel**, descend into
The Drains.

The early rooms introduce vertical shafts and strong air currents. Climb helps
with the walls, but the drafts are not something you are expected to overpower
with ordinary jumping.

You also meet **Willow**, who came into the region looking for friends that
disappeared after following candied spider eggs into the drains.

His route and the frog memorials give you useful landmarks as you move through
the area, but you do not need to solve every frog-related branch before the main
route can continue.


<h2
  id="wind-ride"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Unlock Wind Ride Before Chasing the Elevator Parts
</h2>

Keep exploring the opening Drains rooms until you reach the ability pickup for
**Wind Ride**.

The input is simple:

**hold jump while standing in a wind draft.**

Instead of fighting the upward current, let it carry Glimmer.

Once Wind Ride is unlocked, several rooms that looked awkward on the way in
become straightforward. Drafts can lift you through shafts and give you the
height needed to reach the upper paths around the elevator search.

Do not treat Wind Ride like a free-flight ability. It only works where the
environment provides a wind current, so leaving the draft ends the lift.


<h2
  id="willow"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Follow Willow Through the Frog Memorial Rooms
</h2>

Willow's story continues deeper into The Drains.

At one point you reach a frog shrine with a memorial for **Eagle**. Other
plaques mention missing frogs such as **Pip and Sprig**, which makes these rooms
useful landmarks when the area starts looping back on itself.

The game also uses the missing children and the candied spider eggs to explain
what happened in the region: the children followed the eggs into The Drains
and never returned.

For progression, the important thing is that these memorial rooms overlap with
the Elevator Part search. You will naturally pass them while collecting the
three components.


<h2
  id="elevator-part-1"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Get Elevator Part 1 on the Upper Route
</h2>

After the Willow section, you reach a split with a route downward and another
path leading up.

Take the **upper path first**.

That branch runs past a large swinging mace and the candied spider eggs. Keep
climbing until you reach the first Elevator Part.

The objective updates to:

**1/3 — Find two more.**

A locked golden gate is nearby, but it is not required for the elevator. Leave
it alone if you cannot open it yet.

The same part of the route also gives access to the **Melted Candle** Trinket,
which makes Glimmer invincible for **2 seconds after healing**. It can be useful
for players who are already relying heavily on Oil Vials, but it is optional
for progression.


<h2
  id="broken-elevator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Find the Broken Elevator
</h2>

Dropping into the lower branch eventually brings you to the lift.

Interacting with it confirms the requirement:

**3 Elevator Parts.**

There is no nearby switch that bypasses the repair, and the elevator does not
partially activate at 1/3 or 2/3.

Remember this room. Once the third part is collected, this is where you return.


<h2
  id="elevator-parts"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Collect Elevator Parts 2 and 3
</h2>

The second part comes from another branch where a **candied spider egg** moves
upward. Follow it into the upper route and continue until you reach the section
near the memorial for **Eagle**.

That pickup brings the counter to **2/3**.

The final part is more misleading because one nearby pit looks almost possible
with Climb, Slingshot, and Matchick placement. The geometry prevents a clean
crossing with your current tools.

Leave that pit.

The route to Part 3 is farther across The Drains. Backtrack, move **all the way
left** through the regular platform path, and look for the last unexplored
route that drops downward.

That lower branch leads to the final Elevator Part.

If you are already at **2/3** and want the exact turns, landmarks, and return
path, <Link href="/well-dweller/elevator-parts-locations">follow all three Elevator Part locations from the first pickup through Part 3</Link>.


<h2
  id="return-to-elevator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return to the Elevator at 3/3
</h2>

The repair objective is finished once the counter reaches **3/3**.

Work back toward the nearby safe tent before taking the elevator if your Oil
Vials are depleted. The lift does not open another long exploration section;
the next room is the boss arena.

There is no requirement to clear the locked golden gate, cross the earlier
blocked pit, or finish every high route first.

Ride the elevator down when your healing is ready.


<h2
  id="lady-of-the-drains"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Beat Lady of the Drains
</h2>

The elevator deposits Glimmer directly into **Lady of the Drains**.

Her most useful visual cue is the moment her face opens. That animation comes
before the lightning attack, giving you enough warning to move before the
strike lands.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-lady-of-the-drains-lightning.webp"
    alt="Lady of the Drains opening her face before a lightning attack in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Her face opens before the lightning strike.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The arena is more dangerous when you stay airborne without a reason. Avoiding
the lightning with a panic jump can put Glimmer onto the spikes, turning one
mistake into two hits.

A steadier rhythm works better:

1. watch Lady rather than the floor;
2. move when the face-open animation begins;
3. land somewhere safe before attacking again;
4. use the next quiet window for damage or healing.

The **Flaming Vessel** is useful here, but save it for a moment when Lady has
finished an attack. Throwing it while you are already correcting a bad jump
makes it easier to lose control of the arena.

Healing follows the same rule. Create enough room to finish the heal rather
than using an Oil Vial while lightning and spikes are already forcing another
movement input.


<h2
  id="lady-of-the-drains-failures"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Lady of the Drains Attempts Fall Apart
</h2>

The fight usually goes wrong through positioning rather than lack of damage.

Three mistakes compound quickly:

- jumping after the lightning tell has already started;
- escaping one attack only to land on the spikes;
- trying to heal or throw the Flaming Vessel while another movement correction
  is still needed.

When the fight speeds up, give priority to the next safe landing.

Lady leaves enough openings to finish the fight without attacking through every
pattern. Losing one damage opportunity costs less than losing an Oil Vial and
another chunk of health during the same scramble.

There is no need to return to the castle immediately after receiving it.
Continue through the kingdom until the remaining Feather objectives are
complete.

<h2
  id="after-the-boss"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What to Do After Lady of the Drains
</h2>

The rooms above the boss still contain routes that are not meant to be fully
cleared on this visit.

After the fight, you can see higher exits, locked doors, and optional
challenges. Some remain out of reach even though Lady is dead.

That does not block progression.

At this point the required Drains sequence is complete:

- Wind Ride is unlocked;
- the elevator is repaired;
- Lady of the Drains is defeated;
- the Golden Feather is collected.

Tent Travel makes the unfinished rooms inexpensive to revisit after later
movement upgrades.

Move back into the wider kingdom instead of spending another long loop trying
to force every high passage now. From here, multiple family objectives and
regions begin to overlap, so <Link href="/well-dweller/walkthrough">continue with the main progression order when you need to decide which region to tackle next</Link>.
