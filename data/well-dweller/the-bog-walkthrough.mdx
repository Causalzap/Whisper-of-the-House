  <GuideQuickAnswer title="What Do You Do in The Bog?">

  Follow the reachable Bog routes until you meet **Ilda**, pass the early
  Queen's Castle gate, and reach **The Swarm**. Defeat the Swarm to unlock
  **Climb**, then work back toward Night Garden and activate **Tent Travel**.
  With both unlocked, continue into **The Drains**.

The Bog is the first area where Well Dweller starts showing more paths than
Glimmer can reasonably finish on the same visit.

You arrive with Slingshot and the Flaming Vessel, which opens several routes
that were impossible around Night Garden. The Bog immediately answers that
progress by placing taller walls, locked golden gates, high platforms, and
Queen's Castle itself in view.

The important sequence is simpler than the map first suggests:

**meet Ilda → reach the Queen's Castle gate → continue through the lower Bog
route → defeat the Swarm → unlock Climb → activate Tent Travel → head for The
Drains.**


<h2
  id="enter-the-bog"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Follow the Reachable Bog Routes First
</h2>

The opening Bog rooms contain a mixture of combat paths, side loops, Trinket
Points, Spirits, and pickups sitting just outside Slingshot range.

Take the routes that work cleanly with the movement you already have. If a
platform still requires Glimmer to scrape the edge of a wall or make a jump
that feels just beyond Slingshot's normal reach, leave it for later.

One useful early pickup is the **Hair Pin**.

Its effect is straightforward: if you go **8 seconds without attacking**, your
next attack deals double damage. That can reward a patient opening hit or a
long defensive pause, but nothing in The Bog requires it.

The main path keeps pushing farther into the region.


<h2
  id="ilda-camp"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Meet Ilda and Light the Campfire
</h2>

Farther into The Bog, you meet **Ilda** while she is gathering firewood.

Follow her to the camp. She already has the wood ready but needs Glimmer's fire
to light it, which starts the camping sequence.

Afterward, Ilda leaves a note and later appears at the tent with a new map
station.

The first map can point out major places around the kingdom when you no longer
know which large objective to pursue.

The second deals with collectibles you have already passed. It only works for
rooms you have visited, so a marker does not mean every item on that route is
currently reachable.

For now, keep moving through The Bog rather than turning the new map into a
full collectible sweep.


<h2
  id="queen-castle-gate"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Queen's Castle Requires 7 Golden Feathers
</h2>

One Bog branch reaches the base of **Queen's Castle** surprisingly early.

Use the intercom at the gate.

The guard asks about the birds and feathers you have brought, then explains
that the Queen now wants **Golden Feathers** for her dress. Entry requires:

**7 Golden Feathers.**

  <Image

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Queen's Castle stays closed until the Golden Feather counter reaches 7/7.
  </figcaption>

There is no Bog switch or nearby combat room that removes this requirement.

Once the guard gives you the 7-Feather objective, leave the castle and continue
through the kingdom. When this requirement becomes the thing blocking late-game
progress, <Link href="/well-dweller/golden-feathers-locations">finish the seven Golden Feather events and return to the gate at 7/7</Link>.


<h2
  id="golden-gates"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Leave the Golden Gates Closed for Now
</h2>

The Bog also begins showing **golden gates** that Glimmer cannot open yet.

These are different from the Queen's Castle entrance. They are smaller locked
routes scattered around the kingdom, and seeing one does not mean you missed a
local key.

The key arrives later through another objective.

Mark the gate if there is something obvious behind it and continue. Spending
time searching adjacent Bog rooms for a key will not open it.


<h2
  id="ground-dash"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Dash From the Ground
</h2>

The late Bog route contains an obstacle that can look like it needs another
air-movement upgrade.

The missing detail is the dash itself.

**Glimmer has to be on the ground when the dash begins.**

Trying to turn it into an aerial dash makes the gap and the incoming attack
pattern look much harder than they are. Land first, then dash from the floor
when the opening appears.

This becomes especially important as you approach the Swarm encounter, where a
long ground-level attack can cover more space than a normal jump comfortably
clears.


<h2
  id="swarm"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Defeat the Swarm
</h2>

The major fight at the end of this Bog stretch is **The Swarm**.

Its arena rewards controlled movement more than repeated jumping. One of the
most troublesome attacks comes from a slam followed by a long wave across the
ground. Trying to clear the entire wave with a normal jump can leave Glimmer
inside it as the attack continues.

Use the movement you just practiced: stay grounded until the attack commits,
then slide or dash through the dangerous line instead of trying to float over
the whole thing.

Between larger attacks, use normal hits to refill the Flaming Vessel and throw
it when the arena gives you enough room to finish the animation safely.

The fight ends with **Buggy Business** and reveals an ability egg.

That egg contains the movement upgrade you came here for.


<h2
  id="climb"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Unlock Climb After the Swarm
</h2>

Collect the egg after the fight to learn **Climb**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-climb-unlock.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Climb ability showing wall climbing and the stamina meter"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Climb lets Glimmer scale walls until the wing-shaped stamina meter runs out.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Hold jump while pressing against a wall to climb.

Clinging and climbing consume stamina. Reaching solid ground restores it.

Wall jumping does not behave exactly like holding onto one wall continuously,
so tall shafts often give you more room to work with than the stamina meter
first suggests. Alternate walls where possible rather than trying to crawl up
one uninterrupted surface.

Climb immediately opens several vertical Bog connections and also makes older
Night Garden routes easier to understand.


<h2
  id="after-climb"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use Climb to Work Back Toward Night Garden
</h2>

After unlocking Climb, continue through the newly usable wall routes instead of
returning to every collectible you passed.

The most important destination is the connection back toward **Night Garden**.

Some earlier high passages now work cleanly, and the route gradually brings
Glimmer back toward a safe tent near the opening side of the kingdom.

This return trip is part of progression rather than a signal that The Bog was a
dead end.


<h2
  id="tent-travel"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Activate Tent Travel
</h2>

At the tent near Night Garden, you unlock **Tent Travel**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-fast-travel-unlock.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Tent Travel allowing travel to previously discovered safe tents"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Tent Travel lets you select previously discovered safe tents from the map.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

From this point onward, any safe tent you have already found can become a fast
travel destination.

The game also broadens the main objective here: search the kingdom for your
missing family and collect Golden Feathers.

You no longer need to finish every branch in the exact order you first
encountered it. Tent Travel lets you leave a blocked route, advance somewhere
else, and come back later without repeating the entire journey.


<h2
  id="blocked-bog-routes"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Some Bog Routes Still Need Later Abilities
</h2>

Climb does not make every remaining Bog marker reachable.

A few high exits and awkward side passages still ask for movement you have not
earned yet. If Climb gets you noticeably closer but the final crossing still
has no sensible landing, there is no need to force it now.

The same applies to the golden gates.

The Bog remains relevant later, including during objectives that send you back
for gated pickups, but those returns are much faster once Tent Travel and the
later movement tools are available.


<h2
  id="where-next"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go to The Drains Next
</h2>

With **Climb** and **Tent Travel** unlocked, the next major region is
**The Drains**.

If you are looking for **Wind Ride**, keep going. It is obtained inside The
Drains rather than The Bog.

The opening there introduces Willow, wind currents, the broken elevator, and
the search for **3 Elevator Parts**. Repairing that lift eventually takes you
to Lady of the Drains and another Golden Feather.

<Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">Continue into The Drains for Wind Ride, the elevator repair, and Lady of the Drains</Link>.
