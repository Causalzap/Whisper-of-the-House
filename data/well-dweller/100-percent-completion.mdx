Well Dweller's completion requirements are easier to manage once the different
counters are kept separate.

The biggest totals to watch are **32 Trinkets**, **50 Trinket Slots**, and
**20 ordinary Spirits**, but those alone do not finish everything. The full
achievement set also includes three complete story-page collections, every
Trinket upgraded to its maximum level, story bosses and family events, the
post-Queen route, and several actions that never appear as collectible markers.

<h2
  id="completion-plan"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Main Route and Unlock Soar First
</h2>

Well Dweller regularly shows you rooms and pickups before your movement kit can
reach them. Climb opens one layer of the map, Wind Ride opens another, later
flight improvements extend long crossings, and **Soar** finally makes several
high routes practical.

For that reason, the cleanest point to begin the final sweep is after:

- collecting all **7 Golden Feathers**;
- entering Queen's Castle;
- finishing the remaining family objectives;
- battling the Queen;
- continuing the save after the credits;
- unlocking **Soar**.

If Queen's Castle is still closed,
<Link href="/well-dweller/golden-feathers-locations">finish whichever Golden Feather event is still missing and return when the counter reaches 7/7</Link>.

The post-Queen route also contains the five Spirit Fragment objectives and
**Reunite the Flock**. <Link href="/well-dweller/true-ending">Follow the Soar and Spirit Fragment route when you are ready to finish the remaining ending sequence</Link>.

Once Soar is available, most of the kingdom can be checked without repeatedly
discovering that a route still needs another movement upgrade.


<h2
  id="map-cleanup"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use Map Markers for Rooms You Have Already Visited
</h2>

The late-game **Map Markers** station can mark uncollected objects in rooms
Glimmer has already entered.

The available categories are:

- **Trinkets — 100**
- **Trinket Slots — 75**
- **Spirits — 75**
- **Pages — 50**
- **Tokens — 75**

That last condition matters.

Buying a marker does not reveal an untouched section of the kingdom. A blank
part of the map can still contain a Trinket, Spirit, Page, Slot, or Token if
you never entered the room in the first place.

A late cleanup therefore needs two passes working together: clear the remaining
icons in explored rooms, then inspect obvious map gaps and unopened connections.

Tent Travel keeps that process short. Move to the nearest safe tent, clear the
small group of remaining markers around it, then jump to the next region rather
than crossing the kingdom on foot.


<h2
  id="story-progress"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Story and Boss Achievements
</h2>

A large part of the 41-achievement set comes naturally from the main route and
major side events:

- **Dark Garden** — defeat the Groundskeeper
- **Snatched Up** — meet the Hunter
- **A Warm Fire** — go camping with Ilda
- **Buggy Business** — defeat the Swarm
- **Down the Drain** — defeat Lady of the Drains
- **Littlest Bird** — collect Egbert
- **Hide and Seek** — evade the Queen
- **Stormy Night** — defeat the Shrine of the Seven Sons
- **Into the Deep** — gain access to a water vessel
- **Song of the Stars** — defeat Starsinger
- **Double Trouble** — collect Thimble and Thistle
- **Fancy Feast** — win over the Forest King
- **In the Dollmaker's House** — collect Magda
- **Stitched Up** — put Looter back together again
- **Dig Deep** — defeat the Gravedigger
- **Late Night Guest** — escape Webdrench Inn
- **Sharp and Shiny** — defeat the Silver Doll
- **The End** — battle the Queen

Several of these also feed directly into the Golden Feather route. If one is
still missing and the corresponding area has not been completed,
<Link href="/well-dweller/walkthrough">return to the main progression order and finish that stage before continuing the collectible cleanup</Link>.


<h2
  id="trinkets"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Collect All 32 Trinkets
</h2>

The Trinket achievements use three collection milestones:

- **Hobby Collector** — collect 1 Trinket
- **Experienced Collector** — collect 10 Trinkets
- **Expert Collector** — collect **32 Trinkets**

Trinkets can come from exploration, shops, challenge rewards, side rooms, and
routes that only become comfortable after later movement upgrades.

Keep the **32 collected** requirement separate from **Fully Stocked**. Owning a
Trinket and maxing its upgrades are two different completion checks.

During the last sweep, clear Trinket map markers first and inspect any remaining
unvisited rooms before spending large amounts of currency on upgrades. It is
much easier to finish the upgrade requirement once the collection itself is
already complete.


<h2
  id="trinket-slots"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach 50 Trinket Slots
</h2>

Trinket capacity has its own achievements:

- **Ten Slots** — collect 10 Trinket Slots
- **50 Slots** — collect **50 Trinket Slots**

This counter is independent from the 32-Trinket collection.

A run can have all 32 Trinkets and still be below 50 Slots, or reach 50 Slots
while still missing Trinkets. Slots can come from pickups and from Looter, so
check the actual achievement counter rather than assuming one category proves
the other is finished.

If the remaining physical Slots are hard to locate, use the **Trinket Slots**
Map Marker after revisiting any obvious unexplored rooms.


<h2
  id="spirits"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return All 20 Ordinary Spirits
</h2>

Ordinary Spirits have three milestones:

- **Solo Spirit** — return 1 Spirit
- **Halfway There** — return 10 Spirits
- **Full Flock** — return **20 Spirits**

The map tracks these Spirits separately from the five **Spirit Fragments**
introduced after the Queen.

Spirit Fragments belong to the later reunion route. Ordinary Spirits belong to
the 20-Spirit counter used by **Full Flock**.

Do not use the five-Fragment total as evidence that the ordinary collection is
finished. Check the normal Spirit counter directly and return the remaining
birds through the spirit-return system.

If all five post-Queen Fragments are complete but the Night Garden reunion is
not triggering, also check the ordinary Spirit total before searching for a
sixth Fragment. There are only five Spirit Fragments.


<h2
  id="story-pages"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish All Three Story-Page Collections
</h2>

The first Page you collect unlocks:

**Once Upon a Time...** — collect a story page.

Three later achievements require complete sets:

- **Above the Kingdom** — collect all pages for *The Lookout Tower*
- **The Tattered Doll** — collect all pages for *The Dollmaker's House*
- **The First-Born Daughter** — collect all pages for *The Witch's Bargain*

The individual pickup order does not matter.

During cleanup, buy the **Pages** Map Marker and remove the visible page icons
from rooms you have already explored. If one story set still refuses to
complete after those markers are gone, the likely problem is an unexplored room
or unopened connection rather than another page hidden in a room you have
already cleared.


<h2
  id="upgrade-all-trinkets"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Max Out Every Trinket
</h2>

The upgrade achievements begin with:

**In the Workshop** — upgrade one Trinket.

The final requirement is much larger:

**Fully Stocked** — upgrade every Trinket to its maximum amount.

Leave the expensive part of this work until the 32-Trinket collection is
complete. That prevents a newly discovered Trinket from reopening a category
you thought was already finished.

A reliable order is:

1. collect all **32 Trinkets**;
2. clear remaining **Token** markers;
3. check which Trinkets still show another upgrade level;
4. spend the required Tokens and currency;
5. farm currency only after the remaining upgrade costs are known.

There is little benefit in hoarding currency once every other purchase you care
about is finished and **Fully Stocked** is the remaining goal.


<h2
  id="postgame"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish Flying High and Together at Last
</h2>

Two achievements belong to the sequence after the Queen:

- **Flying High** — learn to Soar
- **Together at Last** — Reunite the Flock

**Flying High** comes when the post-credits castle route gives Glimmer Soar.

From there, the kingdom map reveals five marked objectives tied to Spirit
Fragments. Completing those objectives eventually sends Glimmer back toward
Night Garden for the reunion.

The detailed five-Fragment sequence, Soar input, and Night Garden return are
covered in the <Link href="/well-dweller/true-ending">post-Queen ending route</Link>.


<h2
  id="misc-achievements"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Achievements That Have No Collectible Marker
</h2>

The remaining requirements are easy to overlook because the map cannot simply
point to all of them:

- **First Purchase** — buy something from Looter's shop
- **Warming Up** — kill **100 enemies**
- **On Fire** — kill **500 enemies**
- **Pyromaniac** — throw **100 Flaming Vessels**
- **Taking a Break** — visit the spa
- **The Kings Cap** — buy a festive hat
- **Bouncing Bottles** — juggle a Flaming Vessel **10 times without it breaking**

The enemy-kill and Flaming Vessel totals usually rise naturally while finishing
the kingdom. Check them after the story and physical collectibles rather than
farming them early.

**Bouncing Bottles** is different because it requires a specific action rather
than accumulated exploration. If that box is still unchecked, work on the
10-hit juggle directly instead of searching the map for another object.


<h2
  id="cleanup-order"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Final 100% Cleanup Order
</h2>

Once the Queen is defeated and Soar is available, a clean final pass is:

1. open any obvious rooms or connections still missing from the map;
2. buy the Map Marker categories you still need;
3. clear the visible **Spirit, Trinket, Slot, Page, and Token** markers;
4. return all **20 ordinary Spirits**;
5. reach **32 Trinkets**;
6. reach **50 Trinket Slots**;
7. finish all three story-page sets;
8. complete the post-Queen Spirit Fragment and reunion sequence;
9. max every Trinket for **Fully Stocked**;
10. use the 41-achievement tracker to isolate the final action or event.

Do not restart a kingdom-wide sweep just because one achievement remains.

At that point, the tracker tells you what kind of problem is left. A missing
boss achievement means an unfinished event. A collectible achievement means its
counter is incomplete. **Fully Stocked** means upgrades remain. An action such
as **Bouncing Bottles** can be finished without searching another room.

When every box in the tracker is complete, all **41 achievements** are accounted
for.
