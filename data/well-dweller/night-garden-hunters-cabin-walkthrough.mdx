Mother sends Glimmer out for food, and the Hunter's Cabin is the destination.

The opening looks broader than it really is because Night Garden immediately
places high ledges, locked passages, Trinkets, and side rooms around the path.
Your starting movement cannot reach all of them. The required sequence is to
cross Night Garden, defeat the Groundskeeper, work through the Hunter's Cabin,
take the berries, and return to the well.


<h2
  id="leave-the-well"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Leave the Well and Learn How Healing Works
</h2>

Follow the reachable rooms out of the well. Glimmer's normal jump is deliberately
limited at the beginning, so a ledge that sits clearly above your maximum height
is not hiding a special opening technique.

The **Matchick** acts as both your weapon and a visual health indicator. As
Glimmer takes damage, its flame weakens.

Soon afterward you find your first **Oil Vial**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-oil-vial-healing.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Oil Vial tutorial showing how to relight the Matchick and restore health"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    priority={true}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Oil Vials restore health by relighting the Matchick.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Healing uses a timing check rather than an instant press. Hold the heal input,
then release when the moving marker overlaps the glowing section. A successful
relight restores health and briefly increases your attack strength.

Oil Vials refill at safe points, so there is no advantage in dying with a full
supply simply to preserve them. If the Matchick flame is already low, create
some space and heal before the next unfamiliar room.


<h2
  id="groundskeeper"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Beat the Groundskeeper
</h2>

The first major fight in Night Garden is the **Groundskeeper**.

By this point the game has already introduced sliding as an evasive movement,
and the fight is a good place to start using it deliberately. Sliding gives
Glimmer a safer way through attacks that are awkward to clear by jumping.

A common mistake is jumping into the middle of an attack and landing while the
danger is still active. Stay low when the attack pattern allows it, slide
through the opening, then turn back for damage.

Use an Oil Vial when you have enough room to complete the timing check rather
than waiting until the next hit will kill you.

Once the Groundskeeper is down, continue forward into the cabin rather than
returning to the high Night Garden ledges. The movement needed for several of
those paths comes later.


<h2
  id="help-looter"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Help Looter and Get the Flaming Vessel
</h2>

The Hunter is asleep when Glimmer first enters the cabin.

Inside you meet **Looter**, who has filled his bag with so much stolen treasure
that he cannot drag it out. Buying something from him lightens the load enough
for him to leave.

The **Flaming Vessel** is the important purchase here.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-flaming-vessel-unlock.webp"
    alt="Flaming Vessel obtained from Looter inside the Hunter's Cabin in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Attacking enemies recharges the Flaming Vessel after each use.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The Vessel is not a one-use consumable. Normal attacks fill its charge; once
ready, throw it for a heavy explosion. You can also strike the bottle while it
is airborne to send it toward an enemy.

That makes it useful throughout the cabin. Throw it when a dangerous enemy
gives you a clear opening, then rebuild the charge through normal combat.

After the purchase, Looter makes his escape and later reappears as a merchant.


<h2
  id="hunters-locket"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Pick Up the Hunter's Locket
</h2>

A puzzle section deeper inside the cabin rewards the **Hunter's Locket**.

Its effect increases the timing window when using an Oil Vial.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-hunters-locket.webp"
    alt="Hunter's Locket Trinket increasing the healing timing window in Well Dweller"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Hunter's Locket makes the Oil Vial timing check more forgiving.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Trinkets must be equipped at a safe tent, and the Locket occupies **4 Trinket
Slots**.

It is especially useful early if the healing timing is still inconsistent. If
you are already landing heals reliably, those slots can later be used for a
different effect.


<h2
  id="get-slingshot"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Unlock Slingshot to Reach the Upper Cabin
</h2>

Eventually the cabin puts the next path above Glimmer's ordinary jump.

This time the solution is inside the same area.

Collect the ability egg and unlock **Slingshot**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-slingshot-unlock.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Slingshot ability used to launch the Matchick and gain extra height"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Slingshot gives the Matchick another usable point above Glimmer.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Slingshot lets you launch the Matchick and use it again from the new position,
giving Glimmer enough additional height to continue through the cabin.

It is not a normal double jump. The Matchick needs a usable position, and some
early walls still remain too high even after Slingshot is available.

Use the new ability on the cabin path that previously stopped your normal jump,
then keep climbing through the upper rooms.


<h2
  id="get-the-food"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Take the Berries From the Hunter's Meal
</h2>

The original objective is still food for Mother.

The food you need is the **berries from the Hunter's meal**.

Once the berries are collected, the siblings begin talking about going home.
That is the signal to stop treating the cabin like a complete-clear challenge.

You can still see optional rooms and pickups that are not practical with the
current movement set. Slingshot opens part of the cabin, not every route in
Night Garden and the surrounding world.

Take the berries and start heading back toward the well.


<h2
  id="optional-challenge"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The No-Damage Challenge Is Optional
</h2>

One early combat challenge rewards **100 Glimmer** if you complete the death
match without taking damage.

The reward is useful this early, but it does not block the food objective.

If you can clear the challenge comfortably, take the extra currency. If you
keep losing attempts while the berries are already secured, leave it and
continue home. Stronger movement and a larger health margin make revisiting
optional fights much less expensive later.


<h2
  id="return-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Bring the Berries Back to Mother
</h2>

Return to the well and give Mother the food.

The moment the berries are turned in, the opening fetch objective is finished.
A disturbing sound outside immediately changes the situation, and Glimmer is
sent back out to investigate.

That leads to the first direct encounter with the **Hunter**.

<h2
  id="hunter-escape"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Run From the Hunter
</h2>

The Hunter encounter is an escape.

Do not stop to test the Flaming Vessel or try to turn the sequence into a boss
fight. Move in the direction the chase opens and keep advancing through the
obstacles as they appear.

The safest approach is to value forward movement over optional pickups. Losing
time for a side item gives the Hunter more opportunity to close the gap, while
the same area remains accessible later.

Complete the chase and the opening phase of the game changes completely:
Glimmer's family has been taken, and the search expands beyond Night Garden and
the cabin.


<h2
  id="where-next"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Toward The Bog Next
</h2>

After the Hunter sequence, continue into the wider kingdom rather than trying
to finish every blocked Night Garden path with Slingshot.

The next major destination is **The Bog**.

There you will meet Ilda, learn the Queen's Castle requirement, defeat the
Swarm, unlock Climb, and gain access to Tent Travel.
