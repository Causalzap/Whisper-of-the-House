  <GuideQuickAnswer title="Where Do You Go Next in Well Dweller?">

  Go from **Night Garden and the Hunter's Cabin** into **The Bog**, then descend
  into **The Drains**. After Lady of the Drains, the game opens into several
  family and Golden Feather objectives. Keep completing reachable major
  objectives until the Feather counter reaches **7/7**, then return to
  **Queen's Castle**. Continue the same save after the Queen for the remaining
  post-credits route.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>


<div className="my-4 text-sm text-slate-400">

Well Dweller is fairly directed at the beginning and much less rigid once
The Drains are finished.

A useful progression line is:

**Night Garden → Hunter's Cabin → The Bog → The Drains → Graven Valley and
family objectives → Dollmaker's House → upper Golden Feather routes → The
Docks and underwater progression → remaining Golden Feathers → Queen's Castle
→ post-Queen route.**

The middle of that sequence can shift. If two major objectives are available,
finishing either one may give movement, family progress, or another Golden
Feather that makes the other route easier.

The number that eventually removes all ambiguity is **7/7 Golden Feathers**.
Once the seventh is collected, Queen's Castle becomes the next main destination.


<h2
  id="night-garden-opening"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Night Garden and Hunter's Cabin
</h2>

The opening objective is to leave the well, cross Night Garden, reach the
Hunter's Cabin, and bring food home.

Defeat the **Groundskeeper** on the way forward. Inside the cabin, helping
Looter introduces the Flaming Vessel, while the first movement upgrade that
meaningfully changes exploration is **Slingshot**.

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Slingshot gives the Matchick enough height to open early vertical routes.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Use it to finish the cabin sequence and take the food back home.

The high ledges still visible around Night Garden are not all meant to be
cleared immediately. Slingshot helps, but several of those spaces remain
dependent on later wall and air movement.

If the opening route itself is still incomplete,
<Link href="/well-dweller/night-garden-hunters-cabin-walkthrough">follow Night Garden and the Hunter's Cabin through the Groundskeeper, Slingshot, food, and the Hunter sequence</Link>.


<h2
  id="bog-progression"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Bog: Get Climb and Tent Travel
</h2>

The Bog is the point where the map starts offering more routes than you can
finish in one visit.

Two upgrades matter most here.

The first is **Climb**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-climb-unlock.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Climb ability showing wall climbing and the stamina meter"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Climb opens vertical shafts while solid ground restores the stamina meter.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Holding jump against a wall lets Glimmer climb until the wing-shaped stamina
meter runs out. Wall jumps give you extra freedom, but very long walls and high
exits can still remain inaccessible.

The second major unlock is **Tent Travel**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-fast-travel-unlock.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Tent Travel unlock allowing travel to previously discovered safe tents"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Tent Travel lets you return to any safe tent already discovered on the map.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Once Tent Travel is active, there is less reason to spend time forcing a ledge
that still looks wrong. Mark it, continue the current objective, and return
when another movement upgrade makes the route practical.

The Bog also makes the longer story requirement clear: find the missing family
and collect Golden Feathers.

Queen's Castle can already be reached around this stage, but the guard will
not admit Glimmer without **all seven Golden Feathers**.

For the full sequence through Climb, Tent Travel, the Bog routes, and the exit
toward The Drains,
<Link href="/well-dweller/the-bog-walkthrough">continue through The Bog before dropping into the next region</Link>.


<h2
  id="drains-progression"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Drains: Wind Ride, Elevator Parts, and Lady
</h2>

The Drains add **Wind Ride** to the movement kit.

Hold jump while standing in a wind current and the draft carries Glimmer
upward. This is the ability that turns several of the region's vertical shafts
into usable paths.

From there, the main task becomes repairing the broken elevator.

It needs **3 Elevator Parts**.

The surrounding rooms contain Willow, frog memorials, Trinkets, locked routes,
and optional branches, but the lift is what leads to the next required fight.

If your counter is stuck at 1/3 or 2/3,
<Link href="/well-dweller/elevator-parts-locations">collect all three Elevator Parts and return to the broken lift once the counter reaches 3/3</Link>.

Ride the repaired elevator down to **Lady of the Drains**. Defeating her awards
a Golden Feather and finishes the required part of this visit.

<Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">Follow The Drains from Wind Ride through the elevator and Lady of the Drains</Link> if you still have unfinished progression in the region.


<h2
  id="graven-valley"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Graven Valley: Rescue Egbert
</h2>

After The Drains, the game stops giving you a single obvious corridor to
follow.

**Graven Valley** is one of the strongest next objectives because one of the
missing siblings, **Egbert**, is being held for the town's witch burning.

Reaching the event area is not enough. The guard requires a ticket, so work
through the local route until you can obtain one and enter the burning.

During the event, use the distraction to free the prisoners and rescue Egbert.

Once that family objective is complete, check the map again rather than
assuming Graven Valley must be exhausted before you leave. Other active routes
can now provide more movement, siblings, and Golden Feathers.


<h2
  id="dollmaker-house"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dollmaker's House: Rescue Magda
</h2>

Another major family branch leads into **Dollmaker's House**.

Complete the sequence and rescue **Magda**.

That rescue improves traversal by letting you place the Matchick into a wall
while climbing and use it as another movement point. Routes that previously
ended with Glimmer just short of a ledge become much easier once this is
available.

This is the stage where revisiting an old blocker can be worthwhile, but only
when it connects to another active objective. The map still contains many
optional pickups that become even easier after later movement upgrades.


<h2
  id="upper-routes"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Shrine of the Seven Sons and Starsinger
</h2>

As the movement kit improves, the upper routes begin connecting more cleanly.

Two important Golden Feather encounters during this portion of the game are
the **Shrine of the Seven Sons** and **Starsinger** at the Lookout Tower.

The Starsinger climb can be reached through slightly different paths depending
on which movement upgrades you already completed. The important checkpoint is
not reproducing one exact route through every room; it is continuing the tower
until your flight improvements let you reach the final safe tent and the boss
above it.

Both encounters add to the Golden Feather total.

If you later reach 6/7 and cannot remember which event was left unfinished,
<Link href="/well-dweller/golden-feathers-locations">compare the seven Golden Feather events against the ones you have actually completed</Link>.


<h2
  id="docks"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Docks: Unlock Water Vessels and Torpedoes
</h2>

The Docks have a progression gate that is easy to mistake for another movement
problem.

Simply reaching a vessel does not let you use it.

Speak with the shipyard NPC and advance the missing-crew objective. Once the
required crew member has been rescued, Glimmer becomes **assistant captain**
and gains access to the water vessels.

  <Image

    width={1600}

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">

Take the nearby vessel into the underwater route.

The first underwater push eventually reaches barriers the vessel cannot break.
Continue until **torpedoes** are unlocked, then use them to open those blocked
sections and push deeper.

This branch continues the crew storyline and eventually reaches the
**Thimble and Thistle** objective.


<h2
  id="late-game-feathers"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Golden Feather Events Until You Reach 7/7
</h2>

By the late game, the remaining route is controlled more by unfinished events
than by one fixed region order.

Your Golden Feathers can come from:

- Lady of the Drains;
- Shrine of the Seven Sons;
- Starsinger;
- rebuilding Lutter from all four pieces;
- Squinton's birthday party;
- the Forest King event;
- escaping Webdrench Inn.

The order can vary.

What does not vary is the castle requirement: **7/7**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-seven-golden-feathers-complete.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Golden Feather total reaching seven out of seven"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The seventh Feather changes the objective to returning to Queen's Castle.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

At 5/7 or 6/7, do not search random rooms for another glowing collectible.
Every Feather is tied to a named event.

Use the event that is still incomplete, finish it, and return to Queen's Castle
when the counter reaches seven.


<h2
  id="queens-castle"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Enter Queen's Castle at 7/7
</h2>

Return to the same castle entrance that rejected Glimmer earlier.

With **7 Golden Feathers**, the guard finally lets you through.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-queens-castle-entry.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller Queen's Castle entrance after all seven Golden Feathers have been collected"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Queen's Castle opens when the Golden Feather counter reaches 7/7.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Continue through the castle and resolve the remaining family objectives.

The late sequence includes **Silver Doll** before the final confrontation with
the Queen. Finish the castle route, reach Mother's chamber, and complete the
Queen encounter.

The first credits follow, but they do not close the save.


<h2
  id="after-queen"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Continue the Same Save After the Queen
</h2>

Load back into the game after the credits.

Another route opens inside Queen's Castle and leads to **Soar**, followed by
five newly marked Spirit Fragment objectives around the kingdom.

After those five objectives are complete, the route returns to Night Garden
for **Reunite the Flock**.

<Link href="/well-dweller/true-ending">Continue from the Queen through Soar, the five Spirit Fragments, and the Night Garden reunion</Link> when you are ready to finish the post-credits sequence.


<h2
  id="where-to-go-next"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  If You Are Not Sure Where to Go Next
</h2>

Use your current progression state rather than the amount of unexplored map.

- **Still in the opening:** finish Hunter's Cabin and get Slingshot.
- **No Climb yet:** continue through The Bog.
- **Climb and Tent Travel unlocked:** descend toward The Drains.
- **Inside The Drains:** get Wind Ride, repair the elevator, and defeat Lady.
- **The Drains finished:** follow a reachable family or Golden Feather objective.
- **A high route is barely impossible:** finish another family or movement objective first.
- **Water vessels cannot be used:** advance the Docks crew quest.
- **Underwater barriers will not break:** get torpedoes.
- **Castle guard refuses entry:** reach 7/7 Golden Feathers.
- **Queen defeated:** continue the same save instead of starting over.

Once the story and post-Queen movement are complete, the remaining map becomes
much easier to clean. <Link href="/well-dweller/100-percent-completion">Use the 100% checklist when progression is finished and the remaining work is collectibles, achievements, Spirits, pages, and upgrades</Link>.
