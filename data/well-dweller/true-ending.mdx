The first credits are not the end of Well Dweller.

Continuing the save puts Glimmer back in Queen's Castle with another route
available. It leads to **Soar**, a map showing five hidden objectives, five
Spirit Fragments, and a final return to Night Garden.

The post-credits path is short compared with the main journey, but two things
make it easy to misread: the five entrances are deliberately missing from the
normal map, and the Spirit Fragments are not the same collection as the 20
ordinary bird Spirits.


<h2
  id="after-the-queen"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What to Do After the Queen
</h2>

Finish the Queen sequence and let the credits roll, then continue the same
save.

If Queen's Castle is still inaccessible before this point, the missing
requirement is earlier in the story:
<Link href="/well-dweller/golden-feathers-locations"> collect all seven Golden Feathers and return to the castle gate</Link>.

After the credits, return through Queen's Castle and speak with **Lutter**.
He points out a sealed room with a lever above it. Reach the upper lever and
open the door.

Inside, the route gives you more than another collectible.

The **Queen's Coin** reduces the price of purchases made at safe tents, which
is useful if you still have late-game spending left. The tattered storybook
also explains the objective that follows: a witch protected a flock of birds
from the Queen's hunters by turning them to stone, failed to reverse the spell,
and eventually joined them in stone. Her soul shattered across the kingdom.

That shattered soul is what the five new objectives are leading you toward.


<h2
  id="soar"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Unlock and Use Soar
</h2>

Continue through the newly opened castle route until Glimmer learns **Soar**.

  <Image

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Ground slam, then immediately hold jump to activate Soar.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The input is:

**ground slam → immediately hold jump**

Soar turns the slam into a large vertical launch. Pressing jump as a separate
normal jump after the slam has already finished will not produce the same
height.

Practice it on solid ground before leaving the castle. The new objectives use
vertical approaches that were impossible with the earlier movement set, so a
wall or ceiling that looked like the edge of a room before the Queen can now
hide the correct route.

Unlocking the ability also gives **Flying High**.


<h2
  id="five-locations"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Find the Five Marked Spirit Fragment Locations
</h2>

A map in the post-credits room shows **five circled locations** around the
kingdom.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/well-dweller/well-dweller-spirit-fragments-map.webp"
    alt="Well Dweller map showing five marked post-Queen locations"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Five locations are marked, while the entrances themselves remain hidden
    from the normal map.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The map explicitly warns that the paths leading to those locations have been
wiped from it.

That changes how you should read each marker.

Do not expect a visible corridor to connect directly to the circle. Travel to
the nearest explored room, look for new vertical space around the marked area,
and test places where Soar can carry you beyond the old boundary.

Tent Travel is useful here because the five objectives are spread around the
kingdom. There is no benefit in walking the entire map between attempts once
the nearby tents are available.


<h2
  id="spirit-fragments"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How the Five Spirit Fragment Routes Work
</h2>

Each marked route eventually awards one **Spirit Fragment**.

These fragments belong to the post-Queen objective and should not be confused
with the ordinary Spirits collected during the main game.

The five routes are not five copies of the same challenge.

One of the first marked sections ends with a straightforward Spirit Fragment
pickup after entering a newly reachable path. Another sends you into a proper
combat room.

In that combat challenge, the room throws enemies from earlier parts of the
game back at you. The Fragment is not awarded when you simply reach the room;
you have to clear the encounter.

Stay until the final enemy is gone and the Spirit Fragment actually appears.

A later marked route is built around a completely different test: a diving
board and a falling collection sequence. You are asked to collect the shiny
objects on the way down.

Missing even one means the attempt is incomplete.

The first failed dive can look confusing because reaching the bottom does not
give the reward. Return to the start and repeat the fall, adjusting horizontally
as the objects appear until the full sequence is collected. Finishing it awards
another Spirit Fragment.

The remaining marked routes still follow the same completion rule even when
their traversal differs:

**the location is not finished until the Spirit Fragment is actually awarded.**

Reaching the hidden room, opening its entrance, or completing only part of its
local challenge does not count.


<h2
  id="fragment-not-there"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why a Spirit Fragment Marker Can Look Unreachable
</h2>

The most common trap is searching for an entrance on the existing map.

There may not be one.

The post-credits map tells you that the connecting paths are hidden, while
Soar gives you exactly the movement needed to enter them. Near a marked
location, check tall shafts, upper edges, and vertical spaces that previously
ended with no obvious route.

Start the Soar input from stable ground:

**ground slam → immediately hold jump**

Once inside a marked section, keep going until the local challenge resolves.
A combat room still containing an enemy is unfinished; a diving attempt with a
missed pickup is unfinished; merely reaching the marked area is unfinished.

There are **five Spirit Fragments total**. After the fifth one is collected,
the next destination is Night Garden rather than another hidden marker.


<h2
  id="ordinary-spirits"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Are the 20 Ordinary Spirits the Same as the Five Spirit Fragments?
</h2>

No. They are separate collections.

The ordinary bird Spirits appear throughout the main game and use the
**20-Spirit** counter. Returning all 20 completes **Full Flock**.

The five Spirit Fragments only appear after the Queen and belong to the
shattered witch objective.

A save that already has **20/20 ordinary Spirits** can collect the five
Fragments and proceed directly to the Night Garden reunion.

What is not established by that sequence is whether the reunion also triggers
on a save below 20/20.

So if you already have all five Spirit Fragments but Reunite the Flock does not
appear when you return to Night Garden, check the ordinary Spirit counter
before searching the kingdom for another Fragment. There are only five
post-Queen Fragments.


<h2
  id="reunite-the-flock"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return to Night Garden After the Fifth Spirit Fragment
</h2>

Once all five marked routes are finished, head back to **Night Garden**.

You do not need to return to Queen's Castle for another door or another map.
The next objective resolves where Glimmer's journey began.

Travel back toward the bird-spirit area in Night Garden and approach the
reunion point. **Reunite the Flock** appears there.

Completing the reunion restores the fragmented spirit, changes the scene around
the flock, and unlocks **Together at Last**.

This is the additional ending sequence after the Queen rather than another
normal collectible reward.


<h2
  id="continue-after-ending"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Can You Keep Playing After the Ending?
</h2>

Yes.

The save remains usable after the Queen and after Reunite the Flock. Remaining
Trinkets, Trinket Slots, ordinary Spirits, story pages, Workshop Tokens,
upgrades, and achievement requirements can still be finished afterward.

That makes the post-credits route especially useful before a final completion
sweep because Soar opens vertical spaces that were inaccessible earlier.

When the ending is finished and you want to clear everything still missing,
<Link href="/well-dweller/100-percent-completion"> use the 100% completion checklist and 41-achievement tracker to separate the remaining collectible, upgrade, and achievement requirements</Link>.
