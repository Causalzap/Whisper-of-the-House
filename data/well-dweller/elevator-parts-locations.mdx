  Get **Wind Ride**, then collect the first two Elevator Parts from the upper
  Drains routes. After Part 2, leave the blocked pit, go **all the way left**

The broken elevator in **The Drains** requires exactly **3 Elevator Parts**.

You can reach the lift before finding all of them. Interacting with it shows
your current total, but **1/3** or **2/3** will not move the elevator.

Before chasing the parts, make sure you have **Wind Ride**. The ability lets
Glimmer ride the air currents in this section, and the routes around the
elevator start using those drafts immediately.

If you are still working through Willow, Wind Ride, or the earlier Drains
rooms, <Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">continue through The Drains until Wind Ride is unlocked</Link>, then come back to the three-part search.


<h2
  id="elevator-part-1"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Elevator Part 1 — Take the Upper Path at the Split
</h2>

After the Willow section, the route reaches a clear choice between going down
and continuing upward.

**Go up first.**

The upper path runs past a large swinging mace and the **candied spider eggs**.
Keep climbing through that section until the quest item appears. Picking it up
updates the objective to:

**Elevator Part 1/3 — Find two more.**

    Part 1 is on the upper route beyond the mace and candied spider eggs.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

A **golden gate** is nearby, but it is not required for the elevator.

You do not have to stop here looking for a way through it. Take the Elevator
Part and continue exploring the reachable Drains rooms.


<h2
  id="broken-elevator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Find the Broken Elevator, Then Continue Past It
</h2>

Dropping into the lower branch brings you to the broken lift.

This is where the game confirms that the repair needs all **three** components.
There is no switch beside the elevator that substitutes for the missing parts,
and the lift will not move with a partial set.

Remember this room as your return point.

For now, continue through the surrounding Drains rather than trying to force
your way downward through the elevator shaft.


<h2
  id="elevator-part-2"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Elevator Part 2 — Follow the Candied Spider Egg Upward
</h2>

Continue around the Drains until another **candied spider egg** moves upward.

Follow it.

The route leads to the second Elevator Part, close to the memorial plaque for
**Eagle**. If you reach the plaque left by Eagle's parents, you are in the
correct section.

Pick up the quest item to move the counter to **2/3**.

The route also opens a connection back through rooms you have already visited.
That shortcut is useful, but it is not the path to Part 3.


<h2
  id="elevator-part-3"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Elevator Part 3 — Leave the Blocked Pit and Go All the Way Left
</h2>

Part 3 is the one most likely to waste your time.

After collecting Part 2, you can drop into a room containing the memorial for
**Pip and Sprig**. Across the pit is a route that looks barely reachable with
your current movement tools.

It is not the way to the final Elevator Part.

The room is shaped to make several attempts look close:

- the Matchick can be thrown partway toward the far side;
- Climb can put you close to the wall;
- the ceiling blocks one obvious launch angle;
- the sloping surface prevents another placement;
- the opposite side looks only slightly too far away.

Stop trying to cross it.

From that pit, **backtrack and go all the way left** through The Drains.

The other nearby options are already accounted for: one upper route is locked,
the elevator still cannot descend at 2/3, and the pit does not cross with the
movement available here.

Stay on the normal platform route as you travel left.

Do not take the first familiar drop simply because it leads downward. Keep
crossing the platforms until you reach the part of the map you still have not
explored. The correct opening is the **remaining downward route on the far-left
side of this search**.

Drop there and continue through the lower rooms.

The path loops around instead of sending you back to the blocked pit. Follow it
until you reach the final quest item.

Picking it up completes the objective at **3/3**.

A safe-tent route is close by as you work back out of this lower section, so
there is no need to return through the blocked pit.


<h2
  id="return-to-elevator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return to the Elevator After Reaching 3/3
</h2>

Once the third component is collected, the repair search is over.

Climb back toward the nearby safe tent, restore your Oil Vials if necessary,
then return to the broken elevator. You do not need to open the golden gate,
cross the earlier pit, or find a fourth component.

Interact with the lift with **3/3 Elevator Parts** and ride down.

The descent does not lead into another normal exploration section. It takes
you directly into the **Lady of the Drains** encounter.

For the boss fight, Golden Feather reward, and the route out of the region,
<Link href="/well-dweller/the-drains-walkthrough">continue with The Drains walkthrough after repairing the elevator</Link>.
