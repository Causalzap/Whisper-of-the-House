<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
The **Heart of Vatra** is inside the **Temple of Vatra**, east of the
**One-Legged Wolf** tavern. From the tavern, cross the bridge on the right,
follow the eastern cliffside route, and use the **Bone Gate** to cross the
chasm to the temple.

Before taking the Heart, collect the **Synaptic Vessel** and light all three
Temple offerings to open the chest containing **Berserker's Stone**. Taking
the Heart wakes the petrified enemies and closes the normal way back, so I
escape through the broken side route instead.

The order I use is:

**Gragu → Bone Gate → Synaptic Vessel → three offerings → Berserker's Stone →
Heart of Vatra → side-route escape → return to Gragu → inhabit his Shell.**
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="heart-of-vatra-location">
  Heart of Vatra location and prerequisites
</h2>

The **Heart of Vatra** is held by a large kneeling statue at the top of the
Temple of Vatra. The temple sits east of the **One-Legged Wolf** tavern on a
separate section of land reached through a Bone Gate.

For the normal quest flow, I speak to **Gragu the Insatiable** in the tavern
before heading there. He is the huge figure sitting beside the tavern keeper.
His opening dialogue sounds dismissive, so I keep talking until he explains
that he wants the heart of a god.

<h2 id="temple-of-vatra-route">
  Route from One-Legged Wolf to the Temple of Vatra
</h2>

I use the One-Legged Wolf tavern as my starting point because the local
landmarks are easier to follow than the map once the route disappears into fog.

1. Leave the tavern and take the **bridge on the right**.
2. Continue **east** along the cliffside path.
3. Stay on the route until I reach the **Bone Gate** overlooking the chasm.
4. Use the Bone Gate to cross to the separate temple landmass.
5. Continue into the ruins and follow the main route toward the upper temple.

both are easier to deal with while the temple is still quiet.

<h2 id="before-taking-heart">
  What to collect before taking the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

Taking the Heart is the commitment point for this visit. Before climbing to the
statue, I make sure I have:

1. **Synaptic Vessel**, found along the approach to the upper temple.
2. **Berserker's Stone**, obtained by activating all three offerings around the
   locked chest.

I do not count on coming back later. Once the Heart sequence begins, the temple
changes and the normal route closes, while re-entry after escaping can be
unreliable.

<h2 id="three-offerings">
  All three Temple of Vatra offering locations
</h2>

The locked chest in this part of the temple contains **Berserker's Stone**.
Opening it requires three candle offerings located around the same ruined
chamber.

There is no timed sequence and I do not have to activate them in a particular
order. I use the chest as my reference point and work around the room.

### First Offering — left of the chest

Facing the chest, I move to the **ground immediately on its left side**. The
interaction point sits among the candle clusters near the ruined wall.

<h2 id="temple-sealed-after-heart">
  Temple of Vatra sealed after taking the Heart — are you softlocked?
</h2>

**No. The route that closes immediately after taking the Heart is part of the
Temple of Vatra sequence.** I do not need to find a lever, reopen the barrier,
or return to the Heart statue.

When I turn back and find the normal route blocked, I move away from that
barrier and look for the **broken side opening beside the upper route**.

<h2 id="escape-temple">
  How to escape the Temple of Vatra after taking the Heart
</h2>

Once the main route closes, this is the escape path I follow:

1. Turn back from the Heart and approach the route I originally used to climb
   up.
2. When the normal passage is blocked, **do not search the obstruction for a
   switch**.
3. Move to the broken side opening beside the upper route.
4. Go through the opening and **drop into the lower temple section**.
5. Keep descending through the available passages instead of fighting every
   awakened statue.
6. Look for the **candle-filled offering area below**. Seeing it confirms that I
   am reconnecting with the lower part of the temple.
7. Rejoin the route I used before taking the Heart.
8. Follow it back toward the **Bone Gate**, cross the chasm, and return to
   One-Legged Wolf.

Once I reach the familiar lower temple section, the confusing part is over. I
retrace the approach to the Bone Gate and cross back toward the tavern.

<h2 id="give-heart-to-gragu">
  Give the Heart to Gragu and unlock his Shell
</h2>

Back inside the **One-Legged Wolf**, I speak to Gragu with the Heart of Vatra in
my inventory. His dialogue now lets me hand over the Heart directly; there is
no crafting or merchant step in between.

Gragu eats the Heart and collapses. I interact with his body, complete the
memory sequence, and then inhabit **Gragu the Insatiable** as a playable Shell.

I do not leave after the cutscene. The unlock is only complete once I interact
with the fallen body and finish possessing the Shell.

This sequence also awards **Heartless**. The other completion requirements are
covered in the <Link href="/mortal-shell-2/achievements">Mortal Shell 2 achievements guide</Link>.

Once Gragu is available, the <Link href="/mortal-shell-2/all-shells-locations">all Shell locations guide</Link>
is the better place to compare him with the rest of the roster before spending
heavily on Shell upgrades.

<h2 id="gragu-wont-unlock">
  Gragu and Temple of Vatra troubleshooting
</h2>

### Gragu keeps telling me to leave

His first responses are dismissive. I keep interacting with him until he talks
about wanting the heart of a god. I do not treat the first conversation line as
the end of the interaction.

### Corruption blocks the road to One-Legged Wolf

This is a progression gate, not a Temple puzzle. I need to recover the Ova,
progress the Gloom Siphon, and obtain **Mether's Breath** before I can clear the
corruption blocking this route.

### I have the Heart but Gragu will not unlock

I return to Gragu inside the One-Legged Wolf and continue his dialogue until I
can hand over the Heart. After he eats it and collapses, I interact with the
body and complete the memory sequence.

Simply seeing Gragu collapse is not the final unlock step.

### The Temple of Vatra is locked after I escaped

This is different from the scripted blockade that appears immediately after
taking the Heart.

If I have already escaped and the outer temple entrance remains closed, I do
not rely on being able to return for anything I skipped. If I left an important
pickup behind and have an earlier local save available, that is the recovery
option I would consider.

### I took the Heart before getting Berserker's Stone

The Heart itself still lets me continue Gragu's quest, but I may have lost easy
access to the optional temple rewards.

That is why I collect both the **Synaptic Vessel** and **Berserker's Stone**
before touching the Heart rather than planning a second trip.

### Is the Heart of Vatra missable?

The Heart itself is difficult to overlook once I reach the kneeling statue and
is the item Gragu needs. The more important risk is leaving the temple before
collecting the **Synaptic Vessel** and **Berserker's Stone**, especially if the
temple does not reopen afterward.
