<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To unlock **Gragu** in Mortal Shell 2, I find him inside the **One-Legged Wolf**
tavern and keep talking until he asks for the **Heart of Vatra**.

The Heart is inside the nearby **Temple of Vatra**.

The important part is the order:

**do not take the Heart immediately.**

Before touching it, I light the **three candles** around the temple and open the
chest containing **Berserker's Stone**.

In the current build, taking the Heart changes and blocks the temple routes, so
I treat Berserker's Stone as something I should collect **before** committing to
the Heart.

After taking the Heart, I leave through the remaining side route, return to the
One-Legged Wolf, and give the Heart to Gragu to unlock him as a playable Shell.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="gragu-unlock-order">
  The Gragu unlock order I use
</h2>

Gragu's quest is short once I know where to go.

The part that matters is doing the **Temple of Vatra in the right order**.

My route is:

**Mushroom Village → One-Legged Wolf → talk to Gragu → Temple of Vatra → light
three candles → collect Berserker's Stone → take Heart of Vatra → escape temple
→ return Heart to Gragu.**

I do not reverse the middle of that sequence.

The Heart looks like the main objective, so it is very tempting to grab it as
soon as I reach the altar.

I resist that.

The optional chest comes first.

<h2 id="why-gragu-wont-join">
  Why Gragu will not join when I first find him
</h2>

Finding Gragu is not the same thing as unlocking him.

He is sitting inside the **One-Legged Wolf** tavern, but when I first reach him
he is not simply another Shell I can take immediately.

I keep talking to him.

Eventually he tells me what he wants:

**the Heart of Vatra.**

That is the actual quest trigger.

So if I have found Gragu but cannot unlock him yet, I do not:

- leave and search for a Gragu corpse;
- repeatedly rest at a Beacon;
- assume I need to spend Glimpses first;
- look for a different version of the tavern.

I exhaust his dialogue and then go after the Heart.

Fortunately, the Temple of Vatra is close enough that I can treat the tavern
and temple as one route.

<h2 id="do-not-take-heart-yet">
  Do not take the Heart of Vatra as soon as you find it
</h2>

This is the one mistake I would most want to avoid.

Eventually I reach the Heart.

It is sitting prominently at the far end of the temple and looks exactly like
the item I should grab immediately.

**I leave it alone.**

Before I touch the Heart, I turn around and finish the three-candle objective.

The reason is not optimization.

It is access.

Taking the Heart changes the temple state and closes routes that were open
while I was exploring.

That means my cleanup order is:

**optional temple reward first → Heart second.**

I would rather spend a few extra minutes now than discover the optional reward
after I have already changed the area.

<h2 id="berserkers-stone-warning">
  Get Berserker's Stone before taking the Heart
</h2>

This is the current-build precaution I consider mandatory for my own route.

Before taking the Heart of Vatra, I collect **Berserker's Stone**.

Once the Heart is taken, the Temple of Vatra changes and the previous routes
become blocked.

Because of that state change, I do **not** rely on being able to come back later
and clean up Berserker's Stone normally.

My safe rule is:

**if Berserker's Stone matters to this save, get it before the Heart.**

I treat the Heart as the commitment point for this small area.

<h2 id="return-heart-to-gragu">
  Return the Heart of Vatra to Gragu
</h2>

Once I am out of the temple, I head back to the **One-Legged Wolf**.

I speak with Gragu while carrying the Heart.

That completes the unlock and makes **Gragu the Insatiable** available as a
playable Shell.

I do not need another corpse location or a second quest item after that.

The Heart is the quest item he was waiting for.

<h2 id="if-berserkers-stone-was-missed">
  What if I already took the Heart before Berserker's Stone?
</h2>

If I have already taken the Heart and the temple routes are now blocked, I do
not assume I simply missed another candle.

The important state change has already happened.

In the current build, this is exactly why I treat **Berserker's Stone as a
before-the-Heart pickup** rather than something I plan to clean up later.

So on a fresh route, I do not gamble on recovery.

I use:

**three candles → Berserker's Stone → Heart of Vatra**

every time.

<h2 id="heart-of-vatra-route">
  The Heart of Vatra route I remember
</h2>

The entire Gragu unlock becomes much easier once I reduce it to a few landmarks.

**Mushroom Village**

→ first bridge, sharp left

→ cave

→ secondary Beacon

→ **One-Legged Wolf**

→ talk to Gragu until he requests the Heart

→ bridges and jump gate

→ **Temple of Vatra**

→ light three candles

→ collect **Berserker's Stone**

→ take **Heart of Vatra**

→ use the side exit

→ return to **Gragu**

The part I never reverse is:

**Stone before Heart.**

Everything else is navigation.
