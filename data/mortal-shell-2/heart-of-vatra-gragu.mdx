<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
The **Heart of Vatra** is inside the **Temple of Vatra**, near the
**One-Legged Wolf** tavern where I find **Gragu the Insatiable**.

To unlock Gragu, I talk to him until he asks for the **heart of a god**, then
head to the Temple of Vatra.

The important order inside the temple is:

**activate all three offerings → open the chest → collect Berserker's Stone →
take the Heart of Vatra → escape through the newly opened route → return to
Gragu → give him the Heart → possess his Shell.**

I do **not** take the Heart as soon as I reach the altar. Picking it up changes
the temple, seals the route behind me, and wakes enemies around the escape path.

The safest sequence is:

**Berserker's Stone first, Heart of Vatra second.**
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="heart-of-vatra-location">
  Where is the Heart of Vatra?
</h2>

The **Heart of Vatra** is sitting on an altar inside the **Temple of Vatra**.

The temple is in the same general part of Fainweald as the
**One-Legged Wolf**, where I meet Gragu.

Once I already know what Gragu wants, the route is:

**One-Legged Wolf → bridges → jump gate → Temple of Vatra → Heart altar.**

**Berserker's Stone.**

<h2 id="how-to-unlock-gragu">
  How to unlock Gragu the Insatiable
</h2>

Gragu is not a Shell I find lying somewhere in the world.

I meet him while he is still alive inside the **One-Legged Wolf**.

His unlock is built around bringing him the divine heart he is searching for.
Once I have heard his request, the quest has three distinct parts:

**learn what Gragu wants → retrieve the Heart without leaving the temple reward
behind → return and complete the possession sequence.**

The step that matters most is inside the Temple of Vatra.

I finish the **three-offering chest** before touching the Heart.

After Gragu eats the Heart and collapses, I interact with his body and complete
the memory sequence to establish **Gragu the Insatiable** as a usable Shell.

<h2 id="before-taking-heart">
  What to do before taking the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

When I reach the Heart altar, I stop.

I do **not** pick it up yet.

Nearby is a chest tied to:

**three offerings.**

I finish that puzzle first.

Taking the Heart is a commitment point. The route I used to enter becomes
sealed and enemies around the escape path wake up.

So I treat the untouched temple as my chance to finish the optional reward
cleanly.

My order is:

**explore → activate three offerings → open chest → collect Berserker's Stone →
take Heart.**

<h2 id="three-offerings">
  How the three offerings work
</h2>

The chest inside the Temple of Vatra requires **three offerings**.

I find the offering points around the temple area and activate all three before
returning to the chest.

There is no complicated sequence where one has to be activated before another.

I only need:

**three offerings active → chest unlocked.**

<h2 id="take-heart-of-vatra">
  Take the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

Once Berserker's Stone is secured, I return to the altar and take the
**Heart of Vatra**.

Its description identifies it as the heart of a long-dead revered being, which
matches exactly what Gragu has been asking for.

The moment I pick it up, the route changes.

The original way back closes.

Dormant enemies around the temple begin emerging.

At that point I stop thinking about exploration.

My objective becomes:

**leave the temple with the Heart.**

<h2 id="temple-seals">
  Why does the Temple of Vatra seal after taking the Heart?
</h2>

Taking the Heart triggers the change.

The sealed route is not a bug, and I am not supposed to reopen the exact path I
used to reach the altar.

The temple has shifted from a place I am searching to a place I am escaping.

<h2 id="escape-temple">
  How to escape the Temple of Vatra after taking the Heart
</h2>

Once the altar route seals, I follow the **remaining side path**.

I do not stay at the blocked entrance trying to force it open.

Enemies begin emerging around the route, but I do not treat this as a mandatory
full clear.

If I can move past an enemy safely, I do.

The Heart is already in my inventory, so killing everything in the temple does
not improve the quest.

I just need to survive the route out.

This is also why I prefer reaching the altar with healing still available. The
quiet trip in and the escape out are very different versions of the same area.

Once I reach the Beacon or a safe route outside, I recover before returning to
the One-Legged Wolf.

<h2 id="what-to-do-with-heart">
  What do I do with the Heart of Vatra?
</h2>

I bring the Heart back to **Gragu**.

There is no second altar, crafting recipe, or merchant step.

I return to the **One-Legged Wolf** with the Heart in my inventory and speak to
him.

When the dialogue gives me the option, I hand it over.

Gragu consumes the Heart.

Then he collapses.

That collapse is the point where the quest changes from delivering an item to
claiming the Shell.

<h2 id="give-heart-to-gragu">
  Give the Heart to Gragu and unlock his Shell
</h2>

After Gragu eats the Heart and falls, I interact with his body.

I can now possess him as a Shell.

That begins Gragu's memory sequence, which establishes the character behind the
Shell rather than sending me after another quest item.

Once the sequence is complete, **Gragu the Insatiable** becomes available to
use.

I do not need to search the world for another Gragu body after delivering the
Heart.

The NPC in the One-Legged Wolf is the Shell I eventually possess.

<h2 id="gragu-abilities">
  What does Gragu do after I unlock him?
</h2>

Gragu immediately feels different from the lighter Shells.

His defining active attack is **Staggering Blow**.

I charge a heavy punch, and holding the charge longer increases the attack's
power. The move is built around strong stagger rather than fast repeated
pressure.

His healing also works differently.

Gragu uses a **Revered Heart** to restore health, and that resource can be
replenished by killing enemies.

That gives him a much more aggressive rhythm than a Shell that constantly needs
to return to a Beacon after spending healing.

Whether that playstyle is worth investing in depends on the rest of my build.
The

</Link>
covers the broader roster when I want to compare Gragu with Tiel, Proxima,
Eredrim, Genessa, and the other options.

For this quest, the important result is:

**Heart delivered → Gragu possessed → Shell unlocked.**

<h2 id="gragu-wont-unlock">
  Why is Gragu not unlocking?
</h2>

If Gragu is still unavailable, I work backward through the quest rather than
searching for another unlock requirement.

### Gragu keeps telling me to leave

I keep talking to him.

I need to reach the dialogue where he talks about his hunger and the
**hearts of gods**.

### I found Gragu but cannot possess him

That is normal while he is still alive.

I have not completed the Heart of Vatra quest yet.

### I have the Heart but Gragu is still unavailable

Carrying the Heart is not enough.

I need to return to the **One-Legged Wolf** and actually give it to him.

### Gragu ate the Heart but I still cannot switch to him

I interact with his fallen body and complete the memory sequence.

The unlock is not finished the instant he consumes the Heart.

<h2 id="heart-before-berserkers-stone">
  What if I took the Heart before Berserker's Stone?
</h2>

If I already picked up the Heart, the first thing to understand is that the
temple has changed because of that action.

The sealed route does not mean I missed a hidden switch.

Taking the Heart caused it.

That is why I use this sequence whenever I reach the untouched Temple of Vatra:

**three offerings → Berserker's Stone → Heart of Vatra.**

I do not rely on being able to return through the original temple state later.

The Stone is available before the commitment point, so collecting it first is
the cleanest route.

<h2 id="is-heart-missable">
  Is the Heart of Vatra missable?
</h2>

The Heart itself is difficult to overlook once I reach the correct temple.

It is sitting prominently on the altar and is the key quest item for Gragu.

The more useful concern is the opposite:

**I am more worried about taking the Heart too early than missing it.**

The three-offering chest is optional content I want to finish before advancing
the temple state.

So when I reach the altar, I remember:

**optional reward first, quest item second.**

<h2 id="heart-of-vatra-route">
  Heart of Vatra and Gragu route recap
</h2>

The full route is:

**Mushroom Village**

→ follow the route toward the **One-Legged Wolf**

→ enter the tavern and talk to **Gragu**

→ keep talking until he asks for the **heart of a god**

→ leave the tavern

→ follow the nearby bridges

→ use the **jump gate**

→ enter the **Temple of Vatra**

→ activate all **three offerings**

→ open the chest

→ collect **Berserker's Stone**

→ return to the altar

→ take the **Heart of Vatra**

→ do not waste time trying to reopen the sealed entrance

→ follow the available side route

→ escape the temple

→ return to the **One-Legged Wolf**

→ give the Heart to **Gragu**

→ interact with him after he collapses

→ complete the memory sequence

→ **Gragu the Insatiable unlocked**

The one order I do not reverse is:

**Berserker's Stone before Heart of Vatra.**
