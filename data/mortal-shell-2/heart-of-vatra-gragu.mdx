<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
The **Heart of Vatra** is inside the **Temple of Vatra**, close to the
**One-Legged Wolf** tavern where I find **Gragu the Insatiable**.

To unlock Gragu, I talk to him until he asks for the **heart of a god**, enter
the Temple of Vatra, collect the **Berserker's Stone** reward first, then take
the Heart of Vatra and bring it back to him.

The order I use is:

**find Gragu → enter Temple of Vatra → activate all three offerings → collect
Berserker's Stone → take Heart of Vatra → escape through the newly opened
route → give the Heart to Gragu → possess Gragu.**

I do **not** take the Heart as soon as I see it. Picking it up changes the
temple, seals the route behind me, and wakes the enemies around the exit. I
finish the three-offering chest before touching the Heart.

<h2 id="heart-of-vatra-location">
  Where is the Heart of Vatra?
</h2>

The **Heart of Vatra** is inside the **Temple of Vatra** in the same general
part of the map as the **One-Legged Wolf** tavern.

I first learn that the Heart matters when I meet Gragu.

He is not asking for a random upgrade item.

He tells me he is searching for the **hearts of gods**, and the Heart of
Vatra is the item that satisfies that hunger.

So if I already have the quest and only want the destination, my route is:

**One-Legged Wolf → bridges → jump gate → Temple of Vatra → Heart altar.**

<h2 id="how-to-unlock-gragu">
  How to unlock Gragu the Insatiable
</h2>

Gragu is different from Shells that I simply find as an empty body.

I meet him while he is still alive.

He is inside the **One-Legged Wolf** tavern.

Finding him does not unlock him immediately.

I have to give him what he is searching for.

My full unlock sequence is:

1. Reach the **One-Legged Wolf**.
2. Talk to Gragu until he talks about the **hearts of gods**.
3. Head to the nearby **Temple of Vatra**.
4. Find the chest that requires **three offerings**.
5. Activate all three offerings.
6. Open the chest and take **Berserker's Stone**.
7. Return to the altar and take the **Heart of Vatra**.
8. Escape the changed temple through the available side route.
9. Return to Gragu.
10. Give him the Heart.
11. After he consumes it and collapses, interact with him to possess the Shell.
12. Complete Gragu's memory sequence to finish establishing the Shell.

The two steps I would not swap are:

**Berserker's Stone first → Heart of Vatra second.**

<h2 id="before-taking-heart">
  Do this before taking the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

When I reach the Heart altar, I stop.

I do **not** pick up the Heart yet.

The nearby optional chest displays a requirement for:

**three offerings.**

I finish that first.

This is the most important piece of routing in the entire quest because the
temple changes as soon as I take the Heart.

The route I used to enter becomes sealed, and enemies around the area become
part of the escape.

So my rule is:

**explore first → offerings first → chest first → Heart last.**

<h2 id="three-offerings">
  Find all three offerings in the Temple of Vatra
</h2>

The chest in the Temple of Vatra requires **three offerings**.

The offering points are positioned around the temple area.

I work around the space methodically instead of constantly returning to the
chest after every one.

The useful sequence is simply:

**first offering → second offering → third offering → chest.**

There is no complicated ordering puzzle.

I only need all three active.

Berserker's Stone is built around low-health melee damage.

The version I collect can scale melee damage heavily as my health falls,

Whether I actually use it is a separate decision.

What matters here is that I have it.

Once I take the Heart, the temple seals paths behind me.

I do not build my route around the assumption that I can casually walk back
through the original layout and collect the Stone afterward.

<h2 id="take-heart-of-vatra">
  Take the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

Once Berserker's Stone is safely in my inventory, I return to the altar.

Now I take the **Heart of Vatra**.

The item is described as the heart of a long-dead revered being, which is
exactly the kind of divine heart Gragu has been searching for.

The moment I pick it up, the calm part of the route is over.

The temple changes.

The way back is blocked.

Enemies that were sitting dormant around the temple begin emerging.

That means I mentally switch objectives immediately:

**I am no longer exploring the Temple of Vatra.**

**I am escaping with the Heart.**

Taking the Heart is the trigger.

This is not a bugged door and I am not supposed to retrace the exact path I

I do not need to kill every enemy.

My objective is:

**get out with the Heart.**

<h2 id="escape-temple">
  How to escape after taking the Heart of Vatra
</h2>

Once the altar route seals, I follow the **remaining side path**.

I do not stand at the blocked entrance trying to force it open.

I keep moving through the available route while watching the enemies emerging
from the structures around me.

If I have enough space to bypass one safely, I do.

This is not where I decide to turn the quest into a complete enemy clear.

The Heart is already in my inventory.

I just need to survive long enough to return to the tavern.

Once I reach the Beacon or safe route outside the temple, I heal before
returning to Gragu.

I do this because I do not assume an NPC interaction involving a god's heart
is going to end peacefully.

<h2 id="what-to-do-with-heart">
  What do I do with the Heart of Vatra?
</h2>

I give it to **Gragu**.

There is no second altar, crafting recipe, or merchant involved.

I return to the **One-Legged Wolf** with the Heart in my inventory and talk
to him.

When he asks whether I have it, I hand it over.

Gragu immediately consumes the Heart.

Then he collapses.

That is my signal that the quest has moved from:

**bring him the Heart**

to:

**claim the Shell.**

<h2 id="give-heart-to-gragu">
  Give the Heart to Gragu and possess his Shell
</h2>

After Gragu consumes the Heart and falls, I interact with his body.

I can now enter him as a Shell.

That starts Gragu's memory sequence.

The sequence establishes the character behind the Shell rather than asking me
to find another item.

Once it completes, **Gragu the Insatiable** is available to use.

This is why I do not go searching the world for a separate Gragu body after
delivering the Heart.

The NPC I met in the tavern is the Shell I eventually possess.

<h2 id="gragu-abilities">
  What Gragu does after I unlock him
</h2>

I only need a short test after unlocking him to understand that he plays very
differently from lighter Shells.

His defining active attack is **Staggering Blow**.

He charges a huge punch, and the longer I hold the charge, the harder the
attack hits.

The attack is built around heavy stagger.

His healing is also unusual.

Gragu uses a **Revered Heart** to restore health, and I can replenish that
resource by killing enemies.

That immediately makes him feel like a Shell built for staying aggressive
instead of returning to a Beacon every time I spend healing.

I do not make the entire build decision on this page.

If I am deciding whether Gragu is better for my run than Tiel, Proxima,
Eredrim, Genessa, or another Shell, I use the{" "}

  all Shells locations guide
</Link>
{" "}for the broader comparison.

For this route, the important result is simply:

**Heart delivered → Gragu possessed → Shell unlocked.**

<h2 id="gragu-wont-unlock">
  Why Gragu is not unlocking
</h2>

If Gragu is still sitting in the tavern and I cannot use him, I check where I
am in the sequence.

### Gragu only tells me to leave

I keep talking to him.

I am waiting for the conversation about his hunger and the **hearts of gods**.

### I found Gragu but cannot possess him

That is normal before the Heart quest is complete.

He is still alive.

I need the **Heart of Vatra** first.

### I found the Heart but Gragu is still unavailable

I make sure I actually returned to the **One-Legged Wolf** and handed the
Heart to him.

Simply carrying it does not unlock the Shell.

### Gragu ate the Heart but I still cannot switch normally

I interact with his fallen body and go through the Shell memory sequence.

The unlock is not finished at the instant he takes the item.

<h2 id="heart-before-berserkers-stone">
  What if I took the Heart before Berserker's Stone?
</h2>

If I already picked up the Heart, the important thing to recognize is that
the temple state has changed.

The blocked route is not evidence that I missed a hidden switch.

Taking the Heart itself caused the change.

That is why I always handle the optional reward first on a fresh route:

**three offerings → Berserker's Stone → Heart of Vatra.**

I do not promise that every possible later route or game-state workaround is
impossible.

I simply do not rely on one.

When I am standing in the untouched Temple of Vatra, the safe sequence costs
almost no extra effort, so I collect the Stone first.

<h2 id="is-heart-missable">
  Is the Heart of Vatra missable?
</h2>

The Heart itself is difficult to overlook once I reach the correct temple.

It is sitting prominently on the altar.

The more useful concern is actually the reverse:

**I am more worried about taking the Heart too early than missing the Heart.**

The Heart is the main quest object for Gragu's unlock.

The three-offering chest is the piece I deliberately finish before progressing
that objective.

So when I enter Vatra, I remember:

**optional reward first, quest item second.**
