<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
The **Great Martyr's Blade** is obtained by clearing **Martyr's Prison**. One
of the fastest routes is to start from the **One-Legged Wolf Beacon**, cross
the wooden bridge, turn left into the ruins, and use the hidden interaction
there to reach **City of Penance**.

After clearing the City of Penance boss, I can reach the same area that gives
me access to **Eredrim** and **Warden's Stone**, the Frost Infusion made
specifically for the Great Martyr's Blade. From there, I can also work my way
through a wall opening toward Martyr's Prison without first opening the
ravine-side gate.

The Blade hits extremely hard but has long attack commitments. I collect it
early when the route is convenient, test the moveset before spending heavily,
then pair it with Warden's Stone if I want the Frost setup.

My rule is:

**reach City of Penance → get access to Warden's Stone → continue to Martyr's
Prison → get the Great Martyr's Blade → test it → commit to Frost if the slow
moveset suits me.**

<h2 id="great-martyrs-blade-location">
  Great Martyr's Blade location and how to get it
</h2>

The **Great Martyr's Blade** comes from **Martyr's Prison** on the western side
of the world. It is not sitting in an outdoor chest; I have to reach the Prison
and clear its dungeon route before the weapon is mine.

There are two useful ways to approach this part of the map.

The normal overland route takes me through **Gloomshade Grove** and the
Citadel-side progression. If that route is still blocked, the
**Blood-Cursed Lithopod** is one of the progression checks I deal with before
continuing west.

There is also a much more useful shortcut from **One-Legged Wolf**. That route
takes me through City of Penance, puts me near **Warden's Stone**, and then
gives me a path onward toward Martyr's Prison.

If I want both the Frost Infusion and the Blade, I prefer doing them together.

<h2 id="route-to-martyrs-prison">
  Fast route from One-Legged Wolf to Martyr's Prison
</h2>

I start at the **One-Legged Wolf Beacon** rather than running the entire
northern route from Gloomshade Grove.

The first part is:

1. Leave the Beacon area and **drop down**.
2. Continue across the **wooden bridge**.
3. Take the **left turn** after the bridge.
4. Enter the ruined structure ahead.
5. Climb through the ruins toward the interaction that sends me to
   **City of Penance**.

### Clear the City of Penance boss

Once I arrive:

1. Break through the gate ahead.
2. Move through the enemies inside the city.
3. Find the **lift-like platform** that carries me upward.
4. Ride it up to the boss area.
5. Defeat the boss.
6. Drop into the lower section beyond the fight.

This is also the progression that opens access to **Eredrim** and the
**Warden's Stone** route.

### Continue from City of Penance to Martyr's Prison

After handling the Eredrim and Warden's Stone area, I activate the nearby
**City of Penance Beacon** so I have a reliable respawn point.

There is a gate in this region that can be opened through the ravine-side
progression, but I do not need to wait for that gate if I am using this route.

Instead:

1. Move down from the Beacon area.
2. Run past the ordinary enemies if they are not blocking me.
3. Take the **left opening through the wall**.
4. Continue past the closed gate rather than trying to open it from this side.
5. Keep moving in the same direction.
6. Pass through another **gap in the wall**.
7. Follow the route until the entrance to **Martyr's Prison** appears.

That wall route is what turns the City of Penance shortcut into a useful
Great Martyr's Blade route rather than only an Eredrim detour.

<h2 id="is-great-martyrs-blade-good">
  Is the Great Martyr's Blade good?
</h2>

**Yes, especially with Frost.**

The Great Martyr's Blade has excellent damage and stagger potential, but the
tradeoff is obvious: its attacks require much longer commitments than faster
weapons such as Axatana or Axe & Dagger.

Without Frost, I consider it a powerful but demanding heavy weapon.

With Frost, it becomes much easier to use because I can control enemies long
enough to create the openings those slow attacks need.

My verdict is:

- **Just found it:** strong enough to test immediately.
- **Without Warden's Stone:** powerful, but slow and easy to overcommit with.
- **With Frost:** one of the strongest heavy-control setups I can build.
- **For constant aggression:** I prefer a faster weapon.
- **For stagger and large punish windows:** the Blade is excellent.
- **If I dislike long attack recovery:** I do not force the weapon just because
  its damage is high.

The completed setup succeeds because it works around the Blade's weakness
rather than pretending that weakness does not exist.

### Great Martyr's Blade moveset

The light chain follows:

**thrust → large swing → thrust.**

The heavy chain is slower and demands even larger openings.

I particularly like the running attacks because they let me enter combat
without always beginning with a slow neutral swing. The **running light**
provides a wide opener, while the **running heavy** combines a spinning attack
with a thrust.

My normal rhythm is:

**create space → wait for a real opening → commit → take the damage or stagger
I earned → reset before the enemy recovers.**

If I swing because I hope the enemy will stay still, the Blade feels awful.

If I swing because the opening already exists, the weapon makes much more
sense.

<h2 id="frost-build">
  Warden's Stone location and the Great Martyr's Blade Frost build
</h2>

**Warden's Stone** is the Infusion Tarstone that gives the Great Martyr's Blade
its Frost setup.

The Frost Infusion is tied specifically to this weapon rather than being a
generic option I can move freely across the rest of the melee roster.

The useful part is that Warden's Stone does **not** require a separate trip to
the opposite side of the world after I obtain the Blade.

I can build the route around both rewards:

**One-Legged Wolf → City of Penance → boss → Eredrim / Warden's Stone area →
Martyr's Prison → Great Martyr's Blade.**

That makes the Frost build much less awkward to assemble than treating the
Blade and its Infusion as unrelated late-game pickups.

### Why Frost is so strong on the Blade

Frost does not increase the Blade's attack speed.

It fixes the practical problem caused by that speed.

Without Frost, the matchup is:

**enemy moving normally + slow weapon.**

With Frost:

**slow weapon + enemy I can control.**

<h2 id="wardens-stone-upgrade">
  Should I upgrade Warden's Stone to Level 3?
</h2>

I would **not automatically take Warden's Stone to Level 3** as soon as I get
it.

Level 3 makes the Infusion permanent, which sounds ideal, but the Great
Martyr's Blade already builds Frost quickly enough that I do not need permanent
Frost just to make the setup function.

At **Level 2**, I already have enough infusion charges to use Frost repeatedly.
Because resting or reviving restores Resolve, I can refresh the Infusion again
when needed rather than treating permanent infusion as mandatory.

That changes how I value the final upgrade:

**Level 2 = the build already works.**

**Level 3 = convenience and permanence.**

If the expensive materials required for the final Tarstone upgrade are still
scarce, I keep them until I know permanent Frost is worth more to me than
another high-priority upgrade elsewhere.

I only push Warden's Stone to Level 3 when the Great Martyr's Blade is clearly
one of my main weapons and I value never having to reapply the Infusion.

<h2 id="captives-scabstone">
  Captive's Scabstone and the heavy punish setup
</h2>

The other important piece of my preferred setup is **Captive's Scabstone**.

Its upgraded attack reaches:

- **300% Strike**
- **250 Stagger Damage**
- **75 Resolve cost**

Those numbers fit the Blade because I am already trying to manufacture large
punish windows rather than attack constantly.

Frost creates the control.

Captive's Scabstone gives me a high-value attack to use during that control.

A heavy-finisher Tarstone that adds **30% critical chance** to the finisher
pushes the same idea farther.

The combat loop becomes:

**Frost → controlled opening → heavy punish or Captive's Scabstone → stagger
and damage → reset.**

I am not trying to make the Great Martyr's Blade fast.

I am making every safe opening more valuable.

<h2 id="worth-getting-early">
  Is the Great Martyr's Blade worth getting early?
</h2>

Yes, especially now that I can combine the trip with **Warden's Stone** and
Eredrim progression.

The One-Legged Wolf shortcut changes the decision because I do not have to
treat Martyr's Prison as an isolated cross-map detour.

I am more willing to go early when:

- I can already reach One-Legged Wolf;
- I want Eredrim or Warden's Stone anyway;
- I enjoy slow greatswords;
- I have enough resources to survive Martyr's Prison.

I am more willing to wait when:

- I strongly prefer fast weapons;
- the City of Penance boss is currently too expensive to fight;
- upgrade materials are already tight;
- I have not yet decided whether I like the Blade's moveset.

If I recently picked up the{" "}
<Link href="/mortal-shell-2/kings-crypt-veterans-battle-axe">
  Veteran's Battle Axe
</Link>
{" "}
and it is already performing well, I do not feel forced to abandon it just
because the Great Martyr's Blade has a stronger finished Frost setup.

The distinction I use is:

**worth obtaining does not automatically mean worth maxing immediately.**

<h2 id="upgrade-priority">
  When should I upgrade the Great Martyr's Blade?
</h2>

I commit rare materials once three things are true.

### I actually like the slow moveset

If the light attacks, heavy attacks, and recovery windows already feel
miserable, higher damage will not fix the underlying problem.

### I plan to use Warden's Stone

Frost gives the Blade the control-focused identity that makes its slow attacks
much easier to justify.

### The Blade is becoming one of my main weapons

If I am still switching constantly between Iconoclast, Axe & Dagger, Axatana,
the Veteran's Battle Axe, and the Great Martyr's Blade, I keep expensive
materials in reserve.

Once all three conditions line up, I am comfortable investing heavily.

<h2 id="weapon-comparisons">
  Great Martyr's Blade vs Veteran's Battle Axe and Axatana
</h2>

### Great Martyr's Blade vs Veteran's Battle Axe

I prefer the **Veteran's Battle Axe** when I want a heavy weapon that feels
useful immediately without depending on a specific Infusion.

Its commitments are less extreme, and I can build around it before deciding
whether I want to invest in Frost.

I prefer the **Great Martyr's Blade** when I specifically want:

**Frost → control → heavy punish → stagger.**

If I own both and have not committed to Warden's Stone yet, I am perfectly
comfortable continuing with the Battle Axe.

### Great Martyr's Blade vs Axatana

Axatana solves fights in almost the opposite way.

**Axatana:**

**speed → repeated hits → status buildup → sustained pressure.**

**Great Martyr's Blade:**

**Frost → controlled opening → heavy hit → stagger and burst damage.**

If I want constant aggression, Axatana is easier to use.

If I enjoy waiting for one real opening and making that opening hurt, the
Great Martyr's Blade fits much better.

<h2 id="cant-reach-martyrs-prison">
  Why can't I reach Martyr's Prison or Warden's Stone?
</h2>

### The normal Gloomshade Grove route is blocked

I check whether the **Blood-Cursed Lithopod** progression is still incomplete.

If I do not want to follow that entire approach, I can instead use the
**One-Legged Wolf → City of Penance shortcut**.

### I reached City of Penance but cannot find Warden's Stone

I first make sure I have taken the lift and cleared the **City of Penance
boss**.

After that fight, I drop into the lower section that opens the Eredrim and
Warden's Stone progression.

### A gate toward Martyr's Prison is still closed

I do not assume that closed gate means the dungeon is inaccessible.

From the City of Penance side, I activate the Beacon, move down past the
enemies, take the **left hole in the wall**, continue past the closed gate, and
use the next **gap in the wall** to keep moving toward Martyr's Prison.

### I reached Martyr's Prison but still do not have the Blade

Reaching the entrance is not the reward.

I still have to clear the dungeon route before I receive the **Great Martyr's
Blade**.

Once I have both the Blade and Warden's Stone, I test the setup before spending
everything on it. The combination is extremely strong, but it still plays like
a slow greatsword. Frost makes those commitments safer; it does not turn the
weapon into Axatana.
