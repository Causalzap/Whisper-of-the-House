<h2 id="eredrim-unlock-route">
  The Eredrim unlock route I use
</h2>

Eredrim is not one of the Shells I simply find while walking through an early
area.

There is a real combat gate between me and the unlock.

My normal route is:

**Gloomshade Grove → Citadel Lever → Citadel → Beacon → Warden → Eredrim.**

If I specifically want Eredrim as early as possible, I use:

**One-Legged Wolf area → bridge route → wall bag shortcut → Citadel → Beacon →
Warden → Eredrim.**

The two routes only change **how I enter the Citadel**.

They both end at the same important requirement:

**I still have to defeat the Warden.**

<h2 id="why-eredrim-is-not-an-early-pickup">
  Why reaching Eredrim's area is not enough
</h2>

Eredrim initially looks like another Shell location I should be able to run
toward and collect.

That is misleading.

On the normal route there are two meaningful progression checks:

**gain access to the Citadel**

and:

**defeat the Warden inside it.**

That makes him different from earlier Shell pickups where simply reaching the
correct location is most of the work.

If I can see the Citadel but cannot get inside, I have not necessarily missed
Eredrim.

I may still be missing the route requirement.

And if I manage to get inside early through the shortcut, I have only removed
the travel gate.

The boss gate is still there.

This is where I decide whether rushing Eredrim was actually a good idea.

The wall shortcut can get me here early.

It cannot make my weapon stronger.

It cannot give me more healing.

And it cannot remove the boss.

If normal Citadel enemies are already draining most of my resources, I activate
the Beacon and leave.

I would rather return after improving my current setup than convert a useful
shortcut into repeated underpowered boss attempts.

If the trip through the Citadel feels controlled, I fight the Warden.

Once the Warden dies, the route is effectively complete.

I do not need to return to Marrow Keep with a special item.

I do not need to defeat another boss.

I interact with Eredrim and make the Shell available for use.

That is the end of this unlock route.

<h2 id="worth-unlocking-early">
  Is Eredrim worth unlocking early?
</h2>

For me, Eredrim is worth rushing when I already know **why I want him**.

The shortcut makes sense when:

- I specifically want a Break-focused Shell;
- my current problem is enemies refusing to stagger;
- I am comfortable fighting the Warden earlier than normal progression;
- I want Eredrim more than I want to follow the current story route.

I stay with normal progression when:

- my current Shell is already working well;
- my weapon is the real reason damage feels poor;
- regular Citadel enemies are consuming most of my healing;
- I only want Eredrim because I am trying to collect every Shell immediately.

That is the distinction I care about.

**The shortcut saves travel.**

**It does not lower the combat requirement.**

If I cannot comfortably reach the Warden, I am probably not gaining much by
forcing the unlock early.
