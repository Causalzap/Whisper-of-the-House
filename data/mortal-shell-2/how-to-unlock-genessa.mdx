<h2 id="where-is-genessa">
  Where is Genessa in Mortal Shell 2?
</h2>

I first find **Genessa at Marrow Keep**.

That is important because her unlock works differently from Shells that I
discover as bodies somewhere in the world.

I can already talk to Genessa before she becomes available as a playable
Shell.

So if I am standing beside her at Marrow Keep and wondering why I cannot
switch into her, I am not missing an interaction beside her.

I am missing the item she needs:

**Sester's Censer.**

That item is found through the **Revenant Graves** route.

<h2 id="how-to-unlock-genessa">
  How to unlock Genessa
</h2>

The full route I use is:

1. Open access from **Marrow Keep** to the **Outskirts of Mammon**.
2. Cross the Outskirts battlefield toward the castle walls.
3. At the beginning of the bridge, **drop through the gap**.
4. Pass through the archway below.
5. Follow the lower ledge to the teleport gate.
6. Use the gate to enter the red region leading to **Revenant Graves**.
7. Climb the stairs and keep slightly left.
8. Follow the huge statue-covered wall.
9. Reach **Sester's Censer**.
10. Interact with the Censer and complete the encounter.
11. Take **Sester's Censer** back to Genessa at Marrow Keep.
12. Choose the new Censer-related dialogue option.

The part I am most likely to miss is not the Censer itself.

It is the **drop beside the bridge in the Outskirts of Mammon**.

If I stay on the obvious upper route, I move away from the direct path to
Revenant Graves.

<h2 id="why-genessa-doesnt-unlock">
  Why Genessa does not unlock when I first find her
</h2>

Talking to Genessa repeatedly at Marrow Keep does not unlock her.

Her progression requires **Sester's Censer**.

So if the unlock dialogue is missing, I do not:

* rest repeatedly at the Keep;
* search for a Genessa corpse nearby;
* look for something to buy from her;
* keep exhausting the same dialogue;
* assume the unlock is bugged.

The real objective is outside Marrow Keep.

I leave for **Revenant Graves**, complete the Censer encounter, and come back
with the item.

Until I have done that, there is nothing useful to force at Genessa herself.

<h2 id="outskirts-of-mammon-route">
  Cross the Outskirts of Mammon battlefield
</h2>

Once I arrive in the **Outskirts of Mammon**, I move across the battlefield
toward the large castle walls ahead.

Those structures are my main landmark.

I do not need to clear every enemy between the teleport and the bridge.

The route ends in a combat encounter at Sester's Censer, so preserving healing
is more valuable to me than cleaning up every optional fight on the way.

The point I care about is the **beginning of the bridge**.

That is where the route changes.

<h2 id="genessa-not-unlocking">
  What to check if Genessa still will not unlock
</h2>

If I return to Marrow Keep and the expected dialogue is missing, I work
backward through the actual unlock sequence.

### I can talk to Genessa but cannot unlock her

That is normal before I have Sester's Censer.

I need to complete Revenant Graves first.

### I cannot reach the Outskirts of Mammon

I check my broader progression.

If corruption is still blocking the route, I continue the Ova and Gloom
Siphon progression before trying again.

### I reached the bridge but cannot find Revenant Graves

I check whether I took the **lower drop near the beginning of the bridge**.

The obvious upper route is not the direct Genessa path.

### I reached Revenant Graves but do not have the Censer

I follow the large statue-covered wall toward its far end.

### I found the Censer but cannot take it back

I make sure I actually interacted with it and completed the encounter.

### I have the Censer but Genessa has no new dialogue

I confirm that the encounter completed and that **Sester's Censer is actually
in my possession** before returning to Marrow Keep.

My unlock check is always:

**find Censer → complete encounter → obtain Censer → return to Genessa.**

<h2 id="where-to-upgrade-genessa">

Genessa's post-unlock progression is handled differently from several other
Shells.

After I unlock her, I use the **Stray that replaces her in the Keep** for her
progression rather than looking for the same Shellkeeper interaction I use
with characters such as Tiel or Eredrim.

So if I have successfully unlocked Genessa but cannot find where to progress
her, I do not assume the unlock failed.

<h2 id="worth-unlocking-genessa-early">
  Is Genessa worth unlocking early?
</h2>

I consider Genessa worth an early detour once the **Outskirts of Mammon route
is already reasonably comfortable**.

I go early when:

* the Outskirts route is open;
* the battlefield enemies are manageable;
* I can reach Revenant Graves without spending nearly all my healing;
* I specifically want Genessa rather than simply another Shell for the
  collection.

I wait when:

* corruption still blocks the southern route;
* ordinary enemies on the way are overwhelming my current setup;
* my weapon and Shell choices are still changing constantly;
* another major progression objective is directly in front of me.

Once I know the bridge drop and teleport gate, navigation is not the expensive
part.

The real cost is reaching **Sester's Censer with enough resources left to win
the encounter**.

If I am still uncertain about overall area order, I stay with the{" "}

<Link href="/mortal-shell-2/progression-guide">
  progression guide
</Link>
{" "}until the southern route fits naturally.

<h2 id="genessa-bond">
  I finish the Genessa Bond progression I care about before New Game+
</h2>

If I care about completing Genessa's **Bond** progression, I prefer finishing
it during the same playthrough rather than deliberately leaving it for New
Game+.

In the current build, carrying unfinished Genessa progression forward can be
unreliable.

My safer order is:

**unlock Genessa → complete the Bond progress I care about → finish the
playthrough.**

This does not change the unlock route.

It only changes what I finish after Genessa is already available.
