<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
There are **eight permanent Shells to find after the prologue** in Mortal
Shell 2:

**Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Genessa, Lazlo, and Sariel.**

The easiest early pickups are **Tiel**, in the graveyard southeast of
Widow's Overlook, and **Proxima**, in a cavern at Black Ridge Cliffs.

The other Shells have more substantial requirements:

- **Gragu** — bring the **Heart of Vatra** to the One-Legged Wolf tavern.
- **Eredrim** — progress through the Gloomshade Grove / Citadel route.
- **Smert** — complete the **three blood pools**.
- **Genessa** — recover **Sester's Censer** through Revenant Graves and return it.
- **Lazlo** — progress through the Royal Crypt route.
- **Sariel** — complete the **Chamber of Becoming** encounter.

**Harros is the prologue Shell and is separate from these eight permanent
open-world unlocks.**
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-shell-unlocks-work">
  How Shell unlocks work
</h2>

Finding a Shell and deciding to build that Shell are two different decisions.

I am happy to collect a body when the route is convenient, but I do not
immediately spend heavily on every new character.

**Glimpses** are the resource decision I take more seriously because they open
memories and deeper Shell progression.

My normal order is:

**find the Shell → try the base kit → test its playstyle → decide whether it
deserves more Glimpses.**

That matters especially with the later Shells. A difficult unlock does not
automatically mean the character should replace the build that carried me
there.

<h2 id="tiel">
  Tiel location — graveyard near Widow's Overlook
</h2>

**Tiel is one of the easiest permanent Shells to collect.**

From **Widow's Overlook**, I head southeast toward the Mushroom Village side of
Fainweald.

Before entering the village proper, I check the exposed **graveyard beside the
route**.

Tiel's body is there.

There is no boss requirement, which is why Tiel is usually one of the first
Shells I test.

His kit revolves around **Lingering Shadow**, mobility, stealth, and rewarding
good dodges. I do not need a specialized late-game setup before the character
starts making sense.

His first memory also costs only **one Glimpse**, so trying the opening part of
his progression is relatively cheap.

<h2 id="proxima">
  Proxima location — Black Ridge Cliffs
</h2>

**Proxima is another early Shell I can reach without clearing a mandatory
boss.**

From Widow's Overlook, I head north toward **Black Ridge Cliffs**.

I pass the enemies fighting along the approach rather than clearing every
group, climb toward the small cavern, and find Proxima's body on the altar
inside.

Proxima feels more aggressive than Tiel.

Her **Biosampler** behaves differently depending on the target: it can pull a
weaker enemy toward me or pull me toward a stronger one.

Her **Grafted Armor** also gives her a **15% chance to mitigate a hit**; when it
triggers, that hit deals **50% less damage** and does not stagger her.

I test Proxima early because the acquisition is cheap, not because I assume
every early Glimpse should go into her.

<h2 id="gragu">
  Gragu location — One-Legged Wolf and Heart of Vatra
</h2>

I find **Gragu** at the **One-Legged Wolf tavern** on the Mushroom Village side
of Fainweald.

Finding him is not enough to unlock the Shell.

**Gragu wants the Heart of Vatra.**

That turns this into a real quest chain:

**find Gragu → reach the Temple of Vatra → complete the Temple sequence → take
the Heart of Vatra → escape → return the Heart to Gragu.**

The Temple also contains an important decision involving **Berserker's Stone**,
so I do not duplicate that entire route here.

Gragu's main appeal to me is his healing economy.

**Revered Heart** replaces the ordinary healing rhythm with hearts earned from
enemies. It works well while I am moving through populated areas and less well
when I am repeatedly attempting one difficult boss with few enemies available
to rebuild the resource.

At each pool, I enter the blood, allow the encounter to force me out of my
Shell, recover, and repeat the process.

There are **three pools**.

The useful part is that completed blood-pool progress remains in place. If one
attempt goes badly, I do not have to restart all three objectives from zero.

That makes the unlock awkward, but not a single all-or-nothing sequence.

Smert himself is also a much higher-risk Shell than Tiel or Proxima.

His **Miracle** trades health for dramatic control over the pace of the fight,
while his attacks can build **Chaos** and lead into conditions including
poison, lightning, burn, or freeze.

He is a Shell I unlock because I specifically want that playstyle, not merely
because I am collecting the roster.

<h2 id="genessa">

The key objective is **Sester's Censer**.

Once I recover it, I return the Censer to Genessa to complete the important
part of her unlock sequence.

Because the route is much longer than Tiel or Proxima, I do not recommend
wandering into Revenant Graves merely because I want to add another name to

<h2 id="lazlo">
  Lazlo location — Royal Crypt route

**Lazlo sits behind the later Royal Crypt side of Mammon progression.**

My route runs through the **Deserted Slums**, toward **High Lord's Keep**, and
eventually into the **Royal Crypt** section.

The practical thing I do before committing is activate the nearby Beacon.

waves, and an overheat system.

I do not cross this much of Mammon simply to fill the Shell list. I make the
trip when the route already fits my progression or when Lazlo's durability is
what I actually want.

<h2 id="which-shell-first">
  Which Shell should I unlock first?
</h2>

For a normal first playthrough, I start with **Tiel or Proxima**.

Both are close to Widow's Overlook and neither requires the kind of boss or
quest chain attached to several later Shells.

I prefer:

- **Tiel** for mobility, stealth, and a forgiving first test.
- **Proxima** for a more aggressive early style and condition synergy.

After that, I let the build decide the route.

**Gragu** makes sense when I want his enemy-driven healing loop.

**Eredrim** gives me more Break and stagger pressure.

**Smert** is for a deliberate high-risk setup.

**Genessa** supports aggressive multi-body damage.

**Lazlo** gives me more late-game stability.

**Sariel** is the specialized Pain and Curse option I leave until his route
fits naturally.

I do not rank these eight only by raw power because acquisition cost, current
progression, weapon setup, and the amount of Glimpses I have already committed
can change which unlock is useful.

For this page, the important distinction is simpler:

**Tiel and Proxima are easy early pickups. The remaining six are Shells I route
toward deliberately.**
