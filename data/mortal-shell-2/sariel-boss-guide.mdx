<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
**Sariel, the Endless** is not one normal boss fight.

I reach his temple from the **Outskirts of Mammon**, follow the southern route
through the keep, and activate the nearby Beacon before starting the encounter.

The unlock has three combat stages:

**1. First encounter:** defeat Sariel outside the temple to open the route
deeper inside.

**2. Chamber of Becoming:** reduce Sariel to roughly **half Health**. The fight
transitions instead of requiring a full kill.

**3. Final arena:** destroy the **four statuettes** around the arena before I
finish Sariel. If I kill him while one is still active, he can revive.

After the final clear, the same route gives me **Sariel**, the **Clockwork
Scythe**, and the **Caged Hystrix** Sidearm.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="why-sariel-keeps-reviving">
  Why Sariel keeps reviving in the final fight
</h2>

If I empty Sariel's health bar and he comes back, I stop thinking about damage.

**Damage is not the problem.**

The final arena contains **four statuettes** positioned around its sides.

I need to destroy all four before I commit to the final kill.

This is the one rule I care about most:

**stay alive → destroy all four statuettes → finish Sariel.**

I can damage the boss while moving around the arena if I get a free opening.

What I do not do is let that damage distract me from the actual objective.

A fast health-bar kill is useless if Sariel simply gets back up.

→ temple approach

→ first Sariel fight

→ Chamber of Becoming

The trip is long enough that I treat navigation and the boss as separate

First I reach the checkpoint.

Then I worry about the fights.

<h2 id="final-arena">
  8. Final fight: destroy all four statuettes before killing Sariel
</h2>

The final encounter is not a normal DPS race.

There are **four statuettes** positioned around the arena.

Those are my first objectives.

I rotate around the outside and destroy them one at a time.

My priority is:

**statuette 1 → statuette 2 → statuette 3 → statuette 4 → Sariel.**

I do not need to avoid hitting Sariel completely.

If I get a safe punish while moving between objectives, I can take it.

But I do not finish him while any statuette is still active.

That distinction prevents the single most frustrating outcome in the entire
route:

**empty health bar → Sariel revives → fight continues.**

<h2 id="sariel-revives">
  If Sariel revives, I check the statuettes first
</h2>

If Sariel returns after I think I killed him, I do not immediately change
weapons.

I do not assume my damage is bugged.

And I do not assume the boss has an endless second health pool.

I look around the arena.

One of the four statuettes is still active.

That means I solved the health bar without solving the encounter.

Once all four are gone, I return my attention fully to Sariel and finish the
fight.

That is the real win condition.

<h2 id="boss-strategy">
  The combat rule I use across all three Sariel stages
</h2>

I do not need an attack-by-attack boss script to make this route manageable.

The bigger danger is overcommitting because I am thinking about the next
objective.

My priorities change by stage.

**First fight:** survive and complete the full health bar.

**Second fight:** reach the half-health transition efficiently.

**Final fight:** stay alive while removing the four statuettes.

That makes short, reliable punish windows much more valuable to me than long
melee chains.

If Sariel starts another attack while I am moving between objectives, I give up
the extra hit.

My rule is:

**when the arena gives me an objective, I solve the objective before chasing
more DPS.**

<h2 id="should-i-unlock-sariel-early">
  Is Sariel worth unlocking early?
</h2>

I do not rush Sariel just because I want all eight Shells checked off.

The route is long.

It has several navigation stages.

And the unlock itself asks me to clear multiple encounters.

I make the trip early when I specifically want one or more of the rewards:

- Sariel himself;
- Clockwork Scythe;
- Caged Hystrix;
- several major unlocks from one route.

I wait when:

- the southern keep is already consuming most of my healing;
- the first Sariel fight feels barely manageable;
- my current Shell is working well and I have more useful progression nearby;
- I only want Sariel for collection completion.

If I am deciding whether Sariel is actually worth my limited Shell investment,
I compare him with the other playable options in the{" "}
<Link href="/mortal-shell-2/all-shells-locations">
  all Shells locations guide
</Link>

The boss route itself has a much simpler rule:

**first fight opens the temple → second fight transitions around half Health →
destroy four statuettes before the final kill.**
