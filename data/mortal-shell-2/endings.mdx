<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
**Mortal Shell 2 has two ending sequences I can trigger in the same
playthrough: the main story ending and the secret Baghead ending.**

For the **Baghead secret ending**, I find Baghead on Desolate Peak, sit with
his followers, take the **Special Moonshine**, refuse to give it to him, put it
back where I found it, and return empty-handed. The answer to his request is
literally **nothing**. The credits roll and I unlock **Bag Holder**, but I can
choose Continue and resume the same playthrough.

For the **main ending**, I recover enough Ova to open the **Unfound Path**,
clear the final encounters, and defeat **Zmey, the Unbidden**. Beating Zmey does
**not** end the current cycle.

The real point of no return is choosing **Send Ova** at the Gloom Siphon in
Marrow Keep. That starts the final farewell and advances the save into
**New Game Plus**.

If I want both endings in one run, I do **Baghead first, finish anything else I
care about, then use Send Ova last**.

<h2 id="how-many-endings">
  How many endings are there in Mortal Shell 2?
</h2>

I count **two actual ending sequences**:

1. the **Baghead secret ending**;
2. the **main story ending** tied to Undermether, the Ova, and the Unfound Path.

I do not need two separate playthroughs to see them.

The important difference is what each ending does to the save.

The Baghead sequence rolls credits, but I can load back into the same run and
keep playing. The main ending remains open even after I defeat Zmey, but choosing
**Send Ova** commits the current cycle and moves the save into **New Game Plus**.

That makes my preferred order straightforward:

**Baghead → Continue → finish the main story → clean up anything I still want →
Send Ova.**

<h2 id="baghead-secret-ending">
  How to get the Baghead secret ending
</h2>

The Baghead ending is easy to miss because his request initially looks like a
normal fetch quest.

It is not.

The whole puzzle is built around understanding that Baghead wants
**nothing**.

I find him on **Desolate Peak in western Fainweald**, near the Illusionist's
Cache area. He sits on a throne with a group of followers around him.

My first instinct was to assume he wanted a rare item hidden nearby. The quest
deliberately encourages that assumption.

<h3 id="baghead-steps">
  Baghead secret ending steps
</h3>

This is the sequence I use:

1. **Talk to Baghead repeatedly** until he tells me to sit down and be quiet.
2. **Sit with his followers** using the nearby sitting interaction, wait
   briefly, then stand back up.
3. **Talk to Baghead again** and answer **Yes** when he asks whether I think I am
   different.
4. Let him give me his strange request.
5. Pick up the **Special Moonshine** near the Illusionist's Cache entrance.
6. Return to Baghead, but **do not give him the Moonshine as the answer**.
7. Take the Special Moonshine back and **put it where I found it**.
8. Return to Baghead **empty-handed**.
9. Confirm that I brought him **nothing**, including when he gives me the final
   warning.
10. Talk to him again to trigger the secret ending.

The important answer is:

**What should I give Baghead? Nothing.**

The Special Moonshine is part of the puzzle, but it is not the item I am
supposed to hand over. I need to use it to understand the joke, put it back, and
return with empty hands.

<h2 id="what-happens-baghead-ending">
  What happens in the Baghead ending?
</h2>

Once Baghead accepts that I finally understand nothing, the joke takes over the
entire run.

The Harbinger ends up on the throne as the **king of nothing**, surrounded by
Baghead's followers while time passes.

For a moment it looks like I have thrown away the playthrough.

I have not.

The credits roll, but afterward I can choose **Continue** and return to the same
save. The Baghead ending does not force the current run into New Game Plus and
does not stop me from completing the main story later.

That is why I am comfortable triggering it before the finale rather than saving
it for the very end.

<h2 id="bag-holder">
  How to unlock the Bag Holder achievement
</h2>

Completing Baghead's secret ending unlocks the hidden **Bag Holder**
achievement/trophy.

If the ending does not trigger, the three steps I check are the ones that are
easy to miss:

- I actually used the sitting interaction with Baghead's followers.
- I returned the **Special Moonshine** instead of keeping it.
- I came back with **nothing** and committed to that answer.

The Moonshine is the trap.

It looks like the obvious quest solution because the game intentionally places
it nearby. The puzzle only resolves when I recognize that **the item is not the
answer**.

<h2 id="main-ending">
  How to get the main Mortal Shell 2 ending

The main ending follows the Ova recovery story.

The threshold that opens the final route is **168 Ova**.

Once I have enough, the objective stops being about collecting more Ova for
progression and turns toward dealing with the **foul passenger** and entering
the **Unfound Path**.

I do not need to clear every remaining optional route before this happens.

<h2 id="unfound-path">
  What do I have to do in the Unfound Path?
</h2>

The Unfound Path is more than a straight corridor leading directly to Zmey.

I have to work through its major branches and clear the encounters protecting
the route forward.

The important late-game fights include:

- **Malborn Offspring**
- **The Monolith**
- **Orrem**
- **Zmey, the Unbidden**

I care less about forcing one exact branch order than I do about fully opening
the path toward the center.

This is also where I stop experimenting with completely unfamiliar gear.

I would rather bring the Shell whose dodge and defensive rhythm I already know,
the weapon whose recovery windows feel natural, and the Tarstones and upgrades
I have already tested.

If I have been saving resources for that setup, this is when I spend them.

The word "final" is not a good enough reason for me to rebuild the entire
character around equipment I have barely used.

<h2 id="point-of-no-return">
  Send Ova is the real point of no return
</h2>

The action I treat as the actual point of no return is **Send Ova** at the
Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep.

Neither entering the Unfound Path nor defeating Zmey is the final lock.

**Send Ova is.**

The game warns that sending the Ova concludes this part of the story and
advances the **New Game Plus cycle**.

So after Zmey, I do any current-cycle cleanup I genuinely care about before
selecting that option.

I do not panic when Walk the Unfound Path appears.

I do stop and think before I press **Send Ova**.

The ending finally gives that collection its narrative purpose. I was not only
raising a counter or opening another route; I was recovering enough of the kin
for Undermether to give them a future.

Once they are sent away, the focus shifts to the Harbinger.

<h2 id="harbinger-stays-behind">
  Why does the Harbinger stay behind?
</h2>

The Harbinger does not leave with the others.

Undermether tells him that **he was not made for the heavens**.

He has to stay behind.

More importantly, he is told to:

**find his own meaning.**

That is the part of the finale I find most important.

For most of the journey, purpose is assigned from somewhere else. The Ova must
be recovered. The foul passenger must be removed. Every Shell carries another
life, another history, and another identity that existed before the Harbinger
used it.

Then the final scene removes that structure.

The kin have somewhere to go.

The Harbinger does not.

For the first time, surviving does not automatically come with another
objective.

<h2 id="ending-meaning">
  Mortal Shell 2 ending explained
</h2>

I read the main ending as the Harbinger finally being separated from a life
defined by other people's purposes.

That fits the way the rest of the game is built.

I spend the journey inhabiting Shells, uncovering pieces of their memories, and
using abilities tied to identities that existed before me.

Even the main story gives the Harbinger a purpose before he has much opportunity
to choose one himself:

**recover the kin and help Undermether.**

By the time Zmey is gone and the Ova have been sent onward, that purpose has
been fulfilled.

The kin leave.

Undermether's task is complete.

The Harbinger remains.

So when he is told to find his own meaning, I do not read it as a hidden clue
for another quest marker. I read it as the first time the game deliberately
refuses to give him one.

<h2 id="ending-choice">
  Is there a good or bad ending choice?
</h2>

I do not get a final morality choice that splits the main story into a good
ending and a bad ending.

The main finale follows the same basic outcome:

**remove the foul passenger → send the kin onward → Harbinger stays behind.**

The alternate ending sequence is **Baghead**.

It is not unlocked by choosing an opposite answer during the final Undermether
scene, so I would not repeatedly replay Zmey looking for a hidden good/bad
dialogue branch.

If I want the other ending, I return to Baghead's **nothing** quest.

<h2 id="can-you-keep-playing">
  Can I keep playing after the ending?
</h2>

It depends on which point I mean.

### After the Baghead secret ending

**Yes.**

The credits roll, but I can choose Continue and return to the same playthrough.

### After defeating Zmey

**Yes.**

Zmey is the final boss, but the current cycle remains open until I choose
**Send Ova**.

This is the cleanest late-game window if I want to finish something after
beating the final boss.

### After choosing Send Ova

The save advances into **New Game Plus**.

That is the point I treat as irreversible for the current cycle.

So if the question is whether I can defeat the final boss and still explore,
the answer is **yes**.

If I want to stay in the same cycle, I finish that cleanup **before choosing
Send Ova**.

<h2 id="best-ending-order">
  Best order for getting both Mortal Shell 2 endings
</h2>

For one playthrough, this is the order I use:

1. Progress normally until I can reach **Desolate Peak**.
2. Complete Baghead's sitting interaction.
3. Pick up the **Special Moonshine**.
4. Refuse it as the answer and put it back.
5. Return to Baghead with **nothing**.
6. Trigger the **Baghead secret ending** and unlock **Bag Holder**.
7. Choose Continue and resume the same run.
8. Recover at least **168 Ova** and open the **Unfound Path**.
9. Clear the final encounters and defeat **Zmey, the Unbidden**.
10. Finish any current-cycle activities I still care about.
11. Return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep.
12. Choose **Send Ova**.
13. Watch the main ending and continue into **New Game Plus**.

This order gives me both ending sequences without sacrificing the main
playthrough to Baghead's joke.

It also keeps the only action I treat as truly irreversible —
**Send Ova** — until I am ready to leave the current cycle behind.
