<h2 id="how-many-endings">
  How many endings are there in Mortal Shell 2?
</h2>

There are **two distinct ending sequences**:

1. the **Baghead secret ending**;
2. the **main story ending** tied to the Ova, Undermether, the Unfound Path,
   Zmey, and Send Ova.

I do not need two separate playthroughs to see them.

The difference that matters most is what happens to the save. Baghead rolls
credits but lets me choose **Continue** and resume the same cycle. The main
route also remains open after defeating Zmey. The irreversible step comes
later when I choose **Send Ova** at the Gloom Siphon.

That makes the safest order:

**Baghead secret ending → Continue → main story → current-cycle cleanup →
Send Ova.**

<h2 id="true-ending">
  Is there a true ending in Mortal Shell 2?
</h2>

I do not count a separate third **true ending**.

Mortal Shell 2 gives me the Baghead secret ending and the main story finale.
When players refer to the "true ending," they are generally referring to the
main route that resolves the Ova storyline:

**168 Ova → Unfound Path → Zmey → Send Ova.**

There is also no final good-versus-bad morality choice that splits the main
story into two different finales. Baghead is the alternate ending sequence;
the final Undermether route does not branch into another hidden good or bad
ending at the last conversation.

<h2 id="baghead-secret-ending">
  How to get the Baghead secret ending
</h2>

I find **Baghead on Desolate Peak in western Fainweald**, near the
Illusionist's Cache area. He sits on a throne surrounded by followers.

His request looks like a normal fetch quest, but the solution is built around
one word:

**nothing.**

The nearby **Special Moonshine** is part of the trick. It is not the item I
ultimately give him.

### Baghead secret ending steps

1. **Talk to Baghead repeatedly** until he tells me to sit down and be quiet.
2. Use the nearby interaction to **sit with his followers**, wait briefly, then
   stand up.
3. Speak to Baghead again and answer **Yes** when he asks whether I think I am
   different.
4. Continue until he gives me his strange request.
5. Pick up the **Special Moonshine** near the Illusionist's Cache entrance.
6. Return to Baghead, but do **not** give him the Moonshine as the answer.
7. Take the Special Moonshine back and **put it where I found it**.
8. Return to Baghead **empty-handed**.
9. Confirm that I brought him **nothing**, including when he gives me the final
   warning.
10. Speak to him again to trigger the secret ending.

The answer to the part that causes the most confusion is simple:

**What should I give Baghead? Nothing.**

<h2 id="baghead-ending-result">
  What happens after the Baghead secret ending?
</h2>

Once Baghead accepts that I finally understand his request, the Harbinger ends
up on the throne as the **king of nothing**, surrounded by Baghead's followers
while time passes.

The credits then roll, but this does **not** sacrifice the playthrough.

I can choose **Continue** afterward and return to the same save. The main story
is still available, so I can complete Baghead before finishing the Unfound
Path or committing to Send Ova.

The sequence also unlocks the hidden **Bag Holder** achievement.

If the ending does not trigger, I check the three parts that are easiest to
miss:

- I actually used the sitting interaction with Baghead's followers.
- I returned the **Special Moonshine** to where I found it.
- I came back to Baghead with **nothing** and committed to that answer.

<h2 id="main-ending">
  How to get the main Mortal Shell 2 ending
</h2>

The main ending completes the Ova storyline. The important progression
threshold is **168 Ova**.

Once I recover enough, the story shifts from collecting Ova toward removing
the **foul passenger** that prevents the recovered kin from being sent onward.

The main ending route is:

1. Recover at least **168 Ova**.
2. Start **Walk the Unfound Path**.
3. Clear the mandatory endgame encounters.
4. Defeat **Zmey, the Unbidden**.
5. Finish anything I still want to do in the current cycle.
6. Return to the **Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep**.
7. Choose **Send Ova**.

<h2 id="point-of-no-return">
  Send Ova is the real point of no return
</h2>

The actual point of no return is **Send Ova** at the **Gloom Siphon in Marrow
Keep**.

Entering the Unfound Path does not end the cycle.

Defeating Zmey does not end the cycle.

**Choosing Send Ova does.**

The game warns me that sending the Ova concludes this part of the story and
advances the save into **New Game Plus**.

That means the safest time for optional cleanup is **after Zmey but before Send
Ova**. I only use the Gloom Siphon option once I am ready to leave the current
cycle behind.

<h2 id="ending-explained">
  Mortal Shell 2 ending explained
</h2>

I read the main ending as the point where the Harbinger is finally separated
from purposes inherited from other people.

Throughout the game, I inhabit Shells that belonged to other lives, uncover
their memories, and use abilities tied to identities that existed before the
Harbinger entered them. The main story gives him another purpose from outside
himself: recover the kin and help Undermether.

By the finale, that task is finished. The foul passenger is gone, the recovered
kin have been sent onward, and Undermether has no new mission waiting for him.

The Harbinger remains.

That is why **find your own meaning** works as the final note. I do not treat it
as a clue pointing toward a hidden third ending. The story deliberately ends
with the Harbinger having to decide what comes next for himself.

<h2 id="can-you-keep-playing">
  Can you keep playing after the ending?
</h2>

It depends on which part of the ending route I have reached.

### After the Baghead secret ending

**Yes.**

The credits roll, but I can choose Continue and return to the same playthrough.

### After defeating Zmey

**Yes.**

Zmey is the final mandatory boss, but the current cycle remains available
until I choose **Send Ova**.

This is the best late-game window for anything I still want to finish.

### After choosing Send Ova

The save advances into **New Game Plus**.

If I want to remain in the current cycle, I finish that work before selecting
Send Ova.

<h2 id="best-ending-order">
  Best order for getting both Mortal Shell 2 endings
</h2>

For both ending sequences in one playthrough, I use this order:

1. Progress until I can reach **Desolate Peak**.
2. Complete Baghead's sitting interaction.
3. Pick up the **Special Moonshine**.
4. Refuse it as the solution and return it to where I found it.
5. Go back to Baghead with **nothing**.
6. Trigger the **Baghead secret ending** and unlock **Bag Holder**.
7. Choose Continue and resume the same save.
8. Recover at least **168 Ova**.
9. Complete **Walk the Unfound Path**.
10. Defeat **Zmey, the Unbidden**.
11. Finish anything else I still want in the current cycle.
12. Return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep.
13. Choose **Send Ova** and continue into **New Game Plus**.

This lets me see Baghead without sacrificing the main run and keeps the only
irreversible step — **Send Ova** — until I am ready for it.
