<h2 id="axe-dagger-route">
  The Axe & Dagger route I use
</h2>

The whole route becomes much easier once I stop treating the Axe & Dagger as a
normal map-marker pickup.

My route is:

**Mushroom Village**

→ cross the nearby bridge

→ walk through the hidden archway

→ pass through the hidden statue

→ collect **Chapel Key**

→ return to the chapel

→ enter **Shrine of Trials**

→ clear the Shrine

→ collect **Axe & Dagger**.

The biggest navigation mistake is trying to approach the weapon icon directly.

The weapon is at the end of the route.

The **Chapel Key** is what actually gets me there.

<h2 id="why-i-couldnt-reach-axe-dagger">
  Why following the Axe & Dagger marker did not work
</h2>

The Axe & Dagger looks close enough to Mushroom Village that I initially expect
a short road to lead directly toward it.

That is what sends me in circles.

I can get physically close to the marker without reaching the route that
actually leads to the weapon.

Once that happens, I stop chasing the icon.

I reset my navigation from **Mushroom Village** instead.

<h2 id="shrine-of-trials">
  3. Use the Chapel Key at the Shrine of Trials
</h2>

With the Chapel Key collected, I return toward the chapel outside Mushroom
Village.

This opens the route into the **Shrine of Trials**.

At this point, the nature of the route changes.

The difficult part before the Shrine is recognizing the illusions.

Inside the Shrine, the difficult part is surviving the encounters.

I do not keep looking for another hidden key.

I move forward through the trial.

<h2 id="inside-shrine">
  4. Clear the Shrine instead of sprinting straight for the weapon
</h2>

The Shrine of Trials contains enough enemies that I do not treat it like a room
I can simply run through because I already know the reward is at the end.

I take the encounters in manageable groups.

One useful landmark inside is the inscription:

**“Gaze upon your king. So true. Back and back you shall pass through.”**

<h2 id="test-before-upgrading">
  What I test before spending resources on the Axe & Dagger
</h2>

I try the weapon before committing to it.

The things I care about are simple:

- the normal light chain;
- the running attacks;
- how far each attack carries me forward;
- how quickly I can recover into a dodge;
- whether I actually enjoy fighting this close to enemies.

The last point matters more than the raw attack speed.

A faster weapon gives me more opportunities to attack.

It also gives me more opportunities to press one extra time when I should
already be leaving the enemy's range.

So I do not interpret:

**fast weapon**

as:

**safe to keep attacking.**

Some enemies will still fight through my pressure.

When that happens, I stop trying to turn speed into permanent stagger.

I take the safe hits, dodge out, and start the next opening cleanly.

<h2 id="duality">
  Duality is the first upgrade that makes me consider investing
</h2>

The Axe & Dagger's normal light chain contains **three strikes**.

With **Duality**, each part of that sequence hits twice.

That changes the basic rhythm from:

**one → two → three**

to:

**one-two → one-two → one-two.**

<h2 id="worth-getting-early">
  Is the Axe & Dagger worth getting early?
</h2>

For me, yes — **when I already know I want a faster early weapon**.

I make the detour when:

- the Iconoclast feels slower than I want;
- I prefer rapid close-range pressure;
- I want to test a high-hit-frequency weapon before spending heavily elsewhere;
- the Shrine encounters are manageable with my current setup.

I skip it for now when:

- I already like the range and rhythm of my current weapon;
- I am collecting it only because it is available early;
- I do not enjoy staying close enough to exploit the faster moveset;
- Shrine enemies are costing more healing than the weapon detour is worth.

That is why I do not call the Axe & Dagger a mandatory early upgrade.

It is an **early alternative**.

If its speed solves something I dislike about my current weapon, the route is
worth doing.

If my current setup already feels good, I lose nothing by leaving the Shrine
for later.
