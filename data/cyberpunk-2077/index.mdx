Load your existing save and make sure **The Heist** is complete. Finish two Wakako gigs to unlock **Clampdown**, or reach **15 Street Cred** for **Welcome to Paradise**. For dual wielding, open the Cool tree and unlock **Double Trouble** at Cool 9.

**Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5**, the free Edgerunners 2 update released on October 22, 2026, adds two substantial side jobs, a new dual-wielding combat system, 12 Minor Arcana Tarot Cards, cyberware appearance customization, and built-in mod support.

You can experience the new content on an existing character. There is no need to restart the main story or buy Phantom Liberty. The additions also have separate unlock requirements, so completing one new quest does not unlock the other.

For a returning character, the most useful starting point is your current story and attribute progression. Someone who has already finished The Heist can begin working toward both jobs immediately. A character with 9 Cool can also try Double Trouble without touching either quest.

<section id="starting" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which New Update 2.5 Content Should You Start First?

The update adds several activities, but they do not form one continuous questline.

**Clampdown** is the action-heavy option. You infiltrate Night City Prison to help King rescue Miguel, and the operation includes Reaper Chip decisions, a prison breakout, and King's new equipment.

**Welcome to Paradise** is an investigation. You follow a guided tour narrated by Richard Night, examine the stolen NC Revitalization Project shard, and uncover what happened to a missing hacker named Haze.

**Double Trouble** is available through the Cool perk tree. It is unrelated to either side job, so you can start testing paired Power handguns as soon as your character meets the attribute requirement.

For a character who qualifies for everything, the order is flexible:

- Want the new Edgerunners-related equipment? Start with Clampdown.
- Interested in Night Corp's plans and an investigation with a final dialogue choice? Play Welcome to Paradise.
- Already have a handgun-focused character? Check Double Trouble before changing your build.
- Prefer exploration or customization? The new Tarot Cards and Glamdocs do not require finishing the two new side jobs.

That separation matters when returning on a completed save. Waiting for the wrong quest, leveling the wrong attribute, or searching a vendor for equipment obtained through a mission can waste time.

## Clampdown: Start King's Prison Rescue

Finish **The Heist** and **two gigs for Wakako Okada**, then read Wakako's message about King's request. Follow the marker to the meeting point outside Night City Prison.

King's clothing and the iconic Coronation revolver are obtained during the prison infiltration, not purchased from a clothing vendor.

Once the guards take V inside, <Link href="/cyberpunk-2077/clampdown-walkthrough" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">recover the bathroom package and disable the Reaper chips before continuing the prison escape</Link>.

## Welcome to Paradise: How to Get Aera's Call

Complete **The Heist** and reach **Street Cred 15**. Aera then contacts V about her missing sister, Haze. Character level does not replace the Street Cred requirement.

The investigation begins with a hidden shard in Kabuki and continues through nine Night Corp tour stops. You will eventually need to decrypt an Identity Cache and use its contents to identify a suspicious red Caliburn.

If the tour stops advancing or the shard objective remains active, <Link href="/cyberpunk-2077/welcome-to-paradise-walkthrough" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">follow Haze's investigation from the first terminal through the final decision with Aera</Link>.

## Can Your Existing Character Dual Wield?

At **Cool 9**, you can unlock Double Trouble and equip two compatible Power Pistols or Power Revolvers. Tech and Smart handguns do not qualify.

Check your unspent Attribute Points before using your one-time attribute reset. Characters who already meet the Cool requirement can refund Perk Points to try the new ability.

For the weapon slots, damage penalty, Heywood Hurricane controls, and six-point perk investment, <Link href="/cyberpunk-2077/dual-wield" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">set up Double Trouble with your existing handguns</Link>.

## How to Start the 12 New Minor Arcana Tarot Quest

Update 2.5 adds **12 Minor Arcana Tarot Cards** across Night City.

They form a separate collection from the older Major Arcana cards. The Tarot menu now divides the two sets, so returning players who already finished Fool on the Hill should check the Minor Arcana section instead of assuming their previous collection is incomplete.

The new quest activates when you scan **any one** of the 12 newly added cards.

Each suit contains three cards:

- **Swords:** Queen, Knight, and Page.
- **Wands:** Queen, Knight, and Page.
- **Cups:** Queen, Knight, and Page.
- **Pentacles:** Queen, Knight, and Page.

This gives you four suits with three cards each, for 12 total.

Misty explains that the suits represent different aspects of V's personal relationships. Swords concern intellect and judgment, Wands concern ambition and action, Cups concern emotions and attachment, and Pentacles concern material life and achievement.

### What Should You Do After Scanning the First Card?

Check the Minor Arcana collection in the Tarot menu and follow the newly activated objective.

You can speak with Misty about the meaning of the cards as you discover them. Her dialogue helps distinguish the new collection from the original Major Arcana quest.

There is no reason to search for another copy of an old Major Arcana mural simply because the number of uncollected cards has increased.

When you find another Minor Arcana, scan it and check whether the collection progress advances.

### What Happens After Collecting All 12?

Return to **Misty** after completing the collection.

She asks which of the four suits you feel most connected to. Choose the suit that best matches the response you want to give.

Once the conversation advances to the reward, look for the package placed on the table and collect it.

The final pickup is a separate step from simply completing the dialogue. Check the nearby table before leaving Misty's shop.

### What if You're Stuck at 11/12?

Open the Minor Arcana collection and identify exactly which card remains missing.

The new cards are associated with places and personal connections around Night City. It is worth checking whether the missing location is connected to a character's unfinished side-job sequence before assuming the map marker is broken.

One location worth checking carefully is **Edgewood Farm** for the Knight of Cups, which is connected to River Ward's storyline. If the card does not appear there, continue the relevant River quests and return to inspect the location again.

The precise appearance condition for that card is not established, so an absent marker alone does not prove your save is bugged.

Avoid revisiting the same wall repeatedly without changing anything about your quest progress. Check the remaining card name, the relevant area, and your ongoing side jobs first.

## Where Are Glamdocs and How Do You Change Cyberware Appearance?

The new **Glamdocs** allow you to customize the appearance of equipped arm cyberware.

There are **five Glamdocs** around Night City. Open your map and look for their **spray-paint icons** rather than searching only for the usual Ripperdoc symbols.

Before visiting, check which arm cyberware you actually use. The new appearance options apply to:

- Mantis Blades
- Gorilla Arms
- Monowire
- Projectile Launch System

Each Glamdoc operates in a distinct shop with a different selection of available designs. The update introduces **40 cyberware styles**, with additional color and decal customization after buying a style.

### What Should You Buy First?

Choose a design for the arm cyberware you already have equipped.

These are appearance customizations, not substitutes for buying or upgrading functional cyberware. Purchasing a new look is not the same thing as increasing damage, changing an ability, or unlocking another Operating System.

It makes sense to visit multiple Glamdocs when looking for a particular design, since their catalogs differ. There is no reason to purchase a style you do not like simply to access the next shop.

Players focused on combat upgrades should prioritize their actual cyberware setup and treat Glamdoc purchases as optional customization.

## New Clothing Sets and Where to Shop

The new fashion collections are distributed across ordinary clothing vendors rather than being concentrated in a single store.

Check the following areas based on the collection you want:

- **Maxiwear:** Heywood, Arroyo, and Little China.
- **Jinguji:** City Center.
- **NetWatch:** Kabuki and Japantown.
- **IconAmerica:** Pacifica, Badlands, and Northside.
- **NUS Infiltrator:** Kabuki and Japantown.

An additional announced outfit set is scheduled to arrive later.

This is separate from King's outfit acquired during Clampdown. Clothing vendors are useful for the new purchasable collections, but they are not an alternative route for obtaining the prison job's equipment.

Update 2.5 also makes some previously NPC-exclusive hairstyles available to V, with certain styles restricted by body type.

For players interested in visual customization, the new Glamdoc appearances and clothing collections can be explored without finishing either of the new story missions.

## How Cross-Platform Mods Work in Update 2.5

Update 2.5 introduces built-in mod support through **mod.io**.

The game now has an integrated Mods panel for browsing and installing a supported selection of community-created content.

This feature is available on PC, Mac, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 once the update is available for that platform.

The important distinction is between the mods accessible through this new integration and the much broader range of modifications that have traditionally been available for PC.

Not every PC mod is automatically available through the new cross-platform library. The supported selection can expand over time, and individual mods may have their own compatibility requirements.

### Before Enabling Mods on Your Main Save

Create a separate manual save before experimenting with mods.

This is especially important for players still collecting achievements or trophies. On consoles, **achievements and trophies cannot be earned while playing from a modded save file**.

The Load Game menu identifies modded saves with a dedicated icon, making it easier to distinguish them from other playthroughs.

A practical approach is to keep:

- One unmodded save for normal progression and achievements.
- A separate save for experimenting with modded gameplay.

Do not assume that switching a mod off immediately restores achievement eligibility to a save that has already been marked as modded.

There is no need to install community mods to use Double Trouble, the new story quests, Glamdocs, or Minor Arcana. Those are part of the official update.

## Other Update 2.5 Changes Worth Checking

Some smaller changes are immediately useful on a character with a long play history.

### Saves Are Grouped by Playthrough

The Load Game menu now groups saves belonging to different playthroughs.

This is useful if you have multiple Vs with different attributes, story decisions, or relationships. Before replaying a new side job to compare a choice, check that you are saving into the intended character's progression.

### Apartment and Vehicle Stashes Have Filters

Both apartment and vehicle stashes now support item filters.

For returning players with large inventories, this makes it easier to locate stored weapons and equipment instead of manually inspecting every item.

### Console Field of View Can Be Adjusted

An FOV slider is now available on consoles.

Changing FOV can make movement and peripheral awareness feel different, but it does not change the underlying attack mechanics or enemy behavior. Adjust it based on visibility and personal comfort.

### New Radio Station and Photo Mode Options

**HKGN 102.0 FM** features music connected to Cyberpunk: Edgerunners and Edgerunners 2.

Photo Mode also gains dual-wielding poses and frames inspired by the Cyberpunk Trading Card Game.

Players on supported hardware can additionally benefit from graphics and performance changes, including DLSS 4.5 support, updated FSR options, HDR improvements, and further platform optimizations. The exact settings available depend on your system.

## Do You Need Phantom Liberty or a New Save for Update 2.5?

**No.** Update 2.5 is free for owners of the base game on supported platforms.

The update launched on October 22, 2026 for PC, Mac through Steam, GOG and Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. The Nintendo Switch 2 and Mac App Store versions have a separate rollout schedule.

Your existing save remains usable.

If the game has updated but no new side job appears, check the individual unlock conditions rather than starting a new character. The Heist is the shared story milestone, while Wakako's completed gigs and Street Cred determine which of the two new jobs can begin.

The update's combat, customization, and quality-of-life features can be used independently of the new quest endings.
