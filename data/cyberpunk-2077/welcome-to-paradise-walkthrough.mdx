**Welcome to Paradise** sends V across Night City to investigate the disappearance of Haze, a hacker who stole information from Night Corp. The shard she left behind contains a guided tour narrated by Richard Night, but her own messages gradually reveal what the corporation is planning.

The investigation is largely about following clues rather than winning fights. Most tour stops take only a terminal interaction, while two separate operations involving the same shard can interrupt progression. The first happens after NCART, when the file must be decrypted. The second comes after the Med Center, when you have to open the decrypted file again and identify a Red Caliburn.

At the end, you discover what happened to Haze and decide whether her sister Aera should receive the information that put Haze in danger.

<section id="unlock" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Start Welcome to Paradise in Cyberpunk 2077

Welcome to Paradise is a side job added in Update 2.5. It has two requirements:

- Complete **The Heist** main job.
- Reach **Street Cred Level 15**.

Once both conditions are met, Aera contacts V and explains that her sister, Haze, has disappeared.

Haze was investigating Night Corp and stole a shard containing sensitive information. She feared someone was following her, so she hid the shard before disappearing. Aera asks V to recover it and discover what happened.

Accept Aera's request to begin the investigation.

### Why Aera Hasn't Called

Check your Street Cred rather than your character level. These are separate progression values, and reaching character Level 15 does not satisfy the Street Cred requirement.

Players returning on an existing save do not need to replay The Heist. Make sure Update 2.5 is installed, confirm both prerequisites, and continue playing or advance the in-game clock while waiting for Aera's call.

Welcome to Paradise is separate from Clampdown. Completing Wakako's gigs or rescuing Miguel is not required to receive Aera's job.

## Find Haze's Lost Shard Near Creek Loop

Aera directs V to **Kabuki, Watson**, where Haze concealed the stolen shard.

The closest fast-travel point is **Creek Loop**. Follow the job marker into Kabuki and look for the lower passage beside a corrugated metal wall. A yellow railing runs along the raised walkway above it.

Search the corner around the metal panel and nearby pipe for Haze's hidden **NC Revitalization Project** shard. Once you collect it, the objective changes to **Slot the shard**, followed by **Listen to the recording on the shard**.

Continue the recording to begin Richard Night's guided tour.

## Night Corp Tower: Find the First Tour Terminal

The first tour destination is Night Corp Tower in **Downtown, City Center**.

This is the most confusing entrance in the entire tour because the job marker can lead directly to an elevator without showing an obvious terminal at street level.

Enter Night Corp Tower and take the elevator to **Floor 3**. Walk straight ahead after exiting the elevator until you reach the **City Tour terminal** beneath the large illuminated display.

Interact with **Start the Night Corp Tower tour** to begin the presentation.

Activate the terminal to begin the first stop. Richard Night appears and explains his ambitions for the city, including how Miriam Knight assumed control following his death.

When requested, connect to the nearby drone to continue the presentation. The drone interaction belongs to the tour; it is not a separate puzzle requiring a Cyberdeck build.

### The Quest Marker Keeps Pointing to the Elevator

Reaching the correct building does not automatically advance this objective. You must find and interact with the Night Corp kiosk.

If the marker still points toward the elevator, select Floor 3 and walk straight ahead after exiting. Look for the illuminated display and the City Tour terminal rather than searching the other floors.

Once the first presentation finishes, the next stop is **Corpo Plaza**.

## Continue the Night Corp Tour From Corpo Plaza to NCART

After completing the presentation at Night Corp Tower, the next five stops take V through City Center, Heywood, the Badlands, and the transit system. Each stop requires activating its marked terminal and letting the presentation finish before the next destination becomes available.

Follow them in this order:

1. **Corpo Plaza, City Center:** Activate the terminal near Arasaka Tower. Haze's interruption raises questions about Richard Night's death. Contact Aera when prompted afterward.
2. **Arasaka Memorial, City Center:** Continue the presentation about the 2023 bombing and Night City's reconstruction. The additional historical account is optional.
3. **City Hall, The Glen:** Follow the marker to City Hall and complete the drone-view interaction concerning the city's security infrastructure.
4. **Biotechnica Farms, Badlands:** Activate the terminal at the agricultural facility. After the presentation, Aera reports that Militech has searched Haze's apartment.
5. **NCART:** Continue Richard Night's transportation presentation until V discovers that the investor's identity information is stored on the tour shard.

The NCART stop introduces the first major interruption to the route. Once the objective changes to decrypting the tour shard, open **Journal → Shards** rather than traveling directly to Coronado Dam.

## How to Decrypt the NC Revitalization Project Identity Cache

The tour shard is not decrypted by interacting with another street terminal.

Open the in-game menu and navigate to **Journal → Shards**. Find the **Encrypted** category and locate:

**NC Revitalization Project – Identity Cache**

Open that entry and use the displayed **Crack Security** command to decrypt it.

The exact button depends on your control scheme. The important distinction is that you are opening an encrypted file from the Shards interface, not searching for a physical computer at the current tour stop.

The decryption exposes information about the investor connected to the Night Corp project.

### The Objective Still Says "Decrypt the Tour Shard"

Make sure you are viewing the **Encrypted** category rather than the regular list of readable shards.

This distinction is easy to miss on a character who has collected hundreds of documents. The required file is not another object waiting at NCART, and moving to a different location will not complete the decryption.

After the security operation, close the menu and allow the current objective to update.

The tour can now continue to Coronado Dam.

## Coronado Dam and Med Center: Finish the Tour Before Searching the Shard Again

### Coronado Dam

Follow the next tour marker toward Coronado Dam.

This section brings V back into a potentially hostile area. Deal with enemies obstructing the objective and reach the marked terminal.

Richard discusses the redevelopment of Rancho Coronado and presents the dam as part of Night City's industrial progress. Haze interrupts with memories of the district's residents and the consequences of corporate redevelopment.

The presentation may require you to move to the indicated observation position beyond the railing to finish the dialogue.

Once the stop completes, travel to the Med Center.

### Med Center

The eighth stop concerns the medical system and Richard Night's plans for privileged healthcare.

Activate the terminal and let the presentation run. The recording eventually reaches information about healthcare access, illegal organ trading, and the difference between corporate promises and ordinary citizens' experiences.

Haze's commentary is interrupted when she suspects someone is following her.

Contact Aera after the tour stop. V connects Haze's earlier warning to the mysterious message:

**Red Cal.**

The next objective requires looking for clues in the tour shard.

This is a second interaction with the Identity Cache, but it works differently from the earlier decryption.

## Where to Find the Red Caliburn Clue in the Shard

Return to **Journal → Shards**, but this time open the **Other** category.

Look for **NC Revitalization Project** under Other. This is the Identity Cache you decrypted earlier, so it should no longer require the Crack Security interaction.

Open the document and scroll through its contents until V identifies information linking the investor to a **red Caliburn**.

The car is the missing connection between the investor and Haze's message.

Haze was not telling Aera to retrieve a vehicle. She was leaving a clue about the person who had been following her.

Once V recognizes the connection, close the Shards menu and allow the dialogue and objective to update.

### Why "Look for Clues in the Tour Shard" Won't Complete

Three separate actions are involved:

- Decrypting the Identity Cache earlier in the mission.
- Opening the decrypted file from the **Other** category.
- Reading or scrolling through the file until the Red Caliburn connection is recognized.

Completing the first action does not automatically satisfy the later investigation.

When the file is missing from Encrypted, that can simply mean it has already been decrypted. Check Other before assuming the mission has lost your item.

After V identifies the investor's vehicle, the tour advances to its final destination in Kabuki.

## Kabuki Tour Stop: Find Haze at the Bridge

The ninth stop takes V back to **Kabuki, Watson**.

Activate the final Night Corp tour terminal and listen as Richard describes the planned transformation of the district.

Haze's final interruption is much more personal than the earlier messages. She talks about resisting Night Corp's plans and recalls spending time at a bridge in Kabuki.

That bridge becomes your next destination.

Follow the job marker toward the bridge. Once there, look over the edge toward the lower area instead of continuing to search along the main roadway.

Militech soldiers can be found below.

### Scan Haze's Body and Collect the Evidence

Scan Haze's body to complete the investigation.

Collect the recording associated with **Haze and Kaiwen Chiang**, and examine the evidence before leaving.

The discovery confirms that the wealthy investor connected to the red Caliburn was involved in Haze's death. She had been targeted because of what she learned about Night Corp's redevelopment plans.

The final evidence connects three details that previously seemed unrelated:

- The stolen Night Corp presentation intended for an investor.
- Haze's message mentioning a red Caliburn.
- The Militech presence and Haze's death near the bridge.

Contact Aera to tell her that you have found out what happened.

She asks to meet in person.

<section id="aera-choice" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Should You Give Aera Haze's Shard in Welcome to Paradise?

Meet Aera at the marked basketball court and tell her about her sister.

The conversation is not simply about collecting payment. Aera wants the evidence Haze died trying to protect, and she believes it can help continue her sister's work.

V understands the danger. Night Corp and the people connected to the stolen data have already demonstrated how far they are willing to go to suppress the investigation.

At the end of the conversation, Aera asks for the shard.

### Give Aera the Shard

Handing over the information fulfills the original request.

V can also provide the additional evidence connecting Chiang to Haze's death.

Aera thanks V for discovering the truth. She is devastated by her sister's death, but the evidence strengthens her determination to act against the people responsible.

She understands that the information is dangerous and remains committed to pursuing Haze's cause.

Choose this outcome if you want Aera to know the full truth and decide for herself whether to continue the investigation.

### Refuse to Give Aera the Shard

V can instead refuse, arguing that sharing the information will put Aera in the same danger that killed Haze.

Aera does not accept the decision as protection.

She hired V to recover the information, and refusing to surrender it leaves her without the evidence she believes belongs to her sister.

The conversation ends angrily, with Aera telling V not to contact her again.

This outcome keeps the dangerous information away from Aera, but it also denies her the opportunity to act on it.

### Does the Choice Change the Reward or Lead to Another Mission?

Both decisions conclude Welcome to Paradise and provide the normal job payment.

The established differences are Aera's reaction and whether she receives Haze's evidence. The conversation suggests different possibilities for her future, but it does not demonstrate a separate follow-up mission or a guaranteed later death caused by handing over the shard.

There is also **no red Caliburn awarded for completing the investigation**. The vehicle is a clue to identifying the person involved in Haze's death, not the mission's car reward.

For players who want to experience both conversations, make a manual save before speaking to Aera at the basketball court.

After the decision and final dialogue, Welcome to Paradise is complete.
