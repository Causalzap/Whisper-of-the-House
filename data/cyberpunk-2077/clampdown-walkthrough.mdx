<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Clampdown in Cyberpunk 2077?">
After **The Heist** and two Wakako gigs, read Wakako's message and meet King near Night City Prison. Inside, collect the bathroom package and disable the Reaper chips using the prison server.

Clampdown begins as a rescue job for King's captured netrunner, Miguel, but the plan puts V inside Night City Prison with a remotely controlled execution chip implanted in their head. Getting arrested is part of the operation. The real danger begins once the guards can activate that chip during combat.

There are two moments worth paying particular attention to. The first is the Security Cortex, where accessing the prisoner records does not actually disable the Reapers. The second comes in Block C, where King and Meshuga disagree about using the prisoners' execution chips to clear the crew's escape route.

You can also obtain King's clothing and the iconic **Coronation Tech Revolver** during the mission, rather than waiting until the prison break is complete.

<section id="unlock" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Start Clampdown in Cyberpunk 2077

Clampdown was added in Update 2.5. Its unlock requirements are:

- Complete the main job **The Heist** and progress beyond the early post-Heist sequence.
- Complete **two gigs for Wakako Okada**.
- Wait for Wakako's message, **A Request from King**, and reply to accept the introduction.

After the message exchange, track Clampdown in your journal. The meeting location is near **BuryGer**, across from Night City Prison. **MLK & Brandon** is a convenient fast-travel point for the approach.

At the meeting spot, sit down and wait. King contacts V remotely instead of appearing in person. He explains that Miguel, a member of his crew, is imprisoned and needs to be extracted. His team will enter the maximum-security section while V provides support from inside the prison.

### Wakako's Message Is Not Appearing

Check the completed gig entries in your journal first. Merely receiving several gigs from Wakako is not enough; you need two completed jobs from her.

Next, make sure the game has updated to 2.5 and that you have progressed beyond The Heist. With those requirements fulfilled, continue playing or advance the in-game clock and check your messages again.

There is no need to start a new character or purchase Phantom Liberty specifically for Clampdown.

Once the meeting with King is complete, prepare for an extended prison sequence. You will temporarily lose access to your usual equipment, although you recover it later. A manual save before entering is useful if you want to revisit the mission's decisions.

## Enter Night City Prison and Get Through Security

The visitors' entrance is across from the meeting area. Walk to the entrance and use the intercom to identify yourself as a scheduled visitor.

Follow the guard through the security checkpoint. You will be instructed to pass through the gate and place your hand on a scanner for a criminal-record check.

The scanner triggers an arrest. Darius and the prison staff overpower V, and a **Reaper Chip** is implanted before V wakes inside the prison's BD therapy area.

This is not a failed infiltration. King has arranged the false criminal record to get V inside, although he deliberately concealed how unpleasant that part of the plan would be.

### Contact Meshuga and Open the Holding Room

After regaining control, you meet **Wex**, a prisoner subjected to repeated Braindance therapy. Wex explains that the Reaper implants allow the guards to execute inmates remotely.

Eventually, King's netrunner **Meshuga** contacts you. The next objective is to escape the holding room so you can reach the smuggled equipment.

Look toward the security cameras above the room. With camera control available, you can work through the surveillance system, including **Camera 117: Security Cortex**, to reach the control panel that opens the holding-room door.

If the camera interaction is not available to your character, Meshuga can also work from location details you provide during the conversation. The useful landmark is that you are near the **BD therapy entrance**, with another waiting room across from you.

Once the door opens, move out into the corridor and follow Meshuga's instructions toward the bathroom.

## Where to Find King's Outfit and Coronation Revolver

King has arranged for a package to be left in the prison bathroom.

Follow the route out of the holding area and look for the **duffel bag beneath the bathroom bench**. Interact with the package before leaving the room.

The package provides King's distinctive clothing, including his coat and glasses, along with **Coronation**, his iconic Tech Revolver.

King's clothing is useful as a temporary disguise in the prison, while Coronation provides a weapon for the upcoming fights.

A few details are worth knowing before continuing:

- **Coronation is a Tech Revolver**, not a Power Revolver.
- The prison sequence does not require you to buy the new outfit sets sold by Night City clothing vendors.
- Your original equipment is still elsewhere in the prison. You will recover it at the Armory later in the mission.

Once you have the package, Meshuga explains that Miguel also has a Reaper Chip. The rescue cannot proceed safely until those implants are disabled.

<section id="security-cortex" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Reach the Security Cortex and Disable the Reaper Chips

The route to the Security Cortex takes you through guarded corridors.

Your immediate danger is not simply incoming weapon damage. Guards can attempt to **activate V's Reaper Chip** while you are fighting.

Watch for the Reaper activation warning and deal with the guard attempting to trigger it. Eliminating the remaining guards is the straightforward way to clear the passage.

One of the guards drops an **Access Badge** needed to continue. Collect it before leaving the area.

Proceed into the Security Cortex and use the terminal to inspect the prison's inmate records.

### Why the Prison Records Terminal Does Not Work

The records contain information connected to V, Miguel, and optionally Wex. Meshuga initially expects to use those entries to disable the implants.

However, the relevant files are **read-only**.

You have reached the correct objective, but this terminal cannot change the chip states. Looking for another file in the same records interface will not solve the problem.

Meshuga instead directs V to the Reaper server itself.

### Find the Correct Reaper Server

Leave the records terminal and move into the connected server area. Look for the red-lit section of the room rather than returning to the previous security corridor.

Once inside the server room, turn back toward the entrance. The correct server connection is on the **left side when facing the doorway**.

Jack into the correct server. The prison's intrusion detection activates, and V's Reaper begins its execution sequence.

**Do not disconnect just because the activation warning appears.** Meshuga needs to complete the intrusion so you can shut the implants down.

Once access is granted, work through the deactivation options:

1. **Deactivate Your Reaper** to remove the immediate threat to V.
2. **Deactivate Miguel's Reaper** so King's team can extract him.
3. **Deactivate Wex's Reaper**, if you want to complete the optional rescue objective.

The order of the first two does not change the need to complete both. Make sure you deal with Wex before leaving the server interface if you intend to help that prisoner.

### Should You Deactivate Wex's Reaper?

Wex is not a member of King's rescue crew, so disabling this implant is optional.

The prisoner you met after waking up has already suffered from the prison's BD treatment, and disabling the chip removes the execution threat that otherwise remains over Wex.

This is a separate decision from the later Block C choice. Saving Wex does not replace the required deactivation of V's and Miguel's implants.

Disabling Wex's chip is optional. V and Miguel's Reapers are the ones that must be disabled for the rescue to proceed, while Wex's chip can be handled during the same server interaction.

Once the required Reapers are disabled, Meshuga can start the next stage of the operation.

<section id="armory" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Fight Through the Prison Yard and Recover Your Weapons

After the Reaper shutdown, Meshuga prepares to trigger an alarm and draw security forces toward V.

Confirm that you are ready, then push through the newly opened route.

King's team is simultaneously working toward Miguel's cell in maximum-security **Block B**. Your job is to keep moving toward the Armory while the guards are being redirected.

The escape passes through three main combat areas:

1. The corridors leading away from the Security Cortex.
2. The elevator route and prison yard.
3. The Armory approach, where additional guards respond as the crew's extraction progresses.

You encounter groups of guards and a security hoverbot in the yard. Clearing the immediate threats is necessary to continue, but the mission does not ask you to search every cell or defeat every remaining enemy in the facility.

During this section, King reports that Miguel has been secured. That does not mean V can leave yet.

Continue toward the Armory and retrieve **your original equipment and the explosive mine**.

The mine is a required item for the final escape. The normal equipment you brought into the mission also becomes available again here.

Before leaving the Armory, re-equip the weapons and Cyberware configuration you actually want to use. The prison's toughest armored opponent is still ahead, and the temporary revolver from the bathroom does not have to remain your primary weapon.

Meshuga soon interrupts with another emergency. King's crew has reached Block C, but the attempt to create an escape route has released prisoners who are now attacking the team.

You must reach the Crisis Control Room to intervene.

</section>

## Block C Choice: Activate or Deactivate the Prisoners' Reaper Chips?

Follow the objective toward the Crisis Control Room. The access door requires security authorization, but the nearby wall terminal can be used to open the way.

Enter the control area, take the elevator, and clear the guards. Jack into the Reaper terminal when prompted.

King and Meshuga disagree over what to do with Block C's prisoners.

King wants to avoid executing the inmates and instead use the resulting riot as cover for the crew's escape. Meshuga is worried that the hostile prisoners will overwhelm the rescue team.

The decision is between **activating** and **deactivating** the prisoners' Reaper Chips.

### Listen to King: Deactivate the Reaper Chips

Choosing to deactivate the chips prevents the prison from executing the Block C inmates through their implants.

The prisoners remain active, and the situation becomes chaotic as they clash with security forces. That disturbance also creates an opening for Miguel and the rest of King's crew.

The escape continues even though the immediate danger around Block C has not disappeared.

During the later call, Meshuga acknowledges that V's decision saved lives, and King responds favorably to the way the situation was handled.

### Listen to Meshuga: Activate the Reaper Chips

Activating the Block C Reapers uses the prison's execution system against the inmates.

This removes the immediate prisoner threat to the crew, but it also causes the deaths King wanted to avoid.

The mission still proceeds toward the escape sequence. King's response to this choice is less favorable, and the later dialogue reflects the cost of using the chips.

### Which Block C Choice Is Better?

**Deactivate the Reaper Chips** if your priority is keeping the prisoners alive.

The main difference established during the mission is the fate of those prisoners and how the crew responds. Both routes continue to the prison escape, and neither has been shown to require a different route to collect Coronation or King's outfit.

You do not need to kill the prisoners to obtain the mission equipment.

After choosing, follow Meshuga's instructions until the access code is accepted and the Crisis Control Room can be exited.

From there, look for the passage leading into the ventilation system. Leave the control room through its eastern-side route and follow the vent toward the extraction path.

## How to Beat Darius Bale in Clampdown

Darius Bale returns during the final prison escape, this time operating a heavily armored combat suit. His ranged attacks make the open center of the arena dangerous, but the barriers scattered around the area give you places to break his line of fire.

Scanning Darius reveals a useful weakness: **20% vulnerability to Electrical damage**. His scan also displays a separate 50% resistance, so checking his defenses is more useful than simply relying on your usual weapon damage.

### Use Short Circuit and Fight From Cover

With a Cyberdeck equipped, **Short Circuit** is a practical option against Darius. It costs **10 RAM** in the observed configuration, while Overheat costs 9 RAM.

The arena's barriers are particularly valuable while a Quickhack is uploading. Keep moving between cover positions instead of standing in the open while Darius fires at you.

A workable sequence is:

1. Scan Darius and confirm his Electrical weakness.
2. Upload Short Circuit when you have enough RAM and a valid target.
3. Move behind a barrier while the upload completes.
4. Continue attacking from cover, repositioning when Darius turns his weapons toward you.

The Electrical weakness provides a useful damage option, but you can still defeat him with conventional weapons. Characters without a Cyberdeck do not need to change their Operating System just for this encounter.

Once Darius is defeated, follow the objective toward the Prison Morgue and prepare the explosive mine for the final escape.

<section id="morgue" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Escape Through the Prison Morgue

The final extraction route leads to the morgue.

Meshuga tells V that the **back wall of the Prison Morgue** is the correct place for the explosive mine recovered from the Armory.

Reach the marked wall and place the explosive.

Do not stand beside it waiting for the explosion. Move into cover when the objective changes, then detonate the mine after reaching a safe position.

The blast opens the escape route through the wall.

King confirms that the remaining crew has made it out, but the prison break has triggered a major NCPD response outside.

### How to Lose the NCPD After Escaping

Leaving the prison does not immediately complete Clampdown.

You must lose the police pursuit before the final calls can occur. The response can reach **four wanted stars**, making a prolonged firefight an unnecessary risk.

The objective is to break contact and keep out of sight until the wanted status disappears.

You can use surrounding buildings and streets to interrupt the pursuit, or put distance between yourself and the police before finding somewhere to hide. Remaining in open combat only keeps the chase active.

Once the NCPD loses V, Meshuga contacts you again.

Meshuga confirms that the extra criminal records created to get you into prison have been removed. King subsequently calls to acknowledge the success of Miguel's rescue and the restoration of his crew.

The mission concludes after these communications.

## Clampdown Rewards and What Carries Over

The most distinctive equipment from Clampdown is available before the mission ends:

- **King's clothing set**, including his signature coat and glasses.
- **Coronation**, the iconic Tech Revolver recovered with the bathroom package.
- **7,000 eddies** as the reported completion payment.
- The return of V's original equipment from the prison Armory.

The central objective is rescuing Miguel. Disabling Wex's Reaper and choosing how to handle the Block C prisoners add decisions within that rescue, without changing the need to complete the final escape.

A useful completion check is the sequence of events after the morgue explosion: the wanted level disappears, Meshuga confirms your record has been cleaned, and King contacts you about the successful operation.

You do not need to hunt for another prison exit, revisit the Control Room, or return to the BuryGer meeting location after those calls.

Clampdown is complete, and V can continue other jobs in Night City with the recovered equipment.
