**How Many Dudes has 69 achievements.** The fastest route is not to force all
69 conditions into one perfect run.

I use the first reliable Tier 1 clear for normal progression achievements, then
separate the difficult cleanup into dedicated runs: no-consumable challenges,
single-Type or Family-restricted teams, stat-stacking builds, economy runs and
fixed five-Dude compositions.

That keeps a promising run from collapsing because I tried to earn an unrelated
achievement at the wrong checkpoint.

<h2 id="best-route">
  The Best Order for All 69 Achievements
</h2>

I split the list into runs with one clear purpose.

### Run 1: complete Tier 1 normally

My first serious target is a stable five-Type roster that reaches the
G.O.A.T. at Round 105.

This run naturally covers or advances:

- Boss Blaster;
- Fair Trade;
- I've Seen Things;
- Silver Star Dude;
- YOLO;
- The G.O.A.T.;
- several basic stat and discovery achievements.

I do not restart this run because a niche achievement becomes awkward. A
completed Gold-Star route is more valuable than forcing one stack condition and
losing the account progression.

### Run 2: use a safe build for restrictions

Once I know the build can win, I attempt conditions that remove my emergency
tools:

- Thirsty Dudes;
- Iron Dude;
- Mob Mentality;
- I Wasn't Worried.

I prefer a roster with reliable healing and a front line that works without
consumable intervention. Warlock / Chef / Time / Mutant / Ranger is a better
starting point for this than a fragile all-damage experiment.

### Run 3: force one roster shape

I keep these separate because their requirements conflict:

- Single-Minded Dude wants at least 40 of one Dude Type;
- Sensitivity Training wants five different Families;
- the six Family-only wins restrict the entire team;
- four achievements require exact five-Type compositions.

Trying to combine incompatible roster goals only makes every one of them
harder.

### Later runs: stack, economy and collection cleanup

I leave the most specialized achievements until the account already has more
Stars, discoveries and starting resources. Extra Pocket Dice and broader
Relic access make targeted builds much easier than they are on a fresh file.

### Boss, Silver Star and Gold Star progress

Boss Blaster should appear on the first boss win. Silver Star Dude belongs to a
run that reaches the Silver-Star checkpoint beyond Round 60.

The G.O.A.T. is different: I need the full Tier 1 clear at Round 105. I treat
that as the first major achievement objective because the same victory also
earns Gold-Star progress for the five Dude Types in the roster.

### Fair Trade and YOLO

I loan a non-essential Relic to the Scavenger Dude once for Fair Trade.

For YOLO, I take the Business Dude's Baller Package only when the roster can

easy; taking the deal immediately before an expensive recovery shop is the real
mistake.

### Probably Needed Those

This hidden achievement requires losing while two Relics are still loaned to
the Scavenger Dude.

I do it in an intentional throwaway run:

1. loan two Relics that are not needed for account progress;
2. confirm both are still away;
3. let the run end.

I do not risk a strong Round 80+ route for this condition.

<h2 id="challenge-runs">
  Challenge Achievements I Keep in Separate Runs
</h2>

### Thirsty Dudes

I must beat Round 80 without using Dude Juice anywhere in the run.

The real checkpoint is not Round 80 itself. The run usually fails earlier when
I allow two or three ordinary battles to remove support Dudes and have no legal
way to restore them.

I choose safer matchups from Round 60 onward and stop treating the highest cash
reward as the default choice.

### Iron Dude

Iron Dude allows consumables during ordinary battles but not during boss
fights.

Before entering a boss, I check whether the build can survive the opening
without Meteor, Bottled Lightning, Health Potion or Dude Juice. If the answer is
no, I abandon the achievement attempt and preserve the run.

### Single-Minded Dude

I commit early to one scalable Dude Type and keep recruiting it until the
roster reaches 40.

I still use the other four Types to cover missing jobs. The achievement asks
for 40 of one Type, not a roster containing only that Type.

### G.O.A.T. Crusher

The under-15-second condition needs much more than simply surviving Round 105.

I want:

- a carry that is already far ahead before Round 100;
- immediate area damage for the 250 Rams;
- no long recovery phase after the fight starts;
- enough damage amplification to finish the G.O.A.T. as soon as the screen
  clears.

I treat this as a late account challenge, not part of the first Tier 1 clear.

### Baby Boomer

Defeating 50,000 Toddlers is a long-term counter. I do not farm weak runs only
for this achievement unless Toddler-heavy battles also advance another current
goal.

<h2 id="consumable-achievements">
  Meteor, Bottled Lightning and Nuke Achievements
</h2>

These achievements depend more on battle selection and timing than on overall
build strength.

### You In Particular

I need at least 20 enemies present but only one target struck by Bottled
Lightning.

I wait for a fight with one separated enemy or clear enough of the surrounding
pack before firing. Using it immediately into a packed screen usually spreads
the strikes and misses the condition.

### Juggler

The same enemy must be thrown by Meteor three times in one battle.

I hold multiple Meteors for a durable boss or elite enemy that will survive all
three casts. A weak swarm is bad for this achievement because the intended
target dies or the battle ends before the third throw registers.

<h2 id="stat-stacking-achievements">
  Stat-Stacking Achievements
</h2>

I attempt these only after the shop starts offering several pieces for the same
stat. One attractive Relic is not enough reason to redirect the whole run.

### Regeneration, Max HP and Temporary Health

Self-Help needs 15% Regeneration on one Dude. Copper Bolts is useful on a
Frankendude route because direct damage can add temporary Regeneration stacks,
but I still need other sources to reach the full threshold.

Can't Touch This, Never Felt Better and Who Needs Healing? all favor a build
that is already creating Max HP or Temporary Health. I do not try to add these
conditions to a run whose real problem is insufficient damage.

### Crit, Cleave, Evasion and Attack Speed

The Perfect Crit, Mega Crits, Cleavin' Steven, Dancing Shoes and Way of the Fist
are threshold builds.

My rule is:

- select one target Dude;
- keep compatible Relics on that same target;
- choose a long enough battle for temporary stacks to accumulate;
- stop splitting upgrades across several carries.

### Specialized damage achievements

Big Bunga, Don't Tase Me, Doomer and Anonymous require one specific Dude,
weapon or mechanic to produce a very large battle total.

The checklist gives the exact number. I treat the fastest method for these as
unverified until I have a complete successful run for that mechanic. I can
recommend the build direction, but I do not present an untested shortcut as a
guaranteed route.

### Damage prevention

No Thanks and Try To Hit Me are different:

- No Thanks is a single-battle prevention total;
- Try To Hit Me is a long-term Deflection counter.

A defensive roster can advance both, but I do not assume one successful tank
battle will finish the account-wide Deflection grind.

<h2 id="summon-healing-achievements">
  Summon, Healing and Lifesteal Achievements
</h2>

### Skeledude achievements

De-Boned is the short version: make one Dude generate three Skeledudes from
Gravestone in one battle.

Necrodudicon is a dedicated long battle requiring 300 Skeledudes. I want a
fight that lasts, a build that does not immediately kill the target and enough
summon support for the count to keep climbing.

Liches Get Stitches belongs to the same kind of long setup because Revenant
needs time to reach 10 stacks.

### Healing achievements

Alternative Medicine requires one Dude Type to heal 50,000 in one battle while
using Medical License.

I use a large late battle and one established healing Type rather than spreading
the healing investment across several small support lines.

Yoink requires 10,000,000 HP Lifestealed and is a long-form cleanup target.
Who Needs Healing? is another cumulative-style build project centered on
Temporary Health generation. Neither belongs in the first completion route.

<h2 id="economy-achievements">
  Business Dude, Insurance, Merchant and Scavenger Achievements
</h2>

### Profit-Driven Dude

I need $30,000 profit from Business Dude in one run.

This requires enough remaining rounds for repeated investments to mature. Once
the run enters the final Round 100–105 corridor, I stop starting long economy
cycles and protect the completion instead.

### Actuary

Actuary needs $2,000 in Life Insurance payouts during one run.

Because I do not yet have a fully verified fastest route, I build around the
insurance mechanic deliberately and track the payouts instead of assuming
ordinary knockouts will eventually be enough.

### Out of Stock and Coupon Clipper

These are long Merchant counters:

- 100 shop restocks;
- 100 discounted Relic purchases with Coupon.

I let them accumulate across appropriate runs. Repeatedly restocking an early
shop with no useful purchase is poor progression even when it moves a counter.

<h2 id="family-and-fixed-team-achievements">
  Family-Only and Exact-Team Wins
</h2>

The six Family-only wins are best handled after I understand at least one
functional five-Type composition inside each Family:

- Undead;
- Fantasy;
- Sci-Fi;
- Employed;
- Action;
- Warrior.

The restriction does not remove the need for a front line, healing, sustained
damage and crowded-screen damage. Before starting, I choose which Dude in that
Family will cover each job.

Sensitivity Training is the opposite route: five different Dude Families plus
a Smuggler Dude Relic.

The four exact-team achievements are:

- Big Pumpers;
- On The Move;
- Retaliation;
- Support Squad.

I publish all five required Types in the checklist and avoid adding unrelated
Flyers that dilute the personal and Family Relic pools. The listed five Types
must remain the run's actual team identity, not an afterthought added at the
final shop.

<h2 id="collection-and-tier-achievements">
  Gold Stars, Collectibles, Tier 2 and Tier 3
</h2>

These are the achievements I leave until normal progression has already filled
a large part of the board:

- Dude Master;
- Family Dude;
- Dude Dabbler;
- Universal Dude;
- I'm Every Dude;
- Trinket Tinkerer;
- I've Seen Too Much.

A completed run can advance many of them at once, so I avoid grinding one
Collectible in isolation when a full Gold-Star clear would move several rows.
