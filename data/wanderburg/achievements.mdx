<h2 id="achievement-roadmap">
  The fastest route is progression first, cleanup later
</h2>

Wanderburg does not hide most of its achievements behind collectibles, unusual interactions, or one-off challenge conditions. Twenty of the 26 belong to four counters that keep increasing during normal play: distance travelled, vehicles destroyed, units consumed, and bosses destroyed.

The remaining six are tied to the account moving forward:

- **Spiderburg unlocked**
- **Welcome to the Dark Forest!**
- **Tankenburg unlocked**
- **Welcome to the Desert!**
- **You won!**
- **All Lands won!**

That changes how I approach completion. A run toward a new Land is already adding kilometers, vehicle kills, consumed units, and bosses. If I spend an hour farming one counter before the map is finished, much of that work would have happened anyway during the progression I still needed to do.

I therefore use the achievement list as a by-product of the account until **All Lands won!** is complete. Only then do the four cumulative totals become the main objective.

<h2 id="all-lands">
  All Lands won! is the last achievement I finish before farming
</h2>

Revealing a Land is not the same as winning it. **All Lands won!** requires the actual win states, which makes it the progression achievement I use as the dividing line between normal play and achievement cleanup.

There is little downside to doing it first. An unfinished Land still gives me everything the cumulative achievements need: travel distance on the way through the map, enemy vehicles during combat, units along populated routes, and another set of bosses.

It also exposes weaknesses that an easy farming route can hide. If the account can generate thousands of vehicle kills but still cannot reliably win a harder Land, I would rather fix that now than come back after the counters are nearly finished.

The First Win and all-Lands achievement checks were fixed during Early Access, so continuing into Overtime no longer needs to be treated as a workaround for those earlier trigger problems.

<h2 id="cumulative-achievements">
  What to farm after the map is finished
</h2>

Once every required Land has been won, I compare the four remaining counters rather than assuming one particular achievement will always be last. Their current caps are close enough to normal progression that the answer depends heavily on how I played.

### Distance ends at 1,000 km

The current chain is:

**10 → 100 → 250 → 500 → 1,000 km**

I do not drive empty circles for the early milestones. Almost every productive run is already adding distance, and the current final requirement is only one tenth of the original 10,000 km target.

If distance is still unfinished late, I favor a castle that can remain alive and mobile for a long time. I am less concerned with producing the highest burst damage possible and more interested in avoiding the kind of bad position that ends a run while there is still useful distance left to cover.

Overtime is particularly convenient here because the castle is already developed and I can keep moving without rebuilding from level one.

### Vehicles still end at 10,000 destroyed

The vehicle chain remains:

**100 → 500 → 1,000 → 5,000 → 10,000**

Unlike the other three cumulative categories, this chain was not reduced. That makes **10,000 vehicles destroyed** one of the most plausible final cleanup achievements.

The first milestones should arrive without effort because every run is full of normal combat. If the 10,000 target survives longer than the others, I switch my priorities slightly: persistent damage becomes more valuable than a setup that needs careful manual alignment for every ordinary target.

Auto Attacks, wide coverage, summons, mines, and other damage that keeps working while I drive all help maintain throughput. The exact combinations are in the <Link href="/wanderburg/best-builds">Wanderburg best builds guide</Link>.

I still avoid sacrificing the run simply to chase one more cluster. A long clear that keeps producing enemies is usually more useful than a reckless route that kills quickly for three minutes and then ends.

### Units now end at 25,000 consumed

The current thresholds are:

**1,000 → 5,000 → 7,500 → 10,000 → 25,000**

This chain became much more reasonable after the rebalance. I consume dense groups when they are naturally close to my route, but I do not constantly turn the castle around for a few isolated units while progression objectives are still active.

Once 25,000 is the only reason for the run, route density matters more. I want populated areas where the castle can continue moving from one useful group to the next rather than spending most of the session crossing empty ground.

The Count can change how consumption fits into survival, but he is not necessary for this achievement. I care more about having a castle strong enough to pass through dense sections without repeatedly abandoning the route to recover.

### Bosses now end at 100 destroyed

The boss chain is:

**10 → 25 → 50 → 75 → 100**

This is dramatically shorter than the original 500-boss requirement.

A normal win contains four boss fights, so **100 boss kills are equivalent to 25 complete four-boss sequences if every kill came from full clears**. In practice, progression and failed attempts have already contributed before I ever start counting dedicated farming runs.

That changes my approach completely. There is little reason to build a special boss farm early. I clear the map, play the vehicles I want to test, and let those runs remove most of the requirement first.

If bosses are still the final counter, I use a familiar setup that reaches all four reliably. For this achievement, repeatedly finishing a stable sequence matters more than whether one individual boss dies a few seconds faster.

<h2 id="overtime-for-achievements">
  Overtime is best when more than one counter still matters
</h2>

A strong Game Won run is already holding a developed castle, so Overtime can save the setup time of beginning another run immediately. Distance continues increasing, more vehicles appear, units can still be consumed, and the extra play time remains useful when several achievement chains are unfinished.

I do not continue automatically.

If the castle leaves the normal win with poor movement, badly depleted recovery options, or a layout that is barely surviving, I would rather bank the successful run and begin another one that can produce counters consistently. A short Overtime collapse adds less than another complete run that remains productive from start to finish.

This becomes especially relevant now that most of the achievement caps are lower. I no longer need heroic Overtime records to make the numbers realistic; I just want to avoid wasting a strong castle when it still has useful work to do.

The broader decision about continuing after Game Won, including its Silver value, is covered in the <Link href="/wanderburg/progression-unlocks">Wanderburg progression and unlocks guide</Link>.

<h2 id="completion-order">
  My Wanderburg achievement completion order
</h2>

From a fresh account, I use this route:

**1. Get the first normal win.**  
I want the account capable of completing the four-boss sequence before I worry about farming statistics.

**2. Keep advancing through Lands and permanent vehicle unlocks.**  
Spiderburg, Dark Forest, Tankenburg, Desert, and the rest of the overworld all generate progress toward the cumulative achievements at the same time.

**3. Win every Land.**  
I finish **All Lands won!** while every run still has more than one purpose.

**4. Check what is actually left.**  
At this point the current thresholds are only 1,000 km, 10,000 vehicles, 25,000 units, and 100 bosses. Depending on how I played, one may already be much closer than the others.

**5. Combine compatible cleanup.**  
A long run can add distance, vehicles, units, and bosses together. I only isolate one counter when it has clearly become the last bottleneck.

**6. Change the run around the final requirement.**  
For distance I want survival and continuous movement; for vehicles I want steady damage while travelling; for units I want dense routes; for bosses I want dependable full clears.

The important part is not finding a separate trick for all 26 achievements. Most of the list is asking me to keep playing the same core game while progressing efficiently. By the time every Land is won, the remaining work is usually a question of throughput rather than discovery.
