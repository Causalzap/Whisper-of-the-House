Retro Rewind has **23 Steam achievements**. Most reward ordinary store activities, but their requirements are not interchangeable. Helping 20 customers with movie requests does not mean processing 20 returns, and owning 100 video games is different from renting out 20 entertainment systems.

Several longer achievements depend on what your store currently owns, not how many items you have purchased over its lifetime. Check the wording before selling inventory or clearing out collectible movies.

## How Do You Unlock the Five Video Game Update Achievements?

The Video Game update added five achievements. Each one checks a different activity, so unlocking the new features is only the beginning.

### Tech Shed is Open — Enter the Electronics Store

**Requirement:** Set foot inside Tech Shed Electronics.

Enter **Tech Shed Electronics** once to unlock this achievement. You do not need to buy a game or repair any equipment.

If the entrance is locked, <Link href="/retro-rewind/tech-shed-electronics">check the shop's opening days and hours</Link> before returning.

### Good as New — Repair 20 Broken VHS Tapes

**Requirement:** Repair **20 broken VHS tapes** using the Repair Desk.

Use the backstore Repair Desk to restore damaged movie tapes. Each completed repair contributes to the total; discarding a broken copy does not count.

Discarding a damaged movie does not count. Neither does repairing a broken VHS player or gaming console at Tech Shed, since those are electronic devices rather than VHS tapes.

Keep damaged movie copies that you intend to restore. If the Repair Desk is not available yet, <Link href="/retro-rewind/when-to-replace-damaged-tape">unlock and use the tape repair station</Link> before attempting this milestone.

### The Ban Hammer — Ban One Customer

**Requirement:** Ban a customer.

Open the customer profile through the monitor at your checkout counter. From the membership and customer-management options, choose to ban a customer.

A banned customer cannot return to your store after leaving until you unban them. This means the decision affects more than the achievement itself.

You can wait until someone refuses to pay a fee or causes a troublesome interaction instead of banning a customer you would prefer to keep. A single ban is enough for the achievement.

### Home Entertainment — Rent Out 20 Entertainment Systems

**Requirement:** Rent out **20 entertainment systems**.

Complete **20 entertainment-system rentals** involving VHS players or gaming consoles. This counts rental transactions, not the number of individual machines you own.

You can rent the same device again after it has been returned and made available. If customers cannot request your systems, <Link href="/retro-rewind/video-game-rentals">check your System Rentals setup</Link> before buying additional equipment.

### The Video Game Geek — Own 100 Video Games

**Requirement:** Own **100 video games in your inventory**.

Keep **100 video games in your inventory** to complete this achievement. The requirement measures games owned, not the number of game rentals completed.

Check your inventory total before selling or removing copies. The achievement description does not explicitly require 100 unique titles, so do not assume every game must be different.

## How Many Movies Do You Need for the Collection Achievements?

Three achievements track the number of movies owned in your inventory:

- **The Movie Enthusiast:** Own **250 movies**.
- **The Movie Collector:** Own **500 movies**.
- **The Movie Empire:** Own **1,000 movies**.

The thresholds are inventory totals, not the amount of money spent buying movies.

Selling off a large collection before reaching your next target can delay the achievement. If you're approaching 500 or 1,000 movies, check your current inventory before moving surplus copies into clearance or disposal.

### Is It Worth Buying Movies Just to Reach 1,000?

Build toward the 250- and 500-movie milestones through ordinary restocking. For **The Movie Empire**, keep expanding your collection until you own **1,000 movies at once**.

Check your inventory before selling duplicates or clearing out old titles. Reducing your collection before reaching the target can delay the achievement, even when those sales would otherwise make financial sense.

The 100-video-game requirement is tracked separately, so owning more movies will not advance that milestone.

## How Do You Complete Good Customer Service and Return Routine?

These achievements both involve customers, but they track different interactions.

**Good Customer Service** requires helping **20 customers with movie requests**. Respond to customers who ask for a movie and complete the request successfully.

A customer browsing independently or returning a movie is not necessarily completing the specified movie-request action. Read the request and supply the matching movie rather than counting every person who visits your shop.

**Return Routine** requires checking in **1,000 movies at the Return Station**.

This is one of the longer routine achievements. Returning customers supply the items you need, so keep the Return Station working and process incoming movies rather than leaving a growing pile of unchecked returns.

The requirement is specifically 1,000 **movies checked in**. It is not 1,000 sales, 1,000 rentals, or 1,000 pieces of equipment returned.

## Which Achievements Require Employees or Store Upgrades?

Five achievements involve staffing, store progression, or purchased equipment.

**First Hire** unlocks when you hire your first employee. **Fully Staffed** requires hiring **two employees**.

The second achievement checks the number hired, not whether both employees are assigned to the same job. Once you can afford their wages, a second worker can help keep ordinary store tasks moving while you handle customer requests and inventory.

**Game On!** requires buying an **arcade machine**. Purchasing the machine is the achievement condition; you don't need to reach a particular arcade revenue total.

### When Do You Unlock Certified Movie Buff?

**Certified Movie Buff** requires unlocking all movie genres.

Check the Store Level Unlocks calendar. The final movie genre shown on that progression chart is **Western at Level 19**.

Keep leveling the store until the remaining genre unlocks are complete. Buying more shelves won't substitute for reaching the required store level.

### What Counts Toward Big Store Energy?

**Big Store Energy** requires unlocking **all store expansions**.

Open the renovation menu and unlock the remaining store expansions. Filling a room with furniture does not count as unlocking another expansion; check which spaces are still available for purchase.

## How Do You Earn the Food Sales and $10,000 Revenue Achievements?

The three concession achievements have matching targets but use different products:

- **Brain Freeze!** — sell **20 slushy drinks**.
- **Popping Off!** — sell **20 popcorns**.
- **Sugar Rush!** — sell **20 cotton candies**.

Each product has to be sold through its own concession setup. Selling 60 slushies will not replace the separate popcorn and cotton candy requirements.

Buy the required machines when your store can support them, keep their products available, and let normal customer purchases accumulate.

**Business Is Booming!** requires generating **$10,000 in total revenue**.

This counts money generated by the business, not a requirement to hold $10,000 in cash simultaneously. Continue earning revenue through your normal store operations; spending money on inventory or necessary equipment does not change the fact that you've already earned it.

You can pursue these goals alongside ordinary store progression rather than interrupting customer service to focus on a single product.

## How Do You Unlock RIP, Holographic Showcase, and Off The Books?

These three achievements require specific actions rather than a store-level threshold or a long cumulative total.

### RIP — Throw Away One Movie

**RIP** requires putting a movie in the trash.

Choose a movie you can afford to lose and dispose of it. This is different from selling a movie through clearance or repairing a damaged copy.

Avoid using a holographic movie or another collectible copy just to complete the achievement. An ordinary unwanted movie is sufficient for the stated action.

### Holographic Showcase — Display Four Holographic Movies

**Holographic Showcase** requires displaying **four holographic movies in a display case**.

Collect the necessary holographic copies, place a suitable display case, and put four holographic movies on display.

Ordinary movies won't replace the holographic requirement. Keep any holographic copies you find until the display is ready rather than selling them before you have all four.

This achievement is about the display, not merely owning four holographic movies somewhere in your inventory.

### Off The Books — Order a Movie with an SKU Code

**Off The Books** requires ordering a movie through the black market using its **SKU code**.

Use a valid movie SKU when ordering from the black-market dealer. Simply meeting the dealer or buying an ordinary random movie bundle is not the same as placing a specific SKU-based order.

For the code entry and available movie identifiers, <Link href="/retro-rewind/sku-codes">use the movie SKU codes</Link> when you're ready to place the order.

## How Do You Get First Day Complete?

**First Day Complete** unlocks after you finish your first day running the video store. Complete the day normally; no special purchase or additional collection target is required.
