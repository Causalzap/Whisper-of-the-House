<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Find the ground-level Sewer Hatch on Tower Farm, then check its objective list. Remove the boards and overgrown plants, and unlock the entrance with the item required by its lock: **Bolt Cutters for Chains, or a Key or Bolt Cutters for a Padlock**. Open the hatch to escape.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Sewer Escape is the biggest change to escaping Tower Farm, but finding it is only half the job. The new map spreads its buildings across open roads, farmland, a harvest festival, and a trailer park. A hatch can be close to your current route without being obvious from a distance, and the tools needed to open it may be somewhere else entirely.

Tower Farm was added as the fifth multiplayer map in the October 27, 2026 Tower Farm Terror update. It is free to play in Matchmaking and Private Matches. You do not need to purchase a Michael Myers skin or a Civilian character pack to access it.

The most useful way to learn this map is to establish a few reliable landmarks first, then choose an escape based on the items you have actually found.

<h2 id="tower-farm-map">
  Where Are the Main Areas on the Tower Farm Map?
</h2>

The **roadside gas station and service garage** are useful landmarks. Look for the covered fuel pumps, repair bay, and nearby road junction when trying to orient yourself between the residential streets and agricultural areas.

Unlike the more compact residential neighborhoods, Tower Farm has long stretches where reaching another building requires crossing visible ground. That changes the value of the shortest route: a direct road crossing can save distance, but it can also leave a Civilian exposed with very little room to interrupt Michael's pursuit.

The major areas are easiest to recognize by what you can do in them.

- **Farmhouses:** Larger residential properties, including an upper-area house with a basement entertainment room. Check the doors and basement connections instead of assuming every house follows the same layout.
- **Barns and farm buildings:** Large agricultural structures, equipment, and contextual execution points. Some barns can be entered, while others are landmarks with no usable interior.
- **Cornfields:** Traversable crop sections mixed with boundaries that cannot always be crossed. A field is not automatically a shortcut just because the other side is visible.
- **Irrigation ditch:** A trench separating parts of the farm, with bridges connecting the surrounding ground.
- **Harvest festival:** Festival stalls, a picnic area, pumpkins, and a hayride. The pumpkin patch is a distinctive landmark.
- **Trailer park and residential stretch:** Trailers, smaller houses, and outbuildings near the road network.
- **Gas station:** A recognizable road junction with a service building and garage area.

The practical difference between these areas matters more than their appearance. Farmhouses and accessible barns provide rooms and corners where you can interrupt a chase. Roads and open ground provide visibility but less immediate protection. The irrigation ditch is especially important because reaching a bridge can determine whether you have a usable crossing.

<h2 id="farmhouse-barn-festival-route">
  How to Navigate from the Farmhouses to the Harvest Festival
</h2>

One useful orientation run begins around the upper farmhouse and works outward through the agricultural area.

This is a way to learn the geography, not a required match objective or a guaranteed route from every spawn.

**1. Locate the large farmhouse and check the basement connection.**

The upper property has a basement entertainment area containing a bar and pool tables. It also provides an interior landmark that is quite different from the exterior farmland.

A basement route is worth checking when Michael controls the obvious doorway. Do not assume the stairway returns to the same side of the building where you entered; farmhouses and garage connections can change the direction of an escape.

**2. Move toward the large barns and identify which buildings are accessible.**

One barn contains agricultural equipment and contextual execution opportunities. Another prominent barn is not freely enterable despite looking like a useful destination.

Test the actual doorway or opening before relying on a barn as your next hiding spot. Running directly at an inaccessible structure during a chase leaves little opportunity to correct course.

**3. Follow the fields toward the irrigation ditch.**

The crop fields are partly traversable, but fences and other boundaries can cut off the path you expected. Once you reach the ditch, find a bridge or an accessible crossing rather than continuing along an obstructed bank.

**4. Use the festival grounds to identify the next area.**

Festival stalls, picnic tables, and the pumpkin patch provide much clearer landmarks than the adjoining fields.

From here, you can continue toward nearby residential buildings, the road network, and the trailer park. The festival also gives you a recognizable point to return to when you need to locate a teammate or revisit a discovered escape.

Do not spend the opening minutes exploring every decorative structure. Some small sheds and barns cannot be entered. Prioritize usable buildings, residents, containers, and known escape locations.

**5. Reorient at the gas station when the roads become confusing.**

<h2 id="sewer-escape-location">
  Where Is the Sewer Escape on Tower Farm?
</h2>

Tower Farm introduces the **Sewer Escape**, a low entrance in the ground rather than an ordinary door in a building.

Look for a ground-level hatch with a **KEEP OUT** marking and a removable barrier covering the entrance. Its appearance makes it easier to overlook than a car or a large gate, especially when you are scanning the road or nearby buildings instead of the ground.

Use the in-game map to track the escape locations revealed during your current match. Once you find a Sewer Hatch, remember its position before leaving to collect an unlocking item. The surrounding buildings and roads are more reliable navigation references than assuming a hatch will always appear at the same spot.

More than one Sewer entrance was encountered during a Tower Farm match. That makes it worth checking other discovered locations when your first approach is unsafe or heavily contested.

However, those observations do not establish that every match uses the same Sewer positions or that a particular pair of entrances will always appear together.

Avoid treating a single observed hatch position as a permanent spawn.


<h2 id="open-sewer-escape">
  How Do You Open the Tower Farm Sewer Escape?
</h2>

The Sewer Hatch has four objective conditions, but the item needed for its lock can differ. Check the objective list before leaving the entrance to search for tools.

A hatch secured with **Chains** requires Bolt Cutters. A hatch secured with a **Padlock** explicitly accepts either a Key or Bolt Cutters. The distinction matters when you already have a Key: you can finish a padlocked hatch without searching the farm for another tool.

<h3 id="sewer-hatch-steps">
  Clear the Sewer Hatch and Open the Exit
</h3>

**1. Destroy the Boards**

Approach the hatch and remove the wooden barrier using a suitable weapon. The boards obstruct access to the entrance; unlocking the chains or padlock alone does not complete this task.

Choose a moment when Michael is occupied elsewhere. Working beside an exposed hatch while he approaches can leave you trapped between an unfinished objective and the surrounding open ground.

**2. Clear the Overgrown Plants**

Remove the vegetation covering the hatch. The objective tracks this separately from destroying the boards, so check both conditions rather than assuming that breaking one obstruction clears the entire entrance.

**3. Unlock the Chains or Padlock**

Read the exact requirement displayed in your match:

- **Chains:** Use Bolt Cutters to cut the chains.
- **Padlock:** Use a Key or Bolt Cutters on the padlock.

The required interaction is shown in the objective list. Do not spend time collecting Bolt Cutters for a padlocked hatch when you already possess a usable Key.

**4. Open the Sewer Hatch**

Finish the remaining hatch interaction after clearing its blockers and lock. The four objective conditions matter more than the order in which they appear on the HUD.

Once opened, the Sewer provides a usable escape route. Rescued Residents can also be directed through it; one Tower Farm match successfully evacuated three Residents after the entrance was opened.

That result demonstrates a working rescue route, not a guaranteed three-person capacity. Confirm the exit remains available before guiding another Resident across exposed terrain.

<h2 id="sewer-escape-not-working">
  Why Won't the Sewer Escape Open?
</h2>

An entrance that looks ready may still have one unfinished interaction. Before abandoning the hatch or searching the whole map again, separate the likely failures.

- **The wooden barrier remains:** Use a melee weapon to finish removing it. Trying to operate the lock first will not solve an obstruction that is still covering the entrance.
- **Overgrowth is still present:** Look for its removal interaction. A cleared board does not automatically mean the entire hatch is accessible.
- **You have the wrong unlocking item:** Check whether the objective says Chains or Padlock. Chains require Bolt Cutters, while a Padlock accepts a Key or Bolt Cutters. A weapon used to remove boards does not replace the required unlocking item.
- **The interaction is interrupted:** Stop trying to finish the unlock under direct pressure. Create distance or have another Civilian occupy Michael before returning.
- **The entrance is not where you expected:** Check the discovered escape marker and nearby ground-level structures. Another match's hatch location is not a dependable coordinate for yours.

One attempted shortcut is particularly questionable: using a **Lawn Dart** against the Sewer's barrier did not work in the observed attempt, despite a similar technique having worked against cellar boards before.

That is a reason to carry an actual melee weapon for the obstruction instead of depending on a thrown item to complete the job. It is not proof that every thrown weapon has identical behavior.


<h2 id="finding-sewer-items">
  Where Can You Get the Key or Bolt Cutters for the Sewer?
</h2>

Start with the lock requirement. For a padlocked hatch, an existing Key may already solve the problem. For Chains, keep looking for Bolt Cutters.

Search accessible buildings along your route and speak with Residents you encounter. Conversations can provide useful escape items: during a Tower Farm match, Resident interactions yielded both a Key and Bolt Cutters. Those results are not guaranteed drops, so do not repeatedly visit the same character expecting a particular item.

Once you have the required tool, return to a discovered Sewer rather than searching the entire farm for another possible entrance.

Keep a weapon available for the boards as well. The unlocking item and the physical obstruction are separate tasks, and reaching the hatch with only one part of the preparation can leave the route unfinished.

If Michael is guarding the Sewer, check whether another discovered exit is already usable before returning to the hatch. For the existing Sedan, Gate, and Cellar objectives, the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/escape-helper">Escape Helper</Link> can help you prioritize your next move.

<h2 id="civilian-escape-route">
  How Should Civilians Move Through the Cornfields and Roads?
</h2>

The wide farm roads make Tower Farm easier to read from a distance but harder to cross safely.

A Civilian moving between two houses may need to leave the protection of walls, vehicles, and corners. Michael has fewer obstructions to deal with once a chase reaches a clear stretch of road.

The best movement decisions depend on where the next usable cover actually is.

**Use the ditch and bridges deliberately.** The irrigation ditch separates areas of the farm, and bridges provide crossings. Find the crossing before Michael is directly behind you; reaching an inaccessible bank while exhausted forces a dangerous reversal.

**Use accessible barns and farmhouses to interrupt pursuit.** Interior routes, basement connections, and corners can help break visual contact. A closed barn with no entrance provides none of those benefits.

**Treat the cornfield as mixed terrain.** Some crop sections can be crossed, while other boundaries obstruct movement. A straight line through the crops may not lead to an exit. Keep the next road, bridge, or usable building in mind before entering.

**Do not stop in the open to organize an entire rescue group.** Residents can be persuaded and guided toward exits, but additional followers do not remove the danger of a road crossing. Prepare the destination first, then move them along a route you can actually complete.

On Tower Farm, search accessible buildings and speak with nearby Residents before committing to a long crossing toward an undiscovered marker. The <Link href="/halloween-the-game/multiplayer-guide">multiplayer guide</Link> explains how those interactions reveal additional objectives during a match.

<h2 id="michael-tower-farm">
  How Should Michael Myers Defend Tower Farm?
</h2>

Tower Farm's open roads create strong sightlines, but they also make it possible for Civilians to spread across distant buildings. Chasing a single player deep into the wrong farmhouse can leave another escape unattended.

The key is to defend the places that actually connect routes.

**Bridge approaches are natural interception points.** A Civilian moving across the irrigation ditch may have limited alternatives if the intended bridge is already controlled.

**The gas station and farm roads are useful for spotting movement.** Players crossing open ground are easier to identify than those inside buildings. Spending the entire match in the station, however, gives the opposing team room to work elsewhere.

**Guard discovered Sewer entrances before committing to a distant chase.** The hatches are easy to overlook, and Civilians may return once they have collected the required items. In one Tower Farm match, Blackout failed to close a Sewer, so intercepting the unlock or physically guarding the entrance is more dependable than relying on that ability alone.

For the broader Blackout and Reality Tear changes, see the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/progression-perks">progression and perks guide</Link>.

The farm also contains a Scythe that Civilians can pick up, while Michael can use the weapon as a starting option once unlocked. It is not a requirement for opening the Sewer. For the weapon's unlock conditions and Progressive Challenges, see the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/challenges">Challenges guide</Link>.

<h2 id="tower-farm-pumpkins">
  Where Is the Pumpkin Patch on Tower Farm?
</h2>

Look for the pumpkin patch inside the harvest festival grounds, near the stalls, picnic areas, and hayride. Several pumpkins are grouped in this area, making it easier to locate them than searching individual residential properties.

If you are completing the Halloween pumpkin event, check the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/challenges">Challenges guide</Link> for the required count, eligible actions, reward, and deadline before spending match time destroying them.
