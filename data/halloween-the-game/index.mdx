<h2 id="where-to-start">
  What Should You Do Next in Halloween: The Game?
</h2>

The right next step depends on what you are trying to finish. Story Mode follows a fixed sequence, while multiplayer matches change direction as Residents, escape items, police support, and Michael's position become known. Progression and completion have different requirements again.

Choose the route that matches your current problem instead of treating every available objective as equally urgent.

<h3 id="story">
  Stuck in Story Mode?
</h3>

For a first clear, follow the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/walkthrough">complete Story Mode walkthrough</Link> from Smith's Grove through Chapter 5. Finish the required route before turning every optional interaction into a detour.

Two chapters have particularly demanding sequences. The <Link href="/halloween-the-game/main-street-walkthrough">Main Street walkthrough</Link> covers Chapter 2's connected objectives, while <Link href="/halloween-the-game/the-babysitters-walkthrough">The Babysitters walkthrough</Link> handles the Chapter 4 Wallace House sequence.

If you have already finished the campaign and want another finale, use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/endings">endings guide</Link>. Alternate ending cleanup begins near the end of the campaign rather than requiring you to rebuild the entire story route from the beginning.

<h3 id="challenges">
  Trying to Complete a Challenge?

<h2 id="patch-1-2-0">
  What Should Civilians and Michael Do Differently in Patch 1.2.0?
</h2>

The opening now puts Civilians and Michael under different information pressures. Civilians must explore to uncover Residents and Special Targets, while Michael gains more target information as his Bloodthirst rises. Early conversations and useful escape discoveries can therefore matter more than spending the opening searching every container.

Later in the match, the danger shifts toward unfinished escape interactions. Noise Pings can attract Michael to an active objective, Blackout can close an open exit, and prolonged group fights can strengthen him. When the team has the last required item, finishing the escape and disengaging is often safer than starting another confrontation.

For Resident discovery and escape coordination, use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/multiplayer-guide">Civilian Multiplayer Guide</Link>. For Michael's abilities, Bloodthirst generation, and updated perk values, use <Link href="/halloween-the-game/progression-perks">Progression & Perks</Link>.

<h2 id="escape-search">
  Which Escape Should Your Team Finish First?
</h2>

The best escape is not necessarily the first one discovered or the closest marker on the map. Compare what remains unfinished, whether the needed item has been found, and how much control Michael has over the area.

**No usable escape discovered:** Search accessible buildings, uncover nearby objectives, and speak with Residents. Carry useful defensive equipment without filling every inventory slot with parts for routes that may never become relevant.

**An escape is found but several items are missing:** Check what the team already possesses. A Sedan missing Gas and Keys may be less practical than a Cellar whose final unlocking tool is already in someone's inventory.

**An escape is nearly complete or already open:** Deliver the last item and protect the interaction. Continuing to search for a theoretically better route adds risk, especially when Michael can interrupt or close an open exit.

The <Link href="/halloween-the-game/escape-helper">Escape & Spawn Helper</Link> can compare Sedan, Exit Gate, and Storm Cellar progress and help narrow the remaining search.

Tower Farm's Sewer Escape is not yet included in that helper. For its four opening conditions and the difference between Chains and Padlocks, use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/tower-farm-map-guide">Tower Farm map guide</Link>.

<h2 id="multiplayer-maps">
  Is Tower Farm Free, and What Does the New Map Add?
</h2>

Halloween: The Game now has five multiplayer maps: East Haddonfield, Haddonfield Heights, Orange Grove Estates, Haddonfield Town Center, and Tower Farm.

**Tower Farm is free** for players who own the base game. It is available in both public Matchmaking and Private Matches. Purchasing a new Michael skin or Civilian pack is not required to enter the map.

Tower Farm trades familiar residential streets for farmhouses, barns, cornfields, an irrigation ditch, a harvest festival, and exposed roads. The important difference is not simply the scenery: moving between useful buildings can mean crossing more open ground.

The new **Sewer Escape** also gives the team another route to evaluate. A discovered hatch is not automatically ready; it may still require obstruction removal and a matching unlocking tool.

Use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/tower-farm-map-guide">Tower Farm map guide</Link> for the main landmarks, Sewer Hatch requirements, escape failures, and safe crossing decisions.

<h2 id="free-paid-unlocks">
  Do You Need DLC to Unlock the New Characters or Scythe?
</h2>

The October update contains both free gameplay additions and separately purchased characters and cosmetics.


**The Scythe does not require a purchase.** It can be found on Tower Farm, and Michael can unlock it as a Starting Weapon through its Progressive Challenge collection. If that collection is locked and its first requirement is not visible, repeating unrelated Michael challenges will not reveal the missing objective.

The Halloween 5 Michael skin provides an immediate Starting Weapon unlock. For the free challenge route and locked-collection problem, see the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/challenges">Challenges guide</Link>.

**Eugene Kelly and Michelle Blanton are paid DLC Civilians.** They are included in Civilian Pack 1, not earned simply by increasing your Profile Level. The <Link href="/halloween-the-game/best-civilians">Best Civilians guide</Link> compares their attributes and traits with the original roster.

The Halloween 5 and Halloween 2018 Michael skins are separate paid additions. They are not required to access Tower Farm.

For the Scythe's challenge requirements, use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/challenges">Challenges guide</Link>. If you are deciding whether to spend time unlocking it, check the required actions before starting another match.

<h2 id="halloween-event">
  Are the Halloween Event Rewards Earned In-Game or Through Twitch?
</h2>

The Jack Ghoul Goggles are earned through the limited-time pumpkin challenge inside the game. Smashing pumpkins with weapons advances that objective, and the harvest festival on Tower Farm is a useful place to look for them.

The Halloween Twitch Drops are separate rewards obtained through participating streams during the event window. Watching a stream does not replace completing the pumpkin challenge.

For the pumpkin count, deadline, and challenge progress, use the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/challenges">Challenges guide</Link>. To find the harvest festival, follow the <Link href="/halloween-the-game/tower-farm-map-guide">Tower Farm map guide</Link>.

<h2 id="arrest-michael">
  Should Your Team Escape or Try to Arrest Michael?
</h2>

Knocking Michael down is not the same as having a workable detainment route. A capture attempt depends on police support, controlling Michael, and completing the required interaction.

If an escape is already open and the team has no coordinated detainment setup, leaving is usually the clearer decision. If police support and the required tools are already in place, stopping Michael may become a realistic alternative.

Follow <Link href="/halloween-the-game/how-to-arrest-michael-myers">How to Arrest Michael Myers</Link> for the full detainment sequence instead of treating ordinary stuns as proof that an arrest is ready.
