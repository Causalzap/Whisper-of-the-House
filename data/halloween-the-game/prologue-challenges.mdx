<h2 id="best-run-order">
  Split the six Prologue challenges into two runs
</h2>

The awkward part about the Smith's Grove challenges is that they reward two
opposite ways of playing.

Five of them tolerate — and often require — direct contact with the orderlies.
Challenge 5 wants the opposite: get through Smith's Grove without alerting
them.

Trying to turn those goals into one route creates more resets than it saves.

I would clear them like this:

- **Clean run:** Challenge 5.
- **Combat run:** Challenges 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6.

Challenge 1 fits especially well into the Challenge 6 attempt. An orderly who
flees still removes himself from the active group, so you do not need every
one of the 12 to die by Michael's hand.

If you are only trying to finish Smith's Grove and continue the story, follow
the natural HUD route in the
<Link href="/halloween-the-game/walkthrough"> story walkthrough</Link>.
The challenge route deliberately spends more time around orderlies that the
normal escape does not require.

<h2 id="challenges-1-to-4">
  Clear Challenges 1–4 while working toward the full orderly sweep
</h2>

The first four challenges are short once you know what each one is actually
checking.

The mistake that costs time here is escaping as soon as the gate route opens.
For a normal story clear, that is exactly what I would do. For this run, stay
inside Smith's Grove until the challenge-specific interactions are finished.

<h3 id="challenge-1">
  Challenge 1 — Terrify an orderly and make him flee
</h3>

You do not need a special location for this one.

Grab an orderly and let the encounter break in his favor instead of finishing
him. Once he gets loose, give him room to run.

Do not chase him down immediately.

The result you care about is the game reporting that the orderly has escaped.
The challenge can take a little longer to register because he actually has to
make it far enough away to count as having fled.

This is also why I prefer doing Challenge 1 during the Challenge 6 run. An
orderly who escapes can still count as one of the people removed from
Smith's Grove, so the flee is not necessarily wasted progress.

<h3 id="challenge-2">
  Challenge 2 — Execute three orderlies with bare hands
</h3>

This is the easiest objective to accidentally overcomplicate.

Grab an orderly and choose the normal bare-hand execution rather than carrying
him toward an environmental kill. Repeat that until the third bare-hand
execution registers.

I would do these early.

Later parts of Smith's Grove have useful environmental opportunities for
Challenges 3 and 4. Spending those targets on ordinary executions first can
leave you walking back through the grounds looking for another workable NPC.

Three is the number that matters. Once the third one counts, stop using
orderlies on this objective and preserve the next convenient targets for the
two environmental challenges.

<h3 id="challenge-3">
  Challenge 3 — Execute an orderly with the telephone pole
</h3>

The telephone pole is near the **power box used late in the Smith's Grove
escape route**.

When you reach the power-box area, look to the right before pushing straight
through the objective.

The important resource here is the orderly, not the pole.

If you kill everyone nearby before reaching this part of the grounds, finding
the interaction is useless because there is nobody left to bring to it.

Keep one orderly alive as you approach the power box, grab him, move him into
the telephone-pole interaction zone, then use the environmental execution
prompt.

Once the kill finishes, move directly into Challenge 4. Its useful target is
close enough that there is no reason to wander back toward the opening area.

<h3 id="challenge-4">
  Challenge 4 — Throw an orderly to his death
</h3>

From the telephone-pole area, continue onto the nearby path and take the right.

There is an orderly farther along who can be seen shining a flashlight toward
a Smith's Grove patient.

Approach from behind, grab him, and carry him a short distance down the road
until the environmental interaction appears.

This one is much faster when it follows Challenge 3.

If you leave the power-box area and start exploring another branch of the
grounds, you are adding distance without improving the setup. Telephone pole
first, nearby throw second, then return to clearing the remaining orderlies.

<h2 id="challenge-5">
  Challenge 5 — Escape Smith's Grove without alerting orderlies
</h2>

Challenge 5 deserves its own attempt.

Combat habits from the other five challenges are exactly what ruin it. The
opening patrols need to move before Michael moves through their space, and
there are stretches where walking is safe while running is not.

The attempt also has a real reset point: if a required orderly gets stuck
instead of continuing his patrol, waiting longer does not necessarily rescue
the route.

The full path is in the
<Link href="/halloween-the-game/prologue-challenge-5">
  {" "}Prologue Challenge 5 route
</Link>
including the patrol checks, safe movement windows, where running becomes
safe, and the point where I would restart rather than spend several more
minutes hoping the NPC corrects itself.

Do that challenge on its own. Once it is registered, the rest of the Prologue
becomes much more forgiving.

<h2 id="challenge-6">
  Challenge 6 — Remove all 12 orderlies before escaping
</h2>

Challenge 6 is the long run, but the requirement is simpler than it first
sounds.

There are **12 orderlies to account for** before you leave Smith's Grove.

They do not all need to be executed. An orderly who is driven away and
successfully escapes can still count, which is useful if Challenge 1 is not
finished yet.

I use the map as a loop rather than chasing every NPC the moment he appears.

Start around the opening stairs.

The first two orderlies can be dealt with after their movement separates them.
Once those are handled, move back down through the route instead of waiting
around the ambulance area for more people to arrive.

Take the next orderly on the lower section, then continue along the path the
mission naturally uses.

When a group appears and starts moving, avoid turning the run into a blind
chase. Let the path narrow the options first.

The route continues through the lower ground and drainage section, then climbs
again toward the later part of Smith's Grove. This is where the count becomes
more useful than memory. If one orderly runs away, include him in your running
total instead of assuming the challenge has failed.

Past the power-box side of the route, work uphill and remove the remaining
accessible orderlies.

The last awkward target is easy to see but initially annoying to reach: one
orderly can be standing back near the area where the mission began, separated
from Michael by a gate.

Michael cannot simply climb through that gate.

Keep going, take the left route downhill, loop back toward the beginning of
Smith's Grove, and approach him from the accessible side.

grounds.

At that point the valuable move is to finish Smith's Grove, not search for
another NPC that is no longer part of the requirement.

<h2 id="finish-the-chapter">
  Finish Smith's Grove before you restart or leave
</h2>

A challenge checking off during the run is not the end of the job.

**You still need to complete the Prologue for that progress to save.**

That changes how I treat mistakes near the end.

If Challenge 3 is complete and Challenge 4 is complete, I do not restart just
because the rest of the route was messy. Finish the chapter and bank those
objectives.

The same applies after the full 12-orderly sweep. Once Challenge 6 has
registered, leave Smith's Grove and let the chapter complete.

Restarting is useful when the challenge you are currently attempting has
already become impossible. Restarting after a successful challenge check only
throws away work you have already done.
