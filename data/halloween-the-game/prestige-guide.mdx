<h2 id="how-to-prestige">
  How to Prestige in Halloween: The Game
</h2>

Prestige starts with the progression track I am actually trying to advance.

The current level caps are:

- **Profile:** Level 100
- **Michael Myers:** Level 50
- **Civilian:** Level 25
- **Weapon:** Level 10

Those tracks are separate. Reaching Profile 100 does not make a Level 18
Civilian or Level 7 weapon ready to Prestige.

If I am still working toward one of those caps, I keep that grind separate
from the Prestige attempt itself. The
<Link href="/halloween-the-game/progression-perks">
  {" "}progression and perks guide
</Link>
covers Profile, Michael, Civilian, Weapon XP, and the Perk Point economy before
Prestige becomes the next decision.

Once the correct track is capped, I open its Prestige section and check the
challenge before committing.

That last step matters because Prestige is not an automatic reset or reward.
Starting it activates an objective that has to be finished within its time
limit.

Only **one Prestige Challenge can be active at a time**.

<h2 id="starting-a-prestige-challenge">
  Check the Cost and Objective Before You Start
</h2>

A Prestige button is not something I press immediately after reaching the cap.

On Jennifer's Prestige 1 screen, starting the challenge costs **3,000 Perk
Points**.

The same screen also warns that failing to complete the challenge in time
means spending **more Perk Points** to try again.

**3,000 Perk Points is the displayed cost for Jennifer's Prestige 1 attempt**, not a confirmed universal price for every Prestige track.

Before activating Profile, Michael, another Civilian, or a Weapon Prestige challenge, check the amount shown on that specific screen. The cost of a later attempt may also differ after a failed or expired challenge.


It still changes how I manage Perk Points.

Burning the balance on card rolls immediately before Prestige can leave me

close, keeping a reserve starts to matter more than squeezing one more perk
roll out of the account.

I also check that the objective text has actually appeared.

If the screen is blank, gives an error, or does not tell me what action will
advance the challenge, I do not start guessing. Prestige has already had
problems with missing challenge criteria, so I would rather restart and check
again than activate a timer without knowing the requirement.


<h2 id="confirmed-prestige-challenges">
  Profile and Civilian Prestige Challenge Requirements
</h2>

Prestige objectives differ between progression tracks. Profile Prestige can require repeated executions as Michael, while Civilian Prestige may focus on actions performed with a particular character.

The two challenges below require different preparation. Check the active objective before deciding which match type and route will help you finish it.


<h3 id="profile-prestige">
  Profile Prestige: 30 Bloodthirsty Pitchfork Executions
</h3>

One Profile Prestige challenge requires:

**Perform 30 Bloodthirsty Pitchfork Executions.**

The reward shown for that challenge was **Clown Myers**.


The important requirement is **Bloodthirsty**, not simply using a Pitchfork.

The challenge requires 30 qualifying executions, so a normal Pitchfork kill does not advance it. Reach **Bloodthirst Tier 3** before attempting the required execution.

Patch 1.1.0 increased the Bloodthirst needed to advance through its tiers, while also increasing Bloodthirst gained from Stalking, Executions, successful Grabs, and attacks. Building Bloodthirst through Stalking is therefore more useful than relying entirely on basic kills.

For each qualifying execution:

1. Build Bloodthirst to Tier 3.
2. Equip or recover the Pitchfork.
3. Choose a target with enough clear space for the execution.
4. Perform the Bloodthirsty Pitchfork execution.
5. Check the Prestige counter before repeating the process.

If the execution interaction does not appear, try moving the target away from furniture, walls, or narrow doorways.

Patch 1.1.0 improved Execution spacing detection, but positioning is still worth checking before assuming the weapon or Bloodthirst requirement is wrong.

Michael can also lose the Pitchfork after being knocked down.

When that happens, recovering the weapon is more important than immediately
taking another normal kill. A regular execution may help the match, but it
does not advance an objective that specifically wants the Bloodthirsty
Pitchfork version.


<h3 id="profile-prestige-private-match">
  Can You Complete the Pitchfork Prestige Challenge in Private Matches?
</h3>

Private Matches can make repeated Pitchfork executions easier to set up against NPC targets. However, do not assume every successful execution will automatically count.

Perform one qualifying Bloodthirsty Pitchfork execution, return to the Prestige screen, and check whether the counter increases before committing to all 30 attempts.

For a controlled attempt:

1. Start a Private Match as Michael.
2. Stalk until Bloodthirst reaches Tier 3.
3. Get a Pitchfork.
4. Favor NPCs already standing in open areas.
5. Perform the required Bloodthirsty Pitchfork execution.
6. Return to the challenge screen and confirm that the counter increased before repeating the route.

That sixth step is important.


In one attempt, completed executions appeared successful, but the Prestige counter had not increased after returning to the menu.

Check progress after the first one or two qualifying executions. If the counter remains unchanged, recheck the conditions before spending another hour repeating the same action.


Even with controlled matches, the Profile challenge is not short. One attempt
was approaching **three hours** and was still one execution away from 30.

A 24-hour limit sounds generous until every qualifying kill also depends on
Tier 3 Bloodthirst, the correct weapon, enough execution space, and a counter
that is actually recording the result.

<h2 id="prestige-not-working">
  Why Your Prestige Challenge May Not Be Working
</h2>

Prestige has several different failure states, and they do not all need the
same response.


### The Challenge Is Active, but the Counter Does Not Increase

First confirm that the action satisfies the exact active objective.

For the Profile Pitchfork challenge, the requirement is a **Bloodthirsty Pitchfork execution at Bloodthirst Tier 3**, not merely a normal Pitchfork kill.

Complete one qualifying action, finish the match if necessary, and compare the counter before and after.

If the counter does not increase, check the weapon, Bloodthirst tier, execution type, and active Prestige Challenge before repeating the action.

If those conditions match and the counter still does not move, stop the grind rather than spending hours repeating the same result.

A missing counter increase does not by itself establish whether the cause is an unmet condition, delayed registration, or a tracking problem.


### The Prestige Challenge Is Missing or Cannot Be Activated

An earlier Prestige issue produced the message:

**"Cannot receive a new Prestige Challenge at this time."**

Patch 1.0.2 re-enabled Prestige Challenges and addressed missing challenge information, including a follow-up correction for Weapon Prestige objectives.

If the error or a blank objective appears:

1. Check whether another Prestige Challenge is already active.
2. Return to the previous menu and reopen the Prestige screen.
3. Fully restart the game if the objective remains missing.
4. Check whether the challenge text and timer now appear before activating anything.

A restart was the recommended action for the earlier missing-text issue, but it does not guarantee recovery from every possible progression problem.


### The objective finishes, but Prestige does not resolve

An earlier Jennifer Prestige attempt showed a more serious failure state.

The required townsfolk interactions took roughly **six and a half hours** to
finish, but completing the displayed objective did not lead to the expected
Prestige result.

At that point I stop treating the issue as player error.

If the counter never moves, I verify the requirement.

If the counter reaches its target and Prestige still fails, repeating the same
multi-hour grind is not the next useful move.

<h2 id="before-starting-prestige">
  When I Would Start a Prestige Challenge
</h2>


A Prestige attempt is worth starting when four conditions are met:

1. **The correct track is capped.** Profile, Michael, Civilian, and Weapon levels advance separately.
2. **The objective is readable.** Check the required action, counter, reward, and time limit before activating the challenge.
3. **The remaining time is realistic.** A challenge with a 24-hour timer can still require several hours of repeated qualifying actions.
4. **The Perk Point cost is affordable.** Leave enough currency for another attempt if the active challenge expires or fails.

Jennifer's recorded Prestige 1 attempt cost 3,000 Perk Points and used a 24-hour timer. Those values should not be assumed to apply to every track or later Prestige rank.

Once the challenge is active, test its counter early. A long Prestige grind is much easier to manage when you know the required action is registering correctly.


<h2 id="after-prestige">
  What Happens to Your Challenges After Prestige?
</h2>

Prestiging affects the progression track you selected, but **Passive Challenge progress is not supposed to stop accumulating after Prestige**.

Patch 1.0.3 restored Passive Challenge progress after Prestiging. Patch 1.1.0 also fixed the problem where those challenges appeared locked afterward.

This distinction matters when deciding whether to Prestige before finishing a long Passive Challenge. You should not need to abandon that challenge simply because you are ready to Prestige another track.

<h3 id="prestige-reset-rewards">
  What Do You Keep or Unlock After Prestige?
</h3>


Prestige rewards depend on the track and challenge. The Profile challenge shown here lists **Clown Myers** as its reward.

Patch 1.2.0 also adds **three new Michael Prestige Tints**, expanding the customization rewards available through Prestige progression. Check each tint's unlock requirement before choosing which track or rank to pursue; do not assume that all three are awarded by completing Profile Prestige 1.

Before activating Prestige, check the reward attached to the current challenge and whether there are level-based unlocks you still want to use.

Do not assume that Profile, Michael, Civilian, and Weapon Prestige all have identical reset consequences. If you are planning around a specific Starting Item, weapon appearance, or character unlock, check that track's current Prestige information before committing.
