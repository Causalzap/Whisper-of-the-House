<h2 id="how-endings-work">
  Choose the exit, not a hidden challenge combination
</h2>

The ending branch happens in **Chapter 5 — Into the Shadows**.

There is no final dialogue wheel asking Michael which ending to take. The route
changes when you leave Haddonfield through a different exit.

The three confirmed paths are:

- **Railroad / Full Ending**
- **Cornfield Ending**
- **Sewer Ending**

That distinction matters because Chapter 5 also puts optional objectives around
the map. Police kills, residents, flashlights, Loomis-related conditions, and
the police car can all make the finale look as though the ending depends on a
specific challenge combination.

It does not need to become that complicated.

If an exit is already available and your goal is simply to finish the story,
use it.

If you came back specifically for another ending, stop spending time on
unrelated challenge objectives and move toward the route you have not completed.

If you are not at Into the Shadows yet, the

covers the route through the earlier chapters.

<h2 id="railroad-ending">
  Railroad / Full Ending — wait for the train, then cross
</h2>

The railroad is the cleanest first ending because the game gives you an obvious
condition once the route becomes active.

When I reached the railroad exit, the screen showed that the train would arrive
in **one minute**.

That is the clue.

There is no extra key to hunt down and no final switch hidden somewhere else in
the neighborhood. The remaining job is surviving near the crossing until the
train arrives.

This is one of the worst times to chase police.

Chapter 5 already makes authority pressure dangerous for Michael. Staying near
multiple officers gives them more chances to interrupt or detain him while the
train timer is doing the only work that still matters.

Use nearby cover and movement to stay alive.

When the train reaches the crossing, let it clear enough space to pass through.

Once Michael makes it across, the route moves into the ending sequence and
Loomis' account of what happened in Haddonfield.

For a blind first playthrough, this is the route I would finish as soon as it is
ready.

Searching the outer edges of the finale for another exit while a known one is
already open adds risk without improving the first clear.

<h2 id="cornfield-ending">
  Cornfield Ending — reach the field and keep moving into the rows
</h2>

The Cornfield route ends differently because Michael escapes through terrain the
police cannot control as easily.

The important landmark is not a special item.

It is the **cornfield itself**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/halloween-the-game/halloween-the-game-cornfield-ending-entrance.webp"
    alt="Michael Myers approaching the cornfield while police try to stop him"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Once the police line and corn rows are in front of you, the field is the
    destination. Push through the open ground and enter it.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The police reaction makes the commitment point clear.

They are trying to stop Michael **before he gets into the corn**, because once
he disappears into the rows they may lose him.

That tells you how to play the final approach.

Keep moving forward.

Turning back toward the police cars for another kill only gives the authorities
another chance to stop a route that is already almost complete.

The Cornfield Ending does not require you to defeat everybody at the edge of
the field.

It requires Michael to get away from them.

There is also no verified requirement tying this route to a particular Chapter
5 challenge, score, or police kill.

If the cornfield route is available during the run, take it.

If it is not available and you already have another safe exit, finish the
chapter and check the cornfield again on a replay rather than sacrificing a
nearly completed run to search blindly.

<h2 id="sewer-ending">
  Sewer Ending — drop into the gully and follow the objective away from the streets
</h2>

The Sewer route is easier to miss because it sends Michael away from the streets
where most of Into the Shadows takes place.

You will know the branch is active when the objective changes to:

**Escape Through the Sewer**

From there, drop into the lower gully and follow the objective marker.

On the route I used, the sewer target showed **52m** remaining once Michael
reached the lower path.

The search continues after Michael gets inside, but the payoff is different

**The mask.**

Because the sewer approach is less obvious than the railroad, I prefer saving it
for a Chapter 5 replay rather than turning a first completion into a search for
the gully.

Once you know the finale's layout, the route is much easier to commit to.

<h2 id="what-changes">
  The escape changes more than the final narration does
</h2>

Do not expect three completely different closing stories.

The largest differences happen **before** the narration:

**Railroad:** Michael waits out the train, crosses the tracks, and gets away
under police pursuit.

**Cornfield:** he reaches terrain the authorities are desperate to keep him out
of and disappears into the rows.

**Sewer:** he escapes below ground, and the search party later finds only his
mask.

After those route-specific scenes, the story converges into substantially the
same Loomis narration about the destruction left across Haddonfield and
Michael's disappearance.

That changes what an ending replay is actually for.

You are replaying Chapter 5 to see a different **escape sequence**, not to unlock
an entirely different final story arc.

The Sewer route stands out the most visually because of the mask sequence.

The Cornfield route changes the pursuit.

The railroad is the most direct way to finish the campaign.

<h2 id="replay-order">
  Finish one ending first, then use Chapter 5 as the cleanup point
</h2>

There is no gameplay requirement forcing you to complete the endings in a
particular order.

For efficiency, I use:

**Railroad first → Sewer → Cornfield**

The railroad gives a new player the clearest finish because the train timer
makes the remaining objective obvious.

The Sewer route is a good second run because the gully and abandoned mask make
the alternate sequence easy to recognize.

The Cornfield route can then be approached deliberately instead of searched for
while you are still learning the finale.

That order is only a routing preference.

It does not unlock one ending from another.

Most importantly, you do **not** need to replay the Prologue, Main Street,
Returning Home, or The Babysitters every time.

Return directly to **Into the Shadows**.

On an ending-cleanup run, treat unrelated Chapter 5 challenges as optional.
Their rewards are separate from the reason you came back.

A useful exit is already progress.

A second optional police fight is only valuable if that challenge is also part
of the run.

Once the route you want appears, commit to it and finish the escape.
