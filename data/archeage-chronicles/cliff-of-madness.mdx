<GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Complete Cliff of Madness">
Finish **Missing Husband** by finding Renald, then enter **Cliff of Madness**. Speak to the prisoner, retrieve the **Prison Key** from the water, and unlock the cell. Use the **Cannon** to breach the gate, defeat **Mad Anchor Krogal**, and follow the remaining objectives through the fortress.

The cannon is one of the first things you notice inside Cliff of Madness, but it cannot solve the rescue objective before the prisoner has been dealt with. The key you need is underwater, close to the prison area. That small detail can send you searching through the fortress or fighting enemies that have nothing to do with the locked cell.

Cliff of Madness is the pirate stronghold sequence toward the end of the ArcheAge Chronicles demo. It takes you through shipwrecks, prison cells, breached gates, and several demanding encounters before the story reaches the preview's final departure.

There are two progression checks worth paying attention to: **Missing Husband must be completed before entering the instance**, and the prison rescue must advance before you can use the cannon as intended.

## Why Can't You Enter Cliff of Madness?

The entrance can display **"Quest requirements not met"** even when you have reached the correct location.

This happens when the **Missing Husband** objective has not been completed.

Return to the pirate hideout area and check the quest in your journal. The objective involves finding **Renald**, whose whereabouts become part of the rescue investigation. He is not automatically counted as found just because you have reached the entrance to Cliff of Madness.

Use the active quest marker and compass distance to locate him. The hideout has several changes in elevation, so a marker that appears close can still point to an NPC above or below your current position.

Once you have spoken to Renald and **Missing Husband** registers as completed, the next objective becomes **Enter the Cliff of Madness**.

![Cliff of Madness entrance requirement showing that Missing Husband must be Quest Complete.](/images/archeage-chronicles/cliff-of-madness-missing-husband-requirement.webp)

In a party, check your own journal rather than assuming another player's progress has automatically cleared the entrance requirement. Players can reach the same instance while holding different quest stages.

There is no benefit to repeatedly interacting with the entrance while Missing Husband remains incomplete. Finish the conversation and return when the objective has changed.

## Find the Prisoner Before Trying to Fire the Cannon

Inside Cliff of Madness, the route passes through a partially ruined pirate stronghold with wooden structures, water channels, ships, and ladders.

It is easy to wander into a combat area before completing the nearby quest interactions. The cannon is particularly misleading because it is visible before you are ready to use it.

Follow the rescue objective toward the prisoner and speak to the relevant character to advance the **Rescue Plan**.

The conversation matters. Simply reaching the cell or killing enemies nearby does not necessarily move the quest forward.

Once the rescue is active, you are asked to find a **Prison Key**.

At this point, keep the prisoner and surrounding water in mind. You do not need to search the entire fortress or assume that the next named enemy is carrying the key.

### Where Is the Prison Key?

The **Prison Key is in the water near the prison area**.

Approach the water beside the prisoner and nearby ship structures, then dive to check below the surface.

![Prison Key collected underwater near the prison area in Cliff of Madness.](/images/archeage-chronicles/cliff-of-madness-prison-key-underwater.webp)

The key is easy to miss when you are looking only at the walkway or wooden structures above it. The quest conversation indicates that it fell into the water, which is the clue that matters here.

The safest approach is to stay near the prison rather than swimming around the entire harbor.

Use the compass marker as you move through the water. Once you reach the pickup, collect the key and return to the locked cell.

Interact with the prison door to release the captive and continue the rescue.

If the door still says **"Prison Key required"**, check whether your character actually collected the pickup. In multiplayer, another party member opening the cell does not guarantee that every player has completed the same individual objective.

The quest state is more reliable than watching another player perform the interaction.

## How to Use the Cannon and Open the Gate

After the prison rescue advances, the next major objective is **Use the cannon to breach the gate**.

The cannon is mounted above the water near the wooden ship and fortress structures.

Return to the upper route using the ladders and connected walkways. Some parts of the area are easier to reach from the ship than by trying to jump directly from the water.

![Cannon beside the fortress gate with the Alight the Cliffs objective to breach the gate.](/images/archeage-chronicles/cliff-of-madness-cannon-gate.webp)

Interact with the cannon when the breach objective is active.

The shot opens a passage through the fortifications. The next route leads toward the combat area on the other side of the breached gate.

A common navigation mistake is continuing to search along the original walkway after firing. Return toward the water-facing side of the structure and look for the newly opened breach.

You may need to swim through the opening to reach the next encounter. The passage is not necessarily reached by staying on the upper wooden platform.

### The Cannon Still Won't Fire

There are three things to check before treating the cannon as broken:

- **Missing Husband:** complete it before entering the instance.
- **Rescue Plan:** speak to the captive and complete the Prison Key interaction.
- **Active quest:** make sure the objective has advanced to using the Cannon.

The cannon is a progression mechanism, not a shortcut that allows you to skip the rescue sequence.

## How to Beat Mad Anchor Krogal

**Mad Anchor Krogal** is the major encounter reached after the cannon breach.

The boss has heavy melee attacks that punish players who remain too close after finishing a combination. Its anchor swings can also catch you while recovering from an earlier dodge.

![Mad Anchor Krogal during the pirate fortress boss encounter after breaching the gate.](/images/archeage-chronicles/cliff-of-madness-mad-anchor-kro.webp)

This is where stamina becomes more important than squeezing another attack into every opening.

### Read Krogal's Anchor Swings Before Attacking

Krogal's anchor attacks are most dangerous when you remain beside him after finishing a melee combination. Watch the direction of the swing before closing the distance again. Dodging away without clearing the anchor's reach can still leave you exposed.

Melee characters should punish a completed attack with a short combination, then reposition before Krogal begins another swing. Ranged characters have more space to work with, but the boss can close that distance while a long cast or firing animation is still underway.

Healing is safest after Krogal finishes an attack sequence. Trying to drink a potion during his wind-up risks interruption, especially when your health is already low. Use the recovery window for either damage or healing rather than attempting both before his next attack.

### Return Through the Portal After Dying

The encounter has a **Portal shortcut** that allows you to return toward the combat area.

Use it rather than assuming that every failed attempt requires another full run through the previous fortress route.

A difficult solo attempt is also a reasonable point to reconsider your equipment and healing supplies. Repairing gear and obtaining additional potions before another attempt may save more time than repeatedly entering the fight with the same shortages.

A party provides another option, although players can interfere with one another's spacing or unexpectedly change the boss's target.

Defeating Mad Anchor Krogal clears this major obstacle and allows the fortress progression to continue.

## Where Do You Go After Mad Anchor Krogal?

Defeating Krogal opens the way to the remaining fortress objectives. Follow the active quest marker through the upper structures and ship areas, using the ladders and connected platforms to move between levels.

Several passages branch toward optional enemies or loot. The water-level routes can also make the next marker appear closer than it really is. Check its direction before leaving the main path, especially around the watchtower.

Another encounter involves **Poldat**, who can evade attacks and follow up aggressively. Leave enough room to react when he changes position, and avoid committing to a long attack while he is moving into range.

After clearing the remaining objectives, follow the departure marker toward the ship to finish the playable story.

## Why Can't You Open the Treasure Chest?

There is an optional chest in Cliff of Madness that requires the **Cliffs of Madness Treasure Map**.

Reaching the chest is not enough to open it. An interaction can fail because the required map is missing.

This is separate from the Prison Key and the cannon sequence.

The treasure map can be discovered while exploring the surrounding area, including the bridge-side route. Its precise acquisition method should not be confused with the mandatory prison rescue.

A locked treasure chest does not mean your main quest has failed.

Continue following the active rescue and fortress objectives if your immediate goal is to reach the end of the demo. Return for optional loot when you have the correct item.
