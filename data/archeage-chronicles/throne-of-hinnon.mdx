The entrance to **Throne of Hinnon** is at the Altar of Seals, but its key is hidden on an elevated ledge in the Sunken Ruins. Reaching the dungeon door without collecting the key means returning across Diamond Shores to retrieve it.

This optional dungeon is considerably tougher than the opening story encounters. Inside, you must activate four Sigils and operate a Lever before reaching **Helkum, King of Hinnon**. The final fight demands different dodge timings for quick teleport attacks and slower, red-marked strikes.


<section id="throne-of-hinnon-key" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Is the Throne of Hinnon Dungeon Key?

Leave **Watchtower of Hope** and travel northeast toward the **Sunken Ruins**. The key is not on the ground floor or along the ordinary passage through the structure. You need to climb toward the upper jumping area, near a Nui Tear, and use your Glider to reach a small side platform.

Equip the Glider before climbing. If you have not unlocked it yet, continue the opening quests until you receive **Flite's Prototype Glider** and can assign it to a quick slot.

### Cross the Stone Pillars

Near the upper part of the ruins, look toward the broken stone structures extending away from the main tower. These provide the landing points needed to reach the key.

![The elevated stone structures and intermediate landing points used to glide across the Sunken Ruins.](/images/archeage-chronicles/throne-of-hinnon-sunken-ruins-glide-route.webp)

Launch from the elevated edge and aim for the next reachable surface. After landing, wait for your Glider to become available again before attempting the following crossing.

Trying to cover the entire distance in one flight can drop you below the platforms. The safer route uses intermediate landings rather than relying on maximum flight distance.

Continue until you reach the **small ledge away from the main climbing route**.

### Collect the Dungeon Key

Search the ledge for the hidden pickup and collect it. Open your Bag afterward to confirm you received **Throne of Hinnon Dungeon Key**.

![Throne of Hinnon Dungeon Key showing Uncommon rarity, 0.1 weight, and its dungeon entry description.](/images/archeage-chronicles/throne-of-hinnon-key-ledge.webp)

The key is an **Uncommon** item weighing **0.1**. Its description identifies it as the item used to unlock the Throne of Hinnon entrance. It also states that the key cannot be destroyed or sold.

Check your inventory before repeating the climb. The pickup will not appear again for a character who has already collected it.

<section id="dungeon-entrance" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Open the Throne of Hinnon Dungeon

With the **Throne of Hinnon Dungeon Key** in your inventory, return to the **Altar of Seals**. This is the area where you encounter the skeletal boss **Bound Tyrant**.

Use fast travel if you have the required Hereafter Stones; otherwise, return through Diamond Shores. The key is found in the Sunken Ruins, but the dungeon entrance itself is at the Altar of Seals.

Find the sealed doorway and interact with it while carrying the key. Once unlocked, it leads into the hidden dungeon.

If the entrance remains locked, check the exact item name in your Bag. Other collectibles from the Sunken Ruins cannot replace the Throne of Hinnon Dungeon Key.

## What Level and Equipment Do You Need?

**Level 8–9 is a reasonable preparation target**, not a confirmed entry requirement. Even a Level 11 character can take substantial damage from the dungeon's elite enemies.

Bring healing potions, repair damaged equipment, and check your carried weight before entering. An upgraded weapon helps shorten difficult encounters, while excessive equipment weight can make repeated dodging more costly.

A party gives you more flexibility during the final fight, although Helkum switching targets can alter the timing of his attacks.

The interior enemies can also provide useful equipment materials. Whether to fight them or push directly toward the final chamber depends on your remaining healing supplies and upgrade needs.

</section>

## How Do You Open the Final Gate in Throne of Hinnon?

Inside the dungeon, you must activate **4 Sigils** and operate a **Lever** to reach the final boss chamber.

Follow the interior passages and interact with the Sigils as you encounter them. Some ordinary enemies can be bypassed, but running straight toward the final chamber without completing the mechanisms leaves the route blocked.

### Pull the Lever Along the Dungeon Route

One passage contains a Lever that must be operated.

![The Lever inside Throne of Hinnon being operated as a heavy door opens elsewhere in the dungeon.](/images/archeage-chronicles/throne-of-hinnon-dungeon-lever.webp)

Pulling it triggers the message **"Somewhere, a heavy door creaks open."** Continue along the accessible route and look for the remaining Sigils.

The enemies along these passages can still be dangerous when several attack together. Long Fatal Attack animations leave you exposed to other opponents, so clearing a group safely may require more than focusing on one weakened target.

### Activate the Fourth Sigil

The **fourth Sigil** opens the gate leading toward the final boss.

If the gate remains closed, retrace the accessible passages and check for a missed interaction. Killing additional ordinary enemies will not replace an incomplete Sigil sequence.

Once the gate opens, continue to the boss chamber and prepare for **Helkum, King of Hinnon**.

## How Do You Beat Helkum, King of Hinnon?

**Helkum, King of Hinnon** is the final boss of Throne of Hinnon. His most dangerous attacks involve teleporting into melee range, followed by strikes that do not all share the same timing.

![Helkum, King of Hinnon, attacking inside the Throne of Hinnon boss arena with red combat effects.](/images/archeage-chronicles/throne-of-hinnon-reaper-red-heavy.webp)

The critical distinction is between his ordinary teleport slash and his slower red-marked heavy attack. Dodging both at the same moment makes the delayed strike much harder to survive.

### Recognize the Teleport and Delayed Strike

Helkum opens aggressively with a teleporting slash. Be ready to evade as the attack arrives, then use the brief recovery to land damage.

His red-marked heavy attack has a different rhythm. After recognizing the warning, watch for the actual strike rather than dodging immediately when the effect appears.

An early dodge can finish before the delayed attack connects.

The practical difference is:

- **Regular teleport slash:** react quickly to the incoming strike.
- **Red heavy attack:** wait for the delayed attack animation before dodging.
- **Follow-up swings:** avoid attacking until the combination has finished.

Spend the first few exchanges learning which attack Helkum is preparing. Short attacks during recovery are more reliable than trying to finish a long combination while he is still moving.

### Use the Break Window for Heavy Damage

Watch Helkum's combat indicators as you continue attacking. When he approaches a **Break** opportunity, prepare your stronger abilities.

Once the Break occurs, use the opening for a Fatal Attack or a committed damage combination.

This is the best time to spend resources on a longer offensive sequence. During ordinary exchanges, preserve enough Stamina to evade the next teleport or heavy strike.

### Adjust When Helkum Falls Below 50% HP

At approximately **50% HP**, Helkum introduces an additional attack combination.

His earlier teleport and delayed-strike patterns still matter, but the new sequence gives you another reason to wait until the full animation has finished before moving in.

Watch the follow-up attacks, dodge the completed sequence, and punish its recovery. Stronger abilities are especially valuable when you can combine that opening with a Break.

Do not rush the final portion of the fight simply because Helkum has little health remaining. One mistimed response to the delayed heavy attack can still end the attempt.

</section>

<section id="death-and-retry" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Happens If Helkum Kills You?

A **Portal near the dungeon's starting area** provides a shortcut back toward Helkum's chamber after you respawn.

Use it instead of retracing every room and Sigil interaction.

During a party attempt, a surviving teammate may be able to keep the encounter active while a defeated player returns through the Portal. Helkum can change targets when someone rejoins, so be ready for his next teleport or heavy strike.

Do not assume that the boss's remaining HP carries over after the encounter resets. The shortcut reduces the return journey, but it does not guarantee that a failed attempt will preserve your damage.

</section>

<section id="rewards-and-farming" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Is Throne of Hinnon Worth Farming?

The enemies inside Throne of Hinnon can provide **Weapon Tempering Stones** and **Armor Tempering Stones**. Ancient Soldier equipment, including the Helmet and Armor, can also be obtained in the dungeon.

These materials are useful if your current equipment needs Tempering before another difficult encounter.

Clearing ordinary enemy groups is worthwhile when you need upgrade materials. Otherwise, you can bypass some of them and concentrate on the mandatory Sigils, Lever, and final boss.

The **Ancient Soldier** pieces are heavy armor. For a Mage or Ranger relying on frequent repositioning, the additional equipment weight may be a poor trade for the extra defense.

Do not assume that every run guarantees the same equipment drops or a fixed reward quantity.

Defeating Helkum completes the optional Throne of Hinnon challenge. It is not required to reach the demo's main story ending.
