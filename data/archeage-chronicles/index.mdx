The ArcheAge Chronicles demo ends after the pirate fortress and final departure, even if much of **Diamond Shores** remains unexplored. You can finish the story without clearing every optional boss, collecting every item, or completing the regional exploration progress.

The main progression obstacle comes before **Cliff of Madness**, where the entrance remains locked until **Missing Husband** is complete. Earlier ruins, exploration objectives, and optional encounters provide equipment and character improvements, but they do not all need to be completed before the final story sequence.

<section id="choose-class" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which Class Should You Choose Before Starting?

The demo offers four classes: **Warrior, Ranger, Berserker, and Mage**. Each has multiple weapon styles, although some of the options shown during character creation are not yet playable.

![Ranger class selection showing Hatchet, Longbow, and Crossbow weapon options in ArcheAge Chronicles.](/images/archeage-chronicles/classes-and-weapons-selection.webp)

For a first trip through Diamond Shores, the most practical choices depend on how comfortably you handle Stamina and enemy positioning.

- **Ranger:** Hatchet and Longbow provide different attack ranges. Thrown Hatchets are especially useful against moving enemies, while the Longbow allows sustained ranged pressure.
- **Mage:** Elemental Staff is a good choice for ranged Fire and Lightning attacks. Its charged spells demand careful Stamina management. Wave Sword provides a closer-range Frost alternative.
- **Warrior:** Longsword and Greatsword suit players comfortable reading melee attacks, guarding, and dodging at close range.
- **Berserker:** A heavier frontline class with Axe, Warhammer, and Great Shield styles presented in character creation. Check the demo's availability restrictions before choosing a specific weapon combination.

Several styles, including Ranger's Crossbow and Mage's Vitality Staff, are unavailable in the demo. Their appearance in the selection menu does not mean you can obtain them through normal quest progression.

### How Do You Switch Between Two Weapons?

You can equip **two weapons** at once. Open your Bag, assign compatible weapons to the available slots, and press **Q** to switch between them using the default keyboard controls.

For Ranger, **Hatchet + Longbow** is a useful combination. The Hatchet supports close-range attacks and thrown axes, while the Longbow gives you another option when fighting from a distance.

Try both weapons against ordinary enemies before spending Trait Points. Knowing when to switch is more useful during the opening quests than committing immediately to an elaborate build.

## Complete the Tutorial and Defeat Herald Knight

The opening begins in the **Recalling Grounds**, where you learn movement, targeting, normal attacks, heavy attacks, and defensive timing.

Pay attention to the attack indicators. They establish combat rules that continue to matter throughout the demo:

- **Heavy attacks** can break an enemy's guard.
- **Red warning attacks** cannot be blocked normally and require an appropriate defensive response.
- **Dodging** consumes Stamina, so using all your resources offensively can leave you exposed.
- **Long attack animations** make it harder to react when an enemy begins a new attack.

The tutorial eventually leads you to a lever and the **Herald Knight** encounter.

Defeat Herald Knight to continue out of the opening area. The fight is a useful early test of whether you understand the difference between attacking an enemy that is recovering and attacking while it is preparing another strike.

For an Elemental Staff Mage, charging a spell during a clear opening works better than attempting to complete every long cast while the boss is moving toward you. Melee characters benefit from short attacks between defensive responses.

Once the encounter is cleared, proceed through the remaining introduction and enter the open world.

## Leave Watchtower of Hope and Prepare for Diamond Shores

**Watchtower of Hope** is one of the first important landmarks after the tutorial.

Before following the roads into Diamond Shores, interact with the nearby **Nui Statue**. Awakened statues serve as useful return points, and they can also provide travel and utility interactions.

Check your Bag after obtaining new equipment. Having a second weapon in your inventory does not mean it has already been assigned to your active weapon slot.

The surrounding area introduces gathering, recipes, and equipment tools. One early task sends you toward the hunter **Harai** and involves collecting Roseberries.

Gather the requested items, check the crafting interaction, and continue once the task has registered. Harvesting and cooking are useful introductions to the survival and resource systems, but there is no need to spend a long time collecting unrelated materials before proceeding.

### Keep Some Healing Available

As soon as you begin exploring, basic enemies and optional encounters start competing for your healing supplies.

Nui Statue utility interactions can help restore consumables. Use those opportunities before entering an unfamiliar fight rather than carrying an almost empty supply simply because your health is currently full.

For most of the early route, maintaining a usable weapon and enough healing is more important than buying every available gathering tool.

A Hunting Knife or Pickaxe matters when you actually need to harvest a particular resource. Check the task and your existing inventory before buying another tool. Equipping unnecessary items can also affect your carried load.

## Explore Diamond Shores Without Losing the Main Quest

Diamond Shores contains several overlapping types of objectives. You will encounter local NPC requests, discoverable areas, dungeon entrances, hostile groups, and collectibles while moving toward your next story destination.

The important habit is to **check the current quest objective before treating a nearby map marker as mandatory**.

Some discoveries provide regional progress or character improvements without moving the main story forward. You can stop for those rewards when convenient, but chasing every visible collectible is not required to reach the demo ending.

Likewise, a nearby strong enemy does not necessarily block the intended route. Some world encounters are easier with other players, and spending every potion trying to defeat one can leave you underprepared for your next story fight.

### Unlock and Equip the Glider

During the coastal exploration, you can encounter the **Scaling the Tower** quest and a researcher investigating the ruins.

The investigation introduces a Glider and directs you toward the **Sunken Ruins**.

Once you have the item:

1. Open your **Bag** and equip the Glider.
2. Assign it to an accessible quick slot.
3. Activate it from a suitable jumping point to cross toward the ruins.

![Flite's Prototype Glider showing Uncommon rarity, 0.5 weight, two-star flight ratings, and Easy handling.](/images/archeage-chronicles/archeage-chronicles-flites-prototype-glider-stats.webp)

**Flite's Prototype Glider** is an **Uncommon** item weighing **0.5**. It has **2/5 Flight Speed**, **2/5 Airtime**, and **2/5 Maneuverability**, with **Easy** handling difficulty.

Equip the Glider and assign it to a quick slot before approaching the jumping point. If it does not deploy, check the assigned item control instead of repeatedly attempting the same jump.

Its modest Airtime makes intermediate landing points especially useful when crossing the separated platforms around the Sunken Ruins.

### Complete the Sunken Ruins Investigation

Follow the researcher's active objective through the **Sunken Ruins** and interact with the relevant objects before returning to report your findings.

The structure has multiple elevations and optional pickups, so climbing into another section does not necessarily advance the investigation. Check the current quest objective before spending time searching the side platforms.

A hidden ledge beyond the upper stone pillars also holds the key to **Throne of Hinnon**. Reaching it requires a separate Glider crossing, and the dungeon itself is optional. Follow the [Throne of Hinnon key route](/archeage-chronicles/throne-of-hinnon/) if you want to attempt that challenge.

## What Can You Do at the Altar of Seals?

The **Altar of Seals** contains the **Bound Tyrant** encounter, which can be attempted alone or with a party.

This fight is separate from the pirate fortress story progression. You can return later with repaired equipment or additional healing supplies rather than repeatedly attempting it while underprepared.

The Altar also contains the entrance to **Throne of Hinnon**, but reaching that locked doorway does not mean you already have the required dungeon key.

</section>

## How Do You Reach Cliff of Madness?

The entrance to **Cliff of Madness** remains locked until **Missing Husband** is complete. Find Renald and finish the required conversation before returning to the fortress. Otherwise, the entrance displays **"Quest requirements not met."**

Once inside, [rescue the prisoner and breach the fortress gate](/archeage-chronicles/cliff-of-madness/) to continue the story toward the final departure.

</section>

<section id="equipment-progress" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When Should You Upgrade Gear or Spend Trait Points?

Visit a **Blacksmith** when your equipment needs repairing or a difficult encounter is taking too long to finish. Weapon Tempering requires Weapon Tempering Stones, while armor uses separate Armor Tempering Stones.

Before consuming an upgrade material, compare your current equipment with any replacements already in your Bag. Improving a weapon you intend to keep is more useful than spending scarce materials on one you will immediately replace.

Weapon Traits unlock or improve abilities within the corresponding weapon tree. You can experiment with different Trait allocations, but resetting character Stat Points uses a separate reset item.

During the demo, prioritize a reliable weapon combination and enough survivability to complete the next encounter. There is little reason to spend all your resources optimizing equipment for ordinary enemies that are already easy to defeat.

## How Do You Enter PvP in ArcheAge Chronicles?

PvP is available before finishing the demo story. Once you can use the relevant **Nui Statue** interactions in Diamond Shores, approach a statue and select **Move to Conflict Channel**.

After transferring, other players can become hostile targets. Protected town areas may still prevent combat, so move into an appropriate open-world area before attempting to attack another player.

### What Happens If You Die in PvP?

Dying during PvP can cost both experience and currency. The demo displays the message **"You lost some experience and currency due to death."** after a defeat.

This makes repeated PvP deaths costly when you are still leveling or saving resources for equipment improvements. The exact amount lost depends on the current game state, so do not assume a fixed penalty.

### How Do You Join Your Party's Channel?

Players in the same party can still end up in different channel instances.

To join a teammate:

1. Open **Esc → Community → Party**.
2. Select the party member you want to join.
3. Use **Move to Channel** when the option appears.

The movement prompt may not appear when you are already in the same channel. Once the transfer completes, check that you can see your teammates before entering a group encounter.

## How Do You Know When the Demo Is Finished?

After completing the pirate fortress story, follow the remaining departure objectives until **Use the Dimensional Rift** becomes available.

The final transition displays:

**"You've reached the end of the preview."**

![The End of Preview message displayed after completing the ArcheAge Chronicles demo story.](/images/archeage-chronicles/archeage-chronicles-end-of-preview.webp)

Following the prompt returns you to character selection. When you log back in, your character can continue from **Watchtower of Hope**.

### Do You Need 100% Diamond Shores Progress?

No. The main story can end with regional exploration still incomplete.

A Mage character reached the final transition with only **20% Diamond Shores progress** showing at Level 9. The End of Preview message appeared normally.

That percentage represents the character's regional progress, not a requirement for finishing the playable story. You can reach the ending without completing every local quest, collectible, or optional encounter.

</section>

<section id="after-preview" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Can You Do After Finishing the Demo?

After returning to **Watchtower of Hope**, you can continue exploring Diamond Shores with your existing demo character.

Your remaining options include:

- **Explore undiscovered locations** and complete unfinished regional objectives.
- **Upgrade equipment** using materials collected from enemies and exploration.
- **Challenge Throne of Hinnon**, the optional dungeon with a hidden key and a difficult final boss.
- **Enter a Conflict Channel** through a Nui Statue to try open-world PvP.

There is no additional main-story destination to unlock by repeatedly revisiting the final departure point. Choose the unfinished activities you want to complete instead.
