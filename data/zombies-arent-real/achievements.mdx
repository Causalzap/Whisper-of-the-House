**Zombies Aren't Real** has **14 Steam achievements**, ranging from actions you can complete during an ordinary battle to challenges that apply across an entire Campaign.

The difference matters when planning a completion run. Destroying a bridge or calling a helicopter only requires a particular action, while **Flawless**, **Sparkly**, **Masterful**, and **No Friendly Fire** have conditions that extend across multiple scenarios.

A successful final battle does not automatically satisfy every Campaign achievement. Check the requirement before starting a challenge so that a missed badge, unwanted ability deployment, or civilian killed by your own weapons does not undermine the result.

## What Is the Best Order to Complete All 14 Achievements?

Begin with achievements that can be earned during ordinary progression. Your first successful Scenario is an opportunity for **Beginner's Luck**, while later Gold and Diamond results count toward **Golden** and **Freshly Cut**. Once bomber and helicopter support becomes available, call each during battle and look for a suitable bridge to destroy for **Choke Point**.

After learning the Campaign's stages, concentrate on its longer challenges. **Flawless** requires a run without Scenario losses, while **Glittery** and **Sparkly** depend on the badges earned across every stage. A Diamond result on every stage meets the stated Gold-or-better requirement as well, making those two objectives natural candidates for the same attempt.

Leave **Masterful** and **No Friendly Fire** until you know where outbreaks develop and which weapons can threaten civilians. **Daring** is easier to isolate because its no-Safe-Zone restriction applies to just one Scenario. Work toward **Completionist** by finishing the remaining Campaigns rather than repeatedly improving badges in cities you have already cleared.

</section>

## How to Unlock the Single-Scenario Achievements

These achievements are linked to winning individual scenarios rather than completing an entire Campaign.

### Beginner's Luck

**Requirement:** Clear a scenario on the first attempt.

The simplest opportunity is your first battle. Establish a protected area, cover the approaches that are most exposed, and complete the Scenario without an unsuccessful attempt.

The achievement is tied to the first-attempt condition, not to reaching a specific Gold or Diamond badge.

If you lose your first attempt, do not assume replaying the same Scenario will count as a first-time clear. The achievement does not specify whether a retry restores that eligibility.

### Golden

**Requirement:** Win a scenario with a Gold badge or better.

Gold is an individual Scenario result. You do not need Gold on every Campaign stage to unlock this particular achievement.

After a battle, check the awarded badge on the results screen. A successful clear with a lower badge does not meet the stated requirement.

### Freshly Cut

**Requirement:** Win a scenario with a Diamond badge.

This requires a stronger individual result than an ordinary victory. A single Diamond-rated clear satisfies the stated achievement condition.

Do not use one civilian survival percentage as the Diamond target for every map. One Scenario awarded Diamond with approximately 25% saved, while another awarded Sapphire with 39%. Check the actual badge after each victory instead of applying the same percentage to every city.

### Back from the Brink

**Requirement:** Beat a scenario after a Safe Zone has been breached.

To earn **Back from the Brink**, finish a Scenario successfully after at least one Safe Zone has been breached. A breach by itself does not satisfy the condition; the battle still needs to end in victory.

If a wall breaks during an otherwise successful attempt, continue fighting rather than immediately restarting. There is no need to deliberately weaken your defenses to create a breach. If you want to understand why the perimeter failed, check the <Link href="/zombies-arent-real/safe-zones">wall breach and exposed Gate troubleshooting steps</Link>.

</section>

## How to Get Choke Point, Air Support, and GET TO THE CHOPPA

Three achievements depend on actions performed during the battle.

### Choke Point — Destroy a Bridge

**Requirement:** Destroy a bridge.

A bomber airstrike can damage and destroy bridges. To attempt the achievement:

1. Choose a Scenario with a bridge that can be targeted.
2. Make the bomber ability available in your Loadout.
3. During battle, direct an airstrike at the bridge.
4. Continue attacking it if the first strike does not destroy it.
5. Confirm the bridge has actually been destroyed.

The key distinction is between damaging a bridge and removing it. The achievement requires destruction.

Once the bridge collapses, check where the remaining zombies are heading. Destroying a crossing may concentrate them at another bridge, so keep the surviving approaches defended while completing the Scenario.

### Where's the Air Support!? — Call a Bomber Airstrike

**Requirement:** Call in a bomber airstrike.

Select the bomber ability in your Loadout and deploy it during a battle once it is available.

One airstrike call satisfies the stated action requirement. You do not need to destroy a bridge specifically to earn this achievement.

The bomber can therefore help with **Where's the Air Support!?** and **Choke Point** during the same battle, provided you actually destroy a bridge.

### GET TO THE CHOPPA — Call a Helicopter

**Requirement:** Call in a helicopter.

Make a helicopter ability available in your Loadout, then summon it during battle.

The achievement requires the helicopter call, not a particular number of zombie kills.

You can earn this while using helicopters as part of an ordinary defensive strategy. There is no need to design a separate Campaign run around it.

</section>

### How to Unlock Glittery

**Glittery** requires completing a Campaign with a Gold badge or better on **every stage**.

This is not the same as Golden, which only needs one qualifying Scenario.

When planning a Glittery attempt, pay attention to each stage's badge. One weaker result is enough to leave the full-Campaign requirement unsatisfied.

The useful objective is consistency rather than achieving the highest possible result on a single map.

### How to Unlock Sparkly

**Sparkly** requires completing a Campaign with **Diamond on every stage**.

Do not confuse the final Scenario's badge with the Campaign-wide result.

One Detroit run finished with Diamond, Diamond, Sapphire, Diamond, and Diamond across its five stages. The final battle earned Diamond, but Stage 3 remained Sapphire, so the displayed results did not meet Sparkly's all-Diamond condition.

The player considered replaying Stage 3. However, that attempt does not confirm whether replaying an individual stage is enough to trigger Sparkly afterward. If you are correcting a missed badge, check the achievement after the replay rather than assuming the entire Campaign record has been updated.

### Should You Attempt Glittery and Sparkly Together?

A Campaign that meets Sparkly's all-Diamond condition also meets Glittery's stated all-Gold-or-better condition.

You can therefore aim for Diamond throughout rather than treating these as completely unrelated objectives.

However, a first Gold-focused Campaign can be a more manageable preparation step. It gives you experience with the later stages before you deliberately chase Diamond across the entire run.

## How to Unlock Flawless Without Losing a Scenario

**Flawless** requires beating an entire Campaign without ever losing a Scenario.

The official achievement condition includes an important recovery rule: after a loss, you can restart from **Scenario 1** and make another attempt.

Do not confuse that with the assumption that retrying only the failed stage will necessarily restore Flawless eligibility.

To approach Flawless:

1. Choose a Campaign whose earlier stages you understand.
2. Begin at Scenario 1.
3. Prioritize reliable victories over experimenting with unfamiliar tactics.
4. Check the outcome of each stage before proceeding.
5. If you lose, restart the Campaign from Scenario 1 for another Flawless attempt.

A lower badge does not by itself violate the stated Flawless requirement. The restriction is losing a Scenario, not failing to achieve Gold or Diamond.

</section>

<section id="daring" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Unlock Daring Without Building a Safe Zone

**Daring** requires winning a Scenario **without placing any Safe Zone**.

You do not need to complete an entire Campaign under this condition.

The restriction changes the usual opening. Instead of drawing a Safe Zone around civilians, you must rely on whatever defensive options are available without establishing that protected area.

For your own attempt:

1. Choose a Scenario where you can deploy useful defenses without creating a Safe Zone.
2. Leave the Safe Zone tool unused.
3. Position available defenders where they can attack approaching zombies.
4. Use permitted battle abilities if needed; the stated Daring condition does not prohibit them.
5. Win the Scenario without having placed a Safe Zone.

Do not mistake Daring for a no-damage or no-civilian-loss achievement. Its stated restriction concerns placing a Safe Zone.

That does not guarantee that every no-zone layout will win. The challenge is to complete the battle despite giving up the game's usual protected civilian area.

For ordinary Campaign progression, the <Link href="/zombies-arent-real/safe-zones">Safe Zones guide</Link> explains how protected areas and civilian movement normally work.

</section>

<section id="masterful" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Unlock Masterful Without Battle-Phase Abilities

**Masterful** requires completing an entire Campaign, starting fresh from **Scenario 1**, without deploying any **battle-phase abilities**.

This is a Campaign-wide restriction, not a single-stage challenge.

Battle-phase abilities include the support actions used after combat begins, such as calling paratroopers, bombers, or helicopters.

The normal advantage of these abilities is that they allow you to respond after seeing where the zombie outbreak develops. Masterful removes that option.

### What Should You Prepare Before Starting the Battle?

You need a defensive setup capable of surviving without relying on a mid-battle rescue.

Before starting a Scenario, check the coverage of the defenses you can place during preparation and avoid leaving major areas dependent on an emergency support deployment.

The <Link href="/zombies-arent-real">beginner guide</Link> explains initial deployment, Garrison positioning, building coverage, and the differences between defensive units.

For Masterful, keep the achievement's restriction clear:

- Start the Campaign from Scenario 1.
- Do not deploy battle-phase abilities.
- Maintain that restriction throughout the complete Campaign.
- Finish the Campaign successfully.

The restriction concerns deploying battle-phase abilities, rather than simply having them available in your Loadout. Keep those abilities unused from Scenario 1 through the final battle. If a defense is likely to depend on emergency support after combat begins, strengthen its prepared positions before starting the Scenario.

</section>

## How to Unlock No Friendly Fire

**No Friendly Fire** requires completing a Campaign without killing any civilians with your own weapons. The condition concerns deaths caused by your attacks, not simply whether every civilian survives the outbreak. A victory therefore does not automatically establish that your weapons avoided civilian casualties.

The greatest risk comes when zombies and civilians occupy the same streets. Mortars and other area attacks can strike people moving toward a Gate, while a bomber aimed at a crowded approach may hit more than the intended zombies. Before starting a Scenario, look for weapon positions that separate the expected firing area from civilian movement. During combat, avoid placing explosive attacks directly over groups you are trying to rescue.

One Campaign attempt finished without unlocking No Friendly Fire despite efforts to limit friendly fire. The cause was not identified, so do not assume that avoiding a single weapon type guarantees success. Attempt the challenge on a Campaign you already understand, pay attention to your own attacks throughout every stage, and keep the restriction in mind even when a battle is nearly won.

</section>

<section id="completionist" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Unlock Completionist

**Completionist** requires beating **every Campaign**.

Finishing one Campaign, even with Diamond on every stage, is not enough.

Unlike Glittery or Sparkly, the stated Completionist condition does not specify a particular badge requirement for each stage. Its goal is Campaign completion.

Open the Campaign selection and identify which cities still have unfinished stages. Clear the remaining Campaigns rather than repeatedly replaying cities you have already completed; earning Diamond in one city does not replace finishing another.

In the Early Access build, custom and Workshop maps appeared under Free Play instead of the Campaign selection. When checking your progress toward Completionist, concentrate on the Campaigns rather than treating additional Free Play maps as unfinished Campaign stages.

</section>

<section id="final-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Didn't an Achievement Unlock?

A successful battle may still be missing the required badge or a condition that applies across the whole Campaign. Start by checking whether the achievement calls for one Scenario, every stage of one Campaign, or every Campaign in the game. **Golden** and **Freshly Cut** depend on individual results, while **Glittery** and **Sparkly** require qualifying badges across an entire Campaign.

For restricted runs, examine what happened before the final victory. A lost Scenario interrupts a Flawless attempt, and restarting from Scenario 1 is the stated way to begin again. Masterful requires avoiding battle-phase ability deployments throughout the Campaign. For No Friendly Fire, pay particular attention to explosive attacks near civilians, but do not assume that an apparently clean final battle proves the entire run met the condition.

If a challenge still does not unlock, avoid changing several things at once on the next attempt. Choose a Campaign you know, concentrate on the exact restriction, and inspect each stage's result before continuing. For ordinary deployment or weapon-placement problems, return to <Link href="/zombies-arent-real">the first-Scenario defense and battle ability instructions</Link>.

</section>
