<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To progress in **Potato Please**, open the first sack, take potatoes to the workbench, peel them, and deliver the finished potatoes through the chute. Complete the **Warm-up Shift**, purchase the required upgrades from the supply terminal, and follow the Journal objectives as your rank increases. Move from manual peeling into machine production, use the **Flamethrower** to thaw the pile after the storm, and finish the **60,000-potato Quota**. Once the remaining stock is processed, a special potato leads to the final escape and three possible train endings.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Potato Please starts with a simple assignment: peel potatoes and send them to the Ministry. But the work gradually expands into a production operation with new tools, automated machinery, outdoor transport, and an increasingly large pile to clear.

The easiest way to stay on track is to follow the current work objective instead of buying every upgrade as soon as it appears. Most of the important equipment arrives through that progression.

There are also tasks that interrupt normal peeling. You may need to clear a dangerous object from the pile, read new Ministry orders, deal with the cold, or thaw the yard before production can resume.

This guide follows the main progression from the first potato to the final Quota.

## How Do You Start Your First Shift in Potato Please?

When the shift begins, read the initial directive and follow the assignment to open a potato sack.

Your first job is to peel potatoes at the workbench and deliver the cleaned potatoes through the chute.

### Peel and deliver your first potatoes

1. Find the starting potato sacks and open one.
2. Pick up a potato and carry it to the workbench.
3. Place it in the workstation's raw-potato bin.
4. Begin peeling. On the default PC controls, hold **LMB** to press the blade into the skin.
5. Adjust the potato as needed to remove the remaining skin.
6. Collect the cleaned potatoes and carry them to the chute.
7. Drop the finished potatoes into the chute to complete the delivery objective.

![Potato Please opening work objective showing cleaned potatoes being delivered through the chute.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-first-chute-objective.webp)

*Carry the cleaned potatoes to the chute and deliver them before returning to the supply terminal.*

The important distinction is that peeling and delivery are separate actions. A finished potato sitting at the workbench has not yet been sent through the chute.

### Why does the potato lose so much material?

The starting blade can remove more than just the skin if you press too deeply.

Use controlled movements rather than holding the blade in one position for too long. Rotate or reposition the potato when necessary so you can reach areas that remain covered.

Peeling quality affects how much of the potato remains usable. At the beginning, finishing the assigned work matters more than chasing a perfect grade on every potato.

Once the first delivery is complete, the Ministry grants access to the supply window.

## How Do You Complete the Warm-up Shift?

The opening objectives introduce the supply terminal, replacement tools, and worker skills.

After your first delivery, return to the terminal and follow the new instructions.

### What should you do next?

The early sequence includes:

1. **Requisition the Rusty Knife** when the terminal objective becomes available.
2. Return to the workbench and continue the required peeling.
3. Complete the early objective to peel 10 potatoes.
4. Open the Journal when you earn your first skill point.
5. Finish the Warm-up Shift objective.
6. Obtain the required Carry Capacity upgrade.
7. Follow the instruction to open the door and continue into the larger work area.

The Warm-up Shift is an introductory milestone, not the end of the game.

### What should you do with your first Journal point?

The first Journal skills include **Stamina**, **Auto Rotate**, and **Tremor Treatment**.

The choice depends on what is making manual peeling difficult.

If you frequently run out of stamina, improve endurance. If the blade is difficult to keep steady, hand-control improvements may be more useful.

Do not spend points randomly just to unlock the next row. Check what each available skill changes before choosing it.

The complete knife progression, Journal abilities, and machine upgrade sequence are covered in the [Potato Please automation and upgrades guide](/potato-please/automation).

## How Do You Earn Money, and Is Hard Mode Worth It?

Sell cleaned potatoes through the chute to earn currency for equipment. **Hard Mode** reduces payment per potato by **50%** and removes peel forgiveness. That makes early upgrades slower to afford while increasing the penalty for rough peeling.

None of the 20 listed Steam achievements explicitly requires Hard Mode. For a first run focused on learning the objectives and completing the Quota, the standard setting avoids those extra restrictions.

## What Should You Do About Grenades, Coughing, and Other Interruptions?

Not every interruption is caused by a broken production machine.

The work area introduces hazards, character-related problems, and new Ministry instructions as the shift progresses.

### What should you do when you find a grenade?

A Ministry notice warns that explosive devices can be hidden among the potatoes.

A disturbed grenade can explode after a short delay.

If you uncover one:

1. Stop treating it like an ordinary potato.
2. Move or throw it well away from your working area.
3. Do not put it into a potato sack.
4. Return to the current processing objective once the immediate danger has passed.

The notice explicitly warns against packing explosive devices with potatoes. Do not assume that the normal potato-handling process is safe for them.

### Why does the worker keep coughing?

Coughing can interrupt the peeling process and make manual work more awkward.

The game provides character-related purchases such as Medication to address this problem.

If the interruptions repeatedly prevent you from completing work, check the relevant Character upgrades instead of assuming a better knife will solve the issue.

### Why does the worker get cold outside?

Outdoor work exposes the worker to freezing conditions.

Avoid spending unnecessary time outside when the next objective can be completed indoors. The Winter Coat becomes a relevant character upgrade if cold exposure repeatedly interrupts your outdoor tasks.

These problems are separate from whether your peeling machines are functioning.

## How Do You Clear the Storm and Thaw the Potato Pile?

Later in the shift, a storm interrupts the normal production routine.

The yard becomes buried in snow, and the outdoor potato pile freezes.

The game instructs you not to go outside until the storm has passed. Once you receive the new objective, you must thaw the yard before returning to normal potato processing.

### How to complete the thawing objective

1. Wait until the game provides the storm recovery instructions.
2. Requisition the **Flamethrower** when the objective makes it available.
3. Go outside to the frozen potato pile.
4. Use the Flamethrower to melt the frozen area.
5. Continue until the game confirms that the potatoes in the yard have been thawed.
6. Return to the normal production objective.

![Potato Please Flamethrower being used to thaw the frozen outdoor potato pile after the storm.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-storm-flamethrower.webp)

*Thaw the frozen potato pile with the Flamethrower until the yard-clearing objective is complete.*

Do not assume that melting a visible patch of snow is enough. The objective concerns the frozen potatoes in the yard, and the game provides a completion state when the work is finished.

### What happens after the storm?

The Ministry issues another notice explaining that shipments must proceed by rail and that part of the stored stock has been removed.

The remaining potatoes still need to be processed.

At this point, the major objective is the **60,000-potato Quota**.

You can continue upgrading machinery as required, but the overall goal is now to finish the remaining stock.

</section>

## How Do You Finish the 60,000-Potato Quota?

After the storm, the remaining stock becomes your final processing objective.

You must reach **60,000 / 60,000** on the Quota counter.

This is a cumulative production task, not a requirement to peel 60,000 potatoes manually at the starting bench.

### What should you do when progress slows down?

First, identify whether potatoes are still available and where they have stopped moving.

If the outdoor pile still contains a substantial number of potatoes, continue supplying your production system.

If the machines have potatoes waiting to be processed, allow them to finish or address the relevant bottleneck.

If only a few potatoes remain, inspect the yard and work area rather than assuming all the stock has already reached the machines.

The game also offers a **repile** interaction for displaced potatoes, and the late-game Potato Vacuum can help with loose potatoes.

For the actual repile controls, Vacuum operation, and stalled machine troubleshooting, use the [Potato Please automation guide](/potato-please/automation).

### Does completing the Quota finish the game?

Not yet.

Reaching the final Quota triggers a new objective involving a special potato and the facility's locked exit.

The game then moves from processing potatoes into its final escape sequence.

To avoid repeating a long shift when pursuing multiple endings, pay attention to your available save options before making the final railway choice.

## Do Resistance Notes, Pavel's Letters, and the Puppy Matter?

As you work, you encounter characters and messages that provide more context about the depot and the world outside it.

These interactions are worth paying attention to, even when they are not part of the main peeling objective.

### Resistance Notes

Some potatoes contain messages from the Resistance.

The game may present a choice to report the message to the Ministry or keep it.

Read the available options before responding. These are narrative decisions, not ordinary production upgrades.

The three confirmed main train endings are ultimately selected through the railway destinations. Do not assume that reporting a message is the same as selecting the Ministry ending.

### Pavel's Letters

Your son sends letters describing his experiences away from home and sometimes asks for advice or assistance.

These letters appear as the shift progresses.

You can respond to the situations presented, but the exact effect of every choice has not been established.

One hidden achievement, **Your Own Executioner**, has a description that may point toward this storyline, though its specific unlock method remains unconfirmed.

### The Puppy

A hungry puppy appears outside during the shift.

Helping the puppy can also lead to it assisting with potatoes.

The interaction is connected to the **Good Boy** achievement.

For the confirmed Good Boy steps, the remaining hidden achievements, and an interactive completion tracker, see the [Potato Please achievements guide](/potato-please/achievements).

## How Do You Finish Potato Please and Reach the Endings?

The shift continues after **60,000 / 60,000**. A special parachute delivery leads to the locked Iron Door and then to a train with three destinations. Choose **Northern Front**, **Varenka**, or **Western Industry** depending on whose outcome you want to see.

For the key, door controls, destination levers, and how to revisit the other routes, follow the [Potato Please ending steps](/potato-please/endings).

## Potato Please Beginner Questions

### What should I do if my next upgrade is locked?

Check the current Journal objective and your available money.

Some equipment appears during a later stage of the production progression. Continue completing the active work requirements before assuming that the next machine is immediately available.

### Why aren't my peeled potatoes counting?

Check whether they have actually been delivered through the chute or processed by the relevant equipment.

During the opening shift, peeling and chute delivery are distinct actions. Later, transport and peeling machines can also be at different points in their processing cycle.

### Should I buy every upgrade?

No. Your immediate priority is completing the current objective and removing the biggest obstacle to further progress.

You do not need every optional tool to complete the Quota.

### Can I keep peeling potatoes after finishing the game?

A completed shift may leave you inside the facility, but that does not guarantee you can reopen the final exit and freely choose another ending.

If your goal is to see all three endings, use the save guidance in the endings guide before committing to the first railway destination.

### How many Steam achievements are there?

Potato Please has **20 Steam achievements** in the November 3, 2026 achievement list.

They cover opening tasks, manual peeling, machines, the final Quota, story interactions, and train endings.

You can track them in the [all 20 achievements checklist](/potato-please/achievements).
