<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To reach the three main endings in **Potato Please**, complete the **60,000-potato Quota**, find the special potato delivered by parachute, and peel it to reveal the **Golden Key**. Use the key to unlock the **Iron Door**, reach the train, and choose a destination: **Line 01 (Northern Front)** for the Ministry ending, **Line 02 (Varenka)** for the Villagers ending, or **Line 03 (Western Industry)** for the Resistance ending. Completing all three route endings unlocks **Every Path Has a Price**. A reloadable save from before departure can let you choose the other routes without repeating the entire shift.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The final shipment does not have to go where the Ministry wants it. Once you unlock the Iron Door and reach the train, the control panel offers three destinations. The route you select determines the ending report, so check the line label before departure and keep a reloadable pre-departure state if you intend to see the other outcomes.

## How Many Endings Are There in Potato Please?

Potato Please has **three main train-route endings**. Each corresponds to a different destination and outcome.

## How Do You Unlock the Final Escape in Potato Please?

The Iron Door cannot be opened as part of the final escape until the remaining stock has been processed and the Quota reads **60,000 / 60,000**.

### What If the Quota Stops Progressing Near 100%?

If the counter has stopped short, unfinished potatoes may still be waiting in sacks or inside the processing line. Fix the blockage using the [machine and collection instructions](/potato-please/automation); the special delivery will not appear while the Quota remains incomplete.

### What happens at 60,000 potatoes?

When the Quota reaches 100%, the objective changes to finding a potato containing a key and proceeding to the Iron Door.

You do not need to search the entire original potato pile for the key before completing the Quota. The special potato appears during the final sequence.

</section>

<section id="hidden-key" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Do You Find the Hidden Golden Key?

After completing the Quota, look outside in the yard.

A **single potato arrives by parachute**. This is the special potato you need for the final escape.

The sequence is:

1. Finish the 60,000-potato Quota.
2. Wait for the objective to change.
3. Look for the parachute-delivered potato in the outdoor yard.
4. Pick up the special potato.
5. Peel it to reveal the **Golden Key**.
6. Collect the key and follow the objective toward the Iron Door.

![Peeling the special potato delivered by parachute after completing the final Quota in Potato Please.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-hidden-key.webp)

*Peel the parachute-delivered potato to obtain the Golden Key needed for the Iron Door.*

### Why can't you find the potato with the key?

Check the active objective first.

If the game is still asking you to finish the Quota, continue processing the remaining potatoes. Do not spend time searching the yard for the final key before that objective changes.

If the Quota is complete but you have not found the key, check the outdoor delivery area for the newly arrived potato.

The key is obtained by peeling that special potato, not by buying another upgrade at the terminal.

Once the key is collected, move to the Iron Door instead of continuing to purchase production equipment.

</section>

<section id="iron-door" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Open the Iron Door and Escape the Facility?

The **Golden Key** is used to unlock the heavy Iron Door inside the facility.

After finding the key:

1. Return from the outdoor yard to the facility.
2. Follow the objective toward the Iron Door.
3. Approach the reinforced door and use the Golden Key.
4. Go through the opened passage.
5. Continue toward the railway area.

![Iron Door showing the E Unlock interaction prompt in Potato Please.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-iron-door.webp)

*Approach the Iron Door and press E to unlock it after obtaining the key.*

### What happens after you open the door?

The escape sequence continues toward the rail yard.

You may encounter guards and gunfire as you move through this final area. Continue along the escape route toward the train rather than returning to the potato-processing equipment.

Reaching the railway is not the end of the sequence. You still need to choose a destination and depart.

If you are trying to unlock the separate **No Escape** achievement, that is a different objective from completing the normal train escape. Its unlock requirements belong in the [achievements guide](/potato-please/achievements).

</section>

## How Do You Choose Your Ending at the Train?

At the train's control panel, three labeled levers correspond to **Western Industry**, **Varenka**, and **Northern Front**.

The on-screen line label identifies the currently selected destination. This is the main decision point for the three train-route endings.

![Potato Please railway switch showing the final train destination choices.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-train-route-selection.webp)

*Choose the train's destination before departure. The selected railway line determines which main ending you receive.*

Select the lever for the destination shown in the table above, then check the on-screen line label before using the departure control. **Western Industry** is the control-panel label; the Resistance ending report calls the area the Western Industrial Zone.

The game presents the corresponding ending report after departure.

**Important:** If you want all three endings, preserve a recoverable save from before the route selection whenever the save system allows it. Do not assume that a completed shift will automatically return you to an editable train switch.

</section>

## How Do You Get the Resistance Ending (Traitor)?

At the train controls, select **Line 03 — Western Industry (to the Resistance)** and depart. The resulting Ministry Bulletin is titled **New Masters**.

![Resistance ending report after choosing Line 03 in Potato Please.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-resistance-ending.webp)

*Line 03 leads to the Resistance ending and unlocks the Traitor achievement.*

### What happens in the Resistance ending?

The Ministry is dissolved, and the People's Liberation Council takes power. However, the new government bans opposition, and the official record lists the Depot No. 5 worker as deceased.

This route unlocks the hidden Steam achievement **Traitor**.

You do not need to change the earlier Resistance-letter replies just to select Line 03 from a suitable pre-departure save.

</section>

<section id="villagers-ending" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Get The Last Harvest Ending?

At the same control panel, set **Line 02 — Varenka (to the villagers)** and depart. This produces **The Last Harvest**.

![The Last Harvest ending report after sending the train to Varenka.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-villagers-ending.webp)

*Line 02 sends the train toward Varenka and unlocks The Last Harvest.*

### What happens in the Villagers ending?

The train reaches Varenka, but nobody comes to collect the shipment. The potatoes rot on the tracks, and the Depot No. 5 worker is sentenced to death for treason.

Sending the shipment to the villagers does not prevent the punishment shown in the report. This departure unlocks **The Last Harvest**, separate from finishing the potato Quota.

</section>

<section id="front-line-ending" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Get the For the Motherland Ending?

Choose **Line 01 — Northern Front (to the Ministry)** and dispatch the train. The report that follows is **A Loyal Worker of the Motherland**.

![Ministry Bulletin showing A Loyal Worker of the Motherland after choosing the Northern Front route.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-front-line-ending.webp)

*Line 01 produces the Ministry-aligned ending, A Loyal Worker of the Motherland.*

### What happens in the Front Line ending?

The train reaches the Northern Front on time. The Ministry suppresses the uprising in the west, while Varenka receives no food that winter. This route unlocks **For the Motherland**. If it completes your set of three endings, **Every Path Has a Price** unlocks too.

## Can You Get All Three Endings Without Replaying the Whole Game?

Yes, the three main train endings can be completed by reloading a suitable save from before the final destination choice.

The important part is preserving that save **before departing on your first route**.

### Return to the train switch between endings

1. Reach the train controls and check whether you have a reloadable state from before departure.
2. Choose one destination and finish its ending.
3. Reload that earlier state, if available, and select a different line.
4. Repeat for the remaining destination, then check for **Every Path Has a Price**.

You can finish the routes in any order. This method depends on having a usable pre-departure save; finishing one route does not itself create a new train choice.

### What if the Iron Door is locked after an ending?

A completed shift may leave the Iron Door locked. Check **Load Shift** or any earlier available save state before starting over, and avoid overwriting a state that still lets you reach the railway. If no pre-departure state is available, the reload method cannot be used.

</section>

## Potato Please Ending Questions

### Do Earlier Letters or Resistance Reports Change the Train Endings?

The train destination determines which of the three main route endings you receive. You can select a different destination from the same earlier state without changing all your replies to Resistance messages or Pavel's letters.

Those earlier choices may still affect other story events. The trigger for **Your Own Executioner** remains unclear; see the [Potato Please achievements list](/potato-please/achievements) for the other remaining goals.

### Is there a best or true ending?

None of the three routes avoids a harsh consequence. The Ministry, villagers, and Resistance each receive a different outcome, so choose the destination whose story you want to see. For **Every Path Has a Price**, complete all three.

### What should you do after finishing all endings?

After completing all three train routes, check which Steam achievements remain locked.

Some require specific actions during the production shift instead of another ending choice.

Use the [Potato Please achievements checklist](/potato-please/achievements) to separate those remaining objectives from the completed ending routes.
