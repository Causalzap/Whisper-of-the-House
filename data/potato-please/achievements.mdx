Getting every achievement in Potato Please involves more than finishing the potato pile. Some unlock during the ordinary shift, while others require you to perform specific actions before automation takes over or to complete different endings.

The biggest distinction is between **total potatoes peeled** and **potatoes peeled by hand**. The game tracks these as different achievement requirements. You can make substantial progress with machines while still needing to peel hundreds of potatoes yourself.

The checklist below covers all 20 achievements. Mark each one after it unlocks, then use the relevant sections to find the next requirement.

## How Do You Unlock First Cut, One Last Puff, and Warmed Up?

These three achievements belong to the opening part of the game.

### First Cut — Peel Your First Potato

**First Cut** unlocks as soon as you peel your first potato with the starting knife. If you are unsure where to put the next peeled potatoes, follow the [opening workbench and chute steps](/potato-please#first-shift).

### One Last Puff — Clear Your Lungs

**One Last Puff** uses the achievement description **"Take a moment to clear your lungs."**

The worker has a smoking interaction during ordinary play. Use the available smoking action when the game presents it.

This is separate from purchasing Medication or improving the worker's stamina. Do not assume that buying a treatment automatically satisfies an achievement about taking a smoke break.

You can deal with this during the early shift rather than waiting until the production line is automated.

### Warmed Up — Finish the Opening Shift

**Warmed Up** unlocks after the opening work objectives are complete, not just after buying the Rusty Knife. Continue until the Journal marks the Warm-up Shift finished; the [starting-shift instructions](/potato-please#warm-up-shift) cover the earlier tasks.

## How Do You Unlock Good Boy and A Heart of Gold?

Both achievements use descriptions about kindness, but they should not be treated as the same interaction.

### Good Boy — Feed the Puppy

The **Good Boy** achievement is associated with helping the hungry puppy outside the facility.

During the shift, a puppy appears in the cold. The terminal provides a **Dog Food** purchase for **3 units of in-game currency**.

To unlock Good Boy:

1. Progress until the puppy appears outside.
2. Check the terminal for Dog Food.
3. Purchase the food.
4. Return to the puppy.
5. Approach it and use the **E** interaction to feed it.
6. Check for the Good Boy achievement.

![The puppy recovering and returning to work after being fed in Potato Please.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-good-boy-achievement.webp)

*After you feed the puppy, it recovers and starts helping with potatoes. This interaction unlocks Good Boy.*

Once the puppy is up and moving, leave it to help with potatoes while you continue the shift.

### A Heart of Gold — What Triggers It?

Steam describes **A Heart of Gold** as **"Even the smallest act of kindness matters."** Its exact trigger is unclear. Feeding the puppy unlocks **Good Boy**, so do not count that action as a guaranteed way to earn both achievements. Keep A Heart of Gold unchecked until it appears in your Steam achievements.

## How Do You Unlock the 500, 1,000, and 10,000 Potato Achievements?

There are four different peeling milestones, and the wording matters.

**Old School** and **These Hands Were Made for Peeling** require manual peeling. **A Thousand Peels** and **Ten Thousand** measure broader peeling progress.

### Old School — Peel 500 Potatoes by Hand

**Old School** unlocks at **500 hand-peeled potatoes**. Count potatoes you peel at the workbench, not those processed by machines. The same manual progress leads toward the 1,000-potato achievement below.

### These Hands Were Made for Peeling — Peel 1,000 by Hand

**These Hands Were Made for Peeling** raises that manual count to **1,000**. Keep using the hand-peeling bench even after machines become available; the hand-operated Crank Peel Machine should not be assumed to count toward a "by hand" requirement. For faster manual peeling, compare the [knife and Journal upgrades](/potato-please/automation#knife-skills).

### A Thousand Peels — Peel 1,000 by Hand or Machine

**A Thousand Peels** requires **1,000 potatoes peeled by hand or by machine**.

Unlike the manual achievements, its description explicitly allows either method.

Once you have machine processing available, continue completing the normal production objectives. Machine-peeled potatoes can contribute to this milestone.

There is no need to peel a separate 1,000 potatoes by hand solely for A Thousand Peels.

### Ten Thousand — Peel 10,000 Potatoes

**Ten Thousand** unlocks at **10,000 total peeled potatoes**. Manual and machine processing both advance the total; let the production line run while you work toward later objectives. If its throughput stalls, check the [machine troubleshooting steps](/potato-please/automation#automation-problems).

## How Do You Unlock Industrial Revolution and Fully Automated?

**Industrial Revolution — Auto Peel Machine.** Purchase the first motorized peeling machine when it becomes available and start machine peeling. You do not need a Claw Crane for this achievement.

**Fully Automated — Crane Autopilot.** After obtaining the Claw Crane, purchase **Crane Autopilot**. Its unlock is the achievement milestone; if the crane does not begin loading potatoes, follow the [Autopilot setup and controls](/potato-please/automation#claw-crane).

</section>

## How Do You Unlock Employee of the Month?

**Employee of the Month** appears when you reach the **Master Peeler** progression milestone. Work through the Journal's rank objectives, including the required earnings and peeling totals. Buying every optional upgrade is not the goal; check the rank tasks still open in your Journal if the achievement has not appeared.

## How Do You Unlock The Quota and No Escape?

These achievements involve two different situations: completing the assigned work and trying to leave while it is still unfinished.

### The Quota — Peel Every Last Potato

**The Quota** unlocks when the remaining production counter reaches **60,000 / 60,000**. It is separate from the train endings that follow. If the counter stops short, use the [final-Quota checks](/potato-please#quota) to find what remains unprocessed.

### No Escape — Attempt to Leave Before the Shift Is Finished

The hidden **No Escape** achievement has the description **"Your shift isn't over yet."**

Before the shift is complete, attempt to cross the guarded outer perimeter. Guard fire is triggered, and **No Escape** unlocks.

![Gunshot subtitle appearing during an attempted escape beyond the facility perimeter in Potato Please.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-no-escape-achievement.webp)

*Crossing the guarded boundary before finishing your shift triggers gunfire and the No Escape achievement.*

This is not the same as opening the Iron Door with the final Golden Key.

If you are attempting No Escape, do it during an unfinished shift rather than treating the normal ending route as its trigger.

The exact boundary interaction and resulting checkpoint behavior may depend on where you attempt to leave. Check the Steam achievement result before repeating the action.

Once you are ready to finish the actual escape, the [Potato Please endings guide](/potato-please/endings) covers the Golden Key, Iron Door, and train routes.

## How Do You Unlock Traitor, For the Motherland, The Last Harvest, and Every Path Has a Price?

At the final train controls, the chosen destination determines the corresponding achievement:

- **Traitor:** **Line 03 — Western Industry (to the Resistance)**; ending report: **New Masters**.
- **For the Motherland:** **Line 01 — Northern Front (to the Ministry)**; report: **A Loyal Worker of the Motherland**.
- **The Last Harvest:** **Line 02 — Varenka (to the villagers)**.
- **Every Path Has a Price:** Finish all three destinations, using a suitable pre-departure save to revisit the other routes if one is available.

For the Golden Key, Iron Door and departure controls, follow the [complete train-ending steps](/potato-please/endings).

## How Do You Get Your Own Executioner and Unauthorized Passenger?

### Your Own Executioner

Steam description: **"You taught him well."** The trigger is still unclear. Pavel's letters offer different responses, but no particular answer sequence is known to unlock this achievement. Choosing a strict reply or reporting Resistance messages should not be treated as a guaranteed solution.

### Unauthorized Passenger

Steam description: **"This is not an approved transport method."** An attempt to climb onto the Sack Cableway did not unlock it, so simply standing on a sack is not a reliable method. The correct position or interaction remains unknown. For ordinary Cableway operation, see the [loading instructions](/potato-please/automation#sack-cableway).

</section>

## What Is the Best Order to Finish the Remaining Achievements?

Check the achievements still marked incomplete before starting another shift. A missing hand-peeling milestone requires more work at the bench; a missing route ending requires a different train destination; and a missing machine milestone calls for the relevant purchase. Do not reset a finished production shift just because one of the unresolved hidden achievements is still locked.
