<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To automate potato peeling in **Potato Please**, progress from the **Rusty Knife** and **Steel Knife** to the **Kitchen Table**, then buy the **Crank Peel Machine** and **Auto Peel Machine**. Add the **Sack Gantry** to feed the machines, upgrade to the **Sack Cableway** for outdoor transport, and eventually purchase the **Claw Crane** and **Crane Autopilot**. Keep the peeling machines fast enough to process incoming potatoes; moving more sacks into a full machine bay will not solve a processing bottleneck.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A full sack beside an idle peeling drum is not useful until something delivers it to the machine. Likewise, a fast Gantry cannot clear a queue if the drums are already occupied. Before purchasing the next upgrade, look at which part of your operation is standing still: collection, delivery, or peeling.

## What Should You Upgrade First in Potato Please?

Follow the active work objectives rather than saving for a machine you cannot use yet.

A practical progression through the main equipment is:

1. **Rusty Knife and Steel Knife:** Complete the early tool objectives and make manual peeling more manageable.
2. **Journal Skills:** Improve stamina and hand control as you gain rank points.
3. **Kitchen Table:** Increase the workbench's handling capacity and add its Auto Launcher.
4. **Crank Peel Machine:** Begin processing potatoes through a machine instead of individually peeling every one.
5. **Auto Peel Machine:** Add motorized peeling capacity.
6. **Sack Gantry:** Move filled sacks into the processing area with less manual handling.
7. **Sack Cableway:** Improve transport from the outdoor pile to the bunker.
8. **Claw Crane and Crane Autopilot:** Automate the large-scale supply of potatoes.
9. **Potato Vacuum:** Use the late-game collection tool to help clear remaining loose potatoes.

Follow the next work objective rather than treating every optional purchase as a mandatory step.

These prices are shown at the corresponding purchase stages. They are not the total cost of fully upgrading each machine. Additional levels, supporting equipment, and later automation purchases require more money.

### Which upgrades can wait?

There are three purchases worth separating from your immediate production goals.

**Streak Bonus and Streak Clock** improve the reward side of manual peeling. They make more sense while you are still regularly peeling at the bench. Once machines handle most of your quota, compare their benefit with the cost of improving machine throughput.

**Carry Capacity** is useful when repeated trips between the pile and workstation are slowing you down. However, another small carrying upgrade becomes less important once sacks and transport machines begin doing that work.

**Winter Coat and Medication** address interruptions rather than potato processing speed. If coughing or extended exposure to the cold keeps breaking your work, resolving that interruption may be more useful than another minor blade upgrade.

The most useful purchase is usually the one that removes the action you currently repeat most often.

## Which Knife and Journal Skills Should You Upgrade?

The early objectives introduce the **Rusty Knife**, followed by an upgrade to the Rusty Knife and the purchase of the **Steel Knife**.

The first Steel Knife purchase appears after the objective to upgrade the Rusty Knife. You do not need to keep struggling with the starting blade once the next tool becomes available.

### How to spend your first rank points

Rank points are spent through the **Journal**. Several early skills affect the peeling interaction directly:

- **Stamina:** Reduces stamina drain while peeling and improves recovery between potatoes.
- **Auto Rotate:** Automatically rotates the potato while you peel, reducing manual repositioning. The current Journal interface shows this skill at Level 0/1.
- **Tremor Treatment:** Reduces hand tremor and drift while peeling, regardless of which knife you use. It can be upgraded through three levels.

![Potato Please Journal skills showing stamina, Auto Rotate, and hand-control upgrades.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-journal-skill.webp)

*Spend Journal points on the part of manual peeling that is causing the most difficulty rather than upgrading every skill evenly.*

If you are constantly waiting for stamina to return, improve stamina first.

If holding a stable cutting position is the main difficulty, Tremor Treatment helps with that specific problem.

Auto Rotate becomes useful when manually turning every potato interrupts your cutting rhythm. It is not necessarily the best first purchase for everyone: faster rotation also changes how you control the blade.

### When should you buy the Peeler?

The later **Peeler** is another manual tool option. It can make individual potatoes easier to process, but it does not replace the need for machine automation when thousands of potatoes remain.

Before investing heavily in manual blade upgrades, check whether your next objective is already asking you to move into the machine system.

A better knife can improve the next potato. A working machine changes how you handle the next thousand.

<section id="kitchen-table" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Kitchen Table and Crank Peel Machine?

The **Kitchen Table** is an important transition between manual peeling and machine processing.

Its upgrade description includes a larger bench, a deeper raw-potato bin, and an **Auto Launcher**. The Auto Launcher reduces the need to repeatedly stop and move potatoes out of the workstation.

Purchase the Kitchen Table from the automation options when the corresponding objective becomes available.

After buying it, return to the workstation and check that the new table and launcher are operating before continuing.

### Buy the Crank Peel Machine

The **Crank Peel Machine** is the next major step.

1. Complete the Kitchen Table objective and continue until the Crank Peel Machine becomes available.
2. Open the upgrade terminal and purchase the Crank Peel Machine.
3. Go to the installed machine inside the processing area.
4. Use its crank interaction to begin processing potatoes.
5. Watch the output before purchasing additional equipment.

In the PC controls shown during the early machine sequence, the interaction is **hold E to turn the crank**.

The main benefit is no longer having to peel each potato individually at the original workbench.

However, a hand-operated crank is not the same as a fully automated production line. You still spend time operating the machine.

That is why the next major purchase is the Auto Peel Machine.

</section>

<section id="auto-peel-machine" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Auto Peel Machine?

The **Auto Peel Machine** adds motorized peeling capacity.

Once it becomes available at the terminal, purchase the first unit and check that the machine actually begins processing potatoes.

The initial purchase is shown at **$280**.

This is also the point where you can begin working toward a setup that processes potatoes while you handle other tasks.

### How to make the machine more useful

The first motorized machine solves the peeling interaction, but potatoes still need to reach it.

That creates two separate questions:

- Can your existing processing equipment peel potatoes fast enough?
- Can you deliver enough potatoes to keep that equipment working?

If the machine frequently has nothing to process, improve how potatoes reach it.

If sacks are arriving faster than the machines can clear them, improve peeling capacity before purchasing more transport equipment.

Additional Auto Peel Machine upgrades and units become available as you progress. Inspect what the next upgrade actually adds instead of assuming it only increases the speed of the first machine.

In one later upgrade sequence, purchasing the next machine improvement added another processing unit.

### When should you buy Overclock?

**Overclock** makes every peeling drum run **twice as fast**. It is locked during earlier progression and becomes available later.

This is particularly useful when the Sack Gantry or Claw Crane delivers potatoes faster than the peeling machines can process them.

Before buying more transport upgrades, check whether Overclock is available. Doubling peeling speed can address the processing bottleneck directly, provided the machines are receiving enough potatoes to stay busy.

</section>

<section id="sack-gantry" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Use the Sack Gantry?

The **Sack Gantry** is an overhead transport system that carries filled sacks from its pickup location into the machine bay.

The initial purchase is shown at **$280**.

After purchasing it, the most important detail is where you leave each filled sack.

### Put sacks at the actual pickup spot

1. Fill a sack with potatoes using the available handling equipment.
2. Carry the filled sack toward the gantry's pickup area.
3. Place it inside the designated pickup location.
4. Let the gantry collect the sack and transport it toward the machines.
5. Confirm that the potatoes enter the processing system.

![Potato Please Sack Gantry collecting filled sacks from the marked pickup area.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-sack-gantry-pickup.webp)

*Place full sacks at the gantry's collection point. Leaving them elsewhere in the room does not establish a working pickup route.*

The Sack Gantry automatically takes each full sack to the **emptiest peeling machine** and pours the potatoes into it. You do not need to assign a machine manually.

If the gantry is not collecting anything, check whether the sack is inside the marked pickup area. Placing it elsewhere in the room may leave the gantry idle.

Further Gantry upgrades improve its travel, hoisting, and pouring speed. Upgrade it when sack delivery is slowing production, rather than when the peeling machines are already overloaded.

![Sack Gantry upgrade menu showing automatic delivery to the emptiest peeling machine and faster transport upgrades.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-sack-gantry-upgrades.webp)

*The Sack Gantry feeds the emptiest peeling machine automatically. Later levels increase its transport speed.*

### How to keep sacks moving

The **Packaging** upgrade unlocks the ability to sack potatoes directly from the pile by holding **E**. This makes it possible to prepare full sacks for the transport machinery without moving every potato individually.

The **Wheelbarrow** provides a separate way to carry multiple sacks. It is useful before the larger transport machines take over repeated deliveries.

A useful approach while the gantry is operating is to prepare the next load instead of standing beside it waiting for every individual sack.

However, do not create an enormous backlog without checking the peeling machines.

If the gantry is delivering sacks successfully but they accumulate inside the facility, transport is no longer your main problem.

The next improvement should target the machines processing those sacks.

</section>

<section id="sack-cableway" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Use the Sack Cableway?

The **Sack Cableway** extends the transportation system toward the outdoor potato pile.

Its purpose is different from the Sack Gantry:

- **Sack Gantry:** Carries filled sacks from a pickup point into the processing area.
- **Sack Cableway:** Transports sacks from the outdoor supply area toward the bunker.

This matters because the large outdoor pile eventually becomes the biggest source of repeated handling work.

### Get the Cableway working

Purchase the Sack Cableway once its objective appears, then locate its outdoor loading area.

Prepare a filled sack and bring it to the Cableway's loading position.

Place the load where the Cableway can accept it. Do not assume the machine can collect sacks from any point around the pile.

![Potato Please Sack Cableway with its outdoor loading and transport route.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-sack-cableway-loading.webp)

*Check the Cableway's loading position before assuming the transport system is broken.*

Once the first load moves, follow where it arrives and confirm that the rest of the processing system can receive it.

### What should you upgrade after the Cableway?

The Cableway can be upgraded, but faster transport is only valuable when the downstream system can handle additional potatoes.

If the bunker is already full of sacks, spending more on transport speed may increase the backlog rather than improve the amount processed.

If the machinery is waiting for potatoes and the outdoor supply is slow, improving the Cableway makes more sense.

The next major step is the Claw Crane, which reduces the need to prepare and move outdoor sacks manually.

</section>

<section id="claw-crane" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Claw Crane and Crane Autopilot?

The **Claw Crane** is one of the largest changes to the production system.

It takes potatoes directly from the outdoor pile and delivers large loads toward the processing hopper.

The Claw Crane purchase is shown at **$2,100**.

Unlike the Sack Gantry, the crane is designed to handle much larger quantities from the pile itself.

### How to operate the Claw Crane

After purchasing the crane:

1. Go to the crane controls.
2. Move the claw into position over the outdoor pile.
3. Lower the claw to collect potatoes.
4. Move the captured load toward the processing hopper.
5. Release the load above the hopper.
6. Check that the potatoes are delivered into the processing system.

The key action is **releasing the load over the hopper**, not simply collecting potatoes with the claw.

A successful pickup does not help production if the load is dropped in the wrong location.

### Unlock Crane Autopilot

The crane initially requires manual control, but **Crane Autopilot** lets it repeatedly collect potatoes from the outdoor pile and empty each load into the hopper without a driver.

After unlocking Autopilot, watch one complete collection and unloading cycle to confirm the potatoes reach the hopper.

You can still take manual control of the crane whenever needed. Additional Autopilot levels increase its operating speed, making later upgrades useful when the processing machines can handle more incoming potatoes.

![Crane Autopilot upgrade menu showing automatic hopper loading and additional speed upgrades.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-claw-crane-autopilot.webp)

*Crane Autopilot loads potatoes into the hopper automatically while preserving manual crane control.*

The change is substantial because you can now spend time on tasks that still require direct interaction instead of repeatedly driving the crane.

Once Autopilot is active, watch the bunker and peeling equipment.

A continuously operating crane can deliver potatoes faster than the processing system can clear them. If that happens, improve the peeling machines rather than trying to make the crane collect even faster.

</section>

## Is the Potato Vacuum Worth Buying?

The **Potato Vacuum** is a late-game handling tool that can collect and peel potatoes without sending each one through the main processing machines.

It occupies **tool slot 3** and holds up to **1,000 potatoes**, whether raw or already peeled.

- **Hold LMB:** Suck nearby potatoes into the vacuum tank.
- **Hold RMB:** Fire the stored potatoes back out in a peeled state.

![Potato Vacuum upgrade menu showing its 1,000-potato capacity and LMB/RMB controls.](/images/potato-please/potato-please-potato-vacuum.webp)

*The Potato Vacuum collects up to 1,000 raw or peeled potatoes and fires them back out peeled.*

This is especially useful when loose potatoes remain around the yard or when manually transporting small batches would slow down cleanup. Unlike a normal transport upgrade, the Vacuum also handles peeling during its output interaction.

### When should you buy it?

The Vacuum makes the most sense when:

- You already have substantial processing capacity.
- Scattered potatoes are taking more effort to collect than large loads.
- The remaining supply is difficult to handle efficiently with sacks or the crane.

The Vacuum is a useful supplement to the automated production line rather than a reason to stop using it.

The Claw Crane can continue delivering large loads into the hopper while you use the Vacuum to handle smaller groups of potatoes directly. Its 1,000-potato capacity also makes it useful for clearing scattered potatoes near the end of the Quota.

### What if potatoes are stuck or scattered?

The game also provides a **repile interaction** for loose or displaced potatoes.

An on-screen prompt identifies **hold R to repile** during a later cleanup sequence.

Use the interaction when the game offers it, then check where the potatoes have been returned before continuing with the crane or Vacuum.

Do not rely entirely on the crane for the very last potatoes. If the objective stops advancing near completion, look for small remaining groups and scattered potatoes that have not entered the processing system.

Once the overall Quota is complete, the next task moves into the final escape sequence. Follow the [hidden key and endings guide](/potato-please/endings) for that route.

</section>

## Why Is My Potato Automation Not Working?

Trace one load from the outdoor pile to the peeling drum. The point where it stops tells you what to check next:

- **Crank Peel Machine does nothing:** Stand at the crank and hold **E**; this model still needs manual input.
- **Sack Gantry ignores a full sack:** Move the sack into the marked pickup area, not beside it.
- **Cableway leaves sacks behind:** Check the outdoor loading position and confirm the load reaches the bunker.
- **Claw Crane collects but does not supply:** Make sure the load is released over the hopper. With Autopilot, watch one full loading cycle.
- **Sacks accumulate indoors:** The peeling drums are the bottleneck; another transport upgrade will not empty them.

Coughing or cold exposure is a separate interruption. Check Medication or the Winter Coat in the Character upgrades if the worker cannot stay on task.

## Which Upgrades Matter Most in the Late Game?

By the time the Claw Crane and Autopilot are working, the central problem has changed.

You no longer need to solve every individual peeling interaction. You need to keep the entire processing system moving until the remaining Quota is finished.

Choose your next purchase based on the current bottleneck:

- **Peeling machines are overloaded:** Check Overclock or add processing capacity.
- **Machines are waiting for potatoes:** Improve sack delivery or crane supply.
- **The crane still needs manual control:** Unlock Crane Autopilot.
- **Loose potatoes remain around the yard:** Use the Potato Vacuum for direct collection and peeled output.
- **The Quota is nearly complete but progress has stopped:** Check for remaining potatoes, blocked machinery, and the repile interaction before buying anything else.

One achievement-related distinction is worth remembering: automated peeling does not replace the requirement for achievements that specifically say **by hand**. If you are pursuing those milestones, consult the [Potato Please achievements checklist](/potato-please/achievements) before relying entirely on machines.

The goal of automation is not to purchase every possible upgrade. It is to eliminate the repeated work that is slowing down your current objective.

Once the production line can keep receiving, processing, and clearing potatoes without constant manual intervention, you can focus on completing the Quota and preparing for the final part of the game.
