**The Asterkarn Incident** sends me northwest from Fort Ikon into the
Frostveil Highlands to find a missing Black Legion scouting party.

The route itself is straightforward once I stop following every new side
quest marker: I activate the first Frostveil Riftgate, continue north, turn
east in the snow, defeat Garmir the Lost, then speak to the Scouts and
Ulfgarn in that order.

<h2 id="quest-start">
  How The Asterkarn Incident Starts
</h2>

After completing **Trouble in Asterkarn**, I speak with **Scout Avirs** at
Fort Ikon.

The quest journal explains that Inquisitor Creed cannot spare more troops and
that the missing Black Legion scouting party entered Asterkarn. My first
objective is to leave Fort Ikon and explore the Frostveil Highlands.

<h3 id="first-riftgate">
  Activate the Frostveil Riftgate Before Exploring
</h3>

The Riftgate is the best checkpoint on this opening route.

I activate it even when the enemies nearby are easy. The useful part is not
only death recovery: I can return to town, clear inventory, change components,
or switch skills without repeating the Fort Ikon approach.

I do not chase Rock Feathers or enter the northwest cave until this Riftgate
is active.

<h2 id="garmir">
  How I Beat Garmir the Lost
</h2>

Garmir waits near the end of the eastern route.

Before engaging, I clear nearby normal enemies and Hero packs. The fight is
far less reliable when another pack enters the arena from behind.

<div className="my-6 rounded-xl border border-rose-200 bg-rose-50 p-5 text-sm leading-7 text-slate-700">
  <strong className="text-rose-900">Before Garmir:</strong>{" "}
  I check Fire and Cold Resistance, keep healing available, and avoid spending
  my movement skill before his first major attack.
</div>

The two attacks I watch most closely are:

- a frontal fire breath or fire cone;
- ground attacks that create spreading lines of ice.

For melee, I stay near Garmir's side rather than directly in front of his
head. When the fire begins, I move around his body instead of retreating
through the whole cone.

For ranged characters, I keep enough distance to see the ice pattern forming,
but I do not stand at the edge of the arena where terrain can block my escape.

I attack between the telegraphed patterns and reposition when the ground
becomes crowded. I do not buy a new weapon solely for this encounter when the
build already kills the Frostveil enemies at a reasonable pace; clearing the
extra pack and fixing a weak Fire or Cold resistance is usually the more useful
change.

<h2 id="after-garmir">
  What I Do After Defeating Garmir
</h2>

Garmir's death does not finish the quest by itself.

I complete two conversations:

<ol className="my-6 list-decimal space-y-3 pl-6">

    <strong>Speak to the rescued Scouts.</strong>{" "}

    enemies first so combat does not interrupt the interaction.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Speak to Ulfgarn, Son of Garn.</strong>{" "}
    I continue his dialogue and ask about Kurnhold. This completes The

If an NPC does not respond, I do not immediately reload the entire area. I:

1. clear nearby enemies;
2. wait for combat status to end;
3. move away and return;
4. leave wereform if I am transformed;
5. try the interaction again.

I treat leaving wereform as a troubleshooting step, not as a universal quest
requirement.

<h3 id="rewards">
  The Asterkarn Incident Rewards
</h3>

Completing the final Ulfgarn conversation gives:

- quest completion experience;
- **+100 Kurn Reputation**;
- **Frozen Aster ×1**;
- and the next main quest, **Journey to Kurnhold**.

The Frozen Aster is a distinct named reward, not one of the Rock Feathers
found during the optional side quest.

<h2 id="optional-quests">
  Optional Frostveil Quests I Keep Separate
</h2>

Two optional objectives can appear before I reach Garmir.

<h3 id="hearth-in-the-blizzard">
  Hearth in the Blizzard
</h3>

This branch sends me northwest to hunt a large beast for meat. The cave and
its rewards are optional and do not advance the missing-scout objective.

I either complete it deliberately or leave it for later. I do not continue
searching northwest after the cave ends while expecting to find Garmir.

<h3 id="feathers-of-a-bird">
  Feathers of a Bird and Rock Feathers
</h3>

**Feathers of a Bird** requires three Rock Feathers found around the broader
Frostveil route.

These feathers are not required for The Asterkarn Incident, and a Rock Feather
nest is not evidence that I am moving toward the Scouts.

<h2 id="faq">
  The Asterkarn Incident FAQ
</h2>

### Does the quest marker show the route from Fort Ikon?

No. The objective remains broad until I move close to the missing Scouts. I
navigate by leaving Fort Ikon northwest, activating the Frostveil Riftgate,
continuing north, and then turning east in the snow.

### Can I skip Hearth in the Blizzard and Feathers of a Bird?

Yes. Both are optional side quests. Neither is required to defeat Garmir,
rescue the Scouts, speak with Ulfgarn, or unlock Journey to Kurnhold.

### Does the route change on Normal, Elite, or Ultimate?

The main locations and conversation order remain the same. Difficulty changes
enemy scaling and how dangerous Garmir feels, but not the north-then-east
route or the required Scout and Ulfgarn interactions.
