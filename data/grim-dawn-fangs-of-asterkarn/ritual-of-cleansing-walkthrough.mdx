**Ritual of Cleansing** splits into three material quests, and none of the
required items are found by searching more of Asterkarn.

The real blockers are usually more specific: the Tomb of the Eldritch Sun is
still locked on the current difficulty, the World's Tear remains sealed after
only two Blood Basins, Essence of Ugdenbog is stuck at 1 of 2, or Bargoll is
hidden behind a path that barely looks open on the map.

</h2>

Ritual of Cleansing becomes available after **Source of Corruption**.

Inside Kurnhold, I speak with **Skald Magwyn**, then accept all three branch
quests from his apprentices before opening the Rift map. The branches can be
completed independently, but taking all three at once saves an unnecessary
return to Kurnhold.

<h3 id="essence-of-ugdenbog">
  How I find the two Essence of Ugdenbog
</h3>

The two Ugdenbog essences are tied to the smaller yellow Bargoll-like targets
used by the **Secrets of Ugdenbog** objective. I check the enemy name and the
quest marker instead of killing every ordinary Bog Golem I see.

My first sweep starts at the **Barrowholm Rift**:

1. I leave Barrowholm and move south into Ugdenbog.
2. I clear useful side branches instead of following only the widest road.
3. I watch for the quest marker when I approach a valid target.
4. After the first essence, I keep moving into a different branch.
5. I do not circle the same clearing expecting the second target to appear beside the first.

If that sweep leaves the counter at **1 of 2**, I do not keep searching the
same few screens. I start a second pass from **Coven&apos;s Refuge** and work
toward the eastern side of Ugdenbog.

<h3 id="essence-of-bargoll">
  Where I find Essence of Bargoll
</h3>

The single Essence of Bargoll comes from **Bargoll, the Mouldering Mound**.

In the version I tested, Bargoll was not roaming between several old guide
locations. I found him in a concealed pocket south-east of the **Ugdenbog
Rift**.

My route is:

1. Travel to the Ugdenbog Rift.
2. Move south and stay near the eastern edge.
3. Continue toward the nearby bridge.
4. Before committing to the bridge route, check the swamp wall for a narrow hidden opening.
5. Follow that opening into the off-map pocket.
6. Kill Bargoll and confirm the Essence of Bargoll objective advances.

The hidden entrance is the real blocker. It looks like part of the swamp edge,
and the boss area appears outside the normal walkable map. If I reach the
bridge without finding the opening, I turn around immediately and trace the
eastern wall instead of searching the western half of Ugdenbog.

<h3 id="essences-not-dropping">
  What I do when the Ugdenbog essences are not dropping
</h3>

I use the quest counter to decide what to do next:

- **0 of 2 Essence of Ugdenbog:** I confirm Secrets of Ugdenbog is active, then begin a full Barrowholm-side sweep.
- **1 of 2 Essence of Ugdenbog:** I leave the first clearing and search another branch or restart from Coven&apos;s Refuge.
- **2 of 2 Essence of Ugdenbog but no Essence of Bargoll:** I stop hunting smaller golems and go directly to Bargoll&apos;s hidden pocket.
- **Bargoll is not at an old marked location:** I use the hidden path south-east of the Ugdenbog Rift rather than checking legacy random-spawn maps.
- **A correct-looking yellow target does not advance the counter:** I continue the sweep. I do not assume every similar golem is a guaranteed quest drop.

Once all three essences are collected, I return them to **Koryn Earthurn**.

<h2 id="best-order">
  The material order I use
</h2>

The branches can be completed in any order. When every destination is already
open, I normally use this order:

1. Embers of the First Flame.
2. Ashen Heart of the Void.
3. Essence of Ugdenbog and Essence of Bargoll.

The Ember is a direct interaction. The Heart follows a fixed three-Basin route
and a boss. Ugdenbog is the least predictable because the two smaller targets
require a broader search, so I leave it until last when I want a smooth full
quest clear.

I change that order when an older route is locked:

- If the **Tomb of the Eldritch Sun** is closed, I complete Ugdenbog and the Heart first.
- If I do not have three Blood of Ch&apos;hon, I complete the Ember and Ugdenbog branches before entering the Black Citadel.
- I do not return to Kurnhold after every item unless I am already passing through town; otherwise I collect the remaining materials first.

<h2 id="embers-first-flame">
  Where I find the Embers of the First Flame
</h2>

The Embers are inside the **Tomb of the Eldritch Sun**. They are not on the
open Remnants of Korvan City map and they are not anywhere in Asterkarn.

When the Tomb Rift is available, I use it and head north into the large
circular room. I move toward the section that projects inward toward the
center, clear the enemies around the marked altar, and interact with it.

I do not search every floor of the Tomb. Once I am in the correct circular
room, the objective is a short direct interaction.

<h3 id="tomb-locked">
  What I do when the Tomb route is locked
</h3>

A blocked Tomb route means Forgotten Gods has not progressed far enough on the
current difficulty.

I continue the Forgotten Gods main story through the Korvan areas and the
Astral Fields. Searching Kurnhold, the Dread Wastes, or another Fangs of
Asterkarn cave will not open this branch.

<h2 id="ashen-heart">
  How I get the Ashen Heart of the Void
</h2>

The Heart branch starts in the **Dread Wastes** and ends inside the
**World&apos;s Tear**.

I travel to the Dread Wastes Rift near **Tamarog, the Black Citadel**, then
enter the nearby Chthonian structure. The descent stays sealed until all three
Blood Basins have been activated.

<h3 id="blood-basins">
  The three Blood Basin route
</h3>

Each Basin consumes one **Blood of Ch&apos;thon**, so three are required. I carry
at least three before entering; when I have only one or two, I delay this branch
instead of reaching the final sealed route and backtracking through the whole
structure.

My route is:

1. Enter the Black Citadel structure and take the eastern branch.
2. Offer one Blood of Ch&apos;thon at the first Basin.
3. Move northwest through the interior to the second Basin.
4. Continue southwest into the next section.
5. Cross the wooden bridge and keep moving southwest.
6. Activate the third Basin.
7. Return toward the newly opened descent and enter the World&apos;s Tear.

<h3 id="worlds-tear">
  How I move through the World's Tear
</h3>

Inside the World&apos;s Tear, I advance generally north and northeast toward
the final boss area.

I clear Hero enemies near narrow passages instead of dragging them into
Naddo&apos;s room. Pulling one extra Hero pack into the boss fight creates a
bigger problem than Naddo alone.

Before entering the final area, I wait until:

- no pack is still following me;
- my movement skill is available;
- my healing is ready;
- and the resistance being punished by the dungeon is not badly undercapped.

I do not buy a new weapon only because Naddo feels dangerous. When normal
enemies already die at a reasonable pace, cleaning up the approach and fixing a
defensive gap helps more than a small sheet-DPS increase.

<h3 id="naddo">
  How I beat Naddo the Anointed
</h3>

Naddo forced me to react to three attacks:

- a large spreading ground effect;
- a rapid cone of red projectiles;
- and a leap or forward strike that closes distance.

I move sideways across the projectile cone instead of backing directly away.
When the large ground effect expands, I stop attacking and move to a clean
section of the room.

I also save my movement skill during the opening seconds. Spending it too early
can leave me without an escape when the leap and ground effect overlap.

After the boss dies, I pick up the **Ashen Heart of the Void** before using the
exit Rift. Killing Naddo without collecting the Heart does not complete
Witra&apos;s branch.

<h2 id="finish">
  How I finish Ritual of Cleansing
</h2>

Having every material in the inventory does not close the main quest.

I make three separate deliveries:

1. Ashen Heart of the Void to Witra.
2. Embers of the First Flame to Talgir.
3. Two Essence of Ugdenbog and one Essence of Bargoll to Koryn.

I check the tracker after each conversation. If one branch remains active, I
return to that apprentice instead of repeatedly speaking with Magwyn.

After all three branch quests close, I speak with **Skald Magwyn** and complete
the final Ritual of Cleansing dialogue.

The next main quest is **Ascend the Roof of the World**, which sends me north
from the Dread Wastes toward the Aurora Peaks.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Ritual of Cleansing stuck fixes
</h2>

### No material marker appears in Asterkarn

I open the world Rift map. The three branches point to Ugdenbog, the Tomb of
the Eldritch Sun, and the Dread Wastes rather than another local Asterkarn cave.

### Essence of Ugdenbog is stuck at 1 of 2

I stop circling the first clearing. I continue into another Ugdenbog branch or
start a new sweep from Coven&apos;s Refuge.

### Bargoll is missing from an old guide location

I use the concealed path south-east of the Ugdenbog Rift. In the version I
tested, Bargoll was in that hidden pocket rather than rotating among the old
random locations.

### The World's Tear remains sealed

I confirm that I spent three Blood of Ch&apos;hon at three separate Blood Basins.
When only one or two were activated, the descent stayed closed.

### All materials are collected but the quest remains active

I check all three apprentice branches. After each turn-in is closed, I return
to Skald Magwyn for the final dialogue.

<h2 id="faq">
  Ritual of Cleansing FAQ
</h2>

### Do the older expansion areas need to be unlocked on my current difficulty?

Yes. The quest can point to Ugdenbog or the Tomb of the Eldritch Sun even when
that route is still closed on the current character and difficulty. I continue
the relevant Ashes of Malmouth or Forgotten Gods progression rather than
searching Asterkarn for an alternate entrance.

### Does every yellow Bog Golem drop Essence of Ugdenbog?

No reliable rule in my run supported that. I followed the quest marker and
checked the smaller Bargoll-like targets, but one apparent target did not
advance the objective. I kept searching other branches until the counter
updated.

### Can I return one ritual material before collecting the other two?

Yes. Each apprentice controls a separate branch. I only return early when I am
already near Kurnhold or need to clear the tracker; otherwise I collect the
remaining materials first.

### Are the two Root targets beside Bargoll?

I do not plan around that. Bargoll is reached through the hidden pocket, while
the two Ugdenbog essence targets require a wider swamp sweep.
