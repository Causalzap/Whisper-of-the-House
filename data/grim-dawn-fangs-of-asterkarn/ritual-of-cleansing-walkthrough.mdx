**Ritual of Cleansing** is not a single material hunt inside Asterkarn. It
splits into three connected quests that send me back to the Dread Wastes,
the Tomb of the Eldritch Sun, and Ugdenbog.

The main trap is assuming that a missing objective marker means I should
explore more of Asterkarn. When a material has no local marker, I open the
world Rift map and check whether the required older expansion area is
available on my current character and difficulty.

<h2 id="before-you-start">
  What I check before leaving Kurnhold
</h2>

Ritual of Cleansing becomes available after **Source of Corruption**.

Inside Kurnhold, I speak with **Skald Magwyn**. He explains that Beronath may
still be saved, but the ritual requires materials associated with three
different magical traditions.

Magwyn has assigned one material search to each of his apprentices.

Before leaving Kurnhold, I confirm that:

- all three branch quests are visible in the tracker;
- the Dread Wastes and Ugdenbog are available on this difficulty;

every late-campaign route is open on the character and difficulty I am

<h2 id="best-order">
  The order I use for the three materials
</h2>

The branches can be completed in any order. When every destination is already
unlocked, I use this sequence:

<ol className="my-5 list-decimal space-y-2 pl-6">
  <li>Embers of the First Flame.</li>
  <li>Ashen Heart of the Void.</li>
  <li>Essence of Ugdenbog and Essence of Bargoll.</li>
</ol>

The Ember is a direct interaction once the Tomb route is available. The
World&apos;s Tear follows a fixed ritual and boss route. I leave Ugdenbog until
last because finding the two Root of Bargoll elites is the least predictable
part.

If the Tomb is still locked, I change the order immediately. I complete the
Heart and Ugdenbog branches first, then continue Forgotten Gods until the Tomb
route opens. Searching another FoA cave does not bypass the old campaign
requirement.

<h2 id="embers-first-flame">
  Embers of the First Flame in the Tomb of the Eldritch Sun
</h2>

The Embers are inside the **Tomb of the Eldritch Sun**. They are not on the
open Remnants of Korvan City map and they are not located anywhere in
Asterkarn.

When the Tomb Rift is available, I use it and head north into the large
circular room. I move toward the section that projects inward toward the
center and interact with the marked flame altar.

I do not need to search every floor of the Tomb.

<h3 id="tomb-locked">
  What I do when the Tomb route is locked
</h3>

A blocked Tomb route means Forgotten Gods has not progressed far enough on
the current difficulty.

I continue its main quest through the Korvan areas and the Astral Fields.
Searching Kurnhold, the Dread Wastes, or another Fangs of Asterkarn cave will
not open this objective.

<h2 id="ashen-heart">
  How to get the Ashen Heart of the Void
</h2>

The Heart branch starts in the **Dread Wastes** and ends inside the
**World&apos;s Tear**.

I travel to the Dread Wastes Rift near **Tamarog, the Black Citadel**, then
enter the nearby Chthonian structure. The descent remains sealed until I
complete all three Blood Basin interactions.

<h3 id="blood-basins">
  How to activate the three Blood Basins
</h3>

Each Basin consumes one **Blood of Ch&apos;thon**, so I carry at least three
before entering the structure.

My route is:

<ol className="my-5 list-decimal space-y-2 pl-6">
  <li>Enter the Black Citadel structure and take the eastern branch.</li>
  <li>Offer Blood of Ch&apos;thon at the first Basin.</li>
  <li>Move northwest through the interior to reach the second Basin.</li>
  <li>Continue southwest into the next section.</li>
  <li>Cross the wooden bridge and keep moving southwest.</li>
  <li>Activate the third Basin.</li>
  <li>Return toward the newly opened route and descend into the World&apos;s Tear.</li>
</ol>

If the entrance remains closed, I do not keep clicking the same door. I
retrace the interior and check for a missed quest marker.

If I run out of Blood of Ch&apos;thon, I clear the Chthonian enemies inside and
around the structure before returning to town. The missing material may drop
along the route I am already using.

<h3 id="worlds-tear">
  How I move through the World&apos;s Tear
</h3>

The World&apos;s Tear is longer and more dangerous than the Ember branch. I
advance generally north and northeast, then work toward the final boss area.

I clear Hero enemies around narrow passages instead of dragging them deeper
into the dungeon. Pulling another Hero pack into Naddo&apos;s room creates more
danger than fighting Naddo alone.

Before entering the final area, I make sure:

- no Hero or normal pack is still following me;
- my healing and movement skills are available;
- and the dungeon is not exposing a badly undercapped resistance.

I do not buy a new weapon merely because Naddo feels dangerous. When my damage
against normal enemies is already sufficient, removing the extra Hero pack or
fixing the defense being punished gives me a more reliable result than a
small sheet-DPS increase.

<h3 id="naddo">
  How I beat Naddo the Anointed
</h3>

Naddo forced me to react to three main attacks:

- a large spreading ground effect;
- a rapid cone of red projectiles;
- a leap or forward strike that closes distance quickly.

I move sideways across the projectile cone rather than backing directly away
from it. When the large ground effect expands, I stop attacking and move to a
clean section of the room.

I also preserve my movement skill during the opening seconds. Using it
immediately can leave me without an escape when Naddo combines the leap with
the ground effect.

After the boss dies, I pick up the **Ashen Heart of the Void** before using
the exit Rift. Killing Naddo without collecting the Heart does not complete
Witra&apos;s branch.

<h3 id="root-of-bargoll">
  How I find the two Root of Bargoll elites
</h3>

For the two Roots, I start at the **Barrowholm Rift** and explore south through
Ugdenbog.

I check enemy names instead of killing every creature that resembles a Bog
Golem. In my run, a quest marker appeared once I moved close enough to a
required Root of Bargoll.

After killing the first Root, I continue into another branch. I do not circle
the same clearing expecting the second elite to appear beside it.

My search rule is:

- keep moving while useful branches remain unexplored;
- watch for a nearby quest marker;
- move into another section after finding the first Root;
- and reset the session only after completing a broad sweep.

If the Barrowholm route produces only one Root, I begin another pass from
**Coven&apos;s Refuge** and work toward the eastern side of Ugdenbog.

<h3 id="bargoll-location">
  Bargoll&apos;s fixed area and hidden entrance
</h3>

Bargoll is no longer a multi-location random boss.

I start at the **Ugdenbog Rift**, travel south, and remain close to the eastern
edge of the map. I look for a concealed side passage leading away from the
normal road before the nearby bridge.

The opening leads into an unmarked pocket containing **Bargoll, the Mouldering
Mound**.

The route is easy to pass because the entrance blends into the swamp edge. If
I reach the bridge without finding it, I backtrack along the eastern wall
instead of searching the western half of Ugdenbog.

This objective is separate from the Root search:

- Root of Bargoll supplies the two Essence of Ugdenbog;
- Bargoll supplies the single Essence of Bargoll;
- Bargoll has a fixed location;
- the two Root targets require the wider search.

Older guides that show several possible Bargoll positions are using the
previous spawn system.

After obtaining all three essences, I return them to **Koryn Earthurn**.

<h2 id="finish">
  How I finish Ritual of Cleansing
</h2>

Having the three material sets in my inventory does not complete the main
quest.

I make three separate deliveries:

1. Ashen Heart of the Void to Witra;
2. Embers of the First Flame to Talgir;
3. two Essence of Ugdenbog and one Essence of Bargoll to Koryn.

I check the tracker after each conversation. If one branch remains active, I
return to that apprentice rather than repeatedly speaking to Magwyn.

After all three branch quests close, I return to **Skald Magwyn** and complete
the final Ritual of Cleansing dialogue.

The next main quest is **Ascend the Roof of the World**, which sends me north
from the Dread Wastes toward the Aurora Peaks.

<h2 id="faq">
  Ritual of Cleansing FAQ
</h2>

### Do the older expansion areas need to be unlocked on my current difficulty?

Yes. The quest can point to Ugdenbog or the Tomb of the Eldritch Sun even when
the current character has not yet opened the complete route on that
difficulty. Continue the relevant Ashes of Malmouth or Forgotten Gods
campaign progression.

### Can I return one ritual material before collecting the other two?

Yes. Each apprentice controls a separate branch. I can deliver the Heart,
Embers, or essences as soon as that branch is complete and then continue the
remaining searches.

### Can the two Root of Bargoll enemies spawn beside Bargoll?

Do not rely on that. Bargoll is the fixed boss objective, while the two Root
of Bargoll elites require a broader Ugdenbog search. Treat them as three
separate targets.
