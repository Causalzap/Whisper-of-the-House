**Grim Dawn: Fangs of Asterkarn** begins northwest of Fort Ikon and adds the
Frostveil Highlands, the Kurn people, the Berserker mastery, Werewolf and
Wereraven transformations, Ascendant mechanics, item Ascension, and Awakened
Epic recipes.

I use this Hub as a route map for the expansion. It shows where to begin,
which detailed guide matches the current problem, and how the story, build,
and endgame systems connect without repeating every step from the dedicated
walkthroughs.

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How to Start Fangs of Asterkarn
</h2>

I begin at **Fort Ikon** and use the northwest expansion route.

I do not need to create a Berserker or restart the campaign. An existing
character can enter the new story as long as the character can reach Fort Ikon
and the required expansions are installed.

Before leaving, I:

- repair equipment;
- clear inventory space;
- check the main resistances on the current difficulty;
- assign a movement skill;
- and confirm that the correct expansion quest is selected.

The first Frostveil Riftgate is the opening checkpoint. I activate it before
taking long side paths so I can return to town or recover from a death without
repeating the Fort Ikon approach.

<h2 id="kurnhold-systems">
  Kurnhold and Endgame Systems
</h2>

Kurnhold introduces several related services, but they are not the same
interaction:

- the **Altar of Ascension** controls available Ascendant mechanics;
- **Jornn the Runekeeper** performs the initial item Ascension;
- an **Inventor** alters parts of an already Ascended item;
- **Elderveil** contains Elder Noltukari and the separate Runed Orb service.

The most important equipment rule is:

> **Awaken the Epic first, then Ascend the finished Legendary.**

Awakening consumes the original Epic and creates a new item. Ascending the blue
Epic first can waste the Ascended Affix when that base item is replaced.

<h3 id="journey-to-kurnhold">
  Journey to Kurnhold
</h3>

Journey to Kurnhold begins after I speak with Ulfgarn at the end of The
Asterkarn Incident.

It continues the main story away from the opening Frostveil route and toward
the Kurn settlement. I keep it selected because optional objectives in the
surrounding region can replace its marker in the tracker.

Reaching Kurnhold opens the next stage of the expansion, but the Altar and
late crafting services still require additional local progression.

<h3 id="hearth-in-the-blizzard">
  Hearth in the Blizzard
</h3>

Hearth in the Blizzard is an optional Frostveil quest that sends me northwest
toward a cave and a large beast.

The opening main quest eventually turns east toward Garmir and the missing
Scouts. I complete the northwest branch deliberately or return to it after
advancing the story rather than treating it as the only route forward.

<h3 id="feathers-of-a-bird">
  Feathers of a Bird and Rock Feathers
</h3>

Feathers of a Bird requires three Rock Feathers found around the broader
Frostveil region.

A Rock Feather nest belongs to the optional quest and does not indicate that
the missing Scouts are nearby. I collect the feathers only when the optional
quest is active, and I do not delay the route to Kurnhold solely to search
every unexplored branch.

<h3 id="frozen-aster">
  Frozen Aster
</h3>

Frozen Aster is a named expansion resource and can appear as a quest reward.

I keep at least the first copy in storage. Until I need it for a confirmed
recipe or quest, I do not sell it simply because its later use is not
immediately visible.

<h2 id="returning-player-checklist">
  Returning-Player Checklist
</h2>

An older character may enter the expansion with enough damage but outdated
defenses or sustain.

Before moving deeper into Asterkarn, I check:

- important resistances on the current difficulty;
- Health and Defensive Ability;
- empty component slots;
- movement-skill availability;
- Energy or resource sustain;
- devotion bindings;
- and whether old equipment is being kept only for sheet damage.

I adjust the specific failure instead of rebuilding the entire character:

- frequent enemy critical hits suggest a Defensive Ability problem;
- one damage type removing most of the Health bar suggests a resistance gap;
- an empty resource bar suggests sustain;
- slow bosses with fast normal clearing suggest Offensive Ability, resistance
  reduction, or devotion uptime;
- repeated deaths after changing Altar settings suggest the enabled mechanics
  are too aggressive for the current route.

<h2 id="faq">
  Grim Dawn: Fangs of Asterkarn FAQ
</h2>

### Do I need to start a new character?

No. An existing character that can reach Fort Ikon can begin the expansion.
A fresh character is useful when I specifically want to level Berserker from
the beginning, but it is not required for access.

### Do I need Ashes of Malmouth and Forgotten Gods?

Yes. Fangs of Asterkarn requires both expansions. Some later quests also
return to their campaign regions, so those routes may need to be unlocked on
the current character and difficulty.

### Where does Fangs of Asterkarn start?

The story begins from Fort Ikon and heads northwest into the Frostveil
Highlands.

### Are Hearth in the Blizzard and Feathers of a Bird required?

No. Both are optional Frostveil quests. They can be completed during
exploration or left until after the main eastern route is secure.

### Is full Ascendant Mode required for every Ascension feature?

No. Full Ascendant Mode is the Level 100 Ultimate setting. Individual Altar
mechanics and related services can be used separately as they become
available.

### Should I Ascend an Epic before Awakening it?

No. Awakening consumes the original Epic and produces a Legendary. Awaken the
Epic first, then Ascend the finished Legendary.
