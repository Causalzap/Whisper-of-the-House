<h2 id="dark-waters-route">
  Dark Waters route from Takali Canal to Tel-Rea
</h2>

**Dark Waters** sends Zero Company to Cortilius after Republic Intelligence
learns that Fathom is moving into the caverns beneath the city.

The route is fairly linear once I understand what the mission expects, but two
moments can make it feel as though progression has broken.

The first is at **Takali Canal**.

The objective points me toward the lock, but the tunnel I need is hidden under
the water. If I keep searching nearby streets for a separate cave entrance, I
am moving away from the solution.

The second happens much later when I finally get a clear view of **Fathom**.

Hawks does not fire.

That is also intentional.

My route is:

**reach the distal canal lock → secure the lock → drain the canal → enter the
tunnels → follow the downed guards → reach Fathom's initiation → wait through
the blocked shot → hold the Coil while the converts escape → return to the
surface → meet Tel-Rea.**

Dark Waters has substantial combat both before and after the Fathom scene, so I
do not treat the lock fight as though I am already near the end of the Mission.

<h2 id="reach-canal-lock">
  Reach the distal Takali Canal lock first
</h2>

From the opening area, I keep following the route toward the **distal canal
lock**.

There are conversations and side details around the undercity, but I do not
wander several streets away trying to locate Fathom's cavern entrance directly.

The important clue comes at the lock:

**the passage is beneath the canal.**

That means the next objective is not finding a door.

It is changing the water level.

The Slugrats around the lock have to be dealt with before I can comfortably use
the controls.

I keep the squad together around the available cover instead of rushing one
Operator straight to the console.

More enemies can enter the fight from the canal side, so I also avoid spending
every heal or high-value ability on the first targets I see.

Once the area is under control, I move to the console.

<h2 id="drain-takali-canal">
  Drain the canal to reveal the tunnel entrance
</h2>

The console at the lock controls the water.

I use it to **drain Takali Canal**.

That is the navigation step that opens Dark Waters.

If I am standing near the objective marker and cannot find the entrance, I do
not leave the area and search another block.

I stay at the lock and use the canal controls.

The water is covering the route forward.

Dark Waters still has another major fight ahead.

If somebody has lost a large amount of Health, this is a better moment to deal
with it than entering the caverns assuming the Mission is almost finished.

Then I go underground.

<h2 id="follow-tunnels">
  Follow the downed guards deeper into the caverns
</h2>

The underground section is easier to navigate than the canal.

I keep moving deeper through the tunnels.

The first important story clue is a group of guards who have already been taken
out.

There are **no obvious blaster marks**.

Zero Company did not do it.

That tells me somebody else has moved through the same route ahead of the
squad.

I do not double back to the surface looking for another objective after finding
them.

I continue deeper toward the voices and activity in the caverns.

That eventually brings Zero Company to Fathom.

<h2 id="find-fathom">
  Why Hawks cannot shoot Fathom during the initiation
</h2>

This is the easiest moment in Dark Waters to misread as a failed trigger.

Zero Company finally reaches an **Infinite Coil initiation**, and Fathom is
standing in clear view.

Howerin asks whether Hawks has a shot.

Hawks does not take it because **civilians are mixed in with Fathom's group**.

There is no hidden firing angle I need to find.

There is no missed interaction.

I do not reload and try to position the squad differently before the scene.

I simply let it continue.

<div className="my-6 rounded-xl border border-slate-700/70 bg-slate-900/40 p-5">
  <strong>You did not miss a Fathom attack.</strong> The shot is blocked by the

  and the Mission returns control to the squad.
</div>

The sequence also explains what Fathom is doing with the people who come to
her.

They are being exposed to the **Shadow Plague**.

Some do not survive.

Those who do are taken farther into the Coil.

Once Fathom leaves with most of her people, Dark Waters changes from
observation back into combat.

<h2 id="free-converts">
  Hold the Coil while the Jedi gets the converts out
</h2>

The civilians who survived the initiation are still trapped.

This is where the person who silently passed through the guards earlier finally
reveals herself.

She is a Jedi.

When Hawks asks whether she has a weapon, her answer makes her role in the next
fight clear:

**the Force is her ally.**

The division of responsibility is straightforward:

**the Jedi handles the converts; my squad handles the Coil.**

I do not pull Operators away from the fight to follow the civilians.

I do not need to interact with each convert individually.

The rescue progresses while I survive the combat encounter.

That lets me focus on the enemies still threatening Zero Company.

I keep damaged Operators in safer positions instead of chasing a weakened enemy
just to finish it one turn faster.

There is no reason to make the rescue harder by creating an unnecessary
downed-Operator problem while the civilians are already being handled.

Eventually I get confirmation that the converts have reached the surface.

At that point, the dangerous part of Dark Waters is over.

<h2 id="meet-tel-rea">
  Tel-Rea does not join Zero Company after Dark Waters
</h2>

After the fight, I return to the surface and finally get the Jedi's name:

**Tel-Rea.**

Tel-Rea has been following the Infinite Coil independently for months.

She also gives Hawks a much clearer picture of the **Shadow Plague**.

It does not affect everyone the same way.

Some victims die, while survivors can become unusually resilient and less
responsive to pain.

That helps explain what Zero Company just watched during the initiation and why
Fathom's followers are willing to treat surviving the plague as proof of
something larger.

The important progression detail is what happens when Hawks suggests working
together.

**Tel-Rea refuses.**

She still has her own mission.

So after finishing Dark Waters, I do not repeatedly check Personnel, Recruitment,
or the Den expecting a hidden Jedi unlock.

I also do not reload the conversation looking for a different dialogue choice.

Nothing has gone wrong.

I continue the Cortilius story.
