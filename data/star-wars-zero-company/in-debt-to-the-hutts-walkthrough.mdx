<h2 id="how-to-pay-80000-credit-debt">
  How I pay the 80,000-Credit debt without grinding
</h2>

The number is intimidating the first time it appears.

Zero Company owes Gorga **80,000 Credits**, and I have **nine Cycles** to deal
with it.

My first instinct was to start protecting every Credit and look for the highest
paying jobs.

The early rewards quickly show why that is the wrong approach.

One of Gorga's early Operations paid **500 Credits**.

The Lothal Tactical Mission later paid **1,000 Credits**.

The next Gorga job I chose paid **1,200 Credits**.

Those rewards are useful for normal company expenses, but even several payouts
of that size barely touch 80,000.

So I stop treating the debt as an economy puzzle.

I treat it as a **Critical progression chain**.

The route I follow is:

**Brookner → Lothal → Kaller or Dantooine → Republic Intelligence contract →
collect the payment → Payback.**

The real risk is not failing to save another 500 Credits.

It is letting the Critical chain stall while the deadline keeps moving.

<h2 id="brookner-operation">
  I keep enough Intel available for Brookner
</h2>

Gorga's first major problem is **Brookner**, one of his clerks who has been
sending confidential information off-world.

This is where Intel becomes more important to me than the Credit reward.

On my early route, I had **100 Intel**, while Brookner's work could ask for
**50 Intel** at a time.

That means one careless optional Operation can consume half the pool I need for
the next important step.

Operations themselves do not advance the Cycle the way Tactical Missions do,
but spending the Intel can still leave me unable to resolve the Critical
Operation I actually care about.

So during this chapter my rule is simple:

**Gorga's Critical work gets first claim on my Intel.**

The first Brookner step gives me **500 Credits** and some Influence.

The follow-up reveals that the problem is much larger than ordinary bookkeeping
and leads into the chapter's first meaningful choice.

If the difference between Operations, Tactical Missions, and Cycle advancement
is still unclear, I keep the full system explanation in my
<Link href="/star-wars-zero-company/beginner-guide">
  {" "}Zero Company beginner guide
</Link>
.

<h2 id="brookner-choice">
  Should I let Brookner disappear or hand him over?
</h2>

The two outcomes I saw were:

**Let Brookner disappear**

- **3 Influence**
- **500 Credits**

**Hand Brookner over to Gorga**

- **5 Influence**

I took the first option on my early route because the extra 500 Credits were
useful while Zero Company was still short on cash.

But I do not choose it because I think that 500 is necessary to repay Gorga.

It is only **0.625% of the 80,000-Credit debt**.

If I value the additional Influence more, handing Brookner over is also a
perfectly workable route.

**I do not need one specific Brookner choice to continue to Lothal.**

That makes this a reward decision rather than a hidden debt-route trap.

<h2 id="lothal-pirates">
  After Brookner, I complete Gorga's Lothal job
</h2>

Once Brookner is dealt with, Gorga sends Zero Company after pirates who have
been raiding his smuggling warehouses.

The Tactical Mission takes place in an abandoned settlement on **Lothal**.

</figure>

I do not delay this Mission waiting for a perfect roster.

It is part of the progression that opens more of Zero Company's systems in the
first place.

My completion rewards included:

- **1,000 Credits**
- **100 Intel**
- **2 Focus Points**
- **Crew Quarters**

The 1,000 Credits are useful, but they are not why this Mission matters.

The Intel lets me keep using Operations, the Focus Points strengthen the active
roster, and Crew Quarters helps the company grow.

Most importantly, completing Lothal moves Gorga's chain forward.

That is worth far more than trying to squeeze another few hundred Credits out
of unrelated work while the debt deadline is still active.

<h2 id="kaller-or-dantooine">
  Kaller or Dantooine both keep the debt route moving
</h2>

After Lothal, Gorga gives me another choice.

**Kaller** sends Zero Company against droids.

**Dantooine** sends the squad against local outlaws.

Both open a Tactical Mission.

For the debt route, I do not see a reason to obsess over which one is the
"correct" choice.

I picked **Kaller** in my run.

Its completion reward included **1,200 Credits** and a weapon mod.

That is another useful payout, but it reinforces the same conclusion I reached
earlier:

**normal Mission rewards are still nowhere close to an 80,000-Credit repayment
plan.**

So I choose the job I want to play and keep advancing.

I do not spend extra Cycles searching for a supposedly better farming route.

What I am waiting for is the next story change, not a jackpot.

<h2 id="republic-intelligence-contract">
  Republic Intelligence is the real solution to the debt
</h2>

The financial problem changes when **Jae** returns to the Den with **Lieutenant
Howerin of Republic Intelligence**.

Republic Intelligence wants Zero Company involved in the investigation of the
**Infinite Coil**.

The next lead points toward Cortilius, but the part that matters immediately
for Gorga's chapter is the contract itself.

Howerin knows Zero Company is trapped under Hutt debt.

The Republic job gives Hawks a way out.

That is the moment I stop wondering whether I somehow missed a hidden
80,000-Credit farming method.

The payment combines money still owed for the earlier Serelonus work with an
advance on the new Republic Intelligence contract.

It is explicitly enough to pay Gorga **with some money left over**.

I do not throw money away, but I also do not cripple the roster by pretending

The Critical progression is what solves the debt.

<h2 id="launch-payback">
  I resolve Payback before advancing another Cycle
</h2>

Receiving the Republic payment does not finish the chapter by itself.

I still need to settle things with Gorga.

Back at the Holotable, **Payback** becomes the final step.

In my run it cost **0 Intel** and showed a **one-Cycle expiry window**.

Because Payback is an Operation, resolving it does not require me to spend
another Cycle.

That is why I do it immediately.

I do not launch a Tactical Mission first and risk advancing past the visible
one-Cycle window.

Once Payback resolves, Gorga is surprised that Zero Company can repay him in
full so quickly.

The debt problem is over.

From that point on, I stop routing the campaign around Gorga.

**Republic Intelligence and the Infinite Coil become the main story thread.**
