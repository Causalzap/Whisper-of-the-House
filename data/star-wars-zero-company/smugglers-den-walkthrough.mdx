<h2 id="smugglers-den-route">
  Smuggler's Den route from Three Tower District to Tel-Rea
</h2>

**Smuggler's Den** begins after Tel-Rea identifies the Slugrats' main
distribution center on Cortilius.

The target is an abandoned industrial facility in the **Three Tower District**
last registered to **Vinsar Materials**.

My route is:

**follow the Coil crates → reach the service entrance → clear the Slugrats
outside → enter Vinsar Materials → push deeper through the warehouse → survive
the later Coil wave → expose the battle-droid shipment → return to the Den →
finish Tel-Rea's conversation.**

There are two progression points I keep in mind.

The first is that the **service entrance is the correct way inside**.

The second is that clearing the first large warehouse group does **not** mean
the Mission is over.

More enemies arrive afterward, including a Destroyer Droid.

<h2 id="find-service-entrance">
  Follow the Coil crates to the Vinsar Materials service entrance
</h2>

Tel-Rea's intel points specifically toward a **service tunnel**.

As I move through the Three Tower District, the Coil cargo gives me the best
confirmation that I am heading toward the right industrial building.

I do not keep circling the district looking for a main-door interaction.

The service entrance is guarded by Slugrats and leads directly into the
warehouse route.

<h2 id="enter-vinsar-materials">
  Enter Vinsar Materials and keep moving deeper
</h2>

After the exterior group is dealt with, I go through the service route and
enter the warehouse.

The facility still identifies itself as **Vinsar Materials** even though it has
been condemned for **12,140 rotations**.

That detail confirms what the Mission is already suggesting:

this is not an ordinary warehouse the Slugrats recently started using.

It is an abandoned facility being used to move a much larger amount of cargo
without attracting attention.

Once I am inside, I keep advancing.

I do not turn around toward the service entrance looking for another exterior
objective after the first interior sequence.

The evidence Zero Company needs is farther inside.

<h2 id="warehouse-fight">
  The warehouse fight continues after the first major wave
</h2>

Inside Vinsar Materials, the operation stops looking like a simple Slugrat
cleanup.

The **Infinite Coil** is directly involved.

The important thing I learned here is not to interpret the first cleared group
as the end of the battle.

More Coil forces arrive afterward.

Eventually a **Destroyer Droid** joins the fight.

<h2 id="battle-droid-shipment">
  The warehouse is hiding a Separatist battle-droid shipment
</h2>

Once Vinsar Materials is secured, the scale of the Slugrat operation becomes
clear.

The Coil has not been moving ordinary contraband through Cortilius.

It has smuggled **thousands of battle droids** onto the planet.

The Slugrats were supporting preparations for a much larger Separatist attack.

Exposing the shipment gives Republic forces a chance to respond before the
droids are deployed.

Completing the Mission also unlocks **One Less Clanker**.

For Zero Company itself, though, the most important progression happens after I
return to the Den.

<h2 id="unlock-tel-rea">
  How to recruit Tel-Rea in Zero Company
</h2>

If I am trying to unlock Tel-Rea, completing
<Link href="/star-wars-zero-company/dark-waters-walkthrough">
  {" "}Dark Waters
</Link>
is not enough.

Dark Waters is only the first meeting.

Hawks asks Tel-Rea to work with Zero Company there, but she declines because she
still has her own mission.

**Smuggler's Den is the progression step that changes that.**

After the warehouse operation, I return to the Den and continue Tel-Rea's
conversation.

She explains why she has not gone back to the Jedi Order.

Her master, **Hadrix**, recognized that the Shadow Plague was more than an
ordinary disease.

Hadrix later died from it.

Tel-Rea believes returning to the Order would risk being reassigned while
Fathom continues spreading the plague, so she has continued Hadrix's work on
her own.

Hawks offers her another option:

**stop hunting Fathom alone and work with Zero Company.**

This time, Tel-Rea agrees.

My sequence is:

**meet Tel-Rea in Dark Waters → continue the Cortilius story → complete
Smuggler's Den → return to the Den → finish the Hadrix conversation → Tel-Rea
joins Zero Company.**

Once that conversation is complete, I can treat Tel-Rea as part of the normal
roster instead of an independent Jedi contact.

<h2 id="after-smugglers-den">
  What changes after Smuggler's Den
</h2>

Smuggler's Den resolves several pieces of the Cortilius storyline at once.

Zero Company exposes the Slugrats' distribution center.

The Republic learns that **thousands of battle droids** have been staged on
Cortilius.

And Tel-Rea joins the company.

That is the progression checkpoint I use to know I am finished with this
Mission.

I do not keep searching the Three Tower District for another warehouse
objective after the shipment is exposed.

The next story progress comes from returning to the Den and continuing Zero
Company's contracts.
