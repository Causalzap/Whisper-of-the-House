  Let Normal Campaign Progress Clear the Easy Milestones
</h2>

I would not interrupt the Campaign just to farm objectives that ordinary progression is already advancing.

The main milestones are:

**Junior Defuser** → complete 5 levels

**Expert Defuser** → complete 15 levels

**Master Defuser** → complete 30 levels

**Wirecutting Expert** → cut 5 wires

**BOOM!** → explode for the first time

Those are better treated as progress markers than separate farming sessions.

**First Defusal** is the one entry I handle carefully. Its achievement listing currently shows no description, so I treat the name as confirmed without inventing a more specific trigger.

If the Campaign itself is blocking progress, I go back to the <Link href="/bombanana/levels">BOMBANANA Levels & Walkthrough</Link> before worrying about cleanup achievements.

<h2
  id="repeat-and-dice"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Carry the Short Counter Straight Into the Longer One
</h2>

Two pairs are particularly inefficient to split across separate sessions.

For repeated levels:

**Here We Go Again** → play the same level 3 times

**Still?** → play the same level 6 times

If I have deliberately started this grind, the third run is only a checkpoint. I continue to six while I am already repeating the same level.

The dice pair follows the same logic:

**Indecisive Monkeys** → roll the dice 3 times

**Rollaholic** → click the dice 30 times

Stopping at three means almost the entire hidden follow-up is still waiting.

The listed requirements do not confirm whether either counter must be completed inside one session, so I would not assume a reset or persistence rule that the game does not state.


<h2
  id="slap-repair"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Slaps, Repairs, and Bottle Hits Track Different Actions
</h2>

The slap achievements look related, but two different sides of the interaction are being counted.

**Slapper** → 30 slaps given

**Slap Maniac** → 1000 slaps given

**Punching Bag** → 300 slaps received

If all three players simply hit each other without a plan, several counters may move while the one I actually need advances slowly.

For deliberate cleanup, I give each player one job: one works on slaps given, another receives, then the roles swap when the required total is finished.

Repairs form another clean chain:

**Repairman** → 5 repairs

**Fixaholic** → 50 repairs

If I have already switched into cleanup mode, I keep going after the fifth repair rather than setting up the same grind again later.

**OUCH!** is separate:

**get hit by 20 bottles**

I leave these interaction-heavy objectives until normal bomb runs are comfortable. Adding side counters while the group is still learning defusal modules usually makes both jobs slower.


<h2
  id="basket"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Once One Basket Counts, Keep Going to Ten
</h2>

The basketball achievements are another natural pair:

**Basket!** → score 1 basket

**ŞENGÜN!** → score 10 baskets

Once a shot is registering, I keep using the same session to work toward ten.

What I would not rely on yet is a supposedly guaranteed shooting position or angle.

The achievement requirements confirm the targets. They do not establish one reproducible best shot.

Until I have a setup that works consistently, I treat **1 basket** and **10 baskets** as confirmed and the exact shooting method as something to test in-game.


<h2
  id="just-in-time"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Solve the Last Input Before the Final Three Seconds
</h2>

**Just in Time!** requires the bomb to be defused in the **last 3 seconds**.

I do the difficult work before that window begins.

Near the end, I want one simple final action whose answer is already known.

Good candidates include:

- one confirmed Cable cut;
- one known Direction press;
- one Calculator final digit that has already been resolved.

Then one player watches the clock and Blind waits.

If the answer is still being debated with five seconds remaining, I finish the bomb. A live run is a poor place to improvise a timing achievement.


<h2
  id="custom-endless"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Custom Has One Trigger; Endless Has Three Checkpoints
</h2>

**Fearless Monkeys** asks for:

**Choose Hard difficulty in Custom Mode.**

The listed requirement does not state a Campaign prerequisite, so I would not add one.

Endless has three milestones:

**Endless Junior** → complete 5 waves

**Endless Expert** → complete 15 waves

**Endless Master** → complete 30 waves

The September 2 SteamDB snapshot showed:

**1.2%** at 5 waves

**0.1%** at 15 waves

**0.1%** at 30 waves

Those launch-day percentages show how few players had reached those checkpoints at that moment. They do not yet tell me how rare the achievements will be once players have had much more time with Endless.

For broader decisions about Campaign, Endless, and Custom, I use the <Link href="/bombanana#endless-custom">main BOMBANANA guide</Link>.


<h2
  id="cleanup-order"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Route I Use for 23 / 23
</h2>

After normal Campaign progress has done everything it can, I work through the remaining achievements in this order:

1. Finish Campaign through Level 30.
2. Replay one level until both the 3-run and 6-run achievements are finished.
3. Take the dice counter from 3 all the way to 30.
4. Finish the 5 / 50 repair pair and the 20 bottle hits.
5. Coordinate the slap-given and slap-received counters.
6. Score one basket and continue to ten.
7. Prepare a solved final input for **Just in Time!**.
8. Choose Hard difficulty in Custom Mode.
9. Push Endless through Waves 5, 15, and 30.

Each block keeps me doing one kind of action long enough to finish the achievements attached to it instead of repeatedly rebuilding the same grind.
