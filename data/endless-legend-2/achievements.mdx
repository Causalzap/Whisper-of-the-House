<h2 id="campaign-plan">
  How should you plan a 59 / 59 achievement run?
</h2>

Do not try to force all 59 achievements into one campaign.

Several requirements are mutually exclusive or tied to different factions. **Red Sky at Night**, **Saiadha Reborn**, and **The End of Magic** require Master, Worship, and Impress victories respectively, while the faction achievements require separate Kin of Sheredyn, Necrophage, Last Lords, Aspects, Tahuks, and Sandshapers campaigns.

Other goals are better left running in the background:

- **Seasoned Campaigner** — win 100 battles
- **Curious Mind** — collect 200 Curiosities across all games
- **Moneymaker** — accumulate more than 200K Dust across all games
- **Diplomatic Marvel** — sign 100 Treaties across all games
- **No Stone Unturned** — play 10,000 turns

These counters continue moving while you work on more specific achievements, so there is little benefit in stopping a strong campaign to grind them in isolation.

The ordinary progression achievements can usually be picked up the same way. **First Steps!** arrives after 25 turns, **Full House** needs five different District types in one City, and **Homewrecker** only needs one razed City. World-threat achievements such as **Doomslayer**, **Down with Tyranny**, **Prison Break**, and **Perfection Bested** can be taken when the matching target appears in a campaign already capable of defeating it.

A practical completion route is to give each faction run a few goals that naturally fit its mechanics, then use the later cleanup campaigns for unusual map states, combat triggers, and difficulty requirements.

<h2 id="victory-and-difficulty">
  Which victory and difficulty achievements need separate planning?
</h2>

The three Awakening achievements require three different victories:

- **Red Sky at Night** — win through **Master**
- **Saiadha Reborn** — win through **Worship**
- **The End of Magic** — win through **Impress**

Each path still requires both of its current objectives and the final maintenance period. The exact requirements and the 10-turn finish are covered in <Link href="/endless-legend-2/victory-conditions">victory conditions</Link>.

I pair each path with a campaign already moving in the right direction rather than trying to force one faction through all three consecutively. A population-and-Industry-heavy Empire is easier to steer toward Worship, strong Science plus controlled pacified Villages can make Impress cleaner, and a wide Empire is naturally closer to Master.

**Last Saiadhan Standing** is separate because it requires eliminating every other Empire rather than completing an Awakening path.

Three other achievements can be stacked into the same hard-difficulty campaign:

- **Grandmaster** — win on Hero difficulty or above
- **Peer of the Old Ones** — win on Endless difficulty
- **Mic Drop** — win against 7 AIs on Endless difficulty on a Huge map

A successful Mic Drop run already satisfies the difficulty settings needed for the other two, so I save that campaign until I am comfortable with one faction's opening, army replacement, and expansion.

**More Fun with Friends** does not belong in that stack. It only requires playing a multiplayer game with at least one other human and does not require winning.

<h2 id="faction-achievements">
  Which faction achievements are easiest to miss?
</h2>

The faction achievements are much easier when they are treated as part of that faction's normal campaign rather than remembered after the Empire is already close to winning.

### Kin of Sheredyn

Kin have three goals:

- **Dreadnought Nation** — have 4 active Chosen simultaneously
- **Braced for Impact** — reach the last threshold of Strength of Our Zeal
- **Feel My Wrath** — completely destroy an enemy Army with Wrath of the Skies

The first two can develop together because both reward deep investment in Kin progression. For Dreadnought Nation, keep earlier Chosen alive until the fourth is active. For Feel My Wrath, weaken the enemy Army first and use Wrath of the Skies when it can actually remove the remaining force.

Strength of Our Zeal, Chosen, and capital Fortification are covered in the <Link href="/endless-legend-2/kin-of-sheredyn">Kin of Sheredyn guide</Link>.

### Necrophage

Necrophage also have three major faction requirements:

- **A Feature not a Bug** — create one non-Necro Unit
- **Bugtropolitan Network** — connect 20 Burrows to the underground network
- **Infectious Agent** — create 6 Larvae through infection in one battle

The Burrow target is a long infrastructure goal, so let it grow with normal territorial expansion instead of building poor attachments only for the counter.

Infectious Agent needs one sufficiently large battle. Six Larvae must come from the same fight, so tiny neutral groups cannot satisfy it no matter how many are defeated across separate battles.

A Feature not a Bug is much simpler: take the first suitable opportunity to create the required non-Necro unit and move on.

### Last Lords

The three Last Lords goals require very different kinds of preparation.

**Gotta Catch 'Em All** needs Lord's Estates on **8 different Minor Factions** at the end of a turn. The word *different* is the important part; repeated Villages belonging to the same Minor Faction do not fill additional unique slots.

**Why Won't You Die?** requires winning **5 fights with the Marquis Galardi Army**. Once Galardi is available, use manageable local or neutral fights to build the count instead of parking the Army after the quest step is finished.

**A Mortal Coil** depends on the faction quest ending and requires choosing the outcome where the Last Lords move away from their primal nature.

Lord's Estates, Galardi, population conversion, and the Dust economy are covered in the <Link href="/endless-legend-2/last-lords">Last Lords guide</Link>.

### Aspects

Aspects have three requirements whose scales are very different.

**Diplomatic Powerhouse** needs **15 treaties active simultaneously** across the other Empires. This is not the same as the cumulative Diplomatic Marvel counter, so letting old treaties lapse while signing new ones can prevent the achievement.

**Corraled!** requires pacifying **20 Villages with Coral**. Ordinary pacification does not substitute because the method matters.

**Great Saiadhan Reef** requires ending a turn with **50% of all land tiles covered in Coral**. This is a campaign-scale objective, so Coral expansion needs to begin long before another Empire is close to winning.

### Tahuks

Tahuks have two achievements where the exact final state matters.

**Called Me Maybe** requires the Empire to contain **only Called population when the game ends**.

**Heart of Glass** requires **all Territories to be glass tiles when the game ends**.

Do not assume reaching those states once during the campaign is enough. Check every City and Territory before the final victory resolves.

**Coup de Grâce** is tied to the faction quest and requires choosing the coup outcome.

### Sandshapers

The Sandshapers achievements are:

- **Monument Valley** — end a turn with one City whose Territories contain all 3 different Ruin types
- **Dune** — cover an entire Territory with Sand
- **Stone Cold Killer** — teleport an Army and attack another Empire during the same turn

For Monument Valley, the Ruins must all belong to Territories attached to the same City rather than merely existing elsewhere in the Empire.

For Dune, choose a Territory with as few awkward conversion obstacles as possible and finish that region instead of spreading Wishes across several half-complete areas.

Stone Cold Killer needs a useful teleport destination. A Restored Ruin must leave the Army close enough to an enemy that it can still attack before the turn ends.

Ruin types, Wishes, Sand conversion, and teleportation are covered in the <Link href="/endless-legend-2/sandshapers">Sandshapers guide</Link>.

<h2 id="hero-achievements">
  Which Hero achievements need special setup?
</h2>

Several Hero achievements depend on the exact Army or relationship state rather than ordinary Hero progression.

The main ones to watch are:

- **Lord of All They Survey** — raise one Hero to Level 15
- **The A-Team** — place 4 Heroes in one Army
- **Out of this World** — have 3 Stellar Heroes simultaneously
- **Great Enemy Felled!** — defeat a Hero's arch-enemy in battle
- **Power Couple** — have a Notable who is both the Hero's Companion and sitting on the Council with that Hero
- **Heartbreaker** — have 3 different Companions with one Hero during one game

Power Couple is easy to misread. Completing the relationship alone is not sufficient; the Notable also has to occupy the required Council position alongside the Hero.

Heartbreaker works the other way around. Keep one Hero as the center of the relationship chain instead of spreading Companion progress across several Heroes.

For The A-Team and Out of this World, avoid accidentally splitting the required Heroes immediately before ending the turn or triggering another major event. The required simultaneous state has to exist at the same time.

<h2 id="combat-and-map">
  Which combat and map achievements have unusual triggers?
</h2>

A handful of achievements are simple to describe but awkward to create deliberately.

**Most Valuable Platoon** asks one Unit to eliminate **10 other Units without dying**. Once a unit has built a significant streak, stop using it for unnecessary sacrificial trades.

**Punching Up** requires winning a battle while starting with **500 less Army strength** than the opponent. Choose a favorable matchup where terrain, composition, or manual play can overcome the displayed strength gap instead of challenging the largest enemy stack available.

**Out of their Depth** requires eliminating an Army by **drowning it in a river**, and the requirement allows your own Army. The river interaction is therefore the important setup rather than ordinary damage.

**Bait and Switch** needs **Bait** to move a Doomwraith accompanied by at least **3 Horrors** to another Capital. Check the Horror count before using the action; moving a Doomwraith accompanied by fewer than three does not satisfy it.

**Flattened** requires cutting down **30 Forests or Ridges in one game**, so it fits better in a campaign already clearing terrain repeatedly.

**Landmark Achievement** requires controlling **all Natural Wonders on Saiadha in one game**. Discovery is not enough — every Natural Wonder has to be under your control before that campaign ends.

These goals are much easier when the right map situation appears naturally. Forcing them onto an unsuitable campaign can take longer than carrying the requirement into another run.

<h2 id="city-economy">
  Which City and economy achievements require a simultaneous state?
</h2>

Several economic achievements are less about accumulating progress and more about ending a turn with the correct Empire state.

Important examples include:

- **Impenetrable Fortress** — end a turn with one City at 3,000 Fortification Health
- **Land of Luxuries** — have 5 Luxury boosters active simultaneously
- **No Two Cities Alike** — own 5 Cities whose City Halls are on 5 different terrain biomes
- **Stockpiler** — possess 100 of each Strategic Resource
- **Scientific Marvel** — research every Technology in one game
- **Cultural Exchange** — simultaneously possess 4 different Populations at maximum threshold

The word *simultaneously* matters for Land of Luxuries and Cultural Exchange.

Four Luxury boosters followed by a fifth after one expires is not enough. The same applies to population thresholds: all four population types must be at maximum threshold together.

No Two Cities Alike rewards planning earlier. Once four City Hall biomes are already occupied, scout specifically for the missing biome before placing the fifth City. Waiting until all five Cities are established can leave no easy way to correct the layout.

Stockpiler and Scientific Marvel are easier to approach late, when the Empire already has a broad resource network and enough Science to finish technologies that would otherwise have little strategic value.

<h2 id="cleanup">
  What is the fastest way to clean up the remaining achievements?
</h2>

After several faction campaigns, most ordinary progression counters should already be well advanced. The remaining list is usually dominated by requirements that need a deliberate setup.

I clean them up in this order:

1. Finish any missing **Master, Worship, and Impress** victories.
2. Complete unfinished faction achievements during the matching faction campaign.
3. Do **Mic Drop** once Endless difficulty and Huge-map play are comfortable.
4. Set up simultaneous states such as 5 Luxury boosters, 4 max-threshold Populations, 4 Heroes in one Army, and 3 Stellar Heroes.
5. Build the large map states: 3,000 Fortification, five City Hall biomes, all Natural Wonders, 30 cleared Forests or Ridges, and 100 of every Strategic Resource.
6. Finish unusual combat triggers such as Punching Up, Bait and Switch, Out of their Depth, and Most Valuable Platoon.
7. Leave any unfinished cumulative counters running while the rest of the list is cleaned up.

That order keeps the longest passive counters moving while each campaign is already doing something useful for a faction, victory, quest ending, difficulty setting, or one-time trigger.
