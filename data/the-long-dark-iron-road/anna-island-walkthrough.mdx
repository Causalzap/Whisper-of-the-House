The first major obstacle on Anna Island is reaching the church. You can see it above Port Remnant, but the direct hillside route is blocked. The railway looks like the obvious alternative, although it does not lead straight up to the church either.

The journey becomes much longer after the church. Alice's second map points toward Track Section Five, where a waterfall formation and a climbing rope mark the next transition. Even after reaching Section Six, you still have a considerable distance to travel before finding the Prototype Train.

<section id="port-remnant">

## Where Do You Go First in Port Remnant?

The Fulmar brings you from Desolation Point to a sheltered landing beneath the **Port Remnant lighthouse**.

Your first objective is to reach the lighthouse and examine Alice's instructions. There is no need to explore the entire railway before doing this.

If you have not yet reached Cauldron Island, follow the [Fulmar boat guide](/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-boat) for the departure location and required supplies.

### Find Alice's first map in the lighthouse

Enter the lighthouse from the harbor passage and climb through the interior.

Near the upper part of the building, you will find Alice's recording, a hand-drawn map, and the key needed to leave the lighthouse.

Collect the recording and inspect the map before unlocking the exit.

![Alice's hand-drawn map found in the Port Remnant lighthouse, showing the cave approach and church.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-lighthouse-map.webp)

*The first drawing connects a cave near the railway to the church on higher ground. It is a landmark clue rather than a complete region map.*

The drawing is useful because it shows why walking directly toward the church is unlikely to work. The entrance you need is below the church, near the railway and rocky terrain.

Open the map in **Notes** when you need to compare its landmarks with the environment.

### Why doesn't the map lead directly to the church?

The map is a hand-drawn route clue, not a precise overhead chart.

Its cave, trees, railway, and church are recognizable landmarks, but their spacing and proportions do not correspond exactly to the distances you will walk.

Use the drawing to identify the correct approach rather than expecting every bend in the path to match the picture.

Once the lighthouse door is unlocked, head toward the railway and begin looking for Port Remnant Cave.

<section id="church-route">

## How Do You Find Port Remnant Cave and Reach the Church?

The entrance to **Port Remnant Cave** is near the railway in the rocky area below the church.

Do not try to climb directly up the hillside beneath the church. The surrounding cliffs and broken approaches prevent you from reaching the upper grounds that way.

### Find the cave entrance

From the lower Port Remnant area, move toward the railway and follow the rocky side of the route.

Look for the dark opening set into the cliff near two distinctive bare trees. The entrance blends into the surrounding rocks, especially during fog or poor daylight.

![The entrance to Port Remnant Cave among the rocky terrain near the railway.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-port-remnant-cave.webp)

*Find the rock opening near the bare trees. This is the passage toward the church, not the later tunnel containing the Prototype Train.*

A useful check is your position relative to the church: if the building is still high above you and the slope ahead is blocked, return to the lower rocky approach and look for the cave opening.

You do not need to force a route directly up the cliff.

### Follow the correct passage through the cave

Bring a reliable light source before entering. The passage contains branches that can make it difficult to recognize whether you are heading toward higher ground or looping back to a lower exit.

Continue through the cave toward the route that gains elevation.

After emerging, check the surrounding landmarks. The correct church approach eventually leads through higher terrain, including a birch grove, before reaching the church grounds.

If you emerge below the same inaccessible cliffs, return to the cave route and reconsider the passage you took rather than attempting an unsafe climb.

The church is not the end of the expedition. Beyond it, the route continues into exposed railway terrain and eventually reaches a rope climb. Keep your load below the climbing limit and carry enough food, water, and sleeping equipment for a journey that extends well beyond Port Remnant.

<section id="church">

## What Should You Do at the Desecrated Church?

![The Desecrated Church in Port Remnant, reached from the elevated route beyond the cave.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-church-entrance.webp)

*The church is the destination indicated by Alice's first lighthouse map.*

Inside the **Desecrated Church**, collect the three recordings before heading back outside. **Wax Cylinder 03** is near the front of the room; **04 and 05** stand together on a window ledge.

These recordings establish what happened on the island, but collecting every cylinder across Anna Island is not a prerequisite for reaching the train. The [remaining Wax Cylinder locations](/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinders) can be checked during later railway exploration.

### Do not rely on the church for a sleeping bed

The church provides shelter from the open landscape, but it does not contain a ready-made sleeping bed.

If you arrive exhausted without a Bedroll, reaching the building will not automatically solve your fatigue problem.

Rest before committing to the climb if your condition is deteriorating. Once the church visit is complete, you still need to continue toward Alice's next clue.

### Find the next map at the Whale Bone Shrine

After investigating the church, continue through the elevated terrain toward the nearby **Whale Bone Shrine**.

Look for the distinctive whale bones and an outdoor bench holding Alice's next recording.

Collect **Wax Cylinder 06** and examine the attached map fragment.

This second drawing is different from the lighthouse map. Its faded writing identifies **Anna Island Track Section Five**, and it depicts prominent mountain peaks, waterfalls, and a route turning upward through the rocks.

The recording establishes your next destination. You are no longer trying to reach the church; you are now looking for a passage from the railway into higher mountain terrain.

Before leaving the shrine area, locate the nearby rope descent that reconnects the elevated ground with the lower railway approach.

If you cannot find the way down, return toward the whale-bone landmark and inspect the edges of the established path rather than dropping directly down the cliffs.

## How Do You Reach the Prototype Train After the Church?

The journey from the Whale Bone Shrine to the Prototype Train passes through **Track Sections Five and Six**.

This is not a short walk. Several stretches contain little more than railway, exposed terrain, wildlife, and scattered places to shelter.

### Follow the railway into Track Section Five

Once you have returned to the lower route, follow the railway away from Port Remnant.

Continue into **Anna Island Track Section Five**. You can stay near the tracks for much of the approach rather than searching every side path.

Keep Alice's second map available in Notes.

![Alice's second hand-drawn map, marked Anna Island Track Section Five, showing the waterfalls and climbing route.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-track-section-five-map.webp)

*The second map points toward the waterfall formation and the route up into Track Section Six.*

The most useful landmark is the waterfall formation. Look for **two waterfalls below a higher third waterfall**, with prominent rocky peaks behind them.

The climbing approach is on the **right-hand side of the railway route**, near this formation.

Do not abandon the main route simply because the train has not appeared. The walk is significantly longer than the sketch suggests.

### Find the rope into Track Section Six

Continue along the railway until you are near the waterfall area and the blocked track farther ahead.

Look to the right for the path leading toward a climbing rope. The turn is easy to overlook when you are focused only on following the rails.

If you reach the obstruction at the end of the accessible track, check the nearby right-hand approach instead of trying to pass through the barrier.

Before climbing:

- Reduce your load until you are no longer encumbered.
- Check your fatigue and remaining warmth.
- Keep your Bedroll and essential food and water available for the next stretch.

**You cannot climb the rope while over your carrying limit.** If the climbing interaction is unavailable because you are too heavy, drop or store nonessential items before trying again.

The rope leads into **Track Section Six**.

### Continue through Section Six to the Train Shelter

After climbing, follow the established mountain route.

You will pass narrow paths and elevated sections before returning toward the railway. Continue along the accessible path rather than attempting shortcuts down steep cliffs.

The Train Shelter does not appear immediately after the transition. Expect another substantial walk before you reach the structure containing the locomotive.

If the weather becomes dangerous, manage your warmth and fatigue before pressing forward. The shelter is an important destination, but reaching the building does not guarantee that its interior will be warm enough to recover safely without additional heat.

Eventually, the railway leads into the **Train Shelter**, where the Prototype Train is housed.

### Where do you find the Prototype Train Key?

At the Train Shelter, search the nearby office and work rooms before trying to start the locomotive.

You are looking for three important discoveries:

1. **Prototype Train Key**, needed to unlock the locomotive.
2. **Alice McLeod's Journal 01**, which provides additional information about her investigation.
3. **Alice's recording at the train**, which explains why the locomotive is necessary for the next stage.

The office contains a table with the journal and another recording nearby.

![Alice McLeod's Journal 01 on a table inside the Train Shelter office, near a Wax Cylinder.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-train-shelter-journal-desk.webp)

*Search the Train Shelter office carefully. Alice's journal is on the table, and the Train Key is obtained in the same shelter area.*

![The Prototype Train Key, required to open the locomotive at Track Section Six.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-train-key.webp)

*Collect the Prototype Train Key before attempting to unlock the locomotive.*

If the train is locked, return to the nearby rooms and check for the key. Reaching the locomotive alone does not complete the required collection step.

Read the journal and play Alice's recording before moving on. They establish the purpose of the locomotive and the next stage of the railway journey.

### What should you do once the train is unlocked?

With the locomotive unlocked, inspect its Whale Oil and Water levels. Light the firebox, wait until pressure reaches at least **25 PSI**, release the brake, and select Slow gear to leave the shelter.

If the train remains stationary or the next section is blocked, check the [driving controls and railway obstacles](/the-long-dark-iron-road/prototype-train-guide) before attempting another departure.
