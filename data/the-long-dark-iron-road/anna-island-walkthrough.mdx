## Port Remnant: Find the Lighthouse and Alice's First Clue

The Fulmar brings you into **Port Remnant**, the starting area on Cauldron Island. You arrive near the waterfront, with an industrial settlement, railway infrastructure, and a lighthouse close enough to establish your first objective.

The immediate destination is the lighthouse, not the distant end of the tracks.

For the crossing from Great Bear and the boat's required provisions, use the [Fulmar boat instructions](/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-boat).

### Reach the lighthouse from the port

From the Fulmar landing, head toward the lighthouse overlooking Port Remnant. Enter the building and play Alice's recording.

Alice explains that the island's history was deliberately concealed, then directs you to investigate the **church**. Before leaving, examine the hand-drawn map associated with her message. The church stands on higher ground, and the drawing provides the first clue to reaching it.

### Examine the map fragment instead of relying on the world map

A map clue accompanies the lighthouse stage.

![Alice's map clue at the Port Remnant lighthouse, indicating the route toward the church.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-lighthouse-map.webp)

*Examine Alice's map clue after listening to the lighthouse recording. The church is the next destination, but the route requires finding a passage into the higher terrain.*

Open the hand-drawn map in your **Notes**. The drawing places the cave near the railway and the church above the surrounding rock formations. Use those landmarks to identify the approach; the drawing is not an automatic objective marker on the region map.

## How to Reach the Church: Find Port Remnant Cave

The church sits above the lower railway area. Walking directly toward the building is tempting, but the cliffs and collapsed approaches can prevent you from climbing to it.

The route involves **Port Remnant Cave**, a passage near the railway that provides access to the higher terrain.

### Locate the cave near the tracks

Return toward the railway side of Port Remnant and use the landmarks indicated by Alice's drawing.

Look for the area where the tracks run near the rocky terrain and distinctive trees. The cave entrance is set into the rock rather than standing out as a large industrial doorway.

![The entrance to Port Remnant Cave near the railway, used to reach the church's higher ground.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-port-remnant-cave.webp)

*Port Remnant Cave is the passage to investigate when the direct route toward the church is blocked. It is not the tunnel containing the Prototype Train.*

The cave entrance is easy to miss because it blends into the rock wall. Look for the dark opening beyond the two bare trees shown in the approach, rather than searching for a climbable slope directly below the church.

Do not continue along the railway expecting it to take you straight to the church. The blocked hillside route and surrounding cliffs will not get you onto the church grounds.

### Navigate Port Remnant Cave

Bring a light source before entering. Port Remnant Cave contains branching passages, and reaching an outdoor exit does not necessarily put you on the correct approach to the church.

After emerging, check your elevation against the church and nearby waterfall. A lower route can take you back toward blocked ground instead of the upper approach.

If the path ends beneath inaccessible cliffs, return to the established trail and look for the higher route rather than trying to climb directly up the rock face.

The **birch grove** provides another recognizable landmark as you approach the church. Continue toward the elevated church grounds and surrounding graveyard once the route opens up.

## What to Do Inside the Church

![The church on Anna Island, reached after following the upper route from Port Remnant Cave.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-church-entrance.webp)

*The church stands on the higher ground above the lower railway area. Reaching the building is the first major objective after the lighthouse.*

Enter the church and investigate the interior.

You will find **Wax Cylinder recordings** connected to the island's past, including the history surrounding Angus Hornslow and Elsa.

These recordings explain why Alice directed you here before introducing the train. They establish the background to the island's abandoned industry and the mystery she is investigating.

Listen to the recordings and examine the available clues before leaving.

The important distinction is between completing the current story visit and collecting every recording across the island. You should investigate the church thoroughly, but there is no need to abandon your current route to search distant regions for every optional Wax Cylinder.

### Do not assume the church provides a sleeping bed

The church can look like a useful shelter, but it does not provide a ready-made sleeping bed in the opening exploration shown here.

Arriving exhausted without a Bedroll can therefore be a serious inconvenience.

There are buildings with usable sleeping arrangements in the lower settlement. Resting before climbing to the church is a better choice than reaching it at the end of a long day and discovering that you cannot sleep there.

The church also contains books and other ordinary items, but those should not distract from its story recordings.

After completing the church visit, the next lead takes you back toward Port Remnant.

## After the Church: Follow Alice's Clues Toward the Train

After investigating the church, check the clues collected in your Notes before returning toward the lower railway area.

The next stage of Alice's investigation leads toward the **Prototype Train Tunnel**, where her journal and the train key become relevant.

Do not confuse this location with **Port Remnant Cave**. The earlier cave provides access to the church's elevated approach; the train tunnel belongs to the later railway sequence.

### Find Alice's Journal and the Prototype Train Key

The train-tunnel stage contains three important discoveries:

- **Alice's Journal:** Connects her investigation to the locomotive.
- **Prototype Train Key:** Provides access to the locomotive.
- **Train-related instructions:** Establish the next part of the railway journey.

Investigate the tunnel and collect the key before attempting to operate the train.

After unlocking the locomotive, [check its Whale Oil and Water tanks and build enough steam pressure to move](/the-long-dark-iron-road/prototype-train-guide).
