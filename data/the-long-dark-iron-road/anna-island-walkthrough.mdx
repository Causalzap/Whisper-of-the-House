## Port Remnant: Find the Lighthouse and Alice's First Clue

The Fulmar arrives at **Port Remnant**, a waterfront settlement on Cauldron Island. Railway tracks and industrial buildings are visible nearby, but Alice's first instruction sends you to the lighthouse rather than along the tracks.

If you have not reached the island yet, prepare the crossing using the [Fulmar boat instructions](/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-boat).

### Listen to Alice's recording at the lighthouse

From the landing area, head toward the lighthouse overlooking Port Remnant. Enter and play Alice's recording.

She explains that records of Cauldron Island were deliberately concealed and directs you toward the **church**. Examine the map fragment associated with her message before leaving.

![Alice's map clue from the lighthouse, showing the relationship between the cave and the elevated church.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-lighthouse-map.webp)

*Open Alice's first map in Notes to identify the cave passage beneath the higher church grounds.*

The drawing places a cave near the railway, with the church beyond the surrounding rock formations. This is a landmark clue, not an automatic objective marker on the regular region map.

You can leave the lighthouse once you have listened to the message and inspected the map. The next obstacle is finding the passage up from the lower railway area.

## How to Reach the Church Through Port Remnant Cave

The church stands on terrain that cannot be reached by simply climbing toward the building from below. A cave passage near the railway provides the approach.

### Find the cave entrance near the railway

Return toward the rocky ground beside the tracks and look for **two bare trees** near a dark opening in the rock wall.

![The dark entrance to Port Remnant Cave, partly concealed by the surrounding rock and trees.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-port-remnant-cave.webp)

*Look for the opening beyond the bare trees. Port Remnant Cave is not the later tunnel containing the Prototype Train.*

The entrance blends into the hillside, making it easy to walk past while searching for a more obvious road up to the church.

Following the railway farther away does not solve this particular climb. Likewise, the cliffs immediately below the church are not a usable shortcut.

### Navigate the cave and find the upper approach

Bring a light source. Port Remnant Cave has branching passages, and emerging into daylight does not necessarily mean you have reached the correct elevation.

Use the surrounding terrain to judge your progress:

- **Still below the church:** You have not yet reached the useful upper approach. Avoid forcing a climb up the rock face.
- **Back near blocked ground or the waterfall:** Recheck the established paths instead of repeatedly attempting the same impassable slope.
- **Approaching the birch grove and higher terrain:** Continue toward the church and its surrounding graveyard.

The aim is to reach an accessible path above the lower railway area. Once the church grounds open up, approach the building from that higher level.

### Prepare for the journey beyond the church

The church does not provide a ready-made sleeping bed. A **Bedroll** is therefore important, particularly if the cave approach has already consumed most of your remaining daylight and energy.

Rest and replenish supplies before committing to the next railway journey. Buildings around the lower settlement offer more dependable opportunities to recover than an exposed route through unfamiliar track sections.

Carry enough food and water for a long trip on foot. A cooking can or pot is also valuable: running short of drinkable water far from Port Remnant can force an extended search for shelter and cooking equipment.

Once the church investigation is complete, look for the nearby **whale-bone site**. It leads to the next part of Alice's investigation.

## After the Church: Find Alice's Next Map and the Prototype Train

### Find Alice's sixth recording near the whale bones

Investigate the whale-bone site near the church and play **Alice's sixth Wax Cylinder**.

Alice's message directs you to take another map fragment and follow it farther into the island. She also makes clear that returning to the lighthouse and boarding the Fulmar remains an option if you decide not to continue.

Collect the fragment and open it in **Notes**.

This is a different map from the one used to reach the church. The first map helps you find the elevated church approach; the second points toward a later section of the railway.

### Read the Anna Island Track Section Five map

![Alice's hand-drawn Anna Island Track Section Five map, showing waterfalls, mountain peaks and a marked approach.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-track-section-five-map.webp)

*Compare the waterfall arrangement and surrounding peaks before choosing a route through Track Section Five.*

The item is labeled **Anna Island Track Section Five**. Its drawing shows several waterfalls, distinct mountain peaks and a marked path.

The waterfall arrangement is the difficult part. Look for the overall pattern rather than assuming that any two waterfalls identify the correct location. Two falls appearing at different heights, or a single isolated waterfall without the surrounding peaks, are not enough to establish a match.

Searching every waterfall can consume a substantial amount of food, daylight and energy. The railway provides a longer but recognizable route toward the later track sections when the terrain on the drawing remains uncertain.

### Follow the railway toward Track Section Six

Continue exploring the railway corridor beyond the early Port Remnant area. The route passes through **Track Section Five** and eventually reaches **Track Section Six**, where the Prototype Train can be found.

This is not a short walk from the church. Plan for changing weather, fatigue and the need to recover along the way. Nearby cabins or caves can provide opportunities to stop, but do not assume that every stretch of railway has a convenient building or a ready supply of food.

Two practical mistakes can make the trip considerably harder:

- **Running out of cooking equipment:** Finding water or fuel is less useful when you cannot prepare drinking water.
- **Carrying too much unnecessary equipment:** Heavy items slow a journey that already requires extended walking and may leave too little capacity for useful supplies discovered along the route.

Follow the track-section transitions rather than treating the first blocked gate or industrial structure as the locomotive's location.

The train is found farther along the railway, not back at the lighthouse or the church.

### Collect the Train Key and Alice's Journal

At the Prototype Train area, investigate the nearby equipment and story items before attempting to drive.

The **Train Key** is the mechanical key used to unlock the locomotive.

![The Train Key item, used to unlock the Prototype Train.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-train-key.webp)

*Collect the Train Key before trying to operate the locomotive.*

Also collect **Alice McLeod's Journal 01**.

![Alice McLeod's Journal 01, a story item found during the train discovery sequence.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-alice-journal-01.webp)

*Collect Alice McLeod's Journal 01 and read it in Notes before continuing.*

Read Alice's journal and play the recording at the train before attempting to start the locomotive. These clues explain her work on the prototype and establish the next stage of the railway journey.

### Unlock the locomotive and prepare to move

Use the Train Key to access the locomotive, then inspect the controls and its current condition.

Before releasing the brake, [check the Whale Oil and Water tanks, build steam pressure and select the correct driving gear](/the-long-dark-iron-road/prototype-train-guide).

The railway now becomes a journey you can undertake with the locomotive rather than entirely on foot. Keep the train's condition and nearby refueling opportunities in mind as you leave the initial train area.
