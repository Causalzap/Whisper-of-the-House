Start **Survival Mode**, travel to the **Hibernia Processing docks in Desolation Point**, and use the **Fulmar** to reach Cauldron Island. After arriving at **Port Remnant**, visit the lighthouse to begin following Alice's clues.

The Long Dark: Iron Road begins in familiar territory, but its first major decision comes before boarding the boat. You can bring an established Survival character to Cauldron Island, or start a fresh run rather than risk a save you have spent hundreds of days developing.

Once you arrive, the objectives begin to diverge. Alice's recordings lead you through the island's mystery, the Prototype Train opens railway exploration, and new equipment offers reasons to return to Great Bear. Trying to do all of these at once can leave you overloaded with supplies that are not yet useful.

<section id="how-to-start">

## How to Start Iron Road in Survival Mode

**Iron Road does not begin from WINTERMUTE, Episode Five, or a separate story campaign.** It is an expansion to The Long Dark's Survival Mode.

You can begin with an existing Survival game or create a new one. In either case, the route into the expansion starts on Great Bear Island.

1. Load a Survival game with Iron Road installed and enabled.
2. Travel to **Desolation Point**.
3. Find the **Fulmar** at the Hibernia Processing docks.
4. Prepare the boat for its crossing and travel to **Port Remnant**.

Cauldron Island is not available as a starting region when creating a new Survival game. Choosing a different starting location and making your way to the Fulmar is part of reaching it.

The boat requires a significant supply commitment before departure. Do not assume that food, fuel, or water deposited for the crossing will remain available as personal supplies when you arrive.

For the exact requirements, resource contributions, and return-trip interaction, use the [Fulmar boat instructions](/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-boat).

### Should you use an old Survival save?

**Existing Survival saves are compatible with Iron Road**, so a character with established equipment and nearby stockpiles can access the expansion without starting over.

However, there is a difference between being compatible and being completely free of risk.

Iron Road launched in Early Access with new gameplay systems and a new region. Bugs or stability problems could affect an existing run, particularly during the first updates.

Choose your starting point based on what you would lose:

- **Established save you cannot afford to lose:** Preserve that run and consider starting a separate Survival game for the initial exploration.
- **Existing save you are comfortable experimenting with:** Use its established supplies and equipment to reach Desolation Point more easily.
- **Brand-new run:** Accept the additional preparation time in exchange for learning the new area without risking a long-running character.

This decision matters more than selecting the shortest geographical route. A well-equipped survivor can recover from several wrong turns; a valuable old save lost to an Early Access problem cannot be recovered so easily.

## Where Should You Go Next on Cauldron Island?

Your next destination depends on how far you have followed Alice's instructions.

- **Just arrived at Port Remnant:** Enter the lighthouse and listen to Alice's recording. The [route through Port Remnant Cave to the church](/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-walkthrough) is your next objective.
- **Already visited the church:** Review Alice's clues and continue toward the railway tunnel, where the Prototype Train Key and her journal become relevant.
- **Found the Prototype Train:** Unlock the locomotive, then [check its steam pressure, fuel, water, and component condition](/the-long-dark-iron-road/prototype-train-guide) before attempting to move it.

The railway is not simply a shortcut across the island. Alice's clues determine where to investigate, while the locomotive provides transport and shelter once you have gained access to it.

<section id="packing">

## What Should You Leave Behind Before Exploring Anna Island?

Do not carry a full load of train repair materials through the church approach. The opening route is primarily on foot, and the Prototype Train is not available immediately after landing.

Keep a Bedroll, a reliable light, personal food and water, and the equipment needed for ordinary Survival travel. Leave bulky supplies in a safe location around Port Remnant until you know what the railway journey requires.

The church has no ready-made sleeping bed, making fatigue a more immediate concern than hauling spare machinery parts uphill. Once the train is accessible, its storage and shelter facilities give you a better place to organize equipment for longer railway trips.

<section id="whale-oil-heater">

## How Long Does the Portable Whale-Oil Heater Last?

The **Portable Whale-Oil Heater** weighs **2.5 kg** without fuel and holds up to **2.5 L of Whale Oil**. A full tank brings its carried weight to approximately **5 kg**.

It provides portable, wind-resistant heat and has a cooking position for a pot. You can also take it back to Great Bear Island, making it useful beyond Cauldron Island.

### Which Heat Setting Should You Use?

The heater has three settings with different fuel consumption and warmth:

- **Low:** +10°C, burning **0.25 L/hour**. A full tank lasts approximately **10 hours**.
- **Medium:** +20°C, burning **0.50 L/hour**. A full tank lasts approximately **5 hours**.
- **High:** +30°C, burning **0.75 L/hour**. A full tank lasts approximately **3 hours and 20 minutes**.

Its heat reaches approximately **5 meters**, with stronger warming closer to the heater.

Low is the economical setting for a long rest when a modest temperature increase is enough. Medium gives you twice the nominal warmth at twice the fuel cost. High is better reserved for situations requiring substantial immediate heat, since it empties the tank much faster.

A **5-L Whale Oil Can** contains enough fuel for two complete heater refills. At Low, that represents approximately **20 hours of total heating**.

### Fuel Weight and Repairs

The heater may already contain between **1 and 2.5 L** of Whale Oil when found, so check its tank before relying on the maximum runtimes.

Carrying the heater fully fueled costs around 5 kg of inventory capacity. Bringing an additional full 5-L Whale Oil Can adds considerably more weight, making it a more practical arrangement for a fixed shelter or short transport route than a long mountain expedition.

If the heater's condition deteriorates, repair it using:

- **1 Scrap Metal**
- **Simple Tools or Quality Tools**
- **1 hour of repair time**

A successful repair restores **20 condition points**.

Whale Oil is also needed by the Prototype Train. Reserve enough for the equipment you intend to use next rather than emptying your portable supplies into the locomotive unnecessarily.

<section id="wax-cylinders">

## How Many Wax Cylinders Are on Anna Island?

The initial Iron Road release contains **16 Wax Cylinders**, preserving recordings about Cauldron Island's history and the people involved in its industrial past.

As you follow Alice's investigation, play the recordings found along your route, including those aboard the Fulmar, at the lighthouse, and inside the church. These provide context for the island's mystery without requiring you to search every location before continuing.

When the next objective leads toward the Prototype Train Key, continue following those clues. Collect nearby optional cylinders during exploration and return for more when you have sufficient supplies.

<section id="launch-content">

## How Much of Iron Road Is Playable Right Now?

The October 27, 2026 release is the beginning of a larger Early Access expansion.

The current build includes:

- **Anna Island**, the first of five planned islands.
- Approximately **30 km of railway**, including multiple track sections and exploration areas.
- The initial **Prototype Train** and its driving, maintenance, and shelter systems.
- The **Fulmar** connection between Great Bear and Cauldron Island.
- The **Portable Whale-Oil Heater**.
- The first **16 Wax Cylinders**.

The wider plan includes more than **100 km of railway** and four additional islands. Not every system, upgrade, railway obstacle, or region shown in the broader Iron Road plans is present in the first release.

### Is a blocked railway the end of the available content?

Not necessarily.

The railway includes obstacles and progression mechanisms that can prevent you from continuing even when the track visibly extends beyond your current location.

A blocked route may involve a damaged section, an environmental control, an Aurora-related mechanism, or a part of the world that is not yet accessible.

Check the active clues and the immediate railway interaction before assuming that a visible barrier marks the final playable area.

At the same time, reaching track that exists visually is not proof that the entire planned railway network is already available.

The current island should be explored on its own terms. There is no benefit in searching for a hidden crossing to one of the four additional islands when those islands have not yet been released.

### What happens when you finish the available story objectives?

Iron Road remains part of Survival Mode.

You can continue exploring the accessible parts of Cauldron Island, collecting recordings, gathering equipment, maintaining the train, or returning to Great Bear.

Completing the currently available objectives does not require abandoning your Survival run.

The wider story and railway expansion will develop through Early Access updates; the currently accessible content should not be confused with the complete final journey.

</section>

<section id="missing-dlc">

## Iron Road Not Showing Up: Check the DLC Installation

When the Fulmar is missing from the Hibernia Processing docks, first confirm that Iron Road is actually enabled.

Purchasing the expansion should normally install it automatically on Steam, but the DLC can be enabled manually.

1. Open your Steam Library and right-click **The Long Dark**.
2. Select **Properties**.
3. Open the **DLC** tab.
4. Make sure **The Long Dark: Iron Road** is checked.
5. Confirm that Steam has finished downloading any pending updates.

When Iron Road is active, the game's main menu and Survival Mode menu display Iron Road branding.

After confirming that installation is complete, load a Survival game and visit the Fulmar's location in Desolation Point.

Creating a new save will not fix a missing DLC installation. Likewise, Cauldron Island will not appear in the new-game region selector after installing the expansion; the Fulmar remains the required way to reach it.

If the DLC is enabled but the boat still does not appear, restart the game after completing the downloads and check for an available update.
