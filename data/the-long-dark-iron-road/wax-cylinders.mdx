<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Check **Collections** for the recording number you are missing. Before leaving Port Remnant, collect **01** on the Fulmar, **02** in the lighthouse, and **11** near the wrecked boat. After reaching the Prototype Train, follow the railway through Sections 4, 3, 2, and 1, then return to Port Remnant for **16**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The 16 recordings are scattered across the Fulmar, Port Remnant, the church, and Anna Island's railway. Their numbers do not follow the order in which you encounter them: **11** can be picked up near the lighthouse, while **12** and **15** share a Repair Depot on different floors.

You can reach and unlock the Prototype Train without completing the collection. For the remaining recordings, use the location table to plan your stops around the railway instead of following numerical order.

<section id="fulmar-port-remnant">

## Wax Cylinders 01, 02, and 11: Fulmar and Port Remnant

These three recordings are available before you begin the long journey to the church. Collecting them early is especially useful because Cylinder 11 is numbered much later than the recordings found along Alice's opening route.

### Wax Cylinder 01 — Fulmar Cabin

The first recording is aboard the **Fulmar** at Hibernia Processing in Desolation Point.

Enter the boat's cabin before departing. Look for the cylinder player, the nearby recording, and the note marked **Play Me!**

![Wax Cylinder 01 beside the cylinder player and Play Me note inside the Fulmar cabin.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-01-fulmar-cabin.webp)

*Wax Cylinder 01 is on the Fulmar, before the crossing to Cauldron Island.*

Play the recording to begin Alice's investigation. Prepare the boat and depart once the required provisions have been supplied.

You do not have to return to the Fulmar later to obtain this recording if you collect it before the first crossing.

### Wax Cylinder 02 — Port Remnant Lighthouse

After the Fulmar reaches Cauldron Island, follow the harbor passage into the lighthouse.

Climb through the interior until you find **Alice's hand-drawn map**. Wax Cylinder 02 is nearby, beside the papers in the lighthouse.

![Wax Cylinder 02 inside the Port Remnant lighthouse, beside papers and Alice's map area.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-02-port-remnant-lighthouse.webp)

*The second recording is part of the lighthouse clues that direct you toward the church.*

### Wax Cylinder 11 — Wrecked Boat Near the Lighthouse

**Cylinder 11 is the easiest early recording to overlook.**

It is not necessary to finish the railway before collecting it. The recording is available in **Port Remnant**, on the wrecked boat near the lighthouse.

Before committing to the church route, explore the shoreline around the lighthouse and inspect the stranded vessel.

![Wax Cylinder 11 lying in the snow in Port Remnant, near the wrecked boat.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-11-port-remnant-boat.webp)

*Cylinder 11 can be collected during your first visit to Port Remnant despite its later number.*

The cylinder may be easier to notice when you are deliberately checking the vessel rather than scanning the railway from a distance.

If you have already reached the train without collecting 11, it remains a straightforward Port Remnant cleanup objective when you return.

</section>

<section id="church-whale-bones">

## Wax Cylinders 03–06: Desecrated Church and Whale Bone Shrine

The church contains three recordings. The fourth is outside, farther along the elevated terrain.

Reach the **Desecrated Church** through Port Remnant Cave rather than trying to climb the blocked hillside directly from the railway.

For the complete lighthouse, cave, and church approach, follow the [Anna Island walkthrough](/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-walkthrough).

### Wax Cylinder 03 — Inside the Church

Enter the church and examine the front interior.

Cylinder 03 is beside the raised stone furnishings near the front of the building.

![Wax Cylinder 03 near the front interior of the Desecrated Church in Port Remnant.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-03-desecrated-church.webp)

*The Desecrated Church contains three separate recordings. Check the front of the room before searching the window ledges.*

Collect 03 before leaving the building. Finding one recording inside the church does not mean you have finished collecting everything there.

### Wax Cylinders 04 and 05 — Church Window Ledge

The other two recordings are close together.

Examine the window ledge inside the same church. Two red-capped cylinders stand side by side.

![Wax Cylinders 04 and 05 together on a window ledge inside the Desecrated Church.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-04-05-desecrated-church.webp)

*Both recordings are on the same ledge, so collect each one separately before leaving.*

These recordings provide additional accounts of the island's industrial past.

Before departing, confirm that you have collected **03, 04, and 05**. Their proximity makes them easy to finish in one visit.

### Wax Cylinder 06 — Whale Bone Shrine

Leave the church and continue farther through the elevated area toward the **Whale Bone Shrine**.

Look for the distinctive whale-bone structures. The recording is resting on an outdoor bench near the shrine, alongside papers and the next map clue.

![Wax Cylinder 06 on a bench near the Whale Bone Shrine in Port Remnant.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-06-whale-bone-shrine.webp)

*Cylinder 06 provides the next stage of Alice's route, including the map fragment pointing toward the railway.*

Take both the recording and its attached map fragment before leaving the shrine. The map leads into Track Section Five; from there, [follow the waterfall and rope route toward the Prototype Train](/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-walkthrough).

</section>

<section id="train-shelter">

## Wax Cylinders 07 and 08: Track Section 6 Train Shelter

The next pair is at the **Train Shelter** in Track Section 6.

Follow the railway through Track Section 5, locate the waterfalls indicated by Alice's map, and use the climbing rope to reach Section 6.

Continue until the abandoned Prototype Train and its surrounding shelter come into view.

The important distinction here is between the **office beside the train** and the **work area**.

### Wax Cylinder 07 — Office Beside the Train

Enter the small office near the locomotive.

Cylinder 07 rests on a table beside **Alice McLeod's Journal 01** and other supplies.

![Wax Cylinder 07 on a table next to Alice McLeod's Journal 01 in the Train Shelter office.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-07-train-shelter-office.webp)

*Check the table carefully. The journal is another important item in the same room.*

### Wax Cylinder 08 — Train Shelter Work Area

Cylinder 08 is separate from the office recording.

Search the wooden work surfaces in the Train Shelter near the locomotive. The cylinder sits on a broad wooden surface among the workshop equipment.

![Wax Cylinder 08 on a wooden surface inside the Track Section 6 Train Shelter.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-08-train-shelter-workbench.webp)

*Cylinder 08 belongs to the shelter work area rather than the office table containing Cylinder 07.*

You should leave the Train Shelter with both **07 and 08** collected.

Before continuing around the railway, unlock the train and prepare it to travel. Later collection sites are distributed across sections that are much easier to reach with the locomotive.

</section>

<section id="track-section-four">

## Wax Cylinders 09 and 10: Track Section 4

Track Section 4 is the first major collection area after leaving the Train Shelter by locomotive.

It contains two different destinations:

- A **Repair Depot** with Cylinder 10.
- A **small hut near the blocked connection to the next island** with Cylinder 09.

These are not the same building.

### Wax Cylinder 09 — Hut Near the Future-Island Railway Bridge

At the Track Section 4 junction, take the branch toward the blocked railway connection to the next island. **Cylinder 09 is inside the small hut on the accessible side of the bridge.**

The next island cannot be entered in the current release. Search the hut, then return to the junction and continue around Anna Island.

### Wax Cylinder 10 — Track Section 4 Repair Depot

Return to the railway junction and approach the **Repair Depot**.

This is a large railway maintenance facility where you can bring the train into a sheltered workshop.

Enter the building and go upstairs. Cylinder 10 is on a shelf inside the upper level.

![Wax Cylinder 10 on an upper-floor shelf at the Repair Depot in Track Section 4.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-10-track-section-4-repair-depot.webp)

*Track Section 4 contains a Repair Depot with Cylinder 10 on the upper floor.*

Do not leave after inspecting only the main maintenance area. The recording is inside the building, above the ground-floor workshop space.

Once you have collected both 09 and 10, return to the main railway route toward Track Section 3.

</section>

<section id="track-section-three">

## Wax Cylinder 13: Track Section 3 Coastal Cabin

Cylinder 13 is away from the central railway.

In **Track Section 3**, continue past the first railway holding area and look for the coastal cave opening. The route leads toward a cabin on a headland overlooking the water.

Follow that side route rather than continuing past every branch on the train.

Inside the cabin, examine the shelving. Cylinder 13 rests on an upper shelf beside a large container.

![Wax Cylinder 13 on the upper shelf of a cabin in Anna Island Track Section 3.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-13-track-section-3-cabin.webp)

*The location label confirms Track Section 3. The cylinder is on the upper shelf rather than the main floor.*

This is one of the recordings most easily missed by players who remain aboard the train and only stop at railway service buildings.

Wildlife can be a concern around the coastal approach, so prepare for an outdoor detour before leaving the train.

After collecting 13, return to the railway and continue toward Track Section 2.

</section>

<section id="track-section-two">

## Wax Cylinders 12 and 15: Track Section 2 Repair Depot

Track Section 2 contains another **Repair Depot**.

Do not confuse it with the earlier depot in Track Section 4. The Track Section 2 facility contains **two recordings on different floors**, making it one of the most efficient locations for finishing multiple entries.

### Wax Cylinder 12 — Ground Floor

Enter the Track Section 2 Repair Depot and inspect the ground-floor work area.

Cylinder 12 lies on a flat metal cabinet or work surface.

![Wax Cylinder 12 on a metal cabinet inside the Track Section 2 Repair Depot.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-12-track-section-2-repair-depot.webp)

*The recording is inside the depot on the ground floor.*

You can also find the **McLeod Ultra-Efficiency Whale Oil Heater** in this facility. Check the ground floor for both items before moving upstairs.

The heater is useful equipment, but collecting it is not a requirement for completing the Wax Cylinder collection.

### Wax Cylinder 15 — Upstairs Sleeping Area

After collecting 12, go upstairs.

Look for the sleeping area with beds and a small bedside table. Cylinder 15 rests on the table, close to a bottle.

![Wax Cylinder 15 on a bedside table in the upstairs sleeping area of the Track Section 2 Repair Depot.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-15-track-section-2-repair-depot.webp)

*The upstairs room contains Cylinder 15, while Cylinder 12 is downstairs.*

This is an important distinction when checking your collection:

- Have **12** but missing **15**? Return upstairs.
- Have **15** but missing **12**? Search the ground-floor work area.

Collecting one does not automatically collect the other.

</section>

<section id="track-section-one">

## Wax Cylinder 14: Track Section 1 Cabin

Cylinder 14 is in a cabin beside the railway in **Track Section 1**.

As you follow the track toward Port Remnant, watch the left side for a group of buildings. One of the cabins can be entered.

Go inside and search the interior for Wax Cylinder 14.

The discovery label identifies the building as:

**Cabin — Anna Island, Track Section 1**

This is the easiest way to confirm you are searching the correct region rather than another visually similar cabin elsewhere on the island.

The recording can be difficult to spot in poor lighting. Bring a reliable light source, search the interior deliberately, and make sure the interaction identifies **Wax Cylinder 14** before leaving.

Do not assume every cabin beside the tracks is the correct one. Confirm the Track Section 1 location first.

Once you have 14, continue around the remaining railway toward Port Remnant.

</section>

<section id="return-port-remnant">

## Wax Cylinder 16: The Previously Locked Port Remnant Building

Cylinder 16 is the final recording on the railway loop.

It is inside a **previously inaccessible building in Port Remnant**.

During your first visit to Port Remnant, you can encounter a fenced industrial area with buildings you cannot fully access from the outside. You are not meant to complete this location during the opening walk to the church.

After obtaining the Prototype Train and following the railway around the island, return to Port Remnant by rail.

Approach the fenced area using the locomotive and break through the obstruction blocking the track.

![The damaged industrial building in Port Remnant reached near the end of the railway loop, where Wax Cylinder 16 is found.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/wax-cylinder-16-port-remnant-locked-building.webp)

*The building is associated with the previously inaccessible industrial area in Port Remnant. The train provides the route back into it.*

Enter the abandoned building after gaining access and search the interior for Cylinder 16.

The room is dark, so use a light source rather than relying on the recording to stand out against the background.

This final recording provides more context for the journey and the railway connections beyond Anna Island.

If you reach the building without the Prototype Train, the earlier barrier is the problem. Return after opening the railway route instead of searching for a hidden entrance from the settlement.

</section>


<section id="missing-cylinders">

## Missing One Wax Cylinder? Check These Locations First

Open **Collections** and identify the missing number before making another railway circuit. The recordings are numbered independently of the route you travel.

If you have already searched the main buildings, pay particular attention to three situations:

**A recording outside the expected sequence:** Cylinder 11 is near the Port Remnant lighthouse, even though its number comes after the Train Shelter recordings. Cylinder 09 requires a detour from the Track Section 4 junction toward the closed island connection.

**More than one recording at a stop:** The Desecrated Church contains 03, 04, and 05. The Train Shelter has 07 in the office and 08 in the work area. The Track Section 2 Repair Depot has 12 downstairs and 15 upstairs.

**A location away from the main railway:** Cylinder 13 requires a coastal cabin detour in Track Section 3, and 14 is inside a cabin in Track Section 1. Cylinder 16 is reached after returning to Port Remnant by train and opening access to the previously blocked industrial area.

For any missing number, use the location table above and inspect the named building before continuing to the next railway section.

</section>

<section id="completion">

## How Do You Confirm All 16 Wax Cylinders Are Collected?

After collecting the last recording, open **Collections** and inspect the Wax Cylinders entries. The initial collection runs from **01 through 16**; check that every number is present.

If an entry is missing, use its number to identify the location in the table above. Several recordings are available before the train is unlocked, while the later railway locations can be revisited after you obtain the locomotive.

Finishing the collection does not require entering the next island.

</section>
