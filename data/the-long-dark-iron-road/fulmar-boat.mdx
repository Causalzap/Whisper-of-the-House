<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Go to the **Hibernia Processing docks in Desolation Point** and board the **Fulmar**. Load its supply crate with **5,000 Calories of food, 20 kg of Fuel, 20 L of Water, and 5 Tinder Plugs**, then light the boat's boiler and use the throttle to travel to **Port Remnant** on Cauldron Island.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Fulmar is the boat that connects Great Bear to the new Cauldron Island region in The Long Dark: Iron Road. You can find it in an existing Survival save or a new game, but the crossing requires far more preparation than simply reaching the dock.

The real expense is the voyage itself. The boat demands enough supplies to cover an extended journey, and those provisions are consumed when you travel. Food placed into its supply crate is no longer available for your own survival after arriving. Food, water, fuel, and Tinder Plugs must be deposited into the boat's travel provisions rather than simply carried in your backpack.

A poorly prepared crossing can leave you spending several game days gathering firewood, melting snow, and searching Desolation Point for food before you ever reach Anna Island.

<section id="fulmar-location">

## Where to Find the Fulmar in Desolation Point

The Fulmar is moored at the **Hibernia Processing docks**, on the waterfront beside the large industrial facility in Desolation Point.

Cauldron Island is not a selectable Survival starting region. Reach it through the Fulmar after Iron Road is installed and enabled.

To reach it:

1. Travel to **Desolation Point**, using the normal connections from the existing Great Bear regions.
2. Make your way to **Hibernia Processing**, the large industrial complex near the coastline.
3. Head toward the waterfront docks outside the facility rather than spending time searching its interior rooms.
4. Look for the small vessel moored beside the dock and board it.

![The Fulmar boat at the Hibernia Processing docks in Desolation Point.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-hibernia-docks.webp)

*The Fulmar is docked outside Hibernia Processing. This is the departure point for Cauldron Island, not the Riken shipwreck.*

### What to do when you first board

There is a Wax Cylinder recording marked **"Play Me"** aboard the boat.

Play it before beginning your journey. Alice explains that the Fulmar has an improvised automatic navigation system that can bring someone from Great Bear to her location.

Her instructions establish three actions: load the required supplies, get the boiler running, and engage the throttle.

The recording also introduces the journey's destination, **Cauldron Island**. You do not have to manually steer across the ocean or navigate between islands during this crossing.

Once the recording finishes, inspect the boat's supply container and boiler. These are separate interactions, and preparing one does not automatically complete the other.

## How Much Food, Water, Fuel, and Tinder Does the Fulmar Need?

The Fulmar's initial crossing requires four categories of provisions.

- **Food — 5,000 Calories**
- **Fuel — 20 kg**
- **Water — 20 L**
- **Tinder — 5 Tinder Plugs**

These are supplies you deposit into the boat, not merely items that need to remain in your backpack.

![The Fulmar supply container showing its food, water, fuel, and tinder requirements.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-supply-requirements.webp)

*Check the remaining requirements in the Fulmar's supply container before leaving the dock. The counters determine what is still needed for departure.*

### Food: deposit 5,000 Calories without sacrificing your best provisions

The food requirement is based on calories rather than the total weight of the items you contribute.

This matters because two foods of similar weight can make very different contributions toward the 5,000-Calorie target.

Use the nutritional values shown in your inventory and the boat's supply interface. A collection of ordinary packaged foods, harvested meat, or other available provisions can fill the requirement; there is no need to reserve a particular named food for the voyage.

Before transferring everything, separate your food into two groups:

- **Voyage provisions:** Food that will be consumed by the Fulmar's supply requirement.
- **Personal provisions:** Food you want available after arriving at Port Remnant.

The distinction is easy to overlook when the boat's counter is nearly complete. Eating from your backpack reduces your own food stock without helping the deposited supply total, while transferring an item to the boat removes that food from your usable reserves.

One crossing preparation involved a player depositing maple syrup and later regretting the choice because the voyage consumed the provisions. The same calories could have been supplied using less valuable food.

For an established Survival character, bringing food from an existing stockpile is straightforward. Starting with an almost empty inventory is much more demanding, especially while gathering the other three categories.

### Water: prepare 20 liters before the final trip to the dock

Water is likely to be one of the longest preparation tasks.

The required **20 L** is substantially more than a normal short trip's drinking supply. At the usual water weight of 1 kg per liter, that quantity also represents a significant temporary carrying burden.

You do not need to transport all 20 liters in a single journey. Use the boat's supply container to accumulate provisions while you gather more resources.

A practical preparation sequence is:

1. Establish a safe fire and cooking location around Hibernia Processing.
2. Melt snow and boil water using your available cooking equipment.
3. Deposit completed water into the Fulmar's supply container.
4. Continue gathering wood and breaking down nearby crates while additional water is being prepared.
5. Repeat until the supply counter is satisfied.

A Cooking Pot is especially useful for reducing the number of separate water-cooking interactions. Without one, repeatedly preparing smaller quantities can turn this into a lengthy process.

Keep enough water for your own hydration instead of depositing every last liter. The boat's required provisions serve the voyage, not your drinking needs after arrival.

The efficient approach is to prepare water while completing other jobs around the industrial site, rather than making a separate expedition for every missing liter.

### Fuel: How Much Reclaimed Wood Does the Fulmar Need?

The Fulmar requires **20 kg of Fuel** in its travel provisions. The supply counter tells you how much more combustible material you need before departure.

A transfer of **12 Reclaimed Wood**, weighing **3.00 kg** in total, increases the Fuel counter from **8.70 kg to 11.70 kg**. In this case, the full inventory weight of the Reclaimed Wood counts toward the boat's fuel requirement.

![Twelve Reclaimed Wood being transferred to the Fulmar, increasing its fuel supply from 8.70 kg to 11.70 kg.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-fuel-contribution.webp)

*Transferring all 12 Reclaimed Wood adds 3.00 kg of Fuel, matching the wood's combined inventory weight.*

Reclaimed Wood is a practical choice because Hibernia Processing contains wooden objects that can be broken down without making a long trip away from the docks. Search accessible crates and pallets, gather the resulting wood, and deposit it into the Fulmar's supply container.

Continue adding accepted fuel until the counter reaches **20.00 kg**. The Reclaimed Wood example confirms its contribution, but it does not establish the conversion rate for every other combustible item.

Keep enough fuel and fire-starting equipment available to finish preparing drinking water and complete the departure sequence. Filling the boat's supply container should not leave you without the resources needed for your own survival.

### Tinder: finish the smaller requirement before the expensive gathering

Tinder is much easier to overlook because the water and fuel requirements dominate the preparation time.

Deposit **5 Tinder Plugs** before attempting departure. Completing the food, water, and fuel requirements will not compensate for a missing Tinder Plug.

Tinder Plugs can be prepared from sticks through the normal harvesting interaction where the relevant action is available. Keep the tinder you need for the boat separate from any fire-starting materials you are still using around Desolation Point.

## The Fastest Practical Way to Prepare the Fulmar

The largest avoidable loss of time comes from carrying supplies back and forth without checking which requirement is actually limiting departure.

An established Survival save has a substantial advantage. A character who already has tools, clothing, cooking equipment, and food stored near Coastal Highway or Desolation Point can use those reserves instead of beginning from nothing.

For a new or poorly stocked character, Hibernia Processing itself is the natural preparation area.

### Use Hibernia Processing as your supply base

The industrial facility gives you access to materials that serve two jobs at once.

Breaking down wooden objects supplies fuel for the Fulmar and can also support the fires needed to produce water.

Plan your preparation around those overlapping requirements.

Start by inspecting the boat and depositing any surplus food or water you already possess. Then check what remains. Gather combustible materials while a fire is available for melting snow, and return to the boat when you have enough supplies to make a meaningful deposit.

This is more efficient than carrying every piece of wood to the vessel immediately, discovering that water is still the main obstacle, and then returning to gather additional firewood for cooking.

A useful preparation order is:

1. **Inspect all four supply requirements.** Do not spend resources until you know what is missing.
2. **Deposit existing surplus food and water.** Keep your own immediate survival needs covered.
3. **Build up the fuel supply using accessible materials.** Test how much the counter increases before committing valuable fuel.
4. **Produce the remaining water while dismantling nearby wooden objects.**
5. **Complete Tinder and make a final supply check.**
6. **Rest, repair essential clothing, and prepare your personal inventory before departure.**

This order also helps avoid doing difficult inventory management after your character is already exhausted, overburdened, or running low on food.

### Do not carry the full boat requirement in your backpack

Twenty liters of water plus the food and fuel needed for the voyage can exceed the carrying capacity of a normal survivor.

Treat the Fulmar as a destination for staged deliveries.

Bring what you can, unload it into the appropriate supply interaction, and return for the rest. Carrying everything at once offers no meaningful advantage when the game allows the requirements to be filled over multiple visits.

The remaining weight matters most during the final journey, when you need enough capacity for personal equipment without leaving essential tools behind.

### What should stay in your personal inventory?

Before departure, decide what belongs to the voyage and what belongs to your actual exploration load.

The new island contains loot and shelter opportunities, but arriving with almost no usable supplies makes the first exploration more dangerous than necessary.

Prioritize:

- Reliable clothing appropriate for the conditions.
- A fire-starting kit with remaining uses.
- A way to prepare drinking water.
- Some food and water that have not been deposited into the boat's provisions.
- Essential medical supplies.
- Tools appropriate to your difficulty and existing equipment.

There is no need to carry every spare tool or weapon from your main base. Excess equipment reduces the room available for new supplies found after arrival.

The same principle applies to heavy raw materials. Do not move an entire stockpile to Cauldron Island before you know which materials you will actually need there.

## How to Start the Fulmar and Sail to Cauldron Island

Once the supply requirements are complete, the remaining task is to get the vessel running.

The Fulmar uses a boiler and an automatic navigation system. It is not a freely steerable boat with manual sea navigation.

Complete the departure sequence aboard the vessel:

1. Return to the Fulmar and check that the supply container's requirements are satisfied.
2. Prepare and light the boat's boiler using the available fire-starting interaction.
3. Use the boat's throttle or departure interaction.
4. Confirm the travel prompt for Cauldron Island.

The game transfers you to **Port Remnant** on Anna Island. Once ashore, head to the lighthouse for Alice's next clue. To reach the church after landing, [follow Alice's map clues through Port Remnant Cave](/the-long-dark-iron-road/anna-island-walkthrough).

You do not need to chart a route across the water. The Fulmar follows its established crossing automatically.

If interacting with the throttle does not initiate travel, inspect the supply container again before gathering more resources at random. Check the boiler separately as well; a lit boiler does not complete an unfinished food, water, fuel, or tinder requirement.

### The supplies are consumed during the crossing

After arriving at Port Remnant, the provisions deposited for the voyage are gone. Food, water, and fuel committed to the travel requirement should be treated as spent resources, not cargo waiting at the destination.

This is why valuable food belongs in your personal supplies rather than the voyage container whenever ordinary provisions can satisfy the requirement. Equipment stored separately aboard the Fulmar can still be retrieved after arrival.

<section id="return-trip">

## Can You Take the Fulmar Back to Great Bear?

Yes. The Fulmar provides a connection between **Port Remnant on Cauldron Island** and **Desolation Point on Great Bear**.

After the initial crossing, the vessel remains accessible at Port Remnant, and the return-travel interaction is available.

![The Fulmar at Port Remnant showing the option to return toward Great Bear.](/images/the-long-dark-iron-road/fulmar-port-remnant-return.webp)

*The Fulmar remains at Port Remnant after arrival. Use its travel interaction when you want to return to Desolation Point.*

This means the first trip to Cauldron Island is not a permanent one-way commitment.

You can use the connection to move between your existing Great Bear survival areas and the new island as your expedition progresses.

However, do not assume the provisions used for the outward journey remain stocked. Inspect the boat's current supply status before planning another crossing, especially when you have limited food or fuel.
