<h2 id="how-achievements-work">
  How I approach achievements in Breathedge 2 Chapter 1
</h2>

Breathedge 2 does not treat achievements like a separate challenge mode.

Most of them appear naturally while I am:

- following the main objectives;
- exploring wreckage and stations;
- interacting with strange objects;
- testing equipment;
- finishing optional projects.

The current Early Access build is structured around one playable Chapter 1
route, so I do not need to plan a perfect achievement run from the first minute.

The better approach is to keep a small checklist of unusual actions.

Normal exploration will handle many progression achievements, while the stranger
ones usually come from asking a simple question:

**"Would I interact with this if there was no marker telling me to?"**

That is where many hidden achievements come from.

<h2 id="before-ending">
  What I check before finishing Chapter 1
</h2>

The current story ends after the Chapter 1 sequence, but reaching that point
does not mean every achievement opportunity has disappeared.

Before completing the final story steps, I make sure I have checked:

- unusual objects around explored areas;
- optional interactions that look like jokes or experiments;
- hidden achievements already discovered;
- projects that require exploration rather than story progression.

The achievement system currently rewards curiosity more than grinding.

If I rush directly between objectives, I am more likely to miss a strange
interaction than a required story event.

<h2 id="after-ending">
  Can I clean up achievements after the Early Access ending?
</h2>

Yes.

Reaching the current Chapter 1 ending does not turn the save into a finished
achievement checklist.

After the story cutoff, I use the save as a cleanup file.

This is the right time to:

- revisit areas I skipped;
- complete optional interactions;
- finish exploration achievements;
- collect remaining resources;
- test objects I ignored while following the main route.

The current ending is a story boundary, not a completion boundary.

For the exact story cutoff and what remains available afterward, see the
<Link href="/breathedge-2/ending">

<h2 id="faq">
  Frequently asked questions
</h2>

<h3>
  Are Breathedge 2 achievements missable?
</h3>

The current Early Access build does not require a separate achievement-only
playthrough. The safest approach is checking unusual interactions before
finishing Chapter 1.

<h3>
  Can I unlock achievements after the current ending?
</h3>

Yes. The current ending stops the story progression, but exploration and
cleanup remain available in the save.

<h3>
  Should I collect every resource before chasing achievements?
</h3>

No. Resource gathering is useful, but achievement completion is mostly about
exploration and interaction rather than building a large stockpile.

<h3>
  Do I need to restart my save for achievements?
</h3>

No. A normal Chapter 1 save is enough for the current achievement system.
