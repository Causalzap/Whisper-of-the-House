<h2 id="assemble-attendant">
  Assemble the Attendant needs the body, head and missing arm
</h2>

I find the damaged Attendant close enough to the starting train that it is easy
to begin this objective while doing other Outskirts work.

The important part is knowing that **three separate pieces** have to come back.

Attaching only the body and head can make the model look almost complete, but
**Assemble the Attendant** remains active until the missing arm is installed as
well.

distant debris field.

<h2 id="attendant-arm">
  The missing arm is beside the blue goo bridge with three orange balls
</h2>

The arm is the real reason this quest can take much longer than expected.

Compass-style directions are not very useful here because one rotation in zero
gravity changes what “left” and “right” mean.

I use a cluster of physical landmarks instead:

- **blue goo / blue material** around the machinery;
- a **small metal bridge or platform**;
- **three orange ball-shaped objects** around the same structure.

Once I find all three together, I am in the correct search area.

The piece is floating **just off the small bridge**, not beside the damaged
Attendant.

And it is much smaller than the quest name makes it sound.

I am really looking for something closer to a **small forearm and hand** than a
complete shoulder-to-hand robot limb.

Once I reach the bridge, speed becomes the problem.

Flying broad loops past the structure makes the item easy to miss. I stay
close enough for interaction labels to appear and inspect the space immediately
around the bridge edge.

The search I use is:

1. get beside the small metal bridge;
2. face the blue machinery;
3. check just beyond the edge rather than the middle of the platform;
4. move slowly enough for the item prompt to appear;
5. look slightly above and below the same edge before changing position.

Depth matters here.

The arm can appear close to another object on screen while actually sitting a
few meters in front of or behind it, so a flat side-to-side sweep is not always
enough.

<h2 id="cant-find-arm">
  Still missing the arm? Return to the bridge instead of widening the search
</h2>

My least productive attempt was the one where I kept increasing the search
radius.

I checked around the Attendant, then farther under the station, then moved into
unrelated debris because I assumed a piece this hard to find had to be farther
away.

It was still beside the bridge.

If the arm has not appeared, I reset at the **blue machinery + three orange
balls** landmark and inspect that same small volume of space again.

The surroundings are full of narrow pipes, robot parts and scraps that can
imitate the silhouette. Getting close enough for the interaction prompt is more
reliable than trying to identify the arm visually from a distance.

A simple boundary also helps:

**if the blue goo machinery is no longer close to me, I have expanded the
search too far.**

If I have to leave for oxygen, I return to this same landmark afterward. I do
not restart from a random piece of wreckage and try to reconstruct the search
from memory.

<h2 id="complete-attendant">
  Installing the arm brings the Attendant online immediately
</h2>

With the body and head already attached, I carry the arm straight back to the
remaining socket.

There is no extra repair recipe after the third piece.

The Attendant activates and begins the **“Welcome to the Outskirts”**
introduction.
