For the hidden achievements, send a dead creature and then yourself into the **transport tube**. Separately, get a living creature and your character into a **Grinder**.

<h2 id="all-achievements">
  What Are All 12 Achievements in Can 'Em All?
</h2>

**Can 'Em All has 12 Steam achievements**, including four hidden achievements. Most come from regular factory progression, but several require actions you would not normally attempt while trying to maximize production.

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementColumns}
  rows={achievementRows}
/>

The **First Blood** achievement unlocks when you kill your first creature. The permanent upgrade with the same name is a separate purchase and is not required for this achievement.

Four hidden achievements involve unusual interactions with the transport tube or Grinder. **Collateral Damage** also requires a deliberate setup, since ten creatures must die from a single explosive bullet.

<h2 id="transport-tube-achievements">
  How Do You Get Not a Can and Special Delivery?
</h2>

Both achievements involve the same **transport tube**, but the object entering it determines which one you unlock.

The tube normally accepts finished cans for delivery. For these achievements, you deliberately send something else through it.

### Not a Can — Throw a Body Into the Transport Tube

**Not a Can** requires a dead creature, not a living one and not a processed can.

1. Kill a creature on the factory floor.
2. Collect its body before it enters a Grinder.
3. Carry or launch the body toward the transport tube used for finished cans.
4. Make sure the body actually enters the tube opening.

The body can be rejected and thrown back out. That reaction is normal: the tube is designed to receive cans, not unprocessed creatures.

Using the Grinder or delivering an ordinary can will not unlock **Not a Can**. The transport tube must receive an unprocessed creature's body.

You can attempt this early, before automated systems begin handling most bodies for you.

### Special Delivery — Throw Yourself Into the Transport Tube

**Special Delivery** uses the same tube, but this time you must enter it yourself.

Jump directly into the canned food transport tube rather than throwing an object inside. The interaction ends the workday, and Happy Meat Food Corp sends an **Unauthorized Tube Usage** warning afterward.

Finish any urgent deliveries before jumping in, since the workday ends without giving you another chance to collect those cans.

<h2 id="grinder-achievements">
  How Do You Unlock Fresh Meat and Human Meat?
</h2>

The other two hidden achievements involve the **Grinder**, the machine that normally converts creature bodies into cans.

One requires a creature to enter while still alive. The other requires your own character to enter.

### Fresh Meat — Grind a Living Creature

The requirement for **Fresh Meat** is:

**Grind a creature while it is still alive.**

The important distinction is the creature's condition when it enters the machine. Processing a dead body does not qualify, even though the Grinder performs its usual job.

To attempt it:

1. Leave at least one creature alive near an accessible Grinder.
2. Avoid shooting or otherwise killing that creature before it reaches the machine.
3. Get the living creature into the Grinder's intake.
4. Check whether Fresh Meat unlocks once the machine processes it.

Try this while the Grinder intake is clear enough to distinguish a living creature from the bodies waiting nearby. Keep the target alive until it enters the processor; a corpse sent through the same machine will not satisfy Fresh Meat.

### Human Meat — Enter the Grinder Yourself

**Human Meat** requires you to enter the meat processor.

This is separate from Special Delivery. Jumping into the canned food transport tube does not complete Human Meat.

Find an accessible Grinder and enter its processing opening with your character.

After this interaction, the company can send a message explaining that employees are not approved for processing and that human meat is not part of its product lineup.

Since you are deliberately entering dangerous machinery, finish any important work for the day before trying it.

<h2 id="collateral-damage">
  How Do You Get Collateral Damage With One Explosion?
</h2>

**Collateral Damage** requires **10 creatures to die at once from an explosive bullet**.

This is more specific than killing 10 creatures during the same workday. It also differs from gradually clearing a crowded factory using ordinary bullets.

The regular upgrade tree includes a chance for bullets to explode. Once that effect is available, a tightly grouped delivery provides an opportunity to hit the required number of creatures in a single blast.

A practical attempt looks like this:

1. Unlock the regular upgrade that gives bullets a chance to explode.
2. Wait for a truck to deliver a sufficiently large group of creatures.
3. Let the creatures remain close together instead of immediately spreading them across the factory.
4. Fire into the group and aim to trigger an explosion that kills at least 10 creatures at once.

A successful explosive-bullet attempt can happen soon after unlocking the effect. There is no need to wait until every late-game weapon has been purchased.

### Why Isn't Collateral Damage Unlocking?

The achievement may remain locked even when explosions are happening regularly.

Check the distinction between these situations:

- **Ten kills across several explosions:** Does not meet the single-event requirement.
- **A large explosion with survivors:** Only creatures actually killed by that explosion count.
- **Creatures killed by ordinary shots before the blast:** Those kills are not part of the explosive-bullet total.
- **Other explosive weapons or effects:** Do not assume they satisfy a requirement explicitly naming an explosive bullet.

Keep your next group together and try again with a fresh delivery.

The general damage and weapon-upgrade choices are covered in the <Link href="/can-em-all">factory upgrades and weapons route</Link>. For this achievement, the decisive part is how the ten kills occur.

<h2 id="feeding-achievements">
  How Do You Feed 100, 10,000, and 100,000 Children?
</h2>

Three achievements share the same objective but require different totals:

- **Feeding the Future:** Feed **100 children**.
- **Ending World Hunger:** Feed **10,000 children**.
- **World's Greatest Humanitarian:** Feed **100,000 children**.


Watch the **CHILDREN FED** counter on the factory display, not the **SLAUGHTERED** counter. These achievements track children fed through your canned food production, and there is no separate NPC or feeding location to visit.

Keep producing and delivering cans until the counter reaches each milestone. A creature killed on the factory floor is not the same as a completed delivery.

If the number is barely increasing, check whether finished cans are actually reaching the delivery system. For Grinder bottlenecks, conveyor setup, and production upgrades, follow the <Link href="/can-em-all">factory progression route</Link>.


<h2 id="remaining-achievements">
  How Do You Unlock A Fresh Start, The Perfect Employee, and Freedom at Last?
</h2>

These three achievements are tied to major progression decisions rather than isolated interactions with factory machinery.

They are also easy to confuse because the factory's ordinary upgrade tree, permanent upgrade tree, and final ending are three different objectives.

### A Fresh Start — Sell Your First Factory

**A Fresh Start** unlocks after you confirm your first **Sell Your Factory** transaction. Receiving the purchase offer alone does not count, and you do not need to complete the permanent upgrade tree.

Selling restarts your ordinary factory progress, so check <Link href="/can-em-all/prestige-ending">what you keep and lose after the sale</Link> before confirming.

### The Perfect Employee — Buy Every Permanent Upgrade


**The Perfect Employee** requires you to purchase every upgrade in the **Permanent Upgrade Tree**.

Completing all regular factory upgrades does not count, and simply holding unused Permanent Upgrade Points is not enough. Check that every permanent node has actually been purchased.

If you still have unpurchased nodes, earn more points by following the <Link href="/can-em-all/prestige-ending">factory sale and permanent upgrade route</Link>.


### Freedom at Last — Discover the Ending

The **Freedom at Last** achievement requires you to reach the ending, not merely purchase the final upgrade with the same name.

After paying off your debt, return to the upstairs exit and leave the factory. If the door is still locked, check the <Link href="/can-em-all/prestige-ending">Freedom purchase and exit conditions</Link> before attempting to leave again.
