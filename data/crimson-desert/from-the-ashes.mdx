<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Complete **Journey's End**, switch to **Damiane**, and open **Journal → Main Quests → From the Ashes**. Track **Reclaimed and Remaining**, then go to the protest in Demeniss and speak with **Countess Azerian** to begin.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="start-from-the-ashes">
  Where Do You Start From the Ashes in Crimson Desert?
</h2>

**From the Ashes** begins in the City of Demeniss, where Damiane returns to find that defeating Caliburn has not brought peace. People are fighting in the streets, supplies are running low, and supporters of the old regime are trying to turn public anger against the new government.

This is Damiane's nine-quest storyline in **Charting the Unknown**. You need to finish the main story through **Journey's End** before it becomes available.

Open the Journal and look for **From the Ashes** under Main Quests. Select **Reclaimed and Remaining** and follow the marker into Demeniss.

The opening marker leads to a public gathering near the pond in the city. **Countess Colette Azerian** is confronting an increasingly hostile crowd. Approach her to start the conversation.

Kliff intervenes when protesters begin throwing stones, and Colette returns to her manor. Damiane then sets out to restore order before the unrest spreads further.

You do not need to finish Kliff's **Beyond the Margins** or Oongka's **Forged Resolve** before progressing through this storyline.

<h2 id="all-nine-quests">
  All Nine From the Ashes Quests in Order
</h2>

The Journal follows this sequence:

1. **Reclaimed and Remaining**
2. **Demeniss in Upheaval**
3. **Countess Azerian's Whereabouts**
4. **Judgment of Fire**
5. **Schemes**
6. **Wavering Sentiment**
7. **Chasing a Shadow**
8. **Secret Summons**
9. **Path of the Politician**

Most objectives take place within Demeniss or the surrounding countryside. The route eventually leads to **Scorchrock Castle** before returning to the city for the conclusion.

Several objectives require more than reaching a map marker. You will need to resolve disturbances in Demeniss, examine two clues inside a burned depot, capture a fleeing agitator, and defeat a boss with two combat phases.

<h2 id="reclaimed-and-remaining">
  Reclaimed and Remaining: How Do You Quell the Chaos in Demeniss?
</h2>

After the opening confrontation, a highlighted area appears over part of Demeniss. Your objective is **Quell the Chaos in Demeniss (3x)**.

You need to resolve three disturbances. The route below covers the street attack, a trapped civilian, and a pickpocket. Watch your active quest counter as each incident finishes.

### Stop the Street Attack

Near the pond, find a group threatening a civilian in an alley.

Normal weapon attacks are restricted during this encounter. Approach the attackers and fight them unarmed.

Once the attackers retreat, speak with the civilian and make sure the incident has been resolved before searching for the next disturbance.

### Free the Man Trapped Beneath a Heavy Object

Look for a man calling for help because he cannot breathe.

Approach him and respond to his request to remove the object pinning him down.

Free him, then finish the conversation. Once he confirms that he can stand, the incident is resolved.

You do not need to carry this man to a healer during this encounter.

### Catch the Pickpocket and Hand Him Over to the Guards

Continue through the highlighted district until you hear someone call out about a stolen coin purse.

The victim identifies a fleeing pickpocket.

1. Chase the thief through the streets.
2. Catch and restrain him.
3. Follow the objective **Hand the thief over to the guards**.
4. Deliver him to the guards to complete the incident.

<GuideCallout title="Caught the Pickpocket but the Objective Is Still Active?">
Check whether the current objective says **Hand the thief over to the guards**. Keep the suspect restrained and complete the handover rather than leaving immediately after the tackle.
</GuideCallout>

After the three incidents have been resolved, follow the next objective. Damiane decides to seek Countess Azerian's counsel about the continuing unrest in Demeniss.

<h2 id="demeniss-in-upheaval">
  Demeniss in Upheaval: Where Is Countess Azerian?
</h2>

Head to **Azerian Manor** near the western gates of Demeniss and go upstairs to Colette's office. A servant explains that the Countess left on urgent business without saying where she was going, and nobody knows when she will return.

The search now moves outside the manor. Look for the **Wagon Driver** near the stables; he is the first witness who can help Damiane retrace Colette's movements.

<h2 id="countess-whereabouts">
  Countess Azerian's Whereabouts: Follow the Missing Countess
</h2>

The search follows a chain of witnesses. Each one points toward the next location.

### First Witness: Wagon Driver at the Manor

Leave the manor and look for the **Wagon Driver** near the stables.

He saw Colette leave on horseback with an armed escort, apparently heading beyond the city walls.

Speak with him until the objective advances.

### Second Witness: Woman at the Farmhouse

The next lead takes you to a farmhouse across the road from the manor.

Find the woman carrying a broom and ask about the Countess.

She initially hesitates, but eventually reveals that several men with their faces covered followed Colette toward the forest.

The important clue is the group following her, not the possibility that she went hunting.

### Third Witness: Noble Near the Forest Camp

Travel southwest of Demeniss toward the forest, west of **Mistveil Forest**.

Find the camp indicated by your objective and speak with the Noble there.

He confirms seeing the Countess's party and a suspicious group moving in the same direction. He then points you toward an abandoned house across the road.

Go directly to that house.

### Examine the Suspicious Traces at the Abandoned House

The house does not contain Colette waiting to be rescued.

Instead, the courtyard contains a clue to what happened.

Raise your **lantern** and inspect the **Suspicious Traces** memory.

The memory reveals that Colette was confronted by hostile men who demanded that she withdraw from the political struggle. They threatened to bring a **judgment of fire** upon Demeniss if she refused.

Her escort was overwhelmed, and she disappeared during the confrontation.

After examining the memory, the next emergency arrives: a soldier reports an explosion at the city's Central Supply Depot.

You do not need to continue searching the abandoned house for a hidden captive. The storyline now moves to the depot.

<h2 id="judgment-of-fire">
  Judgment of Fire: Where Are the Two Fire Investigation Clues?
</h2>

The depot investigation is one of the easiest places to lose time because **both clues must be examined**, and they are located on different floors.

Damiane arrives at the burning **Demeniss Central Supply Depot** and helps coordinate the immediate response.

After the opening sequence, the objective changes to **Investigate the Cause of the Fire (2x)**.

Finding one suspicious object does not complete the investigation.

### Clue 1: Torchlight and Oil

Start from inside the damaged depot.

Go **up one floor** and search for an investigation prompt near the floor.

Examine the discarded torch.

The clue, **Torchlight and Oil**, establishes that the torch was soaked with oil. The blaze was deliberately accelerated rather than spreading from an ordinary accident.

### Clue 2: Fire's Deepest Scar

On the next floor, locate the body of the **Warehouse Manager**.

Interact with the body and enter the examination view.

Move the examination focus toward his **head and neck**, then inspect the highlighted injury.

The second clue, **Fire's Deepest Scar**, indicates that the manager suffered a lethal wound from a sharp object before the fire consumed the building.

<GuideCallout title="Stuck at Investigate the Cause of the Fire (1/2)?">
Check the upper floor for the Warehouse Manager's body. Reaching him is not sufficient: complete the examination interaction and inspect the highlighted injury to register the second clue.
</GuideCallout>

### Leave the Depot and Report Your Findings

Once both clues are registered, exit the building and speak with the **Politician** outside.

The depot has lost rations, medicine, grain, and other supplies. Damiane suspects that Caliburn's remaining supporters are responsible, but she cannot yet prove who ordered the attack.

This distinction matters to the story. You have identified **arson**, not the person who organized it.

The next objective begins when Damiane notices someone watching her from nearby.

<h2 id="schemes">
  Schemes: Catch the Suspect and Find the Hideout
</h2>

Move toward the marked area where the suspicious person disappeared.

Kliff arrives during the confrontation and warns Damiane that acting out of anger could make the situation worse.

Two suspects attempt to escape. Kliff pursues one; Damiane goes after the other.

### How to Catch the Fleeing Suspect

Follow the runner immediately.

Stay close enough for the **tackle** interaction to appear, then use it before the suspect escapes.

This is a pursuit objective. Stopping to fight unrelated enemies or trying to attack the suspect from a distance is unnecessary.

Although the suspect dies before he can answer Damiane's questions, he carries orders that reveal a hideout.

Continue with the new location marker.

### Find the Entrance Near Marshell Estate

The orders lead to a site northwest of **Azerian Manor**, near the Marshell estate.

Look for the marked entrance in the ground.

Approach the opening to trigger the infiltration sequence.

Inside the hideout, Damiane discovers plans for an upcoming assembly. The documents include details about those attending, their travel, and the event's timing.

The conspirators are not simply trying to frighten the city. They intend to disrupt the assembly and make the new leadership appear incapable of governing.

Kliff joins Damiane during the investigation.

After the scene, follow him on horseback back toward Azerian Manor.

### Speak to Naira and Andrew

At the manor, meet **Naira**, **Andrew**, and the other allies investigating the attacks.

Their evidence identifies bulk purchases of materials used for arson, connected to someone working for **Countess Marshell**.

The investigation produces the **Assembly Sabotage Plan**.

At this point, the plot's immediate objective is clearer: the enemy wants the city to lose confidence in the reformers before the assembly can take place.

The next step is to deal with the fear already spreading through Demeniss.

<h2 id="wavering-sentiment">
  Wavering Sentiment: How Do You Find the Rumor Spreader?
</h2>

Find Kliff downstairs at Azerian Manor and accompany him into the city.

The pair encounter citizens breaking into a warehouse because they believe the remaining food will soon run out.

After the confrontation, Kliff takes responsibility for securing provisions while Damiane speaks directly with those affected by the disaster.

You must complete **two citizen observations** before the investigation moves to the agitator.

### First Observation: The Woman Near the Burned Area

Follow the marker to a ruined area and talk to the woman holding a broom.

She lost her husband in the depot explosion and no longer trusts promises from those in power.

After the conversation, pick up the nearby **broom** and equip it as instructed.

Sweep the ashes until the next scene begins.

Do not immediately leave after talking to the woman. The cleanup interaction is part of the objective.

### Second Observation: Citizens Near the Abyss Nexus

The next marker is north of the **Abyss Nexus** in Demeniss.

Talk to the woman standing near a table.

The citizens here are frightened by food shortages and angry about Colette's disappearance. During the exchange, Damiane learns that a man nearby has been telling the crowd that the Countess abandoned them.

Follow this lead toward the gathering at the end of the street.

### Identify and Chase the Agitator

The suspect is addressing a crowd, spreading the claim that Colette fled and that the reformers have deliberately failed the city.

Confront the **Agitator**, identifiable in the crowd by his green hat.

After the argument, he attempts to escape.

1. Chase him through the streets.
2. Catch and restrain him using the available capture interaction.
3. Keep him secured instead of attacking him.
4. Take him to the **guards near the southwestern outer walls**.
5. Complete the interrogation.

<GuideCallout title="Caught the Agitator but the Quest Has Not Advanced?">
Capturing him is only the first part. He must be delivered to the guards for questioning. The location of his meetings is revealed during that interrogation, not simply by tackling him.
</GuideCallout>

The prisoner describes a hooded contact who met him at the ruins of **Scorchrock Castle**.

Andrew and Naira arrive with another report pointing to the same location.

That is enough to begin the castle operation.

<h2 id="chasing-a-shadow">
  Chasing a Shadow: How Do You Enter Scorchrock Castle?
</h2>

Scorchrock Castle lies west of Mistveil Forest and north of the **Lower Nas River**.

Travel to the marked ruined stronghold and meet the others.

The group decides against a direct assault by everyone at once. If Colette is being held inside, warning the guards too early could put her in danger.

Andrew and Naira create a distraction while Damiane enters the stronghold.

Follow the active marker through the **main gate** and fight your way into the central courtyard.

You must defeat the hostile soldiers blocking the route before reaching the enemy commanding them.

This leads to the battle against **Geago the Flamecrazed**.

<h2 id="geago-boss">
  How Do You Beat Geago the Flamecrazed?
</h2>

Geago is the arsonist responsible for much of the destruction in Demeniss.

He has been working for Countess Marshell, but his dialogue makes clear that he also takes pleasure in violence and fire.

The crucial mechanic is that **Geago has two combat phases**.

Defeating his first form does not end the encounter.

### Phase 1: Geago in Heavy Armor

Geago enters the fight in heavy armor, attacking with a large weapon that makes careless close-range exchanges dangerous.

Let his committed attacks finish before attempting a counter. Damiane can use the recovery window to deal damage, but continuing the attack for too long risks getting caught by his next strike.

Preserve enough Health and recovery supplies for what follows. The first time his Health is depleted, the encounter changes rather than ending.

### Phase 2: Geago Removes His Armor

Geago removes his heavy armor after the first phase. The fight continues immediately, so do not lower your guard when the change begins.

His movement is no longer restricted by the same equipment. Watch his new attack timing before committing to another combination, and create distance if you need to recover.

Only after defeating this second form can Damiane continue the rescue objective inside Scorchrock Castle.

### What Happens After Geago Is Defeated?

Move close enough to collect the **Abyss Artifact** dropped after the fight.

Then continue the rescue objective.

The castle still contains a prisoner, so defeating Geago does not immediately establish Countess Azerian's whereabouts.

This quest also awards **Ashen Blitz**.

<h2 id="rescue-countess">
  Where Is the Prisoner in Scorchrock Castle?
</h2>

After the boss fight, go upstairs and follow the objective toward the guarded room.

Defeat the soldiers blocking the entrance and enter.

Andrew and Naira arrive to assist, but the prisoner is **not Countess Colette Azerian**.

This is an important story outcome. The operation at Scorchrock Castle exposes the conspiracy and defeats the arsonist, but it does not successfully locate Colette.

There is no need to search every tower for a second hidden prisoner after the rescue sequence concludes.

Finish the conversation and return toward Demeniss as directed.

Damiane now faces a different problem: the immediate attackers have been dealt with, but the Countess's absence continues to undermine the political reforms.

<h2 id="secret-summons">
  Secret Summons: Where Do You Find the House in the Letter?
</h2>

Return to **Azerian Manor**.

A bird delivers a secret message.

Read the **Secret Letter** to advance the objective. Its wording describes a secluded house along a westward path, away from the city and beneath the surrounding trees.

The clue sounds as though you might need to search the entire countryside, but the quest directs you toward a specific area.

### Where to Go After Reading the Letter

Travel **southwest of the City of Demeniss**, toward the area near **Meadowlark Ranch**.

Follow the updated location marker.

Near the road, find the person waiting for Damiane and approach them.

They lead her to a secluded building.

Follow the encounter into the house and down into its hidden lower level.

The basement conceals a secure meeting place where Colette has been hiding.

### What Is Inside the Safehouse?

Damiane finds **Countess Azerian** alive, along with **King Edward Thorel**.

The king's apparent illness and the Countess's disappearance were connected to a secret effort to protect him after an attempted poisoning.

The person lying in the royal palace is a body double. Colette moved the real king to safety while investigating the conspiracy.

This discovery changes the political situation completely.

The king orders the nobles to assemble and prepares to challenge Countess Marshell openly.

You do not need to fight another boss inside the safehouse. Continue the conversations and follow the story into the assembly.

<h2 id="kings-edict">
  Secret Summons: What Happens at the Assembly?
</h2>

At the gathering, King Thorel reveals that he has recovered and that the old order cannot continue.

He announces that the law must apply to everyone, including the king himself.

Countess **Ada Marshell** attempts to challenge him, but he orders her arrest on charges relating to treason, kidnapping, and the fire.

The confrontation is resolved through the story sequence rather than a duel with Damiane.

After the assembly, King Thorel privately entrusts Damiane with his final will.

The document announces the end of the monarchy and a new political future for Demeniss.

You receive the **Edict of King Thorel**.

Completing this part of the storyline also awards the **Demenissian Council** clothing set:

- Demenissian Council Robes
- Demenissian Council Cloak
- Demenissian Council Gloves
- Demenissian Council Boots

The story is not quite finished when you receive the document. Damiane still needs to present it to the people.

<h2 id="path-of-the-politician">
  Path of the Politician: How Do You Finish From the Ashes?
</h2>

The final quest returns Damiane to the public square where the storyline began.

Leave the **Demeniss Audience Hall** and follow the objective outside.

Approach the nobles near the balcony to trigger the next sequence.

Then continue toward the speaker's platform overlooking the plaza by the pond.

Damiane must formally proclaim **King Thorel's Edict** to the people of Demeniss.

Kliff and the other Greymanes are present as Damiane addresses the crowd.

Complete the speech and the concluding scene to finish **Path of the Politician**, the ninth and final quest of From the Ashes.

The story ends here. Returning to Scorchrock Castle, searching Azerian Manor again, or revisiting the depot is not required for the main questline's completion.

<h2 id="from-the-ashes-rewards">
  What Rewards Do You Get for Completing From the Ashes?
</h2>

The most notable rewards arrive during the final three quests, rather than from the early investigations.

**Chasing a Shadow** awards:

- **Abyss Artifact**
- **Ashen Blitz**

**Secret Summons** awards the four-piece **Demenissian Council** clothing set: Robes, Cloak, Gloves, and Boots.

You also receive the **Edict of King Thorel** during the concluding political sequence.

The first six quests primarily advance the story and do not award equivalent named equipment.

If you are looking for the Abyss Artifact after the Geago encounter, check the boss's defeated location before leaving the castle.

<h2 id="not-progressing">
  Why Is From the Ashes Not Progressing?
</h2>

Several objectives require a second interaction after the obvious action has been completed.

The most common points to check are:

- **Reclaimed and Remaining:** Resolve three disturbances in the highlighted district. If you catch the pickpocket, deliver him to the guards; stopping him alone does not finish that incident.
- **Countess Azerian's Whereabouts:** The abandoned house contains a Suspicious Traces memory. Simply reaching the courtyard does not finish the investigation.
- **Judgment of Fire:** Both clues must be collected. The Warehouse Manager's body contains the second clue.
- **Wavering Sentiment:** The broom interaction and delivery of the captured Agitator are both required.
- **Chasing a Shadow:** Geago has two phases, and the prisoner rescue continues after his defeat.
- **Secret Summons:** Read the letter, follow the updated location marker, and approach the waiting contact near Meadowlark Ranch.

If the **From the Ashes** questline never appeared in the first place, confirm that **Journey's End** is finished and Damiane is selected. The other two characters' questlines do not substitute for that requirement.
