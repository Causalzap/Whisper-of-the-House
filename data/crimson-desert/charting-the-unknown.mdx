Update and activate **Charting the Unknown**, then open your **Journal**. For ships, finish **Howling Hill** and expand Greymane Camp to unlock **A Wider World**. For the three new character stories and **Garden of Trials**, complete **Journey's End** first.

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How Do You Start Charting the Unknown DLC?
</h2>

**Charting the Unknown** does not begin with one quest that unlocks everything. The ocean, new character stories, housing, and combat challenges each have different requirements.

That distinction matters when returning to an existing Crimson Desert save. A character who has not finished the main story can still unlock ocean exploration, while a character with a completed story may need to finish earlier Greymane Camp objectives before the shipbuilding quest appears.

Start by checking three things:

1. Make sure **Charting the Unknown** is installed and your game has been updated to version **2.04.00 or later**.
2. Open the **Journal** and check whether **Howling Hill**, the Greymane faction quests, and **Journey's End** are complete.
3. Choose the activity you want instead of searching the map for a single DLC starting location.

The earliest major addition to pursue is usually **A Wider World**, provided your Greymane Camp progression is far enough along. It introduces shipbuilding and the first ocean expedition without requiring the final main-story quest.

Players who have already completed Journey's End can also begin the three character stories and challenge Garden of Trials.


Your next activity depends on what you want to accomplish with your current save.

### Continue the Ocean Expedition

Open **Journal → Faction Quests** and track **A Wider World**. Begin with **A Call from Beyond the Horizon**, then follow the objectives until Shipbuilding becomes available.

Choose this route if you want to explore islands, manage a ship, or reach the new maritime activities.

### Start a Character Story

Open **Journal → Main Quests** and select the character you want to play. Kliff's **Beyond the Margins**, Damiane's **From the Ashes**, and Oongka's **Forged Resolve** have separate progress counters.

You can switch between their objectives without finishing all three in a fixed order.

### Enter Garden of Trials

Travel toward the **Axiom Archive Abyss** and locate the challenge entrance. Prepare your characters and equipment before entering, especially because food recovery is disabled inside.

For other activities, select the corresponding destination or interaction from the unlock table above.

<h2 id="a-wider-world">
  Where Does A Wider World Start?
</h2>

The ocean storyline begins with **A Call from Beyond the Horizon**, the opening objective within **A Wider World**.

Once the relevant camp expansion is complete, open the faction quest section of your Journal and track it. The first objective sends you to speak with your comrades about exploring beyond Pywel's shores.

After the opening conversation, select a port on the world map and open **Details → Missions → Shipbuilding**.

Start a construction dispatch with eligible comrades, or purchase a vessel from a Fleetmaster. A newly built ship still needs its required crew assigned before departure.

If the dispatch cannot start, inspect the worker skills and available camp resources rather than visiting another port. The <Link href="/crimson-desert/ships">shipbuilding and crew instructions</Link> cover the Engineering requirement, construction materials, purchase prices, and sailing controls.

### What Happens on the First Ocean Expedition?

After your ship is ready, continue the faction objectives toward the harbor and prepare for **Where the Wind Blows**.

The opening voyage introduces sailing, the Harpoon Gun, and an uncharted island. As you explore the island, you encounter the **Pioneering** system, which allows the Greymanes to establish an Anchorage and begin further missions.

You do not need a large ship for this first expedition. A functioning small vessel with its required crew is enough to begin learning the ocean systems.

Save expensive ship upgrades until you know whether you need more cargo space, more crew capacity, or greater combat capability.

<h2 id="new-character-stories">
  How Do You Start the New Kliff, Damiane, and Oongka Quests?
</h2>

Three separate character storylines become available after **Journey's End**, the final quest of the original main story.

Open the Journal and look under **Main Quests**. Each storyline has its own progress counter, and the game does not require you to complete all three in a fixed order.

### Kliff — Beyond the Margins (13/13)

After **Journey's End**, open **Main Quests** and begin **A Moment's Respite**. Kliff's 13-objective storyline starts in Pailune before leading into the desert beyond the sandstorm.

The difficult stretch involves corrupted Marker Stones, isolated villages, and the final encounter with **Kharzek, Archon of Antumbra**. Defeating Kharzek is not the final requirement; return to Sebastian to finish the storyline.

Follow the <Link href="/crimson-desert/beyond-the-margins">Beyond the Margins walkthrough</Link> for the full quest order and desert navigation steps.

### Damiane — From the Ashes (9/9)

Damiane's storyline is recorded as **From the Ashes**, with nine objectives.

Once Journey's End is complete, select her storyline in the Main Quests Journal and follow its opening objective.

Damiane's new content is connected with political conflict and the events affecting Pywel after the original campaign.

One important later requirement involves **Politician**, a Damiane quest tied to access to Flame Knight's Castle.

Do not assume that completing Kliff's desert story will also unlock Damiane's castle-related rewards. Her quest progression and the relevant stronghold liberation are separate requirements.

### Oongka — Forged Resolve (10/10)

Oongka's individual storyline is **Forged Resolve**, containing ten objectives.

Select it in Main Quests after finishing Journey's End, then follow the active objective for Oongka.

Like Damiane's story, it has a separate Journal counter and does not require completing Beyond the Margins first.

The three new character stories can be progressed independently, so returning to the Journal is the correct move when you want to switch between them.

<h2 id="underwater-exploration">
  How Do You Dive Underwater and Explore the Ocean?
</h2>

Underwater exploration does **not** require completing A Wider World or Journey's End first.

The expansion introduces a **Breath** gauge that limits how long your character can remain underwater. Entering the sea begins consuming that resource, so check it before swimming toward a distant wreck or diving deep beneath the surface.

A practical approach is to begin with a shallow coastal dive, become familiar with the Breath gauge, and return to the surface before attempting a longer underwater route.

### How to Increase Breath Capacity

Your diving suit can be refined to increase maximum Breath capacity.

Food that restores Breath provides another way to extend underwater exploration, but it does not replace the need to manage the gauge while moving between locations.

For underwater combat or exploration requiring the **A.T.A.G**, obtain the equipment that allows it to operate below the surface.

You can also encounter new underwater creatures that may be registered as mounts. Depending on the creature, raising Trust involves feeding it or subduing it.

### What Should You Explore First?

Start near accessible coastal locations before committing to deep-water treasure hunting. The expansion adds underwater ruins, treasure chests, shipwrecks, and new creatures, but traveling farther from the surface increases the cost of running out of Breath.

Once you have a ship, you can reach more distant islands and explore nearby waters from your vessel.

Uncharted islands can also be pioneered through Greymane missions, turning a discovered location into a more useful base for repeated visits.

<h2 id="romance-marriage">
  How Do Romance and Marriage Work in Charting the Unknown?
</h2>

Romance uses the expanded **Trust** system.

Approach an eligible NPC, open **Interact**, and select **Flirt**. From there, use **Gift** or **Chat** to build the additional relationship level.

The important limit is that each type of interaction can raise this additional Trust level only **once per day**.

Repeatedly selecting the same interaction before its cooldown expires will not provide another Trust increase.

### How to Propose Marriage

Build the additional romance Trust level to **100** with an eligible NPC.

Once that requirement is satisfied, the proposal option becomes available, provided the NPC's other conditions have been met.

The fastest reliable routine is to return when the relevant interactions are available again instead of repeatedly attempting gifts or conversations during their cooldown.

You can check the interaction interface for the wait time before the same action can increase Trust again.

### Why Is Flirt Missing for Some NPCs?

Not every NPC can be approached through the same relationship conditions.

Some eligible characters require additional personal quests before romance is available.

**Witches are a notable example:** you must complete the relevant witch's **Sanctum quest** before pursuing that relationship.

Changing playable characters can also affect an NPC's Trust. Flirting with a particular NPC as one character reduces that NPC's Trust with the other playable characters.

For that reason, decide which character you want to pursue the relationship with before repeatedly building romance Trust across the roster.

### What Changes After Marriage?

A spouse gains a map and minimap icon. You can travel together, and the spouse may occasionally provide gifts.

The expansion also allows you to change your spouse's appearance at a barber shop.

After building or obtaining a residence, you can designate it as a home for your spouse.

Marriage is not irreversible, but ending the relationship has a cost. Avoid treating a proposal as a temporary interaction if you are still deciding which character and NPC you want together.

<h2 id="housing-castles">
  How Do You Unlock Free Construction and New Houses?
</h2>

The upgraded Housing system has a different requirement from shipbuilding.

You must establish your Greymane Camp at **Howling Hill** and complete **Luke's Request** under the **Grounds of the Sunrise** Greymane faction quests.

Once both conditions are met, enter housing customization and choose:

**Home Decoration → Layout → Free Construction**

This mode allows you to change the actual structure of your home rather than merely place decorations inside an existing room.

You can adjust the layout and add structural elements such as walls and windows.

If the option does not appear, check Luke's Request and your camp setup before looking for different building materials.

### How Many Residences Were Added?

Charting the Unknown adds **17 residences** that can be constructed through dispatch missions.

Once you have an appropriate residence, you can designate it as the home where your spouse will live.

This is separate from placing decorative objects. Building a residence and decorating a house are related activities, but they are not the same menu action.

### How to Unlock Drakesfall Castle and Flame Knight's Castle

Two castle properties have specific progression requirements.

**Drakesfall Castle**

- Complete **Journey's End**.
- Liberate the corresponding stronghold.
- Claim the castle once its requirements are satisfied.

**Flame Knight's Castle**

- Complete Damiane's **Politician** quest.
- Liberate the corresponding stronghold.
- Claim the castle after the necessary progression.

Both properties can be decorated after acquisition.

Do not spend time searching for an ordinary housing merchant to unlock these castles. Their access is tied to story and stronghold progression, not simply the amount of money available.

<h2 id="facility-management">
  How Do You Rent and Manage Facilities?
</h2>

Facility management allows you to operate businesses in liberated regions.

First establish the Greymane Camp, then complete the faction quest needed to liberate the region containing the facility you want to manage.

Open the world map and select the **Facilities** tab. This is the starting point for checking available management locations.

The new businesses include:

- Restaurants
- Sawmills
- Smithies
- Tailor's shops

The main distinction from ordinary camp production is that these facilities are rented and managed rather than simply harvested as resources.

### What Should You Check Before Renting?

Select the facility and inspect its rental cost, operating expenses, and any available staffing or management options.

A sawmill can require an upfront payment and continuing upkeep, so the starting rental fee alone does not tell you whether the business will be profitable.

For example, one early sawmill setup displayed a **15,000** rental cost and **20,000** in daily upkeep. Those figures make the ongoing expense more important than the initial entry price.

Before committing, consider whether you can sustain the operating cost and whether the facility has the workers and resources needed to function.

If your existing camp activities are already short of personnel or materials, taking on another operating expense can make the shortage worse.

Once active, facilities can continue operating while you are away. Special guests may also visit managed businesses, and completing management activities can provide rewards.

A profitable outcome should be judged from the facility's actual operation rather than assuming the rental price guarantees a return.

### What Are Camp Festivals For?

Greymane Camp can also host festivals once the relevant mission becomes available.

NPCs with sufficient Trust may visit for a limited time. Interacting with them during the festival helps build Trust more quickly.

Certain visitors can become **Honorary Greymanes**, allowing them to participate in dispatch missions, although they may not have access to every feature available to ordinary comrades.

This makes festivals particularly useful when you want to improve relationships or expand the personnel available for activities outside combat.

<h2 id="garden-of-trials">
  How Do You Unlock and Survive Garden of Trials?
</h2>

**Garden of Trials** becomes available after completing **Journey's End**.

The entrance is located near the **Axiom Archive Abyss**. Travel to that area after finishing the main story and enter the challenge when you are ready for consecutive battles.

Garden of Trials contains **10 challenge groups**.

You can enter alone or choose a party of up to three characters from **Kliff, Oongka, and Damiane**. The game also allows you to select the order in which your characters enter combat.

### Why Does Character Order Matter?

If your active character is defeated, the next character in the selected order continues the battle.

This changes how you should prepare.

Put the character whose moveset and defensive timing you know best in the opening position. Keep another capable fighter available if the first character cannot finish the encounter.

Starting with your least familiar character simply to save your strongest one may create unnecessary risk, especially before you understand the enemies in the current group.

### Can You Use Food to Heal in Garden of Trials?

**No. Food cannot be used for recovery during Garden of Trials.**

This is the most important preparation difference from ordinary boss encounters.

Check your skills and equipment before entering rather than planning to solve every mistake with consumable healing.

The <Link href="/crimson-desert/abyss-artifacts-guide">Abyss Artifacts guide</Link> can help you decide whether Health, Stamina, Spirit, or a skill upgrade is the most useful investment for your current build.

### What Do You Get for Completing the Challenges?

Completing each Garden of Trials group enhances one of your existing skills.

Because the challenge consists of ten groups, the progression is not just a single final-boss reward.

You can treat each completed group as a meaningful upgrade milestone before attempting the next.

<h2 id="dlc-not-showing">
  Why Is Charting the Unknown Content Not Showing Up?
</h2>

When the expansion is installed but a feature seems missing, check its specific requirement before assuming the entire DLC has failed to activate.

The most useful checks are:

- **A Wider World is missing:** finish Howling Hill, then advance Greymane Camp expansion through the relevant faction quests.
- **Ships are unavailable:** complete the opening ocean quest conversation, then use a port's Shipbuilding mission or a Fleetmaster.
- **Beyond the Margins, From the Ashes, or Forged Resolve is missing:** confirm that Journey's End is complete and check Main Quests.
- **Garden of Trials is unavailable:** complete Journey's End and look near the Axiom Archive Abyss.
- **Free Construction is locked:** finish Luke's Request after setting up Howling Hill Camp.
- **A facility cannot be managed:** check whether the relevant region has been liberated and its faction progression completed.
- **Flirt is missing for a particular NPC:** check that the NPC is eligible and whether a personal requirement, such as a witch's Sanctum quest, remains unfinished.

If every relevant condition is complete, check that the DLC is enabled on your platform and that the game has installed the expansion update.

### Can You Use a DLC Save Without Charting the Unknown Installed?

A save created while Charting the Unknown is installed cannot be loaded in a game version without the DLC.

That restriction also applies to cross-save between a DLC-enabled installation and one that does not contain the DLC.

Before switching platforms or loading the same progression on a different installation, make sure the destination has the required DLC available.

Keep a separate backup of an older save if you intend to preserve the option of playing without the expansion.

For players returning after a long break, checking the installed game version and the Journal is more useful than immediately starting a new campaign. Most of the new activities are designed to unlock through existing progression rather than requiring a fresh character.
