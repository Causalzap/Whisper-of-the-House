<h2 id="reach-auronui-island">
  How Do You Reach Auronui Island in Crimson Desert?
</h2>

Auronui Island is the first major island destination introduced by the **A Wider World** faction quest in **Crimson Desert: Charting the Unknown**.

The island lies in the southern sea, beyond the coast near Vellua. Reaching it is straightforward once you own a functioning ship, but establishing an Anchorage is a separate objective.

This distinction causes much of the confusion surrounding the first island expedition. Landing on Auronui does not automatically complete the Pioneering mission.

Before leaving the mainland, make sure you have:

- Unlocked **A Wider World** through Greymane Camp progression.
- Built or acquired a ship that can carry your crew.
- Assigned the necessary Deckhands through a Ship Manager.
- Enough available comrades and currency for the island dispatch.

If you have not unlocked ships yet, follow the <Link href="/crimson-desert/ships">Crimson Desert ships guide</Link> to complete the first Shipbuilding mission at Vellua.

You do not need to complete the main story's **Journey's End** to begin this ocean questline.

### A Wider World Quest Order

The introductory ocean storyline contains six quests:

1. **A Call from Beyond the Horizon** — Speak with your comrades to unlock Shipbuilding.
2. **Where the Wind Blows** — Prepare the vessel and set sail.
3. **A Beginning Beyond the Waves** — Discover and land on Auronui Island.
4. **Footsteps in Unfamiliar Lands** — Dispatch comrades to pioneer the island.
5. **Secret of the Winding Island** — Investigate the island and find its treasure map.
6. **Signpost of the Great Ocean** — Speak with your comrades after establishing the foothold.

If you have already landed but the next quest says **Dispatch Auronui Island Pioneering Mission**, you are at the fourth stage. You do not need to sail back to Vellua and rebuild your vessel.

<h2 id="prepare-ship-crew">
  How Many Comrades Do You Need for Auronui Pioneering?
</h2>

The Auronui Island **Pioneer** mission requires **three available comrades**. Check the mission's material costs before assigning them, and make sure they are not already committed to other camp work.

These are the workers sent to establish the Anchorage, not the five workers used by the separate Small Shipbuilding construction mission.

If your vessel is not ready for departure, arrange its crew through a Ship Manager. The <Link href="/crimson-desert/ships">shipbuilding and crew instructions</Link> explain those requirements.

<h2 id="start-pioneering">
  How Do You Start the Auronui Island Pioneering Mission?
</h2>

Once Auronui is discovered, the game introduces **Pioneering**.

The purpose of the mission is to establish an **Anchorage** where your ship can be retrieved and where further resource-gathering operations can become available.

This is a map-based dispatch. You do not build the Anchorage by placing structures manually on the beach.

### Step 1: Position Your Ship Near the Anchorage

Sail to Auronui Island and approach the coastline indicated by the quest.

Look for the Anchorage's highlighted area on the map.

**Both your character and your ship need to be close enough to the island's Pioneering area.**

Merely standing on Auronui while your ship is anchored far offshore may not satisfy the mission's location condition.

If the dispatch screen tells you that the captain and ship must be near the island, return to your vessel and move it into the marked area before trying again.

### Step 2: Use Map → Facilities

Open the world map.

Switch to **Facilities** rather than trying to start the mission from the ordinary quest list.

Look for the **Anchorage** marker at Auronui Island.

Select the marker and open its **Mission** menu.

On keyboard and mouse, the in-game Pioneering instructions direct you to select the Anchorage from the map and enter its mission details.

On a controller, follow the displayed button prompt for **Mission** after selecting the Anchorage.

<GuideCallout title="Do Not Use the Wrong Dispatch Screen">
The general Greymane Camp dispatch interface can display the Auronui mission, but it may also report that your captain and ship are too far away. Start the operation from Auronui's own Anchorage marker in Map → Facilities while your ship is nearby.
</GuideCallout>

This is especially important if the ordinary quest marker seems to be pointing somewhere you cannot interact with.

### Step 3: Select the Pioneer Mission

Inside the Anchorage menu, find the **Pioneer** operation.

Review its displayed currency and personnel requirements.

For the introductory Auronui mission:

1. Open **Pioneer**.
2. Select **three eligible comrades**.
3. Check that the displayed requirements have been met.
4. Select **Confirm Dispatch**.
5. Verify that the mission has entered **In Progress**.

Do not treat the selection of three portraits as proof that the mission has started.

A successful dispatch should display a running operation with a remaining-time counter.

If you close the menu and the mission still says it needs to be dispatched, check the operation's status again before sailing away.

<h2 id="pioneering-time">
  How Long Does Auronui Pioneering Take?
</h2>

The Pioneer mission displays a **12-hour duration**, measured in game time. Once the three comrades are dispatched and the operation shows **In Progress**, you can leave the menu and continue exploring or advance time by sleeping.

When the countdown ends, look for the mission-ready notification. You still need to claim the result at Auronui's Anchorage before **Footsteps in Unfamiliar Lands** advances.

<h2 id="complete-pioneering">
  How Do You Complete the Auronui Island Mission at 100%?
</h2>

This is the step most worth checking if your quest refuses to advance.

**A Pioneer dispatch reaching 100% does not automatically finish the quest.**

Return to Auronui Island and bring your ship into the Anchorage's marked area.

Then:

1. Open the **world map**.
2. Switch to **Facilities**.
3. Select the Auronui **Anchorage**.
4. Open **Mission**.
5. Find the completed **Pioneer** operation.
6. Select **Complete Mission** and confirm.

The completed operation should trigger the island's completion sequence and advance the corresponding A Wider World objective.

If you see a notification that the mission is ready, do not immediately assume that the quest has broken. The result still needs to be claimed.

If the camp dispatch list shows a finished operation but the quest remains active, reopen **Auronui Anchorage → Mission** and claim the Pioneer result there.

<h2 id="pioneering-troubleshooting">
  Why Is Auronui Island Pioneering Not Working?
</h2>

### What If Confirm Dispatch or Complete Mission Does Nothing?

First, check that your ship is inside Auronui's Anchorage area, the required comrades are assigned, and you are using **Map → Facilities → Anchorage → Mission**.

If the operation shows **Awaiting Completion**, select **Complete Mission** rather than starting another dispatch.

Some Pioneering missions have experienced progression bugs. Update the game to the latest version if the button remains unresponsive despite meeting the displayed requirements. Avoid canceling an existing dispatch or changing assignments without a save you can restore.

<h2 id="secret-winding-island">
  Secret of the Winding Island: Where Is the Treasure Map?
</h2>

After establishing Auronui as an ocean foothold, the storyline directs you to investigate the island.

This stage is called **Secret of the Winding Island**.

An important item here is the **Forgotten Treasure Map**, which leads to the blueprint for a ship teleportation device.

The first chest is not the final treasure. It contains the map needed to locate the next one.

### Find the Cave on Auronui Island

Explore the **western side of Auronui Island**, looking for a cave entrance near the coastal cliffs.

Enter the cave and search for a treasure chest.

Open the chest and collect the **Forgotten Treasure Map**.

You may also find materials and other discoveries while exploring the cave, but the map is what directs you to the next treasure location.

Before leaving, open your inventory and examine the map.

Do not assume that the treasure is inside the same cave.

### Read the Forgotten Treasure Map

The map points toward Auronui's western coastline.

Use the shape of the coast and the cliffs shown in the drawing to identify the area.

A useful starting point is the coastline near the northern part of the western shore, around the area above the beginning of the island's name on the world map.

Look for the shore access and move toward the nearby elevated ground and trees.

If you are still standing deep inside the original cave, you are searching in the wrong place.

<h2 id="teleporter-blueprint">
  Where Is the Ship Teleportation Device Blueprint?
</h2>

The **Ship Teleportation Device Blueprint** is inside a buried treasure chest on Auronui Island.

Once you have the Forgotten Treasure Map:

1. Travel to the western coastal area indicated by the drawing.
2. Compare the cliff and shoreline shapes against the map.
3. Approach the elevated ground near the trees.
4. Search for the buried treasure location.
5. Equip a **shovel** and dig up the chest.
6. Open the chest and collect the blueprint.

The shovel is important. Finding the correct spot does not necessarily give you an ordinary chest-opening interaction while the treasure is still buried.

If you have reached the right location and cannot open anything, check whether you actually have a shovel equipped.

Do not waste time repeatedly attacking the ground or attempting to pull up the buried chest with unrelated combat abilities.

### What If You Forgot to Bring a Shovel?

You can leave the island, acquire a shovel from a suitable tool merchant or provisioner, and return to the marked area.

The treasure does not require purchasing a more expensive ship.

Once the chest is dug up, open it and inspect the blueprint in your inventory.

The device is not automatically installed on your ship when you collect the blueprint.

<h2 id="craft-ship-teleporter">
  How Do You Craft and Install the Ship Teleportation Device?
</h2>

Collecting the blueprint unlocks the next step: making the device itself.

The recipe requires:

- **15 Shiny Ore**
- **5 Azurite**
- **10 Iron Ore**

Check the crafting screen before gathering materials so you can confirm which quantities you still need.

### Where to Find the Materials

**Shiny Ore** is the material most likely to require a separate gathering trip.

Search underwater rock formations and mineral deposits in the ocean, including waters around the islands near Auronui.

Some deposits can be broken with **Force Palm** or an appropriate mining ability, such as the Knuckledrill.

Bring diving equipment and pay attention to your Breath gauge. Do not continue mining until your air runs out.

**Azurite** can be obtained from mineral deposits and other sources available through ordinary progression. Inspect the materials you already own before making a separate mining trip.

**Iron Ore** is a common land-based mining material. You may already have enough from earlier exploration.

### Craft the Device at a Workbench

After collecting the necessary materials, return to **Greymane Camp**.

Use the crafting Workbench associated with the **Furniture Shop**, and look for the device under the available ship-related crafting options.

Craft the **Ship Teleportation Device**.

Learning the blueprint and crafting the item are separate steps. If the device does not appear in the ship equipment menu, check whether you have actually made it.

### Install It on Your Ship

Return to a Ship Manager, such as **Hopkins at Vellua**.

1. Speak to the Ship Manager.
2. Open **Manage Ships**.
3. Select the vessel you want to equip.
4. Choose **Change Equipment**.
5. Select an available Ship Equipment slot.
6. Install the Ship Teleportation Device.

With the device installed, you can select your ship as a fast-travel destination from the world map.

This is especially useful when exploring distant islands, collecting resources on the mainland, or returning to the same expedition without making the entire sailing trip again.

<h2 id="anchorage-benefits">
  What Does Pioneering Auronui Island Unlock?
</h2>

Completing the Pioneer mission establishes an **Anchorage** on Auronui Island, allowing further Greymane operations and resource-gathering missions.

It also advances **A Wider World** and provides a foothold for exploring the surrounding islands.

The **Ship Teleportation Device** is a separate upgrade. You must find its blueprint, craft the device, and install it on your vessel.

<h2 id="finish-a-wider-world">
  How Do You Finish A Wider World After Auronui Island?
</h2>

Once the island has been pioneered and its exploration objective is complete, continue to **Signpost of the Great Ocean**.

The final objective is to **speak with your comrades**.

This conversation recognizes Auronui as a foothold for further voyages across the Great Ocean.

The questline's concluding reward includes **five Ancient Relics**.

If you have already dug up the treasure chest but A Wider World remains unfinished, check your Journal.

The blueprint treasure and the faction quest's concluding conversation are not the same objective.

You may still need to return to your comrades and complete the final dialogue.

At this point, Auronui has served its introductory purpose: your ship can reach the Great Ocean, the island can support maritime operations, and you have the means to begin working toward ship-based fast travel.
