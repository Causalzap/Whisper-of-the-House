<h2 id="where-i-start">What Should You Do Next in Crimson Desert?</h2>

A map full of available activities does not mean you have to finish all of them before advancing. The next useful action depends on what your current objective actually needs. A locked quest, an empty supply of Silver, and a combat encounter you cannot survive may all stop progress, but they call for different fixes.

Check the requirement that is stopping you, then choose the shortest way to remove it:

- **A quest, character, or Research project will not progress:** inspect its Journal state and earlier requirements before traveling to another location.
- **You need Silver for a purchase:** compare the buyer and selling destination before loading a trading wagon.
- **A camp item or animal product is missing:** check crops, feed, ranch space, and production rather than gathering unrelated materials.
- **A particular animal will not appear:** distinguish a normal wildlife spawn from a named hunting challenge.
- **Your build cannot handle the next fight:** check whether Health, Stamina, Spirit, or a specific skill is the limiting factor before spending Abyss Artifacts.

For the new expansion, the key distinction is whether you have reached the required Greymane Camp progression or completed the original main story. Those two milestones unlock different activities.

<h2 id="charting-the-unknown">
  How Do You Start Charting the Unknown DLC?
</h2>

**Charting the Unknown** adds shipbuilding, ocean exploration, three new character stories, and endgame challenges.

To unlock the ocean content, complete **Howling Hill** and the required Greymane Camp expansion, then check **Journal → Faction Quests** for **A Wider World**. The new character stories for Kliff, Damiane, and Oongka require **Journey's End**.

These are separate unlock paths. You can begin sailing before completing the original main story, but the three post-game character questlines require the final main-story quest.

Use the <Link href="/crimson-desert/charting-the-unknown">Charting the Unknown DLC guide</Link> to check which activities are available on your current save and where to begin.

<h2 id="trading">How Do You Make Silver Without Wasting a Wagon Trip?</h2>

Before buying packaged goods, inspect the destination where you intend to sell them. A tempting purchase price means little if the buyer is too far away, the route exposes your wagon to unnecessary danger, or the extra profit does not justify the detour.

I prefer a shorter route with a known buyer when the profit difference is small. When two worthwhile stops lie in the same direction, combining them can make the wagon trip more productive. Use the current prices at Royal Trading Posts rather than expecting one permanent route to remain best on every visit.

The <Link href="/crimson-desert/trading-guide">trading route and wagon guide</Link> covers how to compare prices, choose destinations, and avoid carrying valuable goods on a poor route.

<h2 id="farming-ranching">What Should You Fix First at Howling Hill Camp?</h2>

Return to camp when a particular crop, livestock product, or capacity limit is blocking the next objective. Check the crops that are actually needed, then inspect feed and ranch space. Keeping every plant growing and every animal type breeding can consume attention without producing the item you are waiting for.

A slow-growing crop may need watering or fertilizer, while a crowded ranch can prevent useful breeding progress. Once production is running again, leave the timer to advance while you continue other objectives. For crop requirements, Rubber Trees, Cacao, feeding, and breeding capacity, use the <Link href="/crimson-desert/farming-ranching-guide">Howling Hill farming and ranching walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="hunting">What Should You Do When the Animal You Need Is Missing?</h2>

A familiar wildlife spot does not guarantee a specific animal every time you visit. Check the area thoroughly once, then move on when the wrong animals are present rather than circling the same location indefinitely. Return after making progress elsewhere if you still need that ordinary spawn.

Named hunts require a different decision. **White Crow's Gaze**, special-animal encounters, and **The End of Myth** have particular targets or conditions; completing an ordinary wildlife run may not satisfy them. The <Link href="/crimson-desert/hunting-guide">hunting and animal-capture guide</Link> covers those conditions and when to stop waiting on a normal spawn.

<h2 id="abyss-artifacts">Should You Spend Abyss Artifacts Now or Save Them?</h2>

Spend an Artifact when the upgrade fixes a weakness you are actually experiencing. Running out of Stamina while dodging calls for a different investment from losing fights to heavy damage or exhausting Spirit during your most-used abilities. An available upgrade is not automatically the most useful purchase.

Check every requirement on the desired node before collecting more Artifacts. Some upgrades also need Research knowledge or other materials, so additional Artifacts alone may not remove the lock. If your current setup already handles the next objective comfortably, keeping them for a later choice is reasonable. The <Link href="/crimson-desert/abyss-artifacts-guide">Abyss Artifact upgrade breakdown</Link> compares those decisions and their prerequisites.

<h2 id="side-quests">Why Is a Quest, Character, or Research Project Not Progressing?</h2>

Open the Journal before traveling to another NPC or repeating the same interaction. A missing quest may be waiting on earlier faction progress, a character can be unavailable while assigned elsewhere, and Research can stop until an interruption is resolved. The right destination may already be known; the missing requirement is what matters.

For a halted Research project, inspect the interruption instead of waiting through another timer. For a locked character, check current assignments. For a quest that has never appeared, work backward through its prerequisite chain rather than searching the final location first. The <Link href="/crimson-desert/side-quests-guide">side quest and Research troubleshooting steps</Link> cover these cases in more detail.
