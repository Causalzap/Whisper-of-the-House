<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Complete **Journey's End**, then open **Journal → Main Quests** and track **Beyond the Margins**. Start **A Moment's Respite** at the tavern in Pailune, finish the Matthias and Trukan duels, and follow the new objectives toward Sebastian and the sandstorm barrier.

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How to Start Beyond the Margins
</h2>

**Beyond the Margins** is Kliff's 13-part story in **Crimson Desert: Charting the Unknown**. It becomes available after you complete **Journey's End**, the final main-story quest of the base game.

The starting point is easy to miss if you return to an old save and immediately begin exploring the new islands. Kliff's storyline does not begin at a harbor, and completing the early camp requirements for shipbuilding does not replace the main-story prerequisite.

Open your Journal, select **Main Quests**, and look for **Beyond the Margins**. The first objective is **A Moment's Respite**, which takes you back to Pailune to spend time with the Greymanes.

The Journal tracks **13 objectives** for Kliff. You do not need to finish the separate Oongka or Damiane stories before completing this one.

The opening encounters follow familiar characters and clearly marked objectives. The more difficult section begins after the Eastern Witches open the sandstorm barrier, where navigation depends on Marker Stones instead of simply following a distant map pin.

<h2 id="familiar-faces">
  Familiar Faces: Return to Pailune and Defeat Matthias
</h2>

Begin **A Moment's Respite** by heading to the tavern and meeting your comrades. After the opening conversation and fishing sequence, the story turns toward a familiar visitor looking for Kliff.

The next quest, **Familiar Faces**, sends you to meet **Matthias** in Pailune.

Matthias wants another duel. Accept his challenge to begin the fight, and finish the encounter before returning to your comrades.

### How to Beat Matthias

His sword can reach farther than you might expect from the space immediately around him. Staying close and repeatedly attacking works poorly when he recovers quickly enough to answer your next strike.

I found the safer approach was to let Matthias commit first, dodge or defend against his attack, then retaliate with a short sequence.

- **Keep room to retreat.** Avoid standing directly inside the reach of consecutive sword strikes.
- **Punish completed attacks.** His recovery gives you a better opportunity than trying to interrupt every swing.
- **Avoid extending combos after a knockdown.** He can regain control before your next lengthy animation finishes.
- **Watch your Stamina.** A final attack that leaves you unable to dodge is rarely worth the extra damage.

You do not need a new DLC weapon for this encounter. Your existing main-story equipment is sufficient if you can survive his attacks and maintain a consistent counterattack rhythm.

Once Matthias concedes, the conversation reveals that he wants to become a Greymane. Finish the dialogue and return to your comrades as directed.

The next objective leads to **Jijeong Temple**.

<h2 id="trukan-duel">
  Teachings to Light the Way: Defeat Trukan
</h2>

At **Jijeong Temple**, speak with the chief monk to begin the next part of the story.

The monk points you toward **Trukan**, who has returned after the events of the original campaign. Follow the quest marker, find him, and accept the duel.

Trukan punishes close-range trades with powerful kicks and follow-up strikes. Watch for the full attack sequence before moving back into range; responding to his first movement can leave you exposed to the next hit.

His recovery after a committed attack gives you enough time for a controlled counter, but extending the exchange increases the chance of being interrupted. Keep healing items available for the later desert encounters rather than exhausting them trying to force a quick victory here.

After the duel, **return to the chief monk**. Leaving Jijeong Temple without completing that conversation prevents the storyline from advancing toward Sebastian and the Eastern Witches.

<h2 id="find-sebastian">
  How to Find Sebastian and Open the Sandstorm Barrier
</h2>

The Eastern Witches have detected something beyond the barrier, but entering an unexplored area without a reliable route would leave Kliff vulnerable to the storm.

You need **Sebastian**, a mapmaker who can help chart the hidden region.

Follow the active quest toward the desert settlement and ask local residents about Sebastian. The search involves conversations around the town before you locate him.

Spend your time on the NPCs tied to the tracked objective. Searching every house or exploring unrelated desert ruins adds travel without advancing the quest.

Once Sebastian agrees to assist, continue toward the barrier and meet the Eastern Witches, **Meu and Woosa**.

They help open the path into the previously inaccessible territory. Sebastian then accompanies the expedition as its mapmaker.

### What to Check Before Entering

The new desert is different from ordinary open-world exploration. The sandstorm limits visibility, and some of the objects intended to guide travelers have been deliberately corrupted.

Before heading deeper:

- Keep enough healing supplies for several encounters, not only one boss.
- Leave inventory room for quest items and anything useful collected from enemies.
- Make sure you can use **Blinding Flash** and **Axiom Force**, which become important for navigating the area.
- Use nearby fast-travel points when available.

You do not need to buy a separate navigation item before entering. The quest introduces **Glowing Seeds** and provides an initial batch of **100** as the route opens.

Those seeds are useful for marking places you have already traveled and maintaining your bearings when the sand obscures distant landmarks.

<h2 id="marker-stones">
  How to Navigate the Sandstorm and Fix Corrupted Marker Stones
</h2>

The most important rule in the new desert is that **a visible beam of light is not necessarily pointing in the correct direction**.

Marker Stones are supposed to guide travelers between settlements. Examine a stone to identify the place it points toward, then follow its light to the next landmark.

Near **Pilgrim's Peak**, you begin using these stones to navigate the area. Standing near a functioning beam helps you identify the direction of the next destination, but corrupted stones can send you along a false route.

The game introduces a specific interaction for restoring them.

### How to Remove Dark Stakes

A corrupted Marker Stone has been affected by a nearby **Dark Stake**. The stake must be found and destroyed before the stone can point correctly again.

Follow these steps:

1. **Inspect the Marker Stone** and check whether its beam is corrupted.
2. Activate **Blinding Flash** to reveal the Dark Stake affecting it.
3. Locate the exposed stake, including any part concealed by the surrounding terrain.
4. Use **Axiom Force** to pull the stake out.
5. Destroy the stake and recheck the Marker Stone's direction.

The objective tracks **five Dark Stakes** across the affected desert routes.

Do not assume that destroying one stake restores the entire network. Continue checking stones as you approach the different settlements.

### Where to Look When the Dark Stake Is Hidden

Some Dark Stakes are easy to identify in open terrain. Others are partially concealed by nearby objects.

One particularly confusing location involves a cracked tree.

The cracks are a useful visual clue when you are standing near a suspicious marker but cannot identify the object affecting it.

Use Blinding Flash around the tree and surrounding ground rather than repeatedly aiming at the Marker Stone itself.

Axiom Force can also feel unresponsive when the target is partially embedded in another object.

Release the ability, change your position, and aim more directly at the exposed section. Pulling from the same obstructed angle repeatedly can waste time without completing the interaction.

The objective does not advance simply because you interacted with the area. The stake must be removed and destroyed.

<h2 id="desert-villages">
  Complete the Desert Village Quests
</h2>

After entering the region, Kliff discovers settlements that have lost contact because the Marker Stone network is no longer reliable.

The village objectives are connected to the larger investigation. Restoring routes lets residents exchange medicine, return home, and recover missing animals.

You will need to move between settlements while dealing with the corrupted stones. It is more efficient to complete nearby objectives as you reach each village instead of postponing everything until after visiting the entire region.

### Find Medicinal Herbs for the Sick Village

One settlement is suffering from a spreading illness. Speak with the resident caring for the sick villagers to learn that their medicinal herbs have run out.

The medicine is normally obtained from a neighboring village, but the damaged route has prevented people from traveling between them.

The resident gives you useful directions:

- Pass the **dry well** near the village.
- Head **northeast** from the well.
- Look for the **red rocks** marking the old route.

Follow those landmarks while checking the direction of each Marker Stone.

The red rocks provide a useful secondary clue when the storm makes the terrain hard to read. However, a nearby corrupted stone can still send you away from the destination, so repair the route before relying on its beam.

At the neighboring settlement, speak with its residents. They explain that they have also been isolated and did not deliberately refuse to help the sick village.

Collect the medicinal herbs they provide and return to deliver them.

Skip the long herb-gathering detour. The neighboring villagers provide the medicine needed to finish this objective.

### The Missing Calf: Find and Return the Bison Calf

Another resident has lost a **bison calf** during the storm.

The pasture lies beyond the low rocky hills to the south of the settlement. Follow the objective toward that area and locate the calf before trying to return.

Be careful around sandy depressions and unstable terrain. Quicksand and poor visibility can turn a short retrieval into a long detour.

Once you find the animal, **pick up the calf and carry it back**.

The objective points toward **Hazran**. Use the accessible Marker Stones and the currently tracked location to guide your return.

Carrying the calf across the desert is the actual retrieval requirement. Merely finding it does not complete the task.

Keep the correct quest tracked while returning so you do not carry the calf toward a different settlement.

### The Lost Child: Bring the Child Home

You may also encounter a child who was gathering herbs when the sandstorm began.

Talk to the child and begin the return journey. The damaged navigation network is the reason the child could no longer find the correct way home.

Use the village landmarks and the light from functioning Marker Stones to reach the child's home near the large cliff.

Keep the escort close enough that you can confirm the child is still following. Traveling too far ahead is unnecessary when the objective depends on bringing another character safely back to the settlement.

Complete the conversation with the family after reaching the destination.

### Village in Darkness: Clear the Hostile Area

The storyline also leads through a settlement affected by hostile enemies.

Clear the enemies connected to the objective before looking for the next conversation or route marker.

Ranged opponents can interrupt your attacks while you concentrate on a nearby enemy. When several attackers are present, reposition so that you are not taking damage from behind throughout the fight.

The area can become dangerous quickly if you remain surrounded.

After completing the encounter, check the Journal. The quest directs you to report the situation to Sebastian.

<h2 id="survey-records">
  Return the Survey Records to Sebastian
</h2>

As the desert objectives progress, Kliff collects **Survey Records** describing settlements, Marker Stone locations, and the routes connecting them.

These records have a purpose beyond ordinary exploration rewards.

Sebastian uses them to understand the geography of the sealed region and identify where the altered routes lead.

Once a quest asks you to return to Sebastian, use an unlocked fast-travel point rather than retracing every Marker Stone on foot.

Speak with him and complete the conversation to update the investigation.

The Journal can show **Beyond the Sandstorm** at **9/13** while you still have work remaining in the region. That progress count is not the end of the storyline.

Continue resolving outstanding village objectives and reporting the resulting information.

### Why You Must Return to Sebastian Again

The corrupted Marker Stones were not simply damaged to prevent travel.

When Sebastian compares the survey information, he realizes that the altered beams deliberately direct travelers toward another location.

The routes from different settlements are being manipulated to converge on a destination connected to **Antumbra**.

Sebastian's discovery reveals a new destination beyond the restored village routes. Follow the updated objective toward the force corrupting the Marker Stones.

If a quest notification appears but the next destination is unclear, reopen **Journal → Main Quests** and select the latest Beyond the Margins objective manually.

The Journal navigation can place the newly selected entry in a different section from the previous objective. Checking the active quest text is safer than assuming that the previous map marker remains correct.

<h2 id="antumbra-location">
  Follow the Traces of Antumbra
</h2>

After Sebastian identifies the altered routes, follow the new objective toward the location where the corrupted beams converge.

Expect hostile enemies as you approach. The area is not an empty destination marker, and ranged attacks can interrupt you while you are trying to move through the ruins.

Clear enough space to investigate without being surrounded.

You will find a document titled **Plans for the Vessel of the Void**.

Read it before moving forward. It explains how Antumbra intends to manipulate people through their weaknesses and reveals part of the plan connected with the hidden desert region.

The final encounter begins beyond this investigation.

This is the last sensible moment to check your equipment and recovery items. Spending time on an unrelated upgrade chain is unnecessary if your current build already handles the enemies here, but a serious shortage of healing items or Stamina makes the following fight substantially harder.

For permanent Health, Stamina, Spirit, and skill investment decisions, use the [Crimson Desert Abyss Artifacts guide](/crimson-desert/abyss-artifacts-guide).

<h2 id="kharzek-boss">
  How to Beat Kharzek, Archon of Antumbra
</h2>

The final major encounter in Beyond the Margins is against **Kharzek, Archon of Antumbra**.

Kharzek is difficult to pressure continuously because he can reposition, interrupt your attacks, and break your target lock.

His large attacks also cover enough space that attempting to heal beside him can become dangerous.

### How to Survive Kharzek's Attacks

The fight becomes much more manageable when you treat his movement as part of the attack pattern rather than chasing him every time he moves.

**Avoid committing to a long attack while Kharzek is repositioning.** Your character can finish the animation after the boss has already left its effective range, leaving you vulnerable without dealing damage.

Instead:

1. Keep enough Stamina available to dodge a sudden attack.
2. Watch Kharzek's movement and wait until he commits to a strike.
3. Evade the attack, then counter during the opening.
4. Reacquire the target after he teleports or breaks lock-on.
5. Heal only after creating a safe distance.

One of his powerful attacks produces a dangerous explosion. Get clear of the affected area rather than attempting to block everything at close range.

Counters are effective when the timing is correct. However, trying to force them repeatedly while he is moving often results in missed attacks and wasted recovery opportunities.

### Why Kharzek Becomes Difficult at Low Health

The final portion of the fight is particularly frustrating when Kharzek keeps changing position.

Even with very little health remaining, he can avoid attacks that would normally finish the encounter. Chasing that last section of his health bar with a lengthy combo can leave you exposed.

I would preserve a defensive reserve here instead of spending every available resource on the final attack. A short, controlled counter after he commits is a safer finish than repeatedly swinging at his previous position.

If you need to heal, disengage first. Trying to recover health while Kharzek is already beginning another attack can result in the heal being interrupted or the recovered health being lost immediately.

### What Reward Do You Get for Defeating Kharzek?

Defeating Kharzek awards **1 Abyss Artifact**.

Do not confuse this with other equipment or resources arriving from active camp activities. Those rewards may appear around the same time but are not necessarily direct rewards from the boss.

Winning the fight is not the last action required for Beyond the Margins.

You still need to report what happened.

<h2 id="finish-13-of-13">
  How to Finish Beyond the Margins 13/13
</h2>

After Kharzek is defeated, return to **Sebastian**.

Tell him what you discovered and complete the final conversation. The story closes with Kliff reflecting on the people and settlements connected through the expedition.

Once the conversation concludes, the Journal records:

**Beyond the Margins — 13/13**
