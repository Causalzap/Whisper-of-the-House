<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How Do You Start Forged Resolve in Crimson Desert?
</h2>

**Forged Resolve** is Oongka's ten-part main questline in **Crimson Desert: Charting the Unknown**. It begins after the base game's final story quest, **Journey's End**, and follows Oongka as he learns engineering, develops new equipment, and confronts the consequences of a dangerous experiment.

To begin the story:

1. Complete **Journey's End**.
2. Switch to **Oongka**.
3. Open **Journal → Main Quests** and select **Forged Resolve**.
4. Track **A New Path**.
5. Enter the workshop in **northwestern Pailune**, west of the Saddlery and Stable.

The opening location is in Pailune, not Scholastone Institute. The institute becomes your destination after the first conversation.

If you cannot find Forged Resolve in your Journal, first check whether Journey's End is complete. The three character storylines have the same main-story prerequisite, but you do not need to complete Kliff's or Damiane's new quests before starting Oongka's.

<h2 id="quest-order">
  Forged Resolve Walkthrough: All 10 Quests in Order
</h2>

Forged Resolve begins with relatively simple travel and conversations. The difficult sections come when Oongka starts working with machinery at Scholastone and later becomes trapped inside Marni's Manor.

Follow the ten quest entries in this order:

1. **A New Path**
2. **To Scholastone**
3. **Unfamiliar Workshop**
4. **Faltering Hands, Iron Will**
5. **Fractured Door**
6. **Fateful Encounter**
7. **The Scattered Pieces**
8. **Twisted Fate**
9. **Path of the Engineer**
10. **Unidentified Attack**

The main route moves from **Pailune → Scholastone Institute → Marni's Manor → Scholastone → Crimson Desert**.

Three sections deserve particular attention:

- **Unfamiliar Workshop:** Restore the power regulator by pressing five specific buttons.
- **Fractured Door:** Illuminate the required tiles using a 15-move route.
- **Fateful Encounter:** Find Marni's hidden workshop using a book, bookshelf, picture frame, and trapdoor.

The final stage, **Unidentified Attack**, takes Oongka to a machine threatening travelers in the Crimson Desert. Prepare for combat before heading to its location.

<h2 id="a-new-path">
  A New Path: Visit the Workshop in Pailune
</h2>

Enter the workshop marked by the first quest and complete the opening conversation.

Oongka wants to improve the power and reliability of his hand cannon. The discussion makes it clear that obtaining another component is not enough. He needs to understand the principles behind the weapon before he can create something more dependable.

The conversation points him toward **Scholastone Institute**, where engineering and mechanical research are taught.

You do not need to buy a weapon upgrade to advance A New Path. Finish the workshop dialogue, open the Journal if necessary, and track the next objective.

<h2 id="to-scholastone">
  To Scholastone: Find the Institute and Speak to Vargas
</h2>

Follow the objective toward Scholastone.

On the approach, Oongka meets a scholar who guides him along the route through **Coronza**. During the conversation, you learn about an earlier accident at the institute, when **Octavius** attempted to open a gate into another realm.

Continue toward the institute above the surrounding cliffs.

Once inside, find the **dean** and explain why Oongka wants to study engineering.

The dean initially suggests that Oongka might be better served by a smith or equipment specialist. Continue the conversation until Oongka makes clear that he wants to learn the underlying principles, not simply purchase a stronger weapon.

The dean then directs him to **Vargas**.

Follow the quest marker to Vargas and speak with him. He introduces Oongka to a damaged laboratory power regulator.

This is where the first major puzzle begins.

<h2 id="unfamiliar-workshop">
  Unfamiliar Workshop: How Do You Fix the Broken Device?
</h2>

The broken device is a power regulator with **two rows of six buttons**.

Your task is to restore its operation without overloading the calculation.

The important detail is that you do not need to activate every working button. Two positions are visibly damaged, and only five specific buttons are needed for the solution.

### Broken Device: Exact Five-Button Solution

Face the panel directly.

Count **columns from left to right**, starting at C1. The top row is R1, and the bottom row is R2.

Press the buttons in this exact order:

1. **C1, R2** — bottom row, first button.
2. **C3, R1** — top row, third button.
3. **C3, R2** — bottom row, third button.
4. **C4, R1** — top row, fourth button.
5. **C5, R1** — top row, fifth button.

The regulator's two rows contain the following correct inputs:

- **Top row:** C3 (press 2), C4 (press 4), C5 (press 5).
- **Bottom row:** C1 (press 1), C3 (press 3).

The numbers indicate the press order, not the order in which the buttons appear on the panel. The two cracked buttons in column two are not used.

After the fifth correct input, the regulator should activate and allow the quest to continue.

### Why Does the Broken Device Overload?

Inspecting the device reveals a clue involving the base sequence **110110** and calculations that carry into another position when their limit is reached.

You do not need to work out a new binary sequence to complete the quest.

The practical failure point is pressing extra buttons because they appear operational. The objective is to restore the regulator using the required inputs, not to turn on every available switch.

<GuideCallout title="Broken Device Not Activating?">
Compare each press with the six-column layout. In particular, do not confuse the cracked second-column buttons with the working third-column buttons. Follow the numbered order rather than pressing all five selected buttons in reading order.
</GuideCallout>

Once the machine is running, finish the conversation with Vargas and follow the next objective.

<h2 id="faltering-hands">
  Faltering Hands, Iron Will: Continue Oongka's Engineering Training
</h2>

After repairing the regulator, Oongka begins applying what he has learned to his own equipment.

Continue the institute objective and complete the conversations related to his hand cannon and engineering studies.

This stage is associated with the **Skyway Rocket Pack**, one of the useful pieces of equipment obtained during the storyline.

Keep the equipment available as you progress. Mobility becomes particularly relevant when the story eventually takes Oongka into combat against a large armored machine.

Your next major obstacle is not another power regulator. Vargas has moved deeper into the laboratory, behind a locked mechanism known as the **Fractured Door**.

<h2 id="fractured-door">
  Fractured Door: How Do You Solve the 15-Move Puzzle?
</h2>

The Fractured Door prevents Oongka from reaching Vargas in the next laboratory area.

Unlike the broken regulator, this puzzle is about movement. You must illuminate the required tiles without exceeding the **15-move allowance**.

Start from the puzzle's initial position. The route below is one complete solution.

### Complete 15-Move Direction Sequence

Follow these three groups in order.

**Moves 1–5**

1. Right
2. Up
3. Right
4. Up
5. Left

**Moves 6–10**

6. Down
7. Right
8. Down
9. Left
10. Up

**Moves 11–15**

11. Left
12. Down
13. Up
14. Right
15. Down

For a quick reference, the complete route is:

**→ ↑ → ↑ ← | ↓ → ↓ ← ↑ | ← ↓ ↑ → ↓**

The vertical bars separate groups of five moves. They are not additional inputs.

Follow the directions from the initial tile without inserting other movements.

### Can You Step on a Tile More Than Once?

Yes. The route can pass through a tile that has already been illuminated.

Do not restart only because your path crosses an earlier position. What matters is completing the required pattern within the move limit.

If you have already made part of an unsuccessful attempt, do not append the fifteen directions to that partial route. The sequence assumes the puzzle's starting arrangement.

### How Do You Know Fractured Door Is Complete?

After solving the mechanism, the doorway becomes accessible and Oongka can reach Vargas.

He is attempting to revive the dimensional research previously conducted by Octavius. His experiment uses Vision technology to reach another world.

The experiment transports Oongka to an unfamiliar version of **Marni's Manor**.

<h2 id="fateful-encounter">
  Fateful Encounter: How Do You Find Marni's Secret Room?
</h2>

Inside Marni's Manor, Oongka meets a mechanical butler who explains that a **homecoming device** may offer a way back.

The butler leads you through the mansion. During this section, you obtain the book **Marni and the Secret Chamber**.

Keep track of the book because it is the key to opening the first hidden passage.

### Step 1: Plant the Book on the Correct Bookshelf

When the objective tells you to find the secret room, return to the hallway and locate the tall bookshelf.

Do not simply interact with the front of the cabinet. The correct position is a particular section of its upper shelf.

1. Stand in front of the bookshelf in the hallway.
2. Raise your view toward the **far-right side of the upper shelf**.
3. Adjust your aim until the **Bookshelf** interaction appears.
4. Select **Plant** to place *Marni and the Secret Chamber* in its correct position.

If you are aiming at the right bookshelf but cannot see the prompt, move closer and raise your camera rather than repeatedly trying the middle shelf.

The room is not unlocked by reading the book alone. You must use the **Plant** interaction.

### Step 2: Enter the Passage and Find the Crooked Picture Frame

After planting the book, enter the concealed passage and examine the information inside.

Your next clue concerns a **crooked picture frame**.

Return downstairs and look near the doorway for the frame hanging at an angle.

Interact with the crooked frame to continue the secret-room sequence.

The route opens access to another chamber containing a trapdoor.

### Step 3: Use the Trapdoor to Reach the Hidden Workshop

Proceed through the doorway and stand on the trapdoor to descend.

This leads to Marni's private workshop, where he developed early prototypes.

This is an important progression point, not an optional room to search for ordinary loot.

The workshop contains technology relevant to Oongka's hand cannon. Continue interacting with the objective-related machinery and complete the weapon development sequence before leaving.

The storyline also awards the **Enhanced Blaster Blueprint** during Fateful Encounter.

<h2 id="scattered-pieces">
  The Scattered Pieces: Complete the Blaster and Return Home
</h2>

The next stage connects the hidden workshop to Oongka's attempt to return from the other dimension.

Two objectives can be easy to confuse:

- Improving the hand cannon using knowledge and equipment discovered in the workshop.
- Finding and using the **homecoming device** to escape Marni's Manor.

Completing the weapon modification does not automatically mean that Oongka has escaped.

Keep the active quest tracked and finish the remaining device interaction before moving on.

This section is associated with the **Enhanced Fatum Blaster**, an important outcome of Oongka's engineering work.

Do not abandon the mansion route immediately after finding the hidden workshop. Follow the current objective until the dimensional return sequence begins.

<h2 id="twisted-fate">
  Twisted Fate: Survive the Encounter with Goyen
</h2>

Oongka's return does not proceed normally.

The journey passes through distorted versions of familiar events, eventually leading to a confrontation with **Goyen** in an altered reality.

This encounter is a fight, not another switch puzzle.

### How to Fight Goyen

The main danger is remaining too close while Goyen is completing a string of attacks.

Goyen can punish an attack that continues after its opening has closed. Focus on surviving his current sequence before beginning another of your own.

If your attacks are repeatedly interrupted, shorten the exchange. Defend against his approach, counter when he finishes, and create room before recovering Health. Trying to finish a long combination while he is already preparing another attack exposes Oongka to unnecessary damage.

Your objective is to survive the encounter and advance the subsequent conversation.

This is part of the dimensional storyline. You are not required to find a new key, return to the bookshelf, or operate the Fractured Door again.

The story then brings Oongka back toward his own world.

<h2 id="path-of-engineer">
  Path of the Engineer: Return to Scholastone
</h2>

After the dimensional sequence, Oongka returns to Scholastone.

Find Vargas and complete the conversation about the experiment. He is interested in what happened, but Oongka is far less enthusiastic about opening another uncontrolled dimensional gate.

Continue the dialogue involving the dean as well.

This conversation closes the institute portion of the story. Oongka has learned to apply engineering knowledge to his own equipment, but the danger of using that knowledge without restraint remains central to what follows.

When the quest directs you back to the Greymanes, leave the institute and continue the tracked objective.

You learn that **Yann's trade wagon** has been attacked near the Crimson Desert by an enormous machine.

This begins the final major combat sequence.

<h2 id="unidentified-attack">
  Unidentified Attack: Find the Machine in the Crimson Desert
</h2>

Track **Unidentified Attack** and head toward the trade wagon's last reported location near the **Sunrest Ruins**.

The damaged wagon points toward a machine operating in the surrounding desert. The threat is not an ordinary group of bandits, so prepare your combat equipment before approaching the objective.

Make sure Oongka has access to his upgraded blaster and the equipment acquired during his engineering storyline.

The encounter leads to the **Scrap Metal Queen Stoneback Crab**, a giant armored machine.

<h3 id="stoneback-crab">
  How Do You Beat the Scrap Metal Queen Stoneback Crab?
</h3>

The machine's armor and reach make it especially dangerous to fight as if it were a normal close-range enemy.

Oongka recognizes two immediate problems during the confrontation: ordinary weapons struggle to penetrate the armor, and the machine's attack range makes dodging entirely on foot unreliable.

The most important preparation is the equipment Oongka developed earlier in Forged Resolve.

The machine's armored body makes prolonged close-range attacks a poor choice. Use Oongka's upgraded blaster and keep enough room to escape its larger movements.

If you are caught repeatedly beneath the machine, reposition before attacking again. The mobility equipment gained during Forged Resolve gives Oongka another way to move away from its immediate reach rather than relying only on short ground dodges.

Continue damaging the machine until the encounter advances to Harkan. Destroying the machine does not automatically finish Unidentified Attack.

### What Happens After the Machine Is Destroyed?

Defeating the machine reveals the engineer responsible for its creation: **Harkan**.

He calls the machine his **Ironclad Queen** and explains that he built it after being rejected by the Helms.

Do not assume the entire objective is finished the instant the machine stops moving.

Continue the confrontation with Harkan and complete the remaining combat and dialogue sequence. The story ends with Oongka challenging the idea that engineering exists only to make stronger weapons.

<h2 id="complete-forged-resolve">
  How Do You Confirm Forged Resolve Is Complete?
</h2>

The final named objective is **Unidentified Attack**.

Once you have dealt with the machine and finished the concluding confrontation, watch for the **Main Quest Complete** notification.

Remember that returning from Marni's Manor is not the end of the ten-part story. Oongka still needs to complete the later Scholastone and Crimson Desert objectives.

You also do not need to complete Kliff's Beyond the Margins or Damiane's From the Ashes to finish Forged Resolve.
