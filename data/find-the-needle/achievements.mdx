## Which Achievements Can You Earn at the Start?

**First Handful** unlocks when you dig up your first handful of hay. **Bale of Fun** follows when you manufacture your first bale, and **First Delivery** requires completing your first order. You can track these as separate milestones: producing a bale is not the same as delivering an order.

Two early achievements depend on what you do over time rather than building a particular machine. **Night Shift** asks you to keep working through an entire night, while **On a Roll** requires selling **50 times in a row without a break**. For On a Roll, watch the selling streak rather than assuming that 50 hay strands or 50 items produced will satisfy a requirement specifically described as 50 consecutive sales.

If you are still setting up the factory, check these ordinary early achievements before spending time on special challenge runs. You will encounter several of the required actions while learning how to dig, sell, manufacture, and deliver.

## Which Products Have Their Own Achievements?

Manufacturing achievements count the first successful output of a particular product. They do not require you to complete every order that uses that material.

The early products include **Hot off the Press** for your first Straw Panel, **That's a Wrap** for a Wrapped Bale, **Melting Pot** for an Ingot, and **Cog in the Machine** for your first Part. Each one has its own achievement, even when the relevant machines are connected to the same factory.

Later production goals include **Paper Trail** (first Paper Roll), **Sweet Hay** (first Sugar Sack), **Plastic Fantastic** (first Bioplastic Sheet), and **Black Belt** (first Carbon Fibre). Keep those unchecked until the named product has actually been produced. Merely unlocking a machine or seeing a recipe in the build menu is not the same as manufacturing its output.

If a production achievement has not unlocked, make sure at least one finished item came out of the relevant machine. Buying its plans or placing it in the yard is not the same action as manufacturing the product.

## Which Orders Count Toward Book Closed?

**Book Closed** requires finishing **all 20 orders**. **First Delivery** counts the first completed order, while five further achievements identify larger named customer orders across the production stages:

- **The Dairy:** finish the big order for The Dairy.
- **The Garden Centre:** finish the big order for The Garden Centre.
- **The Paper Merchant:** finish the big order for The Paper Merchant.
- **The Builders' Merchant:** finish the big order for The Builders' Merchant.
- **The Engineering Works:** finish the big order for The Engineering Works.


If you are working through the opening deliveries, <Link href="/find-the-needle">start with the first orders and production upgrades</Link> before attempting the later customer milestones.


Those five named achievements are not a substitute for Book Closed. The complete order count is 20, so do not mark the full-order achievement complete simply because all five named customers appear in your completed achievements.

The order book is the place to check your current delivery objective and remaining progress. A machine unlock or completed production milestone does not mean the requested goods have already been delivered.

## Which Achievements Require Finding Needles or Filling the Cabinet?

**Needle in a Haystack** requires finding your first needle. **The Golden Needle** is a separate achievement for finding the Golden Needle; its name alone does not establish where that needle is buried. Do not assume it will be in the same place in every run without a confirmed location or generation rule.


Four achievements each require completing a different row of the Needle Cabinet:

- **Pin Cushion:** fill the row of pins and sewing needles.
- **All Threaded Up:** fill the row of threading tools.
- **Odds and Ends:** fill the third row, from the splinter to the compass.
- **Ouch!:** fill the last row, from the cactus to the gold.

If you are still searching for missing needles, <Link href="/find-the-needle/scanner">use the Metal Detector and Scanner to narrow down the search and inspect hay</Link>. The cabinet tracks your collection, while each achievement has its own completion condition.

Check the individual rows rather than relying only on the total number of needles found. A row achievement has its own named requirement, and adding a needle elsewhere in the cabinet does not necessarily finish it.

To earn **Every Last Needle**, collect all **24 needles** and **press the final button**. Picking up the last needle alone is not enough; check the cabinet before marking the achievement complete.

## What Are the Eight Hidden Achievements in Find The Needle?

There are eight hidden achievements, each tied to a specific action:

- **What Is That Smell?** — dig up the Surströmming tin.
- **Oops** — sell a needle by mistake.
- **Bad Manners** — use a rude emote.
- **Nature Calls** — answer the call of nature.
- **Too Hot to Handle** — burn a needle.
- **Gravity Wins** — fall from too high.
- **In the Red** — borrow money from the bank.
- **Wrecking Crew** — take down a machine with a needle still inside.

For **What Is That Smell?**, you need the Surströmming tin, but there is no fixed location to follow here. **Nature Calls** gives you the goal without a specific button sequence. In both cases, use the achievement unlock itself to confirm that the action counted.

**Oops**, **Too Hot to Handle**, and **Wrecking Crew** risk losing a needle or destroying a machine that contains one. If you also want all 24 needles in the cabinet, use a separate attempt or a save you can return to, if available. Do not assume a sold or burned needle can be recovered.

**Gravity Wins** is separate from **Hay 10, Bubba 0**, which requires falling from too high **ten times**. One qualifying fall does not complete the ten-fall milestone.

## Which Achievements Involve Building, Customization, and Tools?

Several achievements reward interacting with systems that are easy to ignore while concentrating on orders. **Hello There** requires using an emote once; **Strike a Pose** requires **five different emotes**. **New Look** asks you to change Bubba's outfit, **Fresh Coat** requires painting a machine, and **Picasso** asks you to draw on the yard whiteboard.

On the construction side, **Upstairs** requires a second floor, **Busy Builder** requires building **500 things**, and **Copy and Paste** requires placing a blueprint you made. Buying the **Hay Excavator** earns **Big Digger**. **Turn It Up** specifically requires speeding a machine up **past 100%**, not simply switching it on or installing a normal conveyor.

The movement and equipment challenges have their own conditions: **Boing** requires being thrown by a Jump Pad, **Rocket Farmer** requires flying the jetpack, **Hose Launch** requires throwing something with the Vac Hose **while you are in the air**, and **Nice Catch** requires catching a coin from the till in midair.

Wait until the matching tool is available before attempting its action. An ordinary jump will not satisfy **Boing** without a Jump Pad, and throwing an item from the ground does not meet **Hose Launch**'s airborne condition.

## What Are the Money, Digging, and Playtime Milestones?

**First Million** and **Hay Tycoon** both involve $1,000,000, but they measure different things. First Million is for **earning $1,000,000 in total**. Hay Tycoon is for **having $1,000,000 at once**. Spending your earnings along the way can still contribute to the first condition, but it will not satisfy the cash-on-hand requirement.

Digging milestones are also cumulative: **Haymaker** requires digging **1,000,000 hay**, while **Mountain Mover** requires **100,000,000 hay**. Those are amounts dug, not dollars earned or items shipped. **Long Shift** requires **10 hours of play**.

Keep the digging totals separate from your sales and income totals. **Long Shift** also counts playtime, not how much your production line earns during those hours.

## Which Achievements Need a Special Run or Pile Size?

Three challenges are worth considering **before** you build a normal factory:

- **Old School** — find all **24 needles without building a belt**. Do not place a conveyor during the attempt if you want to preserve this condition.
- **Haste Makes Hay** — finish a **Standard** pile in **under three hours**. Completing a different pile size is not the stated requirement.
- **Quick Eye** — find a needle within the **first two minutes of a new game**. This is a new-game opening challenge, not a reward for discovering a needle late in an existing save.

For **Quick Eye**, begin searching as soon as the new game starts. If two minutes pass without finding a needle, that attempt can no longer meet the time limit; begin another new game for a fresh chance. Do not assume the needle always starts in the same place.

There are also completion achievements for larger pile sizes: **Go Big** for a **Big** pile, **Going Up** for a **Huge** pile, and **Mountain Climber** for **The Mountain**. These are separate from finishing Standard quickly and from the no-belt restriction in Old School.

**Hay 10, Bubba 0** is a repeat-action challenge rather than a pile-size victory: it requires ten falls from too high. **Hose Launch**, meanwhile, depends on using the Vac Hose while airborne, not simply jumping or firing it on the ground.

## What Should You Check Before Going for All 60 Achievements?

Start by marking the achievements already earned in your current save. Continue normal production and deliveries until you have identified which first-product, order, and cabinet-row milestones are still missing. This separates work you can finish during a normal run from actions that interfere with it.

Before deliberately selling or burning a needle, or dismantling a machine with a needle inside, decide whether you want to use a separate attempt. Keep **Old School**, the **three-hour Standard completion**, and the **two-minute first-needle challenge** on your list before beginning a new game, since those restrictions affect how you start.

When only a few entries remain unchecked, compare them with your Steam unlocks to see whether you need another delivery, a missing cabinet row, a specific tool action, or a fresh challenge run.

The checklist saves your manual selections in this browser; it does not read Steam achievement progress automatically. Use the completed achievements in Steam to confirm your final unlocks.
