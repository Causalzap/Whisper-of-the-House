Find The Needle begins with a huge haystack, a small selling stand, and no useful machinery. The early goal is not to strip the entire pile by hand. You need enough money to replace repeated trips to the stand with a moving production line, then complete orders that introduce new products and buildings.

The game has **20 orders across five stages**, and the order you are currently working on matters. Making a new product, storing it, and delivering it to the truck are three different steps. If the next building seems locked or the truck's counter is not moving, check which of those steps is missing before rebuilding your factory.

<section id="first-money" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Start Find The Needle and Earn Your First Money?

Pick up the first handful of hay and take it to the selling stand. The opening tasks then introduce **Bigger Handful** in the Tech Tree, followed by the **Toy Shovel**, **Bucket**, and **Spade**. These are not separate production chains: they simply make the next sale less laborious.

The Toy Shovel initially digs **18 strands at a time**, but it still needs to be emptied. The Bucket gives you somewhere to gather a larger load instead of running to the stand with every scoop. When the tutorial asks you to fill the bucket, put it on the ground, empty the shovel into it, pick up the full bucket, and tip the contents into the selling stand.

Watch the direction you are carrying a loaded shovel. Hay can spill while you move, so sprinting back toward the stand with an exposed load can waste some of the work you just did. A short walk with a full shovel is more useful than repeatedly dropping hay and clearing it from the floor.

You may also uncover cans, pipes, and other scrap before you have a Smelter. In the opening hours, those loose objects can be sold directly for cash. Later, metal becomes relevant to processing, so don't assume every unusual object is just another piece of hay.

</section>

<section id="early-upgrades" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which Tools and Upgrades Should You Buy First?

Follow the opening objectives rather than buying every new item the moment it appears. The early purchases establish a useful sequence:

1. **Bigger Handful:** spend your first earnings on carrying more hay per click.
2. **Toy Shovel — $3.25:** use the first digging tool instead of relying on bare hands.
3. **Bucket — $25:** collect repeated shovel loads in one place before selling them.
4. **Spade — $30:** move larger amounts of hay as your cash begins to increase.
5. **Conveyor Plans — $40 to unlock:** start moving hay without carrying every load across the yard.
6. **Electricity — $90 to unlock:** gain access to the Hay Generator and Power Poles for powered machines.

The Conveyor Plans unlock does not pay for the belt itself: building conveyor sections costs **$8 per meter**. Likewise, unlocking Electricity does not build the generator for you. Budget for the actual machine after buying the corresponding technology.

A short belt to the selling stand is enough for the first upgrade; save the rest of your cash for a powered collector and the coming Bale order. If the collector misses the belt, the arrows run backward, or a machine loses power, <Link href="/find-the-needle/automation">set up and troubleshoot the automatic hay line</Link> before extending it.

</section>

<section id="first-bales" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Complete the First Bales Order?

Open the **Order Book** near the truck bay and pin **First Bales (Stage 1, Order 1)**. The Standard-pile example shows a target of **20 Bales**, while another run using a much larger hay pile shows **36 Bales**. Follow the number displayed in your own Order Book. Selling loose hay earns money, but only finished Bales count toward this delivery.

First, buy the **Bale Compressor Plans** in the Tech Tree for **$150**. The Bale Compressor itself costs **$350** to build. Save enough for both costs, then put the compressor on the route that carries hay toward the truck. Feed hay through it and check that the output is Bales rather than loose strands.

Let the finished Bales reach the order's delivery point. The truck counter is more important than the number of Bales you have made: a Bale sitting on a belt or stored elsewhere has not necessarily been delivered. If the counter stays at zero, follow one finished Bale to see where it goes.

Once the delivered Bale count reaches your Order Book's target, the First Bales order is complete. Return to the Order Book and check the newly available machines. The next delivery calls for Straw Panels, so keep your Bale Compressor in place.

</section>

<section id="after-first-bales" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Unlocks After You Complete First Bales?

Completing **First Bales** opens up the next part of the yard. Among the available building options are the **Panel Press**, **Depot**, **Scanner Mk I**, and **Smelter**. A building appearing in the menu does not mean you can construct it without materials or that all its later upgrades have already been unlocked.

The immediate production change is the **Straw Panel**. Some later buildings cost both money and materials, so simply selling everything you manufacture is no longer enough. You need a way to make Straw Panels, keep some in stock, and still send the panels required by an order to the truck.

One of the first buildings that uses stored Straw Panels is the Scanner. Rather than selling every panel you produce, <Link href="/find-the-needle/scanner">check the Scanner's building requirements and installation steps</Link> before deciding how much to keep in the Depot.

</section>

<section id="straw-panels" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Make Straw Panels in Find The Needle?

Build a **Panel Press** on a route that carries Bales from your Bale Compressor. Its conversion is **four Bales into one Straw Panel**. The Panel Press needs finished Bales as input; sending loose hay into the same route without the compressor does not produce the required panels.

Keep the Bale Compressor running after completing First Bales. Each Straw Panel requires **four Bales**, and Bales already delivered to the truck cannot be reused for construction. Make additional panels when the Depot needs materials for a new building.

The next decision is where the Straw Panels go. Route the panels through a **Depot** on the way toward the truck. The Depot can hold products and parts for new buildings, while material not retained in its inventory can continue down the belt. This lets one production line support both construction and deliveries, provided you manage what it keeps.

Before changing to another product, check the current Order Book objective. If the order asks for Straw Panels, producing them is only the first half of the job; they still need to reach the delivery point.

</section>

<section id="depot-troubleshooting" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Are Straw Panels Stuck in the Depot Instead of Reaching the Truck?

The Depot **stores products and parts** used to build new machines, and it does **not need electricity**. In the Straw Panel inventory shown below, one panel is held in a slot with room for 90. Material counted in the Depot is not yet a delivery to the truck.

This creates a confusing situation during a Straw Panel delivery: the Panel Press is working, the panels arrive at the Depot, but the truck receives nothing because the Depot is keeping them. If you have reached this point, don't immediately buy a second press or tear up your belts.

Open the Depot and **click the Straw Panel inventory cell** to allow those panels to continue along the conveyor toward the truck. Click the cell again when you need the Depot to retain panels for another building. Watch one panel pass through and confirm that the truck's order counter increases.

Without an outgoing belt, excess material may be sold instead of reaching the truck. Keep a route toward the delivery point when you need an order filled, and avoid dismantling a Depot holding construction parts before using or moving that stock.

</section>

<section id="orders-progression" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Progress Through Orders and Unlock New Machines?

There are **20 orders spread across five stages**. The Order Book shows the goods needed for your current delivery and which machines or upgrades become available as you advance. Do not assume that unlocking a machine automatically completes the order that introduced it.

The first four orders follow this sequence: **First Bales → Straw Panels → Wrapped Bales → The Dairy**. Each introduces a different production requirement. By the fourth order, the truck needs both ordinary Bales and Wrapped Bales, so keep an output for unwrapped Bales after adding a Bale Wrapper. Sending every Bale through the wrapper can leave the ordinary Bale requirement unfinished.

When progress seems to stop, compare the item leaving your last production machine with the item shown in the Order Book. A factory can run continuously while the wrong product moves toward the truck.

**The Dairy is Order 4 of 20.** Completing it unlocks additional construction options, including the **Enclosed Conveyor**, **Tube Thrower**, and **Blueprint Designer**. Later named deliveries include **The Garden Centre**, **The Paper Merchant**, **The Builders' Merchant**, and **The Engineering Works**. Check each order's required products before changing your production lines.

If a technology upgrade says it requires a named order such as **Wrapped Bales**, completing that order is the gate. Accumulating more cash will not bypass the requirement. Use the Order Book first when an upgrade remains locked despite having enough money.

</section>

<section id="production-chains" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Produce After Bales and Straw Panels?

Once you can reliably make Bales and Straw Panels, avoid rebuilding the entire yard every time a new order appears. Preserve a route that supplies the current order, then leave room for a separate processing branch when the next product becomes available.

Once you reach Wrapped Bales and begin making machine parts, the production routes become more specialized:

- **Hay → Bale Compressor → Bales.** Keep ordinary Bales available for orders that require them.
- **Bales → Panel Press → Straw Panels.** Deliver panels to the truck or hold them in the Depot for construction.
- **Bales → Bale Wrapper → Wrapped Bales.** Place the wrapper after a Bale Compressor; it accepts finished Bales, not loose hay.
- **Hay → Briquetter → Eco-Briquettes.** The Briquetter converts 45 hay strands into one Eco-Briquette. These can feed the Fabricator.
- **Eco-Briquettes → Fabricator → Hay Rods or Gear Sets.** Select the part you need before starting production.
- **Scrap metal → Smelter → Copper or Iron Ingots → Fabricator.** Copper Ingots supply Wire Coils, while Iron Ingots supply Motor Cores.

The **Fabricator works on one selected recipe at a time**. If another Depot needs Hay Rods, feed Eco-Briquettes into the Fabricator and select the Hay Rods recipe. Having extra Straw Panels in storage will not cover a missing part requirement.

Later orders introduce products such as **Paper Rolls**, **Sugar Sacks**, **Bioplastic Sheets**, and **Carbon Fibre**. Read the recipe on each newly unlocked machine before connecting its input line.

Before switching the line back on, check which products the truck currently needs. For The Dairy, send ordinary Bales and Wrapped Bales along their respective delivery routes. For construction, confirm that the required parts are actually entering the Depot rather than being sold.

</section>

<section id="finding-needles" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Find Needles While Working Through Orders?

You can uncover a needle during ordinary digging, well before completing every delivery. Use a Metal Detector to search within the pile; after **First Bales** unlocks Scanner Mk I, place that machine on a hay route you want inspected. For detector range, the Scanner's metal output, and its needle drawer, <Link href="/find-the-needle/scanner">follow the Scanner and Metal Detector controls</Link>.

Place newly collected types in the **Needle Cabinet** and check your progress there. The game contains **24 needles** to collect; discovering a needle, clearing the haystack, and finishing a customer order are separate milestones. If you want to track collection rows and the longer challenges, use the <Link href="/find-the-needle/achievements">60-achievement checklist</Link>.

</section>

<section id="next-step-troubleshooting" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Do When Your Factory Stops Making Progress?

Use the symptom to decide which part of the yard needs attention:

- **The next machine is locked:** inspect the Order Book for an unmet delivery requirement or a named order gate.
- **You made the correct product, but the truck counter is not changing:** follow the finished item to its destination, then check whether the Depot is retaining it.
- **You have enough money but cannot place a building:** read the full material cost and inspect the Depot's stored inventory.
- **A powered collecting or processing line slows down or jams:** check the specific warning before buying more equipment. The <Link href="/find-the-needle/automation">conveyor, power, and machine-jam steps</Link> cover those faults.
- **A Scanner stops a belt or its scrap lands on the floor:** inspect its drawer and output settings before altering the rest of the factory. See the <Link href="/find-the-needle/scanner">Scanner controls and troubleshooting</Link>.

After a repair, follow one finished item from the machine that produced it to the truck or Depot. Movement alone is not enough: the order counter must increase, or the intended material must appear in storage.

</section>

<section id="completion" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Counts as Finishing Find The Needle?

Finishing all **20 orders** and collecting all **24 needles** are different goals. The Order Book shows your outstanding deliveries; the Needle Cabinet shows which types are missing and includes the final button needed after completing the collection. For extra challenges that change how you start a run, <Link href="/find-the-needle/achievements">check the remaining achievements</Link> before building a new factory.

</section>
