<h2 id="pottsville-route">The Pottsville route I use</h2>

Pottsville initially sounded like a straightforward civilian rescue.

It is longer than that.

My clear broke into these mission states:

1. Search the Suburbs around Shady Pines.
2. Locate the fallen CRC response team.
3. Recover the required tools.
4. Repair the local transformer.
5. Enter the Community Center.
6. Protect the Gymnasium while the survivors evacuate.
7. Leave the Community Center and head downtown.
8. Reach the Midtown Gate.
9. Enter Town Square.
10. Complete the research objectives around the stores and streets.
11. Finish the NIDC research work.
12. Move to City Hall only after the research phase changes.
13. Hold the rooftop until the CRC helicopter arrives.
14. Extract.

The map gets confusing when I mentally jump ahead.

The Community Center is visible before I can properly use it.

City Hall is visible before I need to be inside it.

I let the active objective decide when each landmark becomes relevant.

<h2 id="shady-pines">
  Finish the Shady Pines objectives before trying the Community Center
</h2>

My opening HUD in the Suburbs showed several jobs at once:

**Search Neighborhoods (0/3)**

**Search CRC Bodies for Tools (2/2)**

and:

**Repair Transformer**

That tells me the opening is still a neighborhood-access phase.

I do not run directly to the Community Center and circle the entrance waiting
for it to open.

The first response team has already failed, and their equipment is part of what
lets me finish the local power objective.

I work the nearby houses and streets until the CRC tool requirement is
satisfied, then finish the transformer work.

The important check is the HUD.

If **Repair Transformer** is still active, I am still solving access.

If the Community Center has not opened yet, I do not assume there is another
entrance hidden around the building.

I finish the Suburbs objective first.

<h2 id="go-downtown">
  When the survivor evacuation ends, leave for downtown
</h2>

Once the Gym objective completes, the rescue half of Pottsville is over.

The mission shifts toward unfinished NIDC research.

That means I leave the Community Center.

I do not stay inside because there are still unexplored rooms.

The next useful landmark is downtown.

This is the first major route transition:

**survivor evacuation complete**

→ **stop defending the Community Center**

→ **move toward Town Square**

<h2 id="town-square-research">
  Town Square has several research objectives active at the same time
</h2>

Once I reached Town Square, the HUD became more specific.

In one run I saw:

**Research (0/4)**

**Search Stores for Scientist**

**Collect Water Samples (0/2)**

and a separate **Research Drives** progress value.

At one point, the Research Drives progress was only:

**22%**

That is the state I use to decide whether downtown is actually finished.

I do not judge the section by how many stores I remember entering.

I judge it by which research objectives are still active.

The search can send me across:

- storefronts;
- street-side research areas;
- tents;
- water-sample locations;
- Research Drive interactions.

If one of those objectives remains, I stay in the Town Square phase.

<h2 id="scientist-and-research-drives">
  Search the stores while the Scientist and Research Drive objectives remain
</h2>

This is where I made the most useful Pottsville mistake.

We reached **City Hall** while the HUD still said:

**Search Stores for Scientist**

and:

**Collect Research Drives**

I kept moving higher through City Hall because it looked like the obvious final
building.

Eventually it became clear that the active objectives were still outside.

We went back down and returned to the main street.

That changed how I read Pottsville.

If **Search Stores for Scientist** is still active, I search the stores.

If **Collect Research Drives** is still active, I keep working the downtown
research area.

City Hall does not become correct just because it is nearby.

<h2 id="water-samples">
  Finish both Water Samples when the 0/2 objective is active
</h2>

Another Town Square research requirement in my run was:

**Collect Water Samples (0/2)**

That is a separate part of the broader `Research (0/4)` phase.

If the scientist search or Research Drives are finished but Water Samples still
shows:

`0/2`

or:

`1/2`

then the research phase is not complete.

I follow the water-sample marker until both are collected.

This prevents another version of the City Hall mistake:

finishing one research category and assuming the whole downtown objective must
be done.

The parent research state is what matters.

<h2 id="finish-research">
  Finish the research before moving to the final extraction
</h2>

Once the downtown requirements are complete, the mission finally changes out
of the search phase.

This is when I stop:

- entering new stores;
- checking tents;
- looking for another scientist;
- searching for another Research Drive;
- looking for another water sample.

The NIDC research work is complete.

In the route I cleared, the next state eventually moved toward:

**Get on CRC helicopter**

That is the real transition into the finale.

Until that change happens, I stay with the research objective.

After it happens, I stop treating Town Square as a search area.

<h2 id="pottsville-stuck">
  What I check when Pottsville stops progressing
</h2>

### The Community Center will not open

I check the Suburbs objectives.

If **Search Neighborhoods**, CRC tools, or **Repair Transformer** are still
active, I am too early.

### I expected to escort the survivors but nobody is leaving with me

I stay in the Gym.

The objective in my run was **Assist with Survivor Evacuation / Protect
Gymnasium**, not a street escort.

### The Gym timer finished

I leave the Community Center and follow the downtown objective.

The rescue phase is done.

### I reached the Midtown Gate

I complete **Open Gate** and follow **Get to Town Square**.

The `Research (0/4)` phase begins after this transition.

### I am inside City Hall but the mission is not advancing

I check the HUD.

If it still says **Search Stores for Scientist** or **Collect Research Drives**,
I go back outside.

That was the wrong turn that cost me the most time.

### Research Drives are not complete

I keep working the Town Square search area and use the shared progress instead
of counting my own pickups.

### Water Samples still says 0/2 or 1/2

I finish that objective before leaving the research phase.

### All research objectives are complete

I follow the new extraction state instead of continuing to search downtown.

### The CRC helicopter is inbound

I stay with the City Hall rooftop holdout.

My confirmed run showed about **1m27s** remaining during the final wait.

### The helicopter is ready

I board.
