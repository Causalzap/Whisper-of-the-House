<h2 id="power-plant-route">The Power Plant route I use</h2>

Power Plant became much easier once I separated **optional preparation** from
**required progression**.

The plant is completely locked at the start.

The important opening rule is:

> **I need both security overrides before the plant becomes the destination.**

My confirmed route went:

1. Take useful equipment that is already close to the route.
2. Complete the first outside security objective.
3. Confirm the first security override.
4. Continue toward the second active perimeter objective.
5. Reach the Field Hospital.
6. Restore its power.
7. Upload the HERA virus data.
8. Confirm the second override.
9. Follow the plant-gate objective.
10. Enter the facility.
11. Reach the Control Center.
12. Locate the main console.
13. Continue the restart until Black Start completes.
14. Move immediately to the extraction zone.
15. Board the helicopter.

The clean dividing line is:

**one override active**

→ I am still outside solving access.

**two overrides complete**

→ I am going to the plant.

That one distinction prevents most of my unnecessary backtracking.

<h2 id="perimeter">
  Church and Farmhouse gear are preparation, not the main route
</h2>

The opening radio traffic points out several equipment opportunities around the
plant.

Two I encountered were:

- the **church attic**;
- the **farmhouse**.

The church can provide useful gear upstairs.

The farmhouse becomes another supply opportunity after its local power is
restored.

I use them when they are convenient.

If I spawn with poor equipment and one of those locations is already along my
route, I take the supplies.

If reaching one means crossing a large part of the perimeter in the wrong
direction, I keep working the active security objective.

The plant itself is still the mission.

I do not try to turn every supply location into a mandatory stop.

Once my current firearm, healing, and melee backup are good enough to continue,
another optional building needs to solve a specific shortage before I detour
for it.

The second half of my access route sent me to the **Field Hospital**.

This is a real mission objective, not merely a medical loot stop.

The task was to:

**restore power**

and:

**upload the HERA virus data**

to the CRC servers.

In this run, command then confirmed:

**Override activated.**

**proceed to the plant gate.**

That is the message I wait for.

<h2 id="second-security-override">
  The second override is the point where I leave the perimeter
</h2>

Once the Field Hospital data was through and the second override activated, the
outside phase was finished.

This is where I change priorities.

Before that confirmation, a church, farmhouse, or supply point can still make
sense if it helps with the next outside objective.

After it, I do not make another loop around the map looking for unopened
containers.

The mission has already told me what comes next:

> **Proceed to the plant gate.**

If I am missing something genuinely critical, I take the nearest useful supply.

Otherwise, I go.

This is the most important pacing decision on Power Plant.

The outside area is large enough that another unnecessary detour can consume
several minutes before I even enter the facility.

<h2 id="plant-gate">
  East Gate in the radio call, Filtration Gate on the physical status
</h2>

There is a small naming mismatch in this part of the run that can make the
route sound more confusing than it is.

**the East Gate to the Power Plant is now open.**

But the physical status I saw at the access point read:

**FILTRATION GATE IS OPEN**

and the nearby system status showed:

**FILTRATION GATE LOCKDOWN LIFTED**.

I do not treat those as instructions to search for two completely different
progression gates.

I follow the plant-access marker generated by the current objective.

<h2 id="power-plant-stuck">
  What I check when Power Plant stops progressing
</h2>

### The plant entrance is still locked

I check how many security overrides have completed.

The plant requires **two**.

If only the first has been confirmed, I go back to the active perimeter route.

### I finished the flood-control objective but still cannot enter

That objective confirmed only **one security override** in my run.

I still need the second.

### I reached the Field Hospital but nothing has changed

I check whether I have actually:

**restored power**

and:

**completed the HERA data upload**.

The second override confirmation came after that sequence in my route.

### The radio says East Gate but the screen says Filtration Gate

I follow the active plant-access marker.

My run used both labels around the same transition: the radio referred to the
East Gate while the physical status read **FILTRATION GATE IS OPEN**.

### I entered the plant but do not know where to go next

I stop searching the perimeter side of the facility and follow the
**Control Center** objective.

### The main console worked but extraction did not start

I keep completing the restart tasks.

I wait for:

**Black Start completed successfully**

before leaving the Control Center phase.

### Black Start is complete but enemies are still inside

That is not another objective.

I follow the extraction marker.

### The helicopter is active and I still have unopened loot nearby

I leave it.

The Power Plant route is already complete.
