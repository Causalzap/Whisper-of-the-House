automatic ending.

**follow the Funeral Home objective**

→ **Search Library / Search Catacomb**

→ **search 3 HERA bodies for the access code**

→ **search the Library for 4 Research Drives**

→ **follow the extraction branch generated by the run**

→ **enter the Train Yard**

→ **repair the tracks**

→ **restore underground power**

→ **open the Train Fuel Valve**

→ **initiate fueling**

→ **finish Prepare Train for Extraction**

→ **board the train**

<h2 id="lewiston-route">The Lewiston route I use</h2>

Lewiston is one of the maps where the landmarks are memorable enough to create
bad habits.

I know where the Train Yard is.

That does not mean the current run wants me there yet.

The route I confirmed moved through these major states:

1. Follow the early town objective.
2. Reach the Funeral Home phase.
3. Search the Library and Catacombs.
4. Search HERA bodies for the access code.
5. Collect the required Research Drives from the Library.
6. Wait for the mission to reveal the extraction branch.
7. If it gives the train route, move to the Train Yard.
8. Repair the broken track.
9. Restore underground power.
10. Open the Train Fuel Valve.
11. Initiate Train Fueling.
12. Finish the remaining Train Yard preparation.
13. Extract on the train.

The key decision happens before step 7.

I have also played Lewiston with a **boat extraction**.

So:

**Train**

and:

**Boat**

are alternative late routes.

I do not try to complete both in one run.

<h2 id="opening-route">
  Get enough gear to function, then follow the town objective
</h2>

Lewiston has enough stores and side buildings that I can lose a surprising
amount of time before the real route starts.

The loot can genuinely be good.

In one run I had reached **165 rifle rounds**, while another player had more
than **100 Magnum rounds**, and we were still discussing another care package.

That was past the point where another storefront was improving the run.

Once I have:

- a firearm with useful reserves;
- a melee option;
- healing;
- enough flashlight battery to move through dark interiors;

I stop shopping unless the active route takes me through another supply area.

The wider decision I use for ammo, weapon swaps, and inventory space stays the
same as in my

On Lewiston, the important part is knowing when loot is starting to pull me

<h2 id="funeral-home">
  When the Funeral Home phase appears, follow Library and Catacomb separately
</h2>

A useful Lewiston state appears at the **Funeral Home**.

My HUD showed two search directions:

**Search Library**

and:

**Search Catacomb**

That immediately narrows the map.

I stop checking unrelated stores and treat those two areas as the current
mission.

This is important because Lewiston contains plenty of buildings that look like
they should contain an objective.

A church, hardware store, pawn shop, or open storefront can be worth entering
for supplies.

That does not make it progression.

When the HUD says Library and Catacomb, those are the places I care about.

<h2 id="catacombs-access-code">
  Search 3 HERA bodies in the Catacombs for the access code
</h2>

The Catacombs branch gave me one of the clearest counters on the map.

The objective became:

**FIND ACCESS CODE**

and:

**Search HERA Bodies for Access Code (0/3)**

That tells me exactly what kind of search I am doing.

I am not searching every corpse in the Catacombs.

I am looking for the **HERA bodies** tied to the objective.

The `0/3` counter is also my progress check.

After each relevant body, I watch whether the objective advances.

If I have searched a corpse and the counter has not changed, I do not assume
that body was one of the required three.

I continue through the Catacombs until the access-code objective completes.

Once it does, I stop searching bodies and move back into the route that the new
mission state opens.

<h2 id="research-drives">
  The Library requires 4 Research Drives in my confirmed route
</h2>

The Library has its own search objective.

My HUD showed:

**FIND RESEARCH DRIVES**

and:

**Search Library for Research Drives (1/4)**

That gives me a better completion check than memory.

With several Responders spread through the building, I may not personally pick
up every drive.

Someone on another floor can advance the shared objective while I am still
searching downstairs.

So I watch the HUD.

If it still shows:

`1/4`

I know there are three remaining.

If the objective changes, I stop searching.

I do not keep opening rooms because the Library still contains loot.

The mission state tells me when the Library phase is finished.

<h2 id="train-or-boat">
  Wait for Lewiston to tell you whether this is a Train or Boat run
</h2>

This is the most important late-route decision on Lewiston.

I have completed a run that sent me to the **Train Yard**.

I have also played Lewiston with a **boat extraction**.

I therefore do not start moving rail parts, looking for underground power, or
planning the Train Yard before the current mission confirms that branch.

If the next objective points toward the harbor, I follow the boat route.

If it points to the Train Yard, I start the sequence below.

The mistake is thinking:

> I escaped Lewiston by train last time, so the train must be the ending.

The previous extraction does not decide the current one.

<h2 id="train-yard">
  Read all Train Yard objectives before moving the first rail
</h2>

The Train Yard is not one repair task.

When I entered the train route, my HUD showed:

**Extract on the Train**

**PREPARE TRAIN FOR EXTRACTION**

with multiple unfinished requirements:

**Restore Underground Power**

**Open Train Fuel Valve**

**Repair Train Tracks (0%)**

I read that list before picking up a heavy mission item.

Otherwise it is easy to grab rail, carry it across the yard, then realize I

The HUD gives me the structure:

**track**

+ **power**

+ **fuel**

→ **train ready**

<h2 id="repair-tracks">
  Carry the Railroad Rail directly to the damaged track
</h2>

The track repair uses heavy rail pieces from around the Train Yard.

When I pick one up, I stop exploring.

I take it back toward the actual damaged track.

One of my screenshots showed:

**RAILROAD RAIL x1**

while:

**Repair Train Tracks (50%)**

was active.

That is a very useful state check.

If I have reached 50%, I do not move on to extraction.

The rail objective still needs another successful repair step.

I search the nearby yard for the remaining required piece and bring it back to
the damaged section.

What I do **not** do is carry a rail through unrelated buildings.

The rail belongs to the track objective.

If I cannot see where to place it, I return to the rails and inspect the broken
section again.

<h2 id="underground-power">
  Track repair does not replace the Underground Power objective
</h2>

Finishing the rails is only part of `PREPARE TRAIN FOR EXTRACTION`.

The Train Yard still requires:

**Restore Underground Power**

The lower route is easy to misread because it mixes dark interiors, electrical
hazards, and a separate mission item.

In my run I had:

**PULLOUT FUSE x1**

while the HUD still listed:

**Restore Underground Power**

That prevents one common mistake:

> the rails are repaired, so maybe I missed another rail.

No.

If the rail objective is gone but Underground Power remains, I stop searching
the platform for track pieces and work the electrical route instead.

I also avoid wandering through the dangerous lower section without a reason.

I follow the active breaker/fuse objective, complete it, and come back out.

<h2 id="train-fueling">
  Open the fuel route, then Initiate Train Fueling
</h2>

Fueling is another separate Train Yard state.

Early in the finale my HUD listed:

**Open Train Fuel Valve**

Later, after the route advanced, the objective changed to:

**Initiate Train Fueling**

That distinction matters.

Opening the valve does not necessarily mean fueling is already underway.

I wait for the objective text to change and complete the next interaction.

During one part of the finale I could see:

**Restore Underground Power**

and:

**Initiate Train Fueling**

active at the same time.

That told me the train was still waiting on more than one subsystem.

I do not stand beside the train expecting extraction to start while either of
those remains unfinished.

<h2 id="prepare-train">
  Use Prepare Train for Extraction as the final checklist
</h2>

The most reliable late-game check is the parent objective:

**PREPARE TRAIN FOR EXTRACTION**

I keep reading the remaining sub-objectives underneath it.

If track repair is still present, I finish the rails.

If Underground Power remains, I go back to the electrical route.

If the fuel objective remains, I finish the valve/fueling sequence.

I do not try to guess which subsystem the train is waiting for.

The HUD already tells me.

Once those requirements disappear and `Prepare Train for Extraction` completes,
I stop working the yard.

<h2 id="train-extraction">
  When Prepare Train for Extraction completes, get on the train
</h2>

My confirmed train run ended with:

**Trainyard**

**Extract on the Train**

and:

**Prepare Train for Extraction**

completed.

<h2 id="lewiston-stuck">
  What I check when Lewiston stops progressing
</h2>

### The town feels too open and I do not know which building matters

I read the current HUD.

If it says **Search Library / Search Catacomb**, I stop checking unrelated
stores.

### I am in the Catacombs but cannot find the access code

I look specifically for the HERA bodies tied to:

**Search HERA Bodies for Access Code (0/3)**

and watch that counter.

### The Library looks cleared but the objective remains

I check the Research Drive count.

My confirmed route used **4 drives**, and the HUD showed `1/4` while the search
was still in progress.

### I finished the Library and went straight to the Train Yard

I check the newly generated extraction branch first.

Lewiston can send the run to a boat instead.

### The track is repaired but the train is still not ready

I stop looking for more rail.

I check:

**Restore Underground Power**

and:

**Open Train Fuel Valve / Initiate Train Fueling**

instead.

### The rail objective says 50%

The track repair is not finished.

I return to the yard and locate the remaining required rail piece.

### I opened the Train Fuel Valve but extraction did not start

I check whether the objective has advanced to **Initiate Train Fueling** and
whether Underground Power is still incomplete.

### All Train Yard sub-objectives are gone

I follow **Extract on the Train** and leave.
