<h2 id="raven-rock-route">The Raven Rock route I use</h2>

Raven Rock looks more linear than the town maps because so much of it happens
inside the bunker.

In practice, I found it easier to get turned around here.

Several corridors, staircases, security rooms, and locked passages look equally
plausible, and completing one system objective can suddenly make an older door
or tunnel useful.

My confirmed multiplayer route is:

1. Reach **West Gate**.
2. Complete the bunker access sequence.
3. Work the numbered-letter blast-door controls.
4. Enter Raven Rock.
5. Follow the Expansion Tunnel and water-pressure objectives.
6. Use objective distance when the bunker route becomes unclear.
7. Enter the **Reservoir Tunnel**.
8. Follow the route marked toward **Central Command**.
9. Reboot the servers.
10. Keep the Bio-Scanners clear.
11. Read the code name requested for the Expansion Tunnel terminal.
12. Enter the matching six-digit code.
13. Follow the newly active green-light route.
14. Use the ladder and zipline toward the surface.
15. Restore topside power.
16. Finish the remaining startup/extraction state.
17. Take the final Fast Rope.

The habit that matters most is checking what changed after every major
interaction.

If a system objective completes, I look for the newly opened route before
backtracking through the corridors I already searched.

<h2 id="west-gate">
  Start at West Gate and solve the bunker access objective
</h2>

My run entered Raven Rock through **West Gate**.

The HUD makes the immediate goal simple:

**Enter Raven Rock**

The bunker is locked down, so I do not waste time trying exterior doors in the
hope that one bypasses the main sequence.

The blast-door controls are the route.

I take supplies that are directly around the access area if I need them, but I
do not delay the bunker objective to build a perfect loadout.

Once I can fight and move safely, I work the controls.

<h2 id="blast-door-controls">
  My blast-door sequence was 2B → 1B → 2C
</h2>

The first bunker access problem uses numbered-letter labels above the doors.

In my multiplayer clear, the sequence was:

**2B**

→ **1B**

→ **2C**

I read the labels instead of running between switches randomly.

With several Responders, the fastest approach is simply to call out the panels
people can already see.

Once one panel in the requested sequence has been handled, I stop checking it
and move to the next.

If I return to Raven Rock later and the active objective presents a different
state, I still trust the current panel instructions over the sequence I
remember.

Once the blast-door objective advances, I leave the entrance controls behind.

<h2 id="expansion-tunnel">
  Solve the water-pressure objective before searching for the Expansion Tunnel itself
</h2>

Deeper inside Raven Rock, the mission begins pointing toward the
**Expansion Tunnel**.

The mistake I made was hearing that destination and immediately trying to find
the tunnel entrance.

The current objective was actually **water pressure**.

So I stopped searching for the final doorway and completed the system that was
blocking the route first.

My progression became:

**water-pressure objective**

→ **new bunker passage**

→ **Expansion Tunnel route**

That distinction matters because Raven Rock repeatedly shows me a destination
before the path to it is actually ready.

If the HUD is still asking me to fix a system, I solve that system.

I do not keep testing the locked tunnel entrance.

<h2 id="objective-distance">
  If the marker goes from about 40m to 50m+, I am moving the wrong way
</h2>

The Expansion Tunnel section gave me one of the best navigation checks on the
whole map.

I was climbing through the bunker because the route felt like it should be
upstairs.

Then I watched the objective distance move from roughly:

**40 meters**

to:

**50+ meters**

I turned around.

That is now my rule when Raven Rock starts looking repetitive:

**distance steadily falling**

→ keep moving.

**distance steadily increasing**

→ stop forcing that corridor or staircase.

I do not need to memorize every bunker junction if the live objective is already
telling me whether I am getting closer.

This is especially useful where several floors overlap vertically and the
marker can look close even though I am on the wrong level.

<h2 id="reservoir-tunnel">
  Use the Reservoir Tunnel route to reach Central Command
</h2>

Once the correct lower route opened, the mission pulled me through the
**Reservoir Tunnel**.

The HUD then changed to:

**Enter the Central Command**

The physical route also becomes easier to confirm here because the stairway is
marked toward **COMMAND**.

At that point I stop treating nearby bunker rooms as possible progression.

I follow the Reservoir Tunnel into the command section.

This is a good Raven Rock state check:

> **Reservoir Tunnel + Enter the Central Command = I am past the earlier
> Expansion Tunnel navigation problem.**

I do not backtrack toward West Gate looking for another access panel.

<h2 id="expansion-tunnel-code">
  Match the terminal's code name before entering the six digits
</h2>

After the Central Command systems finished, my run provided the information
needed for the Expansion Tunnel access terminal.

The terminal requested:

**PPJ Beta**

The matching six-digit number in that run was:

**959728**

I do not use that as:

> Raven Rock password = 959728.

I use it as one confirmed example of how the code system works.

My check is:

1. Read the **code designator** requested by the terminal.
2. Find that same designator in the current code information.
3. Enter its six-digit value.
4. Stop searching code information once the mission advances.

That matters because another code from a different route or version can be a
perfectly real number and still be wrong for the terminal in front of me.

If the terminal asks for **PPJ Beta**, I use the PPJ Beta entry from that run.

In my multiplayer clear, that was `959728`.

There is no reason to keep searching Central Command after the correct code has
advanced the objective.

With teammates, I make sure the next person has found the vertical route before
I disappear too far ahead.

I do not send half the squad topside while the other half is still wandering
around Central Command.

  Reaching the surface does not mean Raven Rock is finished
</h2>

The first time I reached the topside area, it felt like the bunker section was
over and extraction should be next.

There is still another infrastructure objective.

At **NEXRAD Station NWCX**, my HUD required power restoration.

The useful state here is:

**Restore Power (2/2)**

Once both required power steps are complete, I stop searching the area for
additional fuses.

Earlier in this phase, a fuse matters because the objective still needs it.

After `2/2`, another spare fuse is no longer evidence that I missed a power
objective.

I follow whatever startup or extraction step the HUD gives me next.

That is a much cleaner check than carrying every fuse I find and wondering
whether there is another hidden slot somewhere.

The only useful question is:

> **Where is the current pickup marker?**

<h2 id="raven-rock-stuck">
  What I check when Raven Rock stops progressing
</h2>

### West Gate is locked and I cannot enter

I stay with the bunker access objective.

I do not search the exterior for a shortcut around the blast door.

### I cannot tell which blast-door control comes next

I read the numbered-letter panels above the doors.

My confirmed multiplayer sequence was:

**2B → 1B → 2C**

### I am looking for the Expansion Tunnel but every corridor feels wrong

I check whether the current objective is actually asking me to solve water
pressure first.

If it is, I finish that system before searching for the tunnel itself.

### The Expansion Tunnel marker was around 40m and is now above 50m

I turn around.

That is the clearest sign I have moved onto the wrong level or corridor.

### I reached the Reservoir Tunnel

I follow **Enter the Central Command** and the physical COMMAND route instead of
backtracking through the bunker.

### Central Command is not advancing

I check whether the servers and Bio-Scanners are still the active objective.

I stay with that process until the security state changes.

### The Expansion Tunnel terminal rejects 959728

I stop treating `959728` as a universal password.

That number matched **PPJ Beta** in one multiplayer run. I check which code name
the current terminal actually requests.

### I entered the correct code but still feel lost

I look for the **green lights**.

Once that route activates, it is the path toward the ladder, zipline, and
surface.

### I reached the surface but extraction did not start

I check the NEXRAD power objective.

My route still required **Restore Power (2/2)** before the final state.

### Restore Power already says 2/2

I stop looking for more fuse slots and follow the next startup or extraction
objective.

### The Fast Rope is active

I take it.

Raven Rock is finished.
