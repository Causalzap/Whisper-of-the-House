<h2 id="ancient-anomaly-walkthrough-for-all-three-missions">
  Ancient Anomaly walkthrough for all three missions
</h2>

Ancient Anomaly begins with Combat Synths and gun turrets in Dr. Pierce's quarry, shifts to Pathogen inside the Engineer ship, and ends against the surviving Engineer hybrids. Its three missions are linear, but several objectives can look complete before the next step appears.

The main traps are easy to recognize once you know what the game expects.

- Quarry turrets are disabled from nearby terminals rather than destroyed from range.
- A corrupted intel upload cannot be repaired at the same console.
- The hybrid cryopods cannot be broken with gunfire.
- Finally, the fourth Engineer console destroys about 95% of the hybrids but leaves a final group for you to fight.

<h3 id="before-starting-ancient-anomaly">
  Before starting Ancient Anomaly
</h3>

I bring one weapon that can deal with distant Synths and one that can control enemies rushing through a narrow room. If the current loadout already does both jobs, I keep it. This is not a good campaign for testing two unfamiliar weapons because the enemy mix changes during Mission 5-1 and both later missions contain terminal defenses.

A turret, grenade, enhanced ammunition, or damage stim is useful, but I save the strongest option for Mission 5-3. The early quarry fights give you room to use cover; the final command-room defense does not. For broader weapon, attachment, damage-type, and Perk Grid decisions, use the <Link href="/aliens-fireteam-elite-2/beginner-guide">beginner guide</Link>.

Ancient Anomaly unlocks after completing all three Rock Bottom missions and returning to the Endeavor for the required conversations. If it remains locked after Hive Mind, do not replay the mission immediately. Finish the final pickup, return to the ship, and complete the marked debriefs described in the <Link href="/aliens-fireteam-elite-2/rock-bottom-walkthrough">Rock Bottom walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="mission-5-1-find-dr-pierces-quarry-lab">
  Mission 5-1: Find Dr. Pierce's quarry lab
</h2>

<h3 id="cross-the-quarry-and-collect-the-first-intel">
  Cross the quarry and collect the first intel
</h3>

Follow the marked route from the landing point into the quarry. Combat Synths control the opening area, so use the available cover and remove enemies firing from elevated or distant positions before crossing exposed ground.

The first intel confirms that Dr. Pierce had been trying to condition Xenomorph behavior. Send it, clear the remaining Synths, and continue toward the facility. If the objective marker remains beyond a locked door, keep moving forward until Raptor warns you about the turret corridors; there is no key to collect in the quarry.

I avoid spending a limited-use item during this opening unless the team is already losing control. The quarry provides enough space to break line of sight, and the later terminal rooms are more dangerous places to be caught without crowd control.

<h3 id="disable-the-turret-corridors-from-the-terminals">
  Disable the turret corridors from the terminals
</h3>

Raptor warns that a series of gun turrets covers the next few corridors. The first instinct is to shoot them from the doorway, but sustained fire wastes ammunition and does not replace the required interaction.

Each turret section has a nearby terminal. Clear the Synths that can fire on the interaction point, stay behind cover until the immediate lane is safe, and use the terminal. Move through only after the turrets shut down and the next objective appears.

Advance one corridor at a time. I do not start the next interaction while a Synth is still firing from the previous room because the terminal leaves the player exposed. If a door does not open after the fight, confirm that the marked terminal was fully activated and check for a surviving enemy before backtracking.

Raptor eventually confirms that the turret route is clear. From that point, continue deeper into the quarry. None of the disabled corridors contains another progression item.

<h3 id="continue-past-the-second-intel-and-prepare-for-pathogen">
  Continue past the second intel and prepare for Pathogen
</h3>

The middle of Mission 5-1 is a longer combat push with another record from Dr. Pierce. It explains that Weyland-Yutani was sending the Demetrius and that she still needed classified research to finish her work.

Once that record has played, follow the objective toward the laboratory. Do not return to the turret area looking for another switch. Use the ammunition and health supply encountered on the approach before entering the darker facility rooms.

Pale, white Xenomorph-like creatures appear near the laboratory. Raptor identifies them as Pathogen. This enemy change is the signal to stop fighting as though the rest of the mission is Synth-only. I switch to the weapon with the better magazine and closer-range control rather than continuing to rely on a narrow anti-mechanical option.

<h3 id="search-the-dead-hybrid-and-cryogenic-displays">
  Search the dead hybrid and cryogenic displays
</h3>

After clearing the Pathogen rooms, enter Dr. Pierce's laboratory and search the marked objects. The room contains a dead Engineer hybrid on a slab and several altered creatures inside cryogenic displays.

Do not run through the laboratory looking for an extraction zone. The objective ends inside this room. Search the hybrid, inspect the marked displays, and retrieve the final intel.

The final record shows that Dr. Pierce released the Xenomorphs after Weyland-Yutani attempted to take her Engineer research. Let the conversation finish. There is no boss behind the cryogenic displays and no separate extraction sequence.

If Mission 5-1 does not complete, check the laboratory rather than returning to the quarry. One of the marked displays or the final intel interaction has probably been missed.

<h3 id="before-mission-5-2">
  Before Mission 5-2
</h3>

Back on the Endeavor, complete the required conversations before selecting Mission 5-2. The next operation is dominated by Pathogen inside the Engineer ship, so this is the right time to restore used consumables and replace a weapon that only worked well against Synths. Keep any ranked general-purpose weapon that performed reliably in the second half of 5-1.

<h2 id="mission-5-2-search-the-engineer-ship">
  Mission 5-2: Search the Engineer ship
</h2>

<h3 id="follow-the-intel-markers-into-the-ship">
  Follow the intel markers into the ship
</h3>

Mission 5-2 begins outside the Engineer ship and moves into its interior after the opening Pathogen fight. The first record explains that miners discovered the ship in the quarry and that Dr. Pierce redirected the operation to excavate it.

Continue through the metallic passages, heavy plant growth, multi-armed Pathogen, and the chamber containing pale fluid and eggs. The Engineer architecture makes the route appear more open than it is, but the mission does not branch.

I follow the active marker and check the motion tracker after each wave. If the room is clear and the fireteam reports that no terminal is present, continue forward. That dialogue does not mean the missing terminal is behind you.

A later record describes the preserved Engineer body Dr. Pierce found aboard the ship. Inspect the marked relief when prompted and continue following the investigation. These objects advance the same intel chain; they do not unlock optional side routes.

<h3 id="leave-the-corrupted-intel-and-find-another-terminal">
  Leave the corrupted intel and find another terminal
</h3>

Eventually, an intel upload fails because the data is corrupted. The original terminal remains visible, which makes it look as though you should wait or interact with it again.

Do not stay at that console. The objective has moved to a different terminal. Turn away from the failed upload and follow the new marker through the next section of the ship.

Starting the second attempt brings another Pathogen attack. Move away from the interaction point once the upload begins so the team has a clear firing line. Stay close enough to return when the record is ready, but do not let the whole squad become trapped against the terminal.

The recovered intel explains how Dr. Pierce was exposed to Acidium and why she began searching for a cure. Once it finishes, continue toward the next marker. The console does not also open a hidden door in the same room.

<h3 id="wait-for-the-database-to-identify-esther">
  Wait for the database to identify Esther
</h3>

The final terminal sequence contains another apparent failure. Dr. Pierce's first request cannot access the Engineer research database, but the sequence is not over.

Stay in the area and allow the request to continue. It changes from directly accessing the database to searching for anyone near LV-558 who already has authorization. The search returns one result: **Esther, model 1089-12/A7**, from Fireteam Banshee.

This discovery explains the distress signal: Dr. Pierce deliberately brought Fireteam Banshee to LV-558 because she needed Esther's classified Engineer research.

Mission 5-2 ends after the complete access record plays. There is no boss and no additional extraction terminal. If the mission does not transition, clear any remaining enemy in the room and wait for the final dialogue instead of retracing the ship.

<h3 id="before-mission-5-3">
  Before Mission 5-3
</h3>

Return to the Endeavor and speak with Esther and General Shipp. Mission 5-3 unlocks after the debrief confirms that the distress signal was bait and that Dr. Pierce used Esther to complete the hybrids.

Before launching the last mission, replace the consumables used during the terminal defenses. I keep the weapons that already handled the Pathogen in 5-2 and save the strongest damage item for the final hybrids. Buying an unranked weapon at this point only makes sense if the current loadout has an obvious weakness and the replacement still meets the mission's displayed Combat Rating requirement.

<h2 id="mission-5-3-destroy-the-engineer-hybrids">
  Mission 5-3: Destroy the Engineer hybrids
</h2>

<h3 id="decrypt-the-first-record-and-find-the-cryopods">
  Decrypt the first record and find the cryopods
</h3>

Return to the Engineer ship and fight through the opening Pathogen rooms. Pass the large Engineer-head sculpture and continue into the interior until the fireteam finds an encrypted record.

The record cannot be read where it is found. Follow the objective to a decryption terminal, start the process, and defend the room while the data is prepared. The decrypted message confirms that Dr. Pierce moved the hybrids back into the Engineer ship.

Continue forward until the fireteam reaches a chamber containing hundreds of altered cryopods. Do not shoot the pods. They are part of an investigation objective and cannot be destroyed with normal weapons.

Search the marked pod data and clear the Pathogen that enter the chamber.

The cryopod intel also requires decryption at another terminal. Follow the new marker rather than returning to the first decrypt. Once the second record is ready, it reveals the hybrids' vulnerability: restoring power to the Engineer ship will destroy the experiments inside their pods.

Only after this record finishes does the mission change from searching for intel to activating the ship.

<h3 id="activate-all-four-engineer-consoles">
  Activate all four Engineer consoles
</h3>

You must activate **four Engineer consoles**. They are large and clearly marked, but each interaction can expose the player to another Pathogen attack.

Clear the immediate room before using a console. After activating it, wait for the confirmation and the next marker instead of running into an unmarked corridor.

The first two consoles follow the forward route through the ship. Once the second activation registers, Ko confirms that only two are left. If you do not hear that confirmation, check the last console before moving deeper.

A massive hologram of an Engineer ship appears as the systems come online. It is a progress landmark, not another objective. Clear the Pathogen in the room and continue toward the marked console.

The route then reaches the command center and a larger hologram. Clear the active wave before committing to the next interaction. The consoles are easy to see, but their activation animation leaves you vulnerable if the team runs past surviving enemies.

<h3 id="prepare-before-using-the-final-command-station">
  Prepare before using the final command station
</h3>

The fourth console is the command station in the middle of the last room. Refill both main weapons before activating it. If an Aid Kit is available, use it only when it restores enough health to justify leaving the command-room supply behind.

This is the point to deploy a saved turret and use the damage consumables carried through the mission. Place area control where it covers an entry lane while leaving the center open for movement. Saving those items beyond the fourth console provides no advantage because the final Ancient Anomaly fight begins in this room.

<h3 id="kill-the-hybrids-that-survive-the-95-percent-casualty-event">
  Kill the hybrids that survive the 95% casualty event
</h3>

Activating the fourth console destroys most of the experiments, but it does not finish the mission. Ko reports an approximately **95% casualty rate** and warns that several hybrids survived.

Stay in the command room. This is a defense objective, not an escape sequence.

Fight close enough to the ammunition supply that the team can refill between targets, but leave space around the command station to reposition. The hybrids withstand far more damage than the Pathogen encountered on the approach, so spreading fire across several targets makes the defense longer.

I focus on the same hybrid as the rest of the team and use crowd control to prevent smaller enemies from interrupting that firing line. The player being chased should create distance around the room while teammates with clear angles continue dealing damage.

Do not stand directly in front of a hybrid and trade health for damage. In solo play, make short rotations around the command station rather than running to the far edge of the room and separating from the bots.

If the objective remains active after the visible hybrids die, do not return to the fourth console. Search behind the command station and along the outer edges of the room for a surviving enemy. The mission completes only after Ko confirms that all remaining hybrids are down.

<h2 id="what-to-do-after-ancient-anomaly">
  What to do after Ancient Anomaly
</h2>

Completing the command-room fight finishes Ancient Anomaly and unlocks **The Story of the Quarry**.

Return to the Endeavor and finish the conversations with Piper and General Shipp. Dr. Pierce escaped the Engineer ship and returned to the mines, and Shipp's debrief unlocks the final operation.

If Final Endeavor remains unavailable, confirm that Mission 5-3 shows complete and speak to every marked crew member before replaying anything. Once **Reckoning** appears at the campaign terminal, continue with the <Link href="/aliens-fireteam-elite-2/final-endeavor-walkthrough">Final Endeavor walkthrough</Link>.
