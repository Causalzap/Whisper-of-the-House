<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Complete **Bomb Squad**, **Dumpster Diving**, and **Mission 3.3** aboard the Demetrius.

In **Bomb Squad**, move below deck, clear the hangar, activate all three warhead terminals, and survive the pickup.

In **Dumpster Diving**, search each marked intel terminal, continue through Maintenance and Life Support, then defend the final upload in the Cargo Bay.

In **Mission 3.3**, investigate the empty cryopods, collect the remaining records, clear the hive, reach the Engine Bay, neutralize the engines, and hold until Captain Ko retrieves the fireteam.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="boarding-party-walkthrough-for-all-three-missions">
  Boarding Party walkthrough for all three missions
</h2>

Boarding Party begins after Esther is recovered during the <Link href="/aliens-fireteam-elite-2/rescue-and-recovery-walkthrough">Rescue and Recovery campaign</Link>. Her records reveal a hidden Weyland-Yutani warship called the **Demetrius**, still carrying live warheads in orbit around LV-558.

All three missions take place aboard the same damaged ship, but the objective changes on every visit. First I disable the warheads, then I search the control sections for corporate records, and finally I follow the evidence linking Dr. Pierce to the missing colonists.

The route is mostly linear. What causes trouble is ammunition management through long interior fights and sealed doors that sometimes wait for one surviving enemy rather than another terminal interaction.

<h3 id="before-boarding-the-demetrius">
  Before boarding the Demetrius
</h3>

Wrench warns that Weyland-Yutani ships use tight, claustrophobic corridors. I follow his advice and bring a shotgun, SMG, or another weapon that can stop a Xenomorph before it reaches grappling distance.

I do not replace both main weapons with close-range options. Boarding Party gradually introduces more Security Synthetics, and they are much easier to handle with accurate fire or Piercing damage.

My practical loadout has:

- one close-range weapon for the corridors and Facehuggers;
- one accurate weapon for Synthetics and distant targets;
- a filled Perk Grid rather than several empty unlocked spaces;
- at least one ability capable of interrupting or controlling a swarm.

I check the enemy icons on the launch screen before every mission. Bomb Squad leans heavily toward Xenomorph pressure, while the later Demetrius visits mix Xenomorphs with armed Synthetics.

I also avoid spending all my Requisition on a new weapon immediately before boarding. An unranked weapon without its trait can feel weaker than the gun I already know, even if its base numbers look better in the Armory.

<h2 id="bomb-squad">
  Bomb Squad walkthrough: disable the Demetrius warheads
</h2>

Bomb Squad requires the fireteam to activate **three terminals** connected to the live warheads. Piper directs the operation while I move from the upper ship sections to the hangar and warhead chamber.

<h3 id="search-the-bodies-and-move-through-the-med-bay">
  Search the bodies and move through the Med Bay
</h3>

The first bodies appear almost immediately after boarding. I search the marked group for intel before continuing into the Med Bay.

The record confirms that Weyland-Yutani knew Xenomorphs were loose aboard the Demetrius. It does not activate one of the three warhead terminals, so I keep following the main objective deeper into the ship.

The Med Bay and adjoining corridors are narrow enough that a Xenomorph can cross the room before I finish a long reload. I use the sidearm or short controlled bursts against isolated enemies and keep the close-range weapon loaded for grapples and larger groups.

When the objective directs me below deck, I take the downward route rather than continuing to search the upper rooms. Piper does not mark the first terminal until I approach the hangar.

<h3 id="restock-below-deck-before-entering-the-hangar">
  Restock below deck before entering the hangar
</h3>

The lower section contains Facehuggers, a growing hive presence, cargo vehicles, and several sustained attacks. I refill at the ammo crate before pushing toward the Waste Room and hangar.

This supply point matters because the three terminal interactions are close together near the end of the mission. Arriving there with one empty main weapon makes the final holdout much harder.

If a Facehugger attaches to a teammate, I create space before firing into it. Shooting at point-blank range can leave the rescued player standing in acid, especially when the rest of the swarm is still entering the corridor.

The route eventually passes trucks positioned near the warhead storage area. These vehicles are part of the story evidence rather than separate search objectives, so I keep moving until Piper identifies the hangar.

<h3 id="activate-all-three-warhead-terminals">
  Clear the hangar and activate all three warhead terminals
</h3>

The first warhead terminal is **outside the hangar door**. The marker can appear while enemies are still active in the room, so I clear the nearby attackers before committing to the interaction.

After the first terminal is complete, I move through the hangar and into the chamber containing the warheads. The remaining two terminals are inside this area.

Clear the immediate attackers before using the second and third controls. They are marked separately, so completing one does not automatically finish the other side of the chamber.

<h3 id="hold-the-warhead-room-after-the-third-terminal">
  Hold the warhead room after the third terminal
</h3>

The third interaction neutralizes the warheads and immediately changes the objective to a holdout. I stay in the room instead of running back toward the entrance looking for an extraction marker.

I remain near the warhead room's supplies and use its open space to circle larger enemies. Retreating deep into the previous corridors can separate the fireteam and leave no safe angle for a revive.

Once the required attackers are dead, the pickup completes automatically.

<h2 id="dumpster-diving">
  Dumpster Diving walkthrough: search the Demetrius terminals
</h2>

Dumpster Diving returns to the Demetrius through its Operations section. This time the objective is to recover corporate records explaining what the ship was preparing to transport from Storr's Boon.

<h3 id="search-the-operations-and-intelligence-terminals">
  Search the Operations and Intelligence terminals
</h3>

The first terminal is close to the mission entrance. I search it immediately, send the record to Raptor, and prepare for the Synthetics that enter after the upload begins.

The route then moves through the Intelligence section. These terminals are not optional collectibles. Each marked search advances the mission and reveals another part of Weyland-Yutani's plans.

One upload arrives in poor condition and takes Raptor additional time to recover. I hold the room until he finishes instead of leaving the terminal and looking for a different route.

If the next door remains locked after the transmission, I check the motion tracker for a surviving enemy. A Synthetic left behind cover or a Xenomorph caught in the previous corridor can make the route appear stuck.

<h3 id="leave-maintenance-from-the-second-floor">
  Leave Maintenance from the second floor
</h3>

After Raptor recovers the damaged record, the route turns toward the Cargo Bay. I leave Maintenance through the **second floor** and continue into Life Support.

A Siren can appear shortly after this transition. I focus it before chasing the smaller enemies because its pressure makes it difficult for the team to hold formation in the narrow ship corridors.

I restock after this fight. Dumpster Diving is long enough that conserving every consumable for the final room is counterproductive. If a turret or crowd-control item prevents a down here, I still reach the Cargo Bay with more resources than I would after spending a first aid kit on the entire squad.

Life Support contains another marked intel interaction. Once it is transmitted, the route continues toward the large Cargo Bay.

<h3 id="refill-before-the-final-cargo-bay-upload">
  Refill before starting the final Cargo Bay upload
</h3>

The Cargo Bay is much larger than the earlier rooms and contains the final terminal. I identify the ammo crate and first aid supplies before using it.

Activating this terminal begins a sustained defense. I keep one side of the team near the terminal while watching the open approaches rather than pushing deeper into the Cargo Bay.

The room provides more space than the corridors, but that space can work against the squad. Chasing one enemy too far can leave the terminal exposed or isolate a teammate from the rest of the fireteam.

When ammunition becomes low, I rotate past the crate between waves rather than waiting until both main weapons are empty. The upload finishes after the required groups are killed, and the mission returns the fireteam to the Endeavor.

<h2 id="mission-3-3">
  Mission 3.3 walkthrough: uncover Dr. Pierce's experiment
</h2>

The last Boarding Party mission searches the remaining section of the Demetrius. Captain Ko wants evidence of Dr. Pierce's unknown experiment, while Esther wants to know why her systems were compromised.

<h3 id="investigate-the-empty-cryopods">
  Investigate the empty cryopods
</h3>

The opening route leads directly into a room of empty cryopods with medical charts still active. I investigate the marked pod area, then search the nearby terminal.

The first record states that the excavation was complete and the colonists were ready to be moved. That is the clue I need; there is no body, key, or separate item hidden inside each pod.

After transmitting it, I follow the objective into the next ship section. The route mixes Xenomorphs and armed Synthetics, so I stop relying exclusively on the shotgun and use accurate fire against enemies holding distant cover.

<h3 id="clear-the-cryofuel-room">
  Clear the Cryofuel Room and wait for the transmission
</h3>

The Cryofuel Room contains enough storage for hundreds of people. I eliminate the Synthetics before moving through it because the story transmission does not safely pause the fight.

Dr. Pierce then interrupts and reveals that she tried to destroy the Demetrius. When control returns, I leave the Cryofuel Room through the marked exit and continue into Electrical.

The transmission is not an instruction to search the Cryofuel containers individually. The next required evidence is farther ahead.

<h3 id="find-the-second-record-in-electrical">
  Find the second record in Electrical
</h3>

Electrical contains another marked intel source and a fresh Xenomorph attack. I clear the immediate enemies, send the record, and listen for the objective update before moving on.

This record confirms that Dr. Pierce moved her experiment and the missing colonists into the mines beneath Storr's Boon.

Captain Ko reports a hive ahead. I push through it while staying near the fireteam; the tighter passages make grapples especially dangerous, and acid can block the most direct revive path.

I refill at the later ammo and first aid point before entering the Engine Bay. This is the last reliable preparation window before the final fight.

<h3 id="secure-the-engine-bay">
  Secure the Engine Bay before neutralizing the engines
</h3>

When I enter the Engine Bay, the engines are already overheating. I clear the room before interacting with the machinery.

I stay near the room's supply side and avoid pursuing enemies through the far exits. The fight continues long enough to empty a primary weapon, so I use the infinite-ammo sidearm against isolated smaller targets rather than spending the last rifle rounds on them.

Once Captain Ko confirms that the room is secure, I use the marked engine objective. Neutralizing the engines begins the final holdout.

<h3 id="hold-the-engine-bay-until-captain-ko-arrives">
  Hold the Engine Bay until Captain Ko arrives
</h3>

There is no separate route back through the Demetrius. I remain in the Engine Bay and continue clearing the incoming enemies while Captain Ko approaches.

The engines being neutralized can feel like the end of the mission, but the pickup still requires another wave. I stay within quick reach of the supplies and keep a clear revive route between teammates instead of pushing toward the exits.

The pickup completes automatically after the last required wave.

<h2 id="what-to-do-after-boarding-party">
  What to do after Boarding Party
</h2>

Returning to the Endeavor finishes Boarding Party and unlocks **“Worst Party Ever.”**

The recovered records point back to the mines beneath Storr's Boon. That investigation begins in the <Link href="/aliens-fireteam-elite-2/rock-bottom-walkthrough">Rock Bottom walkthrough</Link>.
