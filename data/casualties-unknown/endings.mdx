<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
**Casualties: Unknown has three main completion endings:** **Bio-pod** (return the cargo and comply in the lab), **Escape** (complete an **Unchipped** run with a firearm, rebel in the lab, remove the cargo, and escape), and **M.E.R.C.** (on a later **chipped**, non-Endless run, assemble the **M.E.R.C. Scarf** from six chips and reach the surface carrying it). Death and unsuccessful escape attempts can produce different end screens, but they are not additional main endings.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

All three endings require you to return to the surface with the cargo. Bio-pod and Escape continue inside the company's facility, while M.E.R.C. triggers at the surface when its conditions are met. If you are still trying to reach the surface, follow the <Link href="/casualties-unknown/progression">11-layer progression guide</Link>.

<h2 id="biopod-ending">
  How to Get the Bio-pod Ending (Company's Best)
</h2>

After returning to the surface with the cargo, board the arriving pod to return to the company's facility.

Once the scientist finishes talking, **comply and proceed through the indicated door**. The guards escort you to a fluid-filled bio-pod. You relinquish your equipment and enter the capsule, ending the run under the company's control.

<Image
  src="/images/casualties-unknown/casualties-unknown-biopod-ending.webp"
  alt="Bio-pod ending text explaining that the experiment follows company orders and enters the fluid-filled capsule"
  width={1392}
  height={900}
  className="rounded-lg"
/>

A chipped character cannot use weapons to rebel during this sequence. An Unchipped character can also receive the Bio-pod ending by choosing not to resist. **You do not need to start a new run simply to obtain this ending if you already reached the lab with no firearm.**

<h2 id="escape-ending">
  How to Get the Escape Ending (Company's Worst)
</h2>

The Escape ending requires preparation **before** you retrieve the cargo. Start the run in **Unchipped** mode and **carry a working firearm into the final lab**. Do not assume you can obtain one after arriving at the facility.

1. **Reach the surface with the cargo.** Keep your working firearm accessible for the laboratory sequence.
2. **Board the surface pod** and let it carry you to the facility. You must reach the lab; the initial surface scene is not the Escape ending by itself.
3. **Listen to the scientist's introduction.** Once item use becomes available, draw your firearm and shoot the scientist rather than following the orders to leave through the door.
4. **React to the breach alarm.** Armed security will pursue you. Move through the facility toward the area where the cargo can be removed. Bring your firearm and enough supplies to survive the fighting and movement.
5. **Perform the abdominal procedure.** Follow the on-screen prompts: apply the local anesthetic, operate the surgical device to remove the cargo, and put the tubes over the wound when prompted.
6. **Continue toward the exit** until the corridor sequence and freedom text appear. The procedure is not the end—you still have to leave the facility alive.

After the surgical device interaction, the game prompts you to place tubes over the wound. Do not leave this stage half-finished just because the cargo appears to have moved. Follow the current on-screen instruction, then continue the escape.

### How do you know the Escape ending worked?

<Image
  src="/images/casualties-unknown/casualties-unknown-escape-freedom.webp"

The final freedom text, not merely the laboratory alarm, confirms that you successfully escaped.

**Common ways the escape fails:** arriving with a chip, forgetting to bring a firearm, getting overwhelmed by security, or leaving the surgical sequence incomplete. If you are captured or become incapacitated, you may be returned to the Bio-pod outcome instead of escaping.

<h2 id="merc-ending">
  How to Get the M.E.R.C. Ending (To Be Continued...)
</h2>

The M.E.R.C. ending triggers at the surface before the company takes you back to its laboratory. Its required components must be collected during the descent.

### 1. Complete a previous run and choose the correct settings

First obtain a completed Bio-pod or Escape ending to unlock this route. Then start a new run with the **brain chip enabled** and **Endless disabled**. An Unchipped or Endless run is not the intended route for this ending.

### 2. Find the Nano-tech Scanner in Layer 1

Find the malfunctioning **Tech-Ball** in Layer 1, take its **Nano-tech Scanner**, and read it **before leaving that layer**. It reveals the M.E.R.C. Scarf recipe and provides signals for the hidden chip locations.

The scanner tracks signals within your current layer. Follow the indicated direction and search before descending again, because the chip's position changes with the generated cave layout.

### 3. Collect the six required chips

<GuideTable
  caption="All six M.E.R.C. chips and their required layers."
  columns={mercChipColumns}
  rows={mercChipRows}
/>

**There is no required M.E.R.C. chip in Layers 6 or 8.** Do not waste a run searching those layers for another scarf component. If the scanner reports a signal in one of the listed layers, search that layer before descending again.

### 4. Craft the M.E.R.C. Scarf at 18 INT

Gather a **Scarf**, a **Circuit Board**, and **all six chips**. The M.E.R.C. Scarf recipe requires **18 INT**. Keep the required materials with you until you can craft it; reaching the cargo without the completed scarf will not unlock this ending.

Milky can be a practical choice because of the character's intelligence advantages, but **Milky is not a required character**. The requirement is the crafting level and the completed item, not a particular character name.

### 5. Carry the scarf to the surface with the cargo

Return to the surface with the cargo and **keep the completed M.E.R.C. Scarf on your character**. The ending triggers before the usual return trip to the company's laboratory.

Instead of boarding the pod, the character refuses the company's orders. The cargo causes a fatal rupture, but the technology incorporated into the scarf allows consciousness and the body to be restored. The final scene points toward the events of *Gunsaw*.

<h2 id="character-endings">
  Do Experiment, Milky, and Dune Have Different Endings?
</h2>

Experiment, Milky, and Dune can receive different dialogue and character-specific scenes within the same ending route. Dune also has a special Escape variation when an Unchipped run is completed without brain damage.
