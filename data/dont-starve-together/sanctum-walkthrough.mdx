Enter the **Sanctum** through the Giant Whirlpool, equip a **Waymark Compass**, and bring **4 Security Pulses** to the Key Room. Activate the four Pulse Cradles, then release **4 Lustrous Weevils** and push their curled shells into the four sockets to obtain the **Keystone**.

Four Ancient Towers stand motionless around the Keystone chamber. They only awaken after you bring in four Security Pulses, and even then, defeating them is not what unlocks the Keystone. The final mechanism requires four smaller creatures to be moved into sockets while the towers threaten the room.

Reaching that chamber is a separate challenge. The Sanctum is a maze of connected rooms, its Waymarks do not always lead where their positions suggest, and one crossing forces you to manipulate creatures that copy your jumps across collapsing stone pillars.

You can avoid most of the unnecessary backtracking by finding a Waymark Compass, collecting four Security Pulses before reaching the Key Room, and leaving optional rooms until you have a clear route through the Sanctum.

## How to Enter the Sanctum

The Sanctum is reached through a **Giant Whirlpool** in the ocean. Entering it takes you underground but destroys the boat, so bring a **Rope** to establish a more convenient return route.

Inside the Sanctum lobby, a Chasm connects back toward the Ancient Archives. Using the Rope there allows you to travel between the two areas without sacrificing another boat on every visit.

It is worth preparing the Ancient Archives first:

1. Locate the **Ancient Archives** in the caves.
2. Activate the **Archive Switch** using an **Iridescent Gem**.
3. Bring a Rope, reliable light, armor, healing supplies, and a weapon.
4. Enter the Giant Whirlpool and locate the Sanctum lobby.
5. Use the Rope at the Chasm to establish the route back toward the Archives.

The Archive Switch powers the Sanctum lobby's Waymark. Without that connection, you may reach the lobby but find that its transport mechanism is inactive.

The Sanctum has its own powered mechanisms. You do not need to solve every Archive-related puzzle before exploring its rooms, but powering the Archives makes the main travel connection usable.

### Navigating the Sanctum Waymarks

The Sanctum is made of individual chambers linked by **Sanctum Waymarks**. These transport you between rooms rather than opening ordinary doors, so a visually nearby passage may not lead toward the Keystone.

Look at the markings beneath a Waymark. Their directional symbols indicate the connection, which helps distinguish it from other Waymarks in the same room.

Some Waymarks are broken and must be repaired before they can transport you. Others remain inactive until the relevant power is available.

Multiplayer groups also need to coordinate their travel. When multiple survivors are inside the Sanctum, everyone must touch the same Waymark before the group can teleport. A teammate waiting at another Waymark can prevent the transfer.

Exploring every branch is unnecessary for the first Keystone. The Waymark Compass provides a much more useful direction to follow.

## Where to Find the Waymark Compass

A **Waymark Compass** can appear in several Sanctum rooms, including the Playbill Room, King Statue Room, and Generator Room.

![A Waymark Compass inside the Sanctum, showing the tool used to locate the Key Room and collect Security Pulses.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-waymark-compass.webp)

Equip the Compass as soon as you find it. Its indicator points toward the Sanctum Waymark leading to the **Key Room**, helping you distinguish the route to the Keystone from optional branches.

While equipped, it also attracts nearby **Security Pulses** released by Ancient Guard Posts. You need **4 Security Pulses** to activate the Key Room, so collect them before following the Compass through the remaining chambers.

### Collect four Security Pulses before following the Compass to the Key Room

Ancient Guard Posts throughout the Sanctum provide Security Pulses, but you can also collect them in the Ancient Archives.

Returning to the Archives is often easier than searching multiple Sanctum rooms. Gather four Pulses there, then follow the Compass toward the Key Room.

![Four glowing Security Pulses following a player equipped with the Waymark Compass.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-security-pulses.webp)

A single Compass can attract **up to four Security Pulses** at once.

The important part is keeping the Compass equipped. Unequipping it causes the Pulses to stop following, potentially leaving them behind before you reach the final chamber.

Count the four glowing orbs moving with you, rather than assuming every Pulse visible in the room has joined your group.

An alternative is to carry the Pulses in **Spark Arks** and transfer them into the final cradles. When you already have a Compass, however, gathering four Pulses directly is straightforward and does not require preparing four separate charged containers.

### Why are the Security Pulses not following?

A Pulse must be close enough to respond to the equipped Compass. Simply walking past a distant Guard Post does not guarantee that its energy is following you.

Move toward the Pulse, allow it to join the group, then continue.

A Pulse may also stop following after entering a compatible mechanism, including a Pulse Cradle. Once deposited, it is no longer one of your carried Pulses.

If you reach the Key Room with fewer than four, collect the missing Pulses before expecting the final mechanism to activate.

## Sanctum Jumping Puzzle Solution: Which Directions Do You Press?

The **Sequitor's Puzzle** consists of narrow rock pillars suspended over a dark pit. Two creatures called Sequitors copy the survivor's directional movement, while the pillars collapse after being used.

For the layout with the starting positions matching the arrangement described below, one successful movement sequence is:

**Up → Left → Up → Left → Up → Up → Up → Right → Right → Up → Up**

That is **11 directional inputs**.

The sequence is easiest to execute in four groups:

1. **Up → Left → Up → Left**
2. **Up → Up → Up**
3. **Right → Right**
4. **Up → Up**

Follow the sequence from its matching starting state, before making any extra jumps.

This is one valid solution, not the only possible route through the puzzle. The Sanctum has different pillar arrangements, and some are mirrored. The position of the two Sequitors and the available rock columns must match the arrangement for which the sequence works.

![The Sanctum jumping puzzle, showing the rock columns and two Sequitors that mirror the player's movements.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-sequitor-puzzle.webp)

### How to recognize the matching layout

The sequence applies to the arrangement where the survivor approaches from the lower side and the two Sequitors initially occupy separate columns toward the right-hand portion of the crossing.

Check the two creatures' starting positions and the available rock columns before entering the first input.

A different pattern of pillars changes which jumps remain available. A mirrored arrangement can also reverse the horizontal movements needed to keep the Sequitors away from the exit route.

The Lever resets the puzzle without changing its pillar arrangement. If your starting layout differs from the one used for the 11-move sequence, you will need a route suited to that arrangement.

### Why the 11-move sequence works

Each Sequitor copies your directional inputs from its own starting position. Because the creatures occupy different columns, the same movement can take you onto an intact pillar while leaving one of them blocked by a missing or collapsing pillar.

The 11-move sequence takes advantage of those different positions, using lateral jumps to restrict the Sequitors' available paths without closing off your route to the far platform.

**First four moves: Up → Left → Up → Left**

The alternating movements reposition the survivor and make the Sequitors respond from their separate columns.

These early horizontal adjustments are important. Moving straight toward the opposite platform may place a Sequitor on a column needed for the final crossing.

**Next three moves: Up → Up → Up**

With the creatures following the earlier directional changes, the route advances through the remaining usable pillars.

Each jump consumes part of the available path. The point is to keep progressing without returning to a stone that has already been occupied.

**Next two moves: Right → Right**

This is the main lateral adjustment in the second half of the route.

By this stage, the survivor and Sequitors are no longer in the same relative positions they held at the entrance. Their movements have also removed different pillars from their own routes.

The rightward movement changes the survivor's approach to the exit while the Sequitors remain constrained by the columns they can still reach.

**Final two moves: Up → Up**

Use the remaining forward path to reach the far platform.

Once the exit is accessible, continue forward instead of making additional adjustments. Every unnecessary jump creates another opportunity to lose a usable column.

### Why copying the same movement can trap a Sequitor

Every Sanctum Rock can be stepped on only once before it collapses. It also falls after a short period of occupation, so waiting on a pillar is not a safe way to plan the next move.

The survivor and the Sequitors are affected by these disappearing columns independently.

Consider a leftward jump. Your destination might be intact, while a Sequitor attempting the corresponding movement has no usable pillar to its left.

The creature cannot simply take your path because it is starting from a different column.

This is the reason controlled sideways movement can be more useful than rushing straight toward the exit. You are changing the set of moves available to each creature, not merely moving your own character.

A successful route leaves the survivor with an exit path that the Sequitors cannot occupy in time to block it.

### What happens when you make a wrong jump?

A failed attempt is not always an immediate fall.

Sometimes you remain standing on a pillar but have no valid next move. One of the Sequitors may be occupying the next required column, or a pillar you need to cross has already disappeared.

There are three common dead ends:

- **The next pillar has collapsed.** You cannot reuse it to return or change direction.
- **A Sequitor occupies the remaining route.** Continuing the planned sequence would lead into the creature instead of an open pillar.
- **Your movement no longer matches the solution.** An extra jump changes both your position and the creatures' positions, invalidating the remaining directions.

The solution assumes you start from the corresponding arrangement and enter the moves consecutively. Do not insert an additional left or right movement to compensate for a mistake.

### How to reset the Sanctum jumping puzzle

Return to the puzzle's **Pull Lever** and activate it to restart the challenge.

The Lever is the starting and reset mechanism. It is not the objective at the far platform.

Resetting restores the puzzle to a playable state, allowing another crossing attempt without restarting the entire Sanctum.

The Lever restores the original pillar arrangement and resets the Sequitors to their starting positions. You can then retry the same movement sequence from the beginning.

When you reach the far platform, continue through the available Sanctum Waymark toward the rooms beyond the crossing.

## How to Solve the Nine-Flame Sanctum Puzzle (Optional)

The nine-flame puzzle opens an additional route to the Fountain of Knowledge rooms. **You do not need to complete it to obtain the Keystone** if you can already continue through the Sequitor's Puzzle and Purifier Room.

This room contains **9 Flummoxing Flames**. Your objective is to light all nine at the same time.

Activating a normal Flame changes its own state and the states of the immediately adjacent Flames above, below, left, and right. Diagonal Flames are not affected.

Two unusual Flames change the solution:

- **Stuck Flame:** Always lit and cannot be toggled normally.
- **Broken Flame:** Cannot be activated directly, but can be turned on by activating neighboring Flames.

These special Flames can occupy different positions, so there is no single permanent sequence of nine switches that works for every arrangement.

The useful approach is to work through the current pattern rather than repeatedly selecting dark Flames at random.

Start by finding the Broken Flame. Identify which of its neighbors can turn it on, and consider how those same interactions change the other adjacent Flames.

You can then work through the remaining rows, using each activation to correct more than one position where possible.

The Stuck Flame must remain lit, but attempting to toggle it directly is pointless. Treat its position as a fixed part of the current arrangement.

### Why does a completed Flame turn off again?

You may have activated an adjacent Flame after setting the earlier one correctly.

Remember that each ordinary activation changes multiple positions. A switch that lights one Flame can extinguish another.

There is also a separate danger around the Broken Flame: after it becomes lit, a **Shadow Hand** can attempt to extinguish it. Step on the hand to interrupt it before it reverses your progress.

Once all nine Flames are active together, Sanctum Rocks appear and open access to the connected rooms, including Fountain of Knowledge areas.

These rooms offer additional exploration and items, but completing every optional Fountain of Knowledge interaction is not necessary to obtain your first Keystone.

## How to Open the Purifier Room Barrier

The route beyond the jumping puzzle leads toward the **Purifier Room**, which has two sets of Inner Sanctum Barriers separating the entrance, middle section, and exit.

Move into the middle section, then pull the Lever to activate the Sanctum Purifiers. The Purifiers clear nearby Shadow Creatures, and the rear barrier lowers to reveal the Waymark leading toward the Key Room.

In multiplayer, make sure **every survivor has entered the middle section** before operating the controls. The barriers control passage in one direction at a time, so a teammate left on the wrong side may need the Lever operated again.

The Purifier Room's Levers are separate from the reset Lever in the jumping puzzle. They open the route forward rather than restarting an earlier challenge.

Before using the final Waymark, count the **4 Security Pulses** following your Compass. All four are needed to activate the Key Room.

## Key Room: How to Activate the Four Ancient Towers

The Key Room contains **4 Pulse Cradles** arranged around the center, with **4 dormant Ancient Towers** nearby.

Bring your four Security Pulses into the room while holding the Waymark Compass.

Each Pulse Cradle requires one Security Pulse. Approach the cradles to deposit the energy, or transfer it from a charged Spark Ark.

![The four Pulse Cradles and dormant Ancient Towers in the Sanctum Key Room.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-pulse-cradles.webp)

Once the fourth cradle receives its Pulse, the Ancient Towers awaken as **Ancient Guard Towers**.

The Pulse Cradles are replaced by Levers, and four Lustrous Sockets become available on the floor.

This is not the moment the Keystone appears. You have only activated the final mechanism.

### Should you kill the Ancient Guard Towers?

**You do not need to kill all four towers to obtain the Keystone.**

Each tower has **6,000 HP**, making a full clear expensive for a player who only wants the quest item.

The towers initially remain neutral after awakening. They become hostile when you attack one or operate a Key Room Lever.

Use that neutral period to inspect the sockets, prepare weapons, move into a favorable position, and decide whether you intend to fight the towers or distract them.

The towers are dangerous because they can deal heavy damage and knock players away from their positions. This is particularly disruptive when you need to push another creature into a floor socket.

One useful alternative is to exploit their reaction to Shadow Creatures.

### Distracting the towers with Shadow Creatures

A **Nightmare Amulet** lets you maintain a low-sanity state even though the Key Room strongly restores sanity.

When Shadow Creatures appear, they can attack nearby Ancient Guard Towers. The towers respond to them, creating an opportunity to work with the Levers and Lustrous Weevils.

The Shadow Creatures are primarily a distraction. Damage exchanged between the towers and those creatures is heavily reduced, so waiting for them to kill every tower is not a practical shortcut.

Avoid attacking a tower yourself immediately before attempting the distraction. Recent player aggression can affect which target the tower follows.

You still need to move carefully around the arena, but this approach can save substantial weapon durability compared with defeating four 6,000-HP enemies.

### When fighting the towers directly makes sense

Direct combat is reasonable when you have strong equipment, reliable healing, or want the towers' crafting drops.

Their movements and attacks are comparatively slow, creating opportunities to strike after they commit to an attack or jump.

The towers' behavior changes as they lose health, so continue watching their attack animations rather than relying on a single repeated attack count throughout the fight.

Another advantage comes from the Key Room's Weevils.

A falling Lustrous Weevil can stun the tower it lands on for **15 seconds**. A stunned tower takes **50% additional damage**, providing a useful opening if you choose to fight it.

The four Weevils can only be released once each from their corresponding mechanisms during the encounter. Do not assume you can repeatedly use one Lever to create unlimited stun opportunities.

## How to Put the Four Lustrous Weevils Into the Sockets

When the four Security Pulses have activated the Key Room, the cradles change into **Levers**.

Each Lever releases one **Lustrous Weevil** from the corresponding hanging mechanism.

There are also **4 Lustrous Sockets** in the floor. Placing one Weevil into each socket completes the Keystone mechanism.

![A Lustrous Weevil curled into its movable shell near the floor sockets in the Keystone chamber.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-lustrous-weevil-sockets.webp)

Each Lustrous Weevil has **1,000 HP** and deals **40 damage** when it attacks.

Unlike an ordinary creature, reducing its health to the final point does not kill it. Instead, the Weevil curls into its shell, which can be pushed into a socket.

Complete the process one creature at a time:

1. Pull a Key Room Lever to drop a Lustrous Weevil.
2. Draw the Weevil near an empty floor socket.
3. Attack it until it curls into its shell.
4. Push the shell directly into the socket.
5. Repeat with the remaining three Weevils.

There is an important time limit.

A curled Lustrous Weevil remains in its shell for only about **6 seconds**. If it is not socketed in time, it emerges again with **500 HP**.

Positioning the creature before dealing the final damage is therefore more useful than simply attacking it wherever it happens to stand.

### Why the Weevil keeps standing up again

The shell state is temporary.

If you curl the Weevil on the far side of the arena, the distance to a socket may be too great to push it into place before the creature reactivates.

Move or lure it closer to the intended socket first. Then reduce its health and immediately push the shell into position.

Avoid fighting several unsocketed Weevils at once if they become difficult to distinguish. Completing one socket before releasing another keeps the available targets and floor positions easier to manage.

### What blocks a Lustrous Socket?

A socket only accepts a Weevil in its curled state.

A living Weevil standing beside a socket does not complete the mechanism. Nor does reducing a tower's health or dragging another enemy onto the socket.

Check the following:

- The Weevil has entered its curled shell state.
- The target socket has not already been filled.
- The shell is being pushed into the socket rather than merely positioned beside it.
- The Weevil has not recovered before reaching the socket.

An Ancient Guard Tower can also stand directly over a socket. Move it away, distract it with Shadow Creatures, or defeat it before trying to push the shell through that space.

Do not leave the fourth socket incomplete after clearing the towers. The Keystone is connected to the **four occupied Lustrous Sockets**, not the towers' defeat count.

## How to Get the Keystone From the Reliquary

When all four Lustrous Weevils have been pushed into their sockets, the central **Reliquary** rises.

Approach it and collect the **Keystone**.

![The Reliquary in the Sanctum Key Room after the four Lustrous Sockets have been completed.](/images/dont-starve-together/sanctum-keystone-reliquary.webp)

No additional boss needs to be summoned to retrieve the item.

After the Keystone is taken, the Reliquary retracts. A **Sanctum Smithy** appears in its place, and a **Chasm** opens as another connection out of the Key Room.

The Keystone is now available for the next part of the Shadow storyline.

### What should you do with the Sanctum Smithy?

The Sanctum Smithy allows you to process and craft items associated with this area.

For example, components dropped by Ancient Guard Towers can be used toward constructing a **Guard Tower Kit**.

These additional crafts are not required to take the Keystone. You can leave after obtaining it and return to the Smithy when you want to make use of those resources.

### Should You Repair Every Broken Sanctum Waymark?

A **Portation Orb** found near a broken Sanctum Waymark can be used to repair that connection. Once the Sanctum Smithy is available, the same Orb can instead be processed into **3 Portation Orb Fragments**.

Those fragments have a different purpose: they can be used with a Telelocator Focus for teleportation.

Do not repair every broken Waymark automatically. Check where the connection leads and whether you actually need it. An optional shortcut may be less useful than keeping the Orb for later crafting.

Repairing a Waymark also consumes a resource that could have become three fragments. Decide which benefit matters before using the Orb.

The Keystone itself only requires **4 Security Pulses** and **4 Lustrous Weevils**. Repairing every Sanctum Waymark is not part of that requirement.

## Where to Go After Getting the Keystone

Take the Keystone back to the **Ancient Gateway**. You will also need the **Ancient Key** from the Ancient Guardian progression.

With the required keys available, return to the Gateway to continue [Charlie's ritual and the three Blighted offerings](/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-part-2-walkthrough).
