Defeating Ancient Fuelweaver opens the next stage of the Shadow storyline, but it does not finish Charlie's ritual. You still need to repair the Ancient Gateway, enter the Sanctum for the Keystone, and eventually face Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination.

Where you should go next depends on your world's current progress. You may still be collecting the Ancient Key and Shadow Atrium, waiting to activate Shadow Rifts, or returning from the Sanctum with the Keystone. Each stage unlocks a different interaction at the Gateway, so check what you have already completed before preparing another expedition.

## Don't Starve Together Shadow Progression: What Should You Do Next?

There are seven major milestones between beginning the Shadow storyline and completing the final confrontation.

You can obtain some early items in a different order, but the later Gateway interactions depend on completing the earlier requirements.

### 1. Defeat the Shadow Pieces and obtain a Shadow Atrium

Restore the three broken Marble Sculptures of the Knight, Bishop, and Rook using their corresponding Suspicious Marbles. Mine the restored sculptures during a **New Moon** to summon the Shadow Pieces, then defeat them to obtain the **Shadow Atrium**.

The moon phase determines what happens when you break the sculptures. A Full Moon produces Clockwork enemies instead, so check the calendar before committing to the encounter. Keep the Shadow Atrium for the Ancient Fuelweaver summoning process.

### 2. Defeat Ancient Guardian and obtain the Ancient Key

Travel through the Ruins to the Labyrinth and defeat **Ancient Guardian**.

The reward you need for Shadow progression is the **Ancient Key**. It activates the Ancient Gateway in the Atrium.

The Ancient Guardian and Shadow Pieces objectives can be prepared independently. You do not need to delay exploring the Labyrinth until you have finished the surface sculpture encounter.

When both the Ancient Key and Shadow Heart are available, you are ready to prepare the first Ancient Fuelweaver fight.

### 3. Defeat Ancient Fuelweaver in the Atrium

Locate the **Atrium** through its connection to the Ruins.

Insert the Ancient Key into the Ancient Gateway, reconstruct the nearby Odd Skeleton with **8 Fossil Fragments**, and use the Shadow Atrium obtained from the Shadow Pieces to summon Ancient Fuelweaver.

Defeating him causes the Gateway to destabilize and initiates the Ruins renewal.

The Ancient Key is not permanently consumed by this process. Recover it when the Gateway releases it, since the later ritual also requires the Key.

After the first victory, a Beckoning Hand can appear near the Gateway. That is your next Shadow progression interaction.

### 4. Repair the Ancient Gateway with 5 Dreadstone

The Beckoning Hand requests **5 Dreadstone**.

Giving it the required amount allows Charlie to repair the Ancient Gateway and activates the Shadow Rift cycle in the Caves.

This decision permanently changes the normal world progression. Shadow Rifts begin appearing underground, bringing additional enemies, resources, and hazards.

If your Gateway is already repaired, you do not need to repeat the initial Ancient Fuelweaver victory just to reach the next stage.

### 5. Enter the Sanctum and recover the Keystone

The next major objective is the **Keystone**, hidden inside the Sanctum.

Its entrance is the Giant Whirlpool in the deep ocean. Once inside, you must navigate Sanctum Waymarks, collect four Security Pulses, complete the required puzzles, and solve the final Key Room mechanism.

The Keystone is obtained from the Reliquary after completing the Key Room.

To recover the Keystone, [navigate the Sanctum and complete its Key Room](/dont-starve-together/sanctum-walkthrough).

The Keystone should be brought back to the Ancient Gateway in the Caves, not carried up to the Surface.

### 6. Complete Charlie's three-offering ritual

With the repaired Gateway and Keystone available, Charlie's next ritual becomes accessible.

You need a **Possessed Shadow Atrium** to bring back Ancient Fuelweaver as a friendly companion outside the Atrium. He can corrupt three creatures, allowing you to obtain:

- **Blighted Claw**
- **Blighted Larynx**
- **Blighted Guts**

The items are obtained from the Blighted Rock Master, Hound Batilisk, and Great Depths Worm.

To collect all three, [find and defeat the Blighted creatures](/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-part-2-walkthrough).

Do not confuse the Possessed Shadow Atrium with the ordinary Shadow Atrium used to summon the original hostile Ancient Fuelweaver.

### 7. Defeat Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination

Once all three offerings are placed around the active Ancient Gateway, the final confrontation begins.

**Shrouded Queen** appears first, followed by **Umbral Abomination**.

The encounter is continuous, and both fights require different movement and attack decisions. A melee-only setup that works against ordinary Caves enemies is not sufficient for all of Shrouded Queen's defensive states.

Before completing the final offering, prepare equipment for both encounters.

The [Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination fight](/dont-starve-together/shrouded-queen) explains the stage transitions, counterattacks, Dreadstone Outcrops, optic laser, and final finishing action.

## Should You Activate Shadow Rifts Immediately?

Giving **5 Dreadstone** to the Beckoning Hand permanently changes the Caves. Once Charlie repairs the Ancient Gateway, Shadow Rifts begin appearing underground, introducing additional enemies, resources, and environmental hazards.

Under default world settings, the first Shadow Rift opens **5 days after the offering**. Later Rifts follow their own cycle, with only one active Shadow Rift in the Caves at a time. This does not affect the separate Lunar Rift system on the Surface.

### When to wait before giving Charlie the Dreadstone

You can defeat Ancient Fuelweaver and explore the Ruins without immediately activating Shadow Rifts. This leaves time to establish underground travel routes, prepare lighting and equipment, or finish building near areas you expect to revisit.

Waiting is particularly useful when your Caves exploration is still taking priority over the new Shadow enemies. Once you make the offering, you cannot treat the underground world as though those additional encounters have never been activated.

### When to continue with the Shadow Rifts

Activating the Rifts gives you access to the enemies and materials needed for later Shadow progression. Delaying the offering also delays your progress toward the final confrontation in a normally configured world.

You do not need to be ready to defeat Shrouded Queen on the day you activate the Rifts. The Sanctum and Charlie's later ritual still stand between those two milestones. Prepare for the ongoing changes underground first; the final boss equipment can be assembled while you work through the remaining objectives.

## How Do You Get the 5 Dreadstone for Charlie?

The **Nightmare Werepig** encounter in the Caves is an important early opportunity to obtain Dreadstone.

Find the imprisoned Nightmare Werepig in the **Muddy Biome**. It begins chained between three Cracked Pillars.

Mine the outer Marble layer from all three Cracked Pillars. Then strike the exposed pillars with a **Pick/Axe** or **Brightshade Smasher** until all three are shaking at the same time. This releases Nightmare Werepig from its chains.

During the encounter, clear the Parasitic Shadelings preventing you from damaging the boss and continue fighting until Nightmare Werepig begins using its ground slam. Lure that attack into the exposed Cracked Pillars rather than pulling the boss away from them.

Each destroyed pillar drops **3–4 Dreadstone**. Breaking two provides at least six, enough to reserve the **5 Dreadstone** required by Charlie.
### Why did you beat Nightmare Werepig without getting Dreadstone?

The most likely reason is that the Cracked Pillars were left intact.

The boss drops other materials and blueprints after defeat. Those rewards do not replace the Dreadstone obtained by destroying the pillars.

If you intend to use Nightmare Werepig for Charlie's offering, make sure the ground slams actually hit and destroy enough pillars during the encounter.

Keep **5 Dreadstone** reserved once you have them. Spending the entire supply on armor or construction can leave you unable to repair the Gateway when Ancient Fuelweaver has already been defeated.

Other Dreadstone opportunities become available after Shadow Rifts are activated, but those do not help with the first offering that enables the Rifts in a normally configured world.

## Why Isn't the Beckoning Hand Appearing at the Ancient Gateway?

A missing Beckoning Hand does not always mean you need to fight Ancient Fuelweaver again.

Its availability depends on your progression, the Ancient Key, and the world's Rift settings.

### The Ancient Key is still inserted

The Beckoning Hand retreats while the Ancient Key is socketed into the Ancient Gateway.

Remove the Key and check the area again.

The Key does not need to remain permanently installed while you are trying to interact with Charlie's Hand.

### You have not defeated Ancient Fuelweaver

The initial Dreadstone request follows the first Ancient Fuelweaver victory.

Merely locating the Atrium or inserting the Ancient Key does not complete that requirement.

Finish the original Ancient Fuelweaver encounter before looking for Charlie's first offering interaction.

### Your world has Shadow Rifts disabled

Open the Caves world settings and check **Wild Rifts**.

- **Auto:** Shadow Rifts follow the normal progression requirements.
- **Always:** Shadow Rifts can be active from the beginning of the world.
- **Never:** Shadow Rifts are disabled, and the normal Beckoning Hand activation will not appear.

The relevant option is in the **Caves** settings, not the Surface settings used for Lunar Rifts.

Changing a Surface Lunar Rift option does not solve a missing Shadow Rift interaction underground.

### You are expecting the Keystone interaction too early

The Hand's initial Dreadstone request and the later Keystone interaction are different milestones.

The Gateway must first be repaired before the Keystone can advance Charlie's later ritual.

If the Gateway is still broken, finish the Dreadstone activation rather than repeatedly offering unrelated items.

## Do You Need to Finish the Lunar Storyline Before the Shadow Finale?

Shadow and Lunar progression have different activation requirements.

The Shadow Rift system operates in the **Caves** and begins through Charlie and the Ancient Gateway. Lunar Rifts operate on the **Surface** and follow their own progression involving Wagstaff and the Enlightened Shard.

You do not have to finish the entire Lunar storyline merely to activate Shadow Rifts or begin exploring the Sanctum.

However, being able to unlock an encounter and being well equipped to finish it are different things.

Late Lunar equipment can make the Shadow finale substantially easier, particularly for a solo survivor who lacks high damage or reliable ranged attacks.

Brightshade weapons and appropriate mobility equipment give you more options against enemies that punish prolonged close-range combat.

### Should a solo player complete Lunar progression first?

It is a sensible preparation route when you are struggling with the later Shadow bosses.

A stronger ranged weapon helps during Shrouded Queen's anti-melee states, and additional mobility can reduce the pressure of the second boss's tracking attacks.

But completing the Lunar storyline also takes considerable time and resources. If your Shadow equipment already handles Ancient Fuelweaver comfortably and you have appropriate ranged weapons, defensive tools, and healing supplies, you can continue without treating every Lunar milestone as mandatory.

One equipment detail matters near the end: a fully upgraded Enlightened Crown can force Enlightenment, which changes how Dreadstone Outcrops behave against Umbral Abomination.

An item that provides excellent damage and lighting during one encounter is not automatically the safest equipment for the next.

## What Happens After You Finish Cursed Confrontation Part 2?

Defeating **Umbral Abomination** completes the Shadow finale, but your world continues. Collect the **King's Staff**, **Queen's Torch**, and **Queen's Visage** at the Ancient Gateway.

Your first victory also unlocks the Sanctum's later reset cycle. Before renewing the Ruins, remove any belongings you want to keep from the Sanctum. To fight again, follow the [repeat ritual requirements](/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-part-2-walkthrough#repeat-finale).
