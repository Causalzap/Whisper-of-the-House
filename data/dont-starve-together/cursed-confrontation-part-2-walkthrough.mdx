<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Give the **Keystone** to the Beckoning Hand at the repaired Ancient Gateway. Obtain a **Possessed Shadow Atrium**, then use it on an Odd Skeleton in the Caves to summon a friendly Ancient Fuelweaver.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Three circular markings appear around the repaired Ancient Gateway after Charlie receives the Keystone. They require offerings from the Blighted Rock Master, Hound Batilisk, and Great Depths Worm, but defeating these creatures in their ordinary forms will not provide the required items.

To obtain the offerings, summon a friendly Ancient Fuelweaver and bring him close enough to corrupt each target before killing it. The encounters can be completed in any order. Once all three offerings are placed at the Gateway, Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination emerge for the final confrontation.

## How to Start Cursed Confrontation Part 2 at the Ancient Gateway

Return to the **Atrium** in the Caves, where you previously fought Ancient Fuelweaver. Bring the Keystone obtained from the Sanctum's Reliquary.

The Gateway must already have been repaired by Charlie. After your first Ancient Fuelweaver victory, a Beckoning Hand appears and requests **5 Dreadstone**. Offering them repairs the Gateway and activates the Shadow Rift cycle.

If the Gateway is still in its original broken state, complete that interaction before attempting to begin the new ritual.

Once it is repaired, approach the **Beckoning Hand** with the Keystone.

![Charlie standing beside the repaired Ancient Gateway during the Shadow ritual.](/images/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-keystone-gateway.webp)

Give the Keystone to the Hand. Charlie's next objective is to collect three sacrifices from creatures touched by shadow.

The area around the Gateway now has **three circular floor markings**. Each will eventually hold one ritual offering. You cannot finish them using ordinary monster drops.

You also need the **Ancient Key**, originally obtained from the Ancient Guardian. It must be socketed into the Gateway when you are ready to complete the ritual.

### What if the Beckoning Hand is missing?

Check the Ancient Key before looking for another entrance.

The Beckoning Hand withdraws while the Ancient Key is inserted into the Gateway. Removing the Key allows the Hand to appear again, provided the earlier requirements have been met.

For a straightforward first attempt, give the Keystone to the Hand while the Key is not socketed. Keep the Ancient Key available for the final offering sequence.

The Keystone belongs in this underground progression. Do not carry it to the Surface: it cannot travel between the Caves and Surface normally and will be left behind at the Stairs.

<section id="possessed-shadow-atrium" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Get the Possessed Shadow Atrium

The three ritual creatures must be corrupted by a friendly **Ancient Fuelweaver**. To summon him, first obtain a **Possessed Shadow Atrium**.

This is different from the ordinary Shadow Atrium used to fight Ancient Fuelweaver at the original Gateway. An ordinary Shadow Atrium does not create the companion you need.

### Turn a Shadow Atrium into a Possessed Shadow Atrium

The item comes from an **Ornery Chest**, a creature that disguises itself as an Ornate Chest in the Ruins after Shadow Rifts have been activated.

You need an ordinary Shadow Atrium, obtained from the Shadow Pieces, and an Ornery Chest that can consume it.

1. Search the Ruins for an Ornate Chest that is actually an **Ornery Chest**.
2. Place the ordinary **Shadow Atrium** into the Chest while it is still disguised, or let the revealed creature eat the item from the ground.
3. Kill the Ornery Chest after it has consumed the Shadow Atrium.
4. Collect the resulting **Possessed Shadow Atrium** immediately.

An Ornery Chest can convert only **one Shadow Atrium**. After the creature dies, collect the resulting Possessed Shadow Atrium immediately; if left alone, it eventually begins running away and cannot be picked up normally.

A **Trap** can catch the moving Atrium. You can also let the Ornery Chest swallow a Trap before killing it, allowing the dropped Trap to catch the item as it escapes. Make sure you recover the Possessed version rather than an ordinary Shadow Atrium, since only the converted item summons the friendly Ancient Fuelweaver.

</section>

<section id="friendly-fuelweaver" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Summon and Move the Friendly Ancient Fuelweaver

You can summon this version of Ancient Fuelweaver at a repaired **Odd Skeleton** almost anywhere in the Caves. You do not need to return to the Atrium for every summoning.

Reconstruct an Odd Skeleton using **8 Fossil Fragments**, then give it the Possessed Shadow Atrium.

![The friendly Ancient Fuelweaver accompanying the player in the Caves.](/images/dont-starve-together/possessed-atrium-friendly-fuelweaver.webp)

The resulting Fuelweaver is passive. He does not begin the original Ancient Fuelweaver boss fight, and his purpose here is to corrupt specific creatures into their Blighted versions.

### Make Fuelweaver follow you

Hold the **Shadow Thurible** obtained from Ancient Fuelweaver's earlier encounter.

Fuelweaver follows the player carrying it. He can also catch up by teleporting when separated, which is useful while traveling through the Caves.

Keep the Thurible ready when approaching a ritual target. Once Fuelweaver gets close enough to a suitable creature, he briefly immobilizes it and transforms it into its Blighted form.

He can corrupt only the required types:

- **Rock Master**
- **Hound Batilisk**
- **Great Depths Worm**

Being near ordinary Rock Lobsters, small Batilisks, or standard Depths Worms does not replace finding the proper target.

### Can you summon another Fuelweaver somewhere else?

Only one Ancient Fuelweaver can exist at a time, including the original hostile boss and this passive companion.

For long journeys, you can simply carry the Shadow Thurible and let the companion follow. But leading him through an inconvenient route is not always necessary.

Killing the passive Fuelweaver returns the **Possessed Shadow Atrium and Fossil Fragments** used to assemble him, without the usual Ancient Fuelweaver boss rewards. You can then reconstruct him closer to the next target.

This is a practical way to move between distant creature locations. Do not accidentally use the recovered heart in the Ancient Gateway's original skeleton: the possessed version is for summoning the passive NPC elsewhere in the Caves.

<section id="three-offerings" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which Three Blighted Offerings Do You Need?

Each corrupted creature drops exactly the ritual item associated with it.

- **Blighted Rock Master:** Blighted Claw
- **Blighted Hound Batilisk:** Blighted Larynx
- **Blighted Great Depths Worm:** Blighted Guts

You need **one of each**, and the order of collection does not matter.

The Rock Master and Hound Batilisk have fixed habitats worth checking immediately. The Great Depths Worm instead depends on a Cave Worm attack, so it makes sense to arrange that encounter around the time you are already spending underground.

There is one equipment decision worth making before you start.

A normal Rock Master drops the **Craggy Carapace**, a chest armor piece with **95% damage absorption**, **1,050 durability**, and immunity to knockback. It also slows the wearer by **30%**.

That armor is especially useful for a mounted strategy against the final bosses, where avoiding forced dismounts matters. But killing a normal Rock Master first means waiting for another one to spawn before corrupting it for the Blighted Claw.

If you only need the ritual and already have suitable armor, corrupt the first Rock Master you find instead of delaying the quest for the Carapace.

</section>

<section id="rock-master" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where to Find the Rock Master and Get the Blighted Claw

Find the **Rock Master** among Rock Lobster herds in the **Rocky Plains biome** of the Caves. Its much larger body and distinctive shell distinguish it from ordinary Rock Lobsters, and it becomes hostile when approached.

Only one Rock Master can exist in the Caves at a time. After it dies, another can normally appear within **10–20 days**, provided an eligible large, sleeping Rock Lobster is available. Unsuccessful spawn attempts are retried later, so killing the normal Rock Master for its armor may leave you waiting before you can obtain the Blighted Claw.

### Corrupt the Rock Master

Bring your passive Ancient Fuelweaver close to the living Rock Master while holding the Shadow Thurible.

Wait until Fuelweaver's corruption interaction finishes and the creature changes into **Blighted Rock Master** before attacking for the ritual drop.

![The Blighted Rock Master lunging forward with a shadow trail during its dash attack.](/images/dont-starve-together/blighted-rock-master-dash.webp)

The Blighted Rock Master has **5,000 HP** and deals **100 physical damage plus 20 planar damage** with its main attacks.

Its forward claw strike covers more distance than a short-range punch, but the attack area is comparatively narrow. Dodge to the side of the direction it is facing rather than retreating straight backward.

The corrupted version also performs chained dashes. It can disappear briefly between attacks and reappear along the direction of its target. As its health falls, expect more successive dashes before you get a safe opportunity to attack.

A practical pattern is to keep circling laterally, let the dash sequence finish, and strike during the recovery rather than chasing after each individual dash.

Do not rely on mining the Blighted Rock Master out of its shell. That method applies to the normal Rock Master's defensive hiding behavior, which the corrupted version no longer uses.

Once defeated, collect the **Blighted Claw**.

If Fuelweaver was too far away and you killed the normal Rock Master instead, the Blighted Claw will not drop. You must find another Rock Master and corrupt it before the kill.

</section>

<section id="hound-batilisk" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Find the Hound Batilisk and Get the Blighted Larynx

Search the **Tall Stalagmite Terrain** in the Caves for a **Big Bat Cave**. This structure is the Hound Batilisk's home.

The creature emerges during Dusk or Night, and it can also appear when players approach its cave.

A regular Hound Batilisk has **1,500 HP**. You need Fuelweaver to corrupt it before the kill; otherwise, you obtain its ordinary loot rather than the ritual organ.

A Big Bat Cave normally takes **10 days** to replace a Hound Batilisk that has been killed.

### Corrupt it before starting the real fight

Bring the friendly Ancient Fuelweaver close to the Hound Batilisk and let the transformation happen.

The **Blighted Hound Batilisk** has **3,000 HP** and its attacks include additional planar damage.

![Two Blighted Hound Batilisks after the corrupted creature duplicates during combat.](/images/dont-starve-together/blighted-hound-batilisk-duplicates.webp)

### Remove the Batilisk immediately when it grabs your head

The creature can leap toward you and attach itself to your head slot.

When this happens, it displaces the head equipment you were wearing and begins inflicting repeated damage. The Blighted version adds planar damage while attached.

**Open your equipment and unequip the Batilisk immediately.**

Trying to finish the fight while it remains attached wastes health and leaves your head armor unavailable. Recover any displaced equipment before it gets lost among nearby enemies and loot.

The lunge can hit a mounted survivor particularly awkwardly because of the difference in elevation. Fighting this creature on foot is more manageable than relying on a Beefalo to outrun the grab.

### Kill both copies before the duplication restarts

Below roughly half health, the Blighted Hound Batilisk can split into two versions of itself.

These are active enemies that can continue attacking and duplicating. Killing only one while leaving the other with substantial health allows the surviving copy to split again once its ability becomes available.

That is why this fight is significantly more troublesome for a low-damage single-target character than its starting health suggests.

Use an area-damage weapon or alternate attacks between the two copies to keep their health reasonably close. Once both are low, finish them quickly rather than eliminating one and leaving the other alive.

A **Brightshade Staff** can help because its attacks bounce between available targets. However, nearby ordinary Batilisks may intercept those bounces, reducing the damage reaching the two copies. Clear distracting enemies when possible.

After the corrupted encounter is defeated, collect the **Blighted Larynx**.

If you have the Blighted Larynx, this part of the ritual is complete. You do not need to continue hunting ordinary Batilisks.

## How to Get the Blighted Great Depths Worm and Blighted Guts

The **Great Depths Worm** is different from the other two targets because you cannot simply travel to its permanent home.

It appears during a **Cave Worm attack**, replacing the ordinary worm wave.

Under normal conditions, a Great Depths Worm has an increasing chance to appear during later Cave Worm waves, starting at **5%** and rising by **5 percentage points** after unsuccessful waves, up to **50%**.

However, once you have started the Atrium ritual quest, the currently planned Cave Worm attack is set to bring a Great Depths Worm. You do not need to wait through an indefinite series of ordinary waves just to reach this task's target.

The catch is that you still need the attack to occur. Use the waiting period to prepare an open underground arena and collect any remaining equipment.

### Prepare the arena before the worm arrives

The Great Depths Worm can swallow items on the ground. Eating them makes it pause, giving you a chance to deal damage without chasing its moving body.

Place several expendable items around an open area before the attack. Keep valuable equipment out of the bait pile, since the worm can consume objects that you do not want destroyed.

When the attack approaches, you may notice ground disturbances and distinctive sounds before the large worm emerges.

Have the friendly Ancient Fuelweaver close enough to reach the worm as soon as it becomes available.

Let Fuelweaver transform the target into the **Blighted Great Depths Worm**.

![The Blighted Great Depths Worm emerging in the Caves after Ancient Fuelweaver corrupts it.](/images/dont-starve-together/blighted-great-depths-worm.webp)

The Blighted version has **6,000 HP**, more body segments, and additional planar damage.

Its most dangerous advantage is movement. The worm is already awkward to attack while traveling underground, and its corrupted form becomes **50% faster** once its health falls below half.

Rather than pursuing its head constantly, draw it toward the prepared items and use the chewing pauses to attack.

Weapons that damage multiple body segments or bounce between targets are especially effective while several parts of the worm are exposed.

### Can you stun the worm?

Yes, but ordinary freezing and sleeping effects are not reliable ways to stop it.

The Great Depths Worm can be stunned by digesting active explosives or certain sleep-inducing foods. This also creates an opportunity for additional damage.

Those methods require deliberate setup. A pile of ordinary disposable items is simpler when your immediate goal is to create attack windows, rather than maximize damage with special consumables.

If the worm moves out of your arena, avoid fighting it through important structures. It can destroy objects while moving and may leave the ground littered with hazards.

Once the Blighted Great Depths Worm dies, collect the **Blighted Guts**.

At this point, check that you have the Larynx, Claw, and Guts. Killing the correct three creatures is sufficient; you do not need to clear other Shadow Rift enemies before continuing.

</section>

<section id="gateway-offerings" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Place the Three Offerings at the Ancient Gateway

Return to the repaired **Ancient Gateway** in the Atrium carrying:

- **Blighted Larynx**
- **Blighted Claw**
- **Blighted Guts**

The Keystone should already have been offered to the Beckoning Hand.

You also need the **Ancient Key** socketed into the Gateway for the completed offering ritual.

Look around the Gateway for the three circular markings on the floor. There is one marking for each offering.

**Drop one different ritual item directly onto each circle.**

When an offering is placed correctly, it rises and floats above its marking. That visible change confirms the item has registered.

Repeat with the other two offerings. Their placement order does not matter.

### Why won't an offering activate?

Check the item itself before moving repeatedly around the Gateway.

A normal creature's loot cannot replace the required Blighted organ. The three objects must specifically be the **Blighted Larynx, Blighted Claw, and Blighted Guts**.

They must also be placed on the circles, not merely dropped somewhere in the Atrium or stored in a container near the Gateway.

Finally, make sure the **Ancient Key** is inserted. The ritual requires the Gateway to be in the correct active state.

### Prepare before placing the third offering

There is no separate confirmation step after completing all three markings.

**The third successful offering immediately triggers Charlie's ritual sequence and the final confrontation.**

Before placing it, make sure you have your combat equipment on your character and that your food, healing, armor, and emergency movement items are ready.

Leave the three-offering ritual incomplete until you have finished preparations.

</section>

<section id="final-fight" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Survive Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination

Completing the ritual takes you into a dark arena where **Shrouded Queen** and **Umbral Abomination** fight in succession. Your supplies must last through both encounters, so finish healing and arranging your equipment before placing the third offering.

Shrouded Queen changes which attacks she will accept. When her vines punish close-range attacks, switch to your ranged weapon and keep moving away from incoming tendrils. Once she begins reflecting projectiles, stop shooting and return to melee, circling around her frontal swipe. An equipped light is more dependable than a stationary fire because Shadow Hands can extinguish lights placed in the arena.

Umbral Abomination requires a different approach. Avoid the temporary Ink Blights rather than trying to kill them, keep moving when the boss becomes a pursuing puddle, and leave room around the Dreadstone Outcrops. Later, watch for the airborne laser and use your Lazy Explorer if its tracking path traps you.

The [Shrouded Queen and Umbral Abomination encounter](/dont-starve-together/shrouded-queen) becomes especially dangerous when the laser widens near the end of the second fight.

When Umbral Abomination's main body collapses, **step on the remaining eye inside the puddle** until it is crushed. Use the central exit to return to the Ancient Gateway and collect your rewards.

<section id="final-rewards" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Do You Get for Beating the Final Shadow Bosses?

Defeating Umbral Abomination opens the exit back to the Ancient Gateway area.

The principal rewards waiting there are:

- **King's Staff**
- **Queen's Torch**
- **Queen's Visage**

![The King's Staff available to pick up beside the Ancient Gateway after the final battle.](/images/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-final-rewards.webp)

The Umbral Abomination itself also drops Shadow-related materials, including Pure Horror, Nightmare Fuel, Dreadstone, and Dark Tatters, along with its figure sketch.

### King's Staff: 30% movement speed and interchangeable gem powers

The **King's Staff** grants **30% additional movement speed** while held. That is faster than a standard Walking Cane.

It can also hold a basic Gem and perform the corresponding staff's function. For example:

- **Orange Gem:** Lazy Explorer-style teleportation.
- **Yellow Gem:** Star Caller's Staff effect.
- **Green Gem:** Deconstruction Staff effect.
- **Purple Gem:** Telelocator Staff effect.
- **Red or Blue Gem:** Fire Staff or Ice Staff effect.

An **Iridescent Gem** cannot be socketed.

Unlike ordinary staves, King's Staff has infinite durability. The trade-off is a **Corruption** meter that increases when you cast spells.

The Corruption level reaches a maximum of **100**.

Below that maximum, it gradually decreases over time, and you can switch the socketed Gem to use a different ability.

At maximum Corruption, the Staff changes behavior:

- The current Gem becomes stuck and cannot be swapped normally.
- Spell use becomes subject to cooldowns.
- Corruption no longer decreases on its own.
- The corrupted Staff gains additional shadow-related interactions.

This distinction matters because **you can change Gems before the Staff is fully corrupted**. Permanent gem locking is not its normal behavior at every Corruption level.

### How to remove maximum Corruption from King's Staff

Take a fully corrupted King's Staff to the **Moon Stone** used for the Moonstone Event.

Socket the Staff there and complete the event. The interaction removes the Staff's Corruption, allowing its Gem to be changed again.

The Staff does not turn into an Iridescent Gem as a result.

Using powerful spells repeatedly without monitoring Corruption may leave you with a Staff locked to a Gem that is inconvenient for your next task. Consider whether the flexibility of changing Gems is more valuable than deliberately reaching maximum Corruption.

### Queen's Torch: permanent light and shadow protection

The **Queen's Torch** burns indefinitely. It provides a large light radius without requiring fuel and continues emitting light while dropped on the ground.

It also repels several Shadow-related creatures, including Shadow Creatures, Grim Harriers, Void Masqued creatures, and Parasitic Shadelings.

The protective effect applies within a limited radius, approximately **2.5 tiles** for creatures affected by the Torch's repulsion.

It can also dissipate certain shadow hazards and keep Obelisks lowered regardless of your sanity.

Unlike an ordinary Torch, it cannot be used to ignite objects.

For cave exploration, its most valuable features are permanent illumination and the ability to prevent some shadow enemies from approaching.

### Queen's Visage

The **Queen's Visage** is the distinctive one-time reward associated with completing the final Shadow confrontation.

Unlike the King's Staff and Queen's Torch, it should not be treated as a replacement for an ordinary weapon or light.

Keep the Visage rather than assuming another copy will be available from the next successful fight in the same world.

<section id="repeat-finale" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Can You Repeat Cursed Confrontation Part 2?

Yes. Defeating Umbral Abomination does not end your world. You can return to the Ancient Gateway for later attempts once its cooldown has expired.

After your first Umbral Abomination victory, defeating Ancient Fuelweaver and renewing the Ruins will also reset the Sanctum. Its broken Waymarks and Portation Orb opportunities become available again, while player-built structures and items left inside the Sanctum may be removed.

The Ancient Gateway also has a **20-day cooldown** after activation. Wait until it can accept the Ancient Key again before attempting another full ritual.

### Do you need to escort Fuelweaver again on later attempts?

The friendly Fuelweaver remains a working way to corrupt the three required creatures.

There is also another option once you have obtained the **King's Staff** and allowed it to reach maximum Corruption.

A corrupted King's Staff can transform the Rock Master, Hound Batilisk, and Great Depths Worm into their Blighted forms. That transformation has a **30-second cooldown**.

This provides an alternative to assembling an Odd Skeleton and transporting Ancient Fuelweaver to each target during subsequent ritual attempts.

The ritual itself still needs the three specific offerings, and the Ancient Gateway must be ready to accept them.
