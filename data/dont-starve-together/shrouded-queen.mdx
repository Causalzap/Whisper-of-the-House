**18,500 HP** separates you from defeating Shrouded Queen, but the first dangerous mistake can happen almost immediately. Once her vines emerge, continuing to attack at close range triggers a counterattack. Later, the same boss will block your ranged attacks and turn them into projectiles that hunt you through the darkness.

Defeating Charlie is only the first half. Umbral Abomination follows with **16,000 HP**, temporary Ink Blights, creeping puddles, Dreadstone Outcrops, and a tracking laser that becomes much wider near the end of the fight.

Both bosses are fought in the same arena. You cannot leave as a living survivor between encounters to refill your supplies, and the equipment that works well against Shrouded Queen can create new hazards during the second battle.

## Where to Find Shrouded Queen and Start the Fight

Shrouded Queen appears at the end of the **Ancient Gateway ritual** in the Atrium.

The Gateway must be repaired, the Keystone must have been offered, and the three ritual items must be placed on their circular markings:

- **Blighted Claw**
- **Blighted Larynx**
- **Blighted Guts**

If you are missing an offering, complete [the three Blighted creature encounters](/dont-starve-together/cursed-confrontation-part-2-walkthrough#three-offerings) before starting the fight.

The third successful offering immediately begins the ritual sequence. Players near the Gateway are transported into the Shrouded arena.

There are no natural light sources inside. The arena is separated from the normal Caves, and living survivors cannot leave until the encounter has been completed.

This makes your inventory at the moment of activation particularly important. A missing ranged weapon cannot be fixed by quickly returning to your base after Charlie begins reflecting projectiles.

## What Should You Bring for Both Bosses?

The two bosses have **34,500 combined HP** before accounting for their summons, knockback, downtime, and special mechanics.

Both are Shadow-aligned, have Planar Entity Protection, and cannot be frozen, put to sleep, or set on fire normally. Conventional control methods cannot replace learning their attack patterns.

The most useful preparations address specific situations rather than simply maximizing damage.

### Bring a real ranged weapon, not only melee damage

Shrouded Queen periodically punishes attacks from nearby players. When her vine counter is active, a powerful melee weapon can be more dangerous to its wielder than a weaker ranged weapon used from outside the counterattack.

A **Gloomerang**, **Howlitzer**, or **Brightshade Staff** gives you a way to damage her without standing next to her during that state.

However, she later becomes immune to projectiles and turns them into homing threats. Carry a dependable melee weapon as well.

A ranged-only setup is therefore just as problematic as arriving with no ranged weapon.

### Choose lighting that Shadow Hands cannot easily extinguish

From early in Shrouded Queen's fight, Shadow Hands travel in from the arena edges and extinguish many player-made lights.

That includes ordinary fires, Dwarf Stars, Polar Stars, and light sources dropped onto the ground.

An equipped **Magiluminescence** supplies both light and movement speed without relying on an extinguishable stationary fire. An upgraded **Enlightened Crown** is another strong option, particularly when it also provides combat benefits.

A **Night Light** can remain lit when ordinary fires are extinguished, but building around a stationary light makes movement less flexible.

Night vision is useful for visibility, although it behaves differently from actual darkness when Shrouded Queen's homing projectiles are active.

There is a trade-off between equipment slots. Magiluminescence occupies the body slot, which you may also want for protective armor. Have a plan for switching equipment under pressure rather than treating permanent lighting and permanent chest protection as simultaneously available from that same slot.

### Keep an emergency teleport ready for Umbral Abomination

The **Lazy Explorer** becomes particularly valuable in the second encounter.

Umbral Abomination can chase you as a moving puddle, appear overhead with a tracking laser, and create obstacles that interfere with ordinary kiting.

The late laser attack increases in width. Its brief slowdown before expanding offers a recognizable escape window, but relying on raw movement speed alone is risky because the tracking behavior adapts to the target's speed.

Bring a Lazy Explorer with enough uses for multiple emergency escapes. Do not plan around a single teleport being sufficient for the entire second fight.

### Armor, healing, and sanity

Carry enough healing and armor durability for both HP pools. Planar damage and repeated knockback make the second encounter especially dangerous when armor breaks unexpectedly.

Useful options include **Bone Armor**, Void equipment with the corresponding repair materials, and strong conventional armor.

Bone Armor can protect against carefully timed incoming hits, including Shrouded Queen's homing barrage, but its defensive timing must be respected. It cannot be treated as continuous immunity.

Sanity food deserves its own inventory space. Both bosses have a strong negative sanity aura, and low sanity makes Dreadstone Outcrops appear more frequently during Umbral Abomination.

Bring **sanity-restoring food** to reduce that pressure and **health-restoring food** such as Pierogi to recover from damage. These solve different problems: restoring health alone will not reduce the frequency of Dreadstone Outcrops.

## Shrouded Queen Phase 1: Dodge the Frontal Swipe

Shrouded Queen begins with **18,500 HP** and a movement speed of **5**. Her opening attack is a broad frontal swipe accompanied by three outward-moving Shadow Creatures. Stepping backward directly in front of her leaves you inside much of the attack area, so move around her side and strike from behind while the animation finishes.

This opening lasts until approximately **95% HP**, when Charlie screeches and vine tendrils emerge from her back. That animation marks the end of the initial melee pattern: staying close and continuing to attack can now trigger her counterattack.


</section>

<section id="shrouded-queen-melee-counter" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Shrouded Queen Phase 2: Why Does She Knock You Back When You Attack?

At **95% HP**, approximately **17,575 remaining health**, Shrouded Queen gains a vine counterattack.

When the player who damaged her is within close range, she can immediately retaliate with an area attack that damages and knocks back nearby targets.

The counter also destroys structures in its radius.

![Shrouded Queen extending shadow vines during her anti-melee counterattack.](/images/dont-starve-together/shrouded-queen-vine-counter.webp)

The counter is activated by nearby attacks, not merely by another player standing beside her. This distinction matters in multiplayer: a ranged player hitting Charlie from far away does not automatically make her retaliate against a teammate who did not attack.

### How to deal damage without triggering the counter

Move outside the immediate counterattack area and switch to ranged damage.

Stay far enough away that she cannot punish the attack with her close-range vines, but do not retreat so far that you lose track of her movement and the approaching arena hazards.

Her counterattack state has a longer attack interval than the opening phase, creating useful opportunities for controlled ranged damage.

There is no benefit to repeatedly testing whether she is still countering melee while standing beside her. Watch for the next screech and animation change.

### When do the Shadow Hands appear?

The 95% transition also introduces **Shadow Hands**.

Up to three can approach from the arena edges and extinguish vulnerable light sources.

If your Dwarf Star suddenly disappears, that is an intended part of this fight rather than a failure of the light-producing item.

Personal lighting provides more reliable mobility. Recover your position and continue using ranged attacks until Charlie enters her next state.

</section>

<section id="shrouded-queen-ranged-counter" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Shrouded Queen Phase 3: Why Are Ranged Attacks Being Reflected?

At approximately **85% HP**, or **15,725 remaining health**, Charlie raises her arms and screeches again.

Her vine counterattack stops, but she gains a different defense.

**Projectiles striking her are blocked and converted into homing shadow projectiles.**

![Homing shadow projectiles chasing the player after Shrouded Queen switches to her anti-ranged state.](/images/dont-starve-together/shrouded-queen-homing-projectiles.webp)

This is the moment to stop firing your ranged weapon.

Continuing to shoot does not slowly wear down her defense. Those attacks fail to deal their intended damage and add more projectiles to the arena.

Instead, switch to melee, circle around her frontal swipe, and attack from her side or rear.

Her screech can also release a separate barrage of homing projectiles, so the danger is not limited to projectiles generated by your own weapon.

### How do the homing projectiles track you?

The projectiles follow illuminated survivors, accelerating as they pursue their target.

Entering **complete darkness** can cause them to lose their target and dissipate.

That creates an unusual counterplay option: temporarily extinguishing your own light can remove the barrage.

However, darkness has its own danger in Don't Starve Together. Removing your light can expose you to Darkness attacks, particularly if you remain unlit for more than a brief interval.

Night vision does not provide the same missile-breaking interaction as being genuinely unprotected in darkness.

Use the darkness method only when you understand the timing and can immediately restore a safe light source. Bone Armor or well-timed defensive movement is a less delicate alternative when you cannot afford another mistake.

### When should you switch back to ranged?

After Charlie loses another portion of health, she returns to the vine-counter state.

Watch for the animation with tendrils extending from her back. At that point, ranged attacks become useful again and close-range attacks become dangerous.

The boss repeatedly alternates between these defenses as her health decreases.

</section>

<section id="shrouded-queen-vines" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Avoid Shrouded Queen's Tracking Vines

Once Charlie returns to her melee-counter state at around **75% HP**, the fight becomes more complicated.

Her screeches and vine counterattacks can release fast-moving vines across the arena.

Some travel toward players, while others move in circular patterns.

A vine that catches you applies a disabling effect that prevents normal movement. Charlie can then follow with another damaging attack while you are unable to reposition.

This is the main reason players can survive several clean attack cycles and still die suddenly after a single mistake.

### Do not stand beside Charlie after a phase transition

When she screeches and her back tendrils emerge, move before committing to another attack.

Continuing to attack at close range can trigger a vine counter at the same moment additional moving vines appear.

That combination may lead to being trapped, knocked back, and struck by her next frontal attack.

With a ranged weapon equipped, circle laterally and attack between vine movements. Avoid moving in a straight line toward the next gap until you have checked whether another vine is already approaching it.

### Why the Same Attack Pattern Stops Working

Charlie alternates between her two defensive states at roughly **10% HP intervals**, although an ongoing animation can delay the transition beyond the expected health threshold. Watch her movement rather than treating the displayed HP value as an exact instruction: emerging back tendrils warn of the close-range counter, while a raised-arm screech signals that projectiles will be reflected.

Switch weapons as the animation changes, but continue moving around the vines already crossing the arena. Choosing the correct weapon prevents you from triggering the wrong counterattack; it does not protect you from a vine that reaches your position.

</section>

## Shrouded Queen Below 20% HP: How to Deal With Grim Harriers

At **20% health**, approximately **3,700 HP remaining**, Grim Harriers begin entering from the arena edges.

These shadow creatures walk across the battlefield and become aggressive when a survivor comes close.

An alerted Harrier accelerates while chasing and eventually performs a damaging lunge.

![Shrouded Queen at 3,028 HP with additional shadow enemies appearing in the arena.](/images/dont-starve-together/shrouded-queen-grim-harriers.webp)

Up to **12 Grim Harriers** can be active at once.

They are different from Charlie's homing projectiles. Grim Harriers are attackable enemies, and killing them can provide Nightmare Fuel.

That can be useful if your Bone Armor requires fuel, but clearing every Harrier is not required to finish the Queen.

### Should you fight the Harriers or focus on Charlie?

It depends on how much health and equipment you have left.

When only a few Harriers are active and Charlie is already close to death, maintain your movement and concentrate on finishing the boss.

If the creatures are surrounding you or repeatedly forcing you into Charlie's attacks, clear the nearest threats before resuming damage.

A Brightshade Staff can help handle several targets, although its bounce behavior depends on which enemies are in range.

Do not mistake the final 20% for a phase where Charlie stops alternating between melee and ranged defenses. The Harriers add pressure on top of the existing fight.

When Shrouded Queen dies, the remaining Grim Harriers and Shadow Hands disappear.

That removes the first boss's hazards, but the full encounter is not finished.

</section>

<section id="umbral-abomination-transition" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Happens After Shrouded Queen Dies?

Shrouded Queen drops **2–3 Nightmare Fuel**, **5–6 Pure Horror**, and her Figure Sketch.

Soon afterward, **Umbral Abomination** appears in the center of the arena.

The battlefield expands as its outer edges recede, providing more space for the second encounter.

The boss initially remains dormant as a puddle of dark Icker goop. It emerges when a survivor comes close or after approximately **20 seconds**.

This provides a short opportunity to restore health, change weapons, and recover from the previous fight.

Use that time deliberately. Do not approach the dormant puddle until you are ready.

Umbral Abomination has **16,000 HP** and movement speed **3**, but those numbers are misleading if you expect an ordinary slow melee fight.

Its movement is built around portals, pursuing puddles, summoned creatures, and airborne tracking attacks.

</section>

<section id="umbral-ink-blights" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Umbral Abomination Phase 1: How to Dodge the Ink Blights

The boss attacks by opening a portal in front of itself.

The portal can damage and knock back survivors, destroy nearby structures, and summon temporary **Ink Blights**.

Unlike ordinary shadow monsters, these summoned Ink Blights cannot be targeted or killed directly. They disappear after their behavior finishes or they leave the arena.

![Umbral Abomination summoning hostile Ink Blights through its portal attack.](/images/dont-starve-together/umbral-abomination-ink-blights.webp)

The initial portal attacks commonly produce two types.

**Shrieks**

These flying creatures orbit outward while scattering projectiles toward the survivor.

Their damage pressure comes from the number of projectiles occupying the arena. Step away from their trajectories and avoid standing still to attack while several projectiles approach.

**Jitters**

The portal sends out **3 Jitters**, shortly followed by **2 more**.

They leap forward, making it dangerous to move directly along the path of the group.

Back away from the initial spread, then change your movement direction instead of running along the creatures' travel path.

At high combat pressure, Umbral Abomination may also summon **Rictuses** earlier than their usual later-phase appearance.

Their presence alone does not necessarily mean the boss has already reached its later health threshold.

</section>

<section id="umbral-puddle" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Umbral Abomination at 95% HP: Escape the Moving Puddle

At approximately **95% HP**, or **15,200 remaining health**, Umbral Abomination gains an underground pursuit attack. It collapses into an Icker puddle, follows the survivor at increased speed, and leaves slowing goop along its path.

The pursuit usually lasts up to approximately **3 seconds** before the boss erupts from the floor, dealing damage and knockback to nearby targets. Because the trail remains a movement hazard, escaping the initial eruption does not necessarily leave you with a safe route for the next attack.

### Guide the puddle away from your next route

Start moving as soon as Umbral Abomination sinks into the ground.

Rather than leading it through the middle of every usable path, draw it toward an outer area while keeping enough distance from the arena boundary to turn safely.

The trail it leaves can reduce your movement speed. Crossing that same trail during the next attack makes escaping harder.

When the puddle closes the distance, change your movement direction or use an emergency teleport rather than attempting to outrun it along a straight path.

Umbral Abomination can also return toward the arena center and immediately follow with another portal attack. Reaching the center is therefore not proof that the danger has ended.

</section>

<section id="dreadstone-outcrops" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Do Dreadstone Outcrops Keep Spawning Around You?

Beginning in the same early portion of the fight, clusters of **Dreadstone Outcrops** emerge from the arena floor.

They can appear beneath the survivor or along the direction of travel.

These objects are not ordinary decoration. They block movement, can damage survivors, and create dangerous situations when the pursuing puddle or laser forces you into an already crowded part of the arena.

![Dreadstone Outcrops forming obstacles around Umbral Abomination and the player.](/images/dont-starve-together/umbral-abomination-dreadstone-outcrops.webp)

The spawning pattern responds to the survivor's mental state.

### Low sanity increases the frequency

At low sanity, the Outcrops appear more often.

At **0 Sanity**, they can emerge as frequently as approximately **every 4 seconds**.

This creates a feedback loop: more obstacles restrict your movement, restricted movement makes the boss's attacks harder to evade, and the resulting damage leaves fewer opportunities to recover.

Maintaining sanity is therefore a direct part of your movement strategy, not merely a way to prevent ordinary Nightmare Creatures from interfering.

### Why a Fully Upgraded Enlightened Crown Can Make the Rocks Explode

An Enlightened Crown provides light and offensive benefits against Shrouded Queen. However, inserting **5 Celestial Jewels** into the Crown locks the wearer at full Enlightenment.

During Umbral Abomination, an Enlightened survivor can cause Dreadstone Outcrops to emerge in a ring around them. Nearby Outcrops may then flash and explode, dealing planar damage.

Before the second fight begins, check the Jewels in your Crown. Removing at least one Jewel or switching to another light source prevents the Crown from forcing full Enlightenment.

Other sources of Enlightenment can still trigger the same hazard. Pay attention to your current state rather than assuming every Enlightened Crown behaves identically.
### Should you mine the Outcrops?

An Outcrop takes **3 hits** from an appropriate mining tool such as a Pick/Axe or Brightshade Smasher.

Destroying one yourself can yield **1 Dreadstone**.

Mining can clear an important escape route, but doing it while Umbral Abomination is preparing another attack is risky. Clear only what is necessary for movement unless the boss has given you an unusually safe opening.

The later optic laser can also destroy Outcrops, but deliberately standing in front of that attack just to clear terrain is a dangerous trade.

</section>

<section id="umbral-laser" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Umbral Abomination at 83% HP: How to Dodge the Tracking Laser

At approximately **83% HP**, or **13,280 health remaining**, the boss gains its airborne **optic laser** attack.

Umbral Abomination leaps out of view, reappears above its target, and fires a tracking beam toward the ground.

The beam inflicts rapid damage and knockback, including planar damage. Being caught can lead to repeated hits before you recover control of your movement.

The boss then falls back toward the arena, damaging anything close to its landing position.

### Why simply running faster may fail

The laser tracks the survivor while accounting for movement speed, so simply running faster does not guarantee an escape. A speed boost is still useful for repositioning, but you need enough open ground to change direction once Umbral Abomination appears overhead.

Move into clear space as the boss leaves the floor, then adjust your direction to avoid the tracking beam. Keep the **Lazy Explorer** ready when Dreadstone Outcrops block the route or the laser is about to overlap your position. An early escape is more reliable than trying to teleport after repeated hits have already interrupted your movement.

### Use the landing window carefully

Umbral Abomination has to return to the ground after completing its laser attack.

Once it has landed and the dangerous effect has ended, you can resume damage.

Do not rush directly underneath the airborne boss merely because the beam has moved elsewhere. Its landing can also damage nearby survivors.

</section>

<section id="umbral-late-summons" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Umbral Abomination at 66% HP: Rasps and Rictuses

At approximately **66% HP**, around **10,560 remaining health**, the portal attack gains additional possibilities.

The boss becomes more aggressive, and its attacks can follow one another with less room to recover.

Two especially dangerous summoned creatures are **Rasps** and **Rictuses**.

### Rasps: move away when the boss roars

A portal can release **3 Rasps** mid-leap.

Rasps attempt to land on survivors and devour them. They are much more dangerous when you remain close to the portal opening.

A Rasp summon is preceded by a roar.

When you hear it, create distance before committing to another attack. This gives you more time to recognize the incoming creatures and move clear of their landing area.

A successful grab can interrupt your movement at exactly the moment you need to avoid Umbral Abomination's next attack.

### Rictuses: do not run through the biting swarm

Another portal pattern summons **5 Rictuses**.

Each can bite repeatedly, up to **8 times**, either toward nearby survivors or along its current movement direction.

A swarm can interrupt movement and lead to additional damage from the boss or surrounding Outcrops.

Retreat from the portal when the group appears, then move around its approach rather than trying to push directly through the middle.

Rictuses may also appear earlier in the battle under higher threat conditions, so treat the appearance of the swarm as an attack signal rather than relying entirely on the current health percentage.

### Why the fight feels faster after this point

From this stage onward, Umbral Abomination can attack quickly after emerging from its puddle.

It can also transition from a portal attack into an optic laser when the laser is ready.

That makes long attack commitments increasingly dangerous.

Prioritize shorter attack windows, then move before the next portal or airborne attack begins.

</section>

<section id="umbral-wide-laser" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Umbral Abomination at 33% HP: Surviving the Wide Laser

At approximately **33% HP**, around **5,280 remaining health**, Umbral Abomination upgrades its optic laser.

Near the end of the normal tracking beam, the boss briefly slows before the attack **widens**.

The expanded beam covers a larger area and lasts slightly longer.

![Umbral Abomination firing its optic laser while Dreadstone Outcrops restrict movement around the player.](/images/dont-starve-together/umbral-abomination-optic-laser.webp)

This is one of the most dangerous points in the entire encounter, particularly when Dreadstone Outcrops and slowing Icker trails have already reduced your movement options.

### Watch for the slowdown before the beam expands

The important signal is not merely that Umbral Abomination is airborne. You have already seen the ordinary optic laser earlier.

Watch the beam toward the end of its tracking period.

When the boss briefly slows, prepare to escape the wider attack.

A **Lazy Explorer** can move you out of the beam's path, but the direction and timing matter. Teleport toward open space, away from the existing laser track and the surrounding Outcrops.

The attack becomes dangerous when you continue in the same direction until the beam widens around you.

If you have enough movement room, a well-timed change of direction may also help. Do not assume that a Magiluminescence, Walking Cane, or mounted movement speed alone guarantees safety against the expanded beam.

### Keep space available for the last few attacks

The boss is close to defeat, but trying to end the fight with one uninterrupted damage burst can be costly.

At this point, allow the dangerous attack to finish before returning to damage.

Keep at least one clear route for the next puddle or laser. Avoid getting trapped between a ring of Dreadstone Outcrops and a slowing trail.

A few controlled attack windows are preferable to losing the encounter while Umbral Abomination has only a small portion of its HP remaining.

</section>

## Is It Better to Fight on Foot or on a Beefalo?

Both approaches can work, but they create different problems.

A mounted survivor benefits from Beefalo movement and can use riding-based damage strategies against Shrouded Queen. Strong equipment and high character damage can shorten her melee and ranged cycles considerably.

However, a successful mounted run should not be used as the expected baseline for every survivor.

### Mounted approach

A **Craggy Carapace** can prevent ordinary knockback while mounted, making it useful against several attacks that would otherwise interrupt your position.

That does not remove every danger.

During Umbral Abomination, **Rasps can still interfere with your mount**, and a crowded arena can expose the Beefalo to repeated hits from the laser or exploding Outcrops.

Your mount's health becomes another resource that must last until the end.

An Enlightenment-generating crown setup can also create dangerous rings of Outcrops around the mounted survivor, so reassess your equipment when the second boss begins.

### Fighting on foot

On foot, precise lateral movement and emergency teleports become more important.

You can respond to Shrouded Queen's melee counter by attacking from range, then return to melee during her projectile immunity without depending on your mount's positioning.

Against Umbral Abomination, fighting on foot avoids managing mount health and some mounted interactions, although you must still handle the puddle, knockback, and wide laser yourself.

For a solo character without exceptional damage, prioritize a reliable ranged weapon, a light and speed setup you can maintain, and enough healing to survive repeated short attack cycles.

The deciding question is whether your equipment lets you **continue moving after a mistake**. A very high-damage setup that leaves you trapped during the next laser is not necessarily the safer build.

</section>

<section id="finish-fight" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Finish Umbral Abomination and Leave the Arena

When Umbral Abomination's main body is defeated, its summoned Ink Blights and Dreadstone Outcrops disappear.

The remaining form is a helpless eye inside a dark puddle.

Walk over the eye repeatedly until it is crushed. The boss cannot be finished using ordinary attacks while in this form.

After that, the exit appears in the center of the arena.

Use it to return toward the Ancient Gateway.

The completed confrontation unlocks the **King's Staff**, **Queen's Torch**, and **Queen's Visage** at the Ancient Gateway. Collect them after returning to complete the Shadow finale.
