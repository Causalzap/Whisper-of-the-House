I start with **Last Rites**.

Wildlands again before I decide whether I want dozens more hours of cartel
progression.

After I finish that contained story, I return through the main menu and enter
the regular campaign.

**Do I still enjoy the normal Wildlands loop?**

and:

**Do the new settings make that loop better enough that I want another long
run?**

If the answer is yes, I already have Predator, the regular campaign, and the
new customization waiting for me.

<h2 id="returning-player-route">
  My returning-player route through the new content
</h2>

The order I use is a **recommendation**, not one continuous mission chain.

I start with:

**Last Rites.**

It is the cleanest way for me to see genuinely new Wildlands content without
replaying old provinces first.

When that story finishes, I return to the main menu and enter the regular
campaign.

From there I decide what I want next.

If I want another self-contained piece of content, I go after **Predator**.

If I want the old campaign to feel mechanically different, I start testing the
new **Ghost Parameters and wider gameplay settings**.

If neither of those makes me want to stay, I do not force another full
playthrough simply because Wildlands has been updated.

That is the advantage of this route.

I can make the decision after playing meaningful content instead of before it.

<h2 id="settings">
  I change Wildlands slowly instead of switching every new option on
</h2>

The new customization is the main reason the old campaign can feel different
after I finish the new missions.

But I would not build an extreme realism preset before I remember how Wildlands
plays.

My starting principle is:

**change a small number of rules → play → decide what problem still exists.**

The new options now cover more than one menu.

They can change my loadout, reload behavior, survival rules, HUD, Difficulty,
squad composition, and the world around me.

The exact settings matter less on this Hub than the decision process.

If I want a more deliberate replay without blindly making everything harder, I
use the{" "}
<Link href="/ghost-recon-wildlands/ghost-parameters">
  Ghost Parameters and settings guide

That page owns the setting-by-setting decisions.

The separate Definitive Edition only becomes a buying decision if I also care

<h2 id="what-i-would-do-next">
  What I would do after the first return session
</h2>

After I have finished or at least tested the new content, I make one decision.

Do I want:

**more story**

**a contained boss challenge**

or:

**a longer Wildlands replay?**

If I am still in Last Rites, I stay with that story instead of wandering back
into unrelated province cleanup.

If I have finished Last Rites and want another short target, Predator is my next
stop.

If I am ready for a longer campaign, that is when I spend time tuning the
settings instead of constantly changing them during the new missions.

And if none of those routes make me want another long session, I stop there.

I do not need to replay every cartel province for the 2026 return to have been
worth checking.
