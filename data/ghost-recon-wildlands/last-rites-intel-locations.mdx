<h2 id="what-counts-as-intel">
  What I treat as an intel checkpoint
</h2>

Last Rites mixes normal intel pickups with objectives that work very
differently.

Sometimes I need to:

**interact with a document or device.**

Other times I need to:

**listen to someone, interrogate a target, or finish a specific investigation
trigger.**

That difference caused me more trouble than physically reaching most of the
locations.

The clearest example is the cultist. I can get right next to him and still fail
to advance because the mission wants me to **listen through the drone**, not
capture him.

So whenever I think the story is stuck, I ask two questions:

**What was I actually supposed to interact with?**

and:

**What new location should that interaction have unlocked?**

If I already have the intel and need the full combat route, Data Server
defense, tripwire section, or final extraction, I switch to the{" "}

<h2 id="unidad-outpost-intel">
  Operation: Lazarus — Unidad outpost intel
</h2>

The first investigation starts with almost nothing except the name **La
Llorona**.

Unidad is the first useful lead.

I head to the marked Unidad outpost and clear enough of the immediate threat to
reach the objective safely.

The mistake is assuming the firefight itself completes the step.

It does not.

I still need the marked intel interaction.

Inside the outpost, I look for an office-style room.

The landmarks that make it easy to recognize are:

**desk → corkboard on the wall → file cabinets → yellow objective marker.**

I interact with the marked papers/evidence at the desk.

The confirmation comes immediately afterward.

The team still has little useful information about La Llorona herself, but the
files mention an:

**off-the-books intelligence office / black site.**

That black-site lead tells me the interaction registered.

If I clear the outpost and never get it, I go back into the marked building
instead of leaving for another objective.

The actual objective is still much simpler:

<h2 id="cultist-drone-intel">
  And Your Enemies Closer — listen to the cultist with the drone
</h2>

This is the most misleading investigation trigger because the “intel” is a
person.

I found the cultist.

I got close enough to interact with him.

That made me assume I was supposed to grab or knock him out.

I was solving the wrong objective.

The actual instruction is:

**Use the drone to listen to the cultist without being detected.**

There is another problem here:

**the drone signal is being jammed.**

So I work through the objective in this order:

**stay hidden → get a usable drone signal → move the drone close enough →
listen until the conversation finishes.**

I do not touch the target early.

When it works, the conversation gives me a new clue about La Llorona's
**shrine** and the Penitentes' plans.

That shrine clue is my confirmation.

If I reach the cultist but never hear the new lead, I check whether I actually
completed the drone eavesdrop.

<h2 id="high-level-cultist">
  Vow of Silence — the high-level cultist is an interrogation trigger
</h2>

The next clue reverses the previous rule.

This time I **do** need to reach the high-level cultist and interrogate him.

The confusing part is what happens afterward.

His answers are mostly fanatical and do not immediately give me a useful
location.

The first time through, that made it feel as if I had missed another document
inside the compound.

I had not.

After the interrogation, Price continues working through what the cultists have
been saying and reports a:

**large buildup of Ines Cortez's forces.**

She sends a new location.

That new marker is the real progression reward from the interrogation.

So if the cultist gives me nothing that sounds useful, I do not restart and I
do not begin searching every nearby room.

I wait for Price's follow-up.

**Search the Penitentes base for intel.**

Clearing nearby enemies is not enough.

I stay inside the marked search area until I find the objective marker in the

<h2 id="intel-not-progressing">
  How I identify the intel step I missed
</h2>

When Last Rites appears stuck, I work backward from the destination I should
have received.

**No black-site lead:**  
I return to the Unidad outpost and finish the marked Investigate interaction.

**No Vargas villa location:**  
I return to the black-site files.

**No objective to search the villa:**  
I check Vargas' bodycam/laptop again.

**Searching the villa forever:**  
I stop checking normal rooms and go down to the hidden Data Server.

**Reached the cultist but got no shrine clue:**  
I make sure I listened through the drone instead of interacting with him
directly.

**High-level cultist gave me nothing useful:**  
I wait for Price to convert the interrogation into the new force-buildup
location.

**Cleared the Penitentes base but received no final waypoint:**  
I return inside and interact with the marked laptop on the desk.

That is the pattern I use for the whole investigation:

**complete the trigger → watch for the next destination.**

When the destination never appears, the last interaction is the first place I
check.
