The final operation begins when the east bridge controls briefly raise the deck
and then fail before Resolute can pass. Bravo has to abandon the console, cross
the damaged bridge, survive the Impaler encounter, and reach the detonator before
the evacuation route can be cleared.

If Resolute is still in the dry dock, the third flood valve is still jammed, or
you have not reached the east control room yet, finish the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 bridge approach</Link> first.


<h2
  id="bridge-controls-fail"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do After the Bridge Controls Fail?
</h2>

The east control room still has enough power to start raising the bridge, but the
mechanism stops again before Resolute has enough clearance to pass.

Do not keep trying the same console. Bravo changes plans immediately: **drop
through the window, cross the deck, and continue toward the other side of the
bridge**.


<h3
  id="impaler-bridge-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Beat the Impaler on the Bridge?
</h3>

The **Impaler** intercepts Bravo shortly after the squad crosses onto the deck.
The warning during the fight is direct: watch the grenades and keep your distance.

Use separate pieces of solid cover instead of clustering around one position.
The Impaler keeps closing distance and throwing explosives, so move when a
grenade forces you out, then resume fire once you have another clear angle.

<h2
  id="dom-marcus"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens Between Marcus and Dom After the Impaler Fight?
</h2>

Dom tries to finish the operation by sacrificing himself. Marcus stops him and
refuses to leave him behind.

Marcus tells Dom that he will not lose him the way he lost Carlos. Dom argues
that Carlos died saving people and says he is prepared to do the same. The
conversation then turns to Dom's children before Marcus makes his position clear:
Dom is his family.

They end the argument by agreeing to finish the mission together.

<h2
  id="reach-detonator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Reach the Detonator?
</h2>

After the Impaler sequence, Bravo continues toward the control-room side of the
bridge. The structure is coming apart around the squad, and the direct route
eventually becomes unusable.

Keep following the surviving path until Bravo reaches the **Skytrain**.


<h3
  id="use-skytrain"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Through the Skytrain When the Route Is Blocked
</h3>

Use the Skytrain passage to continue forward. Do not backtrack looking for a
street-level route once the squad reaches this point.

Fight through the remaining enemies inside and around the train, then continue
through the exit on the far side.

<h2
  id="final-boss"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Beat the Final Boss?
</h2>

There is no additional switch or environmental puzzle to solve once the final
enemy appears. The fight begins immediately in the area around the detonator.

The enemy can push Bravo out of cover and chase individual squad members, so do
not keep everyone stacked in one position. Spread out across the available space,
move when your current cover stops being safe, and keep dealing damage whenever
the enemy changes targets.

This fight ends when the enemy goes down; there is no second puzzle phase
afterward.


<h3
  id="hit-detonator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Activate the Detonator as Soon as the Final Enemy Dies
</h3>

Once the final enemy falls, Bravo immediately calls for the detonator.

Go straight to the **detonator** and activate it. Do not wait for another enemy
wave or search the arena for another objective.

<h2
  id="ending"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens in the Gears of War: E-Day Epilogue?
</h2>

Marcus, Dom, and the surviving members of Bravo make it back to the evacuation
force. Not everyone in Kalona has escaped, but the ship cannot remain in the city
any longer.

The surviving group leaves Kalona and heads toward **Port Ferrell**.


<h3
  id="port-ferrell"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Are the Survivors Going to Port Ferrell?
</h3>

The group expects another convoy to be waiting when they reach **Port Ferrell**.
The conversation aboard the ship also makes clear that conditions outside Kalona
are bad as well, so leaving the city has not ended the wider crisis.

Helena Stroud's daughter is mentioned as someone the survivors are counting on
when they reach the next stage of the evacuation.


<h3
  id="dom-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Does Dom Say He Needs to Go Home?
</h3>

Near the end of the Epilogue, Dom tells Marcus that he has to go home and **see
it for himself**.

Earlier, Dom has already spoken openly about losing his children. The ending does
not show what he finds at home; it ends with Dom deciding that he needs to face
what happened there himself.


<h3
  id="ending-conversation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do Marcus and Dom Say at the End?
</h3>

Dom says that Kalona was lost despite the lives they managed to save. He asks
Marcus whether the Locust can actually be stopped.

Marcus does not claim to know. His answer is that they still have to keep
fighting, because the whole world is finished if they do not.
