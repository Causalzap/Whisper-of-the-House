**Vraahk is the Impaler** that has been shadowing Bravo through the later
Campaign, and he becomes the final named boss of Gears of War: E-Day.

The last operation is easy to misread because his fight is interrupted by story
and navigation. Bravo first faces Vraahk after the east bridge controls fail.
Marcus and Dom then continue toward the control room, the direct route closes,
and the squad has to pass through the Skytrain before reaching the detonator.
Vraahk returns there for the fight that actually ends the Campaign.

This section contains major ending spoilers.

If Resolute is still trapped in the dry dock, the third flood valve is still jammed, or you have not reached the east bridge control room yet, finish the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 walkthrough</Link> first.


<h2
  id="bridge-controls-fail"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do After the East Bridge Controls Fail?
</h2>

The east control room works long enough to start raising the bridge, but the
mechanism stops before Resolute can get through. The console is no longer the
answer once that happens, so do not stay there looking for another interaction.

The route changes to:

- **Drop through the window.**
- **Cross the bridge deck.**
- **Push toward the control route on the other side.**

The bridge is still occupied, so move between usable cover rather than treating
the crossing as a straight sprint. Clear whatever is stopping Bravo from
advancing and keep working forward until Vraahk appears.


<h2
  id="first-vraahk-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Beat Vraahk on the Bridge?
</h2>

The first instruction Bravo gives during the fight is also the most useful one:
**watch the grenades and keep your distance**.

Vraahk becomes much harder when you try to turn the bridge into a static cover
fight. He keeps applying pressure until the firing position that looked safe a
few seconds earlier is no longer worth defending.

A reliable rhythm for the bridge fight is:

- **Fire while Vraahk is still at a manageable distance.**
- **Leave once grenades make the current cover unsafe.**
- **Reload after reaching the next position, not while he is closing on you.**
- **Resume damage when another clean firing angle opens.**

Spacing matters in squad play as well. When everyone stays around the same
obstacle, one explosive attack can force the entire group out at once.


<h3
  id="vraahk-keeps-closing"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Back Off When Vraahk Starts Closing the Gap
</h3>

Vraahk does not stay at a comfortable shooting distance. Once he starts advancing
directly toward your position, protecting the firing angle is no longer worth the
risk.

Create distance first, then look for another opportunity to shoot. I would rather
lose a few seconds of damage here than finish a reload in place, because once
Vraahk reaches the cover itself, recovering a clean firing lane becomes much
harder.


<h3
  id="incinerator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Is the Incinerator Good Against Vraahk?
</h3>

The **Incinerator** is a practical choice if you are already carrying one.

Its advantage comes from the way this fight interrupts your firing time. You can
land the shot, start the burn, and keep contributing damage while you are already
moving away from the position Vraahk has pressured. That fits this encounter
better than depending entirely on a long uninterrupted firing window.

Do not interrupt the fight to search for a perfect loadout if you arrived without
one. Movement matters more than having one specific weapon. Use the strongest gun
you can fire cleanly, then relocate before Vraahk closes the distance or grenades
take away your cover.


<h3
  id="running-out-of-ammo"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  When Should You Reload During the Vraahk Fight?
</h3>

The worst reload is the one that starts just as Vraahk begins pushing toward you.

Use repositioning as the reload window instead. Reach the next safe piece of
cover, refill the weapon there, then resume damage. This also avoids burning
every magazine simply because Vraahk is still visible.

The bridge encounter eventually moves into a story sequence, but that does not
mean the operation is over. You will fight Vraahk again before reaching the end.


<h2
  id="dom-marcus"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Does Marcus Stop Dom After the Bridge Fight?
</h2>

Dom is prepared to sacrifice himself if that is what it takes to finish the
operation, but Marcus refuses to let him do it alone.

The argument is personal rather than tactical. Marcus has already lost Carlos and
will not accept losing Dom the same way. Dom, still carrying the loss of his
children, sees sacrificing himself to save the others as something he is willing
to do.

Marcus finally makes his position clear: **Dom is his family**, and they will
finish the operation together.

<h2
  id="reach-detonator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Reach the Detonator After the Bridge Fight?
</h2>

After the conversation, keep moving with Bravo toward the **control-room side of
the bridge**.

The next stretch returns to normal combat and navigation. Continue forward until
collapsed debris completely blocks the direct route. There is no switch that
repairs this path and no hidden opening through the rubble, so searching around
the obstruction only wastes time.


<h3
  id="use-skytrain"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Through the Skytrain When the Road Is Blocked
</h3>

The continuation is through the nearby **Skytrain**:

- Enter the train.
- Clear the enemies blocking the interior.
- Follow the carriage through to the far side.
- Exit and continue toward the objective.

Do not double back looking for another street-level path. Once Bravo calls for
the Skytrain, that is the route forward.

<h2
  id="final-vraahk-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Beat Vraahk at the Detonator?
</h2>

The final encounter puts much more pressure on your ability to leave cover.

Vraahk can lock onto one member of Bravo and run directly at them, so a strong
piece of cover is useful only when you also know where you will move next. I
found it more useful to think in terms of **two positions**: the firing position I
have now and the escape position I can reach when he commits to me.

The priorities are straightforward:

- **Leave early when Vraahk starts chasing you.**
- **Do not immediately return to cover he just forced you out of.**
- **Turn and deal damage when he redirects toward another squad member.**
- **Spend your remaining heavy ammunition when you get a clean opening.**


<h3
  id="vraahk-chasing"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Run Before Vraahk Reaches Your Cover
</h3>

When Vraahk turns toward you and starts closing the distance, move before he
reaches your position.

Waiting until he is already beside the cover leaves very little room to escape
and makes a reload especially dangerous. Create distance first, let him redirect
toward another member of Bravo, then use that change of target as a damage window.

You are much safer attacking while somebody else has his attention than trying
to win a direct trade while he is already rushing you.


<h3
  id="forced-out-of-cover"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Do Not Keep Returning to the Same Cover
</h3>

Repeatedly fighting for the same position after Vraahk has already forced you out
is one of the easiest ways to lose control of the final arena.

Let that cover go. Move laterally, establish another firing lane, and make Vraahk
turn or cross the area before he can pressure you again. The space around the
detonator exists to be used, and staying planted removes that advantage.


<h3
  id="final-ammo"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Spend Your Remaining Heavy Damage Here
</h3>

There is no full combat chapter after Vraahk, so resource management changes once
you reach the detonator.

You still want clean shots, but there is no reason to finish the battle carrying
your strongest ammunition for an encounter that never comes. If you kept an
Incinerator, its burn remains useful while you are moving. Otherwise, spend
whatever weapon gives you the best damage during the short windows when Vraahk is
attacking someone else or recovering from a push.

At this point the tradeoff is no longer saving resources for later. It is
**dealing damage now without staying exposed long enough to get caught**.


<h2
  id="hit-detonator"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do After Vraahk Dies?
</h2>

Once Vraahk falls, the combat is finished and Bravo immediately calls for the
detonator.

There is no third boss phase and no extra arena puzzle:

- Go straight to the **detonator**.
- Activate it immediately.
- Do not wait for another enemy wave or search for another route.

<h2
  id="ending"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens in the Gears of War: E-Day Ending?
</h2>

Marcus, Dom, and the surviving members of Bravo make it back to the evacuation
force, but the ending does not present the escape as a victory.

They have saved people, yet **Kalona is still lost**. Survivors remain behind,
and the ship cannot stay in the city any longer. The operation has opened an
escape route for part of the population; it has not stopped the Locust attack.

The surviving force leaves Kalona and heads toward **Port Ferrell**.


<h3
  id="port-ferrell"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Are the Survivors Going to Port Ferrell?
</h3>

Another convoy is expected at **Port Ferrell**, giving the survivors somewhere to
regroup and continue moving away from Kalona.

Nobody aboard treats the destination as guaranteed safety. Conditions outside the
city are already bad enough to make it clear that Emergence Day is not a local
disaster.

The conversation also mentions **Anya Stroud**, Helena Stroud's daughter, as
someone the group expects to come through when they arrive.


<h3
  id="dom-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Does Dom Say He Needs to Go Home?
</h3>

Dom tells Marcus that he needs to go home and **see it for himself**.

Escaping Kalona has not resolved what happened to his family. He has heard enough
to understand the loss, but that is different from facing what remains himself.

The ending does not show Dom reaching the house. His decision to go home is where
that part of his story is left.


<h3
  id="ending-conversation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Does the Final Marcus and Dom Conversation Mean?
</h3>

Dom looks back at Kalona and sees a city they failed to save. Marcus points to the
people who survived, but neither man mistakes that for a victory over the Locust.

Dom asks whether they can actually stop what has started. Marcus does not claim
to know; his answer is that they still have to keep fighting, because the
alternative is letting the rest of the world suffer the same fate.
