  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 4 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Take the **second train** toward Octus Way, then wait for the Prospect Bay
    generator to reach **85%** before closing the breaker. Leave the road for the
    uphill trail to the weather station, broadcast the evacuation from the admin
    building, and enter the refinery. Inside, use the **backup coolant feed**,
    get the **Zone 5 key card**, stop the enriched emulsion feed, kill both
    **Scoria**, and **dump the tanks**.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>

Act 4 sends Bravo from Fort Vigil toward Prospect Bay, but almost every planned
route breaks somewhere along the way. A downtown street is blocked, the first
train goes the wrong direction, the power grid has to be restarted before
communications work, and the road to the weather station eventually gives way to
a foot trail.

The final two chapters shift from travel problems to evacuation and refinery
shutdown. By then, the important question is no longer simply where Bravo should
go, but which system has to be restored or disabled before the next route opens.

Act 4 has six chapters:

- **Chapter 1: Hope and Ruin**
- **Chapter 2: Technical Difficulties**
- **Chapter 3: Tough as Nails**
- **Chapter 4: Signals**
- **Chapter 5: Paths Diverge**
- **Chapter 6: Critical Failure**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-hope-and-ruin"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Hope and Ruin — Reach Prospect Bay
</h2>

Bravo leaves Fort Vigil with two immediate problems to solve. The evacuation
needs a route toward the dry dock, and the surviving COG forces still need
long-range contact with high command.

The planned road through downtown does not survive long. Once destruction blocks
the main street and Reyes redirects the squad, stop searching around the
roadblock for another opening. Head **east around Kalona Square** and stay on the
cathedral side of the district.


<h3
  id="cathedral-route"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Around the Cathedral and Use the Far Gate
</h3>

Fight through the cathedral grounds and continue toward the **far-side gate**.
That gate carries Bravo into the next section of the city and toward the rail
network.

The original downtown road never becomes useful again during this sequence. Once
you have committed to the cathedral route, keep moving forward rather than
circling back to the first obstruction.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-technical-difficulties"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Technical Difficulties — Take the Train Toward Octus Way
</h2>

Bravo reaches a rail platform while trying to cut across Kalona, but the first
train that arrives is a trap for anyone reacting too quickly to the objective.

It is **not the train you need**. Reyes identifies that line as heading uptown
instead of toward Bravo's destination.


<h3
  id="board-correct-train"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Wait for the Second Train
</h3>

Stay on the platform after the first train leaves. There is no alternate platform
to find and no reason to chase the train that just passed.

The **second train** is the correct one. Board it when the doors open and ride
toward **Octus Way Station**.


<h3
  id="move-to-front-car"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Move to the Front Car When the Train Starts Breaking Apart
</h3>

The trip falls apart before Bravo reaches the destination. When the rear cars
begin failing and the squad is told to move toward the **front car**, treat that
as a movement objective rather than a normal train-clearing fight.

Keep pushing forward through the surviving cars. Enemies left in sections already
being lost behind you are less important than reaching the front before the train
breaks apart.

The crash eventually puts Bravo back on foot and leads into the Prospect Bay
power problem.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-tough-as-nails"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: Tough as Nails — Restore the Prospect Bay Power Grid
</h2>

Port control cannot reach high command because the communications network has no
power. Survivors at **Lion's End** point Bravo toward the local station, where
Locust damage has taken the grid offline.

The power station is not just a switch interaction. The technicians need time to
restart the machinery while Bravo keeps the area under control.


<h3
  id="power-station-defense"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Place the Sentries Before Starting the Repair
</h3>

Pick up the available **sentry kits** before the repair begins and cover the
approaches the Locust will use to reach the technicians.

Once the restart is underway, stay close enough to respond to whichever side
starts taking pressure. The repair only matters if the station survives long
enough for generator output to climb to the required level.


<h3
  id="power-station-breaker"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  When Do You Close the Power Station Breaker?
</h3>

Do **not** close the breaker as soon as the machinery starts running.

Watch the generator output:

- **45%** — keep waiting.
- **65%** — keep waiting.
- **85%** — close the breaker.

Closing the breaker at **85%** brings the district grid back online. If nothing
useful seemed to happen at 45% or 65%, the problem was timing rather than another
missing control.


<h3
  id="port-control-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Port Control After Restoring Power
</h3>

Power returning does not immediately restore communications because Locust still
control the area around port control.

Push back toward the tower, clear the position, and secure the equipment now that
electricity has returned. Bravo can then move on to the longer-range
communications site.

Act 4 also contains several optional weapon upgrades around Hope and Ruin and
Tough as Nails. If you are stopping for those objectives before moving on, the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/weapon-mods">weapon mod locations</Link> show which detours lead to the Lancer, Hammerburst, Torque Bow, Incinerator, and Gut Puncher upgrades.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-signals"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Signals — Find the Old Weather Station
</h2>

Bravo gets vehicle support for part of the climb, but the road does not reach the
old communications site.

After the first enemy position is cleared, Reyes points out a **trail leaving the
road**. That turn is easy to miss if you keep expecting the vehicle route to
continue uphill.

<h3
  id="reach-weather-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stay on the Uphill Trail to Reach the Weather Station
</h3>

Follow the trail through the Locust positions and keep climbing. Returning to the
road will not reveal another vehicle path; the rest of the approach is on foot.

At the old weather station, Bravo finally makes contact with outside command.
What the squad learns there changes the evacuation plan almost immediately, and
the next emergency pulls them back toward Prospect Bay.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-paths-diverge"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Paths Diverge — Warn the Prospect Bay Civilians
</h2>

The **refinery evacuation siren** begins sounding while Bravo is still trying to
deal with the wider evacuation. When the siren suddenly stops, do not treat the
silence as an all-clear. The refinery problem is still active.

Before entering the refinery, Bravo needs to give the remaining civilians a way
out of Prospect Bay.


<h3
  id="admin-building"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the Admin Building
</h3>

The plan is to use the **Sarahine admin building PA system** and direct civilians
toward the docks, where small boats can move them across the bay.

Fight through the gate and parking area, enter the building, and secure the
interior before using the public-address system.


<h3
  id="broadcast-evacuation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Broadcast the Evacuation Message Before Leaving
</h3>

Use the PA system to send the remaining civilians toward the **Port of Kalona**.
Do not leave the building as soon as the interaction begins; stay through the
broadcast and deal with the immediate Locust pressure around the site.

Once the warning has gone out, Bravo can finally turn toward the refinery and the
light mass converter emergency.


<h2
  id="chapter-6-critical-failure"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 6: Critical Failure — Stop the Light Mass Converter
</h2>

The **light mass converter** is already close to failure when Bravo enters the
refinery. Its pressure is around **80% of the tolerance limit** and continues to
rise as the squad works deeper into the facility.

**Cara Lynch** makes contact from the security office and helps direct the
shutdown, but damaged refinery sections keep breaking the normal route. From this
point, the critical sequence is:

1. Avoid exposed raw emulsion.
2. Enter the **backup coolant feed** when the catwalk is locked.
3. Get the **Zone 5 key card**.
4. Stop the **enriched emulsion feed**.
5. Kill both **Scoria** blocking the shutdown.
6. Return to the controls and **dump the tanks**.


<h3
  id="avoid-raw-emulsion"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stay Out of the Raw Emulsion
</h3>

Do not use exposed pools of **raw emulsion** as shortcuts. Marcus takes damage
quickly after entering the liquid, and the refinery provides raised routes around
the dangerous sections.

Use walkways, pipes, and platforms even when crossing the emulsion directly looks
faster.


<h3
  id="backup-coolant-feed"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Backup Coolant Feed When the Catwalk Is Locked
</h3>

The normal catwalk toward the processing area eventually stops at a locked route.
Searching that section for another switch will not open it.

Carter identifies the **backup coolant feed** as the way through. Look to the
**left side** for the hatch.

Enter the pipe and stay with it toward the next refinery section. Converter
pressure reaches roughly **85%** during this stretch, but there is no alternate
shutdown control hidden inside the coolant route.


<h3
  id="zone-5-key-card"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Is the Zone 5 Key Card?
</h3>

The planned descent fails when the staircase collapses, forcing Bravo toward an
elevator through the **research labs**. The restricted route will not open without
the correct access card.

Go into the security area and collect the **Zone 5 key card**, then use it on the
restricted entrance and continue through Zone 5 toward the research section.

If the door will not open, do not keep searching around the elevator itself. The
missing step is the security-room card.


<h3
  id="stop-enriched-emulsion-feed"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stop the Enriched Emulsion Feed
</h3>

Inside the labs, Bravo discovers that **enriched emulsion is being fed into the
light mass converter**.

Follow the refinery route to the intake controls and shut the feed down. The
converter cannot be brought under control while enriched material is still
entering the system.


<h3
  id="converter-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Kill Both Scoria Before the Shutdown Can Continue
</h3>

The shutdown is interrupted when **two Scoria** attack around the converter.
Carter cannot continue the procedure while they are still alive, so both enemies
have to be killed before the objective moves forward.

Do not return to the converter controls after dropping only one. If the shutdown
still will not continue, check the arena for the second Scoria.

For their attacks and the safest way to handle the pair, use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses">boss guide</Link>. Once both are down, return to the converter and continue the emergency procedure.


<h3
  id="dump-the-tanks"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dump the Tanks to Stop the Converter
</h3>

Defeating the Scoria does not stop the converter by itself. The system remains
unstable, and Carter identifies the final emergency measure: **dump the tanks**.

Use the control as soon as it becomes available. Dumping the tanks ends the
immediate converter crisis and closes the refinery operation.


<h2
  id="before-act-5"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens After the Refinery?
</h2>

By the end of Act 4, Bravo has restored long-range communications, warned the
remaining Prospect Bay civilians, and stopped the light mass converter from
failing. The wider evacuation from Kalona is still unfinished.

Act 5 begins with Bravo being carried away from the city on an automated train.
