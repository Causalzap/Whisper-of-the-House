  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 4 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Go east around the cathedral when the downtown street is blocked, wait for the
    **second train** toward Octus Way, then restore the Prospect Bay grid by closing
    the breaker at **85% generator output**. Follow the uphill trail to the old
    weather station, broadcast the civilian evacuation from the admin building,
    then enter the refinery. Use the **backup coolant feed**, find the **Zone 5 key
    card**, stop the enriched emulsion feed, and **dump the tanks** to shut down the
    light mass converter emergency.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>

Act 4 takes Bravo from Fort Vigil toward Prospect Bay, but damaged streets,
broken rail lines, a dead power grid, and the loss of long-range communications
keep changing the route. The second half then turns into an evacuation and
refinery emergency around the **light mass converter**.

The six chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: Hope and Ruin**
- **Chapter 2: Technical Difficulties**
- **Chapter 3: Tough as Nails**
- **Chapter 4: Signals**
- **Chapter 5: Paths Diverge**
- **Chapter 6: Critical Failure**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-hope-and-ruin"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Hope and Ruin — Reach Prospect Bay
</h2>

Bravo leaves Fort Vigil with two immediate goals: move the evacuation toward the
dry dock and restore contact with high command. The planned route crosses
downtown toward **Prospect Bay**, but destruction blocks the first major street.

Once Reyes redirects the squad, stop searching the original roadblock. Head east
around **Kalona Square** and follow the cathedral side of the district.


<h3
  id="cathedral-route"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Around the Cathedral and Use the Far Gate
</h3>

Fight through the cathedral grounds and continue toward the **far-side gate**.

The blocked street behind Bravo never becomes the route forward again. Once the
far gate opens access to the next section of the city, stay with that path toward
the rail network.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-technical-difficulties"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Technical Difficulties — Take the Train Toward Octus Way
</h2>

Bravo reaches a rail platform while trying to shorten the trip across Kalona.

The first train that passes is **not** the correct one. It continues uptown
without taking Bravo toward the next objective.


<h3
  id="board-correct-train"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Wait for the Second Train
</h3>

Do not chase the first train or look for another platform. Reyes identifies it as
the wrong line.

Stay where you are until the **next train** arrives and opens its doors. Board
that train for **Octus Way Station**.


<h3
  id="move-to-front-car"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Move to the Front Car When the Train Starts Breaking Apart
</h3>

The ride does not remain intact. Once the rear sections begin failing and Bravo
is told to reach the **front car**, keep moving forward through the train.

There is no reason to clear every enemy in cars that are already being lost
behind you. Reach the surviving front section and stay with it until the crash
forces Bravo back onto foot travel.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-tough-as-nails"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: Tough as Nails — Restore the Prospect Bay Power Grid
</h2>

Port control cannot contact high command because the Prospect Bay communications
network has no power.

Survivors at **Lion's End** confirm that the Locust damaged the local power
station, making the grid repair Bravo's next priority.


<h3
  id="power-station-defense"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Place the Sentries Before Starting the Repair
</h3>

At the power station, technicians need time to restart the damaged machinery.
Pick up the available **sentry kits** and place them to cover the approaches
before the repair begins.

Once the station starts coming online, protect the technicians and equipment
until the generators can produce enough power for the grid.


<h3
  id="power-station-breaker"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  When Do You Close the Power Station Breaker?
</h3>

Do **not** close the breaker as soon as the machinery begins moving.

The generator output rises through **45%**, then **65%**, and finally **85%**.
Wait for the display to reach **85% output**, then close the breaker.

Closing it at the final threshold restores power to the district and lets Bravo
return to the communications objective.


<h3
  id="port-control-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Port Control After the Power Returns
</h3>

Power alone does not make the communications equipment usable. Locust still
occupy the area around the tower.

Push back to port control, clear the position, and secure the powered equipment.
Bravo can then move on to the longer-range communications site.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-signals"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Signals — Find the Old Weather Station
</h2>

Bravo gets a ride part of the way uphill, but the road stops being useful before
the squad reaches the old communications site.

After clearing the first enemy position, Reyes points out a **trail leaving the
road**. Take that trail rather than continuing along the vehicle route.

<h3
  id="reach-weather-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Follow the Trail All the Way to the Weather Station
</h3>

Keep climbing through the Locust positions along the trail. Do not return to the
road looking for another vehicle route.

At the old weather station, Bravo finally establishes contact with outside
command. The information received there changes the evacuation plan, but another
emergency begins almost immediately.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-paths-diverge"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Paths Diverge — Respond to the Refinery Alarm
</h2>

Bravo soon hears the **refinery evacuation siren**. The light mass converter is
already in trouble.

When the siren suddenly stops, keep moving. The silence is not an all-clear; the
refinery emergency is still active.


<h3
  id="admin-building"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the Admin Building and Warn the Civilians
</h3>

Before Bravo enters the refinery, the remaining civilians in Prospect Bay need an
escape route.

The plan is to use the **Sarahine admin building PA system** to direct them toward
the docks, where small boats can move them across the bay.

Fight through the gate and parking area, then enter the admin building.


<h3
  id="broadcast-evacuation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Broadcast the Evacuation Message
</h3>

Secure the building and use the PA system to direct the remaining civilians
toward the **Port of Kalona**.

Stay in the area until the broadcast is complete and the immediate Locust attack
is under control. Once the message has gone out, Bravo can leave the admin
building and continue toward the refinery.


<h2
  id="chapter-6-critical-failure"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 6: Critical Failure — Stop the Light Mass Converter
</h2>

Bravo reaches the refinery with the **light mass converter** already close to
failure. The pressure is around **80% of its tolerance limit** and continues
rising as the squad moves deeper into the facility.

Inside, Bravo contacts **Cara Lynch** in the security office. She can help with
the shutdown, but several damaged sections of the refinery block the normal route
to the converter.


<h3
  id="avoid-raw-emulsion"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Do Not Walk Through Raw Emulsion
</h3>

Treat every exposed pool of **raw emulsion** as a hazard. Marcus takes damage
quickly after entering it.

Use the walkways, pipes, and raised platforms around the refinery instead of
trying to cross the liquid directly.


<h3
  id="backup-coolant-feed"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Backup Coolant Feed When the Catwalk Is Locked
</h3>

When the normal catwalk toward the processing area is locked, do not keep
searching it for another switch.

Carter identifies the **backup coolant feed pipe** as the alternate route. The
hatch Bravo needs is on the **left**.

Enter the pipe and continue toward the converter. The pressure reaches **85%**
during this section, so keep following the route rather than stopping to explore
areas that do not lead forward.


<h3
  id="zone-5-key-card"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Is the Zone 5 Key Card?
</h3>

The next planned descent fails when the staircase collapses. Bravo needs to reach
an elevator through the **research labs**, but the restricted area cannot be
opened without the correct card.

Go to the security area and collect the **Zone 5 key card**. Use it to open the
restricted route, then continue through Zone 5 toward the research section.


<h3
  id="stop-enriched-emulsion-feed"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stop the Enriched Emulsion Feed
</h3>

Inside the labs, Bravo discovers that enriched emulsion is being deliberately fed
into the light mass converter.

Follow the route to the intake controls and shut down the feed. The converter
remains a threat while that material is still entering the system.


<h3
  id="converter-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Survive the Fight at the Light Mass Converter
</h3>

A large Locust creature interrupts the shutdown before Bravo can finish the
emergency procedure.

Use the open space around the converter to avoid getting pinned behind one piece
of cover. Move when its larger attacks force Bravo out, then keep firing whenever
a safe angle opens.

Once the creature is down, return to the converter controls. There is no second
combat phase before the shutdown sequence continues.


<h3
  id="dump-the-tanks"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dump the Tanks to Stop the Converter
</h3>

The converter remains unstable even after the fight. Carter identifies the final
emergency option: **dump the tanks**.

Use the control as soon as it becomes available. Dumping the tanks stops the
immediate converter crisis and ends the refinery operation.


<h2
  id="before-act-5"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Does Bravo Go After the Refinery?
</h2>

By the end of Act 4, Bravo has restored long-range communications, warned the
remaining Prospect Bay civilians, and stopped the refinery emergency. The
evacuation route out of Kalona is still unfinished.

Act 5 begins with Bravo being carried away from the city on an automated train.
