  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 2 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Retake **Legacy Bridge**, but do not stop at the first control room when the
    mechanism jams. Use the **Skytrain high ground** and lower deck to reach the
    eastern controls, then return to the FOB and follow Bravo's assignments
    through **Octus Way Station**, **All Father's Plaza**, and Charlie Squad's
    last known position. After the Raven flight, abandon the runway when the
    airfield collapses and fall back toward the **west control tower**.

Act 2 puts Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes together as **Bravo Squad** and sends
them across a series of COG positions around Kalona. The route starts at Legacy
Bridge, continues through the downtown defensive line and the search for Charlie
Squad, then ends with a Raven deployment and the collapse of the airfield.

The five chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: Bravo Squad**
- **Chapter 2: Hold the Line**
- **Chapter 3: No Gear Left Behind**
- **Chapter 4: Flyboys**
- **Chapter 5: Overrun**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-bravo-squad"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Bravo Squad — Retake Legacy Bridge
</h2>

Act 2 opens with Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes formally assigned to **Bravo
Squad** under Captain Gray.

Their first objective is **Legacy Bridge**, where Charlie Squad is still holding
the near side after an earlier COG attempt failed.

Leave the stadium FOB and follow the route toward Charlie. Clear the Locust
pressing their position before moving farther onto the bridge.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-snipers"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Snipers Before Charlie Can Advance
</h3>

Elevated shooters pin Charlie down on the bridge approach. Do not rush the
controls while those positions are still active.

Suppress the snipers and use the flank to remove them. Once Charlie can move
again, join the squad and continue toward the first control room.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-first-control-room"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do When the First Bridge Controls Jam?
</h3>

Captain Gray wants the **central span** lowered, but the first control room cannot
finish the job.

Use the console once. The bridge begins moving, then the damaged mechanism jams.

Do not remain at the first control room looking for another switch. Command
redirects Bravo and Charlie toward the **eastern control room**.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-eastern-control-room"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the Eastern Control Room Through the Skytrain Position
</h3>

Bravo and Charlie take different approaches toward the eastern controls. Follow
Bravo's route on the right.

When Charlie becomes pinned again, move up to the **Skytrain position** above the
bridge and use the elevation to clear the enemies blocking their advance.

Once Charlie starts moving, continue until Bravo is sent down to the **lower
deck**. The eastern control room can be reached from there.

Activate the controls and the central span finally begins lowering correctly.


<h3
  id="secure-legacy-bridge"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Legacy Bridge Before Returning to the FOB
</h3>

Lowering the span does not immediately finish the assignment. Bravo and Charlie
still have to remove the Locust holding the rest of the crossing.

Clear the remaining resistance until command confirms that Legacy Bridge is back
under COG control, then return toward the stadium FOB.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-hold-the-line"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Hold the Line — Follow Bravo's Downtown Assignments
</h2>

Bravo returns to a fortified COG position before being sent back into downtown.
Use the stop to resupply before leaving again.


<h3
  id="quartermaster-weapon-modification"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Resupply and Take the Quartermaster's Weapon Modification
</h3>

Report to the **quartermaster**. The weapon modification shown here adds a
quick-start lever, increases the fire rate, and provides an extended magazine.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/gears-of-war-e-day/gears-e-day-first-weapon-modification.webp"

    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Take the quartermaster upgrade and refill ammunition before Bravo deploys
    downtown.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

A **deployable supply cache** is also available at the position. Check it before
leaving if Bravo is short on ammunition or equipment.

The next order is to push the Locust back and reinforce the COG line downtown.


<h3
  id="octus-way-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Help Charlie Secure Octus Way Station
</h3>

At **Octus Way Station**, Charlie Squad is pinned down again.

Clear the enemies controlling the upper level, then push into the station with
Charlie and finish securing the position.

A supply cache becomes available after the fight. Use it if needed before Bravo
is reassigned.


<h3
  id="all-fathers-plaza"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Continue to All Father's Plaza
</h3>

Captain Gray next sends Bravo to the **All Father's Plaza muster point**, where
the assigned squad has stopped responding.

Move to the plaza and clear the fighting along the way. At the muster point,
Bravo finds the COG position destroyed and encounters the creature responsible
for the corrosive damage around the bodies.

Keep some distance from its corrosive attack rather than fighting directly in
front of it.

Once the enemy is down, command redirects Bravo toward the **Amberstone storage
facility** farther east.


<h3
  id="kalona-hotel"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Secure the Kalona Hotel Communications Team
</h3>

Later in the same chain of assignments, Bravo reaches **Kalona Hotel**, where a
communications team has been attacked.

Clear the hotel and locate the surviving corporal. The long-range relay still
has to be connected, so hold the position until the communications work is
finished.

Only then does Bravo receive the next major change in orders.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-no-gear-left-behind"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: No Gear Left Behind — Find Charlie Squad
</h2>

An all-hands order tells the surviving COG forces to return to base, but Reyes
receives a location connected to Charlie Squad before Bravo leaves.

Charlie's last known signal is **due east**.

Rather than following the normal return route, Bravo decides to investigate.


<h3
  id="subway-route"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Take the Subway Tunnels Toward Charlie's Last Ping
</h3>

Reyes identifies the **subway tunnels** as the faster route east.

Find the descent and continue underground toward Charlie's location. The subway
does not loop Bravo back through the positions cleared during Hold the Line; it
carries the squad toward the missing Gears.

When Charlie comes into view, continue through the encounter instead of trying to
retreat to the subway entrance.


<h3
  id="leave-after-charlie"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Charlie Squad Sequence Before Leaving
</h3>

The return-to-base order is still active, but Bravo does not leave until the
Charlie situation is resolved.

Afterward, follow the new route forward. The next stage moves Bravo toward air
transport rather than sending the squad back through downtown.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-flyboys"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Flyboys — Defend the Raven
</h2>

Bravo boards a Raven with the flight crew. The trip begins quietly, then the
aircraft comes under heavy fire.

When the crew calls Bravo forward, move onto the mounted guns.


<h3
  id="raven-guns"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Shoot the Positions Attacking the Raven
</h3>

Do not spend the flight firing at every target visible below. Concentrate on the
positions actively hitting the Raven.

Switch targets as new firing positions begin hitting the Raven. Stay with the
flight sequence until the aircraft reaches the airfield.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-overrun"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Overrun — Escape the Collapsing Airfield
</h2>

Bravo initially arrives to reinforce the airfield, but the Locust tear apart the
runway and make the tarmac impossible to hold.

Once the retreat order comes through, stop treating the runway as a defensive
position.


<h3
  id="leave-tarmac"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Leave the Runway and Regroup at the Hangar
</h3>

Move away from the collapsing tarmac and head toward the **hangar**.

  <Image

Clear the enemies directly blocking Bravo's escape, but keep following the
fallback route instead of trying to retake ground behind the squad.


<h3
  id="west-control-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Fall Back Toward the West Control Tower
</h3>

As the airfield continues collapsing, command changes the rally point again.

The next fallback is the **west control tower**, where Ravens are supposed to
extract the remaining force.

Follow whichever route through the damaged structures remains open. When another
section collapses and creates a new opening, use it rather than trying to return
to the runway.

The retreat follows this progression:

**runway → hangar → interior fallback → west control tower**

Keep moving whenever command shifts the rally point.


<h2
  id="before-act-3"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Does Bravo Go After the Airfield Falls?
</h2>

The airfield collapse leaves Bravo separated from the main COG force. The next
attempt to regroup begins after the Raven crash west of Kalona, where the squad
has to find another way back toward friendly lines.
