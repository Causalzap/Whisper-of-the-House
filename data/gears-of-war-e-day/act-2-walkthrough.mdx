  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 2 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    At **Legacy Bridge**, use the first controls once, then follow Bravo toward the
    **eastern control room** when the mechanism jams. Use the Skytrain position to
    free Charlie, drop to the lower deck, and finish lowering the bridge. Later,
    follow the changing Hold the Line orders, take the subway toward Charlie
    Squad's last signal, defend the Raven, and retreat from the collapsing
    airfield through the **hangar** toward the **west control tower**.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>

Act 2 is the first time Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes operate together as
**Bravo Squad**. The five chapters move from the fight for Legacy Bridge into a
series of changing assignments across Kalona, then finish with Charlie Squad's
disappearance, a Raven deployment, and the collapse of the airfield.

The chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: Bravo Squad**
- **Chapter 2: Hold the Line**
- **Chapter 3: No Gear Left Behind**
- **Chapter 4: Flyboys**
- **Chapter 5: Overrun**

The main thing to watch in this Act is the objective text. Several locations look
finished before Bravo is actually done there: Legacy Bridge has a second control
route, Hold the Line repeatedly changes Bravo's destination, and the airfield
retreat keeps moving the rally point as the runway falls apart.


<h2
  id="chapter-1-bravo-squad"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Bravo Squad — Retake Legacy Bridge
</h2>

Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes are formally organized as Bravo Squad under
Captain Gray before being sent to **Legacy Bridge**.

Charlie Squad is still holding the near side after an earlier attempt to secure
the crossing failed. Leave the stadium position, reach Charlie, and clear the
Locust pressing the bridge approach before moving toward the controls.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-snipers"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Snipers Before Moving Deeper Onto the Bridge
</h3>

Charlie cannot advance while elevated shooters control the approach. Deal with
those positions first instead of trying to push directly toward the bridge
controls.

Once the snipers are gone and Charlie starts moving, join the advance toward the
first control room.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-first-control-room"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do When the First Bridge Controls Jam?
</h3>

Captain Gray needs the **central span** lowered. The first control room appears to
be the answer, but the mechanism only moves briefly before jamming.

Use the console once. When the bridge stops and the objective changes, leave that
room.

The next destination is the **eastern control room**. Staying at the damaged
console and searching for another interaction will not restart the bridge.


<h3
  id="legacy-bridge-eastern-control-room"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Reach the Eastern Bridge Controls?
</h3>

Bravo and Charlie split their approaches toward the far side. Follow Bravo's
route on the **right**.

Charlie becomes pinned again during the crossing. Move up to the **Skytrain
position** above the bridge and use the elevation to clear the enemies stopping
their advance.

After Charlie starts moving again, continue until Bravo is sent to the **lower
deck**. That route leads into the eastern control room.

Use the second controls to get the central span moving properly.


<h3
  id="secure-legacy-bridge"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish Clearing Legacy Bridge Before You Leave
</h3>

Lowering the bridge is not the end of the assignment. Locust still hold part of
the crossing, so Bravo and Charlie have to finish clearing the remaining
resistance.

Stay with the bridge fight until command confirms that the position is secure.
Only then return toward the stadium FOB.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-hold-the-line"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Hold the Line — Follow Bravo's Changing Orders
</h2>

Back at the fortified COG position, Bravo gets a brief chance to resupply before
being sent into downtown again.

This chapter is less about following one uninterrupted route and more about
reacting to new orders as each position is secured. A cleared area does not
necessarily mean Bravo is heading back to base; listen for the next assignment
before settling in.


<h3
  id="quartermaster-weapon-modification"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Resupply Before Leaving the FOB
</h3>

Visit the **quartermaster** before Bravo deploys again. The modification available
here adds a quick-start lever, increases fire rate, and extends the magazine.

There is also a **deployable supply cache** at the position. Use the stop to top
up ammunition and equipment before leaving, because the next assignments move
Bravo away from the FOB.

Act 2 also contains optional weapon upgrades away from the main route. If you are
deciding which of those detours are worth taking, the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/weapon-mods">weapon mod locations</Link> cover the rewards and where to find them.


<h3
  id="octus-way-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Help Charlie at Octus Way Station
</h3>

Charlie Squad is under pressure again at **Octus Way Station**.

The immediate problem is the upper level. Clear the enemies controlling that
position, then push into the station with Charlie until the remaining resistance
is gone.

A supply cache becomes available after the fight. Restock there if needed before
Bravo receives another assignment.


<h3
  id="all-fathers-plaza"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens at All Father's Plaza?
</h3>

Captain Gray next sends Bravo toward the **All Father's Plaza muster point** after
the assigned squad stops responding.

At the plaza, the COG position has already been destroyed. The enemy responsible
for the corrosive damage around the bodies attacks Bravo there; keep enough
distance to stay out of the corrosive attack rather than standing directly in
front of it.

When the area is secure, the next order points Bravo toward the **Amberstone
storage facility** farther east.


<h3
  id="kalona-hotel"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Secure the Communications Team at Kalona Hotel
</h3>

Later in the assignment chain, Bravo reaches **Kalona Hotel**, where a
communications team has come under attack.

Clear the hotel and find the surviving corporal. The job is not finished as soon
as you locate him: the long-range relay still needs to be connected.

Hold the position until the communications work is complete. Bravo receives the
next major change in orders only after the relay is functioning.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-no-gear-left-behind"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: No Gear Left Behind — Find Charlie Squad
</h2>

An all-hands order calls the surviving COG units back to base, but Reyes receives
a signal connected to Charlie Squad before Bravo leaves the area.

Charlie's last known position is **due east**. Bravo chooses to investigate
instead of immediately following the normal return route.


<h3
  id="subway-route"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Take the Subway Toward Charlie's Last Signal
</h3>

Reyes identifies the **subway tunnels** as the faster route east.

Find the descent and follow the underground path toward Charlie's position. The
subway is not a loop back through the areas Bravo just cleared during Hold the
Line; it carries the squad farther toward the missing Gears.

Once Charlie comes into view, continue through the encounter rather than trying
to retreat to the tunnel entrance.


<h3
  id="leave-after-charlie"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Finish the Charlie Squad Sequence Before Following the Next Order
</h3>

The return-to-base order still exists in the background, but Bravo does not leave
until the situation with Charlie has played out.

Afterward, follow the newly opened route forward. The campaign shifts away from
downtown ground operations and toward air transport.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-flyboys"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Flyboys — Defend the Raven
</h2>

Bravo boards a Raven with the flight crew, but the trip becomes a defensive
sequence once enemy fire starts hitting the aircraft.

When the crew calls Bravo forward, take control of the mounted guns.


<h3
  id="raven-guns"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Shoot the Positions That Are Actually Hitting the Raven
</h3>

There are more targets visible below than Bravo needs to destroy. Focus on the
positions currently firing on the Raven rather than sweeping every enemy you can
see.

As new firing positions become active, change targets with them. Continue
protecting the aircraft until the Raven reaches the airfield.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-overrun"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Overrun — Escape the Collapsing Airfield
</h2>

Bravo arrives expecting to reinforce the airfield, but the Locust tear through
the runway and make the tarmac impossible to hold.

The important change comes when command orders the retreat. From that point on,
do not spend time trying to recover ground that the squad has already been told
to abandon.


<h3
  id="leave-tarmac"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Leave the Runway and Regroup at the Hangar
</h3>

Move away from the collapsing tarmac and head for the **hangar**.

  <Image

    toward the hangar instead of trying to retake the tarmac.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Clear enemies that directly block Bravo's escape, but keep following the fallback
route. The goal is movement, not reclaiming each section of the airfield behind
you.


<h3
  id="west-control-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Fall Back to the West Control Tower
</h3>

The rally point changes again as more of the airfield comes apart. Ravens are
supposed to extract the remaining force from the **west control tower**, so follow
whatever route through the damaged structures remains open.

The retreat moves through four clear stages:

- Leave the **runway**.
- Regroup at the **hangar**.
- Follow the surviving **interior fallback route**.
- Push toward the **west control tower**.

If another collapse blocks the path you were following, use the new opening
created by the destruction instead of trying to return toward the runway.


<h2
  id="before-act-3"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Does Bravo Go After the Airfield Falls?
</h2>

The airfield collapse leaves Bravo separated from the main COG force. The next
attempt to regroup begins after the Raven crash west of Kalona, where the squad
has to find another route back toward friendly lines.
