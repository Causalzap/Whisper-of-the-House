  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 5 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Take the train facing **back toward Kalona**, push through Ghost Town, clear
    the Raven's Nest landing zone, then flood the dry dock by opening **three
    valves**. When the third valve jams, use the access hatch and manual release
    to finish flooding the dock. Once Resolute floats, return to the ship and
    fight toward the **east bridge control room**. When those controls briefly
    work and then fail again, the final operation begins.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>

Act 5 begins with Bravo already being carried away from Kalona. Getting back to
the city is only the first problem: Ghost Town cuts off the safer streets,
Resolute is trapped in a dry dock, and the bridge across the evacuation route
still has to be raised after the ship is finally afloat.

The five chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: One Problem at a Time**
- **Chapter 2: Ghost Town**
- **Chapter 3: Storm Clouds**
- **Chapter 4: Resolute**
- **Chapter 5: The Fall of Kalona**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-one-problem-at-a-time"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: One Problem at a Time — Find a Train Back to Kalona
</h2>

Act 5 opens on an automated train moving away from the city while Reavers attack
from outside. Stay with the train until its damaged fuel system forces Bravo to
abandon it.

When the engine catches fire and the squad is told to jump, move onto the
**adjacent train**. The original train is already becoming unusable, so there is
no reason to stay behind trying to finish every attacker.


<h3
  id="push-to-front"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Push to the Front of the Second Train
</h3>

The replacement train gets Bravo out of immediate danger, but it is still moving
farther from Kalona.

Fight forward through the cars until you reach the front. The train eventually
slows near a transfer point where the squad can look for another line running in
the opposite direction.


<h3
  id="which-train-goes-to-kalona"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Which Train Goes Back to Kalona?
</h3>

At the transfer point, use the train **facing the direction you came from**.
Bravo confirms the choice once the platform is secure: the line pointing back
toward the city is the one you need.

Clear the Locust around the platform, then move into the control area at the
front of the train.


<h3
  id="start-return-train"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Lever on the Control Panel to Start the Train
</h3>

There is no second route-selection puzzle inside the cab. Use the **lever on the
control panel** to start the train back toward Kalona.

Stay aboard through the next attack. The chapter continues until the rail route
fails again and Bravo has to continue on foot.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-ghost-town"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Ghost Town — Get Through the Ruined City
</h2>

Ghost Town starts below street level. Find the route upward and keep moving once
Bravo reaches the surface. Long open streets are exposed to sniper and mortar
fire, while several damaged buildings become the safer path through the district.


<h3
  id="hospital-route"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Hospital and Stay Off the Open Street
</h3>

The **hospital** is one of the clearest landmarks on the way through Ghost Town.
Move through it rather than staying exposed on the street.

When mortar fire begins landing around Bravo, continue beneath the tracks using
the covered route instead of remaining in the open.


<h3
  id="apartments-elevator-shaft"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go Through the Apartments When the Fountain Route Dead-Ends
</h3>

Later, the route near the fountain stops completely. Do not keep circling the
street looking for another opening.

Go back into the **apartments**, then use the **elevator-shaft cables** to climb
higher. Once Bravo starts moving upward, keep following the building route toward
the roof instead of returning to street level.


<h3
  id="hold-rooftop"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Hold the Rooftop Until the Raven Arrives
</h3>

The rooftop is not another navigation puzzle. There is no hidden exit to find
after Bravo reaches the top.

Hold the position until the Raven arrives, then board as soon as the extraction
route opens.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-storm-clouds"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: Storm Clouds — Reach the Raven's Nest
</h2>

The evacuation still cannot leave Kalona because the bridge remains down. While
another COG force moves toward that problem, Bravo heads for the **Raven's Nest**
to support the next stage of the evacuation.

The approach happens in the air, with Reavers following the Raven through heavy
weather. Stay on the mounted weapon during the flight, then shift your attention
to the landing zone as the aircraft descends toward the Raven's Nest.


<h3
  id="clear-ravens-nest"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Raven's Nest Landing Zone
</h3>

Locust have occupied the cranes and the area around the landing zone.

During the Raven's attack pass, concentrate on enemies close to the LZ rather
than targets farther away from the pad. Once the immediate landing area is clear
enough, the Raven touches down and Bravo can move into the operation around
**Resolute**.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-resolute"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Resolute — Flood the Dock and Float the Ship
</h2>

Resolute cannot leave because the Locust destroyed the main dry-dock flood
controls. Bravo has to raise the water level manually until the ship can float.

Head downstairs to the **flood-control room**. The main console is too badly
damaged to restore, so the crew switches to the mechanical backup near the
floodgate.


<h3
  id="three-flood-valves"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Open All Three Flood Valves
</h3>

The backup system uses **three valves**. The floodgate safety system will not
allow the ship out while the water remains too low, so the valves have to be
opened first.

Move past the crane toward the floodgate and use the mechanical backup. Defend
the chamber while the first valve opens, then repeat the process at the second.
The objective updates to show **two open, one remaining** before the third
mechanism fails.


<h3
  id="third-valve-jammed"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do When the Third Flood Valve Jams?
</h3>

The third valve fails differently. Its chain system breaks and leaves the valve
clamped shut.

Do not return to the first two valve controls. A facility map points Bravo toward
an **access hatch on the far side of the floodgate**, which becomes the route
around the broken mechanism.

  </figcaption>
</figure>

Climb onto the upper structure and follow the top of the gate toward the access
point while the rest of Bravo keeps the pressure away from your route.


<h3
  id="manual-release"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Manual Release Below the Spillway
</h3>

The failed third valve forces the flooding sequence onto a manual backup. Continue
through the access route until you reach the release **below the spillway**.

Operate the remaining mechanism there. Once it starts working, the dock begins
filling while Locust attack the area around Resolute.


<h3
  id="protect-resolute"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Protect Resolute Until the Ship Floats
</h3>

Do not stop defending when water first starts entering the dock. Resolute still
needs more depth before it can float.

Focus on the enemies attacking the braces around the ship while the water level
continues rising. Once the dock is full enough, Resolute levels out and begins
floating, which completes the flood-control sequence.


<h3
  id="get-back-on-ship"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Return to Resolute Once It Floats
</h3>

Work back toward the ship after it stabilizes. The normal boarding route is no
longer available from one side, so use the upper route and crane access to get
back aboard.

The crew begins launch preparation once Bravo returns. Resolute can now leave the
dry dock, but the bridge across its escape route is still down.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-the-fall-of-kalona"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: The Fall of Kalona — Raise the Bridge for Resolute
</h2>

Bravo is dropped as close to the bridge as possible while Resolute waits exposed
behind it. The immediate target is the **east control room**.

Heavy damage blocks the straightforward upper approach, so move onto the lower
deck and continue toward the controls from there.


<h3
  id="east-control-room"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the East Control Room From the Lower Deck
</h3>

Drop onto the lower deck and follow the damaged bridge toward the east control
room. The room is still accessible despite the destruction around it.

Clear the enemies immediately outside, then use the controls.

The damaged controls still respond at first. The bridge begins moving, but the
mechanism fails again before Resolute can pass.

That failure starts the final operation. Continue from the broken east controls with the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/ending-final-boss">final operation and ending</Link>.
