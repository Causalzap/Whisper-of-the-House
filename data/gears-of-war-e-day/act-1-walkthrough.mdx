  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 1 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Reach **Police HQ**, find the armory and take a **Lancer**, then send the
    rescued civilians back to the station. Use the **truck beside the blocked
    underpass** to reach the recruitment center, cross the damaged apartments to
    the **Pay N Save**, and meet Tai and Mayor Shaw. Escort Shaw to **City Hall**,
    seal the Emergence Holes with grenades, then hold the front until she can
    enter the building.

Act 1 covers the first hours of Emergence Day, before Marcus and Dom are folded
back into an organized COG unit. The route moves from Police HQ into a series of
civilian rescues across Kalona before ending with Mayor Shaw's push to City Hall.

The four chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: Emergence**
- **Chapter 2: Survival of the Fittest**
- **Chapter 3: A Gear in Need**
- **Chapter 4: Back in the Fold**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-emergence"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Emergence — Reach Police HQ
</h2>

Stay with Dom once the attack begins and follow the damaged streets toward the
police station.

The opening route is mostly linear, but several collapsed structures cut off the
obvious street path. When the road ahead ends, use the opening through the
damaged building and continue downward rather than trying to circle back to an
earlier street.

Once you return outside, keep moving toward **Police HQ**.


<h3
  id="police-hq-entrance"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Police HQ Entrance
</h3>

Police HQ is already under attack when Marcus and Dom arrive. Clear the Locust
around the entrance before moving inside.

Entering the station starts **Survival of the Fittest**.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-survival-of-the-fittest"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Survival of the Fittest — Find the Armory
</h2>

Inside Police HQ, move deeper into the station toward the **armory**. The route
continues forward through the building rather than back toward the entrance, even
after Marcus hears a surviving voice nearby.

A stronger enemy blocks the path before the weapons room. Deal with it, then
enter the armory.

Take a **Lancer**, resupply, and clear the remaining Locust inside Police HQ.


<h3
  id="make-police-hq-safe"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Send Rescued Survivors Back to Police HQ
</h3>

Once the station is secure, Marcus begins using Police HQ as a rally point for
civilians. Survivors only need their immediate route cleared before they can be
directed back toward the station.

The first group is trapped inside a **bus**. Clear the enemies around it, open the
bus, and send the civilians to Police HQ.

One of the survivors reports another group under attack at a nearby
**recruitment center**.


<h3
  id="reach-recruitment-center"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Reach the Recruitment Center?
</h3>

The recruitment center is about **one block away**, on the **left side of the
plaza**, but the direct underpass is blocked.

There is no switch or alternate tunnel entrance. Use the nearby **truck** to get
across the obstruction.

After crossing, continue through the plaza until Marcus reaches the trapped
survivors. Clearing this area leads directly into the next distress call.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-a-gear-in-need"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: A Gear in Need — Reach the Pay N Save
</h2>

A police scanner picks up a Gear calling for backup from the **Pay N Save**.

The store is on the far side of an apartment block. The surrounding streets have
been torn apart, so do not search for a clean road around the damage.

Move **through the damaged apartments** and use the broken structures as the
route to the other side of the block. The Pay N Save comes into view once Bravo
reaches the correct side.


<h3
  id="clear-pay-n-save"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Rescue the Gear Inside Pay N Save
</h3>

Push into the store and clear the Locust attacking the trapped Gear.

Stay inside until the immediate attack is finished rather than leaving after the
first group falls. More enemies enter the area before the rescue is secure.

Once the fighting stops, the rescued Gear explains that his squad was wiped out
and points Marcus toward more survivors nearby.


<h3
  id="meet-tai"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Meet Tai and Find Mayor Shaw
</h3>

The route eventually reconnects Marcus with **Tai Kaliso**, who has been
protecting survivors from the mayor's escort.

Follow Tai to the group sheltering behind the nearby shutters. Inside, Marcus
meets **Mayor Shaw**.

Shaw refuses to remain behind at Police HQ. She wants to reach **City Hall** so
the city can continue organizing medical support and emergency shelter.

That becomes the main objective for the rest of Act 1.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-back-in-the-fold"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: Back in the Fold — Escort Mayor Shaw to City Hall
</h2>

Resupply before leaving the shelter. Marcus and the combat group move ahead while
Tai stays close to Mayor Shaw.

The escort only advances after the next stretch of street is safe. Move ahead,
clear the immediate fighting, then allow Shaw's group to catch up before pushing
toward the next block.


<h3
  id="seal-emergence-holes"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Close the Emergence Holes?
</h3>

Near City Hall, fresh **Emergence Holes** begin opening in the street. Locust
continue climbing out for as long as each opening remains active.

Do not try to solve this by killing every enemy as it appears. Throw a
**grenade directly into the hole**.

A successful blast collapses the opening. Save at least one grenade for this
section if possible.


<h3
  id="reach-city-hall"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach City Hall After Closing the Holes
</h3>

City Hall is only about **one block beyond the Emergence Holes**.

Follow the sound of the COG fighting toward the defenders outside the building
rather than searching the surrounding streets for another entrance.

Help the Gears clear the attackers at the front, then use the brief pause to
resupply before the next assault.


<h3
  id="grenade-launcher"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Does the Grenade Launcher Work?
</h3>

A **grenade launcher** becomes available during the City Hall defense.

Its firing timing is different from the standard weapons:

**Hold the trigger, then release it when you want the grenade to detonate.**

Avoid releasing it too close to Marcus's own cover. It is most useful when
several enemies are pushing the same approach.


<h3
  id="defend-city-hall"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Defend City Hall Until Mayor Shaw Can Enter
</h3>

Hold the front of City Hall through the final attack. Keep enemies from breaking
through the barricade until the assault loses momentum and Shaw's group can move
inside safely.

Once Mayor Shaw enters City Hall, Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes are brought back
under COG command. Their improvised civilian rescue route is over, and Bravo
Squad is formed for the next operation.

  Where Does Bravo Go After City Hall?

The next assignment sends Bravo from the stadium FOB toward **Legacy Bridge**,
where Charlie Squad is already under pressure and the central bridge span has to
be restored.
