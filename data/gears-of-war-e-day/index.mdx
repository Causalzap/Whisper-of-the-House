Gears of War: E-Day follows Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago through the Locust
attack on **Kalona**, beginning with improvised survival and ending with the fight
to get Resolute out of the city.

The Campaign contains a **Prologue, five Acts, 26 numbered chapters, and an
Epilogue**. Early objectives are fairly direct, but later Acts combine navigation,
large combat encounters, optional areas, transport changes, and objectives that
continue after you appear to have reached the destination.

That last point causes many of the real progression stalls. Legacy Bridge still
has another control route after the first console jams. Prospect Bay still needs
power before its communications equipment can work. Resolute still needs the dry
dock prepared before it can leave.

Use the section matching what is actually stopping you rather than replaying an
entire Act looking for one missed interaction.

## Act 1 — Police HQ, Survivor Rescues, and City Hall

Act 1 stays close to the first hours of Emergence Day. Marcus and Dom move through
**Police HQ**, survivor locations around the city, Pay N Save, Mayor Shaw's group,
and eventually **City Hall**.

Go to the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-1-walkthrough">Act 1 walkthrough</Link>
if you are stuck finding the Police HQ route, reaching a rescue location, closing
Emergence Holes, or finishing the City Hall sequence.

</section>

## Act 2 — Legacy Bridge, Bravo Squad, and the Airfield

Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes now operate as **Bravo Squad**. Their assignments
run from Legacy Bridge through downtown COG positions, Charlie Squad's last known
location, a Raven mission, and the collapsing airfield.

The bridge is an early example of an objective changing underneath you: the first
controls move the span, then jam, forcing Bravo toward the eastern controls
instead of asking for another interaction in the same room.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough">Act 2 walkthrough</Link>
for Legacy Bridge, Hold the Line, Charlie Squad, the Raven sequence, or the
airfield retreat.

</section>

## Act 3 — Fairlight Outpost, Enemy Lines, and Fort Vigil

After the Raven crash, Bravo is cut off from the main COG force. The squad tries
to use **Fairlight Outpost** as an extraction point, falls into the Locust tunnel
network, reaches Cooper Station, returns to the stadium, and joins the evacuation
toward **Fort Vigil**.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-3-walkthrough">Act 3 walkthrough</Link>
for the Fairlight breaker, Cooper Station exit, convoy bridge, rooftop evacuation,
or Fort Vigil defense.

The Corpser appears during Enemy Lines. If the route is clear and the fight itself
is the problem, use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses/#corpser">Corpser strategy</Link>
instead.

## Act 4 — Prospect Bay, the Power Grid, and the Refinery

Act 4 repeatedly breaks Bravo's planned route. The first train is not the one you
need, the Prospect Bay grid has to be restored before communications work, the
road stops before the weather station, and the refinery forces the squad through
damaged industrial routes before the converter can be shut down.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-4-walkthrough">Act 4 walkthrough</Link>
for the second train, **85% power-station breaker**, weather-station trail, backup
coolant feed, Zone 5 key card, enriched emulsion controls, or final tank dump.

If you have reached the converter and the two Scoria are what stop your progress,
go directly to the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses/#scoria">Scoria fight</Link>.

## Act 5 — Ghost Town, Resolute, and the East Bridge

Bravo begins Act 5 moving away from Kalona and has to reverse course before the
evacuation can continue. The return runs through **Ghost Town**, Raven's Nest, the
Resolute dry dock, flood controls, and finally the bridge blocking the ship's
escape.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 walkthrough</Link>
for the return train, Ghost Town route, three flood valves, jammed third valve,
manual release, Resolute launch, or east bridge controls.

Once the east bridge controls fail and Bravo leaves the console, the Campaign has
entered its final operation.

## Final Operation — Vraahk, the Skytrain, and the Ending

The failed bridge controls lead directly into the last operation. Bravo crosses
the damaged deck, encounters **Vraahk the Impaler**, works toward the Skytrain,
reaches the detonator, and fights through the final confrontation before the
Epilogue.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/ending-final-boss">final boss and ending guide</Link>

sequence and ending spoilers.

## If a Boss Fight Is Stopping You

The Campaign's major encounters include Brumaks, Corpsers, the Weaponized Brumak,
Scoria, and Vraahk. Several of them depend on more than simply staying in cover
and dealing damage; the arena or the boss's current state changes what you should
do next.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses">Gears of War: E-Day boss guide</Link>
when you have already reached the arena and need the fight mechanics rather than
the surrounding Campaign route.

## Which Optional Weapon Upgrades Are Worth Taking?

You do not need every optional weapon upgrade to finish the Campaign.

The strongest detours are usually the ones that improve a weapon you already
carry. Act 2 contains several useful early rewards, while Act 4 adds upgrades for
weapons such as the Torque Bow, Incinerator, Hammerburst, and Gut Puncher.

If you are deciding whether to leave the main route for an objective, check the
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/weapon-mods">weapon mod locations and priorities</Link>
before crossing the district for an upgrade you may not use.

</section>

## What Should You Check Before Leaving an Area?

You can follow the story without clearing every optional marker, but some
transitions make returning inconvenient.

When Bravo is about to board a **train, Raven, convoy, or other transport that
moves the squad into a new area**, check whether a nearby Hard Case, Secondary
Objective, Collectible, or Supply Cache is still worth a short detour.

There is little reason to turn a first playthrough into a full sweep of every
street. Pick up optional content that is close to the route you are already
taking, especially when it also provides equipment you intend to use, then keep
the Campaign moving.

</section>

## If You Want Full Campaign Completion

Campaign completion extends beyond reaching the Epilogue.

You may still need:

- **Secondary Objectives**
- **Hard Cases**
- **Collectibles**
- **Supply Cache unlocks**
- Every Act in **Co-Op**
- Campaign completion on **Insane**

Steam has **55 achievements**, while Xbox has **54**. Horde Siege, Versus,
account progression, revives, and specific combat interactions make up much of
the remaining work outside the normal story run.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/achievements">achievement tracker</Link>
to see which requirements are still unfinished and which ones can progress
together.

</section>
