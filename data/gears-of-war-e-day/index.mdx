Gears of War: E-Day follows Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago through the first
Locust assault on **Kalona**. The Campaign begins with street-level survival,
then expands into COG operations, bridge crossings, the Locust tunnel network,
civilian evacuation, damaged city infrastructure, and the final attempt to get
Resolute out of Kalona.

The story has a **Prologue, five Acts, 26 numbered chapters, and an Epilogue**.
The opening route is fairly direct, but later areas give Bravo more room to
explore while the main objectives become more complicated.

You do not need to turn a first playthrough into a completion sweep. Follow the
main route, but check nearby optional content before obvious transitions such as
trains, Ravens, convoys, major bridge crossings, or other sequences that move
the squad into a new part of the Campaign.

## Act 2 — Legacy Bridge, Bravo Squad, and the Airfield

Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes become **Bravo Squad** in Act 2. Their first
formal operations take them through **Legacy Bridge**, Octus Way, All Father's
Plaza, the search for Charlie Squad, a Raven flight, and the collapsing
airfield.

Use <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough">Act 2</Link> if the first Legacy Bridge controls jam, Charlie cannot advance, Hold the Line keeps changing your destination, or you are unsure where to retreat after the runway collapses.

Act 3 begins with Bravo separated from the main COG force. The squad reaches

The final operation starts after the east bridge controls briefly work and then

## What Should You Check Before Leaving an Area?

Bravo can often step away from the direct route for **Hard Cases, Secondary
Objectives, Collectibles, and Supply Caches** while the current district is
still open.

The useful question is not whether you have cleared every corner. It is whether
the optional content is still close to where Bravo already needs to go.

A short detour is usually worth taking during a first playthrough. A long return
trip through an area the squad has already left is better saved for later.

Major transitions are the easiest warning that the current area may be ending.
If Bravo is about to board transport or enter another operation that clearly
moves the squad elsewhere, check nearby side paths first.

</section>

## How Do You Find Hard Cases in the Campaign?

**Hard Cases** are optional yellow containers placed away from the main Campaign
route. When one is nearby, a notification appears on the left side of the
screen.

Open **Tac-Com** while the notification is active to help locate the Hard Case
before leaving the area. On an Xbox controller, Tac-Com is opened with **LB**.

Hard Cases also contribute to Campaign progression rather than existing only as
collectibles. Along with Secondary Objectives, they can unlock equipment mods.

Finding every Hard Case completes **Relax. I'm the Map.** The Campaign
completion requirements are tracked in the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/achievements">achievement guide</Link>.

</section>

## What Do Secondary Objectives Unlock?

Secondary Objectives are worth doing when they appear close to the current
Campaign route because they contribute to both completion and equipment
progression.

Completing every Secondary Objective unlocks **Side Piece**, while the optional
missions can also provide mod unlocks that affect Bravo's equipment.

That makes a nearby Secondary Objective more useful than a simple checklist
item. Completing it while Bravo is already in the same district can improve the
rest of the run and reduce the amount of cleanup left after the story.

## How Do You Find Collectibles in the Campaign?

Finding every Collectible unlocks **Memories From E-Day** on any difficulty.

A sound cue plays when a Collectible is nearby. Some fights also open side rooms,
upper floors, or other spaces that were inaccessible earlier, so the end of an
encounter is a good time to look around before following the next objective.

Missing a Collectible does not stop Campaign progression. If you are playing
mainly for the story, keep moving. If you are working toward full completion,
the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/achievements">achievement tracker</Link> keeps the Collectible requirement alongside the other Campaign goals.

</section>

## What Do Supply Caches Unlock?

Supply Caches begin as convenient places to refill ammunition and equipment, but
their role expands as the Campaign progresses.

The **Quartermaster** achievement requires every Supply Cache item in the
Campaign, so do not treat the first cache interaction as the end of the system.
Check later caches when new items become available as Bravo advances.

In Act 2, the relationship between resupply and equipment progression becomes
clearer: Bravo can receive a weapon modification from the quartermaster before
deploying downtown, while a Supply Cache is available at the same COG staging
area.

</section>

## Where Do Weapon and Equipment Mods Come From?

Campaign equipment progression does not come from one source.

Bravo receives a weapon modification from the quartermaster during Act 2, while
**Hard Cases** and **Secondary Objectives** can also provide mod unlocks for the
equipment used throughout the Campaign.

That makes optional exploration more valuable after the opening hours. You still
do not need to search every corner, but a Hard Case or Secondary Objective close
to the current route can improve more than a completion counter.

Take upgrades that fit naturally into the route, then keep the Campaign moving
instead of forcing a long backtrack for every missed item.

</section>

## Do You Need Co-Op or Insane for a First Playthrough?

No. Finishing every Act on any difficulty is enough for the ordinary Campaign
completion achievement, **Remember Kalona**.

Co-Op and Insane matter when you start working toward the broader completion
requirements. **You're My Brother** requires all Acts to be completed in Co-Op
on any difficulty, while **Today We Are Canceling the Apocalypse** requires
every Act on Insane.

Other completion goals can also overlap with an Insane run, so there is no need
to force every requirement into your first story playthrough.

The <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/achievements">achievement guide</Link> separates those requirements from the normal Campaign run.

</section>
