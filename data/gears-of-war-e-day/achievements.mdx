Gears of War: E-Day currently has **55 achievements on Steam**. Xbox has
**54 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore**; the Steam list also includes
**The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day**, the 0-point completion achievement.

Many requirements overlap. One Campaign run can advance story completion,
Secondary Objectives, Hard Cases, Collectibles, and Supply Cache unlocks at the
same time, while Horde Siege lets you combine missions, side objectives, revives,
class levels, Energy spending, kills, and Weapon Mod progress.

For a full completion run, this order avoids repeating the same work more than
necessary:

1. Finish the Campaign while exploring optional areas.
2. Clean up Secondary Objectives, Hard Cases, Collectibles, and Supply Cache items.
3. Complete every Act in Co-Op.
4. Finish the Campaign requirements tied to Insane.
5. Stack Horde Siege missions and cumulative counters together.
6. Work through Versus while the 25,000-kill counter is still progressing.
7. Return for any combat-specific achievements that remain.

## Campaign Achievements That Unlock Through Story Progress

The regular story achievements unlock as you move through the five Campaign
Acts. They do not require a separate hidden challenge beyond reaching the
required story progress.

**Act 1**
- **Emergence Begins**
- **We Must Improvise**
- **The Bigger They Are…**

**Act 2**
- **The Scorched Legion**
- **You Want What On the Front of a Gun?!**
- **Got to the Choppa**

**Act 3**
- **Get Back You Eight-legged Freak**
- **Twice the Pride, Double the Fall**
- **…The Harder They Fall**

**Act 4**
- **End of the Line**
- **The Only Good Bug is a Dead Bug**

**Act 5**
- **Nothing Left but the Fight**
- **For My Boys**

Finishing every Act on any difficulty also unlocks **Remember Kalona**.

There is no need to force the first playthrough onto Insane just to secure these
ordinary story achievements. If a Campaign objective is holding up progress, use
the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/walkthrough">complete Campaign walkthrough</Link>
to jump to the correct Act and chapter.

## Campaign Achievements Worth Planning From the Start

Four Campaign achievements can create substantial cleanup if their optional
content is ignored until the story is already finished:

- **Side Piece** — complete all Secondary Objectives
- **Relax. I’m the Map.** — discover all Hard Cases
- **Memories From E-Day** — find all Collectibles
- **Quartermaster** — unlock all Supply Cache items

These requirements overlap with normal exploration, so optional areas are most
useful when they sit close to the route you are already following.


### Take Nearby Hard Cases Before a One-Way Transition

Hard Cases count toward **Relax. I’m the Map.**, but they can also contain
equipment progression that helps during the Campaign.

If the Hard Case warning appears while you are still free to explore the current
district, take the short detour. Once the Campaign has committed Bravo to a
train, Raven, convoy, bridge crossing, or similar transition, returning to the
previous area may no longer be practical.


### Complete Secondary Objectives While You Are Already in the District

Secondary Objectives count toward **Side Piece** and can also contribute to
equipment progression.

If an optional objective is close to the main route, completing it immediately
usually saves a second trip later. A distant objective that requires reversing a
large section of the map can be left for cleanup if you are prioritizing story
progress.


### Quartermaster Tracks Supply Cache Unlocks

Campaign Supply Caches eventually provide more than ammunition. Armor mods can
come from **Hard Cases** or **Secondary Objectives**, while **Quartermaster**
requires all Campaign Supply Cache items to be unlocked.

as additional rewards become available during the Campaign.

## You’re My Brother — Complete Every Act in Co-Op

**You’re My Brother** requires every Campaign Act to be completed in **Co-Op on
any difficulty**.

Mixing a handful of co-op chapters into an otherwise solo playthrough does not
cover the full requirement. Track completion by Act so you know exactly which
parts still need another run.

Because the difficulty is unrestricted, this does not need to be combined with
Insane unless you specifically want to attempt both goals together.

## Insane Campaign Achievements

Two major completion goals require Insane difficulty.

**Today We Are Canceling the Apocalypse** requires every Campaign Act to be
completed on **Insane**.

**For Whom The Bell Tolls** adds further Campaign completion requirements:

- complete the Campaign on Insane
- complete all side missions
- find all hidden items

Knowing the routes before beginning the Insane run saves repeated navigation
mistakes during the harder encounters. The five Act routes are:

- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-1-walkthrough">Act 1 walkthrough</Link>
- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough">Act 2 walkthrough</Link>

- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-4-walkthrough">Act 4 walkthrough</Link>
- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 walkthrough</Link>

On Insane, there is little value in extending a fight after progression has
already opened. Once the objective changes and the route forward is safe, move on
with enough ammunition for the next forced encounter.

## Stack the Horde Siege Achievements Together

Horde Siege contains several of the longest cumulative requirements, so most of
them should progress during the same runs.

**Lift Outta Hell** requires **50 completed and survived Horde Siege Missions on
Infantry difficulty or higher**. While working toward those 50 missions, you can
also advance:

- **100 Secondary Objectives**
- **50 Sonic Disruptor executions**
- **1,000 Pulse Drone marks**
- **20 revives of players from other squads**
- **250 Stim Vaporizer DBNO revives**
- **150,000 Energy spent**
- **25 Invasion Events**
- Horde kill totals
- class levels
- Weapon Mod unlocks

Check these counters throughout the 50-mission run rather than waiting until the
end. Anything left at zero or near zero after mission 50 becomes a separate grind.


### Never Fight Alone — Keep Everyone Alive for Extraction

**Never Fight Alone** requires an **Allfather extraction with all squadmates
alive**.

Once extraction is available, the survival condition matters more than taking an
unnecessary final fight. A death immediately before extraction can break the
requirement even after the rest of the mission has gone well.


### I’m All Amped Up — Hold Adrenaline for 30 Seconds

**I’m All Amped Up** requires **30 continuous seconds of Adrenaline** while using
the Injector.

Choose a fight long enough to keep Adrenaline active for the full duration.
Triggering it near the end of an almost-finished wave is less useful because the
combat may end before the timer is satisfied.


### Stacks, On Stacks, On Stacks — Spend 150,000 Energy

This achievement tracks **Energy spent at Supply Caches**, not Energy held.

Spend during normal Horde progression instead of stockpiling everything. The
150,000 total can rise alongside the other mission and class goals.


### Siege Specialist — Max Four Classes

The required classes are:

- **Assault**
- **Breacher**
- **Marksman**
- **Medic**

Once one class reaches its required maximum, switch to another unfinished class
while your mission, kill, Energy, and objective counters are still moving.

</section>

## Complete the Situational Versus Achievements Before 25,000 Kills

Four achievements are tied directly to Versus.

**Somebody Set Us Up the Bomb** requires you to plant the bomb in **Demolition**
and have it explode without the enemy defusing it.

**It Only Game, Why You Heff to Be Mad?** requires a **Conquest win with the enemy
team scoring zero**.

**Days Since Last Accident: 0** requires the specific workplace-safety interaction
on **Battleship**.

The long grind is **HEY NOW, YOU’RE AN ALL STAR…**, which requires **25,000 Versus
kills**.

Work on the three situational achievements while the 25,000-kill counter is still
moving. Leaving them until after the kill grind only creates extra matches.

</section>

## Field Service and Got Your Wings

These two achievements follow account progression rather than one specific mode.

**Field Service** requires you to:

**Reach Player Level 100 and Re-Up for the first time.**

**Got Your Wings** requires you to:

**Re-Up for the final time and claim your wings.**

Field Service comes much earlier. Horde Siege and Versus progression will also
advance your account level, so let those longer requirements do as much of the
leveling work as possible before starting a separate XP grind.

</section>

## Combat Achievements to Clean Up Separately

Several achievements depend on one specific weapon, enemy, or interaction and may
not happen during normal play.


### I Am Heavy Weapons Guy

Get **10 kills in a row using a single detached turret**.

Stay on the same detached turret until all ten kills are complete. Switching to a
different turret before the streak finishes can break the attempt.


### Brotector

Save another player from a struggle with a Locust.

This is different from an ordinary DBNO revive. Wait for the struggle state, then
interrupt the Locust before the other player is lost.


### Okay, Boomer.

**Carve-up a Boomer.**

An ordinary Boomer kill does not satisfy this requirement; the achievement calls
for the specific execution-style action.


### Right in the Ticker

This requirement involves converting a **Ticker** and sending it back.

Treat it as a Ticker-specific interaction rather than a normal explosive kill.


### Chainsaw Go Brrrrrrrrr

Use a chainsaw against a Locust in **Campaign or Horde Siege**.

Because either mode counts, this is easy to leave until a convenient target
appears instead of creating a separate run.


### Explosive Indigestion

This achievement is tied specifically to a **Bilefrog** interaction.

Explosive kills against unrelated enemies do not substitute for the required
Bilefrog action.


### Two Grubs, One Gut-Punch

Hit **two Grubs with one Gut-Puncher round**.

Wait until two enemies line up before firing instead of spending ammunition on
isolated targets.


### Payback’s A Pitch

Let an **Armored Wretch stun you**, then kill that enemy afterward.

If the Wretch dies before the stun occurs, the revenge condition has not been
created.

</section>

## The Three Revive Counters Track Different Actions

Do not treat every revive as progress toward every revive achievement.

- **Just Let Me Get One More…** — get **100 Revives**
- **Who Loves Ya, Baby?** — revive **20 players from other squads** in Horde Siege
- **Down, but Not Dead** — revive **250 DBNO squadmates with the Stim Vaporizer**

A normal squad revive can add to the 100-revive total without advancing the Stim
Vaporizer counter. Likewise, reviving your own squadmate does not satisfy a
requirement that specifically asks for players from **other squads**.

Keep the three totals separate in the tracker.

</section>

## The Last Completion Achievements

Two achievements sit at the end of the longer completion path.


### The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day

On Steam, **The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day** requires:

**Earn all other Achievements.**

It is worth **0 points** and unlocks only after the rest of the Steam achievement
list is complete. This achievement is not part of the current Xbox 54-achievement
list.


### Dead Serious

**Dead Serious** is worth **85 Gamerscore** and combines several long-term goals:

- 100% the Campaign on Insane
- 25,000 Versus kills
- max Player Level
- max the required four Horde Siege classes

There is no separate activity to grind specifically for Dead Serious. Campaign
completion, Versus kills, Horde class levels, and account progression should
already be moving while you finish their individual requirements.
