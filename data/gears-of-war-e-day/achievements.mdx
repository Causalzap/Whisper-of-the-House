Gears of War: E-Day has **55 achievements on Steam**. Xbox has **54 achievements
worth 1,000 Gamerscore** because the Steam list includes the additional
0-point achievement **The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day**.

The longest requirements overlap heavily. Campaign exploration can advance four
different completion goals at once, while a Horde Siege run can contribute to
missions survived, side objectives, revives, class levels, Energy spending,
kills, and Weapon Mod unlocks.

A low-cleanup order is:

1. Finish the Campaign while taking useful optional objectives along the route.
2. Clean up missing Secondary Objectives, Hard Cases, Collectibles, and Supply
   Cache items.
3. Complete every Campaign Act in Co-Op.
4. Finish the Campaign requirements tied to Insane.
5. Use the 50 Horde Siege missions to build the cumulative Horde counters.
6. Work through the Versus-specific requirements while the 25,000-kill total is
   still increasing.
7. Finish any weapon, enemy, revive, or interaction achievement still missing.

## Which Achievements Unlock Through the Campaign Story?

The normal story achievements unlock as you complete the five Acts. They do not
require an additional hidden condition beyond reaching the relevant Campaign
progress.

**Act 1**

- **Emergence Begins**
- **We Must Improvise**
- **The Bigger They Are…**

**Act 2**

- **The Scorched Legion**
- **You Want What On the Front of a Gun?!**
- **Got to the Choppa**

**Act 3**

- **Get Back You Eight-legged Freak**
- **Twice the Pride, Double the Fall**
- **…The Harder They Fall**

**Act 4**

- **End of the Line**
- **The Only Good Bug is a Dead Bug**

**Act 5**

- **Nothing Left but the Fight**
- **For My Boys**

Completing every Act on any difficulty also unlocks **Remember Kalona**.

You do not need to start on Insane simply to earn these story achievements. If
you lose track of an objective or chapter route, the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/walkthrough">complete Campaign walkthrough</Link> covers the five Acts and the final operation.

## Which Campaign Achievements Can Cause the Most Cleanup?

Four achievements reward completion beyond simply reaching the ending:

- **Side Piece** — complete all Secondary Objectives
- **Relax. I’m the Map.** — discover all Hard Cases
- **Memories From E-Day** — find all Collectibles
- **Quartermaster** — unlock all Supply Cache items

These are easiest to manage during the Campaign because several optional areas
sit close to routes you already have to travel.


### Take Hard Cases Before Leaving a District

Hard Cases count toward **Relax. I’m the Map.** and can also provide equipment
progression.

The most awkward time to remember a nearby Hard Case is after Bravo has already
boarded a train, Raven, convoy, or another one-way transition. If the warning
appears while the surrounding streets are still open, taking the detour then can
save a later replay through the area.


### Fit Secondary Objectives Into the Route You Are Already Taking

**Side Piece** requires all Secondary Objectives.

Some optional assignments also reward equipment you can use for the rest of the
Campaign, so completing one while Bravo is already in the same district can serve
both purposes.

Act 2 and Act 4 contain several weapon upgrades tied to optional activities. The
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/weapon-mods">weapon mod locations</Link> show
which rewards are worth the detour if you are choosing between several nearby
objectives.


### Quartermaster Requires the Full Supply Cache Unlock Set

**Quartermaster** is about unlocking all Campaign Supply Cache items, not simply
opening one cache.

**Quartermaster** does not finish until the complete Supply Cache unlock set has
been obtained.

## You’re My Brother — Do You Need Every Act in Co-Op?

Yes. **You’re My Brother** requires every Campaign Act to be completed in
**Co-Op on any difficulty**.

Playing a few individual chapters with another player does not cover Acts that
were completed only in solo play. Track the requirement by Act so the missing
portion is obvious when you return for cleanup.

There is no difficulty restriction, so a separate low-difficulty Co-Op run is
valid. Combining it with an Insane run is optional rather than required.

</section>

## Which Achievements Require Insane Difficulty?

Two major Campaign completion goals involve Insane.

**Today We Are Canceling the Apocalypse** requires every Campaign Act to be
completed on **Insane**.

**For Whom The Bell Tolls** combines Insane completion with additional Campaign
requirements:

- Complete the Campaign on Insane.
- Complete all side missions.
- Find all hidden items.

If you want route references beside you during that run:

- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-1-walkthrough">Act 1 walkthrough</Link>
- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough">Act 2 walkthrough</Link>

- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-4-walkthrough">Act 4 walkthrough</Link>
- <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 walkthrough</Link>

Insane punishes wasted ammunition and unnecessary exposure more than normal
Campaign play. Once an encounter has opened the progression route and the
objective moves forward, carrying enough ammunition into the next forced fight is
usually more valuable than extending combat for enemies that no longer block you.

## Which Horde Siege Achievements Should You Work On Together?

Horde Siege contains several of the longest cumulative achievements, and most can
advance during the same set of missions.

**Lift Outta Hell** requires **50 completed and survived Horde Siege Missions on
Infantry difficulty or higher**.

Use those 50 missions to build as many other counters as possible:

- 100 Secondary Objectives
- 50 Sonic Disruptor executions
- 1,000 Pulse Drone marks
- 20 revives of players from other squads
- 250 Stim Vaporizer DBNO revives
- 150,000 Energy spent
- 25 Invasion Events
- Horde kill totals
- Assault, Breacher, Marksman, and Medic class levels
- Horde Siege Weapon Mod unlocks

Check the counters during the 50-mission run instead of waiting until mission 50.
A requirement that is still near zero at that point becomes a separate grind.


### This Is My Rifle Uses Horde Siege Weapon Mods

**This Is My Rifle** is tied to **Horde Siege Weapon Mods**.

The Campaign weapon attachments found through Secondary Objectives and other
Campaign activities are a different progression path. Collecting those Campaign
mods does not replace the Horde Siege Weapon Mod requirement.


### Never Fight Alone — Keep the Squad Alive Through Extraction

**Never Fight Alone** requires an **Allfather extraction with all squadmates
alive**.

Once extraction is available, survival becomes the condition that matters. An
unnecessary fight immediately before leaving can turn a successful mission into a
failed achievement attempt if a squadmate dies.


### I’m All Amped Up — Hold Adrenaline for 30 Seconds

**I’m All Amped Up** requires **30 continuous seconds of Adrenaline** while using
the Injector.

Start the attempt early enough in a sustained fight that combat will still be
active for the entire duration. Triggering Adrenaline just as a wave is ending
can leave too little time to satisfy the requirement.


### Stacks, On Stacks, On Stacks — Spend 150,000 Energy

This achievement counts **Energy spent at Supply Caches**.

Energy sitting in your total does not advance the requirement, so spend it during
normal Horde progression. That lets the 150,000 total rise while mission,
objective, kill, and class goals are progressing at the same time.


### Siege Specialist — Max the Four Required Classes

The four classes are:

- **Assault**
- **Breacher**
- **Marksman**
- **Medic**

Once one is finished, move to another unfinished class rather than continuing to
pour progression into the one that has already reached its target.


### Keep the Three Revive Requirements Separate

Three achievements count different revive actions:

- **Just Let Me Get One More…** — get **100 Revives**
- **Who Loves Ya, Baby?** — revive **20 players from other squads** in Horde Siege
- **Down, but Not Dead** — revive **250 DBNO squadmates with the Stim Vaporizer**

These counters are not interchangeable. Reviving your own squadmate can add to
the general revive total without satisfying the other-squad requirement, while a
normal revive does not substitute for one that specifically requires the Stim
Vaporizer.

## Which Achievements Require Versus?

Several requirements belong specifically to Versus, and the best time to handle
the situational ones is while the much longer kill total is still increasing.

**Somebody Set Us Up the Bomb** requires you to plant the bomb in **Demolition**
and have it explode without the opposing team defusing it.

**It Only Game, Why You Heff to Be Mad?** requires a **Conquest win with the enemy
team scoring zero**.

**Days Since Last Accident: 0** uses the workplace-safety interaction on
**Battleship**.

The long-term requirement is **HEY NOW, YOU’RE AN ALL STAR…**, which needs
**25,000 Versus kills**.

Whenever the correct map or mode appears, work on the situational requirement
instead of postponing all three until after the kill total is complete. The kills
earned during those attempts still contribute to the 25,000.

</section>

## How Do Field Service and Got Your Wings Work?

These achievements follow account progression rather than a single Campaign,
Horde, or Versus condition.

**Field Service** requires:

**Reach Player Level 100 and Re-Up for the first time.**

**Got Your Wings** requires:

**Re-Up for the final time and claim your wings.**

Field Service arrives much earlier. Horde Siege and Versus already provide
progress toward account levels, so finish as much of those longer requirements
as possible before deciding whether a separate XP grind is still necessary.

</section>

## Combat Achievements That May Need a Separate Attempt

These requirements depend on a particular weapon, enemy, streak, or interaction.
Some will happen naturally; others are easy to miss because a normal kill does
not always satisfy the specific condition.


### I Am Heavy Weapons Guy

Get **10 kills in a row using a single detached turret**.

Keep using the same detached turret for the full streak. Changing to another
turret before all ten kills are recorded can interrupt the attempt.


### Brotector

Save another player from a **struggle with a Locust**.

This is not the same as reviving a player who is already DBNO. Wait for the
struggle interaction, then interrupt the Locust before the other player is lost.


### Okay, Boomer.

**Carve-up a Boomer.**

Simply killing the Boomer is not enough. The achievement requires the specific
Carve-up action.


### Right in the Ticker

Convert a **Ticker** and send it back.

Treat the Ticker interaction as the objective rather than detonating it through a
normal attack before the conversion can happen.


### Chainsaw Go Brrrrrrrrr

Use a chainsaw against a Locust in **Campaign or Horde Siege**.

Either mode counts, so there is little reason to create a separate run if a
suitable target appears while you are already working on Campaign or Horde
progress.


### Explosive Indigestion

This requirement uses a specific **Bilefrog interaction**.

Killing unrelated enemies with explosives does not replace the required Bilefrog
action.


### Two Grubs, One Gut-Punch

Hit **two Grubs with one Gut-Puncher round**.

Hold the shot until two targets line up closely enough for the same round to hit
both instead of spending the ammunition on isolated enemies.


### Payback’s A Pitch

Let an **Armored Wretch stun you**, then kill that same enemy.

Killing the Wretch before the stun happens prevents the revenge condition from
being established, so allow the required interaction first and finish it
afterward.

</section>

## What Are the Final Completion Achievements?

Two achievements sit at the end of much longer progression paths.


### The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day

On Steam, **The Lucky Ones Died on E-Day** requires:

**Earn all other Achievements.**

It is worth **0 points** and unlocks after the rest of the Steam achievement list
is complete. Xbox does not include this extra achievement, which is why its total
is 54 instead of 55.


### Dead Serious

**Dead Serious** is worth **85 Gamerscore** and combines four large requirements:

- 100% the Campaign on Insane
- 25,000 Versus kills
- Max Player Level
- Max the required four Horde Siege classes

There is no additional activity after those four requirements. Campaign
completion, the Versus kill total, Horde class progression, and account levels
are the work that leads to Dead Serious.

</section>
