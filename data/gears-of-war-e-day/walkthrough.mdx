  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Long Is Gears of War: E-Day and How Many Chapters Are There?">

    Gears of War: E-Day has **28 Campaign sections**: a Prologue, **26 chapters
    across five Acts**, and an Epilogue. A first playthrough is roughly **14–16
    hours**, with difficulty and optional exploration affecting the total. If you

Gears of War: E-Day follows Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago from the first hours of
Emergence Day through the evacuation of **Kalona**. The Campaign starts with
street-level survival, grows into organized COG operations, then pushes Bravo
Squad through broken transport routes, the Locust tunnel network, evacuation
convoys, failing infrastructure, and the final attempt to get Resolute out of the
city.

The most important thing to know before jumping between chapters is that reaching
the named destination often does **not** mean the objective is finished. Legacy
Bridge still needs a second control room after the first mechanism jams, Prospect
Bay communications remain useless until power is restored, and Resolute cannot
leave the dry dock until the flood-control sequence is complete.

If the route itself is the problem, use the Act links below. If a major creature
is stopping you rather than navigation, the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses">boss guide</Link> covers the Campaign's major boss encounters. Optional weapon rewards from side activities are collected in the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/weapon-mods">weapon mod locations</Link>.

<h2
  id="prologue"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Prologue — Reach the Train Before Emergence Begins
</h2>

The opening starts underground after a tremor knocks out the tunnel lights.
Follow the tunnel toward the stopped train and stay with the route as Marcus and
Dom make their way into Kalona.

There is no major branching path here. Once the underground sequence ends, the
attack reaches the city and **Act 1: Emergence** begins.


<h2
  id="act-1"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 1 — Police HQ, Survivor Rescues, and City Hall
</h2>

Act 1 keeps Marcus and Dom close to the first wave of destruction. The route moves
from **Police HQ** into a series of survivor rescues, then eventually reaches
**City Hall**, where the fighting becomes more organized.

The broad path is:

**Police HQ → recruitment center → Pay N Save → Mayor Shaw → City Hall**

The places most likely to interrupt progress are the blocked approach around
Police HQ, the recruitment-center route, survivor objectives inside Pay N Save,
Emergence Holes that keep adding enemies until they are dealt with, and the City
Hall sequence.

Continue with the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-1-walkthrough">Act 1 walkthrough</Link> if one of those objectives is keeping you from reaching the next chapter.


<h2
  id="act-2"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 2 — Legacy Bridge, Bravo Squad, and the Airfield
</h2>

Act 2 brings Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes together as **Bravo Squad**. Their
first assignment is Legacy Bridge, but the Act quickly expands into a chain of
COG positions across Kalona, Charlie Squad's disappearance, a Raven flight, and a
retreat from an airfield that can no longer be held.

The route runs through:

**Legacy Bridge → stadium FOB → Octus Way Station → All Father's Plaza → Charlie
Squad → Raven → airfield**

Legacy Bridge is the first place where the objective can look finished too early:
the first controls jam, forcing Bravo toward the eastern control room through the
Skytrain and lower-deck route. Later, Hold the Line keeps replacing one assignment
with another, so the next destination can change immediately after a position is
secured.

The <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough">Act 2 walkthrough</Link> covers the jammed bridge controls, Charlie's advance, downtown assignments, subway route, Raven defense, and the fallback from the runway to the west control tower.


<h2
  id="act-3"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 3 — Fairlight Outpost, Enemy Lines, and Fort Vigil
</h2>

After the Raven crash, Bravo is separated from the main COG force west of Kalona.
**Fairlight Outpost** offers what looks like a way back, but that extraction falls
apart and sends the squad below the city into the Locust tunnel network.

The Act then climbs back toward the surface, reaches the stadium evacuation, and
turns into a running effort to get civilians safely to **Fort Vigil**.

The main progression is:

**Fairlight Outpost → Locust tunnels → Cooper Station → stadium → convoy → Fort
Vigil**

Three areas cause most route confusion: restoring the Fairlight helipad, finding
the way upward through Cooper Station, and lowering the convoy bridge from the
upper controls. The Corpser appears during Enemy Lines, but its fight mechanics
are separate from the surrounding navigation.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-3-walkthrough">Act 3 walkthrough</Link> for the Fairlight route, Cooper Station, stadium evacuation, convoy bridge, rooftop escort, and Fort Vigil defense. For the Corpser itself, go to the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses#corpser">Corpser fight</Link>.


<h2
  id="act-4"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 4 — Prospect Bay, the Power Grid, and the Refinery
</h2>

Act 4 starts as a trip toward **Prospect Bay**, but damaged transport and broken
infrastructure keep forcing Bravo to change plans. The first train is wrong, the
communications network cannot operate without the local grid, and the road to the
old weather station stops before the destination.

By the final chapters, the priority has shifted to getting civilians out and
preventing the refinery's **light mass converter** from failing.

The progression is:

**Kalona streets → Octus Way → Prospect Bay → power station → weather station →
admin building → refinery**

Useful state checks in this Act are unusually specific: wait for the **second
train**, close the power-station breaker only when output reaches **85%**, leave
the road for the uphill trail toward the weather station, and use the backup
coolant route after the refinery catwalk becomes inaccessible.

The <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-4-walkthrough">Act 4 walkthrough</Link> carries those steps through the Zone 5 key card, enriched emulsion controls, converter shutdown, and tank dump. If the two Scoria at the converter are the problem, their fight is covered in the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses#scoria">Scoria section</Link>.


<h2
  id="act-5"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 5 — Return to Kalona and Get Resolute Out
</h2>

Bravo begins Act 5 on an automated train moving away from Kalona and has to reverse
course before the evacuation can continue. Ghost Town, Raven's Nest, and the dry
dock all stand between the squad and **Resolute**, the ship that still needs a
clear route out of the city.

The Act moves through:

**Return train → Ghost Town → Raven's Nest → Resolute dry dock → east bridge**

Launching Resolute is not just a matter of reaching the ship. The flood-control
sequence has to put enough water beneath the vessel, and a jammed third valve can
stop that process even after the other controls have been handled.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough">Act 5 walkthrough</Link> for the return train, Ghost Town escape, flood controls, jammed valve, Resolute launch, and the approach to the east bridge.


<h2
  id="final-operation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens After the East Bridge Controls Fail?
</h2>

The east bridge begins to rise, then the controls fail before Resolute can pass.
Bravo leaves the console, drops out through the alternate route, and crosses the
deck where the **Impaler** intercepts the squad.

From there, the objective changes from opening the bridge to surviving the final
operation: fight through the encounter, reach the Skytrain, get to the detonator,
and finish the last confrontation before the Epilogue.

Continue with the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/ending-final-boss">final boss and ending</Link> for that sequence.


<h2
  id="after-campaign"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Should You Do After Finishing the Campaign?
</h2>

Finishing the story does not automatically finish Campaign completion. Secondary
Objectives, Hard Cases, Collectibles, Supply Cache unlocks, Co-Op Acts, and Insane
requirements can still remain, while Horde Siege and Versus add their own
long-term goals.

Use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/achievements">achievement tracker</Link> to see what is still missing and which requirements can be completed together.
