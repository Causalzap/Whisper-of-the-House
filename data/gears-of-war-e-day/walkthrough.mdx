Gears of War: E-Day follows Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago from the opening hours
of Emergence Day through the evacuation of **Kalona**. The Campaign begins with
street-level survival, then expands into military operations, broken transport
routes, the Locust tunnel network, evacuation convoys, damaged infrastructure,
and the final attempt to get Resolute out of the city.

Later missions often continue after Bravo reaches the location named in the
objective. Legacy Bridge redirects the squad to another control room, the
Prospect Bay communications equipment cannot work without power, and Resolute
still needs enough water beneath it before the ship can leave the dry dock.

<h2
  id="prologue"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Prologue — Follow the Tunnel to the Train
</h2>

The opening begins underground after a tremor knocks out the tunnel lights.
Follow the tunnel toward the stopped train and stay with the route until the
sequence carries Marcus and Dom into Kalona as Emergence Day begins.

There is no major route split before the attack reaches the city. Once the
underground sequence ends, **Act 1: Emergence** begins.


<h2
  id="act-1"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 1 — Police HQ, Survivor Rescues, and City Hall
</h2>

Act 1 follows Marcus and Dom through the first hours of the attack, from the push
toward **Police HQ** through the recruitment-center rescue, Pay N Save, Mayor
Shaw's group, and finally **City Hall**.

**Route:** Initial attack → Police HQ → recruitment center → Pay N Save → Mayor Shaw → City Hall

If the blocked route near Police HQ, the recruitment-center approach, the Pay N
Save rescue, the Emergence Holes, or the City Hall defense is stopping progress,
pick up from your current objective in
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-1-walkthrough"> Act 1</Link>.


<h2
  id="act-2"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 2 — Legacy Bridge, Bravo Squad, and the Airfield
</h2>

Marcus, Dom, Carter, and Reyes enter Act 2 as **Bravo Squad**. Their orders take
them from Legacy Bridge through a chain of COG positions across Kalona, then into
the search for Charlie Squad, a Raven deployment, and the collapse of the
airfield defense.

**Route:** Legacy Bridge → stadium FOB → Octus Way Station → All Father's Plaza → Charlie Squad → Raven → airfield

Open
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-2-walkthrough"> Act 2</Link>
when Legacy Bridge will not lower, Charlie cannot advance, Hold the Line keeps
redirecting Bravo, or the airfield retreat leaves you unsure which position is
still being held.


<h2
  id="act-3"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 3 — Fairlight Outpost, Locust Tunnels, and Fort Vigil
</h2>

Act 3 begins west of Kalona after Bravo is separated from the main COG force.
Fairlight Outpost offers the first possible extraction, but the route eventually
drops the squad into the Locust tunnel network before returning them to the
stadium evacuation and the retreat toward **Fort Vigil**.

**Route:** Fairlight Outpost → Locust tunnels → Cooper Station → stadium → convoy → Fort Vigil

The
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-3-walkthrough"> Act 3 route</Link>
covers the Fairlight helipad, underground escape, Corpser fight, convoy bridge,
rooftop evacuation, and Fort Vigil defense.


<h2
  id="act-4"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 4 — Prospect Bay, Communications, and the Refinery
</h2>

Act 4 pushes Bravo through damaged sections of Kalona toward **Prospect Bay**.
Transit fails along the way, the local power network is down, and the squad has
to restore long-range communications before the refinery emergency changes the
mission again.

**Route:** Kalona streets → Octus Way → Prospect Bay → power station → weather station → refinery

Use
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-4-walkthrough"> Act 4</Link>
if the train route, power station, weather-station trail, communications
equipment, or refinery sequence is blocking the way forward.


<h2
  id="act-5"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Act 5 — Return to Kalona, Launch Resolute, and Raise the Bridge
</h2>

Bravo begins Act 5 on an automated train moving away from Kalona. The squad has
to reverse course, survive Ghost Town, reach the Raven's Nest, get **Resolute**
out of the dry dock, and open the bridge blocking the evacuation route.

**Route:** Return train → Ghost Town → Raven's Nest → Resolute dry dock → east bridge controls

The
<Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/act-5-walkthrough"> Act 5 route</Link>
covers the correct train, Ghost Town escape, flood-control sequence, jammed third
valve, Resolute launch, and the first attempt to raise the bridge.


<h2
  id="final-operation"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Happens After the East Bridge Controls Fail?
</h2>

The east controls briefly start raising the bridge, then fail before Resolute can
pass. Bravo abandons the console and crosses the deck, where the **Impaler**
intercepts the squad.

The <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/ending-final-boss">final operation and ending</Link> continues from that fight through the Skytrain route, detonator, last confrontation, and Epilogue.
