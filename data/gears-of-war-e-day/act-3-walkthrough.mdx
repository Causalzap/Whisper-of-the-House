  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 3 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Reach **Fairlight Outpost**, turn on the helipad lights, and clear the landing
    zone. After Bravo falls underground, follow the Locust tunnels through
    **Cooper Station** and beat the **Corpser by baiting it into the pillars**.
    Return to the stadium, lower the convoy bridge, move the civilians across the
    rooftops, then defend **Fort Vigil** until the assault breaks.

Act 3 begins west of Kalona after Bravo survives the Raven crash. What starts as
a search for another extraction point soon drops the squad into the Locust tunnel
network beneath the city, then sends them back to the surface for the stadium
evacuation and retreat toward Fort Vigil.

The six chapters are:

- **Chapter 1: Stranded**
- **Chapter 2: Enemy Lines**
- **Chapter 3: Last Foothold**
- **Chapter 4: The Convoy**
- **Chapter 5: Desperate Times**
- **Chapter 6: Desperate Measures**


<h2
  id="chapter-1-stranded"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Stranded — Reach Fairlight Outpost
</h2>

Bravo survives the Raven crash west of Kalona while the surviving COG forces are
regrouping at the stadium. The squad cannot get there directly, so the first goal
is finding another extraction point.

Head **east** until Bravo gets a view toward downtown. Control identifies
**Fairlight Outpost**, a decommissioned military base south of the squad's
position, as the nearest place where a Raven may be able to land.

When the outpost appears below the cliff, keep following the upper path instead
of trying to drop straight toward the compound. The usable descent is marked by
a rope left near the abandoned campers.


<h3
  id="enter-fairlight-outpost"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Fairlight Outpost and Reach the Helipad
</h3>

Locust have already occupied the old base. Clear the elevated shooters around
the guard towers first, then work through the compound toward the helipad.

Reaching the landing zone does not bring the Raven in yet. The pilot cannot see
the pad because the **helipad lights have no power**.


<h3
  id="helipad-breaker"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Is the Fairlight Outpost Breaker Switch?
</h3>

Search the electrical area near the helipad. Bravo initially looks for the old
substation, but the usable control is a **breaker switch** inside the facility.

Flip the breaker and return toward the landing zone. Restoring power also opens
part of the old facility and draws Locust back toward the outpost.


<h3
  id="clear-helipad"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Helipad Before the Raven Lands
</h3>

The Raven cannot touch down while enemies still control the pad. Push through the
gate and clear the landing zone.

Once the immediate area is secure, move onto the LZ and signal the pilot. The
extraction fails before Bravo can escape, dropping the squad below the surface
and starting **Enemy Lines**.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-enemy-lines"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Enemy Lines — Escape the Locust Tunnels
</h2>

Bravo wakes inside the Locust tunnel network with no working long-range radio.
The squad cannot return the way it entered, so Marcus keeps moving east toward
the last known position of the stadium.

Most side tunnels are not meaningful route choices. Stay with the infrastructure
the Locust are already using and keep following the squad deeper underground.


<h3
  id="use-raven-radio"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Crashed Raven's Radio
</h3>

Deeper in the tunnels, Bravo finds another crashed Raven. Its crew is dead, but
the aircraft's radio is stronger than the squad's damaged equipment.

Use the radio, then continue when Bravo becomes separated. The split is
temporary; each route eventually reconnects around the old subway system, so
there is no need to backtrack looking for the rest of the squad.


<h3
  id="cooper-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Regroup at Cooper Station and Find a Way Up
</h3>

The separate routes reconnect at **Cooper Station**, an abandoned subway station
below Kalona.

After clearing the station, Bravo discovers that the Locust have been monitoring
COG radio traffic. The priority changes from contacting command remotely to
reaching the surface and warning the forces at the stadium.

Look **above the station** for the route out. When you return to the tracks, use
the trains to climb toward the upper level. The normal surface route remains
blocked, so continue through the underground path beneath the collapsed
courthouse.


<h3
  id="corpser-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Beat the Corpser Boss in Act 3?
</h3>

The large **Corpser** appears near the end of Enemy Lines. Gunfire can damage it,
but standing in the open and trading shots is not the fastest way through the
fight.

Use the **pillars around the arena**. Position yourself so the Corpser's attacks
carry it toward the structures, then move away before it reaches you.

When the Corpser burrows, stop shooting and watch for where it reappears. Move
out of its path, then resume firing once it is exposed again.

Repeated impacts damage the pillars and make the **ceiling increasingly
unstable**. Keep using the arena against the Corpser until the collapse ends the
fight and opens the route back toward the surface.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-last-foothold"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: Last Foothold — Return to the Stadium
</h2>

Bravo reaches the surface again without restoring reliable long-range
communications. The last order was for every surviving Gear to regroup at the
**stadium**, so keep moving toward it.

When the first street route appears to stop, use the **alley** rather than
waiting for the radio or searching the blocked road.


<h3
  id="reach-stadium"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Follow the Short-Range Signal to the Stadium
</h3>

Bravo eventually picks up short-range contact from surviving COG forces. Follow
that signal toward the stadium.

Clear the Locust around the perimeter before entering. Bravo's warning that the
Locust have been listening to COG communications changes the evacuation plan as
soon as the squad reaches the defenders.


<h3
  id="evacuate-stadium"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Get the Evacuation Trucks From the Hotel
</h3>

The stadium is serving as both a defensive position and field hospital. After
Bravo reports what happened underground, the surviving force decides to move
everyone toward **Fort Vigil**.

The convoy still needs the trucks being held by **Corporal Harris at the hotel**.
Resupply at the stadium, go to the hotel, and tell Harris to prepare the vehicles.

Once the trucks are ready, return with the convoy.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-the-convoy"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: The Convoy — Lower the Raised Bridge
</h2>

The convoy gets away from the stadium but stops when it reaches a bridge with the
**deck raised**.

The control room is not responding from below, so Captain Gray sends Bravo onto
the **upper deck**.


<h3
  id="convoy-upper-deck"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the Bridge Control Room
</h3>

Move onto the upper deck and fight toward the control room. Clear the enemies
covering the approach before trying to operate the console.

Use the controls once the room is secure. The bridge begins lowering slowly while
more Locust attack the convoy below.


<h3
  id="defend-bridge-controls"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stay on the Upper Deck Until the Bridge Is Down
</h3>

Do not leave the control room immediately after activating the bridge. Captain
Gray orders Bravo to hold the upper deck until the deck is fully lowered.

Use the higher position to protect the convoy while the bridge moves. Once it
reaches the road and the immediate attack is cleared, return to the convoy at the
central gate.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-desperate-times"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Desperate Times — Move the Civilians Across the Rooftops
</h2>

The convoy can no longer carry everyone safely through the remaining streets.
Bravo is still several blocks from Fort Vigil, so the civilians have to continue
using the **rooftops and upper floors**.


<h3
  id="rooftop-escort"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Route Before Moving the Civilians
</h3>

Move ahead while the civilians remain behind cover. Clear the next rooftop or
building before calling the group forward.

The sequence follows a simple rhythm:

**Bravo clears → civilians move → Bravo advances → civilians wait**

An open doorway does not necessarily mean the next room is safe, so do not pull
the group forward until Bravo has cleared the next section.


<h3
  id="sniper-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Flank the Sniper Tower Before Going Back to Street Level
</h3>

Explosive sniper fire eventually blocks the evacuation route. Leave the civilians
above street level while Bravo takes the flank through the tower.

Follow the side path upward and remove the shooters from solid cover.

After the tower is secure, head back down and clear the remaining enemies at
street level. Only then tell the civilians to make the final run toward the fort.


<h2
  id="chapter-6-desperate-measures"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 6: Desperate Measures — Defend Fort Vigil
</h2>

The civilians reach Fort Vigil, but the Locust attack before Bravo has time to
recover. Head straight for the wall when the defenders call the squad into
position.


<h3
  id="fort-wall-guns"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Wall Guns Against the Climbers
</h3>

Get onto the mounted weapons as soon as they become available. Prioritize enemies
already climbing the wall and heavy targets moving directly toward the defenses
before spending fire on distant infantry.

The assault comes in repeated pushes, so stay on the guns after the first wave
slows down. Air support eventually joins the defense, but Bravo still has to hold
the wall until the Locust attack breaks.

Once Fort Vigil survives the assault, Act 3 ends with the surviving force inside
the fort.


<h2
  id="before-act-4"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Does Bravo Go After Fort Vigil?
</h2>

The immediate evacuation is over, but the surviving COG forces still need to
reconnect with the rest of the operation. Bravo's next route leads toward
**Prospect Bay**, where failed transit, a dead power network, and lost
communications become the next obstacles.
