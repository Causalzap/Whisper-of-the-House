  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Act 3 in Gears of War: E-Day?">

    Restore power to the **Fairlight Outpost helipad**, follow the underground
    route through **Cooper Station**, then return to the stadium. From there,
    lower the convoy bridge, move the civilians across the rooftops, and hold
    **Fort Vigil** through the final assault.

  </GuideQuickAnswer>

Act 3 starts with Bravo cut off west of Kalona after the Raven crash and ends
with the surviving force holding Fort Vigil. The route changes repeatedly along
the way: an extraction point fails, the squad is pushed underground, the stadium
becomes an evacuation site, and the convoy eventually has to abandon the streets
for the rooftops.

There are six chapters:

- **Chapter 1: Stranded**
- **Chapter 2: Enemy Lines**
- **Chapter 3: Last Foothold**
- **Chapter 4: The Convoy**
- **Chapter 5: Desperate Times**
- **Chapter 6: Desperate Measures**

Several objectives continue after you reach what looks like the destination.
Getting to the helipad does not mean the Raven can land, operating the bridge
controls does not mean the convoy can leave immediately, and reaching Fort Vigil
does not end the attack. Wait for the objective to update before leaving a
position that still has an active task.


<h2
  id="chapter-1-stranded"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 1: Stranded — Reach Fairlight Outpost
</h2>

Bravo survives the Raven crash west of Kalona while the remaining COG forces are
regrouping at the stadium. The squad cannot return directly, so the first job is
to find another extraction point.

Move **east** until Bravo gets a view toward downtown. Control identifies
**Fairlight Outpost**, a decommissioned military base south of the squad's
position, as the nearest site that may still support a Raven landing.

When the compound becomes visible below the cliff, stay on the upper path rather
than trying to drop directly into the base. Continue along the cliff until you
reach the abandoned campers; the rope nearby marks the usable descent.


<h3
  id="enter-fairlight-outpost"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Fairlight Outpost and Reach the Helipad
</h3>

Locust already occupy the outpost, with shooters covering the approach from the
guard towers. Deal with the elevated enemies before crossing the exposed parts of
the compound, then work toward the helipad.

Reaching the landing zone is only the first part of the objective. The Raven
cannot safely come in because the **helipad lights have no power**, so the next
step is restoring electricity rather than waiting at the pad.


<h3
  id="helipad-breaker"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Is the Fairlight Outpost Breaker Switch?
</h3>

Search the electrical section near the helipad. Bravo initially looks for the old
substation, but the control you actually need is a **breaker switch inside the
facility**.

Using the breaker restores the lights and opens part of the old facility, but it
also draws more Locust back toward the outpost. Follow the newly available route
toward the landing zone instead of staying around the electrical room.


<h3
  id="clear-helipad"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear the Helipad Before the Raven Can Land
</h3>

The landing lights are only useful if the pad itself is secure. Push through the
gate and clear the enemies controlling the LZ, then move onto the helipad when
Bravo is told to signal the pilot.

The extraction does not go as planned. The sequence drops Bravo into the Locust
network beneath Kalona and starts **Enemy Lines**.


<h2
  id="chapter-2-enemy-lines"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 2: Enemy Lines — Escape the Locust Tunnels
</h2>

Bravo wakes underground with unreliable long-range communications and no way to
return through the route that brought them down. The stadium remains the best
reference point above ground, so the squad keeps moving east through the tunnel
network.

Most side passages here are not meaningful route choices. Follow the tunnels and
infrastructure the Locust are already using rather than repeatedly doubling back
to check dead ends.


<h3
  id="use-raven-radio"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Crashed Raven's Radio
</h3>

Deeper underground, Bravo finds another crashed Raven. Its crew is dead, but the
aircraft still has communications equipment stronger than the squad's damaged
radio.

Use the Raven radio and continue forward when Bravo becomes separated. The split
does not create a permanent route choice; the paths reconnect around the old
subway system, so there is no benefit in backtracking to look for the others.


<h3
  id="cooper-station"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Do You Get Out of Cooper Station?
</h3>

The separated routes reconnect at **Cooper Station**, an abandoned subway station
beneath Kalona.

After the station fight, Bravo discovers that the Locust have been listening to
COG radio traffic. Reaching the surface now matters more than trying to contact
command again from underground.

The exit is **above the station**. Return toward the tracks and use the trains to
climb to the upper level. The normal surface path remains blocked, so keep
following the underground route beneath the collapsed courthouse.


<h3
  id="corpser-fight"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Do You Do at the Corpser Fight?
</h3>

A **Corpser** blocks the route near the end of Enemy Lines. The arena pillars are
part of the solution, so use them to advance the encounter instead of treating it
like a normal open firefight.

For the Corpser's attack windows, pillar setup, and later phase, use the <Link href="/gears-of-war-e-day/bosses">boss guide</Link>.

Once the encounter ends and the collapse opens the way forward, continue through
the new route toward the surface.


<h2
  id="chapter-3-last-foothold"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 3: Last Foothold — Get Back to the Stadium
</h2>

Bravo returns above ground without reliable long-range communications. The last
standing order is still to regroup at the **stadium**, so there is no reason to
wait for the radio to recover before moving.

The first blocked street can make the route look closed. Use the **alley** beside
it and continue through the surviving buildings until short-range COG traffic
comes back into range.


<h3
  id="reach-stadium"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Follow the Short-Range Signal to the Stadium
</h3>

Once Bravo starts receiving short-range transmissions, use them to close the
remaining distance to the stadium.

Clear the Locust holding the perimeter before entering. Bravo's warning that the
enemy has been monitoring COG communications changes the situation as soon as the
squad reaches the defenders: the stadium is no longer somewhere the survivors can
rely on holding.


<h3
  id="evacuate-stadium"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Get the Evacuation Trucks From the Hotel
</h3>

The stadium is functioning as both a defensive position and a field hospital.
After Bravo reports what happened underground, the remaining force prepares to
move everyone toward **Fort Vigil**.

The evacuation cannot leave yet because the required trucks are with **Corporal
Harris at the hotel**. This is also a good point to refill ammunition before the
next stretch.

Go to the hotel, reach Harris, and get the vehicles moving. Once the trucks are
ready, return with the convoy rather than continuing farther into the surrounding
streets.


<h2
  id="chapter-4-the-convoy"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 4: The Convoy — Lower the Raised Bridge
</h2>

The evacuation gets away from the stadium but stops at a bridge with the **deck
raised**. Waiting with the vehicles does not advance the objective because the
controls cannot be reached from the road.

Captain Gray sends Bravo onto the **upper deck**.


<h3
  id="convoy-upper-deck"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Reach the Upper-Deck Bridge Control Room
</h3>

Fight across the upper deck toward the control room and clear the enemies covering
the approach before using the console.

Activate the controls once the room is secure. The bridge starts lowering, but
that does not finish the sequence; the convoy is still exposed while the deck is
moving.


<h3
  id="defend-bridge-controls"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Stay on the Upper Deck Until the Bridge Is Fully Down
</h3>

Captain Gray tells Bravo to hold the upper position while the bridge lowers.
Leaving immediately after using the console gives up the best firing angle while
the convoy below is still under attack.

Use the elevation to cover the vehicles until the deck reaches road level and the
immediate Locust push is cleared. Then rejoin the convoy at the central gate.


<h2
  id="chapter-5-desperate-times"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 5: Desperate Times — Move the Civilians Across the Rooftops
</h2>

The streets become too dangerous for the convoy to carry everyone the rest of the
way. Fort Vigil is still several blocks away, so Bravo has to move the civilians
through **rooftops and upper floors** instead.

This section works better when Bravo stays ahead of the group. Sending civilians
into the next room simply because a doorway has opened can put them directly into
an uncleared fight.


<h3
  id="rooftop-escort"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Clear Each Section Before Calling the Civilians Forward
</h3>

Treat every new rooftop or interior space as a separate checkpoint:

- Move Bravo ahead while the civilians stay in cover.
- Clear the next rooftop, room, or connecting building.
- Call the group forward only after the immediate route is secure.

Once they move up, repeat the same pattern rather than trying to rush the whole
escort in one continuous push.


<h3
  id="sniper-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Flank the Sniper Tower Before Returning to Street Level
</h3>

Explosive sniper fire eventually stops the evacuation route. Keep the civilians
above street level while Bravo takes the side route through the tower.

Climb toward the shooters using solid cover instead of crossing their firing lane
directly.

Clearing the tower is not quite enough. Return to street level and remove the
remaining enemies below before bringing the civilians down. Once the route is
clear, send the group toward the fort.


<h2
  id="chapter-6-desperate-measures"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Chapter 6: Desperate Measures — Defend Fort Vigil
</h2>

The civilians make it inside Fort Vigil, but Bravo gets almost no recovery time
before the Locust assault reaches the walls.

Head for the defensive position as soon as the call comes in. This part is less
about finding another route and more about choosing the targets that can actually
overrun the fort.


<h3
  id="fort-wall-guns"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Use the Wall Guns Against the Closest Threats
</h3>

Take the mounted weapons as soon as they become available and work from the wall
outward.

Prioritize:

- Enemies already climbing or reaching the defenses.
- Heavy targets moving directly toward the wall.
- Distant infantry only after the immediate pressure is under control.

The assault arrives in repeated pushes, so a lull after the first wave is not the
end. Stay on the guns until the next attack is clear and command confirms the
pressure is breaking.

Air support eventually joins the defense, but Bravo still has to keep the wall
from being overrun until the assault ends.


<h2
  id="before-act-4"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Does Bravo Go After Fort Vigil?
</h2>

Fort Vigil survives the immediate attack, but the wider evacuation is still in
trouble. Bravo's next route leads toward **Prospect Bay**, where failed transit,
the dead power network, and lost long-range communications become the next set of
obstacles.
