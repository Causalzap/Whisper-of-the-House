<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Get All 72 Achievements in Blacksmith Master?">
Complete the long-term crafting, Merchant Offer, shop sales, and staff milestones while progressing through the Mastery Tree. Establish every required Trade Route, produce each special Ingot type, and finish the one-time achievements such as **Hidden Riches**, **Just in Time**, and **Do it yourself!**. **Blacksmith Master** has **72 Steam achievements**, covering workshop production, Merchant Offers, retail sales, staff recruitment, resource gathering, crafting, research, and late-game completion.

Many unlock through ordinary progression. Others require deliberately using a feature that is easy to overlook, or continuing production after the main Mastery Tree is complete.

The longest objectives are usually the cumulative milestones and the final Blueprint requirements. Reaching the last Mastery node does not automatically complete every Blueprint-related achievement.

Use the checklist below to track your completed achievements, search for specific requirements, and filter unfinished goals. Your progress is saved in this browser. Check Steam for your official achievement status.

## What Is the Best Order for 100% Completion?

The easiest way to avoid a long cleanup at the end is to let the cumulative achievements progress while you work on other objectives.

Keep completing Merchant Offers toward **250**, crafting items toward **10,000**, and supplying the Store toward **5,000 sales**. The day milestones continue advancing as the workshop operates. Complete the one-time actions, such as a timed offer and Direct Control crafting, when the required conditions are readily available.

Save the final Blueprint checks for the later stages, when you have access to Set Blueprints and sufficient Design Points. **Full Deck**, **A True Master**, and **A Legend is Forged** have different conditions, so finishing one does not automatically complete the others.

## Merchant Offer Achievements — All 7

Merchant Offers contribute five cumulative achievements, one timed challenge, and one mystery chest achievement.

### How Do You Complete 250 Merchant Offers?

**Master of the Market** unlocks after **250 completed Merchant Offers**. Accepted or unfinished orders do not satisfy that requirement.

Continue submitting achievable offers throughout the game instead of leaving the entire target until the end. Orders that also reward new Blueprint unlocks can advance your collection at the same time, but the achievement itself is tied to the number of completed offers.

### How Do You Get Just in Time?

**Just in Time** requires completing a timed Merchant Offer before its deadline.

When a timed offer appears:

1. Check the requested items and remaining time.
2. Make sure you have access to the necessary Blueprints and materials.
3. Assign enough production capacity to complete the order.
4. Deliver the requested items before the timer expires.

Attempt this when the required production chain is already functioning, rather than immediately after unlocking an unfamiliar material or crafting station.

You only need one successful timed offer.

### How Do You Unlock Hidden Riches?

**Hidden Riches** requires opening a mystery chest that actually contains treasure.

Look for Merchant Offers that reward a mystery chest instead of an ordinary payment.

Complete the offer and open the chest.

If the achievement does not unlock, continue accepting eligible chest-reward offers until you open one containing treasure.

## Crafting Achievements — All 8

Four crafting achievements count your total item production. The remaining four require specific types of products or manual crafting.

### How Do You Craft 10,000 Items?

**Hammer Never Rests** requires **10,000 finished items**.

Keep manufacturing products whose materials you can replenish without repeatedly interrupting production. Simple items can be useful for this cumulative target, especially while the same workshop is also fulfilling Merchant Offers and supplying the Store.

If items stop being completed because workers are waiting for materials or equipment, correct that delay using the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">production troubleshooting steps</Link>.

### How Do You Get Do it yourself!?

**Do it yourself!** is earned by manually crafting an item through Direct Control.

Choose a simple item whose crafting stations and materials are already available.

1. Select a Blacksmith.
2. Choose **Take Control**.
3. Collect the required material.
4. Carry out the crafting actions at the appropriate workstation.
5. Finish the item and deliver it if the active task requires delivery.

You do not need to craft a complicated product. The achievement checks your use of direct crafting, not the value of the item.

### How Do You Get Kiln Ignited and First Sparkle?

**Kiln Ignited** requires one completed Pottery item.

Unlock Pottery, establish access to Clay, obtain an appropriate Blueprint, and make the item using its required equipment.

**First Sparkle** requires one completed Jewelry item.

Unlock Jewelry, obtain the materials required by your chosen Blueprint, and finish a piece of Jewelry.

The <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Mastery Tree progression guide</Link> explains when Pottery, Jewelry, and the resources needed for them become available.

For **First Line of Defense**, unlock a Shield Blueprint and craft one Shield. You do not need to complete every Shield Blueprint for this achievement.

## Shop and Customer Achievements — All 8

These achievements measure actual store sales and daily customer traffic.

### How Do You Sell 5,000 Items?

**Master Seller** requires 5,000 completed store sales.

The main challenge is keeping enough products available for customers over many days.

You can work toward this achievement while completing the Mastery Tree, improving Blueprints, and producing items for Merchant Offers.

If finished products are not reaching Store Racks, follow the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">Rack restocking checks</Link>. For pricing, Cashiers, and customer management, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">Blacksmith Master beginner guide</Link>.

### How Do You Attract 500 Customers in One Day?

**Best in the Town** requires at least **500 customers during a single day**.

This is different from Master Seller, which counts items sold over time.

Check the daily customer count while operating your Store. If you are still below 500, consider available bonuses that increase customer arrivals before investing in further expansion.

The achievement counts customers attracted during a single day, not lifetime sales or completed purchases. For Store operations and customer management, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">beginner guide</Link>.

The smaller **Popular** achievement requires 100 customers in one day. It can be completed earlier, while your shop is still growing.

## Staff and Worker Achievements — All 5

These achievements cover hiring ordinary staff, obtaining Elite and Super Workers, and raising a Blacksmith to maximum level.

### How Do You Get Golden Goose and Natural Talent?

For **Golden Goose**, hire your first Elite Worker.

For **Natural Talent**, unlock access to Super Workers and hire one.

These are separate worker categories. Hiring an Elite Worker does not satisfy the requirement to hire a Super Worker.

The achievement triggers when the appropriate worker is recruited, rather than simply when a candidate becomes available.

### How Do You Reach 50 Hires?

**Best Employer** requires hiring 50 staff members.

Continue recruiting as your operation expands, but account for hiring expenses and ongoing wages.

You do not need to expand every production system immediately just to chase the staff milestone.

It is more practical to work toward the target while you are already growing the workshop.

### How Do You Get Maxed out!?

**Maxed out!** requires one Blacksmith to reach maximum level.

Select a Blacksmith who can continue crafting regularly and keep that worker assigned to useful production.

Frequent crafting provides opportunities to gain experience.

Check the worker's actual level and XP progression instead of assuming that many days of employment are sufficient.

This achievement can take longer if the selected Blacksmith spends much of the time waiting for equipment or materials.

</section>

## Mining and Forest Achievements — All 6

These achievements measure resources collected from Mines and Forests, plus the complete clearing of a Forest.

### How Do You Get Deforester?

**Deforester** requires cutting down an entire Forest.

This is not the same as gathering 1,000 Wood.

Once the relevant Forest is unlocked, keep Lumberjacks gathering until the Forest has been completely cleared.

The exact amount of time depends on your workforce and how much collection has already taken place.

### Do You Need to Store 1,000 Resources at Once?

The resource achievements specify amounts **gathered or mined**, not an inventory quantity that must remain in the Warehouse simultaneously.

Continue normal resource production while you use Wood and Ore for crafting.

If a resource milestone remains incomplete, check whether the correct Mine or Forest is active and producing the specified material.

Iron, Copper, and Silver count toward their respective achievements; they are not interchangeable.

## Mastery Tree Achievements — All 14

These achievements come from unlocking production systems, resources, construction options, and finally the entire Mastery Tree.


### How Do You Complete Master of All Crafts?

**Master of All Crafts** requires the entire Mastery Tree to be completed.

Check for remaining locked nodes across every available branch. An unlocked workshop floor or the ability to make a late-game material does not mean every Mastery has been completed.

Continue meeting the crafting-tier requirements displayed in the Mastery menu and unlock the remaining nodes.

For the progression order and prerequisite decisions, follow the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Blacksmith Master Mastery Tree guide</Link>.

This achievement does not automatically grant **Full Deck** or **A True Master**. Those depend on Blueprints, which are tracked separately.

## Smelting and Ingot Achievements — All 12

The Ingot achievements require producing specific metals and sizes at least once.


### Why Are Some Ingot Achievements Still Locked?

Check both the **metal** and the **Ingot size** in the achievement description.

Producing an ordinary Gold Ingot does not complete the achievements requiring Medium or Large Gold Ingots.

Likewise, Steel, Bronze, and Sterling Silver are distinct materials.

As your Smelting progression expands, you gain access to more advanced Ingot recipes and equipment.

To finish the remaining achievements:

1. Find the specific metal and size listed in the achievement.
2. Confirm the appropriate Smelting upgrade is available.
3. Obtain the raw materials or intermediate Ingots required by that recipe.
4. Assign production for the specific Ingot.
5. Wait for one completed Ingot of the correct type.

### How Do You Get the Gold Ingot Achievements?

The Gold achievements are:

- **Golden Flame** — First Gold Ingot.
- **Worth Its Weight** — First Medium Gold Ingot.
- **Bar Worth a Kingdom** — First Large Gold Ingot.

Start by obtaining access to Gold through the relevant Trade Route.

Then work through the required Smelting stages until you can produce the Medium and Large versions.

Do not stop after making the first Gold Ingot. The three achievements require three distinct production results.

## Trade Route Achievements — All 5

Trade Routes give access to special resources and have their own establishment and upgrade achievements.


### How Do You Get A Well-Oiled Wagon?

**A Well-Oiled Wagon** requires upgrading one Trade Route to **Level 4**.

You do not need every Trade Route to reach Level 4 for this particular achievement.

Choose a route whose resource you already use and upgrade that route until it reaches the required level.

This lets the investment support your ongoing production rather than upgrading a route only for its achievement.

### Do You Need to Complete a Trade to Unlock the Route Achievements?

The achievements for Clay, Birch Wood, Gems, and Gold require establishing their corresponding Trade Routes. Their descriptions do not require completing a shipment.

If one remains locked, check whether that specific route has actually been established. For route unlocks, resource requirements, and trading progression, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Mastery Tree and Trade Routes guide</Link>.

</section>

## Blueprint Achievements — All 3

These are among the most demanding achievements because they depend on item collection and research progress rather than ordinary production volume alone.

### How Do You Get A Legend is Forged?

Improve one Blueprint until it reaches **Legendary** quality.

You only need one Legendary Blueprint for this achievement.

A practical approach is to select a product you already make frequently, then continue investing Design Points in that Blueprint until the Legendary upgrade becomes available and is completed.

This is different from A True Master, which requires improving every Blueprint to a lower minimum quality threshold.

### How Do You Unlock Full Deck?

**Full Deck** requires unlocking every Blueprint.

If **Full Deck** remains locked after collecting the ordinary Blueprints, open the **Sets** category and look for missing items. Set Blueprints count toward the achievement alongside weapons, tools, Pottery, Jewelry, and other products.

### How Do You Get A True Master?

**A True Master** requires **every Blueprint to reach at least Rare quality**.

Legendary quality is not required for every item.

Follow this order:

1. Unlock every Blueprint.
2. Generate Design Points through Scholars.
3. Open the Blueprints menu and inspect the quality of each item.
4. Upgrade any Blueprint that is still below Rare.
5. Check the ordinary item categories and the Sets category.
6. Continue until no Blueprint remains below the required quality.

Newly obtained Blueprints may still need quality upgrades even if the rest of your collection is already at Rare or better.

This is one reason to continue generating Design Points while finishing the last Blueprint unlocks.

If Full Deck is complete but A True Master is still locked, look for remaining low-quality Blueprints rather than continuing to collect unrelated Mastery unlocks.

## Day Milestones and Debt — All 4

These include three time-progression achievements and one achievement for going into debt.

### How Do You Get the Day 25, 50, and 100 Achievements?

The milestones are tied to completing in-game days:

- **Time Flies...** — Day 25.
- **Already 50!** — Day 50.
- **Anniversary!** — Day 100.

Continue operating the workshop through the required days.

You do not need to finish the Mastery Tree before Day 100, and reaching Day 100 does not automatically finish the other completion milestones.

Use the time to progress your remaining Merchant Offers, item production, sales, and Blueprint research.

### How Do You Unlock Oh no...?

**Oh no...** requires going into debt.

If it has not appeared naturally, leave it until you have a workshop that can recover from a temporary negative balance.

Check that your balance actually falls below zero, then check whether the achievement unlocks. Spending almost all your money is not enough; the requirement is to enter debt.

After obtaining it, restore a positive balance and continue your remaining completion goals.

## What Should You Check If You Are Missing the Last Achievements?

When only a few achievements remain, use the checklist above to filter unfinished goals and identify the exact requirement.

If you are missing a **Blueprint achievement**, first distinguish between collecting every Blueprint, upgrading every Blueprint to Rare, and obtaining one Legendary Blueprint. The <Link href="#blueprint-achievements">Blueprint achievements section</Link> explains these separate targets.

For a missing **Mastery achievement**, check the remaining locked nodes rather than assuming that advanced production means the tree is complete. See the <Link href="#mastery-achievements">Mastery achievements section</Link>.

For **cumulative achievements**, check the specific number being counted: completed Merchant Offers, crafted items, shop sales, daily customer arrivals, or individual Ingot types. Progress in one category does not replace progress in another.

If a **special-action achievement** remains unfinished, return to its instructions instead of repeating ordinary production. Hidden Riches, Just in Time, Direct Control crafting, and entering debt all require specific conditions.
