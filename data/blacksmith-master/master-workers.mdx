**Master Workers** are one of the major additions to Blacksmith Master 1.0. Unlike ordinary employees, these specialists unlock abilities that affect your entire workshop or give you access to production options you could not use before.

There are six types, and each can only be hired once.

Choosing your first Master Worker is important because their hiring costs and wages can be substantial. A useful specialist may solve a long-standing limitation, but hiring several before your workshop can support their costs can leave you short of money for materials, equipment, and Merchant Offers.

The biggest late-game addition comes from the **Master Blacksmith**, who unlocks Siege Machine orders. These large projects reward Mystery Blueprints, allowing you to collect the new Set Items.

## How Do You Unlock Master Workers?

First, progress far enough through the Mastery Tree to unlock the **Master Workers** system.

After unlocking it, open the Master Worker hiring options and inspect the available specialists.

The hiring screen shows each Master's unique ability, hiring requirements, and cost.

### Step 1 — Unlock the Master Workers Mastery

Continue crafting items that meet the current Mastery tier requirement.

As your workshop progresses, the Mastery Tree gives access to more specialized employee systems.

Once you unlock Master Workers, the special hiring options become available.

This does not mean all six Masters can be recruited immediately.

### Step 2 — Check the Individual Hiring Requirements

Open the Master Worker hiring screen and select the specialist you want.

Read the displayed conditions before spending money on unrelated upgrades.

Some Master Workers require an employee of the corresponding profession to reach maximum level.

The requirements for Master Scholar, Master Miner, and Master Lumberjack include reaching maximum level with their respective worker types.

For the remaining specialists, use the exact condition displayed on the hiring card rather than assuming that all six have identical requirements.

If a requirement is incomplete, identify which worker or progression milestone it refers to.

For a worker-level requirement, keep a suitable employee active in their normal job until the necessary level is reached.

### Step 3 — Check the Hiring Cost and Wage

Each Master Worker has a hiring cost and an ongoing wage displayed in the hiring screen. Check both before recruiting.

For example, unlocking Tier 3 Smelters through the Master Assistant does not automatically provide the equipment for free. Make sure you can afford to use the new ability after hiring the specialist.

If you cannot yet cover the hiring cost, continue operating your workshop and return when the requirement and price are manageable. For general income and wage decisions, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">Blacksmith Master beginner guide</Link>.

### Step 4 — Complete the Hire

Once the selected Master's requirements are satisfied and you have enough Gold, use the available hiring option in the Hire Masters screen.

After recruiting the specialist, check the newly unlocked equipment or orders before purchasing additional machines or hiring another Master.

Check the effect of the specialist before deciding who should be hired next.

<section id="master-blacksmith" className="scroll-mt-24">

### Master Blacksmith — Unlock Siege Machine Orders

The **Master Blacksmith** unlocks special Merchant Orders for Siege Machines.

These are larger projects than ordinary item orders. They require many components and provide access to Mystery Blueprints.

This is the Master to prioritize when your goal is collecting the new Set Items.

After hiring the Master Blacksmith, check the available special orders rather than expecting Siege Machines to appear among your ordinary item Blueprints.

Completing these projects is the main route toward obtaining the 36 new Set Items.

You do not need to clear the entire Mastery Tree before beginning to collect these Blueprints, provided the necessary Master Worker and production requirements have been satisfied.

However, the orders demand considerable production capacity, so prepare the workshop before taking on repeated projects.

</section>

In an expanded workshop, switching to higher-tier Smelting equipment can provide more output from a more concentrated production area.

The benefit is not merely another employee. It makes additional Smelting equipment available.

After hiring the Master Assistant, check the building menu for Tier 3 Smelters. These machines become more useful when your workshop already has enough materials to support additional Ingot output.

**When is this Master worth hiring first?**

Choose the Master Assistant when your workshop already has substantial demand for Ingots and the existing Smelting operation is consistently working at capacity.

If your main limitation is missing Ore deliveries, solve that first.

<section id="master-scholar" className="scroll-mt-24">

### Master Scholar — Unlock the Fifth Blueprint Upgrade Level

The **Master Scholar** unlocks a fifth upgrade level for Blueprints.

This provides an additional way to improve the selling value of products you already manufacture.

The hiring requirements include having a Scholar reach maximum level.

After hiring the Master Scholar, inspect the Blueprints menu for the additional upgrade level. The ability improves existing items but does not unlock missing Blueprints.

For Design Point production and choosing which items to upgrade, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Blueprint Research guide</Link>.

### What Changes After Hiring the Master Scholar?

The fifth Blueprint upgrade level becomes available after the Master Scholar is hired. This increases the upgrade potential of existing items; it does not unlock missing Blueprints.

Check which items can receive the additional upgrade. For Design Points, ordinary Blueprint upgrades, and research priorities, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Blueprint Research guide</Link>.

</section>

<section id="master-miner" className="scroll-mt-24">

### Master Miner — Find Gems While Mining

The **Master Miner** provides a chance to discover Gems during mining.

The hiring requirements include reaching maximum level with a Miner.

This makes the Master Miner a useful addition when you already operate Mines and want the extra Gem discoveries.

However, discovering a Gem is not the same as having every Gem-related crafting system unlocked.

### Why Can't You Use Gems Found by the Master Miner?

The Master Miner's bonus provides a chance to find Gems while mining, but it does not automatically unlock every Gem-related production requirement.

If Gem Storage or another required resource system is locked, check the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Gem Mine Trade Route and resource unlocks</Link> before recruiting additional Miners.

</section>

<section id="master-lumberjack" className="scroll-mt-24">

### Master Lumberjack — Unlock Larger Woodpile Storage

The **Master Lumberjack** unlocks two larger Woodpile options:

- **Medium Woodpile:** 80 storage slots.
- **Large Woodpile:** 150 storage slots.

The hiring requirements include reaching maximum level with a Lumberjack.

These additional storage options help large workshops accommodate greater quantities of Wood.

They become more useful as Wood consumption increases through Carpentry, combined items, and other advanced manufacturing.

### When Is the Master Lumberjack Worth Hiring?

Prioritize this Master when your Wood production would benefit from the larger 80-slot or 150-slot Woodpiles.

The new storage options increase capacity, but they do not replace resource deliveries. If Wood is failing to reach the workshop, use the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">resource restocking checks</Link>.

</section>

<section id="master-cashier" className="scroll-mt-24">

### Master Cashier — Stop Checkout Patience Loss

The **Master Cashier** prevents customers from losing patience while waiting at the cash register.

This is especially useful for busy shops with long checkout queues.

During normal retail operation, customers may leave because they are waiting too long to pay.

The Master Cashier removes that specific patience problem, allowing customers to remain in line rather than becoming impatient at checkout.

### Does the Master Cashier Fix Every Store Problem?

No. The Master Cashier prevents patience loss while customers wait to pay. This ability does not produce items, restock Racks, or increase customer arrivals.

For checkout management and customer sales, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">beginner guide</Link>. If finished products are not reaching Racks, use the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">Rack restocking guide</Link>.

</section>

## Which Master Worker Should You Hire First?

The **Master Blacksmith** is the priority when you want to begin collecting Set Blueprints, because the special Siege Machine orders depend on that hire.

For an established workshop, the better choice is often the specialist whose ability solves an existing limitation. Tier 3 Smelters matter when Ingot production is falling behind; the Master Cashier matters when customers are leaving checkout queues; the Master Scholar becomes more useful when you have Blueprints ready for additional upgrades.

Do not recruit the remaining Masters just because their hiring options have opened. First confirm that you can use the equipment, materials, or storage capacity their abilities provide.

## How Do You Unlock Siege Machines in Blacksmith Master?

**Siege Machines become available through the Master Blacksmith.**

Once hired, this specialist unlocks new Merchant Orders involving large siege constructions.

Version 1.0 adds five Siege Machine types. The available projects include Ballistas, Battering Rams, Cannons, and Catapults.

Unlike an ordinary crafted weapon or tool, a Siege Machine is a much larger order with more than one hundred pieces.

You do not need to treat it as a normal item Blueprint that must be purchased from the standard Blueprints menu before construction begins.

### Step 1 — Find a Siege Machine Order With a Mystery Blueprint Reward

After hiring the Master Blacksmith, open the available special Merchant Orders and inspect their rewards.

Choose an eligible Siege Machine project that offers a Mystery Blueprint. These special orders are separate from selecting an ordinary weapon or tool Blueprint, and not every large Merchant Offer necessarily provides the same reward.

### Step 2 — Let the Workers Produce the Required Pieces

After accepting a Siege Machine order, watch its progress.

These constructions require many pieces, so they do not finish as quickly as an ordinary item order.

A Ballista project can display a completion percentage while the workers continue contributing pieces.

In a large workshop, production can continue normally while the Siege Machine order progresses.

You do not need to find a conventional Ballista Blueprint among your ordinary weapons.

Likewise, you do not need to build a separate workshop floor just to place a finished Siege Machine before fulfilling its order.

Check the special order's progress rather than the ordinary Blueprint list.

### Step 3 — Complete the Order and Claim the Reward

When the required pieces are finished, the Siege Machine order completes.

The completed order can reward a Mystery Blueprint.

Claim the reward and check which Set Item has been unlocked.

The reward may be more important than an immediate Gold payout, especially if you are still missing many of the 36 new Set Items.

Before starting the next project, check the newly unlocked item and your current Set collection.

## How Do You Get Mystery Blueprints?

Mystery Blueprints are special rewards tied to Siege Machine orders.

They differ from ordinary Blueprint unlocks obtained during earlier progression.

An ordinary Blueprint unlock lets you choose from available items. A Siege Machine reward reveals a new Set Blueprint when the special project is completed.

### What Happens After You Claim a Mystery Blueprint?

After the reward appears:

1. Claim the revealed Blueprint.
2. Open the Blueprints menu.
3. Check the **Sets** category.
4. Identify which Set Item was unlocked.
5. Continue collecting the other items belonging to that Set.

A completed Ballista order, for example, revealed a **Shaman Bow**.

The Shaman Set still required additional items, including the **Shaman Shield** and **Shaman Scepter**.

Obtaining the Bow was useful progress, but it did not automatically complete the full Set.

### Can You Choose the Exact Set Item?

A Mystery Blueprint reveals a reward when the special order is completed.

Do not rely on a particular Siege Machine order to provide a named Set Item unless its reward information explicitly identifies that item.

Track which Set pieces you receive and continue fulfilling eligible orders for the remaining collection.

This is more reliable than assuming that every Ballista, Cannon, or Catapult will always award a specific Set.

Complete eligible **Siege Machine merchant orders** to obtain Mystery Blueprints for Set Items.

If a Set item is locked, its tooltip may tell you to complete more Merchant Offers. Check the missing item in the Sets menu and continue working toward the special Blueprint rewards.

### How Do You Complete a Set?

Start by obtaining one of the Set Blueprints.

Then inspect the remaining pieces associated with the same Set.

For example, the Shaman Set includes:

- Shaman Bow
- Shaman Shield
- Shaman Scepter

Continue completing Siege Machine orders until the missing Set Blueprints become available.

Once all three Shaman Blueprints have been obtained, the Set's collection is complete. You can then check the materials and crafting requirements for each unlocked item.

### Why Does Your Sets Collection Look Incomplete?

Obtaining several Mystery Blueprints does not necessarily complete one particular Set.

You may collect items belonging to different Sets before receiving every piece of a single collection.

Keep checking the Sets category rather than relying on the total number of Mystery Blueprints earned.

That still represents collection progress.

The important target for full collection is to unlock the missing item Blueprints, not simply finish a particular number of Siege Machine orders.

### Does a Completed Set Mean All Three Items Have Been Crafted?

No. Collecting all three Set Blueprints and physically crafting all three items are separate tasks.

After unlocking a complete Set, open the individual items to check their material requirements. Some may still depend on advanced resources or manufacturing equipment that your workshop has not prepared.

If your immediate objective is collecting Blueprints, check which Set items are unlocked rather than counting how many finished weapons or shields you have produced.

## Do Set Blueprints Count Toward Full Deck?

Yes. **Full Deck** requires every Blueprint, including the Set Blueprints obtained from Siege Machine orders.

If you have unlocked your ordinary items but still have missing Set pieces, continue collecting Mystery Blueprints. For the final Full Deck and A True Master completion checks, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/achievements">Blacksmith Master 72 Achievements checklist</Link>.

## Why Are Master Workers or Siege Machine Rewards Still Locked?

There are three different points where progress can stop.

A **locked hiring card** means the selected Master's requirements or hiring cost have not been met. An **absent Siege Machine order** calls for checking whether the Master Blacksmith has actually been recruited. A **missing Mystery Blueprint reward** should be checked against the special order's completion status.

Set Items have their own collection status. A tooltip telling you to complete more Merchant Offers means that item remains locked, even if another piece from the same Set has already been claimed.

If the newly available equipment exists but cannot produce anything, inspect its materials and worker assignments through the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">workshop production checks</Link>.

</section>

If you still need earlier resource or crafting unlocks, return to the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/mastery-tree">Mastery Tree guide</Link>.
