<GuideQuickAnswer title="What Should You Unlock First in Blacksmith Master?">
Start with **Carpentry and Assistants**, then work toward **Construction** and a reliable supply of Oak Wood and Iron. Once basic production can support further expansion, unlock the Store and begin Scholar research. Prioritize **Advanced Metalworking** when you want combined items, or improve Smelting first if Ingot production is holding you back. Save Trade Routes, Pottery, Jewelry, and specialized late-game systems for when you can supply their materials.

The **Mastery Tree** determines how your Blacksmith Master workshop develops. You begin with simple products and a small operation, then gradually unlock more workers, buildings, resources, production methods, and item categories.

The difficult part is deciding which unlock will actually help you next.

You might have enough Mastery progress to open a new production system but not enough money or materials to operate it. Alternatively, you may have a productive workshop that cannot make the next category of items because the necessary Mastery is still locked.

A useful progression plan follows the problems your workshop is ready to solve. Early unlocks establish basic production, the middle of the tree expands your materials and research, and later unlocks open specialized manufacturing and Trade Routes.

## How Do You Unlock More Masteries in Blacksmith Master?

The Mastery Tree displays a crafting requirement at the top of the screen. Early on, this can mean producing a certain number of items at **Normal tier or higher**; later requirements can demand higher-quality items.

Watch the counter as your workshop produces goods. If it stops increasing, compare the required tier with the items currently being crafted before accepting another large order.

### Check the Required Tier Before Starting Another Order

Open the Mastery Tree and inspect the next requirement at the top of the menu.

Then check which items your workers can currently produce.

If you are not making progress toward the next unlock, compare the items being crafted with the tier required by the current Mastery goal.

Do not assume that a large order of inexpensive items will always advance the tree as effectively as a smaller order of items that meet its requirement.

When you reach a new tier of progression, check your available Blueprints again.

The next useful unlock may depend on producing items you were not using earlier.

### Mastery Unlocks vs. Blueprint Unlocks vs. Design Points

These progression systems serve different purposes:

- **Mastery unlocks** open new equipment, resources, workers, and production systems after you meet the current crafting requirement.
- **Blueprint unlocks** let you choose new items when an unlock becomes available in the Blueprints menu.
- **Design Points** are used to improve the quality of Blueprints you already own.

You can advance one system while falling behind in another.

For example, you might have enough Mastery progress to unlock Advanced Metalworking but still lack the Blueprint needed for the combined item you want to make.

You may also have several unlocked Blueprints but insufficient Design Points to improve their quality.

When progression stalls, check whether you need another Mastery unlock, a new item Blueprint, or more Design Points.

## What Is the Best Blacksmith Master Unlock Order?

There is no single route that solves every workshop's problems in the same order.

The early game rewards a few practical unlocks, but later decisions depend on whether your main limitation is production space, materials, research, or the ability to sell what you make.

This is a progression priority rather than a requirement to purchase every listed node consecutively. Follow the connections available on your current Mastery Tree, and take the prerequisite nodes needed to reach your next target.

The most important decisions occur when two useful branches become available at roughly the same time.

### Carpentry — Open Wooden Item Production

Carpentry becomes available early and gives you access to the Carpentry Workbench and wooden products. It provides another group of items to manufacture while the workshop's metalworking equipment is occupied.

Once Carpentry is unlocked, the more important decision is whether to develop a Wood supply, increase the number of workers, or reserve money for the next production station. For the opening Mace order and the first workbench purchase, follow the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">first-day instructions</Link>.

### Assistants — Reduce the Work That Interrupts Crafting

**Assistants** unlock workers who can transport items, restock materials, and produce Ingots.

Prioritize this unlock when deliveries or resource handling are beginning to interrupt crafting. You do not need to hire several Assistants immediately; first identify what part of your production chain needs support.

For the specific jobs, worker assignments, and resource delivery setup, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">workshop layout and production guide</Link>.

### Construction — Expand Before the Starting Room Becomes a Problem

**Construction** allows you to build and alter the workshop using additional walls and floors.

It becomes valuable when you need room for more crafting stations, staff facilities, or a different working arrangement.

You do not need to spend money creating a large building immediately after unlocking Construction.

Expand enough to support the next useful machines, then continue earning money.

The Basement and additional building levels have their own later progression requirements. Construction does not automatically provide every possible floor expansion.

## Should You Unlock the Oak Forest, Iron Mine, or Store First?

This is one of the most important early decisions because each option addresses a different problem.

The **Oak Forest** helps establish a continuing supply of wood.

The **Iron Mine** creates a source of Iron Ore for your metal production.

The **Store** introduces retail sales through displayed products and customers.

All three are useful, but they are not interchangeable.

### Oak Forest — When Wood Is Becoming an Ongoing Expense

Choose the Oak Forest when you expect to keep producing wooden items and manual wood purchases are becoming inconvenient.

Once the Forest is available, hire Lumberjacks to begin collecting wood.

The collected resources then become part of your workshop's supply system.

If Carpentry is already supplying a steady stream of Merchant Offers, the Oak Forest can make more sense than opening retail immediately. The Forest supports continued Wood gathering, while the Store introduces another destination for products that still need to be crafted.

It is especially reasonable if you already have Carpentry workers making items regularly.

### Iron Mine — When Metal Production Needs a Reliable Supply

The Iron Mine becomes more useful as you expand beyond a small number of metalworking orders.

Unlocking the Mine gives you access to Iron Ore production, while Smelting allows you to turn Ore into Ingots.

The two systems work together.

Opening a Mine without being ready to process and transport its resources may not immediately improve the workshop.

Likewise, expanding your metalworking workforce without enough Ingots creates a shortage further along the production chain.

When you are considering Iron Mine and Smelting upgrades, ask whether your current metalworking output is limited by purchased materials or by the amount your workers can process.

If the equipment is already available but supplies keep running out, use the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">Ore and Ingot troubleshooting steps</Link> before buying unrelated upgrades.

### Store — When You Can Supply Retail Stock

The **Store** unlocks retail sales through Racks and Checkout Counters, but it also creates demand for products that would otherwise go toward Merchant Offers.

If your workshop is still struggling to maintain basic materials or complete existing orders, establishing the Oak Forest or Iron Mine first may be more useful.

Unlock the Store when you can reserve some crafting output for customers without disrupting essential production. For pricing, Cashiers, customer management, and early sales decisions, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master">Blacksmith Master beginner guide</Link>.

## How Do You Unlock More Blueprints and Research Upgrades?

Unlocking new product categories and improving existing Blueprints are separate parts of progression.

The distinction becomes important once your workshop can produce more than basic wooden and metal items.

You may have access to a new material but no useful Blueprint for it. Alternatively, you may have several profitable products that could become more valuable through research.

### How Do You Get New Blueprint Unlocks?

Look for **Merchant Offers with Blueprint rewards**.

Not every offer is equally useful for progression.

Some primarily provide money. Others include rewards that help unlock new items or advance other systems.

When you need another Blueprint, inspect the reward before accepting the next offer.

When the Blueprints menu shows that a new unlock is available, choose an item you can produce and check the material cost displayed beneath its card.

Do not automatically unlock an item just because it appears at the top of the list.

Check whether you have the required material and production capability.

For example, a combined metal-and-wood product may be an attractive unlock, but you cannot take full advantage of it until the appropriate crafting system is available.

An item you can begin making immediately often provides more short-term value than an advanced Blueprint whose materials remain inaccessible.

### What Do Scholars Actually Do?

**Scholars generate Design Points**, which support Blueprint quality upgrades.

After unlocking the relevant research system, hire a Scholar and provide the research equipment needed for the work.

Once your first Scholar is producing Design Points, choose an existing Blueprint that can benefit from an upgrade. Compare the available research with the cost of employing another Scholar; expanding the research workforce is most useful when you already have several Blueprints waiting for improvements.

As research expands, you can add more Scholars and improve their ability to generate Design Points.

But research workers also add to the operating cost of the business. There is little reason to expand research indefinitely if you cannot yet use the points being produced.

### Which Blueprints Should You Upgrade First?

Begin with products you are already making regularly.

An upgrade is more immediately useful when the improved Blueprint belongs to an item that is being produced and sold.

For example, a workshop consistently making Wooden Clubs, Iron Goblets, and Tankards can improve those existing products instead of spending all its Design Points on rarely produced items.

This is not a fixed ranking of the most profitable items in the game.

The better question is:

**Which Blueprint will actually be used enough to benefit from the upgrade?**

Consider three factors:

1. Whether the required materials are available.
2. Whether workers are already producing the item.
3. Whether the item is regularly needed for sales or Merchant Offers.

When a newly unlocked product becomes an important part of your output, it can become the next research priority.

### When Should You Add More Scholars?

Add another Scholar when several Blueprints need upgrades and your current Design Point income cannot keep up with the cost.

If one Scholar already generates enough points for the upgrades you are making, hiring another may only increase wages without providing an immediate benefit.

When working toward **A True Master**, check the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/achievements">requirement to upgrade every Blueprint to at least Rare quality</Link>.

## Advanced Metalworking or Better Smelting: Which Should You Unlock First?

These unlocks solve different production problems.

**Advanced Metalworking** expands the kinds of items you can craft, including products that require multiple components.

Improved **Smelting** opens more advanced Ingot production and supports a larger variety of metalworking recipes.

The best choice depends on what your workshop needs next.

### Choose Advanced Metalworking for Combined Items

Advanced Metalworking becomes valuable when you want to move beyond simple products and begin crafting items with multiple parts.

Products such as the Short Hoe require both metal and wood.

This introduces additional production requirements, including the Workbench used for assembly.

Choose this direction when your existing metal and wood supplies are stable and you want access to more advanced item Blueprints.

Before purchasing the next item Blueprint, check that you can produce every component it requires.

Unlocking the item itself does not remove the need for its materials and crafting stations.

### Choose Smelting Upgrades When Ingots Limit Production

Improved Smelting is the more practical priority when your workshop needs greater Ingot capacity or access to more advanced metal materials.

This can become important before you have unlocked every combined-item Blueprint.

If your Blacksmiths are already waiting for Ingots, adding more complicated recipes may simply create additional demand.

Improve material production first, then return to Advanced Metalworking when the required components can be supplied.

### When Do Steel, Bronze, and Sterling Silver Become Important?

As your metalworking progression develops, you gain access to more advanced Ingot types.

These include **Steel**, **Bronze**, and **Sterling Silver**, with larger Ingot recipes becoming available through further progression.

The material alone is not the entire unlock.

To make useful products, you also need the corresponding Blueprints and access to the required resources.

When choosing between new metal processing and a new resource source, inspect the recipes you actually want to produce next.

If your next group of items requires Copper, there is limited immediate benefit in investing only in Silver production.

If a desired item requires a larger or more advanced Ingot, make sure that stage of Smelting is available first.

## How Do You Unlock Copper, Silver, Pine Wood, and Trade Routes?

Later progression introduces additional resources that expand the products your workshop can make.

Some are associated with Mines and Forests. Others require Trade Routes.

Unlocking new resources should follow a clear production goal rather than simply selecting every available source as soon as it appears.

### Copper Mine and Silver Mine

The Copper Mine and Silver Mine expand your available metals.

Copper supports a different group of Ingots and products from Iron, while Silver becomes useful for more advanced crafting and later Jewelry.

When you unlock a new Mine, check which Blueprints can use the material.

You may need to unlock additional items before the new resource contributes much to your current production.

Do not assume that opening a Mine immediately makes its entire product category available.

### Pine Forest

The Pine Forest introduces Pine Wood as another material for woodworking and combined items.

As with other resource unlocks, its value depends on what your workers are ready to produce.

If you have several Pine Wood recipes waiting to be made, unlocking the Forest can support that transition.

If you still have a large backlog of basic items and no immediate use for Pine Wood, another production upgrade may be more useful first.

### How Do You Unlock Trade Routes?

The **Trade Routes** Mastery opens access to the trading system and its associated equipment.

Once available, you can establish individual routes for additional resources.

These include sources associated with:

- Clay
- Birch Wood
- Gems
- Gold

The individual routes are not automatically active just because you unlocked the overall Trade Routes system.

You must establish the resource route you want to use.

### What Should You Do After Establishing a Route?

Check the items requested by that route and the resource it will provide.

Trade Routes exchange produced goods for incoming materials.

This means the route needs a supply of suitable finished products before it can begin contributing resources.

Place a Trade Route Crate near your Blacksmiths and assign workers to produce the items required by the selected route.

Once enough items have been crafted for the trade, the offered resources arrive at the beginning of the next day.

Choose a route whose reward supports an item category you are ready to use.

There is little benefit in establishing several expensive routes simultaneously if your workshop cannot yet produce the goods needed to support them.

## Should You Unlock Pottery or Jewelry First?

Pottery and Jewelry are both later crafting categories, but they depend on different materials.

The most practical route is to unlock the category whose required resources you can already obtain.

### Pottery — Start With a Clay Supply

Pottery becomes a natural next step once you have established access to Clay.

For a Clay-first Pottery route:

1. Unlock the Trade Routes system.
2. Establish the Clay Pit route.
3. Unlock Pottery.
4. Begin collecting the required Clay.
5. Unlock and produce Pottery Blueprints.
6. Expand into additional Pottery products as production becomes sustainable.

Start with a Pottery Blueprint whose Clay requirement you can meet, then expand into more elaborate items as additional materials become available.

That sequence is useful because it avoids opening a manufacturing category before obtaining its main resource.

Further Pottery upgrades provide access to more elaborate items, including decorated varieties.

Choose these when the required crafting capabilities and materials are already available.

### Jewelry — Prepare Metals and Gems First

Jewelry becomes more practical once you have access to the metals and special resources needed for its recipes.

Depending on the item, that may involve Silver, Gold, or Gems.

For Gem-based products, make sure the relevant Trade Route is established before selecting Blueprints that cannot be supplied.

A Gem-based Jewelry Blueprint remains unusable while its required Gem supply is locked. Check the Gem Mine Trade Route before investing in more Jewelry production.

Opening the Jewelry system did not automatically make every required material available.

Check both the Blueprint and its resource requirements before committing to the next Jewelry unlock.

### Which Category Is Better for Progression?

If you already have a reliable Clay supply and can begin producing Pottery, that is often the more straightforward next step.

If your workshop has strong metal production and access to the resources needed for Jewelry, Jewelry may fit your next development stage.

Neither category should be selected solely because it is farther along the Mastery Tree.

A useful unlock is one that your workshop can support immediately or after a clearly identified next step.

## What Should You Unlock in the Late Game?

As the basic production systems become available, the remaining Mastery Tree shifts toward advanced materials, improved capabilities, and specialized systems.

At this stage, it is useful to separate three goals:

- Completing the remaining Mastery nodes.
- Unlocking and improving the remaining ordinary Blueprints.
- Developing the specialized systems introduced in version 1.0.

These goals are related, but they do not always progress at the same speed.

### Finish the Mastery Tree Without Neglecting Production

Once you have access to advanced materials and stable crafting output, continue completing the requirements shown in the Mastery menu.

You may still have optional upgrades, additional floors, or other nodes available.

Do not assume that completing the Mastery Tree also means every Blueprint has been unlocked or upgraded.

The Mastery Tree is therefore one major milestone, not the end of every progression system.

### When Should You Work Toward Master Workers?

Master Workers become a later progression goal once your workshop has developed enough to meet their specialized hiring requirements.

You can begin preparing for those requirements while finishing the remaining Mastery nodes. Check which conditions are already satisfied before committing more resources to the next unlock.

For the individual hiring requirements, special abilities, Siege Machines, and Mystery Blueprints, see the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/master-workers">Master Workers and Siege Machines guide</Link>.

### Is a Completed Mastery Tree the End of Progression?

No. Completing the Mastery Tree confirms that its unlocks are finished, but you may still be missing item Blueprints or quality upgrades. The remaining requirements are separate from the crafting counter used to earn Mastery unlocks.

If your goal is 100%, check the unfinished conditions in the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/achievements">72-achievement checklist</Link> instead of continuing to craft for a Mastery unlock you have already earned.

## Why Are You Stuck in Blacksmith Master Progression?

When progress stops, determine whether you need another Mastery unlock, a new item Blueprint, or more Design Points for an upgrade.

A new product might require a material source, a Blueprint reward, a research upgrade, or a crafting system you have not opened yet.

### How Do You Find the Missing Unlock?

Start with the requirement shown on the screen where progress has stopped.

If the Mastery Tree requires more items of a particular tier, craft products that meet that tier. If the Blueprints menu shows no available unlock, look for another Merchant Offer that grants one.

If you already own the Blueprint but cannot upgrade its quality, check your Design Points and available research upgrades. If a new item requires unavailable materials, inspect the relevant Mine, Forest, Smelting, or Trade Route unlock.

When the required systems are already unlocked but workers cannot produce the item, check the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">workshop production and delivery steps</Link> instead of spending another Mastery unlock.

<section id="next-steps" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Do After Your Next Mastery Unlock?

After spending a Mastery unlock, open the relevant Blueprint or building menu and confirm what actually became available. A newly unlocked Mine still needs workers; an advanced item may require another Ingot recipe; a Trade Route needs the goods requested for its exchange.

If the new system cannot operate yet, identify the missing requirement before purchasing more equipment. For production and transport failures, use the <Link href="/blacksmith-master/workshop-layout">workshop troubleshooting steps</Link>. The <Link href="/blacksmith-master/master-workers">Master Workers</Link> become the next major target when you are ready for specialized late-game abilities.
