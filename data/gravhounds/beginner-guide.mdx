Your first shift in Gravhounds teaches you how to build, mine, defend, and extract. These jobs depend on one another: without a working Pipeline, you cannot set up your first Fabricator; without delivered resources, you cannot fund your defenses; and without a defended Collector, you may not make it through launch.

The most confusing parts are usually placing the first building, getting mined resources into the Collector, and recognizing when to stop fighting and board for extraction.

<section id="start-first-shift">

## How Do You Start Your First Shift in Gravhounds?

After the opening sequence, follow the instruction to report to the **Holo Table** aboard the ship. This starts your first shift and introduces the Collector, which serves as the center of your landing zone.

The initial objective is not to explore the entire planet or defeat every enemy. You need to establish a working base around the Collector before you can complete the resource and defense tasks.

The opening sequence follows this order:

1. Connect the Collector's Pipeline.
2. Place a Fabricator on a valid build node.
3. Mine and deliver the required resources.
4. Build a Turret and defend the landing zone.
5. Repair damaged defenses.
6. Initiate Launch Prep and return to the Collector.

Pay attention to the objective instructions as each step is completed. The tutorial introduces new tools and interactions in sequence, so trying to skip ahead can leave you searching for a building or action that has not been introduced yet.

<section id="pipeline-fabricator">

## How Do You Connect the Pipeline and Build the Fabricator?

Start at the **Collector**, not at the nearest resource deposit.

The Pipeline gives you places to connect buildings and defenses. Your first Fabricator needs one of those connected positions.

### Connect the first Pipeline

1. Approach the Collector and locate its Pipeline connector.
2. Use your **Grav Beam** to interact with the connector.
3. Extend and place the Pipeline as instructed.
4. Confirm the placement and wait for the connected build node to become available.

Once the connection is established, the tutorial confirms that the Pipeline is connected and its build node is ready.

If the objective does not advance, check whether you have actually confirmed the connection. Moving a connector around without finishing the placement is not enough.

### Place the Fabricator on a build node

After the Pipeline is ready, the tutorial asks you to build a **Fabricator**.

Enter placement mode, move the Fabricator over a valid position on the Pipeline, and align it with the build node. Confirm the placement when it snaps into position.

The placement point matters. Trying to place the Fabricator beside a Pipeline without aligning it to a valid node can leave the build action unavailable.

![Positioning the Fabricator over the highlighted Pipeline build node during the first shift.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-first-shift-fabricator-placement.webp)

*Position the Fabricator over the highlighted Build Node, then confirm placement to complete the assembly objective.*

If placement is still unavailable, return to the Pipeline connection and make sure the build node is active. Move the Fabricator over the highlighted position until it snaps into place, then confirm the build. The objective will not advance while the kit is merely being held in placement mode.

The Fabricator is your starting point for producing build kits. With it connected, you can begin gathering resources and preparing the Collector's defenses.

<section id="mining-resources">

## How Do You Mine Titanium and Deliver Resources?

Your first **Resource Run** objective is to locate a **Titanium** deposit, mine the material, and deliver it to the Collector.

Look for the resource indicator on your HUD before leaving the Collector. The deposit marker may be easier to find by looking around than by watching only the compass.

The important distinction is that mining a deposit and delivering its contents are separate actions.

### Mine and deliver the resource

1. Follow the marked deposit on your HUD.
2. Use your **Mining Laser** to break resources free.
3. Switch to the **Grav Beam** to pick up and move the mined material.
4. Bring the resource toward the Collector or an appropriate connected Pipeline.
5. Feed the resource into the Collector's processor port, or place it on the Pipeline so it can be transported back.

![Mining a Titanium deposit during Resource Run with the objective checklist visible.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-titanium-mining-objective.webp)

*Follow the marked Titanium deposit and use the Mining Laser before collecting the material with the Grav Beam.*

You do not need to carry every resource all the way to the Collector by hand if an accessible Pipeline can transport it.

Dropping material onto the Pipeline lets the transport system carry it back to the Collector. You can also deliver material directly into the Collector's processing area.

### Why isn't your resource delivery counting?

If you have mined enough material but the objective has not advanced, check where the pieces actually landed.

Throwing a resource toward the Collector does not help if it misses the processor or fails to reach the Pipeline.

Move closer, use the Grav Beam to reposition the piece, and watch whether it enters the collection system.

If the resource marker appears to have disappeared, check the objective indicator and the area around your last deposit. Avoid wasting time mining unrelated materials when the tutorial is asking for a specific delivery.

Successful delivery provides build funds that you can spend on defenses through the Fabricator.

<section id="first-turret">

## How Do You Build and Repair Your First Turret?

Once you have delivered resources and earned build funds, return to the Fabricator.

Your next priority is protecting the Collector. Enemy waves begin attacking the landing zone, and the Turret lets your defenses handle some of the fighting while you move or gather resources.

### Build your first Turret

1. Interact with the **Fabricator**.
2. Purchase a **Turret Build Kit** when prompted.
3. Use the Grav Beam to pick up the kit.
4. Switch to placement mode.
5. Position the Turret over an available build point and place it.

The Turret begins contributing to your defense once it has been placed successfully.

Place your first defense where it can protect the Collector rather than putting it far away simply because you have room to build.

You can expand your defensive layout later, but the immediate objective is to keep the landing zone secure.

### Use Drone View to locate incoming enemies

When the tutorial announces enemy movement, switch to **Drone View**.

This provides an overhead look at the surrounding area, with nearby detected enemies marked in red.

Use it to identify which side is under threat before running away from the Collector. If the attack is approaching your existing defenses, you may be better off supporting those Turrets than chasing enemies at a distant location.

When a wave ends, use the breathing room to gather additional resources, prepare defenses, or repair structures that took damage.

### Repair damaged Turrets with the Mining Laser

The Mining Laser is not only for gathering resources. It can also repair damaged defensive structures.

When a Turret takes damage:

1. Return to the damaged structure.
2. Equip your Mining Laser.
3. Aim at the Turret and use the repair interaction.
4. Continue until the structure is repaired sufficiently to survive the next attack.

Do not assume that a Turret will remain effective without maintenance. A damaged defense left unattended can leave the Collector exposed during the next wave.

### Be careful around incoming Reward Pods

Reward Pods can arrive during combat and offer upgrades, including weapon-related improvements.

However, the incoming pod itself is a physical drop. Do not stand underneath its landing position while waiting to collect your reward.

A falling pod can be dangerous if it lands on you.

Collect useful rewards when it is safe, then return your attention to defending the Collector. You do not need to chase every reward immediately if enemy pressure is already threatening the landing zone.

<section id="launch-prep">

## How Do You Start Launch Prep and Finish the First Shift?

After completing the required resource and defense objectives, the tutorial allows you to begin the launch process.

This is not an instant extraction. Activating **Launch Prep** starts another defense period while the Collector prepares to leave.

### Start the launch sequence

Before activating the terminal, check that your nearby Turrets are still operational and repair any structures that need attention.

Then:

1. Return to the Collector.
2. Find one of the terminals used to initiate Launch Prep.
3. Activate the launch preparation sequence.
4. Defend the Collector while the engines warm up and the final enemy wave arrives.
5. Return to the Collector when the game announces that launch is ready.


![Launch Prep underway during the first shift before the final extraction countdown.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-launch-prep-underway.webp)

*Launch Prep has started. Keep the Collector defended until the game instructs you to return for liftoff.*


The final wave can bring sustained pressure. Keep enemies away from the Collector and repair a threatened Turret when you can safely do so.

If your defenses are holding, there is no need to chase every remaining enemy far away from the extraction area.

### Don't miss the final boarding countdown

Completing the defense is not the last action.

Once Launch Prep finishes, the game tells you to return to the Collector. Move back immediately and board before liftoff.

This is an easy mistake to make in multiplayer: players can remain outside fighting or building while the launch countdown continues.

Do not spend the last moments placing another Turret or collecting unnecessary resources when the Collector is preparing to depart.

The safest sequence is to finish the objective, defend the final wave, return to the Collector when instructed, and remain aboard until departure.

A successful extraction returns you to the ship, where the next stage of progression begins.

## How Do You Use the Research Lab After Your First Shift?

Returning from the opening mission brings the ship's Research Lab online. The next instruction is to unlock a new **Turret design**, giving you more defensive options for subsequent shifts.

Open the **Turrets** category and inspect the available designs. Check their research and material requirements, then choose one you can afford. Rocket and Laser Turrets are among the early options, but the important step here is completing the initial unlock rather than committing to a particular long-term setup.

Once you've purchased a design, you're ready to leave the first-shift tutorial behind. **Master Control** is where you select another destination and see what that mission requires.

New Mods can also become available as you progress. When you're ready to change your selected rewards, follow the [Mod Deck Builder instructions](/gravhounds/mod-deck-builder). For resource objectives, Fuel Pump defense, and later missions, see the [Gravhounds progression guide](/gravhounds).
