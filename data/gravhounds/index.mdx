After the tutorial, complete your first Turret research unlock and open **Master Control**. Check the next planet's mission objective, resource type, and remaining Blueprints before deploying. On the planet, protect the structure or target required by the mission rather than defending only the Collector.

Finishing the opening shift gets your ship moving again, but the next planets introduce more than a larger version of the tutorial. Some missions require specific resources, others involve defending a Fuel Pump, and certain objectives send you away from your base to recover or escort something.

The most important decision is knowing what must survive and what actually completes the job. A strong Collector defense will not save an unprotected Fuel Pump, and an escort mission can fail even when the base is untouched.

If you are still learning how to connect Pipelines, place a Fabricator, mine Titanium, or board the Collector, start with the [Gravhounds beginner guide](/gravhounds/beginner-guide). Once those basics are familiar, use your current objective to decide what comes next.

## What Should You Do After Your First Shift?

Your first extraction restores the ship's research options. Once you've completed the initial Turret unlock, the next important decision is which planetary assignment to take and what that assignment will require you to protect or collect.

### Return to Master Control

Once you have finished your initial research, return to **Master Control** to select the next destination.

Check the information displayed for the planet before starting the mission. In addition to the mission objective, the destination interface can indicate remaining Blueprint collectibles.


![Yorezen Basin mission selection showing Resource Grab, Organic Nitrate, challenge rating, and one remaining Blueprint.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-planet-selection-objectives.webp)

*Yorezen Basin shows the mission type, available resource, challenge rating, and remaining Blueprint count before deployment.*


The next task may involve gathering a particular resource, protecting a structure, or completing another objective before extraction becomes available.

The first shift establishes the basic controls. From this point onward, the mission objective determines how you should use those controls.

<section id="mission-objectives">

## How Do You Complete Missions and Unlock the Next Destination?

Each shift has an objective that must be satisfied before you can leave.

You can continue fighting, mining, or investigating points of interest while a mission is active, but clearing enemy waves alone does not necessarily finish the job.

### Check what the current mission requires

Different missions change what you need to protect or deliver.

In a resource collection mission, your main job is to gather the requested material and deliver enough of it to meet the objective.

For example, an early mission asks you to collect **Nitrate**. Other destinations can have different resource requirements, so do not assume that Titanium is always the material you need.

In a Fuel Pump mission, the objective involves connecting the Pump, filling its tank, and keeping it intact during the transfer.

An escort objective instead requires protecting a moving target until she reaches the Collector.

The underlying building and combat systems are familiar, but these objectives require different preparation.

### What Actually Unlocks Extraction?

Extraction becomes available when the required objective is complete: the requested resource amount has been delivered, the Fuel Pump has filled its tank, or Coffee Cat has reached the Collector. Surviving an enemy wave alone does not satisfy those objectives.

Once the game confirms completion, start **Launch Prep** at the Collector and hold through the final attack until boarding is announced. The [beginner guide](/gravhounds/beginner-guide) covers the first-time terminal and boarding interactions.

### What happens if a mission-critical structure is destroyed?

Protect the structure named in your current objective as well as the Collector.

Losing a mission-critical structure can end the attempt even if your character remains alive. The Fuel Pump is a particularly important example because enemies can attack it while also threatening the rest of your base.

If a task repeatedly fails, identify which objective was lost before changing your weapons or researching more upgrades.

A mission that fails because the Pump is unprotected needs a different setup from one where you simply cannot gather enough resources.

<section id="research">

## Which Research Upgrades Should You Prioritize?

Research becomes more useful when it addresses something that prevented you from finishing the last shift. Stronger Turrets help when defenses collapse under pressure, while Health or Movement Training may be more relevant when reaching an objective is the difficult part. Weapon upgrades and mining improvements solve different problems again.

Before spending accumulated Research Points, compare the requirements of the upgrades you can currently purchase. An attractive upgrade is not necessarily your best immediate choice if it consumes resources needed for another unlock.


![Research Lab Turrets tab showing the Base Turret upgrade tree, current level, and Research Point requirement.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-research-base-turret-upgrade.webp)

*The Base Turret is shown at LVL 1, with its next upgrade costing one Research Point. Check each upgrade's requirements before spending your points.*

### Why can't you purchase an available upgrade?

An upgrade can remain unavailable even when its research node is visible. Check both the Research Point cost and any additional materials shown beside the selected upgrade.

The Base Turret example above shows a **LVL 1 → 2** upgrade requiring one Research Point. Other upgrades can have different requirements. If either resource is insufficient, look for an affordable alternative or continue gathering what the next unlock needs.

### Should you unlock Weapons, Turrets, or Training first?

The result of your last mission is a useful starting point. Losing a Collector or Fuel Pump suggests that defensive coverage needs attention; being repeatedly downed away from the base makes survivability or movement more valuable. When resource gathering consumes most of the shift, mining-related upgrades become more attractive.

Additional weapons are useful when your own damage output is the limiting factor, but they cannot make up for leaving a mission target exposed. Choose the next research purchase for the problem you actually encountered rather than following a fixed order of upgrades.

### What about Mods?

Before deploying with newly researched Mods, check your selection using the [Mod Deck Builder instructions](/gravhounds/mod-deck-builder).

<section id="blueprints">

## How Do You Find and Scan Blueprints in Gravhounds?

Blueprints are collectibles you can encounter while exploring mission areas.

Before deploying, check whether the selected destination still shows any remaining Blueprints. This helps you decide whether to investigate points of interest while completing the main objective.

Some Blueprints are found away from the Collector, so restricting your entire shift to the landing zone can mean missing them.

### Scan a Blueprint when you find one

When you locate a Blueprint, approach it and check the available interaction.

In the early missions, the collection process can involve picking up the Blueprint and using its **Scan** interaction while holding it.

A practical sequence is:

1. Locate the Blueprint during exploration.
2. Pick it up using the available interaction.
3. Check for the Scan prompt while holding it.
4. Activate the scan and confirm that the interaction completes.
5. Check the destination's Blueprint progress when you return to mission selection.

Do not assume that simply seeing a Blueprint or moving it toward the Collector means its collection has registered.

![A glowing Turret Blueprint with the confirmation message that it has been scanned.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-blueprint-scan.webp)

*The "TURRET BLUEPRINT HAS BEEN SCANNED" message confirms that the scan succeeded.*

If the item is still in your hands and offers a Scan interaction, complete that step before leaving it behind.

### Should you collect every Blueprint before extracting?

Check the danger around the area first.

Points of interest can contain collectibles, but enemy waves continue to threaten your mission-critical structures while you explore.

If your Collector is already under attack or the team has not established defenses, securing the mission objective is more important than wandering farther away.

The destination's remaining-Blueprint indicator is useful for tracking what you have missed. It is not a reason to abandon a failing defense.

<section id="fuel-pump">

## How Do You Complete the Fuel Pump Defense Mission?

The Fuel Pump objective requires you to connect the Pump, fill its tank, and defend the structure during the transfer.

The most dangerous mistake is starting the Pump while your defenses are still concentrated entirely around the Collector.

Enemies can converge on the Pump itself. If you are still placing Turrets when the transfer begins, you may not have enough time to protect both locations.

### Set up defenses before starting the transfer

When the mission introduces the Fuel Pump, locate it and identify the route back toward your existing Pipeline network.

Prepare the connection, but do not rush to activate the transfer.

A safer order is:

1. Establish your initial Fabricator and protect the Collector.
2. Gather enough resources and build funds to expand your defenses.
3. Extend the Pipeline toward the Fuel Pump.
4. Place Turrets where they can protect the Pump and its approaches.
5. Check the Collector's defenses before committing to the objective.
6. Activate the Fuel Pump once the essential defenses are ready.

The Pump can be connected and prepared before you activate its transfer. Use that opportunity to improve its protection rather than triggering the objective immediately.

If you are playing cooperatively, make sure someone can respond to attacks on the Collector while other players protect the Pump.

### Defend the Pump until its tank is full

After activation, the Pump begins filling while enemies approach.


![Fuel Pump defense mission showing incoming threats and the instruction to protect the Pump until its tank is full.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-fuel-pump-defense.webp)

*Incoming threats appear during the fuel transfer. Protect the Pump until the tank is full.*


Watch both the Pump and the Collector. The mission can become difficult when several structures are under attack at once.

Keep defensive coverage near the Pump throughout the transfer. If enemies begin attacking from another direction, adjust your position instead of assuming your original Turret placement covers every approach.

The progress announcements indicate when the tank is partway filled and when the transfer is complete.

Do not leave the Pump unattended simply because the tank is already past halfway.

### What Happens When the Fuel Pump Tank Is Full?

Wait for the announcement that the Pump has filled completely. The crew can then return to the Collector and begin Launch Prep, but extraction still requires surviving the final wave and boarding successfully.

A failed attempt before the tank fills usually calls for a change in defensive positioning. Give the Pump its own Turret coverage instead of concentrating everything around the Collector. During the transfer, respond to attacks on whichever structure is exposed and repair damaged defenses when it is safe.

<section id="coffee-cat">

## How Do You Complete the Coffee Cat Escort?

**Bring Her Home** requires you to escort **Coffee Cat** safely to the Collector. Unlike a stationary defense objective, the person you need to protect moves through hostile territory. Enemies can converge around her even while the Collector remains secure, so the crew needs to follow the escort rather than waiting for every attack to reach the base.

### Get Into Position Before Coffee Cat Starts Moving

Travel to the marked point of interest and complete the required interaction when your team is ready to support the escort. Starting while other players are scattered across the map leaves Coffee Cat vulnerable before they can reach her.

For cooperative runs, bring your team close enough to respond to the first attackers and keep the route toward the Collector in mind. Once the game announces that Coffee Cat is mobile, shift your attention from exploring the area to protecting her return.

### Protect the target on the way back

Once she begins moving, follow the route toward the Collector and fight the enemies converging on her position.


![Bring Her Home mission showing the objective to escort Coffee Cat to the Collector, with progress at zero out of one.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-coffee-cat-escort-objective.webp)

*Bring Her Home requires you to escort Coffee Cat to the Collector. The 0/1 objective counter shows that she has not yet reached her destination.*

Move alongside Coffee Cat and concentrate your attacks on enemies threatening her directly. Clearing the route back to the Collector matters more than chasing hostiles that are no longer near the escort.

In multiplayer, players accompanying Coffee Cat should stay within reach when new enemies arrive. Anyone remaining at the Collector needs to be ready to help if the escort comes under heavier pressure. Losing track of her position while the group splits up can turn an otherwise manageable wave into a failed objective.

### What Happens After Coffee Cat Reaches the Collector?

The escort objective advances when Coffee Cat arrives safely. Launch Prep is the remaining challenge: defend the Collector through the final wave and board when the departure countdown begins.

If Coffee Cat died before reaching safety on an earlier attempt, avoid starting the next escort while your team is still occupied elsewhere. The priority is keeping attackers off her during the journey, not simply rebuilding a stronger base afterward. Solo players should also be prepared to move with her as soon as the escort begins.

</section>

<section id="next-shift">

## Which Problem Should You Fix Before Your Next Shift?

A successful resource mission gives you room to pursue remaining Blueprints or prepare for a harder objective. A failed Fuel Pump defense or escort requires a different response: identify where the protection broke down before spending more research on equipment that may not solve it.

Back aboard the ship, compare your available upgrades with the next destination's requirements. Prioritize the change that will let you complete that objective, then deploy with enough resources and defensive options to support it.

</section>
