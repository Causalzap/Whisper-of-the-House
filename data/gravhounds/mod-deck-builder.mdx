## How Do You Unlock the Mod Deck Builder?

The Mod Deck Builder unlocks after you research a few Mods. You do not need to complete a separate mission specifically for deck editing.

1. Complete the opening shift and return to your ship.
2. Enter the **Research Lab** and select **MODS**.
3. Research a few available Mods.
4. Watch for the **MOD DECK BUILDER UNLOCKED** notification.
5. Confirm the notification and check the **MOD LOADOUT** beneath the research tree.

![The Mod Deck Builder unlock notification explaining the selected deck and Reward Pod system.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-mod-deck-unlock.webp)

The unlock notification specifies an **11-Mod deck** in this version and explains that Reward Pods only draw Mods from your selected loadout.

Unlocking the Deck Builder gives you control over your reward pool. It does not immediately grant every researched Mod as an active upgrade during a mission.

</section>

<section id="edit-mod-deck">

## How Do You Add or Replace Mods in Your Deck?

Once Deck Builder is available, you can replace a selected Mod with another Mod you have unlocked.

This is especially important after researching a new upgrade. Simply purchasing the research does not mean your next Reward Pod will be able to offer that Mod.


To change your selected Mods:

1. Open the **Research Lab** and select the **MODS** tab.
2. Find **MOD LOADOUT** beneath the research tree.
3. Review the Mods currently selected in your deck.
4. Select an existing Mod you want to replace.
5. Choose the researched Mod you want to use instead.
6. Confirm that the replacement appears in your loadout before starting another shift.

![The Research Lab MODS tab showing the Mod research tree and the current MOD LOADOUT.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-research-lab-mod-loadout.webp)

The **MOD LOADOUT [11/11]** counter shows the selected deck in this version. Researching additional Mods gives you more options, but you must check which ones are included in your current loadout.

You are changing the available reward pool, not directly selecting every upgrade you will receive during the mission.

For example, if you unlock **Field Mechanic** but have not included it in your deck, you should not expect it to appear as a Mod reward. Replace an existing selection with Field Mechanic if you want it to become eligible.

The same principle applies to other researched Mods.

</section>

<section id="reward-pods">

## Why Aren't Your Unlocked Mods Appearing in Reward Pods?

The most important thing to understand is that **Research, Deck Builder, and Reward Pods are separate parts of the upgrade process**.

Research determines which Mods you have unlocked. Deck Builder determines which of those Mods you include in your available pool. Reward Pods offer randomized rewards during a shift.

![Industrial Grade unlock confirmation beside its next-level upgrade requirements.](/images/gravhounds/gravhounds-industrial-grade-mod-unlock.webp)

Unlocking **Industrial Grade** displays a confirmation that the Mod can now appear in Reward Pods. Its separate **LVL 1 → 2** upgrade requires additional resources. Unlocking a Mod, upgrading its level, and selecting it for your current deck are different decisions.

Check your MOD LOADOUT to confirm whether the researched Mod is actually included.

That creates three different situations:

A Mod included in your deck is eligible to appear, not guaranteed to appear in the next Reward Pod. If the choices are not what you expected, return to **MOD LOADOUT** and check the selection. A researched Mod left outside the deck is excluded from its reward pool; a selected Mod can still be absent from an individual randomized offering.

## Which Mods Should You Put in Your Deck First?

A good early deck should address the problem that is actually stopping your runs.

Some Mods improve direct damage, others help with survival, mobility, repairs, or resource economy. Choosing only by how exciting an effect sounds can leave you without the support you need when enemy pressure increases.

Several early Mod options illustrate the different roles.

<GuideTable
  columns={modChoiceColumns}
  rows={modChoiceRows}
/>

The right selection depends on what happens during your shifts. **Quick Shot** and **Big Shot** offer straightforward weapon improvements, increasing fire rate and damage respectively. **Breaker** works differently because its critical-hit bonus follows incoming damage, while **Last Stand** becomes more relevant at low health. Those conditions matter when you're deciding between dependable offensive upgrades and bonuses that only activate in dangerous situations.

Defensive problems call for a different choice. **Field Mechanic** speeds up structure repairs with the Mining Laser, making it useful when damaged Turrets are keeping you away from mining or combat. It will not compensate for leaving a Collector completely undefended, though. If you're still struggling with the basic placement and repair interactions, the [first-shift walkthrough](/gravhounds/beginner-guide) shows how those systems work.

Mobility and spending power offer another reason to change the deck. **Critical Surge** restores booster fuel through critical hits, which can help you stay mobile during fights. **Hazard Pay** provides extra credits from kills, making it more attractive during combat-heavy shifts where additional construction funds would be useful. Neither has to compete directly with a weapon-damage Mod; they address different limitations.

</section>

<section id="showstoppers">

## How Do Showstopper Mods Affect Your Choices?

Showstoppers are another part of the Mod system, and some research or training upgrades interact with them specifically.

For example, one training upgrade reduces **Showstopper Mod cooldowns**, allowing the affected abilities to become available again sooner. **Rocket Barrage** is also among the Showstopper options encountered during progression.

This creates a different consideration from ordinary weapon damage or repair-speed upgrades.

Before investing in a Showstopper-related upgrade, check whether you have access to a compatible Showstopper and whether its effect is useful for your current missions.

A cooldown improvement has little immediate value if you are not using the ability it improves.

The precise number of selectable Mods and Showstopper positions should be checked in your current Deck Builder interface rather than assumed from an older configuration.

</section>

<section id="before-next-shift">

## What Should You Check Before Your Next Shift?

Before deploying, return to **MOD LOADOUT** and confirm that the Mods you recently researched are actually selected. If a new upgrade better fits your next mission, replace one of your existing selections.

Your deck determines which Mods can appear in Reward Pods, not the exact order in which those rewards arrive.

Choose Mods that address your immediate problem—damage, repairs, mobility, or credits—rather than unlocking upgrades without checking your current loadout.

For broader research priorities and your next destination, see the [Gravhounds progression guide](/gravhounds).
