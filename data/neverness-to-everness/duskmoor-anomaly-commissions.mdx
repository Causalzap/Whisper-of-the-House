<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Open **Common Commission → Duskmoor** and track one commission at a time.

For **Fawn Alone**, set the clock to around **8:00 PM** and stay on a vehicle for the chase and final catch. **Lantern Parade** starts with the paper on the road sign, then the nearby pole. **Twin Blooms** needs all **three Divergent Flower groups** cleared. In **Finale: Da Capo**, contain the Scarlet Phantoms and take the ring. **Sunken Door** is reached by boat in Teardrop Lake and ends with the Mirror Wraith. **Storybook Skirmish** is a direct fight: kill Saddy Teddy, then clear the Tin Soldiers.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Six Anomaly Commissions are tied to Duskmoor exploration. Five are 2-star commissions worth **20 Annulith each**; **Sunken Door** is the only 3-star commission and pays **30 Annulith**. Clearing the six base commissions gives **130 Annulith** before the optional post-Finale target.

If you are moving through the district at the same time, use the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-100-percent-guide">Duskmoor 100% route</Link> for Oracle Stones, roads, jumps, and check-ins. Stay on this page once the map location is correct but the commission itself will not start, spawn its target, or finish.

<h2 id="fawn-alone">
  Fawn Alone — Empty Marker, Night Chase, and the Vehicle Finish
</h2>

An empty Fawn Alone marker usually means the chase has not been allowed to spawn yet.

Set the clock to **around 8:00 PM**, track Fawn Alone, and approach the marker on a vehicle.

If nothing is there, ride away from the intersection far enough to leave the immediate area, then turn around and approach again.

Once the anomalies start moving, follow the red glow they leave behind.

The route leaves the city road, moves onto dirt, drops toward the river, then runs past the houses toward the construction side of Duskmoor.

After the houses, stay to the **right** instead of cutting through the bamboo forest. The bamboo looks shorter on the map, but a motorcycle loses too much speed and control between the trees.

Continue toward the construction site and start climbing.

The road becomes harder to read under the trees. The **smoke on the hillside** gives the next direction, followed by the **electricity tower** higher up.

### Walk the steep hill if the motorcycle stops climbing

There is no penalty for getting off the vehicle on the steepest part.

Run up the slope, reach the better ground above it, then summon the motorcycle or car again.

What matters is the finish.

**You need to be back on a vehicle before catching the anomalies at the end.**

That is the point where a correct route can still fail.

If you reach the end on foot, summon the vehicle and complete the final approach again rather than restarting the whole chase.

A car is not required for Fawn Alone. The motorcycle can make the route; walking the bad hill and remounting costs far less than buying another vehicle for one commission.

<h2 id="lantern-parade">
  Lantern Parade — The Shadow Does Not Start the Commission
</h2>

The first interaction is easy to walk past because it is attached to ordinary roadside scenery.

Find the **paper on the road sign**.

Interact with it.

Then use the nearby **pole**.

  <Image
    src="/images/neverness-to-everness/nte-duskmoor-lantern-parade-pole.webp"
    alt="Road sign and pole used to start Lantern Parade in Duskmoor"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Paper first, pole second. The Leech Spawn route begins after those interactions, not by attacking the shadow as soon as you see it.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

A shadow emerges after the pole interaction.

Follow it.

Trying to hit it on the road does not move the commission forward.

The later room contains **four lights**. Turn them on as you advance.

The Leech Spawn is dangerous in darkness, so the lights give you safe positions while it moves through the room. There is no need to chase it from one dark corner to another.

Move with the lit areas, wait for the shadow to expose the next route, and clear the enemies when the final combat opens.

Once that group is dead, Lantern Parade is complete.

There is no second hidden interaction after the fight.

<h2 id="twin-blooms">
  Twin Blooms — Find All Three Divergent Flower Groups
</h2>

The enemies rushing around the fog are good distractions.

They are not the targets that decide whether Twin Blooms closes.

Look for the **Divergent Flowers** deeper inside the red mist.

There are **three separate groups** to clear.

Clear the first group, move to the next fog pocket, and repeat until all three have counted.

If the completion banner never appears after the third fight, check the objective before leaving. One group may still be alive or may have failed to spawn.

### When a Divergent Flower group is missing

Reloading the game is worth trying once.

It was not reliable for me. The same empty spawn remained through repeated reloads.

The reset that worked was movement-based:

1. stay in the affected fog area briefly;
2. ride completely out of the anomaly zone;
3. keep going until the area behind you is clearly unloaded;
4. make a U-turn;
5. enter the same fog pocket again.

The missing target can appear as you cross back into the zone.

If it is still empty, ride farther away before the next return. Waiting at the expected spawn point did not help.

One side effect matters: leaving far enough to reload the area can also restore a target that had already been killed.

Watch the current commission progress after returning instead of assuming every earlier clear was preserved.

Once all three Divergent Flower groups are counted, leave. The remaining explosive enemies do not add anything to the commission.

<h2 id="finale-da-capo">
  Finale: Da Capo — Scarlet Phantoms, the Ring, and the Optional 20 Annulith
</h2>

Finale: Da Capo is resolved through interaction before combat.

At the marker, approach the two **Scarlet Phantoms** until the **Contain** prompt appears.

Use it.

The pair performs its final dance.

After the scene, interact with the **ring**.

That ring is the step that closes the main commission sequence. Leaving the area before taking it can make Finale: Da Capo look unfinished even though the containment scene already played.

Once the ring sequence resolves, the base commission is done.

A separate anomaly remains afterward.

Defeating that target gives an additional **20 Annulith**.

That fight is optional. If the goal is only to close all six Duskmoor commissions, the ring finishes the required path. If you want the extra Annulith, stay and kill the remaining target before leaving.

With the optional fight included, Finale: Da Capo can contribute **40 Annulith total**: 20 from the commission and another 20 afterward.

<h2 id="sunken-door">
  Sunken Door — Teardrop Lake to the Mirror Wraith
</h2>

Sunken Door is the only **3-star** Duskmoor commission.

Fast travel to the **ReroRero Phone Booth nearest Teardrop Lake**, move down to the water, and use a boat.

The marker is not on the shoreline.

Drive toward the center of the lake.

A strange gate appears on the water as you approach the correct area.

Enter it.

The commission ends with the **Mirror Wraith**.

Sunken Door pays **30 Annulith**, and its higher tier also carries the better material payout: **40 Silver Carrota** and **4 Anomaly Material Selection Box I**.

<h3 id="mirror-wraith">
  Mirror Wraith — Watch Which Character It Copies
</h3>

The Mirror Wraith is much more dangerous than the fights in the other five commissions.

It can copy the character currently active and turn that character's own attack style back against you.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/neverness-to-everness/nte-duskmoor-sunken-door-mirror-wraith.webp"
    alt="Mirror Wraith boss during the Sunken Door Anomaly Commission"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The boss takes the form of the active character. A character that is excellent at dealing ranged burst can create an equally uncomfortable opponent.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

That makes the character on screen before the copy more important than it first appears.

A fragile ranged carry can be awkward because the Wraith gains long reach and enough damage to remove most of a health bar very quickly.

A mobile damage dealer gives me more control over spacing.

Keep a sustain option available rather than spending every slot on damage.

The safest punish window comes after the Wraith commits to an attack. Let the skill miss, close the distance or reposition, then spend your own skill or Ultimate during its recovery.

Auto-lock can also behave poorly here.

Keep the camera on the Wraith manually before committing a long animation. Losing the target and firing an Ultimate into empty space is more expensive than waiting another second.

When health drops into one-shot range, recover first. The fight does not have a short enough timer to justify gambling the whole attempt on one extra combo.

<h3 id="mirror-wraith-not-spawning">
  If the Mirror Wraith Does Not Spawn
</h3>

The center of the lake can be empty even when the marker looks correct.

Slow the boat down as you approach the middle.

Cross the trigger gradually rather than passing through at full speed.

If nothing appears, leave the game, return, and approach the lake center again.

After reloading, move slightly closer than before. The encounter can appear once the center trigger is crossed correctly.

Do not search the shoreline for another entrance. The fight is tied to the lake-center gate.

Once the Mirror Wraith is dead, Sunken Door is complete.

<h2 id="storybook-skirmish">
  Storybook Skirmish — Saddy Teddy First
</h2>

Storybook Skirmish has no time condition, vehicle check, or object puzzle.

Track the commission and go to the marker.

The named target is **Saddy Teddy**, accompanied by **Tin Soldiers**.

Kill Saddy Teddy first, then clear the remaining Tin Soldiers.

That closes the commission.

If you are already passing this part of Duskmoor during a region clear, finish the fight immediately. There is no later trigger that makes the encounter easier and no reason to schedule a second trip.

<h2 id="all-six-rewards">
  What All Six Duskmoor Commissions Pay
</h2>

The six base commissions give **130 Annulith** in total:

**5 × 20 Annulith + Sunken Door's 30 = 130 Annulith.**

Finale: Da Capo has the optional post-commission target worth another **20 Annulith**, bringing the available total from this set to **150 Annulith** if you kill it.

Sunken Door is also the material standout because it is the only 3-star commission, with **40 Silver Carrota** and **4 Anomaly Material Selection Box I** in addition to its 30 Annulith.

After a commission closes, check its entry in the Anomaly Commission menu and claim any reward still waiting there before moving on.

<h2 id="after-all-six">
  All Six Are Done but Duskmoor Is Still Incomplete
</h2>

Six completed commission files do not mean the whole district is at 100%.

At that point, stop revisiting the anomaly markers.

The missing progress belongs somewhere else in Duskmoor: Oracle Stones, check-ins, jumps, the volleyball activity, or another exploration trigger.

Return to the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-100-percent-guide">Duskmoor 100% route</Link> at the nearest unfinished section rather than repeating these six locations.
