<h2 id="start-chapter-6">
  Start Fogden Game After Fighting with a Dragon
</h2>

**Fighting with a Dragon must already be complete before Fogden Game becomes available.**

Once Chapter 6 appears in the Episode Quest list, no extra item or purchase is required to begin.

A full story run with the dialogue left intact took about **four hours**, so I would not start it expecting a short unlock quest. The chapter moves through several enclosed spaces, character switches, Fogden routes, and a long ending sequence before the quest log finally clears.

The first spending decision is easy to make from your current account.

If ordinary story enemies are already comfortable, continue with the team you have. Most of the places that stop progress in Chapter 6 are **route triggers, investigation points, Fogden rules, and changing entrances**. More damage does not make a missing elevator or an unfinished investigation appear.

Repeated deaths to ordinary enemies are a different signal. That is the point where an upgrade has a clear purpose.

The opening around Eibon is linear. Follow the Episode Quest through Nanally, Hotori, and the Scarlet Letter operation until the group reaches the abandoned building.

<h2 id="abandoned-building">
  The Abandoned Building: Solve the TV Chain Before Looking for a Door
</h2>

The objective makes the entrance sound as though it should already exist.

It does not.

Checking every doorway around the first floor only sends you in circles. The clue that changes the building is Chaos's drawing.

The drawing gives you four useful landmarks:

**tree → red line → TV → entrance near the roots**

Interact with the nearby TV first.

When it shuts down, one red connection disappears and the tree reacts. That is the confirmation that the route is progressing.

More red lines remain, so go upstairs.

### Follow unfinished investigation prompts upward

The upper floors contain several objects that look like ordinary environmental detail but are tied to progression.

Check the highlighted investigations as you climb.

One room contains a letter and a full-length mirror that looks far too intact for the ruined space around it. Inspect the mirror before leaving that floor.

If the entrance still has not changed, look for an unfinished investigation on the current or previous floor.

**A blocked tree means the TV/investigation chain is still incomplete.**

There is no benefit in going back to test the ground-floor doors again.

Continue upward until the remaining TV interactions are finished.

<h2 id="right-corridor">
  The Night Mothling Trail Leads to a False Room
</h2>

After the elevator, follow the Night Mothling traces.

The scent trail eventually pulls the group toward the wrong room. Do not keep waiting there for the next entrance.

The correct location is:

**the last room at the end of the right corridor.**

The entrance does not stay visible continuously.

Reach the final room and inspect the marked evidence there. The investigation includes the bloodstained lighter, the pen, and the other highlighted objects around the scene.

Standing in the correct room is not enough if one of those investigation prompts is still untouched.

When nothing advances, scan the room once for a remaining interaction before leaving.

<h2 id="fogden-rules">
  Inside the Fogden, 10 Lumen Circles Are the First Gate
</h2>

The Black Coffin changes how the chapter works.

The cast is split across different spaces, control moves between characters, and the Fogden starts enforcing its own game rules.

The first number worth remembering is:

**10 Lumen Circles.**

Reach the required count and take the next route when it opens.

Do not treat Lumen Circles like an open-world collectible hunt. The current route feeds progression through fights, bodies, and marked encounters.

Before the requirement is full, those encounters still matter.

After the path opens, more searching only keeps you in a section that the quest is ready to leave.

A character switch is another place where backtracking becomes tempting. Stay with the objective belonging to the character now on screen. The separated routes eventually reconnect without requiring you to return to the previous group's area.

<h2 id="daffodil-route">
  Daffodil Route: Clear the Required Encounters and Keep the Circle Count Moving
</h2>

Daffodil's route moves through camps, caves, Scarlet Letter participants, and the areas tied to the Fogden experiments.

The quickest way through is to keep two things visible:

**the current objective** and **Lumen Circle progress**.

Investigation markers directly on the route can advance the sequence. Optional-looking corners become much less useful once the objective has moved somewhere else.

### Daffodil vs Sweeper

The **Sweeper** encounter belongs to this branch.

This is not a fight that justifies leaving Chapter 6 to rebuild an otherwise functional account. If the preceding combat has been comfortable, use the story setup you already have and finish the encounter.

A wipe here is worth separating into two causes:

- dying during normal combat → improve survivability or execution;
- winning the fight but getting no new route → check the active objective and remaining Lumen Circle progress.

The second problem is not solved by adding damage.

Once the branch has enough progression and the objective moves deeper into the Fogden, follow it.

<h2 id="shackled-past">
  Shackled Past: Keep Going When the Fog Removes Your Usual Landmarks
</h2>

**Shackled Past** creates a route problem by taking away most of the information normally used for navigation.

The objective tells you to continue forward.

Follow that instruction more literally than usual.

Trying to sweep the entire fog bank from side to side makes the area feel much larger because there is almost nothing visible enough to build a useful map.

Move from one readable landmark or scripted encounter to the next.

When the stone pillars appear, use them as route anchors.

There are three signs that make me consider turning back:

- the objective marker has stopped getting closer;
- dialogue has stopped updating;
- no new encounter has triggered.

Fog alone is not one of them.

If the quest is still reacting to your movement, continue.

<h2 id="linko-nitsa-route">
  Linko and Nitsa: Use the Eye Markings and Take the Left Route After the Collapse
</h2>

Linko and Nitsa get a more readable landscape than Shackled Past, but the valley has enough side paths to create unnecessary detours.

The recurring clue is an **eye-shaped marking**.

Follow the direction indicated by those markings and keep climbing.

Near the cliff route, the normal path collapses.

When that happens, **go left**.

Trying to recover the original route over the broken terrain only pushes you back into the blocked path.

Continue through the tunnels and toward the waterfall area.

### Fogden-Wright

The **Fogden-Wright** encounter sits in this part of the Fogden progression.

As with the earlier Daffodil fight, the useful question is what stopped the run.

If the fight itself is killing the current team, handle the combat problem.

If the fight ends but the route still feels closed, return to the active branch objective instead of searching the valley for another exit.

The story is still moving this group through the Present side of the Fogden.

<h3 id="giant-snake">
  Run From the Giant Snake Until the Objective Changes
</h3>

The first giant-snake encounters are escape sequences.

When the objective says to move, move.

The group is not asking you to stand in the tunnel and force a full fight during the early appearances.

Later, the route changes and sends you toward the snake's nest. That is when the encounter becomes a real combat objective.

After the required fight and Lumen Circle progression are complete, leave the tunnels.

Rechecking old caves does not add anything to the branch.

<h2 id="eternal-present">
  Eternal Present Advances Through Conversation
</h2>

The Present route later shifts away from navigation and into a memory/identity sequence.

Characters begin losing track of names and personal details, while talking reduces the effect of the noise around them.

Keep the dialogue moving.

There is no password hidden in an earlier room and no collectible that must be brought into this sequence.

The child conversation also resolves through the prompts already in front of you.

If dialogue is still progressing, let it finish before searching the environment for another solution.

<h2 id="future-route">
  The Future Route Is Not Finished When You Find a Good Outcome
</h2>

The Appraiser moves through several possible versions of Eibon.

Some are obviously bad. Others look safe enough that stopping there can seem reasonable.

Keep following the active objective.

These spaces are possible futures, not an endpoint that can be selected simply because one looks comfortable.

When oddities appear as the objective, clear them and continue through the next transition.

There is no separate route choice that permanently ends the chapter here.

Once the objective changes to **Catch up with Hotori**, follow it. The Future branch is moving back toward the main confrontation.

<h2 id="final-confrontation">
  Past, Present, and Future End at Madam T
</h2>

When the separated groups converge on **Madam T**, the route-solving part of the Fogden is over.

There is no fourth anomaly branch to search.

The remaining sequence is combat and phase changes.

<h3 id="madam-t">
  Madam T: Follow the Phase, Not the Previous Target
</h3>

The finale changes active targets several times.

When an enemy disappears or stops taking meaningful damage, reacquire before spending another Ultimate.

Story characters also take over during parts of the sequence, so forcing the same permanent-team rotation through every phase is unnecessary.

The practical checks are:

- attack the target the game currently allows you to damage;
- move when a large arena attack is covering your position;
- stop spending skills during a phase transition;
- use the character the story hands you when control changes.

If the account reached this arena without struggling through ordinary Fogden fights, a new pull is not the answer to this sequence.

<h3 id="fog-coffin-colossus">
  Fog Coffin Colossus: Keep Playing After the Fight Breaks Apart
</h3>

The **Fog Coffin Colossus** escalates the battle, but even this is not the final completion signal.

**Code Zero** intervenes and attacks inside the Fogden. The Colossus begins failing and the entire realm starts collapsing.

At that point, the objective is no longer combat optimization or exploration.

Leave with the story.

There is nothing worth backtracking for once the Fogden is breaking apart.

<h2 id="finish-chapter-6">
  Chapter 6 Ends at the Bureau Report, Not the Arena
</h2>

The collapse looks like a final cutscene transition.

The quest is still active.

Continue through the return to Eibon and the remaining conversations. When the Episode Quest sends you back to the Bureau, complete the report.

That report is the reliable completion signal.

A full story run did **not** finish when:

- the final arena battle ended;
- the Fogden collapsed;
- the group returned to Eibon.

The quest cleared after the final reporting sequence.

With the story watched, the full run took roughly **four hours** from the start of Chapter 6 to that confirmation.

If Fogden Game is still visible in the quest log after the arena, keep following it. You have not missed a hidden boss or optional objective.

Once the Bureau report clears and Chapter 6 disappears from the active quest chain, the story route is done.
