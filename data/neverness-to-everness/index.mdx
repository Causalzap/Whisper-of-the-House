<h2 id="where-to-go">
  Start With the Progress That Is Actually Blocked
</h2>

Neverness to Everness opens several kinds of progression at the same time, but they do not need to be solved together.

The fastest next step depends on what is still unfinished.

**Chapter 6 is locked**  
Finish **Fighting with a Dragon** first. Chapter 6 does not open directly after Chapter 5.

**Fogden Game is active**  
Stay with the Episode Quest. The abandoned building, Fogden routes, and final return to the Bureau all belong to the same chapter.

**The story is done and Duskmoor is still incomplete**  
Switch to a region-clear route instead of following random nearby icons.

**One Duskmoor commission is active but will not finish**  
Solve that commission before resuming the wider map clear.

**The open-world work is done and you want a combat target**  
Gauntlet of Valor is the Version 1.3 progression branch built around a fixed tower clear followed by Infinity.

That keeps story, exploration, commission mechanics, and combat progression from competing for the same session.

<h2 id="chapter-order">
  Why Chapter 7 Comes Before Chapter 6
</h2>

The chapter numbers and the required play order do not match.

The story chronology is:

**Chapter 5 → Chapter 6 → Chapter 7**

The required release/play order became:

**Chapter 5 → Chapter 7 → Chapter 6**

**Fighting with a Dragon** was released first as a later point in the story. **Fogden Game** was added afterward to fill in the Scarlet Letter events that happen before it.

So if Chapter 6 is still locked after Chapter 5, there is no hidden trigger to search for. Finish Fighting with a Dragon.

Once Fogden Game opens, the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/chapter-6-fogden-game-walkthrough">Chapter 6 walkthrough</Link> takes over.

<h2 id="after-fogden-game">
  After Fogden Game: Duskmoor or Gauntlet?
</h2>

Once the Chapter 6 Bureau report is finished, the next choice is no longer about story order.

It is about what kind of progress you want.

### Choose Duskmoor for permanent map completion

Duskmoor has a large amount of permanent exploration progress packed into one district.

The <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-100-percent-guide">Duskmoor 100% guide</Link> handles the region as a continuous clear instead of a collection of disconnected icons.

That is the better next stop when the goal is:

- Oracle Stones
- check-ins
- region completion
- missed exploration
- cleaning up the Version 1.3 map

### Choose Gauntlet when you want the account tested

Gauntlet of Valor is the better branch when exploration is not the goal.

Its Normal climb ends at **Floor 99**, then opens **Infinity**.

Pick the one that matches the session you actually want to play.

<h2 id="duskmoor-commissions">
  Duskmoor Commissions Are Their Own Problem
</h2>

Six Anomaly Commissions are tied into Duskmoor:

- Fawn Alone
- Lantern Parade
- Twin Blooms
- Finale: Da Capo
- Sunken Door
- Storybook Skirmish

They belong inside the wider Duskmoor clear, but their mechanics are separate from Oracle Stones and road routing.

That distinction matters when the map location is correct but the objective still refuses to progress.

At that point, stop changing the exploration route.

Use the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-anomaly-commissions">Duskmoor Anomaly Commissions guide</Link>, finish the active commission, then resume the region clear from the same part of the map.

The broader 100% route should not be restarted because one commission has its own trigger, spawn, or boss problem.

<h2 id="what-can-wait">
  What Can Wait Until It Becomes the Actual Goal
</h2>

Version 1.3 also leaves plenty of reasons to spend time outside these four routes.

Builds, farming, housing, vehicles, and currency efficiency can all matter.

They do not all need attention while a more immediate progression problem is already open.

### Character builds

A build question becomes useful when combat exposes a specific weakness.

If the current story and normal exploration fights are comfortable, changing a build only because a new chapter started usually delays progress without solving anything.

Gauntlet gives a much clearer reason to make changes because repeated failures expose whether damage, survival, or team function is falling short.

### Vehicles

Duskmoor has enough road travel to make vehicle choice noticeable.

That does not make a new vehicle a prerequisite for region completion.

A purchase makes more sense when you want the vehicle for the wider game, not because one exploration objective took two attempts.

### Farming

Farming becomes worth comparing when currency or materials are the goal of the session.

If the immediate objective is still Chapter 6, Duskmoor completion, or the first Gauntlet clear, stopping to optimize a farming loop changes the task instead of finishing it.

### Housing

Housing can be handled on its own schedule.

It does not gate the Chapter 6 route, the Duskmoor clear, or the Gauntlet climb, so it can wait until property progression itself is what you want to work on.

<h2 id="current-route">
  A Clean Version 1.3 Progression Route
</h2>

For a returning player moving through the current content without trying to complete everything at once, the route is:

**Finish Fighting with a Dragon if needed**  
→ **Clear Chapter 6: Fogden Game**  
→ **Finish the Bureau report**  
→ **Clear Duskmoor or start Gauntlet, depending on your goal**  
→ **Resolve individual Duskmoor commissions only when they interrupt the region clear**  
→ **Return to builds, farming, housing, or other systems when one of them becomes the next objective**

That order keeps one type of progress in focus at a time.
