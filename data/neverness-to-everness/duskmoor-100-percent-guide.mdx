<h2 id="duskmoor-100-route">
  Start Duskmoor 100% at the Wertheimer Tower
</h2>

Duskmoor is much easier to finish when the map is treated as a driving route rather than a collection of isolated icons.

There are **50 Oracle Stones** in the district. The 100% route also passes the region's check-ins, **3 ReroRero Phone Booths**, six Anomaly Commissions, and the short volleyball activity near the early city loop.

Activate the **Wertheimer Tower** first.

The tower clears the map fog, which matters here because later sections depend on recognizing roads, intersections, lake edges, and mountain routes rather than following one quest marker.

Activate each **ReroRero Phone Booth** when the route passes it. A booth that has not been visited cannot help when the next loop starts on the opposite side of Duskmoor.

### Car or motorcycle?

A car makes the long paved sections easier, especially where a jump needs a wide run-up.

It is not required.

The full route works on a motorcycle, including the jumps. Dirt roads are less forgiving on two wheels, but one failed landing is not a reason to spend Fons on another vehicle.

The purchase starts making sense when you already want a car for Duskmoor's long highway sections and other driving content. For this clear alone, the motorcycle is enough.

Clear weather also helps on the mountain and dirt-road sections. Night is only useful when the route reaches content that requires it; otherwise, daylight makes the road edges and jump approaches easier to read.

<h2 id="oracle-stones">
  All 50 Duskmoor Oracle Stones: Use the Road, Not the Map Cursor
</h2>

Oracle Stones are collected automatically when the vehicle passes close enough to the red bird.

That changes how I handle the 50-stone clear.

Stopping to stand directly underneath every icon is slower than driving the correct side of the road and watching for the **Oracle Stone x1** pickup notification.

Several stones in the later half of the route sit close enough to the highway that they register without getting off the vehicle.

If you are missing one stone after finishing the full exploration loop, the road name is faster to work with than restarting all 50.

The 50 Oracle Stones are spread across the major Duskmoor roads, including:

- Onion Road
- Blue Jay Mountain Road
- Aurifloria Way
- Teardrop Ring Road
- Villard Corner Road
- Whitetail Ridge Road
- Foliage Row
- Raccoon Mountain Road

The full loop below mixes the Stones with check-ins and commissions, so the pickup order is chosen to reduce driving rather than to follow the numerical order used on some maps.

<h3 id="oracle-stone-number-index">
  Oracle Stone Number Road Index
</h3>

If you are checking a specific numbered Stone rather than doing the full route, these ranges narrow the search quickly:

- **#1–18:** Onion Road into Blue Jay Mountain Road
- **#19–23:** the road loop continuing north and back south
- **#24–26:** Aurifloria Way
- **#27–31:** Teardrop Ring Road and its nearby side road
- **#32–37:** side roads leading back toward Blue Jay Mountain Road
- **#38–39:** Villard Corner Road
- **#40–43:** Blue Jay Mountain Road into Whitetail Ridge Road
- **#44–46:** Foliage Row
- **#47–50:** Foliage Row and the return toward Raccoon Mountain Road

For a first clear, I still stay with the continuous route below. The numbered index becomes useful when the region is nearly finished and only one or two Oracle Stones remain.

<h2 id="tower-city-loop">
  Wertheimer Tower to the First Speed Check-In
</h2>

From the tower, face the nearby building and collect the first Oracle Stone.

Return toward the tower for the Stone on the stairs, then climb to the main road.

Turn right and continue toward the gas station.

Take the road-side Stone, then leave the vehicle briefly for the Stone on top of the nearby building. A character with fast vertical movement shortens the climb, but normal movement works.

Drop back to the road and head toward the tunnel.

Another Stone sits close enough to the driving line to collect as you pass.

The first check-in comes immediately after this stretch.

This is the first point where a successful-looking run can still fail to count.

<h2 id="80-kmh-check-in">
  First Check-In: Cross the Center Above 80 km/h
</h2>

On a motorcycle, **anything below 80 km/h failed to register** during this check-in.

Speed is only half of it.

Passing the side of the trigger can also fail even when the jump itself looks clean.

Reset completely after a failed attempt.

Get back onto the road, create a full run-up, straighten the bike before reaching the jump, then cross the center above 80 km/h.

Trying again from a short angle usually reproduces the same failure.

A car gives more speed margin, but buying one for this check-in is unnecessary.

Take the Oracle Stone beside the check-in before continuing.

The road then reaches the volleyball court. Collect the nearby Stone and leave the volleyball activity for later if stopping now would break the route. The game itself only takes about **two minutes** once you come back to finish it.

Fast travel back to the Wertheimer Tower after this city loop.

<h2 id="eastern-night-loop">
  Set the Clock Past 8 PM Before the Eastern Loop
</h2>

Change the time to **after 8:00 PM** before leaving the tower again.

The next drive reaches the first Duskmoor Anomaly Commission, so setting the time now avoids another trip across the district.

Take the Oracle Stone on the left side of the road before the commission marker.

The road then intersects **Fawn Alone**.

The commission's trigger and chase mechanics belong in the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-anomaly-commissions">Duskmoor Anomaly Commissions guide</Link>. For the exploration route, clear it when you reach the marker and resume the road from the same location.

Continue onto the dirt road.

Take the Stone beside the signs, follow the route down toward the river, then turn left at the houses for the next Stone.

Two check-ins appear around this part of the map.

Leave them for the return pass.

Taking them immediately forces an awkward turn across the terrain and sends you back toward a road the loop already visits later.

The bamboo forest has the same problem. It looks like a shortcut on the map but slows a motorcycle down more than the road around it.

Take the right-hand route toward the construction site.

Collect the Stone at the entrance and continue uphill.

When the tree cover starts hiding the road, the smoke line and electricity tower provide a better direction than the minimap.

After that section is complete, switch back to daylight.

<h2 id="lake-crossroad-loop">
  Crossroad to the Lake: Brake After the Jump
</h2>

Keep the lake visible as you move from the crossroad toward the purple trees.

Once the route returns to pavement, collect the Stone beside the yellow fence.

Another Oracle Stone sits near the racing challenge.

Stay on the road until the gas-station jump.

Build enough speed to clear the check-in, then **brake while airborne**.

The goal here is not maximum distance.

Landing too far below the road creates a long recovery drive, and the next pickup sends you back toward that same road.

After landing, take the Stone on top of the sign to the left and make the U-turn onto the dirt road.

The following jump uses the same landing logic: clear the trigger, then brake so the vehicle stays close enough to rejoin the road.

Once you are back on the pavement, collect the Stone beside the traffic sign.

Use the next **ReroRero Phone Booth** if it is already activated. If it is not, drive there and activate it now rather than passing it again.

<h2 id="teardrop-lake">
  Wooden House to Teardrop Lake
</h2>

From the Phone Booth, turn toward the wooden house.

The next Oracle Stone sits on the fence.

Reverse after collecting it; the open field behind you gives enough space for the next jump.

Take the jump and continue toward the houses near the lake.

Another Stone is beside the house before the route moves onto the water.

The Stone near the boat can be reached by swimming, aerial movement, or sending the motorcycle into the water and continuing from there.

If you swim out, take the boat back.

This is also where **Sunken Door** enters the route. Finish the commission before leaving Teardrop Lake; returning here later costs much more time than resolving it during the first pass.

Its Mirror Wraith mechanics and spawn troubleshooting stay in the Anomaly Commission page.

Once the lake area is clear, use the nearby Phone Booth and return to daytime.

<h2 id="central-road">
  Central Duskmoor: Long Roads Favor a Car, but Do Not Require One
</h2>

This section contains enough straight pavement for a car to save noticeable driving time.

That still does not change the vehicle requirement: the motorcycle completes it.

From the fast-travel point, drive toward the traffic lights and turn right.

Collect the road-side Oracle Stone.

Continue until the construction workers come into view, then take the next right and follow the lane to the jump.

Launch from the edge, drift slightly left, and brake before landing.

The route needs to turn back toward the convenience store, so distance beyond the landing does not help.

Take the Stone in front of the store and U-turn.

The road now leads to the dirt-road entrance for **Twin Blooms**.

Collect the nearby Stone and finish the commission before returning to the main road.

If the commission itself is stuck, use the Anomaly page rather than driving circles around the fog. The 100% route is already in the correct location.

<h2 id="finale-lantern-road">
  The Long Road Through Finale: Da Capo and Lantern Parade
</h2>

From the next Phone Booth, collect the Oracle Stone ahead and stay on the main road.

Another Stone sits near the houses on the left.

Continue to the dirt road on the right. Two more road-side Stones sit close together here.

The route then reaches **Finale: Da Capo**.

Finish the commission and return to the same road.

The next Stone is easy to miss at driving speed: watch the **green fence on the right**.

Continue to the construction site and collect the Stone on top of its fence.

Take the next road-side Stone before changing the clock.

Set the time to **after 8:00 PM** again.

The next commission is Lantern Parade, so making the change here keeps the route continuous.

Drive toward the tunnel, collect the Stone along the way, then take the Oracle Stone near the gas station before entering **Lantern Parade**.

After the commission, return to the road.

<h2 id="western-jumps">
  Western Duskmoor: The Landing Matters More Than the Jump
</h2>

The western dirt roads are where a motorcycle feels least forgiving.

At the first jump, straighten the vehicle before accelerating.

Once the trigger is cleared, **brake immediately**.

The next route goes back uphill. Overshooting can turn a few seconds of airborne distance into several minutes of climbing.

Continue around the outer road and climb the bridge section.

Follow the map until the next turn, drop down, and collect the Oracle Stone beside the traffic light.

The next jump is different.

This time the destination is farther away near the gas station, so carry more speed and **do not brake immediately after takeoff**.

Take the Stone to the left after landing.

Summon the vehicle again and follow the road.

Another Stone appears shortly after the next left turn.

The dirt route ahead reaches **Storybook Skirmish**. Finish it while you are already here, then continue toward the nearby lake.

<h2 id="western-lake">
  Western Lake to the Main Highway
</h2>

Take the Oracle Stone beside the lake before starting the next check-in.

The best launch line depends on the vehicle.

A car has enough stability for the wider road-side approach.

A motorcycle is easier to control from the shorter near-side line.

After landing, collect the next Stone and continue past the traffic light.

Take the third road on the right.

Watch the fences along the right side for the next pickup.

Another Stone sits farther down the same road. Collect it and make the U-turn immediately.

A narrow side road appears ahead.

With a car, leave it near the entrance; this pocket returns to the main road quickly.

A motorcycle can be summoned inside.

Once you are back on the highway, several Oracle Stones sit close enough to the road to collect automatically.

Watch for the **Oracle Stone x1** notification rather than stopping at every bird.

This is also a good place to activate the next ReroRero Phone Booth if you have not already done so.

Collect the Oracle Stone near the top.

A jump/check-in appears before the final Stone on the mountain.

<h2 id="missing-oracle-stone">
  Missing One of the 50 Oracle Stones
</h2>

A missing Oracle Stone is easier to isolate once the full road loop has been completed.

First, check whether any road-side pickup failed to display **Oracle Stone x1** when you passed it.

The easiest places to lose track are:

- stones collected automatically at speed;
- a Stone beside a jump where attention was on the landing;
- the lake-side pickup near Sunken Door;
- the western highway sequence where several Stones appear close together;
- the mountain Stone beyond the jump;
- the final bridge Stone.

If the missing Stone still cannot be identified, the numbered road index near the top of the page narrows it by area without repeating the entire route.

An Oracle Crystal is also more efficient than driving the whole district again when only one isolated Stone remains.

<h2 id="stuck-below-100">
  Duskmoor Is Still Below 100% After All 50 Stones
</h2>

All **50 Oracle Stones** can be collected and the region can still be incomplete.

That means the next check should not be another Stone sweep.

The fastest order is:

### Check failed check-ins first

The motorcycle speed check near the start is the first suspect.

A pass below **80 km/h** or too far to the side can look successful while failing to register.

The western jumps deserve the same check if you landed and immediately continued without watching for completion.

### Finish the volleyball activity

The volleyball court appears early enough that it is easy to postpone and forget.

The actual activity only takes around **two minutes**.

Finish it before starting another map loop.

### Check the six Anomaly Commissions

The region route intersects:

- Fawn Alone
- Lantern Parade
- Twin Blooms
- Finale: Da Capo
- Sunken Door
- Storybook Skirmish

If one remains active, finish it through the <Link href="/neverness-to-everness/duskmoor-anomaly-commissions">Duskmoor Anomaly Commissions guide</Link>.

The individual trigger, spawn, and boss problems belong there; repeating those mechanics here does not help locate missing exploration progress.

### Only then suspect the completion display

The Duskmoor completion display can fail to show the expected result even after the route appears complete.

I only treat that as the likely explanation after confirming:

**50 Oracle Stones → check-ins → volleyball → six commissions**

in that order.

A visible unfinished objective is more actionable than assuming a display bug.

Once those checks are clean, repeating the entire route is unlikely to be the best next move.
