<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
I start the Secret Ending route **after the hidden 0% gate has reached 100% and opened**.

From there, my route is:

**enter the opened gate**

→ reach the clone room

→ use clone loops to generate Energy

→ spend Energy on speed, production, and more clones until the progress counter reaches **200**

→ complete the four mining layers: **Duck → Cash Register → Piñata → Anomaly**

→ take the **UV Lamp**

→ go back through the hidden route and collect the number clues

→ use the UV pickaxe clue to make the **Access Card**

<h2 id="enter-the-gate">
  I only start this route after the hidden gate is open
</h2>

Once the 0% device reaches 100% and the gate opens, I stop treating the upper area like another factory puzzle.

The 200 kg platform and 0% blocker are finished.

I go through the opened gate and stay on the hidden route.

That boundary matters because I do not want to keep bouncing between two different problems.

If the gate is still closed, the answer is back in the <Link href="/project-pitt/200kg-platform-what-next">200 kg / 0% guide</Link>.

If the gate is open, everything below belongs to the Secret Ending route.

<h2 id="clone-room">
  The clone room is an incremental minigame
</h2>

The first major room after the hidden route opens looks much more complicated than it really is.

Clones run a loop.

Completing loops generates **Energy**.

I spend that Energy on:

**Speed**

**Production**

**more Clones**

The loop is self-reinforcing:

**clones run**

→ **Energy increases**

→ **I buy upgrades**

→ **the system produces Energy faster**

I do not try to solve this room as a movement puzzle.

I treat it like a small incremental game and keep improving the system until the progress counter is ready to finish.

<h2 id="clone-progress">
  I care about the 200 progress target more than the Energy bank
</h2>

The Energy total is only the currency.

The number I actually watch is the progress counter moving toward **200**.

In one run I saw it climb through:

**117**

→ **140**

→ **168**

→ **179**

→ **192**

and eventually the final stretch.

Near the end, my board showed:

**196 / 200**

**46 clones**

**5.9k Energy**

**Best: 16.72s**

**the minigame resolves**

not:

**I have a huge pile of Energy**

<h2 id="mining">
  After the clone room, I mine through four product layers
</h2>

When the clone minigame finishes, I take the pickaxe and move into the mining section.

This is where I first thought the room might continue forever.

It does not.

There are **four layers**, and they follow the same product order as the main factory:

**Duck**

→ **Cash Register**

→ **Piñata**

→ **Anomaly**

I do not leave after the first quota finishes.

One completed layer only opens the next layer.

I also avoid digging myself into a hole I cannot easily climb out of.

Leaving a small staircase or usable ledges behind me makes the later layers less annoying.

My mining rule is:

**finish the current quota**

→ **move into the next product layer**

→ **repeat until the Anomaly layer is finished**

<h2 id="access-card">
  If the keypad is still useless, I get the Access Card before guessing more codes
</h2>

Finding all four digits is not enough by itself.

The route also needs an **Access Card**.

I keep the UV Lamp active and look for the **red pickaxe symbol**.

That marking tells me the pickaxe still matters even though the mining section is already finished.

I bring the pickaxe to the marked interaction and complete it to create the Access Card.

<h2 id="how-long">
  My first full run took 13–14 hours because I did not know this order
</h2>

My first complete attempt reached roughly **13–14 hours** of total play time.

That does **not** mean the Secret Ending route itself takes 13–14 hours.

A large part of that time came from:

- rebuilding automation;
- testing wrong hidden-route ideas;
- searching old rooms without knowing what the UV Lamp changed;
- trying incomplete number combinations;
- not knowing the mining room had four layers;
- not knowing the Access Card was a separate requirement.

Once I know the order, the route is much shorter.

The useful path is:

**gate**

→ **clones**

→ **200**

→ **four mining layers**

→ **UV**

→ **0 / 4 / 6 / 2 clues**

→ **Access Card**

→ **2640**

→ **Secret Ending**

<h2 id="achievement">
  If END? does not unlock, I separate the ending from the achievement state
</h2>

If the Secret Ending screen appears, I treat the route itself as completed.

I do not replay the entire hidden sequence just because one achievement notification looks wrong.

For achievement cleanup or hidden badges that remain locked after progression, I use the <Link href="/project-pitt/achievements">Achievements guide</Link> and the <Link href="/project-pitt/troubleshooting">Troubleshooting guide</Link>.

This page owns the ending route.

It does not own achievement-state debugging.

<h2 id="secret-vs-main">
  The Secret Ending is not the same as the Main Ending
</h2>

The game tracks the Secret Ending and Main Ending separately.

Completing this route gives me the hidden ending path described here.

It does not automatically explain the Main Ending.

I keep those two objectives separate instead of inventing another step after the Secret Ending screen.

For this page, the stop condition is simple:

**Secret Ending displayed → route complete**
