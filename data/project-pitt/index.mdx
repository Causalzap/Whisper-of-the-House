<h2 id="where-to-start">
  I start with progression unless I already know the exact blocker
</h2>

Project P.I.T.T. is much easier to follow when I separate two questions:

**What stage am I in?**

and:

**What specific system is blocking me?**

If I cannot answer the first question, I start with the <Link href="/project-pitt/progression-guide">Progression Guide</Link>.

That page gives me the broad route from early Ducks through the later product stages and into the hidden branch.

If I already know the problem — a Duck layout, Cash Register landing, 200 kg platform, achievement cleanup, or Secret Ending route — I skip the broad walkthrough and open the page that owns that problem.

That is how I use this hub.

I do not read every guide in order.

I use it as a map.

<h2 id="early-game">
  Early game: progression first, automation only as complicated as it needs to be
</h2>

The opening revolves around Ducks.

My first objective is not to build the final warehouse.

It is to get a simple automatic line running well enough that I can stop handling every product manually.

<h2 id="cash-and-phase-3">
  Cash Registers are the first point where I stop copying old geometry
</h2>

When Cash Registers unlock, I move forward even if the Duck side is not perfect.

The new product behaves differently enough that I give it its own landing and transport logic.

That is when I switch to the <Link href="/project-pitt/cash-register-automation">Cash Register Automation guide</Link>.

I do not use this hub to repeat the Cushion, Slick, Piston, merge, or stronger-Fan decisions.

The important navigation rule is only:

**Cash Registers are available → stop treating Ducks as the only factory problem.**

As the next phase approaches, I also stop overbuilding the current floor plan.

Phase 3 can change the room.

If the transition itself makes the game look broken, I use <Link href="/project-pitt/troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</Link>.

If the transition completed normally and Piñatas are now available, I move forward.

<h2 id="hidden-route">
  The 200 kg platform is where I stop using broad progression advice
</h2>

The upper route contains several distinct progression checks.

I do not try to solve them from this hub.

If I am still working through the camera / fuse route, the <Link href="/project-pitt/progression-guide">Progression Guide</Link> keeps the stage order clear.

Once I am physically standing at the **200 kg platform** or the device showing **0%**, I switch immediately to the <Link href="/project-pitt/200kg-platform-what-next">200 kg Platform / 0% guide</Link>.

That page owns the entire blocker until the hidden gate opens.

I do not repeat the weight totals or the 0% requirement here.

<h2 id="achievements">
  I leave achievement cleanup until the run gives me enough tools to make it efficient
</h2>

Project P.I.T.T. has achievements tied to:

**normal progression**

**hidden progression**

**collectibles**

**factory grind**

**endings and minigames**

I do not chase all of them from the opening hours.

I use the <Link href="/project-pitt/achievements">Achievements Guide</Link> as the master checklist and let normal progression remove the easy entries first.

Late grind goals are much easier once the stronger product systems already exist.

If an achievement appears not to register even though the environment clearly progressed, I move from the checklist to <Link href="/project-pitt/troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</Link> instead of replaying the whole route immediately.

<h2 id="troubleshooting">
  I separate a bad layout from a game-state problem
</h2>

This is the most important split in the cluster.

If products are physically moving badly, I use:

<Link href="/project-pitt/automation-layouts">Automation Layouts</Link>

That includes problems such as:

**jams**

**overshoot**

**undershoot**

**bad catches**

**airflow**

**containment**

**moving tools**

If the factory route itself may be fine but the **game state** looks wrong, I use:

<Link href="/project-pitt/troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</Link>

That includes things such as:

**Phase 3 looks like it destroyed the save**

**a progression device appears stuck**

**the Anomaly workbench appears inactive despite the route being built**

**a hidden achievement stays locked**

**the game hitches specifically while recording**

Keeping those two pages separate stops me from rebuilding a factory to solve a progression-state problem.
