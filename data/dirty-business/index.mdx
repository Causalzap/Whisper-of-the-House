Dirty Business is easier when I treat the underground base as one connected
operation rather than a collection of unrelated tutorials.

Inputs feed production. Production creates identifiable output. Customer Orders
turn that output into cash and Reputation. Reputation opens Weapon Printing,
Acid Disposal, Workers, Dealers, Gunsmiths, LSD, Companies, stronger staff, and
aircraft access. Storage, quality control, packaging, and distribution decide
whether those unlocks become useful capacity or another blocked room.

This Hub has two jobs:

1. send the player to the focused guide that solves the current problem;
2. answer the smaller but valuable topics that do not yet need separate pages.

<h2 id="best-production-priorities">
  Best production priorities
</h2>

The most useful priority is not the newest unlock. It is the step that releases
the current stopped timer or blocked output.

<GuideTable
  caption="The production decision I make from the visible queue instead of the unlock screen."
  columns={priorityColumns}
  rows={priorityRows}
/>

My short operating order is:

1. clear finished output before it blocks the next cycle;
2. restore a completely stopped station;
3. pack and deliver clean products already supported by stock;
4. restock a Worker before the next task reaches an empty input;
5. clear processing or distribution queues before expanding upstream capacity;
6. buy only the upgrade that changes one of those states.

<h2 id="quality-and-deformed-products">
  Quality and Deformed products
</h2>

Customer Orders check more than the product name.

For Weed, verify strain, quantity, and displayed quality. For Weapons, verify
the exact type, quantity, and whether the product is normal or Deformed.

A correct quantity can still lose money and Reputation. In one Murkwood OG
comparison:

- clean batch: **1,040 U-Notes and 24 Reputation** with **0% Penalties**;
- wrong-quality batch: **842.40 U-Notes and 18 Reputation** with
  **33.3% Penalties**.

```text

→ known Low Quality Weed

Middle Rack
→ known Medium or higher Quality Weed

Right Rack

Floor beside Order Delivery Elevator
→ current outgoing boxes only

```

one Lamp, Soil, Fertilizer, or other single input always produces a fixed Weed

<h2 id="acid-disposal">
  Acid Disposal summary
</h2>

Acid Disposal opens at Reputation Level **13** in the documented route. The
first setup used an Acid Drum and **two Hydrochloric Acid containers**.

The system has two separate flows:

1. receive the incoming dump or evidence;
2. dissolve its items using the Acid route.

Those are also separate achievement counters. Receiving more dumps does not
automatically finish the dissolution milestones.

I finish and supply one Drum before accepting enough jobs to create a blocked
pile. Acid Disposal fits between Weed, Weapon, packing, and delivery cycles; the
current material does not prove that it is the fastest Reputation activity.

<h2 id="coop">
  Solo and Co-op priorities
</h2>

Solo needs earlier automation and stricter control over the number of manual
production lines. Co-op reduces walking and idle time, but shared interfaces,
money, stock, and tutorials need clear ownership.

My two-player split is:

- **Player 1:** activates Orders, controls Delivery, checks penalties, completes
  the batch, and collects payment;
- **Player 2:** runs production, counts and packs products, and places closed
  boxes on the Order Delivery Elevator;
- **Both:** clear elevators, restock assigned lines, and confirm shared cash
  before a large purchase.

With more players, assign one owner to each major system instead of crowding the
same machine. Only one player should control the tablet during a delivery batch.
The player who needs a one-time room, Worker, Dealer, Company, sale, or aircraft
achievement should perform that action because Co-op credit ownership is not
fully verified.

<h2 id="worker-automation">
  Worker automation summary
</h2>

Level 15 opens the first Botanist. Prepare one strain, a matching task, unpacked
inputs, available equipment, clear Product Rack space, and enough Worker balance
before expecting automation.

When the Worker stops, read **Current Status** first. A higher-tier Worker does
not fix missing Seeds, Soil, Watering Cans, Plastic Packs, tasks, equipment, or
output space. Avoid moving or manually taking over the exact Pot or station the
Worker is trying to use.

<h2 id="recommended-order">
  Recommended reading order
</h2>

For a new save:

1. **Hub:** understand the full operating loop.
2. **Orders:** learn box assignment, penalties, completion, and payment.
3. **Leveling:** plan Reputation breakpoints and purchases.
4. **Workers:** automate one stable Weed route after Level 15.
5. **LSD:** build one complete Sheet after Level 30.
6. **Achievements:** convert the mature base into a long-counter route.

For an existing save, start with the symptom rather than the level.

<h2 id="faq">
  Dirty Business guide FAQ
</h2>

### Which guide should a beginner read first?

Read Customer Orders first when the delivery or payment workflow is unclear.
Read Leveling first when Orders already work and the next question is
progression or purchasing.

### What is the best production priority?

Clear finished output and restore completely stopped stations first. Add
capacity only after the input, processing, output, and distribution queues are
understood.

### Why does a correct product still receive a penalty?

The Order can also check strain, quality, condition, and quantity. Wrong-quality
Weed or a Deformed Weapon can reduce the result.

### How should two players divide the work?

Let one player control Orders and Delivery while the other handles production,
packing, and box placement. With more players, assign one owner to each major
system.

### Can the Demo save transfer to Early Access?

Developer messaging said transfer was intended, but preserve the original Demo
data and verify the imported level, cash, rooms, inventory, Workers, and
tutorials before deleting or overwriting anything.

### Where is the Dirty Business save folder?

The exact current Early Access path has not been verified for this guide.
Confirm which files change when saving before claiming that a manual backup is
complete.

### When should I hire a Dealer or buy a Company?

After the base produces repeatable surplus that clean customer Orders no longer
consume. Do not add distribution while the original production route is
underfunded.

### Does Level 38 immediately give me a usable aircraft?

No. Level 38 opens the next access check. The tutorial, aircraft, pilot, cargo,
funding, and route still have to be prepared.
