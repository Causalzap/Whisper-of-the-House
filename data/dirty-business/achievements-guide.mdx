<h2 id="one-save-strategy">
  How I plan the one-time achievements on one save
</h2>

I divide the list into:

- **one-time system actions**, completed when the system becomes useful;
- **lifetime counters**, left running through normal production and distribution.

The rule is simple: complete cheap actions at the natural progression point, but
do not sacrifice the cash or floor space needed to keep the base operating.

### Opening achievements, the first room, and the first Weapon

The opening route naturally covers **Fresh Stock**, **Sold**, and **Green Light**.
At Level 5, I print and sell one valid Weapon for **Loaded** while the Weed line
continues growing.

I purchase the first room for **Corner Office** only when the current floor needs
storage or another production zone. **Breaking And Entering** is paired with a
locked board on an expansion route I already intend to use.

3. consume fuel that still counts toward the target;

The trolley goals are **25 km** and **100 km**. I leave them until the trolley is
already useful for moving boxes rather than pushing an empty platform during the
early production grind.

<h2 id="coop-achievements">
  How I handle achievements in co-op
</h2>

No achievement description explicitly says co-op is required, but the supplied
data does not explain who receives credit in a shared session.

Until ownership is tested, I use a conservative rule:

- the player who needs a room, Worker, Dealer, Company, or first-sale achievement
  performs the action;
- the player who needs an aircraft counter drives or completes the route;
- both players check Steam progress before and after a controlled Order or sale;
- I avoid changing Host immediately before a large counter threshold.

This prevents a long shared grind from depending on an unverified assumption
about Host, executor, passenger, or all-player credit.

<h2 id="faq">
  Dirty Business achievements FAQ
</h2>

### How many achievements are in Dirty Business?

The July 2026 Early Access achievement list contains **75 achievements**.

### Is this a verified one-save 100% roadmap?

Not yet. It is a one-save strategy designed to overlap the long counters. Every
trigger, co-op ownership rule, reset behavior, and late counter has not been
verified across all builds.

### How long does 100% completion take?

The current evidence does not support an honest time estimate. The 10,000-Order,
25,000-product, aircraft, trash, and Store counters need measured progress rates.

### What is the hidden Six. Seven. achievement?

Reach **Reputation Level 67**.

### What unlocks Back in Business?

The visible description only says `Old habits die hard`. The exact opening
trigger has not been isolated, so I do not publish a guessed action.

### Are any achievements confirmed missable?

No confirmed missables are identified by the supplied descriptions, but that is
not proof that every one-time trigger, reset, and co-op state is recoverable.

### Does ordering 100 Store items mean 100 separate Store transactions?

The description says items, not transactions. The exact behavior of bulk boxes
should be checked against visible achievement progress before a 15,000-item grind.

### Are Acid dumps and dissolved items the same counter?

No. The achievement list has separate milestones for dumps received and items
dissolved.

### Should I grind aircraft achievements as soon as access opens?

No. Stabilize the aircraft, cargo, fuel, and funding route first, then combine
distance, speed, fuel, delivery, and airdrop goals wherever the current system
allows it.

### Does every product sale count toward total products?

That should be verified by sales route. Weed, Weapons, LSD, banknotes, Dealer,
Company, and aircraft distribution may not all update the broader counter in the
same way.
