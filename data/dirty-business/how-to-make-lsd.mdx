<h2 id="unlock-cost-equipment">
  LSD Synthesising unlock level, visible cost, and equipment
</h2>

The LSD Synthesising tutorial unlocks at **Level 30**. The first Store screen
shows three immediately available purchases:

- Basic Slug: **4,500 U-Notes**;
- Slug Tank: **12,000 U-Notes**;
- Slug Food: **1,200 U-Notes**.

The visible Level 30 subtotal is:

```text
4,500 + 12,000 + 1,200 = 17,700 U-Notes
```

That number does not cover the complete production line. It excludes the
Distiller, Blotting Paper, the final dispensing station, repeat Slug Food, room
space, tables, storage, and any other supporting purchases whose prices were not
shown.

Do not treat the visible 17,700 subtotal as a complete setup budget. Before
placing the first Tank, I would collect unpaid customer orders and leave enough
cash to buy the remaining tutorial equipment without stopping the rest of the
base.

<h2 id="first-batch">
  How to make the first LSD Sheet
</h2>

Clear one dedicated work lane before placing anything. Keeping the
stations in production order makes every stoppage easier to identify:

```text
Slug Tank and Slug Food
→ Slug Extract collection
→ Distiller
→ Blotting Paper and final dispensing station
→ Finished-sheet box
```

<h3 id="place-slug-tank">
  1. Place the Slug Tank and load the slugs
</h3>

Place the Tank where its Slug slots, food input, and extract collection side can
all be reached without moving other boxes first.

The standard Tank has three Slug slots. Unpack the first Basic Slug delivery,
fill that Tank, and stop there until one LSD Sheet has passed through the final
station.

In co-op, ordering six slugs and two Tanks before checking the shared balance
briefly blocked the next tutorial purchase. The useful rule is the same in solo:
check the remaining cash before scaling the raw-production stage.

<h3 id="add-slug-food">
  2. Add Slug Food
</h3>

Unpack the Slug Food and load it at the Tank. A closed Store box placed beside
the machine does not count as a supplied input.

Use the machine state instead: when the Tank stops because the food is empty, restock the active
Tank before buying more slugs.

<h3 id="collect-slug-extract">
  3. Collect Slug Extract manually
</h3>

The first thing that caused confusion was the prompt **Collect Slug Extract**.

The output did not move to the Distiller automatically. Wait for the Tank to
produce ready extract, move to the collection point, and interact with the
extract directly.

Keep collected extract between the Tank and Distiller. That prevents raw extract
from being mixed with Slug Food, Blotting Paper, or finished LSD Sheets.

<h3 id="load-distiller">
  4. Load five Slug Extract and set the temperature
</h3>

The tutorial requires **five Slug Extract** for the first Distiller cycle.

After loading the machine, adjust the temperature and press Start. When it
reports **Too Hot**, lower the setting before trying again.

Use the machine feedback instead of aiming for a fixed number: lower the temperature whenever the Distiller reports Too Hot. Follow the station feedback:

- `Too Hot`: lower the temperature;
- the machine accepts the setting: start the cycle;
- the machine remains inactive: confirm that the previous tutorial objective
  has registered.

What finally allowed the route to continue was completing the earlier objective
before trying to force the next machine. Moving or replacing correctly supplied
equipment would not have fixed that progression lock.

<h3 id="blotting-paper">
  5. Add Blotting Paper and processed liquid
</h3>

When distillation finishes, move the processed liquid to the final station.

This guide calls it the **final dispensing station** because its exact Store
name was not visible. Use this order:

1. place Blotting Paper;
2. add the processed liquid;
3. interact with the movable arm;
4. use the final Dispense interaction.

<h3 id="dispense-sheet">
  6. Dispense and box the LSD Sheet
</h3>

Once both inputs are loaded, use the arm to apply the liquid and then activate
**Dispense**.

<h2 id="production-bottleneck">
  Where the first LSD production line bottlenecks
</h2>

The useful production signal is not a rough total time. It is which side of the
line is waiting.

In the starter setup:

- Slug Extract was already available;
- the Distiller was still processing;
- finished LSD Sheets were waiting on the processing stage rather than the Tank.

Use that queue to decide what to buy next:

- **Distiller waiting for extract:** add Slug capacity.
- **Extract waiting for the Distiller:** add processing capacity.

This is more useful than assuming the newest Slug or the larger Tank is always
the correct upgrade.

The first LSD customer offer shown after production was **3,400 U-Notes for one

That offer is not enough to calculate a reliable payback period.

The known **17,700-U-Note** subtotal excludes:

- the Distiller;
- Blotting Paper;
- the final dispensing station;

The current data supports three direct conclusions:

<h2 id="slug-and-tank-upgrades">
  Basic Slug, later Slug tiers, and the Large Tank
</h2>

The Store confirms these later unlocks:

- Amped Slug: Level 34, **12,000 U-Notes**;
- Large Slug Tank: Level 36, **22,000 U-Notes**;
- Super Slug: Level 37, **28,000 U-Notes**.

The Level 30 first-sheet route does not require Amped Slug or Super Slug.

The Large Slug Tank has one visible mechanical difference: it supports up to
**six slugs**, compared with three in the standard Tank.

Buy the Large Tank only when the Distiller is regularly waiting for extract. If extract is
already accumulating, doubling Tank capacity adds more raw material to the same
processing queue.

<h2 id="starter-layout">
  The small-room layout I use
</h2>

Arrange the stations in one direction:

```text
[Slug Tank and Slug Food]
            ↓
[Slug Extract collection]
            ↓
[Distiller]
            ↓
[Blotting Paper + final dispensing station]
            ↓
[Finished-sheet box]
```

The useful layout rules are:

- keep the Tank's food and collection sides open;
- store Slug Food beside the Tank;
- place raw extract between the Tank and Distiller;
- keep Blotting Paper beside the final station;
- reserve one separate box for finished LSD Sheets;
- keep raw materials and completed products off the same floor pile.

In co-op, one player can handle the Tank and extract while the other handles the
Distiller and final station. During a tutorial objective, let one player finish
the active interaction before the other touches the same machine.

<h2 id="common-problems">
  Common LSD problems and fixes
</h2>

<h3 id="slug-tank-not-working">
  The Slug Tank is placed, but it is not producing
</h3>

Confirm that slugs are inside the Tank and Slug Food has been unpacked and
loaded. A closed Store box beside the machine is not a valid input.

<h3 id="cannot-collect-slug-extract">
  I cannot collect Slug Extract
</h3>

Wait for the Tank to show a ready output and interact with its collection point.
The first manual setup does not transfer extract to the Distiller automatically.

<h3 id="distiller-not-starting">
  The Distiller will not start
</h3>

Verify that five Slug Extract are loaded, adjust the temperature, and confirm
that the previous tutorial objective is complete. If the UI still shows the
earlier step, finish it before moving or replacing the machine.

<h3 id="distiller-too-hot">
  The Distiller says Too Hot
</h3>

Lower the temperature and press Start again. Do not repeat the same setting or
rely on a fixed number that was not shown in the machine UI.

<h3 id="dispensing-arm-not-working">
  The dispensing arm does nothing
</h3>

Check both required inputs: Blotting Paper and processed liquid. Then interact
with the movable arm rather than the table edge or nearby storage.

<h3 id="extract-backlog">
  I have plenty of extract but few finished LSD Sheets
</h3>

The queue is at the processing stage. Add Distiller capacity before buying
another Tank or more slugs.

<h2 id="when-to-expand">
  When to expand the LSD line
</h2>

Expand only after one LSD Sheet has passed through every production station.

Pause and return to existing customer orders when:

- the next required tutorial item is unaffordable;
- boxes are blocking the LSD room or Store Elevator;
- the missing production stage is still unclear;
- a completed Weed or Weapon batch is already ready to sell.

Add Slug capacity when the Distiller is waiting. Add processing capacity when
extract is waiting.

<h2 id="faq">
  Dirty Business LSD FAQ
</h2>

### What level does LSD unlock?

LSD Synthesising unlocks at Reputation Level **30**.

### What equipment do I need for the first LSD Sheet?

The route begins with Basic Slug, a Slug Tank, and Slug Food, then continues
through a Distiller, Blotting Paper, and the final dispensing station.

### How many slugs fit in a standard Tank?

A standard Slug Tank supports up to **three slugs**.

### How many Slug Extract does the Distiller need?

The first tutorial cycle requires **five Slug Extract**.

### How much does one LSD Sheet sell for?

The first customer offer shown after production was **3,400 U-Notes** for one
LSD Sheet. That should not be treated as a guaranteed value for every order.

### Is LSD profitable?

The available numbers are not enough for a complete net-profit calculation. The
visible 17,700 subtotal excludes processing equipment and repeat inputs.

### Is the Large Slug Tank worth 22,000?

It raises capacity from three to six slugs. Buy it when the Distiller is waiting
for extract, not when extract is already queued for processing.
