<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete All 6 Beacon Dungeons?">
Speak with **Loraine Crim** to start **Activating the Gate to the Stars**, then enter the **Springlands Beacon Dungeon** in Cinder's Cradle. Complete its Embervale Museum objective to unlock the remaining five dungeon markers. Clear each marked dungeon, activate its beacon, and return to Loraine when the quest advances to **Talk To The Ancient Researcher**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

</section>

The six Beacon Dungeons are the main obstacle between reaching Cinder's Cradle and traveling to the Flying Isles. The entrance markers make them look like separate regional quests, but they all belong to the same objective: power the Ancient teleport platform at Cinder's Ascent.

Each entrance takes you somewhere different. Springlands sends you into a museum, Revelwood leads through a library and planetarium, while Nomad Highlands and Veilwater Basin have separate versions of the Engine Chambers. Their barriers do not all unlock the same way.

The easiest way to keep your progress straight is to follow the actual route through each facility and check the result of every important interaction. A glowing switch, an open door, and an updated quest counter are three different things.

<section id="all-locations" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Are All 6 Beacon Dungeons in Enshrouded?

**All six entrances are in Cinder's Cradle.** Their regional names identify the areas you travel to after using each entrance's teleportation pad, not the region where you must search for the entrance.

  caption="All six Beacon Dungeons, their interior locations, levels, and main obstacles."
  columns={beaconColumns}
  rows={beaconRows}
/>

Every dungeon entrance is found in the high-level Cinder's Cradle region, even though the interior areas have very different enemy levels. You do not need to return to Springlands or Revelwood on the main world map to find their Beacon Dungeon entrances.

The quest requires **Springlands first**. After its completion, the other five markers become available, and you can clear those dungeons in any order.

For the walkthrough below, the route is **Springlands → Revelwood → Albaneve Summits → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes → Veilwater Basin**. This order is useful for reaching several entrances from higher terrain, but it is not an ascending difficulty order. If the Albaneve lab is too difficult, complete the lower-level Nomad Highlands and Kindlewastes dungeons before returning.

<section id="start-quest" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Start Activating the Gate to the Stars

The quest begins after you find and summon **Loraine Crim, the Ancient Researcher**, during the Cinder's Cradle main story.

Speak with her at your base. She explains that the Ancient teleport platform cannot carry you to the Flying Isles until its six connected beacons have been activated.

Your journal initially directs you to:

**Reach And Use The First Dungeon Entrance**

This refers to Springlands. Track the objective and open your map to locate the marked entrance within Cinder's Cradle.

Before setting out, check your equipment. A Grappling Hook and Glider are needed for the broken cliffs and elevated approaches between several entrances. Bring healing, stamina recovery and enough inventory space for Ancient Construct materials.

Two items are particularly useful inside these dungeons:

- **Spark:** Used at Ancient alarm mechanisms. Carry some before entering rather than depending on finding a replacement while an alarm is active.
- **Archaic Lockpicks:** Required by Ancient chests and certain locked areas. They are different from ordinary Lockpicks. Collect Archaic Shards from Constructs and use them to craft more when needed.

You can complete the mandatory beacon objectives without collecting every optional weapon or collectible. If you want the Embervale miniature pieces, however, open the relevant chests before leaving each dungeon.

</section>

<section id="springlands" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Springlands Beacon Dungeon: Embervale Museum Walkthrough

The first entrance lies down in a ruined gorge in Cinder's Cradle. Follow the marked approach through the volcanic terrain until you reach the entrance structure beside the broken stone walkways.

The glowing teleportation pad takes you into the **Embervale Museum**.

### Clear the museum's first barrier

The opening museum contains exhibition displays, wildlife and Ancient Constructs. The barrier you need to open is tied to a visible enemy counter above the central passage.

This is not a switch hunt. You need to defeat enough Constructs to satisfy the counter.

Start with the enemies on the museum's main floor, then take the spiral staircase to check the upper balconies. The required enemies are spread across the room's different elevations, so clearing only the ground level may leave the barrier closed.

Watch the counter rather than trying to identify one particular enemy as the final target.

Once the requirement has been met, **BARRIER UNLOCKED** appears. Return to the central passage and move through the newly accessible section.

The important distinction is that the notification confirms the current combat barrier has opened. It does not mean the entire Springlands Beacon Dungeon is complete.

### Descend into the laser and alarm rooms

Beyond the first barrier, use the circular elevator to reach the lower portion of the museum.

This section adds two hazards. Glowing floor grates can burn you, while laser beams across the corridors can trigger an Ancient alarm.

When **ALARM RAISED** appears, locate the nearby Ancient alarm pedestal. Interact with the glowing mechanism and use Spark to resolve the alarm. The console displays **ALARM RESOLVED** when the interaction succeeds.

There are two alarm mechanisms along the lower route. Continue beyond the first rather than assuming the alarm objective is finished for the entire dungeon.

If you cross another laser and the alarm returns, check for the next console in that section. The warning does not mean your earlier interaction failed.

After resolving the alarm sections, follow the opened route into the final beacon chamber.

### Activate the first Beacon

Reach the central beacon mechanism at the far end of the museum and activate it.

## Revelwood Beacon Dungeon: Library and Grand Planetarium

The Revelwood entrance is another Cinder's Cradle location, reached by crossing the rocky approaches north of Drak'dal Camp.

The final ascent passes through a cave route and up the cliffside structures. Use the Grappling Hook and climbable surfaces to reach the entrance teleportation pad.

Inside, the route connects three areas: **Library Foyer → Library → Grand Planetarium**.

### Open the first Library Foyer barrier

The first room contains Ancient Constructs and a circular doorway leading into a smaller side area.

Clear enough space to interact safely with the illuminated wall switch beside the doorway. Activate the switch and confirm that the barrier changes state.

Do not remain in the foyer searching for a particular enemy after the door has opened. The first lock here is controlled by a switch.

You will also find Ancient chests on the way through. Collect Archaic Shards from the Constructs if you intend to open the locked ones. The ornate chest in this part of the route can provide **Astral Daggers**, but opening it is not the same as completing the beacon objective.

Continue to the teleportation pad that takes you into the Library.

### The Library's four-switch door

The next major obstacle is a sealed door connected to **four wall switches** spread through the Library rooms.

The important point is that these are wall-mounted interactions, not four enemies that must be killed.

Begin in the central Library area and work through the accessible side rooms. After pressing a switch, return to the connecting passage rather than looking for another button on the same wall.

Several of the controls are separated by short corridors and adjacent rooms, so you need to search the connected Library sections rather than standing in front of the locked door.

Check the door's status as you progress. The mechanism only opens after the full set of required switches has been activated.

When the last switch registers, the game displays **DOOR OPENED**.

Return to the main locked passage, now open, and proceed through it. The next route descends by an exterior spiral staircase and crosses toward the Grand Planetarium.

### Solve the Grand Planetarium telescope puzzle

After crossing the laser corridor, enter the Grand Planetarium. A locked ornate chest along this route contains the **Candlelight Wand**, but opening it is not required to solve the telescope puzzle.

The central barrier is controlled by two large telescopes on opposite balconies. Each telescope has a circular pressure plate directly in front of its base.

Go through the first side door and step onto the pressure plate. Watch the telescope rotate before returning through the central chamber.

<section id="albaneve" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Albaneve Summits Beacon Dungeon: Frontier Research Lab

The Albaneve Summits entrance is on a high ridge in Cinder's Cradle, across a large gap from the surrounding ruins.

You can approach the elevated area through the Lift Ruins, using the lifts and higher platforms to gain enough altitude for the final glide.

Before crossing, a temporary Flame Altar on suitable high ground can give you a convenient return point if you fall. Make sure you have sufficient Stamina for the glide rather than jumping from the first ledge that faces the entrance.

Land on the stone stairway at the far-side structure and use the teleportation pad to enter the **Frontier Research Lab**.

### Open the circular doors and reach the inner lab

The first part of the dungeon uses circular Ancient doors connected to illuminated wall switches.

When a door blocks the passage, search the nearby accessible side section for its control. Activate the switch and watch for **DOOR OPENED** before returning to the sealed passage.

The route continues through additional corridors into a chamber containing a **Shroud Root**. Destroying it awards a Skill Point and clears the corruption in that chamber.

Follow the opened passages deeper into the Frontier Research Lab.

The interior eventually leads to a large central structure with a glowing platform. This is the route down into the boss chamber, rather than another ordinary research room.

### Ancient S.P.D.R. Construct boss and final barrier

The **Ancient S.P.D.R. Construct** guards the final arena.

Its attacks cover substantial portions of the room, especially with tracking beams and explosive strikes. The more reliable approach is to keep moving around the arena structures and avoid standing in one exposed position while attacking.

After the Construct encounter, follow the accessible route toward the final reward chamber and beacon.

Proceed into the newly opened chamber, where the locked silver chest contains the **Albaneve Summits Miniature Piece**.

Finish the beacon interaction and confirm that the quest counter advances. If this is your third completed dungeon, it should read **3/6**.

The Albaneve Beacon Dungeon is not the same location as the <Link href="/enshrouded/albaneve-summits-hollow-halls" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls</Link>. The latter has the Hollow Titan and its own Bone Key quest; it does not satisfy this beacon objective.

</section>

## Nomad Highlands Beacon Dungeon: Engine Chambers

The Nomad Highlands entrance is hidden in a mountain fissure near the elevated Albaneve side of Cinder's Cradle. Look for the opening between the rock walls rather than searching the exposed summit.

Glide into the fissure and reach the entrance teleportation pad. Inside, continue through the Vukah Cave Pillar and the connecting passages until another teleportation pad takes you into the **Engine Chambers**.

The main hall is a tall industrial chamber with several levels of walkways and moving machinery above lava. The Ancient S.P.D.R. Construct is farther along this route, but you must first open the connected doors and reach the upper passages.

### Activate Both Wall Controls to Open the Door

From the central staircase, make your way toward the two side passages. Each contains a glowing square control mounted on the wall between the machinery.

Activate the control in one passage, then cross to the opposite passage and activate its matching control.

The **Ancient S.P.D.R. Construct** waits at the top of a broad staircase. The stairs are useful for controlling the distance and your line of sight during the fight.

## Kindlewastes Beacon Dungeon: Hydropower Plant

The Kindlewastes entrance is on an elevated ruin in Cinder's Cradle. Approach from higher ground and glide toward the marked structure, then use the teleportation pad to enter the **Hydropower Plant**.

Inside, the route passes through Ancient machinery, Shroud-filled rooms, broken walkways and timed floor traps. The main challenge is moving between these disconnected areas rather than fighting a major boss.

## Veilwater Basin Beacon Dungeon: Flooded Engine Chambers

The final entrance in this route is the **Veilwater Basin Dungeon Entrance**, north of Cinder's Ascent.

Approaching it from the upper ridges can be confusing. The entrance lies lower down in a green, partially sheltered area with Ancient ruins and palm trees, while the surrounding mountain slopes make it easy to end up on the wrong side.

Do not keep climbing a steep wall simply because the objective marker looks close. The more useful approach is to reach a high point with a clear view of the basin, then glide down toward the entrance structure.

You will find an Ancient Construct guarding the teleportation pad. Use the pad to enter another area called the **Engine Chambers**.

This is a different facility from the Nomad Highlands Engine Chambers.

### First flooded shaft: Check the optional chest

Near the beginning, there is a deep circular flooded shaft.

A chest at the bottom contains loot, including the **Rattlesnake Bow**. Retrieving it is optional for completing the beacon.

The main danger is the return swim. Your Oxygen can fall to critical levels before you reach the surface.

If you are only trying to finish 6/6, do not treat this deep chest as a mandatory route objective. Continue into the facility instead.

### Open the conduit and swim into the next section

The mandatory water traversal occurs farther into the Engine Chambers.

The facility contains illuminated wall buttons controlling circular vault doors and connecting pipes. As you reach a sealed passage, explore the adjacent accessible rooms for its wall control.

One of these switches opens a **round water conduit**. This is the important flooded route: it connects to the next section of the facility.

Once it opens, enter the pipe and swim through the green-lit passage to the adjoining machinery area.

Do not confuse this with the optional shaft near the start. The conduit takes you forward; the earlier dive takes you down to loot and back again.

### Restore access through the machinery rooms

Beyond the flooded connection, the route loops between passages around a central machinery chamber.

You will encounter more wall switches, circular doors and laser hazards. Work through the accessible side rooms and activate the controls before returning to the central area to check which passage has opened.

An alarm can be triggered in one of the connecting corridors. Resolve it at the available Ancient console, then continue along the newly accessible route.

Later passages include retracting metal floor spikes and another switch hidden away from the central doorway.

At the final circular door, interacting with it immediately shows **Locked**. Do not leave the area to search for another route.

Find the illuminated square switch beside the doorway and activate it first. Then return to the circular door and press **F** to open it. Continue through the newly accessible passage.

### Finish the sixth Beacon

The last chamber contains the final beacon objective. Complete the interaction and check the quest tracker.

The **Veilwater Basin Miniature Piece** is found in a locked chest along the final section, completing the six-piece regional set if you collected the other five.

With Veilwater completed as your sixth dungeon, the objective advances from **Complete All Beacon Dungeons: 5/6** to the next story step.

Look for:

**Talk To The Ancient Researcher**

That is the confirmation that the six-dungeon requirement has been satisfied.

</section>

<section id="rewards" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Rewards Do You Get From the Six Beacon Dungeons?

The most important reward is access to the next part of the Cinder's Cradle story. Completing the six beacon objectives powers the teleportation platform required to reach the Flying Isles.

There is also an optional collectible set. Each dungeon holds one **Embervale Miniature Piece**, named after its corresponding region.

<GuideTable
  caption="The six Embervale Miniature Pieces found across the Beacon Dungeons."
  columns={collectibleColumns}
  rows={collectibleRows}
/>

If you leave a dungeon after lighting its beacon but before opening the relevant chest, the main quest may be complete while your miniature collection remains unfinished.

Several dungeons also contain distinctive equipment, including the Candlelight Wand in Revelwood, the Electric Initialiser in Kindlewastes and the Rattlesnake Bow in Veilwater Basin.

These are separate from the mandatory beacon interactions. You can prioritize finishing the quest, or bring sufficient Archaic Lockpicks and collect the chests while you are already there.

## What Do You Do After Completing All 6 Beacon Dungeons?

Once the final beacon is completed, open your journal and look at **Activating the Gate to the Stars**.

The quest should no longer ask you to complete another Beacon Dungeon. Instead, it directs you to speak with **Loraine Crim**.

Return to the Ancient Researcher and complete her conversation. She confirms that the route to the Flying Isles is now accessible and that the next objective lies at the Ancient teleportation platform in Cinder's Ascent.
