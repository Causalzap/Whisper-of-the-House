<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete the Revelwood Hollow Halls?">
Craft the **Revelwood Bone Key** with **5 Ectoplasm Gems and 20 Bones** at the Collector, then enter the Hollow Halls in Revelwood. Follow the gates and wall controls, use your Glider and Grappling Hook to cross the larger chambers, and collect the green runes blocking your route. Defeat the **Hollow Cyclops**, take its head, and return to the Collector to craft the **Ectoplasm Press**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

</section>

The Revelwood Hollow Halls are longer and more complicated than the first Hollow Halls expedition in Springlands. The difficulty is not limited to the Hollow Cyclops at the bottom. Several passages loop around the same large chambers, a locked barrier can send you searching across different elevations, and Hollow enemies can continue spawning while you are trying to find the next switch.

Not every closed-looking passage needs a puzzle. Some iron gates can be opened directly. Others depend on wall controls, collecting green runes, or clearing the enemies guarding the route. Knowing which obstacle you are dealing with makes the dungeon considerably easier to navigate.

The main objective is **Ectoplasm Press For The Collector**. You need the Hollow Cyclops Head from the final encounter to finish the crafting portion of that quest.

This dungeon is separate from the Revelwood Beacon Dungeon in Cinder's Cradle. If you are completing **Activating the Gate to the Stars**, follow the <Link href="/enshrouded/six-beacon-dungeons" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">six Beacon Dungeons walkthrough</Link> instead.

## Where Is the Revelwood Hollow Halls Entrance?

The Revelwood Hollow Halls are in western Revelwood, within the Shroud-covered area northwest of the **Revelwood Ancient Spire**. The entrance is also northwest of the **Mark of Sameth** and west of the **Blue Goblet Tavern**.

The Collector's quest marks the dungeon on your map. Approaching from the Ancient Spire gives you a useful high starting point for gliding toward the western ruins.

The quest recommends **Level 17**. You can enter with suitable lower-level equipment, but the Hollow Cyclops and the larger enemy groups will punish a character without reliable damage, healing, and mobility.

### Craft the Revelwood Bone Key

Speak with **Alden Crowley, the Collector**, at your base and open his crafting menu.

The Revelwood Bone Key requires:

- **5 Ectoplasm Gems**
- **20 Bones**

The Ectoplasm Gems are crafted through the Collector using Bonemeal and Ectoplasm materials. Having Ectoplasm Fragments in storage is not the same as having the finished gems required by the key recipe.

Make the key before leaving your base and keep it in your inventory. The entrance is a considerable distance from a convenient crafting station, and reaching the sealed door without the key means another trip back.

### What should you bring?

A **Grappling Hook and Glider** are essential movement tools for the route. Several platforms are separated by significant gaps, and some green runes sit above the floor or inside passages reached from another elevation.

Bring enough healing for a long expedition, along with a repaired weapon that can deal with groups of Hollow enemies. A ranged weapon or spell is particularly useful when enemies gather around their spawning structures.

Make room in your inventory for Ectoplasm, treasure, and the Hollow Cyclops Head. You should not need to collect every optional chest to finish the Collector's quest.

Once prepared, use the Bone Key at the dungeon entrance and descend into the Hollow Halls.

## Open the First Gate and Find the Wall Controls

After entering the dungeon and progressing through the opening rooms, you encounter a large iron gate.

**This first gate can be opened directly.** Walk up to it and use the interaction prompt. You do not need to search the surrounding chamber for an extra switch just to open this particular door.

Move forward through the available passage until you reach the larger connected rooms.

The dungeon starts introducing green barriers and elevated platforms around this part of the route. When a barrier displays missing green runes, look into the adjoining chambers and above the ground-level passages. The Grappling Hook is useful for reaching platforms that appear inaccessible from below.

Collect the available runes and return to the blocked route when the barrier releases. Do not confuse these rune barriers with the ordinary iron gate you opened earlier.

### Find the illuminated wall switch

Farther inside, a large chamber connects multiple side passages. Hollow enemies and spawning structures occupy parts of this area.

Clear enough enemies to move safely, then examine the raised passages for an illuminated square wall control.

One control is reached through an elevated side route. A working approach is to follow the connected platforms, use the Grappling Hook to climb, and continue along the upper passage until you find the switch.

Activate it before leaving that side of the chamber.

The larger door sequence also involves a control on the opposite side. If the main passage remains locked after the first activation, do not keep pressing the same switch. Return toward the central chamber and cross to the other side.

## Glide Across the Chamber to Reach the Second Gate

After operating the first wall control, use the height you gained to cross toward the opposite side of the room.

You can open your **Glider** to cover the gap rather than returning all the way to the lowest floor. Aim for the accessible platform or opening across the chamber, then continue toward the next iron doorway.

This is the point where the route can become confusing: the next area is not necessarily reached by following a single continuous ground-level corridor.

### Find the second door

On the opposite side, look for the next gate connecting the upper passages.

Continue through the rooms and check for the matching wall control associated with the larger door mechanism. Some routes require you to clear Hollow enemies before the nearby barrier releases and the control becomes accessible.

If **BARRIER UNLOCKED** appears during combat, check the previously blocked passage before searching for more runes. A combat-related barrier is not solved the same way as a collectible-rune barrier.

### Continue past the chest to the third door

Farther along this route, a treasure chest provides a useful landmark.

**The third doorway is behind the chest area.** Do not assume the chest itself is the required quest interaction.

Collect its contents if you want the optional reward, then continue through the passage beyond it.

This sequence is easier to follow by recognizing the gate, switch, opposite-side platform, and chest than by trying to memorize every turn through the identical-looking stone corridors.

Beyond the third doorway, the dungeon begins opening into larger areas filled with Hollow enemies, green symbols, and additional passages.

## Find the Green Runes and Unlock the Remaining Barriers

The next major progression obstacle is a large chamber with green rune targets spread across several elevations and adjoining rooms.

The runes are not all beside the barrier. Some are accessible from the upper platforms, while others require you to descend into the lower passages.

When you reach this area, look across the chamber before jumping down. Green light on a distant wall or beneath a ledge can identify a route you will need to visit later.

### Search the lower chamber and spider cave

One of the more important detours takes you into the lower part of the chamber, where a cave-like passage contains spiders and another glowing green rune.

If you have explored the visible platforms but the barrier still shows missing targets, return to the lower passages and check the cave entrances.

Collect the green rune you find there. Once the remaining required runes have been activated, **BARRIER UNLOCKED** confirms that the corresponding route is open.

The important distinction is that the cave rune contributes to the barrier's required activations. You do not need to defeat a separate boss or find an unrelated wall switch inside the cave to make that rune count.

### Clear enemy-controlled barriers

Other sections involve large groups of Hollow enemies, including rooms with spawning structures.

These areas can produce a **BARRIER UNLOCKED** notification while you are clearing the enemies. That does not mean you missed a glowing collectible rune.

If enemies continue appearing around the same position, look for a nearby Hollow spawning structure and destroy it when you can reach it safely. Otherwise, you can spend unnecessary time fighting new enemies while trying to explore the room.

After a barrier unlocks, return to the newly accessible doorway and continue.

There is no need to treat every enemy pack as a unique puzzle. The meaningful checks are whether the required passage opens and whether you can reach the next room.

### Descend toward the final encounter

After the larger rune areas, the dungeon becomes more linear.

The later route includes a dangerous descent with broken sections of flooring and harmful patches along the path. Watch where you land, use your movement tools to cross the larger gaps, and take advantage of the next return point before continuing.

Do not rush straight down a slope without checking for missing floor sections. A fall can cost more progress than a brief pause to identify the next safe platform.

Once you reach the lower encounter area, look for the route into the Hollow Cyclops chamber. An elevated Grappling Hook point helps you reach the boss-side passage.

Prepare your equipment and restore your health before starting the fight.

## How Do You Defeat the Hollow Cyclops?

The **Hollow Cyclops** is the main boss at the bottom of the Revelwood Hollow Halls.

The arena has enough open space to fight at range, but the Cyclops is not your only concern. Additional Hollow enemies can interrupt attacks, drain your stamina, and make it harder to escape the boss's larger movements.

### Deal with the smaller enemies before they overwhelm you

The fight becomes more dangerous when skeletons and other Hollow enemies gather around the Cyclops.

If several appear together, move away from the boss long enough to thin their numbers. Trying to attack the Cyclops while surrounded can leave you without enough stamina to dodge its next attack.

Area damage is useful here because it lets you clear smaller enemies without spending too long targeting them individually.

Do not assume that every enemy near the boss must be defeated before you can deal damage to the Cyclops. The practical goal is to keep the arena manageable.

### Watch your stamina and the hazardous ground

A failed dodge or prolonged attack sequence can leave you exposed when the Cyclops closes the distance.

Avoid spending your entire stamina bar on repeated movement or melee attacks. Save enough to escape the next swing or ground attack.

Also watch for harmful green effects and patches around the arena. Move out of the affected area rather than standing still to finish another attack.

A strong ranged weapon or spell helps you recognize the next attack without needing to remain directly beside the Cyclops.

### A useful ranged strategy: Ice Totems

One effective approach is to use **Ice Totems** for continuing damage while you reposition around the arena.

Place the totem where it can attack the Cyclops, then maintain enough distance to react to the boss's movements. Recast when needed, but do not stand exposed while preparing another spell.

This approach works particularly well for a magic-focused character who has invested in additional totem capacity.

It is not mandatory. Other builds can defeat the boss using their normal damage and defensive tools. The main advantage of the totems is that they continue contributing damage while you concentrate on staying alive.

### Defeat the Cyclops and confirm the barrier unlock

Once the Hollow Cyclops falls, **BARRIER UNLOCKED** appears and the route beyond the boss becomes accessible.

If you leave without that material, you may have to return to the dungeon to obtain another one.

## Hollow Cyclops Rewards and How to Craft the Ectoplasm Press

The most important reward from the Revelwood Hollow Halls is the **Hollow Cyclops Head**. This is not merely a decorative trophy: the Collector uses it to craft the Ectoplasm Press.

One Hollow Cyclops defeat produced the following loot:

- **Greatsword of the Old Order**
- **5 Legendary Runes**
- **Hollow Cyclops Head**
- **6 Giant Bones**

The Greatsword is worth inspecting if you use two-handed melee weapons. Whether it is an upgrade depends on your current equipment and the attributes of the item you receive.

### Where do you go after beating the Hollow Cyclops?

Once the final barrier unlocks, follow the newly available passage toward the reward and exit area.

The dungeon contains additional treasure chests, including containers intended for different equipment types. Opening every optional chest is not required to obtain the Hollow Cyclops Head or finish the main dungeon objective.

When you have collected the materials you want, use the exit portal to leave the Hollow Halls and return to your base.

### Craft the Ectoplasm Press at the Collector

Speak with **Alden Crowley** again. The quest **Ectoplasm Press For The Collector** now directs you to craft the station using the head recovered from the Hollow Cyclops.

The Ectoplasm Press recipe requires:

- **1 Hollow Cyclops Head**
- **120 Ectoplasm Fragments**
- **5 Copper Bars**
- **10 Charcoal**
- **4 Stone**
- **4 Wood Logs**

Craft the station through the Collector and place it at your base.

The Ectoplasm Press unlocks additional crafting options, including processing Hollow Halls materials into refined Ectoplasm products and producing Purified Slime.

If the Ectoplasm Press recipe does not appear, check that you collected the Hollow Cyclops Head, advanced the Collector's quest, and have given the Collector a sheltered crafting area.

Craft and place the Ectoplasm Press to complete this Collector objective. Optional treasure rooms can be revisited later; opening every chest is not required.

Much later in the Collector's progression, the <Link href="/enshrouded/albaneve-summits-hollow-halls" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls</Link> and <Link href="/enshrouded/veilwater-basin-hollow-halls" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls</Link> introduce different dungeon mechanics and stronger encounters. Neither is required to obtain the Ectoplasm Press.
