Craft the **Veilwater Basin Bone Key** at the Collector, then equip the **Underwater Grappling Hook** and diving gear. Bring a **Mining Sieve** for underwater mining and a ranged attack for the waterwheel switch. Inside, work through the water controls and barriers to reach the **Hollow Wispwyvern**.

A glowing Grappling Hook anchor sits directly above you. You swim closer, point at it, and wait for the interaction prompt—but nothing happens. Later, the waterwheel starts moving and the elevator works, yet another door remains locked.

These are two different obstacles in the Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls. The first requires the correct underwater equipment; the second involves separate floor switches. Between them are submerged passages, changing water levels, Hollow enemies, and machinery that can be operated from across a room.

The dungeon ends with the **Hollow Wispwyvern**, followed by a reward chamber containing two gold and four silver sarcophagi.

<section id="entrance" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where Is the Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls Entrance?

The entrance is in **Veilwater Basin**, approximately **280 meters southwest of the Cyclops Cave Passage**. Accept **The Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls** quest from **Alden Crowley**, the Collector, to obtain the dungeon objective and its map marker.

This expedition comes after the Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls in the Collector's progression. You need the **Veilwater Basin Bone Key** to open the entrance.

The key is crafted through the Collector using materials from the Hollow Halls progression, including **Icy Giant Bones**. The <Link href="/enshrouded/albaneve-summits-hollow-halls" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls</Link> are an earlier place to obtain those bones from the Hollow Titan.

Once the key is ready, travel to the Veilwater entrance and use it on the sealed door. The quest then directs you into the dungeon to reach its end.

The Collector's quest recommends Level 32, but the interior can still punish an underprepared character. Several fights involve tightly grouped Hollow enemies, and the water sections require equipment that ordinary land exploration does not.

</section>

<section id="equipment" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Bring Before Entering?

**The Underwater Grappling Hook is the most important equipment check.** The regular Grappling Hook cannot replace it for underwater anchors, even when the anchor itself is clearly visible.

The Carpenter can craft an Underwater Grappling Hook. Equip it in your Grappling Hook slot before entering the dungeon's flooded sections.

Bring the following as well:

- **Diving helmet and swimming equipment:** Several passages require swimming below the surface, moving through underwater doorways, and returning to air pockets.
- **Mining Sieve:** Bring this tool for mineable material below the water. It is designed for underwater mining, unlike the ordinary Pickaxe used on land.
- **Ranged weapon or spell:** A later mechanism must be struck from a distance to release water into a channel.
- **Healing and mana or stamina recovery:** The underwater routes connect to rooms filled with Hollow enemies and spawners.
- **A backup weapon:** Repair stations are not always immediately beside the next major encounter.

The diving equipment comes with a combat trade-off. Replacing ordinary armor can reduce the offensive or defensive bonuses you normally rely on, so use the diving pieces for long swimming sections and switch back when you reach dry combat rooms.

Do not confuse swimming endurance with oxygen. A **Diver's Endurance Flask** reduces swimming stamina consumption, but it does not replace the need to reach air before your oxygen runs out.

One more preparation detail matters for magic and summoning builds: summoned skeletons can be left behind or disappear when their owner travels through deep water. Refresh your summons once the route returns to dry ground rather than relying on them to follow every dive.

## Lower the First Water Level and Open the Initial Route

After passing through the first enemy-filled chambers, the route descends into water. Near the beginning of this flooded section is an interactive control that changes the water level.

Operate the control before exploring the lower passages. The water recedes and a nearby doorway becomes usable, allowing you to continue toward the next rooms.

In this section, the mechanism may look like an unreachable switch behind the wall. A submerged obstruction can also block access to the next control. Use the Mining Sieve for underwater mining and check whether clearing the obstruction changes the water level.

Once the second mechanism registers, the connected doors open. You can then continue into the next chambers rather than returning to the beginning of the flooded section.

## Why Is the Underwater Grappling Hook Not Working?

A particularly frustrating section has a visible Grappling Hook anchor above the water. It can appear close enough to use, but the normal interaction prompt may never appear.

Moving directly beneath it, looking upward, and adjusting the camera will not solve the issue when the wrong Grappling Hook is equipped.

The required item is specifically the **Underwater Grappling Hook**.

This is easy to mistake for a positioning problem, especially in multiplayer when another player can use the anchor from the same water-filled room.

Check your equipped tool before spending more time moving around the anchor. The Underwater Grappling Hook works on land as well, so you can use it throughout this dungeon without repeatedly changing back to a standard hook.

### What can you do if you entered without it?

You may still be able to progress through some submerged routes without using every Grappling Hook point, but an underwater ascent can leave you stranded below the next dry platform.

In multiplayer, a teammate carrying the correct hook can operate a water-level mechanism, open another route, or temporarily share the tool. That avoids forcing both players to abandon their expedition.

For solo exploration, returning with the proper hook is the more dependable option. Continuing deeper without it risks reaching another flooded chamber where you cannot climb back out normally.

### Watch your oxygen while searching for the exit

The underwater chambers include openings where you can surface and recover air. These are important stopping points between dives.

When oxygen becomes critical, stop searching for optional chests or alternate passages. Follow the nearest opening toward the surface, recover, and then make another attempt.

One narrow underwater passage is easier to cross by deliberately swimming downward rather than trying to move straight through it at surface height.

<section id="toxic-sludge" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Cross the Toxic Sludge Without Losing All Your Stamina

Beyond the flooded rooms, several Hollow encounters take place beside dangerous green sludge.

The sludge drains stamina quickly. That creates an especially dangerous situation for melee characters: an enemy may be standing just beyond the hazardous ground, but approaching it directly consumes the stamina needed to jump or escape.

Getting knocked into the sludge can leave you unable to jump back onto safe ground until enough stamina regenerates.

Use the solid edges of the chamber whenever possible. Ranged attacks are useful against enemies standing in the sludge, particularly when multiple Hollow enemies and flying threats are occupying the same area.

Destroy nearby Hollow spawners before spending time collecting loot. Clearing the immediate threats first also makes it easier to find the route out of the room without repeatedly entering the hazardous ground.

From here, the passage eventually reconnects with higher platforms and a water-driven machinery section.

<section id="waterwheel" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Start the Waterwheel and Activate the Elevator?

The waterwheel room initially looks like it has a broken elevator. There is a water channel nearby, but insufficient water is reaching the wheel to operate the machinery.

The control is not on the elevator itself.

Look across the upper part of the surrounding chamber for a switch that can be struck from a distance. A ranged attack is needed to trigger it.

Move into a position with a clear line of sight and hit the switch. A close-range wand attack may not reach it from the first position you try, so adjust your position or use a weapon with better range.

Once activated, water begins moving through the channel.

The sequence is:

1. Find the ranged switch overlooking the water channel.
2. Hit it and watch for water to flow through the channel.
3. Return toward the waterwheel.
4. Board the elevator once the machinery is moving.

The running waterwheel confirms that this part of the mechanism is working.

However, there is another locked doorway beyond the machinery. It is controlled separately and should not be confused with the waterwheel puzzle.

</section>

## The Door Is Still Locked: Find Both Floor Switches

After restoring water flow, you may reach another door that remains closed even though the nearby mechanisms appear to be functioning.

There are **two floor switches**, positioned on opposite sides of this part of the chamber.

Activating only one does not complete the door mechanism.

This is the point where the machinery section transitions into the moving laser-platform challenge.

### Ride the laser elevator

The next elevator moves upward through a shaft containing laser barriers.

Watch the position of the beams while the platform rises. You need to adjust your position and jump as the barriers approach, rather than standing still and waiting for the lift to reach the top.

## How Do You Beat the Hollow Wispwyvern?

The final large encounter takes place near the end of the upper route. A return point and repair opportunity appear before the final approach, making this a sensible place to prepare your weapons and consumables.

The boss is the **Hollow Wispwyvern**, a large Hollow creature capable of dealing heavy damage with its special attacks.

One noticeable warning occurs when the creature appears to channel an attack. Its next ability can deal heavy damage, so this is a poor moment to stand still and continue casting.

The encounter also rewards keeping some separation between players and the boss. A summoning build can temporarily distract it with skeletons, but the Wispwyvern is capable of destroying those summons quickly.

For this fight:

- **Ranged and magic builds:** Use the available distance to recognize incoming attacks. Hold enough resources for healing rather than spending the entire Mana pool on damage.
- **Melee builds:** Close in after a major attack has resolved and avoid remaining beside the creature throughout its next ability.
- **Groups:** One player can recover or heal while another keeps attacking, but do not rely on summoned creatures to hold the boss indefinitely.

The fight does not require an elaborate multi-stage mechanic to reach the reward room. Defeat the Wispwyvern, collect its materials, and continue through the final passage.

### What does the Hollow Wispwyvern drop?

The notable rewards include **Hollow Wispwyvern Head**, **Icy Giant Bones**, Ectoplasm Aggregate, and Runes.

The defeated Wispwyvern dropped **44 Ectoplasm Aggregates** in one encounter. The amount may vary, so check what you collected before planning your next crafting upgrade.

Additional equipment collected during the encounter included **Venerable Hollow Chest Plate** and **Aurora Sword**. Check their attributes before equipping them; the usefulness of a Hollow-specific armor bonus depends on whether you intend to keep fighting Hollow enemies.

Ectoplasm Aggregate is also used in later Collector crafting, so keep any amount you still need for spell and equipment recipes.

## Final Rewards: Two Gold and Four Silver Sarcophagi

After the Wispwyvern, continue into the final chamber.

The room contains **six major reward sarcophagi**:

- **2 gold sarcophagi**
- **4 silver sarcophagi**

They are arranged symmetrically, with one gold and two silver containers on each side.

The contents are not all predetermined upgrades. Equipment obtained from this chamber can vary, so the named weapons from one run should not be treated as guaranteed rewards.

Examples of items collected from the final containers included Fool's Demise, Venerable Hollow equipment, several wands, and other weapons.

Check the item level and bonuses before replacing your existing equipment. A higher-level weapon may still be a poor choice for your build if its damage type or special bonuses do not match your skills.

### Red Marble decorations

The dungeon also contains lore and crafting discoveries connected to **Red Marble decorations**.

Explore the upper passages for the relevant writings, including **The Path of Red Marble** and **Crimson Arches**. Completing the connected objective unlocks additional decorative building pieces.

These discoveries are useful for building, but they are separate from the main requirement to defeat the Wispwyvern and reach the final chamber.

Once you have collected the materials and opened the reward containers you want, you can leave the Hollow Halls. There is no need to continue searching every flooded side room to finish the main expedition.
