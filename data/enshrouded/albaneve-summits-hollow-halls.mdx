The hardest part of the Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls is not necessarily the final boss. A locked door in the early rooms can send you searching across several floors, while the freezing sections give you limited time to figure out where the next passage leads.

Several rooms look like they should connect directly, but the dungeon frequently sends you upward through side passages, elevators, and gliding sections. There are also optional routes that let you reach the other side of a locked door without opening it. That can save time, although it makes the layout more confusing when you return later.

## Where Is the Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls Entrance?

The Hollow Halls entrance is in the snowy **Albaneve Summits** region. The Collector, **Alden Crowley**, marks its location when you receive **The Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls** quest.

The entrance lies roughly **250 meters north of the Spooky Grave**, with the Pumpkin Patch farther to the south. Follow the quest marker toward the large ruined structure rather than searching the nearby mountain caves.

The quest recommends **Level 32**, but your equipment and frost protection matter just as much as your character level.

### How to unlock the entrance

Craft the **Albaneve Summits Bone Key** through the Collector before traveling to the dungeon. Keep it in your inventory for the entrance interaction.

Forgetting the key means returning to your base, even after reaching the building. It is worth checking your inventory before making the journey across the frozen terrain.

The Collector's quest has three main objectives:

1. Craft the Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls Key.
2. Reach the dungeon entrance.
3. Descend through the Hollow Halls and reach the end.

The outside approach is already cold enough to drain your protection timer. Once you enter the structure, use the teleportation platform to reach the dungeon interior.

The Albaneve Hollow Halls are separate from the six Beacon Dungeons required for the Flying Isles. Those Beacon objectives belong to the <Link href="/enshrouded" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Cinder's Cradle main story progression</Link>.

## Bring Frost Protection, Healing, and Movement Tools

Cold exposure remains a danger inside the Hollow Halls. The first teleportation area is relatively safe, but later rooms contain frozen floors, icy ledges, and passages where your remaining warmth decreases while you are fighting or looking for switches.

Before entering, prepare the following:

- **Frost protection:** Bring cold-resistance food, rings, or potions. Equip the rings before entering a freezing section rather than waiting until the timer is nearly empty.
- **Healing and stamina recovery:** The dungeon contains repeated Hollow encounters, including rooms where enemies arrive in large groups.
- **Glider and Updraft:** These help you cross open chambers and reach elevated platforms. Updraft is especially useful for an optional upper-room shortcut.
- **Lockpicks:** Useful for optional treasure chests, including a chest reached through the higher icy platforms.
- **Spare inventory space:** You will collect Ectoplasm, equipment, and materials needed after the dungeon.

A frost-resistance ring can be a trade-off for a combat ring with lifesteal or other bonuses. More protection gives you time to cross frozen sections, but replacing your normal ring may make prolonged fights harder.

Use the equipment that keeps the timer manageable without losing too much survivability. Some rooms let your warmth recover after you leave the freezing area, so you do not need to remain fully equipped for cold weather during every fight.

There is a repair station near the beginning of the interior route. Take advantage of it before pushing deeper into the dungeon.

## First Barrier: Find the Green Runes Above the Room

After entering through the teleportation platform, you encounter the first barrier objective. The nearby green runes are spread across different elevations rather than grouped at ground level.

The first group requires **three rune activations**. One is accessible during your initial exploration, while the others draw you toward the raised ledges and chest platforms.

Look above and behind your starting position when a rune appears unreachable. There is a route up from the side of the chamber, and an apparently distant rune can be approached by climbing around the upper edge instead of trying to jump directly toward it from below.

Once the required runes are collected, continue through the next passages. Some sections contain optional chests or dead ends, so keep the barrier and the next open doorway as your reference points.

The dungeon is full of Hollow enemies, but defeating everything in the current room is not always what opens the next barrier. Pay attention to the rune indicators and interactive switches.

## The Locked Door Puzzle: Make Sure You Actually Press the Switch

One of the most confusing rooms contains a closed door with an indicator above it. The door cannot be opened directly, and the surrounding architecture makes it seem as though the route must continue through the high platforms.

There is a switch in the nearby side-room sequence. The key is to **interact with it and confirm that the mechanism changes state**.

It is possible to reach the switch, search the nearby chest, and leave without activating it. The locked door then stays closed even though you have already visited the correct room.

When the door remains closed, return to the switch room before attempting a full search of the dungeon.

In a group, have one player operate the switch while the other checks the doorway. Otherwise, both players can reach the correct place without either completing the interaction.

The surrounding chambers are tall enough to create another complication: you can reach parts of the upper route without following the intended door sequence.

### Optional shortcut: Glider and Updraft from the icy ledges

An alternative route uses the elevated stone structures near the icy platforms.

Reach the higher position along the western side of the chamber, then:

1. Stand on the raised stone or sarcophagus near the upper corner.
2. Double Jump and open your Glider toward the higher opening.
3. Use **Updraft** to gain enough height.
4. Turn into the elevated passage and land on the upper ledge.

The timing takes some practice. Activating Updraft too early or turning before you gain enough height can leave you sliding back down the icy surface.

This shortcut is useful for exploring the upper rooms and reaching an optional chest, but it does not replace the switch itself. You may end up on the far side of the locked door and still need to return later to activate the mechanism.

For a straightforward completion route, open the normal door before using the upper passages to search for treasure.

## How to Stop Dying From Hypothermia Inside the Dungeon

The cold becomes dangerous when you spend too long searching a room after the enemies are dead.

Your frost timer keeps running while you inspect walls, climb platforms, and collect loot. It is easy to finish a fight successfully and then die because there is too little warmth left to reach the next safe area.

The most punishing failure is a repeated revival cycle. A revived player may return with very little protection time remaining. Reviving each other while standing in the same freezing room can lead to another immediate death rather than solving the problem.

Once your timer becomes critical, stop looking for optional loot and move toward the nearest warmer passage or return point.

A nearby heat source may increase your frost resistance, but that does not always reset the timer completely. In one outside section, standing beside a flame extended the available time while the countdown continued.

The safer approach is to prepare resistance before entering a long icy section and use the nonfreezing rooms between major obstacles to recover.

If the entire group is trapped with only seconds remaining, restarting from the last safe point may be more effective than repeatedly reviving one another in the same spot.

## Cross the Icy Slides and Activate the Runes While Gliding

Farther inside, the route leads through icy slides, suspended platforms, and a large open chamber with multiple Updraft currents.

Some of the barrier targets are positioned along the descent or above the floor. Running around the bottom of the room will not bring you close enough to every one.

The route here is built around moving through the air:

- Follow the slide into the open chamber.
- Open the Glider and use the Updraft currents to stay airborne.
- Pass through or activate the green barrier targets as you descend and rise.
- Return toward the door once the remaining rune indicators have been cleared.

One target can be collected while dropping past it. Others require changing direction in the air and catching the next current.

A missed rune can force another circuit through the freezing chamber. Check your remaining warmth before repeating the glide.

Clear the visible targets before searching the distant corners for hidden loot. Once the final required rune is collected, **BARRIER UNLOCKED** confirms the way forward.

The route continues toward an elevator and another return point. The area beyond the elevator lets you recover warmth before the next frozen stretch.

## Find the Four Runes Near the Upper Elevator and Side Rooms

The later barrier sequence is spread across another large, multi-level space.

The barrier here shows **four required activations**. The important detail is that these targets are not all on the main floor.

A working elevator leads to an upper level where one of the green targets is accessible. Additional targets are positioned higher around the chamber and inside connected side rooms.

Begin by checking the elevator landing rather than clearing every side chamber at ground level. From there, look across the opposite walls for the other elevated targets.

At least one target is reached by following a side passage through another enemy-filled room. The room also contains a Hollow spawner, so clear it when practical before searching the walls and upper platforms.

Return to the barrier after collecting all four required targets. The next major section contains heavier combat and the final encounter.

## How to Beat the Hollow Titan in Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls

The **Hollow Titan** appears during a large fight near the end of the dungeon. The challenge begins with repeated waves of Hollow enemies, which can make it difficult to identify the larger enemy immediately.

The room contains several smaller chambers and enemy entry points. Leaving the surrounding enemies alive while focusing only on the Titan can quickly fill the floor with additional threats.

Clear nearby groups and destroy accessible Hollow spawners when you find them. That gives you more space to move and reduces the number of enemies competing for your attention during the fight.

The Titan is large enough to block movement through parts of the chamber, and one of its attacks covers an area around it. Avoid staying directly beside it while the smaller enemies are gathering around you.

A useful approach is to deal with the smaller threats first, move around the larger enemy when its attack begins, and return damage during the openings between attacks. Ranged damage or area attacks help when several Hollow enemies are clustered together.

After defeating the Titan, loot its body before leaving the room.

The defeated Titan can provide:

- **Icy Giant Bones** — required for the next Bone Key and useful in later Collector crafting.
- **Hollow Titan Head** — a distinctive trophy from the encounter.
- **Ectoplasm materials** — additional resources from the dungeon.
- Other equipment and loot, depending on what you collect during the fight.

A defeated Titan yielded **21 Icy Giant Bones** in one encounter, but that should not be treated as a guaranteed quantity for every kill.

Defeating the Titan does not necessarily complete the quest at that exact moment. Continue through the newly accessible rooms and proceed toward the final objective.

## Finish the Quest and Open the Final Ectoplasm Chests

Beyond the Titan encounter, explore the newly accessible side rooms and continue toward the final chamber.

There are additional Hollow spawners and treasure rooms in this section. Some optional chests require a Grappling Hook to reach, while the more important reward chamber lies beyond the final quest passage.

The objective **Master The Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls** completes when you reach the end of the dungeon. You do not have to purchase every optional reward to finish the quest.

The final chamber contains **four gold and two silver sarcophagi**. Opening them consumes Ectoplasm Clusters, so check each container's cost before interacting. One of the reward containers requires **7 Ectoplasm Clusters**.

Several rewards are random equipment pieces rather than guaranteed upgrades. Higher-level characters may find that some of the weapons and armor are worth salvaging for Runes instead of equipping.

Use your remaining Ectoplasm according to what you still need. Opening every optional container is not required for the dungeon's main completion objective, and the Collector also uses Ectoplasm materials for crafting.

### What Comes After Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls?

The next dungeon is in **Veilwater Basin**. Its Bone Key requires **Icy Giant Bones**, which you can obtain during the Albaneve Summits expedition.

After collecting the bones, check the Collector's available recipes. The Veilwater Basin Bone Key uses **one Icy Giant Bone**, so keep at least one rather than spending the entire supply on other crafting.

You can also save additional bones for Hollow-themed spells and other Collector recipes.

The Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls have their own entrance, enemies, and challenges. Completing the Albaneve Summits dungeon does not automatically complete the next one.
