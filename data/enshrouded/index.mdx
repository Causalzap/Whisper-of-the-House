Enshrouded 1.0 raises the character level cap to **50**, expands Embervale with Cinder's Cradle, and brings the main story to its conclusion. The route through the new endgame involves finding an Ancient researcher, activating six beacons, reaching the Flying Isles, and fighting Vorgoth at the end of the journey.

The game still gives you considerable freedom. You can spend hours building, collecting materials, or exploring regions outside your current main objective. That freedom also makes it easy to lose track of which upgrade or quest actually opens the next area.

For a new character, the most useful early milestones are rescuing crafting survivors, unlocking movement tools, and clearing Shroud Roots for skill points. For an established character, the important decisions are how to prepare for Cinder's Cradle and when to return to the teleportation platform that leads to the Flying Isles.

## What Should You Do First in Enshrouded 1.0?

Your first priority is to establish a working base and unlock enough equipment to explore without repeatedly returning for missing tools.

A practical opening route is:

1. Leave the starting Cinder Vault and gather enough Stone to craft your first Flame Altar.
2. Establish a base in the Springlands and build a Workbench.
3. Collect Plant Fiber, Wood, and early Shroud resources for tools and equipment.
4. Follow the Sleeping Survivor quest and rescue the Blacksmith from his Ancient Vault.
5. Craft a Grappling Hook and Glider as their required materials become available.
6. Clear your first Elixir Well and destroy its Shroud Root for an additional skill point.
7. Reach the Springlands Ancient Spire to unlock a useful fast-travel position.

You can change the order of nearby exploration and crafting tasks. The important thing is not to spend all your early resources on a permanent base before you have the equipment needed to travel efficiently.

### How to reach the Blacksmith's Ancient Vault

The Blacksmith is one of the most useful early survivors because he unlocks equipment and crafting options beyond your starting Workbench.

Track the Sleeping Survivor quest and approach the Ancient Vault through the surrounding ruins. The entrance is recognizable by the location notification **Ancient Vault – Blacksmith**.

![The entrance to the Blacksmith's Ancient Vault, where you rescue your first crafting survivor.](/images/enshrouded/enshrouded-blacksmith-vault-approach.webp)

Enemies occupy the area around the vault. With limited health and basic weapons, fighting every guard is unnecessary. The surrounding elevations provide opportunities to avoid some of them, especially if you already have the Grappling Hook.

If you reach the vault before crafting the hook, concentrate on reaching the entrance safely rather than treating every nearby enemy as a required fight.

After rescuing the Blacksmith, return to your Flame Altar and use a Summoning Staff to place him at your base. Speak to him to access his available crafting options and quests.

### Why you should unlock the Glider and Grappling Hook early

Double Jump improves ordinary movement, but some routes are designed around Grappling Hook anchors or long gaps that are much easier to cross with a Glider.

These tools become especially useful around Ancient Spires, tall ruins, and elevated quest locations. Reaching the Springlands Ancient Spire gives you a high starting point for future exploration, reducing long trips across the same terrain.

Keep an eye on Shroud Wood, Shroud Spores, String, and Metal Scraps while gathering early materials. Those resources support the movement equipment that makes the next objectives easier.

For weapon upgrades, salvage equipment you do not intend to use and save the resulting Runes. Spending heavily on every weapon found at low levels leaves fewer resources for stronger gear later.

## Enshrouded Progression: Where Should You Go Next?

Your equipment, Flame upgrades, crafting stations, and available quests determine when you are ready to move into more dangerous parts of Embervale.

There is no need to clear every optional location before entering another region. However, pushing far ahead without the required crafting materials or movement tools can turn a straightforward quest into a difficult return trip.

### Early progression: unlock the main survivors

After rescuing the Blacksmith, continue the survivor quests for the Hunter, Alchemist, and Carpenter.

These survivors provide different crafting options that become useful as enemy levels and resource requirements increase:

- **Hunter:** Equipment and crafting options that support ranged combat and survival.
- **Alchemist:** Magic-related equipment, spells, and consumables.
- **Carpenter:** Building materials, furniture, and later equipment progression, including improved Glider options.

Return to your base after important rescues and discoveries. Talking to survivors can reveal quests or crafting recipes that were not previously available.

Elixir Wells and Shroud Roots are also worth clearing during normal exploration. Destroying a Shroud Root awards a skill point, giving you more ways to improve combat and movement without relying entirely on character levels.

### Mid-game: follow materials, Flame strength, and crafting upgrades

Revelwood, Nomad Highlands, Blackmire, Kindlewastes, and Albaneve Summits introduce stronger enemies, new materials, and more demanding exploration.

Their quests and optional objectives overlap, so you do not need to treat every region as a chapter that must be completely finished before moving onward.

A useful sign that you are ready to advance is whether you can craft equipment appropriate for the enemies ahead.

When damage begins to feel inadequate, review your weapon level and available upgrades. New resources can unlock recipes through your survivors, while additional Flame upgrades help you explore more dangerous Shroud areas.

Before a difficult expedition, refresh your Rested buff, carry healing, and unlock nearby Ancient Spires when possible. Their fast-travel positions make repeated trips to distant quests far less costly.

### Late progression: Veilwater Basin and Cinder's Cradle

Veilwater Basin adds water-focused exploration and another stage of progression before the new northern endgame.

In version 1.0, the world extends into **Cinder's Cradle**, where the main story continues toward the Ancient civilization and the Flying Isles.

The enemies here are much stronger than those encountered during the opening hours. Higher-level equipment, sufficient healing, and reliable movement abilities become important before attempting the new main quest.

## Should You Restart or Continue Your Save in Enshrouded 1.0?

You can continue an existing Early Access character and world in version 1.0. A new save is not required to play Cinder's Cradle or reach the final boss.

There is one change you should check immediately: **your skill points are reset when you first load your character after the 1.0 skill-tree changes.**

Open the skill tree before leaving your base and redistribute your points. Without the skills you previously relied on, even an established character can feel unexpectedly weak.

Version 1.0 also raises the maximum player level to **50** and equipment level to **55**. Wands receive additional combat options, new spells expand magic builds, and the skill tree supports different combinations from earlier versions.

### Is starting fresh worth it?

A new character lets you experience the revised opening, survivor progression, and changes to previously familiar areas.

Continuing an existing save makes more sense when your immediate goal is the new endgame. You already have access to crafting stations, fast-travel points, and equipment that would otherwise take many hours to recover.

Restart for the early-game experience, not because the new story requires it.

## How Do You Get to Cinder's Cradle in Enshrouded 1.0?

Cinder's Cradle is the new northern region beyond Veilwater Basin. It introduces powerful enemies, Ancient structures, and the main quest leading to the Flying Isles.

Continue your Flame upgrade and main quest objectives before entering the new region. One important late-game milestone is **Flame Level 10**, shown alongside completion of the **Crown of Sorrow** quest.

![The Flame reaches Level 10 after completing Crown of Sorrow.](/images/enshrouded/enshrouded-flame-level-10.webp)

From there, follow the quest markers toward the northern gate and the land of the Ancients.

The route becomes dangerous quickly. Strong enemies guard sections of the approach, and several parts of the terrain favor gliding, grappling, and moving between elevated positions.

You can avoid some optional fights by using the terrain, but do not assume every shortcut is safe. A missed jump or poorly timed glide may leave you far below your intended route.

### Find the Ancient researcher

One of your first major objectives is to find **Loraine Crim, the Ancient Researcher**, who explains how to reach the islands above Cinder's Cradle.

Follow the quest markers through the ruins until you reach the researcher's location. Collect the soul, return to your base, and summon the survivor.

The researcher explains that the Ancient teleportation platform cannot take you to the skies until its connected beacons are activated.

**Six Beacon Dungeons must be completed before you can continue to the Flying Isles.**

This is the main progression barrier after entering the new region. Simply reaching the teleportation platform does not open the way upward.

<section id="six-beacons" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Complete the Six Beacon Dungeons?

After speaking with the researcher, track the quest **Activating the Gate to the Stars** and inspect your map for the marked Beacon Dungeons.

All **six Beacon Dungeon entrances are in Cinder's Cradle**, even though each dungeon is named after a different region of Embervale.

The quest begins with the **Springlands Beacon Dungeon**. Follow its marked entrance in Cinder's Cradle and use the teleportation platform to enter the Embervale Museum. Reach the end of the museum and activate the first beacon.

Once the Springlands beacon is complete, the quest changes to **Complete All Beacon Dungeons: 1/6**. The remaining five dungeons can then be completed in any order.

Their names correspond to Revelwood, Nomad Highlands, Kindlewastes, Veilwater Basin, and Albaneve Summits. You do not need to travel back to those regions to find their entrances.

### How do you know a beacon is complete?

The quest tracker records your progress as each beacon is activated.

![The quest tracker confirms that one of six Beacon Dungeons has been completed.](/images/enshrouded/enshrouded-first-beacon-activated.webp)

After completing the first beacon, the tracker displays:

**Complete All Beacon Dungeons: 1/6**

This is the confirmation to look for. Reaching the dungeon, defeating ordinary enemies, or opening a chest does not necessarily mean the beacon objective has been completed.

### What happens inside the first Beacon Dungeon?

The Springlands beacon sequence includes Ancient Constructs and an alarm encounter.

During the encounter, pay attention to the larger Constructs and the active alarm state. Defeating the relevant enemies allows the encounter to progress toward its resolution.

Once the alarm is resolved, continue to the final interaction and confirm that the beacon objective has registered in your quest tracker.

Do not assume the other five dungeons use the same alarm mechanism. Their layouts and objectives must be handled individually.

### What if the gate to the stars is still locked?

Check **Activating the Gate to the Stars** in your journal.

The requirement is to activate all six beacons. A counter below **6/6** means there is still at least one incomplete dungeon objective.

Use the map markers to identify the remaining locations. After finishing the final beacon, return to the Ancient teleportation platform and continue the main quest.

<section id="flying-isles" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Unlock the Flying Isles?

Once all six beacons have been activated, return to the quest location connected to the Ancient teleportation platform.

The next story dialogue confirms that the route has opened:

![Loraine Crim confirms that the gate to the Flying Isles is open.](/images/enshrouded/enshrouded-floating-isles-unlocked.webp)

The dialogue identifies the Ancient Capital as the next major destination, directing you toward the highest part of the floating structures where the Mother Flame is threatened.

Follow the new objective into the **Flying Isles of Cinder's Cradle**.

### How to cross the Floating Isles

The islands contain broken Ancient structures, dangerous drops, and passages that require movement between different elevations.

Grappling Hook anchors, Glider routes, and jump pads are important here. Some of the highest visible structures cannot be reached by traveling directly toward them from your current position.

Look for lower platforms and intermediate landing points. Moving around the edges of an island can reveal the route to a higher section.

Ancient Constructs patrol parts of the approach. Their ranged attacks can deal substantial damage, making it risky to remain exposed while preparing spells or attempting long jumps.

A protective spell or solid cover can give you time to attack. It also helps to keep your healing items accessible rather than relying on a long uninterrupted damage combination.

### Avoid losing progress on the way up

The route toward the Ancient Capital is long enough that a bad fall can be frustrating.

Repair your gear and prepare healing before committing to the upper islands. Use available return points when possible, especially before entering sections filled with Constructs or dangerous Shroud.

The Glider makes crossing open gaps easier, but it does not replace the need to locate the correct platforms and jump pads.

## How Do You Open the Barrier Near the Ancient Capital?

The barrier on the route to Vorgoth has **two lock indicators**. The corresponding purple switches are positioned around the elevated Ancient structures, not both on the floor directly beneath the barrier.

Activate the required switches and continue when **BARRIER UNLOCKED** appears. Beyond this point, the next major challenge is Vorgoth's final encounter.

## How Do You Beat Vorgoth, the Final Boss?

At the end of **Cinders of the End**, you face **Vorgoth, Champion of the Shroud**, followed by his transformed form, **the Wanderer's Vessel**.

The second form introduces sweeping sword attacks, tentacles, and dangerous Shroud growths. **Shroud-based damage is ineffective against Vorgoth**, so check your weapon or spell before entering the fight.

The <Link href="/enshrouded/vorgoth-final-boss" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">two-form Vorgoth fight and rewards</Link> cover the attack patterns, effective damage types, arena organs, and how to survive the encounter.

After defeating Vorgoth, collect his rewards and follow the objective to **Commune with the Flame** to complete the final story sequence.

## Where Are the New Hollow Halls in Enshrouded 1.0?

Version 1.0 adds two Hollow Halls in **Albaneve Summits** and **Veilwater Basin**. These are separate from the six Beacon Dungeons required to unlock the Flying Isles.

The **Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls** require a Bone Key crafted through the Collector. The dungeon combines freezing chambers, elevated green runes, locked barriers, and a final encounter with the **Hollow Titan**.

Bring frost protection before entering. The <Link href="/enshrouded/albaneve-summits-hollow-halls" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">Albaneve Summits Hollow Halls walkthrough</Link> explains how to open the locked doors, reach the high runes with Updraft, survive hypothermia, and obtain Icy Giant Bones.

The **Veilwater Basin Hollow Halls** are the next destination in the Hollow Halls progression, with their own Bone Key and dungeon challenges. Completing Albaneve Summits does not automatically clear Veilwater Basin.

## Best Early Skills and Mage Build in Enshrouded 1.0

Version 1.0 adds new Wand attacks and expands the available spells, making magic useful for much more than ordinary ranged damage.

Early on, mobility and resource management are the most dependable investments.

**Double Jump** is valuable for exploration, while **Mana Currents** improves mana regeneration. **Quick Charge** reduces spell preparation time, helping you cast without remaining vulnerable for too long.

Wand abilities become useful as more skill points are available. Attacks tied to movement and evasion help maintain pressure while repositioning away from enemies.

### Blizzard, Terror, and Bloodletting

For a magic-focused character, **Blizzard** is especially useful against groups of enemies.

Its repeated damage can work with **Terror**, which can stun enemies when the relevant critical-hit conditions are met. **Bloodletting** adds recovery opportunities during longer encounters.

This combination is valuable when enemies would otherwise surround you or interrupt repeated spellcasting.

Pillar of Flames gives you another area-damage option, while Entangle is useful for controlling enemies that approach too quickly.

Against flying targets, consider ranged lightning spells that are easier to land than ground-focused effects.

### Which equipment matters most?

A Staff with **Mana Leech** supports repeated casting. Critical strike bonuses help activate skills that rely on critical hits, while defensive gear and healing effects can make a substantial difference during long fights.

Blizzard control, healing, and mana recovery work together, but an effective Mage does not need to rely on a single unusual gem interaction.

For difficult regions, prioritize reliable damage, sufficient healing, and enough mobility to escape when enemies close the distance.

## How Do You Get All 58 Achievements in Enshrouded 1.0?

Enshrouded 1.0 has **58 Steam achievements**, including six new objectives covering Cinder's Cradle, Level 50, and Lord Vorgoth.

Reaching the end of the main story does not complete every regional achievement. You may still need to visit Flame Shrines and Sanctums, read Obelisks, destroy Shroud Roots, or mine valuable materials in regions you previously explored.

For 100% completion, use the <Link href="/enshrouded/achievements" className="font-semibold underline underline-offset-4">58-achievement checklist and regional completion route</Link> to identify unfinished tasks and avoid repeating locations you have already cleared.

<section id="after-ending" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Can You Keep Playing After the Enshrouded 1.0 Ending?

Yes. Completing **Cinders of the End** does not prevent you from continuing with your existing character and world.

Unfinished Hollow Halls, side quests, and unexplored locations remain available throughout Embervale. You can continue using your existing character to collect materials, upgrade equipment, and finish activities you skipped during the main story.

You can also work toward the final Flame upgrade using materials obtained during the endgame. There is no need to restart your character to continue exploring after the main story concludes.
