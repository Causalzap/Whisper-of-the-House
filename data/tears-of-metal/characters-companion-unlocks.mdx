Heroes and the Companion system use different unlock rules.

I first open their Village system, then meet them through Campaign events and
assign them during War Preparations.

<h2 id="playable-heroes">
  How many playable Heroes are in Tears of Metal?
</h2>

The Early Access build I tested has **three playable Heroes**:

- William Wallace;
- Brienne Ironleg;
- Ruadh Stonecrusher.

The extra locked portraits are not another secret launch route that I could
complete by repeating Act 1. I stop searching once the menu no longer shows a
current unlock requirement.

<h2 id="wallace">
  William Wallace: when I stay with the starting Hero
</h2>

Wallace is available immediately. I stay with him while I am still learning:

- which attacks can be Blocked or Parried;
- when a Heavy Finisher is safe;
- how long I can remain inside a Captain group;
- and when to stop extending the route before Threat and lost health become the
  real boss.

Wallace is not automatically obsolete when Brienne or Ruadh unlocks. His
slower sword rhythm gives me more time to read the battlefield, protect
Commanders, and use one confirmed punish instead of forcing a second combo.

### When I buy another Wallace upgrade

I buy a permanent Wallace upgrade when the previous failure had a clear stat
cause:

- I reached the boss but died to one survivable mistake;
- repeated Blocks emptied the stamina bar;
- or the safe Heavy opening worked but did too little damage.

In one developed save, a **+50% Max HP** investment brought Wallace to
**173 HP**. That made the learning run more forgiving, but it did not repair
route greed or Coins spent before the boss.

I do **not** buy more Wallace damage when the actual problem was:

- taking another optional battle at high Threat;
- passing a Healing Font with low health;
- losing every useful Commander;
- or attacking through a boss recovery.

<h2 id="unlock-brienne">
  How I unlock Brienne Ironleg
</h2>

The requirement I follow is:

`Complete The Fallen Star in the Mire`

The Fallen Star appeared as a **chance encounter** during a Mire Campaign in
the tested progression. Finishing unrelated battles did not force it to appear
on the next map.

My route is:

1. progress until the Mire is available;
2. check that Brienne is still locked in the Village;
3. start a Mire Campaign with enough health and Coins for an event detour;
4. take a manageable chance-encounter route;
5. complete The Fallen Star when it appears;
6. finish or leave the Campaign normally;
7. return to the Village and confirm that Brienne is selectable.

### When I keep searching and when I take the safer route

I keep the event route when:

- the party still has enough health for the next battle;
- I have preserved Coins for healing or a co-op revive;
- and the detour does not push Threat beyond what the build can handle.

I turn toward the safer route when:

- the encounter path adds several battles;
- the party is already low before the Banshee;
- or I am risking a successful progression run only to reroll one event.

Cartographer progression can add more encounter possibilities, but one purchase
does not guarantee The Fallen Star on the next Campaign. I do not spend every
Gem and repeatedly restart the first node.

### What I do when Brienne's route is blocked

An Early Access patch fixed gates that could remain closed during Brienne&apos;s
unlock quest.

When the objective is active but the route still refuses to open, I:

1. confirm the game is updated;
2. leave and reload the area once;
3. reproduce the route from its previous objective;
4. and only then abandon the Campaign.

I do not replace the displayed Fallen Star requirement with an old guide that
uses another boss or a full-game clear.

<h2 id="brienne-playstyle">
  How Brienne plays after I unlock her
</h2>

Brienne rewards uninterrupted pressure:

- Direct Hits increase her damage;
- taking damage resets the Direct Hit progress;
- Dodge does not cancel her combo;
- and her equipped weapon changes the available attack strings.

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    I treat Brienne&apos;s combo as a reward for clean movement, not a reason

  </figcaption>
</figure>

My basic sequence is:

1. build Direct Hits on Fodders and isolated Captains;
2. Dodge sideways without giving up the chain;
3. use the Heavy Finisher when the target is actually trapped;
4. leave before a counterattack resets the damage ramp.

I stop chasing the combo through fire, charging enemies, or several Captains.
When the next area contains a long empty walk, I accept the reset instead of
running ahead of the army.

### The Brienne numbers I used to judge upgrades

One Brienne progression offered:

- **+10% Finisher Damage**;
- a **25%** Heavy Finisher follow-up chance;
- a Dodge option that applied Vulnerable for **5 seconds** to enemies within
  **5 meters**;
- and a permanent purchase costing **950 Triskeles** for
  **+35% Strike Damage**.

I bought the 950-Triskeles upgrade only after Strike was already the engine of
the build. I would not buy it while every Direct Hit chain was still ending to
avoidable damage.

<h2 id="unlock-ruadh">
  How I unlock Ruadh Stonecrusher
</h2>

The current requirement is:

`Defeat the Banshee in the Mire`

Older routes that require completing the whole game describe a previous
version. I do not search for a second Ruadh event after the Banshee victory; I
return to the Village and inspect his Hero screen.

Before selecting the Banshee route, I check:

- current health;
- remaining Coins;
- whether the surviving co-op player can afford a revive;
- whether I can move between the spinning projectiles;
- and whether useful Commanders are still alive.

I buy healing when one recovery can preserve the unlock attempt. I do not spend
the same Coins on a random Chest whose reward may not help the boss.

<h2 id="ruadh-playstyle">
  How Ruadh plays after I unlock him
</h2>

Ruadh uses a giant hammer and has the slowest starting rhythm of the three
Heroes, but his attacks cover a large area and break through shielded groups.

His Special adds Life Steal. In one hard-mode setup, another effect shared
**50% of the healing value** with allied Heroes within **10 meters**.

I do not stand inside a boss attack while the meter is empty. Early in a
Campaign, the Special can charge slowly, so I preserve Dodge and Block until the
healing is actually active.

<h2 id="choose-a-hero">
  Which Hero should I choose?
</h2>

I choose the Hero by the failure I am trying to fix.

### I stay with Wallace when

- I am learning boss cues;
- Block and Parry are reliable;
- Ally Command timing matters;
- or permanent HP and Block upgrades already make him the safest developed
  Hero.

### I use Brienne when

- normal stages take too long;
- I can move sideways without breaking the combo;
- isolated Captains give me room to build Direct Hits;
- and I am willing to leave before one hit resets the chain.

### I use Ruadh when

- shields and dense groups are the problem;
- the team benefits from Life Steal;
- I can safely build Special charge;
- and the current boss does not punish every slow commitment.

I do not divide permanent currency evenly among all three. I test one complete
Campaign with the base kit first, then buy the upgrade that addresses a measured
failure.

I buy a **15-Dragonstone-Shard** weapon when:

I wait when the starting weapon already supports the build and the actual
bottleneck is health, Block Stamina, healing access, or route Threat.

After buying a weapon, I test it in an early battle. I do not bring an unfamiliar
combo directly into the Mire or a boss attempt.

<h2 id="companions-vs-commanders">
  Companions and Commanders are different
</h2>

Commanders belong to the Army roster. They lead troop groups, provide Ally
Commands, receive promotions, and can be lost during a Campaign.

Companions occupy separate War Preparations slots and provide their own effect
or command. Increasing Commander capacity does not automatically create another
Companion slot.

One tested Companion upgrade survived a lethal blow and recovered **25 HP once
per Campaign**. I take that while learning; I move toward damage after survival
becomes consistent.

<h2 id="companion-unlocks">
  How Companion unlock events work
</h2>

Companions do not use the same fixed boss checklist as Brienne and Ruadh.

My verified process is:

1. progress until Companion slots are available;
2. unlock additional encounter possibilities through Village progression;
3. enter a new Campaign;
4. take an event node that the current route can afford;
5. complete the event objective;
6. wait for an explicit joined-the-ranks message;
7. inspect the Companion during the next War Preparations.

One event introduced **Caolan, The Wayward**. After the required battle and
dialogue, the game stated that Caolan had joined the ranks.

That distinction matters. Another event can unlock a future battlefield object
or occurrence without adding a selectable Companion.

### When I take an event detour

I take the detour when:

- it adds only one manageable battle;
- the party has enough health and Coins;
- the Companion system is already available;
- and the boss attempt is not the run&apos;s only priority.

I skip it when:

- Threat is near the next dangerous threshold;
- the detour removes the only Healing Font;
- I need the Coins for a revive;
- or the event reward does not fix the current Campaign.

Unlocking more events expands the pool. It does not guarantee that the desired
Companion appears on the next map.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Character and Companion unlock fixes
</h2>

### Brienne is still locked after several Mire Campaigns

I confirm that I completed The Fallen Star itself. Entering the Mire or defeating
another boss does not satisfy that requirement.

### The Fallen Star never appears

I continue Village and Cartographer progression and prioritize manageable event
nodes. I do not sacrifice every viable Banshee run to search one more branch.

### Ruadh is still locked after the Banshee

I update the game, return to the Village, and inspect the Hero menu. An older
full-game requirement does not match the current route.

### An event ended but no Companion appeared

I look for the explicit joined-the-ranks message. Some events add another
battlefield occurrence instead.

### I found a Companion but cannot equip it

I check the active Companion slots during War Preparations. Commander capacity
and Companion capacity are different systems.

<h2 id="faq">
  Tears of Metal character and Companion FAQ
</h2>

### Are Hero weapon unlocks the same as Hero unlocks?

No. Unlocking Brienne or Ruadh makes the Hero available. Their additional
weapons, equipment, and skills remain separate progression.

### Can I bring three Companions?

The captured War Preparations screen shows three available slot positions with
two currently filled. The number I can actually use depends on the permanent
progression unlocked in that save.

### Should I replace Wallace immediately after unlocking Brienne?

No. I keep Wallace when his permanent upgrades and safer Block rhythm are
carrying the Campaign. I switch when Brienne&apos;s Direct Hit loop solves a real
clear-speed problem.
