<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start **Slotbound** with **Open Forest on Easy** and complete the tutorial. Match three identical symbols across a row, column, or diagonal to summon units, use **Nudge** to finish reachable winning lines, and **Absorb** spare units to strengthen the ones you want to keep. Your first Open Forest campaign ends at **Wave 30**. After clearing it, open the permanent upgrade tree: **Advanced Units** is a useful early unlock, while **Command Limit**, **Faction Synergy**, and other upgrades become valuable as your army develops. To use **Awakening**, unlock **Dormant Power** in the permanent upgrade tree and raise an eligible unit to level 30.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Open Forest Easy begins with a short tutorial that introduces the slot machine, a Warrior-and-Archer formation, unit absorption, Class Promotion, and the Item shop. These actions connect directly: a winning line gives you a unit, Absorb strengthens a unit you already have, and combat tests whether your formation can survive the next wave.

After the Wave 30 clear, the permanent upgrade tree becomes the next destination. Advanced Units expands the available summons, Command Limit addresses deployment restrictions, and Dormant Power unlocks Awakening for eligible units. The first clear opens these progression choices; you do not need to purchase all of them before starting another run.

## What Should You Do First in Slotbound?

Choose **Open Forest — Easy** when you enter the Campaign for the first time.

Normal and Hard are not available immediately. The opening tutorial introduces the controls before you begin making every decision independently.

The tutorial begins with the first winning spins and a simple Warrior-and-Archer battle. Keep the Warrior where it can intercept enemies and the Archer where it can attack from behind. The next lessons introduce Nudge, absorbing a Spearman into the Archer, selecting a Core, and promoting the Archer after it reaches level 5.

The shop lesson then requires an Item purchase using Essence. Follow each prompt and inspect what changes on the screen before continuing. Once the tutorial ends, you can decide whether another spin, a promotion, or a position change is worth the resources available.

## How Do Spins, Winning Lines, and Nudge Work?

Slotbound uses a **3×3 slot machine**. Three matching symbols in a valid line produce a winning result and summon the corresponding unit.

There are eight possible straight winning lines:

- Three horizontal rows.
- Three vertical columns.
- Two diagonals.

Check all of them before deciding that a spin has failed. A diagonal match is just as important as a horizontal one.

The symbols represent different unit families. As you unlock more content, additional unit types and rarities can become available.

### When Should You Use Nudge?

Nudge shifts the symbols on one reel. In the starting tutorial, you can use it **once per wave**, so check the entire board before committing.

For example, if two Archer symbols already occupy a winning line and the third can be moved into place, Nudge can complete the match without another ordinary spin.

Before using it, check three things:

- Which row, column, or diagonal can become a winning line.
- Whether the missing symbol is on a reel you can move.
- Whether the available movement actually brings that symbol into the line.

If no movement completes a match, don't force a Nudge. Use another spin if you can afford one, or proceed to the battle with your current army. The starting allowance resets by wave; do not plan on accumulating unused Nudges across later waves.

### Why Can't You Use Blast Yet?

The starting tutorial uses Nudge. **Blast Slot Machine** is a separate option in the permanent upgrade tree.

If you see Blast used in another player's run but cannot select it yourself, inspect your upgrades after completing a battlefield.

Unlocking Blast expands your available reel-control options. It does not mean every early run begins with both systems already available.

## How Do You Absorb Units, Get Imprints, and Unlock Class Promotions?

Summoning more units is only the beginning. **Absorb** lets you strengthen an existing unit using another unit.

The unit you keep gains progression, while the absorbed unit is consumed. Depending on the available choice, you may also gain or improve an **Imprint**.

An Imprint provides an additional effect to the recipient. It is separate from the unit's ordinary class and should not be confused with a Core or a purchased Item.

### How Does the First Absorption Work?

The tutorial demonstrates the process with a spare unit and an Archer.

Select the unit you want to use as material, drag it onto the intended recipient, and choose the appropriate Absorb action.

Check the recipient and the offered effect before confirming. The important result is not simply that one unit disappears; it is that the surviving unit gains something useful.

If you need space for another summon, consider whether an underdeveloped spare unit can be absorbed into a stronger character rather than discarding the opportunity to improve your team.

### When Do Class Promotions Begin?

In the opening tutorial, raising an Archer to **level 5** leads to its first Class Promotion.

The offered branches include **Crossbowman** and **Patroller**. They change how that unit develops instead of merely adding the same amount of damage.

### Why Can't You Keep Every Summoned Unit?

The opening tutorial can reach a **six-unit capacity limit**.

Once the limit is reached, additional summons create a space problem. You need to decide which units deserve to remain in the army and which should become absorption material.

Keep units that perform different necessary jobs. A second weak attacker may be less useful than strengthening your existing carry, but absorbing your only defender or healer can make the next battle much harder.

The permanent **Command Limit** upgrade becomes relevant once you regularly encounter this restriction.

## How Do You Clear Open Forest for the First Time?

Your first Campaign objective is to survive Open Forest through **Wave 30** and defeat its final encounter.

The battlefield includes ordinary waves, Judgment waves, and Boss waves. The distinction matters because not every loss has the same consequence.

Ordinary wave losses can cost lives without immediately ending the entire run. **Losing a Judgment or Boss wave ends the run even if you still have lives remaining.**

Do not treat an upcoming Judgment wave as another disposable fight.

### What Should You Check Before a Judgment Wave?

Look at the current army rather than focusing only on how many summons you have collected.

A useful frontline should be able to take the first enemy attacks. Your main damage dealer should have enough time and space to contribute.

If the last fight went badly, identify what failed:

- If enemies reached your ranged units, reposition the frontline.
- If a defender died immediately, consider improving its survivability.
- If the team survived but could not kill enemies, strengthen the damage source.
- If you are out of deployment space, absorb a low-impact unit before spinning again.

A promotion or level increase that directly improves one of these weaknesses can matter more than several extra units.

Wave 30 is the final encounter of the first Open Forest campaign. Apply the same checks before starting it: make sure the frontline can receive the initial attacks and that your damage dealers can reach their targets.

Defeating the final boss ends the campaign and displays the Victory result. Return to the permanent upgrade tree before starting your next run.

## What Should You Unlock First After Your First Slotbound Clear?

After the first Open Forest clear, the game introduces the **permanent upgrade tree**.

The initial clear displays a **Sigil of Unsealing** and opens new upgrade possibilities. This is where you begin changing what can appear in future runs.

Some upgrades unlock entire systems. Others improve probabilities, increase limits, or provide additional options.

Do not assume that a feature shown in the tree is already active. Check whether the corresponding upgrade has actually been purchased or unlocked.

### Which Early Upgrades Are Worth Prioritizing?

**Advanced Units** is a practical early choice because it expands the available unit pool. Once that system is open, **Advanced Research** can increase how frequently those units appear.

### Advanced Units or Command Limit: Which Should You Buy?

The two upgrades solve different restrictions. **Advanced Units** changes which units can appear in later runs; **Command Limit** changes how many units you can field.

If your available summons lack the unit roles you need, Advanced Units provides more possibilities. If you already have an attacker, defender, and useful support units but cannot deploy them together, additional capacity is more relevant. Check which restriction is actually affecting your army before spending the next upgrade point.

### When Should You Unlock Faction Synergy?

**Faction Synergy** unlocks the faction bonus system, but purchasing the upgrade does not automatically activate a bonus. You still need eligible unit types in your army to reach the required thresholds. For specific faction combinations and promotion choices, see the <Link href="/slotbound/builds">Slotbound builds guide</Link>.

## Should You Save 500 Gold for Interest?

Do not automatically copy a 500-Gold saving rule from a different player's run.

**Interest income is tied to economy progression**, so do not assume a fresh account receives the same returns as a player with additional upgrades. Check the upgrade tree and your actual income before treating 500 Gold as a balance you must always preserve.

Once interest is available, keeping a gold reserve can provide more spending power over later waves. But saving gold is only useful if your team survives long enough to benefit.

If you are losing the next Judgment wave because you lack units or damage, spending some of that reserve may be better than preserving an ideal balance.

## How Do You Unlock Awakening in Slotbound?

Find **Dormant Power** in the permanent upgrade tree and enable it. This opens the unit Awakening system, but an individual unit must still reach **level 30** before its first Awakening choice appears.

If a level-30 unit cannot access Awakening, return to the upgrade tree and check whether Dormant Power is enabled. Class Promotion is a separate step and does not unlock Awakening by itself.

For example, a Jester developed toward Ringmaster can receive role-specific Awakening choices after meeting these requirements. The <Link href="/slotbound/jester">Jester class and Awakening instructions</Link> explain those decisions.

## Where Do You Go After Open Forest?

Slotbound's Campaign contains six battlefields:

1. **Open Forest**
2. **Deep Cave**
3. **Wasteland**
4. **Lost Ruins**
5. **Crimson Hell**
6. **Split Space**

Each battlefield introduces different enemies or combat conditions, while difficulty progression provides another reason to return to earlier maps.

After completing Open Forest Easy, return to the Campaign selection and inspect which difficulties and battlefields are now available.

**Open Forest Normal** is an early progression target. Continue working through the available clear requirements rather than assuming that beating Easy automatically opens every other battlefield.

### Should You Play Normal or Hard Next?

Normal is a useful place to develop the next stage of your permanent upgrades and become comfortable with a larger unit pool.

Hard becomes more approachable after you understand which units are worth developing, which abilities your formation needs, and how to prepare for stronger bosses.

There is no benefit to repeatedly entering a difficult encounter with the same unresolved problem. If you cannot survive the next Judgment wave, adjust your unit development or use another run to unlock something that addresses the weakness.

For tested Hard Mode teams, the <Link href="/slotbound/builds">Slotbound Best Builds guide</Link> covers Open Forest, Deep Cave, Wasteland, and Lost Ruins.

### What Should You Work Toward After Clearing All Six Battlefields?

Campaign clears are not the only goals.

You can continue developing the upgrade tree, recording missing units and Items in the Collection, and pursuing higher difficulty or Endless Mode objectives.

If you are aiming for complete Steam progress, the <Link href="/slotbound/achievements">Slotbound achievements checklist</Link> tracks all 64 achievements, including Hard battlefield clears, Collection requirements, special challenges, and Endless wave milestones.
