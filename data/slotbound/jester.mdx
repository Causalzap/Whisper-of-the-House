<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To **summon the Jester in Slotbound**, first obtain a Core that allows **Jester Hat symbols** to appear, such as **Joker Seed**. Then complete a winning line of **three Jester Hats** on the 3×3 slot machine. **Black Note**, **Torn Note**, and **Wildcard** improve your opportunities, while a carefully saved **Nudge** can turn two visible hats into a completed line. Once summoned, Jester can develop toward **Illusionist** for enemy control or **Ringmaster** for allied cooldown support. Unlock the Awakening system and raise the unit to level 30 to begin its Awakening progression.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Seeing a Jester Hat does not mean you have summoned a Jester. The hat is a slot-machine symbol; the Jester is the Special-rarity unit awarded when the correct winning line is completed.

This distinction matters because a run can produce several hats without ever giving you the unit. The challenge is not only making the symbol available. You also need enough spins to see a workable arrangement and enough control over the reels to finish it.

## How Do You Unlock the Jester in Slotbound?

The Jester does not appear through the ordinary unit progression used by Fighters, Mages, or other common families. Its hat symbol needs an enabling Core before you can begin hunting for the summon.

**Joker Seed** is the clearest starting point. Its displayed effect gives each slot a **1.5% chance** to show a Jester Hat. This is the probability of a symbol appearing in an individual slot, not a 1.5% chance of receiving the Jester unit.

That difference explains why the first hat often feels much easier to obtain than the actual Jester.

### How Do You Prepare for a Jester Attempt?

Jester Hat Cores are useful only if your army survives long enough to complete a winning line. Keep a reliable attacker and enough protection to get through the next Judgment wave instead of spending everything on Core rerolls.

Look for Joker Seed or another Core that makes Jester Hats available. Once the symbols begin appearing, preserve Nudge for arrangements that can produce a valid three-hat line.

If your team is repeatedly losing before the setup comes together, improve your carry and frontline first. ## Which Cores Help You Summon a Jester?

The timing of each Core determines when a Jester attempt becomes possible. A hat appearing early in a wave gives you a different decision from one that appears after several spins, especially when Gold or Nudge is limited.

If the next result contains two hats, inspect whether moving one reel can complete a third.

### Does Wildcard Guarantee a Jester?

No. Wildcard gives each slot a 2% chance to show a Jester Hat, but you still need three hats on a valid winning line.

Its second effect is particularly useful: a Joker win summons one additional unit of the winning symbol. This can help you obtain multiple Jesters from a successful match, but the Core does not guarantee that the required hats will appear or align.

## How Do You Line Up Three Jester Hats?

Three Jester Hats must occupy one winning line. In the example below, the hats run diagonally from the top-right slot through the center to the bottom-left slot. Three hats scattered across unrelated positions do not qualify.

### How Do You Finish a Jester Hat Line With Nudge?

When two hats are already visible, identify the exact third cell needed to finish their row, column, or diagonal. Then inspect the reel containing that cell. The remaining hat must be reachable through the available Nudge movement; simply seeing another hat elsewhere on the board is not enough.

In one successful summon, moving the remaining reel downward completed the line and produced the Jester unit. If the third symbol cannot reach the missing position, another spin is necessary rather than repeatedly adjusting an impossible arrangement.

### What Happens After a Successful Match?

The completed Jester Hat line summons the **Jester unit**.

Jester starts with **Two Faced Card**, which randomly plays **Prank** or **Cheer** on a six-second cooldown.

- **Prank** makes an enemy's next basic attack target another enemy.
- **Cheer** causes one ally to perform a basic attack immediately.

This gives Jester both disruption and offensive support, although you cannot choose which card it uses on each activation.

Jester has two important development paths. They lead to different combat roles, so the best choice depends on which problem your army needs to solve.

The distinction is straightforward: **Illusionist changes which side enemies fight for**, while **Ringmaster helps existing allies use their abilities sooner**.

### Trickster to Illusionist: When Is Charm Better?

The Trickster path introduces **Charm**, temporarily making an eligible enemy fight for your side.

Its advanced form, **Illusionist**, gains **Mass Charm**.

In the current Wasteland Hard skill display, Mass Charm:

- Targets up to **two nearby non-boss enemies**.
- Turns them into allies for **three seconds**.
- Has a **10-second cooldown**.

That makes Illusionist particularly useful when enemy groups threaten to overwhelm your frontline.

Temporarily converting two enemies can reduce the number of incoming attacks while adding extra bodies to your side of the battle. The value is not limited to the Illusionist's personal damage.

The restriction is important: **Mass Charm does not target bosses**.

Against a final boss, you still need reliable damage and defensive support. Do not expect the Illusionist to turn the boss into an ally.

### Ferret Tamer to Ringmaster: When Is Cooldown Support Better?

**Ferret Tamer** develops the Jester into a unit that sends a ferret to help its allies.

The advanced **Ringmaster** uses **Ferret Express**.

Its current Wasteland Hard skill display shows:

- The ferret delivers cards to up to **two allied units**.
- Each affected ally's remaining skill cooldown is reduced by **two seconds**.
- The ability has an **eight-second cooldown**.

This makes Ringmaster useful when your other units already have valuable skills and would benefit from activating them more often.

For example, reducing a healer's remaining cooldown can help the army survive another burst of damage. Helping a strong attacker activate its skill sooner can also increase the team's overall pressure.

The Ringmaster is supporting those allies; it does not replace their abilities.

## How Do You Awaken the Jester?

A Jester can receive its first Awakening after **Dormant Power** is enabled and the unit reaches **level 30**. The upgrade is purchased outside the run; the unit level determines when the Awakening choice becomes available.

Once Jester develops into Illusionist or Ringmaster, examine the offered options in relation to that class's ability. For the permanent unlock location, see the <Link href="/slotbound">Slotbound progression instructions</Link>.

### What Should You Choose for Ringmaster?

Ringmaster is most useful when it can deliver its cards consistently and the receiving allies have valuable skills.

Ringmaster's first Awakening offers three choices. **Extra Delivery** sends an additional card effect to another ally at reduced strength. **Send-off** grants a nearby ally of a different troop type a shield at the start of battle. **Rear Card** provides a bonus when an ally stands immediately ahead of Ringmaster.

These choices solve different problems. Extra Delivery is useful when multiple teammates can benefit from the ferret's cards. Send-off adds early protection, while Rear Card depends on positioning. Inspect the options available in your current run before choosing; the demo selection does not establish that the same three cards will appear in every version.

## Can You Have Two Jesters in the Same Run?

**Yes. Two Jester units can coexist in one army.**

A Wasteland Hard run used two Jesters in the same formation, developing one toward Illusionist and the other toward Ringmaster.

That combination gives the team two different forms of assistance: Illusionist can temporarily turn ordinary enemies into allies, while Ringmaster reduces skill cooldowns for teammates.

You need two Jester units in your army, but that does not necessarily require two separate winning lines. **Wildcard** states that a Joker win summons one additional unit of the winning symbol, so inspect your army after a successful Jester match before spending more resources on another attempt.

Keep both Jester units in your army instead of absorbing one into the other. **Jester Troupe** checks whether you own two simultaneously; the <Link href="/slotbound/achievements">Slotbound achievement checklist</Link> contains the remaining Steam unlock conditions.
