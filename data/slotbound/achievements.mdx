Some achievements unlock as you learn to summon and absorb units. Others require you to avoid an action for an entire run, keep a particular unit alive, or collect progress across several attempts. A strong final team will not satisfy a rule that you broke ten waves earlier.

The most useful distinction is **when the game checks the condition**. *Close Call* needs a single battle won with one life remaining; *Last Heart* requires an entire campaign clear with one life left. *One Against Many* checks a battle from wave 5 onward, while *Few but Elite* restricts how many units you may ever own during a Hard clear.

## Which Slotbound Achievements Can You Get in Your First Runs?

**Become One** and **Engraved Power** reward your first absorption and imprint. **First Victory** needs one battle win; **First Clear** needs an entire campaign battlefield cleared on any difficulty. You can earn these while following the normal opening tutorial and completing Open Forest.

A few early objectives reward paying attention to your units rather than rushing for a stronger army:

- **Friends to the End:** Keep at least one unit that you owned in the first wave until the battlefield is cleared. Do not accidentally absorb the unit you intend to keep.
- **Three Stars:** Raise one unit to three stars.
- **The King's Army:** Win one battle with at least five three-star units deployed. One strong three-star unit is not enough.
- **Pride of the Commons:** Win a boss wave using only **Normal-grade units**. This refers to unit rarity, not Normal difficulty. Check the grades of units taking part in the boss fight.

If Become One or Engraved Power is still locked, perform an Absorb during your next run and inspect the surviving unit's Imprint. The <Link href="/slotbound">opening tutorial instructions</Link> show the Spearman-to-Archer example.

## How Do Flawless, Last Heart, and Undying Legion Differ?

These achievements measure different kinds of survival. A battle can be won while your units die, and a battlefield can be cleared even after you have lost lives. Neither situation should be confused with a clean run.

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementSurvivalColumns}
  rows={achievementSurvivalRows}
/>

A no-unit-loss run and a no-life-loss run are separate challenges. **Beyond Judgment** specifically monitors Judgment waves; **Undying Legion** applies to the entire battlefield. Meanwhile, **First Judgment** is a hidden achievement for a particular loss, so it is not something to attempt during a flawless run.

### Which Achievements Limit Your Units or Actions?

**Few but Elite** requires a Hard clear without **ever owning more than three units**. This is stricter than finishing with three deployed: acquiring a fourth unit earlier can break the condition. **One Against Many** is different. It asks you to win **one battle on wave 5 or later with only one unit deployed**; it does not state that you must own only one unit for the entire run.

Other restrictions are equally specific:

- **Ordinary Is Special:** Clear Normal or Hard without raising any unit to **two stars**. Check star rank rather than assuming a low unit level is sufficient.
- **Frugal Victory:** Clear Normal or Hard without **buying shop items**. Do not confuse this wording with a ban on every kind of upgrade or Core selection.
- **True Gambler:** Clear Normal or Hard without using **Nudge or Blast**. Choose your opening and Core decisions with that restriction in mind.
- **David and Goliath:** Win a boss wave while **owning just one Normal-grade unit**. The ownership and rarity requirements matter; this is not the same condition as One Against Many.

### How Do Pure Blood and Babylon Work?

Both require defeating the **final boss on Hard**, but they demand opposite faction arrangements.

**Pure Blood** requires your units to belong to **a single faction**. **Babylon** requires **every unit to come from a different faction**. Before starting the final encounter, inspect your deployed units' factions rather than assuming that a generally successful team qualifies. You cannot meet both conditions with the same ordinary mixed-faction arrangement.

For how to assemble a team around faction bonuses, use the <Link href="/slotbound/builds">build and faction synergy guide</Link>.

## Which Achievements Need Special Spins, Nudges, or Blasts?

These goals are about a **single spin or action**, not totals accumulated over a run. Watch the full 3×3 grid before committing a Nudge or Blast.

- **Four Lines:** Make at least four winning lines in one spin.
- **God of Nudges:** Complete three or more winning lines with **one Nudge**.
- **Baby Boomer:** Summon at least eight units from **one spin**.
- **Perfect Demolition:** Remove at least six symbols with **one Blast**.
- **God of Destruction:** Complete four or more winning lines with **one Blast**.
- **Jackpot!:** Land the **same symbol in all nine slots**.

A large number of summons does not automatically mean you completed the required number of lines, and removing six symbols is not the same condition as completing four lines. Read the exact target before deciding whether to use your last available board adjustment.

</section>

## How Do You Get Jester Troupe, Prism Light, and the Faction Achievements?

**Jester Troupe** unlocks when you own two Jester units simultaneously. Matching hats without receiving both units does not satisfy the requirement. To obtain the units, including Wildcard's additional summon opportunity, see the <Link href="/slotbound/jester">Jester summon instructions</Link>.

**A Legend Appears** requires summoning your first **Legendary unit**, whereas **Prism Light** requires summoning a **Prism unit**. Those are distinct rarity targets; do not assume a Legendary summon satisfies both.

The remaining unit milestones reward different forms of progression:

- **Devourer of All:** Have **one unit** absorb units from all six families. Spreading the six families across the whole team does not meet the condition.
- **Bloated Beast:** Raise one unit's **Absorb LV to 100**. This is an Absorb-level target, not an ordinary three-star promotion.
- **Faction Bond:** Activate the **highest tier** of one faction synergy.
- **Ten Banners:** Activate the highest synergy tier of **all ten factions across runs**. You do not need to fit all ten into one battle according to the stated requirement.

A single powerful build may help with the leveling goals, but the two faction achievements are measured differently: one highest-tier activation versus ten separate faction milestones.

## How Far Must You Go in Endless Mode for Every Medal?

Endless Mode has six explicit wave targets. The first one requires beating a boss rather than merely reaching a numbered wave.

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementEndlessColumns}
  rows={achievementEndlessRows}
/>

**Six Abysses** is the larger map-wide task: reach **wave 110 on all six battlefields in Endless Mode**. A wave 130 run on one map does not complete the requirement for the remaining five maps.

Two separate achievements involve leaderboards:

- **Name on the Board:** Get an **Endless Mode run** onto its leaderboard.
- **Stop the Clock:** Get a **campaign clear** onto the clear-time leaderboard, played **in one sitting without resuming**.

Stop the Clock does not state a fixed nine- or ten-minute cutoff. The relevant checks are leaderboard qualification and completing the run without a resume; do not invent a universal time limit from someone else's record.

## Which Collection, Fate Stone, and Upgrade Achievements Take Longer?

Several achievements measure account-wide progress rather than a single winning team. Their wording identifies precisely what needs to be recorded or purchased.

- **Origin Seeker:** Buy 30 upgrades.
- **The Full Tree:** Buy every upgrade.
- **Roll of Fate:** Use a Fate Stone for the first time.
- **Fate Collector:** Complete **30 different** Stone quests across runs. Repeating the same quest is not the same as completing a different one.
- **Rolling in Gold:** Hold **10,000 gold** at once, rather than earning that total over many runs.
- **Know Your Enemy:** Record **100% of enemies** in the Collection; Endless Mode enemies are excluded by the stated requirement.
- **Core Collector:** Record **100% of Cores** in the Collection.
- **Every Soldier:** Record **100% of units** in the Collection.
- **Item Collector:** Record **100% of Items** in the Collection.

The Collection achievements ask what is **recorded**, not merely what was offered during a spin or shop visit. Check the relevant Collection category to see what is still missing. For choosing Cores and Items that affect your current run, use the <Link href="/slotbound/cores">Slotbound Cores and Items guide</Link>.

## What Counts Toward the Long-Term Achievements?

**Battle-Hardened** asks you to **finish 100 runs**, whether you win or lose; **restarts do not count**. If you are farming this milestone, finish each attempt instead of restarting at the first unfavorable spin.

**Boss Hunter** requires defeating **100 bosses** across campaign and Endless Mode combined. **Regular** asks for **50 hours of actual runs**; the requirement does not simply say to leave the application open for 50 hours.

Finally, **Slotbound Master** requires all **63 other Steam achievements**. It is an overall completion check, not an additional battlefield or hidden boss. The checklist at the top can help you isolate whatever remains after your Hard clears, Collection records, and Endless goals are done.
