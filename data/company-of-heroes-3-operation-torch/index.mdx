From the nighttime landings in Operation Flagpole to the defense of Sened Station, **Company of Heroes 3: Operation Torch** changes the available units and objectives as the campaign progresses. Unlike the Italian campaign, these battles follow a linear sequence without a strategic campaign map.

The mission instructions below follow Standard difficulty. Enemy positions, attack patterns, and available units may differ on higher difficulties.

Standard difficulty can already become demanding when multiple enemy attacks occur at the same time.

Medjez El Bab is one of the early missions where defensive preparation becomes particularly important. Later missions introduce limited-force engagements and specialized objectives that cannot always be solved by producing more units.

If you are returning to Company of Heroes 3 after a long break, Easy is a reasonable starting point.

If completing all achievements is your priority, review the <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/achievements">Operation Torch achievement requirements</Link> before choosing your campaign difficulty.

### How Do You Use Tactical Pause?

Press **Space** to activate Tactical Pause.

While paused, inspect the battlefield and issue orders to your units before resuming the action.

Tactical Pause is especially helpful when:

- Several squads are taking fire at the same time.
- Enemy grenades or artillery threaten your positions.
- You need to reposition anti-tank guns and infantry.
- Enemy attacks approach from multiple directions.
- Damaged vehicles need to withdraw while other units continue fighting.

Use the pause to resolve immediate threats first rather than trying to issue every order simultaneously during combat.

## Operation Flagpole Walkthrough

Operation Flagpole begins before the main Allied landings.

Your task is to reach the scattered Commando teams and withdraw them from the area before the naval bombardment destroys the nearby fortifications.

The mission is primarily about managing a limited infantry force rather than establishing a large base.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-flagpole-objectives.webp"
  alt="Operation Flagpole opening mission objectives and Commando positions"
  width={1536}
  height={774}
  caption="Follow the Rendezvous With the First Commando Squad objective."
/>

### How Do You Complete Operation Flagpole?

1. Survive the opening enemy ambush.
2. Follow the objective leading toward the first Commando squad.
3. Contact the remaining Commandos before the bombardment reaches their positions.
4. Deal with the enemy armored car blocking your progress.
5. Secure the available light machine guns when the opportunity appears.
6. Clear the heavy machine gun positions obstructing the withdrawal route.
7. Reach the boat and follow the remaining evacuation objectives.

The objectives change as you move through the mission. Do not remain in an area simply because enemy soldiers are still present after the game directs you elsewhere.

### Why Are Your Squads Unable to Move?

Enemy machine guns can suppress or pin infantry attempting to cross their firing lanes.

Sending another squad directly behind an already pinned squad may leave both units unable to advance.

Use available cover, look for another approach, and redirect units that are still able to move. If an ability becomes unavailable while a squad is pinned, do not repeatedly issue the same command.

If a Commando squad is pinned, check whether Flanking Maneuver is available. Use it to help the affected squad regain mobility and move away from the machine gun's firing lane.

Once the enemy positions blocking your exit have been resolved, continue toward the withdrawal objective.

### What Happens After Operation Flagpole?

The mission ends with the Commandos withdrawing from the immediate combat area and seeking shelter after their boat is destroyed.

The campaign then presents its first permanent upgrade choices before moving into Charlie Beach.

## Charlie Beach Walkthrough

Charlie Beach begins with Allied troops landing at the wrong beach and encountering substantial enemy resistance.

Your immediate task is to reorganize the scattered infantry, move inland, and secure the objectives needed for the landing force to continue its advance.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-charlie-beach-objectives.webp"
  alt="Company of Heroes 3 Operation Torch Charlie Beach landing and infantry objectives"
  width={1564}
  height={842}
  caption="The landing force receives orders to advance into town and secure the airfield."
/>

### How Do You Get Off Charlie Beach?

Clear the machine gun positions blocking the inland approaches and collect available Munitions before moving the landing force farther forward. Reinforce damaged squads when the mission provides the opportunity.

Securing the initial landing area allows the second Allied wave to come ashore. The next orders direct you toward the town and airfield.

### How Do You Clear the Town and Enemy Artillery?

The inland advance introduces additional artillery and fortified positions.

- Destroy the artillery threatening the landing force.
- Collect available Munitions when your supply runs low.
- Regroup damaged squads before advancing farther.
- Use available naval support against fortified enemy positions.
- Check for enemy vehicles before sending infantry into the next area.

When naval bombardment begins, keep your infantry outside the affected area until it is safe to advance.

Do not occupy a building or fortified position simply because you have reached it if that position is about to be bombarded.

After the fort is destroyed, use the available Medical Tents to heal and reinforce your infantry. The next phase requires securing four positions around the town. Check previously captured positions for enemy counterattacks while completing the remaining objectives.

### How Do You Capture the Airfield?

After securing the town, German tanks arrive before your infantry has enough anti-vehicle firepower to defeat them. Follow the Regroup Outside the City objective instead of continuing the engagement.

Rendezvous with the Commandos from Operation Flagpole. Their Bazookas provide the anti-vehicle support needed to defeat the enemy armor and resume the advance toward the airfield.

Another enemy tank threatens Allied Engineers near the approach to the airfield. Defeating it allows you to rescue those Engineers.

At the airfield, use the available anti-vehicle weapons and abilities against the remaining armored forces. The airfield cannot be secured until the required enemy armor is eliminated.

</section>

## Medjez El Bab Walkthrough

Medjez El Bab introduces a larger battle built around Whizbang rocket artillery, reconnaissance, infantry, and defensive preparation.

Your first objective is to rescue the threatened artillery units. You must then advance toward the town, rescue captured Engineers, secure the required positions, and survive German counterattacks.

The defensive phase is considerably more demanding if you reach it without preserving your supporting units.

### Step 1: Rescue the Whizbangs

Your Whizbangs are under threat at the beginning of the mission.

Move available forces toward the threatened units and clear the enemies preventing their recovery.

Once rescued, the Whizbangs become important tools for clearing defended positions across the battlefield.

Use them against concentrations of enemy units and entrenched positions rather than relying entirely on your infantry.

### Step 2: Use Reconnaissance Before Bombarding

Whizbangs need suitable targets, and enemy positions may be hidden by the fog of war.

The 1/4-ton 4x4 Truck can help you obtain information about the next defended area.

When its scouting upgrade is available, it can detect enemy positions while stationary.

Keep the reconnaissance vehicle protected. Losing it makes it harder to identify threats before advancing your artillery and infantry.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-whizbang-barrage.webp"
  alt="Medjez El Bab M4A1 Sherman Whizbang rocket artillery targeting an enemy position"
  width={1408}
  height={1100}
  caption="Use reconnaissance to identify defended positions before committing your Whizbang bombardments."
/>

After rescuing the Whizbangs, you receive an objective involving the enemy Propaganda Tower across the river. Use the available rocket artillery to destroy the tower before continuing toward the captured Engineers.

### Why Are Whizbang Bombardments Available So Frequently?

Medjez El Bab gives your Whizbangs unusually short bombardment cooldowns. Check whether another barrage is ready before committing infantry to a newly discovered defended position.

This cooldown behavior is specific to the mission and should not be expected from every Whizbang encountered later.

### Step 3: Rescue the Captured Engineers

The Germans have captured Engineers needed by the Allied force.

Follow the Rescue POWs objective and recover them before the defensive phase.

The rescued Engineers can repair damaged Whizbangs and help prepare your defensive positions.

Preserving them is particularly valuable when enemy counterattacks threaten your artillery.

### Step 4: Capture Both Enemy Outposts

Medjez El Bab includes an optional objective to capture two enemy outposts.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-bonus-objective-icon.webp"
  alt="Medjez El Bab Capture Enemy Outpost objective showing zero of two outposts captured"
  width={1232}
  height={814}
  caption="The Capture Enemy Outpost objective tracks the two optional positions that can provide an additional campaign reward."
/>

Secure both outposts before committing your army to the final defense. A completed outpost objective unlocks an <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/campaign-upgrades#torch-objectives">additional Barracks perk choice</Link> after victory.

### Step 5: Prepare for the German Counterattack

Begin preparing your defenses before the German reinforcement countdown appears. Once the mission announces that reinforcements are approaching, check the remaining time even if you have not secured every position.

Repair damaged Whizbangs and establish defensive coverage of the bridge approaches before the first enemy wave arrives. Subsequent waves can attack from additional directions.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-medjez-el-bab-defend-point.webp"
  alt="Medjez El Bab Wave 1 completed during the German counterattack"

Useful preparations include:

- Repairing damaged Whizbangs.
- Positioning anti-tank guns to cover vehicle approaches.
- Keeping infantry near defensible cover.
- Placing mines at likely attack routes.
- Retaining units that can respond to attacks from another direction.

### How Do You Survive the Three-Bridge Defense?

On Standard difficulty, the first wave concentrates on the central approach. Subsequent attacks expand to additional crossings, so keep the other bridges covered even while the initial battle is underway.

The defended crossroads is more important than pursuing isolated enemy units. If the enemy begins capturing it, redirect your available forces immediately.

Whizbang bombardments help against concentrated attack waves. Use Tactical Pause when several approaches come under pressure at once.

## Iron Rain Walkthrough

Iron Rain shifts the campaign toward a convoy route threatened by enemy ambushes and air attacks.

The forward headquarters has been overrun, and your army must reestablish control before advancing through the valley.

Halftracks, infantry, Engineers, and available air support become particularly useful during this mission.

### How Do You Secure the Forward Headquarters?

Begin by following the objectives around the threatened headquarters.

Recover the position and establish a force capable of defending it while the rest of your army advances.

Do not leave the headquarters completely unprotected when you begin clearing enemy positions farther along the route.

Pay attention to newly contested sectors and recover them when required.

### How Do You Capture the Forward Supply Bases?

The Capture the Forward Supply Bases objective requires securing four enemy supply positions. Check both sides of the river for the remaining objectives.

Use the M16 Multiple Gun Motor Carriage to provide anti-aircraft support against German air attacks as your infantry advances. Keep captured sectors protected and withdraw damaged vehicles for repairs when necessary.

### Why Is the River Objective Not Complete?

The river objective requires control of both banks. Securing only one side is not enough to complete it.

Check both sides of the river for remaining objective markers and enemy positions before advancing farther.

Keep enough units behind to protect the secured area while your remaining forces continue along the route.

Once the remaining required positions are secure, continue to the next mission.

</section>

<section id="chouigui" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Chouigui Walkthrough

Chouigui is an armor-focused mission that begins with Stuart tanks leading the advance.

You must establish control of a bridge, push beyond the pass, and deal with the German forces operating around the area and airfield.

### How Do You Secure the Bridge?

Use the provided Stuart tanks to establish a foothold across the bridge.

Keep supporting units close enough to help when the leading tanks encounter resistance.

After taking control of the bridge, follow the reconnaissance and advance objectives beyond it.

Watch for opportunities to recover equipment, including the artillery that the enemy attempts to tow away.

### How Do You Keep Your Stuart Tanks Alive?

Preserve the Stuart force during the bridge crossing and subsequent advance. Withdraw damaged tanks for repairs or recovery while healthier vehicles maintain the current position.

Avoid committing the entire armored force against stronger German tanks without infantry and anti-vehicle support.

### How Do You Protect the Airfield From German Armor?

During the airfield defense, the objective tracks enemy vehicles reaching the air base with a counter out of three. Prevent enemy vehicles from reaching the base rather than focusing solely on destroying enemy infantry.

Watch the wave countdown to determine how much preparation time remains. Repair damaged Stuarts between engagements and keep enough vehicles available to defend the airfield.

On Standard difficulty, the defense continues through three enemy attack waves.

</section>

## Hornet's Nest Walkthrough

Hornet's Nest involves supporting a British battalion under heavy pressure.

The mission initially emphasizes reconnaissance and British artillery support rather than a direct assault using a large American force.

Your objectives then develop into preparation for the British evacuation.

### How Do You Spot Enemy Forces for British Artillery?

Move your reconnaissance units toward the designated enemy positions and wait for confirmation that their coordinates have been relayed to the British guns. Spotting unrelated enemy units does not complete the required artillery objective.

### How Do You Use Stealth Reconnaissance?

The reconnaissance ability available during this mission allows the affected unit to become camouflaged without requiring cover.

However, the unit moves more slowly and cannot fire its weapons or capture territory while the ability is active.

Nearby enemies can still detect it.

Use this mode when locating the enemy positions required by the artillery objectives.

If your observers reach the correct area but the objective does not advance, check whether the required reconnaissance ability is active.

### How Do You Clear the Roadblock?

The roadblock requires another artillery spotting objective. Identify the marked position and wait for the British guns to clear the obstruction before moving through it.

### How Do You Use the Artillery Radio Beacon?

Later objectives introduce artillery-related beacons.

Follow the instructions to place the required beacon using the available Artillery Observers.

The beacon helps locate or engage enemy concentrations as the mission progresses.

Keep your observers protected, since their specialized abilities are important for completing the remaining reconnaissance objectives.

### How Do You Protect the British Evacuation?

The final phase requires holding the enemy back while British troops prepare their evacuation.

Protect the positions needed to keep the withdrawal route open.

Use the available infantry, anti-tank units, vehicles, and artillery support to respond to enemy attacks.

Pay attention to the British evacuation progress rather than advancing away from the area you are required to protect.

Continue defending until the British forces have withdrawn safely.

The next mission changes the campaign's focus from large defensive engagements to a solo Commando infiltration.

</section>

<section id="sened-station" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Sened Station Walkthrough

Sened Station returns to a larger battlefield involving infantry, support weapons, vehicles, and urban combat.

The mission requires securing the railway junction and surrounding positions before defending the area against a German counterattack.

Your initial attacking force will not necessarily remain available in its entirety during the final defense.

### Step 1: Secure a Foothold Near the Railway Junction

Establish control of the initial position near the railway junction and recover the machine guns held by German forces. These weapons can support both the advance into town and the later defensive objectives.

### Step 2: Stop the Escaping Supply Trucks

Intercept the escaping German supply trucks while their objective is active. Destroying or stopping these vehicles can provide additional weapons, including heavy machine guns, for your force.

Complete the moving objective before advancing farther into town.

### Step 3: Secure the Railway and Town

Advance through the railway area and surrounding neighborhoods, securing the required sectors as the objectives develop. Enemy counterattacks can threaten positions you have already captured, so keep an eye on newly contested areas during the advance toward the town center.

### Step 4: Prepare for the German Counterattack

Once the town is secure, the main Allied force continues toward Maknassy, leaving a smaller force to defend Sened.

The briefing recommends preparing defenses toward the east. Establish the available field defenses and position your support weapons before the attack begins.

### Step 5: Rally Retreating Allied Soldiers

Retreating Allied soldiers provide an opportunity to strengthen the remaining defense force. Follow the rally objectives, using the 4x4 Truck where appropriate, to gather additional troops before the counterattack develops.

### Step 6: Hold Sened Until the Attack Ends

Protect the required sectors as the German counterattack develops. Reposition support weapons when necessary and keep surviving vehicles operational.

The objective is to hold Sened until the attack ends, rather than advance beyond the defended area.

</section>
