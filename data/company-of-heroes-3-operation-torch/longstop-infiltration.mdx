**Longstop Infiltration** is the stealth-focused Commando mission in Company of Heroes 3: Operation Torch. Instead of commanding an army, you control a single operative who must complete the sabotage objectives and withdraw before the Allied assault.

The walkthrough below follows Standard difficulty. On Expert, check the current objectives and enemy positions before attempting the same approaches.

<section id="mission-start" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Start Longstop Infiltration?

After Hornet's Nest, you take control of a single Commando on Longstop Hill. His objectives are to eliminate designated snipers, prepare explosive charges, and withdraw before the Allied assault.

<GuideTable
  columns={missionObjectiveColumns}
  rows={missionObjectiveRows}
/>

These objectives are introduced as you progress through the mission. Pay attention to new orders, particularly when the game directs you back toward a previously completed charge site.

At the beginning of the mission, move into the indicated concealed position and enter stealth before attempting to pass the approaching enemy soldiers.

</section>

<section id="concealment" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Does Concealment Work in Longstop Infiltration?

The Commando gains camouflage while in cover and out of combat. Once he leaves cover, that camouflage lasts for another 4 seconds unless an enemy gets close enough to reveal him.

Firing from camouflage also grants +25% damage and +40% accuracy for 5 seconds.

### What Does the Blue Circle Around the Commando Mean?

Watch the blue circle surrounding your Commando while moving through enemy positions.

An enemy entering this detection area can expose the Commando even when you are attempting to remain in stealth.

Do not judge whether a route is safe solely by the direction an enemy appears to be facing.

A patrol that moves too close can reveal your position before you reach the next objective.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-longstop-detection.webp"

### How Do You Maintain Concealment While Moving?

Treat the 4-second camouflage duration as your movement limit between covered positions. Before leaving your current hiding place, identify another reachable position and wait for nearby patrols to move away.

Use short movement orders around fences, buildings, and fortified positions. A distant command may send the Commando along an unintended route, exposing him before he reaches cover.

Tall grass and suitable cover can restore camouflage while out of combat. Check the Concealment indicator after each crossing rather than assuming every terrain object provides protection.

## How Do You Eliminate the Four Snipers?

The early objective requires eliminating four designated snipers.

You do not need to eliminate every German soldier guarding the surrounding positions.

The mission explicitly warns that if too many members of the garrison fail to check in, additional enemy reinforcements will arrive.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-longstop-sniper-objectives.webp"
  alt="Longstop Infiltration mission showing the required sniper elimination objective"
  width={1272}
  height={776}
  caption="Prioritize the designated sniper targets instead of attempting to eliminate the entire enemy garrison."

### Which Enemies Should You Eliminate?

Prioritize the four designated snipers. Use the Commando's throwing knife against isolated targets when necessary, but leave unrelated guards alone unless they prevent access to a required objective.

Clearing additional enemies can make later sections harder by bringing more reinforcements into the area.

### Why Are More Enemies Appearing?

If additional reinforcements arrive, reassess your intended route before moving again. A passage that was previously accessible may now be occupied by another patrol.

Continue toward the remaining mission objectives instead of attempting to clear the newly arrived enemies.

### What If You Cannot Find the Last Sniper?

Use the objective counter and remaining mission markers to identify unfinished sniper targets. Once the required count is complete, additional snipers do not need to be eliminated unless they block another objective.

</section>

## How Do You Plant the Demo Charges?

The sabotage objectives require placing explosives at designated positions around Longstop Hill.

Follow the marked charge sites as they become available during the infiltration.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-longstop-demo-charge.webp"
  alt="Plant Demo Charge Here objective marker beside a fortified position in Longstop Infiltration"
  width={1022}
  height={734}
  caption="Look for the Plant Demo Charge Here marker to identify the required demolition location."
/>

Before planting a charge, identify a nearby covered position that you can reach after completing the sabotage action. Approach the marked location when the surrounding enemies have moved away, plant the explosive, and return to concealment.

Sniper and demolition objectives can overlap during the mission. You do not need to finish every sniper objective before interacting with an available charge site.

### What If Machine Guns Block the Next Charge Site?

Several sections of Longstop Infiltration contain machine gun positions and nearby patrols that make direct movement difficult.

Do not repeatedly send the Commando through an area that exposes him to overlapping enemy fire.

Look for an accessible route around the position. Be careful around barbed wire and other obstacles, since apparently open paths may not be traversable.

If no direct approach is safe, move toward another concealed position and reassess the route from there.

</section>

## How Do You Complete the German Captain Optional Objective?

During the infiltration, the mission informs you that a German Captain is arriving at the base.

This provides an additional sabotage opportunity involving the Captain's car.

The optional objective becomes available during the mission rather than appearing as the first infiltration task.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-longstop-bonus-objective.webp"
  alt="Major George Russo announcing the arrival of the German Captain during Longstop Infiltration"
  width={1280}
  height={760}
  caption="Follow the optional objective to sabotage the German Captain's car, then wait in concealment."
/>

### How Do You Blow Up the German Captain's Car?

1. Follow the optional objective marker to the Captain's vehicle.
2. Plant the Demo Charge at the designated location.
3. Move the Commando into the nearby bushes.
4. Wait for the Captain to approach the vehicle.
5. Check the objective status when the charge detonates.

You do not need to approach the Captain directly while waiting for the explosion.

If there is no separate detonation command available after placing the charge, remain concealed and give the event time to progress.

The charge can detonate as part of the objective sequence without a separate manual detonation command.

### Is the German Captain Objective Required?

No. Sabotaging the Captain's car is an optional Torch Objective. Complete it before extraction if you want to earn the additional campaign reward described below.

After some explosives have been placed, Colonel Becker reports that a German soldier is tampering with the second charge site. This interrupts your current demolition objectives.

Return to the second charge site and eliminate the designated soldier. Patrols may have moved since your first visit, so do not assume the previous route is still clear.

<section id="mission-failure" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Does Your Commando Keep Getting Detected or Killed?

Longstop Infiltration can become frustrating when patrols, machine guns, and exposed routes overlap.

A Commando who is detected in the middle of an enemy position has limited opportunities to escape before taking heavy fire.

<GuideTable
  columns={infiltrationProblemColumns}
  rows={infiltrationProblemRows}
/>

### What Should You Do Immediately After Being Detected?

Move away from enemy fire and reach nearby cover if possible.

Concealment requires the Commando to be both in cover and out of combat, so reaching cover does not necessarily restore camouflage immediately.

Check the Concealment indicator before attempting another exposed crossing.

### Do You Have to Restart the Entire Mission When the Commando Dies?

On Standard difficulty, checkpoint recovery can return you to a recent mission position rather than the beginning of Longstop Infiltration. Inspect your active objectives after restarting to determine which tasks remain unfinished.

If you repeatedly die at the same checkpoint, pause before issuing another movement order. Enemy patrols may still occupy the route that caused your previous failure. Identify another reachable covered position and resume your approach from there.

Checkpoint behavior on Expert may differ.

</section>

<section id="extraction" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Finish Longstop Infiltration?

Planting the final Demo Charge does not immediately complete Longstop Infiltration. You must still withdraw the Commando before the final demolition sequence begins.

1. Wait for confirmation that all required charges are ready.
2. Follow the newly assigned extraction objective.
3. Continue using short movement orders between concealed positions.
4. Avoid unnecessary combat along the withdrawal route.
5. Reach the extraction objective to trigger the final demolition sequence.

<GuideImage

The mission ends after the final demolition sequence.

The After Action screen then presents your available campaign upgrade choices.
