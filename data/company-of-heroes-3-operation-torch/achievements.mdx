## How Do Operation Torch Achievements Work?

Operation Torch has two achievement requirements for every campaign mission.

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementDifficultyColumns}
  rows={achievementDifficultyRows}
/>

**Sand Castle** requires completing Operation Flagpole on any difficulty, whereas **This Is Fine** requires an Expert victory. Every mission follows this same pair of difficulty requirements.

### Do Achievements Require Optional Objectives or Collectibles?

None of the 30 Operation Torch achievements requires a collectible, optional story decision, specific unit ability, or Torch Objective as a separate unlock condition.

Focus on completing each mission at the required difficulty. Pursue <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/campaign-upgrades#torch-objectives">Torch Objectives</Link> when their additional campaign rewards can help with later Expert missions.

### Are All 30 Achievements Hidden?

All 30 Operation Torch achievements are listed as hidden achievements.

Their names do not always reveal the mission or difficulty required to unlock them. Search the checklist by mission name rather than trying to interpret an achievement's title.

## What Is the Best Way to Complete All 30 Achievements?

Starting directly on Expert avoids committing to a lower-difficulty campaign before attempting the hardest achievement requirements.

After winning Operation Flagpole on Expert, inspect Sand Castle and This Is Fine in your Steam achievement list. If both unlock, continue pursuing the Expert achievements without planning an unnecessary second campaign.

Players unfamiliar with Company of Heroes 3 may benefit from learning the missions on a lower difficulty first. Pay particular attention to battles that cause repeated failures, since those are the ones worth preparing for before an Expert attempt.

Campaign upgrades can help with later challenges. Review the available <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/campaign-upgrades">perk choices</Link> rather than selecting an upgrade solely for achievement completion.

</section>

## Which Operation Torch Achievement Names Can Be Misleading?

An achievement's name can suggest a special combat challenge even when its only requirement is a mission victory.

<GuideTable
  columns={misleadingAchievementColumns}
  rows={misleadingAchievementRows}
/>

**Don't Forget The Engineers** requires an Expert victory in Medjez El Bab, not a separate Engineer-related action. Likewise, you can build defensive structures during Hill 609 and still meet the requirement for **Defense Is Overrated**. Avoid imposing extra restrictions on your Expert attempt.

### Do You Need to Use Every Campaign Perk?

No. None of the 30 achievements requires selecting a particular Campaign Perk or collecting every available upgrade. You do not need separate playthroughs solely to select different perks.

## How Do You Track Your Expert Achievement Progress?

Victories on Easy, Standard, or Hard do not satisfy the Expert achievement requirements.

After each Expert mission, compare its two achievements against your Steam unlocks and mark the ones you have earned. This is particularly important if you have already completed parts of the campaign at another difficulty.

If an Expert achievement remains locked, keep that mission marked as unfinished. Consult the <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch">campaign walkthrough</Link> when preparing for another attempt.

</section>

## Does Longstop Infiltration Require a Deathless Run?

Far Back On The Leg Side and Directly Behind The Wicket Keeper require mission victories at their respective difficulties. Neither lists a deathless attempt as an additional requirement.

If the stealth or demolition objectives are preventing you from finishing the mission, follow the <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/longstop-infiltration">Longstop Infiltration walkthrough</Link>.
