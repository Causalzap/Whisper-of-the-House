## How Do Campaign Perks Work in Operation Torch?

The After Action screen separates campaign rewards into two categories:

- **Upgrade:** Earned by completing the mission.
- **Bonus Upgrade:** Earned by completing the mission's Torch Objective.

Each available reward presents two perk alternatives. Your selected perks carry forward into subsequent campaign missions.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-campaign-perk-selection.webp"
  alt="Operation Torch campaign perk selection showing rewards for mission completion and Torch Objectives"
  width={2048}
  height={730}
  caption="The star marks the standard mission reward, while the bull-head icon identifies the Bonus Upgrade."
/>

### Can You Choose Both Campaign Upgrades?

No. You can select one perk from each available pair. Choosing one locks out its alternative for the current campaign playthrough.

If you earn both an Upgrade and a Bonus Upgrade, you can select one perk from each reward group.

Compare the effects before accepting your choices, especially when one option improves combat performance while the other reduces costs or adds a new ability.

### Do Campaign Perks Carry Over Between Missions?

Selected perks remain active throughout your current campaign whenever the affected units or abilities are available.

Temporary mission-specific bonuses are separate from these permanent rewards.

## How Do You Unlock Extra Campaign Upgrades?

Look for optional objectives marked with the **gold bull-head icon of the 34th Infantry Division**.

These Torch Objectives provide additional campaign rewards when completed.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-bonus-objective-icon.webp"
  alt="Red Bull objective icon identifying an additional campaign reward in Operation Torch"
  width={1232}
  height={814}
  caption="Look for the special Red Bull emblem beside objectives that provide additional campaign rewards."
/>

Check the objective list whenever you begin a mission or receive new orders.

Some optional tasks appear alongside the main objectives, giving you an opportunity to strengthen your army before moving into the next battle.

### Should You Complete Every Torch Objective?

Complete Torch Objectives whenever the additional objective is achievable without putting the entire mission at unnecessary risk.

An extra upgrade can benefit several subsequent missions, making its long-term value greater than a temporary battlefield advantage.

A useful approach is to:

1. Identify the Torch Objective before advancing too far.
2. Check whether the objective can be completed during your normal mission progression.
3. Complete it before triggering the final mission stage whenever possible.
4. Check the available additional perk choice after victory.

Do not assume that completing the main mission automatically completes its optional objectives.

### What Do You Get for Capturing the Enemy Outposts in Medjez El Bab?

Medjez El Bab provides an early example of why Torch Objectives matter.

Capturing both enemy outposts rewards you with an additional Barracks upgrade choice. You can choose between reducing the Manpower cost of Barracks units or having newly trained Barracks units begin with additional Veterancy.

## Which Infantry Campaign Perks Should You Choose?

Infantry losses create two separate problems: depleted combat strength and rising reinforcement costs. Early Riflemen, Barracks, and Engineer rewards can address these problems before the campaign introduces more specialized units.

### Captain: Lead from the Front or Offensive Focus?

Lead from the Front is particularly valuable when your Captain regularly takes damage alongside the infantry. Its 50% incoming damage reduction helps him survive direct engagements, while the offensive bonuses improve his fighting ability.

A Captain who spends most of his time supporting other squads may benefit more from Offensive Focus. The shorter ability cooldowns let him provide support more frequently.

### Riflemen: Standoff or Grab 'em by the Belt?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-riflemen-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Operation Torch Riflemen campaign perks comparing improved protection in cover and increased rifle damage"
  width={2048}
  height={936}
  caption="Standoff and Grab 'em by the Belt are the Riflemen rewards after Charlie Beach."
/>

Standoff rewards Riflemen that spend engagements behind cover. It is a sensible choice when casualties repeatedly drain your reinforcement budget, but its protection does not apply throughout every exposed movement.

Grab 'em by the Belt provides +50% damage to standard rifles. If you already preserve your squads reliably, the additional damage can help them defeat enemies sooner.

### Grenade Range or Additional Requisition?

Consider what normally prevents you from throwing a grenade. Grenade Range addresses difficult approaches by increasing ability range by 50%, whereas Additional Requisition saves 10 Munitions per grenade.

The cost reduction becomes more valuable when you throw grenades frequently. Range is the stronger consideration when approaching a defended position exposes your infantry to excessive damage.

### Engineers: Prepared Repair Kits or Prepared Stacked Mines?

Prepared Repair Kits doubles Engineer construction and repair speed. Its benefit extends beyond a single vehicle type, making it useful when your army frequently depends on repairs or battlefield construction.

Prepared Stacked Mines is more specialized. The increased mine damage is valuable when you regularly prepare mined approaches, but it contributes less during missions where your Engineers spend most of their time repairing vehicles.

### Barracks: Bureau of Personnel or Veteran Trainers?

A recruitment shortage favors Bureau of Personnel, which reduces the Manpower cost of Barracks units by 10%.

Veteran Trainers gives newly trained Barracks units Veterancy 1. Choose it when your recruitment budget is sufficient and you want those units to enter combat with additional experience.

### Reinforcement: Triage or Benzedrine?

Recruitment and reinforcement are different expenses. Bureau of Personnel helps you purchase new Barracks units, while Triage reduces the cost of replacing casualties in existing squads by 10%.

Benzedrine addresses recovery time instead. It improves Medical Tent healing and halves infantry reinforcement times, making it useful when your squads spend too long recovering between engagements.

Combining Triage with Bureau of Personnel reduces two separate sources of Manpower expenditure.

### Attacking Spirit or Hold the Line?

Territory ownership determines the value of these perks. Attacking Spirit restores nearby infantry and team weapon crews when friendly squads decapture territory. Recovery continues outside combat, and another qualifying decapture refreshes its duration.

Hold the Line reduces infantry damage received by 20% inside friendly territory. It suits an army defending controlled sectors, whereas Attacking Spirit has more opportunities to activate during repeated advances into enemy-held territory.

### Snipers: M1E7 Prototype Rifle or SSB Sniper?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-sbeitla-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Sbeitla campaign rewards featuring M1E7 Prototype Rifle, SSB Sniper, Grenade Sumps, and Offensive Grenades"
  width={1105}
  height={510}
  caption="Sbeitla offers Sniper upgrades alongside two infantry bonus options."
/>

A Sniper equipped with the M1E7 Prototype Rifle can suppress enemy infantry and gains +100% attack speed. SSB Sniper changes the unit's role instead, upgrading all Snipers into teams capable of calling artillery barrages.

Favor the rifle upgrade when direct infantry engagements are your main problem. Take SSB Sniper when you need additional artillery against defended positions.

### Grenade Sumps or Offensive Grenades?

Grenade Sumps reduces incoming infantry damage while in cover. It complements an army that already relies on protected Riflemen and established defensive positions.

Offensive Grenades equips Riflemen with cooked M2 Frag Grenades that explode quickly. Consider it when your infantry frequently needs to dislodge enemy squads during an advance.

Earlier investments such as Standoff or Grenade Range can help determine which of these later rewards better suits your infantry.

## Which Vehicle and Anti-Tank Campaign Upgrades Are Worth Taking?

Halftrack and Stuart upgrades become available relatively early, while Motor Pool, Hellcat, and Tank Depot rewards arrive during the later battles.

Consider which vehicles you regularly deploy and whether their main limitation is survivability, armor penetration, mobility, or the cost of replacing them.

### Halftracks: Exchangeable Parts or Assault Carriers?

One Halftrack choice improves the flexibility of converted US Halftracks, while the alternative upgrades M3 Armored Personnel Carriers into Assault Carriers.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-halftrack-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Operation Torch Halftrack campaign perks comparing Exchangeable Parts and Assault Carriers"
  width={2048}
  height={924}
  caption="Halftrack upgrades offer a choice between conversion flexibility and improved Armored Personnel Carriers."
/>

Exchangeable Parts becomes valuable when a Halftrack's original conversion no longer suits the battle. After its first conversion, subsequent changes are free, take 8 seconds, and can be performed once every 120 seconds.

Assault Carriers is the more specialized option for an army that regularly deploys M3 Armored Personnel Carriers. It improves that particular vehicle type rather than providing the flexibility to change between conversions.

### Stuart Tanks: Lessons of Chouigui or No Governors?

The Stuart upgrade choice offers increased movement speed or automatic access to both the Survival Package and Tank Commander upgrades.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-stuart-perk-choices.webp"

  width={2048}

  caption="Chouigui offers separate Stuart and artillery support reward groups."

Lessons of Chouigui removes the need to obtain the Survival Package and Tank Commander upgrades separately. It is useful when you want newly available Stuarts to have both packages equipped immediately.

No Governors doubles their movement speed. That benefit matters most when repositioning or withdrawing light vehicles is a recurring problem. Neither perk replaces the need for supporting units when stronger enemy armor is present.

### Anti-Tank Guns: Pre-positioned AP Ammo or Maneuver Drills?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-kasserine-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Kasserine campaign rewards for anti-tank guns and Motor Pool units"
  width={1095}
  height={509}
  caption="Kasserine introduces anti-tank gun improvements and Motor Pool bonuses."

Pre-positioned AP Ammo removes the Munitions cost of Armor Piercing Rounds. This is useful when frequent use of the ability competes with grenades, artillery, and other support abilities for Munitions.

Maneuver Drills addresses the time needed to set up and reposition AT Guns. Consider it when your anti-tank teams regularly need to change their firing positions.

Both rewards become available after Kasserine, so neither is required to defeat the Tiger during that mission.

### Motor Pool: Reinforced Armor or Tungsten Rounds?

Are your Motor Pool vehicles dying too quickly, or are their attacks failing to penetrate enemy armor? Reinforced Armor addresses survivability with +33% health; Tungsten Rounds addresses penetration with a +50% bonus.

Neither reward applies universally to every tank in your army. Check which production building supplies your vehicles before making the selection.

### Hellcats: HVAP-T Rounds or Pintle Mount M2?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-return-to-longstop-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Return to Longstop Hellcat and support ability campaign upgrades"
  width={1089}
  height={488}
  caption="Return to Longstop offers improvements to Hellcat weapon performance and support abilities."
/>

With reliable infantry support, the Hellcat can concentrate on enemy armor. HVAP-T Rounds reinforces that role by increasing weapon penetration by 50%.

Pintle Mount M2 changes the vehicle's available firepower by adding a heavy machine gun. Its anti-infantry benefit matters more when your Hellcats frequently operate without adequate infantry support.

### Shermans: Stabilizer Calibration or Easy Eight Production?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-hill-609-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Hill 609 Sherman and Tank Depot campaign upgrade choices"
  width={1098}
  height={469}
  caption="Hill 609 rewards improve Sherman mobility protection, unlock Easy Eight production, or strengthen Tank Depot recruitment."
/>

Stabilizer Calibration makes Shermans 20% harder to hit while moving. It benefits existing Sherman units without requiring you to produce a different tank model.

Unlock M4A3E8 Sherman Easy Eight Production adds the Easy Eight to the Tank Depot's available production options.

Choose the production unlock if you intend to field Easy Eights in the remaining battle. Stabilizer Calibration is more relevant when you expect to continue using your existing Sherman force.

### Tank Depot: Crew Rotations or Elite Trainers?

Crew Rotations reduces the Manpower cost of training Tank Depot units by 10%. Elite Trainers gives units trained at the Tank Depot Veterancy 3 immediately.

Unlike Bureau of Personnel and Veteran Trainers, these rewards arrive just before the final mission. Their value depends heavily on how many new vehicles you intend to produce.

Elite Trainers favors immediate combat effectiveness, while Crew Rotations helps when Manpower limits the size of your armored force.

## Which Reconnaissance and Support Perks Should You Choose?

Support upgrades become particularly valuable when your army struggles to identify threats, approach defended positions, or maintain effective defensive fire.

### Scouts: Pathfinders or Artillery Observers?

Both Scout upgrades increase squad health and add another soldier.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-scout-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Operation Torch Pathfinders, Artillery Observers, Attacking Spirit and Hold the Line campaign perks"
  width={2048}
  height={931}
  caption="Hornet's Nest introduces Scout upgrades alongside an infantry support reward for completing its Torch Objective."
/>

Choose Artillery Observers if you regularly use reconnaissance units to direct artillery through camouflaged beacons. Pathfinders remain an alternative when scouting is your primary concern.

### Grasshopper Recon Run or Smoke Screen?

Use the type of problem your infantry faces to distinguish these two abilities. Grasshopper Recon Run reveals positions and camouflaged enemies, while Smoke Screen blocks enemy vision for up to 20 seconds.

Reconnaissance helps when you do not know what is defending an area. Smoke is useful when the threat is already known but its firing position prevents your army from advancing safely.

### 155mm Rapid Artillery Barrage or Dual P-47 Rocket Strike?

Stationary defenses favor the wide blast radius of 155mm Rapid Artillery Barrage. Dual P-47 Rocket Strike addresses a different target type: enemy vehicles.

If your army already has reliable anti-vehicle weapons, the artillery barrage adds another way to deal with fortified positions. Repeated difficulty against enemy armor makes the rocket strike worth considering.

### Commandos: Cross-Trained or Grenades Pouches?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-commando-perk-choices.webp"
  alt="Operation Torch Commando campaign perks comparing improved attacks from stealth and Willy Pete smoke grenades"
  width={2048}
  height={881}
  caption="The Commando rewards appear alongside the Weapon Support Center bonus choices."
/>

Cross-Trained strengthens attacks made when leaving stealth, whereas Grenades Pouches adds another way to deal with enemy infantry and obstruct vision.

Commandos used mainly for attacks from camouflage benefit from the first option. Players who rely more on smoke support may find the additional grenade ability useful in subsequent missions.

Both perks become available after <Link href="/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/longstop-infiltration">Longstop Infiltration</Link>, so neither is needed to complete the mission itself.

### Weapon Support Center: Concealment Training or Spotters?

Support teams that repeatedly suffer casualties benefit from Concealment Training's improved protection. Spotters instead increases vision range, helping these units identify approaching threats sooner.

The vision increase does not directly extend weapon range. Concealment Training applies to Weapon Support Center units, not the Commando's camouflage ability.

### HMGs: Link Belts or Gun Shields?

Link Belts is described as removing the need for HMGs to reload, with a displayed modifier of +100% reload speed. Gun Shields instead doubles the effects of Defensive Set-up and makes HMGs an additional 20% harder to hit while set up.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-hmg-perk-choices.webp"

  width={1123}

  caption="Sened Station offers HMG improvements and an additional choice between casualty recovery and vehicle salvage."

Choose Link Belts when interruptions in sustained fire are a problem. Gun Shields is better suited to HMG teams that spend most of their time defending established positions.

### No Man Left Behind or Salvage Crew?

No Man Left Behind gives infantry squads another way to recover from casualties. It can also generate Manpower when the recovering squad is already at full strength.

Salvage Crew lets Engineers recover resources from vehicle wrecks, returning part of the original Manpower and Fuel costs.

The decision depends on whether infantry casualties or vehicle replacement costs place greater pressure on your economy. Salvage Crew also requires accessible wrecks and surviving Engineers to make use of its benefits.

### Mortars: Heavy HE Rounds or Light HE Rounds?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-faid-perk-choices.webp"

  caption="Faid rewards improve Mortar performance and provide additional frontline recovery options."

Heavy HE Rounds changes the size of the impact area, increasing its radius by 30%. Light HE Rounds changes how far your Mortars can fire, extending their range by 20%.

Take Heavy HE Rounds for wider blast coverage or Light HE Rounds when enemy positions are difficult to reach from your current firing locations. Neither upgrade directly increases the listed damage of an individual explosion.

### Field Repairs or Frontline Medical Station?

Field Repairs restores 20 health per second to a designated friendly vehicle for up to 20 seconds. The repair effect is disabled during combat, so the vehicle still needs an opportunity to disengage.

Frontline Medical Station provides healing, casualty collection, reinforcement, and an additional retreat point in friendly territory.

Field Repairs helps an armored force recover without depending entirely on conventional Engineer repairs. Frontline Medical Station is more useful when infantry repeatedly spends too much time traveling back to base for recovery.

### Resistance Fighter Infiltration or Tunnel Networks?

<GuideImage
  src="/images/company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch/operation-torch-fondouk-perk-choices.webp"

  width={1108}
  height={512}
  caption="Fondouk rewards unlock additional options for frontline infrastructure and Fighting Positions."

Resistance Fighter Infiltration turns suitable garrisoned buildings into Forward Headquarters, providing infantry and team weapon production, reinforcement, and increased infantry rate of fire. Tunnel Networks instead allows you to establish an HQ-sector network and additional Tunnel Hideouts.

The choice is between expanding your frontline infrastructure and improving movement across the battlefield. You can use the tunnel abilities provided during Fondouk without selecting Tunnel Networks afterward; the reward determines your permanent campaign access to the ability.

### Anchor Points or Dig Any Time You Stop?

Anchor Points gives nearby infantry and team weapons a 25% accuracy bonus through Fighting Positions.

Dig Any Time You Stop instead allows Fighting Positions to be constructed in neutral territory.

The first perk improves units around established positions. The second removes a territorial construction restriction, offering greater flexibility before an area has been secured.

### Fire Direction Center or Pre-Registration?

Fire Direction Center halves the Munitions cost of support abilities, allowing more frequent calls within the same resource budget. Pre-Registration offers +100% weapon scatter accuracy instead.

If Munitions shortages prevent you from calling support when needed, prioritize the cost reduction. Neither perk improves the accuracy of every weapon in your army.

## How Should You Choose the Best Campaign Upgrades for Your Army?

An upgrade's value depends on the problem it solves, the units it affects, and when you receive it.

  columns={upgradePriorityColumns}
  rows={upgradePriorityRows}
/>

These recommendations address different battlefield problems. If two recommended perks belong to the same reward group, you must choose between them.

### Which Early Campaign Perks Provide Long-Term Value?

Prepared Repair Kits becomes available after Medjez El Bab and improves both construction and vehicle repair. Bureau of Personnel and Triage can reduce recurring Manpower expenses from recruitment and reinforcement.

These rewards arrive early enough to influence many subsequent battles. They remain useful even when the campaign changes the composition of your army.

### Infantry-Focused Upgrade Combination

An infantry-heavy army can combine Standoff, Bureau of Personnel, Triage, and Hold the Line for protection and lower operating costs.

No Man Left Behind and Grenade Sumps offer additional recovery and protection later in the campaign. Frontline Medical Station is another option when infantry recovery becomes the main limitation.

An offensive infantry force may prefer Attacking Spirit and Offensive Grenades instead of their defensive alternatives.

### Vehicle-Focused Upgrade Combination

Start with Prepared Repair Kits and select the Halftrack and Stuart options that match the vehicles you regularly deploy.

Later, Salvage Crew can recover resources from wrecks, while Field Repairs provides another way to restore damaged vehicles.

Tungsten Rounds strengthens Motor Pool penetration, and HVAP-T Rounds improves the Hellcat's anti-armor performance. These bonuses have different unit restrictions.

### Artillery and Defensive Upgrade Combination

155mm Rapid Artillery Barrage, Artillery Observers, Heavy HE Rounds, and Fire Direction Center offer different improvements to battlefield support.

For an army built around established defensive positions, Hold the Line, Gun Shields, and Anchor Points offer protection or accuracy under their respective activation conditions.

Choose between additional protection, reduced support costs, and improved firepower according to the limitations of your current force.

### Are Hill 609 Upgrades Worth Taking?

Only Hill 604 remains after these rewards become available. If you intend to recruit new Tank Depot units, Elite Trainers provides immediate Veterancy 3, while Easy Eight Production opens another Sherman production option.

Base your choice on the force you expect to field in the final battle, not the long-term savings that would matter earlier in the campaign.

<section id="upgrade-mistakes" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Is Your Campaign Upgrade Not Helping?

### Your Bonus Upgrade Is Missing

Check whether you completed the mission's Torch Objective before finishing the main mission.

A mission victory satisfies the standard Upgrade requirement but does not automatically satisfy its Bonus Upgrade condition.

### Your Chosen Perk Has No Visible Effect

Check the affected unit and any activation conditions.

For example, Standoff requires Riflemen to use cover, Hold the Line applies inside friendly territory, and Gun Shields improves HMG protection while set up.

An upgrade may offer little immediate benefit during a mission that does not provide access to the affected unit.

### Your Perk Is Not Reducing the Expected Cost

Bureau of Personnel affects Barracks recruitment, Triage affects reinforcement, and Crew Rotations affects Tank Depot recruitment.

Additional Requisition reduces grenade ability costs by a fixed 10 Munitions, while Fire Direction Center reduces support ability Munitions costs by 50%.

Check the specific building, unit, or ability involved before expecting a discount.

### Your Vehicle Upgrade Is Not Applying

Reinforced Armor and Tungsten Rounds affect Motor Pool units. HVAP-T Rounds affects Hellcats, while Stabilizer Calibration affects moving Shermans.

A vehicle-related perk does not necessarily apply to every vehicle available to your army.

### Field Repairs Stops Working During Combat

Field Repairs is disabled during combat. Withdraw the affected vehicle from the engagement so it can recover outside combat.

### Your New Unit Does Not Have the Expected Veterancy

Veteran Trainers applies to units trained at the Barracks. Elite Trainers applies to units trained at the Tank Depot.

Neither perk automatically grants Veterancy 3 to every existing unit in your army.
