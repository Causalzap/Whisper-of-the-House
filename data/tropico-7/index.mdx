Build **two Cattle Ranches** on fertile land and connect them to roads. Place a **Tannery** close enough to receive Hides directly, then add a **Transport Office** to deliver the first **1,500 Leather** to the Docks.

The **Tropico 7 Demo** takes place in **Little Great Britain**, the opening Campaign mission. You govern a British colony under Lord Roger Wyndham, whose demands become increasingly expensive while your mandate counts down. The ultimate goal is independence, although the successful route takes an unusual turn: you convince Wyndham to take over your developed island and leave his private island to you.

The first half introduces cattle production, Leather exports, trade income, and faction politics. Later, you must keep the colony functioning while improving living conditions, recruiting Revolutionaries, and preparing a special operation to obtain a Castle.

The Demo is limited to the Colonial Era and the first Campaign mission. Later Campaign maps and the World Wars, Cold War, and Modern Eras are not playable here.

<section id="little-great-britain" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Little Great Britain: Build Your First Two Cattle Ranches

Lord Wyndham's opening order is **Have 2 Ranches with the production Cattle**.

Your island already has a Dock, some housing, agricultural buildings, and basic services. Those facilities provide a starting economy, so there is no need to redesign the settlement before completing the first assignment.

Open **Production → Ranch** and choose Cattle. Use the fertility overlay to find suitable ground; greener areas offer better agricultural conditions.

![The opening Campaign objective requires two Ranches producing Cattle.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-cattle-ranches.webp)

Place the two Ranches close enough that you can connect them economically to the existing road network. Reserve a nearby space for the Tannery, which becomes the next required building.

Road connections deserve attention during placement. A road can pass beside a building without connecting to its entrance, especially where the free-placement and grid systems meet. Check the connection point before letting construction proceed.

A sensible opening order is:

1. Place the two Cattle Ranches on fertile land.
2. Connect both to the existing road network.
3. Let the construction workers finish the buildings.
4. Reserve the neighboring industrial space for Leather processing.

Quick Build can finish construction immediately for an additional cost. One opening attempt shows a **$2,000 Quick Build** option, but using it on every early building consumes money that could support later assignments.

The first two Ranches are enough for the initial objective. Additional Ranches become useful when you have the workers and processing capacity to benefit from them.

### Why start with Cattle instead of other animals?

The Ranch menu includes Goats, Sheep, Pigs, Llamas, Crocodiles, and Cattle, each producing different goods.

Cattle provide **Hides and Meat**. Hides feed the required Leather industry, while Meat contributes to the food supply.

Other animals become relevant later. Goats, for example, can produce Milk for a Revolutionary assignment. Building several different Ranch types immediately is unnecessary unless their outputs serve a current objective.

## How to Produce and Export 1,500 Leather

After the two Cattle Ranches are completed, Lord Wyndham demands Leather.

The production chain is:

**Cattle Ranch → Hides → Tannery → Leather → Docks**

Build a Tannery beside the Ranches. The placement interface highlights compatible neighboring producers that can supply raw materials directly.

![The Tannery placement preview shows how nearby production buildings can supply raw materials directly.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-ranch-tannery.webp)

That direct connection is valuable because Hides can reach the Tannery without requiring ordinary freight transport for the intermediate delivery.

A Tannery creates considerable pollution, so keeping this industry near the Ranches and away from densely populated residential streets is a reasonable arrangement.

Once the Tannery is staffed and operating, Hides enter its input storage and Leather begins accumulating as output.

The first shipment requires **1,500 Leather** to be exported. Producing the goods inside the Tannery does not complete the objective; they must reach the Docks and leave on a freighter.

### Why are my goods not being transported?

The mission introduces an additional Transport Office and explains a particularly important rule:

**Transporters normally wait until at least 1,500 goods are ready for pickup.**

![The transportation instructions show the 1,500-goods pickup threshold and delivery priority.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-transport-threshold.webp)

The transportation system prioritizes goods needed by stores, followed by production buildings, and finally the Docks. Goods delivered to the Docks are exported when the next freighter arrives.

A Tannery containing 700 Leather may therefore be working correctly even though nothing has been collected yet.

When the export counter refuses to move, inspect the process in order:

- **Ranches:** Are workers producing Hides?
- **Tannery:** Are Hides arriving, and is Leather accumulating?
- **Roads:** Are the production buildings and Transport Office connected?
- **Transport:** Have enough goods accumulated for collection?
- **Docks:** Has the Leather arrived, and has a freighter departed?

A building displaying a road warning needs its connection fixed before adding another Transport Office. Similarly, a fully functioning freight network cannot export goods that haven't been produced.

### Second Shift or Overload: which Transport Office upgrade helps?

A Transport Office offers ways to increase freight capacity, but they have different costs.

The **Second Shift** upgrade adds workers to its transportation operations. One early setup shows the upgrade costing **$2,500**, making it useful when the existing office is busy and enough employees are available.

The **Overload** Work Mode allows transporters to carry up to **50% more cargo**, but they lose an average of **5% of transported goods**.

That trade-off matters when you're relying on limited production to meet an exact shipment requirement. Faster or larger deliveries are helpful only when the extra losses don't undermine your target.

Start by checking the existing Office's workforce and the amount of stored goods. Expanding transportation capacity makes sense once production is regularly generating freight that the current workers cannot keep up with.

### Should you sign a Leather trade contract?

Open **Goods and Trade** to inspect available export contracts.

A Crown contract for **1,500 Leather** can offer a sale price **14% above the average world-market price**, along with an improvement in Crown relations.

This makes a profitable opening industry more attractive, especially when the required production chain is already operating.

One successful early run also receives **12 additional months of mandate time** after completing the relevant Crown progression. That mandate reward belongs to the completed assignment, rather than being a permanent bonus attached to every Leather contract.

The first objective finishes when Wyndham acknowledges the Leather shipment. From there, faction assignments and the independence campaign become more important.

## How to Make Money and Reach $25,000 in Trade Profit

After the initial export, the Crown eventually demands **$25,000 in monthly profit through trade**.

This is not the same as having $25,000 in the treasury. Your production network must generate enough export income during the required period.

The initial Leather operation is a useful foundation because both the raw-material buildings and processing facility are already in place. Expanding it can increase export revenue without requiring an entirely different industry.

However, building more Ranches and Tanneries is only worthwhile when the existing facilities can supply and operate them.

### When should you expand Leather production?

The $25,000 trade-profit requirement rewards sustained exports, not the number of production buildings you own.

A second Tannery becomes useful when the first is consistently operating at capacity and your Ranches still have enough Hides to support another processor. Adding one before the raw-material supply is ready creates another building that needs workers without guaranteeing more Leather sales.

Crocodile Ranches provide another possible source of Hides, but they also require labor and suitable land. Expand them when the existing processing capacity can use the additional materials.

Before increasing production, compare the expected output with the workers and transport capacity available. Expanding a profitable industry is usually more useful than opening several unrelated industries that your population cannot yet operate.

### Why are homes empty or workers missing?

New industrial districts often create employment problems before they create additional profits.

A completed building can remain understaffed even though the island has citizens who are not working. In one larger colonial settlement, dozens of unemployed citizens coexist with numerous vacant jobs.

More vacancies alone therefore don't establish that another building is needed.

Use the Almanac and individual building panels to inspect staffing, housing, wages, and the positions available. Citizens must also be able to reach their workplaces.

The housing system considers several factors:

- Rent relative to what a household can afford.
- Distance from employment.
- Access to nearby stores and services.
- Whether suitable housing is available in the neighborhood.

A Cottage can display **No Store Nearby** even when a Small Store exists elsewhere in the settlement.

One practical solution is to move the Cottage closer to the existing store's service area. If several homes are outside coverage, placing another Small Store may be the better investment.

Do not automatically replace poor housing with expensive Townhouses or Mansions. The building must be affordable for the households who need it.

### When should you build a Bus Terminal?

A Bus Terminal allows you to create a route with up to **five stops**.

Connecting the town center to outlying agricultural or industrial areas can reduce the distance workers must travel on foot.

Set stops around existing residential and employment areas rather than distributing them solely according to road length. A route that passes through the middle of nowhere offers little benefit.

The bus system helps with mobility, but it doesn't guarantee that every vacant position will be filled. Check individual workplace requirements and actual employment after the route begins operating.

The same principle applies to expanding your city: a finished road network is useful, but it cannot replace a sufficient population of suitable workers.

<section id="mandate" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Extend Your Mandate Without Running Out of Time

During the Colonial Era, your position depends on the British Crown. The remaining mandate determines how long you can continue governing.

A profitable island can still fail when the mandate expires. Keeping that timer under control is more important than accumulating a large treasury for its own sake.

Open the **Domestic Policy Council** at the Palace to inspect Crown and faction assignments.

Available tasks include construction, production, exports, Edicts, and direct payments. Their requirements and rewards vary, so choose according to the buildings you already operate and the months remaining.

### Choose production tasks differently from export tasks

The Monarchists may request **2,500 Cocoa exported**, while the Revolutionaries can offer **400 Milk produced**.

A Cocoa export requires a Plantation, workers, harvested goods, transport, and a departing freighter. A Milk production objective can progress once a suitable Ranch produces the required quantity.

That makes the type of task important, even when the number is smaller.

A Goat Ranch can produce Milk, while Cocoa comes from a Plantation. Both facilities need appropriate staffing and operating time.

The same assignment may offer different rewards or time conditions depending on the choice presented. Read those details before accepting rather than assuming the first available task is the fastest.

### Tropico Hat can be a faster Crown assignment

One early Monarchist assignment asks you to issue the **Tropico Hat** Edict.

In that setup, Tropico Hat costs **100 Knowledge** to unlock. After unlocking it, activate the Edict through the political controls.

Its effects are:

- Monarchist faction Approval: **+3%**
- Citizen Liberty: **-3%**

Completing this particular assignment awards **eight additional months** of mandate time.

This can be much faster than establishing a new Plantation solely to fulfill an export order, provided you have the required Knowledge and can issue the Edict.

The Approval benefit and Liberty penalty continue to matter after the task has been completed. Political assignments aren't necessarily free of longer-term consequences.

### When paying the Crown is safer

A later Crown option offers **seven months of mandate time for a $21,000 payment**.

It's a substantial expense, but it completes the assignment without waiting for construction, production, or shipping.

When the treasury is strong and only a few months remain, a direct payment may be preferable to an export that cannot realistically finish before the deadline.

Another quick assignment can involve **Employee of the Month**. This Edict increases efficiency in production buildings and Mines by **20%**, while reducing job quality by **15%**.

Choose these options based on both their immediate completion conditions and the ongoing changes they introduce.

### Why a Banana export can cost you the mandate

A later Crown assignment can offer **2,500 Bananas exported**.

The danger is not only growing enough Bananas. The crop must be harvested, transported to the Docks, and loaded onto a freighter before the available time expires.

An attempt can reach roughly 1,000 Bananas in storage with only a few months remaining. Even additional Plantations and Quick Build spending may not produce and ship the rest in time.

Cancelling an accepted assignment can also remove **three months from the mandate**, making a last-minute switch particularly dangerous.

An export order is a reasonable choice when the required crop is already producing at scale. Starting the entire production chain with almost no time remaining is a much greater risk.

## How to Advance the Revolutionary Objectives

After the first Leather export, **Victoria Guerra Pérez** approaches the Governor on behalf of the Revolutionaries.

Her movement wants independence from Britain. The early tasks introduce fishing, Ranch work modes, and eventually political recruitment.

These assignments develop alongside the Crown's demands, rather than replacing them entirely.

### Build two Fisherman's Wharves

The first major Revolutionary objective asks for **two Fisherman's Wharves**.

Open the production menu and inspect the available coastal fishing areas. Each Wharf must be positioned within range of a suitable fishing resource and connected to the island's infrastructure.

The Wharves provide another food source while advancing the independence campaign.

One early setup prices them at **$2,000 each**. Inspect your current treasury before using Quick Build on both, since other production and transport expenses are still approaching.

### Raise Ranch salaries and change Work Modes

The Revolutionaries subsequently ask for higher wages at the Cattle Ranches and introduce **More Animals**.

Select a Cattle Ranch, raise its budget until the salary requirement is satisfied, then open its Work Mode controls.

More Animals increases the number of animals a Ranch can support, improving production potential while reducing job quality.

A later objective requires **five Ranches** to use More Animals.

Before building additional facilities, check the Ranches already operating on the island. The Goat Ranch used for Milk production can also count toward the work-mode requirement when configured correctly.

The **Animal Vaccinations** upgrade provides a **10% efficiency increase** with additional upkeep. It can help offset production limitations, but improved efficiency still depends on having workers.

The broader choice is between increasing output and maintaining acceptable working conditions. Higher production is valuable for trade, yet job-quality penalties can create other management problems.

### Recruit 10 Revolutionaries with a Newspaper

The next political objective requires a **Newspaper** using **The King Is Dead** Work Mode.

Build the Newspaper near a populated neighborhood, then open its Work Mode controls and select The King Is Dead.

![The Newspaper uses The King Is Dead Work Mode to encourage citizens to join the Revolutionaries.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-revolutionary-newspaper.webp)

This Work Mode encourages nearby residents to become Revolutionaries. Its description gives an average conversion rate of **50% of influenced citizens per year**.

The displayed objective requires **10 citizens** to join.

A Newspaper surrounded by residences can influence more nearby people than one placed beside an isolated industrial district.

The conversion occurs over time, so the task may remain active even after the Newspaper is staffed and operating. Keep an eye on the mandate while waiting for the faction count to rise.

Completing the recruitment objective strengthens the Revolutionary progression, but it is not the final independence requirement.

## Gold Exports and Food Satisfaction Requirements

The later colonial objectives overlap with your ongoing Crown and Revolutionary assignments.

Among the possible requirements are **1,500 Gold exported to the Crown**, **2,500 Bananas exported**, and **52% Food Satisfaction**.

![The later colonial objectives show a 1,500 Gold export, a 2,500 Banana export, and a 52% Food Satisfaction target.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-gold-food-objectives.webp)

These aren't necessarily one combined mandatory order. Check which faction issued each objective and whether it is currently active.

### Export 1,500 Gold to the Crown

Open **Production → Mine** and locate a Gold deposit.

Build the Mine within the deposit's workable area, connect it to roads, and provide workers. A Mine placed near a different mineral deposit will not satisfy the Gold requirement.

Once production begins, check the output storage and the available trade arrangement with the Crown under **Goods and Trade**.

Mining can be slow. A Mine with a large remaining deposit may still produce very little when it has only one worker.

The remaining resource figure represents the material available at that deposit, not the amount already mined or ready for export.

Adding another Mine can help when you have sufficient workers and access to another suitable deposit. Otherwise, staffing and transport should be examined first.

As with Leather, the export is completed through shipment rather than simply storing the required quantity in the Mine.

### How to increase Food Satisfaction to 52%

Food Satisfaction can remain around **42–44%** even after the island has several food-producing buildings.

The first distinction is between food being produced and food being accessible to residents.

Inspect the existing Ranches, Plantations, fishing facilities, and Small Stores. Check whether production buildings are staffed and whether residential neighborhoods can reach stores with available goods.

Additional food varieties may help, but growing more crops takes time. It also places more demands on workers and transport.

There is a political alternative worth checking.

An available Edict increases citizens' **Food Satisfaction by 10%**, but also raises **Food Demand by 50%**.

This can provide an immediate improvement in the satisfaction figure, yet the increased consumption puts additional pressure on your food supply. It is a poor long-term choice when stores are already struggling to stay stocked.

Use it when you understand the extra demand and your economy can support it. An Edict cannot substitute indefinitely for accessible food.

A later Revolutionary assignment may request **54% Food Satisfaction** instead of 52%. Read the current target before making further changes.

## Logging Camps, Sawmills, and the 4,500 Planks Export

The Crown eventually turns its attention to timber production.

One assignment introduces **two Logging Camps and a Sawmill**, establishing another production chain:

**Logging Camp → Logs → Sawmill → Planks**

Logging Camps need access to forested areas, while the Sawmill processes the Logs into Planks.

Place the buildings where they can connect to the road network without creating an unnecessarily long freight route.

The later export objective requires **4,500 Planks**.

![The Crown requires 4,500 Planks to be exported during the later colonial objectives.](/images/tropico-7/little-great-britain-planks-objective.webp)

This is a larger shipment than the opening Leather requirement, making efficient production and transport increasingly important.

A Sawmill can have a high fire risk, so nearby firefighting coverage becomes valuable as the industrial district expands.

The Planks assignment is one part of the continuing Crown demands. Completing it does not, by itself, end British control of Tropico.

Build the required facilities first. Add more Logging Camps only when the Sawmill can process their output and the island has workers available; otherwise, extra buildings won't make the 4,500-Plank shipment arrive any faster.

</section>

## Terraforming, Plantation Fields, and Political Decisions

Little Great Britain introduces several systems that can make construction and policy decisions more flexible.

They are useful, but none should distract you from the active mission requirements.

### Terraforming is expensive early on

Terraforming allows you to reshape hills, flatten terrain, and create land where there was previously water.

The price depends on the terrain affected and the size of the change.

One large mountain-flattening preview displays a cost of approximately **$441,000**. Extensive coastal land creation can also cost hundreds of thousands.

These are examples of particular projects, not fixed Terraforming prices.

During the opening Colonial Era, a large land-reclamation project can consume money needed for transportation, exports, housing, and mandate extensions.

Smaller adjustments can be useful when a required building doesn't fit its intended site. Inspect the preview cost before confirming the change.

### Edit Plantation fields instead of rebuilding the entire farm

Plantations allow you to edit their cultivation areas, including while their buildings are under construction.

Select the Plantation and use the field-editing controls to remove or expand cultivation zones.

This helps when the original layout overlaps a road or fails to use nearby fertile ground.

However, expanding the cultivation area increases the work required. A large Plantation with too few employees may perform worse than expected.

Before accepting a crop export task, check the crop's fertility, available workers, and whether you have enough time for harvesting and shipping.

### Why are some Edicts unavailable?

The **Political Matrix** displays the political positions of El Presidente, citizens, and factions.

Edict availability depends on your political alignment and Constitution. Some policies cannot be activated from your current position.

A change to the Constitution may unlock different laws while restricting others. Certain faction rewards can also provide access to otherwise unavailable Edicts.

The four political directions include Social, Conservative, Liberal, and Authoritarian.

This matters when a faction assignment asks you to issue a specific Edict. Check whether the law is available before accepting a short-deadline task that requires it.

The Demo remains in the Colonial Era, so later-era election mechanics and other advanced political options are not fully available.

<section id="next-objective" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to Finish Chapter 1 and Complete the Tropico 7 Demo

The final part of Little Great Britain is not a conventional military victory over the British.

Instead, Penultimo develops a plan involving **Lord Wyndham's private island**, which has a special independent status.

The idea is to make Tropico so attractive that Wyndham wants to move there. If he takes possession of the developed island, the Governor can acquire Wyndham's former island through an exchange.

Several tasks contribute to making the colony appealing, including housing, employment, public satisfaction, and British cultural influence.

One set of objectives involves a **Theater and ten beautification objects**, while other requests concern the population and working conditions.

Complete the Campaign tasks as they appear. Their exact order can overlap with optional Crown and faction assignments.

### Wait for Wyndham to take the bait

Eventually, Wyndham announces that he is returning to England to seek permission to come back.

Penultimo recognizes that the plan is working. The island has become appealing enough that Wyndham may want to make it his own.

Victoria Guerra Pérez objects to the increasingly British character of the colony and argues for a more direct struggle.

Penultimo instead explains that they are close to securing independence without starting a war. What the island still needs is an impressive residence worthy of Wyndham.

That leads to the Pirate Cove.

### Build a Pirate Cove and prepare the Castle operation

The **Pirate Cove** introduces Secret Operations, which can obtain goods, landmarks, and other rewards.

Build the Pirate Cove when the relevant Campaign objective becomes available and connect it to the road network.

![The Pirate Cove building panel shows its workforce and the Special Operations controls.](/images/tropico-7/tropico-7-pirate-cove-castle-operation.webp)

Choose its location carefully. Pirate Cove operations can reduce nearby Crime Safety and Liberty, so placing it away from dense residential neighborhoods can limit its local impact.

The building automatically trains pirates for raids and special operations. Its workforce and training progress matter: a completed Pirate Cove does not mean every operation can launch immediately.

Once the required operation is accessible, prepare the mission to acquire the Castle intended for Lord Wyndham.

The exact preparation depends on the operation requirements displayed in the Pirate Cove. Inspect the available pirates, resources, and operation status rather than assuming the building alone completes this stage.

Let the operation finish before trying to place the Castle.

### Place Neuschwanstein Castle in a beautiful location

After the Secret Operation succeeds, the Pirate Cove offers **Neuschwanstein Castle** for placement. Confirm the prompt to begin positioning the landmark.

![The successful Neuschwanstein Castle operation allows you to place the landmark, with a separate objective requiring a beautiful location.](/images/tropico-7/tropico-7-stolen-castle-placement.webp)

The Campaign requires **one Neuschwanstein Castle** and specifies that it must be built in a **beautiful location**.

Open the **Beauty & Pollution** overlay before choosing a site. Look for an attractive coastal area with enough space for the Castle and surrounding parks. Avoid a heavily polluted industrial district.

Finish the Castle's construction and check that both the building requirement and its location condition have been satisfied. Once Wyndham returns and sees the completed residence, the island exchange can move toward its final stage.

### Why does Wyndham want you to demolish the Pirate Cove?

When Wyndham returns from England, he admires the developed island and the impressive Castle.

He announces that he intends to use Tropico as his new place of government instead of his private island.

This is the moment Penultimo's plan succeeds.

However, Victoria and some Revolutionaries have fled, and the Pirate Cove must be removed before Wyndham moves in.

**Demolish the Pirate Cove when this final objective appears.**

Do not demolish it immediately after launching the Castle operation. The Cove is still needed until the Castle has been obtained and the mission reaches the final preparation stage.

After the demolition requirement is completed, Wyndham moves to Tropico and leaves his former private island to the Governor.

### What happens at the end of the Demo?

The exchange completes **Chapter 1**.

![Chapter 1 successfully completed after the island exchange with Lord Wyndham.](/images/tropico-7/tropico-7-chapter-one-complete.webp)

Lord Wyndham occupies the developed colonial island, while El Presidente and Penultimo depart to begin again on the island Wyndham previously owned.

The chapter ends with a short account of their journey across the ocean and their arrival at the new island.

The Demo stops there. You do not continue building on Wyndham's former island within this playable chapter.
