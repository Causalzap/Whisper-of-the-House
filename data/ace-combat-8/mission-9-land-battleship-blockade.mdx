<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Protect the **IED VEHICLE** until it reaches the Land Battleship, then destroy all
**8 secondary treads — 4 per side**. When **Remaining Distance** appears, hit the
marked containment building immediately. After the battleship moves again, destroy
both **rocket thrusters**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Mission 9 keeps changing what actually stops the Land Battleship. The hull itself
is not the objective: first the ground assault has to expose its mobility system,
then Joker Flight has to trigger a prepared demolition before the battleship
crosses the containment line, and finally an emergency propulsion system has to
be disabled.

That is why a run can fail even while Joker One is still healthy. The Land
Battleship continues advancing toward the **ENO Special Zone**, so spending too
long on fighters, UAVs, or an objective that has already ended gives the vehicle
more distance.

<section id="secondary-treads" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Protect the bomb trucks, then destroy all 8 secondary treads

The secondary treads begin behind armored panels along the sides of the Land
Battleship. Joker Flight cannot simply shoot through that protection. Allied bomb
trucks marked **IED VEHICLE** have to reach the ship and detonate underneath the
armor before the mobility targets become available.

During the approach, use your weapons on enemies that can actually stop those
vehicles. AA positions close to the route, ground weapons firing into the convoy,
and aircraft repeatedly attacking the trucks deserve attention; enemies already
moving away from the ground assault can wait. Saving some anti-surface ammunition
here also makes the exposed-tread phase much faster.

![An IED vehicle reaching the Land Battleship and exposing its secondary tread armor.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-bomb-truck-exposes-secondary-tread.webp)

The explosion removes the side protection and the operation confirms that a
secondary tread has been exposed. That radio call is the point to stop guarding
the approach and turn your weapons onto the battleship's mobility system.

There are **8 secondary treads in total, 4 on each side**. Once you have a clean
angle on one side, destroy its four targets before crossing around the ship unless
defensive fire forces you away. The treads sit low on the hull, so a controlled
surface-attack pass is usually more productive than repeatedly switching sides.

The battleship may slow as these components disappear, but a slowdown is not the
completion cue. Keep working until the operation confirms that the secondary
treads are gone.

Players returning for **Judex** have an additional timing condition involving the
secondary-tread armor before the second slowdown line. The exact requirement is
listed with the other named aircraft in the
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records).

</section>

<section id="final-containment" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When the battleship moves again, stop looking for another tread

Destroying all eight secondary treads does not permanently immobilize the Land
Battleship.

A prepared demolition interrupts its advance for a while, but the situation
deteriorates and the vehicle eventually starts moving again. At this point there
is **no hidden ninth tread** and no reason to circle the hull searching for
another mobility marker.

Follow the operation toward the high-rise district instead. Ground forces are
falling back toward another prepared containment point, and the next useful target
does not appear until the unit responsible for the demolition can no longer finish
the job itself.

During that gap, stay alive and clear only threats interfering with the retreat.
The important update is the moment Joker Flight is ordered to trigger the
demolition from the air.

![The final containment building becomes the target as the Land Battleship closes on the demolition line.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-final-containment-building.webp)

The HUD now displays **Remaining Distance** and marks the building that has to
collapse across the battleship's route. The margin can already be tight when the
target becomes yours; one run shows only **895 m** remaining at the objective
change.

Once that counter is visible, abandon everything else. The building is already
prepared with explosives, so Joker Flight only needs to land the attack that
triggers the collapse. A wide setup turn, one more fighter kill, or a pass at a
UAV can spend the distance you no longer have.

Lock the structure, hit it with a reliable surface attack, and bring it down before
Remaining Distance reaches zero.

The demolition stops the battleship again, but only briefly.

</section>

<section id="rocket-thrusters" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Destroy both rocket thrusters to stop the Land Battleship

After the containment building collapses, the Land Battleship reveals its final
mobility trick: **2 rocket thrusters**.

![AWACS marks the Land Battleship's rocket thrusters as the final propulsion targets.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-land-battleship-thrusters.webp)

This phase is more direct than the tread and containment sequences because there
is no ground unit to wait for and no demolition target away from the ship. Both
thrusters are exposed propulsion components, and both have to be destroyed.

Defensive fire and UAVs can still fill the radar, but the battle no longer rewards
clearing the area. Set up passes on the rear of the Land Battleship, damage one
thruster until it is destroyed, then transfer immediately to the other.

The surrounding buildings are useful for breaking lines of fire while you turn
around. Use them to reset your angle instead of staying on one long, predictable
run behind the ship. The objective is propulsion damage, not surviving as close to
the hull as possible.

Mission 9's failure pattern becomes easy to read once the three movement states are
separated:

- while **IED VEHICLE** units are approaching, protect the trucks;
- while **Remaining Distance** is counting down, hit the marked building;
- once **rocket thrusters** are marked, stop fighting the wider battle and destroy
  both propulsion targets.

Every costly mistake comes from continuing the previous phase after the Land
Battleship has already changed how it is moving.

</section>

<section id="after-mission-9" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What happens after both thrusters are destroyed?

With both rocket thrusters gone, the Land Battleship can no longer continue toward
the **ENO Special Zone**. You do not need to destroy every visible weapon,
defensive system, or remaining section of the vehicle once its final movement
system is disabled.

Mission 10 changes the type of threat completely. Joker Flight enters the Rainband,
where visibility and radar become unreliable and Tonitra Spear attacks depend on
laser-guidance airships hidden inside the clouds.

Continue with the
[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) for Mission 10.

</section>
