Mission 9 is not a normal damage race. The Land Battleship keeps advancing
toward the **ENO Special Zone**, while different parts of the containment plan
take turns trying to stop it. Joker Flight cannot force the operation forward by
simply pouring missiles into the hull; each stage exposes a specific part of the
ship or hands you a new demolition target.

The important distinction is between **damaging the battleship** and **stopping
its movement**. If the objective changes but you keep attacking the previous
target, the Land Battleship continues consuming the distance the ground force
needs.

<section id="secondary-treads" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Open and destroy the secondary treads

The battleship's first real vulnerability is its mobility system, but the
secondary treads begin the operation behind heavy side armor. Joker Flight
cannot expose them from the air alone. The ground assault has to open the armor
first, which means the early part of the fight is about keeping the bomb trucks
alive rather than trying to damage the hull.

### Why can't you damage the secondary treads?

The allied bomb trucks appear on the HUD as **IED VEHICLE** units. One of them
has to reach the space underneath the side armor before the treads become
available.

Until that happens, concentrate on threats that can stop the approach:

- land-attack weapons firing into the route;
- nearby AA guns;
- drones or aircraft making repeated passes;
- other enemies directly threatening the IED vehicles.

You do not need to clear the entire radar. Attacks that keep the bomb-truck
route open are more useful than chasing aircraft that are already moving away,
and saving some anti-surface ammunition makes the next phase much easier.

### Protect the IED vehicle until the armor opens

Stay close enough to the Land Battleship to react when the ground assault
succeeds, but do not get so focused on the ship itself that an IED vehicle is
destroyed on the way in.

![An IED vehicle reaches the Land Battleship's secondary tread armor in Mission 9.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-bomb-truck-exposes-secondary-tread.webp)

Once the truck reaches the correct position, the explosion tears away the
protective armor and the radio confirms that the **secondary tread is exposed**.
That is the moment to switch from protecting the approach to attacking the
mobility system.

Missiles fired into the armored side before this point do not move the phase
forward. After the exposure call, however, the treads become the priority.

### How many secondary treads are there?

There are **8 secondary treads total — 4 on each side**.

Once one side is open, staying there long enough to finish its four treads keeps
the attack cleaner than repeatedly crossing around the battleship. The targets
sit low along the hull, so a stable surface-attack line is more useful than
constantly changing angles.

Anti-surface weapons work well, but no specific aircraft is required. What
matters is maintaining access to the exposed side and finishing each tread
before moving on.

The Land Battleship may slow while you are working through them, but slowing
down is not the completion signal. Continue until the operation explicitly
confirms that **all secondary treads are destroyed**.

</section>

## Final containment and Remaining Distance

Destroying all eight treads does not end the operation. The Land Battleship can
still force its way forward, and the ground force eventually falls back to one
last prepared containment position in the high-rise district.

This part of the mission is where many runs become confusing because the ship
appears to have been stopped more than once, yet the objective keeps changing.

### Why does the Land Battleship move again after all 8 treads are destroyed?

After the tread system is disabled, another prepared demolition halts the
battleship for a time. The pause looks convincing after such a long mobility
phase, but the situation does not remain stable. Defensive systems come back
online, friendly units begin withdrawing casualties, and the Land Battleship
eventually resumes its advance.

There is **no hidden ninth tread**.

If the ship starts moving again, do not circle it looking for another tread
marker. Move with the operation toward the next containment position and attack
only the enemies that threaten the retreating ground force or interfere with
the next objective.

### Wait for the final containment objective

The ground team has another building prepared to collapse across the
battleship's route, but the unit responsible for triggering the demolition is
hit before it can finish the job.

For a short stretch, there is no hidden target to discover. Stay alive, remove
immediate threats, and wait for the objective update. Joker Flight is eventually
ordered to trigger the demolition from the air.

At that moment, the HUD adds a **Remaining Distance** counter and marks the
building that has to be attacked.

![Mission 9 marks the final containment building while the Land Battleship closes on the demolition line.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-final-containment-building.webp)

The margin is already tight when control passes to Joker Flight. In the example
above, the counter is at **895 m** when the new target appears.

Once the counter is visible, the surrounding enemy count no longer matters.
Turn toward the marked structure immediately.

### Destroy the containment building before the distance reaches zero

The building has already been prepared with explosives, shells, and mines, so
Joker Flight only needs to trigger the demolition with an air strike.

Do not waste time creating a perfect attack pattern. As soon as the structure
can be locked, use a reliable surface attack and get the hit before the Remaining
Distance reaches zero.

This is not the moment to finish an interceptor, chase a UAV, or make a wide
turn for a cleaner approach. The only result that matters is bringing the
building down before the Land Battleship reaches the containment line.

A successful strike collapses the structure across the route and immobilizes
the ship again.

That still is not the final stop.

</section>

## Destroy the rocket thrusters

After the building collapses, the Land Battleship reveals an emergency
propulsion system and begins pushing forward again. The operation no longer
depends on another ground unit or another exposed armor section; Joker Flight
finally receives a direct propulsion target.

### Why isn't Mission 9 over after the building collapses?

The battleship activates **2 rocket thrusters**.

![AWACS orders Joker Flight to destroy the Land Battleship's rocket thrusters during Mission 9.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-9-land-battleship-thrusters.webp)

Both have to be destroyed.

UAVs and defensive fire still crowd the area, so this is an easy place to get
dragged back into the wider battle. Once the thruster objective appears, ignore
easy targets that pull you away from the rear of the ship and set up clean
passes on the propulsion system.

The surrounding buildings can help break long lines of fire while you
reposition. Use them to reset your angle, then come back low enough to keep the
thrusters visible without committing to one long straight run through the
battleship's defenses.

After the first thruster is destroyed, move across to the second instead of
continuing to attack the same damaged side. The Land Battleship finally loses
its ability to continue toward the ENO Special Zone once both are gone.

### Why Mission 9 can fail while Joker One is still alive

Keeping Joker One intact is not enough because the Land Battleship is advancing
throughout the operation. A run can collapse simply because too much time is
spent fighting targets that no longer matter.

Three situations need an immediate response:

- **IED vehicles are under attack:** without a bomb truck reaching the side
  armor, the secondary treads cannot be exposed;
- **the objective changes:** continuing to attack the previous target gives the
  battleship more time to advance;
- **Remaining Distance appears:** the marked building must come down before the
  ship reaches the containment line.

The safest way to read the fight is to pay attention to new HUD markers and
radio calls whenever the battleship appears to stop. Mission 9 repeatedly
changes the thing that actually controls its movement.

</section>

<section id="after-mission-9" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What happens after Mission 9?

Destroying both thrusters **immobilizes the Land Battleship** and prevents it
from reaching the ENO Special Zone. The operation is finished once its remaining
movement system is disabled; you are not required to destroy every visible part
of the vehicle.

Mission 10 changes the problem completely. Joker Flight leaves the exposed
ground-component fight behind and enters poor visibility, radar interference,
and laser-guided Tonitra Spear attacks inside the Rainband.

Continue with the [Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) for
Mission 10.
