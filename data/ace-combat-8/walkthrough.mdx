Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve runs from the Prologue through **30 numbered
missions**. The early campaign mostly asks you to intercept threats, protect
friendly units, and learn when a target is worth abandoning. Later sorties become
less predictable: an escort can turn into missile interception, a fleet battle
can become a structural attack, and destroying what looks like the final target
may simply reveal the next one.

The most useful habit is to watch what the operation is protecting. A fighter near
your nose is rarely more important than a bomber already approaching the fleet, a
missile seconds from impact, or a friendly ground unit that has stopped advancing.

## Missions 1–5: Learn what actually matters

### Mission 1 — Jokers Wild

The opening UAVs are forgiving enough to learn missile range, target switching,
and radar reading. The real test begins when reconnaissance aircraft arrive with
fighter escorts.

Those recon aircraft are the objective. Get close enough for a stable lock and
remove them before spending time on their escorts. A fighter forcing you defensive
deserves attention; one dragging you away from an escaping reconnaissance aircraft
does not.

Once the reconnaissance targets are gone, the sortie moves toward recovery rather
than hiding another major combat phase.

### Mission 2 — Nomad

Carrier defense rewards staying close enough to the Endurance to react when a new
formation breaks through.

Ordinary fighters can occupy Joker Flight while bombers and low-flying attackers
continue toward the ship. When a heavier formation appears, abandon a long chase,
turn back toward the carrier, and intercept the aircraft that can actually damage
it.

Grouped attackers are also a better use of multi-target special weapons than one
isolated fighter on the edge of the battle.

### Mission 3 — Purple Magic Takes Flight

White Valley has **three radar sites** around the port. The sortie succeeds by
keeping the defense network functioning while successive enemy formations attack.

Bombers should move ahead of ordinary fighters in your target order. A dogfight
can drift away from the base; a bomber can keep pressing the radar sites while you
are gone.

Purple Magic joins once the battle expands, so Joker Flight does not need to erase
every contact alone. Follow the pressure moving toward the installations and save
stronger ammunition for the formations that arrive later.

### Mission 4 — The Fall of the Numbered Fleets

Two expeditionary fleets are spread across Newfield Island: the Third Fleet at
Beltaport in the northwest and the Fifth Fleet at Anderson Port in the east.

An anti-surface setup suits the mission because ships remain the central task even
when shore defenses and the defected 262nd Squadron try to pull you elsewhere.

A reliable attack rhythm is:

- finish ships already close to destruction;
- remove AA or radar positions blocking the next run;
- deal with fighters only when they are breaking your attack;
- move to the other port once the first fleet is no longer the main threat.

The Sharks can turn this into a much longer dogfight than necessary. When allied
ships are taking losses, return to the fleet battle instead of following one
aircraft across Newfield Island.

### Mission 5 — Retaking of Theve

The landing force determines whether Mission 5 keeps moving.

During the approach, eliminate ships, aircraft, artillery, and vehicles directly
threatening the landing craft. After the troops reach shore, follow their advance
and clear whatever blocks the next section of ground.

The useful question is not whether an enemy remains alive, but whether that enemy
can still stop the assault. A fighter leaving the operation area matters much less
than a tank or howitzer holding the ground force in place.

## Missions 6–10: Drinwich, Faith Park, and Land Battleship 1

### Mission 6 — Attack on the Drinwich Airfield

Mission 6 starts with aerial refueling, then splits friendly forces between
helicopters and mechanized units approaching Drinwich Airfield.

Support whichever route is currently under pressure. SAMs, fighters, and armored
vehicles all matter when they are stopping that advance, but the airfield itself
has another restriction: **do not destroy the fuel and ammunition facilities**.

The FCU is trying to capture those supplies. Large chain explosions can wipe out
the resources you came to recover, so precise attacks are safer than dropping
weapons indiscriminately into a crowded vehicle group.

### Mission 7 — A Lifeline in the Desert

**24,000 points** is the important threshold at Faith Park Valley. The operation
wants the supply network supporting Land Battleship 1 crippled; it does not require
every target on the map to disappear.

The four main sectors are:

- **North:** solar power plant
- **West:** airfield and fighters
- **East:** ammunition storage
- **South:** refinery and heavier ground defenses

Choose the next sector by distance, remaining weapons, and whichever cluster is
already lined up for an efficient attack. At the northern plant, guns can remove
many lighter structures without spending missiles needed elsewhere.

The eastern ammunition sector has dense targets and useful secondary explosions,
but one depot is different: **the tunnel target cannot be destroyed from above**.
Drop toward the hillside entrance and fire through the opening.

A return line allows resupply and weapon changes. Use it while substantial sectors
still remain and your current loadout is becoming a limitation. When you are
already close to 24,000, crossing the map just to reload can cost more time than
finishing with guns and remaining missiles.

After reaching the requirement, the HUD can look like this:

![Mission 7 after passing the required Faith Park score.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-7-faith-park-bonus-time.webp)

A display of **25,470 / 24,000** accompanied by an instruction to keep destroying
targets means the required damage is already done. The remaining period is bonus
time, not a demand for 100% destruction.

Use it on nearby facility clusters, ammunition storage, or aircraft already in
position rather than crossing Faith Park for one low-value target.

### Mission 8 — Retaking of Theve II

The second battle for Theve mixes naval, ground, and air threats around multiple
friendly groups.

Bombers move to the front of the queue whenever they appear. Escorts can keep you
busy while the aircraft carrying the dangerous payload continues toward friendly
forces.

For the ground fight, follow allied movement rather than the total number of red
contacts. When a unit reports that it cannot advance, remove the vehicles or
weapons immediately in front of it. Once that group starts moving again, the local
blocker has been solved.

### Mission 9 — The Land Battleship Blockade

Firing at the Land Battleship as though it were one large health bar wastes time.
The operation first needs ground vehicles to expose its mobility system; once the
protected sections open, attack the newly vulnerable components immediately.

The battleship changes the way it moves more than once after that. When bomb trucks,
secondary treads, final containment, or the last propulsion targets are where the
run breaks down, follow the full
[Mission 9 Land Battleship sequence](/ace-combat-8/mission-9-land-battleship-blockade).

### Mission 10 — Over the Rainband

Searching the clouds randomly is inefficient. **The targeting laser tells you where
to go.**

Cloud cover prevents the Tonitra satellite from keeping a clean lock on moving
targets, so airships inside the Rainband provide laser guidance. Jamming makes them
hard to locate until that guidance begins.

When a strike window opens:

- protect the fleet from nearby attackers;
- watch the Spear countdown;
- wait for the visible laser;
- follow the beam back toward its airship.

![A Mission 10 targeting laser revealing a guidance airship in the Rainband.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-10-airship-laser.webp)

Later, **two airships can guide simultaneously**. Both active guidance sources need
to go before the strike reaches the carrier. Clear only enough of the drone screen
to expose the airship, destroy it, then transfer to the other laser instead of
trying to wipe every drone from the sky.

## Missions 11–15: Podarges, Fort Grays, and escape

### Mission 11 — Maximum Payload

Thunderclouds and electronic warfare reduce radar usefulness. Contrails become the
fastest way to pick out the Podarge transports carrying Land Battleship components.

Once you find one, stay on that aircraft long enough to convert propulsion damage
into a kill rather than spreading attacks across several large transports.

The mission mixes jamming, defensive weapons, propulsion components, and later
fighter pressure. When the transports are visible but still escaping, use the
complete
[Mission 11 Podarge route](/ace-combat-8/mission-11-maximum-payload).

### Mission 12

Fort Grays defense changes sharply once C-130s begin dropping cruise-missile
pallets.

Each attack can be stopped before or after launch:

- destroy the pallet before the missiles leave it;
- or intercept the missiles once they are airborne.

The first option gives you more room. Once a missile is already moving toward the
base, however, everything unrelated can wait. Distance calls can fall from
**15,000** to **10,000** and then **5,000** while Joker Flight is still closing.

More C-130s arrive from different directions, so remain near Fort Grays after the
first interception instead of letting one fighter pull you far away.

### Mission 13 — The People of the Lost Century

Reconnaissance becomes escort duty once Joker Flight commits to the resistance
transport.

Fly slightly ahead of its route to clear the next AA position, vehicle group, or
portable launcher, then come back toward the transport instead of continuing deep
into hostile territory.

A fuel leak raises the pressure later. Clear the landing zone before the transport
arrives and remain close enough to react if another launcher appears during the
final approach. Losing the transport ends the mission.

### Mission 14 — Two on the Run

You cannot fight your way through this escape: the aircraft is **unarmed**.

Stay low inside the canyon and use terrain to break radar and quadcopter coverage.
Detection does not instantly fail the mission, but identified aircraft bring
fighters into a chase while fuel makes wide detours increasingly expensive.

Later, the **mushroom cloud** can be used as concealment. Dive through it to disrupt
visibility and missile tracking, then return to terrain masking once you emerge.

### Mission 15 — Wings of Wax

Heavy bombers pose the greatest danger to the fleet.

Stay between their routes and the allied ships, especially when the operation
warns that another lost vessel could force an abort. Fighters matter when they
stop you making a bomber pass; otherwise, keep the large aircraft ahead of them in
your priority order.

When exposed engines, weapon bays, or other vulnerable components appear, work
those parts instead of scattering missiles across nearby escorts. Break away after
a successful attack rather than sitting behind a damaged bomber inside its blast
zone.

## Missions 16–20: Disguised ships, rockets, and the Space Elevator

### Mission 16 — Singer

There are **three terrorist container ships** hidden among civilian traffic. Fire
only after confirming **three cranes on deck plus a sun emblem on the hull**.

If the search runs long enough for the EXPLOSIVE UAVs to launch, surface
identification immediately stops being your priority; turn upward and intercept
the drones before they reach their target altitude.

![A disguised Mission 16 container ship after the cranes and emblem match is confirmed.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-16-disguised-ship-sun-emblem-three-cranes.webp)

The full identification rules, launch timer, **40,000 ft** UAV climb, and Currus
condition are in the
[Mission 16 Singer route](/ace-combat-8/mission-16-singer).

### Mission 17 — Land Battleship No. 3

Prepare the bomber corridor before trying to damage the Land Battleship itself.

SAMs, radar sites, and anti-air positions threatening the friendly strike force
come first. The planned attack depends on those bombers reaching their release
point, so damaging the battleship early is less useful than keeping the route open.

Its rail gun remains dangerous after much of the local defense is gone. Avoid long,
predictable lines while waiting for the bomber strike, then follow the newly
exposed targets once the attack changes the battleship state.

### Mission 18 — Moonlight and Shadows

The key restriction is unusual: **do not destroy the rockets on the ground or at
low altitude**.

Cross the Rainband, respond when Shadow Squadron hits Queen Flight, and head south
when the operation returns Joker Flight to the launch objective. After both rockets
lift off, destroy them before they climb beyond the ceiling. Their falling debris
becomes one final target afterward.

For the complete Shadow interruption, two-rocket ascent, ceiling calls, falling
wreckage, and Curis requirement, use
[Mission 18: Moonlight and Shadows](/ace-combat-8/mission-18-moonlight-and-shadows).

### Mission 19 — Clearing the Lighthouse

The second Land Battleship sits beside the Space Elevator, so this fight has a
collateral-damage constraint that the earlier battles do not.

Clear threats blocking the friendly bomber's approach and let the planned strike
open the next stage. As new components become vulnerable, follow the marked
weapons rather than firing indiscriminately through the elevator area.

Main guns and launch systems threatening the Space Elevator or allied forces remain
more important than convenient hull sections.

### Mission 20 — Escort Orbiter

The shuttle transport draws plenty of fighters, but the dangerous interruption is
the **large missile**.

![A large Mission 20 missile closing on the shuttle transport.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-20-escort-orbiter-large-missile.webp)

It uses **image recognition**, so chaff and flares will not make it abandon the
transport.

When the warning appears, leave the current dogfight, turn toward the missile, and
destroy it directly. Stay close enough to the shuttle that another launch cannot
begin while you are several kilometers away.

Later, other aircraft take over escort duty. Once that handoff occurs, stop circling
the transport and deal with the remaining air battle.

## Missions 21–25: Launch defense and Rocky Island

### Mission 21 — The Star-Bound Bird

The shuttle and its control center are the assets that cannot be lost.

Enemy aircraft and ships converge on the launch complex while the countdown is
running. Keep whichever threat is moving toward those facilities at the top of the
target list.

Magnolia later provides **ESM support**. Fight within that coverage when heavier
formations arrive, and avoid wasting all special ammunition on the opening wave
when the countdown still has several phases to survive.

The eastern air base also allows resupply and loadout changes. Use it when the next
wave is worth the landing cycle; do not leave the shuttle exposed during an urgent
attack just because the runway is available.

### Mission 22 — Pursuing the HSCA

The HSCA is trying to reach Fatsia's defensive zone.

Stay with the aircraft and attack propulsion components when they become available.
The nearer it gets to the Mega Float, the more dangerous the long-range defenses
become, so maintain lateral movement rather than following one straight pursuit
line.

After the HSCA is neutralized, withdraw instead of lingering under Fatsia's guns.

### Mission 23 — The Destruction of Expo City

Expo City is too large to clear by simply moving clockwise around the map. Friendly
movement is a much better indicator of where Joker Flight is needed.

Land destroyers, rail guns, artillery, and heavy vehicles can stall an assault
group outright. When a unit reports that it cannot advance, clear the weapons in
front of it, then move on once the ground force resumes.

Naval bombardment later creates another blocker from the bay. Silence those ships
before returning inland for the next resistance point.

A return line is available, but use it because ammunition is limiting the next
heavy engagement, not merely because the option exists.

### Mission 24 — The Battle of Rocky Island, Day 1

Alpha, Bravo, and Charlie approach separate beachheads, so their danger levels do
not rise at the same time.

Open by destroying coastal fire threatening the landing craft, then shift toward
whichever assault group reports the most pressure. Once the beachheads hold, follow
the advance inland toward the Turntable and geothermal plant.

The geothermal complex is important because the force needs it as a protected
position before the Tonitra Spear threat arrives. Keep enough ground-attack
capacity for the inland defenses instead of spending everything on ships near the
beach.

### Mission 25 — The Battle of Rocky Island, Day 2

Day 2 splits the assault between eastern and western routes.

Do not permanently attach Joker Flight to one side. Watch which force has stopped,
clear the unit pinning it, and then check the other route. The dangerous side can
change several times during the operation.

A return line is useful during the long ground battle. Guns can handle lighter
targets while missiles and stronger weapons are preserved for land ships and later
defensive positions.

As the assault nears the main objective, stay with the final ground push rather than
peeling away toward aircraft that no longer threaten the advance.

## Missions 26–30: Fleet battle, Fatsia, Sema Island, and the last sorties

### Mission 26 — Crossing Fleets

Mission 26 stacks naval and air threats densely enough that proximity is a poor
targeting rule.

Listen to fleet reports instead:

- intercept bombers and anti-ship threats while allied vessels are exposed;
- clear radar or AA positions when friendly bombers need an approach;
- use the return line before a heavy surface phase if your anti-surface weapons
  are nearly gone.

The battle keeps shifting its center, so clearing one corner of the radar is less
important than responding to whichever part of the fleet is currently taking
damage.

### Mission 27 — Fatsia, The Ocean Fortress

Exterior damage is only the first part of Fatsia.

Support pillars and explosive industrial targets can collapse larger sections of
the Mega Float, but the objective later changes completely: **stop firing when the
surrender order arrives**. After Joker One enters the central structure, exterior
targets no longer matter.

The structural attack, hold-fire transition, central opening, closing shutter, and
fusion reactor are all handled in
[Mission 27: Fatsia](/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fatsia-ocean-fortress).

### Mission 28 — A Thousand Points of Light

Sema Island becomes the center of an extended air-defense battle against repeated
enemy formations.

Bombers should jump ahead of ordinary fighters whenever they are moving toward the
island. TU-160 and other heavy formations can keep advancing while one fighter
tries to lure Joker Flight into a long dogfight.

The sortie is long enough that ammunition becomes part of the mission rather than
a pre-flight decision. When AWACS warns that you are burning through ammunition,
keep fighting until the urgent bomber pressure is under control. Later, the
operation explicitly confirms that **the base is ready to resupply you**.

That is the better moment to turn back: the runway is available, ammunition has
already been consumed heavily, and more formations are still expected. Do not wait
for every missile counter to reach zero before considering the return.

Fresh allied units and enemy reinforcements keep entering after that, including
multiple bomber waves. The final stages bring stronger enemy pilots into the fight,
so preserve enough weapons—or use the base—to avoid reaching them with only guns.

Players repeating this mission specifically for the **16,000,000 / 25,000,000 MRP
milestones** can use the
[Mission 28 MRP farming route](/ace-combat-8/mrp-farm) for the farming-specific
target and resupply decisions.

### Mission 29 — Selene

Moon 11's targeting laser matters as much as the dogfight itself. She is guiding a
Tonitra Spear toward the disabled, nuclear-powered Land Battleship near Theve.

That means merely surviving her high-G turns is not enough. You have to keep pressure
on the aircraft until the guidance threat is stopped.

She can sustain very hard maneuvers, so a brief missile lock is not always a good
shot. Preserve speed, stay inside the fight, and fire when her path becomes more
predictable. Wingman pressure can force those openings from another angle.

Cloud cover may break your lock completely. Reposition and reacquire instead of
throwing missiles into a solution that has already disappeared.

### Mission 30 — Song of Wings

The submarine begins underwater, so searching for its hull wastes the opening.
Protect the Endurance from incoming anti-ship missiles until the operation creates
a way to counterattack.

The fight then moves through guidance UAVs, the Rainband, a redirected airship
laser, another guidance battle, and finally the surfaced submarine.

After the visible VLS weapons are gone, an **underwater launch tube** appears as
another target. Destroying it still does not end the mission; the final threat is
the submarine turning toward the Endurance for a ramming attack.

When the guidance order, hidden launch tube, or final collision phase is unclear,
follow the complete
[Mission 30: Song of Wings sequence](/ace-combat-8/mission-30-song-of-wings).

## What should you do after Mission 30?

Once the first campaign clear is finished, there is no reason to replay missions
randomly.

Named aircraft and their mission-specific appearance conditions belong to the
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records). If currency is the
remaining bottleneck after your other replays, move to the
[MRP farming route](/ace-combat-8/mrp-farm). For the full set of ACE ranks, medals,
aircraft purchases, kill counters, and other remaining requirements, check the
[Ace Combat 8 trophy and achievement list](/ace-combat-8/trophies-achievements).

Pick the next sortie from what is still incomplete. A missing named Ace needs its
specific mission and trigger; an unfinished MRP total benefits from a productive
repeatable fight; an S-rank gap needs a clean rank attempt rather than another
general-purpose farming run.
