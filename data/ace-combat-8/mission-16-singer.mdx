Mission 16 turns a crowded shipping lane into an identification test. The radar is
not enough: the three targets are hiding among civilian vessels, and firing at the
wrong ship is exactly what the operation tells you not to do.

There are **3 disguised terrorist ships** in total. All three use the same visual
combination — a container ship with **three cranes on its deck** and a **sun emblem
on the hull**. Once that combination is confirmed, the vessel is safe to attack.

The search also has a clock behind it. The terrorists need **less than 10 minutes**
to prepare their self-destruct UAVs, so spending several passes checking the same
ship or chasing unrelated fighters can turn the second half of the mission into an
altitude race.

<section id="identify-disguised-ships" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How to identify the 3 disguised ships

Ignore ship color and general silhouette. What matters is finding **both identifying
features on the same vessel**:

- **3 cranes on the deck**
- **a sun emblem on the hull**

![The Mission 16 disguised container ship with three cranes and a sun emblem.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-16-disguised-ship-sun-emblem-three-cranes.webp)

A vessel matching only one feature is not enough reason to fire. Get a broad enough
view to read the deck layout and hull marking together, then attack once the game
confirms the ship.

The blockade makes the search easier than it first appears. Maritime traffic has
been ordered to stop, so the terrorist ships are not racing across the map while
you inspect them. Use that stationary layout to make deliberate passes rather than
circling tightly around one contact.

The count is fixed at **three**. After sinking one confirmed ship, move on
immediately; after the second, there is only one left to locate.

</section>

<section id="avoid-civilian-ships" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Do not attack unconfirmed civilian vessels

Mission 16 is unusually strict about target identification. The instruction is not
to thin out suspicious shipping and correct mistakes later — civilian vessels are
off limits.

That changes how I would use missiles here. A lock is not the same thing as positive
identification, so keep the weapon safe until you can see the crane-and-emblem
combination.

The briefing treats unmarked vessels as already checked traffic. That means there
is no benefit to testing them with a shot, and there is no hidden requirement to
clear every container ship in the area.

Enemy aircraft can appear during the search as well. Early on, they are not the
main objective. Deal with a fighter when it is actively disrupting an identification
pass, but do not let a bandit pull you several kilometers away while the UAV timer
continues running.

The useful rhythm is:

1. inspect a stationary container ship;
2. confirm **three cranes + sun emblem**;
3. sink it;
4. break away toward the next cluster of shipping.

That is faster and safer than trying to clean the airspace before searching the
water.

</section>

<section id="uav-timer" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What happens if you do not sink all 3 ships in time?

The ships are assembling self-destruct UAVs while Joker Flight searches.

The first warning gives you **less than 10 minutes**. The operation then calls out
the remaining preparation time — around **9 minutes**, **8 minutes**, and later
**7 minutes** — as the search continues.

Those calls are not a score timer. They tell you how long remains before the mission
changes state.

If the terrorists finish the launch sequence, do not keep looking for the last ship
as though nothing changed. The objective switches to the airborne threat.

![EXPLOSIVE UAVs entering the battle after Mission 16 confirms the launch.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-16-currus-uav-launch.webp)

The HUD labels the new targets **EXPLOSIVE UAV**, and the pictured target is worth
**+750 score**. More importantly, the drones are now moving toward the Space
Elevator, so horizontal search time turns into vertical interception time.

</section>

<section id="stop-explosive-uavs" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Destroy the EXPLOSIVE UAVs before they reach 40,000 ft

The launch does not instantly fail the mission. It creates a second chance.

The UAVs climb toward **40,000 ft**. Once that objective begins, point the aircraft
upward and start reducing the drone count instead of fighting whatever happens to
be closest on radar.

The altitude calls show how quickly the window closes:

- a UAV can be reported at **20,000 ft**;
- **30,000 ft** follows later;
- **40,000 ft** is the critical mark they are trying to reach.

The drones do not fly like normal fighters. They climb on irregular paths and are
not trying to win a turning fight, so chasing one from directly behind for too long
can waste the advantage of your next target already crossing overhead.

Work from the highest or most advanced UAV back through the group. When one target
is nearly at 30,000 ft, finishing a lower drone first only gives the leading one
more room to escape.

The game helps by reporting the remaining count. During the attack you can hear
calls such as **5 UAVs left**, then **3 more**, and finally **1 more UAV**. Keep
climbing and switching targets until the operation explicitly confirms that all
drones are destroyed.

Once that call arrives, you have stopped the attack. There is no additional hidden
UAV wave to search for afterward.

</section>

<section id="currus" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why would you deliberately allow the UAV launch?

Only do this on a replay when you are collecting **Currus**.

A clean Mission 16 clear rewards finding the terrorist ships quickly enough to
reduce or avoid the UAV problem. Currus reverses that priority: its condition
requires the **UAV launch to begin**.

That means an Assault Record run should allow the mission to reach the state shown
above, then deal with Currus and the remaining combat normally. The full named-Ace
requirements and completion rules are in the
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records).

For a normal campaign clear, there is no advantage in waiting around for the
terrorists to finish assembling drones. Find the three ships as quickly as possible.

</section>

<section id="mission-16-clear" className="scroll-mt-24">

## When is Mission 16 actually finished?

The operation has two possible workloads, depending on how quickly you identify the
ships.

A fast search can end the terrorist threat before the UAV phase becomes the main
fight. Once a launch begins, however, the mission is no longer about finding ships;
every surviving EXPLOSIVE UAV becomes the immediate danger.

Do not mistake the destruction of the last disguised vessel for completion while
UAVs are already airborne. The decisive confirmation is the call that **all drones
have been destroyed** and the Space Elevator is safe.

After that, the mission closes without asking you to clear unrelated fighters or
continue checking civilian traffic. Save the remaining ammunition and follow the
return order.

</section>
