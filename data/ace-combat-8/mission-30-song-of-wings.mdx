Mission 30 never gives Joker Flight one boss target to attack from beginning to
end. The submarine spends much of the sortie underwater while the Endurance is
already taking fire, and the last Tonitra Spear turns the middle of the battle
into a contest over terminal guidance.

The safest way to read Song of Wings is to follow the threat that can kill the
carrier next. Early on that means anti-ship missiles. Later it becomes
laser-guidance UAVs, exposed submarine weapons, a hidden launch tube, and finally
the submarine's hull itself.

<section id="protect-endurance" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Protect the Endurance while the submarine stays underwater

The opening gives Joker Flight no useful reason to hunt the submarine directly.
It is submerged, but its missiles are already closing on the fleet.

Some warnings put an incoming weapon only **3,000 away** from the Endurance. At
that distance, finish the missile before turning toward fighters or the
submarine's last known position. Another launch can already be in flight while
you are dealing with the first one, so staying close to the carrier saves more
time than chasing the launch point.

The Endurance can intercept part of the attack itself, but the ship is already in
poor condition and cannot cover every approach. Keep enough distance to maneuver,
but not so much that every missile interception becomes a long return flight.

### Destroy the first laser-guidance UAV wave

The battle changes when the remaining Tonitra Spear projectile enters the
operation.

Because the Endurance is moving, the strike needs terminal guidance. The
submarine supplies that guidance with UAVs.

![Laser-guidance UAVs targeting the Endurance during Mission 30.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-laser-guidance-uavs.webp)

These drones are not just another group of fighters. Remove the
**laser-guidance UAVs** that are keeping the Spear locked onto the carrier.

Anti-ship missiles can overlap with this phase. A missile already seconds from
impact takes priority; otherwise, clear the guidance UAVs before they maintain
the strike solution long enough to finish the attack.

After the first UAV wave is gone, the submarine still does not surface. The
Endurance turns toward the **Rainband**, and underwater launches continue during
the move, including swarm missiles that split into multiple smaller threats.

Stay with the carrier until the fleet reaches the weather instead of flying ahead
toward the next objective.

</section>

<section id="redirect-tonitra-spear" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Let the Rainband airship take control of the Tonitra Spear

Another laser begins tracking the Endurance inside the Rainband, but this one
comes from an **airship**.

The crew realizes the targeting system can be taken over. Joker Flight does not
enter a passcode or interact with the airship directly; keep the carrier alive
while the takeover happens.

Once access succeeds, the laser changes targets:

![The Rainband airship redirects its laser from the Endurance to the submarine.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-airship-laser-retarget-submarine.webp)

The Tonitra Spear can now be guided back toward the submarine.

That does not make the carrier safe yet. The submarine immediately launches
another group of laser-guidance UAVs and tries to take control of the strike
again.

The second guidance fight starts with roughly **five minutes** remaining. During
that window, destroy every hostile guidance UAV while continuing to intercept any
missile already threatening the Endurance.

Do not leave one drone alive simply because the friendly laser is already
pointing at the submarine. The mission counts the hostile group down and even
calls out when only **one laser-guidance UAV remains**.

Finish that last UAV. Once no hostile guidance source remains, the Rainband
airship controls the strike and the Spear can complete the counterattack.

Only after this sequence does the submarine become a direct combat target.

</section>

<section id="dismantle-submarine" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Remove the submarine's weapons when it surfaces

The Endurance is barely moving by this point. Its reported speed drops to only
**1 knot**, so another successful anti-ship attack is much harder for the carrier
to escape.

Do not treat the surfaced submarine as one large hull-health target.

Its active weapon systems still determine how much pressure the Endurance is
under. VLS launchers can continue feeding missiles into the fight, while AA guns
can make your own attack path unsafe. Remove the systems that are currently
interfering with the operation, then add hull damage whenever the next pass gives
you a clean opportunity.

The submarine can dive again after taking damage. When it disappears, clear
missiles and UAVs that are already airborne and stay near the Endurance. Circling
above the last surface position does not force it back up any faster.

### The 20%, 50%, and 80% calls are not hull health

During the weapon-stripping phase, the operation reports progress such as:

- **20% of armaments destroyed**
- **50% of armaments destroyed**
- **80% of armaments destroyed**

Those percentages refer to the submarine's **armament**, not the condition of the
entire hull.

That is why a heavily damaged vessel can keep fighting while the percentage is
still below 100. Continue working through the marked weapon systems rather than
assuming direct hull hits should end the battle.

AA guns are worth taking out when they repeatedly force you off a VLS approach.
Otherwise, keep the launchers ahead of minor defensive targets in your priority
order.

Eventually the operation confirms that every visible VLS has been neutralized.

The submarine still has one more weapon.

Players returning to Mission 30 for a named-aircraft requirement should check the
[Assault Records conditions](/ace-combat-8/assault-records) before the sortie.
That objective changes what must be accomplished during the submarine's missile
attacks, while the normal Mission 30 clear still follows the sequence here.

</section>

<section id="underwater-launch-tube" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Destroy the underwater missile launch tube

After the visible VLS launchers are gone, another anti-ship missile can still
appear from below the water.

Its source is an **underwater missile launch tube** extending from the submarine.

![The hidden underwater missile launch tube becomes the next Mission 30 target.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-submarine-missile-launch-tube.webp)

Attack the tube as soon as the marker appears.

Destroying it removes the submarine's remaining underwater missile capability and
damages the vessel internally. The operation then confirms that **all submarine
armaments have been destroyed**.

Any weapons launched before the tube was destroyed remain dangerous. Clear those
missiles before assuming the Endurance is safe.

Once its weapon systems are gone, the submarine changes tactics completely.

</section>

<section id="final-ram" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Forget the components and attack the hull during the final ram

The disarmed submarine dives, changes course, and comes back toward the Endurance
as a massive ramming weapon.

![The submarine begins its final ramming run toward the Endurance.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-submarine-ramming.webp)

There are no more launchers to dismantle. From this point onward, attack the
**hull**.

The submarine weighs tens of thousands of tons, so removing another external
component would not eliminate the momentum carrying it toward the carrier. Joker
Flight needs enough direct hull damage to cause flooding and sink it before the
collision becomes catastrophic.

This is where all remaining ammunition belongs. Fire missiles, use whatever
special weapons are left, and keep the gun active while the other weapons reload.

A heavy hit is not necessarily the finish. The submarine can continue its run
even after the crew reports major damage, so keep firing until its flooding
finally overtakes its forward movement.

Meanwhile, the Endurance's crew is trying to gain every possible meter. Equipment,
damaged aircraft, fuel, drinking water, and ballast are dumped to reduce weight,
but Joker Flight still has to stop the submarine itself.

The final contact is extremely close. The submarine passes below periscope depth
and its sail scrapes the underside of the carrier rather than delivering a
full-mass collision. The Endurance remains afloat, although it cannot navigate
normally afterward, and the crew reports **very few casualties**.

The submarine is destroyed, ending the final combat operation.

</section>

<section id="after-song-of-wings" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What happens after Mission 30?

With the submarine gone, the campaign moves into its epilogue rather than another
combat sortie. The Endurance survives with the evidence recovered during the
operation while the submarine's wreckage sinks.

At this point, mission progression is finished. Return to the
[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) if you need to revisit an
earlier operation.

For ACE ranks, Assault Records, MRP, aircraft purchases, medals, and the remaining
long counters, use the
[Ace Combat 8 trophy and achievement checklist](/ace-combat-8/trophies-achievements)
to decide which requirement still needs another run.

</section>
