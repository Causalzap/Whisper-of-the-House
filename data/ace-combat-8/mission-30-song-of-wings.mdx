Song of Wings does not begin with a conventional boss target. The submarine
remains underwater while the Endurance is already struggling to survive, and the
last Tonitra Spear turns the middle of the sortie into a fight over who controls
the weapon's terminal guidance.

The mission keeps changing what can actually kill the carrier next. Sometimes
that is an anti-ship missile already closing on the fleet; sometimes it is a
laser-guidance UAV; later it becomes a VLS launcher, a hidden underwater tube,
and finally the submarine itself. Following those changes is more important than
trying to damage the same target from start to finish.

<section id="protect-endurance" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Protect the Endurance while the submarine stays underwater

The opening phase gives Joker Flight very little reason to hunt the submarine
directly. It is submerged, but its anti-ship missiles are already on the way,
and the Endurance cannot intercept every incoming weapon by itself.

Some missile calls put the threat only **3,000 away** from the fleet. At that
distance, finish the incoming missile before turning toward anything else. A new
volley can already be moving while the previous one is being destroyed, so
staying near the carrier matters more than flying toward the suspected launch
point.

### Do not chase the submarine before it becomes attackable

The submarine's position is not the useful problem yet. If it dives or remains
hidden, keep Joker Flight close enough to reverse course onto another missile
instead of orbiting its last known location.

The carrier's own defenses can remove some threats, but the Endurance is already
damaged and cannot cover every angle. Your best position is close enough to
intercept a missile without spending most of its remaining flight time just
getting back to the fleet.

### The first Tonitra Spear wave uses terminal-guidance UAVs

The battle changes when the remaining Tonitra Spear projectile is detected near
the Endurance's route. Because the carrier is moving, the strike needs terminal
guidance, and the submarine launches UAVs to provide it.

![Laser-guidance UAVs targeting the Endurance during Mission 30.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-laser-guidance-uavs.webp)

These are not ordinary combat drones. Destroy the **laser-guidance UAVs** that
are keeping the Spear locked onto the Endurance.

Anti-ship missiles can still overlap with this phase. If one is already seconds
from impact, intercept it first; otherwise, clear the guidance drones before
they can maintain the strike solution.

Destroying the first guidance wave does not expose the submarine. The Endurance
turns toward the **Rainband**, and Joker Flight has to keep defending it on the
way.

### Stay with the carrier during the Rainband approach

The move toward the Rainband can look like a transition between fights, but
underwater attacks continue. The submarine can launch additional anti-ship
weapons, including a **swarm missile** that separates into multiple smaller
threats.

Do not race ahead into the weather and leave the Endurance behind. Stay close
enough to react to another missile while the fleet completes the approach.

</section>

<section id="redirect-tonitra-spear" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Turn the Tonitra Spear back on the submarine

Inside the Rainband, another laser begins tracking the Endurance. This time the
source is an **airship**, and the crew realizes that its targeting system can be
taken over.

The Endurance handles the access and passcode work. Joker Flight does not need
to enter anything manually; your job is still keeping the carrier alive while
the takeover finishes.

Once control succeeds, the airship laser is redirected away from the Endurance
and onto the submarine.

![The Rainband airship laser is redirected toward the submarine in Mission 30.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-airship-laser-retarget-submarine.webp)

That creates the first real counterattack opportunity: the incoming Tonitra Spear
can now be guided onto the submarine.

### Destroy every hostile guidance UAV in the second wave

The submarine immediately tries to take control of the strike back by launching
another group of laser-guidance UAVs.

This second wave is more dangerous because the friendly airship is already
pointing its laser at the submarine. It is easy to assume that the Spear is now
safe, but hostile guidance takes priority while any of those UAVs remain active.

The countdown begins at roughly **five minutes**. During that window:

- keep the Endurance alive against missiles already in flight;
- destroy every hostile laser-guidance UAV;
- do not leave one drone alive because the friendly laser is already active.

The mission tracks the wave closely enough to warn when only **one
laser-guidance UAV remains**. Finish it. Once the hostile guidance is gone, the
airship becomes the only active source and the Spear can complete the
counterattack.

Only after this sequence does the submarine finally become a direct combat
target.

</section>

<section id="dismantle-submarine" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Dismantle the submarine when it surfaces

By the time the submarine comes up, the Endurance is in extremely poor
condition. Its reported speed drops to only **1 knot**, leaving very little room
for another anti-ship attack to get through.

This phase is not just a hull-damage race. The submarine still has active
weapons, and removing them is what reduces the pressure on the carrier while
Joker Flight gradually damages the vessel itself.

### Attack active weapons before making long hull passes

When weapon systems are marked, deal with the ones that can still threaten the
Endurance. AA guns can make the approach dangerous, while VLS launchers continue
feeding missiles into an already fragile fleet.

Hull damage is still useful whenever a clean opening appears, but do not spend a
full pass attacking the center of the submarine while an active launcher is
still firing at the carrier.

The submarine can dive again after taking damage. When that happens, do not fly
circles above the last surface position. Clear missiles and UAVs already in the
air, protect the Endurance, and wait for the next surface opportunity.

### The 20%, 50%, and 80% calls are armament progress

As the submarine loses weapons, the mission reports milestones such as:

- **20% of armaments destroyed**;
- **50% destroyed**;
- **80% destroyed**.

Those numbers are not hull health.

They describe how much of the submarine's weapon system has been removed. That
is why the fight can continue even after Joker Flight has already landed heavy
direct damage on the vessel.

### Work through the AA guns and VLS launchers

Later surface cycles expose more of the submarine's remaining armament. Remove
AA guns when they are making your attack line unsafe, then continue destroying
the VLS targets.

The key call is **every VLS successfully neutralized**.

That sounds final, but the submarine still has one weapon hidden below the
waterline.

</section>

<section id="underwater-launch-tube" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Destroy the underwater missile launch tube

After every visible VLS has been neutralized, another anti-ship missile can still
appear from the water.

The source is an **underwater missile launch tube** extending into the
submarine's hull.

![The underwater missile launch tube becomes the final weapon target in Mission 30.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-submarine-missile-launch-tube.webp)

Target it as soon as it appears.

Destroying the tube stops the submarine from launching more underwater missiles
and also damages the interior of the vessel. Once it is gone, the operation
confirms that **all submarine armaments have been destroyed**.

Any missiles already launched still have to be dealt with. Clear those before
assuming the Endurance is safe.

The submarine then abandons weapons entirely and turns itself into the final
threat.

</section>

<section id="final-ram" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Sink the submarine before it rams the Endurance

With its armament gone, the submarine dives, changes course, and comes back up
aimed directly at the carrier.

![The submarine begins its final ramming attempt against the Endurance.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-30-submarine-ramming.webp)

From this point onward, stop looking for components.

Attack the **hull**.

The submarine weighs tens of thousands of tons, so knocking off another external
part will not remove its momentum. Joker Flight has to damage the hull badly
enough for flooding to sink the vessel before it reaches the Endurance.

This is the right time to spend everything you have left. Fire missiles, use
remaining special weapons, and keep the gun working while other weapons reload.
There is no later combat phase that rewards saving ammunition.

### Keep firing after the first heavy hit

The ramming sequence can create one last false finish. A major strike may land
and the submarine may still continue forward.

That does not mean the attack failed.

The crew can report that the vessel is **not stopping** even after substantial
damage. Keep attacking until the submarine begins losing the race against
flooding.

At the same time, the Endurance is trying to gain every possible meter. The crew
dumps equipment, damaged aircraft, fuel, drinking water, and ballast to reduce
weight while the submarine closes.

Joker Flight still has to provide the stopping force.

### Does the submarine hit the Endurance?

The catastrophic collision is avoided.

During the closest pass, the submarine is below periscope depth and its sail
only scrapes the underside of the carrier instead of striking it with the full
mass of the vessel. The Endurance remains afloat, although it can no longer
navigate normally after the battle, and the crew reports **very few
casualties**.

The submarine is confirmed destroyed, ending the final combat operation.

</section>

## What happens after Song of Wings?

With the submarine gone, the fighting finally ends. The Endurance survives with
the evidence from the operation, while the submarine's wreckage sinks and the
campaign moves into its epilogue rather than another combat sortie.
