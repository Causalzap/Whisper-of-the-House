Mission 11 puts Joker Flight into a strange kind of interception. The targets are
enormous — each Podarge is about **267 meters long**, spans roughly **967 meters**,
uses **eight turboprop engines**, and can carry up to **36,000 tons** — yet the
thunderclouds and electronic warfare can make the transports surprisingly hard
to locate.

They are carrying disassembled Land Battleship components toward Rocky Island.
The operation therefore depends on destroying **every transport** before its
cargo escapes. Fighters, jammers, defensive guns, and the later 55th Unit make
that harder, but none of them replaces the Podarges as the main objective while
a transport is still airborne.

<section id="prepare-for-interception" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Prepare for the transport interception

Maximum Payload is overwhelmingly an air battle, so the useful resource is
air-to-air ammunition. The fleet is already short on missiles before takeoff,
and Joker Flight receives the remaining air-to-air supply because the transport
interception cannot be allowed to fail.

Anti-surface equipment offers little here. Your ammunition has to cover several
large transports, their defensive systems, nearby escorts, electronic-warfare
support, and the stronger fighters that enter later.

### What loadout works best for Mission 11?

There is no need to build the entire sortie around one particular special
weapon. A Podarge can absorb far more punishment than an ordinary aircraft, so
having enough usable air-to-air firepower for repeated passes is more important
than relying on one high-damage attack.

Keep enough ammunition to:

- make several passes against each transport;
- remove an escort that is blocking your approach;
- knock out defensive weapons when they cover the wing you need;
- continue fighting after the stronger enemy unit arrives.

Do not spend the opening minutes trying to erase every fighter from the radar.
If an aircraft is not preventing the interception, the missiles used on it may
be more valuable against a Podarge still moving west.

## Find the Podarges through the jamming

The first real obstacle is not damage output. It is finding the formation.

Thunderclouds cover the operation area while enemy electronic-warfare aircraft
reduce both radar and lock range. Searching by instruments alone can leave you
circling through apparently empty air while the transports continue toward the
edge of the combat zone.

The better clue is visible outside the cockpit.

![Large contrails reveal the Podarge transports through the jamming in Mission 11.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-11-podarge-contrails.webp)

The Podarges leave huge contrails through the cloud layer. Once you find one of
those trails, follow it until the transport comes into view. Closing the distance
also restores the shorter lock opportunities that the jamming has taken away.

### Should you destroy the EW aircraft first?

An EW aircraft is worth removing when it is already close to your attack line,
especially if the interference is making the next pass difficult. It is not
worth spending several minutes hunting one while a transport continues west.

The Podarge reaching the boundary is the larger danger. If the jammer is
somewhere else and the transport is already in front of you, stay with the
transport.

### Stay on the Podarges while Queen Flight is under pressure

Queen Flight is fighting hard elsewhere during the interception, and the radio
traffic can make breaking away sound tempting. That pressure is part of the
larger operation: other forces are buying Joker Flight enough time to stop the
cargo aircraft.

The same logic applies to escorts. Destroy a fighter when it is sitting on your
tail, repeatedly forcing evasive turns, or directly blocking an attack run.
Once that immediate problem is gone, return to the Podarge.

A nearby fighter is not automatically more urgent than a transport that is still
making progress toward Rocky Island.

## Bring down each Podarge

The transport can take damage across its airframe, but the propulsion system
gives you the clearest control over how the fight develops.

Engines, propellers, wing sections, and defensive weapons can all be targeted.
Damaging an engine slows the aircraft, while destroying propellers steadily
removes its ability to remain airborne.

![A Podarge propeller becomes a direct attack target during Mission 11.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-11-podarge-propellers.webp)

The wings expose most of the useful propulsion targets. Make a pass along one
side, hit an engine or propeller, extend far enough to turn cleanly, then come
back across another section rather than sitting behind the transport for too
long.

That rear position becomes dangerous because the Podarge is not defenseless.
Its AA weapons can punish a slow chase, while escorts can attack from outside
your field of view as you concentrate on one large target.

### Engines, propellers, or AA guns — what should you attack first?

Use defensive weapons to decide whether your next approach is safe, but use the
propulsion system to bring the aircraft down.

If an AA gun is directly covering the wing section you want, removing it can
make the following passes much easier. Once the approach is open, return to the
engines and propellers instead of dismantling every weapon on the transport.

A useful order is:

1. remove a defensive gun only when it is interfering with your attack line;
2. damage an engine when slowing the transport will buy you more time;
3. keep working through the exposed propellers and other propulsion targets;
4. finish the damaged Podarge before spreading attacks across another healthy one.

Engine damage matters even when it does not immediately destroy the aircraft.
A slower transport gives you more time to turn, reacquire, and finish the next
pass before it reaches the edge of the operation area.

### Finish one transport instead of damaging all of them

Several Podarges can remain active at once, but dividing your attacks too evenly
creates multiple aircraft that are damaged without actually being stopped.

If one transport is already slowed, missing propellers, and falling away from
the formation, stay with it long enough to secure the kill. Moving to a fresh
target too early can leave the damaged aircraft airborne and still capable of
completing its delivery.

Once the mission confirms the kill, turn toward the next surviving transport.

### How do you know a Podarge is actually destroyed?

Smoke alone is not enough. An engine fire is not enough either, and even severe
wing damage can leave the transport flying for a while.

Wait for the mission call confirming that the aircraft is going down and for the
remaining transport count to update. Until that happens, a badly damaged
Podarge that is still holding altitude remains a threat.

The interception only moves on after **all enemy transports have been
destroyed**.

### Stay clear of falling wreckage

A dying Podarge creates a hazard of its own because of the aircraft's enormous
size. Debris can fall through the formation and may even strike another aircraft
below it.

That occasional extra damage is not something to build a strategy around.
Instead, break away once the transport is clearly going down and avoid flying
directly underneath the wreck.

With shortened radar range, cloud cover, escorts, and several giant aircraft
already competing for attention, getting out from beneath the debris gives you a
cleaner route toward the next target.

## Finish the air battle after the transports are gone

The later arrival of the **55th Unit** adds much more dangerous fighters to the
operation and makes it easier to lose sight of why Joker Flight is there.

As long as a Podarge remains active, do not abandon it simply to chase one of
those fighters across the clouds. Deal with an enemy aircraft when it is
actively preventing another transport attack, then return before the cargo
aircraft gains too much distance.

The priority changes once the final Podarge is confirmed destroyed. With no
transport left to escape, Joker Flight can commit fully to the remaining
critical air threat.

### What happens after the last Podarge is destroyed?

Destroying the transport formation prevents the shipment of disassembled Land
Battleship components from reaching Rocky Island. Joker Flight then finishes the
remaining air battle before the operation ends.

The Endurance is preparing to move toward **Fort Grays** for badly needed fuel
and ammunition, but contact with the base has already been lost. That becomes
the next problem facing the fleet.

defense of Fort Grays.
