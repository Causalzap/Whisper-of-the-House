Open **Data Viewer → Campaign Data → Campaign Assault Records** before starting
cleanup. There are **83 records**, but they do not all work the same way.

Most of the list comes from special enemy units encountered naturally during the
campaign. The harder part is a set of **31 named Aces** that only enter the fight
after you satisfy a specific condition. Some ask for fast kills, some require a
particular target to survive, and several are triggered by flying through a
specific route rather than destroying anything.

That distinction matters because replaying a mission and clearing it normally
will not fill an empty conditional slot.

<section id="how-assault-records-work" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Assault Records actually register

The 83 records split into two groups:

- **52 special-unit records** tied mostly to Lux Lunae formations and other
  scripted elite units.
- **31 conditional Aces** that require Veteran difficulty or higher plus a
  mission-specific trigger.

For the conditional group, difficulty is the first check. Casual or lower
difficulty can still move normal campaign progress forward, but the named Ace
you are trying to collect will not appear there.

The second check happens after the kill: **complete the mission**. Destroying the
Ace and then restarting or abandoning the sortie does not give you a safe cleanup
shortcut. Keep enough ammunition and health to finish whatever objective remains
after the named aircraft goes down.

Each conditional Ace also unlocks a paint scheme for its aircraft type, while a
full **83/83** record completes **A Record of Rivalry**.

For most players, the efficient order is to finish the campaign first, then use
Free Mission to target the missing entries. That lets you choose a suitable
aircraft for each trigger instead of forcing the entire collection into one
campaign run.

## All 31 conditional Ace spawn requirements

For each missing entry, satisfy the condition before progressing the mission too
far, then switch priority to the named aircraft when it appears. The sortie still
has to be completed after the kill.

### Missions 01–05

- **Mission 01 — Gigas — Mirage 2000-5:** Destroy every non-TGT enemy aircraft.
- **Mission 02 — Crematio — A-6E Intruder:** Destroy at least 4 aircraft within
  the first 3 minutes.
- **Mission 03 — Raia — F/A-18C Hornet:** Destroy every bomber before they reach
  White Valley Base.
- **Mission 04 — Auster — Gripen E:** Fly into the tunnel.
- **Mission 05 — callsign and aircraft not listed:** Keep every LCAC alive.

### Missions 06–10

- **Mission 06 — Flamma — MiG-31B Foxhound:** Destroy at least 5 TGTs within the
  first 3 minutes.
- **Mission 07 — Orcus — EA-6B Prowler:** Destroy the Land Aegis with a chain
  reaction.
- **Mission 08 — Tessera — MiG-21bis Fishbed:** Destroy every non-TGT surface
  weapon.
- **Mission 09 — Judex — MiG-29A Fulcrum:** Break the Land Battleship's secondary
  tread armor before the second slowdown line.
- **Mission 10 — Vorago — F-15C Eagle:** Destroy an airship within 70 seconds of
  laser targeting beginning.

### Missions 11–15

- **Mission 11 — Larus — F-14D Super Tomcat:** Destroy all Shadows before
  eliminating the Podarge.
- **Mission 12 — Imber — F/A-18F Super Hornet:** Destroy every LCAC within
  3 minutes of takeoff.
- **Mission 13 — Sicarius — Su-47 Berkut:** Destroy at least 5 radar towers
  before the transport lands.
- **Mission 15 — callsign and aircraft not listed:** Destroy one Ladon, then kill
  the Ace before the second Ladon is destroyed.

### Missions 16–20

- **Mission 16 — Currus — F-16C Fighting Falcon:** Allow the UAV launch to begin.
- **Mission 17 — callsign and aircraft not listed:** Destroy all TGTs before the
  B-1B reaches the halfway point.
- **Mission 18 — Curis — F-15J Eagle:** Destroy Shadow 24 near the launch area
  during the launch sequence.
- **Mission 19 — Aleator — F/A-18E Super Hornet:** Destroy every Su-47.
- **Mission 20 — Periodus — Su-35S Flanker-E:** Destroy at least one B-2.

### Missions 21–25

- **Mission 21 — Alienus — EA-18G Growler:** Sink every enemy ship.
- **Mission 22 — Caligo — Rafale M:** Get close to the Celaeno without being
  detected.
- **Mission 23 — Milvus — A-10C Thunderbolt II:** Destroy all Lux Lunae Ace units
  within 3 minutes of their arrival.
- **Mission 23 — Cantrix — FA-36 Game:** Destroy all Lux Lunae Ace units within
  3 minutes of their arrival.
- **Mission 24 — callsign and aircraft not listed:** Get every LCAC safely onto
  land.
- **Mission 25 — Cinis — F-15E Strike Eagle:** Destroy all Land Destroyers and
  the Land Aegis.
- **Mission 25 — Silacrum — EA-36 Barricader:** Fly between the floating Podarges
  and the ocean.

### Missions 26–30

- **Mission 26 — Lucidius — F-2A:** Destroy every enemy surface unit on Cima
  Island.
- **Mission 27 — Stiria — Typhoon:** Fly through a gap between Fatsia's spokes.
- **Mission 28 — Harmonius — F-35C Lightning II:** Destroy 5 bombers within the
  first 5 minutes.
- **Mission 29 — Prudens — Su-57 Felon:** Fly underneath an urban highway bridge.
- **Mission 30 — Tegimen — F-22A Raptor:** Destroy at least 3
  submarine-launched swarm missile dispensers before they release their missiles.

The four entries without an aircraft or callsign still have actionable conditions.
After meeting one of those triggers, scan the new contacts for a named aircraft
before advancing the objective far enough to close that combat phase.

Several of these requirements are easy to miss because the normal mission route
encourages exactly the opposite behavior.

## Do Mission 18 before trying to finish Mission 28

The most important cross-mission dependency begins in
[Mission 18: Moonlight and Shadows](/ace-combat-8/mission-18-moonlight-and-shadows).

Shadow Squadron interrupts the route to Kimona before the rocket sequence. During
the later launch section, **Shadow 24** is the aircraft that matters for Assault
Record cleanup. Destroy it near the launch area before moving on.

That does two things:

1. it allows **Curis**, the F-15J Ace, to enter Mission 18;
2. it satisfies the requirement for the **five-aircraft Lux Lunae 22nd Unit** to
   appear later in Mission 28.

Finish Mission 18 after taking Shadow 24 and Curis down. Only then move to Mission
28 if those five Su-57 records are still blank.

Mission 28 also has its own separate conditional Ace, **Harmonius**. That trigger
is unrelated to Shadow 24: destroy **5 bombers inside the first 5 minutes**.

![TU-160 bombers entering the early Mission 28 battle over Sema Island.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-28-harmonius-tu160-bombers.webp)

Because Mission 28 keeps feeding additional formations into the battle, it is easy
to get distracted by fighters. During a Harmonius attempt, the early bomber count
takes priority until all five required kills are secured.

## Some records require you to stop playing the mission the fastest way

### Mission 16 — Currus only appears after the UAV launch

A clean first run of Singer encourages you to identify and sink all three
terrorist container ships before they can finish assembling their drones. That is
exactly the wrong outcome when you are trying to spawn **Currus**.

For the Assault Record attempt, let the operation progress until the ships begin
launching UAVs. The objective changes to destroying the drones as they climb
toward **40,000 ft**, and Currus can enter during that state.

![Mission 16 UAVs climbing toward 40,000 feet after the launch begins.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-16-currus-uav-launch.webp)

The [Mission 16 Singer walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/mission-16-singer) covers the
three-crane and sun-emblem identification sequence, civilian ships, the launch
timer, and the UAV interception itself. During Assault Record cleanup, the only
difference is that you deliberately allow the launch state to happen before
finishing the fight.

### Mission 9 — Judex is tied to how quickly you expose the Land Battleship

Judex is not unlocked by simply destroying all eight secondary treads. The
secondary tread **armor** has to be broken before the battleship reaches the
second slowdown line.

That puts extra value on keeping the bomb-truck approach intact and reacting
immediately when the side armor becomes vulnerable. The
[Mission 9 Land Battleship walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/mission-9-land-battleship-blockade)
covers that mobility sequence without repeating it here.

### Mission 11 — Larus can disappear behind normal Podarge progress

Maximum Payload gives you a strong reason to concentrate on the transports, but
Larus requires the opposite priority for a short window: **all Shadows must be
destroyed before the Podarge is eliminated**.

Once the condition is satisfied, return to the transport objective. The
[Mission 11 Maximum Payload walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/mission-11-maximum-payload)
has the Podarge component order and jamming route if the rest of the mission is
still giving you trouble.

</section>

## Four Aces are triggered by where you fly

Several records cannot be forced by increasing score or killing faster. The
aircraft only appears after Joker One physically crosses the required route.

- **Auster — Mission 4:** enter the tunnel.
- **Silacrum — Mission 25:** pass between the floating Podarges and the ocean.
- **Stiria — Mission 27:** fly through a gap in Fatsia's radial structure.
- **Prudens — Mission 29:** pass underneath an urban highway bridge.

Fatsia is particularly easy to misunderstand because its eight outer modules
extend from the central terminal like spokes.

![Fatsia's eight radial spokes extending from the central terminal.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fatsia-eight-spokes-layout.webp)

For Stiria, the useful action is the pass through that structure, not destroying
a particular support pillar. Once the named Typhoon appears, leave the structural
attack long enough to secure the kill, then continue the
[Mission 27 Fatsia sequence](/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fatsia-ocean-fortress).

</section>

<section id="timed-triggers" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Prepare the fast-kill conditions before takeoff

The timed records are much easier when you enter the mission already knowing which
targets count.

- **Mission 2:** destroy 4 aircraft in 3 minutes.
- **Mission 6:** destroy 5 TGTs in 3 minutes.
- **Mission 10:** destroy an airship within 70 seconds of laser targeting.
- **Mission 12:** destroy every LCAC within 3 minutes of takeoff.
- **Mission 23:** destroy the Lux Lunae Ace group within 3 minutes of its arrival.
- **Mission 28:** destroy 5 bombers in 5 minutes.

These are poor runs for experimenting with a weapon that does not fit the target
type. Pick the loadout before launch, move directly toward the required enemies,
and postpone harmless optional contacts until the trigger has fired.

Mission 23 is also unusual because **Milvus and Cantrix share the same condition**.
One successful fast clear of the Lux Lunae Ace group can bring both named aircraft
into the same sortie, so keep enough air-to-air ammunition to kill the new arrivals
afterward.

</section>

## Finish each successful run before moving to the next record

The safest cleanup loop is:

1. check the empty slot in Campaign Assault Records;
2. select that mission on Veteran, Elite, or ACE;
3. satisfy the trigger before advancing the objective too far;
4. destroy the named Ace;
5. finish the entire mission;
6. check the record before starting the next replay.

Do **Mission 18 before Mission 28** if the Lux Lunae 22nd Unit is missing. For
Mission 16, remember that preventing every UAV launch is useful for a normal clear
but prevents the Currus trigger. For the terrain-based entries, cross the required
route before spending time cleaning up unrelated targets.

Mission 30's Tegimen is another case where the trigger sits inside a much larger
fight. You need at least **three submarine-launched swarm missile dispensers**
destroyed before their missiles are released; once the Ace is down, the
[Song of Wings walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/mission-30-song-of-wings) covers the
remaining submarine phases.

Assault Record replays also add naturally to your accumulated aircraft kills and
MRP, so there is little reason to stop halfway through the collection just to
grind money. Once the records are complete, the
[MRP farming route](/ace-combat-8/mrp-farm) is the cleaner place to finish the
16,000,000 and 25,000,000 MRP milestones.

When all **83 entries** are filled, **A Record of Rivalry** is complete. The
[Ace Combat 8 trophy and achievement checklist](/ace-combat-8/trophies-achievements)
can then show which ACE ranks, medals, purchases, combat counters, or other
completion goals remain.

</section>
