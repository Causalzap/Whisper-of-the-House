<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Work along one Fatsia arm at a time and use **support pillars** or explosive
industrial targets to build structural damage quickly. Stop firing the moment
command orders **hold fire**. When the objective moves inside the central
structure, hit the **fusion reactor twice** before the shutter closes.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Fatsia looks like a mission where every red icon needs to disappear. It is not.

The Mega Float is built around a central terminal with **8 enormous outer arms**,
each extending roughly **6 kilometers**. Military facilities and weapons occupy
the upper decks; docks, shipyards, fuel storage, factories, and other industrial
targets sit below.

The operation only needs enough structural damage to force Fatsia into the next
stage. That makes route choice much more important than target count. Crossing
from one side of the fortress to another for every convenient icon wastes time
and repeatedly exposes Joker One to the largest guns.

<section id="break-fatsia-exterior" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Break Fatsia by collapsing useful sections

The eight arms form a radial layout around the center:

![Fatsia's eight outer arms extending from the central terminal.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fatsia-eight-spokes-layout.webp)

Pick an arm, work through the valuable targets already on that route, and stay
there while the next pass can still produce meaningful structural damage.

Calls such as **20% destruction** and later **50% destruction** confirm that the
assault is progressing. They are not stopping points. Keep attacking until the
operation explicitly changes state.

The best targets are usually the ones that damage more than themselves.

### Collapse upper decks with support pillars

Support pillars underneath the arms hold large sections of the upper platform
in place. Destroying one can bring the deck above it down and remove several
facilities in the same collapse.

![A support pillar underneath Fatsia's upper deck becomes the attack target.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-27-mega-float-support-pillars.webp)

The approach is the dangerous part. Getting a lock often means dropping beneath
the upper platform, where AA fire and the Mega Float's larger weapons have cleaner
angles on you.

Treat the underside as a pass-through route rather than somewhere to linger.
Line up one exposed pillar, take the shot, continue through the gap, and regain
space before turning around. Trying to collect several supports in one slow
under-deck pass gives the defenses too much time to track you.

When the angle underneath an arm is bad, there is no need to force it.

Fuel tanks and other explosive industrial targets on the lower level can produce
secondary damage across the surrounding structure. A clean run through clustered
explosive targets is often safer and more productive than repeatedly diving at a
pillar covered by defensive fire.

### Remove guns only when they control your attack route

Fatsia has enough weapons that clearing all of them first turns the mission into
a much longer battle.

Main guns and rail-gun positions are worth removing when they are forcing you off
the arm you are trying to break. Once that firing lane is open, go back to
supports, fuel, factories, or other structural targets instead of starting a
complete sweep of every AA icon nearby.

Small defenses become important when they repeatedly ruin the same approach.
Otherwise, structural damage comes first.

There is also no requirement to destroy all **8 arms**. Fatsia can still have
intact modules when command decides enough damage has been done.

Players returning to Mission 27 for the named-aircraft requirement should check
the [Assault Records conditions](/ace-combat-8/assault-records) before the sortie;
that run includes a flight-path condition around Fatsia's structure that is
separate from the normal destruction objective.

</section>

<section id="hold-fire" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Stop firing when command demands surrender

Eventually the damage is high enough for command to demand Fatsia's surrender.

![The hold-fire order appears while Fatsia's surrender is being demanded.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-27-hold-fire-surrender.webp)

As soon as **hold fire** is ordered, stop attacking.

This moment is easy to mishandle because plenty of weapons and buildings remain
targetable. The HUD does not suddenly become empty, and large sections of the
Mega Float may still be intact. None of that means the exterior attack is still
active.

Let the radio exchange play out.

The situation then changes when laser targeting threatens the allied fleet and
the **Tonitra Spear** becomes part of the operation again. Exterior destruction
is no longer the answer. Joker One is redirected toward the central section of
Fatsia.

At that point, leave the outer arms behind.

</section>

<section id="central-entry" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Line up the central opening before entering

The new objective marks an entry point in Fatsia's central structure. Joker One
goes inside alone; the wingmen remain outside.

Do not dive at the opening from a bad angle simply because the marker has
appeared. The interior is much tighter than the exterior battle, and a poor entry
costs more time than one extra setup turn outside.

Approach from far enough away to straighten the aircraft before crossing the
opening. Keep the nose centered and avoid carrying so much speed that the first
interior correction becomes difficult.

The only target that matters inside is the **fusion reactor**.

There is no reason to explore side passages or look for another switch. Your
entry angle should leave enough room not only to hit the reactor once, but also
to turn back toward it.

</section>

<section id="fusion-reactor" className="scroll-mt-24">

## One reactor hit is not enough

The first successful strike does not finish the reactor sequence.

It damages the outer protection, but the operation can still report **no change
in heat signature**. At almost the same time, the large internal shutter begins
closing across the route.

![The fusion reactor remains active while the internal shutter begins to close.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fusion-reactor-shutter.webp)

That heat-signature call does not mean you hit the wrong object.

Use the remaining interior space immediately. Turn Joker One back toward the
reactor and attack it again before the shutter removes the firing line.

The sequence is:

1. enter through the central opening;
2. hit the fusion reactor once;
3. turn back inside the available space;
4. hit the reactor again before the shutter closes;
5. wait for the **high heat-signature** confirmation.

The first pass determines how comfortable the second one will be. Coming through
the entrance badly offset can leave too little room to reverse direction before
the shutter has already covered most of the target.

Once the first hit lands, do not continue deeper into the structure looking for
another objective. The reactor remains the target throughout the closing-shutter
sequence.

Destroying it disrupts the central system involved in the Tonitra Spear operation.
That does not erase projectiles that have already been released, but Fatsia can
no longer continue the sequence normally once its central system is gone.

</section>

<section id="after-fatsia" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Mission 27 ends with the reactor, not the remaining exterior targets

After the reactor is destroyed, there is no reason to return outside and finish
the surviving arms, weapons, factories, or AA positions.

Fatsia's role as the enemy's major floating military and production complex has
been broken, and its central system is no longer supporting the continuing
orbital-weapon threat.

The campaign continues immediately into the final stretch. Mission 28 shifts
away from structural destruction and turns Sema Island into a prolonged
air-defense battle with repeated enemy formations and mid-mission resupply.

[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) for Mission 28.

</section>
