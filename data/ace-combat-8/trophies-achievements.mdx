<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
On the first campaign, complete every **takeoff and aerial refueling** instead of
skipping them, and use the three wingman command types plus Special Weaponry
permission when a safe opportunity appears. Leave **ACE S ranks, all Assault
Records, 25,000,000 MRP, 3,000 aircraft kills, and the full aircraft / weapon /
part collection** for the replays that already send you back through the
campaign.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Steam has **55 achievements** in Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve. PlayStation uses
the same 55 requirements and adds **Wings of Theve** for earning every other
trophy, bringing the PS5 total to **56 trophies**.

Most of that list is less complicated than the number suggests. The Prologue,
all **30 numbered missions**, and the normal campaign clear award their
requirements through ordinary progression. The things worth remembering before
you start are the campaign-long **By the Book** condition, a handful of wingman
and menu actions, and the long replay goals involving ACE difficulty, S ranks,
Assault Records, medals, MRP, aircraft purchases, and cumulative kills.

<section id="first-campaign" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Protect By the Book on the first campaign

The story achievements do not need special routing. Clearing the Prologue and
Missions 01–30 unlocks their corresponding requirements in sequence, and
finishing Campaign Mode awards **Promised Land**.

The one campaign-long condition that is easy to overlook is **By the Book**. It
requires a finished campaign in which every takeoff and aerial refueling was
successfully completed. When either sequence appears, play it through instead
of skipping it. Missing one turns an otherwise simple requirement into another
full campaign attempt.

For the missions themselves, use the
[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) whenever the objective
changes into an escort, identification check, missile interception, timer, or
multi-stage fight. The story trophies do not ask for special ranks on this run,
so getting through each mission cleanly matters more than forcing replay goals
into the first clear.

</section>

<section id="wingman-and-misc" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Clear the small command, viewer, and training requirements early

Several achievements need only one deliberate action. They are easy to remove
while you are already using the campaign menus and wingman system, and none
needs a separate long grind.

**Yes, Sir!** requires wingmen to destroy enemies after all three commands:
**Forward Atk, Dispersed Atk, and Cover**. Use each command when your wingmen
already have suitable targets. **All Weapons Free** asks you to allow Special
Weaponry use and then have a wingman destroy an enemy with a special weapon.

The remaining short requirements come from systems you can trigger directly:
**Aircraft Inspection** needs an aircraft preview in the Aircraft Viewer,
**Birdwatching** needs a replay watched in the Replay Viewer, **Plane
Coordinator** needs a skin or emblem customization, and **Welcome to Boot Camp**
needs one Training Mode mission clear.

**Like the Wind** is the only one in this group that depends on a flight
technique rather than a menu or command. Perform one successful post-stall
maneuver in Campaign Mode when you have enough open space to attempt it without
risking the mission.

</section>

<section id="aircraft-mrp" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Build the aircraft collection while MRP keeps accumulating

The aircraft-related achievements stack into each other. **Bolstered Forces**
requires **5 aircraft types**, and **Fully Kitted Out** raises that count to
**20**. Special weapons and upgrade parts have their own combined milestones:
**15** for **Budding Buyer** and **100** for **Regular Customer**.

The collection endpoint is **Patron of the Parts**, which requires every
aircraft, every special weapon, and every upgrade part in Campaign Mode.
**Birds of a Feather** adds one smaller purchase condition: buy the same aircraft
for your wingmen through the Aircraft Tree.

MRP has two cumulative thresholds beside those purchases:

- **16,000,000 MRP** — Need A Bigger Bank
- **25,000,000 MRP** — MRP Aplenty

There is little reason to force those totals during the first campaign. ACE
runs, S-rank attempts, Assault Record hunting, medal work, and the large kill
counter all require more sorties, so MRP can continue rising while those other
requirements are still unfinished. Spend toward aircraft, special weapons, and
parts you still need rather than treating the currency total as a separate early
race.

</section>

<section id="long-counters" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Stack the kill counters into campaign replays

**Aircraft Annihilator** is the largest combat counter: destroy **3,000
aircraft in Campaign Mode**. That total is large enough that progress from later
runs matters more than trying to grind it immediately after the first ending.

Two 100-kill requirements can advance at the same time:

- **Air-to-Air Ace** — destroy **100 enemy units** with air-to-air special
  weapons in Campaign Mode.
- **Anti-Ground Ace** — destroy **100 enemy units** with anti-ground special
  weapons in Campaign Mode.

Use air-to-air special weapons when a replay already contains dense fighter
waves and anti-ground weapons during surface-heavy operations. The same sorties
can also produce MRP, mission-rank attempts, medals, or other unfinished
requirements, so a useful replay can move several counters without changing its
main purpose.

</section>

<section id="records-medals" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Track Assault Records and medals separately from mission clears

**Meritorious Service** requires **10 medals**, while **A Record of Rivalry**
requires **every Assault Record**. Neither condition is completed merely by
finishing all 30 story missions.

Keep both counts visible during replays and use the in-game records to confirm
what is still missing. A mission clear can still be useful for MRP, kills, or a
rank attempt even when it does not add a medal or Assault Record, but those two
collections should not be assumed complete just because the campaign has been
finished several times.

When an Assault Record is still missing, return to the mission tied to that
record rather than choosing a random farming sortie. When medals remain, check
which medal conditions your save has not completed before deciding what the next
run needs to accomplish.

</section>

<section id="ace-and-s-ranks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Treat the ACE clear and all-S-rank run as two different goals

**The Ace** requires a full Campaign Mode completion on **ACE difficulty**.
**Ace of Aces** goes much further: earn an **S rank on ACE difficulty in every
campaign mission, including Free Missions**.

Those requirements do not need to be completed on the same pass. An ACE clear
can be used to learn the harder enemy pressure, tighter resource demands, and
mission transitions without restarting every time a sortie finishes below S.
Afterward, replay only the missions that still lack the required rank.

The multi-stage missions are especially worth relearning before turning them
into rank attempts. In Mission 9, the
[Land Battleship fight](/ace-combat-8/mission-9-land-battleship-blockade)
moves from bomb trucks to secondary treads, containment, and rocket thrusters.
Mission 11's
[Maximum Payload battle](/ace-combat-8/mission-11-maximum-payload)
depends on finding and dismantling the Podarge transports before they escape.
Mission 27's
[Fatsia fight](/ace-combat-8/mission-27-fatsia-ocean-fortress)
changes from structural destruction to a hold-fire pause and an internal reactor
attack. Mission 30's
[Song of Wings battle](/ace-combat-8/mission-30-song-of-wings)
moves between Endurance defense, laser guidance, submarine armaments, the hidden
launch tube, and the final ram.

Knowing when those missions change state prevents an otherwise strong rank
attempt from being lost to the wrong target.

</section>

<section id="final-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Choose the next sortie by what is still unfinished

By the time ACE and S-rank work is underway, the story rewards and most
one-action requirements should already be complete. What remains will usually
come from some combination of **Assault Records, 10 medals, 25,000,000 MRP,
3,000 aircraft kills, the two special-weapon counters, aircraft / weapon / part
collection, and any missing S ranks**.
