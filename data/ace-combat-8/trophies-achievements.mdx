<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Ace Combat 8 has **55 Steam achievements** and **56 PS5 trophies** including the
Platinum. Most story requirements unlock naturally while clearing the campaign.
The condition I would protect from the first run is **By the Book**: complete every
takeoff and aerial refueling instead of skipping them. Leave ACE S ranks, Assault
Records, MRP, aircraft collection, and the long kill counters for cleanup after the
story.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Steam has 55 achievements in Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve. PlayStation uses
the same 55 requirements and adds the Wings of Theve Platinum for earning all
other trophies, bringing the PS5 total to 56 trophies.
The list is longer than the number of genuinely difficult tasks. Clearing the
Prologue, Missions 01–30, and Campaign Mode handles the story achievements through
normal progression. The requirements that deserve planning are By the Book,
the wingman-command achievements, The Ace, Ace of Aces, Assault Records,
10 medals, the MRP and aircraft collection milestones, and the long combat
counters.

<section id="completion-roadmap" className="scroll-mt-24">

Best order for 100% completion
I would not try to force every requirement into the first campaign. A cleaner route
is to let the story unlock what it can, protect the one full-campaign condition,
then use later replays for the tasks that actually benefit from repetition.
1. Finish the first campaign without skipping takeoffs or aerial refueling.
   That protects By the Book while the story achievements unlock naturally.
2. Clear the short wingman, viewer, replay, customization, Training, and PSM
   requirements whenever a low-pressure opportunity appears.
3. Complete Campaign Mode on ACE difficulty. Do not restart every mission just
   because the result is below S; the full ACE clear is its own requirement.
4. Replay only what is still missing for ACE S ranks and Assault Records.
   This is where mission-specific cleanup becomes useful.
5. Spend the MRP you earned on aircraft, special weapons, and parts instead of
   hoarding it. The collection milestones and total-earned MRP milestones can
   advance together.
6. Finish the leftovers last: 10 medals, 25,000,000 total MRP, 3,000 aircraft
   kills, special-weapon kills, and any remaining purchases.
That order keeps the longest grinds attached to replays you already need instead
of creating extra sorties too early.
</section>

<section id="first-campaign" className="scroll-mt-24">

Can you fix By the Book with Mission Replay?
No — treat By the Book as a full-campaign condition, not a single-mission
cleanup task.
The story requirements arrive naturally as you clear the Prologue and Missions
01–30. Finishing Campaign Mode also awards Promised Land, so there is no need
to chase ranks, collections, or long counters during the first clear.
By the Book is different. It requires a completed campaign in which every
takeoff and every aerial refueling was successfully completed. When either
sequence appears, play it through instead of skipping it. If you miss one, do not
expect a replay of that mission alone to repair the requirement afterward.
 
That makes the first campaign much simpler: protect every takeoff and refueling
sequence, then concentrate on clearing the actual operations. When a mission
changes into an escort, missile interception, identification check, score target,
or multi-stage battle, the
[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) has the full mission order
without mixing in completion tasks that are better handled later.
</section>

<section id="wingman-and-misc" className="scroll-mt-24">

Which achievements should you clear during normal missions?
Several requirements need one deliberate action rather than a long replay plan.
I would remove these whenever the campaign gives you a safe opportunity so they do
not stay mixed into the late-game grind.
Yes, Sir! needs wingman kills after using all three command types:
Forward Atk, Dispersed Atk, and Cover. Use each command while your wingmen
already have enemies to engage rather than forcing it during a mission-critical
interception.
All Weapons Free requires permission for Special Weaponry and then a wingman
kill with a special weapon. Handle it during an ordinary combat phase with enough
targets for your wingman to use the permission naturally.
Four more requirements are short menu or side-mode tasks:
- Aircraft Inspection — preview an aircraft in the Aircraft Viewer.
- Birdwatching — watch a replay in the Replay Viewer.
- Plane Coordinator — change an aircraft skin or emblem.
- Welcome to Boot Camp — clear one Training Mode mission.
Like the Wind is the only requirement in this group that depends on flight
technique. Perform one successful post-stall maneuver in Campaign Mode. There is
no advantage in attempting it during a dangerous objective when an open,
low-pressure moment satisfies the same requirement.
</section>

<section id="ace-and-s-ranks" className="scroll-mt-24">

Do you need S ranks for The Ace?
No. The Ace and Ace of Aces are separate requirements.
The Ace requires completing Campaign Mode on ACE difficulty.
Ace of Aces requires an S rank on ACE difficulty in every campaign mission,
including Free Missions.
 
That distinction changes how I would handle the ACE run. On the first ACE
campaign, finish each mission and learn where the harder difficulty changes enemy
pressure, timing, or ammunition use. A result below S still advances the campaign
clear for The Ace.
After that campaign is complete, go back only to the missions that are still
missing an S rank. The result screen above is the kind of finish you are looking
for on each required mission; the ACE difficulty requirement comes from Ace of
Aces itself, even though the result screen only needs to show whether that sortie
earned S.
The important thing during an S-rank attempt is not to lose time because the
mission changed state while you kept attacking the old objective. The
[all-missions walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) is the fastest reference
when you need the objective order again. Mission 9, 11, 16, 18, 27, and 30 also
have their own deeper routes when one of those multi-stage fights is the specific
blocker.
</section>

<section id="records-medals" className="scroll-mt-24">

How should you handle Assault Records and the 10 medals?
A Record of Rivalry requires every Assault Record. These are not awarded just
because the story is complete, so replaying random missions is an inefficient way
to fill the collection.
Check which record is missing, return to the mission tied to it, meet that
mission's condition, and finish the sortie so the result is saved. The
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records) has the
mission-by-mission conditions, including records that depend on another mission
before they can appear.
Meritorious Service is a separate goal: obtain 10 medals. Keep an eye on the
medal count while doing ACE runs, S-rank attempts, Assault Records, and other
cleanup, but do not assume the medal requirement is finished just because the
campaign or Assault Records are complete.
</section>

<section id="aircraft-mrp" className="scroll-mt-24">

Should you save MRP or spend it while going for achievements?
Spend it on things you still need.
The aircraft collection has several milestones that naturally stack.
Bolstered Forces requires 5 aircraft types, while Fully Kitted Out
requires 20. Budding Buyer needs a combined 15 special weapons and
upgrade parts, and Regular Customer raises that combined total to 100.
The end of the collection is Patron of the Parts: obtain every aircraft,
special weapon, and upgrade part in Campaign Mode. Birds of a Feather adds one
specific purchase condition by asking you to buy the same aircraft for your
wingmen through the Aircraft Tree.

For the full aircraft list, unlock routes, MRP costs, and a practical purchase
order, use the
[Ace Combat 8 aircraft guide](/ace-combat-8/aircraft-guide).


MRP has two cumulative earning milestones:
- 16,000,000 MRP — Need A Bigger Bank
- 25,000,000 MRP — MRP Aplenty
These are total-earned requirements. Spending MRP on aircraft, weapons, and parts
does not mean you have to rebuild that amount as a cash balance before the
achievement counts. Buy items that still advance the collection or improve the
aircraft you are actually using.
I would leave pure MRP farming until the ACE clear, S-rank attempts, Assault
Records, and other replays have already contributed their natural earnings. If
currency is still the remaining bottleneck after that work, the
[Mission 28 MRP farming route](/ace-combat-8/mrp-farm) covers bomber priority,
the resupply window, and ammunition management during the long fight.
</section>

<section id="long-counters" className="scroll-mt-24">

When should you grind the 3,000-aircraft and special-weapon counters?
Late.
Aircraft Annihilator asks for 3,000 aircraft destroyed in Campaign Mode.
That number is large enough that grinding it immediately after the ending creates
many sorties you would probably have flown later for other requirements anyway.
Two special-weapon counters are much smaller:
- Air-to-Air Ace — destroy 100 enemy units with air-to-air special weapons
  in Campaign Mode.
- Anti-Ground Ace — destroy 100 enemy units with anti-ground special
  weapons in Campaign Mode.
Use the appropriate weapon type when another replay already presents the right
targets. Fighter-heavy missions can advance the air-to-air counter, while
ground-heavy operations can move the anti-ground total. ACE clears, S-rank
attempts, Assault Record runs, and MRP replays can all add progress at the same
time.
That is especially useful for the 3,000-aircraft requirement. Let it accumulate
through the work you already need to do, then check the remaining gap near the end
instead of turning it into a standalone grind too early.
</section>

<section id="final-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

What should you do when only a few achievements are left?
By late cleanup, the story rewards and short one-action achievements should already
be gone. What remains will usually be some combination of missing ACE S ranks,
Assault Records, 10 medals, 25,000,000 MRP, 3,000 aircraft kills, special-weapon
kills, or aircraft / weapon / part purchases.
Let the unfinished requirement choose the next sortie:
- a missing S rank means replay that mission for rank;
- a missing Assault Record means return to its required mission and condition;
- unfinished MRP or aircraft kills can be combined with a productive repeatable
  sortie;
- missing purchases mean spend toward the aircraft, special weapon, or part that
  is still absent;
- an unfinished medal total should be checked against the medals your save still
  lacks before choosing another run.
Use the tracker at the top to filter the unchecked items before launching another
sortie. Once the final requirement is complete, there is no reason to keep running
a farming mission simply because it was useful earlier.
</section>
