<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
On your first campaign, complete every **takeoff and aerial refueling** instead of
skipping them, and use the wingman commands plus Special Weaponry permission while
you are already progressing the story. After the ending, check what is still
unfinished before choosing replays for ACE ranks, Assault Records, MRP, or long
combat counters.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Steam has **55 achievements** in Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve. PlayStation uses
the same 55 requirements and adds the **Wings of Theve** Platinum for earning all
other trophies, bringing the PS5 total to **56 trophies**.

The list looks much longer than the number of truly demanding tasks. Clearing the
Prologue, Missions 01–30, and Campaign Mode handles the story requirements through
normal progression. The easy-to-miss work comes from **By the Book**, several
one-action wingman and menu requirements, then the post-campaign goals built around
ACE difficulty, S ranks, Assault Records, medals, MRP, aircraft collection, and
cumulative kills.

<section id="first-campaign" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Protect By the Book during the first campaign

The story requirements arrive naturally as you clear the Prologue and Missions
01–30. Finishing Campaign Mode also awards **Promised Land**, so there is no need
to force ranks, collections, or long counters into the first clear.

The one condition worth protecting from the start is **By the Book**. It requires
a completed campaign in which every takeoff and every aerial refueling was
successfully completed. When either sequence appears, play it through instead of
skipping it. Missing one means the requirement cannot be repaired by replaying a
single mission afterward.

That makes the first campaign a good time to concentrate on simply finishing the
operations cleanly. When an objective changes into an escort, missile
interception, identification check, score requirement, or multi-stage battle, the
[Ace Combat 8 walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) gives the mission order
without adding completion tasks that are better left for later runs.

</section>

<section id="wingman-and-misc" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Remove the short command and menu requirements while they are convenient

Several achievements need one deliberate action rather than a long replay plan.
Clearing them early keeps the later checklist focused on the requirements that
actually consume time.

**Yes, Sir!** needs wingman kills after using all three command types:
**Forward Atk, Dispersed Atk, and Cover**. Use each command when your wingmen
already have enemies to engage instead of forcing the action during a dangerous
mission phase.

**All Weapons Free** requires permission for Special Weaponry and then a wingman
kill with a special weapon. It can be handled during an ordinary combat mission
where your wingman has enough targets to use the permission naturally.

Four more requirements come directly from menus or side modes:

- **Aircraft Inspection** — preview an aircraft in the Aircraft Viewer.
- **Birdwatching** — watch a replay in the Replay Viewer.
- **Plane Coordinator** — change an aircraft skin or emblem.
- **Welcome to Boot Camp** — clear one Training Mode mission.

**Like the Wind** is the only requirement in this group that depends on flight
technique. Perform one successful post-stall maneuver in Campaign Mode. There is
no benefit in attempting it during a mission-critical interception when an open,
low-pressure moment will satisfy the same requirement.

</section>

<section id="ace-and-s-ranks" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Separate the ACE campaign clear from the all-S-rank requirement

Two achievements use ACE difficulty, but they ask for different results.

**The Ace** requires completing Campaign Mode on **ACE difficulty**.

**Ace of Aces** requires an **S rank on ACE difficulty in every campaign mission,
including Free Missions**.

Treating those as two passes removes unnecessary restarts. On the first ACE
campaign, finish the missions and learn where harder enemy pressure or ammunition
use changes your normal route. A rank below S still advances the full ACE clear.
After that campaign is complete, replay only the missions that are still missing
their S rank.

The important thing during a rank attempt is not to lose time because the mission
changed state and you kept attacking the old objective. The
[all-missions walkthrough](/ace-combat-8/walkthrough) is the fastest reference
when you need the order of objectives again; the individual Mission 9, 11, 16,
18, 27, and 30 routes are most useful when one of those multi-stage fights is the
specific blocker.

</section>

<section id="records-medals" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Handle Assault Records by mission, and keep the 10-medal target separate

**A Record of Rivalry** requires every Assault Record. These are not awarded just
because the story has been cleared, so a random replay is a poor way to fill an
empty record.

Check which record is missing, return to the mission tied to it, meet that
mission's condition, and finish the sortie so the result is saved. The
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records) has the mission-by-
mission conditions, including the entries that depend on another mission before
they can appear.

**Meritorious Service** is a different job: obtain **10 medals**. Keep the medal
count visible while doing ACE runs, rank attempts, and other cleanup, but do not
assume the ten-medal requirement is complete just because the campaign or Assault
Records are finished.

</section>

<section id="aircraft-mrp" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Buy what you still need while the MRP total keeps rising

The aircraft collection has several milestones that naturally stack.

**Bolstered Forces** requires **5 aircraft types**, while **Fully Kitted Out**
requires **20**. **Budding Buyer** needs a combined **15 special weapons and
upgrade parts**, and **Regular Customer** raises that combined total to **100**.

The end of the collection is **Patron of the Parts**: obtain every aircraft,
special weapon, and upgrade part in Campaign Mode. **Birds of a Feather** adds one
specific purchase condition by asking you to buy the same aircraft for your
wingmen through the Aircraft Tree.

MRP has two cumulative earning milestones:

- **16,000,000 MRP** — Need A Bigger Bank
- **25,000,000 MRP** — MRP Aplenty

Because these are total-earned requirements, spending MRP on aircraft, weapons,
and parts does not require you to keep that amount sitting in the balance. Buy
items that still advance the collection or improve the aircraft you are actually
using.

Pure MRP farming is better left until ACE runs, S-rank attempts, Assault Records,
and other replays have already added their natural earnings. If the currency total
is still the remaining bottleneck after that work, the
[Mission 28 MRP farming route](/ace-combat-8/mrp-farm) covers the bomber priority,
resupply window, and long-fight ammunition decisions.

</section>

<section id="long-counters" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Let the long kill counters ride on useful replays

**Aircraft Annihilator** asks for **3,000 aircraft destroyed in Campaign Mode**.
That number is large enough that grinding it immediately after the ending can
create many sorties you would have flown later anyway.

Two special-weapon counters are much smaller:

- **Air-to-Air Ace** — destroy **100 enemy units** with air-to-air special weapons
  in Campaign Mode.
- **Anti-Ground Ace** — destroy **100 enemy units** with anti-ground special
  weapons in Campaign Mode.

Use the appropriate weapon type when a replay already presents the right targets.
Fighter-heavy missions can advance the air-to-air counter, while operations with
dense surface groups can move the anti-ground total. An ACE clear, S-rank attempt,
Assault Record run, or MRP replay can all contribute at the same time.

This is especially useful for the 3,000-aircraft requirement: let it accumulate
through the work you already need to do, then judge the remaining gap near the end
instead of turning it into a separate grind too early.

</section>

<section id="final-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Pick the next replay from the requirement that is still blocking completion

By late cleanup, the story rewards and one-action achievements should already be
gone. What remains will usually be some combination of **missing ACE S ranks,
Assault Records, 10 medals, 25,000,000 MRP, 3,000 aircraft kills, special-weapon
kills, or aircraft / weapon / part purchases**.

Those unfinished requirements point to different next actions:
