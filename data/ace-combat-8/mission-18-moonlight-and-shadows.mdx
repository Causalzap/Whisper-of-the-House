Mission 18 gives you an objective that sounds contradictory at first: **destroy the
rockets, but do not attack them before launch or while they are still at low altitude**.

That restriction is intentional. The FCU wants to capture and reuse the Kimona launch
facility, so Joker Flight has to let the launch happen before opening fire. The route
there also gets interrupted by Shadow Squadron, which means the mission effectively
moves through four different priorities: cross the Rainband, rescue Queen Flight,
intercept two rockets, then destroy the debris falling back toward the launch center.

The easiest way to lose time is to stay committed to the previous phase after the
objective has already changed.

<section id="cross-rainband" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Cross the Rainband without clearing every airship

The route to Kimona passes through the Rainband, where multiple airships are providing
laser guidance for the Tonitra satellite.

This is not Mission 10 again. You are specifically told that those airships do **not**
need to be destroyed during this approach.

Keep your speed up, stay on the mapped route, and get through the cloud belt. Every
missile spent trying to clear a guidance airship is ammunition you may want later,
because the mission still has a full air battle, two rockets, and a falling-debris
sequence ahead.

The practical order through the opening is simple:

1. follow the route through the Rainband;
2. avoid getting dragged into unnecessary airship attacks;
3. preserve ammunition while closing on Kimona;
4. react when Shadow Squadron intercepts Queen Flight.

The mission changes before you ever reach the rockets.

</section>

<section id="shadow-squadron" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Shadow Squadron becomes the immediate threat

Shadow Squadron catches Queen Flight during the approach. Once friendly aircraft begin
going down, the rocket interception temporarily stops being the only thing that matters.

Follow the updated objective and move toward Queen Flight.

This fight is dangerous because the Shadows are not working alone. A precision-guidance
laser can mark the area for a Tonitra Spear strike, followed by an impact and shock wave.
Keep moving through the engagement instead of settling into a slow turning fight while
the strike sequence is active.

The battle also tries to split your attention between several aircraft. When the mission
identifies a high-priority pursuer on Queen Flight, take that threat first. Friendly
aircraft are already under enough pressure that chasing an easier target away from the
fight can cost you more than the kill is worth.

Eventually the remaining enemy breaks away. **Do not chase the retreating aircraft
north just because it is still visible.** The operation calls Joker Flight back to the
original job and gives the next instruction: head south toward Kimona.

Players cleaning up named Aces should handle the special Shadow conditions through the
[Assault Records checklist](/ace-combat-8/assault-records). For a normal Mission 18
clear, once the fight is released, the rockets matter more than the aircraft leaving
the area.

</section>

<section id="wait-for-launch" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why can't you destroy the rockets before they launch?

Because keeping the launch facility usable is part of the operation.

The briefing explicitly forbids destroying the rocket **before launch or at low
altitude**. Kimona is meant to be recaptured and repurposed, so simply bombing the
rocket on its pad defeats part of the mission's purpose.

When you arrive, **two rockets** are preparing to launch.

Do not spend this window firing into the pad. Get yourself in position to follow the
rockets upward, clear only the enemy aircraft that directly prevent that approach, and
wait for the confirmed liftoff.

Once the rockets are airborne, the restriction is gone.

</section>

<section id="destroy-rockets" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Destroy both rockets before they cross the ceiling

The launch sequence gives you a hard vertical race rather than a normal health-bar
fight.

Immediately after liftoff, the mission calls out roughly **33,000 ft to ceiling** and
orders Joker Flight to destroy **both rockets** before they climb beyond the reachable
limit.

![Both Mission 18 rockets climbing toward the ceiling after liftoff.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-18-two-rockets-ceiling.webp)

Stay with one rocket long enough to finish it, then transfer immediately to the other.
Spreading attacks between both only becomes useful if your current pass naturally lines
up with the second target; what matters is getting two confirmed destructions before
either one escapes upward.

The countdown keeps shrinking as they climb. A later call reports roughly **26,000 ft
to ceiling**, so there is not much room for an extended dogfight once the launch has
started.

Enemy aircraft continue to interfere, but they are secondary unless one has actually
forced you off the rocket. Use flares when necessary, regain the climbing target, and
keep your attack pointed upward.

The mission is not finished when the second rocket explodes.

</section>

<section id="destroy-wreckage" className="scroll-mt-24">

## The falling wreckage is the final target

After the rockets are destroyed, a large section of debris begins falling toward
Kimona.

This is easy to misread as a victory animation because you have already completed the
stated rocket objective. Instead, the game immediately warns that the wreckage is on a
collision course with the launch center.

Turn toward the new target and start breaking it apart.

![Rocket wreckage falling toward the Kimona launch center in Mission 18.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-18-falling-rocket-wreckage.webp)

During the descent, the mission can report **30,000 ft from the debris to the ground**.
Damage breaks the wreckage into smaller pieces, but one successful hit is not enough to
assume the danger is gone.

Keep attacking every marked fragment until the operation confirms that nothing remains
on radar. Only then are both conditions satisfied: the rockets are gone and the launch
center has survived.

This final sequence is also why conserving some ammunition during the Rainband and
Shadow phases helps. Mission 18 keeps adding work after moments that look like natural
endpoints.

</section>

<section id="mission-18-order" className="scroll-mt-24">

## The full Mission 18 order

When the mission starts feeling chaotic, reduce it to the objective currently capable
of stopping the operation:

- **Rainband:** keep moving toward Kimona; the guidance airships do not need to be
  cleared.
- **Shadow attack:** support Queen Flight and survive the laser-guided strike sequence.
- **Enemy withdrawal:** let the departing fighter go when the operation sends you
  south.
- **Rocket launch:** wait for liftoff; do not attack the rockets on the pad.
- **Ascent:** destroy both rockets before they cross the ceiling.
- **Debris:** break the falling wreckage apart before it reaches the launch center.

The crucial transition is between the last two phases. Two destroyed rockets are not
the end of the sortie. Wait for the new falling target, finish it, and remain in the
area until the mission confirms the launch facility is safe.

After Mission 18, the campaign immediately has another major threat developing around
the Land Battleship near the Space Elevator, so there is no reason to stay in the area
looking for an additional hidden rocket once the RTB order appears.

</section>
