Mission 29 turns a normal dogfight into a fight against an aircraft that can keep
changing the conditions around you. Moon 11 can extend away from Joker Flight,
reverse into a head-on approach, pull through unusually hard turns, and then add
an APS defense while the targeting laser continues doing its real job.

That laser is the reason the fight cannot simply be waited out. Moon 11 is
guiding a Tonitra Spear toward the stalled land battleship below. The ship uses
nuclear power, so letting the strike land there would turn the battle into a much
larger disaster.

The useful way to approach Selene is to separate the fight into the moments when
she is actually vulnerable. Chasing her through every turn burns missiles and
time. Staying composed through the reversals, reacting correctly when the UAVs
appear, and recognizing that the first takedown is not the end makes the mission
far more predictable.

## Stop Moon 11 before the Tonitra Spear reaches the land battleship

The mission briefing tells Joker Flight to intercept Moon 11, but her objective
only becomes clear after the chase begins.

A targeting laser on the X-40 is aimed at the stalled land battleship. The
Tonitra Spear can use that laser to correct its final trajectory, and destroying
Moon 11's aircraft is the only way Joker Flight has to break that guidance.

![Moon 11 uses the X-40 targeting laser to guide the Tonitra Spear toward the land battleship.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-29-moon-11-laser-designator.webp)

That gives Mission 29 a different rhythm from a normal ace duel. Survival alone
is not progress. Long defensive loops still leave Moon 11 free to continue the
guidance sequence, so the goal is to keep creating useful attack passes rather
than waiting for her to eventually make a mistake.

Do not spend missiles simply because the lock indicator appears. Moon 11 often
creates distance, reverses direction, and then commits to another aggressive
turn. A missile launched while her path is still changing sharply is easy to
waste.

I had better results staying with the turn long enough to see where she was
actually exiting it, then firing once her flight path became more stable. Her
straight return passes and the end of a hard reversal are much better places to
commit ammunition than the middle of the tightest turn.

</section>

## Do not try to match every Moon 11 turn

Moon 11's biggest trap is convincing you to turn harder every time she does.

The X-40 can pull maneuvers that Joker Flight repeatedly reacts to as abnormal.
Later in the fight, her neural load reaches **100%** and the aircraft begins a
forced-disconnection sequence. Moon 11 cancels that sequence and keeps flying.

![Moon 11 reaches 100 percent neural load during the Mission 29 dogfight.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-29-moon-11-neural-load.webp)

That is a useful warning for the player as well: this is not a fight where
staying permanently glued to her tail is always the fastest answer.

When she pitches into another extreme reversal, preserve enough speed to control
your own next turn. Falling into a slow circular chase makes it harder to create
a clean firing line and gives her more opportunities to extend away again.

The sequence that works more consistently is:

1. Keep Moon 11 close enough that you do not have to rebuild the entire chase.
2. Let the sharpest part of her reversal develop before committing missiles.
3. Use acceleration or deceleration to tighten the angle rather than holding one
   speed through the whole turn.
4. Fire when her movement settles into a direction your missile can actually
   follow.
5. Keep the gun available at close range instead of waiting only for another
   perfect missile shot.

This also helps conserve special-weapon ammunition. Mission 29 lasts beyond the
first apparent takedown, so emptying the strongest weapon into poor angles early
can leave very little for the final targets.

</section>

<section id="uav-aps" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What to do when Moon 11 deploys the Escort UAVs and APS

Partway through the fight, Moon 11 deploys small Escort UAVs and activates an
**APS** defense.

![Escort UAVs deploy the APS around Moon 11's X-40.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-29-moon-11-uav-aps.webp)

This is the point where continuing to attack exactly as before becomes
inefficient. The UAVs are part of the defensive layer around the X-40, and the
mission dialogue specifically calls out their limited power.

Treat them as a way to reopen the boss rather than as a separate four-target
checklist.

A UAV that is directly protecting Moon 11 is worth removing because it turns the
next clean approach into actual damage. What you should not do is abandon a good
position and spend a long time chasing every remaining drone around the combat
area just because four of them can appear.

That distinction matters for both the normal clear and S Rank. A Mission 29
S-Rank result can be earned without destroying all four Escort UAVs, so the
useful question is whether the UAV in front of you is currently preventing
damage to Moon 11.

Once the APS stops interfering with your attacks, switch attention back to the
X-40 and keep the main fight moving.

</section>

## Moon 11 is down, but Mission 29 is not over

The easiest place to lose a clean finish comes after the game announces that
Moon 11 is down.

Do not disengage.

The X-40 **disassembles**, and another section of the aircraft climbs away while
the targeting sequence continues. The guidance upload is still active, and the
laser comes back on.

![The X-40 separates after Moon 11 is called down and continues the targeting sequence.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-29-moon-11-x40-disassembly.webp)

Your targets now change from the original Moon 11 aircraft to the remaining
components. The Mission Results screen identifies the scored targets after the
main takedown as the **X-40 Nose Unit** and **Laser Designator**.

Reacquire them immediately instead of waiting for another boss marker to explain
that the fight has continued.

The nose unit is worth **1,050 destruction points**, while the Laser Designator
is worth another **200**. More importantly, these are part of the sequence that
actually stops Moon 11 from completing the Spear correction.

Keep enough ammunition in reserve for this section. The main X-40 dogfight is
the longest part of the mission, but it is not the final target.

Once the remaining guidance hardware is destroyed, the Tonitra Spear misses the
land battleship and strikes the Enso facility instead. Mission 29 then moves into
its return sequence rather than another combat phase.

</section>

## Do you need a specific aircraft or upgrade for Moon 11?

No single aircraft is required just to clear this fight.

Ace-difficulty runs can complete Mission 29 with very different setups, including
an **ADFX-02 Morgan using TLS with no upgrades** and an **X-40** fighting Moon
11's X-40 directly.

That makes handling more important than buying a new aircraft only for this
mission.

Use the plane you are already comfortable controlling through repeated
high-angle turns. A theoretically stronger aircraft is not much help if every
reversal leaves you stalled, overshooting, or unable to rebuild the lock.

For special weapons, prioritize something you can reliably place onto an agile
air target. Range and raw damage matter less when most missed shots happen
because Moon 11 changed direction before the weapon could capitalize on the
lock.

There is also no need to spend MRP on upgrades solely because the APS appears.
The Escort UAV mechanic can be handled through target priority, and the fight
has been completed on Ace without an upgrade-dependent setup.

</section>

## How to get S Rank on Mission 29

Mission 29's result screen calculates the final **Rank Score** from two visible
parts:

**Destruction Score + Time Bonus = Rank Score**

The important lesson is that S Rank does **not** require clearing all four Escort
UAVs.

One S-Rank clear finished in **9:12.80** after destroying:

- 1 Escort UAV — **300**
- Moon 11 / Selene — **1,250**
- X-40 Nose Unit — **1,050**
- Laser Designator — **200**

That produced **2,800 Destruction Score**, a **30,000 Time Bonus**, and a
**32,800 Rank Score**.

![Mission 29 S Rank with one Escort UAV destroyed and a 9:12.80 clear.](/images/ace-combat-8/mission-29-s-rank-results.webp)

Another S-Rank clear took **9:43.14** and destroyed all four Escort UAVs. The
extra three UAVs added another **900 destruction points**, producing 3,700
Destruction Score and a 33,700 Rank Score while the Time Bonus remained 30,000.

That comparison gives the practical route: **do not turn the four UAVs into a
mandatory checklist**.

Destroy the drones that are costing you attacks through the APS, but keep the
fight moving once Moon 11 is vulnerable again. The extra UAV points can give you
more destruction score, yet a long detour to chase them also makes the clear
slower.

The biggest time losses are usually created earlier: getting trapped in repeated
turning loops, firing into bad angles, or failing to reacquire the nose unit
after the X-40 separates.

A clean S-Rank attempt therefore follows the same priorities as a clean boss
fight:

1. Avoid wasting missiles during Moon 11's hardest reversals.
2. Break the APS defense when it blocks your attack.
3. Return to Moon 11 instead of hunting every UAV by default.
4. Reacquire the X-40 Nose Unit immediately after the first takedown.
5. Destroy the Laser Designator and finish the mission without adding an
   unnecessary extra chase.

Players combining the run with a named-aircraft objective should check the
[Assault Records conditions](/ace-combat-8/assault-records) before starting
Mission 29. That objective adds a separate requirement; it is not necessary for
the normal Moon 11 clear.

</section>

<section id="after-mission-29" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What happens after beating Moon 11?

Destroying the remaining guidance system ends the fight with Selene. The
Tonitra Spear is diverted away from the land battleship, and Joker Flight returns
to Snider's Top Base.

The campaign then moves directly into its final numbered operation. Mission 30
changes the objective completely: instead of chasing another ace, Joker Flight
has to keep the Endurance alive while dismantling the submarine threat.

Continue with
[Mission 30: Song of Wings](/ace-combat-8/mission-30-song-of-wings)
when you are ready for the final sortie.
