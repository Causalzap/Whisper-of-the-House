Get the **UV Flashlight** from the vent above the Break Room. Reveal the hidden numbers in the **Breakroom Extension**, **Vehicle Bay**, and **above the Redacted keypad**, then enter the six digits in their marked positions.

The **Redacted door in CRACK uses a randomized six-digit passcode**. Copying a combination from another player's game will not reliably open it, even though the keypad and the locations of the clues remain the same.

The six digits are split across three areas of the laboratory. Each location reveals two numbers when illuminated with the UV Flashlight, along with enough positional information to work out where those numbers belong in the final combination.

The more difficult part is getting the flashlight. It is hidden inside a ventilation duct above the Break Room, beyond a hatch secured with Bolts. Once you have the light, the puzzle can be completed without digging further into the egg or finishing the specimen delivery.

<section id="uv-flashlight">

## Where to Find the UV Flashlight in CRACK

Look above the **Break Room** for a ventilation hatch that is too high to reach from the laboratory floor.

The hatch leads into a concealed duct containing evidence left by earlier Hatch Corp employees. The UV Flashlight you need for the Redacted password is hidden inside.

You will need:

- **Custom Scaffolding** unlocked through the main Work Orders.
- A **Toolbox** and Scaffolding Parts to build a platform.
- A **Hammer** to secure the structure and remove the hatch Bolts.

If Custom Scaffolding is still locked, complete the required [Work Orders](/crack/walkthrough) before attempting to reach the elevated vent.

### Reach the vent and remove its Bolts

Build a stable scaffolding platform high enough to reach the vent above the Break Room. Extend its Floors or Overhangs toward the cover, secure the connections, and climb up with your Hammer equipped.

The vent does not require a password. Its cover is held in place by Bolts that can be removed with your Hammer.

![The ventilation cover with a visible fastener interaction marker and Hammer Mk III equipped.](/images/crack/crack-redacted-vent-hatch.webp)

*Aim at the ventilation cover's fasteners and remove them with the Hammer to enter the concealed duct.*

Move close to the cover and aim at a fastener until its interaction marker appears. Right-click with the Hammer to remove the Bolts, then enter the duct once the cover opens.

Do not confuse these Bolts with the ones used to build your scaffold. Scaffolding connections need to be tightened; the ventilation cover needs to be opened.

Opening this suspicious vent also satisfies the hidden **Cold Case** achievement.

### Find the flashlight inside the ventilation duct

Explore the duct after removing the cover. Inside is a concealed area containing a deceased former employee and a written account of what happened to the previous team.

The note mentions that employees used material from the egg to write messages on the laboratory walls. Those markings dry almost clear and become visible under a special lamp.

That is the clue to the Redacted password.

Find and take the **UV Flashlight** before leaving the duct. A normal Lantern can help you see the area, but ordinary lighting does not reveal the hidden numerical markings in the same way.

You can leave the duct once you have the UV Flashlight. There is no need to break through another wall or complete a separate puzzle inside it.

<section id="three-clues">

## All Three Redacted Passcode Locations

Return to the main laboratory with the UV Flashlight.

The password is divided into **three pairs of digits**, hidden at fixed locations:

1. Breakroom Extension — right-hand wall.
2. Vehicle Bay — right-hand wall.
3. Redacted door — wall above the keypad.

These are not three independent two-digit passwords. Together, they fill the six positions of the Redacted combination.

The important detail is that each clue shows where its digits belong. Record the positions as well as the numbers; don't simply concatenate the pairs in the order you happen to discover them.

### First clue: Breakroom Extension

Enter the **Breakroom Extension**, the room containing the slot machines.

As you pass through its entrance, turn **right** and examine the wall with your UV Flashlight. Look for a faint, yellowish smear that could easily pass for an ordinary stain under standard lighting.

Aim the UV light directly at that area. Two digits become visible.

Record the two digits in their marked slots. The underscores represent positions that must be filled using clues elsewhere in the laboratory.

The Breakroom Extension is an important distinction from the original Break Room. The two areas serve different purposes, and searching only around the first arcade machines can leave you looking at the wrong wall.

Complete the **Burst Open an Artery Sack** Work Order to unlock the Breakroom Extension, the expanded area containing the slot machines. If it is still locked, finish that assignment before searching for the UV marking.

### Second clue: Vehicle Bay

Leave the Breakroom Extension and head to the **Vehicle Bay**.

Walk into the bay, turn toward the **right-hand wall**, and search for another inconspicuous marking. Shine the UV Flashlight over the wall until the next pair of numbers appears.

You do not need to park or upgrade a Golf Cart to read this clue. The puzzle concerns writing on the wall, not the vehicle terminal.

Add these two digits to your six-position code. You should now have four filled slots; the remaining pair is hidden directly above the Redacted keypad.

### Third clue: Above the Redacted keypad

Return to the door marked **Redacted**.

Instead of immediately entering the four digits you already know, raise the UV Flashlight and inspect the wall **above the keypad**.

This last marking provides the remaining two numbers.

![UV light revealing two digits above the Redacted keypad, with the second and fifth positions filled.](/images/crack/crack-redacted-uv-digits.webp)

*The markings show which positions the digits occupy. Copy each number into its marked slot rather than entering the numbers in discovery order.*

Fill the final two slots using the marking above the keypad. With all six positions complete, you can enter the combination.

<section id="enter-code">

## How to Enter the Six-Digit Redacted Code

With all six digits recorded, interact with the keypad at the Redacted door.

Enter the combination in its complete six-digit order, filling every position from left to right.

The numbers are randomized between playthroughs. A combination that opened the room for one employee may be rejected in another game.

The solution is therefore the sequence revealed by **your own three UV markings**, not a universal number.

After entering the correct code, the door opens and you gain access to the restricted room.

The game also unlocks **Access Granted**, the hidden achievement for entering the Redacted area.

### Why Is the Redacted Code Not Working?

A rejected code usually means the digits were placed in the wrong positions or were copied from another playthrough.

Before retrying, check three things:

- **Six filled slots:** Every position must contain a digit.
- **Correct positions:** Each UV marking identifies specific slots. Keep the underscores until another clue fills them.
- **Current combination:** Use the numbers revealed in your own laboratory, not another player's code.

Do not use the number tally on a Clipboard as a replacement for the UV clues. Return to the missing location, illuminate the wall, and fill the remaining slots before trying the keypad again.

<section id="inside-redacted">

## What Is Behind the Redacted Door?

Beyond the keypad is Hatch Corp's restricted security room, containing surveillance monitors, computer terminals, and records concerning previous laboratory personnel.

![The restricted security room revealed after entering the correct Redacted passcode.](/images/crack/crack-redacted-security-room.webp)

*The unlocked room contains monitoring stations and further information about the laboratory.*

Inside the security room, examine the monitoring stations and the Clipboard describing the supervisor's duties. The notes reveal that the surveillance system covers the laboratory and that earlier employees recorded unusual observations.

The Clipboard also contributes to **Paper Trail**. For the remaining collection requirements, use the [CRACK achievements checklist](/crack/achievements).

The Redacted Room is not required for either ending. To help the chicken escape, use the [boarded KEEP OUT passage in the Break Room](/crack/endings) instead.
