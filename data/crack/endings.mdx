<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
For **Ending 1/2**, capture the chicken and deliver it to the Holding Chamber. For **Ending 2/2**, open the Do Not Enter door in the Break Room, dig through the passage to the chicken-marked exit, and bring the chicken there instead.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

**CRACK has two endings**, and both depend on what you do with the specimen after breaking open the innermost containment sack. The chicken can be handed over to Hatch Corp for processing or carried through a concealed escape route that the company never tells you to use.

The standard assignment points straight toward the Holding Chamber. Following those instructions produces Ending 1/2. Ending 2/2 requires finding an old door in the Break Room, excavating the passage behind it, and recognizing the chicken symbol at the far end.

The hidden route is worth opening before your final delivery. Otherwise, you can reach the specimen, carry it through the laboratory, and only discover afterward that the escape passage still needs several minutes of digging.

<section id="ending-choices">

## Where CRACK's Two Endings Split

The meaningful choice happens **after the specimen is loose and has been captured**, not when you initially break through the Containment Plating.

Your final destination determines the result:

- **Ending 1/2 — Specimen Delivery:** Take the chicken to the Holding Chamber, complete processing, and finish the company's assignment.
- **Ending 2/2 — Specimen Escape:** Carry the chicken to the hidden exit reached through the Break Room's Do Not Enter passage.

Both outcomes begin with the same captured specimen. There is no separate second egg to find.

You must reach the inner containment sack and release the specimen first. If you are still working through Bone, Hatchite, or Containment Plating, finish the required excavation in the [CRACK walkthrough](/crack/walkthrough) before attempting the final delivery.

The escape route itself can be investigated separately from that final objective. Opening it in advance reduces the amount of work left once you have the chicken in your hands.

<section id="ending-one">

## How to Get Ending 1/2 — Deliver the Specimen

Ending 1/2 is the normal completion of Hatch Corp's assignment. Follow the on-screen instructions after the containment sack breaks and deliver the living specimen to the company.

### Catch the chicken after the containment sack opens

Once the central sack is breached, the objective changes to:

**THE SPECIMEN IS LOOSE. RETRIEVE IT IMMEDIATELY.**

The specimen is a small chicken. It may move among the broken material instead of remaining where the sack was destroyed.

Look around the immediate excavation area, follow the chicken, and pick it up. You may have to approach from another side or corner it before the interaction becomes easy.

Once captured, the company instructs you to transport it to the Holding Chamber.

At this point, keep the specimen in hand and leave the excavation through the passage you already opened. Further mining is unnecessary for the standard ending.

### Bring the specimen to the Holding Chamber

Return to the laboratory and find the company's large Holding Chamber entrance. This is the conspicuous industrial receiving area associated with the final specimen delivery, not the smaller hidden passage inside the Break Room.

Carry the chicken through the opened chamber entrance and approach its designated receiving unit.

![The captured chicken facing the small coop-shaped receiving unit inside the Holding Chamber.](/images/crack/crack-ending-holding-chamber.webp)

*Place the specimen into this receiving unit to begin Hatch Corp's processing sequence.*

Place the specimen into the chamber's receiving enclosure when the interaction becomes available.

Once the delivery registers, Hatch Corp proceeds with processing. The chicken is no longer available to carry away from that unit during the sequence.

### Complete processing and dispatch

The Holding Chamber is not the entire ending sequence. Watch the machinery complete its work, then finish the dispatch interaction involving the processed specimen and its shipping container.

The process culminates in the assignment-completion announcement. The company treats the specimen as a product to be packed and sent away.

You receive the hidden **Well Done** achievement for delivering the specimen. The ending then concludes with the **Ending 1/2** confirmation.

The result is deliberately different from what the original egg-breaking assignment seems to promise. You recover a living chicken, but following Hatch Corp's orders subjects it to the processing and dispatch machinery.

If your goal is to help the chicken escape, the Holding Chamber is the wrong destination. The alternative is hidden elsewhere in the laboratory.

</section>

<section id="secret-passage">

## Where to Find the Secret Escape Route in CRACK

Go to the **Break Room** and locate the boarded **KEEP OUT** door. It leads to the hidden escape passage, not the [Redacted Room keypad](/crack/redacted-door-code).

### Enter the Do Not Enter room

Use a Hammer to remove the fasteners securing the wooden planks, then enter the restricted room.

![The boarded KEEP OUT door in the Break Room, blocking access to the hidden escape passage.](/images/crack/crack-ending-do-not-enter-door.webp)

*The secret route starts behind this boarded door. Clear the obstruction to enter the restricted room.*

Entering the room unlocks **Bad Listener**, the hidden achievement for going through the forbidden door.

Inside, examine the small room and its clipboard. The note explains that the old Break Room door was never truly locked and that dirt blocks the passage beyond it. Crucially, the passage continues **upward**.

This changes how you should approach the obstruction. Do not search for another key, access card, or numerical combination. Bring a suitable digging tool and start clearing the dirt.

### Dig upward through the blocked passage

The first stretch looks like ordinary excavatable soil. Remove enough material to expose the route behind the entrance.

A stronger Shovel makes this much less tedious than working with a nearly broken early-game tool. Bring a replacement or a tool with adequate remaining durability before committing to the excavation.

The path is not simply a straight hallway. Continue clearing the material ahead and above you, following the opening as it rises toward the concealed upper area.

![The excavated hidden passage behind the Do Not Enter door, showing the upward route.](/images/crack/crack-ending-secret-passage.webp)

*Clear the soil behind the restricted doorway and follow the passage upward toward the hidden exit.*

You may uncover abandoned equipment, loose objects, and additional clipboard notes along the way. These help explain what happened to earlier employees, but they are not separate items that must be delivered to unlock the ending.

Where tougher material blocks your excavation, change to a compatible stronger tool rather than repeatedly striking it with a Shovel that cannot break it.

Avoid spending the entire shift collecting the loose dirt. The route itself is the objective; clearing enough space to reach the exit is more important than extracting every fragment.

### Find the chicken-marked exit at the top

Eventually, the passage reaches a concealed area containing several abandoned objects and a door marked with a **chicken symbol**.

This is the exit you need for Ending 2/2.

![The chicken-marked secret exit and a clipboard explaining that previous employees could not open it.](/images/crack/crack-ending-chicken-door.webp)

*The chicken symbol identifies the exit at the top of the passage.*

The clipboard describes the previous employees' failed attempts to open this door. Return with the living chicken; approaching the exit empty-handed will not complete Ending 2/2.


Once you have reached and identified the exit, turn around and return through the passage to the main laboratory. Keep the route open and accessible so you can carry the chicken back without having to excavate the same obstruction again.

<section id="ending-two">

## How to Get Ending 2/2 — Help the Specimen Escape

With the escape passage prepared, return to the egg and retrieve the chicken.

If you have not yet broken the inner containment sack, do that first. The final objective will instruct you to capture the loose specimen.

This time, ignore the company's Holding Chamber destination.

### Carry the chicken through the Break Room passage

Follow these steps after securing the specimen:

1. Pick up the chicken from the excavation area.
2. Return to the Break Room instead of entering the Holding Chamber.
3. Go through the Do Not Enter doorway.
4. Follow the excavated passage upward.
5. Carry the chicken to the door marked with its symbol.
6. Approach the hidden exit with the specimen still in hand.

The standard delivery doors are a tempting wrong turn because they are large, prominently opened, and associated with your active company assignment. Taking the specimen there and completing the processing interaction gives you Ending 1/2 instead.

The hidden passage is less convenient, but it is the route required for the alternate outcome.

Keep enough space clear to move through the excavated tunnel while holding the chicken. If you left an awkward obstruction or unfinished vertical section on the first visit, fix it before trying to transport the specimen.

### Open the secret exit with the chicken

Return to the chicken-marked door at the far end of the passage.

Approach the exit while holding the specimen and use the available door interaction. The gate now recognizes the condition needed for the escape.

![The captured chicken being carried to the chicken-marked hidden exit.](/images/crack/crack-ending-free-range.webp)

*Bring the chicken to the marked exit at the top of the hidden passage to trigger Ending 2/2.*

This unlocks the hidden **Free Range** achievement, whose requirement is to help the specimen escape.

The game then displays **Ending 2/2**.

You have completed the alternate route without processing the chicken through Hatch Corp's Holding Chamber.

The ending is intentionally unsettling rather than a conventional celebration. The achievement confirms that you helped the specimen escape, although the final sounds and presentation leave the outcome somewhat ambiguous.

</section>

<section id="both-endings">

## Can You Get Both CRACK Endings in One Game?

Both endings can be reached during continued play, but completing the company's delivery does not mean the chicken is immediately available for another handoff.

After Ending 1/2, return to the laboratory and check the active Work Order. If the containment-sac objective is active again, release and retrieve the specimen before bringing it to the hidden exit.

You can save excavation time by opening the Break Room's secret passage before your first final delivery. Once the chicken-marked exit is accessible, the remaining choice is where you take the living specimen.

</section>
