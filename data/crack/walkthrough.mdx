<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start by using the Pickaxe and Chisel to **split and peel 30 Shell Chunks**, unlocking the Resource Depot. Deposit Shell until the Intermediate Catalog opens, then follow each new Work Order to unlock the next equipment tier.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The first few shifts in **CRACK** can make the job look simpler than it is. You break the egg, collect whatever falls out, and turn it into money. Then the company starts asking for materials you cannot reach, equipment you cannot use yet, and increasingly awkward tasks that have very little to do with actually breaking an egg.

The Work Orders are the route through the game. Completing them opens the catalogs, vehicles, scanning equipment, Workbots, and explosives needed to reach the center. Mining deeper without advancing those objectives can leave you surrounded by valuable material but without the tools required to finish the job.

There is no need to excavate the entire shell. One sufficiently wide passage toward the center is enough, provided you can enter it, move equipment through it, and keep it from closing when the specimen reacts.

<section id="first-crack">

## First Crack: Peel 30 Shell Chunks to Unlock the Resource Depot

Your opening assignment is to **split and peel 30 chunks from the outer shell**.

Take a Pickaxe from the starting equipment area and strike the shell until sections begin flashing white. Those flashing sections are ready to be removed with a Chisel. Repeatedly hitting the same already-cracked area with the Pickaxe wastes durability when the Chisel is the tool needed to extract the pieces.

Use the tools in this order:

1. Strike an intact section with the Pickaxe.
2. Wait until the damaged shell flashes white.
3. Switch to the Chisel and peel the loosened section away.
4. Repeat until the Work Order counter reaches 30.

![Cracked outer shell showing the white flashing sections that can be removed with a chisel.](/images/crack/crack-shell-peeling.webp)

*The flashing sections are ready for the Chisel. Peeling them completes the opening assignment and exposes the material beneath the shell.*

The first Pickaxe will not last long enough to carry you deep into the egg. That is normal. The immediate objective is not to preserve it forever, but to complete the assignment and gain access to the next equipment tier.

You will also notice that working continuously in one area increases the egg's agitation. Spread the first cuts across accessible sides of the shell rather than carving an unnecessarily deep hole immediately.

### Deposit shell material to unlock the Intermediate Catalog

After the first 30 chunks, the job shifts to collecting and selling material.

Pick up the starting Bucket, collect loose shell fragments, and bring them to the **Resource Depot**.

The basic Bucket holds **20 pieces**. Once it contains material:

1. Place the Bucket on the Depot's receiving platform.
2. Insert your employee Badge into the terminal.
3. Let the material pass through the machine.
4. Retrieve your Badge and Bucket before returning to the egg.

A full starting Bucket of shell produced **$9** in the opening shifts. Your next important threshold is **$30 worth of deposited shell material**, which unlocks the Intermediate Catalog.

The amount deposited is cumulative. You do not have to earn the entire $30 in a single delivery.

Once the Intermediate Catalog opens, buy a **Shovel** before purchasing optional equipment. The next required material is Membrane, and the Pickaxe/Chisel combination cannot replace the Shovel for that task.

Return to the Employee Quarters before the work cycle closes and use the bed to begin the next shift. You can continue collecting the remaining shell material the following day.

<section id="membrane">

## How to Unlock the Vehicle Bay and Starter Scaffolding

The second layer introduces an important difference between **earning money** and **completing a Work Order**.

Once you dig past the shell, use the Shovel to excavate Membrane. Collect it with a Bucket and return it to the Resource Depot.

The Vehicle Bay assignment requires **two full Buckets of Membrane**.

A partially filled Bucket still generates money when deposited, but it does not count toward an order requiring a full Bucket.

![Work Order feedback showing that only full buckets count toward the required deposit.](/images/crack/crack-full-bucket-requirement.webp)

*Fill the Bucket completely before depositing it when the Work Order specifies full Buckets. Selling a partial load does not advance that counter.*

With the original 20-piece Bucket, make two complete Membrane deliveries. Empty the Bucket after each transaction and verify that the Work Order counter changes.

This is a common early stall: the player has sold plenty of Membrane, the balance has increased, but the Vehicle Bay is still locked. More mining will not solve it unless the required deliveries are full.

### The next Membrane target is $30

After the Vehicle Bay unlocks, the next important target is **$30 worth of Membrane**, which opens Starter Scaffolding.

This time you are working toward a monetary deposit threshold rather than a specified number of full Buckets.

Excavate another accessible Membrane section, collect the loose material, and keep delivering until the order completes.

You do not need the Golf Cart to finish this Membrane assignment. Complete the $30 deposit first; the vehicle upgrade becomes a required Work Order shortly afterward.

Your next material objective is **$40 worth of Blight**.

<section id="blight">

## Where to Find Blight and Complete the $40 Work Order

Blight appears farther beneath the shell and Membrane. Look for the greenish material exposed around the deeper edges of your excavation.

The practical approach is to widen an existing opening until the next layer is visible, then remove the Blight with the Shovel. Keep the Pickaxe or Chisel available for material still blocking access.

For the **$40 Blight assignment**:

1. Expose a sufficiently large patch of Blight.
2. Remove it using a compatible Shovel.
3. Fill your Bucket and deposit the material.
4. Continue until the cumulative deposited value reaches $40.

You do not have to strip Blight from every side of the egg. Clearing another large surface area solely to avoid an existing small tunnel costs time and tool durability without necessarily bringing you closer to the next layer.

An accessible upper patch can be useful here. Starter Scaffolding lets you work above ground level, but a complicated permanent structure is not required for this early deposit assignment.

Once the $40 Blight target is reached, the next progression objective involves upgrading your Golf Cart.

</section>

<section id="golf-cart">

## Upgrade the Golf Cart to Tier 2

The Golf Cart upgrade is a separate Work Order. Simply buying a vehicle does not complete it.

At the Vehicle Bay, the initial Golf Cart unlock costs **$25**. The Tier 2 upgrade costs **$50**.

To complete the assignment:

1. Obtain the Golf Cart from the Vehicle Bay.
2. Remove anything you are transporting so the vehicle is empty.
3. Park it inside the designated upgrade bay.
4. Use the Vehicle Bay terminal with your Badge.
5. Purchase the Tier 2 upgrade.

The upgrade increases the vehicle's usefulness for moving multiple Buckets around the laboratory.

Parking is the part that can hold you up. Merely driving close to the terminal does not necessarily register as a parked vehicle. Reposition the cart until it sits correctly in the bay, then interact with the upgrade terminal.

The Work Order completes when the upgrade is purchased.

The next equipment requirement is the **Deposit Scanner**, which begins directing you toward valuable deposits inside the egg.

</section>

<section id="deposit-scanner">

## How to Use the Deposit Scanner and Find the Required Nodules

You do not need to uncover the whole egg to find a deposit. The scanner identifies material that is still embedded in the surrounding layers.

Go to the Deposit Scanner station and aim its scanning cone toward the egg.

The first scanning assignment asks you to locate **two Nodules**. Sweep the scan cone around the egg at different heights until the required markers appear. Sweep the cone across the area you have already excavated, including higher and deeper sections. A single scan aimed at the center can miss deposits outside the current field.

![Deposit Scanner showing the red scan coverage area on the egg model.](/images/crack/crack-deposit-scanner-coverage.webp)

*Move the red outline toward the area you want to inspect, then press SCAN. Detected deposits appear as markers.*

The scanner can reveal valuable nodules behind material you have not broken yet. Detection alone does not remove the obstruction; you still need to mine toward the marked deposit.

If a target appears farther inward than your current excavation, make a short passage toward its approximate location and scan again once you are closer. This is more efficient than blindly stripping an entire surrounding layer.

### Shred two Gristle Nodules

The next required step is to recover and shred **two Gristle Nodules**.

Once you know where the deposits are:

1. Mine away the material surrounding a detected nodule.
2. Expose enough of it to pick it up.
3. Carry it to the Shredder rather than the ordinary Bucket deposit line.
4. Insert your Badge and process the nodule.
5. Repeat for the second nodule.

A nodule is a physical object, not a loose fragment that must be collected with a Bucket.

The scan and the Shredder solve different parts of the assignment. The scanner finds the target; the Shredder processes it. Do not spend time repeatedly scanning a deposit that you have already physically exposed and can carry.

Completing this stage opens the Break Room and its first required arcade challenge.

</section>

<section id="eggsteroids">

## Score 3,500 Points in Eggsteroids to Continue

The Break Room is not just a place to spend your earnings. Its arcade machines are involved in progression.

Your required target is **3,500 points on the Eggsteroids cabinet**.

Go to the Break Room, find the Eggsteroids machine, and start a game. Starting an Eggsteroids game costs **$3**.

The objective is straightforward: survive long enough to score 3,500 points by shooting the approaching targets.

Keep the ship moving only when needed to avoid an immediate collision. The early scoring requirement is low enough that repeatedly firing while maintaining a clear position can get you through without elaborate movement.

The 3,500-point score is a **Work Order threshold**, not the same as the much higher scores required for certain achievements.

Once you pass it, return to excavation. The following work order requires **$70 worth of Tissue**, which opens the next major facility.

</section>

<section id="tissue">

## Deposit $70 of Tissue and Unlock Workbot Storage

Tissue is deeper than the early Membrane layer. Its appearance differs from the surrounding shell and green Blight, and you will need a tool capable of excavating it.

The **Shovel Mk II** is an appropriate upgrade for this stage.

Do not keep using an unsuitable early Shovel just because it worked on Membrane. The game can reject the interaction when the tool is not strong enough for the exposed material.

Find a visible Tissue section, remove enough of the surrounding material to reach it, and begin collecting it into Buckets.

You need **$70 of deposited Tissue**.

Tissue excavation tends to consume more of your workday than the opening Shell assignment because you may have to travel inside the egg, carry material back out, and replenish tools while the agitation rises.

At this point, a wider entrance is more useful than a deep, narrow hole. Give yourself enough clearance to move between the exposed Tissue and the open laboratory without repeatedly getting caught on the edges of the excavation.

### Why the Workbot area is still locked

The Workbot Storage facility does not open simply because you can see it or have accumulated enough money.

Finish the $70 Tissue Work Order first. Once it registers, the Workbot area becomes accessible.

The next objective is to commission one Workbot.

Use the Workbot terminal and purchase your first available unit. Commissioning your first Workbot costs **$100**.

Commissioning that first robot advances the Work Order and opens the **Specialty Catalog**.

At this point, you do not need to establish an entire automatic transport network before moving on. One commissioned Workbot is enough to satisfy this unlock condition.

Once the first Workbot is commissioned, you can [set up automated Bucket collection and delivery](/crack/workbots) while continuing toward the next Work Order.

For the main progression, the immediate reward is access to the Specialty Catalog, where the proper scaffolding construction equipment becomes available.

</section>

<section id="scaffolding">

## How to Build 10m Scaffolding Without It Collapsing

The Scaffolding assignment is one of the first substantial interruptions to CRACK's excavation loop.

Your Work Order asks you to construct Scaffolding reaching **10 meters**. A few loose poles beside the egg do not satisfy that requirement.

The Specialty Catalog provides the equipment needed to build a taller structure:

- **Toolbox:** Places scaffolding components.
- **Hammer:** Secures the Bolts that turn placed previews into finished structures.
- **Scaffolding Parts:** Supplies the structural pieces used by the Toolbox.

Buy the Toolbox and Hammer before attempting the assignment. A Support Spike serves a different function and cannot replace the scaffolding equipment.

### Place Poles first and secure every Bolt

Select a Pole with the Toolbox and position it on the laboratory floor near the egg.

The first important rule is that **only Poles can stand directly on the ground**. Other components attach to scaffolding that is already present.

The second rule is that placed structures begin as holograms. You must secure their Bolts with the Hammer before the component becomes fully built.

![In-game scaffolding construction instructions showing holograms, bolts, supported parts, and stability colors.](/images/crack/crack-scaffolding-construction-rules.webp)

*Poles establish the ground supports. Additional pieces attach to existing scaffolding and must be secured with the Hammer.*

Complete the first frame before trying to build several levels above it:

1. Place a grounded Pole arrangement beside the egg.
2. Secure every required Bolt with the Hammer.
3. Attach the next supported components.
4. Add the first accessible Floor and Ladder sections.
5. Extend the structure upward, tightening new connections as you go.

Poles cannot be placed directly against the specimen. Build nearby and use connected Floors or Overhangs when you eventually need access closer to the surface.

### Scaffolding Placement and Bolt Colors

The placement outline and Bolt color indicate different problems.

**Placement outline:**
- **Green:** The piece can be placed.
- **Yellow:** The piece can be placed but will collapse once built.
- **Red:** The piece cannot be placed here.

**Bolt condition:**
- **Green:** Tight.
- **Yellow:** Partly tightened.
- **Red:** Loose. Hit it with the Hammer.

Use a green placement outline before building, then tighten the Bolts until they turn green. Hammering cannot fix an invalid placement.

![A scaffolding bolt being tightened with a Hammer.](/images/crack/crack-scaffolding-bolt-stability.webp)

Empty Toolboxes can be refilled at the Scaffolding Part Dispenser. Parts already used in a structure cannot be returned, so establish the Floor and Ladder positions before committing to additional braces.

### Do not block future Floors with D Beams

D Beams can strengthen a frame, but their placement matters.

A diagonal piece crossing a future Floor position can prevent that Floor from being installed. This is especially frustrating when you have already spent the time and materials building the lower levels.

A more reliable construction order is:

1. Establish the Poles and essential H Beams.
2. Place the Floors and Ladders needed for your route upward.
3. Check that you can reach and secure each connection.
4. Add D Beams where they reinforce the structure without obstructing access.

Building the supporting framework before finalizing the walkway can create a structure that looks reinforced but is difficult to climb.

Continue adding supported levels until the Work Order recognizes **10m**.

Completing the 10m Scaffolding assignment awards **$50** and unlocks the next Deposit Scanner upgrade task.

You do not need to build an enormous tower around the entire egg. A smaller, reachable and stable construction meeting the height requirement is enough to advance.

## Upgrade the Deposit Scanner and Find a Crystal

After completing the 10m construction assignment, return to the Deposit Scanner.

The scanner upgrade is available through its terminal. The required scanner upgrade costs **$50**.

Purchase the required scanner improvement and complete the terminal's purchase interaction.

Your next objective directs you to **find a crystal** within the deeper or upper parts of the egg.

A higher scaffolding position helps here because the deposit may be well above the initial ground-level opening.

### Scan upper and deeper sections

Aim the scanner at the upper half of the egg and begin looking for crystal deposits.

If the initial result does not reveal the objective, inspect which scanner improvements are available. Greater depth or coverage can reveal deposits that were not visible in an earlier scan.

Once a crystal appears, note its position relative to your current excavation.

![Deposit Scanner result showing crystal markers deeper inside the upper egg layers.](/images/crack/crack-scanner-crystal-location.webp)

*Use the detected crystal position to choose where to open the next passage instead of excavating an entire side of the egg.*

Climb the scaffolding and begin excavating toward the marked area. You may need to extend the opening inward before you can reach the material with the appropriate tool.

The earlier scaffold should now begin paying for itself: it gives you a stable starting point for deeper, elevated excavation rather than forcing you to rebuild a path every time a target appears above ground level.

### Keep the tunnel accessible

As the excavation becomes deeper, narrow passages start costing more time than the digging itself.

Make the opening wide enough to walk through. Clear obstructing edges before carrying heavy deposits out, and avoid making a deep vertical pocket from which you cannot easily climb back to the platform.

A Support Spike can preserve an important tunnel section against regrowth. For a long passage leading toward a required crystal or the center, protecting that opening can save substantial repeated excavation.

When you find **Chalazite**, recognize that it is also used to upgrade Workbots. You do not need to sell every valuable crystal you uncover.

Continue the listed Work Orders after the crystal objective is completed. The next major route blockers involve an Artery Sack, deeper material deposits, and further catalog unlocks.

</section>

<section id="artery-sack">

## How to Find and Burst an Artery Sack

An Artery Sack is different from a standard loose deposit.

When you expose one, the game indicates that it contains valuable resources and must be broken open. It is a larger embedded target, not a Bucket of material to sell directly.

You may find one deeper inside the egg, sometimes at an awkward height or behind layers that need to be removed first.

A useful approach is to perform a Deposit Scanner sweep before widening your excavation. Look for the appropriate target, then advance toward it from whichever existing entrance requires the least additional construction.

Once you can reach the sack, continue striking it until it bursts.

![An exposed Artery Sack embedded inside the excavated egg before being broken open.](/images/crack/crack-artery-sack.webp)

*An Artery Sack must be opened where it is embedded. Collect its contents afterward instead of looking for a normal Bucket deposit interaction.*

Breaking one open releases valuable material, including crystals that can be useful for later equipment upgrades.

Do not destroy access to the opening while chasing the last piece of material. A sack positioned high above the floor may be easier to open from a supported platform or the edge of a properly widened tunnel.

Completing the Artery Sack assignment opens an additional section of the Break Room.

### The Executive Catalog and the slot machine

The expanded Break Room contains more optional activities, but a later Work Order also uses the slot machine.

Win at least **$100 in a single slot-machine payout** to complete the Work Order and unlock the **Executive Catalog**. Smaller wins do not combine to satisfy this requirement.

The **Jackpot** achievement has a different condition: receiving the machine's maximum payout.

Do not confuse this with the earlier 3,500-point Eggsteroids requirement. They are separate arcade interactions.

The Executive Catalog adds additional equipment, including stronger explosives used for further excavation.

At this stage, prioritize the equipment necessary for opening deeper material rather than spending the rest of your balance on optional Break Room activities.

<section id="deep-excavation">

## Deep-Egg Progression: Calcite, Prime Tissue, Parasites, and Bone

By the time you reach the deeper layers, your work shifts away from the shallow Shell/Membrane loop.

The deeper material assignments require more excavation per delivery, but the stronger equipment can remove much larger sections at once.

Keep the Deposit Scanner useful throughout this phase. Its ability to locate a required material matters more than covering every exposed surface.

### Complete the Calcite deposit assignment

A later Work Order requires **$250 worth of Calcite**.

Find a deposit with the scanner, excavate toward the marked material, and begin collecting it.

When you expose a large pocket, clear enough space to access the entire deposit instead of stopping at the first few loose fragments.

This is a good time to use larger Buckets. They reduce the number of trips between deep excavations and the Depot.

Check that the Bucket contains the intended material before selling. Mixing up which Bucket holds which material can waste a trip when you are trying to meet a specific deposit target.

Once the cumulative Calcite requirement is met, follow the next displayed Work Order.

### Deposit $150 worth of Prime Tissue

The next material Work Order requires **$150 worth of Prime Tissue**.

Prime Tissue sits deeper within the specimen and requires suitable higher-tier excavation equipment.

Instead of opening another route from the exterior, continue through a sufficiently wide existing passage. This saves the time needed to remove a second set of outer layers.

Dynamite becomes increasingly useful for opening large pockets, but positioning matters. Place or throw it where the explosion can remove the obstructing material, then retreat before detonation.

Do not stand in the tunnel and wait to see what happens. The explosion can incapacitate you, and a poorly positioned charge may remove less useful material than expected.

Once Prime Tissue is exposed, collect the loose material and deliver it until the $150 assignment completes.

### Shred two Parasites

A later Work Order asks you to **shred two Parasites**.

This is often slower than an ordinary deposit task because you cannot simply collect loose Tissue until the counter fills.

Use the scanner to identify likely Parasite locations, then excavate until a Parasite becomes exposed.

When you pick one up, head directly to the Shredder.

Parasites can explode if carried for too long. Do not keep one in hand while exploring for the second target, checking equipment, or gathering unrelated resources.

Process the first Parasite before searching for the next one.

The completion of this assignment advances you toward the **Prograde Catalog**.

### Upgrade One Workbot to Unlock Prograde

The next Work Order requires a Workbot level upgrade using Chalazite. Bring a suitable crystal to a Workbot that can still receive an upgrade and apply it.

Once the upgrade registers, the **Prograde Catalog** becomes available.

The Prograde Catalog contains the Jackhammer, stronger excavation equipment, and **Dynamite Mk III**.

Dynamite Mk III costs **$69.99** and has a **seven-second fuse**. You need a Blowtorch to ignite it. Keep this equipment available as you approach Hatchite and the innermost Containment Plating.

![Dynamite Mk III in the Prograde Catalog, showing its price and seven-second fuse.](/images/crack/crack-prograde-dynamite.webp)

*Bring Dynamite Mk III and a Blowtorch before entering the final containment area.*

### Reach Bone and Hatchite

Further in, you encounter Bone deposits and material surrounding the innermost containment area.

Bone Geodes must be cracked with a suitable sharp or heavy excavation tool before their contents can be collected.

The stronger **Jackhammer** greatly reduces the work needed to break the tough material encountered at this depth.

After unlocking Prograde, complete the Work Order to **deposit $150 worth of Bone**. The reward is **$100**.

Break the Bone Geodes with a suitable high-tier tool, collect the material, and continue depositing it until the counter reaches $150.

A later assignment also requires **$300 worth of Hatchite**.

Hatchite is found toward the deeper interior, so you are now working close to the final objective. Continue opening accessible pockets and transporting the required material back to the Depot.

If your tunnel already exposes sufficient Bone or Hatchite, gather those deposits first. Starting a new tunnel through the outer egg solely to find the same material can add a large amount of unnecessary work.

Once the necessary deeper-layer Work Orders are completed, focus on reaching the containment structure itself.

## How to Break Containment Plating and Reach the Egg's Center

Eventually, ordinary excavation reaches a barrier that does not respond to normal tools.

When you reach the innermost barrier, the game displays **"Containment Plating Reached"** and warns that excavation tools are ineffective.

Stop using the Pickaxe, Shovel, or Jackhammer on this barrier. Bring Dynamite Mk III and a Blowtorch, then clear enough room to retreat safely before lighting the charge.

Before setting the charge, widen your approach to give yourself enough space to retreat. The area may be deep, poorly lit, and difficult to navigate if the earlier tunnel was built narrowly.

1. Open a clear path to the Containment Plating.
2. Bring the appropriate Dynamite into the passage.
3. Position the charge against the barrier.
4. Light it and move away immediately.
5. Return after the explosion and check whether the barrier has opened.

![Containment Plating reached, with a warning that excavation tools are ineffective.](/images/crack/crack-containment-plating-breach.webp)

*The warning confirms that ordinary excavation tools cannot break the barrier. Use explosives to continue.*

Dynamite Mk III has a **seven-second fuse**. Retreat immediately after lighting it; do not remain inside the narrow passage to collect materials or adjust equipment.

If the opening is incomplete, use another charge rather than trying to chip away the remaining plating with a Pickaxe.

Once the plating breaks, continue carefully toward the interior. The final objective is to expose and break the containment sack holding the specimen.

</section>

<section id="specimen">

## How to Release the Specimen and Finish CRACK

After the Containment Plating is breached, you still need to open the inner sack.

Clear enough of the surrounding material to reach it. The final opening can be awkward because the sack is surrounded by deep material, leaving little room to approach or maneuver.

Use the suitable late-game tools and explosives to expose the containment sack, then keep damaging it until the release objective completes.

![The laboratory warning that the specimen is loose and must be retrieved immediately.](/images/crack/crack-specimen-release.webp)

*Once the specimen escapes its containment sack, stop mining and retrieve it.*

Do not keep mining after that notification. Look around the newly opened area for the escaped specimen.

The creature is small enough to be easy to lose among the broken material. Follow its movement and catch it before trying to leave the excavation.

Once you have the specimen, the normal assignment directs you to transport it to the laboratory's holding and dispatch equipment.

**There are two possible endings.** Delivering the specimen completes the company's assignment; carrying it through the [chicken-marked hidden exit](/crack/endings) lets it escape.

For the standard company route, take the specimen to the indicated holding chamber and complete the delivery interaction. The processing sequence then plays and confirms that the assignment is finished.

The normal ending displays **Ending 1/2**.

</section>
