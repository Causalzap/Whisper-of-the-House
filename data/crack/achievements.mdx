**CRACK has 40 Steam achievements, including six hidden achievements.** Finishing Hatch Corp's assignment is only part of the challenge. Several achievements require deliberately failing workplace checks, surviving unusual specimen reactions, playing arcade games, or choosing what happens to the chicken at the end.

The difficult achievements are not all difficult for the same reason. **Well Rested** depends on how you handle every night of a complete run. **Team Player** requires another employee and a $1,000 transfer. **Snake Whisperer** is a separate arcade challenge that can remain unfinished long after the main excavation is complete.

## First Crack, Struck Gold, Burst Vessel, and the Final Egg Achievements

Five achievements are directly associated with working through the egg and reaching its innermost layers.

**First Crack** unlocks as soon as you break the egg's outer shell. It normally registers during the opening assignment, before the first major equipment unlock.

**Struck Gold** requires breaking a chunk off a **Gold Geode**. Finding a valuable deposit with the Scanner is not enough; reach the geode and physically break off a piece.

**Burst Vessel** requires opening an **Artery Sack**. These sacks contain valuable resources and are also part of the main Work Order progression. Strike the exposed sack until it bursts and releases its contents.

Near the center, two further achievements become available:

- **EGG: Containment Breach:** Break through the Containment Plating.
- **Yolked:** Break open the yolk sack.

**EGG: Containment Breach** registers when you break through Containment Plating with explosives. **Yolked** requires breaking the yolk sack farther inside; the first breach alone does not satisfy both conditions.

If excavation tools stop working at the plating, follow the [Containment Plating sequence](/crack/walkthrough) before attempting Yolked.

## How to Get the Workplace Audit, Inspection, and Shift Achievements

Several achievements depend on how you respond to Hatch Corp's workplace rules rather than how much of the egg you remove.

### Audit Passed and Corrective Action

A **Productivity Audit** sets a monetary target and a deadline. The active objective appears on-screen while you work.

To obtain **Audit Passed**, deposit enough qualifying material to meet the required target before the deadline. Check the dollar amount displayed by the audit instead of estimating it from the number of Buckets you have carried.

![A CRACK productivity audit requiring $86 in earnings before the 16:30 deadline.](/images/crack/crack-achievements-productivity-audit.webp)

*Meet the displayed earnings target before the deadline for Audit Passed, or let the audit fail for Corrective Action.*

**Corrective Action** requires the opposite result: fail a productivity audit.

The two achievements cannot be obtained from the same audit outcome. When a suitable audit begins, decide whether you are aiming to pass or fail it before selling the materials already waiting at the Resource Depot.

Failing deliberately can result in an unpleasant company penalty, so do not choose that outcome during a shift when you are relying on the earnings for an important equipment purchase.

Once Corrective Action registers, handle later audits normally rather than repeatedly losing money for the same objective.

### Nice and Tidy — Pass a Workplace Inspection

**Nice and Tidy** requires passing a workplace inspection.

When Hatch Corp orders a cleanup, remove surplus equipment and other items that the inspection considers out of place. The Shredder can dispose of unwanted tools, so check around your work area and the equipment you have left behind.

An inspection is not the same as a productivity audit. Selling valuable Tissue may help with an earnings target, but it will not substitute for cleaning the workplace when an inspection is active.

After the inspection completes, check whether the achievement registers. Do not buy replacement tools until you have identified which existing equipment is actually worth keeping.

### #1 Plumber — Fix All Burst Pipes

**#1 Plumber** requires fixing every burst pipe during a pipe rupture event.

When the laboratory reports burst pipes, interrupt ordinary excavation and locate the damaged sections. Continue repairing until the event indicates that all required pipes have been fixed.

Stopping after the first repair is insufficient if other damaged pipes remain elsewhere in the facility.

Keep track of the active event's status rather than assuming that the disappearance of one leak means the entire job is complete.

### Tenure and Well Rested Are Different

**Tenure** unlocks after **10 worked shifts**. It can accumulate through ordinary play as you return to work each day.

**Well Rested** is more demanding: you must **sleep in your Employee Quarters every night**.

A reliable way to approach Well Rested is to treat it as a full-run requirement:

1. Begin the run with the intention of sleeping properly each night.
2. Watch the clock near the end of every shift.
3. Return to your assigned quarters and use the bed before the nightly work cycle is missed.
4. Repeat this throughout the assignment.
5. Complete the normal specimen delivery while preserving that sleeping routine.

![The end-of-shift warning directing employees to return to their assigned quarters.](/images/crack/crack-achievements-return-to-quarters.webp)

*Return to Employee Quarters before missing your nightly sleep when working toward Well Rested.*

Well Rested has been awarded at the completion of a run in which the player slept in the quarters every night, rather than simply after ten ordinary shifts.

Being knocked out by sleep gas or staying at work until the company forces a shift to end should not be treated as equivalent to using your bed. If you have already missed a night, a fresh run with a consistent sleep schedule is the more dependable approach.

### Professional Intern — 10% Dream Bonus on $0 Profit

**Professional Intern** has two simultaneous conditions:

- The shift's profit is **$0**.
- The Dream Bonus awarded is **10%**.

Having a bank balance of $0 is not the same as reporting $0 profit for the shift.

For this achievement, pay attention to the shift's earnings and the bonus calculation. Arrange a shift without profitable deposits, return to the quarters for sleep, and check the final profit and Dream Bonus result.

Do not assume that an ordinary profitable shift qualifies merely because the bonus percentage is correct. Both conditions must apply together.

### Seized Assets — Lose More Than Half Your Money

**Seized Assets** requires losing **more than 50% of your money** during a wealth redistribution event.

The loss is part of the achievement condition, so spending your balance down to almost nothing before the event may make the required loss harder to demonstrate.

When the redistribution event occurs, check your balance and the amount deducted. The achievement concerns the proportion lost, not just whether Hatch Corp took any money.

Because the event is not a permanent shop option you can select whenever you want, it is worth tracking separately from the ordinary spending achievements.

## Automation, IT Specialist, and Overclocked

**Automation** unlocks when you power on a Workbot. One activated robot is sufficient; a complete Collector-and-Delivery fleet is not required.

**IT Specialist** requires **10 Workbot repairs**. Use a Wrench on damaged robots as their Condition deteriorates. Buying new robots does not count as repairing them.

**Overclocked** requires raising one Workbot to **Level 5**. You can combine Chalazite upgrades on the same robot, including the +3 levels granted by Purple Chalazite. Upgrading several robots to lower levels does not satisfy the achievement.

If a robot remains idle despite having healthy Charge and Condition, check its available Buckets and jobs using the [Workbot troubleshooting steps](/crack/workbots) before assuming it needs repairs.

## Eggsteroids, Jackpot, Snake Whisperer, and Arcade Master

The Break Room contains several independent achievement requirements. Progress in one arcade game does not automatically count toward another.

### High Score and Get Back to Work — Eggsteroids

Two achievements require particular Eggsteroids scores:

- **High Score:** Reach **10,000 points**.
- **Get Back to Work:** Reach **50,000 points**.

The early main assignment only requires a score of **3,500** on Eggsteroids. That is enough for the Work Order, but not for either of these achievements.

Return to the cabinet after unlocking the Break Room and play for the higher scores. Your target is the game's displayed score, not the total points you have accumulated over unrelated attempts.

The 50,000-point challenge is substantially more demanding, so leave it for a dedicated arcade attempt if your immediate priority is finishing the egg.

### Jackpot — Maximum Slot Machine Payout

**Jackpot** requires receiving the **maximum payout** on a slot machine.

The Executive Catalog Work Order asks you to win **$100 on a single slot machine payout**. That is a separate requirement from Jackpot.

Do not automatically mark Jackpot complete just because the Executive Catalog unlocks. Check that the slot machine actually awards its maximum payout.

![A CRACK slot machine paying $250 and unlocking the Executive Catalog.](/images/crack/crack-achievements-slot-jackpot.webp)

*A $250 slot machine win unlocks the Executive Catalog. The Jackpot achievement separately requires the machine's maximum payout.*

The slot machines become accessible through the Breakroom Extension, unlocked by bursting an Artery Sack.

A pill effect called **Feeling Lucky** improves gambling odds. If you obtain it from the pill machine, consider using its active period for slot attempts rather than spending money on unrelated tasks while the effect runs.

A favorable effect does not guarantee a maximum payout, so check the actual result before continuing.

### Nothing But Net — Score 10 Baskets

**Nothing But Net** requires **10 successful baskets** in the Break Room.

Use the basketball and hoop rather than confusing the achievement with any arcade cabinet's score counter.

A successful shot counts as a basket. Continue until ten baskets have been registered; there is no need to assume the whole requirement must be fulfilled with one throw or a particular trick shot.

If you are missing this achievement after finishing the main game, return to the Break Room and complete the basketball activity directly.

### Arcade Master — Win Every Two-Player Arcade Game

**Arcade Master** requires defeating a coworker in every qualifying two-player arcade game.

Arrange a multiplayer session and keep track of the games you have won. Starting a match is not enough; you must beat the other employee. Eggsteroids high scores do not replace these head-to-head victories.

### Snake Whisperer — Play a Perfect Game of Snake

**Snake Whisperer** requires a perfect game of Snake. Play the Snake cabinet in the expanded arcade area and finish its full objective.

A normal high score does not establish a perfect completion. The achievement description does not specify a numerical threshold, so check whether Snake Whisperer actually unlocks before marking it complete.

## Hit and Run, Egg on Your Face, and Team Player

Three achievements require interacting with another employee in specific ways. Plan them during a session when at least one coworker is present.

### Hit and Run — Use a Vehicle

**Hit and Run** requires running over a coworker with a vehicle.

Unlock access to the Vehicle Bay, obtain a drivable vehicle, and coordinate with the other player so they are on foot in a position where the vehicle can strike them.

Simply driving past someone or colliding with another vehicle may not satisfy an achievement explicitly requiring a coworker to be run over.

### Egg on Your Face — Hit a Coworker's Head

**Egg on Your Face** requires striking a coworker **in the head with an egg**.

This is not the same as throwing an object anywhere near another player or having it land at their feet.

When you have an egg that can be thrown, have the other player stand still and aim for the head. Confirm the achievement before moving on, rather than assuming any successful object collision qualifies.

### Team Player — Transfer $1,000

**Team Player** requires transferring **$1,000 to a coworker**.

Keep enough money in your account, meet the other player at the transfer interaction, and send the required amount.

The goal is a money transfer, not purchasing $1,000 worth of equipment for the laboratory or dropping valuable materials beside another player.

A successful $1,000 transfer has unlocked Team Player during multiplayer play. Because that is a substantial early-game expense, handle it when losing the cash will not delay tools you still need for the main assignment.

**Arcade Master** also requires a coworker, but it is best completed separately while both players are already visiting the arcade area.

## Out Cold, Structural Collapse, Hot Potato, and Other Hazard Achievements

Some of CRACK's achievements reward events that you would normally try to avoid.

### Out Cold — Fall 10 Meters After Sleep Gas

**Out Cold** requires a **10-meter fall after the sleep gas knocks you out**.

The important sequence is that the sleep gas incapacitates you and the fall follows it. A normal jump from a tall platform does not satisfy the achievement's stated condition.

Build or use Scaffolding with enough height, and arrange to be on the elevated structure when the sleep gas event occurs.

![The sleep aid countdown beginning while the player is positioned above the laboratory floor.](/images/crack/crack-achievements-sleep-gas-fall.webp)

*Stay at least 10 meters above the floor when the sleep gas takes effect. Out Cold requires falling after being knocked unconscious.*

Remain on the elevated platform through the countdown. The achievement registers only when the sleep gas knocks you unconscious and the resulting fall meets the required distance; jumping voluntarily does not replace that sequence.

### Structural Collapse — Go Down With the Scaffolding

**Structural Collapse** requires being on a scaffolding structure when it collapses.

Standing safely on the floor and watching a structure fail does not meet the stated requirement.

Scaffolding can be affected by instability and specimen reactions. Remain on the structure when the collapse occurs so your character goes down with it.

Removing or loosening a random support does not guarantee the exact achievement event. The required result is a genuine collapse while you are caught on the structure.

Avoid dismantling a large, useful construction solely for this achievement if a smaller expendable platform will let you attempt the condition without destroying your main access route.

### Hot Potato — Hold a Parasite Too Long

**Hot Potato** requires keeping a Parasite in your hands beyond the safe handling period.

Normally, a Parasite should be carried quickly to the Shredder. For this achievement, deliberately hold one long enough for the dangerous reaction to occur.

Do not attempt this with a valuable tool or another item you cannot afford to lose still being carried. The Parasite may explode, and holding it is the entire point of the challenge.

### Explosive Personality — Your Own Dynamite

**Explosive Personality** requires being caught in the blast of **your own Dynamite**.

A blast placed or ignited by another employee is not necessarily equivalent to the self-inflicted explosion specified by the achievement.

Use a charge you set yourself and remain within its explosive range. The achievement is tied to being caught in the explosion, not simply using Dynamite successfully on the egg.

### Swallowed — Tentacle Reaction

**Swallowed** requires being pulled in by a tentacle.

This is associated with the specimen's tentacle reaction, not ordinary tunnel excavation or a fall through broken material.

When the reaction occurs, being caught by a tentacle is the required outcome. If you normally retreat whenever the egg becomes agitated, this achievement may remain incomplete despite many shifts of work.

### Pest Control — Squish 25 Beetles

**Pest Control** requires squishing **25 beetles**.

Beetles can appear during specimen reactions. Locate the creatures and strike them with a suitable tool before they disperse.

You are counting squashed beetles, not the number of beetle events or the amount of material mined during the reaction.

When a beetle event begins, prioritize catching as many as you can rather than continuing unrelated excavation. Multiple appearances may be needed to reach the total.

## Hidden Rooms and Paper Trail: Four Secret Achievements

Bad Listener, Cold Case, Access Granted, and Paper Trail require exploring restricted areas and recovering hidden employee records. Well Done and Free Range are awarded for the specimen's two possible outcomes.

### Bad Listener — Enter the Do Not Enter Door

**Bad Listener** unlocks when you enter the forbidden door in the Break Room.

Find the boarded KEEP OUT entrance, clear the obstruction, and enter the room.

The achievement registers for entering the restricted area; you do not need to complete the specimen escape ending at that exact moment.

The passage behind this room also leads toward the alternate ending. Follow the [Do Not Enter passage and chicken-marked exit](/crack/endings) when you are ready to help the specimen escape.

### Cold Case — Open the Suspicious Vent

**Cold Case** requires opening the suspicious ventilation hatch.

Reach the elevated vent near the Break Room and remove the cover's fasteners with a Hammer.

Opening the hatch is the achievement's objective. The duct also contains the UV Flashlight needed for the Redacted security-room puzzle.

### Access Granted — Enter the Redacted Room

**Access Granted** requires gaining access to the **Redacted Room**.

Find the UV Flashlight, reveal the three sets of hidden digits, and enter the completed six-digit combination at the security door.

The passcode changes between playthroughs. Entering another player's six numbers is not a dependable solution.

The [CRACK Redacted door solution](/crack/redacted-door-code) identifies the flashlight, all three clue locations, and the correct way to arrange the digits.

### Paper Trail — Find Every Clipboard

**Paper Trail** requires finding **every Clipboard**.

The relevant documents are distributed through the laboratory, including areas that normal assignment progression does not require you to investigate closely.

Check these locations carefully:

- **Ventilation duct above the Break Room:** Examine the former employee's notes near the UV Flashlight.
- **Room behind the KEEP OUT door:** Pick up the Clipboard explaining that the old Break Room passage was blocked by dirt.
- **Top of the secret passage:** Find the Clipboard beside the chicken-marked exit. It describes the previous employees' unsuccessful attempts to open the door.
- **Redacted security room:** Examine the Supervisor Duties Clipboard after entering the correct six-digit code.

The Clipboard beside the chicken-marked exit and the one near the KEEP OUT entrance are separate documents. Do not leave the passage after collecting only the first.

The Redacted security room is another easily missed location. Opening its door grants Access Granted, but you must also examine the Clipboard inside for Paper Trail.

These locations provide a practical collection route, but do not assume the list establishes the total number of Clipboards. Check the achievement's unlock before marking Paper Trail complete.

## Well Done and Free Range — Both Ending Achievements

The final specimen can be delivered through two different routes.

**Well Done** unlocks by delivering the specimen to Hatch Corp. Capture the chicken, bring it to the Holding Chamber, and complete the company's processing and dispatch sequence. This corresponds to **Ending 1/2**.

**Free Range** unlocks by helping the specimen escape through the secret route. Open the passage behind the Break Room's restricted door, reach the chicken-marked exit, and bring the living specimen there. This corresponds to **Ending 2/2**.

The two achievements require different outcomes. Reaching the specimen and capturing it does not award both.

For the complete choice, door locations, hidden passage, and specimen delivery steps, use the [CRACK endings walkthrough](/crack/endings).

## Junkie, Fashion Police, and Great Asset

Three remaining achievements involve systems that may be easy to ignore while concentrating on excavation.

### Junkie — Try Every Pill Type

**Junkie** requires trying **every pill type**.

Use the pill dispenser in the Break Room and take the available pills across your shifts. Pills can produce different effects, including faster tool swings, improved gambling odds, and a Placebo effect that does nothing.

![The Break Room pill dispenser used to obtain different temporary pill effects.](/images/crack/crack-achievements-pill-dispenser.webp)

*Try the available pill types until Junkie unlocks. Repeating a familiar effect is not proof that every type has been tried.*

This is a collection of distinct experiences, not simply a requirement to take an arbitrary large number of pills.

Keep track of the names or effects you have already received. When you encounter a new one, try it and check the achievement.

Do not assume a specific number of pill purchases guarantees completion. The achievement requires all types, and a repeated result does not establish that the missing type has been obtained.

### Fashion Police — Buy Every Lab Coat Color

**Fashion Police** requires buying **all available lab coat colors**.

Go through the lab coat color options in the employee customization or purchasing area and buy the remaining colors.

Changing into a different coat is not the same as purchasing every variation. Check which color options are still locked or unpurchased.

Because the complete wardrobe can be expensive, treat this as a later spending goal rather than exhausting your starting funds before obtaining required excavation equipment.

### Great Asset — Earn $10,000 for the Company

**Great Asset** requires earning **$10,000 for Hatch Corp**.

Keep depositing valuable material and completing productive work until the company's earnings requirement is met.

The achievement is about money earned for the company, not transferring $10,000 to another employee or merely carrying that amount of cash in your account.

Efficient extraction of high-value deposits becomes more useful than selling only Shell once stronger equipment reaches deeper parts of the egg.

For this achievement, prioritize actual successful deposits over an impressive pile of unsold resources. Material left inside Buckets that have not been processed at the Depot has not yet contributed to a completed sale.

## CRACK 100% Completion: Which Achievements Should You Finish First?

Once the main assignment is complete, the remaining achievements fall into three practical groups.

**First, protect the run-specific requirement.** Well Rested is easiest to plan from the beginning of a run, not after realizing that an earlier night was missed. Combine it with a normal specimen delivery so the condition can register at the end.

**Second, arrange actions that need another employee or a particular event.** Team Player, Hit and Run, Egg on Your Face, and Arcade Master depend on a coworker. Audit failures, pipe repairs, beetles, tentacles, and redistribution require the appropriate workplace situation.

**Finally, work on deliberate repetition and arcade skill.** Ten Workbot repairs, 25 beetles, every pill type, the 50,000-point Eggsteroids score, and a perfect Snake game are different kinds of unfinished progress. Do not spend time trying to advance one through actions that only count toward another.

Use the checklist's unfinished filter to keep those remaining goals visible. When an achievement does not appear, return to its exact action or threshold rather than repeating the entire main assignment.
