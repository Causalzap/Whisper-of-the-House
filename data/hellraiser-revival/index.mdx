<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
If you are stuck in the **Hellraiser: Revival demo**, keep following the active
objective instead of trying to clear every locked container.

The first major trap is the looping apartment. When the clue says **Follow the
Darkness**, wait for the room to go dark, turn around, and walk back through the
door you just used.

Later, keep the main route moving through the Scarlet Church. The four-digit
locked container is not required to reach the end of the demo.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="demo-walkthrough">
  Hellraiser Revival demo walkthrough
</h2>

The opening looks almost completely linear, but the demo starts testing route
reading surprisingly early. Rooms change behind me, familiar spaces loop, and
several locked objects appear before I have anything that can open them. Once I
reached the Scarlet Church, the same idea carried through the rest of the run:
the fastest route was usually to solve the obstacle tied to my current
objective, take useful supplies on the way, and leave unrelated locks alone
until I actually found an item that matched them.

My progression was:

**follow Sunny into the Labyrinth → break the apartment loop → use the Genesis
Configuration to cross the Labyrinth → free Aiden inside the Scarlet Church →
complete the Blemished Fragment and Blemished Diamond lock → get the Tongue Key
→ sneak through Bruno's room → take the keycard → survive the armed cultists →
follow the power-outage clue to Room 206 → collect the ornament pieces → solve
the rotating object → take the duck to the final door.**

That route reaches the end without solving every container in the compound.

<h2 id="follow-sunny">
  Where do you go after Sunny enters the Labyrinth?
</h2>

Follow Sunny through the opening and keep moving through the first Labyrinth
sequence. This part teaches crouching, sprinting, sliding, and dodging while the
environment changes around you. I wasted more time by treating it like a fixed
maze than I did by simply following the newly opened route.

The first genuine progression trap appears when the path drops me back into the
apartment.

The room loops. Sunny's pictures change, the books change, and walking through
the obvious doorway keeps bringing me back into the same space. There is no
light switch I need to find and no small object hidden on a shelf.

The clue is **Follow the Darkness**.

Wait until the light cuts out. As soon as the room goes dark, turn around 180
degrees and go back through the doorway behind you. Walking forward again only
continues the loop.

the open Labyrinth.

<h2 id="labyrinth-fire-puzzle">
  How do you solve the Genesis Configuration Labyrinth puzzle?
</h2>

The Genesis Configuration gains its first useful environmental interaction in
the next Labyrinth section. I can pull fire from glowing objects and throw it
into highlighted targets, but the interaction has limited range. Standing far
back from the mechanism and trying to solve the entire room from safety did not
work.

The important obstacle is the large movable Labyrinth element.

First, use the Configuration to raise the highlighted block. While that part of
the structure is still elevated, move the interaction to the next highlighted
point. The structure shifts into a position that creates the route across the
gap.

The blue block initially looked like something I should climb onto or somehow
carry forward. It is not. Its job is to reposition the surrounding structure so
Aiden can cross.

Keep going once the path is open. There is no reason to remain here looking for
another Configuration interaction.

<h2 id="escape-scarlet-church">
  How do you escape the Scarlet Church?
</h2>

After the next major story sequence, Aiden wakes inside the Scarlet Church and
the objective changes to **Free Yourself**.

Remove the restraint, break the stitches when prompted, and start checking the
immediate rooms for materials. The game introduces crafting almost immediately,
but it deliberately gives me partial recipes before I have enough materials to
make everything.

That means a missing ingredient is not automatically a signal to backtrack.

The first useful benchmark is the Bandage recipe: **one Bandage requires three
Cloth**. Until I had all three, the recipe remained unavailable. I kept taking
Cloth from the rooms on my route instead of circling the same corridor hoping
another piece had spawned.

Save points also begin appearing inside the compound. I use one whenever the
route is about to shift into stealth or combat, especially before carrying a
new progression item into an unexplored area.

<h2 id="blemished-diamond">
  How do the Blemished Fragment and Blemished Diamond work?
</h2>

The first strange lock in the Scarlet Church is a two-piece mechanism.

One pickup is identified as a **Blemished Fragment** and tells you that it
should fit into a slot somewhere. Reaching the lock with only that piece does
not finish the mechanism, so the correct response is to keep searching the same
local section rather than retreating through the entire compound.

The second matching pickup is the **Blemished Diamond**.

The door opens into the next part of the Church.

This section is compact enough that I do not treat the missing second piece as a
reason for a large backtrack. Both parts belong to the same local progression
block.

<h2 id="tongue-key">
  How do you get the Tongue Key?
</h2>

The next puzzle centers on a skull mechanism and a clue that contrasts the head
with the heart.

The text makes the important distinction: the answer is tied to the **heart**
and to passion and life, not to the head, thought, or reason. That clue leads
into the skull interaction.

The skull has nails that must be pressed in a particular sequence. Completing
that interaction releases the **Tongue Key**.

Do not carry the Tongue Key around looking for a normal keyhole. The matching
door is visually obvious once you know what the item is for: it has a mouth-like
lock.

Use the key there and continue into Bruno's section.

<h2 id="bruno-keycard">
  How do you get past Bruno and find the keycard?
</h2>

Bruno changes the pace from puzzle solving to timing.

I stay crouched and watch him before moving. He spends enough time near
Charlotte that it is tempting to assume he will remain there, but his patrol
does continue. Crossing the room while he is still turning back toward me is
the easiest way to turn a clean stealth section into a chase.

Once the objective becomes **Find the Keycard**, the card is on a table inside
his patrol space. Wait for Bruno to commit to moving away, cross behind him,
take the card, and leave.

I do not stop to loot the entire room while he is still active.

There is also a **four-digit locked container** around this stretch of the
compound. I never found a required four-digit combination before completing the
demo, and the main route continues without opening it. When I reached it early,
I marked the location mentally and moved on instead of repeatedly checking old
notes or dead enemies for a code.

<h2 id="combat-and-crafting">
  What should you craft and which resources should you save?
</h2>

Combat becomes much more expensive after Bruno. Enemies begin carrying melee
weapons and firearms, some targets wear armor, and a missed stealth takedown can
pull several cultists into the same fight.

Health comes first. **Three Cloth make one Bandage**, while food, drinks, and
herbs found in the environment can also restore health. When a nearby consumable
can cover the damage, I use that before spending materials on another Bandage.

The pistol changes what I save. **Scrap and Gunpowder are used to craft pistol
ammunition**, so I stop treating Scrap as junk once firearms enter the route.
Ammo matters much more when I have to deal with a gunner from across a room.

Weapon repair is separate. **Repair Kits restore durability**, so I keep them
for a melee weapon I actually intend to continue using rather than assuming
Scrap is the repair resource.

Bladed weapons work well for bleeding and dismemberment, but armor changes the
equation. Against a protected enemy, I switch weapons, use the environment, or
create space for the pistol instead of grinding an entire blade into the same
armor.

Coins appear frequently and are easy to collect from the route. I take them
when they are safe, but I never hit a purchase requirement that blocks the demo
progression. Clearing a dangerous room again just to recover a few missed coins
is not worth the risk.

**If the door locks due to the power outage, override the fuse box in Room

until you reach **206**. There are supplies in this corridor, but the room
numbers are a better navigation tool than opening every door along the way.

I heal, reload, and repair before triggering the next interaction if my
resources are low. The route becomes hostile again quickly, so standing next to
the objective with an empty pistol and damaged melee weapon is an avoidable
problem.

<h2 id="final-puzzle">
  How do you solve the final ornament and duck puzzle?
</h2>

The last exploration section is where the map becomes most useful.

Several rooms connect back into one another, and at least one locked container
can still distract from the actual objective. I use the map to identify rooms
that still contain something useful instead of running the entire floor in a
loop.

One ornament piece appears during the later search, with another matching part
found in a nearby room. Once I have the required pieces, I return toward the
earlier room where the floor changed.

Inspect the assembled object and rotate its parts until the shapes align.

Completing that interaction gives the **duck**.

The duck is not a joke collectible I can ignore. It is the progression item for
the final route.

the duck back to the matching slot, use it, and go through the newly opened

<h2 id="where-demo-ends">
  Where does the Hellraiser Revival demo end?
</h2>

The demo ends as Aiden reaches the club after opening the final route with the
duck.

You do not need to empty every room, solve the four-digit container, spend all
of your coins, or exhaust every crafting material before going through.

That is the most important distinction in the back half of the demo. Scarlet
Church looks increasingly open, but the critical path remains much narrower
than the number of locked doors and side objects suggests.

Once the duck opens the club route, I consider the playable progression
complete.

The full game releases on **October 8, 2026**. The demo already introduces the
Genesis Configuration, crafting, weapon durability, firearms, stealth,
lockpicking, map exploration, and resource management, but it stops before
those systems fully open up.
