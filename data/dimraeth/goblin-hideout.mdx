<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
For **Find the Goblin Hideout**, make **Wildwood Center** your reference point and stay on the surface. **Wildwood Caverns is a convincing wrong turn**; reaching it does not advance the Hideout objective. In the route I followed, the nearby **Farmstead General** became the easiest landmark to navigate from: after dealing with that area, I continued **north** and reached the correct goblin section soon afterward. You will know the search is over when the quest changes from **Find the Goblin Hideout** to **Clear the Raid**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="find-goblin-hideout">
  Why Find the Goblin Hideout is easy to overcomplicate
</h2>

The map gets you into the right part of Wildwood without showing the final approach clearly enough to make the route obvious. That would be a minor inconvenience in a cleaner region, but this section is crowded with ruins, caves, goblin encounters, side objectives, and paths that all look plausible enough to investigate.

My first search drifted northeast through nearby ruins because the terrain looked like it ought to conceal an entrance. Later, with **Find the Goblin Hideout** specifically tracked, I still ended up checking more than one route before the quest moved.

The useful breakthrough was not finding a secret door. It was recognizing which landmarks belonged to the route and which ones merely happened to be nearby.

For this search, the most useful sequence was:

**Wildwood Center → avoid Wildwood Caverns → Farmstead area → north → Clear the Raid**

That is a much more reliable way to read this section than following every goblin or cave entrance that appears along the way.

<h2 id="wildwood-center">
  Re-center the search at Wildwood Center
</h2>

Once **Wildwood Center** appeared, I stopped widening the search into the rest of the region. The Hideout was somewhere around this part of Wildwood; the remaining problem was choosing the correct branch.

This matters because the quest name encourages exactly the wrong assumption. A “hideout” sounds like somewhere tucked into rock or concealed behind a narrow passage, so searching walls and cave entrances feels sensible. In practice, that instinct pulled me away from the route that advanced the objective.

If Wildwood Center is already discovered, I would work outward from there instead of restarting the search from whichever waypoint or quest happened to be active previously.

<h2 id="wildwood-caverns">
  Wildwood Caverns looks right and leads the search in the wrong direction
</h2>

The most persuasive false lead is **Wildwood Caverns**.

It sits close enough to the search area, has exactly the sort of entrance that looks appropriate for a hidden goblin base, and rewards curiosity by giving the player somewhere substantial to explore. None of that made it the solution to **Find the Goblin Hideout** in my run.

I would use the Caverns as a boundary marker rather than another place to clear. If the location name appears and the quest still reads **Find the Goblin Hideout**, turn back toward the surface routes around Wildwood Center.

There is no indication from this run that completing the Caverns is required to reveal the Hideout. Clearing it simply because it looks relevant can turn a short navigation problem into an unnecessary dungeon detour.

<h2 id="farmstead-general">
  The Farmstead is a useful landmark, not a confirmed prerequisite
</h2>

**Defeat the General at the Farmstead** was active in the same part of my route, and finishing it gave me the cleanest point from which to continue searching.

That does **not** establish the General as a hard requirement for the Goblin Hideout. The game did not explicitly connect the two objectives, and both could be present at the same time. I would not tell someone to kill the General because the Hideout supposedly cannot appear otherwise.

What the Farmstead provides is orientation.

Before reaching it, I was comparing several ruins, paths, and cave approaches without a dependable starting point. After the General fight, the route I followed became simple enough to repeat: I left that section and continued **north** instead of swinging back toward the Caverns.

Within roughly a minute, the goblin progression finally lined up with the tracked objective.

For anyone already doing the Farmstead quest, that makes it a better navigation reference than another vague instruction to “search around Wildwood Center.”

<h2 id="route-from-farmstead">
  From the Farmstead area, keep the route north and stay above ground
</h2>

The successful approach did not involve another hidden cave entrance. I came out of the Farmstead section, pushed **north**, and stopped peeling off toward every ruin that looked suspicious.

A repeat attempt would look like this:

1. Track **Find the Goblin Hideout**.
2. Get back into the **Wildwood Center** area.
3. Keep the search on the surface.
4. Turn around if the route drops into **Wildwood Caverns**.
5. Use the **Farmstead** as an orientation point if that objective is still available.
6. Leave the Farmstead section heading **north**.
7. Continue until the quest itself confirms that the Hideout has been reached.

The northward movement is the part that finally removed the guesswork from my run. Before that, I had been treating the Hideout as a hidden-entrance puzzle; afterward, it behaved more like a route-finding problem with one particularly convincing side path.

<h2 id="clear-the-raid">
  Clear the Raid is the confirmation that matters
</h2>

Seeing goblins does not prove you have found the quest location. Wildwood has enough goblin activity elsewhere that enemy type alone is a poor indicator.

The reliable confirmation is the objective text.

When I reached the correct section, **Find the Goblin Hideout** changed to **Clear the Raid**.

Once that wording appears, stop searching. There is no second hidden approach to discover before the raid can be cleared; the navigation portion of the objective has already succeeded.

That distinction is especially useful after spending several minutes lost, because it prevents the opposite mistake: reaching the right area, seeing the quest remain unfinished because enemies are still alive, and assuming the entrance must still be somewhere else.

<h2 id="raid-trouble">
  Clear the Raid on screen means exploration is no longer the answer
</h2>

If **Clear the Raid** is active and the objective will not finish because the enemies are winning, the character has moved into a different kind of problem.

At that point I check the things that affect the fight directly: available healing, inventory load, whether the current damage setup is actually coming together, and whether I arrived already depleted from the search. Encumbrance was becoming noticeable during this stretch of Wildwood, so entering the raid with a nearly full inventory can create an avoidable interruption when the area starts dropping more gear and materials.

There is little value in retracing the map once the objective has updated. A weak damage loop or poor resource state needs to be fixed as a combat problem; the <Link href="/dimraeth/builds">builds and attributes guide</Link> covers the attribute, skill, status, and equipment decisions that matter there.

<h2 id="after-hideout">
  Clearing the Hideout feeds directly into the goblin story
</h2>

The raid is not a regional finale. It removes one of the Wildwood progression blocks and pushes the goblin storyline toward its larger confrontation.

If other Wildwood objectives are still mixed into the quest log, the <Link href="/dimraeth/wildwood-walkthrough">Wildwood walkthrough</Link> shows where the Hideout sits inside the full region route. For the immediate goblin chain, the next major checkpoint is the **Goblin King** progression, including the later objective to collect explosives before the fight.

Once that stage is active, the <Link href="/dimraeth/goblin-king">Goblin King guide</Link> takes over.

The one status check worth remembering from the Hideout search is straightforward: **Find the Goblin Hideout** means the route is still unresolved; **Clear the Raid** means you have arrived.
