<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
DREADMOOR has **44 Steam achievements**: 18 story-related objectives and 26 other challenges. Fishing, refueling, sailing, crafting, and collecting resources all have separate requirements. Check the exact action or total for each achievement, then mark it complete when it unlocks in Steam.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Several achievements reward actions you will perform during the story, but the longer milestones require more attention. Refueling your boat for the first time, for example, is different from refueling it 50 times. Crafting four modules also does not count as having four self-crafted modules installed together.

Start with the checklist to identify what remains locked. The sections below distinguish achievements that depend on a particular story event from those that count repeated catches, repairs, travel, or crafting.

## Which Achievements Unlock During the Main Story?

Several achievements are tied to the actions required to advance through DREADMOOR's Early Access storyline.

The opening objectives introduce the boat, river gates, fishing, and Dry Dock. Later tasks require improved equipment, access through The Throat, and investigations involving the Fuel Larva, Drying Fort, and other locations.


### Opening Achievements

**Captain of Your Own Ship — Give the old boat a new life.**

Restore the abandoned boat at Church Settlement and get it ready to sail. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">opening walkthrough</Link> covers the required generator and crane sequence.

**Water Finds a Way — Open the first river gate.**

Open the first river gate on the route to Betsy and Sons. Interacting with its controls is not enough; the gate must actually open before you move on.

**Snap, Crackle, Catch! — Catch your first fish.**

Land your first fish at the Trading Post. You do not need to finish the entire five-Snapper contract to meet this achievement's requirement. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Fishing Guide</Link> explains the rod controls and Line Tension.

**A Matter of Distillation — Return John the Fifth's source of inspiration.**

Complete John the Fifth's Moonshine Still objective. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">main quest route</Link> covers the required repairs and the Dry Dock unlock.


### Boat Repair and Engine Achievements

**Full Tank — Refuel your boat, paying with... lunch.**

Complete your first story-related refueling objective. This is separate from **Top It Off**, **Regular Customer**, and **Larva on Overtime**, which require repeated refueling.

**No Polyps, No Leaks — Get your boat back in shape.**

Finish the required boat restoration at the Dry Dock. Cleaning polyps alone does not replace the hull repairs needed to complete the objective.

**Handy with Tools — Craft and install an upgraded engine.**

Both actions matter: craft an upgraded engine and install it on your boat. Leaving the completed engine in storage does not meet the installation requirement.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Dry Dock and engine-upgrade route</Link> covers the necessary quest steps.

### The Throat and Later Story Objectives

**Easy There, Beaver — Convince the master of the channel to let you pass.**

Resolve the obstruction caused by the Beaver during the approach to The Throat.

**Across the Throat — Force the old gates to let you through.**

Open the old gates at The Throat. Passing the Beaver and opening the gate are two different achievement objectives.

**Don't Bite the Hand That Fuels You — Cure the Fuel Larva.**

Cure the Fuel Larva. Feeding it for fuel does not satisfy an achievement that specifically requires treatment.

Follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">main story walkthrough</Link> for the sound-related objectives, gate repairs, and Fuel Larva treatment.

## How Do You Unlock Key Specialist, Breathe Deep, and Family Archive?

These achievements are associated with later investigations, after the story has introduced the Baron's assignment involving Junior and the missing Essence recipe.

### Key Specialist — Open a Door Without Its Key

**Key Specialist** requires opening a door without possessing its key.

The locked Control Room encountered during the Drying Fort investigation is a relevant place to use Lockpicks. Follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Nick the Hermit and Drying Fort objectives</Link> to obtain the necessary tool.

Unlike **Other People's Secrets**, which requires ten different locks, Key Specialist calls for opening a single door without its key.

### Breathe Deep — Clear the Drying Fort Room of Blight Fumes

**Breathe Deep** requires clearing the Blight fumes from the affected room in Drying Fort.

Reaching the Fort or opening its Control Room does not replace clearing the fumes. Follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Drying Fort quest route</Link> until the room is accessible and the fumes have been removed.

### Family Archive — Learn What Happened at the Observatory

**Family Archive** requires learning what happened at the Observatory.

Continue the Observatory investigation rather than stopping as soon as you enter the building. Keep the achievement unchecked until the required discovery is completed and Steam registers it.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Observatory walkthrough</Link> covers the related investigation.

</section>

<section id="bone-reef-essence" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Songs Among the Bones and Special Recipe

Two later achievements involve Bone Reef, Estelle, and the missing Essence recipe. Their requirements are different.

### Songs Among the Bones — Restore Bone Reef's Voice

**Songs Among the Bones** requires restoring the "voice" of Bone Reef.

Complete the Bone Reef recording-related objective and its associated follow-up. Reaching Bone Reef alone does not fulfill the stated requirement.

### Special Recipe — Prepare Essence for Sludge Bet

**Special Recipe** requires preparing a batch of Essence for Sludge Bet.

Finding the recipe or catching one of its required ingredients is not the same as preparing the finished batch. Continue until the required Essence has been produced.

Follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Bone Reef and Essence quest route</Link> for the story objectives.

</section>

<section id="special-story-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Broken Heart, Hymn to Ugliness, and Dam Sweet Revenge

These three achievements involve particular events rather than cumulative fishing or crafting totals.

### Broken Heart — Stop the Heart of the Crab House

**Broken Heart** requires stopping the heart of the Crab House.

Finding the Crab House or entering the area is not the same as stopping its heart. Keep the achievement unchecked until that objective is completed.

### Hymn to Ugliness — Create a Work for the Flesh Weaver

**Hymn to Ugliness** requires creating a work worthy of the Flesh Weaver.

Father Matias's shrine work is connected with this part of the story. Complete his fish request and the construction he asks you to perform on the hill. For the related quest sequence, see the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Father Matias objectives</Link>.

### Dam Sweet Revenge — Show Who Rules the Swamps

**Dam Sweet Revenge** uses the requirement **"Show who truly rules these swamps."**

The condition does not identify an ordinary enemy kill or a specific number of encounters. Do not count an unrelated ghoul fight as completion; check whether the achievement has actually unlocked in Steam.

</section>

## How Do You Unlock All Peaceful and Predatory Fish Achievements?

Fishing accounts for several of DREADMOOR's longest achievement milestones.

The game distinguishes peaceful fish from predatory fish, and it tracks their catch totals separately.

### Peaceful Fish Achievements

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementRequirementColumns}
  rows={peacefulFishAchievementRows}
/>

The highest listed peaceful-fish milestone is **150 catches**.

Early Softgrazer fishing introduces the peaceful-fish system. Use bait appropriate to the population shown at the fishing spot.

As you gather peaceful fish for crafting materials, you can also work toward the cumulative achievements.

A completed quest requiring three fish does not automatically satisfy Quiet Hunt, which needs ten total peaceful catches.

### Predatory Fish Achievements

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementRequirementColumns}
  rows={predatoryFishAchievementRows}
/>

Only successfully landed predatory fish count toward these milestones. A fish following your lure without being caught does not complete a catch.

If predators keep abandoning the lure or escaping during retrieval, use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">predatory fishing instructions</Link> before continuing toward the 30- and 100-fish milestones.

### Make Ahab Jealous — Catch a 3-Star Fish

Land a fish with a **3-star quality rating** to unlock **Make Ahab Jealous**. The rating matters, not the species or the bait used to attract it.

If you are losing stronger fish before landing them, review the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Stamina and Line Tension controls</Link>.

</section>

## How Do You Catch Mutated Fish for Achievements?

There are two mutated-fish milestones:

  columns={achievementRequirementColumns}
  rows={mutatedFishAchievementRows}

**Wrong Kind of Fish** unlocks after your first mutated-fish catch, while **Out of Spec** requires **30 mutated fish**.

Check the fish's in-game classification rather than relying on its appearance alone. Some later story objectives also require mutated fish, so you can work toward these achievements while collecting quest ingredients.

If stronger fish are difficult to land, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing controls and equipment advice</Link>.

<section id="boat-crafting" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Custom Build, By the Blueprint, and Whatever Bites

These achievements require crafting, but each checks a different result.

### Custom Build — Install Four Self-Crafted Modules

**Custom Build** requires having **4 self-crafted boat modules installed at the same time**.

Three distinctions matter:

- The modules must be crafted by you.
- They must be installed on the boat, not merely stored.
- All four must be installed simultaneously.

Crafting four modules and leaving one in storage does not match the achievement description.

Before replacing an older module, check how many self-crafted modules you currently have equipped. Replacing one may leave you short of the required four.

### By the Blueprint — Craft From Ten Different Recipes

Craft an item from **10 different recipes**. Each recipe contributes the first time you successfully make its item; learning the Blueprint without crafting anything is not enough.

You can use bait, equipment, and other available crafting recipes toward this variety. Once one recipe has been used, move on to another rather than repeatedly making the same item for this achievement.

### Whatever Bites — Craft Every Available Bait Type

**Whatever Bites** requires making at least one of **every available bait type**.

Wind-Up Tin and Rainbow Fin are two early examples, not the entire collection. Check your crafting options as more recipes become available and make whichever bait types are still missing.

</section>

<section id="refueling" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Many Times Must You Refuel Your Boat?

There are three refueling milestones:

<GuideTable
  columns={achievementRequirementColumns}
  rows={refuelingAchievementRows}
/>

The largest milestone is **50 refueling actions**.

Refueling is already part of normal boat maintenance. Fish processing and the Fuel Larva provide the foundation for the system.

Plan for repeated refueling while working on your exploration and fishing objectives rather than treating every refuel as an isolated task.

The achievements specify the number of refueling actions. They do not establish a particular minimum amount of fuel per action, so do not assume that tiny repeated refills are guaranteed to advance the counter.

Continue using the boat normally and check the Steam achievement when each milestone is reached.

These three achievements are separate from **Full Tank**, which refers to the initial story-related refueling objective.

</section>

<section id="hull-cleaning" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Clean Bottom — Completely Clean Your Hull Five Times
 
**Clean Bottom** requires **5 complete hull-bottom cleanings**.

A partial cleaning or a visit to the Dry Dock without removing all the polyps does not meet the stated requirement. Finish each cleaning before treating it as a completed attempt.

This is separate from **No Polyps, No Leaks**, which concerns restoring the boat during the main story.

For the Dry Dock equipment and required repair steps, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">boat restoration walkthrough</Link>.

</section>

<section id="fish-processing" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Waste Not — Process 100 Fish in the Grinder

**Waste Not** requires processing **100 fish in the Grinder**.

Catching a fish without processing it does not meet the requirement. The achievement specifically names the Grinder, so do not substitute fish processed only in the Thresher.

Use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fish-processing instructions</Link> when you need to prepare Fish Meat or Chromatic Scales.

Keep track of the Grinder processing total separately from your fishing catch milestones.

</section>

<section id="hook-gun-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Finders Keepers and Get over here!

The Hook Gun is useful for both collecting floating resources and gathering bugs. These are separate achievement counters.

### Finders Keepers — Collect 50 Floating Resources

**Finders Keepers** requires collecting **50 floating resource objects with the Hook Gun**.

As you travel by boat, watch for floating resource containers and objects that can be retrieved from the water.

Use the Hook Gun to collect them rather than leaving every salvage opportunity behind.

The requirement specifies floating resource objects. Do not assume that all materials picked up on land count toward this total.

### Get over here! — Collect 30 Bugs

Use the **Hook Gun** to collect **30 bugs**. This is a separate total from the 50 floating resource objects required by **Finders Keepers**.

You first use the Hook Gun for gathering during the opening quests, but additional bug collections can contribute to this achievement as you explore.

</section>

<section id="locks-and-coral" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Other People's Secrets and Coral Lumberjack

### Other People's Secrets — Pick Ten Different Locks

**Other People's Secrets** requires picking **10 different locks with Lockpicks**.

You obtain access to Lockpick crafting through Nick the Hermit's Blueprint during the later investigation.

The condition refers to different locks, not ten attempts.

Repeatedly attempting the same locked object does not meet the stated requirement of ten distinct locks.

As you explore, check previously inaccessible doors and containers for opportunities to use Lockpicks.

The achievement **Key Specialist** may involve the same general tool, but it is a separate objective with different wording.

### Coral Lumberjack — Chop Down 50 Coral Growths

Chop down **50 coral growths** with the Axe to unlock **Coral Lumberjack**. The counter concerns harvested growths, not the number of Coral Fiber items you receive.

Angler's Island introduces coral harvesting early in the story. Keep gathering coral from available deposits as you explore additional locations.

</section>

<section id="exploration-combat" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Wolf Medallion — Kill Your First Ghoul

**Wolf Medallion** requires killing a ghoul for the first time.

Ghouls are hostile enemies encountered during exploration.

The condition is a successful kill, not merely discovering a ghoul or surviving an encounter.

Make sure you have an appropriate weapon before engaging a hostile enemy. Some early locations can be reached before you have suitable equipment.

After defeating your first ghoul, check the achievement rather than assuming that another enemy type satisfies the same requirement.

</section>

## What Is The Early Fish Gets the Worm?

**The Early Fish Gets the Worm** has the description:

> You were first to take the bait. Looks like we're hooked on each other!

This achievement is associated with DREADMOOR's Early Access period. Its description does not name a particular fish, location, or crafting action.

Check whether it has already unlocked on Steam. If it remains locked, do not assume that catching your first Snapper or repeating the opening fishing contract will satisfy it.

</section>


<section id="achievement-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Which Achievements Should You Finish After the Story?

After completing the available story, check which achievements remain locked in Steam before choosing your next activity.

The easiest ones to plan around are the requirements with explicit totals. You can combine fishing with Grinder processing, collect floating resources while sailing, and refuel the boat during trips that also increase your travel distance.

Crafting requires a different check: count recipes you have actually used, confirm which bait types remain missing, and inspect the boat to see whether four self-crafted modules are installed simultaneously.

If an unfinished achievement depends on a story event, revisit its stated condition before repeating unrelated activities. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">story objectives</Link> cover the required locations and interactions, while the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing instructions</Link> can help with difficult catches.

Mark an achievement complete only after Steam registers it.

</section>
