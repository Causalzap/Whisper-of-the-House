<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Reach **Betsy and Sons** first, then concentrate on whatever prevents your next departure: an unfinished fishing contract, a missing repair material, or a boat module that has not been installed. **The Throat** opens the later story rather than ending it. Once **The Adventure Continues** appears, choose Continue to keep exploring in Free Roam.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

DREADMOOR gives you a damaged motorboat, a fishing rod, and a long list of things that need fixing. Some problems are obvious, like an empty fuel tank. Others are harder to diagnose: you may have crafted the right equipment but left it in storage, or completed a fishing contract without collecting the item it unlocks.

The Trading Post becomes your base for much of the early game. Gill and Bozur provide work, John the Fifth handles essential boat repairs, and fishing supplies the materials needed to reach more dangerous waterways.

**The Throat is not the end of DREADMOOR's Early Access story.** Beyond the gate, the Baron sends you after a missing Essence recipe, leading to Drying Fort, the Observatory, and eventually Bone Reef.

The most useful habit is checking what your current objective actually requires before leaving on another trip. A missing material, an uninstalled module, and an unfinished conversation call for different solutions.


<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  What Should You Do Next in DREADMOOR?
</h2>

The next step is not always another island. Some objectives end with a conversation, others with a repair or an item being installed. Find the point where your progress stopped before setting out again.

<h3 id="trading-post">
  I Reached Betsy and Sons, but Nothing Is Happening
</h3>

Your first night in the Bunkhouse starts the fishing work. If Gill and Bozur have already treated you with Essence, bring your Chest inside, repair the wall, and sleep. The Simple Rod assignment follows the next morning.

Still unable to use the bed? Check the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Bunkhouse preparations</Link> before leaving the Trading Post.

<h3 id="fishing-stuck">
  I Have a Fishing Rod, but I Keep Losing Fish
</h3>

A fish that never bites is a different problem from one that breaks your line. Check the bait first; if the fish is already hooked, watch Stamina and Line Tension instead. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing controls and bait instructions</Link> cover both situations.

<h3 id="dry-dock-locked">
  Why Is the Dry Dock Still Locked?
</h3>

John the Fifth will not hand over the key while his Moonshine Still is broken. Once the Still has been repaired, speak to him again. If you can enter the building but the equipment will not start, the problem is inside the Dry Dock, not with the key.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Moonshine Still and Dry Dock steps</Link> cover both stages.

<h3 id="boat-repaired">
  My Boat Is Clean and Repaired. Why Can't I Leave?
</h3>

The boat still needs reinforcement and a replacement engine. Cleaning Polyps, repairing hull damage, crafting a module, and installing that module are different actions.

If the workbench has already produced your equipment, check Module Storage before collecting more materials. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">boat upgrade sequence</Link> shows which parts John requires.

<h3 id="carburetor">
  I Finished the Harpooner Contract, but I Still Need a Carburetor
</h3>

The traders sell the Carburetor after accepting the Harpooners; they do not include it with the contract reward. Open Gill and Bozur's shop and buy it for **50**, then return to the Dry Dock to finish the engine.

<h3 id="beaver-blocking">
  The Beaver Is Blocking The Throat. What Am I Missing?
</h3>

The Beaver responds to sound, not a direct fight. If the channel is still blocked, check whether all three Tuning Forks are working and whether you have finished the Signal Horn objective. John, Estelle, Father Matias, and Keeper Lilo are all involved in that sequence.

See the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Beaver and Signal Horn objectives</Link> for the route between them.

<h3 id="throat-gate">
  I Reached The Throat, but the Gate Won't Open
</h3>

Look at the wording in your journal. A request to repair machinery means a component is still missing or damaged. A request to configure water flow means you have reached the channel controls.

The gate will not open until **both water wheels are turning**. If you cannot get them operating, return to the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">The Throat machinery steps</Link>.

<h3 id="after-throat">
  I Passed The Throat. Where Should I Go Next?
</h3>

The crossing brings you into the Baron's territory. His condition for granting passage toward Sludge-Bet is a finished batch of Essence, but the recipe he wants has disappeared with Junior.

At the traders' new operating point, treat the sick Fuel Larva before continuing the search. Junior's trail leads toward Drying Fort.

<h3 id="drying-fort">
  I Found Drying Fort, but the Control Room Is Locked
</h3>

The Control Room requires a Lockpick. John points you toward Nick the Hermit, who provides the Crude Lockpick Blueprint. Once inside, you will also need to deal with the Blight fumes obstructing part of the Fort.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Drying Fort instructions</Link> cover the Lockpick and pump repairs.

<h3 id="observatory">
  How Do I Find Junior's Missing Essence Recipe?
</h3>

Follow the trail from Drying Fort to the Grotto. Helping Jacqueline gives you access to a key connected to the Observatory, where the investigation continues.

Recover the recipe before returning to the traders. Finding it is only the first half of the Baron's request.

<h3 id="essence-stuck">
  I Found the Essence Recipe. Why Isn't the Story Finished?
</h3>

The first batch does not solve Malcolm's problem. Estelle has more information, but she wants you to recover the recordings at Bone Reef before revealing the special ingredients.

Once she provides the requirements, the remaining work is to catch the correct mutated fish and have the traders prepare the final batch. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Bone Reef and Essence steps</Link> continue from that point.

<h2 id="what-to-do-first">
  Should You Focus on Fishing, Boat Repairs, or Exploration?
</h2>

Early on, the most useful activity is usually the one that removes your current obstacle.

You can explore nearby islands and collect salvage before a quest requires it, but filling your inventory with unrelated materials will not help if the actual problem is an unfinished conversation or an uninstalled module.

Think about what you need for your next departure.

**If the next objective requires fish**, take the correct bait and keep the catches needed for delivery. Do not automatically process every fish you land.

**If John needs a repair material**, search for that specific resource. Metal Plates, Rubber, Bug Slime, and Coral Fiber appear in different kinds of gathering activities.

**If an upgrade is available for research**, check the equipment needed for your current objective before spending all your Scraps of Knowledge on optional unlocks.

**If the boat needs maintenance**, deal with its condition before heading into a more dangerous or distant area. A longer journey becomes much less forgiving when you leave with little fuel or damaged equipment.

**If you have completed an objective but nothing changes**, return to the character or workstation associated with it. Finishing a repair, collecting a reward, and reporting the result are sometimes separate actions.

The game does not require you to collect every resource or craft every available item before advancing. Keeping the boat operational and completing the next mandatory objective should take priority.

<h2 id="boat-upgrades">
  Which Boat Upgrades Should You Get First?
</h2>

The early main story gives you two essential upgrades: **Hull Protection** and the **Upgraded Engine**.

They address different needs. Hull Protection makes the boat more resistant to damage, while the improved engine helps with longer journeys.

Do not spend all your available materials on optional equipment before completing John's required upgrades.

Once the boat is ready for the next story objective, the best additional improvement depends on how you use it.

<h3 id="storage-or-speed">
  Should You Upgrade Storage or Boat Speed?
</h3>

**Choose more storage** if you frequently return to shore because you cannot carry enough fish or salvage.

Additional fish-holding capacity is more useful when you want to remain at fishing spots for longer periods or collect multiple catches before processing them.

**Choose movement upgrades** if your normal trips involve frequent travel between distant locations.

Propeller and other propulsion-related options can make repeated journeys more convenient, particularly when you are visiting multiple islands for materials.

Afterburner-related research is another option to consider as more of the boat's equipment becomes available.

There is no need to unlock every available module immediately. Your current objective should determine which resources you can afford to spend.

<h3 id="boat-module-storage">
  Why Can't I Find the Boat Module I Just Crafted?
</h3>

Check **Module Storage** and the required mounting position before crafting another copy; the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">boat module installation steps</Link> explain how to retrieve and equip the completed part.

<h3 id="fishing-gear">
  Should You Upgrade Your Fishing Gear Before Exploring?
</h3>

The Simple Rod is sufficient for the opening Snapper objective. If stronger fish become difficult to catch, use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing equipment and troubleshooting guide</Link> to decide whether your rod, line, or technique needs attention.

<h2 id="demo-ending">
  Where Does DREADMOOR's Early Access Story End?
</h2>

Opening **The Throat** completed the October 2026 demo, but it does not finish the Early Access storyline. The gate now leads into the Baron's assignment and the search for a special Essence recipe.

You have reached the current story endpoint when **The Adventure Continues** appears. Malcolm has not yet entered the next chapter of the Sludge-Bet storyline, even though the Baron has instructed him to travel there.

If you have opened The Throat but have not seen that message, there is still story content ahead. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">DREADMOOR walkthrough</Link> continues through the remaining objectives.

<h2 id="after-story">
  What Can You Do After The Adventure Continues?
</h2>

Finishing the available story does not mean you have finished everything DREADMOOR offers.

At the ending screen, choose **Continue** to return to Free Roam. You can keep using your boat and exploring the available waterways without starting the story again.

There are several useful ways to spend that time.

<h3 id="free-roam-fishing">
  Catch Fish You Missed During the Story
</h3>

Free Roam lets you return to available fishing spots and attempt catches you skipped or could not land earlier. For bait, tackle upgrades, and difficult fish, use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">DREADMOOR Fishing Guide</Link>.

<h3 id="free-roam-boat">
  Improve Your Boat and Explore More
</h3>

If you finished the story using only the required boat upgrades, you may still have research and equipment options left to try.

Free Roam gives you an opportunity to gather more materials, improve your equipment, and visit available locations you did not fully explore.

There is no need to follow another main quest when the game has already displayed the current story-ending message.

<h3 id="free-roam-achievements">
  Finish the Remaining Steam Achievements
</h3>

DREADMOOR has **44 Steam achievements**, and reaching the story ending does not complete every requirement. Use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/achievements">44-Achievement Checklist</Link> to track unfinished objectives and their completion conditions.

<h2 id="old-saves">
  Can You Continue an Old Demo or Playtest Save?
</h2>

Save compatibility can vary between DREADMOOR's earlier Demo, Playtest, and Early Access builds.

Before opening an older save in a different version, preserve a backup of the original files. If the game fails to load correctly or quest objectives behave unexpectedly, check the current build's save-compatibility information before starting over.

Do not delete an older save solely because it was created in a previous build.
