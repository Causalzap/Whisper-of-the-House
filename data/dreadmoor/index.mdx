<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start by tuning the Radio and sailing west to Betsy and Sons. Complete the traders' fishing jobs, help John the Fifth restore the Dry Dock, and finish the required hull and engine upgrades. Then reach The Throat, restore its waterway machinery, and sail through the opened gate to finish the demo.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Getting out of the swamp in DREADMOOR takes more than a working fishing rod. The traders at Betsy and Sons need fish, John the Fifth needs help with his machinery, and your motorboat is in no condition to make the journey through The Throat.

Most of the demo takes place around the Trading Post and its surrounding waters. You leave to catch fish or gather materials, return to repair and improve the boat, and gradually unlock the equipment needed to escape. The difficult part is often knowing which task is holding up the next one.

A locked Dry Dock, an unfinished fishing contract, and a missing engine component require different solutions. The objective currently displayed in your journal is the best place to identify what needs attention.

<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  What Should You Do Next in DREADMOOR?
</h2>

Your first priority is reaching Betsy and Sons, but the Trading Post remains important long after the opening conversation. It is where you receive fishing contracts, process catches, work with the engineer, and prepare the motorboat for the journey out of the swamp.

<h3 id="trading-post">
  I Reached Betsy and Sons, but Nothing Is Happening
</h3>

Speak to Gill and Bozur inside the shop. They offer Essence to treat the effects of the Blight, but the treatment leaves you owing them a debt.

After accepting it, your immediate destination is the Bunkhouse, not another island. Take your personal Chest from the pier, bring it inside, repair the opening in the wall, and sleep until morning.

The fishing assignment begins after that first night. If you still have the Chest, cannot use the bed, or have not received instructions to collect the Simple Rod, continue the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">opening Trading Post and Bunkhouse quests</Link> before leaving the area.

<h3 id="fishing-stuck">
  I Have a Fishing Rod, but I Keep Losing Fish
</h3>

The **Simple Rod** is sufficient for the required catches. If a fish refuses to bite or repeatedly escapes, check your bait and fishing controls before spending resources on better equipment.

Snappers, Softgrazers, and Harpooners require different handling. The <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">DREADMOOR Fishing Guide</Link> explains which bait to use, how to prevent broken lines, and how to attract predatory fish.

<h3 id="dry-dock-locked">
  Why Is the Dry Dock Still Locked?
</h3>

John the Fifth has the Dry Dock Key, but he will not give it to you until his Moonshine Still is working again.

This sends you out to gather repair materials, including Coral Fiber from Angler's Island and materials recovered from creatures and salvage in the surrounding waters. Once you have repaired the Still, return to John for the key.

The Dry Dock has its own electrical problem after you enter. If the equipment remains inactive, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Dry Dock repair sequence</Link> rather than continuing to search for another key.

<h3 id="boat-repaired">
  My Boat Is Clean and Repaired. Why Can't I Leave?
</h3>

Removing the Polyps and repairing the hull damage are only the first maintenance jobs. John still wants the motorboat reinforced and its original engine replaced.

These objectives introduce Scraps of Knowledge, new blueprints, the Lathe, and the boat's module-storage system. A component may have been crafted successfully without being installed, so check storage before spending resources on another copy.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Hull Protection and Upgraded Engine steps</Link> cover the required parts and installation order.

<h3 id="carburetor">
  I Finished the Harpooner Contract, but I Still Need a Carburetor
</h3>

Delivering the requested fish makes the Carburetor available, but does not put it directly into your inventory. Return to Gill and Bozur's shop and purchase it for **50**.

Once you have it, continue the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">engine replacement objective</Link> at the Dry Dock.

<h3 id="throat-gate">
  I Reached The Throat, but the Gate Won't Open
</h3>

The Throat gate remains closed even after you start the Generator because the waterway machinery still needs repairs. Both water wheels must be operating before you can open the passage.

Check your current objective. If it mentions damaged machinery, finish those repairs first. If it asks you to configure the water flow, the remaining work is at the channel controls. Follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">The Throat repair and gate-opening steps</Link> to complete the crossing.

<h2 id="what-to-do-first">
  Should You Focus on Fishing, Boat Repairs, or Exploration?
</h2>

The active quest is usually the best reason to leave the Trading Post. Fishing contracts supply the traders and introduce materials needed for later equipment. Salvage trips become important when John needs repairs, while the Dry Dock provides the machinery that turns those materials into usable boat improvements.

There is still plenty to collect beyond the current objective, but not every barrel, island, or research option is necessary to keep the story moving.

Before spending time on a detour, consider what is actually preventing progress:

- **Missing a contract fish?** Return to the correct fishing spot with its matching bait.
- **Missing a repair ingredient?** Collect the material requested by the machinery or Workbench rather than gathering unrelated salvage.
- **Crafted something but cannot equip it?** Inspect the relevant storage and mounting positions.
- **Research options are available?** Keep enough Scraps of Knowledge for the equipment John requires before experimenting with other unlocks.

One important difference is that a fish can be valuable either as a whole catch or as a processed material. Turning everything into Fish Meat or selling every catch immediately can leave you without something needed for the next contract or crafting task.

<h2 id="boat-upgrades">
  Which Boat Upgrades Are Worth Getting in the Demo?
</h2>

The two main improvements that matter for leaving the swamp are **Hull Protection** and the **Upgraded Engine**. They address different problems: the reinforcement improves protection at the cost of some boat speed, while the upgraded engine makes the motorboat faster.

The demo also introduces other equipment, including Fish Hold improvements, Propeller upgrades, and Afterburner-related research.

Those options serve different preferences. Extra storage is useful when you want to spend more time fishing before returning to shore, while movement upgrades are more attractive if you are traveling frequently between distant fishing and salvage locations.

You do not need to purchase or install every available module before heading to The Throat. When materials are limited, finishing the equipment required by John is the safer priority.

<h3 id="old-saves">
  Can I Use My Old DREADMOOR Playtest Save?
</h3>

Older Playtest saves may cause bugs or crashes when used with the October 2026 demo.

If you participated in an earlier test, starting a new game with the current demo is the safer choice rather than continuing an old Playtest save. Do not delete an existing save without first considering whether you want to preserve it.

<h2 id="demo-ending">
  Where Does the DREADMOOR Demo End?
</h2>

The October 2026 demo concludes after you repair The Throat, open its gate, and sail beyond the passage.

Once the waterway machinery is functioning, the objective changes to **Set Out**. Board the motorboat and travel through the opened gate until the **Thank You for Playing** screen appears.

That screen marks the end of the current playable demo. The journey beyond the fog is not available as a continuation of this demo's questline.
