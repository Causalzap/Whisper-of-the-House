<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Use the **Simple Rod** with **Earthworm** to catch Snappers. Pull against a hooked fish's movement to drain its Stamina, then reel without letting Line Tension reach its limit. Craft **Wind-Up Tin** for Softgrazers and **Rainbow Fin** for Harpooners, which must chase a moving lure before biting.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A cast can fail before the fish ever bites, during the fight, or at the moment you try to bring it aboard. Those failures have different causes. A Harpooner may ignore a lure that is not moving correctly, while a hooked Snapper can escape if you keep reeling against rising Line Tension.

Start with the Simple Rod and Earthworm to learn how a fish changes direction and regains Stamina. Once you can control that fight, Wind-Up Tin and Rainbow Fin introduce two different ways to attract fish. Processing the catches also supplies Fish Meat and Chromatic Scales for later bait.

<h2 id="get-simple-rod">
  Where to Get the Simple Rod and Start Fishing
</h2>

The **Simple Rod** is stored inside the Bunkhouse at Betsy and Sons. After your first night there, Gill and Bozur direct you to collect it and meet them at the fishing platform behind the Dry Dock.

If you have not reached the Trading Post, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">DREADMOOR walkthrough</Link> through the opening quests.

The first fishing spot is beneath the Trading Post pier. It contains **Snappers**, and ordinary **Earthworms** are enough to catch them. You do not need to craft special bait or upgrade your rod before attempting the opening fishing objective.

Interact with the fishing spot to open the tackle selection interface.

Aim at the area where the fish are splashing and cast your line. Once the bait enters the water, leave it still while waiting for a bite. Unnecessary rod movement can frighten the fish away.

When a fish bites, the challenge changes. You must tire it out while preventing the line from breaking.

<h2 id="fish-stamina">
  How to Drain Fish Stamina and Reel It In
</h2>

A hooked fish tries to escape by pulling away from your rod. Move the rod against the direction in which the fish is swimming to exhaust it.

- **Fish swims left:** Pull the rod right.
- **Fish swims right:** Pull the rod left.
- **Fish reverses direction:** Reverse your pull to keep resisting its movement.

Watch the fish's direction and its **Stamina** meter together. Holding the rod in one direction after the fish turns makes the fight harder than necessary.

As Stamina decreases, the fish becomes easier to retrieve. You can reel while tiring it, but reeling too aggressively against active resistance puts additional strain on the line.

Reaching zero Stamina does not immediately land the fish. It may still be some distance away, and it can recover strength while you retrieve it.

When the fish begins resisting again, return to pulling against its movement. Let it tire before continuing a sustained retrieval.

The opening Snappers give you room to learn this rhythm. Larger or more resistant fish can make the recovery cycle much more noticeable.

### Mouse or Keyboard: Which Fishing Controls Work Better?

DREADMOOR offers mouse and keyboard options for rod movement. You can choose a control method during the introductory fishing sequence and adjust the preference in settings.

Mouse movement allows you to react directly to a fish's direction. Keyboard controls may feel easier if you prefer distinct left and right inputs.

Neither option changes the underlying fishing rules. The goal is to respond quickly to the fish, manage Stamina, and reel without overloading the line.

If you repeatedly lose control when a fish changes direction, try the other input method before spending materials on fishing upgrades.

<h2 id="line-tension">
  Why Your Fishing Line Snaps
</h2>

**Line Tension** rises when the strain on your line becomes too great. Two situations are particularly dangerous:

- Reeling hard while the fish is still resisting.
- Allowing the fish to swim too far away.

If the tension meter reaches its maximum, the line breaks and you lose the fish.

Watch the tension meter as closely as Stamina. When it climbs toward the limit, ease off your retrieval and let the strain fall.

Once the fish begins fighting again, focus on exhausting it. Continue reeling when you have enough control to bring it closer without forcing the line.

The final part of a fight can be especially frustrating. A fish may appear exhausted and almost within reach, only to regain Stamina and pull away.

Do not respond by holding the reel continuously. Another short cycle of tiring the fish is safer than losing a catch that is already close to landing.

<h3 id="land-fish">
  How to Land and Keep a Fish
</h3>

When the fish is close enough, the **Land Fish** interaction becomes available. Complete it to finish the catch.

Pay attention to the next choice. Landing a fish and keeping it are separate decisions. Releasing the fish removes it from your available catch, even if you successfully completed the fight.

Keep fish that are needed for an active contract or for processing. Once you have enough for the current objective, additional catches can supply crafting materials.

<h2 id="wind-up-tin">
  How to Craft Wind-Up Tin Bait
</h2>

**Wind-Up Tin** is the first important crafted bait for peaceful fish. Gill and Bozur introduce its blueprint when you need to catch Softgrazers.

Unlike an Earthworm, Wind-Up Tin uses a clicking metal container with meat inside to attract suitable peaceful fish. Craft it at the workbench before heading to the marked fishing location.

The recipe requires:

- **1 Metal Plate**
- **1 Fish Meat**

Metal Plates can be recovered from salvage containers and barrels around the Trading Post and nearby waterways.

Fish Meat comes from processing suitable fish at the **Grinder**. A whole fish sitting in your inventory is not the same as processed Fish Meat.

If the workbench displays `0/1` beside Fish Meat, check whether you actually processed a catch instead of simply carrying it.

Use the crafting table in the Dry Dock, select the Wind-Up Tin recipe, and combine the required materials. Make sure the selected output is Wind-Up Tin before confirming the craft.

After crafting it, select Wind-Up Tin at a peaceful fishing spot rather than continuing to use Earthworms.

<h2 id="catch-softgrazer">
  Where to Catch Softgrazers
</h2>

Softgrazers appear at the peaceful fishing location marked during the **Catch Peaceful Fish** objective. Sail toward the marker and approach the active fishing area.

The tackle selection screen identifies the population as **Peaceful** and lists **Softgrazer** as the target.

Equip Wind-Up Tin and cast into the fishing area. Let the bait attract the fish before beginning the fight.

A Softgrazer has more Stamina than the opening Snapper, making the retrieval phase less forgiving. It can recover strength after being exhausted, so you may need to tire it several times before landing it.

Avoid reeling continuously when its Stamina recovers. Watch the direction of movement, oppose it, and resume retrieving when the fish becomes easier to control.

If your bait is correct and the fish is biting, a long fight is not evidence that you chose the wrong lure. Check your Line Tension and retrieval timing before abandoning the spot.

Keep the Softgrazers you intend to process for Chromatic Scales rather than selling or releasing them. For the required quest quantity and delivery, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">hull-reinforcement objectives</Link>.

<h3 id="get-chromatic-scales">
  How to Get Chromatic Scales
</h3>

**Chromatic Scales** come from processing suitable peaceful fish, including Softgrazers, at the **Thresher**.

Return to the processing equipment at Betsy and Sons after catching your Softgrazers. Select the fish for processing and collect the resulting Scales.

The Thresher and Grinder serve different purposes:

- **Grinder:** Produces Fish Meat for bait crafting and other uses.
- **Thresher:** Produces Chromatic Scales from suitable fish.

This distinction matters when you prepare Rainbow Fin. Carrying a whole Softgrazer does not satisfy a recipe that asks for Chromatic Scales.

The Scales also have competing uses. They are needed for John's early hull-related objective and for crafting Rainbow Fin.

Avoid spending every Scale on additional bait before checking what your current task requires. If you run short, catch and process more suitable peaceful fish.

<h2 id="rainbow-fin">
  Rainbow Fin Recipe for Predatory Fish
</h2>

**Rainbow Fin** is the crafted lure introduced for the Harpooner contract. Unlike the stationary bait used for the earlier peaceful fish, it is designed to move through the water and attract a predator's attention.

Its feathers flutter during retrieval, imitating something the predator can chase.

Craft Rainbow Fin using:

- **1 Fish Meat**
- **1 Metal Plate**
- **1 Chromatic Scales**

Obtain Fish Meat from the Grinder, Metal Plates from salvage, and Chromatic Scales from processing suitable peaceful fish at the Thresher.

Chromatic Scales deserve particular attention because they are also used for other equipment and crafting objectives. Check your supply before crafting multiple Rainbow Fin lures.

With Rainbow Fin ready, head toward the marked predatory fishing spot.

<h2 id="catch-harpooner">
  How to Catch Harpooners With Rainbow Fin
</h2>

Harpooners behave differently from Snappers and Softgrazers. They do not simply wait for stationary bait. You need to make the lure move so the predator notices it and begins chasing.

Approach the marked fishing location and look for the disturbed surface that identifies the **splash zone**.

Select Rainbow Fin and cast inside that area.

Once Rainbow Fin lands inside the splash zone, begin retrieving it and watch the Harpooner's reaction. The lure must keep moving for the predator to chase it, but pulling too quickly can take it beyond the fish's reach. If the fish follows and then turns away, cast again and change the retrieval pace rather than immediately crafting another bait.

When nothing approaches the lure, check the selected bait and the cast location first. A correct Rainbow Fin cast inside an active predatory fishing area is the starting point; the retrieval speed determines whether the chase continues.

After the Harpooner bites, use the familiar Stamina and Line Tension controls. Predatory fishing changes how you attract the fish, but you still need to tire and retrieve it safely.

Harpooners are required during the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Carburetor quest</Link>. Keep the fish needed for that delivery instead of processing or selling them.

<h2 id="fishing-upgrades">
  When Should You Upgrade Your Fishing Rod and Line?
</h2>

The Simple Rod is sufficient for the first fishing assignment. Upgrading becomes more worthwhile when you can already control a fight but stronger fish still prevent you from making progress.

A stronger rod is useful when difficult catches take repeated attempts to exhaust. An improved line is more attractive when you are losing catches to excessive tension despite easing off the reel. Neither upgrade replaces the correct bait, so deal with a fish that refuses to bite before investing in more tackle.

Prioritize the equipment that addresses your most frequent problem. For the opening contracts, the Simple Rod and the right bait are enough to begin building your supply of fish and crafting materials.

<h2 id="fishing-problems">
  Why Are Fish Not Biting or Escaping?
</h2>

Notice when the attempt goes wrong. A fish ignoring the cast points to bait or lure movement; a fish escaping after the bite points to Stamina, distance, or Line Tension.

<h2 id="after-main-story">
  Can You Keep Fishing After the Early Access Story?
</h2>

Yes. When **The Adventure Continues** appears, select **Continue** and the available fishing spots remain accessible. You can return for catches that were too difficult earlier or use better tackle to attempt new ones.

For peaceful, predatory, and mutated-fish catch milestones, check the <Link href="/dreadmoor/achievements">DREADMOOR achievement requirements</Link>.
