DREADMOOR's fishing challenges change as you move through the demo. Snappers can be caught with ordinary Earthworms, but Softgrazers require Wind-Up Tin, while Harpooners chase Rainbow Fin as it moves through the water.

Using the correct bait is only half the job. A hooked fish can recover Stamina, excessive Line Tension can break your line, and a predator may abandon a lure that moves too quickly. These differences matter more to the required catches than buying a stronger rod.

<h2 id="get-simple-rod">
  Where to Get the Simple Rod and Start Fishing
</h2>

The **Simple Rod** is inside the Bunkhouse at Betsy and Sons. After spending your first night there, Gill and Bozur direct you to collect the rod and meet them at the fishing platform behind the Dry Dock.

If you have not reached the Bunkhouse yet, continue the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">opening DREADMOOR quests</Link> until the traders give you the fishing assignment.

The first fishing spot is beneath the Trading Post pier, close to the merchants. Its fish population includes **Snapper**, and the ordinary **Earthworm** is enough to attract it. You do not have to research a bait blueprint or buy another rod before beginning.

Interact with the fishing spot to open the tackle selection interface.

Select the rod and bait, aim toward the fishing area, and cast. Once the bait lands, keep the rod still while waiting for a bite. Moving the rod too early can frighten the fish away.

After a fish takes the bait, the controls change from waiting for a strike to fighting the hooked fish. This is where Stamina and Line Tension become important.

<h2 id="fish-stamina">
  How to Drain Fish Stamina Without Losing the Catch
</h2>

A hooked fish swims against the rod, trying to pull away. You tire it by moving the rod in the opposite direction.

- **Fish moves left:** Pull the rod right.
- **Fish moves right:** Pull the rod left.
- **Fish changes direction:** Change your pull to oppose its new movement.

Watch which way the fish is swimming rather than continually holding the rod to one side. Pulling correctly reduces its **Stamina**, displayed during the fight. When that meter reaches zero, the fish is exhausted enough for you to begin reeling it closer.

A common mistake is treating zero Stamina as an automatic catch. The fish can still be some distance away, and retrieving it is a separate part of the fight. It may also recover Stamina while you are bringing it toward the boat.

When that happens, stop trying to force the catch through. Oppose the fish's movement again until it tires, then continue retrieving.

### Mouse or Keyboard: Which Fishing Controls Should You Use?

During the introductory fishing sequence, DREADMOOR lets you choose how to control rod movement. Both mouse and keyboard control schemes are available, and you can change your preference later through the settings.

Mouse movement works for the first Snappers, but a stronger fish can repeatedly change direction and require larger movements across your desk. If you struggle to reverse your pull quickly enough, try the keyboard scheme before assuming you need upgraded fishing equipment.

The fishing mechanics remain the same whichever input method you select. Your task is still to oppose the fish's movement, manage the line, and reel at the right moment.

<h2 id="line-tension">
  Why Your Fishing Line Snaps and How to Prevent It
</h2>

**Line Tension** measures the strain on your fishing line. Reeling against a fish that is still actively resisting can increase that strain, and allowing a fish to swim too far away can also put the line under pressure.

If the Line Tension meter reaches its maximum, the line snaps and the fish escapes.

The dangerous moment often comes when a fish is almost within landing distance. Its Stamina may be low, but another burst of resistance can send Line Tension toward the breaking point.

Watch the tension meter during retrieval. If it climbs too high, ease off reeling until the strain drops. When the fish begins fighting again, exhaust it before attempting another sustained pull. Losing a little distance is better than losing the entire catch.

<h3 id="land-fish">
  How to Land and Keep a Fish
</h3>

Once the fish has been brought close enough, the **Land Fish** interaction becomes available. Complete that interaction to bring the fish aboard.

Be careful with the choice that follows. Landing and keeping a fish are not necessarily the same action. If you release a fish by mistake, it will not be available for a contract or for processing.

This becomes especially costly with Softgrazers and Harpooners, which take more effort to catch than the opening Snappers.

The traders' first assignment requires **5 Snappers**. Keep those catches for delivery. Other fish can be processed into Fish Meat, which becomes an ingredient in the crafted lures used later.

<h2 id="wind-up-tin">
  How to Craft Wind-Up Tin Bait
</h2>

**Wind-Up Tin** is the Rare bait introduced when Gill and Bozur ask you to catch peaceful fish. Unlike an ordinary Earthworm, it has to be made at a Workbench.

The lure uses meat enclosed inside a clicking tin to attract scavenging fish. Its purpose is different from Rainbow Fin, which is designed to move through the water and provoke a predator into chasing it.

To craft one Wind-Up Tin, you need:

- **1 Metal Plate**
- **1 Fish Meat**

Metal Plates can be recovered from salvage around the Trading Post and nearby waters. Fish Meat comes from processing fish at the Grinder.

If the crafting interface shows `0/1` for Fish Meat, check whether you have processed an ordinary fish or are still carrying the whole catch. A fish in your inventory is not automatically the Fish Meat ingredient required by the recipe.

Open the Workbench, select Wind-Up Tin, and use the two materials to complete the lure.

Once it has been crafted, equip Wind-Up Tin at the peaceful-fish fishing spot rather than continuing with Earthworms.

<h2 id="catch-softgrazer">
  Where to Catch Softgrazers in DREADMOOR
</h2>

Softgrazers appear at the peaceful fishing location marked during the **Catch Peaceful Fish** objective. Sail toward the quest marker and approach the water until the Fishing Spot interaction is available.

The selection interface identifies the fish population as **Peaceful** and lists **Softgrazer** as the available target.

Equip Wind-Up Tin and cast into the fishing area. Softgrazers have a larger Stamina reserve than the introductory Snappers and can recover strength during retrieval, making the final stretch of a catch particularly risky. A prolonged fight does not mean you have chosen the wrong bait.

Keep **3 Softgrazers** for the peaceful-fish objective. Do not sell them after landing: you need to process the catch into Chromatic Scales at the Thresher.

<h3 id="get-chromatic-scales">
  How to Get Chromatic Scales From Softgrazers
</h3>

**Chromatic Scales** are obtained by processing suitable peaceful fish, including Softgrazers, in the **Thresher**.

Return to the processing equipment at Betsy and Sons after catching the required fish. Select the Softgrazers for processing and collect the resulting Scales.

There are two reasons to keep track of this material:

- John the Fifth requires Chromatic Scales during the hull-reinforcement progression.
- Chromatic Scales are also an ingredient in the Rainbow Fin bait used for predatory fish.

This is why selling every Softgrazer can create an unnecessary return trip. A whole fish and its processed Scales serve different purposes.

If you already used your Scales and later need another piece for Rainbow Fin, catch and process another suitable peaceful fish rather than trying to obtain the ingredient from an unrelated salvage container.

After preparing the Scales for John's objective, continue the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">boat-reinforcement sequence</Link>. The fishing method itself does not change when the next contract becomes available.

<h2 id="rainbow-fin">
  Rainbow Fin Recipe for Predatory Fish
</h2>

**Rainbow Fin** is a Common lure designed for predators. Gill and Bozur introduce it when the next fishing contract requires Harpooners.

The main difference from Wind-Up Tin is the way the fish responds. Rainbow Fin is designed to be retrieved through the water, with its feathers moving to imitate something a predator wants to chase.

Craft Rainbow Fin at a Workbench using:

- **1 Fish Meat**
- **1 Metal Plate**
- **1 × Chromatic Scales**

Fish Meat can be produced at the Grinder, and Metal Plates are available from salvage. Chromatic Scales must come from the appropriate fish-processing route.

Of these ingredients, Chromatic Scales are the easiest to overlook because they are also tied to the earlier hull objective. Check your available supply before leaving the Trading Post for the Harpooner fishing area.

After crafting Rainbow Fin, equip it and travel toward the marked predatory-fish location.

<h2 id="catch-harpooner">
  How to Catch Harpooners With Rainbow Fin
</h2>

Harpooners introduce **Predator Fishing**, which changes what you need to do before a fish bites.

With Snappers and Softgrazers, you cast and wait for a fish to take the bait. A Harpooner instead needs to see and follow a lure moving through the water.

Approach the predatory fishing spot and look for the disturbed surface marking its **splash zone**. Select Rainbow Fin and aim your cast inside that area.

Once the lure lands, begin reeling it through the water. Watch for the predator to notice the movement and start following.

The important part is the retrieval speed. Pulling the lure back to the boat as quickly as possible can leave the fish behind before it reaches the bait.

Two mistakes can end the chase:

- **Reeling too quickly:** The Harpooner cannot catch up with the lure.
- **Leaving the lure motionless for too long:** The fish loses interest in the bait.

Maintain a controlled, steady retrieval. The lure needs to move enough to keep the predator interested without escaping its pursuit.

If a Harpooner starts following but turns away before biting, try another cast with a different retrieval pace. There is no reason to discard Rainbow Fin simply because the first pursuit failed.

If there is no reaction at all, check that you selected Rainbow Fin and placed the cast inside the splash zone rather than outside the active fishing area.

Once a Harpooner bites, the chase is over and the usual Stamina and Line Tension controls apply. Be particularly careful during the final retrieval, when a sudden burst of resistance can cost you the catch.

Keep **3 Harpooners** for Gill and Bozur. After delivering them, continue the <Link href="/dreadmoor/walkthrough">Carburetor and upgraded-engine objectives</Link>.
