<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
At Church Settlement, start the Generator and lower the boat into the water. The first major repairs take place at Betsy and Sons, where John the Fifth helps you prepare the boat for The Throat. After opening that gate, recover Junior's missing Essence recipe, investigate Drying Fort and the Observatory, and complete Estelle's Bone Reef request. Preparing the final Essence batch for the Baron leads to **The Adventure Continues**, the current story-ending screen.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The first thing Malcolm needs is a working boat. Reaching the Trading Post gives him a place to rest and access to fishing equipment, but the Dry Dock remains locked until John the Fifth's Moonshine Still has been repaired.

Each major departure depends on something left unfinished at the settlement: a fishing delivery, a replacement component, a research unlock, or a repair. The same pattern continues beyond The Throat, where recovering a recipe and actually preparing the Essence become separate tasks.

Follow the steps in order when a gate or conversation stops progressing. Optional salvage and equipment upgrades can be handled along the way, but they do not replace the current quest requirement.

<h2 id="how-to-leave-church-settlement-and-get-the-boat">
  How to Leave Church Settlement and Get the Boat
</h2>

The opening takes Malcolm to the abandoned Church Settlement. After the encounter near the water, move through the settlement and search the accessible buildings and containers for supplies.

Your first objective is to get the old motorboat operating.

Look for the **Hook Gun** as you explore. It lets you pull objects that are beyond normal reach and becomes important throughout the game.

The boat is suspended near a crane, but the machinery cannot move it until you restore power.

<h3 id="find-fuel-and-start-the-generator">
  Find Fuel and Start the Generator
</h3>

Inspect the Generator near the boat-launching equipment. Its tank is empty.

Search the nearby working area for a **Fuel Can**, then return to the Generator and supply the required fuel.

Start the machine using its interaction prompt.

If the Generator still reports insufficient fuel, check whether you have actually completed the refueling interaction. Carrying a Fuel Can does not power the equipment by itself.

<h3 id="lower-the-boat-into-the-water">
  Lower the Boat Into the Water
</h3>

With the Generator running, return to the crane controls.

Use the available Hook Gun interaction when the objective requires you to move the crane assembly. Once the boat is correctly positioned, operate the control panel to lower it into the water.

Board the motorboat after it reaches the water.

You will receive a radio transmission from the traders. Tune the radio until you can establish the conversation.

Gill and Bozur direct you toward the first river gate and eventually to **Betsy and Sons**, their Trading Post.

<h2 id="how-to-open-the-first-river-gate">
  How to Open the First River Gate
</h2>

The first river gate blocks your route out of the starting area.

Dock near the gate and find the control mechanism. The symbols on its panel correspond to markings on the gate structure.

Inspect those symbols before interacting with the controls. You need to operate the mechanism in the correct sequence so that the waterway opens.

The gate is complete only when the passage is physically accessible. Activating a control without changing the gate's final state does not finish the objective.

If the passage remains closed, check the symbols again and revisit the control panel rather than leaving the area.

Once the river gate opens, return to the boat and continue toward the Trading Post.

The traders mention **Angler Brothers' Cabin** as a possible stop along the route. Continue following your current objective toward Betsy and Sons rather than treating every abandoned structure as a mandatory detour.

<h2 id="reach-betsy-and-sons-and-get-your-first-essence">
  Reach Betsy and Sons and Get Your First Essence
</h2>

Follow the waterways to the **Betsy and Sons Trading Post**.

Moor the boat and enter the traders' shop. Speak to Gill and Bozur about your condition and the Blight.

They offer Malcolm an injection of **Essence**, which relieves his symptoms. The treatment creates a debt that the traders expect him to repay through fishing.

Accept the treatment to continue the story.

The traders provide a key to a nearby Bunkhouse and tell you to rest before beginning work.

<h3 id="repair-the-bunkhouse-wall-before-sleeping">
  Repair the Bunkhouse Wall Before Sleeping
</h3>

Collect your personal chest and take it to the Bunkhouse.

Inside, inspect the opening in the wall. The room must be secured before you can complete the overnight objective.

Find the nearby material used to patch the damaged wall, then interact with the opening to repair it.

  Catch Five Snappers and Repay the Initial Debt

Go to the fishing platform behind the Dry Dock.

Gill and Bozur ask for **5 Snappers**, which can be caught at the nearby fishing spot using ordinary Earthworms.

Do not sell or process the fish intended for delivery before completing the contract.

Once you have the five Snappers, return to Gill and Bozur.

The traders explain that your boat requires significant repairs before you can safely travel farther into the swamps.

They send you to the engineer **John the Fifth**, who holds the Dry Dock Key.

<h2 id="how-to-get-the-dry-dock-key-from-john-the-fifth">
  How to Get the Dry Dock Key From John the Fifth
</h2>

Find John's workshop near the Trading Post and speak to him.

John refuses to open the Dry Dock until you repair his broken **Moonshine Still**.

Inspect the Still to identify the first problem. A prosthetic leg is lodged inside the equipment and must be removed.

The Still also needs materials to repair its damaged tank.

<h3 id="find-coral-fiber-bug-slime-and-metal-plates">
  Find Coral Fiber, Bug Slime, and Metal Plates
</h3>

John points you toward three nearby sources of materials.

**Coral Fiber — Angler's Island**

Travel to Angler's Island, which John identifies as a nearby location visible from the lodging area.

Go ashore and use the Axe to harvest coral growths. Collect the resulting Coral Fiber.

**Bug Slime — Rocks Behind the Trading Post**

John describes large beetles that produce adhesive slime. Search the rocks and damp surfaces behind the Trading Post.

Look for the creatures associated with bright blue slime and use the appropriate collection interaction.

**Metal Plates — Barrels and Salvage**

Search the nearby salvage areas and recover suitable metal containers or barrels.

Use the Hook Gun when objects are beyond normal reach. Gather the Metal Plates required by the repair interaction.

Bring the materials back to the Still and complete its repair interaction.

After the machine is working again, speak to John. He gives you the **Dry Dock Key**.

<h3 id="what-if-you-cannot-find-more-repair-materials">
  What if You Cannot Find More Repair Materials?
</h3>

An emptied barrel or harvested coral growth will not provide more materials immediately. Search other accessible gathering points, including the remaining coral deposits on Angler's Island and salvage along the waterways. You can return to previously harvested areas later.

Before making another trip, inspect the Moonshine Still's repair prompt. It shows which ingredients are still missing, so you can collect the required material instead of filling your inventory with unrelated salvage.

<h2 id="process-fish-and-refuel-the-motorboat">
  Process Fish and Refuel the Motorboat
</h2>

Gill and Bozur use the **Grinder** and **Fuel Larva** to produce fuel. Take a suitable fish to the Grinder, collect its Fish Meat, and feed the Larva. Once fuel is available, use the **Fuel Gun** to fill the motorboat's tank.

Check the boat's fuel level before leaving. If the Larva has already eaten but the tank is still empty, the missing step is the transfer from the Fuel Gun to the boat.

You will make several trips between the Trading Post, Angler's Island, and the Dry Dock. Refuel before a long salvage trip rather than waiting until the boat is almost empty.

<h2 id="repair-the-dry-dock-fuse-box-and-restore-power">
  Repair the Dry Dock Fuse Box and Restore Power
</h2>

Use John's key to enter the Dry Dock.

The facility cannot operate normally because its electrical equipment has been damaged.

Find the **Fuse Box** and inspect the broken connection.

One early repair interaction requires a Cable. Search the nearby working area for the required component and repair the electrical board.

After fixing the connection, find the separate **Power Switch** and activate it.

Once the Dry Dock has power, bring the motorboat inside and position it on the lift.

<h2 id="clean-the-boat-and-repair-its-hull">
  Clean the Boat and Repair Its Hull
</h2>

The Dry Dock repair sequence has two distinct parts: removing Polyps and repairing the exposed hull damage.

<h3 id="remove-the-polyps-with-the-water-cannon">
  Remove the Polyps With the Water Cannon
</h3>

Operate the **Pressure Pump** to prepare the cleaning equipment.

Then use the **Water Cannon** on the growths attached to the boat's hull.

Sweep across the affected sections until the required Polyps have been removed.

<h3 id="use-bug-slime-to-repair-the-hull">
  Use Bug Slime to Repair the Hull
</h3>

Prepare the **Repair Gun** with the required Bug Slime.

Inspect the damaged hull sections and repair the indicated slots.

If a section is already in good condition, move to the next damaged part instead of repeatedly using the Repair Gun on the same position.

Finish the remaining repairs and speak to John.

He acknowledges that the boat is in better condition but explains that it still needs additional reinforcement before the longer voyage.

<h2 id="catch-three-softgrazers-for-chromatic-scales">
  Catch Three Softgrazers for Chromatic Scales
</h2>

John requires **Chromatic Scales** to reinforce the motorboat.

Return to Gill and Bozur. They provide the **Wind-Up Tin** Blueprint and ask you to catch **3 Softgrazers**.

Craft the bait, travel to the marked peaceful fishing area, and keep all three fish.

Use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Softgrazer and Wind-Up Tin instructions</Link> if you need the bait recipe or help with the catch.

After landing the fish, return to Betsy and Sons and process them in the **Thresher** to obtain Chromatic Scales.

Bring the Scales to John.

He provides **Scraps of Knowledge** and directs you toward the next research objectives: Weak Hull Protection and an Upgraded Engine.

Do not spend all your Scales on unrelated crafting before completing the current hull objective.

<h2 id="research-and-install-weak-hull-protection">
  Research and Install Weak Hull Protection
</h2>

Open the Mastery or research interface and locate the required protection and engine blueprints.

Researching an item unlocks its crafting option. It does not automatically create or install the finished part.

The two important unlocks for this stage are:

- **Weak Hull Protection**, required for the reinforcement objective.
- **Upgraded Engine**, required before the longer journey.

Spend the necessary Scraps of Knowledge and return to John's working equipment.

<h3 id="craft-hull-protection-at-the-lathe">
  Craft Hull Protection at the Lathe
</h3>

Use the **Lathe** in the Dry Dock to craft the required protection module.

Collect the materials listed in the crafting interface before attempting the recipe.

Once the module is finished, check where the completed equipment has been placed.

<h3 id="where-to-find-a-crafted-module-that-is-missing">
  Where to Find a Crafted Module That Is Missing
</h3>

A crafted module may be placed in **Module Storage** rather than directly in your carried inventory.

Inspect the Storage Locker or designated module-storage area before making another copy.

When the necessary reinforcement work is complete, return to John.

The next mandatory improvement is the engine.

<h2 id="get-the-carburetor-and-install-the-upgraded-engine">
  Get the Carburetor and Install the Upgraded Engine
</h2>

John needs a **Carburetor** before the upgraded engine can be completed.

He explains that Gill and Bozur acquired his old Carburetor after a card game.

Return to the traders and ask about it.

They do not hand the part over immediately. Instead, they require another fishing contract.

<h3 id="catch-three-harpooners">
  Catch Three Harpooners
</h3>

The traders ask for **3 Harpooners**, which are predatory fish.

They provide the blueprint for **Rainbow Fin** bait.

Craft the lure and travel to the marked predatory fishing location. Catch three Harpooners and keep them for the contract.

The <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Harpooner fishing instructions</Link> explain how to attract a predator with a moving lure.

Return to Gill and Bozur when you have the required fish.

<h3 id="buy-the-carburetor-separately">
  Buy the Carburetor Separately
</h3>

Completing the Harpooner contract unlocks the purchase opportunity. The Carburetor does not automatically enter your inventory.

Open the traders' shop and purchase it.

The Carburetor is listed for **50** in the traders' shop.

When it has been installed, speak to John.

He explains that the route through **The Throat** remains blocked, even though your boat is now ready to attempt the journey.

<h2 id="how-to-get-past-the-beaver-blocking-the-throat">
  How to Get Past the Beaver Blocking The Throat
</h2>

Before you can open the machinery at The Throat, you must deal with a large mutated Beaver blocking the channel.

John directs you to contact the healer **Estelle** for information about the creature.

<h3 id="repair-the-antenna-and-contact-estelle">
  Repair the Antenna and Contact Estelle
</h3>

Go to the lookout tower above the Bunkhouse and inspect the antenna.

Repair and tune the equipment as required by the objective.

Once the signal is working, contact Estelle by radio.

Explain that a large Beaver is preventing access to The Throat.

Estelle says the creature is extremely sensitive to sound. A powerful sound source should frighten it away.

You do not need to defeat the Beaver in combat.

Return to John and explain what Estelle told you.

He suggests repairing three **Tuning Forks** scattered around the region.

<h2 id="find-and-restore-all-three-tuning-forks">
  Find and Restore All Three Tuning Forks
</h2>

John identifies three locations:

1. The route toward **The Throat**.
2. **Ghoul Tower**.
3. The area near **Father Matias's house**.

Visit the towers and inspect their Tuning Fork equipment.

Repair or restore the mechanisms required by the current objective.

The important distinction is that inspecting a tower is not always enough. Return to the associated installation and complete its interaction until the objective recognizes the fork as operational.

<h3 id="tuning-fork-on-the-route-to-the-throat">
  Tuning Fork on the Route to The Throat
</h3>

John describes this as the newest of the three installations.

Look for the tower along the route toward The Throat and inspect the equipment.

Restore the Tuning Fork before continuing to the next location.

<h3 id="ghoul-tower-tuning-fork">
  Ghoul Tower Tuning Fork
</h3>

The Ghoul Tower's Tuning Fork is mounted on the roof.

Approach the tower carefully and find the route to the upper level.

Inspect the Tuning Fork and complete any repairs required by the objective.

The tower's name is a warning that the area may be dangerous. Prepare for hostile encounters rather than assuming that it is an empty maintenance site.

<h3 id="father-matias-and-the-third-tuning-fork">
  Father Matias and the Third Tuning Fork
</h3>

The third Tuning Fork has been altered by **Father Matias**.

Speak to him about the equipment.

He explains that he considers the Tuning Fork offensive to the Flesh Spinner and intends to turn it into a shrine.

Before he helps, he requests fish for his ministry.

Catch the **three fish** required by his request and bring them back.

After completing his fishing request, speak to him again about the Tuning Fork.

Matias provides access to a shed containing an important component for his shrine.

Take the component to the designated pole on the hill, install it, and complete the decoration using the material requested by the shrine objective.

Return to Matias after the work is finished.

The shrine-related objective should now be complete.

<GuideCallout title="Father Matias Won't Let You Finish the Tuning Fork?">
Complete his fish request before trying to finish the shrine. After receiving access to the shed, install the required component on the hill and complete the shrine decoration. Merely reaching the Tuning Fork does not satisfy Matias's request.
</GuideCallout>

Once all three Tuning Forks are operating, return to John.

<h2 id="find-keeper-lilo-s-signal-horn">
  Find Keeper Lilo's Signal Horn
</h2>

Tell John that the Tuning Forks are working.

He explains that they still need a powerful, focused sound source to drive away the Beaver.

John remembers a **Signal Horn** associated with **Keeper Lilo**, who previously maintained the gates.

He directs you toward Keeper Lilo's cave.

Travel to the indicated area and recover the horn.

Follow the objective to put the sound equipment into use at The Throat.

The horn works with the restored signal system to address the creature blocking the channel.

Once the obstruction has been resolved, you can focus on the gate machinery itself.

<h2 id="how-to-restore-the-throat-generator-and-waterway-machinery">
  How to Restore The Throat Generator and Waterway Machinery
</h2>

The Throat contains several damaged machines. Getting electricity to the site is only the beginning.

Bring your boat to the accessible docking area and go ashore.

Your first task is powering the gate complex.

<h3 id="find-a-fuel-can-for-the-generator">
  Find a Fuel Can for the Generator
</h3>

Locate the Generator and inspect its tank.

Search the nearby working area for the required Fuel Can.

Supply fuel and complete the startup interaction.

Once the Generator is running, return to the **Gate Control**.

Attempt to operate the gate.

The machinery is still damaged, so the initial attempt does not open the passage. The objective directs you to repair the waterway equipment.

<h3 id="find-the-thermal-coil-gear-and-two-buckets">
  Find the Thermal Coil, Gear, and Two Buckets
</h3>

Inspect the damaged mechanisms around the upper walkways, workshop, and lower platforms.

The repair work involves several separate components:

- A **Thermal Coil** for the damaged electrical assembly.
- A missing **Gear or Axle**.
- **Two replacement Buckets** for the water-wheel equipment.
- A damaged **Water Pump**.

Check each installation point to identify the part required.

Do not assume that the entire machinery objective will be completed after you repair only one assembly.

<h3 id="install-the-gear-or-axle">
  Install the Gear or Axle
</h3>

Find the assembly with the designated **Gear Slot**.

Bring the replacement component to the slot and complete the installation prompt.

<h3 id="install-both-replacement-buckets">
  Install Both Replacement Buckets
</h3>

Inspect the water-wheel assemblies and their mounting points.

The repair objective requires **two Buckets**.

Find the missing parts around the accessible workshop and platform areas, then install each one at the corresponding position.

If the objective still mentions a missing Bucket after the first installation, inspect the other assembly.

Do not begin the final water-flow puzzle while the machinery still has an unresolved physical repair.

<h3 id="replace-the-thermal-coil">
  Replace the Thermal Coil
</h3>

Find the electrical assembly requiring a Thermal Coil.

Retrieve the replacement component from the available supplies and install it at the damaged mechanism.

Once the electrical and mechanical parts are in place, check the remaining repair objective.

<h3 id="repair-the-existing-water-pump">
  Repair the Existing Water Pump
</h3>

Find the damaged Water Pump and use its repair interaction.

The task is to repair the installed mechanism, not craft a separate replacement pump at John's Lathe.

Complete the repair, then check whether the objective advances to configuring water flow.

<h2 id="how-to-get-both-water-wheels-turning">
  How to Get Both Water Wheels Turning
</h2>

Once the damaged parts are repaired, the remaining objective is to route water through the channels so that **both water wheels turn**.

The control points are distributed across the complex.

Four water-flow controls are visible in the system. Their individual indicators change as you operate them, but the status of one control does not establish that both wheels are working.

Start by checking the channels that feed each water wheel.

Operate a control, inspect where water is flowing, and see whether it reaches the intended wheel.

Continue adjusting the connected channels until both wheels receive water and begin operating.

Do not stop after getting one wheel to turn.

The objective should advance when the required water flow has been restored to both assemblies.

<GuideCallout title="The Throat Gate Still Won't Open?">
Check the wording of your current objective. If it still asks you to repair machinery, inspect the Thermal Coil, Gear, both Buckets, and Water Pump. If it asks you to configure water flow, check the channel controls and both water wheels. Once the waterway objective is complete, return to the main Gate Control.
</GuideCallout>

<h3 id="open-the-main-gate">
  Open the Main Gate
</h3>

Return to the Gate Control after both water wheels are operational.

Use the control to open the passage.

The large gate lowers, creating a route for the boat.

The next objective becomes **Set Out**.

Return to the motorboat and sail through the opened waterway.

This is the end of the opening region's major gate sequence, but not the end of DREADMOOR's Early Access story.

<h2 id="what-happens-after-you-pass-through-the-throat">
  What Happens After You Pass Through The Throat?
</h2>

After crossing The Throat, Malcolm establishes contact with the **Baron**.

The Baron controls access to Sludge-Bet and refuses to open the next gates without help.

He explains that a young man named **Junior** stole a special Essence recipe developed by Estelle and fled into the Deep Water.

The Baron gives Malcolm two objectives:

1. Recover the missing Essence recipe from Junior.
2. Prepare a batch of Essence for Sludge-Bet.

Junior was last seen heading toward **Drying Fort**.

Recovering the recipe alone will not satisfy the Baron. He requires the finished Essence batch as well.

Follow the updated objective into the newly accessible region.

<h2 id="cure-the-fuel-larva-at-the-new-trading-post">
  Cure the Fuel Larva at the New Trading Post
</h2>

The traders establish a new operating point after the journey beyond The Throat.

When you meet them, they explain that the **Fuel Larva** is unwell after the difficult trip.

The creature cannot produce fuel normally in its current condition.

Gill and Bozur provide a **First Aid Kit Blueprint** and direct you to treat it.

Use the crafting table to make the required treatment item, then apply it to the Fuel Larva.

Check the Larva after treatment to confirm it has recovered.

Treating the Larva and ordinary refueling are separate activities. If the boat later runs low on fuel, you must still produce and transfer fuel as usual.

Once the treatment is complete, return to the traders and discuss the Baron's assignment.

<h2 id="how-to-get-lockpicks-for-drying-fort">
  How to Get Lockpicks for Drying Fort
</h2>

Tell Gill and Bozur that you are searching for Junior and the missing recipe.

They explain that Drying Fort contains a locked **Control Room** and that the original key is missing.

You need a way to open the door without that key.

Speak to John the Fifth about making Lockpicks.

John refuses to produce them himself. Instead, he recommends **Nick the Hermit**, who has experience making tools for the Baron's crews.

<h3 id="find-nick-the-hermit">
  Find Nick the Hermit
</h3>

Travel to Nick's island using the route indicated by the current quest.

Speak to Nick about the locked door at Drying Fort.

He is suspicious of the Baron and the world outside his island, but eventually agrees to help.

Ask specifically about **Lockpicks**.

Nick provides the **Lockpick Blueprint**.

He also has a practice stand near his working area.


After receiving the Crude Lockpick Blueprint from Nick, use a crafting workbench to make the tool.

The recipe requires:

- **1 Rubber**
- **1 Metal Plate**

Both materials can be recovered from salvage. Craft a Crude Lockpick before returning to the locked Control Room at Drying Fort.

Once you have Lockpicks, return to Drying Fort.

<h2 id="how-to-enter-drying-fort-and-clear-the-blight">
  How to Enter Drying Fort and Clear the Blight
</h2>

Drying Fort is part of the search for Junior.

Approach the Fort and find the locked Control Room.

Use the Lockpicks to open the door and access the machinery inside.

The Fort contains a room filled with **Blight fumes**, preventing normal passage through part of the building.

The solution involves restoring the damaged pipe system and pumping the fumes out.

<h3 id="repair-the-broken-pipe">
  Repair the Broken Pipe
</h3>

Inspect the damaged equipment near the affected room.

Find a replacement pipe section and install it at the broken connection.

Do not assume the Blight will clear immediately after unlocking the Control Room. The ventilation or pumping equipment still requires its own repair.

<h3 id="pump-out-the-blight">
  Pump Out the Blight
</h3>

After replacing the missing pipe section, return to the machinery used to clear the fumes.

Operate the pump and allow it to remove the Blight from the affected room.

Wait until the room is accessible before proceeding.

If the objective remains unchanged, check that the replacement pipe is installed and that you have actually activated the pumping equipment.

Explore the newly accessible area for information about Junior.

<h2 id="follow-junior-s-trail-from-drying-fort-to-the-grotto">
  Follow Junior's Trail From Drying Fort to the Grotto
</h2>

The investigation does not end with the Control Room.

Continue searching Drying Fort and speaking with characters who may have seen Junior.

One character in the area mentions that Junior behaved strangely and repeatedly talked about a **Grotto**.

This gives you another lead.

The Grotto is associated with a singer whose voice once drew visitors from the surrounding settlements.

Travel toward the location indicated by the investigation.

Do not return to the Baron yet. At this point you still need to establish where Junior went and recover the missing recipe.

<h2 id="how-to-help-jacqueline-at-the-grotto">
  How to Help Jacqueline at the Grotto
</h2>

At the Grotto, you meet **Jacqueline**, whose condition has deteriorated. Helping her is the next step toward obtaining the Observatory key.

She asks for help and requires:

- **3 Predatory Fish**, kept as whole catches.
- A **Marsh Lily**.

Catch the required predatory fish and collect the flower.

Do not process the three fish into crafting materials before handing them over.

Return to the woman with the requested items.

After helping her, speak to her again about Junior.

She cannot clearly remember meeting him, but explains that she once had two house keys and that one of them has disappeared.

She believes that someone may have taken it and gone to her former home.

That home is the **Observatory**.

The woman gives Malcolm her remaining key.

Take the key and continue the investigation at the Observatory.

<h2 id="how-to-enter-the-observatory-and-find-junior-s-trail">
  How to Enter the Observatory and Find Junior's Trail
</h2>

Travel to the Observatory and use the key obtained at the Grotto to open the relevant entrance.

Search the accessible rooms and examine the information connected to Junior and the people who lived there.

The Observatory contains evidence of what happened to the family and why Junior came to the region.

Pay particular attention to the rooms associated with the telescope and the records left behind.

The investigation connects Junior's worsening condition with his search for Essence.

Continue following the accessible trail until you obtain the **missing Essence recipe**.

Do not treat reaching the Observatory as the end of the Baron's assignment. The actual recipe must be recovered and taken back to the traders.

Once you have the recipe, return to Betsy and Sons.

<h2 id="return-the-missing-essence-recipe-to-gill-and-bozur">
  Return the Missing Essence Recipe to Gill and Bozur
</h2>

Speak to the traders and hand over the recovered recipe.

They examine the instructions and explain that the Essence requires concentrated material from fish.

The recipe calls for catches from different fish groups, including:

- Peaceful fish.
- Predatory fish.
- Mutated fish.

Follow the quantities displayed in your active crafting or quest objective.

The traders mention that more valuable fish can be found in the Deep Water, including areas near the Grotto and Bone Reef, but those locations are dangerous for an under-equipped boat.

Prepare the required fish and bring them back.

You can use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Fishing Guide</Link> for bait selection, fighting difficult catches, and fishing equipment decisions.

<h3 id="why-doesn-t-the-first-essence-recipe-finish-the-quest">
  Why Doesn't the First Essence Recipe Finish the Quest?
</h3>

The traders produce an initial batch of Essence, but the results are not what Malcolm expects.

An experiment using the mixture leads to an unusual effect involving the loss and recovery of a character's voice.

The event makes it clear that the recovered instructions are not sufficient for the Baron's intended treatment.

Do not assume the quest is complete because the first batch has been brewed.

Malcolm needs to return to **Estelle** and discuss the recipe.

<h2 id="speak-to-estelle-about-the-failed-essence-recipe">
  Speak to Estelle About the Failed Essence Recipe
</h2>

Contact Estelle and explain that the recovered recipe does not work as intended.

She reveals that she deliberately gave Junior the recipe and that the Baron's account of the theft does not tell the whole story.

Estelle agrees to provide the recipe needed for the Baron's Essence batch, but first asks Malcolm to recover recordings from **Bone Reef**.

She wants information contained in songs associated with the location.

The task is not simply to collect another ordinary crafting material.

You must visit Bone Reef, recover the recordings, listen to them, and report what they reveal.

Before setting out, check your boat's condition, fuel, and available weapons.

Estelle warns that Bone Reef is dangerous and that Malcolm needs a capable boat to reach it.

<h2 id="how-to-restore-the-recordings-at-bone-reef">
  How to Restore the Recordings at Bone Reef
</h2>

Travel to **Bone Reef** and locate the recording equipment associated with Estelle's request.

The machinery includes a panel that resembles an altar.

Repair the recording panel and listen to the material recovered at Bone Reef. Once the recordings have played, Malcolm's next task is to report their meaning to Estelle.

<h3 id="what-to-tell-estelle-about-the-songs">
  What to Tell Estelle About the Songs
</h3>

The songs return to the same idea: **apart, people are sparks; together, they become a flame**. Bring that message to Estelle rather than searching Bone Reef for another physical item.

After the conversation, she explains why the recordings matter to her work on the Blight and provides the requirements for the special Essence batch.

<h2 id="which-fish-does-estelle-need-for-the-special-essence">
  Which Fish Does Estelle Need for the Special Essence?
</h2>

Estelle explains that the required medicine must use mutated fish with different properties.

The recipe identifies three types of ingredients:

1. **A mutated Bonefish**.
2. **A mutated fish whose hearing has atrophied**.
3. **A deep-dwelling mutated fish**.

Each fish must match the mutation described by Estelle; an ordinary catch does not substitute for one of these ingredients. The three types contribute different properties to the treatment.

Return to Gill and Bozur with the recipe information. Check the required fish and any newly available equipment before setting out again. If your tackle cannot handle the catches, use the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing controls and equipment instructions</Link> before using more bait.

<h2 id="prepare-the-final-essence-batch-for-the-baron">
  Prepare the Final Essence Batch for the Baron
</h2>

Collect the fish required by Estelle's special recipe.

Return to the traders with the catches.

Tell them you have brought the special fish Estelle requested.

They begin preparing the proper Essence batch.

Follow their instruction to rest until morning while the preparation is completed.

When the traders confirm the batch is ready, contact the Baron.

<h3 id="report-to-the-baron">
  Report to the Baron
</h3>

Tell the Baron that the Essence has been prepared.

He agrees to honor the arrangement and instructs Malcolm to bring the batch toward the gates leading to Sludge-Bet.

The Baron also warns about creatures near the gate and tells Malcolm to come directly to him after entering the city.

This is the final stage of the currently available story.

Do not confuse the promise of reaching Sludge-Bet with an already completed visit to the city. The Baron is describing what happens next in Malcolm's journey.

Continue following the final available objective until the game presents the story-ending message.
