<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Sail west to Betsy and Sons, deliver **5 Snappers**, and repair John's Moonshine Still to unlock the Dry Dock. Repair the boat, process **3 Softgrazers** into Chromatic Scales for hull protection, then deliver **3 Harpooners** and buy the Carburetor for **50** to install the Upgraded Engine. At The Throat, power the Generator, repair the waterway machinery, get both wheels turning, and open the gate.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The DREADMOOR demo begins in an abandoned house and ends beyond the massive gate at The Throat. Between those points, the main quest takes you through fishing contracts, fuel production, scavenging, boat maintenance, research, and machinery repairs.

Several objectives sound simpler than they are. Repairing the boat requires more than cleaning its hull, the Carburetor does not arrive automatically when you finish the traders' contract, and The Throat contains multiple broken mechanisms before you can even begin adjusting the water flow.

## Leave the Abandoned House and Reach the Trading Post

The opening objective, **Look Around**, begins inside the house. Search the nearby containers for supplies before leaving. Metal Plates and Rope are available here, and both are useful once crafting and repair work become part of the main quest.

Pick up the Axe, then follow the radio objective. You will hear a call but must tune the radio to establish a conversation.

The caller directs you toward a trading post on an island to the west.

Board the motorboat and follow that direction. You do not need to collect every floating object on this first trip. The immediate objective is reaching the traders and establishing somewhere safe to stay.

The Trading Post is recognizable by its dock and large surrounding structures. Moor the boat, go ashore, and enter the **Betsy and Sons** shop.

### Speak to Gill and Bozur

Gill and Bozur explain the condition of the surrounding waters and offer you a dose of Essence to relieve the effects of the Blight.

Accept the treatment. It is not free: the traders record it as a debt that you will repay through work.

They then give you a key to the nearby Bunkhouse and tell you to rest before beginning your first fishing assignment.

## Take the Chest to the Bunkhouse and Repair the Wall

After speaking to the traders, collect your personal chest from the pier. Carry it to the Bunkhouse using the key they provided.

Inside, place the chest in its designated position. Before sleeping, inspect the large opening in the wall. The room is not secure in its current condition.

Pick up the available repair material and patch the opening. You must complete this repair before the overnight objective can proceed.

Use the bed and sleep until morning. Gill and Bozur will contact you again and direct you toward your first fishing assignment.

Do not leave the Bunkhouse without collecting the **Simple Rod**. It is the starting fishing equipment needed for the contract.

## Catch 5 Snappers for Gill and Bozur

Take the Simple Rod to the fishing platform behind the Dry Dock and speak to the traders.

They want **5 Snappers**. These fish are available at the nearby fishing spot, and ordinary Earthworms are sufficient bait.

Catch the required fish and return to Gill and Bozur. Keep the fish available for delivery rather than processing or selling the contract catch beforehand.

The Simple Rod can handle this assignment. A stronger rod is not required to advance the opening quest.

If fish repeatedly escape, break the line, or never take the bait, the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">fishing controls and bait instructions</Link> explain how to manage rod movement, line tension, reeling, and the different fishing behaviors.

After the Snapper contract, the traders send you to the local engineer, **John the Fifth**, who holds the key to the Dry Dock.

Getting that key is your next obstacle.

## Get the Dry Dock Key From John the Fifth

Find John's workshop near the Trading Post and speak to him.

John is in no condition to begin repairing the motorboat. His Moonshine Still has stopped working, and he wants it repaired before he will hand over the Dry Dock Key.

Inspect the Still to identify the obstruction. A prosthetic leg is lodged inside the equipment and must be removed.

That is only the first part of the problem. The Still also needs repair materials.

### Find Coral Fiber, Bug Slime, and Metal Plates

The material search sends you back onto the water. You will need the Axe and Grappling Hook Gun for different objects.

**Coral Fiber — Angler's Island**

John points you toward Angler's Island for Coral Fiber. Sail there, go ashore, and use the Axe on the coral growths.

The coral deposits are physical gathering objects rather than floating cargo. Break the available growths and collect their fibers.

**Bug Slime — Blue creatures on the water**

Look for the small blue creatures that can be collected with the Grappling Hook Gun. Bring them close enough to retrieve, then collect their material.

Bug Slime is important beyond John's repair. You will need it again when restoring the boat's hull, so avoid spending everything on unrelated crafting.

**Metal Plates — Salvage and barrels**

Search floating barrels, metal containers, and salvage around the islands. Use the Grappling Hook Gun to retrieve suitable objects and collect the materials they contain.

Some ordinary containers yield Rope or other supplies rather than the Metal Plates you currently need. Move between salvage objects instead of repeatedly checking an exhausted location.

Rubber can also be obtained from suitable salvage, including tires. It becomes useful during later boat work.

Gather the required materials shown by the Moonshine Still's repair interaction. The exact requirement is the remaining quantity displayed there, not the number of salvage objects you have collected.

Once the Still is repaired, speak to John again. He gives you the **Dry Dock Key** and turns his attention to the terrible condition of your motorboat.

### What if the island has no more Coral Fiber?

A depleted coral deposit is not the end of the material search.

Check the remaining harvestable growths around Angler's Island and any other accessible collection areas. Previously harvested resources may return after time passes, but a single observed respawn interval should not be treated as a guaranteed timer.

The same approach works when an individual barrel or Bug Slime collection point has already been exhausted: look for another available object instead of waiting beside the empty one.

## Process Fish Meat and Refuel the Motorboat

Around this stage, Gill and Bozur also introduce the equipment used to turn fish into fuel.

Take the fish designated for processing to the Grinder and convert them into Fish Meat. The next step is feeding the **Fuel Larva**, which produces the fuel used by the boat.

Producing fuel and filling the motorboat are separate actions.

1. Process the fish into Fish Meat.
2. Feed the Fuel Larva.
3. Use the Fuel Gun to transfer fuel into the motorboat's tank.
4. Check the boat's fuel level before departing.

If the Larva has been fed but the boat still shows insufficient fuel, return to the Fuel Gun. The creature does not automatically fill your tank.

This matters once the quests begin sending you back and forth between the Trading Post, Angler's Island, and later fishing grounds. The boat has to carry you between them, and the same fuel supply supports your eventual trip to The Throat.

## Repair the Dry Dock Fuse Box and Restore Power

Use John's key to enter the Dry Dock.

The first problem is electrical. The building's Fuse Box is damaged, so the lift and other equipment cannot yet be used normally.

Inspect the electrical board and repair the broken connection. One early repair interaction requires **1 Cable**, with a suitable cable available nearby.

Once the electrical connection is repaired, use the separate Power Switch to restore the Dry Dock's lighting and equipment power.

The repair and the switch are different actions. Fixing the Fuse Box alone does not replace the instruction to turn the power on.

With the facility operational, bring the motorboat inside and position it on the Boat Lift. Use the lift controls to raise it for maintenance.

The Dry Dock has multiple working levels. The first major maintenance jobs are cleaning and repairing the hull; the engine replacement comes later.

## Clean the Boat With the Pressure Pump and Water Cannon

Once the boat is in position, follow the objective to pressurize the cleaning equipment.

Operate the Pressure Pump until the cleaning system is ready. Then use the Water Cannon against the growths attached to the boat.

The objective is to remove the Polyps covering the hull. Sweep the water jet over the affected sections rather than spraying only one area.

Removing the Polyps exposes the boat's damaged sections. It does not repair them.

For the next task, load **Bug Slime** into the Repair Gun and work on the damaged hull slots.

Inspect the sections of the boat individually. A repaired slot can show that it is already in good condition even while another section still needs attention.

When the required repairs are finished, speak to John. He acknowledges that the motorboat is now in better condition and introduces the next problem: strengthening the hull.

## Catch 3 Softgrazers and Obtain Chromatic Scales

John needs **Chromatic Scales** before he can reinforce the motorboat. Return to Gill and Bozur for the Wind-Up Tin blueprint, then catch **3 Softgrazers** at the marked peaceful-fish location. If you need the bait recipe or help landing them, follow the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Softgrazer fishing instructions</Link>.

Bring the catch back to the **Thresher** and process the fish into Chromatic Scales. Take the Scales to John rather than selling them to the traders. He rewards you with **Scraps of Knowledge** and asks you to research Weak Hull Protection and the Upgraded Engine.

## Research Weak Hull Protection and the Upgraded Engine

Open the Mastery or research interface and locate the relevant blueprints.

The two important unlocks for the main quest are:

- **Weak Hull Protection**, needed to reinforce the motorboat.
- **Upgraded Engine**, needed for the later engine replacement.

Research consumes Scraps of Knowledge. If you do not have enough, check the Scraps already collected from the environment and use the available supply before spending them on unrelated unlocks.

John provides Scraps as part of the progression, while additional pieces can be found during exploration or acquired through trade.

These unlocks are not the same as crafting the finished equipment. After learning the blueprint, you still need to use the appropriate workbench and collect the physical module.

### Craft Hull Protection at John's Lathe

Bring the required crafting materials to the Lathe in the Dry Dock and select the Hull Protection blueprint.

Metal Plates and other salvage materials become important here. Keep enough supplies for the reinforcement work before using everything on optional equipment.

Craft the required protection module and collect it from the workbench or its designated storage.

One easy mistake is expecting the new module to appear directly in the boat's inventory or mounting position.

### Where to Find Crafted Hull Protection

Check the **Storage Locker** or the Dry Dock's module-storage area.

Crafted boat components can be placed in storage before installation. If the crafting operation succeeded but you cannot find the module in your carried inventory, inspect the storage location before spending resources on another copy.

The Weak Hull Protection module displays these attributes:

- **Installation Location:** Side front
- **Protection:** 2
- **Boat Speed:** -1

The Side front location matters because boat modules are not freely interchangeable between every mounting position.

The displayed Protection value is **2**. The interface also lists a Boat Speed penalty of **-1**, so reinforcing the boat carries a movement trade-off.

Install the reinforcement in its designated slot. If the quest still requires another protection operation, inspect the remaining highlighted mounting position and the current objective before crafting additional parts.

Once the required reinforcement work is complete, report back to John.

He introduces the next major task: replacing the standard engine.

## Get the Carburetor From Gill and Bozur

John cannot finish the upgraded engine without a **Carburetor**.

His old Carburetor ended up with Gill and Bozur after a card game, so he sends you back to the traders.

The merchants do not simply hand it over. They want another fishing contract completed first.

This time, the target is **3 Harpooners**, which are predatory fish.

### Catch and Deliver 3 Harpooners

Gill and Bozur want **3 Harpooners** before they will sell you the Carburetor. Craft Rainbow Fin, travel to the marked predatory-fish location, and keep all three catches for delivery. Harpooners chase a moving lure; the <Link href="/dreadmoor/fishing-guide">Harpooner fishing instructions</Link> explain how to attract them without losing their attention.

Return to the traders with the fish. Once they accept the contract, the Carburetor becomes available in their shop, but you still have to purchase it separately.

### Buy the Carburetor for 50

Completing the fishing contract does **not** automatically put the Carburetor in your inventory.

Speak to Gill and Bozur again, open their shop, and select the Carburetor.

The displayed price in the demo is **50**.

With the Carburetor obtained, return to the Dry Dock.

## Remove the Standard Engine and Install the Upgraded Engine

Position the boat on the lift and raise it to the level required for engine work.

The first action is removing the existing **Standard Engine**.

Interact with its mounting point, remove the engine, and place it into Module Storage as instructed.

Do not craft another Standard Engine by mistake. The replacement must use the **Upgraded Engine** blueprint learned earlier.

Go to John's Lathe and craft the new engine with the Carburetor and the other required materials. Collect the finished component, then return to the motorboat and install it in the vacant engine position.

The engine upgrade improves the displayed boat speed from **2 kn to 4 kn**.

That increase is useful for the longer journey ahead, especially after the extra travel involved in the fishing and scavenging contracts.

Once the replacement engine is installed, speak to John again.

He recognizes that the boat is ready for a longer journey and explains how to leave the current region.

The route goes through **The Throat**, a massive waterway gate that no longer operates correctly.

John also introduces an optional Axe improvement. The main departure objective, however, is reaching The Throat and restoring its machinery.

## Sail to The Throat

With the upgraded engine installed, follow the objective toward The Throat.

Before departing, check the boat's fuel tank and physical condition. The destination contains broken equipment, and you will need to move around the structure on foot rather than remain safely aboard the motorboat.

The approach leads to a large gate complex surrounded by platforms, machinery, and water channels.

Dock near the accessible structure and head toward the main machinery controls.

Your first task is restoring power.

## Find a Fuel Can and Start The Throat Generator

The gate machinery cannot operate without electricity.

Find the Generator and inspect it. The interaction calls for fuel, which must be supplied using a **Fuel Can**.

Search the nearby working area for the can and bring it to the Generator. A Fuel Can is available within The Throat area, so you do not need to return to the Trading Post simply because the Generator initially reports that it has no fuel.

Use the can to refuel the Generator, then perform the startup interaction.

Once power is available, move to the Gate Control and attempt to operate it.

The gate does not open normally. The failed attempt exposes the next objective: **inspect and repair the damaged waterway machinery**.

This is why getting the Generator running does not finish the puzzle.

## Find the Thermal Coil, Gear, and Two Buckets

The Throat contains several damaged components spread across the waterway structure.

You need to examine the individual machines rather than search for one master repair panel.

The important repairs involve:

- A damaged electrical component requiring a **Thermal Coil**.
- A missing Gear or Axle component.
- **Two Buckets** needed by the water-wheel machinery.
- A damaged Water Pump that must be repaired in place.

These parts are found around the accessible platforms, storage areas, and workshop spaces inside The Throat.

### Search the Workshop and Lower Platform

The workshop area provides an important clue about the missing equipment. Its notes refer to a Thermal Coil and buckets associated with the water wheels.

Check the workshop and the nearby supplies for replacement parts.

The lower platforms also contain useful mechanical materials, including gears and coils. Inspect accessible containers and machinery as you move between levels.

The Throat is not one straight corridor. You will need to move between upper walkways and lower working areas, so keep track of which damaged machine needs each part.

A useful way to avoid unnecessary trips is to inspect every damaged installation point before carrying components back. That identifies whether you are missing a Coil, Gear, Bucket, or pump repair.

### Install the Gear or Axle

One of the damaged assemblies has a designated Gear Slot.

Bring the replacement component to the slot and install it using the interaction prompt.

This is a separate repair from the Thermal Coil and the two Buckets. Installing the Gear alone will not complete the waterway restoration.

### Install Both Replacement Buckets

Inspect the damaged water-wheel equipment and its Bucket mounting points.

The repair sequence calls for **two Buckets**. Finding the first one may make the task appear nearly complete, but the second replacement is still required.

Search the workshop and surrounding platforms for both, then install them in their respective positions.

If the objective does not advance after installing one Bucket, check the other wheel assembly and any remaining Bucket prompt.

Do not begin treating the water-flow controls as the only problem while the machinery is still missing a physical component.

### Replace the Thermal Coil

The damaged electrical assembly also needs a Thermal Coil.

Look through the workshop supplies and mechanical salvage in The Throat, then return to the burnt-out component and install the replacement.

The Coil, Gear, and Buckets are distinct pieces of the repair. A successful installation of one does not imply that the other mechanisms are ready.

Once the missing components are in place, continue to the damaged Water Pump.

## Repair the Water Pump — Do Not Craft a New One

Find the damaged Water Pump inside The Throat and use its repair interaction. You need to fix the existing mechanism, not return to John's Lathe to craft a replacement.

After the pump and the other damaged components are repaired, the objective changes to configuring water flow for both wheels. If the task still asks for machinery repairs, check the Thermal Coil, Gear, and two Bucket mounting points before moving to the channel controls.

## Configure the Water Flow to Both Water Wheels

After the damaged machinery is restored, the remaining problem is routing water to both wheels.

The objective requires you to configure the waterway system so that **both water wheels turn**.

The controls are distributed around the channels rather than concentrated in one location. Four water-flow controls are visible in this section, and interacting with them changes the flow through the connected channels.

### Get Both Water Wheels Turning

With the Generator running and the missing components installed, examine the channels between the incoming water and the two wheel assemblies. Operate the water-flow controls one at a time, checking how each change affects the channels and whether water reaches the wheels.

Both wheels must receive water. A single moving wheel is not enough, and the red or green indicators on individual controls do not by themselves mean the entire system is working. Continue adjusting the channels until both wheels turn and the objective advances to operating the Gate Control.

<GuideCallout title="The Throat Gate Still Won't Open?">
If the journal still asks you to repair machinery, check the Thermal Coil, Gear, both Buckets, and Water Pump. If it asks you to configure the water flow, the remaining problem is at the channel controls, not the Generator. The gate can be opened after both wheels are operating.
</GuideCallout>

## Open The Throat Gate

Once both water wheels are running and the waterway objective has advanced, return to the main Gate Control.

Operate the control to open the passage.

The large gate lowers, exposing the route beyond the waterway structure.

The next objective becomes **Set Out**.

You do not need to return to John for another engine upgrade or repeat the fishing contracts. The required work at The Throat has been completed.

Make your way back to the motorboat and prepare to depart.

## Set Out and Finish the DREADMOOR Demo

Board the boat and follow the newly opened route through The Throat.

Pay attention to obstacles in the water as you pass through the gate. The final stretch still requires steering rather than triggering an immediate ending from the control panel.

Sail through the opened passage until the game transitions to its completion screen.
