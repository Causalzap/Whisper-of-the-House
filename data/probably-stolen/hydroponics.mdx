<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To start Hydroponics in Probably Stolen, choose **Hydroponic Farmer** or unlock the **Retired Farmer** through Joe Wild. Put a seed, a container of suitable water, and a power source in a Hydroponic System. Use Nutrient Tablets to adjust Minerals, and check the water with an AquaScan.

For **Huge Nutrifruit**, keep Minerals within **0.5%–2.5%**, avoid harmful contaminants, and preserve a **Growth Score of at least 96%** at harvest. For advanced seeds, unlock **Plant Genetics**, use the DNA Manipulator to extract or install Traits, and grow a new generation between edits to clear Genetic Heat.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Hydroponics is not simply a matter of putting a seed into a machine and waiting for the next day.

A plant can finish growing but produce smaller or less valuable items because its water contained too many contaminants, too few Minerals, or slightly more Minerals than the crop could tolerate.

The best approach is to get one reliable harvest first, learn how to read the plant's condition, and then improve production with modules or edited seeds.

<section id="start-hydroponics">

## How Do You Unlock Hydroponics and Start Farming?

The **Hydroponic Farmer** starting scenario gives you an immediate way to learn the system.

You begin with farming equipment, seeds, growing water, and a Hydroponic Farming Manual. The scenario also increases your starting rent, so it helps to produce something you can use or sell early.

If you already have another profession, you do not need to restart.

Open Joe Wild's Network and unlock the **Retired Farmer**. The normal base cost is **350 Credits**, although your difficulty and purchase conditions may change the price.

The unlock provides farming supplies and access to a supplier who returns regularly with seeds, nutrients, and Hydroponics equipment.

The Retired Farmer can supply:

- Fillerweed and Nutrifruit Seeds.
- Hydroreed, BloomBerry, Coffee, Dream Cap and Kotton Seeds.
- Hydroponic Nutrient Tablets.
- Plant Analyzers and AquaScans.
- Hydroponic Systems and specialized modules.

### What should you grow first?

If you are still learning the machine, start with **Fillerweed** or **Nutrifruit**.

Fillerweed grows quickly and tolerates the main harmful water contaminants. It is useful when you need a simple first harvest.

Nutrifruit uses much less water per night but requires a suitable Mineral concentration and cleaner water.

If the starting water already contains the right nutrients, Nutrifruit can be a good first choice. If you cannot measure the water yet, Fillerweed is a safer crop for learning how the machine operates.

Do not spend all your starting money buying more seeds before confirming that your first Hydroponic System has enough Energy and growing water.

</section>

<section id="hydroponic-system">

## How Do You Set Up a Hydroponic System?

A Hydroponic System grows one seed at a time.

Its basic inputs are:

1. **Seed:** The crop you want to grow.
2. **Water:** A container holding enough liquid for the growing cycle.
3. **Energy:** A battery or another compatible power source.
4. **Modules:** Optional upgrades that change speed, Energy use, harvest quantity, or crop behavior.

To begin your first crop:

1. Open the Hydroponic System.
2. Insert a container of Ghostwater, Basewater, or another suitable growing-water mixture.
3. Check the seed's water and nutrient requirements.
4. Adjust Minerals if necessary.
5. Place the seed into the Seed slot.
6. Supply enough Energy.
7. Start the process and let the machine advance overnight.

![Hydroponic Instruction explaining the Water, Seed, Energy and Module requirements for operating a Hydroponic System.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-first-system.webp)

The base machine provides **8 work points per active night** and requires approximately **8 kJ of Energy per night**.

A default Nutrifruit Seed needs 16 work points. At 8 work points per night, it finishes in two successful processing nights.

A default Fillerweed Seed needs 8 work points, so it can finish in one.

Different modules and modified seed Traits can change those times.

### Why does the machine say it needs more water?

The machine needs enough water for the crop's current overnight cycle.

For example, ordinary Nutrifruit uses **100 mL per night**. A two-night crop needs enough water for both nights, not just the first.

Fillerweed is more demanding than its easy-growing description suggests: its default seed uses about **451 mL per night**.

A small container can be enough for Nutrifruit but insufficient for a longer or thirstier crop.

Check the seed's actual water requirement before filling the system.

### Can you change water or modules while a crop is growing?

You can replace or refill the water supply as needed.

However, the seed and installed modules are generally locked during the active growing cycle.

This matters when you discover that you selected the wrong crop-specific module.

If you want to abandon a planted crop, a **Hatchet** can clear it from the system, ending that growing cycle.

Try to correct the water before resorting to destroying the crop.

<section id="water-and-minerals">

## How Much Water and Minerals Do Hydroponic Plants Need?

Each crop has a preferred Mineral concentration.

Water that is suitable for one plant may damage another.

For example:

- Nutrifruit tolerates approximately **0.5%–2.5% Minerals**.
- BloomBerry tolerates **1.25%–2.75%**.
- Coffee and Dream Cap tolerate **1.75%–3.25%**.
- Kotton tolerates **2%–5%**.

These ranges apply to ordinary seeds. Modified Traits can change the actual requirements.

Always check the individual seed if you are using Gene Editing.

### How do you check the water?

Use an **AquaScan** on the liquid container before planting.

Inspect:

- Mineral concentration.
- Heavy Metals.
- Chemical Contaminants.
- Microbes.
- Organic Waste.
- Acid and Base.

Physical Contaminants are not part of the usual plant-health damage checks, but other contaminants can be harmful even when their quantities appear small.

If several harmful contaminants are present, the plant can suffer damage from multiple sources during the same growing cycle.

A container labeled High-Quality Water does not automatically contain the right nutrients for your crop.

### How do Hydroponic Nutrient Tablets work?

A Hydroponic Nutrient Tablet adds **15 mL of Minerals** to a liquid container.

For a simple example, adding 15 mL of Minerals to approximately 1,000 mL of clean water produces a concentration near **1.5%**, which is suitable for ordinary Nutrifruit.

But adding the same tablet to a much smaller water volume creates a higher concentration.

Do not add one tablet to every container without checking its size.

If the container already contains Minerals, count those too.

### Why can Minerals become too concentrated overnight?

The plant consumes both water and Minerals while growing, but not necessarily in the same proportion.

As the available water changes, the Mineral percentage can shift.

The relevant concentration depends on the mineral amount relative to the remaining water, not just the amount added at the start.

A practical Nutrifruit example:

- The water initially measured **3.33% Minerals**, above the acceptable range.
- Adding clean water reduced the concentration to **2.46%**.
- During growth, the concentration later reached approximately **2.51%**.
- That small increase pushed the mixture above Nutrifruit's 2.5% upper limit.

![AquaScan showing water at 2.46% Minerals inside a Hydroponic System, within the acceptable Nutrifruit range.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-mineral-dilution.webp)

The lesson is to leave some room below the maximum rather than aiming exactly at 2.50%.

For Nutrifruit, starting near the middle of its acceptable Mineral range is usually safer than preparing water at either boundary.

### Is Pure Water always the best choice?

Not by itself.

Pure Water is useful because it minimizes harmful contaminants, but plants also need nutrients.

If you put nearly mineral-free water into a machine, a crop that requires Minerals may lose Health through nutrient deficiency.

Prepare water for the plant's needs instead of assuming that the cleanest possible water guarantees the best harvest.

For the separate process of producing and selling purified water, see the [Water Purification Guide](/probably-stolen/water-purification).

</section>

<section id="growth-score">

## What Is Growth Score, and Why Does It Affect Harvests?

**Growth Score** measures how well a plant has survived its growing conditions.

A newly planted seed generally starts at **100%**.

When the plant experiences unsuitable Minerals or harmful contaminants, its score can fall during processing.

This score affects what you receive when the crop finishes.

Use a **Plant Analyzer** to inspect the current plant condition and growing requirements.

### How does Growth Score change the harvest?

Different crops react differently to poor growing conditions.

For crops with ordinary size tiers, high Health favors Large products. Lower Health can increase the number of Small or normal-sized products.

Some crops also lose harvest quantity when Health becomes too low.

Nutrifruit is unusual because its total fruit count is generally preserved, while the **sizes of the resulting fruit change**.

A healthy Nutrifruit harvest can contain Large and Huge fruit. A badly damaged harvest may contain mostly ordinary or smaller fruit instead.

### Growth Score vs Machine Quality

These are different mechanics.

**Growth Score** depends primarily on the crop's growing conditions and determines which health-dependent harvest results are available.

**Machine Quality** can increase harvest quantity and interact with certain crop-specific harvest outcomes.

Increasing machine Quality does not automatically repair a plant that has already lost Health from contaminated water.

If you are getting poor crops, fix the water first. Buy additional Quality modules after the basic growing conditions are reliable.

</section>

<section id="huge-nutrifruit">

## How Do You Grow Huge Nutrifruit in Probably Stolen?

To grow Huge Nutrifruit, you need a Nutrifruit Seed that reaches harvest with an extremely high Growth Score.

The important threshold is **96% or higher**.

Under ordinary Nutrifruit harvest rules:

| Growth Score at harvest | Expected result |
|---|---|
| 95% or below | No Huge Nutrifruit |
| 96%–99% | A mixture of Large and Huge Nutrifruit |
| 100% | All fruit can be Huge |

Machine Quality can also affect the final size distribution.

You do not need Gene Editing, Hypertech equipment, or an upgraded seed to obtain your first Huge Nutrifruit.

### Step 1: Prepare suitable Nutrifruit water

Nutrifruit needs approximately:

- **100 mL of water per active night**.
- **0.5%–2.5% Minerals**.
- Very low concentrations of harmful contaminants.

The normal seed needs 16 work points, so an unmodified base-speed machine can finish the crop in two nights.

For your first attempt, prepare more than the minimum required water and keep the Mineral percentage comfortably inside the safe range.

Use AquaScan before planting.

If the water contains too many Minerals, dilute it with clean water. If it contains too few, add a measured amount of nutrient solution.

### Step 2: Plant the Nutrifruit Seed

Put the prepared liquid container and Nutrifruit Seed into the Hydroponic System.

Check that the machine has enough Energy for its growing cycle.

Start the machine.

Do not change your strategy based only on whether the plant is making progress. It can continue growing while suffering Health damage.

### Step 3: Check Growth Score before the final night

Use the Plant Analyzer while the crop is growing.

If the score is already below 96%, the current crop is unlikely to produce Huge Nutrifruit under ordinary harvest conditions.

You can still harvest it for food, but it will not satisfy the same goal.

If the score remains high but is dropping, inspect the water again.

A Mineral concentration that was acceptable on the first night may have changed before the final cycle.

### Step 4: Correct the water if necessary

If Minerals are too high, add clean water to dilute the mixture.

If harmful contaminants are the problem, replace the growing water with a cleaner supply.

Do not keep adding nutrient tablets to a plant whose main problem is already excessive Minerals.

In one successful setup, the growing water briefly exceeded Nutrifruit's Mineral limit and the plant's Growth Score dropped to **97.5%**.

Correcting the water prevented the remaining growth from causing further damage.

![Plant Analyzer showing a growing Nutrifruit Seed with a 97.5% Growth Score.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-nutrifruit-growth-score.webp)

### Step 5: Harvest Huge Nutrifruit

Once the crop completes, collect the fruit from the machine.

A successful high-Health harvest can produce visibly larger Huge Nutrifruit alongside Large Nutrifruit.

![A successful Huge Nutrifruit harvest showing the unusually large fruit produced after maintaining a high Growth Score.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-huge-nutrifruit.webp)

Obtaining Huge Nutrifruit also completes the **Green Thumb** achievement requirement.

You can track it alongside the other unlocks in the [Achievements Checklist](/probably-stolen/achievements).

### Why did the same seed fail in another tank?

A useful comparison is a Nutrifruit crop grown in water with excessive Chemical Contaminants.

Its Growth Score fell to approximately **76.6%**.

The plant still completed its growing cycle, but the harvest did not produce Huge Nutrifruit.

![A Nutrifruit plant with a 76.6% Growth Score after growing in water containing excessive Chemical Contaminants.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-nutrifruit-failed.webp)

The important distinction is that **finishing a crop and producing a Huge crop are not the same goal**.

A Hydroponic System can operate correctly while the water conditions remain unsuitable for a high-quality harvest.

### Why are Nutrifruit Seeds not coming back?

Ordinary Nutrifruit Seeds can carry **Infertile X**, which makes seed return extremely unlikely.

Do not plan a renewable Nutrifruit farm around receiving several replacement seeds from every harvest.

Keep a supplier available and protect any modified seed you intend to preserve.

Some story progression affects newly obtained Nutrifruit Seeds, but seeds already in your inventory retain their existing Traits.

</section>

<section id="hydroponics-troubleshooting">

## Why Are Your Hydroponic Plants Not Growing or Producing Less?

When a Hydroponic System appears to be working but the result is disappointing, identify whether the problem is **processing**, **plant Health**, or **seed behavior**.

<GuideTable columns={problemColumns} rows={problemRows} />

### The Hydroponic System stopped progressing

Start with the inputs.

Check whether:

1. The machine has enough Energy for an active cycle.
2. The liquid container holds the required water volume.
3. A valid seed is installed.
4. The current job has actually started.
5. The machine's Progress is increasing after each night.

Water and Energy shortages can stop the job without destroying the seed immediately.

A machine that cannot run does not necessarily have a problem with its Crop Quality or Traits.

### The machine finishes, but the harvest is small

Use a Plant Analyzer before changing modules.

If Health is low, improve the growing water and try again.

If Health is already high, inspect the seed's Bountiful or Sparse Traits and the machine's Quality.

Do not treat a low harvest count as proof that the machine needs more Performance. Performance changes how quickly work is completed; it does not directly repair poor growing conditions.

### The plant's Growth Score keeps falling

Check the current growing water with AquaScan. Excessive contaminants or Minerals outside the seed's acceptable range can lower Growth Score even while the machine continues processing.

If the plant is still growing, correct its water before the next overnight cycle. See [How Much Water and Minerals Do Hydroponic Plants Need?](#water-and-minerals) for dilution and nutrient adjustments.

For a Huge Nutrifruit attempt, check the [96% Growth Score requirement](#huge-nutrifruit) before committing more Energy to the crop.

### The plant is ready, but nothing appears in the output

Check available inventory and output space.

A completed harvest needs somewhere to place its products.

If every suitable inventory is full, items that cannot be placed may be lost.

Clear enough space before the final growing cycle when you are expecting a large Nutrifruit, Kotton, or Dream Cap harvest.

<section id="crop-comparison">

## Which Crops Should You Grow in Probably Stolen?

There are seven main crops available through Hydroponics.

The following times assume ordinary unmodified seeds, a base Hydroponic System producing 8 work points per night, and enough water and Energy for every cycle.

<GuideTable columns={cropColumns} rows={cropRows} />

These are starting values. Seed Traits and machine modules can change water use, work requirements, and harvests.

### Fillerweed: easy first harvests and seed supply

Fillerweed tolerates the six main harmful water contaminants, which makes it forgiving when your water-treatment equipment is limited.

It also carries useful genetic Traits for later seed editing.

However, it consumes much more water per night than Nutrifruit.

If you have only a small water container, check the volume before planting.

Without its special production module, Fillerweed normally returns a replacement seed, with a chance of an additional seed.

This makes it useful when you want to maintain a renewable supply rather than buying seeds repeatedly.

### Hydroreed: use plants to improve water

Hydroreed does not produce ordinary food.

While it grows, its **Purifying** Trait removes certain contaminants from its water supply.

Its default seed tolerates the main contaminants and uses only about **20 mL of water per night**, making it attractive for water-treatment work.

![Hydroreed Seed requirements showing 0–2% Minerals, 20 mL daily water use and immunity to contaminants.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-hydroreed-water.webp)

### How Do You Grow Hydroreed and Collect Hydroreed Filters?

To grow Hydroreed, place its seed in a Hydroponic System with sufficient water and Energy. Its normal Mineral range is **0%–2%**, and it uses approximately **20 mL of water per active night**.

Hydroreed's **Purifying** Trait removes eligible contaminants as the plant grows, but it does not remove Minerals. Its immunity to the main contaminants makes it suitable for growing in water that would damage more sensitive crops.

If you want **Hydroreed Filters** instead of ordinary plant output, install the **Hydroreed Filter Hydroponic Module** before beginning the crop.

The living Hydroreed plant and the harvested Hydroreed Filter have different uses. For cleaning large water batches, comparing Hydroreed against a Water Purifier, and reaching the final Pure Water grade, follow the [Water Purification Guide](/probably-stolen/water-purification).

### Nutrifruit: food and Huge harvests

Nutrifruit is useful when you want a relatively short growing cycle and a valuable food supply.

It is also the crop required for Green Thumb.

Its main challenges are the need for suitable Minerals, protecting Growth Score, and replacing seeds when the starting seed carries Infertile.

Raise the quality of your water before investing heavily in harvest-quantity modules.

### BloomBerry: a plant that keeps fruiting

BloomBerry is useful for supplying ingredients for [Wine Making](/probably-stolen/wine-making), where berry condition affects the finished Wine Score.

Its **Perennial** Trait means the plant remains in the Hydroponic System after the first harvest.

A default BloomBerry needs about three nights for its initial harvest and two nights for subsequent harvests at base machine speed.

![BloomBerry Seed tooltip describing its Perennial behavior and continued fruiting after the first harvest.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-bloomberry-perennial.webp)

Keep its water supply healthy across repeated cycles.

BloomBerry quality is also affected by Health. Healthy plants can produce a mix of berry grades, including the Small berries useful for high-quality wine.

Do not expect every harvest to return another BloomBerry Seed. The original plant is meant to remain in place.

### Coffee Beans: a renewable chemical ingredient

Coffee Beans are useful when you want to produce Coffee, Caffeine-related ingredients, and Coffee Bean Mush for chemical processing.

Unlike many crops, the harvested Coffee Beans themselves can be replanted.

That makes them convenient for preserving improved genetic Traits across generations.

If you are farming Coffee for chemistry, keep enough harvested beans to restart the next crop before processing the rest.

The detailed extraction and mixture preparation belong in the [Chemist Guide](/probably-stolen/chemist).

### Dream Cap: mushrooms for further processing

Dream Cap produces mushrooms that can be used in chemical recipes.

The plant needs more work than Nutrifruit or Coffee and can suffer reduced harvest quantity when Health is poor.

Its output also changes with certain specialized Hydroponic Modules.

For example, the **Dream Cap Neutralizer Hydroponic Module** converts the harvest into Meal Caps, while other specialized modules can produce different results.

Choose the module according to which ingredient you actually need.

If you want Dream Cap Mush, do not accidentally install a module that changes the harvest into a product that no longer supplies it.

For converting mushrooms into chemical products, see the [Chemist Guide](/probably-stolen/chemist).

### Kotton: fiber and a useful Gene Editing project

Kotton has one of the longest ordinary growing cycles.

Its default seed needs approximately **43 work points** and consumes **250 mL of water per night**.

At base machine speed, this means roughly six active nights before harvest.

Kotton is useful for producing fiber, fabric, and materials used elsewhere in the shop.

It is also an excellent example of how Gene Editing can make slow-growing crops much more convenient.

A modified Kotton Seed with Slow Grower removed and Fast Grower II installed can complete its crop much faster than the original.

<section id="hydroponics-modules">

## Which Hydroponics Modules and Upgrades Are Worth Using?

The best module depends on what is limiting your farm.

Do not upgrade every Hydroponic System in the same way.

### Performance: when you need faster harvests

Performance increases how quickly the machine processes its required work.

For an ordinary machine:

- At 8 work per night, Nutrifruit takes two nights.
- At 11 work per night, Coffee can finish in two nights instead of three.
- At 12 work per night, repeat BloomBerry harvests can finish in one night.
- At 16 work per night, ordinary Nutrifruit can finish in one night.

The important number is the machine's final **Progress per Night**, not just the combined Performance percentage shown by individual modules.

Check the actual speed after installing modules.

### Quality: when you want more usable products

Quality can increase harvest quantity and improve certain harvest outcomes.

It is useful when the growing conditions are already reliable.

For example, a healthy crop that finishes on schedule but produces too few items may benefit from additional Quality.

A crop whose Health is repeatedly falling because of the water will not be fixed simply by adding more Quality.

### Efficiency: when Energy is the bottleneck

Efficiency changes the amount of Energy required to operate the machine.

This is useful when you are running several Hydroponic Systems or using high-Performance modules that increase power consumption.

If you cannot provide enough Energy for the next overnight cycle, additional Performance is not helpful.

Restore reliable processing first.

### Which crop-specific modules matter?

Some modules change what a crop produces rather than merely increasing machine statistics.

Examples include:

- **Nutrifruit Hydroponic Module:** Adds an extra fruit to qualifying Nutrifruit harvests, with machine-stat tradeoffs.
- **Hydroreed Filter Hydroponic Module:** Produces Hydroreed Filters when Hydroreed finishes.
- **Fillerweed Production Hydroponic Module:** Increases Fillerweed output but prevents normal seed returns.
- **Kotton Auto Loom Hydroponic Module:** Converts harvested Kotton Fiber into fabric.
- **Dream Cap Neutralizer Hydroponic Module:** Changes the Dream Cap harvest into Meal Caps.

Do not install a production module merely because it increases output.

A Fillerweed farm intended to provide replacement seeds may become less useful if you install the module that stops seed returns.

### How do Neuro Core and Turbo Boosters help?

Late-game equipment can make repeated growing and genetic operations faster.

The **Neural Core Module** provides +200% Performance and +100% Quality, but its -100% Efficiency penalty substantially increases Energy requirements.

It is especially useful in the DNA Manipulator, where additional speed and success chance can save several nights of work.

![A Neural Core Module tooltip inside the DNA Manipulator showing Performance +200%, Efficiency -100% and Quality +100%.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-neuro-core-dna.webp)

A **Turbo Booster** advances a machine's work without requiring you to wait for another ordinary overnight cycle.

Advanced and Hypertech versions can be recharged and reused.

Hypertech Turbo Boosters can store multiple charges, but they still need sufficient Energy to recharge.

If you upgrade a Hypertech Booster by consuming an Advanced Turbo Booster, use any stored boost on the Advanced version first so that charge is not wasted.

These upgrades are convenient, but **none are required to grow your first Huge Nutrifruit**.

<section id="gene-editing">

## How Do You Edit Seeds with the DNA Manipulator?

Gene Editing lets you change the Traits carried by a seed.

You can remove an unwanted Trait, add a useful Trait from another crop, and then grow a new generation that inherits those changes.

For a practical first project, improve Kotton by removing **Slow Grower** and adding **Fast Grower II**.

The key rule is:

**Edit the seed, grow it, then edit the new generation.**

Do not repeatedly edit the same seed without accounting for Genetic Heat.

### Step 1: Unlock Plant Genetics

Purchase **Plant Genetics** through Joe Wild's Network after unlocking the Retired Farmer.

Its ordinary base cost is **1,250 Credits**.

The unlock provides:

- DNA Manipulator.
- Gene Scanner.
- Four Blank DNA Data Disks.
- Access to additional DNA equipment from Dr. Jackson.

Before purchasing, leave enough inventory or machine space to receive the equipment.

### Step 2: Use Gene Scanner to inspect a seed

Turn on the **Gene Scanner**.

With it active, hover over a seed to inspect its genetic Traits, Trait ranks, Complexity, and Heat.

![An unmodified Kotton Seed showing Slow Grower I, Bountiful III, Thirsty II, Frail II and Genetic Complexity 0 inside the DNA Manipulator.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-gene-scanner-cotton.webp)

An ordinary Kotton Seed includes:

- **Bountiful III:** Improves harvest quantity.
- **Slow Grower:** Reduces growth speed.
- **Frail:** Increases sensitivity to contamination.
- **Thirsty:** Increases water use.

You do not need to remove every negative Trait at once.

For a first editing project, choose one change that makes the crop easier to use.

Removing Slow Grower is a sensible starting point because Kotton normally takes many nights to finish.

### Step 3: Learn the DNA Manipulator slots

The DNA Manipulator uses different disk positions for extraction and application.

![A DNA Manipulator displaying the Target Seed, Trait Disc, Blank Disc, and Output positions used during genetic editing.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-dna-manipulator-slots.webp)

The important slots are:

**Target**

The seed being modified or used as the Trait donor.

**Trait Disc**

A written DNA Data Disc placed here applies its Trait to the Target Seed.

**Blank Disc**

A blank DNA Data Disc placed here extracts an eligible Trait from the Target Seed.

A written matching disc can also be used here to extract more charges of that specific Trait.

**Output**

The machine places completed discs and other results here when there is enough space.

Before starting an operation, hover over the DNA Manipulator and check the preview.

It should tell you which operation is selected, whether the target survives, how much work is required, and the success chance.

### Step 4: Install Genetic Stabilizer before extraction

The **Genetic Stabilizer Module** is one of the most important upgrades for repeated Gene Editing.

Without it, extraction destroys the donor seed, including when the extraction attempt fails.

With the module installed, the seed survives the attempt.

On a successful extraction, one rank of the selected Trait is removed from the donor seed.

This means the same operation can both collect a Trait Disc and remove an unwanted Trait from the seed.

When removing Slow Grower from Kotton, you want the seed to survive so you can grow its improved offspring.

### Step 5: Remove Slow Grower from Kotton

Start with an ordinary Kotton Seed.

1. Insert the Kotton Seed into **Target**.
2. Install the Genetic Stabilizer Module.
3. Put a Blank DNA Data Disc into the extraction slot.
4. Leave the application slot empty.
5. Check the Success Chance and begin extraction.
6. Inspect the resulting disc and surviving seed when the operation finishes.

With no targeted extraction tool, the machine selects an eligible Trait randomly.

You may extract Bountiful, Frail, or Thirsty instead of Slow Grower.

If the result is not Slow Grower, save the disc if useful and try again with another suitable seed rather than repeatedly increasing Heat on the same one.

A **Negative Trait Filter Module** narrows random extraction to negative Traits, making it easier to remove an unwanted negative Trait.

Once you obtain a written Slow Grower Disc, placing that matching disc in the extraction slot allows you to target Slow Grower instead of rolling randomly again.

### Step 6: Grow the modified Kotton Seed

After successfully removing Slow Grower, put the surviving Kotton Seed into a Hydroponic System.

Grow it to harvest.

Keep one of the new Kotton Seeds.

That new generation inherits the changed genetic Traits but starts with **zero Genetic Heat**.

This is the reason growing between edits is so important.

A modified seed can be harder to edit immediately after the operation than a new seed carrying the same permanent changes.

### Step 7: Extract Fast Grower from Fillerweed

Ordinary Fillerweed is a convenient donor for **Fast Grower**.

Use a fresh Fillerweed Seed rather than one that already has substantial Genetic Heat.

1. Insert the Fillerweed Seed into the DNA Manipulator.
2. Use a Blank DNA Data Disc for extraction.
3. Install a Positive Trait Filter Module if available.
4. Start the operation.
5. Check whether the output disc contains Fast Grower.

A fresh Fillerweed Seed has several available Traits, so extraction may require more than one attempt.

Its innate Fast Grower ranks can provide enough disc uses for multiple installations.

If you obtain the required Fast Grower Disc, keep it for the improved Kotton Seed.

### Step 8: Install Fast Grower on Kotton

Take the next-generation Kotton Seed that no longer has Slow Grower.

1. Put it into **Target**.
2. Put the Fast Grower Disc into **Trait Disc**.
3. Leave the extraction slot empty.
4. Check Success Chance and required work.
5. Complete the installation.
6. Plant the resulting Kotton Seed.
7. Harvest a new seed with its Heat reset.
8. Install Fast Grower again to reach Rank II.

Fast Grower and Slow Grower are incompatible, which is why the negative Trait must be removed before adding Fast Grower.

If an installation fails, the disc does not spend a charge, but the seed still gains Heat.

Use a fresh offspring seed for the next attempt when possible.

### What does Fast Grower II Kotton look like?

The final modified Kotton Seed should:

- No longer have Slow Grower.
- Have **Fast Grower II**.
- Retain its other inherited Traits unless separately modified.

![A completed Kotton Seed with Slow Grower removed and Fast Grower II installed, ready to produce faster-growing offspring.](/images/probably-stolen/hydroponics-fast-grower-cotton.webp)

This combination can reduce the crop's growing time substantially compared with ordinary Kotton.

Any replacement seeds produced by growing that modified Kotton will inherit the same successful permanent edits.

That lets you maintain the improved strain without repeating the entire process for every new plant.

### Why does DNA editing get harder after each operation?

DNA editing uses two related values:

**Complexity**

The permanent genetic burden created by changing Trait ranks from the seed's original genome.

**Heat**

A temporary editing penalty that increases when you perform genetic operations, including failed attempts.

The DNA Manipulator combines these values when calculating work and success chance.

With no Quality bonus, a seed with zero effective Complexity begins at approximately **95% Success Chance**.

Each additional point of effective Complexity reduces that chance by 5 percentage points, subject to the game's minimum success chance.

For example, after removing Slow Grower from an ordinary Kotton Seed:

- Permanent Complexity can become **3**.
- The operation can add **5 Heat**.
- The next attempt on the same seed can fall to **55%**.
- Growing the seed and collecting its offspring clears Heat, raising the next-generation chance to **80%** before other bonuses.

Machine Quality can raise the Success Chance.

However, higher Quality does not remove the permanent Complexity you have already built into the seed.

### What should you do when Success Chance becomes too low?

Use this order:

1. Stop making repeated attempts on the same hot seed.
2. Plant it if it can still grow and produce offspring.
3. Harvest a replacement seed with zero Heat.
4. Recheck the Success Chance.
5. Add Quality modules if the permanent Complexity still makes editing too risky.
6. Use suitable Trait filters or a matching written disc to avoid unnecessary random extractions.

A **Precision Extraction Module** can also be configured to target a specific Trait once the relevant DNA Disc is available.

That is useful when a valuable seed contains many Traits and you do not want to remove the wrong one.

### Can you give Coffee or Hydroreed new Traits?

Yes. Gene Editing is not limited to Kotton.

For example, you can improve Hydroreed's purification ability or add growth-related Traits to Coffee.

A **Purifying DNA Data Disk** can transfer a contaminant-removing Trait to another crop.

## What Should You Do After Your First Hydroponics Harvest?

Choose the next improvement based on what is currently stopping your farm.

If you are losing Growth Score, focus on the AquaScan readings and Mineral concentration. Buying more expensive seeds will not solve bad water.

If your plants finish healthy but take too many nights, improve Performance or work toward Fast Grower Traits.

If you need a steady supply of food, keep Fillerweed or Nutrifruit growing. If you need cleaner water, prepare a dedicated Hydroreed system.

If you want berries for brewing, use BloomBerry's repeated harvests rather than constantly replacing the plant.

If you want valuable ingredients for chemical production, grow Coffee or Dream Cap and maintain enough replacement seeds for the next cycle.

For **Huge Nutrifruit**, prioritize clean water and Growth Score before anything else.

For long-term seed improvement, start with a simple genetic project such as Fast Grower II Kotton. Once you understand how extraction, installation, Complexity and Heat interact, you can apply the same process to more specialized crops.

A reliable Hydroponics setup is one where you know exactly why the plant is growing, what it will produce, and which input needs attention before the next night.
