A Water Purifier can run for several nights and still leave its container labeled High-Quality Water. Before buying more filters or waiting another night, inspect two numbers: the water's actual purity and the machine's **Max Purity**.

Pure Water begins at **99.9% purity**. A purifier without Quality upgrades stops at 99%, while the final fractions of contamination take longer to remove than the larger impurities found in dirty water. Reaching the machine's limit and progressing slowly toward that limit require different fixes.

## Should You Use a Moisture Farm or Purify Tap Water?

Tap water is a workable starting material, but its contaminants determine which treatment is worth using. Ordinary pitcher filters and Water Purification Tablets target particular impurities; the powered Water Purifier can process a much wider range, including contaminants that survive those simpler treatments.

A Moisture Farm reduces the amount of preparation needed by producing cleaner water before filtration. It is useful for repeatable production, but you do not need to buy one just to turn an existing container into Pure Water.

A **Moisture Farm** provides a better starting point. An unmodified farm initially produces **Ghost Water**, but adding Quality bonuses improves what it produces:

- **+50% Quality:** Base Water.
- **+100% Quality:** High Quality Water.

These are equipment Quality bonuses, not the water's displayed Purity percentage. A machine showing +100% Quality does not mean its water has 100% Purity.

The distinction becomes important when comparing an upgraded farm with a Water Purifier. Each machine has its own Quality configuration, and upgrading one does not automatically improve the other.

### Check the Water Scanner before upgrading anything

Scan the water you're about to process. The Water Scanner shows its contaminants, their quantities, and the overall Purity percentage used to determine its grade.

![Water Scanner showing the water's contaminants, measured purity, and quality.](/images/probably-stolen/water-scanner-contaminants.webp)

Look at the contaminant readings rather than judging the batch by the name on its container. Two batches described as High Quality Water can still have different measured purity, leaving different amounts of work before they reach Pure Water.

This also matters when purchasing water from a customer. A container labeled Base Water or High Quality Water is not a substitute for checking what's actually inside it. Buying several containers without testing them can leave you with lower-quality water than you planned to process.

### Set up the Moisture Farm's Quality modules

Add Quality-producing modules to the Moisture Farm and inspect the resulting Quality value. For the multi-stage Pure Water setup, **+100% Quality on the farm** is a useful target because it produces High Quality Water before you begin manual filtering.

![Moisture Farm showing a production rate of 1000 mL Ghostwater per day and an energy requirement of 4 per day.](/images/probably-stolen/moisture-farm-output.webp)

After adjusting the modules, inspect the water inside the Moisture Farm rather than relying on the machine configuration alone. This 6000 mL jug shows 99.60% purity and is tested as High-Quality Water.

![Moisture Farm with installed modules and a full 6000 mL jug tested as 99.60% High-Quality Water.](/images/probably-stolen/moisture-farm-high-quality.webp)

Check the water's tested grade after each production cycle. The modules determine the machine's configuration, while the container shows the actual water available for further filtering.

Purchased or collected water can also be filtered, but the amount of contamination determines how much work remains. Scan any existing batch before processing it; water that is already close to High Quality may need much less filtering than heavily contaminated tap water.

## Which Filters Should You Use Before the Water Purifier?

Use the Water Scanner to identify what is contaminating the batch before choosing a treatment. Different tools remove different materials, so repeatedly pouring water through the same filter may stop improving purity even when some durability remains.

- **Water Purification Tablets:** Remove large amounts of microbial contamination and part of the chemical contamination.
- **Water Filter:** Removes impurities, microplastics, and organic waste as water is poured into a Water Pitcher.
- **UV Lamp:** Targets microorganisms rather than every contaminant in the water.

Match each treatment to the contaminants shown by the AquaScan. Removing contamination may increase the measured purity without immediately changing the water's grade. Once a filter stops producing a measurable improvement, switch to a treatment that targets the remaining contaminants or move the batch to a powered Water Purifier.

### Don't exhaust your filters on poor starting water

Heavily contaminated tap water can consume substantial filter durability before reaching a useful grade. Processing several large containers this way may exhaust your filters before the cleaner batches are ready.

This is especially costly when you're trying to prepare a large amount of water at once. Before buying another low-quality batch, check how much usable durability remains in your current setup.

Once a filter runs out of durability, another pass will not provide the expected improvement. Check which filter you are using before replacing it: an Advanced Water Filter can recover durability at the start of the next day, while an ordinary Water Filter may also stop being effective when the water is already above its useful purification range.

## How Do You Get Pure Water With a Water Purifier?

A Water Purifier processes water overnight, but its Quality modules determine how clean the finished batch can become.

Open the machine and check **Max Purity** before starting another processing cycle.

- **No Quality bonus:** 99% maximum purity, which is still High-Quality Water.
- **+50 Quality:** 99.65% maximum purity.
- **+75 Quality:** 99.8% maximum purity.
- **+100 Quality:** 99.9% maximum purity, enough to reach Pure Water.
- **+200 Quality:** 100% maximum purity.

The difference between +75 and +100 is decisive. A purifier capped at 99.8% cannot produce Pure Water by running for more nights, even when the machine has power and the container has been processed repeatedly.

### Prepare the Water Purifier

1. Install an **Energy Cell** or another compatible power supply.
2. Insert the water container into the machine's **CONTAINER** slot.
3. Install Quality modules until **Max Purity** reads at least **99.9%**.
4. End the day and allow the machine to process the container overnight.
5. Check **Contaminant Removed (Last Night)** and test the water's actual purity.

![An upgraded Water Purifier showing +109% Quality, energy consumption, and the amount of contamination removed during the previous night.](/images/probably-stolen/water-purifier-module-stats.webp)

The machine above uses **11 Energy/Day** with its installed modules. That is an example of an upgraded configuration, not the power cost you should assume for every Water Purifier.

### Why Does Purification Slow Down Near Pure Water?

The Water Purifier removes contamination fastest when the incoming water is heavily polluted. As the batch approaches higher purity, the amount removed per night becomes much smaller.

The machine also works through contaminants in a particular order. Large-particle contamination is removed before harder-to-clear substances such as minerals, chemical contaminants, and heavy metals.

That means the last improvement from High-Quality Water to Pure Water can take longer than the earlier improvement from a much dirtier grade.

**Performance modules** increase the amount of contamination the machine can remove per night. **Quality modules** increase the final purity limit. **Efficiency modules** affect energy use.

These upgrades solve different problems.

A machine that is still removing contamination may benefit from additional Performance. A machine that has already reached its Max Purity needs more Quality instead.

### How Do You Know When Processing Is Finished?

Check the machine tooltip after the overnight cycle.

- **Contaminant Removed (Last Night) is greater than zero:** The machine made progress.
- **Contaminant Removed (Last Night) is zero and the water has reached Max Purity:** The current configuration cannot improve it further.
- **The machine did not operate:** Check the power supply and whether a compatible water container was inserted.

Once the AquaScan shows **99.9% purity or higher**, the batch qualifies as Pure Water.

### Can You Make Pure Water Without a Moisture Farm?

Yes. A suitable purchased or collected batch can reach Pure Water in an upgraded Water Purifier.

The Moisture Farm provides repeatable water production, while the Water Purifier handles contamination removal. Buying both is useful for a larger business, but they are not both mandatory for processing a single container.

## Why Is My Water Still Not Pure?

The most useful troubleshooting method is to identify the first stage where the water stops improving. Repeatedly processing the finished batch without checking the earlier steps makes it difficult to tell whether the issue is the source water, an exhausted filter, or the final machine.

### The filter has no durability left

A water-processing session can appear to be working even though the filtering tools have already run out of durability.

One particularly revealing failure occurs when water remains Base Water after a filtering attempt. Checking the equipment shows exhausted tools; once working filtering equipment is used, the water improves to High Quality.

![Advanced Water Filter showing 0/400 durability and recovery of 50 durability at the start of each day.](/images/probably-stolen/water-filter-durability.webp)

Check the tools individually before spending money on new Quality modules. More machine Quality cannot compensate for a manual filtering stage that is no longer removing contamination.

A standard Water Filter can also stop improving water once its purification limit is reached, even when durability remains. Check the item's description before assuming that another pass will help.

An Advanced Water Filter behaves differently when depleted. It has a maximum of 400 durability and recovers 50 durability at the start of each day. A filter showing 0/400 is therefore temporarily unusable, but it does not necessarily need to be replaced.

### The Moisture Farm is upgraded, but the Water Purifier isn't

Check which machine actually received your Quality modules.

The **+100% Moisture Farm** target produces High Quality Water in this setup. The **+100% Water Purifier** target is used for the final step toward Pure Water.

Reaching the farm's target and then transferring its output into an unupgraded purifier is not the same configuration.

Inspect each machine separately, make sure it has the modules you intended, and check the grade after the purifier completes a run.

### The Water Purifier runs every night but purity stays below 99.9%

Check **Max Purity** on the Water Purifier before buying another Energy Cell or letting the batch run for several more nights.

A purifier capped at 99.65% or 99.8% cannot reach Pure Water with its current Quality modules. Additional processing time will not overcome that limit.

When the displayed Max Purity is already 99.9% or higher, check **Contaminant Removed (Last Night)** instead. A positive number means the machine is still cleaning the water, even when the change in the displayed percentage is small.

Upgrade Quality to raise the ceiling, or Performance to speed up processing. Choose the upgrade according to which limitation is stopping the batch.

### How Can Mixing Water Raise a Batch to High Quality?

Water at **98.86% purity** is still below the **99.00% High-Quality Water threshold**. Rather than running every nearly clean batch through another machine, you can blend it with cleaner water to raise the combined purity.

In one Water Merchant run, water measuring approximately **98.86%** was gradually mixed with water around **99.51%**. The resulting supply reached roughly **5,700 mL at 99.03% purity**, qualifying as High-Quality Water.

The amount of cleaner water required depends on both containers' volumes and their actual purity. Adding only a small amount of 99.51% water to a large 98.86% batch may leave the mixture below 99.00%.

Transfer the cleaner water gradually, scan the mixed batch, and stop once it reaches the grade you need. Pouring additional lower-purity water into a completed batch can drop it below the threshold again. Keep finished Pure Water separate unless you deliberately want to use it for blending.

### The purifier hasn't finished processing

A batch placed into the Water Purifier is not finished just because the machine is configured correctly.

Check that the machine can run, that its energy supply is sufficient, and that the processing cycle has completed. Running the purifier overnight or using a Turbo Booster are two ways to complete the purification cycle.

A machine that stops operating partway through is a different issue from one whose Quality bonuses are too low. Inspect its running state before changing modules again.

### The scanner says Pure Water, but the label still says High-Quality

A finished container can hold Pure Water while still carrying its previous label. The scanner below confirms **99.97% Pure Water**, but the container is labeled **High-Quality Water**.

![Water Scanner showing 99.97% tested Pure Water, an outdated High-Quality Water label, and the Match Label button.](/images/probably-stolen/pure-water-scanner-result.webp)

Click **Match Label** to update the container's description to its tested contents. You do not need to purify the water again just because the label is outdated.

Check this before selling expensive water, especially when you have several batches with different purity levels in storage.

## When Should You Keep Water Below Pure Quality?

Not every buyer or production recipe benefits from water at 99.9% purity. Some customers will not purchase Pure Water, while BloomBerry wine benefits from minerals retained in Basewater or High-Quality Water.

Keep water intended for fermentation separate from finished Pure Water. Processing every container to the maximum grade can consume additional time and energy without improving the product you actually intend to make.
