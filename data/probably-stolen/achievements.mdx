Four achievements can be tied to a single purchase: owning the store deed. A fifth becomes possible when you combine that purchase with a clean trading history, and two more involve winning from Rock Bottom. The challenge is deciding which conditions to pursue together without turning an otherwise profitable shop into an unnecessary failure.

**Probably Stolen has 21 Steam achievements.** Some are simple interactions, but several demand a particular state when a new day begins, a specific starting scenario, or a completed victory rather than merely reaching a financial milestone.

For the cleanest completion route, separate the achievements into three groups:

1. **Normal shop progression:** Tutorial, item labeling, business permit, appraisals, wealth, machines, and store improvements.
2. **Deliberate actions:** Jackpot, Aug kills, criminal reports, maximum faction reputation, and specialist production.
3. **Winning conditions:** Store deed, Day 50 deadline, Clean Hands, and Rock Bottom victories.

This prevents a common mistake: reaching a large amount of wealth and assuming the game has been won. Several achievements require an actual store buyout, while others only check the shop's status at the beginning of a day.

## How Do You Get Keys to the Kingdom and Prodigious?

**Keys to the Kingdom** unlocks when you own the store deed. **Prodigious** requires that ownership **before Day 50**.

### Buying the store outright

Check the **Calendar** for the property's current valuation. Save enough Credits for that purchase, then use the buyout option when the Landlord offers it.

In a completed shop run, the owner reached a point where the property was valued at approximately **13,000 Credits**. The Landlord later accepted the store buyout, and the ending confirmed that the shop deed had been signed.

![The Landlord confirms that the store now belongs to the player, with the Deed document visible on the counter.](/images/probably-stolen/store-buyout-deed-confirmation.webp)

The price from that particular run should not be treated as universal. Your difficulty and current game conditions determine how much you need to prepare.

For Prodigious, the important deadline is literal: **own the deed before Day 50**. Planning to have enough money by Day 50 itself leaves no margin for misunderstanding when the game checks the achievement.

Aim to finish the purchase by **Day 49 or earlier**, and make sure the relevant buyout interaction is actually available before committing the entire cash reserve to other equipment.

### Is a mortgage useful for Prodigious?

A **Loan Shark** can offer a mortgage rather than requiring the entire property price immediately.

In one long run, the lender requested **5,000 Credits** before returning on a Sunday to arrange further business. That was the offer for that particular shop, not a universal down payment.

A mortgage can make store ownership possible with less cash on hand, but the ongoing repayment obligation changes the calculation. Compare the offer with the Calendar valuation and the Credits you can realistically earn before the next payment.

Do not spend the entire balance on a machine merely because its eventual production looks profitable. For Prodigious, money earned after the deadline has no value toward the achievement.

### Can you combine the buyout achievements?

Yes. A successful deed purchase before Day 50 satisfies the stated conditions for both **Keys to the Kingdom** and **Prodigious**.

A run that also fulfills Clean Hands can target another achievement at the same time. Starting from Rock Bottom adds further possible combinations, although that makes the financial opening significantly harder.

## How Do You Unlock Clean Hands Without Ruining the Run?

**Clean Hands** requires you to win without selling a **Stolen** or **Contraband** item.

The restriction applies to the conduct of the winning run, not merely to what is left in your inventory when the Landlord arrives.

Buying the store after a long sequence of illegal merchandise sales does not undo those earlier transactions.

### What should you avoid selling?

Inspect incoming stock before putting it on the trading counter. Pay attention to whether the actual item is marked Stolen or Contraband.

A counterfeit or misleading label is not a reliable way to determine whether the underlying product is legal. A Labeler can change an item's displayed name or grade, but it should not be treated as proof that the item is safe to sell during a Clean Hands attempt.

Avoid using risky merchandise to close a cash shortfall simply because the transaction would be lucrative.

This is particularly important when a shop has accumulated several kinds of inventory and the next rent payment is approaching.

### A safer way to structure the run

Concentrate on legitimate merchandise and production that you can sell without violating the restriction.

For example, a water business can provide repeatable stock once its production and selling costs are manageable. The exact returns depend on customers, containers, equipment, and your current market conditions, so [compare your available cash and production costs](/probably-stolen/money-making) before investing.

The hard part is maintaining that discipline across the entire run. A single tempting Contraband sale is not worth the risk when it may invalidate an otherwise successful Clean Hands victory.

Finish by acquiring the store deed rather than assuming the requirement is satisfied as soon as the shop becomes profitable.

<section id="rock-bottom">

## How Do You Get From Nothing and From Nothing, the Hard Way?

Both achievements require a **Rock Bottom** starting scenario and a successful victory.

- **From Nothing:** Win after starting from Rock Bottom.
- **From Nothing, the Hard Way:** Win after starting from Rock Bottom on Hard Mode.

Rock Bottom begins without the convenient merchandise and equipment supplied by other specializations. It is labeled an **Extreme Challenge Mode** at character creation.

### Which difficulty counts as Hard Mode?

The 1.0 difficulty selection includes **Thin Margins** as Hard Mode and **Red Ink** as a more demanding Challenge Mode.

For a straightforward attempt at From Nothing, the Hard Way, select **Rock Bottom + Thin Margins**. That satisfies the hard-difficulty requirement without voluntarily taking on the still harsher Red Ink conditions.

Red Ink offers no necessary advantage for this particular unlock. It reduces the room for error while rebuilding a business that already starts with almost nothing.

### What should you do first in Rock Bottom?

The opening objective is survival, not rapid expansion.

Use the available opportunities to obtain merchandise you can actually resell, protect the first rent payment, and avoid committing scarce Credits to equipment that cannot produce an immediate return.

Once the shop has enough dependable income to cover rent, build toward the deed rather than pursuing expensive optional upgrades.

A failed run means restarting the challenge with the same difficult initial conditions. That makes early financial mistakes more costly than on an Apprentice start.

### Can you combine both Rock Bottom achievements with Clean Hands?

A Rock Bottom Hard Mode win meets the stated conditions for **From Nothing** and **From Nothing, the Hard Way**.

Keeping all sales legal also allows the same run to target **Clean Hands**. Buying the deed additionally fulfills **Keys to the Kingdom**, and completing the purchase before Day 50 would satisfy **Prodigious**.

The potential overlap is attractive, but the Day 50 deadline adds considerable financial pressure. It is often more sensible to secure a reliable Rock Bottom victory than sacrifice the entire attempt trying to force every winning condition into one run.

## Which Achievements Can You Get During the Opening Days?

Three achievements can be handled through normal shop progression without building a specialized factory.

### Lower Levels Special — Change an item label

Use the **Labeler** to change the label on an eligible item.

You do not need to trick a customer into paying a higher price to satisfy the stated achievement condition. The action is changing a label, not completing a fraudulent sale.

That distinction is particularly useful when also attempting Clean Hands: perform the labeling action without using it as an excuse to sell restricted merchandise.

### First Steps — Complete the tutorial

Follow The Mentor's instructions until the tutorial is marked complete.

The opening tasks introduce trading, inventory, shop equipment, and other systems over multiple days. Reaching the first rent payment does not necessarily mean the tutorial is finished.

In one completed 1.0 Apprentice opening, The Mentor announced that the tutorial was over around **Day 14**, after introducing additional shop operations and upgrades.

That timing describes one run, not a mandatory completion day. Continue following the outstanding tutorial tasks until the game confirms completion.

### Authorized — Own the business permit

The **Station Trade Board** introduces the permit requirement after the business has begun operating.

The permit must be purchased to trade legally. Buying the permit is the relevant achievement condition; merely being informed that one will be required does not satisfy it.

In one Normal difficulty setup, the business permit was listed at **1,500 Credits**. Treat that as a cost to budget for on that difficulty rather than a fixed price across every mode.

If the official arrives while your rent reserve is already strained, account for both obligations before buying optional machinery.

<section id="perfect-appraisals">

## How Do You Get 10 Perfect Appraisals for An Eye for Value?

**An Eye for Value** requires **10 Perfect appraisals**, not merely 10 customers requesting valuation services.

First, join the **Appraiser Board** when its representative offers membership. The board charges an entry fee, after which appraisal customers can bring specialized items for you to value.

The fee can differ by game conditions. One Apprentice run received an offer for **85 Credits**, while a Chemist run received a **170-Credit** offer.

Joining unlocks an additional business activity, but it does not make an appraisal automatically correct.

### How to appraise a Cybernetic Hand

Early appraisal work can involve Cybernetic Hands. Use the **Magnifying Glass** to inspect the item, then follow its appraisal instructions.

The value depends on several visible properties:

1. **Material:** Determine whether the hand uses ordinary material or a more valuable option such as gold plating.
2. **Condition:** Check the object's actual damage or wear.
3. **Model and variant:** Look for EMG features, armored variants, blades, and other modifications.
4. **Special combinations:** Apply additional values when the listed combination is present.

![A Cybernetic Hand appraisal showing how material, condition, model and modifications determine the item's valuation.](/images/probably-stolen/cybernetic-hand-appraisal.webp)

An ordinary hand in one valuation began at **100 Credits**. An Armored variant added **50 Credits**, bringing that specific example to **150 Credits**.

A separate Gold-Plated hand began at **300 Credits** before other properties were considered.

The Cybernetic Hand Appraisal Guide also enforces a **50-Credit minimum value**. For example, a hand with a 100-Credit base value, 85 Credits of damage, and a 60-Credit penalty for an unspecified control interface would calculate to -45 Credits. Its correct appraisal is still **50 Credits**, because the minimum value overrides the negative subtotal.

These examples show why every property matters. A valuable material can raise the price, while damage and an unspecified control interface can reduce it. Once the penalties push the calculated value below 50 Credits, use the minimum value instead.

### Why does an appraisal fail to count as Perfect?

The Appraiser Board grades your accuracy. A completed appraisal may be rated Excellent, Good, Mediocre, or Awful without qualifying as Perfect.

During a Chemist run, the player accumulated **10 Perfect appraisals**, after which the board displayed **Promotion Ready** and announced another visit.

The same appraisal quality affected how much commission the shopkeeper earned.

Use the handbook to calculate each item carefully rather than entering an approximate price for a quick service fee. The achievement depends on accuracy, and promotions reward the same skill.

## How Do You Get the Wealth, Machine and Attractiveness Achievements?

Four achievements are easy to misread because three specifically require the game to check your shop **at the start of a day**.

### Comfortably Afloat — More than 10,000 Credits in wealth

The requirement is **estimated total wealth above 10,000 Credits when a day begins**.

This does not simply say that your wallet must contain 10,000 Credits. Valuable stock and business property contribute to the shop's financial position.

Before ending the day, inspect your estimated total wealth and make sure it has crossed the required amount. Avoid selling a valuable piece of stock at a loss just to increase the cash displayed in your wallet.

### Swimming in Credits — More than 100,000 Credits in wealth

This is the larger version of the same requirement.

You must begin a day with **more than 100,000 Credits in estimated total wealth**.

A normal successful buyout does not necessarily put you near that figure. The achievement is a substantial wealth-building objective, separate from simply acquiring the deed.

A profitable production or trading business is more useful than repeatedly buying expensive equipment without a clear resale or production benefit.

### Industrious — Own 10 machines simultaneously

**Industrious** checks whether you own **10 machines at the start of a day**.

Prepare the full set before sleeping. Buying a tenth machine after the morning begins does not satisfy the start-of-day condition until the next check.

Upgrading a machine improves that machine; it should not be treated as owning a separate additional machine.

Plan enough inventory space and cash to keep ten machines simultaneously rather than repeatedly buying and selling equipment and losing track of the count.

### Shiny — Start a day with 1,000 attractiveness

**Shiny** requires **1,000 Store Attractiveness when the day begins**.

Store appearance can change with cleaning and upgrades. The Janitorial Service maintains cleanliness, while store renovations and other improvements increase attractiveness.

One available renovation adds **150 base attractiveness**, making it useful for improving a store that is otherwise approaching the target.

Check the current attractiveness before ending the day. Improving the store after the morning check will not retroactively unlock an achievement that requires a new-day starting value.

</section>

<section id="machine-quality">

## How Do You Get Cranked to Eleven?

**Cranked to Eleven** requires **+250 Quality on any one machine**.

The important distinction is that the achievement checks the machine's **Quality bonus**, not the quality of a product made by that machine.

Install compatible modules that increase Quality and inspect the machine's actual displayed attribute. Combining unrelated bonuses such as Performance or Efficiency does not turn them into Quality.

A machine can produce valuable merchandise without ever reaching +250 Quality.

Choose a machine with enough module capacity and a practical upgrade path before spending heavily on additional modules. Buying several Quality modules that cannot fit or do not produce the required combined value wastes the investment.

Unlike Industrious or Shiny, the achievement condition does not specify that you must wait for the beginning of a new day. Concentrate on reaching the displayed +250 requirement on a single machine.

</section>

<section id="reputation-achievements">

## How Do You Get Diplomatic, Aug Hunter and Snitches Get Riches?

These three achievements involve different kinds of encounters. They are easier to track separately than by treating all Security-related activities as one goal.

### Diplomatic — Reach maximum reputation

Reach **maximum standing with any one faction**.

You do not need to maximize every faction to satisfy the stated requirement.

Choose a faction whose actions fit the way you already operate the shop. A legitimate trading business can gradually build relations through favorable transactions, while a Chemist may develop Sector 13 Cartel reputation by completing deliveries.

The first positive reputation perk does not equal maximum reputation. Continue until the faction actually reaches its maximum state.

For the full threshold list and ways to increase or recover standing, [check the six faction reputation tracks](/probably-stolen/factions).

### Aug Hunter — Shoot 10 Augmented

**Aug Hunter** requires **10 Aug kills**.

The target must actually be an Aug. Threatening a suspicious customer, watching one leave, or shooting an innocent person does not replace a confirmed elimination.

The **Aug Menace** Starting Perk unlocks augmented encounters from the beginning and gives a **25% reward bonus** for kills. It also permanently extends the threat, so it introduces a real chance of losing a run through missed Augs.

Keep track of confirmed kills and use an Aug Scanner when identification is uncertain. The [Aug identification and shotgun instructions](/probably-stolen/augs) explain why the weapon can be unavailable even after it has already been obtained.

### Snitches Get Riches — Report 10 criminals

**Snitches Get Riches** requires **10 criminal reports**.

Use the Wanted List to identify the person, then make the proper Security report while the customer is present.

Do not confuse a Wanted criminal with an Aug. They are separate systems with different responses.

During one successful report, Security paid **75 Credits** and improved the shop's Security reputation, but **Black Market reputation decreased** at the same time.

Reporting can therefore help this achievement while damaging another relationship.

The **Model Citizen** Starting Perk increases the reward for reporting Wanted customers by **50%**, but the achievement condition remains the number of correct reports rather than the total Credits earned.

## How Do You Get Say My Name and Green Thumb?

Both achievements require producing a particular item at a specific quality or growth result. Owning the required machine is only the beginning.

### Say My Name — Produce Lab Pure narcotics

The achievement requires a **Lab Pure grade narcotic item**.

Chemist is the most direct starting scenario because it provides chemistry equipment and the relevant production systems.

The crucial word is **produce**. Changing an item's label to Lab Pure does not establish that its real composition or purity has reached the grade required by the achievement.

During one Chemist run, the player relabeled chemical products as Lab Pure while their actual quality remained lower. That is not a substitute for finishing the chemical production process to the necessary grade.

A proper attempt needs enough suitable ingredients, a completed processing sequence, and a final chemical purity reading that actually qualifies.

For Dream Dust, mistakes can occur before the Chem Finisher even receives the batch:

- Heating the Dream Cap mixture beyond its stated **40–60°C** working range can damage the extract.
- Skipping filtering can leave unwanted material in the batch.
- A mixture still containing excess liquid may trigger **Wet Ingredient Detected**.
- Missing required reagent quantities can prevent the Chem Finisher from completing its output.

Do not spend more ingredients just because the label looks correct. Check the product's actual grade and fix the stage that is reducing purity.

### Green Thumb — Produce a Huge Nutrifruit

**Green Thumb** requires a **Huge Nutrifruit produced by the Hydroponic**.

Hydroponic Farmer is the natural starting scenario for this objective because it begins with farming equipment and seeds.

A Hydroponic needs a suitable seed, water, nutrients, and power to function. Growing an ordinary Nutrifruit does not meet the achievement condition.

For a better chance of obtaining larger, healthier crops, maintain the conditions described in the Hydroponic Farming Manual:

- Use water suitable for the crop rather than ignoring contaminants.
- Supply the required minerals without assuming that unlimited nutrients are always better.
- Keep the machine powered through the growth period.
- Inspect the plant's condition and needs with the Plant Analyser when available.

The Hydroponic can finish an ordinary harvest while still failing to produce the required **Huge** result. Check the actual output instead of assuming that several normal fruits count toward one Huge Nutrifruit.

The achievement condition does not supply a guaranteed recipe or exact module combination for Huge growth. Do not treat a normal Nutrifruit harvest as evidence that the requirement has been satisfied.

## How Do You Unlock We Have Rats!?

**We Have Rats!** requires starting a new game as **Rat Wrangler**. Although the achievement description calls it a hidden scenario, Rat Wrangler is available in the 1.0 starting scenario menu.

Select Rat Wrangler when creating the run. You do not need to discover a secret unlock or breed a rat to meet the stated starting condition.

Once the run begins, [manage the starting cage, feeding, and breeding](/probably-stolen/rat-breeding) to keep the colony productive.

## How Do You Win the Jackpot Achievement?

**Jackpot** requires winning the actual jackpot on a **scratcher ticket**.

A small winning ticket is not necessarily the jackpot.

Scratcher tickets can be obtained through shop transactions and other opportunities. Inspect the ticket's instructions and prize combinations before scratching.

![A scratcher ticket displays the matching-symbol requirements and different payouts for small wins and larger prizes.](/images/probably-stolen/scratcher-ticket-prize-rules.webp)

In one ticket sequence, the player found these listed rewards:

- Two ordinary matching symbols: **10 Credits**.
- Three ordinary matching symbols: **75 Credits**.
- Two Nutrifruit symbols: **30 Credits**.
- Three Nutrifruit symbols: **350 Credits**.

These are examples from the ticket being inspected, not a universal jackpot prize table for every ticket type.

The player also redeemed a ticket for **10 Credits** after paying **20 Credits** for it.

That was a winning ticket financially worth less than its purchase price, not evidence of having won the achievement's jackpot.

Scratch the ticket according to its displayed requirements, inspect the actual result, and redeem the prize when appropriate. Do not confuse the normal matching-symbol prizes with the ticket's jackpot.

### Is buying large numbers of tickets a good strategy?

Not when rent is approaching.

Tickets consume Credits immediately, while the jackpot depends on a successful outcome that cannot be guaranteed through ordinary trading decisions.

Use surplus funds for gambling rather than the money needed for the next lease payment. The achievement is worth pursuing as a separate luck-dependent objective after the shop has a stable income.

<section id="completion-check">

## What Should You Finish Before Going for 100%?

A normal trading run can naturally complete First Steps, Authorized, Lower Levels Special, several wealth milestones, and some reputation or equipment objectives.

The achievements that deserve deliberate planning are those with irreversible conditions or expensive setup requirements.

**Before a Buyout:** Decide whether you are pursuing Clean Hands and whether the deed must be obtained before Day 50. Those conditions cannot be repaired simply by earning more money after the relevant deadline or after making a prohibited sale.

**Before sleeping:** Check the exact thresholds for Comfortably Afloat, Swimming in Credits, Industrious, and Shiny. These four specifically depend on the state of the shop when a day begins.

**Before investing in production:** Decide whether the next batch actually advances Lab Pure, Huge Nutrifruit, or +250 machine Quality. Producing an ordinary item or installing an unrelated module does not satisfy those requirements.

**Before starting another difficult run:** Check the completed achievements in your checklist. A fresh Rock Bottom attempt is valuable when it addresses an unfinished winning condition, not when you already earned that result on an earlier run.

The final few achievements are usually better handled with a clear purpose than by changing an otherwise successful business every day to chase unrelated objectives.

</section>
