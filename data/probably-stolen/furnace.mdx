<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Buy a **Furnace** from **Dr. Jackson**, insert a charged Energy Cell, and load two Metal Ore or Scrap Metal items. It smelts overnight into one Metal Ingot.

For **Perfect Purity Ingots**, the simplest reliable setup is **2 Metal Ore + at least +200% Quality**.

If you want **2 Perfect Ingots per night**, install a **Furnace Module (Blast)**, use **3 Metal Ore**, and raise Quality to at least **+300%**. Blast increases production but lowers the starting purity by one grade.

**Flux Smelting Agent** can help Metal Ore reach Very High Purity, including with Blast, but Flux alone does not produce Perfect Ingots.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A Furnace can turn two Metal Ore into one High Purity Ingot, yet adding a Blast Module to the same production line can leave you with two lower-grade Ingots instead.

That is not a malfunction. Blast changes both the quantity and purity of the result.

The important decisions are how much metal to use, which purity grade you need, and whether your modules can reach the next Quality threshold without exhausting your Energy supply.

<section id="furnace-start">

## How Do You Get and Use a Furnace in Probably Stolen?

**Dr. Jackson** sells Furnaces and other production equipment.

He can offer a Furnace early in a run, often around his first visit near Day 6, although you should check his actual inventory rather than depend on a fixed arrival day.

A basic Furnace has a displayed purchase price of **210 Credits** and requires **8 Energy per working day** before module effects.

![A basic Furnace showing its 210-Credit purchase price, 8 Energy per day, and empty module, input, and output slots.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-base-energy.webp)

### How do you smelt your first Metal Ingot?

1. **Buy and place the Furnace.** Make sure there is room to access its inventory.
2. **Insert a charged Energy Cell.** Check that the battery can cover the Furnace's displayed Energy requirement.
3. **Add two metal ingredients.** Use Scrap Metal, Metal Ore, or one of each.
4. **Leave the Output area available.** Finished Ingots need somewhere to go.
5. **Complete the overnight cycle.** A basic Furnace makes one Metal Ingot from two metal inputs.
6. **Collect the finished Ingot.** Inspect its tooltip to see the actual purity and value.

You do not need a Performance Module to begin smelting.

The basic machine already performs one normal smelting batch overnight, provided its inputs and Energy requirements are met.

![The in-game Furnace instructions showing that two pieces of Scrap Metal or Metal Ore produce one Metal Ingot, with Efficiency affecting power consumption and Quality affecting purity.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-operation-guide.webp)

### What should you smelt first?

If you only have Scrap Metal from scavenging, use it to learn the machine and obtain basic Ingots.

If you have Metal Ore, save it for batches where the additional purity matters.

Two Metal Ore begin at High Purity, making them the easiest ordinary ingredients to upgrade into Perfect Ingots later.

Do not spend all your Credits on Quality Modules before confirming that you can consistently obtain enough metal and keep the Furnace powered.

## Scrap Metal vs Metal Ore: Which Makes Better Ingots?

The purity of a newly smelted Metal Ingot depends on which metal ingredients you use.

Without Quality upgrades, Flux, or Blast, the three basic combinations are:

<GuideTable columns={furnaceInputColumns} rows={furnaceInputRows} />

### What happens when you smelt two Scrap Metal?

Two Scrap Metal produce **one Low Purity Metal Ingot**.

This is a practical early-game recipe when scavenging gives you more Scrap Metal than Metal Ore.

A Low Purity Ingot can still be useful as a material, but its displayed value is lower than that of higher-purity versions.

![A Low Purity Metal Ingot showing an estimated value of 42 Credits and a base value of 50.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-low-purity-ingot.webp)

Do not assume every crafting task needs Perfect Purity.

If a downstream machine only requires a Metal Ingot as material, check its actual input requirements before spending additional Energy and expensive modules on purity.

### What happens when you mix Scrap Metal and Metal Ore?

One Scrap Metal plus one Metal Ore starts at **Fair Purity**.

This is a useful middle option when your inventory contains both materials but not enough Ore for a pure Ore batch.

Fair is also the reference grade for the Ingot's base value.

However, mixed batches need more Quality upgrades to reach Perfect than batches made from two Ore.

### Why is Metal Ore better for Perfect Purity?

Two Metal Ore begin at **High Purity**.

That puts the resulting Ingot two purity steps below Perfect.

With +100% Quality, the result can become Very High.

With +200% Quality, it can become Perfect.

This is why Metal Ore is the preferred ingredient when you want high-value Ingots without filling the Furnace with an unusually large number of Quality Modules.

<section id="ingot-purity">

## What Are All Six Metal Ingot Purity Grades?

Metal Ingots have six purity grades, from Very Low to Perfect.

<GuideTable columns={furnacePurityColumns} rows={furnacePurityRows} />

Purity affects the finished Ingot's value.

A Fair Purity Metal Ingot uses the ordinary **50-Credit base value**.

A Low Purity example shows approximately **42 Credits**, while a Perfect Purity Ingot has a displayed value of **150 Credits**.

These are item values, not guaranteed customer payments. A buyer's offer can differ from the amount displayed on the Ingot.

### How do you move to a higher purity grade?

There are three main influences:

- **Metal inputs** determine the starting purity.
- **Quality Modules** increase purity when their combined effect reaches another complete +100%.
- **Blast Module** lowers purity by one grade in exchange for greater output.

Flux is another way to improve the result, but its behavior differs from the regular Quality progression.

Most importantly, Flux cannot replace the Quality requirements needed to produce Perfect Ingots.

</section>

<section id="furnace-quality">

## How Do Quality Modules Affect Furnace Purity?

The important Quality thresholds are **+100%, +200%, +300%, and +400%**.

Each complete +100% raises the resulting purity by one grade.

A smaller increase that does not cross the next threshold will not necessarily change the finished Ingot's purity.

For example, starting with two Metal Ore:

| Total Quality | Expected Purity |
|---|---|
| Below +100% | High |
| +100% to below +200% | Very High |
| +200% or higher | Perfect |

Adding more Quality after reaching Perfect cannot create a seventh purity grade.

### Does +220% Quality work for Perfect Ingots?

Yes.

A Furnace configured with approximately **+220% Quality** successfully produced Perfect Ingots from two Metal Ore.

The setup used Quality Modules and supporting module connections to reach the required threshold.

![A Furnace with Quality modules showing +220% Quality, +254% Performance, and a displayed Energy requirement of 22 per day.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-quality-modules.webp)

The important value is **Quality +220%**, not the displayed Performance increase.

The in-game Furnace instructions describe:

- **Quality:** Maximum Purity.
- **Efficiency:** Power Consumption.
- **Performance:** No Effect.

Therefore, do not buy Performance Modules solely to increase Furnace output.

A Furnace's normal production quantity is determined by its smelting rules, while Blast changes the number of Ingots produced per batch.

### Should you install Efficiency Modules?

Yes, when Energy is becoming a limitation.

The basic Furnace shows **8 Energy/Day**, but upgraded configurations can require substantially more.

For example, the +220% Quality setup above required **22 Energy/Day**. Another heavily modified Furnace with +314% Quality displayed **39 Energy/Day**.

These are individual machine configurations, not universal costs for those Quality thresholds.

Check the actual Energy requirement after every module change.

If your Furnace cannot complete its nightly cycle, reducing Energy consumption may be more useful than adding another Quality Module that does not cross a new purity threshold.

</section>

<section id="perfect-ingots">

## How Do You Make Perfect Purity Metal Ingots?

**The most straightforward Perfect Ingot recipe is two Metal Ore with at least +200% Quality.**

This avoids the purity penalty of Blast and requires fewer Quality upgrades than a Scrap Metal recipe.

### Method 1: One Perfect Ingot per night

1. Open the Furnace.
2. Remove the Blast Module if one is installed.
3. Arrange Quality Modules until the machine shows **+200% Quality or higher**.
4. Insert two Metal Ore.
5. Make sure the Energy Cell can cover the displayed requirement.
6. Complete the overnight cycle.
7. Inspect the output.

A +220% Quality configuration produced a **Perfect Purity Metal Ingot** with a displayed value of **150 Credits**.

![A Furnace output containing a Perfect Purity Metal Ingot, with an estimated value of 150 Credits.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-perfect-ingot.webp)

This is a useful target for a shop that has reliable Metal Ore supply but does not yet have enough module space for an upgraded Blast configuration.

### Can mixed Ore and Scrap produce Perfect?

The starting grades imply higher Quality requirements for less-pure metal combinations.

<GuideTable columns={furnaceQualityColumns} rows={furnaceQualityRows} />

Two Metal Ore are the simplest demonstrated method.

The +300% requirement for one Scrap plus one Ore, and +400% for two Scrap Metal, follow the same one-grade-per-100% rule.

If you attempt either of those more demanding combinations, check the finished Ingot before committing a large batch of resources.

### Why did +220% Quality stop producing Perfect after adding Blast?

Because Blast lowers purity by one grade.

Without Blast:

**2 Metal Ore → High → +200% Quality → Perfect**

With Blast:

**3 Metal Ore → Fair → +200% Quality → Very High**

The same Quality value does not produce the same final purity when Blast is installed.

To recover Perfect while keeping Blast, you need another full purity step.

</section>

<section id="blast-module">

## How Does the Blast Furnace Module Work?

The **Furnace Module (Blast)** changes the Furnace from ordinary one-Ingot production into a higher-output machine.

Its tooltip lists:

- Increased Input Used.
- Increased Output per Night.
- Decreased Purity.
- Efficiency -100%.
- Does not stack.

The normal Furnace uses **two metal ingredients to make one Ingot**.

Blast uses **three metal ingredients to make two Ingots**.

![The Furnace Module (Blast) tooltip showing increased metal input and output, decreased purity, and an Efficiency penalty.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-blast-module.webp)

### Why does Blast lower the purity?

Blast sacrifices one purity grade for additional output.

Three Metal Ore that would normally begin at High Purity instead produce Fair Purity Ingots without compensating Quality or Flux.

Three Scrap Metal likewise begin one grade below their ordinary Low Purity result.

This tradeoff can still be worthwhile.

### Example: Six Metal Ore with or without Blast

Suppose you have **six Metal Ore** and a basic Furnace without additional Quality.

**Without Blast:**

- Use two Ore each night.
- Complete three overnight cycles.
- Receive three High Purity Ingots.
- At approximately 62 Credits each, their total displayed value is about 186 Credits.

**With Blast:**

- Use three Ore each night.
- Complete two overnight cycles.
- Receive four Fair Purity Ingots.
- At 50 Credits each, their total displayed value is 200 Credits.

Blast produces one additional Ingot while saving one overnight production cycle.

However, these figures compare finished item values only. They do not include the cost of Blast, Energy, purchased Ore, or any downstream crafting requirements.

### How do you make two Perfect Ingots with Blast?

Use this setup:

1. Install the **Furnace Module (Blast)**.
2. Expand module space if the existing slots cannot hold enough Quality upgrades.
3. Raise the displayed Quality to **at least +300%**.
4. Add **three Metal Ore**.
5. Supply enough Energy for the upgraded Furnace.
6. Complete the overnight cycle.
7. Inspect both finished Ingots.

A Furnace expanded to approximately +300% Quality successfully produced **two Perfect Purity Ingots** from three Metal Ore.

The calculation is:

**High starting purity − 1 Blast grade + 3 Quality grades = Perfect**

The Module Expansion Kit is useful because a compact Furnace may not have enough space for Blast and all the Quality components required to reach +300%.

### Is Blast always better?

No.

Blast is a strong choice when you want more Ingots per night and have the Ore, Energy, and modules needed to support it.

A normal Furnace is easier to configure for a single Perfect Ingot.

If your supply of Metal Ore is limited, your batteries are almost empty, or you do not need two finished Ingots at once, ordinary smelting may be the more practical option.

The goal is not to install Blast as early as possible. It is to produce the quantity and purity your next sale or crafting job actually needs.

</section>

<section id="flux-smelting">

## How Does Flux Smelting Agent Work?

Flux Smelting Agent improves the effective purity of a Furnace batch when the installed Quality bonus is too low to achieve the same result.

For Metal Ore, Flux can produce **Very High Purity** even when Blast is installed. However, Flux does not simply add another +100% Quality on top of every module bonus.

If your Furnace already has enough Quality to produce Perfect Ingots, Flux does not prevent that higher result. The important distinction is that Flux alone cannot replace the +200% Quality needed for ordinary Perfect Ore Ingots or the +300% needed with Blast.

### Can Flux make Very High Ingots without Quality Modules?

Yes.

For a normal Ore batch:

- **2 Metal Ore without Flux:** High Purity.
- **2 Metal Ore with Flux:** Very High Purity.

With Blast:

- **3 Metal Ore + Blast:** Fair Purity.
- **3 Metal Ore + Blast + Flux:** Very High Purity.

Flux also works with Scrap Metal, but the resulting grade is lower:

- **2 Scrap Metal + Flux:** Fair Purity.
- **3 Scrap Metal + Blast + Flux:** Fair Purity.

The starting metal still matters. Flux does not turn Scrap Metal into Very High Purity simply because it can produce that result with Metal Ore.

### Can Flux produce Perfect Ingots?

**Flux alone cannot make Perfect Purity Ingots.**

Its demonstrated Ore result stops at Very High.

Adding Flux to a Blast batch does not automatically remove the need for sufficient Quality.

If you specifically need Perfect, use the Quality thresholds described above rather than relying on Flux as a substitute.

### Is Flux Smelting Agent reusable?

Check which Flux item you have.

The **Flux Smelting Agent (Advanced)** tooltip explicitly says that it is **Reusable**.

![The Advanced Flux Smelting Agent tooltip showing its 300-Credit purchase price, Metal Ore purity improvement, and reusable property.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-flux-agent.webp)

The **Basic Flux Smelting Agent** is consumed when it actually improves the resulting purity. If the installed Quality bonus already provides an equal or better result, Basic Flux does not need to be consumed.

The **Advanced Flux Smelting Agent** is reusable, making it more useful for repeated smelting batches.

Advanced Flux also has a displayed purchase price of **300 Credits**.

That upfront cost makes it more attractive for repeated production than for a single low-value batch.

Before purchasing it, consider whether you need a reusable way to make Very High Ore Ingots or whether your existing Quality setup can already reach the target grade.

</section>

<section id="junk-processing">

## How Does the Junk Processing Module Work?

The **Junk Processing Module** gives the Furnace an additional way to use scavenged materials.

When the Furnace is not performing a normal Ingot-smelting cycle, the module can convert:

**6 Junk → 4 Scrap Metal**

This is useful when you have excess Junk but not enough Metal Ore or Scrap Metal for future production.

### When does Junk Processing run?

Junk Processing is associated with idle smelting cycles.

A Furnace that is actively processing a regular metal batch is not simultaneously performing the same idle conversion.

To use the module:

1. Install the Junk Processing Module.
2. Keep enough Junk available for its conversion requirement.
3. Make sure the Furnace has the Energy required to operate.
4. Allow a night without a normal metal-smelting job.
5. Check the resulting Scrap Metal.

You can then use the recovered Scrap Metal in regular Furnace batches or other crafting systems.

### Should you convert every Junk item into Scrap Metal?

Not necessarily.

Some equipment and Starting Perks also use Junk.

For example, a Makeshift Micro Nuclear Reactor can be repaired using Junk or Scrap Metal.

If you depend on that Reactor, reserve enough maintenance materials before converting or selling everything.

The advantage of Junk Processing is that it gives otherwise unused materials another purpose. It does not mean every piece of Junk should automatically become Scrap Metal.

</section>

<section id="furnace-power">

## How Do You Keep a Furnace Powered?

A basic Furnace requires **8 Energy per working day**.

Quality, Efficiency, Blast, and other module effects can change that requirement.

Always use the value displayed on your actual Furnace rather than assuming the original Energy cost still applies.

### Use charged Energy Cells for the first Furnace

A charged Energy Cell is enough for a basic setup when it contains sufficient Energy for the next overnight job.

Before closing:

1. Check the Furnace's **Required Energy/Day**.
2. Check the Energy Cell's remaining charge.
3. Confirm that no other planned equipment operation depends on the same limited Energy supply.
4. Replace or recharge batteries if the Furnace cannot meet its requirement.

If a Furnace repeatedly runs out of power, adding another Quality Module may make the problem worse.

### When should you add Efficiency Modules?

Efficiency is worth considering when the energy cost of the desired purity configuration becomes difficult to support.

For example, reaching +200% Quality may be useful, but the machine still needs enough Energy to complete the cycle.

An Efficiency Module can help reduce power consumption without requiring you to abandon the desired purity target.

Check the total Quality again after rearranging modules. Removing a Quality component to make room for Efficiency could drop the configuration below the next 100% threshold.

### Can a Makeshift Reactor power Furnaces?

Yes. If you have the **Makeshift Micro Nuclear Reactor** Starting Perk, its adjacent charging effect can support nearby Furnaces and rechargeable Energy Cells.

A fully upgraded Reactor in one run provided **4 Energy per night** to adjacent batteries and machines. That can reduce battery replacement work, but it may not cover the full Energy requirement of a heavily upgraded Furnace.

![A fully upgraded Makeshift Micro Nuclear Reactor showing 18 of 18 upgrades and an adjacent charging effect of 4 per night.](/images/probably-stolen/furnace-reactor-power.webp)

Check the Reactor's Integrity before relying on it. Junk, Scrap Metal, and Electronic Parts may be needed for maintenance, so do not consume all of those supplies in your Furnace.

A normal charged Energy Cell is sufficient for ordinary Furnace production. The Reactor is an optional power solution, not a requirement for Perfect Ingots.

</section>

<section id="furnace-troubleshooting">

## Why Is Your Furnace Not Working or Producing the Wrong Purity?

Start with the current machine configuration instead of replacing the Furnace immediately.

<GuideTable columns={furnaceProblemColumns} rows={furnaceProblemRows} />

### The Furnace has ingredients but produces nothing

Check these in order:

1. **Energy:** Does the installed battery cover the full displayed daily requirement?
2. **Input:** Are there two valid metal ingredients for ordinary smelting, or three for Blast?
3. **Output:** Is there room for the finished Ingots?
4. **Timing:** Has the required overnight processing cycle actually completed?

The Furnace normally performs its work overnight.

Putting Metal Ore into the input slots during the day does not immediately create an Ingot.

### The Furnace makes Very High instead of Perfect

First check whether Blast is installed.

For a normal two-Ore recipe, +200% Quality can reach Perfect.

With Blast, the same amount produces Very High because of the one-grade purity penalty.

Raise Quality to +300% if you want to keep Blast and use three Ore to produce two Perfect Ingots.

If you do not have enough module space or Energy, remove Blast and return to the simpler two-Ore recipe.

### More modules are installed but purity has not changed

Look at the displayed **Quality percentage**.

For example, raising Quality from +120% to +180% does not cross the next full +100% threshold.

The result may remain at the same purity grade.

Rearrange the modules or add an appropriate Quality connection until you pass the next threshold.

Do not confuse a large Performance bonus with a large Quality bonus. Performance does not directly improve the Furnace's smelting result.

### The Junk Processing Module is not producing Scrap Metal

Check that the module is installed and that enough Junk is available.

Also check whether the Furnace has been occupied by an ordinary metal-smelting job.

Junk Processing operates during suitable non-smelting cycles, so a Furnace continuously loaded with regular metal inputs may not perform the idle conversion you expected.

</section>

<section id="furnace-best-setup">

## What Is the Best Furnace Setup for Your Stage of the Game?

There is no single configuration that is best for every shop.

Choose the setup according to the metal you can obtain, the purity your next job needs, and how much Energy is available.

### Early game: basic Furnace with available Scrap

**Recommended goal: produce usable Ingots without heavy module spending.**

Start with two Scrap Metal when that is your most reliable material.

If Metal Ore becomes available, use two Ore for higher-purity batches.

Before buying expensive upgrades, confirm that you can regularly complete overnight cycles and use or sell the output.

### Mid game: two Ore with +200% Quality

**Recommended goal: produce one Perfect Ingot reliably.**

This is a strong target when you have enough Quality Modules but limited room, Energy, or Ore supply.

The +200% threshold is easier to achieve than the upgraded Blast requirement.

It also avoids Blast's increased ingredient demand.

If your Furnace already reaches Perfect with a comfortable Energy supply, prioritize reliable production over further Quality increases that do not improve the result.

### Advanced production: Blast with +300% Quality

**Recommended goal: produce two Perfect Ingots in one night.**

This requires:

- The Blast Module.
- Three Metal Ore per batch.
- At least +300% Quality.
- Enough module space.
- Sufficient Energy for the full machine configuration.

This setup becomes more attractive when you have a steady Ore supply and repeated demand for high-purity Ingots.

However, if your shop cannot acquire three Ore for every planned cycle, the extra production capacity may remain unused.

### When is Flux + Blast a better choice?

Use Flux + Blast when **Very High Purity is sufficient** and you want more Ingots per night without assembling the complete +300% Perfect configuration.

This can be a practical middle ground between the basic Furnace and an expensive Perfect-production setup.

Advanced reusable Flux is particularly attractive for repeated Ore batches, provided you can justify its purchase cost.

### Should you sell Ingots or use them for crafting?

A Perfect Purity Metal Ingot has a higher displayed value than a Low or Fair Ingot, but selling it is not always the best use of your metal supply.

Before selling, check whether you need Ingots for another machine. Gunsmith production can use them to print firearm frames and Gun Parts.

Higher-purity Ingots can improve certain printed outputs, but that does not mean every crafting recipe requires Perfect Purity.

For the specific recipes, printing modules, and Gun Parts output by Ingot grade, see the [Gunsmith Guide](/probably-stolen/gunsmith).

To compare the value of selling an Ingot against using it in production, include its materials and Energy costs in the [Money-Making Planner](/probably-stolen/money-making).

</section>
