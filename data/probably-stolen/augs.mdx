Check a suspicious customer's **face, clothing, and dialogue** for signs of augmentation. Use an **Aug Scanner** when the evidence is unclear. Once the shotgun is available and the customer is confirmed as an Aug, equip it and shoot.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A customer can approach your counter looking like an ordinary Spacer, Thief, or Upper Level Citizen, yet reveal something very different during the conversation. One customer asks for medical supplies because a host is awaiting augmentation. Another struggles to describe himself as a normal human. Both are much stronger warnings than a customer simply wearing unusual clothing.

The dangerous part is deciding whether to act. Killing a real person can trigger a criminal investigation, while letting an Aug walk away contributes to an increasingly serious threat. The shotgun also cannot be used against everyone who visits your store, even after you've successfully killed an Aug.

Understanding those separate conditions is essential: **recognizing an Aug, being allowed to equip the shotgun, and receiving a confirmed kill are three different steps.**

<section id="when-augs-appear">

## When Do Augs Start Appearing in Probably Stolen?

In a normal opening without the Aug Menace perk, **The Mentor introduces the Aug threat after the shop has been operating for several days**. He explains that a rogue AI has taken control of people through brain implants and gives you the equipment and instructions needed to deal with them.

His introduction provides two important documents:

- **Mentor's Note: The Augmented**, explaining why Augs visit shops and why calling Security is ineffective.
- **Augmented Identification Guide**, showing the visible and behavioral differences between an ordinary human and an Aug.

![The Mentor's note explaining how Augs obtain materials and medical supplies, and why reporting them to Security is ineffective.](/images/probably-stolen/aug-mentor-note.webp)

The shotgun is positioned near the counter after this introduction. Receiving it does not mean you can immediately equip it against any customer.

Augs seek **Materials** to manufacture more implants and **Medical supplies** to implant additional victims. These requests deserve closer inspection, but the merchandise category alone is insufficient to identify an Aug.

### Can Augs appear from Day 1?

Yes, if you choose **Aug Menace** during character creation.

The perk unlocks Augmented encounters immediately, adds a 25% bonus to Aug-kill rewards, and makes the threat persist throughout the run.

![Aug Menace starting perk showing immediate Aug encounters, a 25% kill reward bonus, and the permanent threat condition.](/images/probably-stolen/aug-menace-starting-perk.webp)

This is a substantial change to the opening. Instead of learning ordinary trading and inspections before dealing with Augs, you may have to identify and eliminate them while your shop still has limited equipment and cash.

The additional kill reward is useful, but the risk is permanent. Choosing Aug Menace means you cannot simply ignore suspicious customers until your business is financially secure.

<section id="identify-augs">

## How Do You Tell if a Customer Is an Aug?

Start with the customer's appearance, then compare their clothing, speech, and requested merchandise.

The Augmented Identification Guide identifies three visual features and five behavioral or contextual warnings.

### Visual signs

- **Visible Aug chip:** Look for exposed implant hardware around the head. Check the forehead as well as the temples.
- **Unusual pupils:** Inspect the eyes when the face is visible.
- **Uncanny expression:** An unusually fixed or exaggerated expression may indicate an Aug.

### Non-visual signs

- **Unnatural stillness:** The customer may remain unusually motionless.
- **Twitching:** Repeated or abnormal movements can be another warning.
- **Outfit mismatch:** Their clothing does not fit the faction shown in the trading interface.
- **Robotic vocabulary:** They refer to people or actions in mechanical terms.
- **Overly formal phrasing:** Their speech sounds like instructions or system output rather than a normal conversation.

The faction label is especially easy to misuse. It identifies the customer's apparent social category; it does **not** reliably certify that the person is human.

Likewise, a customer asking for Medicine is not automatically an Aug. Legitimate customers regularly buy medical goods.

Use multiple observations before taking action. A covered face, unusual outfit, or awkward sentence may be suspicious, but the cost of guessing incorrectly is much greater than the value of an ordinary transaction.

### A confirmed Aug encounter: the customer requesting a host

One Aug encountered during a 1.0 shop run spoke in an unmistakably mechanical manner:

> "A host is awaiting augmentation."

The same customer described himself as a normal human, requested supplies, and stated that he had **238 Credits**. His dialogue also included awkward descriptions of fear and being human.

![A suspicious Lower Level Citizen requests supplies for a host awaiting augmentation, revealing an important Aug identification clue.](/images/probably-stolen/aug-suspicious-host-dialogue.webp)

Three details mattered together:

1. **Purpose:** The customer explicitly connected the requested supplies to augmentation.
2. **Speech:** His sentences sounded like system instructions rather than ordinary conversation.
3. **Behavior:** He continued describing human emotions in an unnatural way.

The customer was subsequently shot, and Security confirmed that he really was an Aug.

The result makes this an excellent example of a strong identification. It does not establish that every Aug will announce its purpose so clearly.

### What if the face is covered?

A mask can prevent you from checking the mouth or expression. A hat, goggles, or other equipment may also make the pupils or implant difficult to inspect.

Move to the clues that remain available: look around the exposed parts of the head, read the faction label, compare it with the clothing, and pay attention to the entire conversation.

An Aug may still expose itself through speech or movement even without an obvious visible chip. When the clues remain ambiguous, the safer option is to use an Aug Scanner rather than turning a suspicion into a shooting decision.

<section id="aug-scanner">

## How Do You Use the Aug Scanner?

The **Aug Scanner** is an electromagnetic detection device designed to confirm whether a suspected customer carries an Augmentation chip. Its item description specifies **100% accuracy**.

It can be obtained through **Dr. Jackson's equipment supply**. Prepare a charged battery before relying on it during an encounter.

To check a customer:

1. Keep the suspected customer at the counter.
2. Prepare the Aug Scanner and ensure it has enough charge.
3. Activate the scanner while the customer is still present.
4. Read the completed detection result before deciding whether to use the shotgun.

A scanner that is merely ready for use has not confirmed anything. Wait for an actual result; a readiness indicator is not a negative test.

### Is the Aug Scanner worth buying?

The scanner is most valuable when identifying a target from appearance alone is difficult.

A customer displaying an Aug chip alongside an explicit request to prepare another host offers several strong clues. A quiet customer wearing a mask and slightly unusual clothes is a different situation.

The scanner reduces that uncertainty, but acquiring it also competes with ordinary shop expenses. Before buying specialized detection equipment, check your next rent payment and whether the shop needs basic storage, food, or production supplies more urgently.

For a run with **Aug Menace**, reliable identification becomes more important because the threat begins immediately and continues throughout the game. On a normal first run, waiting until Aug encounters become an active concern can preserve cash for essential purchases.

### Why not use Threaten (Aug) to test someone?

**Threaten (Aug)** appears in the bargaining interface. It is a negotiation action, not a detection tool.

It accuses a customer of being an Aug and threatens to shoot them to obtain a better trading price. Used against an ordinary human, the accusation fails and costs reputation.

Do not repeatedly use it on uncertain customers hoping to discover who is augmented. The Aug Scanner exists for identification; Threaten (Aug) changes a negotiation and carries its own consequences.

<section id="shotgun">

## Why Can't You Equip the Shotgun?

The message **"Cannot equip the shotgun right now"** does not have a single meaning.

The shotgun is a restricted interaction near the counter, rather than a weapon you can freely carry around the shop. The game controls when it can be equipped.

There are two particularly important situations to distinguish.

### The shotgun has been delivered, but cannot be equipped yet

When The Mentor first introduces the Augs and leaves the shotgun, trying to equip it may display the restriction message.

This is not proof that the weapon is missing or broken.

Watch your **Nightly Report** for a Security warning that an Aug is likely to visit. In a normal progression, that warning can coincide with the shotgun becoming available.

The warning does not identify which customer is augmented. You still need to inspect each person individually instead of firing at whoever arrives first.

### The shotgun worked earlier, but is unavailable against another visitor

In a 1.0 run, the player successfully eliminated an Aug and collected the reward. Later, the **Landlord** appeared to collect rent.

Attempting to use the shotgun against him displayed:

**"Cannot equip the shotgun right now."**

![Landlord collecting rent while the shotgun displays Cannot equip the shotgun right now, after an earlier successful Aug kill.](/images/probably-stolen/aug-shotgun-cannot-equip-landlord.webp)

This establishes an important distinction: a shotgun that was available during an Aug encounter may still be disabled for a different visitor or interaction.

The correct response is not to restart the game, buy another weapon, or assume that Aug progression has been reset. The Landlord is there for a rent interaction, not an Aug elimination.

When the shotgun refuses to equip, check the current customer and whether an Aug encounter is actually active.

### How do you aim and fire?

When the shotgun is available:

1. Move to the shotgun beside the trading counter.
2. **Hold the mouse button** on the weapon interaction to open the aiming view.
3. Confirm the target before firing.
4. Use the displayed **left-click SHOOT** or **right-click HOLSTER** control.

A simple click may not open the aiming view; holding the interaction is important.

Holster the weapon when the customer has not been positively identified. Once you fire, the result cannot be undone through ordinary negotiation.

### Why shouldn't you call Security on an Aug?

The Mentor explains that Augs have compromised Security communications. Calling Security about a suspected Aug can allow it to escape before officers arrive.

That differs from a **Wanted criminal**, who should be matched against the Wanted list and reported through the normal Security interaction.

A person being wanted is not evidence of augmentation, and a suspicious Aug is not automatically a criminal you should report through the alarm system.

Using the wrong response can lose the opportunity to deal with the actual threat.

<section id="shooting-results">

## What Happens When You Shoot an Aug?

A correct kill is followed by Security examining the target and confirming whether the person was augmented.

During the 1.0 encounter involving the customer requesting supplies for another host, officers arrived after the shot and identified the body as an Aug.

The confirmation included:

**"Good kill."**

The player then received **101 Credits**.

![Security confirming a successful Aug elimination and the 101-Credit reward in a 1.0 encounter.](/images/probably-stolen/aug-confirmed-kill-101-credits.webp)

This is a successful elimination, not merely a suspected target disappearing from the window.

### Do you always get 101 Credits?

No fixed 101-Credit payout should be assumed.

That was the reward in one completed encounter. Another successful elimination produced a different amount, and the Aug Menace starting perk explicitly increases Aug-kill rewards by **25%**.

Check the actual reward after the officers finish their inspection instead of using one encounter's payment as a universal amount.

The reward can help with rent, but Aug hunting is not a substitute for a sustainable trading business. Encounters depend on the current threat state and available customers; they are not a guaranteed source of daily income.

### What happens if you shoot a human?

A wrong kill can lead to a **manslaughter investigation** and reputation consequences. This is far more serious than failing an ordinary bargaining attempt.

The important distinction is between suspicion and confirmation. Strange clothing alone does not justify the same decision as a completed positive scan or multiple unmistakable Aug behaviors.

Once a customer has been shot, the game determines the consequences from the target's actual identity, not from what you believed at the time.

<section id="aug-menace">

## Is Aug Menace Worth Taking as a Starting Perk?

**Aug Menace grants one additional Starting Perk point** in exchange for three major changes:

- Augmented encounters are unlocked from the beginning.
- Killing an Aug pays **25% more Credits**.
- The Aug threat never naturally ends, and letting too many Augs escape can end the game **on every difficulty**.

The final condition is the important drawback.

A normal shop run can eventually move beyond its Aug threat. With Aug Menace, the danger persists even as the business expands, and the perk removes the safety of relying on an easier difficulty to avoid an Aug-related defeat.

### When is Aug Menace a good choice?

Take it when you deliberately want an Aug-focused run and are prepared to inspect customers carefully from the first day.

It can also be useful when you're pursuing the **Aug Hunter** achievement, which requires shooting 10 Augmented targets.

The bonus reward is attractive, but getting more Credits per kill does not compensate for frequently letting targets escape. A poorly equipped shop with unfamiliar customers can accumulate threat while you're still learning trading, storage, and rent management.

### When should you avoid it?

A first-time Apprentice player usually benefits more from learning merchandise inspection, negotiation, and daily shop management without another permanent failure condition.

The same caution applies to difficult starts where rent and basic supplies are already restrictive.

Choose Aug Menace for its actual gameplay challenge, not merely because it appears to offer extra income. You must still correctly identify targets and keep the shop financially viable.

</section>

<section id="missed-augs">

## What Happens if You Let an Aug Leave?

A suspicious customer leaving the shop does not always produce an immediate visible penalty at the counter.

Instead, the **Nightly Report** may reveal that an Aug visited the previous day. Another report can warn that Security expects an Aug to visit during the coming day.

These messages serve different purposes:

- **Expected visit:** Be ready to inspect every customer during the next trading day.
- **Confirmed missed visit:** An Aug passed through the store without being eliminated.

Neither report tells you that every customer requesting Materials or Medical supplies is an Aug.

A missed visit also cannot be repaired by shooting an unrelated customer the following day. Resume careful identification rather than trying to compensate for a previous mistake.

### Can missed Augs end your run?

Yes, when the Aug threat reaches its failure condition.

With **Aug Menace**, the perk explicitly makes this possible on every difficulty. If the threat reaches its critical level, the **Augmented** ending describes the AI taking control of the shopkeeper through a forcibly implanted chip.

This is a losing ending, not the successful shop Buyout.

The practical priority is to treat Aug warnings seriously while avoiding guesses that could cause a criminal investigation. Missing one target is bad; shooting an innocent person out of panic creates a different problem.

</section>

<section id="aug-hunter">

## How Do You Get the Aug Hunter Achievement?

**Aug Hunter** requires **10 confirmed Aug kills**. Threatening a suspicious customer, allowing an Aug to leave, or shooting an ordinary human does not satisfy the requirement.

**Aug Menace** makes augmented encounters available from the beginning, but the threat remains active throughout the run. Identify each target before firing and look for Security's successful elimination response afterward.

Track completed eliminations with the [achievement checklist](/probably-stolen/achievements).

</section>
