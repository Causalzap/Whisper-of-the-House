<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Open the **Reputation** box beside your shop entrance and check which faction is closest to its next threshold. Offer favorable trades to customers from that faction, complete its available requests, and avoid actions that would push its standing into a negative perk.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A generous deal can improve your relationship with a customer but leave fewer Credits for rent. Reporting a criminal can bring a cash reward and Security approval while damaging your Black Market standing. A Chemist can deliver valuable Dream Dust and still disappoint the Cartel because the weekly quota was not met.

Those consequences are connected to six reputation tracks: **Lower Levels, Upper Levels, Security, Black Market, Revolution, and Sector 13 Cartel**. Each has its own rewards, restrictions, and potentially fatal outcomes.

The important question is not simply which faction has the highest score. It is what your next transaction changes, which threshold you are approaching, and whether that reward is useful for your current business.

<section id="how-reputation-works">

## How Does Faction Reputation Work?

Every faction maintains its own standing. As that number reaches a positive threshold, the corresponding benefit becomes available. Falling to a negative threshold can remove services, increase costs, or create dangerous encounters.

For example, **Lower Levels +20** adds a daily customer, whereas **Lower Levels -20** removes one. The consequences become much more severe further down the same track: at -80, suppliers refuse ordinary calls.

Open the Reputation box near the shop entrance to examine each relationship. The screen shows the different factions and the effects associated with their standing.

![The shop's Reputation interface showing faction standing and the requirements for Revolution Marketplace Requests.](/images/probably-stolen/factions-reputation-panel.webp)

A faction label on a customer tells you which group they belong to. Tourists and some other customer categories are not separate reputation tracks.

Sector 13 Cartel is also different from the usual customer factions. Its relationship is especially relevant when playing Chemist, where deliveries to a handler determine whether the business is meeting its obligations.

### Do reputation rewards stay unlocked?

Reputation thresholds depend on your current standing. A reward reached at +40 is not something you should permanently count on after the same faction falls below its requirement.

Negative conditions work in the opposite direction. Once your reputation drops to -40 or lower, the corresponding penalty applies.

This makes the distance to your next threshold important. A player at +19 Lower Levels needs only one more point to reach Referral; a shop at -75 is dangerously close to losing supplier access at -80.

</section>

<section id="raise-reputation">

## How Do You Raise Faction Reputation?

The most common way to build standing is through ordinary trade with members of that faction. Negotiation changes how favorable the transaction is to the customer, which in turn affects the reputation gained.

After The Mentor introduces bargaining, the negotiation interface allows two broad approaches:

- **Offer a better price to the customer.** You accept less profit or pay more, but gain more reputation. A customer buying from you can also increase their available budget.
- **Demand a better price for yourself.** You improve the immediate deal if accepted, but gain less faction reputation. The customer's buying budget can also decrease.

Customers accept offers that favor them. Demands in your own favor are less reliable, and your existing standing influences the likelihood of acceptance.

### When is taking a smaller profit worth it?

A shop trying to reach **Lower Levels +20** may benefit more from a reputation-friendly sale than from squeezing every Credit out of the next customer.

Once Referral is active, it provides an extra random customer every day. That creates opportunities for additional trades beyond the single transaction that helped unlock it.

The same logic does not apply equally to every faction. Improving Black Market standing may be valuable for an illegal trading business, while Upper Levels reputation may offer a clearer return for a shop selling expensive merchandise to wealthier customers.

During one negotiation, offering a **10% discount** displayed an expected gain of **2.7 reputation**. The player could see the standing reward before completing the deal.

In one transaction, three Metal Ingots totaled **171 Credits**, exceeding the customer's **163-Credit budget**. A 10% discount would bring the quoted price within that budget, while the bargaining panel also shows the resulting reputation calculation. The tradeoff is immediate income versus improving the customer relationship.

![Bargaining screen showing a 10% discount option, a 163-Credit customer budget, a 171-Credit total, and the Reputation Multiplier.](/images/probably-stolen/factions-negotiation-reputation.webp)

The precise gain depends on the transaction and current modifiers. Check the amount shown in your own negotiation interface instead of assuming that every 10% discount awards the same reputation.

### Other ways to gain reputation

Regular trading is not the only way to improve relationships.

- **Wanted List reports:** Correctly reporting a wanted criminal improves Security standing.
- **Revolution encounters:** Certain choices involving wanted Revolution members can improve your relationship with the Revolution.
- **Marketplace Requests:** Completing an available request earns the faction reward displayed in its requirements.
- **Cartel deliveries:** Supplying the required quantity and purity of Dream Dust improves your standing with Sector 13 Cartel.

These actions are not interchangeable. A normal customer sale does not replace a Chemist's weekly Cartel delivery, and reporting a wanted customer may hurt a different faction even when Security approves.

<section id="security-black-market-conflict">

## Why Does Reporting a Criminal Hurt Black Market Reputation?

A completed Security report can improve one relationship while damaging another.

In a 1.0 trading session, the player reported a Contraband dealer. Security awarded **75 Credits** and confirmed that the shop's Security standing had improved.

The same incident reduced Black Market reputation. Checking the Reputation box afterward showed the Black Market at **-16**.

![Security incident report granting 75 Credits for reporting a Contraband dealer, with a resulting Black Market reputation loss.](/images/probably-stolen/factions-security-report-contraband.webp)

This is a real cost to consider before reporting someone.

A shop that relies on thieves, Contraband purchases, or the Underground Exchange may lose more over time than the value of one reporting reward. At **Black Market -40**, Blacklisted removes access to the Underground Exchange and prevents Black Market customers from offering Contraband.

By contrast, a legitimate shop trying to improve Security standing may value the reward and relationship improvement.

### Should you always report wanted customers?

Check the Wanted List, the reward, and the faction relationship before acting.

One wanted customer connected to the Revolution offered to put in a good word if allowed to leave. The player declined a **100-Credit** reporting opportunity and subsequently gained Revolution reputation. The Reputation interface showed **15 Revolution standing**, only five points below the first positive perk.

That decision had a clear benefit: **Revolution +20** unlocks occasional Marketplace Requests from Revolution cells.

Reporting the customer would have prioritized the immediate Security reward instead. Letting him leave traded that opportunity for progress with the Revolution.

Neither outcome is automatically better. The choice depends on the business you are operating and which relationship you are trying to build.

### What about false reports?

Reporting the wrong person can damage Security standing and cause a financial penalty.

Compare the customer with the Wanted List before making the call. A face that looks similar to a wanted poster is not sufficient evidence, especially when identifying people with different hairstyles or accessories.

Augs require a separate response. For an augmented customer, [check the identification and shotgun conditions](/probably-stolen/augs) rather than treating the encounter as an ordinary Wanted List report.

</section>

## What Are All Lower Levels Reputation Perks?

Lower Levels is the most immediate relationship for a shop operating in the station's poorer district. Its standing affects ordinary customer traffic, merchandise availability, supplier access, and theft risk.

  rows={lowerLevelsRows}
/>

### Why won't suppliers answer the phone?

**Shunned at -80 Lower Levels** is the first condition to inspect.

The effect prevents ordinary supplier calls, and Dr. Jackson stops making normal visits. This can interrupt several businesses simultaneously because manufacturing and resale depend on obtaining materials.

Check the Reputation box before assuming the Telephone or supplier network has malfunctioned. Restoring standing above the Shunned threshold is more important than buying another machine when you cannot obtain the materials to operate it.

A different warning appears at **-60 Marked**: more thieves target the shop, while Insurance costs double. Paying for more protection may help with individual incidents, but it does not repair the underlying reputation problem.

### Which Lower Levels perks are worth pursuing first?

**+20 Referral** has an immediate effect on daily customer opportunities. It is useful across most starting scenarios.

At **+40 Rare Goods**, additional customer types can bring merchandise that ordinary visitors do not usually offer. This matters more once you have enough capital and inspection equipment to evaluate unusual items.

The **+100 Pillar of the Lower Levels** discount reduces the cost of purchases from Joe Wild and Dr. Jackson by 15%. That is particularly valuable when planning expensive upgrades, but it is a distant target for a new shop.

Avoid spending the rent reserve trying to rush directly to +100 through generous trades. Reach the smaller thresholds while maintaining enough cash to continue operating.

</section>

<section id="security">

## What Are All Security Reputation Perks?

Security standing affects inspections, investigations, theft protection, and access to Security-related traders.

<GuideTable
  columns={factionColumns}
  rows={securityRows}
/>

Two negative effects deserve special attention: **No Quarter** and **By Any Means**. By Any Means is associated with -100 standing and immediate arrest. No Quarter is also listed among Security penalties, but its separate numeric activation threshold is not shown; do not treat it as a guaranteed extra unlock at exactly -100.

### Why are Security investigations progressing faster?

**Watchlist (-20)** and **Most Wanted (-60)** increase investigation speed, while **Upper Levels -60 Petition to Security** creates an additional penalty. Security -80 also makes inspections more frequent and less forgiving.

These penalties become especially dangerous when your Security Record already contains substantial criminal evidence. [Check your investigation progress and compare Wild's Fixers costs](/probably-stolen/security-investigation) before allowing another day to pass.

### Is Security +100 worth pursuing?

**VIP** eliminates ordinary Security inspections and prevents thieves from targeting the shop. It is one of the strongest protection rewards available.

However, the path to +100 may be expensive if you're sacrificing profit on every trade simply to increase standing.

A safer early target is **+20 Security Surplus**, which allows occasional visits from the Security Surplus Merchant. At **+40**, Security Protection begins reducing theft risk.

For a store already paying for Insurance and repairs, improving Security can reduce future operating pressure even before reaching VIP.

</section>

<section id="black-market">

## What Are All Black Market Reputation Perks?

Black Market standing controls several opportunities tied to Contraband, thieves, underground exchanges, and Insurance.

<GuideTable
  columns={factionColumns}
  rows={blackMarketRows}
/>

The Black Market handles reputation losses more harshly than an ordinary customer relationship. Honor Amongst Thieves describes a 1.5-times loss multiplier, although the actual applied change can differ because other Black Market modifiers also affect losses. Use the displayed standing change to judge a particular encounter.

### Why is the Underground Exchange unavailable?

At **-40 Black Market**, Blacklisted removes access to the Underground Exchange, stops Black Market customers offering Contraband, and doubles Janitorial Service costs.

This is a serious setback for a Fence or another shop relying on illegal merchandise. The lost service can be more expensive than the reputation penalty initially suggests.

At **-60 Uninsurable**, Insurance becomes unavailable altogether. That is not the same as Insurance becoming more expensive under a different faction penalty.

For an underground trading business, restoring Black Market standing should take priority over chasing a distant positive perk when either of these restrictions is active.

### Which Black Market reward helps trading most?

**+20 Small Time Broker** increases opportunities to buy from Petty Thieves. Their merchandise also improves as your standing grows.

**+40 Safe Hands** guarantees a weekly Thief visit and improves the goods offered by thieves.

These are more directly useful to a Contraband trader than +60 Underground Network, which primarily supplies advance information about events.

A shop that rarely handles illegal merchandise may prefer Security or ordinary customer reputation instead.

</section>

<section id="upper-levels">

## What Are All Upper Levels Reputation Perks?

Upper Levels reputation matters when the shop wants customers who can afford expensive merchandise and larger purchases.

<GuideTable
  columns={factionColumns}
  rows={upperLevelsRows}
/>

The Hired Hitman effect represents a fatal deterioration in relations, but its separate numeric activation threshold is not specified in the perk definition. Treat very low Upper Levels standing as dangerous rather than relying on an exact trigger number.

### Is Upper Levels reputation good for making money?

The most immediately useful positive threshold is **+20 Pourboire**, giving a 15% bonus on sales to Upper Levels customers.

At **+100 Rave Reviews**, those customers have 25% more Credits available. This can help complete expensive transactions that would otherwise exceed a buyer's budget.

There is still a difference between having more purchasing power available and guaranteeing a sale. Customers must want the merchandise you offer, and the shop needs enough valuable inventory to take advantage of their budgets.

For cross-business decisions involving equipment costs and cash recovery, [compare the actual sale proceeds and rent balance](/probably-stolen/money-making) rather than treating a reputation bonus as automatic profit.

### Why can low Upper Levels reputation cause Security trouble?

At **-60 Upper Levels**, **Petition to Security** doubles negative Security reputation changes and accelerates criminal investigations. Recovering Security standing alone will not remove this penalty while Upper Levels remains at -60 or below.

Check both reputation tracks before choosing favorable trades to repair the damage. If an investigation is already active, [calculate how these penalties affect its daily progress](/probably-stolen/security-investigation).

</section>

<section id="revolution">

## What Are All Revolution Reputation Perks?

The Revolution offers a different progression path from normal retail trading. Its most valuable rewards revolve around special requests, discounted materials, and event information.

<GuideTable
  columns={factionColumns}
  rows={revolutionRows}
/>

### How do you unlock the Revolution Quartermaster?

Reach **+40 Revolution reputation**.

At that threshold, a Revolution Quartermaster can occasionally visit and sell Materials at discounted prices. Higher reputation improves those discounts.

Before +40, **+20 Revolution Marketplace Request** gives you access to occasional requests from Revolution cells.

Completing useful requests can strengthen your relationship further, but each request has its own delivery requirements and available reward. Check the requested items and remaining time before committing valuable stock.

### Should you report a wanted Revolution member?

Consider the immediate Security reward against the Revolution standing you might gain by letting that person go.

One player gained **15 Revolution reputation** after sparing a wanted visitor who offered to speak favorably about the shop. That was enough to bring the relationship close to the first positive threshold.

The outcome is not a rule that every wanted criminal should be released. It illustrates why the person's identity and dialogue matter as much as the reward printed on the Wanted List.

Revolution standing also carries a serious negative outcome. Falling to **-100 Hit list** can result in a squad being dispatched to kill the shopkeeper.

</section>

<section id="cartel">

## What Are All Sector 13 Cartel Reputation Perks?

Sector 13 Cartel is particularly important for the **Chemist** start. It operates around deliveries to a handler rather than the ordinary retail relationship used by most customer factions.

<GuideTable
  columns={factionColumns}
  rows={cartelRows}
/>

### How Do Cartel Deliveries Change Reputation?

Completing the Cartel's weekly collection improves Sector 13 Cartel standing. The first delivery requires **10 Dream Dust at 40% purity or higher**; the next requirement rises to **15 at 45% purity**.

The handler evaluates the completed delivery, not simply how many vials you have produced during the week. [Prepare the Dream Dust and complete the collection](/probably-stolen/chemist) before risking a reputation loss.

### What Happens When You Miss the Cartel Quota?

In one collection, the handler accepted **13 of the required 15 Dream Dust**, but the incomplete delivery caused a Cartel reputation loss.

![The Cartel Handler states that 15 Dream Dust were required, while the shopkeeper has placed only 13 containers on the counter.](/images/probably-stolen/factions-cartel-quota-shortfall.webp)

A partial shipment can therefore cost both finished inventory and reputation. Repeated shortfalls become especially dangerous as Cartel standing approaches the negative thresholds shown above.

### Which Cartel perks matter most?

**+25 Increased Stipend** improves the weekly payment associated with your Cartel relationship.

At **+50 Reliable Chemist**, the reputation cost of requesting additional production materials is reduced by half. This is useful when a production shortage threatens the next delivery, but spending reputation to obtain materials is not free.

At **+100 Authorized Exit**, the handler begins processing your release from Cartel obligations.

That is different from buying the shop from the Landlord. The shop deed and the Cartel's authorization are separate matters.

The negative endpoint, **-100 Forced Retirement**, dispatches a Cartel hitman. Repeated shortfalls can therefore endanger the entire run rather than merely reducing a weekly payment.

</section>

<section id="reputation-difficulty">

## How Does Difficulty Change Reputation Gains and Losses?

The same faction decision can produce different standing changes depending on the selected difficulty.

<GuideTable
  columns={reputationDifficultyColumns}
  rows={reputationDifficultyRows}
/>

### Why is reputation so difficult to recover on Red Ink?

On **Red Ink**, positive reputation changes are halved, while losses are doubled.

Compare a reputation change that would ordinarily be worth 10 points:

- **Mark Up:** Approximately +10 for the gain.
- **Red Ink:** Approximately +5 for the gain.
- A normal 10-point reputation loss can instead become approximately **20 points** on Red Ink.

These figures illustrate the difficulty multipliers before other faction or transaction adjustments.

The consequence is substantial. Rebuilding a damaged relationship through generous deals takes longer, and a single mistake can erase progress made through several successful transactions.

**Thin Margins** is less severe, with gains reduced to 75% and losses increased to 125%. **Price Gouge** applies the opposite pressure, rewarding gains more heavily and reducing losses.

Choose your reputation strategy with the difficulty in mind. A risky reporting decision may be tolerable on Mark Up but much harder to recover from on Red Ink.

</section>

<section id="starting-perks-reputation">

## Which Starting Perks Change Faction Reputation?

Starting Perks can put your shop above a useful threshold before the first customer arrives, or impose a penalty before you've made a single trade.

<GuideTable
  columns={reputationStartingPerkColumns}
  rows={reputationStartingPerkRows}
/>

### Is Well Connected better than Diplomatic?

**Well Connected** provides an immediate +15 reputation with every faction. That gives you a stronger starting position across several relationships, but does not automatically unlock most +20 rewards.

**Diplomatic** increases reputation generated through trading by 33%. It becomes useful over repeated transactions, especially when you frequently deal with customers from the same faction.

The difference is timing.

Well Connected helps immediately, before the shop has earned anything. Diplomatic rewards an active trading strategy and requires enough transactions to produce its benefit.

### Why is Convict more dangerous than it looks?

Convict begins with **-30 Security reputation** and reduces Wanted List reporting rewards by 25%.

![The Convict Starting Perk showing -30 Security reputation and a 25% reduction in Wanted List rewards.](/images/probably-stolen/factions-convict-starting-perk.webp)

Because Watchlist activates at **-20**, choosing Convict places you under an investigation penalty from the beginning.

This can be a manageable handicap for a shop that avoids criminal activity and plans to improve its Security relationship. It is much less attractive when combined with frequent Contraband dealings or other actions that increase investigation risk.

A shop choosing Convict for the extra perk budget should account for the cost of recovering Security standing, not only the immediate benefit of another Starting Perk.

### Which Starting Perk unlocks a faction reward immediately?

**Revolution Sympathizer** begins with +25 Revolution reputation.

That crosses the **+20 Revolution Marketplace Request** threshold immediately, allowing the shop to receive occasional requests from Revolution cells.

**Model Citizen** begins with +30 Security reputation, passing the **+20 Security Surplus** threshold.

**Sycophant** begins at +15 Upper Levels reputation, just below Pourboire at +20. It also improves Upper Levels reputation gains and customer budgets.

These are different kinds of advantages: direct access to a relationship reward, a head start toward another threshold, or faster growth over the remainder of the run.

</section>

<section id="reputation-priorities">

## Which Faction Reputation Should You Increase First?

Start with the restriction that is currently hurting your business, not the faction with the most attractive +100 reward.

A shop that cannot telephone suppliers needs to address **Lower Levels -80** before purchasing more machinery. A Fence close to **Black Market -40** should protect Underground Exchange access. A Chemist approaching the weekly collection should preserve qualifying Dream Dust instead of trying to raise ordinary customer reputation with those products.

For an otherwise stable shop, the first positive targets worth considering are:

- **Lower Levels +20:** One more random customer each day.
- **Security +20:** Occasional access to the Security Surplus Merchant.
- **Upper Levels +20:** A 15% sales bonus with Upper Levels customers.
- **Revolution +20:** Occasional Marketplace Requests.
- **Black Market +20:** More Petty Thief opportunities and improved wares.

These rewards serve different businesses. A store earning steady money from legitimate Water or Food sales does not need the same faction priorities as a Fence handling stolen goods.

Keep enough Credits for rent before making deliberate reputation-building discounts. The benefit of another customer or a supplier discount is wasted if the shop cannot remain open long enough to use it.

For Chemist, the weekly Cartel requirement deserves special priority. Missing a collection can damage a relationship that cannot simply be repaired through ordinary retail transactions.
