<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Choose **Apprentice** for your first run. Check the next rent payment and reserve enough Credits to cover it. Put merchandise in the **Display Case** before opening the shop.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The first expensive mistake in Probably Stolen usually happens before the shop has earned anything. A tempting machine, a discounted item, or a customer offering a large batch of goods can absorb the cash you still need for rent. The shelves look more impressive, but the landlord wants credits, not inventory.

Your starting scenario determines the tools and merchandise available at the beginning. After that, the shop runs on a repeating cycle: prepare stock, attract customers, negotiate trades, manage what you bought, and make it through another rent payment. Production machines, faction relationships, and riskier trading opportunities add more decisions as the business grows.

## Which Starting Scenario Should You Choose?

**Apprentice is the best first choice** if you want to learn the trading counter, customer requests, inspection tools, and inventory management without committing to a specialized production business immediately.

Other starts provide equipment for a particular trade or deliberately make survival harder. Pick one based on what you want to manage every day, not just which merchandise sounds most valuable.

The Credits and rent amounts below are the starting values for **Mark Up (Normal)** without additional perk modifiers. Different difficulty settings and Starting Perks can change what your first week costs.

## What Should You Do on Your First Day?

Before opening the shutter, take a moment to inspect the room. The calendar, negotiation area, Display Case, storage spaces, and tools you begin with all affect the day's trades.

Use this opening routine:

1. **Read the calendar and rent information.** Note the amount due and how many days remain.
2. **Clear enough counter space to trade.** Keep tools accessible, but move unrelated merchandise out of the way.
3. **Separate your merchandise.** Keep goods ready for sale apart from items that need inspection and materials you intend to use later.
4. **Fill the Display Case with goods you want customers to buy.** Do this before opening the shop.
5. **Open the shutter and handle the first customer.** Read their request, inspect the goods, and confirm the credits involved before accepting.
6. **After closing, check your inventory and overnight needs.** A machine or animal that requires attention cannot be ignored just because trading has ended.

The first day's priority depends on your starting stock. An Apprentice can learn the trading counter before investing in machinery. A Water Merchant already has equipment for handling water, so selling usable stock can be more valuable than immediately purchasing another production machine.

A Chemist must account for the Cartel's upcoming delivery, while a Rat Wrangler needs enough food and cage space to protect the next litter. A Fence should check the legal status of its starting inventory before exposing it to Security.

Before ending the day, identify the one obligation that cannot be postponed: rent, an overnight machine cycle, livestock care, or a required delivery. Spend the remaining Credits only after that obligation is covered.

### How do you buy and sell items?

Place the goods involved in a transaction on the appropriate trading counter to open the negotiation interface. A customer may be selling merchandise, trying to purchase something from you, or offering a trade that includes several items.

Check what they are actually asking for before choosing goods from storage. A customer looking for Food does not necessarily want the expensive tool or machine you have available, even if its estimated value is higher.

Customer budgets matter just as much as item values. Someone who cannot afford a large piece of merchandise may still purchase a smaller item of the correct category.

When you're buying, inspect the offered item's condition, contents, and relevant details first. A low purchase price is useful only when the item is something you can store, use, or reasonably sell again.

<section id="display-case">

## How Does the Display Case Attract Customers?

The Display Case is positioned to the left of the shutter door. The type of merchandise inside influences which additional customer comes to your shop.

For example, displaying Food can attract someone interested in purchasing Food. Including merchandise from another category can also affect the kind of buyer you attract.

The initial Display Case can attract **one additional customer per day**. Upgrading it increases that capability.

There are two details worth checking before you open:

- **Item category:** This helps determine what the attracted customer wants to buy.
- **Displayed merchandise value:** This influences the social class of the attracted customer, which can matter when you're trying to sell expensive stock.

A shop full of valuable products can still struggle with sales when its display attracts the wrong customers.

### You're attracting buyers, but still not selling

Look at the merchandise in the window and the stock you actually want to sell.

A Food-focused display isn't especially useful when the business needs customers for Water or Medical goods. Adjust the display to match the available merchandise rather than leaving the same products there indefinitely.

Also check whether you need **buyers or sellers**. Purchasing more stock while your storage is already full can make the next day's trading more difficult. Signs and other customer-attraction options should support what the business needs at its current stage.

Display changes won't guarantee that every customer can afford an expensive product. Keep some smaller or lower-priced goods available when appropriate.


Keep Contraband out of the Display Case. Security inspections can discover exposed illegal goods, but the punishment depends on the item's Contraband tier rather than the act of displaying it alone. [Check inspection risks and possible penalties](/probably-stolen/security-investigation) before using restricted merchandise to attract buyers.

<section id="rent">

## How Do You Survive Rent in Probably Stolen?

Rent is collected regularly, regardless of whether your shop has made enough successful trades.

The calendar provides the next payment date. The amount can change with difficulty, selected perks, and the state of the run, so rely on the payment displayed in your own game.

The most dangerous situation is having enough merchandise to appear wealthy but not enough available credits when the landlord arrives.

### What to do when rent is approaching

Look at the amount due before making another large purchase.

- **Several days remain:** Keep trading, but avoid committing so much cash that ordinary business expenses become difficult to cover.
- **One or two days remain:** Concentrate on completing sales with buyers who can afford your available stock. Delay optional machinery and speculative purchases.
- **Rent is due today:** Preserve the required credits. Merchandise that might sell tomorrow cannot cover today's payment.

Suppose a customer offers an expensive machine that would improve production next week. The purchase could still be harmful if it leaves your available cash below the landlord's next payment.

The machine's future value does not make those credits immediately available again.

### How Do the Four Difficulty Settings Change Your Start?

The full game provides four difficulty settings after you choose a starting scenario:

- **Price Gouge (Cozy):** A more forgiving economic start.
- **Mark Up (Normal):** Standard trading and rent conditions.
- **Thin Margins (Hard):** Reduced trading margins, more expensive progression, and greater financial pressure.
- **Red Ink (Challenge):** The harshest economic conditions, making early mistakes especially costly.

Your scenario and difficulty are separate choices. Rock Bottom already begins without stock or starting Credits; pairing it with Hard or Challenge Mode compounds that disadvantage.

Check the starting rent, available Credits, service fees, and property value on the difficulty screen before confirming your run. The same equipment purchase can be reasonable on Normal and unaffordable on Hard.

When comparing businesses, use the [profit and seven-day cash-flow calculator](/probably-stolen/money-making) with the prices and next rent payment shown in your own shop.

<section id="perks">

## How Do Starting Perks Work in Probably Stolen?

**Starting Perks are unavailable on your first run.** If the selection screen is locked, continue with your chosen scenario. After your first run, new characters can select perks before choosing their difficulty.

You begin with **3 Perk Points and 4 Perk Slots**.

- Positive perks spend points.
- Negative perks return points that can fund positive choices.
- Both positive and negative perks occupy slots.
- Some perks cannot be selected together.
- **Colorful Character** costs 1 point and provides 3 additional perk slots.

Extra points do not create extra slots. A combination can have enough points remaining but still be impossible because all four slots are occupied.

### Which Perks Are Useful for Your Starting Scenario?

**Savings** gives an additional **150 starting Credits** for 1 point. The benefit is immediate, which makes it useful when your opening cash is tight or an early equipment purchase would otherwise threaten rent.

**Vigilant** prevents overnight robbery. That can be worth the cost for a business that regularly holds valuable inventory, though it does not increase daily trading revenue.


**Bad Lease** gives you 1 additional Perk Point but increases starting rent by **40 Credits**. On a standard Apprentice start, that raises the first payment from 200 to 240 Credits. Taking Savings alongside it provides 150 extra starting Credits, but the higher rent remains an ongoing expense.


**Hoarder** can turn early clutter into saleable materials, but it also occupies space before the shop has established regular income. In one Red Ink opening, the perk created **three junk piles**. One could be cleared by staying at the shop, another required tools that were consumed during removal, and a third could be handled through a cleanup service costing **50 Credits**.

Using a Crowbar on one pile produced **three Common Electronics valued at 33 Credits each**. That created an opportunity to recover 99 Credits from the materials, but the tool was consumed and the items still needed a buyer. When a Material Surplus event arrived the following week, additional salvage became difficult to sell while the remaining clutter restricted storage.

Hoarder is more attractive when you can use the materials for equipment upgrades or move them through regular sales. If you already have full storage bays, clearing the shop may be more valuable than collecting another batch of low-priced materials.

**Known Snitch** gives 2 additional Perk Points, but customers on the Wanted List will never visit your store. You also begin with **-10 Revolution and -10 Black Market reputation**. Avoid it when your plan depends on reporting wanted criminals for Security rewards or trading with those visitors.

**Aug Menace** adds another risk to customer handling. [Learn how to identify augmented customers](/probably-stolen/augs) before taking a disadvantage that makes them more important to your run.

Starting Perks and reputation perks are separate systems. Your standing with a faction can grant or remove benefits during the run; [check the faction thresholds](/probably-stolen/factions) when choosing reputation-related starting conditions.

## How Do You Tell Whether a Customer Is Selling Counterfeit Goods?

Some merchandise requires more than checking its listed price. A customer can bring items with counterfeit markings, misleading information, or contents that don't match their label.

For inspectable goods, use the **Magnifying Glass** to examine the item before agreeing to buy it.

Look for a specific visible fault rather than treating a suspiciously low price as proof of a counterfeit. Cigarette packaging, for example, can require checking details such as seals and other identifying markings.

When the inspection interface identifies a fault you can select, use that information before choosing **Confront**.

A correct confrontation can force the seller to reconsider their offer. An incorrect accusation can cost reputation and cause the customer to leave, so a doubtful item is not always worth challenging.

### Should you buy an item that might be counterfeit?

Decide based on what you have actually identified.

A genuine item offered cheaply may be useful stock. A counterfeit has different resale implications, especially when a buyer expects the genuine version.

Do not assume that every suspicious item must be purchased and fixed later. When you cannot identify the item confidently, declining the trade preserves both money and space.

A water container's label may not match its actual purity. [Scan the contents and correct the label](/probably-stolen/water-purification) before selling it as a higher-grade product. A label alone doesn't prove that the water inside is genuine High-Quality or Pure Water.

<section id="security">

## How Do You Avoid Security Trouble While Trading?

Security problems are not all solved by inspecting an item or calling an officer. The correct response depends on whether you are handling suspicious merchandise, a wanted criminal, an augmented customer, or an inspection.

**Suspicious merchandise:** Inspect the item before purchasing it. Counterfeit markings and mismatched contents affect the trading decision, but a low price alone does not prove that an item is fake.

**Stolen or contraband goods:** Check the merchandise's legal status and keep illegal stock out of the Display Case. A profitable sale does not eliminate the inspection risk created by exposed inventory.

**Customers on the Wanted List:** Compare the visitor against the Wanted poster before deciding whether to report them. These reports can affect Security reputation, while the customer encounter may also matter to other factions.

**Augmented customers:** An Aug is not an ordinary wanted criminal. Calling Security is not a reliable way to identify one, and an incorrect attack can carry serious consequences. [Check the identification signs and correct responses](/probably-stolen/augs) before acting on suspicion alone.

### Why Can Your Shop Become Riskier After Successful Trades?

Criminal dealings and customer interactions can change faction reputation, which affects later opportunities and penalties.

A shop that sells expensive goods successfully can still end up with unfavorable Security standing or a more dangerous mix of customer encounters. Review reputation changes after significant transactions instead of judging success only by the number of Credits received.

If a faction penalty is already affecting your customers, negotiations, or access to services, [check the active reputation effects](/probably-stolen/factions) before investing in further expansion.

If you have already accumulated suspected crimes, open your Security Record rather than relying on reputation alone. [Calculate investigation progress and compare evidence-removal options](/probably-stolen/security-investigation) before the next overnight calculation.

<section id="after-closing">

## What Should You Do After Closing the Shop?

Closing the shutter ends the trading session, but it doesn't make every business problem wait until the next customer arrives.

Use the time before the next day to prepare the things that cannot be fixed during a busy sale.

**Organize merchandise.** Move newly purchased goods out of the counter area, separate finished products from inputs, and make sure valuable stock is easy to find.

**Check powered equipment.** Machines that work overnight need enough energy for their next cycle. A container placed in a machine does not guarantee a completed product if the equipment cannot operate.

**Check living stock.** Rats need appropriate food, usable enclosures, and attention to health and crowding. A colony that was manageable before closing can look quite different after another round of births.

**Prepare tomorrow's customers.** Reconsider what the Display Case is advertising. A day spent buying supplies may need a different customer mix from a day intended to sell finished merchandise.

**Prepare overnight sales.** If your shop uses a Vending Machine, make sure the merchandise intended for overnight selling is stocked before finishing the day. This gives suitable inventory another opportunity to generate revenue without waiting for the next counter transaction.

**Choose between scavenging and clearing storage.** Visit the Dumping Grounds when you need specific materials for a repair or upgrade, but check your remaining inventory space first. Scavenging loses much of its value when the next haul cannot be stored or sold.

With Hoarder, staying at the shop may be more useful than another scavenging trip if a debris pile is still blocking inventory space. Clear the obstruction before collecting more low-value materials, especially when rent is approaching and existing salvage has not found a buyer.

## How Do You Buy the Store and Win in Probably Stolen?

Owning the store **Deed** is the main victory objective. You can either buy the property outright from the Landlord or complete a mortgage through the Loan Shark.

Check the property's current value in the **Calendar**. This is separate from Estimated Total Wealth: owning expensive equipment or merchandise does not mean you have enough spendable Credits to purchase the shop.

### How Much Does the Store Cost?

  rows={probablyStolenBuyoutRows}
/>

The property stays at its base price through **Day 50**. After that, the price increases daily until reaching the cap for your difficulty.

On Mark Up, the store costs **12,000 Credits** before the increase begins. From Day 51 onward, the price rises by 50 Credits per day. Waiting longer can therefore raise the purchase target while regular rent continues reducing your available cash.

### How Do You Buy the Deed Outright?

Save enough Credits to cover the current property value, then wait for the Landlord's rent collection visit.

1. Open the Calendar and check the current property price.
2. Keep the required amount in available Credits rather than unsold inventory.
3. When the Landlord arrives, select **Buyout the store**.

Completing the purchase gives you the **Deed** and triggers the Buyout ending. You can continue playing afterward without further rent payments.

A business with unsold inventory may be closer to ownership by finding customers for existing stock than by purchasing another production machine.

### How Does the Loan Shark Mortgage Work?

The **Loan Shark** offers an alternative to saving the full purchase price. You pay an initial down payment and then make weekly mortgage payments until the remaining balance and interest are cleared.

On Mark Up at the base property price, the mortgage costs **2,000 Credits upfront**, followed by **930 Credits per week for 12 weeks**. The total payment is **13,160 Credits**, compared with a 12,000-Credit outright purchase.

Mortgage payments replace ordinary rent. They are not charged on top of it.

The Loan Shark can offer you three choices:

- **Accept:** Pay the down payment and begin the mortgage.
- **Ask him to return:** Delay the decision.
- **Decline:** End his mortgage offers for the current run.

A mortgage provides a lower initial purchase requirement but creates a much larger fixed weekly obligation. Before agreeing, check whether your existing sales can cover that payment consistently.

### What Happens If You Miss a Mortgage Payment?

The Loan Shark can repossess merchandise to recover the missed payment. The removed goods are recorded on a **Repo Slip**, and the remaining mortgage schedule continues.

If your shop does not contain enough recoverable value, the run can end in **Repossession**.

Do not judge mortgage affordability from Estimated Total Wealth alone. Credits available on payment day, dependable sales, and the value of stock that can actually be sold are more useful measures.

### Should You Keep Expanding or Save for the Deed?

Once regular sales cover operating expenses, compare the next equipment purchase with the Credits still needed for ownership. Additional production is less valuable when finished inventory is already waiting for buyers.

Before committing to another machine, [compare your cash reserves and equipment payback](/probably-stolen/money-making).

If you're attempting to obtain the Deed before Day 50 for **Prodigious**, [check the achievement requirements](/probably-stolen/achievements) before taking on a long repayment plan.
