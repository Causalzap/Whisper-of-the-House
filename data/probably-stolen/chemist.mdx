## How Do You Start as a Chemist in Probably Stolen?

Select **Chemist** from the starting scenarios when creating a new game. The specialization provides a Chemistry 101 manual, chemistry equipment, containers, and supplies needed to begin experimenting.

Chemist is an advanced start. Your opening inventory is occupied by several different machines, and the first delivery deadline begins before you have established a normal trading business.

The four essential machines serve different stages:

- **Blender:** Breaks Dream Caps or other raw ingredients into mush.
- **Heating Plate:** Heats the liquid mixture to extract the desired chemical.
- **Evaporator:** Removes water and alcohol overnight.
- **Chem Finisher:** Packages sufficiently dry material into pills, vials, or other chemical products.

You also have access to a **Composition Spectrometer**, which examines the contents of liquid containers and completed chemical products.

### What equipment should you set up first?

Start by identifying the Blender, Heating Plate, Evaporator, and Chem Finisher in your inventory. Keep the Water Pitcher or another compatible liquid container nearby, along with Dream Caps, Alcohol, and Kotton Fabric.

The starting Blender can have **35% Content Loss**, meaning the amount of usable ingredient recovered is lower than the raw material put into it.

![The starting Chemist Blender showing its 35% Content Loss attribute and chemical input slots.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-blender-content-loss.webp)

That loss matters because the handler counts finished Dream Dust vials, not the number of mushrooms you processed.

The initial Heating Plate has limited temperature control, while the Evaporator consumes electricity and processes liquid overnight. It is possible to own every required machine and still lose time because one container is being processed in the wrong order.

### Should you buy more machines immediately?

Not before completing the first production cycle.

Chemist begins with the equipment needed to produce Dream Dust. A second Evaporator or improved Blender may eventually increase throughput, but buying equipment too early uses money that could otherwise cover rent, Alcohol, additional Dream Caps, or Energy Cells.

A more useful first investment is often simply securing enough ingredients to finish the next batch.

The starting equipment is also large and awkward to arrange. A chemical storage upgrade can help organize a growing workshop, but storage does not fix an unfinished reaction.

Keep working containers and finished products separate so that an unprocessed mixture is not mistaken for something ready to sell.

## How Do You Make Dream Dust Step by Step?

Dream Dust is produced through a continuous four-machine process. The Chemistry 101 manual separates blending, extraction, evaporation, and packaging.

The sequence is:

**Dream Caps → Dream Cap Mush → Dream Cap Extract → Dry Extract → Dream Dust**

![The Chemistry 101 Dream Dust recipe showing the Blender, Alcohol extraction at 40–60°C, Kotton Fabric filtering, powered evaporation, and Chem Finisher packaging.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-recipe.webp)

Each transfer matters. Skipping the extraction stage cannot be repaired merely by selecting the correct product in the Chem Finisher.

### Step 1: Blend Dream Caps into mush

Open the **Blender** and attach a liquid container to its **CONTAINER** slot.

Place Dream Caps into the ingredient input and press **Start**. The Blender processes them into Dream Cap Mush inside the attached container.

Inspect the container before proceeding. Its contents should include Dream Cap Mush, not merely an empty pitcher or unrelated liquid.

The exact yield depends on how much material is processed and the Blender's extraction loss.

Do not use every remaining Dream Cap on a small experimental batch when your next Cartel collection still needs more finished vials.

### Step 2: Add Alcohol and extract at 40–60°C

Transfer the container of Dream Cap Mush to the **Heating Plate**.

The Chemistry 101 instructions recommend adding solvent Alcohol equal to **3–6 times the volume of Dream Cap Mush**.

For example, if the container holds 100 mL of mush, that corresponds to 300–600 mL of liquid Alcohol.

Use the actual liquid contents of the solvent rather than assuming that a bottle's total capacity is entirely Alcohol.

Heat the mixture until it reaches the extraction range:

**40°C to 60°C**

The purpose is to transform Dream Cap Mush into Dream Cap Extract. Going substantially above the intended range can destroy the valuable ingredient.

![The Heating Plate shows an actual temperature of 52°C with heating switched off, while its target temperature remains set to 99°C.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-cap-heating-temperature.webp)

The starting Heating Plate can display a target temperature as high as **99°C**. That is the machine's available target, not the temperature you should use for Dream Dust.

Watch the actual temperature and use the heating controls to avoid passing 60°C. In one production attempt, the mixture became too hot and the player had to stop heating immediately.

A later attempt was held around **53–55°C**, comfortably inside the intended range.

The important distinction is between a machine being capable of reaching a temperature and the chemical reaction requiring that temperature.

### Step 3: Filter the extract and evaporate the liquid

After the extraction stage, use **Kotton Fabric** to filter large contaminants from the mixture.

Filtering is optional in the basic recipe, but worthwhile when trying to improve the quality of a finished product.

Transfer the filtered mixture into the **Evaporator**.

Before ending the day:

1. Check that the mixture is inside a compatible liquid container.
2. Put the container into the Evaporator's **CONTAINER** slot.
3. Supply an **Energy Cell** to power the machine.
4. Confirm the machine is ready to operate overnight.

The starting Evaporator requires **6 Energy per day**. It removes water and alcohol, preserving the chemical material needed for the final product.

An Evaporator handles one connected batch container at a time. Having several mixtures ready does not mean they can all finish in the same machine overnight.

More importantly, **one night does not guarantee complete drying**.

Large batches or mixtures containing additional solvent may need multiple nights before the water and alcohol have disappeared.

Use the Composition Spectrometer to inspect what remains. Continue evaporation until the ingredient is sufficiently dry for the selected Chem Finisher recipe.

### Step 4: Select Dream Dust in the Chem Finisher

Move the dry extract to the **Chem Finisher**.

Open its control panel, choose **Configure**, and select **Vial of Dream Dust** from the available recipes.

The Chem Finisher requires **2.00 mL of processed material per Dream Dust vial**. That does not mean every vial contains 2 mL of pure Dream Cap Extract.

For example, a 56.50% Dream Dust vial contains **1.13 mL of Dream Cap Extract** within its 2.00 mL total volume. The remaining material includes physical contaminants, minerals, and protein. Improving the share of Dream Cap Extract increases actual purity.

![The Chem Finisher recipe list showing the required volumes and restrictions for Dream Dust, Nightmare Dust, and other chemical products.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-finisher-recipe-requirements.webp)

Once the recipe is ready, press **Output** to produce the vials. Repeat while sufficient processed material remains.

The Chem Finisher can also package other chemical products, so confirm the selected recipe before pressing Output. Choosing Caffeine Pill while trying to package Dream Dust will not produce the item required by the Cartel.

Finally, inspect the finished vial using the Composition Spectrometer.

One opening batch produced **46.95% purity Dream Dust**.

![A completed Vial of Dream Dust showing 46.95% actual purity after processing through the Chem Finisher.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-4695-purity.webp)

That result exceeds the initial **40% Cartel requirement**, but it is not a particularly high purity grade for commercial sales.

This distinction matters: meeting the handler's minimum and producing a premium product are separate objectives.

## How Do You Increase Dream Dust Purity?

The fastest way to waste Dream Caps is to confuse the quantity of finished product with its actual purity.

A larger batch can produce more vials without improving their grade. A correctly selected Chem Finisher recipe can still produce weak merchandise when the earlier extraction or filtering stages introduced too many impurities.

The Chemistry 101 manual uses the following chemical grades:

  rows={chemistPurityRows}
/>

A **46.95%** batch falls into **Cut Product**, which receives a substantial reduction in base value.

The same starting equipment later produced **56.5% Dream Dust**, still within Cut Product but with a stronger actual purity result.

![A later Dream Dust batch showing 56.5% purity after closer temperature control and filtering.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-565-purity.webp)

This improvement matters for deliveries with higher minimum purity requirements. It does not automatically guarantee that the product has reached Street Standard or Pharma Grade.

### Which production stages affect purity?

**Blender extraction loss:** The starting machine loses part of the material being processed. Improving its extraction performance can help recover more useful product.

**Heating:** Dream Cap Extract forms in the 40–60°C window. Excess heat can degrade the extract rather than improve it.

**Filtering:** Removing large contaminants before evaporation reduces unwanted material entering the final processing stage.

**Chem Finisher:** Its chemistry upgrades can reduce the impurities introduced while packaging the product.

The Composition Spectrometer is useful at more than one point. Examine the heated mixture before committing it to overnight evaporation, then inspect the finished vials before deciding whether to deliver or sell them.

### Why does relabeling Dream Dust not improve it?

A Labeler changes the displayed product label. It does not raise the product's actual purity.

Some buyers carry a **Chem Scanner**, allowing them to compare the real composition with the label.

A low-quality vial renamed as a premium chemical remains low-quality merchandise. Trying to sell it at a higher claimed grade can create trading problems without improving the underlying product.

This also matters for the **Say My Name** achievement. The achievement requires producing a Lab Pure narcotic, not merely giving an ordinary vial that name. For the full unlock requirement, [check the achievement conditions](/probably-stolen/achievements).

## Why Does the Chem Finisher Say Wet Ingredient Detected?

**Wet Ingredient Detected** means the current input has not satisfied the Chem Finisher's requirement for a dry chemical ingredient.

This frequently happens when a container has spent one night in the Evaporator and the player assumes that processing is complete.

A Caffeine Pill attempt produced exactly this error after the first evaporation cycle.

![The Chem Finisher refusing a Caffeine Pill batch with the Wet Ingredient Detected warning.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-wet-ingredient-detected.webp)

Changing the recipe or moving the mixture between containers did not immediately solve the problem.

The remaining water and alcohol needed more time to evaporate.

### How to fix Wet Ingredient Detected

Return the mixture to the Evaporator and inspect the following:

- **Power:** The Energy Cell must supply enough energy for overnight operation.
- **Container:** The mixture must be placed inside the machine's processing slot.
- **Remaining liquid:** Water or Alcohol may still be present even after a successful overnight cycle.
- **Batch size:** A larger volume can take longer to dry with the starting Evaporator.

Run another night when necessary, then check the composition again before returning to the Chem Finisher.

One reaction can be chemically successful but still too wet to package. That is why repeating the Blender and Heating Plate stages immediately is usually the wrong response to this particular warning.

### What does Not Enough Reagent mean?

**Not Enough Reagent** is a different warning.

The selected product requires a minimum amount of its active chemical ingredient. The machine cannot complete that recipe when insufficient usable material remains.

Check which recipe is selected and the available amount of the corresponding chemical.

For Dream Dust, the relevant ingredient is Dream Cap Extract. For Caffeine Pills, it is Caffeine.

Do not assume that a large container volume guarantees enough active reagent. Most of that volume may be solvent, water, or other unwanted material.

A batch can also have more than one problem. During a failed Caffeine Pill attempt, both **Wet Ingredient Detected** and **Not Enough Reagent** appeared while the player was troubleshooting.

Resolve the wetness first, then confirm that the remaining active ingredient actually meets the selected recipe's requirement.

### Why did heating produce weak or unusable extract?

The Heating Plate is another common source of failed batches.

Dream Cap extraction requires a controlled 40–60°C reaction. Raising the temperature above the permitted range can destroy valuable material before it ever reaches the Evaporator.

A failed attempt was followed by the discovery that the player had heated the mixture too aggressively and skipped the recommended filtration.

The resulting low-quality product was discarded rather than reserved for the Cartel.

Check the temperature before turning the heater on and monitor the composition afterward. Additional evaporation cannot recover extract that was already destroyed during heating.

### Why does a batch finish but still have poor purity?

That is not necessarily a machine malfunction.

Consider the order of operations:

- Blending determines how much usable mush is recovered.
- Heating determines whether the desired extract forms without excessive degradation.
- Filtering removes large unwanted contaminants.
- Evaporation removes water and alcohol.
- The Chem Finisher packages the remaining chemical and may introduce additional impurities.

A low result after completing all five actions points toward extraction or contamination quality, not a missing output button.

A Composition Spectrometer reading taken before evaporation can help distinguish destroyed extract from a batch that simply needs more drying time.

## How Do You Make Caffeine Pills With Chemist Equipment?

Caffeine Pills use the same main machines, but their reaction temperature is different.

Start with **Coffee Beans** rather than Dream Caps.

The process follows this sequence:

1. Blend Coffee Beans into mush using the Blender and an attached liquid container.
2. Move the mixture to the Heating Plate.
3. Add a suitable solvent; the manual recommends a volume **3–6 times** the Coffee Bean Mush.
4. Heat the mixture to **at least 80°C**.
5. Filter when needed, then evaporate the remaining water and alcohol.
6. Select **Caffeine Pill** in the Chem Finisher and package the dry Caffeine.

The Chem Finisher lists a **5.00 mL required volume** for a Caffeine Pill. The material must also satisfy the recipe's restriction against wet ingredients before it can be packaged.

Do not use the Dream Dust temperature limit for this reaction. Caffeine is more heat-resistant, and the manual gives it a higher extraction temperature.

However, the requirement to remove water and alcohol before packaging still applies.

During one attempt, the player finished the heating and evaporation stages, selected Caffeine Pill, and immediately encountered the Wet Ingredient warning. The remaining solvent was the first issue to address.

A further warning about insufficient reagent means the available Caffeine must also be checked before producing another pill.

### Should you produce Caffeine Pills before the first Cartel delivery?

Usually not when Dream Dust ingredients are limited.

The handler wants Dream Dust specifically. Caffeine Pills do not substitute for its required quantity, even though both products use the same machinery.

Coffee Beans can be useful when extra equipment time is available or when Dream Caps are temporarily unavailable. They are less useful when the first weekly collection is approaching and the Dream Dust quota remains unfinished.

Keeping the two recipes separate prevents a valuable production night from being spent on an item that does not advance your immediate obligation.

## How Do You Complete the First Cartel Dream Dust Delivery?

On **Day 1**, the **Cartel Handler** visits the Chemist shop and assigns the first quota.

His requirements are:

- **10 Dream Dust**
- **At least 40% purity**
- **Collection the following week**

The handler supplies starting materials and warns that the quota must be completed.

The collection is not a normal customer purchase. The products must be handed over to the Cartel, and the handler evaluates whether the delivery meets the specified requirements.

### What should you reserve for the first collection?

Keep **10 finished, qualifying vials** available before selling surplus narcotics to ordinary customers.

This matters because Dream Dust can attract buyers before the handler returns. Selling a few vials may bring immediate Credits, but that does not help if the same vials were needed to fulfill the quota.

One early batch yielded nine finished vials. Although those products were usable, nine was still one short of the required ten.

The correct next task was to finish another qualifying batch, not to sell the existing stock and hope that the handler would accept a smaller delivery.

Check purity separately from quantity. Ten low-grade vials will not meet a 40% minimum simply because their labels all say Dream Dust.

### What Happens When You Deliver All 10?

Handing over **10 qualifying Dream Dust vials** completed the first collection and improved Cartel reputation. The handler then raised the following week's requirement to **15 Dream Dust at 45% purity or higher**.

The earlier **46.95% purity** product could still meet the new quality threshold, but the previous production quantity was no longer sufficient. Preparing five additional vials would be necessary even if the original ten were all eligible for the next quota.

### Why Did the Second Delivery Fail With 13 Dream Dust?

At the next collection, the handler received only **13 of the required 15 vials**. He took the available Dream Dust, rejected the incomplete delivery, and the shop lost Cartel reputation.

The lost materials and production time could not be recovered through that collection. Reserve all 15 qualifying vials before selling any remaining Dream Dust to ordinary customers, especially when your next batch still needs overnight evaporation.

[Cartel reputation penalties](/probably-stolen/factions) become increasingly dangerous after repeated failed deliveries.

## What Should You Buy or Upgrade First as a Chemist?

The first bottleneck is usually **usable ingredients and complete drying**, not the number of machines in the shop.

A starting workshop can already produce qualifying Dream Dust. Spending heavily on production upgrades before learning the extraction and evaporation sequence can leave the business with expensive equipment but too little cash to purchase Dream Caps or Alcohol.

### Priority 1: Dream Caps and liquid Alcohol

Dream Caps provide the extract needed for Dream Dust, while Alcohol is the solvent used during extraction.

The Chemistry 101 manual explains that Chemical Supplies can be unpacked into materials useful for crafting. Suppliers and relevant customers can also provide chemistry ingredients.

Keep enough Dream Caps and solvent for the next batch before selling these items as ordinary merchandise.

A shortage of Dream Caps stops the Blender stage. A shortage of Alcohol can compromise extraction or force the player into an inferior workaround. Neither problem is solved by purchasing another Chem Finisher.

### Priority 2: Enough Energy for overnight evaporation

An Evaporator that cannot operate overnight delays the entire production chain.

With the starting requirement of **6 Energy per day**, check the Energy Cell before sleeping. A large mixture can need another overnight cycle, so budget for more than one day when processing heavily diluted batches.

Do not move a wet container to the Chem Finisher simply because the scheduled delivery is approaching. It will be rejected.

### Priority 3: Better extraction and temperature control

Once ingredient supplies and rent are stable, upgrades become more attractive.

The **Blender** benefits from improvements to extraction yield. The **Heating Plate** can be upgraded for better temperature control and heating performance.

The **Chem Finisher** benefits from modules that reduce impurities introduced during final processing.

Most of these chemistry machines use special **Chemical Equipment Modules**, rather than the ordinary upgrades used by general-purpose equipment.

The **Evaporator** uses normal modules. Its improvements can affect energy efficiency and the amount of liquid processed each night.

A useful equipment choice depends on the stage causing the actual delay:

- Losing too much valuable material? Improve extraction.
- Burning Dream Cap Extract? Improve heating control.
- Waiting multiple nights for every batch? Improve evaporation throughput.
- Finishing below the required grade despite adequate extraction? Inspect filtering and the Chem Finisher's quality-related penalties.

Buying the most expensive available module without checking where the batch is failing is unlikely to be efficient.

### Priority 4: Storage and an additional Evaporator

A larger chemical operation quickly fills the starting inventory with containers, raw ingredients, finished vials, and large machines.

The **Chemist Storage Bay** is useful when equipment and bottles are obstructing normal inventory management.

An additional Evaporator becomes attractive when you regularly prepare more than one mixture and the first machine is occupied overnight.

Neither upgrade should take priority over an imminent rent payment or a shortage of the materials needed to satisfy the handler.

During one Thin Margins run, an expanded chemical storage option was considered at **400 Credits**. That was a meaningful commitment for a shop that also needed supplies and had a weekly collection approaching.

The practical goal is to increase the number of qualifying vials you can consistently finish, not simply the number of machines you own.

## Should You Sell Dream Dust or Save It for the Cartel?

The decision depends on the next collection date and how many qualifying vials are already reserved.

Dream Dust is a **Contraband** product. Some customers are willing to buy it, but ordinary traders may reject it, and buyers with chemical scanners can identify mislabeled purity.

A vial also sells for less when its actual grade is poor. At **Cut Product**, the base value is reduced by **45%** before other trading conditions.

That can make low-quality sales much less profitable than expected, particularly when production consumed valuable Dream Caps, Alcohol, Energy, and several days of machine time.

### When is selling surplus reasonable?

Once the full upcoming Cartel quota is covered, surplus vials can be considered for sale.

Check three things before accepting the offer:

1. The buyer actually accepts the product's category and grade.
2. The price is worthwhile after the purity modifier and any discount.
3. The remaining qualifying inventory still meets the next Cartel collection.

Do not rely on a large stock of low-grade Dream Dust to replace a smaller number of vials that meet the handler's required purity.

The shop also needs ordinary cash flow for rent, tools, batteries, and replacement supplies. A legal trading activity can help cover those costs without using the stock reserved for Cartel deliveries.

### How do Security inspections affect Chemist inventory?

Dream Dust is Contraband, and keeping illegal stock exposed creates inspection risk.

A Chemist shop can satisfy the Cartel and still run into trouble with Security. Poor Security reputation makes that situation more dangerous.

During a difficult run, the player struggled with both falling Security standing and the Cartel's production demands. Later inspections and financial pressure interfered with the ability to restock ingredients.

The business was not short of production machinery at that point. It was short of the cash, materials, and safe operating conditions needed to keep producing.

That is why a Chemist should treat inventory concealment, rent, and chemical supply as parts of the same operating decision, rather than focusing entirely on the highest possible purity.

</section>

<section id="chemist-next-week">

## What Should You Do After Your First Successful Delivery?

The first completed Cartel collection proves that your equipment can produce qualifying Dream Dust. It does not prove that the workshop can keep pace with larger weekly requirements.

After the handler raises the order to **15 vials at 45% purity**, inspect your production capacity before selling anything.

A workable preparation sequence is:

1. Count the finished vials that already meet 45% purity.
2. Calculate how many additional vials are needed to reach 15.
3. Reserve enough Dream Caps and Alcohol for the missing production.
4. Keep the Evaporator operating overnight whenever a batch still contains solvent.
5. Check each finished batch's purity before moving it into the Cartel reserve.
6. Use only the remaining surplus for ordinary sales.

The second weekly requirement is where poor preparation becomes expensive. A shop can survive the first deadline with a small amount of equipment and still fall behind as the handler demands more.

Another production failure makes the situation worse: an overheated mixture may consume scarce Dream Caps without giving you useful extract, while an unfinished wet mixture occupies a valuable processing container.

I would prioritize dependable batches over chasing the highest possible grade while the next collection remains incomplete. A repeatable 50%-plus result is more useful for a 45% quota than an ambitious premium batch that consumes the entire week's supplies and fails.

Once the quota is secure, higher purity, additional machines, and more profitable sales become reasonable investments.
