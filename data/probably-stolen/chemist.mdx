## How Do You Start as a Chemist in Probably Stolen?

Select **Chemist** from the starting scenarios when creating a new game. The specialization provides a Chemistry 101 manual, chemistry equipment, containers, and supplies needed to begin experimenting.

Chemist is an advanced start. Your opening inventory is occupied by several different machines, and the first delivery deadline begins before you have established a normal trading business.

The four essential machines serve different stages:

- **Blender:** Breaks Dream Caps or other raw ingredients into mush.
- **Heating Plate:** Heats the liquid mixture to extract the desired chemical.
- **Evaporator:** Removes water and alcohol overnight.
- **Chem Finisher:** Packages sufficiently dry material into pills, vials, or other chemical products.

You also have access to a **Composition Spectrometer**, which examines the contents of liquid containers and completed chemical products.

### What equipment should you set up first?

Start by identifying the Blender, Heating Plate, Evaporator, and Chem Finisher in your inventory. Keep the Water Pitcher or another compatible liquid container nearby, along with Dream Caps, Alcohol, and Kotton Fabric.

The starting Blender can have **35% Content Loss**, meaning the amount of usable ingredient recovered is lower than the raw material put into it.

![The starting Chemist Blender showing its 35% Content Loss attribute and chemical input slots.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-blender-content-loss.webp)

That loss matters because the handler counts finished Dream Dust vials, not the number of mushrooms you processed.

The initial Heating Plate has limited temperature control, while the Evaporator consumes electricity and processes liquid overnight. It is possible to own every required machine and still lose time because one container is being processed in the wrong order.

### Should you buy more machines immediately?

Not before completing the first production cycle.

Chemist begins with the equipment needed to produce Dream Dust. A second Evaporator or improved Blender may eventually increase throughput, but buying equipment too early uses money that could otherwise cover rent, Alcohol, additional Dream Caps, or Energy Cells.

A more useful first investment is often simply securing enough ingredients to finish the next batch.

The starting equipment is also large and awkward to arrange. A chemical storage upgrade can help organize a growing workshop, but storage does not fix an unfinished reaction.

Keep working containers and finished products separate so that an unprocessed mixture is not mistaken for something ready to sell.

## How Do You Make Dream Dust Step by Step?

Dream Dust is produced through a continuous four-machine process. The Chemistry 101 manual separates blending, extraction, evaporation, and packaging.

The sequence is:

**Dream Caps → Dream Cap Mush → Dream Cap Extract → Dry Extract → Dream Dust**

![The Chemistry 101 Dream Dust recipe showing the Blender, Alcohol extraction at 40–60°C, Kotton Fabric filtering, powered evaporation, and Chem Finisher packaging.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-recipe.webp)

Each transfer matters. Skipping the extraction stage cannot be repaired merely by selecting the correct product in the Chem Finisher.

### Step 1: Blend Dream Caps into mush

Open the **Blender** and attach a liquid container to its **CONTAINER** slot.

Place Dream Caps into the ingredient input and press **Start**. The Blender processes them into Dream Cap Mush inside the attached container.

Inspect the container before proceeding. Its contents should include Dream Cap Mush, not merely an empty pitcher or unrelated liquid.

The exact yield depends on how much material is processed and the Blender's extraction loss.

Do not use every remaining Dream Cap on a small experimental batch when your next Cartel collection still needs more finished vials.

### Step 2: Add Alcohol and control the extraction temperature

Transfer the container of Dream Cap Mush to the **Heating Plate**.

The Chemistry 101 instructions recommend adding solvent Alcohol equal to **3–6 times the volume of Dream Cap Mush**.

For example, if the container holds 100 mL of mush, that corresponds to 300–600 mL of liquid Alcohol.

Use the actual liquid contents of the solvent rather than assuming that a bottle's total capacity is entirely Alcohol.

The Chemistry 101 manual gives a **40–60°C** extraction range, but staying around **40–49°C** helps preserve usable material. Unconverted Dream Cap Mush begins degrading **above 50°C**, while Dream Cap Extract begins degrading **above 60°C**. Watch the actual temperature and the Composition Spectrometer, then stop heating once the desired extract has formed.

![The Heating Plate shows an actual temperature of 52°C with heating switched off, while its target temperature remains set to 99°C.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-cap-heating-temperature.webp)

In the screenshot, the **target** is 99°C but the **actual temperature** is 52°C with the heater switched off. Do not confuse the target setting with the current reaction temperature; adjust or turn off the heater before the mixture overheats.

### Step 3: Filter the extract and evaporate the liquid

After the extraction stage, use **Kotton Fabric** to filter large contaminants from the mixture.

Filtering is optional in the basic recipe, but worthwhile when trying to improve the quality of a finished product.

Transfer the filtered mixture into the **Evaporator**.

Before ending the day:

1. Check that the mixture is inside a compatible liquid container.
2. Put the container into the Evaporator's **CONTAINER** slot.
3. Supply an **Energy Cell** to power the machine.
4. Confirm the machine is ready to operate overnight.

The starting Evaporator requires **6 Energy per day**. It removes water and alcohol, preserving the chemical material needed for the final product.

An Evaporator handles one connected batch container at a time. Having several mixtures ready does not mean they can all finish in the same machine overnight.

More importantly, **one night does not guarantee complete drying**.

Large batches or mixtures containing additional solvent may need multiple nights before the water and alcohol have disappeared.

Use the Composition Spectrometer to inspect what remains. Continue evaporation until the ingredient is sufficiently dry for the selected Chem Finisher recipe.

### Step 4: Select Dream Dust in the Chem Finisher

Move the dry extract to the **Chem Finisher**.

Open its control panel, choose **Configure**, and select **Vial of Dream Dust** from the available recipes.

The Chem Finisher requires **2.00 mL of processed material per Dream Dust vial**. That does not mean every vial contains 2 mL of pure Dream Cap Extract.

For example, a 56.50% Dream Dust vial contains **1.13 mL of Dream Cap Extract** within its 2.00 mL total volume. The remaining material includes physical contaminants, minerals, and protein. Improving the share of Dream Cap Extract increases actual purity.

Once the recipe is ready, press **Output** to produce the vials. Repeat while sufficient processed material remains.

The Chem Finisher can also package other chemical products, so confirm the selected recipe before pressing Output. Choosing Caffeine Pill while trying to package Dream Dust will not produce the item required by the Cartel.

Finally, inspect the finished vial using the Composition Spectrometer.

One opening batch produced **46.95% purity Dream Dust**.

![A completed Vial of Dream Dust showing 46.95% actual purity after processing through the Chem Finisher.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-4695-purity.webp)

That result exceeds the initial **40% Cartel requirement**, but it is not a particularly high purity grade for commercial sales.

This distinction matters: meeting the handler's minimum and producing a premium product are separate objectives.

## How Do You Increase Dream Dust Purity?

The fastest way to waste Dream Caps is to confuse the quantity of finished product with its actual purity.

A larger batch can produce more vials without improving their grade. A correctly selected Chem Finisher recipe can still produce weak merchandise when the earlier extraction or filtering stages introduced too many impurities.

A **46.95%** batch falls into **Cut Product**, so its base value receives the listed reduction. Purity and sale price are related but different: the buyer, market, and trading terms can further change what you receive.

The same starting equipment later produced **56.5% Dream Dust**, still within Cut Product but with a stronger actual purity result.

![A later Dream Dust batch showing 56.5% purity after closer temperature control and filtering.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-dream-dust-565-purity.webp)

This improvement matters for deliveries with higher minimum purity requirements. It does not automatically guarantee that the product has reached Street Standard or Pharma Grade.

### Which Production Stages Affect Purity?

**Blender extraction loss:** The starting machine loses part of the material being processed. Improving its extraction performance can help recover more useful product.

**Heating:** Excessive heat reduces usable extract yield. Follow the temperature and Composition Spectrometer checks in the Dream Dust extraction steps above.

**Filtering:** Removing large contaminants before evaporation reduces unwanted material entering the final processing stage.

**Chem Finisher:** Its chemistry upgrades can reduce the impurities introduced while packaging the product.

The Composition Spectrometer is useful at more than one point. Examine the heated mixture before committing it to overnight evaporation, then inspect the finished vials before deciding whether to deliver or sell them.

### Why Does Relabeling Dream Dust Not Improve It?

A Labeler changes the displayed product label. It does not raise the product's actual purity.

Some buyers carry a **Chem Scanner**, allowing them to compare the real composition with the label.

A low-quality vial renamed as a premium chemical remains low-quality merchandise. Trying to sell it at a higher claimed grade can create trading problems without improving the underlying product.

This also matters for the **Say My Name** achievement. The achievement requires producing a Lab Pure narcotic, not merely giving an ordinary vial that name. For the full unlock requirement, [check the achievement conditions](/probably-stolen/achievements).

## How Do You Get Lab Pure Chemicals and Remove Contaminants?

**Lab Pure starts at 99% purity.** An almost perfect extraction can still miss it if the Acid, Base, Lipobond, or Alcohol in the batch carries impurities. Inspect the ingredients **before** adding them, not just the finished pill or vial.

![A Composition Spectrometer reading of Lipobond Agent showing Heavy Metals, Microbes, Chemical Contaminants, and Physical Contaminants before purification.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-lipobond-reagent-contaminants.webp)

<GuideTable columns={chemistCleaningColumns} rows={chemistCleaningRows} />

Do not send an extract through a **Water Purifier** just because its quality is low. That machine processes water and can destroy the chemical you are trying to preserve. Reagent cleaning and water production use different tools.

### How Do You Remove Heavy Metals with Gutter Tilapia?

Heavy Metals cannot be removed with a normal Water Filter or UV Lamp. A Gutter Tilapia can clean them out of **liquid reagents** inside an Aquaculture System:

![An adult Gutter Tilapia in an Aquaculture System, showing its contaminant-cleaning ability and 750 mL tank-water requirement.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-gutter-tilapia-reagent-cleaning.webp)

1. Check the reagent using the Composition Spectrometer. If it contains Physical Contaminants or Microbes, clean those with a Water Filter or UV Lamp first.
2. Place a Gutter Tilapia in an Aquaculture System, then add the reagent mixture **with some water**. Fish require water to activate the cleaning effect, and the total liquid must meet the fish's current size requirement.
3. Provide food and allow the tank to run overnight. The fish reduces Heavy Metals and some other contaminants each night.
4. Check the tank and fish after each day. As the fish grows, its required liquid volume increases, so a tank that worked yesterday may show **Low Water** tomorrow.
5. When contamination is sufficiently low, remove the reagent and evaporate the added water. Scan it again before using it in a recipe.

An adult Gutter Tilapia can need around **750 mL** of tank liquid, increasing to around **1,000 mL** at the next growth stage. Those are size-specific observations, not a fixed amount for every fish. Cleaning is gradual, and the residual contamination depends on the actual water and reagent mixture.

![An Aquaculture System after reagent cleaning, showing 193 µL Heavy Metal and 1.59 mL Chemical Contaminant remaining in the tank mixture.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-gutter-tilapia-after-cleaning.webp)

### Which Chemical Equipment Modules Improve Purity?

The **Chem Finisher** adds contamination while packaging a dry product. Its Chemical Equipment Modules reduce that extra loss:

<GuideTable columns={chemistModuleColumns} rows={chemistModuleRows} />

![An unupgraded Chem Finisher showing 12% Contaminant Introduced, before Chemical Equipment Modules reduce the packaging contamination.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-finisher-base-contamination.webp)

With no modules, even a perfectly pure input is capped around **88%** after packaging. Four modules eliminate the Finisher's additional contamination, giving the best margin for reaching Lab Pure. The final grade still depends on the input composition: upgrading the Finisher does **not** remove Heavy Metals or excess Acid already inside the bottle.

Upgrade the machine that matches the bottleneck. Blender modules reduce input loss; Heating Plate modules improve control; Evaporator modules address time and solvent recovery. Use the scanner to distinguish a bad extraction from a clean mixture spoiled during finishing.

## Why Does the Chem Finisher Say Wet Ingredient Detected?

**Wet Ingredient Detected** means the input still contains too much volatile material. The Chem Finisher checks the **combined remaining Water, Alcohol, and Lipobond Agent**; in the documented game data, a combined total of **100 µL or less** passes its dryness check. Use the Composition Spectrometer to inspect the actual mixture, rather than assuming that only Water causes the warning.

This frequently happens when a container has spent one night in the Evaporator and the player assumes that processing is complete.

A Caffeine Pill attempt produced exactly this error after the first evaporation cycle.

Changing the recipe or moving the mixture between containers did not immediately solve the problem.

Before trying the Chem Finisher again, inspect the combined Water, Alcohol, and Lipobond Agent residue. If it exceeds the dryness limit, continue evaporation and rescan the mixture.

### How Do You Fix Wet Ingredient Detected?

Return the mixture to the Evaporator and inspect the following:

- **Power:** The Energy Cell must supply enough energy for overnight operation.
- **Container:** The mixture must be placed inside the machine's processing slot.
- **Remaining volatile material:** Check Water, Alcohol, and Lipobond Agent. A mixture can fail the dryness check even when it looks mostly processed.
- **Batch size:** A larger volume can take longer to dry with the starting Evaporator.

Run another night when necessary, then check the composition again before returning to the Chem Finisher.

One reaction can be chemically successful but still too wet to package. That is why repeating the Blender and Heating Plate stages immediately is usually the wrong response to this particular warning.

### What Does Not Enough Reagent Mean?

**Not Enough Reagent** usually means the Chem Finisher does not have enough remaining mixture to package another item using the selected recipe.

Check the required volume:

- Dream Dust needs **2 mL** per vial.
- Nightmare Dust needs **4 mL** per vial.
- Caffeine Pills need **5 mL** per pill.
- Phanta Pills need **0.5 mL** per pill.

Use the Composition Spectrometer to check the amount of mixture remaining in the container. Do not confuse the container's maximum capacity with the actual volume inside it.

The Chem Finisher can package a mixture that contains unwanted material, but this does not mean the resulting product will have good purity.

If the machine also shows **Wet Ingredient Detected**, remove the remaining volatile liquid before trying again. These warnings refer to different problems.

### Why Did Heating Produce Weak or Unusable Extract?

The Heating Plate is another common source of failed batches.

If the expected extract is missing after heating, check whether the mixture became too hot before the reaction completed. Additional evaporation cannot recover extract that was already destroyed.

A failed attempt was followed by the discovery that the player had heated the mixture too aggressively and skipped the recommended filtration.

The resulting low-quality product was discarded rather than reserved for the Cartel.

Check the temperature before turning the heater on and monitor the composition afterward. Stop heating when the expected extract has formed, then inspect the remaining composition before moving the mixture to the filters.

### Why Does a Finished Batch Still Have Poor Purity?

That is not necessarily a machine malfunction.

Consider the order of operations:

- Blending determines how much usable mush is recovered.
- Heating determines whether the desired extract forms without excessive degradation.
- Filtering removes large unwanted contaminants.
- Evaporation removes water and alcohol.
- The Chem Finisher packages the remaining chemical and may introduce additional impurities.

A low result after completing all five actions points toward extraction or contamination quality, not a missing output button.

A Composition Spectrometer reading taken before evaporation can help distinguish destroyed extract from a batch that simply needs more drying time.

## How Do You Make Nightmare Dust Without Destroying the Extract?

**Nightmare Dust uses Nightmare Cap Mush and produces Nightmare Extract, not Dream Cap Extract.** It follows the familiar Blender → Heating Plate → filters → Evaporator → Chem Finisher sequence, but the temperature margin is unforgiving.

1. Blend **Nightmare Caps** and transfer the mush into a container with enough Alcohol for extraction. Scan the actual Alcohol and mush volumes rather than guessing from bottle size.
2. Heat the mixture while watching its composition. Extraction begins near **40°C**; keeping it around **40–49°C** avoids the degradation that begins at 50°C for Nightmare Extract. Stop when the mush is converted.
3. Remove leftover Protein with a measured Acid or Base addition, checking the scanner between doses. Dilute the reagent first if a full drop would leave excess Acid or Base behind.
4. Filter large debris with **Kotton Fabric**, then use a **Water Filter** for finer contamination.
5. Evaporate the Alcohol and Water, then scan the dry **Nightmare Extract**.
6. Configure the **Chem Finisher** for **Vial of "Nightmare Dust"**. Each vial consumes **4 mL of dry mixture**. Check the grade on an actual finished vial before packaging the rest.

One high-purity run showed **99.63% Nightmare Extract** before finishing. That figure describes the extract **before** any extra Finisher contamination; the finished vial's grade still depends on modules and the other contents of the bottle.

![A finished Vial of Nightmare Dust in the Chem Finisher, with four Chemical Equipment Modules installed. The Lab Pure text is an item label rather than a tested purity result.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-nightmare-dust-finished.webp)

### Is Nightmare Dust Better to Sell than Dream Dust?

Nightmare Dust is a different, more restricted product. A strong per-item price does not automatically make it the best routine business: buyers, stocking the rarer caps, and the risk of overheating all affect real returns. **Never replace the Cartel's required Dream Dust with Nightmare Dust**; they are separate Finisher products.

## How Do You Make Caffeine Pills With Chemist Equipment?

Caffeine Pills start with **Coffee Beans**, but there are **two extraction routes**. Do not apply the hot-water temperature to the Alcohol route:

1. Blend Coffee Beans into mush and scan the result. Remove excess Fat with carefully measured **Lipobond Agent** before heating.
2. For the **Alcohol route**, add Alcohol and extract from about **50°C**, keeping below the point where Alcohol boils off (around 78°C). Watch the scanner until Coffee Bean Mush is gone.
3. Alternatively, a **hot-water extraction** begins around **80°C**. This is a different method with different yield, not a reason to heat an Alcohol batch to 80°C.
4. Remove remaining Protein with measured Acid or Base, then use Kotton Fabric and a Water Filter as needed.
5. Evaporate Water and Alcohol, and select **Caffeine Pill** in the Chem Finisher. Each pill consumes **5 mL of dry mixture**.

The Alcohol route is generally more useful for preserving Caffeine yield. Whichever route you choose, inspect the actual ingredient and the batch's dryness before finishing.

During one attempt, the player finished the heating and evaporation stages, selected Caffeine Pill, and immediately encountered the Wet Ingredient warning. The remaining solvent was the first issue to address.

If the Chem Finisher also reports **Not Enough Reagent**, check whether at least **5 mL of usable dry mixture** remains for another Caffeine Pill. This warning does not, by itself, prove that the Caffeine concentration is too low.

### Should you produce Caffeine Pills before the first Cartel delivery?

Usually not when Dream Dust ingredients are limited.

The handler wants Dream Dust specifically. Caffeine Pills do not substitute for its required quantity, even though both products use the same machinery.

Coffee Beans can be useful when extra equipment time is available or when Dream Caps are temporarily unavailable. They are less useful when the first weekly collection is approaching and the Dream Dust quota remains unfinished.

Keeping the two recipes separate prevents a valuable production night from being spent on an item that does not advance your immediate obligation.

## How Do You Make Phanta Pills and Avoid Failed Reactions?

Phanta uses **N-Methylol Base** and **Reducing Agent** from **Chemical Supplies (Restricted)**. It is not a mushroom or coffee extraction, and the Heating Plate needs an upgrade that can reach its reaction temperature.

1. Move N-Methylol Base into a container with enough space. Add **Acid** gradually to remove the **Stabilizing Agent** that blocks the reaction. Dilute the last drop if needed; extra Acid also lowers purity.
2. Filter the resulting debris, then neutralize leftover Acid with a carefully measured amount of Base.
3. Add **Reducing Agent** equal to approximately **75% of the remaining N-Methylol Base**. Use the Composition Spectrometer for the final adjustment rather than relying on rounded bottle volumes.
4. Set the Heating Plate to around **125°C** as a practical target. The Phanta reaction can occur from **110°C up to, but not including, 130°C**. Watch the actual temperature and the remaining N-Methylol Base with the Composition Spectrometer. Stop heating when the reaction finishes; Phanta begins degrading at 130°C.
5. Add some Water and use a **Water Purification Tablet** to reduce Chemical Contaminants. Allow its reaction to finish, then evaporate the Water and Alcohol.
6. Configure the **Chem Finisher** for **Phanta Pill**. Each pill consumes **0.5 mL of dry mixture**. Scan the first finished pill before producing the rest.

Check the reagent ratio before heating. In this example, the scanner shows approximately **120.67 mL N-Methylol Base** and **86.96 mL Reducing Agent**—about **72%**, slightly below the intended 75% starting ratio.

![A Phanta preparation mixture showing N-Methylol Base and Reducing Agent quantities before heating; the displayed ratio is slightly below 75%.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-phanta-reducing-agent.webp)

The Heating Plate screenshot shows a **125°C target**, while the **actual temperature is still 32°C**. It documents the setup, not an already completed reaction.

![An upgraded Heating Plate set to a 125°C target for Phanta preparation, with the actual temperature still at 32°C.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-phanta-heating.webp)

The reaction also works below 120°C, but progresses more slowly; a target near 125°C gives faster synthesis without reaching the 130°C degradation threshold.

In one production run, the prepared Phanta extract reached **90.5% purity** before packaging. The Chem Finisher then produced **24 Phanta Pills**. A tested finished pill showed **89.60% purity (Near Pharma)**, with Heavy Metals and residual reagents still present. This is a successful production result, but not a Lab Pure batch.

Always scan the finished pill rather than assuming its purity matches the extract placed into the Chem Finisher.

![A finished Phanta Pill tested at 89.60% Near Pharma purity, with Heavy Metals, residual reagents, and Toxic toxicity shown in the Composition Spectrometer.](/images/probably-stolen/chemist-phanta-finished-pills.webp)

### Why Can High-Purity Phanta Cause Customer Complaints?

A higher purity grade does not automatically make Phanta safer to sell. In one tested batch, an **89.60% Near Pharma Phanta Pill** was explicitly marked **Toxicity: Toxic**.

High-potency Phanta can cause serious customer reactions, complaints, and compensation demands. Check the actual toxicity shown on the finished item before selling it, especially when preparing multiple pills from the same concentrated batch.

Do not assume that reaching Pharma or Lab Pure makes Phanta a better routine retail product.

## How Do You Complete the First Cartel Dream Dust Delivery?

On **Day 1**, the **Cartel Handler** visits the Chemist shop and assigns the first quota.

His requirements are:

- **10 Dream Dust**
- **At least 40% average purity across the delivered vials**
- **Collection the following week**

The handler supplies starting materials and warns that the quota must be completed.

The collection is not a normal customer purchase. The products must be handed over to the Cartel, and the handler evaluates whether the delivery meets the specified requirements.

### What should you reserve for the first collection?

Keep **at least 10 finished Dream Dust vials** available before selling surplus to ordinary customers. The handler checks the **average purity of the handover**, not whether each vial individually crosses the target. Do not use this as an excuse to deliver an extremely diluted batch: the purity still affects the outcome and Cartel reputation.

This matters because Dream Dust can attract buyers before the handler returns. Selling a few vials may bring immediate Credits, but that does not help if the same vials were needed to fulfill the quota.

One early batch yielded nine finished vials. Although those products were usable, nine was still one short of the required ten.

The correct next task was to finish another qualifying batch, not to sell the existing stock and hope that the handler would accept a smaller delivery.

Check **count and average purity separately**. Put only the vials intended for the delivery on the counter, because the handler takes every Dream Dust vial there. A mislabeled item has not become more pure.

### What Happens When You Deliver All 10?

In one Chemist run, handing over 10 Dream Dust completed the first collection and improved Cartel reputation. The next quota became **15 Dream Dust at 45% average purity**. Later weekly demands keep increasing in quantity, while the target purity eventually stops rising at **70%**.

A vial at **46.95% purity** can contribute to that next average, but the shop still needs 15 total vials. Individual purity above the target does not replace missing items.

### Why Did the Second Delivery Fail With 13 Dream Dust?

At the next collection in that run, the handler received only **13 of the required 15 vials**. He took the offered Dust and the shop lost Cartel reputation for the shortfall.

That failure was a **quantity problem**, not proof that every vial needed to meet 45% on its own. Hold enough stock for the next count, then calculate the batch's average purity before committing the handover.

[Cartel reputation penalties](/probably-stolen/factions) become increasingly dangerous after repeated failed deliveries.

<section id="chemist-resources">

## What Should You Buy or Upgrade First as a Chemist?

The first bottleneck is usually **usable ingredients and complete drying**, not the number of machines in the shop.

A starting workshop can already produce Dream Dust that satisfies the initial average-purity target. Spending heavily on production upgrades before learning the extraction and evaporation sequence can leave the business with expensive equipment but too little cash to purchase Dream Caps or Alcohol.

### Priority 1: Dream Caps and liquid Alcohol

Dream Caps provide the extract needed for Dream Dust, while Alcohol is the solvent used during extraction.

The Chemistry 101 manual explains that Chemical Supplies can be unpacked into materials useful for crafting. Suppliers and relevant customers can also provide chemistry ingredients.

Keep enough Dream Caps and solvent for the next batch before selling these items as ordinary merchandise.

A shortage of Dream Caps stops the Blender stage. A shortage of Alcohol can compromise extraction or force the player into an inferior workaround. Neither problem is solved by purchasing another Chem Finisher.

### Priority 2: Enough Energy for overnight evaporation

An Evaporator that cannot operate overnight delays the entire production chain.

With the starting requirement of **6 Energy per day**, check the Energy Cell before sleeping. A large mixture can need another overnight cycle, so budget for more than one day when processing heavily diluted batches.

Do not move a wet container to the Chem Finisher simply because the scheduled delivery is approaching. It will be rejected.

### Priority 3: Better extraction and temperature control

Once ingredient supplies and rent are stable, upgrades become more attractive.

The **Blender** benefits from improvements to extraction yield. The **Heating Plate** can be upgraded for better temperature control and heating performance.

The **Chem Finisher** benefits from modules that reduce impurities introduced during final processing; use the module table above to see how each upgrade changes the loss.

Most of these chemistry machines use special **Chemical Equipment Modules**, rather than the ordinary upgrades used by general-purpose equipment.

The **Evaporator** uses normal modules. Its improvements can affect energy efficiency and the amount of liquid processed each night.

A useful equipment choice depends on the stage causing the actual delay:

- Losing too much valuable material? Improve extraction.
- Burning Dream Cap Extract? Improve heating control.
- Waiting multiple nights for every batch? Improve evaporation throughput.
- Finishing below the required grade despite adequate extraction? Inspect filtering and the Chem Finisher's quality-related penalties.

Buying the most expensive available module without checking where the batch is failing is unlikely to be efficient.

### Priority 4: Storage and an additional Evaporator

A larger chemical operation quickly fills the starting inventory with containers, raw ingredients, finished vials, and large machines.

The **Chemist Storage Bay** is useful when equipment and bottles are obstructing normal inventory management.

An additional Evaporator becomes attractive when you regularly prepare more than one mixture and the first machine is occupied overnight.

Neither upgrade should take priority over an imminent rent payment or a shortage of the materials needed to satisfy the handler.

During one Thin Margins run, an expanded chemical storage option was considered at **400 Credits**. That was a meaningful commitment for a shop that also needed supplies and had a weekly collection approaching.

The practical goal is to produce enough Dream Dust at a reliable average purity, not simply to own more machines.

</section>

<section id="chemist-selling">

## Should You Sell Dream Dust or Save It for the Cartel?

The decision depends on the next collection date, how many Dream Dust vials are reserved, and their average purity.

Dream Dust is a **Contraband** product. Some customers are willing to buy it, but ordinary traders may reject it, and buyers with chemical scanners can identify mislabeled purity.

A vial also sells for less when its actual grade is poor. At **Cut Product**, the base value is reduced by **45%** before other trading conditions.

That can make low-quality sales much less profitable than expected, particularly when production consumed valuable Dream Caps, Alcohol, Energy, and several days of machine time.

### When is selling surplus reasonable?

Once the upcoming Cartel quantity and expected **average purity** are covered, surplus vials can be considered for sale.

Check three things before accepting the offer:

1. The buyer actually accepts the product's category and grade.
2. The price is worthwhile after the purity modifier and any discount.
3. The remaining Dream Dust still meets the next Cartel **quantity and average-purity** requirement.

A mixed-purity batch can meet the average target, but delivering a low overall average can still cost reputation or end the run. Avoid unnecessary dilution when you cannot confirm the result.

The shop also needs ordinary cash flow for rent, tools, batteries, and replacement supplies. A legal trading activity can help cover those costs without using the stock reserved for Cartel deliveries.

### How do Security inspections affect Chemist inventory?

Dream Dust is Contraband. Keeping it exposed can create inspection risk, while illegal sales can contribute to the criminal evidence recorded against your shop. These are separate problems: passing an inspection does not erase an existing investigation.

A Chemist can meet the Cartel's quota and still face Security penalties. When your Security Record begins accumulating offenses, [check investigation progress and Wild's Fixers options](/probably-stolen/security-investigation) before deciding how much more Dream Dust to sell.

During a difficult run, the player struggled with both falling Security standing and the Cartel's production demands. Later inspections and financial pressure interfered with the ability to restock ingredients.

The business was not short of production machinery at that point. It was short of the cash, materials, and safe operating conditions needed to keep producing.

That is why a Chemist should treat inventory concealment, rent, and chemical supply as parts of the same operating decision, rather than focusing entirely on the highest possible purity.

</section>

<section id="chemist-profit">

## Which Chemistry Product Should You Make After Dream Dust?

Once your next Cartel delivery is secure, choose another product based on the materials you can obtain, the equipment you have upgraded, and whether a suitable buyer will accept it.

- **Dream Dust:** A reliable choice when Dream Caps are available and you can maintain enough qualifying vials for the Cartel.
- **Nightmare Dust:** Requires Nightmare Caps and tighter temperature control. Its Poison and Contraband classifications limit where it can be sold.
- **Caffeine Pills:** Useful when you have Coffee Beans and spare production capacity, without consuming Dream Caps reserved for the Cartel.
- **Phanta Pills:** Require Restricted Chemical Supplies, an upgraded Heating Plate, and careful reagent preparation. Higher purity also creates a significant toxicity risk.

A higher-priced finished product is not always the better business. Failed batches, restricted buyers, slower production, and ingredient shortages can erase an apparent price advantage.

For a calculation using your current purchase prices, production costs, expected sales, and rent deadline, use the [Money-Making Planner](/probably-stolen/money-making).

<section id="chemist-next-week">

## How Do You Keep Up With Later Cartel Quotas?

The Cartel keeps increasing its weekly delivery requirements. After the first two collections, the targets continue rising:

- **Day 22:** 20 Dream Dust at 50% average purity.
- **Day 29:** 25 Dream Dust at 55% average purity.
- **Day 36:** 30 Dream Dust at 60% average purity.
- **Day 43:** 35 Dream Dust at 65% average purity.
- **Day 50:** 40 Dream Dust at 70% average purity.

The required purity stops increasing at 70%, but the number of vials continues to grow.

Plan backward from the next collection. If your Evaporator can finish only one container overnight, adding more ingredients to the Blender will not solve that bottleneck. Increase drying capacity when prepared extracts repeatedly wait for an available machine.

Reserve enough qualifying Dream Dust for the next handover before using the remaining stock for customers. Once production is stable, spend your additional capacity on better grades, alternative chemicals, or more profitable sales.
