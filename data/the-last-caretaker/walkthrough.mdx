<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Restore the starting dock and Beacon 49, then repair Lazarus, retrieve a Human Seed from Prometheus, grow your first Human, and deliver them to Exodus. For Update 6, return to Lazarus, investigate Project Crossing, scan and fabricate a Continuity Token, charge the rooftop Transfer Gate, and travel to North Bastion. Get the first heating system running before exploring the frozen complex.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="starting-dock-beacon-49">
  Power the Starting Dock and Activate Beacon 49
</h2>

The first objective is getting the ship ready to leave, not dismantling everything around the starting dock. The dock outlet and upper-deck power objectives are connected: restore the electrical route through the available batteries, connectors, and cables until power reaches the required equipment upstairs.

The wording around the dock outlet can be misleading. You are not searching for a special item to pick up. Check the actual cable connections and whether power is reaching the upper deck. Carry some spare cable before leaving; later facilities repeatedly require connections that are inconvenient to finish with an empty inventory.

Once the ship has dependable power and enough fuel to travel, head for **Navigational Beacon 49**. The beacon needs its electrical supply restored before its navigation relay can operate. Repair the necessary connections, charge the relay, and activate it to reveal the **Lazarus Complex**.

At this point, I prioritize upgrades that make repairs, carrying supplies, and keeping the ship powered easier. There is little reason to spend everything immediately if the ship already handles the journey comfortably.

Before sailing to Lazarus, make sure the beacon has actually been activated. Restoring power without completing the terminal interaction can leave you without the destination you need.

<h2 id="lazarus-complex">
  Restore Lazarus and Open the Hall of Humanity
</h2>

Lazarus is the first major facility where repairing an individual machine is not enough. Several systems need power before the Human Seed objective becomes available.

Work through the facility in this order:

1. Restore the main electrical supply and repair the required power connections.
2. Follow the power route upward toward the upper facility systems.
3. Open the Hall of Humanity once the required equipment is online.
4. Repair and reactivate the Lazarus Rack.
5. Continue until the objective directs you toward the northern Human Seed Vault.

If the Hall of Humanity remains inaccessible, check the facility's main power route before searching for another entrance. A door that has no power will not become usable just because the surrounding rooms have been explored.

Keep a reserve of basic building materials on the ship. One of the Lazarus repair points requires **plastic, carbon fiber, and titanium**. If the necessary material is missing, returning to your stored supplies is usually more useful than searching unrelated rooms.

When the Lazarus Rack is functioning and the northern Seed Vault objective appears, you can leave. The facility contains additional resources, but collecting all of them is not required before retrieving your first Human Seed.

<h2 id="seed-vault-prometheus">
  Retrieve a Human Seed from Seed Vault Prometheus
</h2>

**Seed Vault Prometheus** lies north of Lazarus. Before entering, park where you can return to the ship without a long detour, make a save, and leave enough carrying capacity for the Seed.

The important distinction inside Prometheus is whether you are still exploring or already transporting a Human Seed.

The first Human Seed I carried had a **two-hour survival window**, with approximately **1 hour 33 minutes** remaining during the return to Lazarus. The available time makes excessive looting a genuine risk. Collect what you need before taking the Seed, then concentrate on getting it safely back to the rack.

The return sequence is:

1. Reach the Seed chamber and collect one Human Seed in its transport pod.
2. Leave additional scavenging for another trip.
3. Return to the ship without overloading yourself.
4. Sail back to Lazarus and carry the pod to the functional rack.
5. Install the Seed before its remaining survival time expires.

Carrying too much biomaterial or scrap can slow the journey considerably. The first Seed is worth more than another inventory of salvage if the timer is already running.

<h2 id="human-seed-lazarus">
  Fix Human Seed Upkeep at Lazarus
</h2>

Returning the Seed does not automatically make the growth process safe. The Lazarus Rack needs a working supply of fresh water, food, memories, and electricity.

  />
  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Check the rack's upkeep warnings before starting the growth cycle.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Fresh water is an especially important early check. My first Seed reached Lazarus safely, but the rack still showed an upkeep warning because the support system was not ready.

Read the rack's requirements rather than assuming the problem is electrical. More batteries will not correct missing food, memories, or fresh water. The Soil Forge and Food Processor help supply the nutritional inputs, while the memory inputs influence the Human's development.

Once those systems are stable, the rack also evaluates growth characteristics such as weight, height, and life expectancy. For the first Human, concentrate on meeting the required minimums and completing a successful growth cycle. More specialized combinations become important when you are preparing Humans for particular professions or Council roles.

For the equipment and attribute requirements involved in later growth, see <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/human-growth-professions-guide/">Human Growth and Professions</Link>.

<h2 id="grow-first-human">
  Grow Your First Human and Prepare for Exodus
</h2>

With the necessary water, food, and memory inputs available, initialize growth at the Lazarus Rack and wait for the cycle to finish.

Do not hold up the first Human indefinitely while trying to maximize every attribute. A Human who meets the requirements can advance the journey, while an unfinished growth cycle leaves the Exodus objective waiting.

Once growth finishes, the objective directs you to **Exodus Station, southeast of Lazarus**.

Human transport is less convenient when the ship is already crowded with machinery and storage. Finishing those preparations at Lazarus avoids having to rearrange the deck during the journey.

<h2 id="exodus">

<h2 id="later-progression">
  Choose Your Next Major Expedition
</h2>

After Lazarus and Exodus, several substantial objectives become available across the existing world. They do not all demand the same preparation, and it is easier to make progress when you finish one expedition before outfitting the ship for another.

- **Project Eden at Pishon:** Construction and facility restoration require a significant material supply. Follow <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/project-eden-guide/">Project Eden</Link> for its objectives and construction requirements.
- **Oil Whale:** If your available fuel or oil supply is restricting further travel and construction, investigate the <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/oil-whale-guide/">Oil Whale</Link>.
- **Research Outpost Theta 9:** Prepare for an underwater expedition rather than treating it as another ordinary surface platform. The objectives are covered in <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/research-outpost-theta-9-guide/">Research Outpost Theta 9</Link>.
- **New Horizons locations:** For the larger destinations introduced during Update 5, see <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/new-horizons-guide/">New Horizons</Link>.

I prefer returning to the ship with a completed objective before switching to another large construction project. It keeps the inventory manageable and reduces the chance of consuming essential materials on unrelated upgrades.

Update 6 also introduces a separate journey through Project Crossing, beginning at Lazarus.

<h2 id="project-crossing">
  Start Project Crossing at Lazarus in Update 6
</h2>

Project Crossing uses the large structure on the roof of the **Lazarus Complex**. That structure is a **Transfer Gate**, and its controls are not limited to the equipment standing on the rooftop.

Start by returning to Lazarus and checking the control-room area near the main elevator, above the main entrance. Look for the room containing the Project Crossing records and the Continuity Token.

The sequence is:

1. Enter the Project Crossing control room and examine the equipment.
2. Scan the **Continuity Token** to learn how to reproduce it.
3. Use a Fabricator to manufacture a token.
4. Return to the Transfer Gate on the roof.
5. Repair the required gate equipment and install the manufactured token.
6. Connect an electrical supply and charge the gate before selecting a destination.

The token in the control room is not something you simply remove and carry to the rooftop. Scanning it provides what you need to manufacture another one. Check the Fabricator's requirements before leaving the room. **Processed Quantum Crystals** are among the materials that can hold up this step, so prepare them before returning to the gate.

The token and electricity solve different requirements. Installing the token allows the gate system to respond, but the transfer equipment still needs to be charged.

<h2 id="transfer-gate-power">
  Charge the Transfer Gate and Select North Bastion
</h2>

The rooftop Transfer Gate consumes a substantial amount of stored electricity. A facility that seems adequately powered for ordinary equipment can still charge the gate very slowly, particularly if batteries are already serving other machinery.

Connect the gate's electrical inputs to your available power network and watch the charge indicator. The gate has multiple power inputs, making it possible to improve charging throughput by supplying additional connections. Battery storage and generation both matter: adding power sources without checking how the network is connected may leave the gate charging much more slowly than expected.

For electrical routing, storage, and outlet problems, see <Link href="/the-last-caretaker/power-fuel-wall-outlets/">Power, Fuel, and Wall Outlets</Link>.

Once the gate has sufficient charge:

1. Open the Transfer Gate interface.
2. Use the destination directory to find **North Bastion**.
3. Select the destination and confirm the address.
4. Activate the gate.
5. Move your equipment through and enter before the opening closes.

**The charge level affects how long the gate remains open.** It does not stay open indefinitely just because the token has been accepted. Check the displayed opening time before activating it, especially if you plan to transfer several containers.

<GuideCallout title="If the Transfer Gate will not activate">
If the interface asks for a Continuity Token, return to the Project Crossing room, scan the original token, and manufacture one. If the token is accepted but the gate will not charge, inspect the electrical inputs and available battery power. If activation reports an unreachable destination, select North Bastion again from the directory and check the destination entry before confirming.
</GuideCallout>

The transfer is easier when supplies are packed in advance. Bringing everything to the rooftop only after the gate has opened wastes the available travel time.

<h2 id="north-bastion-arrival">
  Reach North Bastion and Restore Heat First
</h2>

North Bastion is a frozen region with a different survival problem from the earlier ocean facilities. Low temperatures damage your systems and can reduce movement speed. Walking into the snow without establishing a source of heat quickly turns exploration into a race against your remaining health.

The first priority after crossing the gate is the nearby shelter and its heating equipment, not the distant mountain.

The initial area contains a **Boiler** and heaters. The boiler produces the steam needed to operate the heating system, but it requires fuel and water to function.

Get the first system running in this order:

1. Locate the Boiler and nearby heater connections.
2. Supply the Boiler with **Methane** and **Fresh Water**.
3. Connect its steam output to the heater using the appropriate connection.
4. Activate the equipment and check that it produces heat.
5. Keep the heating system operating before leaving the shelter for a longer expedition.

The heating system also creates an immediate fuel-supply problem. There is a **Methane Extractor** outside that can extract gas trapped beneath the ice, but it requires an electrical connection. Fresh water and the associated pumps must also be supplied correctly.

A working boiler with an empty supply line will not provide lasting protection. If the heat disappears, check whether the methane supply, water supply, and steam connection are still functional before replacing the heater.

Close exterior doors when moving in and out of heated rooms. Leaving them open allows the cold to affect the interior and can undo the protection you just established.

Do not confuse a steam connection with an ordinary electrical outlet or water pipe. The new equipment uses different inputs, and having power available nearby does not mean steam is reaching the heater.

<h2 id="north-bastion-laboratory">
  Enter North Bastion and Search for the Laboratory
</h2>

After establishing a heated area, the next objective directs you toward the mountain to the north. The settlement inside is **North Bastion**, a large complex with corridors, frozen access points, and damaged infrastructure.

Before leaving the initial shelter, check that you have:

- A charged backpack and enough battery capacity for travel.
- Spare healing supplies for exposure to the cold.
- Basic repair materials, cables, and useful power equipment.
- Room to carry additional equipment without becoming badly overloaded.

The new carrying system can also help with transporting supplies. When holding a suitable object, use the on-screen back-carry interaction to secure it rather than relying only on ordinary backpack space.

Approach the mountain complex and look for an accessible entrance. Some doors or passages can be blocked by ice. If a doorway appears unusable, inspect it for ice before assuming the route requires an unrelated key or terminal.

Inside North Bastion, the objective develops toward a **Laboratory deeper in the complex**. Anna comments on the unfamiliar surroundings and the possibility of something recognizable farther inside.

Treat the heated rooms and restored equipment as safe points during exploration. Repairing a useful power source or opening access to another sheltered area can be more valuable than immediately pushing into the next freezing corridor.

The cold remains dangerous inside unheated sections. If your movement becomes restricted or health starts falling, return to a heated space, restore your systems, and check your supply situation before continuing.

You may also need to restore power to damaged equipment along the way. Carrying spare cables and the materials needed to repair local electrical connections reduces the number of trips back to the first shelter.

<h2 id="progression-rule">
  Where to Go When the Next Objective Is Unclear
</h2>

The Last Caretaker frequently advances the story through a repaired system rather than through exploration alone. Reaching a location is only part of the task; the destination often contains a relay, rack, terminal, or other piece of equipment that must be activated.

If progression appears to stop, check the last required interaction:

- At **Beacon 49**, make sure the navigation relay has been activated after restoring power.
- At **Lazarus**, check the Hall of Humanity and Lazarus Rack before leaving for Prometheus.
- At **Prometheus**, take the Human Seed back to a working Lazarus Rack before its timer expires.
- After **Human Growth**, carry the completed Human to Exodus Station.
- At **Project Crossing**, manufacture and install the Continuity Token, then charge the Transfer Gate.
- At **North Bastion**, restore a working heat supply before following the objective into the frozen settlement.

A powered room, a repaired machine, and a completed objective are not always the same thing. Check the terminal or mission prompt after major repairs; it can reveal the next destination even when the surrounding area still contains unfinished equipment.
