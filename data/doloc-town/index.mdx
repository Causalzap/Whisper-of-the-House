Doloc Town becomes much easier once I stop trying to solve every system at the same
time. The farm, story, weather, money, electricity, genetics, fishing, livestock,
and achievement cleanup all open at different points, and pushing the wrong system
too early is one of the easiest ways to burn Gold, Copper, or an entire in-game day.

I use this hub as a **decision page**, not another walkthrough and not a wiki.

If I am stuck on the story, I go to the progression guide. If the Environmental
Modifier itself is the blocker, I use its dedicated solution. If I am broke, I use
the Money Guide. If I am trying to catch a fish, I use the Fishing Guide; if I am
trying to breed a variant, I use Fish Breeding. The same Owner rule applies to
weather, automation, genes, animals, and achievements.

<h2 id="start-with-progression">
  Start with progression when I am unsure which system matters next
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

When several quests and tech nodes open together, I do not choose the newest icon on
the screen. I ask a simpler question:

> **What is actually blocking the next useful part of the game?**

If the answer is a region, story objective, movement upgrade, blocked route, or
late-game handoff, I start with the <Link href="/doloc-town/walkthrough" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Doloc Town Walkthrough and Progression Guide</Link>.

That guide owns the main route from the opening farm through River Valley, Treaty
Port, the Wetlands, Old City, and the later Environmental Modifier stages. I do not
repeat that route here.

My practical rule is to keep progressing until the game turns the next objective
into a **specific problem**. That is the moment I leave the walkthrough and use the
dedicated Owner Page.

<h2 id="environmental-modifier">
  Use the Environmental Modifier page when the story turns into a machine problem
</h2>

The first major example is the Environmental Modifier.

If I know the story wants me to work on Eden technology but I am confused by the
Core Extractor, Core Sample, dead terminal, rear-panel repair, battery type, or Phase
1 activation, I stop using the broad walkthrough and switch to the <Link href="/doloc-town/environmental-modifier" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Environmental Modifier Guide</Link>.

That page owns the exact machine sequence.

I only return to the walkthrough after the Modifier stage is complete and the story
hands me back to regional progression.

<h2 id="money-and-farm">
  Separate money problems from crop-profit problems
</h2>

The farm can look unprofitable for two completely different reasons:

- I am spending Gold badly;
- or I am scaling the wrong crop or sell method.

I do not solve those with the same page.

### If I am broke or unsure what to buy first

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/money-guide" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Doloc Town Money Guide</Link>.

That page owns the practical Gold decisions: when the first **500G** bag is worth
buying, why the **4,000G** bag is a different decision, when I save **1,500G** for
the Copper Pickaxe, when I sell or recycle scrap, what I refuse to liquidate, and
how I recover after spending down to almost zero.

I do not move those numbers into the walkthrough or crop page just because they are
connected to progression.

### If I am asking what to plant

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/best-crops" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Best Crops and Profit Calculator</Link>.

That page owns crop economics, climate filters, genes, direct sale versus processing,
profit per day, profit per tile, seed cost, and the question of whether an expensive
crop is actually worth scaling.

The distinction is:

> **Money Guide = protect and allocate the wallet. Best Crops = decide what farm production deserves that wallet.**

<h2 id="weather-and-automation">
  Protect the farm first, then automate the chore that is actually stealing time
</h2>

### If I am asking whether an outdoor crop will survive

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/acid-rain" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Acid Rain and Crop Protection Guide</Link>.

That page owns End Crop resistance, Plastic Mulch, Weather Station planning,
severe-weather risk, exposed electrical equipment, and the point where I move
valuable production indoors.

The crop calculator can tell me what is attractive to grow. The weather page tells
me whether that plan is safe to leave outside.

### If the farm is consuming too much of my day

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/farming-automation" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Farming Automation Guide</Link>.

That page owns the path from Manual Sprinkler to generators, Automatic Sprinklers,
the Automation Workbench, Drone Stations, Farming Drones, and specialized drones.

My threshold is simple:

> **I automate the repeated chore that is already costing me time. I do not build advanced machinery only because the technology is visible.**

<h2 id="advanced-farm-systems">
  Add genetics only after the basic farm and power grid are working
</h2>

Crop genetics is a separate investment decision.

When I reach **Gene 101**, I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/crop-genetics" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Crop Genetics Guide</Link> for Gene Activation, the Archive Attic handoff, gene seeds, extraction, duplication, Gene Capsules, and multi-gene progression.

I do not treat genes as the fix for a weak early farm.

If electricity is unreliable, important tools still need Copper, or I do not yet
know which crop I want to scale, I solve those problems before turning genetics into
a major project.

The <Link href="/doloc-town/best-crops" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Best Crops and Profit Calculator</Link> continues to own the economic question. Genetics owns **how I create, preserve, and combine the gene line**.

<h2 id="fishing">
  Use one page to catch the fish and another page to breed it
</h2>

Fishing and fish breeding are related, but they answer very different player
problems.

### If I cannot catch the fish

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/fishing-guide" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Doloc Town Fishing Guide</Link>.

That page owns:

- getting the free Old Fishing Rod;
- cast distance;
- lift timing;
- Fishing Meter hold/release control;
- when the yellow extra-reward windows are worth chasing;
- rod upgrade decisions;
- fishing RNG;
- weather and water-type clues;
- Salmon Fest;
- and catch troubleshooting.

I do not put a giant fish-breeding table into that page. The catching problem ends
when the fish is in my inventory.

### If I have the fish but need a variant

I move to the <Link href="/doloc-town/fish-breeding" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Fish Breeding Guide</Link>.

That page owns Fish Tanks, Fish Roe, the Fish Incubator, parent ratios, special
variants, Large Fish Tank decisions, and the route toward Abundant Collection.

The handoff is simple:

> **Fishing owns the catch. Fish Breeding owns what I do with the parents after the catch.**

<h2 id="animals">
  Expand livestock only after feed, space, and the first production loop are stable
</h2>

For the Small Livestock Shed, Feeding Trough, feed, first Stilttail Pheasant, first
Egg, animal purchases, Breeding Room, manure, and livestock troubleshooting, I use
the <Link href="/doloc-town/animals" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Animals and Livestock Guide</Link>.

I do not buy another species simply because it appears in the shop.

My first check is whether the current animals already have:

- enough space;
- reliable feed;
- a useful output loop;
- and enough budget left that another animal does not delay a more important
  progression purchase.

That keeps livestock as a production system instead of turning it into an expensive
collection project too early.

<h2 id="friendship-and-small-topics">
  Keep friendship, gifts, cooking, combat, and small resource questions on the hub for now
</h2>

Not every useful Doloc Town topic needs its own URL.

### Friendship and gifts

I currently treat friendship as something I stack with normal town routes rather
than a separate grind. I talk to people when I am already passing through their
area, and I avoid throwing scarce quest or industrial materials at an NPC just to
test a gift.

I am **not** publishing a giant favorite-gift table until I have enough verified
preferences and real search demand to justify it.

If GSC starts showing a meaningful family of searches such as `Doloc Town gifts`,
`best gifts`, or repeated NPC-name gift queries, that is when a dedicated
friendship/gift Owner Page becomes justified.

### Cooking

Cooking currently stays a supporting system. The achievement page owns cooking
achievement requirements; the Money Guide can own a processing-versus-sale
decision when it matters economically.

I do not need a separate recipe wiki page just because many recipes exist.

### Combat Drone and combat questions

The Combat Drone unlock is part of progression, so the broad handoff stays with the
Walkthrough. I would only split a separate combat or drone page if players begin
searching for a real blocker such as a build, upgrade path, boss interaction, or
specific troubleshooting problem.

### Individual resources, tools, and regions

A single material, one basic tool, or one region name does not automatically need a
spoke.

If the answer is only "where this fits in progression," the Walkthrough or relevant
system guide can absorb it. If the query later develops a repeatable route,
location problem, decision tree, or meaningful GSC impressions, then it can become
an Owner Page.

<h2 id="completion">
  Leave full achievement cleanup until progression has opened the systems it needs
</h2>

Doloc Town has **80 achievements** in the set I am tracking, covering story
progress, farming, fishing, animals, relationships, factions, festivals,
automation, hidden actions, and long counters.

I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/achievements" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Doloc Town Achievements Guide</Link> as the Owner Page for requirements, hidden triggers, completion percentages, and 100% cleanup order.

I do not use that page to relearn a full system.

If an achievement sends me into genetics, fish breeding, livestock, automation,
fishing, or the main story, I follow the link back to the system guide and let that
page own the actual mechanics.

That keeps achievement cleanup from duplicating the rest of the cluster.

<h2 id="priority-order">
  The order I use when several Doloc Town systems compete for resources
</h2>

When my quest log, tech tree, wallet, and farm all want something at once, I use
this priority:

1. **Main-story blocker** — open the next region, movement option, or required story system.
2. **Farm survival problem** — stop crops from dying and keep basic production working.
3. **Required cash reserve** — fund the next progression purchase before optional spending.
4. **Repeat chore that is costing real time** — automate it when the savings justify the materials.
5. **Reliable production loop** — scale crops or processing I can actually support.
6. **Optional production systems** — livestock, fish breeding, and genetics once I can feed or power them comfortably.
7. **Completion work** — achievements, rare variants, relationship cleanup, and large-scale automation after the systems are already open.

This is not a mandatory game sequence. It is the order that prevents one optional
upgrade from creating a new blocker somewhere else.

For example, I do not spend the last important material on an optional machine when
the story is already asking for the same tier of resource. I also do not buy an
expensive animal if the shed is not staying fed, or build a genetics setup while the
farm still has a basic electricity problem.

<h2 id="coverage-boundary">
  What I am not splitting into another page yet
</h2>

At this point I stop adding spokes just because Doloc Town contains more systems,
NPCs, regions, recipes, items, or collectibles.

The working rule is now:

> **keep the short answer on the hub first; create a new URL only after the search demand proves it deserves one.**

I split another Owner Page only when at least one of these becomes true:

- GSC starts showing meaningful impressions for a query family the current owner cannot satisfy cleanly;
- the answer needs a route, tool, decision tree, or troubleshooting flow large enough to bloat the hub;
- players repeatedly hit the same concrete blocker;
- or I have enough verified material to make the new page substantially better than a short hub answer.

Until that happens, the **11 spokes above plus this hub** are the working Doloc Town
cluster.

That gives me a much cleaner expansion rule than trying to predict every possible
wiki page before the searches exist:

> **search demand first, new URL second.**
