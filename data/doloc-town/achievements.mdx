Doloc Town currently has **80 achievements** in the achievement set I am tracking.
I do not try to grind all of them as soon as they appear. A large share are tied to
normal story progress, money, farming, fishing, relationships, factions, festivals,
and late-game systems that become much easier if I leave them until the right stage.

My completion order is:

**main story first → one-off hidden actions while I am already nearby → farming,
fishing, animals, and research → relationships and factions → automation and
late-game cleanup → long counters such as 1,000,000G, 50 treasure chests, and 20
automated drones.**

<h2 id="story-achievements">
  Let the story achievements unlock before I start hunting secrets
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

The easiest way to make achievement cleanup miserable is to stop the main story
every few minutes and chase a requirement that the next region would have unlocked
naturally.

I leave these to normal progression:

- **Beyond Their Grasp** — unlock double jump.
- **A Flash of Light** — unlock sprint.
- **Doloc, Our New Home** — read the sign at the top of River Valley.
- **Eden's Tech** — finish the Environmental Modifier investigation.
- **Mayor's Glory** — clean up River Valley.
- **Old Pal?** — repair Treaty Port with the Kon-Tiki Archipelago Alliance.
- **Through The Roof!** — obtain the Flying Motorcycle.
- **In Boots, and Bare Feet** — arrive in the Wetlands.
- **The Big Guy** — defeat the Autonomous Vessel.
- **Concrete Jungle** — arrive in the Old City Ruins.
- **'Siege Warfare'** — defeat the Guardian of the Old City.
- **Valley Bloom** — complete the final broadcast.

The early numbers support that approach. **Beyond Their Grasp** sits at **59.3%**
in my current snapshot, while the late Old City progression achievements fall into
the low single digits. I read that mainly as a progression-depth signal, not a
reason to rush late-game content.

For the actual main-story order, I use the <Link href="/doloc-town/walkthrough" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Doloc Town Walkthrough</Link>. The Core Sample, Small Battery, rear-circuit repair, and Phase 1 steps stay on the <Link href="/doloc-town/environmental-modifier" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Environmental Modifier Guide</Link> instead of being repeated here.

<h2 id="trash-hidden-achievements">
  Check trash as I play instead of saving every scavenger achievement for the end
</h2>

Trash is one of the few systems I interact with repeatedly even when I am not
actively achievement hunting. That makes the trash-related hidden achievements good
background goals.

The important ones are:

- **One Take** — find the unknown game-test invitation mail from a trash bin. My snapshot shows **30.7%** completion.
- **Bin Philosophy Session** — test my luck at a trash can **30 times**. It is much rarer at **2.9%**.
- **Littering The Wasteland** — toss trash into the mountain of waste.
- **I Have Plenty** — gift junk to Lank.

### I do not spam the same bin just because I want Bin Philosophy Session

Trash checks already fit naturally into trips through town and the outskirts. I
would rather accumulate them while moving between shops, quests, and the farm than
stand in one place trying to force a counter.

That also gives me repeated chances at **One Take** without turning the invitation
mail into a dedicated grind.

### I Have Plenty is specifically about junk, not "anything valuable-looking"

Lank's scavenger theme makes it tempting to assume every rare scrap is a good gift.
That is not a safe assumption.

<h2 id="easy-hidden-achievements">
  Do the simple hidden achievements when the setup is already convenient
</h2>

Several hidden achievements are one-action cleanup jobs. I do these when I am near
the required place or already carrying the required item.

### 8 Elixir

Drink **8 bottles of water in one day**.

This has a **21.8%** completion rate in my snapshot, so it is one of the more
commonly discovered hidden achievements. I wait until I already have eight bottles
in storage; I do not buy or burn scarce supplies early just to force it.

### Sleep Away The Doubt

Sleep for **more than 20 hours in one continuous sleep**.

I do this on a day where skipping time does not cost me a festival, timed meeting,
or crop emergency.

### Fade in Water

Drown once.

This is a deliberate failure achievement. I do it only when I am not carrying a
route I care about finishing first.

### Leap of Faith

Jump from **Mountains - Cliff**.

I save it for a Mountains visit rather than making a special trip the moment I see
the hidden requirement.

### Exactly Right!

Offer a **turnip to the deer statue**.

I keep one turnip aside once I know I am doing completion cleanup instead of
selling every harvest automatically.

### No Rest for the Strong

Collapse from exhaustion once.

Like drowning, this is a controlled failure. I do not trigger it on a day when I
still need to protect crops, hit a timed NPC window, or finish a long trip.

### Inevitable Fate

Force-plant an **off-season crop**.

For the achievement itself, I only care about the deliberate off-season action, so
I use one cheap seed rather than sacrificing an expensive multi-day crop. Normal
season and profitability decisions stay in the <Link href="/doloc-town/best-crops" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Best Crops and Profit Calculator</Link>.

<h2 id="exploration-hidden-achievements">

I keep these attached to region cleanup:

- **Tayzer Was Never Here** — discover Lotus Marsh in the Wetlands.

- **Sanctuary** — discover the survivor camp hidden in the Old City Ruins.
- **Heart** — find Ponka.

**Circular Harvest** is relatively visible at **15.1%**, while **Sanctuary** is only

I take that as a reason to leave the deepest secrets until I have the movement,
regions, and relationship systems needed to reach them comfortably.

I also do not invent a route when my current material only confirms the achievement
trigger. If a secret later develops a verified route and enough independent search
demand, that route can become its own Owner Page.

<h2 id="fishing-achievements">

Fishing has five completion goals I care about:

- **Fish Lord** — catch **200 fish**.

The practical mistake is trying to finish **Fish Lord** too early. Early fishing can
already feel slow enough that a basic catch becomes a longer fight. I would rather
improve the fishing setup naturally and let 20 fish become 200 over the course of
the save.

**Abundant Collection** is different. It is not a catch counter; it requires **10
distinct fish variants**. The Fish Tank, roe, incubator, parent ratios, and variant

<h2 id="animals">
  Raise animals normally before I turn the ranch into an achievement project
</h2>

The animal achievements are simple on paper:

- **Warmth of Life** — raise the first animal.
- **Noah's Ark** — raise **4 different kinds of animals**.

I do not buy four species just to move the achievement counter. The full Small
Livestock Shed, Feeding Trough, first Egg, animal-price, daily-care, and Breeding
Room route belongs to the <Link href="/doloc-town/animals" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Animals and Livestock Guide</Link>.

For the achievement page, my rule is only this:

> **make one animal setup stable before I pay for species variety.**

That keeps Noah's Ark as a completion target rather than an excuse to empty the
wallet during the first livestock tutorial.

<h2 id="automation-achievements">
  Treat the drone achievements as late automation goals

There are two automation achievements:

- **Groundbreaking** — place the Basic Drone onto an automated drone base station.
- **Laid-back Farmer** — operate **20 automated drones simultaneously**.

The first fits naturally into the automation tutorial. The second is a pure
late-game scale target; it sits at only **0.3%** in my snapshot.

The generator, sprinkler, station recipes, Farming Drone order, specialized drones,
and power troubleshooting belong to the <Link href="/doloc-town/farming-automation" className="font-semibold text-sky-700 underline decoration-sky-300 underline-offset-4 hover:text-sky-900">Farming Automation Guide</Link>. I do not repeat Titanium, Battery, Wire, or station-capacity numbers here.

<h2 id="cooking-festivals-combat">

description. I do not invent a recipe or failure count that the material does not
support.

<h2 id="a-false-heir">
  A False Heir: keep the confirmed trigger separate from the unverified route
</h2>

**A False Heir** is one of the achievements I do not pretend is fully solved by the
material I currently have.

The confirmed trigger is:

> **hold both the Special Pickaxe and its replica at the same time.**

The achievement sits at **3.8%** in my snapshot.

My current material confirms Lank's lost-pickaxe storyline and confirms that the
Special Pickaxe and replica are separate items, but it does **not** give me a
reliable complete route to the replica. I therefore do not add a made-up location,
vendor, or quest step.

If that route becomes verified later, or GSC starts showing enough real demand for
the query family, I would split it into its own problem-solving Owner Page instead
of guessing here.

<h2 id="completion-order">
  My final 100% cleanup order
</h2>

When the main story is done, I clean the remaining list in this order:

1. **single-action hidden achievements** — water, sleep, drowning, off-season crop, deer statue;
2. **region secrets** — treehouse, Lotus Marsh, survivor camp, bird, Ponka;
3. **trash and Lank cleanup** — bins, mountain waste, junk gift, A False Heir if the replica route is confirmed;
4. **fishing and animal counters**;
5. **gene, seed, chip, and archive completion**;
6. **relationships and four faction reputation tracks**;
7. **festival results**;
8. **automation scale**;
9. **long counters** — 1,000 crops, 200 fish, 30 guild missions, 1,000,000G, 50 treasure chests;
10. **anything still unresolved from hidden-achievement conditions**.

That order keeps me from wasting early resources on achievements that become much
cheaper after the farm, movement, regions, and automation systems are fully open.
