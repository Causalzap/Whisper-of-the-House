The **Environmental Modifier** is the first Doloc Town story gate where I can reach
the correct machine, bring what looks like the correct battery, and still fail to
advance.

The route has four separate checks:

**Core Extractor → Core Sample → damaged Environmental Modifier circuit → Small
Batteries + Electric Wires → Phase 1 at 30 Energy.**

The two mistakes I avoid are using ordinary Batteries instead of the placeable
**Small Battery**, and leaving after the circuit repair before inserting the Core
Sample and activating Phase 1.

<h2 id="find-core-extractor">
  Find the Core Extractor before searching for the Environmental Modifier
</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

I do not start wandering the outskirts as soon as the story mentions the
Environmental Modifier.

The useful lead is the **Core Extractor**.

After the Eden site plan becomes part of the investigation, I keep following that
objective until I reach the machine matching the site-plan marker. The interaction I
care about is the sample-storage compartment, not the surrounding loot or side
paths.

<h2 id="find-modifier">

The coordinates lead me to a **cave in the outskirts**.

Inside, I look for the large machine surrounded by four pillars. The front terminal
does not work when I first reach it, which makes the location feel wrong even though
I am standing at the correct objective.

<h2 id="ask-modi">
  Report the damage first, then let Modi inspect the circuit
</h2>

After I inspect the rear panel, I report what I found and go to **Modi**.

I wait until this handoff before committing materials because the repair is more
specific than "bring some Batteries and Wires."

Modi identifies the missing parts as energy components and gives me a written repair
plan. The material requirement is:

- **2 Small Batteries**
- **5 Electric Wires**

The important detail is the word **Small**.

A normal Battery is not a substitute. I learned that the expensive way: I already
had ordinary Batteries available, returned to the repair, and still could not start
because the quest wanted the placeable Small Battery equipment.

<h2 id="small-batteries">
  Unlock Basic Power Storage if the Small Battery recipe is missing
</h2>

The Small Battery needed here is the placeable storage equipment from the
**Industrial - Basic Power Storage** tech node.

Each one uses:

- **2 Copper Ingots**
- **2 Rubber**
- **1 Battery**

For the two Small Batteries required by the Modifier, I prepare:

**4 Copper Ingots + 4 Rubber + 2 Batteries**

The repair itself still needs the separate **5 Electric Wires**.

So when I am preparing one complete return trip, I check that I have:

> **2 crafted Small Batteries + 5 Electric Wires + the Core Sample**

### I do not build a Charger just because the quest mentions Batteries

Modi points out that an ordinary Battery can be crafted or an old one can be
recharged, but I do not turn that into another mandatory machine purchase.

If I already have two usable Batteries, I put them directly into the two Small
Battery recipes.

I only bother with a Charger when I actually have depleted Batteries I want to reuse
beyond this one story gate. Otherwise I keep the Copper and Wires for the repair
itself and later progression.

<h2 id="repair-system">
  Return to the rear panel with the full repair kit
</h2>

I do not walk back to the cave with half the materials.

The inefficient version of this route is:

> reach the Modifier → discover normal Batteries do not count → return home → unlock
> Basic Power Storage → craft one Small Battery → realize I still need a second one
> or more Wire → return again.

I avoid that by finishing the entire repair kit before leaving the farm.

At the rear panel, I use **Modi&apos;s written circuit instructions** rather than trying
to improvise the wiring.

The repair note is there because the circuit is complicated enough that guessing is
a bad strategy. Once I have the two Small Batteries and five Wires installed, I
follow the note in order and stay at the rear panel until the electrical repair is
actually complete.

The important progression distinction is:

> **circuit repaired does not mean Environmental Modifier Phase 1 is finished.**

The electrical system only brings the machine back online.

I still need the Core Sample.

<h2 id="repair-troubleshooting">
  The checks I make when the Environmental Modifier still will not progress
</h2>

### The front terminal is dead

I walk around the machine and inspect the **rear panel**.

I do not leave the cave just because the front interaction appears inactive.

### I have Batteries, but the repair will not start

I check whether I brought **Small Batteries**, not ordinary Battery items.

If I cannot craft Small Batteries yet, I unlock **Industrial - Basic Power Storage**.

### I crafted two Small Batteries but I am still missing something

I count the repair materials again.

The repair requires **2 Small Batteries + 5 Electric Wires**. The Core Sample is a
separate item I keep for the activation step afterward.

### I repaired the circuit but the waterways are still blocked

I have stopped too early.

The repaired circuit restores the machine, but I still need to insert the Core
Sample, reach **30 Energy**, and activate **Phase 1**.

### I cannot remember what the circuit is asking me to connect

I reopen **Modi&apos;s repair instructions** and work from the written sequence instead
of changing connections at random.

The key decision is not to rebuild the Batteries or leave the cave when the repair
panel is already accepting the required components.

<h2 id="phase-one">
  Insert the Core Sample and activate Phase 1 at 30 Energy
</h2>

Once the rear-panel repair is complete, I move back to the Environmental Modifier
itself.

I continue in this order:

1. insert the **Core Sample**;
2. let the Modifier accumulate **30 Energy**;
3. activate **Phase 1**.

That final activation is the checkpoint I use for the entire River Valley
Environmental Modifier sequence.
