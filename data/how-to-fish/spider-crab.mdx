<h2 id="how-to-beat-spider-crab">
  How to beat the Spider Crab boss in How to Fish
</h2>

The Spider Crab became much easier once I stopped treating it like a normal
melee fight.

My early attempts went badly because I stayed close and tried to keep hitting
it. The Crab does too much damage for that to be a comfortable trade, especially
with opening-island equipment.

The safer fight is built around its own attack cycle.

I stay far enough away that the Spider Crab has to come toward me, let it commit
to an attack, and watch for the brief dazed state afterward.

When the stars appear over its head, I move in.

I only take a short damage window.

Then I leave.

My full loop is:

**create distance**

→ **let the Spider Crab attack**

→ **watch for the daze**

→ **move in immediately**

→ **land a few melee hits**

→ **back out before it becomes active again**

I repeat that instead of trying to stay beside the boss.

The important part is not squeezing every possible hit into one stun.

It is reaching the next stun without losing most of my health.

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  How to catch and start the Spider Crab fight
</h2>

The Spider Crab is tied to the Lighthouse Keeper's opening-island quest.

After the tutorial, the boat is already nearby, but I still cannot leave because
I do not have the **Boat Keys**.

The Lighthouse Keeper explains that a long-legged creature has been stealing
his Beer.

That creature is the Spider Crab.

To lure it out, I need an:

**Empty Beer Can.**

I do not need a later-island lure or expensive fishing equipment. Everything I
need to trigger the boss is available in the opening area.

<h2 id="empty-beer-can">
  How to get the Empty Beer Can
</h2>

This is the first part of the quest that can waste money.

I buy a **Beer**, but I do **not** drink it myself.

Drinking the Beer does not leave the quest bait in my inventory.

Instead, I bring the full Beer to the Lighthouse Keeper and give it to him.

He drinks it and gives me the:

**Empty Beer Can.**

<h2 id="before-fight">
  What I prepare before fighting the Spider Crab
</h2>

The Spider Crab can kill me quickly if I stand in front of it, but I do not need
to spend a long time grinding the first island.

I mainly want three things:

- enough health and hunger to survive the attempt;
- a melee weapon I am comfortable using;
- enough patience to wait for the Crab's dazed window.

A **Knife** is a much more comfortable option for me than relying on bare fists.
Brass Knuckles can work too, but the weapon does not replace the boss mechanic.

More damage helps once I am already reaching safe openings.

It does not make face-tanking the Spider Crab a good strategy.

I also like having something edible available if my health drops during the
fight. Healing is much safer when I have already created distance rather than
waiting until the Crab is beside me.

The preparation question I care about is not:

**How strong can I make myself before the first boss?**

It is:

**Can I survive long enough to repeat the stun loop cleanly?**

<h2 id="how-to-stun">
  How to stun the Spider Crab
</h2>

I do not stun the Spider Crab by repeatedly attacking it.

That was the mistake that made my first attempts feel much harder than they
needed to be.

The Crab creates its own opening.

After it commits to an attack, it can end up briefly dazed or confused, with
stars appearing over its head.

That is when I attack.

I do not start swinging while the Crab is still actively attacking.

I wait until I can see that the opening has actually happened.

Then I close the distance quickly, hit it, and leave before the stars disappear.

This is why being patient at range works better for me than chasing the Crab
around the island.

I am letting the boss create the safe opening instead of trying to manufacture
one with damage.

<h2 id="why-i-keep-dying">
  Why the Spider Crab fight keeps going wrong
</h2>

When an attempt fails, I look at **where** it failed before spending more money.

If I am getting hit before the Crab ever becomes dazed, I am probably standing
too close or attacking too early.

If I consistently reach the dazed state but barely damage the health bar, then
upgrading my melee weapon makes sense.

If I land good damage but get hit every time the Crab wakes up, I am staying in
the punish window too long.

Those are three different problems.

The fix I use is:

**getting hit before the stun**

→ create more space and let the Crab attack first

**reaching every stun but doing very little damage**

→ improve melee damage

**getting hit as the stun ends**

→ stop attacking earlier

This fight rewards discipline more than aggression.

One extra hit is not worth turning a clean damage window into a trade.

<h2 id="after-kill">
  Keep the Spider Crab Shell after the fight
</h2>

Killing the Spider Crab does not automatically unlock the boat.

I still need the progression drop:

**Spider Crab Shell.**

→ **return to Lighthouse Keeper**

→ **receive Boat Keys**

The boss kill and the boat unlock are two separate steps.

<h2 id="boat-keys">
  Get the Boat Keys and buy the Radar before leaving
</h2>

Once the Lighthouse Keeper gives me the **Boat Keys**, the opening-island gate
is finally cleared.

Before I sail away, I also buy the **Radar for $10**.

I value that more than squeezing another small combat upgrade out of the opening
area.

The Boat Keys let me travel.

The Radar tells me where that progression is going.

Once I have both, I stop grinding the Lighthouse area and move on.
