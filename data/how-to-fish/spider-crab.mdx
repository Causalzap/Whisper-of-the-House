<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To beat the **Spider Crab** in *How to Fish*, I first buy a **Beer** and give the full Beer to the Lighthouse Keeper instead of drinking it myself.

He turns it into the **Empty Beer Can** I need as bait.

After I catch the Spider Crab, I do **not** try to force a stun with attacks. The useful opening happens when the Spider Crab commits to its own attack and knocks itself into a short stunned state.

My fight loop is:

**give it room**

→ **let it attack**

→ **wait for the stun**

→ **move in and deal damage**

→ **back away before it recovers**

After the kill, I keep the **Spider Crab Shell** and return it to the Lighthouse Keeper for the **Boat Keys**.

Before sailing away, I also pick up the **Radar for 10**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-to-start">
  The Lighthouse Keeper is the real opening-island progression gate
</h2>

The first time I finished the tutorial, the boat was sitting right there but I still could not leave.

The problem was not money, a boat upgrade, or another fishing rod.

I simply had:

**no Boat Keys**

The progression gate is the Lighthouse Keeper near the crashed boat and lighthouse area.

He explains that a long-legged creature has been stealing his Beer and that an **Empty Beer Can** can lure it out.

That creature is the Spider Crab.

I do not need another island, a rare lure, or expensive equipment just to start the quest. Everything required to trigger the first boss is still in the opening area.

<h2 id="empty-beer-can">
  Give the Beer to the Lighthouse Keeper
</h2>

This was the first part I got wrong.

I can buy a normal **Beer**, but drinking it myself does not leave a usable empty can behind. The Beer simply disappears.

What works is:

**buy a Beer**

→ **bring the full Beer to the Lighthouse Keeper**

→ **give it to him**

→ **take the Empty Beer Can**

The Empty Beer Can becomes the actual Spider Crab bait.

Once the Empty Beer Can appears in my bait selection, I use it instead of continuing to fish with Hot Dogs or ordinary early bait.

If the Spider Crab is not appearing, the Empty Beer Can is the first thing I check.

I do not start farming for better weapons until I know the boss trigger itself is ready.

<h2 id="before-fight">
  A basic melee setup is enough once the mechanic makes sense
</h2>

The Spider Crab hits hard enough that fighting it with nothing is unpleasant, but I also do not spend a long time farming the opening island for upgrades.

Improving my early weapon helped my damage.

It did not solve the reason I was dying.

The real problem was that I could not find a safe opening.

The early Brass Knuckles felt weak during repeated attempts, so when I have enough money I prefer a **Knife** and sharpening it rather than sinking everything into random equipment.

Before using the Empty Beer Can, I mainly want:

- enough hunger to finish the attempt without stopping to eat;
- a usable melee weapon;
- and enough room to move around the boss.

I do not wait for later-island equipment.

Once I understand the Spider Crab's stun window, the fight becomes much easier without excessive grinding.

<h2 id="how-to-stun">
  The Spider Crab creates its own stun window
</h2>

My first question was:

**How do I stun the Spider Crab?**

I attacked more aggressively.

I tried different melee weapons.

I looked for something resembling a parry.

None of that was the answer.

The Spider Crab creates the opening itself.

When it commits to its attack, it can end up in a vulnerable stunned or confused state.

That is when I move in.

My fight loop is:

**stay far enough away to read the attack**

→ **let the Spider Crab commit**

→ **wait until it enters the stunned state**

→ **move in for a short burst**

→ **back out before it recovers**

Then I repeat it.

The important judgment is knowing when to stop attacking.

The stun does not last long.

If I stay beside the Spider Crab trying to squeeze out one more hit, I often lose the safety I created by waiting for the opening in the first place.

<h2 id="why-i-keep-dying">
  If I keep dying, I diagnose timing before buying upgrades
</h2>

There are different reasons an attempt can fail.

If I reach clean stun windows consistently but barely move the boss health bar, improving melee damage makes sense.

If I keep getting hit because I attack while the Spider Crab is still active, another upgrade does not fix the real problem.

I use three checks:

**I cannot create a safe opening**

→ wait for the Spider Crab to self-stun instead of forcing the fight

**I reach the stun consistently but damage is too low**

→ improve melee damage

**I keep getting hit as the stun ends**

→ shorten the damage window and leave earlier

This is one of those fights where understanding one mechanic is worth more than buying whatever the shop offers next.

<h2 id="after-kill">
  Keep the Spider Crab Shell after the kill
</h2>

Killing the Spider Crab does not unlock the boat by itself.

The important progression drop is the:

**Spider Crab Shell**

I do not immediately sell everything around the boss.

Other drops can be treated as normal loot, but the Shell is what the Lighthouse Keeper needs.

If the Spider Crab is dead but the boat is still unusable, I do not look for another boss.

I check whether I picked up the Shell.

Then I return to the Lighthouse Keeper and hand it in.

The progression sequence is:

**Spider Crab defeated**

→ **Spider Crab Shell collected**

→ **Shell returned to the Lighthouse Keeper**

→ **Boat Keys received**

<h2 id="boat-keys">
  Get the Radar for 10 before leaving
</h2>

Once I have the Boat Keys, the main opening-island progression gate is finished.

Before sailing away, I also pick up the **Radar for 10**.

I consider that a better use of early money than staying behind for another marginal combat upgrade.

The boat gives me movement.

The Radar gives that movement a direction.

Once both are ready, I leave the Lighthouse area.

If I am following the story rather than cleaning up the creature collection, the next real progression problem begins on Island 2 with the <Link href="/how-to-fish/leech-bait">three-Leech requirement</Link>.
