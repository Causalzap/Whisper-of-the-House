<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
If I am stuck at the **0 / 3 Leech** request on Island 2, I stop fishing.

My route is:

**talk to the woman near the shop**

→ **search the island ground for Leeches**

→ **pick up three Leeches**

→ **return to the same NPC**

→ **complete the 3 / 3 hand-in**

→ **receive the Leech-based bait for the big fish**

→ **prepare for a boss before using it**

The important correction is:

**Leeches are ground pickups.**

I do not catch them with a rod, and I do not need to buy another fishing lure to make the 0 / 3 counter move.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="where-this-starts">
  The Leech route starts at the 0 / 3 quest on Island 2
</h2>

After reaching Island 2, I talk to the woman near the settlement and shop.

Her request starts at:

**0 / 3**

She wants help catching a much larger creature around the island, but before I worry about that fight she gives me the important clue:

**it likes Leeches.**

She also explains that if I bring the Leeches back to her, she can turn them into the bait I need.

This is where I separate two different blockers.

If the quest still shows:

**0 / 3 Leeches**

I still need to find the Leeches.

If the Leech hand-in is already complete and the boss bait is ready, I stop looking for Leeches and prepare for the Giant Piranha.

<h2 id="where-to-find-leeches">
  I search the ground around Island 2 instead of casting into the water
</h2>

My first mistake was assuming Leeches were another creature I had to catch.

That sent me back to the shoreline changing fishing gear and waiting for something that was never going to happen.

The NPC's clue is literal:

the Leeches are **crawling around on the ground**.

So once the quest starts, I stop looking at the water.

I move around the island and scan the ground close to my feet.

They are small enough that I can walk straight past one if I am scanning the horizon instead of the ground.

I slow down around the settlement and nearby land rather than sprinting from fishing spot to fishing spot.

The important visual confirmation is the pickup itself:

**Leech**

Once I see that, I interact with it and continue looking for the next one.

<h2 id="complete-three-leeches">
  I collect all three before going back to the NPC
</h2>

The quest gives me an exact target:

**3 Leeches**

I use that counter rather than guessing when I have enough.

My route is:

**find one on the ground**

→ **pick it up**

→ **keep searching the island**

→ **reach 3 / 3**

I do not stop after finding the first Leech.

And I do not assume that because the NPC can make bait from Leeches, one pickup must already be enough.

I trust the quest counter instead of guessing whether I have enough.
This also means I do not need to spend money to solve this step.

If the counter is not moving, my problem is almost certainly:

**I have not picked up enough Leeches yet**

not:

**my fishing rod is too weak**

or:

**I need a different boss lure**

<h2 id="return-to-npc">
  At 3 / 3, I return to the woman instead of trying to use the raw Leeches myself
</h2>

Once the collection is complete, I go back to the same woman.

I do not try to attach a raw Leech to my fishing rod.

The NPC is part of the conversion step.

She takes the completed Leech requirement and prepares the bait for the large creature.

This is the distinction I keep in mind:

**Leeches collected**

does not automatically mean:

**I should start fishing immediately**

I first finish the NPC interaction.

Once she has prepared the bait, she warns me to make sure I have a **gun**, because fighting the target with bare hands is dangerous.

That warning tells me the next interaction is not another normal fishing step.

It is a boss trigger.

<h2 id="when-bait-is-ready">
  Once the boss bait is ready, I stop looking for more Leeches
</h2>

Once the NPC has prepared the bait, I do not keep collecting Leeches.

I only check whether I have actually finished the hand-in before I use it.

My checkpoints are:

**quest still below 3 / 3**

→ keep searching the ground

**3 / 3 complete but no boss bait**

→ return to the NPC and finish the dialogue

**NPC has prepared the bait and warned me about the fight**

→  I am ready to move on to the Giant Piranha

At that point, I stop collecting Leeches for the current attempt.

I prepare for the boss before spending the bait.

<h2 id="giant-piranha-trigger">
  Using the converted bait confirms the Leech route worked
</h2>

When I finally use the converted bait, the result I am looking for is:

**Giant Piranha**

Once the Giant Piranha health bar appears, I know the Leech problem is finished.

Before the boss appears, my problem is:

**find and convert the Leeches**

After the boss appears, my problem is:

**survive the fight and unlock Island 3**

I handle that in the <Link href="/how-to-fish/giant-piranha">Giant Piranha guide</Link>.

<h2 id="leech-quest-not-working">
  If the quest is not progressing, I check the counter first
</h2>

I can usually diagnose the problem without buying anything.

If the counter still says:

**0 / 3**

or:

**1 / 3**

or:

**2 / 3**

I search the ground for the missing Leeches.

If I already reached:

**3 / 3**

but do not have the usable boss bait, I return to the NPC.

If the bait is ready but I have not used it, I am already finished with the Leech collection.

If the **Giant Piranha** health bar is on screen, the Leech route definitely worked.

There is no reason to keep changing lures or searching the ground during that attempt.

The complete route is:

**Island 2 NPC**

→ **0 / 3 Leeches**

→ **search the ground**

→ **collect three**

→ **return at 3 / 3**

→ **NPC prepares the boss bait**

→ **NPC warns me to bring a gun**

→ **use the bait when ready**

→ **Giant Piranha appears**

Once the Giant Piranha appears, I stop worrying about Leeches and focus on the fight.
