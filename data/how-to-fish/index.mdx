<h2 id="how-progression-works">
  I progress by following NPC hand-offs instead of exploring islands at random
</h2>

*How to Fish* looks very open at first.

I can fish, sell creatures, upgrade weapons, gamble, cook, and sail wherever the current route allows.

But the main story is much more structured.

Most islands follow some version of:

**talk to the progression NPC**

→ **find what the NPC wants**

→ **prepare the required bait or trigger**

→ **complete the boss or task**

→ **keep the important quest item**

→ **return to the NPC**

→ **receive new coordinates**

→ **follow the Radar**

That pattern is the most useful thing I learned during the first run.

When I kill something important but the next route does not appear, I do **not** immediately assume I need another boss or a better boat.

I check the hand-in first.

<h2 id="first-island">
  First island: I unlock the boat and learn the boss-to-hand-in loop
</h2>

The opening teaches the basic economy:

**catch creatures**

→ **kill them**

→ **inspect them**

→ **sell what I do not need**

→ **buy useful equipment**

The actual progression gate is the **Lighthouse Keeper**.

His request eventually leads to the **Spider Crab**.

For the main route, I only need to finish the Lighthouse Keeper sequence:

**finish the Lighthouse Keeper request**

→ **defeat the Spider Crab**

→ **keep the quest-related drop**

→ **return to the Lighthouse Keeper**

→ **get the boat**

Once that is complete, I can start following the game's island route.

If the Spider Crab itself is stopping me, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/boss-guide">Boss Guide</Link> for the fight.

The important thing I learned here is that killing the boss and completing the NPC hand-in are two separate steps.

I do not leave the opening area until both are finished.

<h2 id="island-2">
  Island 2: Leeches first, Giant Piranha second
</h2>

Island 2 introduces the first progression chain that is easy to misread.

The local NPC starts with a **0 / 3 Leech** requirement.

If I am still on that counter, I am not solving the boss yet.

I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/leech-bait">Leech Bait guide</Link> for:

**where the Leeches are**

→ **how to complete 3 / 3**

→ **how the NPC turns them into the boss bait**

Once the bait is ready, the blocker changes to the **Giant Piranha**.

For the fight, buying decisions, **Giant Piranha Skeleton**, hand-in, and Island 3 unlock, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/giant-piranha">Giant Piranha guide</Link>.

Before I leave Island 2, I make sure this sequence is complete:

**Giant Piranha complete**

→ **quest trophy handed in**

→ **Island 3 coordinates received**

→ **Radar updates**

Once the new route appears, I leave Island 2.

<h2 id="island-3">
  Island 3: I solve the Tourist requirement before treating the Pufferfish as the blocker
</h2>

Island 3 is really two progression problems in sequence.

The first is the **Tourist**.

He wants an endangered creature before giving me the item needed for the scary fish.

If I do not have the **Carrot** yet, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/endangered-fish">Endangered Fish guide</Link> to finish:

**Endangered Species**

→ **valid catch**

→ **Tourist**

→ **Carrot**

Once the Carrot is in my inventory, I stop fishing for that prerequisite.

The blocker has changed to the **Pufferfish**.

For the Carrot attempt, weapon decisions, tree route, later phase, **Pufferfish Tail**, and Island 4 hand-in, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/pufferfish">Pufferfish guide</Link>.

The progression result I care about here is:

**Pufferfish defeated**

→ **Pufferfish Tail secured**

→ **Tourist hand-in**

→ **Island 4 coordinates**

→ **Radar updates**

Once that happens, Island 3 is finished for main-story progression.

<h2 id="island-4">
  Island 4: I catch Tuna before trying to force the Terrorizing Bird fight
</h2>

The next island changes the boss trigger again.

The local NPC explains that an enormous bird is terrorizing the island and that it returns when someone brings:

**Tuna**

The Tuna is tied to the:

**Professional Boss Lure**

My story route is:

**get the Professional Boss Lure**

→ **catch Tuna**

→ **use the Tuna to bring back the Terrorizing Bird**

→ **defeat the boss**

→ **keep the bird's head**

→ **return it to the NPC**

→ **take the next coordinates**

If the Terrorizing Bird fight itself is stopping me, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/boss-guide">Boss Guide</Link>.

After the kill, the important thing is the trophy hand-in.

Once again:

**boss dead**

does not necessarily mean:

**route complete**

I make sure the bird's head reaches the NPC before I expect the next island to open.

<h2 id="military-volcano">
  Military and volcano route: I follow the scientist's experiment in order
</h2>

Eventually the Radar takes me into the military / volcano part of the story.

This section is less obvious because I can see the volcano before I fully understand what the game wants me to do with it.

I do not start throwing random creatures into the lava.

I follow the military objective to the scientist first.

The route becomes:

**work with the scientist**

→ **bring the smaller creatures she needs**

→ **get the fitting Whale bait**

→ **catch and defeat the Whale**

→ **take the defeated Whale to the volcano**

→ **throw it in**

That volcano step advances the experiment.

I do not sell the Whale and I do not treat the scientist hand-in like the earlier boss trophies.

This time the Whale itself needs to go to the volcano.

<h2 id="final-boss">
  The volcano sequence leads into the Mutated Bowhead Whale
</h2>

Once the Whale experiment reaches the volcano, the route moves into the final major combat sequence.

The boss I prepare for is the:

**Mutated Bowhead Whale**

If the Mutated Bowhead Whale itself is killing me, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/boss-guide#mutated-bowhead-whale">Boss Guide</Link> for the fight.

To keep moving through the story, I follow this sequence:

**scientist's Whale experiment**

→ **volcano**

→ **Mutated Bowhead Whale sequence**

→ **finish the military objective**

→ **gain access to the final route**

This is another point where I avoid confusing:

**combat difficulty**

with:

**progression confusion**

If the final encounter has appeared and is killing me, I improve my combat setup.

If nothing is happening at the volcano yet, I retrace the scientist's task chain instead.

<h2 id="finish-the-game">
  I use the final route to finish the story, then separate story completion from 100%
</h2>

After the final progression sequence, I use the route that becomes available toward the mainland.

That completes the main story route.

But I do not treat:

**finish the game**

as:

**finish everything in the game**

*How to Fish* has **28 achievements**, and several of them require completely different cleanup goals.

Those include challenges built around:

- Killscore;
- cooking;
- casino and money;
- weapon and boat upgrades;
- creature collection;
- Drip creatures;
- a fast boss kill;
- a sub-one-hour clear;
- and the final-boss bare-hands requirement.

After finishing the story, I switch to the <Link href="/how-to-fish/achievements">How to Fish Achievements guide</Link> for the remaining 100% goals.

That keeps my first clear focused on the story instead of mixing in speedrunning, Fishipedia, gambling, and challenge kills.

<h2 id="upgrade-priority">
  I only buy an upgrade when I can say what problem it solves
</h2>

I stopped wasting money once I separated progression problems from combat problems.

My rule is:

**the boss has not spawned**

→ check the quest trigger

**the boss has spawned but I cannot hurt it reliably**

→ improve the weapon

**I am landing shots but the boss takes too long**

→ improve damage

**recoil is wasting most of my shots**

→ improve control

**I keep entering fights hungry**

→ stop shopping and bring food

**the current story blocker is finished and travel is now slow**

→ consider boat upgrades

This keeps me from buying whatever new item happens to appear in the shop.

A useful upgrade is one that fixes the problem I actually have now.

<h2 id="where-i-go-when-stuck">
  When progression stops, I check what is actually blocking me
</h2>

I use the current game state to work out what I am actually missing.

If I am on Island 2 and the Leech requirement is unfinished, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/leech-bait">Leech Bait guide</Link>.

If the Giant Piranha can already spawn, I move on to the <Link href="/how-to-fish/giant-piranha">Giant Piranha guide</Link>.

If I am on Island 3 without the Carrot, I check the <Link href="/how-to-fish/endangered-fish">Endangered Fish guide</Link>.

If the Carrot is already ready, my next problem is the <Link href="/how-to-fish/pufferfish">Pufferfish fight</Link>.

If I am stuck on one of the other major bosses, I use the <Link href="/how-to-fish/boss-guide">Boss Guide</Link>.

If the story is already finished and I am working toward 100%, I switch to the <Link href="/how-to-fish/achievements">Achievements guide</Link>.

The question I ask first is:

**Am I missing a quest requirement, or am I actually losing the fight?**

If the boss has not appeared, I retrace the quest.

If the boss is already attacking me, I work on food, positioning, weapon control, or damage.

That distinction keeps me from farming money or buying upgrades when the real problem is one missing quest step.
