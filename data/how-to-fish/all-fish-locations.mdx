<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
*How to Fish* has **49 normal creature entries** to complete for **Collector**.

I do not try to finish all 49 during my first trip through the story. I unlock the later islands and lure tiers first, then open the encyclopedia with `Tab` and clean up the missing slots by area.

The five collection areas are:

**Lighthouse**

→ **Island 2 / Forest**

→ **Island 3 / Desert**

→ **Island 4 / Rocks**

→ **Island 5 / Volcano**

The encyclopedia also has a separate **49-slot Drip collection** used for **Fishipedia**. Normal creatures and their Drip versions are tracked independently.

If I am close to 49/49 but still missing entries, I check the unusual early creatures, boss-lure pools, and optional bosses before I start fishing every island again.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="collector-vs-fishipedia">
  Collector and Fishipedia track different collections
</h2>

The two collection achievements are easy to mix together because both use the same encyclopedia.

For **Collector**, I need to find and kill every normal creature type.

For **Fishipedia**, I need the separate **Drip creatures**.

I use `Tab` to check which side is actually incomplete.

A normal catch does not fill its Drip slot, and getting a Drip does not mean I should ignore the normal entry.

I also do not stop story progression just to wait for a rare Drip.

If I still have islands or lure tiers locked, I finish the <Link href="/how-to-fish">main progression route</Link> first. Once every major area is accessible, collection cleanup becomes much faster.

The <Link href="/how-to-fish/achievements#collector-fishipedia">Achievements guide</Link> covers where Collector and Fishipedia fit into the full 100% route.

<h2 id="all-49-creatures">
  All 49 creatures by island, lure, and trigger
</h2>

I group the collection by the area where I can actually obtain each creature.

That is more useful to me than sorting by price or by the order I happened to discover them.

Normal creatures come from an island&apos;s regular fishing pool.

Boss-lure catches, quest bosses, and other special creatures have their own trigger.

<h2 id="cleanup-route">
  Best cleanup order after the story
</h2>

Once all five areas are available, I clear the cheap normal pools before I spend money on special lures.

My order is:

**Lighthouse basic entries**

→ **Island 2 normal and Beginner pools**

→ **Island 3 Standard pool**

→ **Island 4 Professional pool**

→ **Island 5 Scientific pool**

→ **remaining Boss Lure catches**

→ **remaining story creatures**

There is no reason to burn expensive boss bait if the missing slot is just a Mackerel, Seahorse, or Flounder.

After the ordinary pools are complete, the remaining blanks become much easier to diagnose.

If the missing entry belongs to a **normal pool**, I stay with that island&apos;s regular lure.

If it belongs to a **Boss Lure pool**, I switch to the corresponding Boss Lure.

If it is a **story creature**, I retrace the NPC progression trigger instead of fishing randomly.

<h2 id="separate-entries">
  Similar names can still use separate encyclopedia slots
</h2>

A few entries look similar enough that I originally assumed one catch covered both.

It does not.

**Pike** and **The Old Pike** are separate creatures.

**Piranha** and **Giant Piranha** are separate creatures.

The early crab species also occupy individual slots.

The same rule applies to the story and boss variants later in the game.

When my collection total looks wrong, I trust the exact encyclopedia entries rather than remembering that I have "already caught a Pike" or "already killed a Piranha."

<h2 id="drip-creatures">
  Fishipedia cleanup starts after the normal 49 are under control
</h2>

Once the normal side reaches **49/49**, I stop repeating the Collector route.

If the Drip counter still has blanks, I switch to those missing variants specifically.

A Drip creature comes from the same general creature pool as its normal counterpart, but it registers separately.

My cleanup is therefore much narrower:

**identify the missing Drip entry**

→ **return to that creature&apos;s island**

→ **use the lure for that pool**

→ **keep fishing until the Drip variant appears**

I still kill Drip creatures whenever I encounter them naturally during progression.

I just do not hold up the story waiting for every variant.

Once all islands and lure tiers are unlocked, deliberate Fishipedia cleanup is much easier to organize.
