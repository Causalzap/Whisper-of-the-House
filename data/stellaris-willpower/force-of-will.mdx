<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To complete **Force of Will** in **Stellaris: Willpower**, choose three Ascension Perks, become the Ideological Authority of an eligible Ideology, and select the Force of Will Ambition. Earn Willpower, adopt an **Absolute Ethic** before Stage 2, use **Doctrinal Proxies** and **Cultural Envoys** to spread your beliefs, and advance through all five stages. For victory, resolve the opposing Fallen Empire conflicts and convert **at least 50% of the galaxy's population** to your Ideology.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Force of Will has two different measures of progress: **Willpower earned** and **Pops converted to your Ideology**. Advancing the Ambition unlocks new abilities, but those unlocks do not automatically produce the population majority required for victory.

Converting foreign Pops advances the final objective, while diplomatic actions, subject relationships, ideological wars, and positive Ideological Strength growth can supply Willpower.

A campaign can therefore reach an advanced Ambition stage while still having too few followers across the galaxy. Check both measures before committing resources to another war or diplomatic arrangement.

<section id="force-of-will-unlock">

## How Do You Unlock Force of Will in Stellaris?

Force of Will becomes available after you have chosen three Ascension Perks and established the necessary ideological position.

<GuideTable
  caption="Force of Will Ambition requirements."
  columns={forceRequirementsColumns}
  rows={forceRequirementsRows}
/>

### Which Ideologies can choose Force of Will?

The eligible Ideologies are based on these Ethics:

- Egalitarian
- Pacifist
- Xenophile
- Materialist
- Spiritualist

**Militarist, Authoritarian, and Xenophobe Ideologies are excluded.**

This restriction concerns the Ideology used for Force of Will, not merely whether your empire has any minor secondary Ethic.

If the Ascension Perk is unavailable, inspect your **Official Ideology** and confirm that you are its Ideological Authority.

### How do you select the Ambition?

1. Establish or adopt an eligible Ideology.
2. Gain the Ideological Authority role.
3. Select three other Ascension Perks.
4. Open your Ascension Perks interface.
5. Choose **Force of Will** when it becomes available.
6. Open the Ambition progression interface to inspect the five stages and their objectives.

![Force of Will Ascension Perk requirements, including three previous Ascension Perks, an eligible Ideology, and an empty Ascension Perk Slot.](/images/stellaris-willpower/willpower-force-of-will-requirements.webp)

Choosing Force of Will commits your empire to its Official Ideology. You cannot simply abandon that Ideology afterward to pursue an unrelated path.

You also cannot combine Force of Will with another incompatible Ambition or Crisis path.

### Can you start Force of Will without founding your own Ideology?

You may initially adopt a foreign Ideology, but selecting the Ambition requires the Ideological Authority role.

If another empire currently controls your Ideology, check the Authority requirements before investing in this strategy.

The [Ideologies Guide](/stellaris-willpower/ideologies) explains how adoption and Ideological Authority work.

</section>

<section id="willpower-currency">

## How Do You Earn Willpower Faster?

Force of Will uses its own progression resource, **Willpower**.

You earn it through actions that support ideological expansion and from ongoing conditions associated with maintaining a successful Ideology.

<GuideTable
  caption="Ways to earn Willpower during the Force of Will Ambition."
  columns={willpowerSourcesColumns}
  rows={willpowerSourcesRows}
/>

![Force of Will Ambition Objectives showing Willpower rewards for converting Pops, maintaining vassals, and positive monthly Ideological Strength gain.](/images/stellaris-willpower/willpower-ambition-willpower-sources.webp)

### What is the most reliable way to gain Willpower?

Maintaining positive monthly Ideological Strength gain provides recurring Willpower. Each month, the reward equals your positive monthly Strength gain, rounded up.

For example, if your monthly Strength gain is +4.3, the Ambition awards 5 Willpower for that month.

Winning an Ideological War or vassalizing an empire grants 150 Willpower, but recurring Strength growth can provide a larger total reward over a long period.

Check the Ambition Objectives panel to see which sources are contributing most to your progress.

A war may be worth fighting because it creates a new adopter or subject, but fighting solely for a one-time Willpower reward can be inefficient.

Before starting another war:

1. Check your current Willpower income.
2. Compare the expected reward with the cost of the conflict.
3. Determine whether victory will produce an additional Ideology follower or Doctrinal Proxy.
4. Keep improving the recurring sources of Willpower while the war proceeds.

If your Ideology is already growing steadily, waiting for its continuing contributions can be preferable to opening an unnecessary front.

### Should you focus on converting Pops or creating subjects?

Both contribute, but they solve different problems.

**Converting Pops** directly advances the population-based victory condition.

**Creating subjects** can provide additional Willpower, Unity, Ideological Strength, and diplomatic influence.

A useful approach is to develop a stable group of subjects while using Cultural Envoys to reach densely populated foreign colonies.

Do not maximize your subject count at the expense of the ability to finish the final conversion requirement.

</section>

<section id="ambition-stages">

## What Are the Five Force of Will Stages?

Force of Will progresses through five stages, unlocking stronger diplomatic, ideological, and late-game abilities.

<GuideTable
  caption="Force of Will stages, main unlocks, and progression goals."
  columns={forceStageColumns}
  rows={forceStageRows}
/>

### Stage 1: Seeds of Unity

Stage 1 unlocks the **Doctrinal Proxy** subject type and **Conviction**.

Conviction prevents your empire from abandoning its Official Ideology and grants **+50% Governing Ethics Attraction**.

This commitment is important: Force of Will is not a temporary diplomatic project that you can abandon to freely change Ideologies later.

Your priorities should be:

1. Keep your Official Ideology stable.
2. Establish useful diplomatic relationships.
3. Identify possible Doctrinal Proxy subjects.
4. Begin accumulating Willpower.
5. Prepare the resources required to adopt an Absolute Ethic.

Do not assume that simply choosing Force of Will unlocks Cultural Envoys immediately.

The Ambition provides additional tools as you progress.

### Stage 2: Growing Consensus

Before entering Stage 2, you must adopt an **Absolute Ethic**.

This is a progression gate, not merely a recommended bonus.

Stage 2 unlocks **Impose Ideological Subjugation**, allowing you to force an eligible empire into a Doctrinal Proxy relationship. Cultural Envoys become available through Force of Will progression and provide a separate way to convert foreign Pops.

Once these tools become available, you can combine diplomacy and direct population conversion.

### Stage 3: Rising Movement

The middle stages provide stronger ideological and diplomatic bonuses.

In the Materialist example, the rewards included faster Cultural Envoys, stronger domestic ideological pressure, and additional economic or defensive improvements.

Some effects depend on the Ethic of your Ideology.

At this point, the limiting factor may no longer be basic Reach. It may be the number of foreign Pops converting, the availability of Officials, or the resources needed to protect and sustain your expansion.

### Stage 4: Galactic Mandate

Later progression improves conversion effectiveness and unlocks additional options.

These include an extra Tenet slot and the ability to convert enemy fleets to your cause.

Check which new Ambition rewards have become active before spending resources on additional Tenet changes or military preparation.

### Stage 5: Utopia

The final stage brings your Ideology toward galactic dominance.

Additional empire-wide bonuses help support the late-game economy and military preparation.

However, finishing the Ambition's progression is not the same thing as automatically winning.

You must still satisfy the final population conversion and Fallen Empire requirements.

</section>

<section id="absolute-ethic">

## How Do You Get the Absolute Ethic Required for Stage 2?

**Growing Consensus cannot advance until your empire adopts an Absolute Ethic.**

Reaching the necessary Willpower alone will not clear this requirement. You must complete the applicable faction action or event route and confirm that your governing Ethic has changed to its Absolute form.

### How do you unlock it?

1. Raise your Ideological Strength to the required threshold.
2. Open the Factions interface.
3. Find the faction associated with your Official Ideology.
4. Inspect its support and the available Embrace action.
5. Complete the required Unity payment and associated project.
6. Confirm that your empire now has the corresponding Absolute Ethic.

In Stellaris 4.6, the faction **Embrace** route can become available at **750 Ideological Strength**, while the event-based Absolute Ethic route uses a higher **800 Strength** threshold.

The change costs **5,000 Unity** and involves a **180-day special project**. The applicable faction, Ideology, and Authority conditions must also be satisfied.

Check the available faction action or event requirements before committing your Unity.

Your faction must also satisfy the requirements displayed in the action tooltip.

If the action is unavailable despite reaching the Strength threshold, check faction support, governing Ethics, and your position as an Ideology follower or Authority.

### What changes after adopting an Absolute Ethic?

Your empire abandons its other Ethics and commits fully to the selected one.

This can affect policy access, Civic eligibility, and other modifiers that depended on your previous Ethic combination.

Review those changes before confirming.

For Force of Will, the immediate benefit is that the required progression gate can be satisfied.

If your empire has reached the next Willpower threshold but Stage 2 remains locked, verify the Absolute Ethic before attempting other actions.

</section>

<section id="doctrinal-proxy">

## How Do Doctrinal Proxies Work?

A **Doctrinal Proxy** is a subject type associated with Force of Will.

Unlike a conventional subject arrangement that primarily supplies resources or military support, this relationship is designed around ideological alignment.

<GuideTable
  caption="Doctrinal Proxy features and their role in Force of Will."
  columns={doctrinalProxyColumns}
  rows={doctrinalProxyRows}
/>

### How do you create a Doctrinal Proxy?

After unlocking the subject type:

1. Open diplomacy with a suitable empire.
2. Check whether you can establish or negotiate a subject relationship.
3. Inspect the available subject types.
4. Select Doctrinal Proxy when eligible.
5. Review the agreement terms, loyalty, and resource obligations.
6. Confirm the arrangement when the benefits justify the cost.

The subject must follow your Ideology, which helps bring another government into your ideological network.

### Why are Doctrinal Proxies useful?

They help reinforce an existing Ideology rather than acting only as independent military allies.

In return for significant restrictions on military capacity, they provide economic and ideological contributions.

That can make them especially useful when you already have enough fleet power to defend your subjects.

However, these restrictions also mean you should not rely on a Doctrinal Proxy to provide the same military support as a conventional combat-focused subject.

### What happens if the subject becomes much weaker?

Military restrictions can substantially change the relative strength of a subject and its overlord.

In one campaign, the player found that otherwise useful subjects could become Protectorates as the power difference increased.

Before converting every subject into a Doctrinal Proxy, consider whether you can maintain the subject type and agreement you actually want.

Check each subject's status after major changes in technology, fleets, or diplomatic agreements.

</section>

<section id="ideological-subjugation">

## How Do You Unlock Impose Ideological Subjugation?

**Impose Ideological Subjugation** becomes available at Stage 2.

It provides a way to force an eligible foreign empire to become your Doctrinal Proxy through war.

This is not identical to the ordinary Impose Ideology war goal.

The outcome targets both ideological alignment and subject status.

### When should you use it?

Consider the war goal when:

- A suitable empire refuses diplomatic subjugation.
- Creating a Doctrinal Proxy would significantly strengthen your position.
- You can win the war without sacrificing more resources than the subject will provide.
- You need the diplomatic or ideological result, not just a small one-time Willpower reward.

Do not begin the war simply because the Casus Belli appears.

An empire that already follows your Ideology may be more useful as a friendly independent ally than as a subject with restricted military capability.

The alternative is to maintain positive relations and continue conversion through Cultural Envoys.

</section>

<section id="cultural-envoys">

## How Do You Build and Use Cultural Envoys?

**Cultural Envoys are civilian ships that convert foreign populations to your Ideology.**

They are one of the most important Force of Will tools because they help with the actual follower count needed for victory.

Unlike ordinary ideological Reach, Cultural Envoys work directly at foreign colonies.

<GuideTable
  caption="Cultural Envoy requirements and behavior."
  columns={culturalEnvoyColumns}
  rows={culturalEnvoyRows}
/>

### Step 1: Unlock the Cultural Envoy

Progress through Force of Will until Cultural Envoys become available.

Once unlocked, inspect your available shipbuilding options and the current resource cost.

Their construction uses resources including Alloys, Consumer Goods, and Influence.

Because exact costs may change with active game modifiers, use the current shipbuilding tooltip to plan how many you can afford.

### Step 2: Assign an Official

A Cultural Envoy requires an **Official** to operate.

Constructing the ship without assigning a suitable leader does not give you a fully operational conversion unit.

Assign the Official before sending the ship to its first target.

Officials serving Cultural Envoys receive a relevant role trait that helps them promote your Ideology.

This makes leader availability an important limit on how many Cultural Envoys you can use effectively.

### Step 3: Send the Ship to a Foreign Colony

Choose a colony containing Pops that do not follow your Ideology.

Move your Cultural Envoy to that target and use the appropriate conversion action.

The ship does not need ordinary diplomatic border access.

However, it is still vulnerable to hostile military forces.

A useful target is one where a successful conversion would make a meaningful contribution to your total galactic following.

Cultural Envoys also apply an Ethic-specific modifier to the colony they visit. Check the active colony modifiers when comparing targets, since the benefit is not limited to ideological conversion alone.

### Step 4: Check Whether Conversion Is Working

Do not use the Ideology Map Mode as your only measure.

After the Cultural Envoy begins operating:

1. Open the target colony's available population information.
2. Inspect the distribution of ideological followers.
3. Allow the operation to continue.
4. Check whether the target's follower count is increasing.
5. Move the ship elsewhere once further conversion is less valuable than reaching a new colony.

This prevents you from confusing an already colored system on the galactic map with a fully converted population.

### Step 5: Use Automate Conversion

The **Automate Conversion** order seeks colonies with few followers of your Ideology and begins converting their Pops. An assigned Official is required to operate the ship.

The operation consumes **Consumer Goods**, and conversion is more effective within your **Ideological Reach**.

![An Academy Ship assigned to an Official using Automate Conversion, showing Consumer Goods consumption and conversion progress.](/images/stellaris-willpower/willpower-cultural-envoy-conversion.webp)

The percentage displayed beside "Converting" indicates the current conversion operation's progress. It should not be treated as the colony's total Ideology follower percentage.

Automation reduces the need to assign every target manually. Use direct orders when a particular colony offers more potential conversions or when an automatic route would expose the ship and its Official to danger.

### How do you stop Cultural Envoys from dying?

Cultural Envoys are fragile civilian vessels.

They can enter closed borders, but that does not make them immune to hostile ships.

Avoid routing them through dangerous systems when a safer route or target is available.

Keep especially valuable Officials away from active military operations, and escort your ships where practical.

Losing a Cultural Envoy can also cost you access to the leader and the conversion work it was performing.

</section>

<section id="fourth-tenet">

## How Do You Unlock the Fourth Tenet in Force of Will?

The additional Tenet slot becomes available through later Force of Will progression. Check the active Ambition rewards to confirm that the unlock has been gained.

Once it appears, open the Ideologies interface and select the new slot.

The fourth slot still uses the existing Tenet Intensity rules. Compare eligible effects and amendment costs in the [Tenets selection reference](/stellaris-willpower/tenets).

</section>

<section id="fleet-conversion">

## Can Force of Will Convert Enemy Fleets?

Yes. Later Force of Will progression unlocks the ability to convert enemy fleets to your cause.

Before using it, inspect the unlocked ability's tooltip for its target restrictions, costs, and activation requirements.

This is different from converting Pops with Cultural Envoys.

It is a military-oriented action that can potentially change the balance of power without building an equivalent fleet yourself.

### When Is Enemy Fleet Conversion Worth Using?

Fleet conversion is potentially valuable when an opposing fleet would otherwise require substantial resources to defeat or replace.

Before attempting it, confirm that the ability has been unlocked and inspect its current target restrictions and activation cost. Do not assume that every hostile fleet is eligible.

Compare the potential benefit with the cost of building or reinforcing your own navy. Against a powerful enemy, fleet conversion may support your military plan, but it should not replace reliable ship production and defensive preparation.

</section>

<section id="fallen-empires">

## Why Do Fallen Empires Attack During Force of Will?

Force of Will is not completed by ideological conversion alone.

As your Ideology becomes a major galactic power and approaches its final objectives, opposing Fallen Empires may challenge its expansion.

These ancient empires can object to the ideological order you are establishing.

### Do you have to destroy every Fallen Empire?

Your goal is to resolve the **required conflicts with opposing Fallen Empires**, not automatically to conquer every Fallen Empire on the map.

Check the Ambition's current victory conditions and any wars that must be settled.

If an opposing Fallen Empire declares war, determine what outcome will satisfy the requirement instead of assuming total conquest is necessary.

### How do you prepare before triggering the final stage?

It is safer to prepare while your economy and diplomatic network are still growing.

A practical preparation sequence is:

1. Keep your main fleets modern and reinforced.
2. Build enough ship production capacity to replace losses.
3. Maintain a stable Alloy and Energy economy.
4. Position fleets to respond to vulnerable borders.
5. Check whether your subjects and allies can provide useful assistance.
6. Avoid beginning unrelated major wars before the expected confrontation.

Force of Will can provide bonuses against Fallen Empires that improve as your Ideology grows.

Those bonuses help, but they do not replace the need for a capable military.

### Is Galactic Contender worth choosing?

**Galactic Contender** can be attractive if you expect to fight powerful Fallen Empires and still have an available Ascension Perk slot.

Its anti-Fallen-Empire combat bonuses can complement Force of Will's own late-game advantages.

However, do not sacrifice an essential economic or strategic Ascension Perk solely for a bonus that may become useful much later.

Compare your fleet readiness and remaining Ascension Perk options before making the decision.

</section>

<section id="victory">

## How Do You Win the Force of Will Victory?

The final objective is to establish a galaxy-wide Utopia under your Ideology.

Two conditions are especially important:

**At least 50% of the galaxy's population must follow your Ideology, and the required Fallen Empire conflicts must be resolved.**

You must also complete the necessary Ambition progression.

<GuideTable
  caption="Requirements for completing the Force of Will victory."
  columns={victoryColumns}
  rows={victoryRows}
/>

### Does 50% Ideological Reach count as victory?

No.

Ideological Reach shows which Ideology dominates particular systems.

The victory condition concerns the population following your Ideology.

A foreign empire may have many systems colored by your Ideology without having converted most of its Pops.

You can also gain a large number of followers from a densely populated foreign empire without controlling its territory.

This is why Cultural Envoys are so important.

### How do you reach 50% galactic followers?

Use several methods together.

**First, convert accessible foreign colonies.**

Target colonies where Cultural Envoys can work effectively, and check actual follower growth.

**Second, expand your ideological alliances and subject network.**

Friendly adopters and Doctrinal Proxies help consolidate political support for your Ideology.

**Third, maintain your own population's commitment.**

If your domestic Pops begin following rival Ideologies, your progress can weaken even as you expand abroad.

**Fourth, avoid wasting conversion effort.**

When a colony already has a high percentage of followers, redirect resources toward populations that remain outside your Ideology.

**Finally, prepare for the required Fallen Empire conflicts before the last conversion push.**

Reaching the population threshold is not enough if an unfinished final conflict still blocks victory.

### Who wins if several empires follow the same Ideology?

**The victory is shared by every empire following the successful Ideology.**

It does not belong exclusively to the empire that started Force of Will.

This creates an unusual diplomatic possibility.

One empire can begin the Ambition, while another empire later becomes Ideological Authority. Both can still share the final victory as followers of the successful Ideology.

The victory screen also identifies the Ideological Authority.

![The final Force of Will Utopia objective showing ideological conversion and the victory conditions.](/images/stellaris-willpower/willpower-force-of-will-victory.webp)

</section>

<section id="best-strategy">

## When Should You Stop Farming Willpower and Focus on Conversion?

Willpower is valuable while it is needed to advance your current Ambition stage. Once the next required unlock is secured, additional income may be less urgent than increasing the number of Pops following your Ideology.

Compare your Ambition progression with the galactic follower percentage. If the remaining problem is population adoption, redirect attention toward colonies where Cultural Envoys can convert substantial numbers of Pops.

A large Willpower reserve does not replace the final conversion requirement. Likewise, reaching the population target does not eliminate unfinished Ambition objectives or unresolved Fallen Empire conflicts.

Keep enough economic and military capacity to finish those remaining objectives rather than continuing to maximize whichever resource was most important earlier in the campaign.

</section>

## What Should You Do After Starting Force of Will?

Once your Ambition is active, choose the next obstacle that actually limits progress.

If you cannot reach the Strength needed for an Absolute Ethic, use the [Ideologies Guide](/stellaris-willpower/ideologies) to diagnose the relevant penalties.

If your next stage offers an additional Tenet, compare its available bonuses and selection costs in the [Tenets Guide](/stellaris-willpower/tenets).

If you are still preparing your empire's early development, return to the [Stellaris Willpower Beginner Guide](/stellaris-willpower).
