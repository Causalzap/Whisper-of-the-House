<GuideQuickAnswer title="What Should You Do First in DRG Survivor: Knockout?">
If you own the **Knockout Expansion**, start by trying the new **Brawler** class with **Crusher**, then explore **Sandblasted Corridors** or attempt the four-stage **Brood Harvest** mission. If you only have the free **Update 1.2**, you can still use **Overtime Upgrades**, complete new secondary objectives, challenge **Hazard 6**, and unlock additional **Weapon Mastery** rewards. Choose your next goal based on whether you want a new class, mission progression, or permanent upgrades.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

**Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor — Knockout** adds several ways to approach a dive, but the paid expansion and the free update do not unlock the same content.

The paid expansion introduces the Brawler, a class that mines and fights with powerful fists, together with a new mission and biome. Update 1.2 expands the base game's progression with Hazard 6, new Mastery rewards, Overtime Upgrades, and additional objectives.

You do not need to learn every new system at once.

If you are returning after completing much of the original game, the free progression changes may be your most useful starting point. If you are beginning the expansion for the first time, learning the Brawler on a familiar mission can make the new challenges easier to understand.

## What Should You Do First After Installing Knockout?

Your first goal should depend on what you have already completed.

For your first Knockout dive, try Crusher on a familiar mission. Pay attention to the gap between Shockwave Emitter attacks and how often you can safely land Bonk. Once those attacks feel manageable, move into Brood Harvest or explore Sandblasted Corridors.

Already comfortable on Hazard 5? Open Weapon Mastery and check which weapon offers a starting-weapon reward you actually want. You can pursue these free Update 1.2 rewards without buying Knockout.

<section id="dlc-vs-update" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Knockout DLC vs Update 1.2: What Is Free?

The **Knockout Expansion** was released on November 17, 2026, alongside free Update 1.2.

Owning the base game gives you the free update. The paid DLC is required for the new Brawler, Brood Harvest mission, and Sandblasted Corridors biome.

<GuideTable
  columns={knockoutContentColumns}
  rows={knockoutContentRows}
/>

Update 1.2 also adds more than 50 community voice lines, alongside additional Anomaly mission choices.

The distinction matters when you cannot find a new class or mission.

Installing Update 1.2 does not automatically grant the paid Knockout content.

On the other hand, you do not need to purchase Knockout to attempt Hazard 6, work toward UltraClock, or access the new Overtime upgrades.

The Brawler also introduces 11 new weapons, some of which can be used by existing classes through the game's Tag system when eligible. You do not have to play Brawler exclusively to benefit from every weapon added with the expansion.

</section>

<section id="brawler" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Start Playing the New Brawler Class?

Once the paid Knockout Expansion is installed and available to your game, open class selection and look for **Brawler**.

Brawler replaces the usual pickaxe mining action with powerful punches. Its Bonk ability also benefits from upgrades carrying the new **[MELEE]** Tag.

The class has three Class Mods:

- **Crusher:** The first available option, with defensive bonuses and Shockwave Emitter.
- **Renegade:** A more mobile option with Plasma weapons and temporary Dodge from Bonk.
- **Enforcer:** A damage-focused option with reduced starting weapon range that can improve through Bonk.

Crusher is available first. Use its Shockwave Emitter to learn close-range combat before unlocking Renegade and Enforcer through Brawler progression.

Once you can choose another Class Mod, compare its starting weapon and Bonk bonuses in the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler builds</Link>.

### Why Are Some Brawler Weapons Still Locked?

Unlocking the class does not make every new weapon immediately available.

Additional weapons and Class Mods become available as you complete the relevant progression requirements.

If a build mentions a weapon that you cannot select, inspect its unlock condition before trying to reproduce that loadout.

You can also use the game's compatible Tag system to access eligible new weapons through other classes once the relevant conditions are met.

</section>

<section id="brood-harvest" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Do You Start Brood Harvest?

**Brood Harvest** is the new four-stage mission included in Knockout.

Select Brood Harvest from your available mission choices, then choose an available sector and difficulty.

The mission centers on fleeing creatures called **Broodlings**. Eliminating them provides **Kytoplasm**, which can earn additional Supply Pods during the dive.

Collecting three Kytoplasm triggers an additional Supply Pod, but the extra support also increases enemy threat.

The final stage is a boss fight against the **Dreadnought Empress**.

Once the Drop Pod countdown begins, stop chasing distant Broodlings. An extra Supply Pod is not worth missing extraction, especially when the horde blocks your return route.

For the four-stage mission and Dreadnought Empress fight, continue with the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brood-harvest">Brood Harvest walkthrough</Link>.

</section>

## How Do You Survive Sandblasted Corridors?

**Sandblasted Corridors** is a mineral-rich desert biome included in Knockout.

Its main danger is not simply a stronger enemy population. The environment changes while you move through it, and several hazards can interfere with your normal mining route.

A valuable mineral deposit may be near a moving storm, an aggressive vine, or a dangerous plant.

Check the surrounding area before stopping to mine.

  rows={sandblastedHazardRows}
/>

### How Do Sand Twisters Work?

Sand Twisters move across the biome and can damage your dwarf if you enter their path.

<figure className="my-6">
  <Image
    src="/images/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/sandblasted-corridors-sand-twister.webp"
    alt="A large Sand Twister moving through Sandblasted Corridors and damaging the dwarf"
    width={1062}
    height={830}
    className="h-auto w-full rounded-lg"
  />
  <figcaption>
    Sand Twisters damage dwarves caught in their path. Keep moving away from the storm before collecting nearby minerals.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

They also move minerals around the environment and leave mineral-rich sand behind them.

That creates a risk-and-reward situation. Following the material displaced by a Twister can help you gather resources, but pursuing it too closely exposes you to damage.

Use this approach:

1. Identify the moving Twister before heading toward nearby minerals.
2. Check its direction rather than running directly behind it.
3. Move around its path and keep enough room to evade a change in direction.
4. Wait until it has moved away before collecting exposed resources.
5. Reorient using visible terrain when the storm disrupts your scanner.

A Twister can interfere with your map equipment when it gets close, so do not rely entirely on the minimap while standing inside the hazard.

The Twister also has a map indicator that can help you identify its movement before you get too close.

If you lose track of your route, move into a safer area and check the terrain again rather than continuing through the storm.

### How Do You Avoid Stabber Vines?

Stabber Vines can pursue you through the terrain instead of remaining fixed in one harmless location.

The danger increases when a vine follows you toward a narrow passage or into another group of enemies.

Avoid standing still near an approaching vine while mining.

Move toward an open route, and watch whether another vine or group of enemies is closing the space you plan to use.

If you are already trapped between terrain and enemies, creating room to escape is more important than collecting a nearby mineral.

### How Do Explosive Spore Plants Work?

Sandblasted Corridors also contains explosive spore plants.

Their bulbs can detonate when approached, damaging nearby targets. The danger affects your dwarf, but explosions can also harm enemies.

<figure className="my-6">
  <Image
    src="/images/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/sandblasted-corridors-hazards.webp"
    alt="Sandblasted Corridors combat near orange spore-like plants with a green blast effect and surrounding enemies"
    width={1142}
    height={694}
    className="h-auto w-full rounded-lg"
  />
  <figcaption>
    Watch for environmental hazards while fighting in Sandblasted Corridors. Nearby enemies and explosive effects can make it difficult to maintain a safe route.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The bulbs can regenerate, so an area that has already experienced an explosion should not automatically be treated as permanently safe.

Watch for the visible detonation warning and move away before the explosion.

If enemies are gathering near an explosive plant, the blast may help clear them. However, do not intentionally remain in the blast radius simply to deal extra damage.

### Is There a Fixed Route Through Sandblasted Corridors?

No. The cave layouts are generated for each dive.

You should not expect the same mineral positions, vine locations, or storm path every time.

Instead, make decisions based on the immediate terrain:

- Avoid entering narrow areas while a moving hazard is approaching.
- Keep a route back toward open space before stopping to mine.
- Collect minerals exposed by Sand Twisters after the danger has passed.
- Recheck the surroundings when the scanner becomes unreliable.

The biome rewards paying attention to environmental movement rather than memorizing one map.

## How Do You Complete the New Fossil and Reactor Core Objectives?

Free Update 1.2 introduces **Fossil collection** and brings back **Morkite Reactor Core recovery** as secondary objectives.

These are not part of the paid Brawler unlock process.

They can appear as additional tasks during compatible dives.

### Where Do You Find All Three Fossils?

When Fossil collection is your secondary objective, look for three fossil deposits distributed around the cave.

The fossils resemble mineable objects protruding from the terrain rather than ordinary enemies or loose experience pickups.

Approach each deposit and mine it to collect the fossil.

Track your progress until all three have been recovered.

Because the map layout changes between dives, the fossils do not have one permanent set of coordinates.

If you have searched most of the map without finding the final fossil, check unexplored terrain and areas you passed while dealing with enemies.

Do not mistake ordinary minerals for objective progress. The collection tracker should reflect the fossils you actually recover.

### How Do You Recover a Morkite Reactor Core?

When the Reactor Core objective appears, locate its mineable deposit.

Unlike the Fossil objective, which requires collecting several separate deposits, Reactor Core recovery focuses on a single location.

The deposit takes longer to mine than an ordinary small mineral pickup.

Clear enough room around it before committing to the mining action.

If enemies are close enough to hit you repeatedly, move away and deal with the immediate threat before returning.

Finish mining the deposit and confirm the objective has updated.

You do not need to search for three Reactor Cores simply because the Fossil objective requires three items.

### Should You Complete Secondary Objectives Before Extraction?

Secondary objectives can be valuable, but they should not cause an otherwise successful run to fail.

If an objective is nearby and you can reach it safely, finish it while you are already collecting resources.

If extraction has started and the remaining objective is on the opposite side of a dangerous horde, prioritize survival.

The main mission and the secondary objective are different completion goals.

## Where Do You Find Overtime Upgrades in Update 1.2?

**Overtime** adds another layer of permanent upgrades for players who want to continue improving their dwarves.

Open the game's Meta Upgrades screen and look for the **Overtime** tab.

  <figcaption>

  </figcaption>

This gives you an additional place to spend accumulated Credits and Meta Minerals.

It is particularly useful if you have been playing for a long time and have already purchased many of the earlier permanent upgrades.

### What Should You Upgrade First in Overtime?

Start by checking what you can currently afford and which upgrades help with the challenges you want to attempt.

Do not spend all your resources without comparing the available bonuses.

One available Overtime upgrade is **Getting Fit**, which increases Max HP. If you repeatedly lose runs after taking contact damage, extra health can give you more room to survive mistakes.

The Overtime screen also includes a Mineral Market and a Refund All option. Check the available upgrades, costs, and current bonuses before changing your purchases.

Choose an upgrade that helps with the challenge you are attempting rather than spending resources simply because they are available.

For a returning player, it is usually more useful to improve a weakness affecting several classes than to spend everything on a benefit relevant to only one experimental build.

### Do You Need Knockout to Use Overtime?

No. Overtime is included in the free Update 1.2.

You can continue earning and spending progression resources without purchasing the new Brawler class or mission.

### Which Artifact Fits Your Current Run?

**D-Tag** becomes more useful when your equipped weapons provide several different Tags, allowing you to earn more XP as the dive continues.

**Rogueish Cravat** is useful while you are still looking for a particular weapon upgrade or Overclock. Its free first reroll gives you another choice without immediately spending resources.

### What About UltraClock?

**UltraClock** is the fourth new Artifact associated with Update 1.2, but it has a different unlock condition.

## How Do You Sell Gear by Rarity Without Losing Items You Want?

Update 1.2 adds a **Sell All of Rarity** option to Gear management.

This allows you to remove multiple unwanted items of the selected rarity instead of selling them individually.

However, the operation depends on your current Gear category.

If you are viewing Armor, the rarity-based action applies to the relevant items in that category. It does not automatically sell every item of the same rarity from unrelated equipment categories.

### How to Sell Gear Safely

Before using the bulk-sell action:

1. Open your Gear inventory.
2. Favorite any unequipped items you want to keep.
3. Check which Gear category is currently selected.
4. Confirm the rarity you intend to sell.
5. Use the bulk-sell action only after reviewing the selection.

Equipped and favorited items are protected by the new operation.

If you want to clean several Gear categories, inspect the wider inventory view rather than assuming a category-specific action affects every item you own.

### Why Did the Bulk Sell Option Miss Some Items?

You may have used the action while viewing only one Gear category.

Check whether the remaining items belong to another category.

If an item is favorited or currently equipped, it should not be included in the ordinary bulk-sell selection.

Do not remove a Favorite marker just to make an item appear in the selection unless you are certain you no longer need it.

## What Do Hazard 6 and Weapon Mastery Unlock?

If you have already completed earlier difficulties, Update 1.2 adds new goals without requiring the paid Knockout Expansion.

**Hazard 6** introduces a higher difficulty where surviving enemies can carry over between stages.

Completing **Hazard 5 Weapon Mastery** unlocks that weapon as a starting option for compatible Class Mods. Completing **Hazard 6 Weapon Mastery** unlocks its UltraClock eligibility.

A regular Hazard 6 clear does not replace the required Weapon Mastery.

For survival tactics, Mastery requirements, starting-weapon selection, and UltraClock troubleshooting, see the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/hazard-6-ultraclock">Hazard 6 and UltraClock guide</Link>.

<section id="common-problems" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Is the Knockout Content Missing or Not Working?

If something from the new release is missing, first identify whether it belongs to the paid DLC or the free update.

### I Updated the Game, but Brawler Is Missing

Brawler belongs to the paid Knockout Expansion.

Check whether you own the DLC, whether it is installed and enabled for the base game, and whether the game has updated correctly.

Installing free Update 1.2 alone does not unlock Brawler.

### I Cannot Find Brood Harvest or Sandblasted Corridors

Both are paid DLC content.

First confirm that Knockout is installed.

Then check the available mission and biome selections. Sandblasted Corridors expands the existing biome choices rather than providing one fixed map destination.

Not every mission selection screen must offer the same biome at the same moment.

### Renegade or Enforcer Is Still Locked

Crusher is available first.

The other Brawler Class Mods require additional progression. Inspect their current in-game unlock requirements rather than assuming purchasing the DLC unlocks all three Class Mods immediately.

Once unlocked, compare their weapons and bonuses in the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler build guide</Link>.

### I Cannot Finish Brood Harvest

The most common decisions involve reaching fleeing Broodlings, collecting enough Kytoplasm, balancing extra supplies against rising threat, and surviving the final Boss stage.

If you are reaching the Empress but failing the fight or extraction, the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brood-harvest">Brood Harvest walkthrough</Link> covers those steps.

### I Finished Hazard 6, but UltraClock Is Not Unlocked

An ordinary Hazard 6 clear does not replace the required Weapon Mastery.

You need the Hazard 6 Mastery completion for the specific weapon.

If you have already met that condition but the Artifact is not appearing, check the eligible equipped weapon and the randomized Artifact offers in the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/hazard-6-ultraclock">UltraClock troubleshooting guide</Link>.

### My Map Is Unreliable in Sandblasted Corridors

Check whether a Sand Twister is close to your dwarf.

These storms can interfere with your scanners.

Move away from the Twister, use visible terrain to orient yourself, and resume your route when the hazard has passed.

<section id="next-steps" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Do After Your First Knockout Runs?

After learning the new content, choose your next objective based on what is stopping your progression.

If your Brawler runs fail because of short range, weak damage, or unsuitable Overclocks, focus on your <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Crusher, Renegade, or Enforcer setup</Link>.

If you can survive ordinary dives but have not defeated the Dreadnought Empress, complete the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brood-harvest">four-stage Brood Harvest mission</Link>.

If you already have comfortable builds and want more permanent progression, challenge <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/hazard-6-ultraclock">Hazard 6 and Weapon Mastery</Link>.

You do not have to complete the paid DLC before using the free update's progression systems.

The best next step is the one that improves your current run: a more reliable weapon setup, a completed mission, a safer route through the biome, or a permanent reward that benefits future dives.

</section>
