<GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Complete Brood Harvest in DRG Survivor?">
In **Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor — Knockout**, enter the **Brood Harvest** mission and kill fleeing **Broodlings** to collect **Kytoplasm**. Every three Kytoplasm collected triggers an additional Supply Pod, but each delivery increases the alien threat, which carries over between stages. Survive all four stages, defeat the **Dreadnought Empress** in Stage 4, and reach the Drop Pod to complete the mission.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

</section>

Brood Harvest rewards aggressive resource gathering, but collecting more Kytoplasm also makes the mission increasingly dangerous. The extra Supply Pods can provide powerful Artifacts, while the enemies become harder to manage with every additional increase in threat.

That creates a different decision from an ordinary dive: **collect enough Kytoplasm to strengthen your build, but do not let the hunt prevent you from reaching the next stage.**

The first three stages are an opportunity to prepare. The fourth begins with the Dreadnought Empress, a boss that retreats, summons additional enemies, and punishes players who stay too close during her attacks.

## How to Start Brood Harvest

Brood Harvest is a mission type included in the paid **Knockout Expansion**. It is not part of the free Update 1.2.

From the mission selection screen, choose **Brood Harvest**, select an available sector and Hazard level, then prepare your class and equipment before starting the dive.

If you are trying the mission for the first time, a lower Hazard gives you more room to learn how Broodlings move and how additional Supply Pods affect enemy pressure.

You do not have to use the Brawler to enter Brood Harvest. The mission can also be attempted with other available classes.

If you use the new Brawler, choose a setup that can catch fleeing Broodlings without leaving you surrounded. For Crusher, Renegade, and Enforcer loadouts, see the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler build guide</Link>.

Unlike ordinary dives, Brood Harvest does not provide the usual stage-based Supply Pods. Your additional Artifact choices come from hunting Broodlings and collecting Kytoplasm, so getting enough supplies depends on how safely you can pursue them.

</section>

## How to Find Broodlings and Collect Kytoplasm

Broodlings are smaller creatures that try to escape instead of engaging you like ordinary enemies.

When you eliminate one, collect the Kytoplasm it drops. The substance appears as a green pickup, and your collection progress contributes toward another Supply Pod.

The challenge is reaching the Broodling without getting trapped by the enemies pursuing you.

### How to Catch Broodlings That Keep Running Away

Approaching a Broodling from directly behind can turn into a long chase, particularly with short-range weapons. While you pursue it, ordinary Glyphids may gather behind you and cut off your return path.

A more effective approach is to influence where the Broodling can escape:

1. Locate the Broodling and check the surrounding terrain.
2. Look for a nearby wall or corner that limits its escape options.
3. Approach from an angle that encourages it to move toward that obstruction rather than across open ground.
4. Attack once it has fewer directions available to escape.
5. Collect the dropped Kytoplasm before continuing toward another target.

A corner can make a fleeing Broodling easier to hit, but it can also trap you if the main horde arrives behind you. Keep an exit available before committing to the chase.

Once a Broodling is down, collect its Kytoplasm only if you still have a safe route away from the surrounding enemies. Do not chase another target so far that you risk missing extraction.

If your Brawler consistently struggles to reach or eliminate Broodlings, check the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler weapon and upgrade choices</Link> before another attempt.

## How Do Kytoplasm Supply Pods and Threat Levels Work?

**Every three Kytoplasm collected triggers another Supply Pod.**

Once you reach the required amount, Management authorizes additional support. Complete the supply event and choose an Artifact to improve your current run.

This is the main reward for continuing to hunt Broodlings beyond the first few kills.

But there is a cost.

Each additional delivery increases the alien threat, and that increased pressure continues into the following stages. You cannot assume that descending will make the enemies return to their earlier strength.

### Should You Keep Collecting Kytoplasm?

The answer depends on what the next Artifact is likely to accomplish for your current build.

Early in a run, another Supply Pod can be particularly valuable. An Artifact that improves damage, experience gain, survival, or another weakness may benefit you throughout the remaining stages.

Later, the same decision becomes more dangerous.

If your weapons already handle regular enemies comfortably, another Artifact may still be useful. But if you are struggling to escape the horde, increasing the threat further can make the remaining stages harder than the reward justifies.

Use three questions before pursuing another Broodling:

1. **Can I reach and eliminate it safely?** A target on the opposite side of a dense horde may not be worth chasing.
2. **Will another Artifact significantly improve my current build?** Look for an upgrade that solves an actual weakness rather than collecting support automatically.
3. **Do I have enough time to reach the Drop Pod?** Once extraction is approaching, surviving takes priority.

There is no need to force the maximum possible number of deliveries during every run.

### Does Threat Level Reset After Each Stage?

No. The additional alien pressure from Brood Harvest carries over when you descend.

That makes Stage 1 decisions relevant to Stage 3 and Stage 4. A run that collects many early Artifacts can become powerful, but it also enters the later stages with a more dangerous enemy population.

During the first three stages, Supply Pod collection is the important source of added mission threat. In Stage 4, the pressure can also escalate as time passes, giving you another reason not to prolong the Empress fight unnecessarily.

### What Happens When You Already Have Enough Artifacts?

At the normal Artifact limit, additional Supply Pods no longer provide the same opportunity to expand your Artifact collection. Further deliveries can instead provide Gold or Nitra.

That makes late Kytoplasm collection less valuable when you already have a complete setup.

If the extra resources do not offer a useful benefit, concentrate on finishing the stage or defeating the Empress instead of taking on more enemies for another delivery.

## Brood Harvest Walkthrough: All Four Stages

Brood Harvest consists of four stages, but the cave layouts are procedurally generated. There is no single fixed path that works for every run.

Use the following progression to decide what matters before moving deeper.

  rows={broodHarvestStageRows}

### Stage 1 — Start Collecting Kytoplasm

Begin by eliminating accessible Broodlings and collecting enough Kytoplasm for your first additional Supply Pod.

Do not spend the entire opening stage pursuing a distant target. You still need experience, minerals, and weapon levels to handle the surrounding enemies.

An early Artifact can help establish your build, but the first stage is also where you decide how much danger you are willing to carry into later stages.

Movement and damage upgrades are useful when your initial weapon has difficulty catching or eliminating Broodlings.

Before leaving, check whether your current weapons are dealing with the growing horde. If ordinary enemies are already forcing you into narrow escape routes, consider ending the stage rather than pursuing another delivery.

### Stage 2 — Improve Damage Without Losing Control

As the mission continues, the enemies reflect the threat accumulated earlier.

Your weapons should now be strong enough to eliminate Broodlings more consistently, but that does not mean you can ignore the surrounding horde.

Continue collecting Kytoplasm when a target is reasonably accessible. Clear space around important pickup locations and avoid being pushed into a corner while attempting to reach a Supply Pod.

Focus upgrades on what is preventing your build from working:

- If Broodlings repeatedly escape, improve usable range or movement.
- If enemies are surrounding you, strengthen area damage or control.
- If your health is dropping too quickly, prioritize survival over another offensive upgrade.
- If your main weapon is close to an important Overclock, consider upgrading it before adding more weapons.

The goal is to enter Stage 3 with a functional build, not simply the largest number of Supply Pod deliveries.

### Stage 3 — Prepare for the Empress

Stage 3 is your final preparation stage before the Dreadnought Empress.

Your most important weapons should now be approaching their stronger upgrades or Overclocks. The remaining decisions should support the final fight rather than introduce unrelated weapon combinations.

A run built around wide plasma coverage, for example, benefits from maintaining reliable damage while the player moves around enemies. A shorter-range setup must solve its positioning and survival problems before the boss appears.

Check your health and movement speed. The Empress is not a stationary target, and reaching her safely matters as much as having enough damage.

You may still collect Kytoplasm for more support, but compare the reward with the increasing threat. Carrying an unnecessarily dangerous horde into the final stage can make positioning much harder.

When the Drop Pod arrives, do not miss extraction while chasing one more Broodling.

### Stage 4 — Defeat the Dreadnought Empress

The final stage starts with the Dreadnought Empress as the main objective.

Broodlings can still provide Kytoplasm during the final stage, allowing further Supply Pod deliveries. However, the threat can continue increasing as time passes, so extending the fight solely to collect more resources is risky.

Keep your primary damage focused on the Empress whenever she is accessible, reposition when she attacks, and clear surrounding enemies when they obstruct your movement.

Once she is defeated, head for the Drop Pod to finish the mission.

## What to Check Before Entering Stage 4

Before taking the Stage 3 Drop Pod, check whether your current setup can handle the Dreadnought Empress without forcing you into dangerous positions.

- **Moving target:** Can you keep damaging the Empress when she retreats, or do you have to follow her into the horde?
- **Surrounding enemies:** Can you clear enough space to move when her cocoons release additional enemies?
- **Remaining health:** Can you survive a missed dodge or an unexpected attack?
- **Accumulated threat:** Is another Kytoplasm Supply Pod worth bringing additional enemy pressure into the final stage?

If your weapons cannot damage a retreating target reliably, use the remaining preparation time to improve that weakness. If your health is already low, prioritize surviving the transition rather than hunting another distant Broodling.

For Brawler-specific weapon combinations and Overclock decisions, see the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Crusher, Renegade, and Enforcer builds</Link>.

## How to Beat the Dreadnought Empress

The Dreadnought Empress is dangerous because she combines evasive movement, defensive behavior, and additional enemies.

Simply chasing her and dealing damage at close range can leave you exposed to acid or trapped by her brood.

The safest positioning depends on your weapons, but you should maintain enough room to move away whenever an attack begins.

### Dreadnought Empress Attacks and Counters

### Keep Moving When She Uses Acid

The acid attack is one of the clearest reasons not to stand beside the Empress continuously.

Circle around her while maintaining damage, but watch for the attack rather than committing to a fixed movement pattern.

When she starts projecting acid toward your position, move away from its path. Do not remain inside the danger area merely to finish a weapon upgrade or collect nearby minerals.

### Manage the Cocoons and Summoned Enemies

The Empress can deploy cocoons that create additional enemies, including Elites and Exploders.

These enemies interfere with your movement and can turn a manageable boss fight into a crowded escape.

Avoid treating every summoned enemy as a separate target that must be pursued immediately. Instead, concentrate on those that threaten your current route.

If a group blocks your path around the Empress, use your area damage to create an opening before returning to the boss.

Weapons with reliable crowd coverage can maintain damage during these interruptions, particularly when the Empress is retreating or temporarily protected.

### Do Not Chase Through Her Defensive Spikes

The Empress can surround herself with protective spikes.

When this happens, forcing a short-range attack through the defensive formation may expose you to unnecessary damage.

Move to a safer position and continue managing the surrounding enemies until you can attack effectively again.

Maintaining a clear path is more important than staying in contact with the boss throughout every moment of the encounter.

### Watch Her Retreat and Reappearance

The Empress may charge away after taking damage, forcing you to close the distance again.

Do not run directly along her escape path without checking the enemies between you and her.

She can also burrow underground before appearing again. If she disappears while the fight is still active, keep moving and look for her return rather than assuming the mission has ended.

Resume attacking once she becomes accessible and your position is safe.

### When Should You Stop Collecting Kytoplasm?

Once the Empress is taking steady damage, keep her within your weapons' effective range instead of running after every Broodling.

A nearby Broodling may still be worth killing if the route is clear and another Supply Pod could improve a critical weakness. But when the Empress retreats, cocoons block your movement, or her remaining health is low, focus on finishing the fight.

The longer you delay, the more dangerous Stage 4 can become. Do not extend the encounter for an Artifact that your build no longer needs.

## What Happens After You Defeat the Empress?

Defeating the Dreadnought Empress completes the main combat objective, but **you must still reach the Drop Pod**.

Once the boss is eliminated, Mission Control calls for extraction and warns you to leave.

Do not stay in the arena to farm ordinary enemies or gather unnecessary minerals while the extraction timer is running.

Instead:

1. Identify the Drop Pod location.
2. Move toward it immediately.
3. Avoid getting trapped by the remaining enemies.
4. Enter the Drop Pod before it departs.
5. Check the post-dive results for successful mission completion.

A successful dive can also advance relevant weapon, class, and other progression objectives, depending on what you achieved during that run.

If you defeated the boss but missed the Drop Pod, do not treat the boss kill alone as confirmation of a completed dive.

## Why Are You Struggling with Brood Harvest?

Brood Harvest failures usually become easier to diagnose when you identify which part of the mission is stopping your progress.

### Broodlings Keep Escaping

Try approaching from an angle that pushes them toward nearby terrain instead of following directly behind.

Improve movement speed or usable weapon range when available. Do not chase one target indefinitely while enemies gather around you.

### You Are Not Getting Additional Supply Pods

Check whether you are collecting the Kytoplasm dropped by Broodlings. Each set of three contributes to another Supply Pod delivery.

Brood Harvest does not follow the ordinary one-pod-per-stage pattern. Look for the Kytoplasm objective and support notification rather than waiting for the usual supply event.

### Enemies Become Too Strong in Later Stages

The threat from additional supplies persists between stages.

If you are collecting every possible delivery during Stages 1 and 2, try a more selective approach on your next run.

Build sufficient damage and survival first, then collect extra Kytoplasm when the reward remains worth the danger.

### You Cannot Reach the Drop Pod

Stop pursuing Broodlings as the extraction countdown approaches.

The moving enemy horde and your position relative to the Drop Pod are more important than another small resource gain.

Keep a clear movement route and use mining or available terrain openings when necessary.

### You Cannot Keep Damaging the Empress

If the Empress repeatedly moves beyond your attack range, stop chasing her through dense enemies. Reposition toward a safer opening and resume attacking when she is accessible.

If this happens throughout the fight, review the <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler range and weapon setups</Link> before retrying.

### You Die Before Defeating the Empress

Identify whether the damage comes from her acid attack, summoned enemies, or a blocked escape route.

Move away from the acid path, clear enemies that prevent you from repositioning, and avoid remaining beside the Empress when another attack begins.

## What Should You Do After Completing Brood Harvest?

After extracting, check the mission result and think about which part of the final stage was most dangerous.

If the Empress survived for too long, improve the damage or range that failed to reach her during retreats. If you struggled to escape the summoned enemies, adjust your movement and crowd coverage before the next dive. The <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/brawler-build">Brawler builds</Link> show different weapon combinations for those problems.

When Brood Harvest no longer presents a serious challenge, <Link href="/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/hazard-6-ultraclock">Hazard 6 and Weapon Mastery</Link> offer a different test of your equipment and survival.
