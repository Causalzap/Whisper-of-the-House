<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
**Liberation has three separate stages.** First, mark Iron Nest's new position,
locate the three infantry targets described by the opening field reports, and
prepare all three firing solutions before buying more ammunition.

After those targets are destroyed, use the issued reconnaissance card over
**P4**. The new image reveals an underground supply cache that must be destroyed
with **AP**. Two infantry units then remain inside a friendly urban sector.

Photograph the largest uncovered part of that sector, locate both final targets,
and check the friendly positions around each one. Use H for the isolated target
and AP for the danger-close target when an H blast would reach friendly or
protected icons.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="liberation-route">
  Liberation route at a glance
</h2>

Liberation is where I stopped treating every new requisition option as
permission to buy something.

The mission begins by authorizing ammunition procurement, but it does not reveal
all five infantry positions or the later AP target at once. Buying a full set of
shells at the opening can leave extra ammunition on the final result without
making any stage easier.

The safe route is:

1. Read the new Iron Nest coordinate and all three field reports.
2. Locate the first three infantry positions.
3. Prepare three separate firing cards.
4. Buy only the H shells and powder charges those shots require.
5. Destroy the opening infantry and read the next order.
6. Use the issued reconnaissance card over P4.
7. Destroy the underground supply cache with one AP shell.
8. Photograph the uncovered friendly urban sector.
9. Locate the final two infantry targets.
10. Use H for the safe target and AP when the other target is danger-close.
11. Prepare both guns and destroy the final pair.
12. Wait for the sector-clear and stand-down message.

The general map tools, firing calculator, shell roles, and loading controls stay
in the <Link href="/iron-nest">Iron Nest guide hub</Link> The full
spotter-intersection method is covered in the
<Link href="/iron-nest/fire-and-light-walkthrough">
  Fire and Light walkthrough
</Link>
This page focuses on the decisions that are specific to Liberation.

<h2 id="mark-new-iron-nest-position">
  Mark Iron Nest's new position before solving anything
</h2>

The opening order states that Iron Nest has been repositioned to support the
operation.

That makes the previous mission's firing origin obsolete.

In one operation, the new Iron Nest coordinate was approximately **I6 5:3**.
The observation assets appeared at different points around the map, including
positions in the I9 and E1 sectors.

Do not leave the old Iron Nest marker on the table. A target can be correctly
located from the field reports while the final shell still misses because the
red firing line begins at the previous mission position.

My first actions are:

1. Delete the old Iron Nest marker.
2. Place the new marker at the coordinate printed in the Liberation order.
3. Place all three spotters.
4. Read the adjacent field reports.
5. Label Infantry 1, 2, and 3 separately.

Only the final target-to-Nest measurements should be copied into firing cards.

<h2 id="locate-opening-infantry">
  Locate the first three infantry targets
</h2>

The opening reports provide enough information to locate three infantry
positions.

They may use different combinations of:

- two reported bearings;
- a bearing and a distance;
- two distances with more than one possible intersection.

Do not rebuild the entire map between each target. Solve one target, place a
numbered marker, delete that target's temporary construction lines, and move to
the next report.

When a report creates two possible points, use the mission description and
urban-sector boundaries to reject the impossible intersection.

Do not fire at the first plausible point when:

- the two lines meet at a very shallow angle;
- the target appears outside the designated urban sector;
- one solution overlaps a friendly or protected position;
- a more certain opening target can reveal useful reconnaissance first.

Liberation has no opening Counter-Battery Timer. Taking another minute to
confirm a weak intersection is safer than buying a replacement shell after a
guess.

<h2 id="prepare-opening-firing-cards">
  Prepare all three opening firing cards
</h2>

Once Infantry 1, 2, and 3 are marked, draw a final line from the current Iron
Nest position to each target.

One operation produced these opening solutions:

- **289.5° / 6.93 km / 2 charges / 41.58° elevation**
- **284.8° / 7.20 km / 2 charges / 43.20° elevation**
- **253.4° / 4.91 km / 1 charge / 58.92° elevation**

These numbers belong to one operation. Your own coordinates and bearings can
change.

They show why buying ammunition first is a poor habit. Two targets required two
charges, while the shorter 4.91 km shot used only one.

For each card, write:

- Infantry number;
- turret bearing;
- range;
- charge count;
- elevation;
- intended gun side.

Do not rely on the cards' order on the clipboard. A card can move or be read
from the wrong side once both guns are being prepared.

<h2 id="destroy-first-three-infantry">
  Destroy the first three infantry without rushing the sequence
</h2>

Use H shells for the normal opening infantry positions.

Buy only enough shells to complete the three confirmed shots. If issued
ammunition already covers part of the route, do not replace it merely to make
both magazines look symmetrical.

I start with the target that has:

- the most certain position;
- the safest surrounding area;
- the least risk to friendly or protected icons;
- a useful reconnaissance area around its impact.

After every shot:

1. Wait for the hit confirmation.
2. Read any updated reconnaissance image.
3. Check whether another target marker needs correction.
4. Delete the completed firing line.
5. Move to the next prepared card.

Do not fire all three shells back-to-back when the first impact could clarify an
uncertain target.

In one route, the first two targets were close enough in bearing to prepare on
different guns, while the third shot required a wider rotation. The important
decision was not maximum firing speed; it was keeping the gun side, firing card,
and loaded charge count matched.

<h2 id="p4-recon">
  Use the issued reconnaissance card over P4
</h2>

After the opening infantry are destroyed, return to the teleprinter.

Command issues a reconnaissance-plane card and orders an overflight of
**grid P4**. The same order tells you to confirm the new target and neutralize
it with AP.

This is a required mission stage, not optional medal cleanup.

Take the card to the requisition console and choose a flight line that passes
cleanly through P4.

A straight north-south flight at approximately **0°** covered the grid in one
operation. The exact line matters less than ensuring the photographed strip
actually crosses the designated square.

Do not:

- spend normal requisition on a second flight before the issued card resolves;
- aim an AP shell at the center of P4 without seeing the target;
- search the old opening photographs for the supply cache;
- assume the P4 objective is one of the original five infantry.

Wait for the developed image, then mark the newly revealed priority target.

<h2 id="destroy-underground-cache">
  Destroy the underground supply cache with AP
</h2>

The P4 image reveals a supply cache marked:

- `Priority`
- `Underground`
- `AP Required`

Now purchase one AP shell.

In one operation, the firing solution was approximately:

- **101.7° bearing**
- **6.86 km range**
- **2 powder charges**
- **41.16° elevation**

This is not the place to substitute a larger H blast. The target card explicitly
requires AP.

Before firing, verify:

- the cache marker is centered on the confirmed icon;
- the final line begins at the repositioned Iron Nest;
- AP is loaded into the selected gun;
- the loader contains two charges for the example solution, or the exact count
  shown by your own firing card;
- the selected gun is set to the matching elevation.

After the hit, return to the teleprinter. The cache-destroyed report states that
**two hostile infantry units remain in the urban sector**.

<h2 id="find-final-two-infantry">
  Find the final two infantry in the friendly urban sector
</h2>

The final infantry are not necessarily visible in the existing photographs.

The order only narrows them to a friendly urban sector. That is why players can
destroy the first three infantry and the supply cache, then assume the mission
has stopped progressing.

Clear the old P4 line and inspect the map coverage.

Look for:

- urban blocks without a current reconnaissance image;
- friendly-sector areas outside the original opening photos;
- blank strips between developed images;
- sectors that still have friendly or hospital icons but no visible enemy.

Use one reconnaissance flight across the largest uncovered section.

One successful search began near **B3** and used a flight bearing of
approximately **341°**. That route is an example, not a fixed answer. The useful
decision is to photograph the missing urban strip in your own map state.

Once both infantry icons appear, place separate target markers and inspect the
friendly positions around them.

<h2 id="choose-h-or-ap-final-targets">
  Use H for the safe target and AP for the danger-close target
</h2>

The final two infantry do not necessarily require the same shell.

In one operation:

- one target was isolated enough for H;
- the other sat among friendly positions and was handled with AP;
- attempting to use one large area shot for both would have created unnecessary
  casualty risk.

The example firing solutions were:

- **270.5° / 7.80 km / 2 charges / about 46.8°** for the H target;
- **258.8° / 8.31 km / 2 charges / 49.86°** for the AP target.

Do not copy those settings. Use them to understand the workflow.

For the first target, use H when:

- its marker is clearly confirmed;
- the surrounding block is free of friendly or protected icons;
- the H impact area does not reach the nearby urban positions.

For the second target, use AP when:

- the infantry coordinate is precise;
- friendly units surround the marker;
- moving an H aim point away from the friendlies would put the enemy at the edge
  of the blast;
- the smaller AP impact gives a safer direct hit.

AP is not required because the infantry is armored. It is chosen because this
specific target is danger-close.

Do not use AP for an uncertain marker. Its smaller impact area removes the
margin that makes H useful against normal infantry.

<h2 id="prepare-final-two-guns">
  Prepare both guns before firing the final pair
</h2>

Once both targets and shell choices are confirmed, prepare the final pair
together.

A clean sequence is:

1. Load H into the left gun.
2. Load AP into the right gun.
3. Load the calculated charges for each side.
4. Set both gun elevations.
5. Rotate to the H target.
6. Confirm the left gun and fire.
7. Wait for the casualty report.
8. Rotate directly to the AP target.
9. Confirm the right gun and fire.
10. Wait for the sector-clear message.

Do not fire the danger-close AP shot first when the safer H target can confirm
that the final route and friendly positions have not changed.

In the completed route, the first shot produced no casualties. The turret then
rotated from approximately 270.5° to 258.8° for the precise AP shot.

This is one of the first campaign moments where the gun-side check matters as
much as the map solution. Before pulling the second lever, confirm:

- right gun selected;
- AP loaded;
- right-side elevation displayed;
- two charges loaded;
- final marker still centered on the infantry icon.

<h2 id="minimum-assets">
  Protect the minimum-assets result
</h2>

Liberation rewards buying only what the current stage needs.

A completed run missed the top minimum-assets result after making an extra shell
purchase near the end. The exact Gold threshold was not shown, so I treat every
unnecessary item as a possible penalty.

The low-waste route is:

- opening: buy only the H shells missing from the three confirmed solutions;
- P4 stage: use the issued reconnaissance card;
- cache stage: buy exactly one AP shell;
- final search: use one well-placed reconnaissance flight;
- final pair: buy one H and one AP only after both targets are confirmed;
- stop purchasing immediately after both guns are prepared.

Do not buy:

- AP during the opening infantry phase;
- several backup shells before the P4 image develops;
- a second scout flight before checking the first image;
- extra ammunition after the final two guns are loaded;
- shells after `Sector clear` appears.

The goal is not to finish with empty magazines. The goal is to avoid paying for
assets that never contribute to a confirmed objective.

<h2 id="liberation-completion">
  Wait for Sector Clear before standing down
</h2>

The second final shot should produce the completion order:

- `Sector clear`
- local garrison will consolidate;
- Iron Nest is to stand down pending further orders.

At that point:

- stop requisitioning ammunition;
- do not photograph more of the city;
- clear the active firing cards;
- close the gun controls;
- return to the bunk.

The mission is not complete when the supply cache is destroyed. It is not
complete when the final two infantry merely become visible. It ends after all
five infantry and the priority cache are destroyed and command issues the
stand-down order.

<h2 id="liberation-stuck">
  Liberation mistakes that block or weaken the clear
</h2>

### Reusing the previous Iron Nest position

Liberation begins after repositioning. Replace the firing-origin marker before
measuring the first target.

### Buying AP at the beginning

The first three targets are normal infantry. The confirmed AP requirement
appears after the P4 reconnaissance.

### Buying ammunition before calculating all three opening shots

The example route used two, two, and one charge. Calculate first so you do not
procure unnecessary powder or shells.

### Searching for all five infantry at the start

Only the first three are part of the opening field-report problem. The last two
become a separate urban reconnaissance stage after the cache.

### Skipping the P4 reconnaissance flight

The issued overflight reveals the underground priority target. Do not fire at a
guess inside P4.

### Firing H at the underground cache

The cache is explicitly marked `AP Required`.

### Assuming the final two infantry failed to spawn

They may be outside the current photographed area. Scan the largest uncovered
part of the friendly urban sector.

### Using H beside a hospital or friendly formation

Use H for the isolated target. Use a precise AP shot when the larger blast would
reach protected positions.

### Trying to hit both final infantry with one area shell

The pair can be separated by enough distance, friendly positions, or urban
objects that one centered H shot is unsafe.

### Buying one more shell as insurance

A late extra purchase can weaken the minimum-assets result even when the mission
is completed.
