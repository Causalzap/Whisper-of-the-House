<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start by completing **Pebble and Milo's first quests** to unlock your home and Project Station. Follow Hazel's farming tasks, expand your Garden Beds through **Amani**, then help **Cliff, Milo, and Ryder** to unlock and craft your Glider. Once Cliff clears you for travel, take the **Airtram to Hanging Gardens**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The first obstacle in Loftia City is usually a missing ingredient, not a difficult fight. Milo needs several kinds of salvage, Hazel needs crops delivered, and Cliff won't open the route to Hanging Gardens until you've built a Glider. Follow those tasks in sequence so you don't use up the materials required by the next craft.

<h2 id="first-quests">
  What Should You Do First in Loftia?
</h2>

After creating your character and arriving in Loftia City, speak to **Pebble**. He introduces you to the city and sends you to **Milo**, who works with salvaged materials.

Your first substantial task is **A Fresh Start**. Milo wants you to collect:

- **5 Screws**
- **5 Plastic Discards**

Start in the **Scrap Quarter**, around the area Milo directs you toward. Search the discarded objects and salvage piles rather than buying anything. Screws and Plastic Discards are early resources you can collect without an upgraded Hammer.

Once you've delivered those items, Milo gives you another collection task:

- **5 Clay** — Construction Site
- **5 Scrap Wire** — Solar Farm

These two resources are easy to confuse with other salvage.

The Construction Site has clay piles around its work area. At the Solar Farm, search the electrical salvage objects for Scrap Wire. Metal Scraps are a different resource, so finding those doesn't mean you've completed the wire objective.

Open your map with **M** if you're unsure where to go. Hover over the resource markers to see what can be gathered at each location. You can also right-click a map position to place a waypoint that remains visible while exploring.

Return to Milo when you have the requested materials. He gives you the **Project Station**, which becomes the foundation of your early crafting.

<h2 id="first-home">
  How Do You Unlock Your House and Start Crafting?
</h2>

After finishing Milo's collection tasks, return to Pebble. He directs you to the **hot air balloon**, which takes you to your Neighborhood Island.

Find your assigned home, then follow Pebble's instructions to place your new Project Station and bed.

The Project Station is where you'll make your early tools, processing stations, and useful equipment. You will also receive access to a Battery and a Small Solar Panel.

Your first practical priorities are:

1. Place the **Project Station** where you can reach it easily.
2. Craft your starting **Hammer** using the materials you've gathered.
3. Clear the nearby litter and collect resources as Pebble requests.
4. Use the Small Solar Panel to recharge your Battery before additional crafting.

You don't need to arrange your furniture perfectly yet. Keep the Project Station, your first energy source, and your storage close together so you aren't constantly running around your island while completing the opening quests.

<h3 id="battery-energy">
  Why Can't You Craft When You Have Enough Materials?
</h3>

Crafting in Loftia uses **Battery charges** rather than a traditional character stamina bar.

Each crafting or processing action has an energy cost. If your Battery is empty, the station cannot complete the craft even if you already have every required material.

To restore energy, interact with your Small Solar Panel or another available energy source and recharge your Battery. Then return to the crafting station.

Charging speed can vary with the solar panel's current efficiency. When you're making several items, recharge between crafts rather than emptying your Battery and forgetting about it.

Later, you can expand your energy production and Battery capacity. For now, your first Small Solar Panel is enough to get through the opening tasks if you recharge regularly.

<h3 id="storage">
  Should You Craft More Storage Chests?
</h3>

Make sure you have access to personal storage before gathering large quantities of scrap.

Loftia uses storage and inventory capacity limits. You do not need to build a separate chest for every material category. Expand your storage capacity as it becomes available instead of filling your house with unnecessary duplicate containers.

Your inventory and storage limits are separate. When both are full, excess items can end up on the ground, so move spare materials into storage before another long collection trip.

Keep some Screws, Plastic Discards, Scrap Wire, Metal Scraps, and Scrap Wood available. These materials continue to appear in early crafting requirements.

<h2 id="first-sprout">
  How Do You Complete The First Sprout?
</h2>

After settling into your home, **Hazel** introduces farming.

The First Sprout is more than a planting tutorial. It teaches you how to grow food, sell crops, cook meals, and increase the number of Garden Beds you can place.

Start by placing your first Garden Bed and planting **4 Lettuce Seeds**.

If the seeds don't appear in your equipped tools, hold **R** to open the Tool Wheel and switch to the seed selection. You can also assign commonly used tools to number keys for quicker access.

Craft a **Watering Can** at your Project Station and water the lettuce.

Your crops show their growth and watering status. If the water runs out, growth stops until you water them again. You can refill your can at the Rain Collector on your island once available, or use a public one in Loftia City.

Harvest the four Lettuce when they're ready.

<h3 id="shipping-cooking">
  How Do You Ship Crops and Cook Carrot Soup?
</h3>

Hazel's tasks introduce the **Shipping Bin** in your neighborhood.

Place your harvested Lettuce in the Shipping Bin and follow the current delivery objective. Shipping qualifying crops earns **Dew**, the currency used for seeds, building permits, equipment, and other purchases.

The next part introduces Carrots and Cooking.

1. Plant **4 Carrot Seeds** and water them until they're ready to harvest.
2. Follow the cooking introduction and craft a **Cooking Station**.
3. Prepare **2 Carrot Soup**.
4. Deliver both soups through the Shipping Bin.

During cooking, follow the on-screen interaction to keep the stirring indicator in the correct area.

Don't spend all your early Dew on decorative items while finishing this sequence. You will shortly need to purchase a Building Permit.

Once you have some spare money and want a repeatable income method, <Link href="/loftia/money-making">see how to earn Dew through farming and cooking</Link>.

<h2 id="garden-bed-permit">
  How Do You Get a Second Garden Bed?
</h2>

Your starting island has room for one Garden Bed, with four planting spaces. The First Sprout eventually asks you to expand beyond that limit.

Speak with **Amani** at the **Permits Office** in Loftia City.

Building Permits determine how many items of certain types you may place on your island. Garden Beds, Energy Stations, and other placeables have separate allowances.

Purchase the Garden Bed permit upgrade when the task directs you to do so. This increases your allowance from **one Garden Bed to two**, giving you eight planting spaces.

Return home and use your Project Station to craft the second Garden Bed. Gather the required Clay and Scrap Wood from the Construction Site if you're short.

Place it next to your existing bed and continue Hazel's instructions.

The farming task now asks you to use all eight planting spaces, with:

- **4 Lettuce Seeds**
- **4 Carrot Seeds**

Plant them and report back to **Amani** when the objective updates.

This is a significant progression point. The story moves from helping your neighborhood to responding to the instability affecting Loftia's districts.

You can also buy additional permits later, but you don't need to maximize every building allowance before moving forward.

<h2 id="permission-to-glide">
  How Do You Unlock Your Glider in Loftia?
</h2>

Following The First Sprout, a stronger tremor raises concerns about the other districts. You're directed toward **Cliff**, who handles travel and safety.

The next major task is **Permission to Glide**.

Cliff won't clear you for inter-district travel without a functioning Glider. He sends you to Milo, who needs you to prepare more advanced crafting materials.

<h3 id="refining-station">
  Step 1 — Gather Materials and Make a Refining Station
</h3>

Milo asks you to collect:

- **5 Metal Scraps**
- **5 Scrap Wood**
- **5 Plant Fiber**
- **5 Tires**

You can find Scrap Wood around the Construction Site, Plant Fiber from vegetation in The Hills, and Tires around the city's salvage areas, including the Solar Farm.

Use the map's resource markers rather than searching randomly across the entire city.

Once you've collected the materials, return to Milo and follow his instructions to make a **Refining Station**.

Craft the station using your Project Station, place it on your island, and interact with it.

Process the four materials requested by the quest:

1. Metal Plate
2. Plank
3. Straw
4. Rubber

These are processed materials, not the original salvage pickups. Gathering the raw ingredients alone will not satisfy Milo's processing objective.

Wait for the station to finish, collect the results, and return to Milo.

<h3 id="ryder-adventure">
  Step 2 — Help Ryder Before Returning to Cliff
</h3>

Milo works on the improved Glider blueprint while you assist **Ryder** at the Adventure Dock.

Ryder needs **3 Scrambled Coordinates** to locate a Research Island.

These can appear while salvaging resources in Loftia City. Continue gathering from suitable salvage objects until you have three, then return to Ryder.

Ryder introduces you to an Adventure, a timed activity involving repairs, exploration, and other objectives.

Follow his introductory Adventure and return to him with the results. After the required story sequence, the Glider blueprint becomes available through Cliff.

After Ryder's introductory Adventure, check **Stories** and return to Cliff when the Glider objective becomes active.

<h3 id="glider-recipe">
  Step 3 — Craft the Glider
</h3>

Return to **Cliff near the Dome** when the story directs you there.

He provides the Glider blueprint. The Glider requires:

- **3 Rubber**
- **3 Straw**
- **5 Glass Cullets**

Rubber is processed from Tires, while Straw is processed from Plant Fiber. If you used your earlier supplies during Milo's refining task, gather and process more.

For Glass Cullets, visit the **Solar Farm** and salvage Microwaves.

With the materials ready, return home and craft the Glider at your **Project Station**.

If the craft is unavailable, check whether you have already received Cliff's blueprint and whether your Battery has enough energy.

After crafting the Glider, jump and press **Space again while airborne** to deploy it on keyboard.

Practice on safe ground before trying to cross a large gap. The Glider gives you much more freedom to move between elevated platforms, but you still need to control your landing.

Return to Cliff after making the Glider to continue the story.

<h2 id="hanging-gardens">
  How Do You Reach Hanging Gardens?
</h2>

After the Glider sequence, the district investigation becomes your next objective.

The earthquakes have affected Hanging Gardens, where much of Loftia's food production takes place. Cliff asks you to travel there and check the damage.

Meet **Cliff at the Airtram Station** and take the Airtram to **Hanging Gardens**.

When you arrive, speak to **Herb** near the entry area.

This is where the next stage of your journey begins. Hanging Gardens introduces new gathering locations, Fishing, Research Stations, and tasks connected to the region's damaged environment.

You can use the Airtram to travel back to Loftia City, so you don't have to finish every City side quest before continuing.

After arriving, speak to Herb and follow the objective that leads to Kassim's research tasks.

<h2 id="early-upgrades">
  Which Upgrades Should You Prioritize Early?
</h2>

Your first upgrades should remove the delays you are actually experiencing. If crafting stops after a few actions, recharge your Battery and plan for an additional energy source when its Permit is available. If you run out of room during salvage trips, increase storage before collecting another large batch.

For **Skill Points**, Salvaging and Foraging bonuses are useful during the opening quests because they affect materials you're already collecting. Save more specialized bonuses until you begin using those activities regularly. Higher-level Hammer and Pruners recipes are unlocked later through Research; <Link href="/loftia/research-and-tool-upgrades">check their requirements</Link> once Kassim introduces the stations.

<h2 id="stuck">
  What Should You Check If Your Progress Is Stuck?
</h2>

Several early problems come from missing an objective rather than needing a rare item.

<h3 id="missing-seeds">
  You Have Seeds but Can't Plant Them
</h3>

Hold **R** to open your Tool Wheel and look for the seed selection. Seeds can be available there even when you aren't holding them as your current tool.

Check that you're standing by an available Garden Bed before trying to plant.

<h3 id="second-bed-locked">
  You Crafted a Second Garden Bed but Can't Place It
</h3>

Visit Amani at the Permits Office and check your **Garden Bed placement allowance**.

Crafting a bed doesn't override your building limit. You must have the permit that allows two Garden Beds.

<h3 id="crafting-disabled">
  Your Crafting Station Won't Work
</h3>

Check three things: the recipe is unlocked, the required materials are available, and your Battery has sufficient energy.

If you're trying to process Metal Plates, Planks, Straw, or Rubber, use the **Refining Station**, not just your Project Station.

<h3 id="glider-locked">
  Cliff Hasn't Given You the Glider Recipe
</h3>

Return to your current story objective. Finish Milo's Refining Station task and Ryder's introductory Adventure, then speak to Cliff again.

Having some Rubber and Straw in your inventory does not automatically unlock the Glider recipe.

<h3 id="lost-in-city">
  You Can't Find the Next NPC or Location
</h3>

Open the **Stories** section of your menu to see your current objective, then press **M** to locate the relevant city area.

If you're physically stuck or need to return to the beginning of the area, the menu includes a **Teleport to Start** option.

<h2 id="what-next">
  What Should You Do After Reaching Hanging Gardens?
</h2>

Speak to **Herb** at the arrival area, then follow the task pointing you toward **Kassim**. His fishing assignment leads to the district's Research Stations. Your original house remains available for crafting and crop growing, so return when you need materials or energy.

If Dew becomes the obstacle, use the <Link href="/loftia/money-making">early money-making methods</Link>. Later, Joanna's task lets you <Link href="/loftia/pets">rescue your first dog</Link>; you don't have to complete that rescue before speaking to Kassim.
