<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Reach **Hanging Gardens** and speak to **Joanna** during Kassim's Field Research quest. Accept **Notes From the Fauna**, investigate **five dog tracks**, and follow the clues to the lost dog's Pet Bowl near **The Grove**. Bring **4 Lettuce and 2 Carrots** to lure out the dog, then adopt it and return to Joanna.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Joanna's missing dog won't appear until you investigate its tracks. The search itself is short; the avoidable mistake is reaching the Pet Bowl without the vegetables it accepts. Bring the food, follow the Dog Location Piece counter to five, and finish by reporting back to Joanna after adoption.

<h2 id="unlock-pets">
  When Can You Unlock Your First Pet in Loftia?
</h2>

The first pet becomes available in **Hanging Gardens**, the second district you reach after completing Loftia City's opening quests and unlocking your Glider.

You won't find the dog adoption task immediately upon creating your character.

After arriving in Hanging Gardens, follow the district's main investigation until **Kassim** asks you to gather information from the local residents during **Field Research**.

As part of that investigation, you'll need to help **Joanna**. She cares for animals that haven't found a permanent home, and one of the pets she was looking after disappeared shortly before the earthquakes.

Speak to her and agree to help.

This starts **Notes From the Fauna**, the quest that leads to your first dog.

If you haven't reached Hanging Gardens, complete the Glider and Airtram objectives in the <Link href="/loftia/beginner-guide">Loftia beginner guide</Link> first.

<h2 id="joanna">
  Where Do You Find Joanna and Start Notes From the Fauna?
</h2>

During Kassim's **Field Research**, follow the resident objective to Joanna in Hanging Gardens. Talk to her about the missing foster dog and accept **Notes From the Fauna**. The next task is to look for disturbed ground; you don't need a pet house or shop purchase to begin.

<h2 id="dog-tracks">
  Find the Five Dog Location Pieces
</h2>

You need **5 Dog Location Pieces** before you can identify the missing dog's hiding place.

These are collected by investigating clues in Hanging Gardens. Look for small patches of disturbed earth rather than expecting the dog to be standing in the open.

Look for **Dog Tracks** interactions in disturbed dirt. The clue messages mention freshly dug ground and mushrooms, and each successful interaction increases the **Dog Location (Piece)** counter. Follow the counter rather than relying on five different landmark descriptions: the rescue location appears after **5/5**.

You don't have to collect these clues in a particular order. Keep searching the nearby paths and vegetation for interactive dirt patches, especially around mushrooms.

The clue interactions aren't ordinary salvage. Inspect the correct patch and check whether your Dog Location Piece count has increased.

After collecting all five, you can locate the missing dog near **The Grove**.

<h3 id="missing-clues">
  What Should You Do If You Can't Find the Last Dog Track?
</h3>

If the counter is stuck at **3/5** or **4/5**, return to the last confirmed patch and inspect the nearby edges of the path, particularly around disturbed ground and mushrooms. A normal salvage pickup does not advance this counter. When **5/5** appears, the next objective is the Pet Bowl near The Grove; further searching for tracks won't help.

<h2 id="dog-food">
  Feed the Lost Dog: 4 Lettuce and 2 Carrots
</h2>

The missing dog's Pet Bowl is near **The Grove** in Hanging Gardens, tucked around the trees.

Finding the bowl isn't enough to adopt the dog. You must also provide the food needed to tempt it out.

Bring these exact ingredients:

- **4 Lettuce**
- **2 Carrots**

If you already have the vegetables, interact with the bowl and provide the requested food.

If you're short, return to your Neighborhood Island and grow what you need.

You can purchase Lettuce and Carrot Seeds from **Hazel's Seed Shop in Loftia City**. Use your Garden Beds, water the crops, and collect the harvest before returning.

These are ordinary early-game crops, so there's no reason to spend time upgrading tools or searching for rare ingredients just to rescue the dog.

A useful way to avoid a second trip is to carry the four Lettuce and two Carrots before starting the track search.

After providing the food, continue the adoption interaction.

<h2 id="adopt-dog">
  Name and Equip Your Small Dog
</h2>

Once the dog has been lured out, Loftia lets you give it a name.

You can choose whatever name you like. The adoption interface also indicates that the name can be changed later, so there's no need to delay the rescue while choosing a permanent one.

After naming the dog, select the option to **equip your pet**.

The equipped dog can accompany you as you travel around Loftia.

You don't need to buy another pet or build a habitat before using your new companion. The adoption and equipment steps are enough to begin exploring together.

Before returning to other activities, however, there is still one quest objective left.

<h2 id="report-joanna">
  Return to Joanna to Finish Notes From the Fauna
</h2>

Adopting the dog doesn't automatically finish **Notes From the Fauna**.

Return to **Joanna** in Hanging Gardens and tell her you've rescued the missing animal.

She recognizes that the dog has chosen you as its new Loftian companion.

The conversation also reveals an important detail for the ongoing investigation: animals appeared to sense the tremors before the earthquakes reached Hanging Gardens.

This information contributes to Kassim's Field Research.

Completing the rescue and reporting back provides:

- **Coastal Overalls**
- **Basic Habitat recipe**
- Progress toward the district's Field Research investigation

The Basic Habitat recipe gives you another way to make your pet feel at home on your island, but it isn't a prerequisite for adopting the dog.

If the wider Field Research quest remains active, continue with the other residents Kassim asked you to help. The pet rescue completes Joanna's part of the investigation, not every task in Hanging Gardens.

<h2 id="pet-customization">
  Pet Styles, Accessories, and Emotes
</h2>

After equipping your dog, open the game's **Customization** menu to view its available appearance options.

Pets can use different styles, clothing, and accessories. The available choices depend on what you've unlocked.

For the Small Dog, customization options include different looks as well as accessories such as headwear.

Select the option you own and apply it to your pet. You can adjust the appearance later without repeating the rescue quest.

If an item in a shop says you haven't unlocked the corresponding pet, that means the cosmetic is not itself an adoption unlock. Finish the pet's rescue first.

You can also interact with the dog using pet emotes.

Open the **Emote Wheel** and use the mouse scroll wheel to switch to the pet-emote selection on keyboard and mouse.

From there, choose an available pet interaction. You can pet your dog and use other unlocked reactions.

Your rescued dog can also gain friendship as you spend time together, so it isn't simply a decorative object that stays on your island.

<h2 id="pet-not-following">
  Why Isn't Your Dog Following You?
</h2>

Open **Customization → Pets** and check that **Small Dog** is selected rather than the empty slot. If you named the dog but skipped the Equip prompt, choose it here; there is no reason to gather another set of tracks or refill the Pet Bowl.

A separate problem is an unfinished **Notes From the Fauna** objective. Even with your dog equipped, return to **Joanna** to complete the rescue report and obtain the quest reward.

<h2 id="next-pet">
  How Do You Start Looking for Another Pet?
</h2>

After finishing Notes From the Fauna, Joanna introduces another missing animal in **Fur Real?**.

This time, the animal is more elusive. Instead of simply following disturbed earth in Hanging Gardens, you must search for clues while **salvaging and foraging in Loftia City**.

The quest requires **10 Cat Location Pieces** before you can identify where the cat is hiding.

Check that Fur Real? is active before gathering resources specifically for those clues.

You can continue collecting resources and completing other tasks while searching. The clues are connected to ordinary gathering activity, so you don't need to abandon every other objective to stand in one spot.

Your first dog can remain your companion while you work toward adopting another animal.

If you're still working on Kassim's Field Research, finish the remaining resident objectives and return to him with the notes. The dog rescue completes Joanna's portion of the investigation.
