<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Complete **Fish Are Friends** in Hanging Gardens and speak to Kassim to unlock the Research Stations. At **Central Lake Research Station**, finish **Anadromous Odyssey** to unlock your Lv.2 crafting stations. Then complete **Heavy-Weight Titans** for Hammer Lv.2 or **Murky Waters** for Pruners Lv.2, gather the crafting materials, and make the upgraded tool at Project Station Lv.2.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

If your Project Station is already Lv.2 but Hammer Lv.2 is still locked, the station itself isn't the problem: its tool recipe comes from a different fish Bundle. The same separation applies to Pruners Lv.2, which also need processed materials from a higher-level Refining Station. Start at **Central Lake Research Station** and follow the unlock you actually need.

<h2 id="unlock-research">
  How Do You Unlock Research Stations in Loftia?
</h2>

Research Stations become available during the **Hanging Gardens** investigation.

After arriving in the district, follow Kassim's research tasks at the Floating Village Research Center. He first introduces the local fish and asks you to make a **Collector Rod**.

Complete **Fish Are Friends** by crafting the rod, catching the requested fish, and reporting your results to Kassim.

The Collector Rod is necessary for the early fish donations, so don't try to finish the Central Lake bundles before completing this introduction.

Kassim then explains that the fish may be carrying traces connected to the strange changes affecting Loftia. To investigate further, he gives you access to three Research Stations around Hanging Gardens.

These are:

- **Central Lake Research Station** — fish collections, crafting stations, and tool upgrades.
- **Community Farms Research Station** — crop collections, farming tools, and equipment.
- **Overgrowth Research Station** — local plants, materials, and other research rewards.

Each station has its own bundles. Submitting the required items unlocks recipes, equipment, or further research pages.

Simply arriving in Hanging Gardens doesn't automatically open every bundle.

Once the stations are enabled, your next priority should be Central Lake.

<h2 id="central-lake">
  Which Central Lake Research Bundles Should You Complete First?
</h2>

Find **Central Lake Research Station** in the Hanging Gardens lake area, near the large balloon landmark.

The station has several pages of research. The first is **Anadromous Odyssey**, which introduces the fish contribution system and unlocks the crafting stations needed for stronger equipment.

The next page contains the Hammer and Pruners upgrades.

**Making a Lv.2 tool starts with Anadromous Odyssey.** After that, the two routes diverge: Heavy-Weight Titans supplies the Hammer recipe; Murky Waters supplies the Pruners recipe. The following steps distinguish the recipe unlock from the materials and station upgrades required to craft it.

<h2 id="anadromous-odyssey">
  How Do You Unlock Project Station Lv.2?
</h2>

Start with **Anadromous Odyssey** at Central Lake Research Station.

This first collection requires four fish:

1. Allis Shad
2. Atlantic Salmon
3. Atlantic Sturgeon
4. Rainbow Trout

Use your Collector Rod to catch the fish and contribute them to their matching slots.

The Floating Village fishing area is useful for the migratory fish, while Rainbow Trout can also be found in lake fishing pools.

You need the actual fish items, not simply a record showing you've caught each species before.

Once the four donations are complete, the station unlocks **Project Station Lv.2**. Completing the first research page also provides the **Refining Station Lv.2** upgrade.

These unlocks are important because they allow the next stage of crafting, but they do not automatically produce an upgraded Hammer or Pruners.

<h3 id="upgrade-project-station">
  How Do You Upgrade the Project Station?
</h3>

Return to your Neighborhood Island and interact with your existing Project Station.

The Lv.2 upgrade requires:

- **3 Copper Scraps**
- **5 Screws**
- **5 Scrap Wire**
- **7 Battery charges**

Open the crafting interface and use the station's **Upgrade** option once you have the required materials and blueprint.

The upgraded station gives access to more advanced equipment and recipes you've unlocked.

**Project Station Lv.2 does not automatically unlock Hammer Lv.2.**

If the upgraded station still shows the Hammer recipe as unavailable, you need to complete **Heavy-Weight Titans** separately.

That distinction also applies to Pruners Lv.2.

<h2 id="hammer-level-two">
  How Do You Unlock Hammer Lv.2 in Loftia?
</h2>

Hammer Lv.2 is particularly important during the Hanging Gardens investigation because it can break tougher obstacles and shake fruit from trees without damaging them.

When the Overgrowth orchard task asks you to clear blocked paths or gather fruit samples, a basic Hammer will not be sufficient.

The recipe comes from **Heavy-Weight Titans** at Central Lake Research Station.

<h3 id="hammer-research">
  Step 1 — Complete Heavy-Weight Titans
</h3>

After opening the station's second page, find Heavy-Weight Titans and collect:

- **Seventyfour Seabream**
- **Nassau Grouper**
- **Giant Sea Bass**
- **Southern Bluefin Tuna**

These are fish associated with the deeper lake pools in Hanging Gardens.

Use your Collector Rod and check the available catches at the deeper fishing areas. Different spots and catch tiers can produce different fish, so repeatedly fishing from the same unsuitable pool may not give you the missing species.

The **Nassau Grouper** and **Southern Bluefin Tuna** are particularly important to check if you've been contributing fish but still cannot unlock the Hammer.

Complete the bundle and confirm that you've received the Hammer Lv.2 recipe.

Having Project Station Lv.2 and one or two of these fish is not enough. The required fish contributions must all be complete.

<h3 id="hammer-materials">
  Step 2 — Gather the Hammer Lv.2 Materials
</h3>

Once the recipe is unlocked, prepare:

- **1 Hammer** — your existing basic tool.
- **10 Rattan**
- **10 Copper Scraps**
- **2 Rubber**

You also need **7 Battery charges** for the final craft.

Rubber is made in the Refining Station from Tires.

At Refining Station Lv.1, one Rubber requires **5 Tires**, so two Rubber require ten Tires if you make them without any extra-output bonuses.

Gather the Rattan and Copper Scraps separately. These are used directly in the Hammer recipe, so don't refine all your Copper Scraps into bars.

That mistake can leave you with plenty of processed metal but too few of the raw scraps needed for the Hammer.

<h3 id="craft-hammer">
  Step 3 — Craft Hammer Lv.2
</h3>

Return to your Project Station Lv.2 after preparing the materials.

Select **Hammer Lv.2**, check the ingredients, recharge your Battery if necessary, and craft it.

Your existing Hammer is consumed as part of the upgrade. You receive the Lv.2 tool rather than keeping an additional basic Hammer.

After crafting it, equip the new Hammer from your tool selection.

The upgraded Hammer is strong enough for tasks that the original tool cannot complete.

In Hanging Gardens, this includes clearing the Overgrowth route and interacting with certain fruit trees for the orchard investigation.

If you're completing that quest, return to the relevant area and use your new Hammer to gather the required samples. Continue the district task with the NPC who requested them.

<h2 id="pruners-level-two">
  How Do You Unlock Pruners Lv.2?
</h2>

Pruners Lv.2 follow a similar research process, but the materials are more demanding because they require advanced refining.

These upgraded Pruners are useful for cutting sturdy vegetation, including Bamboo, and gathering materials unavailable to your original Pruners.

<h3 id="murky-waters">
  Step 1 — Complete the Murky Waters Bundle
</h3>

At Central Lake Research Station, open the second research page and select **Murky Waters**.

Contribute:

1. Spring Pygmy Sunfish
2. Dalhousie Catfish
3. Moapa Dace
4. Red Handfish

These fish are associated with the darker, forested freshwater pools in Hanging Gardens.

If you've been fishing around the Floating Village without finding them, change to the forest fishing areas rather than repeating the same route.

Complete all four donations to unlock the **Pruners Lv.2 recipe**.

You may now have the recipe while still being unable to make the materials it asks for.

The next step is upgrading your Refining Station.

<h3 id="refining-station-two">
  Step 2 — Upgrade Your Refining Station to Lv.2
</h3>

You receive the Refining Station Lv.2 upgrade from the first page of Central Lake research, alongside the earlier Project Station progression.

At home, interact with your existing Refining Station and check its Upgrade option.

To upgrade it, prepare:

- **10 Screws**
- **2 Metal Plates**
- **2 Planks**
- **7 Battery charges**

Metal Plates are refined from Metal Scraps, while Planks are made from Scrap Wood.

At the basic Refining Station, producing two of each takes ten raw Metal Scraps and ten Scrap Wood without additional-output bonuses.

Process those materials first, then upgrade your Refining Station.

Once it's Lv.2, you can make Copper Bars and Flexible Lumber.

<h3 id="copper-flexible-lumber">
  Step 3 — Make Copper Bars and Flexible Lumber
</h3>

Pruners Lv.2 require two of each processed material.

**Copper Bar**

One Copper Bar requires 5 Copper Scraps at Refining Station Lv.2.

For two bars, prepare **10 Copper Scraps**.

**Flexible Lumber**

One Flexible Lumber requires 5 Rattan at Refining Station Lv.2.

For two pieces, prepare **10 Rattan**.

Both processing recipes consume Battery energy. Keep enough charges available to finish the required batches.

These are different from the raw materials used for Hammer Lv.2. You cannot substitute Copper Scraps directly for Copper Bars or Rattan directly for Flexible Lumber in the Pruners recipe.

<h3 id="craft-pruners">
  Step 4 — Craft Pruners Lv.2
</h3>

With the materials prepared, return to Project Station Lv.2.

The final recipe requires:

- **1 Pruners**
- **2 Copper Bars**
- **2 Flexible Lumber**
- **7 Battery charges**

Craft the upgrade and equip it.

Your original Pruners are used in the recipe, leaving you with the stronger version.

Now you can return to areas containing sturdier plants and gather resources that were previously inaccessible.

<h2 id="other-research">
  What Other Research Rewards Are Worth Unlocking?
</h2>

Central Lake is not limited to Hammer and Pruners upgrades.

Its other bundles unlock useful stations that affect building, clothing, and more advanced material production.

**Craftcore** requires Plank, Plastic Sheet, Metal Plate, and Rubber.

Completing it unlocks the **Carpentry Station Lv.2 recipe**, opening additional building and decoration options.

**Eco Weave** requires Plant Fiber, Rattan, Jute Fabric, and Linen.

Completing it unlocks the **Tailoring Station Lv.2 recipe**.

These materials are a mixture of raw resources and processed products. Check the exact names at the station before submitting anything.

If the bundle asks for Linen, for example, raw Flax is not a replacement for the processed item.

<h3 id="community-farms">
  How Do You Unlock the Advanced Watering Can?
</h3>

The **Advanced Watering Can** comes from a different Research Station.

Travel to **Community Farms Research Station** in Hanging Gardens and complete the **Seedy Stories** bundle.

It requires:

- Eggplant
- Tomato
- Watermelon

The reward is the Advanced Watering Can recipe.

To craft it at Project Station Lv.2, prepare your basic Watering Can, 2 Plastic Sheets, and 2 Metal Plates.

This upgrade is useful if you spend a lot of time growing crops, but you don't need to finish it before making Hammer Lv.2.

If your current quest needs a stronger Hammer to open a route, prioritize that task before spending materials on optional equipment.

<h2 id="which-upgrade-first">
  Should You Upgrade Your Hammer or Pruners First?
</h2>

Look at your current objective before spending the Copper Scraps and Rattan you have collected. **The blocked orchard path** needs Hammer Lv.2, which consumes those resources in raw form. **Bamboo and tougher plants** call for Pruners Lv.2, but the recipe uses Copper Bars and Flexible Lumber instead, so you must budget for Refining Station Lv.2 and the extra processing.

If both upgrades are available, keep enough raw Copper Scraps for the Hammer before turning the rest into bars. That avoids completing one tool and discovering you've processed the exact material needed by the other.

<h2 id="locked-upgrades">
  Why Are Your Tool Upgrades Still Locked?
</h2>

There are several different situations that can produce what looks like the same problem.

<h3 id="research-locked">
  The Research Station Won't Accept Contributions
</h3>

Check your current Hanging Gardens research quest.

You must progress through Kassim's introductory tasks and receive access to Research Stations.

If you're still working on Fish Are Friends, finish the Collector Rod and fishing objectives before returning.

<h3 id="project-station-upgraded">
  Project Station Lv.2 Is Unlocked but Hammer Lv.2 Is Missing
</h3>

Open Central Lake Research Station and inspect **Heavy-Weight Titans**. If any fish slot is incomplete, that is why Hammer Lv.2 is absent from the Project Station—even if the station already shows Lv.2.

<h3 id="second-research-page">
  You Can't Find Heavy-Weight Titans or Murky Waters
</h3>

Both bundles are on the second page of Central Lake Research Station.

Complete the first page's Anadromous Odyssey collection and advance to the next research page.

Don't keep searching the Community Farms or Overgrowth stations for those two fish collections.

<h3 id="materials-not-available">
  You Have the Pruners Recipe but Can't Make Copper Bars
</h3>

Check your Refining Station level.

Copper Bars and Flexible Lumber require **Refining Station Lv.2**, not just Project Station Lv.2.

Upgrade the Refining Station first, then process the materials before attempting the Pruners craft.

If the Refining Station upgrade itself is unavailable, verify that you've completed Central Lake's first research page and have the required upgrade materials.

<h3 id="wrong-materials">
  You Have Copper Scraps but the Recipe Still Shows Missing Ingredients
</h3>

Check the ingredient icon and label at the Project Station. **Copper Bar** means the Copper Scraps must first go through Refining Station Lv.2; if the recipe asks for **Copper Scraps**, do not refine them. The same distinction applies to Rattan and Flexible Lumber.

<h3 id="battery-empty">
  All Materials Are Ready but Craft Is Disabled
</h3>

Check the Battery Energy displayed in the crafting interface.

The Lv.2 tools require **7 Battery charges** each.

Refining materials and upgrading stations also consume energy. You may have enough materials but lack the charges needed for the next action.

Recharge your Battery using an available energy station and try again.

<h3 id="original-tool">
  The Upgrade Recipe Is Still Missing One Item
</h3>

Both Lv.2 tool recipes require the previous version.

Hammer Lv.2 consumes one basic Hammer. Pruners Lv.2 consumes one basic pair of Pruners.

Check that you still own the required tool and that the recipe is showing the expected item as available.

You are upgrading an existing piece of equipment, not crafting a second copy from raw materials alone.

<h2 id="later-upgrades">
  When Can You Get Lv.3 Tools?
</h2>

Hammer Lv.3 and Pruners Lv.3 belong to a later stage of the game.

Their recipes come from the **Museum Research Station in Gridfall**, rather than Central Lake.

You'll also need Project Station Lv.3 and later materials, including Electronic Scraps and Rust Resistant Alloy.

You cannot obtain these upgrades just by finishing more of the same Hanging Gardens fish bundles.

For now, complete the Lv.2 upgrades you need and continue the Hanging Gardens investigation. If the district's quests have directed you toward Overgrowth, your new Hammer is the tool to bring.

Return to the active Hanging Gardens objective in **Stories** after equipping the required tool, then report the result to the NPC who requested it.
