<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Check **Stories** for the current objective: finish Pebble and Milo's opening tasks in Loftia City, build the Glider to reach **Hanging Gardens**, and help Kassim investigate fish, plants, and wildlife. Once the district investigation points toward Gridfall, prepare **Bounce Shoes** and follow the next active task. If you're not advancing the story, use the **Daily Summary** to choose an activity.

Loftia's story sends you through several districts, but it rarely asks you to finish every collection or decorate your entire island before moving on. The quickest way to identify your next step is to read the active objective in **Stories** and see whether it calls for an NPC, an item, or a new area.

<h2 id="what-to-do-first">
  What Should You Do First in Loftia?
</h2>

Begin with **Pebble** and **Milo** in Loftia City. Milo's salvage tasks lead to the Project Station; Pebble then sends you to your Neighborhood Island, where you'll place your first equipment. If a recipe won't craft, check the Battery before gathering more materials.

Your next major destination is Hanging Gardens, but Cliff won't send you there until you've completed the Glider preparation tasks. For the exact material counts and NPC order, use the <Link href="/loftia/beginner-guide">Loftia City opening route</Link>.

<h3 id="first-farming-tasks">
  Why Do Hazel's Farming Quests Matter?
</h3>

Hazel's early tasks show you how to plant and ship crops. If you have made a second Garden Bed but can't place it, visit **Amani** and check the Garden Bed Permit limit. Once the required planting and delivery objectives are complete, return to the story rather than buying every available farming upgrade.

<h2 id="region-order">
  What Is the Main Story Progression Order in Loftia?
</h2>

Loftia's Early Access story spans three major regions:

**Loftia City → Hanging Gardens → Gridfall**

Each introduces new tasks, resources, and equipment requirements. You don't need to complete every optional activity in one region before traveling to another.

The most important distinction is between a quest that advances the investigation and an activity that improves your home, collection, or equipment.

When you're unsure what to do next, open your **Stories** menu and check the current objective.

If it asks for a specific tool, NPC, or research result, work toward that requirement before spending time on unrelated upgrades.

<h2 id="glider">
  How Do You Unlock Hanging Gardens?
</h2>

**Cliff won't open the Airtram route until you have a Glider.** Work through Milo's Refining Station task and Ryder's introductory Adventure, then collect Cliff's blueprint and craft the Glider. Speak to Cliff again at the Airtram Station to leave Loftia City. The <Link href="/loftia/beginner-guide">Glider instructions</Link> include the required materials and how to obtain them.

<h3 id="hanging-gardens-first">
  What Should You Do When You Arrive in Hanging Gardens?
</h3>

Speak to **Herb** at the arrival area, then follow the investigation to **Kassim** at the Floating Village Research Center. Craft the Collector Rod and finish his first fishing assignment. When Kassim opens the Research Stations, you can begin working toward upgraded crafting equipment.

<h2 id="research">
  What Should You Research and Upgrade First?
</h2>

A locked station, a missing recipe, and insufficient crafting materials are three different problems. If you cannot submit research items, finish Kassim's fishing introduction first. If Project Station Lv.2 is available but Hammer Lv.2 isn't, complete its separate Central Lake fish bundle. Need Bamboo instead? That points to Pruners Lv.2.

Check the <Link href="/loftia/research-and-tool-upgrades">research and tool requirements</Link> for the exact bundles, processing steps, and materials; don't spend raw Copper Scraps until you know whether the recipe needs scraps or bars.

<h2 id="field-research">
  What Do You Do During Kassim's Field Research?
</h2>

Kassim eventually asks for observations from residents outside the Floating Village. Follow the **Field Research** markers, complete each resident's assigned task, and return with their notes. The errands differ: **Joanna** needs help recovering a missing dog, while **Hazel** and the Overgrowth investigation concern changes to crops and the environment.

Joanna's task can end with your first companion; <Link href="/loftia/pets">the dog rescue steps</Link> explain the tracks, Pet Bowl, and food requirement. If the orchard path is blocked, Hammer Lv.2 is the relevant upgrade. Once the requested field notes have been collected, take them back to Kassim rather than trying to complete every optional research bundle.

<h2 id="gridfall">
  How Do You Progress Toward Gridfall?
</h2>

After the Hanging Gardens investigation, a lookout conversation reveals problems in **Gridfall**. Before the next stage, the story calls for **Bounce Shoes**. Their recipe is tied to later Central Lake research, and the craft uses processed materials, including Aluminum Bars and Banana Leather.

If the recipe is still locked, review the Research Station's page rewards; if you already have the recipe, inspect the crafting requirements and recharge your Battery. When the shoes are ready, return to the story objective shown in **Stories**. Do not assume crafting them alone completes the transfer to Gridfall.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Why Can't You Progress to the Next Area?
</h2>

Work backward from the missing requirement rather than repeating the same action:

- **Airtram unavailable:** Finish Milo's refining work and Ryder's introduction, craft Cliff's Glider, then speak to Cliff.
- **Research Station locked:** Report the initial fishing results to Kassim before attempting Bundle donations.
- **Hammer or Pruners recipe missing:** Check the specific Central Lake fish Bundle; upgrading the Project Station alone doesn't grant either tool recipe.
- **Enough materials but Craft is disabled:** Look at the Battery charge and the station level, then verify whether the recipe needs a refined item.
- **Short of Dew:** Sell a small harvest or prepare Fruit Smoothies; compare the costs in the <Link href="/loftia/money-making">Dew earning methods</Link>.
- **No obvious main objective:** Check **Stories** for a return conversation. For optional activities, open the **Daily Summary**.

<h2 id="daily-summary">
  What Can You Do Each Day in Loftia?
</h2>

Loftia has activities beyond its main storyline.

Some involve gathering resources or completing objectives with other players. Others provide a reason to return to familiar areas.

The **Daily Summary** is a useful starting point when you don't have a specific quest in mind.

Rather than checking every NPC and activity board individually, use it to see which opportunities are currently available.

A practical routine is to look at your current story objective first, check your crops and Battery, then choose an activity from the Daily Summary if you have time.

You don't have to complete everything each time you play.

<h2 id="world-projects">
  Community World Projects and Maintenance
</h2>

**World Projects** are community-wide efforts where Loftians contribute resources toward improvements to the shared world.

Some involve large construction or restoration initiatives, rather than tasks for a single player's house.

Projects can ask for collected materials, crafted items, or other contributions.

To participate:

1. Find an available World Project through its project information or the relevant project area.
2. Check which materials or items are currently requested.
3. Gather or prepare eligible contributions.
4. Submit them through the appropriate project interface.
5. Check your contribution progress and available rewards.

Examples of community initiatives include improvements around Loftia City, celebrations, and infrastructure projects.

**World Maintenance** involves smaller efforts that help maintain or improve an area over time.

These activities can provide rewards and support community progress, but they're not a substitute for completing your current story objective.

If you're saving materials for a required tool upgrade, don't donate the last of them without checking what you'll need next.

<h2 id="adventures">
  Adventures: Entry Cost and Co-op Objectives
</h2>

Adventures are timed activities that can be completed with other players.

Instead of following the ordinary story across the main districts, you enter a separate area with a collection of objectives.

These can involve exploration, repairs, finding objects, and solving puzzles before time runs out.

You'll encounter the Adventure system during the early tasks with Ryder.

After completing the relevant introduction, ordinary Adventure entry requires **3 Scrambled Coordinates**.

You can obtain Scrambled Coordinates while gathering and salvaging resources.

Before entering, make sure you've brought the required Coordinates and have time to finish the activity.

Some puzzles require players to work together. If you encounter an objective that involves several simultaneous actions, communicate with your group rather than attempting everything alone.

Adventure rewards can include:

- Dew
- Clothing Scraps
- Blooms
- Other activity rewards

Blooms can be used for certain housing-related items and customization rewards.

Adventures are a useful change of pace when you want multiplayer activities or additional rewards between story quests.

<h2 id="skills">
  Skill Points: Useful Early Bonuses
</h2>

Many activities in Loftia contribute experience toward associated skill categories.

For example, salvaging increases your Salvaging progression, while gathering plants contributes to Foraging.

As you gain skill points, you can unlock improvements that make those activities easier or more rewarding.

At the beginning, **Salvaging and Foraging** are useful choices because the early story repeatedly sends you to collect raw materials.

Bonuses that improve the amount you gather or increase carrying capacity can reduce repeated collection trips.

Other categories become more useful as you spend time with their activities:

- **Farming:** If you frequently grow crops at home.
- **Cooking:** If you're preparing food for Dew or quests.
- **Fishing:** If you're completing research and fish collections.
- **Crafting:** If you're making large quantities of equipment and materials.
- **Research and Community activities:** If you spend time on contributions and shared objectives.

You don't have to invest evenly in every category.

Choose improvements that support what you're currently doing, rather than unlocking an expensive perk simply because it's available.

<h2 id="housing">
  Housing Upgrades Without Stalling the Story
</h2>

You receive access to your home early in the story, and it remains useful throughout your time in Loftia.

Your Neighborhood Island is where you can grow crops, process materials, cook meals, and arrange decorations.

You can spend a great deal of time expanding and personalizing it, but the main story doesn't require you to finish decorating before visiting the next district.

Early on, focus on what helps you play:

**Crafting space:** Keep your important stations easy to reach.

**Energy:** Make sure you can recharge your Battery between crafts.

**Storage:** Increase capacity when gathering materials becomes inconvenient.

**Garden Beds:** Expand when you're ready to invest more Dew and time in Farming.

Later, you can spend more resources on furnishings, architecture, landscaping, and decorative items.

If you're planning a larger renovation, check the required building allowances and available recipes before purchasing materials.

<h2 id="collections-memories">
  Collections and Memories
</h2>

Loftia includes two related ways to track your activities and completion progress.

**Collections** encourage you to discover the game's available resources, equipment, recipes, outfits, pets, housing items, and other things you can obtain.

Your collection grows as you find and unlock more of the world.

**Memories** are Loftia's achievement-style objectives.

They reward progress across different parts of the game, including story advancement, crafting, gathering, fishing, and other activities.

If you enjoy completing everything, check your Memories and Collections regularly instead of waiting until the end of the story.

You may discover that a task you're already performing contributes toward another objective.

However, you don't need to finish every Memory or Collection before entering Gridfall.

Treat them as longer-term goals that can continue alongside the story and your favorite activities.

<h2 id="after-main-story">
  What Should You Do After Finishing the Main Story?
</h2>

With the available story objectives completed, you can pursue activities that don't depend on the next chapter: finish Research Bundles for equipment you skipped, build out your Neighborhood Island, or chase missing **Memories** and **Collections**. The Daily Summary is a quicker way to find current community activities than visiting every NPC again.

If you prefer multiplayer challenges, return to Adventures with enough Scrambled Coordinates. If you want to make progress alone, check a specific missing recipe, collection entry, or home improvement and gather what it requires. Neither choice depends on completing every optional objective in another district.

<h2 id="faq">
  Loftia Progression FAQ
</h2>

<h3 id="faq-chapters">
  Does Loftia Have Chapters?
</h3>

Yes. The Early Access story spans multiple chapters across Loftia City, Hanging Gardens, and Gridfall.

For practical progression, follow the specific quest objective and region you're currently in. Equipment requirements and NPC tasks are usually more useful than chapter numbers when you're trying to solve a problem.

<h3 id="faq-coop">
  Do You Have to Play With Friends?
</h3>

No. Loftia offers many activities you can enjoy while exploring or working on your home independently.

However, some Adventure challenges include cooperative puzzles, so playing alongside others can make those activities easier or necessary for particular objectives.

<h3 id="faq-farming">
  Can You Focus on Farming Instead of the Story?
</h3>

Yes. You can spend time planting, harvesting, cooking, and improving your island once those systems are available.

Progressing through the story unlocks additional districts, materials, and crafting options, so returning to the main quests eventually helps expand what you can do.

<h3 id="faq-tools">
  Do You Need Every Tool Upgrade?
</h3>

Not immediately.

Upgrade a tool when a quest requires it or when you want access to resources your current equipment cannot collect.

Hammer Lv.2 is a notable example because it becomes necessary for part of the Hanging Gardens investigation.

<h3 id="faq-currency">
  What Is the Main Currency in Loftia?
</h3>

The everyday currency is **Dew**, used for purchases such as seeds, ingredients, permits, and other items.

You can earn it by shipping eligible crops and cooked food, among other activities.

<h3 id="faq-progress">
  How Do You Know What to Do Next?
</h3>

Open the **Stories** menu, identify the active objective, and check whether it requires an NPC conversation, a crafted item, or another activity.

If the objective is blocked by a missing tool or ingredient, solve that requirement first.

If you'd rather do something different for a while, the Daily Summary can help you find another activity without losing track of your story progress.
