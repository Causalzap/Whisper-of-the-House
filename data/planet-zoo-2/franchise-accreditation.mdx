<GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Raise Franchise Accreditation">
Open the **Planet Hub** in Franchise Mode and check **Accreditation**. Raise your first zoo to **3 Zoo Reputation Stars** to unlock Tier 2, then return to Animal Trading to see the newly available species.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A new Franchise zoo can be making money, keeping its animals healthy, and welcoming visitors while most of the Animal Trading list remains locked. More research or a bigger habitat will not necessarily change that list.

In **Planet Zoo 2**, Franchise Accreditation controls how many species you can access across your network. Tier 1 begins with 32 species. Your first major unlock comes from building a zoo with **3 Reputation Stars**, and the later tiers require additional qualifying zoos and Wildlife Reserves.

The important part is understanding which rating counts, how to improve it without spending all your money, and when a new location becomes necessary.

## Franchise Accreditation Requirements for All Five Tiers

From your Franchise's Planet Hub, select **Accreditation** to see the requirements for the next tier.

The five tiers require the following total number of qualifying locations:

  columns={franchiseTierColumns}
  rows={franchiseTierRows}
/>

Every zoo counted toward these requirements must have **at least 3 Zoo Reputation Stars**. Every Wildlife Reserve counted must have **at least 3 Biodiversity Stars**.

These are totals across your Franchise network. Tier 4 does not require two additional zoos after Tier 3; the same two qualifying zoos continue to count. What changes is the number of qualifying Wildlife Reserves.

That distinction becomes important later. Raising one zoo from three to five stars cannot replace the second three-star zoo required for Tier 3. The same applies to Wildlife Reserves: one highly developed reserve cannot satisfy the requirement for three separate reserves.

### What unlocks at each tier?

The additional species are only part of the reward.

- **Tier 1:** 32 starting species, the Observation Platform, and 1 Bronze Marketing Slot.
- **Tier 2:** 33 more species, the Camera Trap, and 2 additional Bronze Marketing Slots.
- **Tier 3:** 31 more species, the Acoustic Monitor, 1 Bronze Marketing Slot, and 1 Silver Marketing Slot.
- **Tier 4:** 30 more species, 50 additional Animal Storage spaces, 1 Bronze Marketing Slot, and 1 Silver Marketing Slot.
- **Tier 5:** The final 10 species, 1 Silver Marketing Slot, and 1 Gold Marketing Slot.

Across all five tiers, the initial roster contains **136 species**, including Deluxe Edition species. Those additional animals still require the appropriate edition to access them.

The rewards build on your previous unlocks. Reaching Tier 3 does not take away the species made available at Tier 2.

## Why Are So Many Animals Locked in Franchise Mode?

Animal Trading is limited when you first establish a Franchise.

You might have enough money for a particular habitat and still be unable to obtain the animal you want. Opening the Zoopedia can reveal that the species requires a higher Accreditation Tier.

![The Zoopedia showing African Leopard locked until Accreditation Tier 3 and Asian Elephant until Tier 5.](/images/planet-zoo-2/franchise-accreditation-locked-animals.webp)

At the beginning, check three things before spending money on an enclosure:

1. **Accreditation Tier:** Does your Franchise currently allow this species?
2. **Animal availability:** Is a suitable animal actually listed in Animal Trading?
3. **Purchase cost:** Can you afford its price in cash or Conservation Credits?

An animal can pass the first check and fail the second. Animal Trading has changing availability, so an unlocked species might not have a suitable male, female, or affordable listing at the moment you search.

The purchase currency matters as well. A zoo with plenty of cash may still be unable to buy an animal listed for Conservation Credits.

Use the trading filters to hide locked species while planning your first habitats. This narrows the list to animals you can actually consider adopting.

You do not have to own all 32 starting species to reach Tier 2. The requirement is your zoo's rating, not collecting the entire starting roster.

## How to Reach Three Zoo Reputation Stars for Tier 2

The first Franchise upgrade asks for **one zoo with 3 Reputation Stars**.

There is no Wildlife Reserve requirement at this stage, and no reason to establish a second zoo just to get Tier 2.

Start by developing your existing zoo and opening its Zoo Reputation breakdown. The overall rating is affected by several aspects of the zoo rather than a single construction milestone.

A useful example comes from an early Franchise zoo that was still struggling to reach three stars. Its management breakdown showed:

- **Average Guest Happiness: 71%**
- **Scenery Rating: 82%**
- **Species Appeal: 26%**

Those figures were the condition of that particular zoo, not fixed requirements every player must achieve. They do, however, explain why adding more decorative scenery was unlikely to be its most effective next investment.

Its Species Appeal was considerably weaker than its Scenery Rating.

Before ordering another expensive animal, compare your own weakest categories. Improving an already strong category may have little effect on the overall star rating.

### Keep the Zoo Financially Stable While Raising Reputation

The first three-star upgrade becomes harder when the zoo cannot afford to maintain its existing animals and guest facilities. Check whether admission is being charged and whether operating costs are leaving enough money for improvements.

A new animal or shop may increase the zoo's appeal, but it also brings additional expenses. When money is tight, address the weakest Reputation categories before expanding again.

For admission prices, donations, shop income, and operating expenses, see [how to start a Franchise Zoo and make money](/planet-zoo-2/franchise-guide).

### Spend on the Rating Categories That Are Holding You Back

The final push toward three stars does not always require another large habitat.

In one Franchise zoo, the rating breakdown identified **Guest Education and Sustainability** as useful areas to improve.

The player added Recycling Bins alongside existing trash bins. The Sustainability rating increased noticeably, so the next work went into education.

That sequence is useful because it separates two problems that require different actions.

**For Sustainability:**

- Check whether recycling facilities are missing from busy guest areas.
- Place Recycling Bins where visitors already use ordinary trash bins.
- Reopen the Sustainability rating and confirm that it has improved.

**For Guest Education:**

- Research the education topics you want to present.
- Place Education Boards and Speakers near the relevant animals.
- Assign appropriate animal or conservation topics to those facilities.
- Allow guests to encounter the displays and watch the rating change.

A Speaker without its selected topic is not the same as an operating education display. Some topics require additional research before they can be assigned.

You also need the facility close enough to the relevant animal or visitor area to do its job.

A fully decorative education corner far from any animal may look attractive while contributing less than expected.

The result of improving education and sustainability was decisive: the zoo reached **3 Reputation Stars**, and the Franchise advanced to Accreditation Tier 2.

![Accreditation Tier 2 Unlocked confirmation screen after the Franchise reaches its first three-star zoo requirement.](/images/planet-zoo-2/franchise-accreditation-tier-2-unlocked.webp)

### Should You Buy Another Animal to Raise Reputation?

Additional species can help when **Species Appeal** is holding back the overall Reputation rating. In one developing Franchise zoo, Species Appeal was only **26%**, compared with an **82% Scenery Rating**.

Before buying another animal, check whether that category is still the weakest part of your zoo. A new species may be worthwhile when appeal is genuinely limiting progress, but it will do little to resolve poor Guest Education or Sustainability.

The final push to Tier 2 in the observed zoo came from improving those weaker categories, including Recycling Bins and education facilities. Check the rating again after each change rather than purchasing animals until the star increases.

## What Changes After You Unlock Accreditation Tier 2?

Once the first zoo reaches three stars, return to the Planet Hub and check the new Accreditation status.

**Tier 2 adds 33 species to the available roster**, bringing the total unlocked selection to 65.

Animal Trading can now offer a broader selection of species, although individual listings still depend on what is currently available in the market.

![Animal Trading showing available species, animal attributes, prices, and remaining listing times.](/images/planet-zoo-2/franchise-accreditation-tier-2-animal-trading.webp)

The trading list shows individual animals rather than a permanent catalog of every unlocked species. A species may be available at your current Accreditation Tier without having a suitable animal listed for sale.

Before adopting anything, check its sex, age, individual attributes, and price. Newly unlocked animals can be expensive to accommodate, particularly when their Habitat and care requirements differ from those of your existing species.

This is a good time to revisit the animal you originally wanted but could not obtain. Check whether it is now permitted and whether an affordable listing is available.

If a species does not appear in the current offers, search for it by name, inspect the filters, and check again later. A temporary shortage of listings is different from an Accreditation restriction.

The new Marketing Slots also give you more room to promote your zoo, but running additional marketing campaigns costs money. Unlocking a slot does not make its campaign free.

With Tier 2 secured, the next major goal is no longer expanding the first zoo endlessly. You need another location.

## How to Reach Accreditation Tier 3

Tier 3 changes the scale of Franchise progression.

It requires:

- **2 separate Zoos**, each with at least **3 Zoo Reputation Stars**.
- **1 Wildlife Reserve** with at least **3 Biodiversity Stars**.

Your original Tier 2 zoo already counts as one of the two qualifying zoos, provided it still meets the required rating.

That leaves a second qualifying zoo and a functioning Wildlife Reserve to develop.

### When Should You Open Your Second Zoo?

Your first three-star zoo already satisfies half of Tier 3's zoo requirement. The second location must earn **its own 3 Zoo Reputation Stars**; adding more stars to your original zoo will not replace it.

Open the second zoo once your first location has dependable income and stable animal care. A new location requires its own facilities, staff, and animals, so starting too early can leave both zoos short of money.

The second zoo does not need to contain every species you have already unlocked. Choose affordable animals, establish a working guest area, and improve its Reputation categories until it independently reaches three stars.

For the practical costs of getting a new zoo running, use the [Franchise starting and money guide](/planet-zoo-2/franchise-guide).

### How Does a Wildlife Reserve Count Toward Tier 3?

A Wildlife Reserve uses **Biodiversity Rating**, not Zoo Reputation.

Releasing a few animals is a beginning, but it does not necessarily produce a three-star reserve. Suitable ecosystems, species balance, and research infrastructure all contribute to its rating.

The progression involves preparing release groups from your zoos, establishing compatible ecosystems in the reserve, and developing its research coverage.

Some released animals also require a suitable supporting species role. A predator's ecosystem cannot be treated as complete merely because the terrain and trees are correct.

For the complete process of preparing Release Groups, managing ecosystems, and improving research coverage, follow the [Wildlife Reserves guide](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves).

Return to the Franchise Accreditation screen after the second zoo and the first reserve each reach three stars.

Tier 3 adds another **31 species**, unlocks the **Acoustic Monitor**, and provides additional Marketing Slots.

## How to Reach Tier 4 and Tier 5 Without Building More Zoos

Once you have the two qualifying zoos required for Tier 3, the final two tiers focus on additional Wildlife Reserves.

**Tier 4 requires:**

- 2 Zoos with at least 3 Reputation Stars each.
- 2 Wildlife Reserves with at least 3 Biodiversity Stars each.

**Tier 5 requires:**

- 2 Zoos with at least 3 Reputation Stars each.
- 3 Wildlife Reserves with at least 3 Biodiversity Stars each.

You can continue developing the zoos you already own. A third zoo is not part of the stated Tier 4 or Tier 5 requirement.

The additional work is in creating and improving more reserves.

### Choose Reserves Around Animals You Can Actually Release

Before creating another reserve, consider which animals you can breed or prepare in your existing zoos.

Different reserves support different ecosystems, so an animal suitable for one location may not be ready for another.

European Wildlife Reserves can provide some overlap in compatible species, making them practical candidates when planning multiple reserves. Still, inspect the destination and release requirements before transferring any animals.

A release group becomes committed once it is finalized. You cannot simply reverse the decision and return those animals to a zoo.

Preparing the required animals and their environment before finalizing the group avoids spending Conservation Credits and breeding time on an unusable release.

At higher tiers, the work is less about making the first zoo bigger and more about maintaining a network capable of supplying several reserves.

Tier 4 rewards that expansion with **30 additional species and 50 more Animal Storage spaces**. That extra storage is particularly useful when several zoos are breeding animals for future releases.

Tier 5 then opens the final **10 species**, completing the launch roster's Accreditation progression.

## Why Isn't Franchise Accreditation Increasing?

Several situations can make the progression appear stuck even when you have been playing for hours.

### My zoo is profitable, but Accreditation is still Tier 1

Profit is not the Tier 2 requirement.

Check the overall **Zoo Reputation** in the Franchise zoo. It must reach three stars. A successful business can still have weak education, welfare, visitor experience, or species appeal ratings.

Open the rating breakdown before spending more money.

### I have one five-star zoo, but Tier 3 is locked

Tier 3 requires two separate qualifying zoos.

One location with a higher rating does not replace the missing second location. Establish or develop another zoo until it independently reaches three stars.

### I have two three-star zoos, but Tier 3 is still locked

You also need a Wildlife Reserve with three Biodiversity Stars.

Inspect the reserve's Biodiversity Rating. Its reputation is not measured by the same system as your zoos.

### I completed Career scenarios, but my Franchise animals are locked

Career and Franchise maintain separate Accreditation progression.

Career uses medals earned from its scenario objectives. Franchise uses Zoo Reputation and Wildlife Reserve Biodiversity.

Your Career medals do not increase your Franchise tier, and completing Career is not a prerequisite for unlocking Franchise animals.

### I unlocked the tier, but the animal I want is missing

First check the species' Accreditation requirement and any edition restrictions. Then inspect the Animal Trading filters and currently available listings.

Species availability changes in Franchise trading. Another player may purchase an animal before you do, or the market may have no suitable listing for the sex or price you need.

A missing market listing is not proof that your Accreditation progress was lost.
