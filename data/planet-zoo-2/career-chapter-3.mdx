<GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Finish Chapter 3">
Restore **Sumatra Reserve** and earn Bronze, then complete Bronze in **Java**, **Rural France**, and **Mountain Forests**. Return to Sumatra for Silver, which unlocks **Sweden Reserve** once you have 11 Career Bronze medals. Finish Sweden's evidence photos, animal releases, and biodiversity target to earn the fifth Bronze.

**Chapter 3: Claws and Effect** begins with a landslide in Sumatra and ends with an investigation into another Envalza project in Sweden. Between those two reserves, three zoos take in rescued animals, raise funds, and prepare wildlife to return home.

The chapter is less linear than it first appears. Sumatra Bronze opens Java, Rural France, and Mountain Forests, but collecting Bronze at those three zoos does not immediately let you travel to Sweden. You must bring the rehabilitation work back to Sumatra and earn its Silver medal.

There are five assignments:

- **Mine Your Business — Sumatra Reserve:** investigate the mine collapse and restore damaged ecosystems.
- **Creature Comforts — Java:** make room for rescued animals without exceeding four Habitats.
- **Trot To Go — Rural France:** fund conservation work through donations and education.
- **Parakeet Paradise — Mountain Forests:** rehabilitate pandas, parakeets, and a pangolin.
- **Howls It Going? — Sweden Reserve:** photograph Envalza's equipment and rebuild a functioning food chain.

The Bronze objectives are the main route below. Sumatra Silver is included because it is necessary to reach Sweden, rather than an optional challenge you can leave until much later.

## How to Unlock Chapter 3 and Reach Sumatra

**Mine Your Business** becomes available at Career Accreditation Tier 3, which requires **7 Bronze medals and 1 Silver medal**.

If Sumatra is still locked after finishing the opening chapters, check your medal totals in the Planet Hub. Earlier zoos and reserves remain available, so an unfinished Bronze objective from Chapter 2 is usually the quickest way to make up a missing medal.

Once Tier 3 is available, travel to **Sumatra Reserve**.

You arrive at the site of a collapsed mine. The affected animals have been moved out of immediate danger, but the industrial debris, damaged landscape, and missing ecosystems must be dealt with before wildlife can return.

## Sumatra Reserve — Mine Your Business Bronze

The first few minutes are an investigation rather than a building project. William believes the mine was connected to Envalza, and the abandoned equipment is the evidence you need.

### Photograph Three Pieces of Envalza Equipment

Enter the Research Vehicle and follow the marked evidence locations around the damaged mine.

Your task is to take **3 Research Photos showing branded equipment**. Focus on the equipment carrying the Envalza markings, not simply the surrounding landslide or scenery.

The locations are spread around the industrial remains. Drive between them, stop where you have a clear view, and take each photograph with the required equipment in frame.

![Envalza-branded industrial equipment at the Sumatra landslide site, used for the Career evidence objective.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3-sumatra-envalza-evidence.webp)

An ordinary landscape photograph will not advance the evidence counter. Keep following the highlighted equipment until all three photographs have been accepted.

The investigation then turns into a cleanup. Use the **Demolish Brush** to remove the remaining Envalza debris. Inspect the marked industrial area for anything the brush has not cleared before moving on to the ecosystem work.

### Rebuild the Lowland Rainforest Before Releasing Animals

The first restoration goal is to establish **one Lowland Rainforest Ecosystem**.

Open the Ecosystems view and designate an area of the damaged land for the rainforest. The designated area must meet its terrain and nature requirements before the game treats it as established.

The important distinction is between drawing an ecosystem boundary and finishing the ecosystem. Painting a large area alone does not satisfy the objective.

As you make changes, watch the individual requirements. Forest-floor coverage, rocky ground, and dense trees all matter to this stage. Increase whichever requirement is below its target rather than continually expanding the boundary.

Once the rainforest becomes established, prepare the two gifted release groups:

- **1 Malayan Tapir — Solitary Male**
- **1 Long-Tailed Parakeet — Family Group**

The parakeet release also needs suitable nesting provision. A completed rainforest does not automatically satisfy a bird's additional nature requirements.

Check the group's remaining conditions before attempting the release. For the full process of preparing animals, assigning Release Groups, and dealing with invalid ecosystems, use the [Wildlife Reserves guide](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves).

### Restore the Peatland and River for the Otters

After the Tapir and Parakeets return to the reserve, William asks you to continue repairing the landscape for the rescued Asian Small-Clawed Otters.

This part requires **two more ecosystems**:

- **1 Peatland Ecosystem**
- **1 River Ecosystem**

The River objective is especially easy to misread. Giving an area the River designation is not the same as providing enough usable water. The ecosystem can remain incomplete when the terrain contains too little water, even if its painted area appears large enough.

Shape the land where necessary, add water, and work through the remaining environment checks. Be careful when changing an existing ecosystem boundary: expanding one zone into another can reduce the area available to the first.

When both ecosystems are ready, release the gifted **Asian Small-Clawed Otter Family Group**.

### Get All Three Otters Into One Research Photo

The final photography task asks for a Research Photo containing **3 Asian Small-Clawed Otters**.

This is not three separate photographs of one otter.

The animals move around after release, and getting all three together can take longer than the earlier evidence pictures. Use the compass and animal location controls to find them, then approach the group.

Wait for three otters to be visible in the same frame before taking the photograph. If one animal is hidden by rocks, foliage, or terrain, adjust your position rather than repeatedly photographing the other two.

Once the three-otter photo is accepted, the Sumatra Bronze sequence finishes and three new destinations become available:

**Java, Rural France, and Mountain Forests.**

There is no need to keep rebuilding the reserve immediately. Those three zoos provide the next part of the conservation work, and their Bronze medals are needed before Sumatra's Silver objectives can begin.

## Java — Creature Comforts Bronze: Stay Under Four Habitats

Java is a luxury zoo being used as a rehabilitation centre for animals rescued from Sumatra.

It starts with **$10,000**, an existing layout, and a restriction that shapes almost every early decision:

**The zoo must have no more than 4 Habitats.**

This makes Java different from a normal expansion assignment. Building another enclosure whenever an animal arrives can leave you unable to satisfy the task, even when the animals themselves are healthy.

### Make Space by Preparing the Resident Animals for Release

Before placing the rescued animals, create and finalize the required Release Groups from animals already at the zoo.

You need:

- **1 Saltwater Crocodile — Solitary Male Release Group**
- **1 Malayan Tapir — Maternal Family Release Group**

Both species must reach **Research Level 3** for their required release work.

The Saltwater Crocodile is an early sticking point. The release option can remain locked even after you select or box the correct animal because the required research tier has not been completed.

Keep the animal available for research until Level 3 is finished. Check the research completion status before attempting to finalize its group.

The Malayan Tapir has its own research requirement and group composition. Select eligible animals for the specified group type instead of assuming that any two Tapirs will satisfy it.

Once the resident release groups are finalized, their former space can be used for the rescued arrivals.

![The Java Career objective restricting the rehabilitation zoo to no more than four Habitats.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3-java-four-habitats.webp)

Keep the total Habitat count at four or fewer while making these changes.

### Place the Rescued Tapir, Siamang, and Sun Bear

The next arrivals are:

- **1 Malayan Tapir**
- **1 Siamang**
- **1 Sun Bear**

Find suitable available Habitats before accepting unnecessary new construction. The Sun Bear needs careful placement because it is not an animal you should casually mix into an occupied enclosure.

After transferring the animals, the scenario requires **100% Average Animal Welfare** for all three species.

The most useful approach is to inspect each animal's actual needs rather than applying identical improvements to every Habitat.

The Sun Bear's early deficiencies can include enrichment and terrain. Adding an appropriate toy or food enrichment may help, but the Habitat still needs its other requirements met.

The Tapir can become stressed by guest visibility. More food will not resolve an animal that feels exposed, so pay attention to shelter and privacy when its other welfare categories are already satisfactory.

The Siamang needs suitable climbing and shelter space. A structure that looks like cover is not necessarily counted as usable shelter, and that can leave welfare just below the required value.

Select the species with the lowest remaining welfare and work through its failed categories. You only need to improve the animals assigned to this stage, not redesign every enclosure in Java.

### Finish the Restaurant, Food Profit, and Reputation Objectives

With the rescued animals settled, Java changes from rehabilitation tasks to zoo management.

The final Bronze requirements include:

- Place **1 Restaurant Kitchen**.
- Change the colour of staff uniforms through a **Staff Member's Info Panel**.
- Earn **$6,000 Food Profit in one season**.
- Reach **3.5 Stars Overall Zoo Reputation**.

The Restaurant Kitchen can be positioned where guests already travel, but it needs a usable path connection. A building that is visually in place is not necessarily operating.

The Food Profit target is a seasonal result, not a request to reach $6,000 total shop sales. Allow the zoo to operate while monitoring the actual profit figure. Additional food outlets may help, but adding too many expensive facilities before they have customers can work against the goal.

For the uniform objective, select a staff member and use their information controls to change the clothing colour. Changing the appearance of an unrelated building will not satisfy it.

The reputation target often remains after the other tasks have completed. Open the reputation breakdown and work on the categories holding the rating down.

Guest Education is one useful lever here. Education Boards and Speakers placed near animals guests can observe can increase that score while the zoo continues earning food profit.

Once **3.5 Stars** is reached and the other requirements are complete, **Creature Comforts earns Bronze**.

The later Java tasks involve additional Sumatran rescues. For now, the important result is that Java has created space and improved the animals that will be needed when you return to Sumatra.

## Rural France — Trot To Go Bronze: Donations, Binturongs, and Animal Talks

Rural France takes a different approach to the Sumatra disaster. This zoo is supporting conservation work by raising public awareness and collecting donations.

**Admission is free in this scenario.** The early tasks are therefore about turning guest interest into conservation funding, not increasing ticket prices.

### Start With the Donation Targets

The opening objectives require:

- **6 Donation Bins** in the zoo.
- **1 Nature's Keepers Adoption Shop**.
- **$10,000 Seasonal Donation Income**.

Place the Donation Bins along paths beside habitats and viewing areas guests actually visit. The Adoption Shop is another required facility, but it does not replace the Donation Bins.

This target depends on money accumulating through the operating zoo. After placing the required facilities, allow time for guests to donate and watch the seasonal donation figure.

Avoid spending the opening stage rearranging distant habitats simply to add more bins. It is more useful to position the six required bins where guests already have a reason to stop.

### Adopt Two Binturongs and Bring Welfare to 100%

Once the donation stage advances, you are asked to adopt and place **2 Binturongs**.

The required result is **100% Binturong Average Animal Welfare**.

Prepare the Habitat before the animals arrive. Binturongs need suitable climbing opportunities, shelter, water, enrichment, and an appropriate environment.

One avoidable problem is installing climbing structures and assuming that the remaining welfare issues will resolve themselves. The animals can still lack usable climbing space, plant coverage, or a comfortable temperature.

Check the welfare panel after both animals are delivered. Work on the lowest categories first and avoid adding decorative objects that do not satisfy an animal need.

### Complete the Conservation Project and Education Content

The next stage connects the Binturongs to conservation education.

Research **Conservation Threats to Level 1**, then configure the required information around the zoo.

The Binturong education objectives call for:

- **1 Conservation Education Board** with **Population Fragmentation**.
- **1 Conservation Education Board** with **Habitat Loss**.
- **1 Education Speaker** playing **Rainforest Ecosystem Ambience**.

The topics matter. Two boards displaying the same information do not satisfy the two separate conservation subjects.

You must also complete the **Binturong Behaviour Conservation Project**.

Open the Planet Hub and find the conservation project controls. Allocate the required resources to the Binturong project and allow its completion to register.

This is separate from the earlier seasonal donation objective. Filling a Donation Bin does not automatically finish a conservation project.

### Why Won't the Animal Talk Point Count?

Rural France introduces Animal Talks as another way to improve education.

The Bronze objectives require **2 Animal Talk Points with connected Seating**. You must also hire **3 Educators**.

![Animal Talk Seating being selected for the Rural France education objective.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3-animal-talk-seating.webp)

The seating requirement is the part most likely to cause confusion.

Ordinary benches nearby do not automatically become Animal Talk Seating. Open the education facilities and select the dedicated **Animal Talk Seating**, then arrange it so that it connects with its Talk Point.

The Talk Point should also be near the animal relevant to the talk. A podium placed far away from its intended animal is not a useful substitute for a functioning education setup.

For the final education stage, place and configure a board about **Przewalski's Horse** and increase **Guest Education to 3 Stars**.

Complete the staffing, seating, and educational content requirements before waiting for the rating to rise.

With **3-Star Guest Education** and the earlier funding and animal-care work finished, **Trot To Go earns Bronze**.

The Silver objectives then introduce more animal species, marketing, and a larger donation target. These are not necessary to proceed to Mountain Forests.

## Mountain Forests — Parakeet Paradise Bronze: Handle All 20 Rescued Birds

Mountain Forests is a crowded sanctuary in China. Some of the animals rescued from Sumatra are too stressed to return to the wild immediately and need permanent care.

The assignment begins with Giant Pandas, but its biggest logistical challenge is receiving **20 Long-Tailed Parakeets** and maintaining their welfare.

### Settle the New Male Giant Panda

Adopt and place **1 new male Giant Panda** with the panda already at the zoo.

The goal is to raise **Giant Panda Average Animal Welfare to 100%**.

Open the panda Habitat and respond to its remaining requirements. Shelter, terrain, enrichment, and suitable vegetation all contribute to this stage.

Pandas also need privacy from visitors.

The scenario asks you to set at least **3 Habitat Barrier sections to 1-Way Glass**. Replace appropriate guest-facing sections so visitors can still see the animals while reducing their exposure.

A Panda with the right food and shelter can still fail the welfare target if stress remains high.

Once the Panda is settled, the first large group of Sumatran rescues arrives.

### Build a Large Quarantine and Prepare Two Aviaries

Place **1 Quarantine - Large** and connect it to the zoo's path and power infrastructure.

The new arrival objective is to place **20 Long-Tailed Parakeets** and raise their **Average Animal Welfare to 90%**.

![The Mountain Forests objective showing 20 Long-Tailed Parakeets and the required average welfare target.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3-twenty-parakeets.webp)

The group-size requirement is important.

The Long-Tailed Parakeet's preferred group range is **2–12 per Habitat**. Putting all 20 into one Aviary creates an avoidable population problem.

A workable arrangement is **two Aviaries with 10 Parakeets each**. This also leaves a little room within the preferred range while you manage the arrivals.

You can use the existing paths and available construction space around the sanctuary, but each Aviary needs its own functional access and an enclosed flight area.

### Fix the Aviary That Says "No Habitat Selected"

A new Aviary can look complete while the game still fails to recognise it as a usable Habitat.

Check the boundary, Staff Gate, and path connection before rebuilding the structure.

In particular, connect the Staff Gate to the path network. A gate standing beside an unconnected path is not enough to make the enclosure properly accessible.

When the Habitat is recognised, finish the roof and ensure the birds have nests, food, water, and appropriate environmental conditions.

Do this for both Aviaries before directing the full group into them.

### Why Are the Parakeet Numbers or Welfare Still Wrong?

Moving 20 birds takes time.

Some can remain in Gifted Storage or be in transit even after you have assigned the animals to their Habitats. Others may be taken away for veterinary care because they arrived injured.

If the counter appears to be missing several birds, inspect Storage and the animal list before assuming you need to adopt replacements.

**90% Average Welfare** also requires more than a correct headcount.

Check the following while the birds settle:

- **Temperature:** a cold Aviary may need heating and a functioning power supply.
- **Nests and shelter:** install suitable bird facilities rather than relying on the roof alone.
- **Food and water:** make sure both groups have access to their own essentials.
- **Stress:** guest visibility and noise can keep the animals below the target.
- **Injuries:** call a Veterinarian promptly and allow treatment to finish.

The large number of rescued animals makes veterinary response particularly important. An animal being carried to treatment may temporarily disappear from its assigned Habitat count.

The objective concerns the health and welfare of the birds, not merely whether 20 transport orders were issued.

Once the parakeets are settled, the sanctuary introduces the next rescued animal.

### Prepare the Sunda Pangolin Habitat

The final major animal-care stage involves **1 Sunda Pangolin**.

You need to raise its **Average Animal Welfare to 100%** while continuing to manage visitor disturbance.

The requirements at this stage include:

- At least **6 Habitat Barrier sections set to 1-Way Glass**.
- Additional **Guest Sign - 'Do Not Disturb'** signs, including the **3** requested during the Pangolin stage.
- At least **1 Animal Burrow** in the zoo.

An earlier stress-reduction objective also calls for **6 'Do Not Disturb' signs**. Pay attention to the current objective counter instead of assuming signs placed for a previous task satisfy every later placement requirement.

Prepare a usable Habitat with a Staff Gate, path access, suitable terrain, and climbing space. Add the Animal Burrow and the appropriate enrichment before expecting full welfare.

The Pangolin may need veterinary treatment after arriving. A well-equipped Habitat will not make an injured animal instantly healthy.

Call the Veterinarian when necessary and allow the animal to recover. Once its needs and the stress-reduction objectives are satisfied, **Parakeet Paradise earns Bronze**.

The sanctuary then introduces further rescued animals, including a Great Hornbill and Red Panda. Those belong to the next medal stage; they are not required for the initial Bronze.

## Why Sweden Is Still Locked: Return to Sumatra for Silver

After earning Bronze in Java, Rural France, and Mountain Forests, it is tempting to keep going forward through the Planet Hub.

However, **Howls It Going? in Sweden requires Sumatra Reserve Silver**, not just Bronze in the four earlier Chapter 3 locations.

You also need **11 Career Bronze medals** in total.

This is why the chapter asks you to revisit Sumatra. The rescue zoos have now made progress toward rehabilitating the animals displaced by the mine collapse.

### What Sumatra Silver Requires

Return to **Sumatra Reserve — Mine Your Business** after completing Bronze at all three rescue zoos.

The Silver stage requires more wildlife releases and research infrastructure. Its key objectives are:

- Release **1 Malayan Tapir Maternal Family Group**.
- Release **1 Siamang**.
- Release **1 Sun Bear**.
- Reach **8 released Release Groups** in the reserve.
- Place **6 Camera Traps** to improve the Research Rating.

The additional animals are connected to the earlier rehabilitation work in Java. Prepare the required groups from eligible animals rather than buying random replacements without checking the group requirements.

Watch the reserve's Release Group total as you progress. The objective is a total of eight groups, not eight animals.

For the Camera Traps, place them around the reserve where they can contribute to research coverage. Having six objects in your inventory does not count as placing six working traps.

The complete reserve research and animal-release controls are covered in the [Wildlife Reserves walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves).

When Sumatra Silver is awarded and your total reaches **11 Bronze medals**, Sweden Reserve becomes accessible.

## Sweden Reserve — Howls It Going? Bronze

Sweden brings the Envalza investigation back into focus.

This time the damage comes from an abandoned logging operation presented as an environmentally friendly luxury development. FEN needs evidence of what happened before restoring the reserve.

Unlike the rescue zoos, Sweden starts with unlimited funds. The challenge is completing the investigation, placing wildlife into suitable ecosystems, and raising the reserve's biodiversity.

### Enter the Research Vehicle and Collect Four Evidence Photos

The first required action is to **enter the Research Vehicle**.

You then need **4 Research Photos of Envalza-branded equipment**.

Drive around the former logging site and inspect the marked machinery and abandoned equipment. Take each picture with the identifying equipment clearly visible.

The investigation is not complete after the first successful photograph. Keep moving between the remaining evidence locations until all four have registered.

After the final evidence photo, the assignment changes from documenting damage to reversing it.

### Restore the Land for Red Deer and Wild Boar First

The wildlife restoration sequence begins with herbivores.

The required Bronze releases include:

- **1 Red Deer Release Group**
- **1 Wild Boar Release Group**
- **1 Grey Wolf Release Group**

Prepare the land and check the environment needed for each group before releasing it.

The Red Deer can use a suitable forest ecosystem, while the other animals must also have compatible habitats and release conditions. The ecosystem's status needs to show that it is established, not simply designated.

The objective sequence matters because the reserve needs prey animals before it can support a natural predator.

Start by preparing the ecosystems for Red Deer and Wild Boar. Release those groups when their requirements are met, then continue with the Grey Wolf.

The wolf is not a replacement for the herbivores. It fills a different role in restoring the natural food chain.

### Raise Biodiversity to 2.5 Stars

The remaining Bronze objective is a **2.5-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

This is a reserve-wide condition rather than a request to place one extra tree or release another animal without checking the score.

Open the Biodiversity Rating panel and inspect the weaker parts of the reserve.

Restored ecosystems and suitable species releases improve the environmental side of the rating. Research infrastructure also matters, so check whether the reserve needs more observation equipment and coverage.

Do not keep adding the same type of scenery once its requirement is already complete. Use the rating breakdown and the individual ecosystem checks to identify what still needs work.

Likewise, a released predator does not automatically complete the biodiversity objective if the other conditions remain below the required level.

After the four evidence photos, three required animal groups, and **2.5-Star Biodiversity Rating** are completed, **Howls It Going? earns Bronze**.

Eric's investigation also reaches its conclusion: Envalza's activities are exposed, and FEN is given the opportunity to restore more of the company's abandoned projects.

After earning **Sweden Reserve Bronze**, check your Career Accreditation in the Planet Hub. Once you reach **Tier 4 (12 Bronze and 2 Silver medals)**, Maluku Islands becomes available.

Start with **Mola Money, Mola Problems**. Earning Maluku Bronze unlocks the other five locations, which are covered alongside their Silver and Gold objectives in the [Chapter 4 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4).
