A filled aquarium in **Planet Zoo 2** is not necessarily ready for animals. The barrier and staff gate create the water-filled enclosure, but filtration, salinity, temperature, and species compatibility determine whether it can support the animals you want to keep.

The **Nordic Fjords** career scenario introduces these systems separately. You install a missing gate, restore the filtration network, provide power, and prepare the water before bringing in Golden Trevally and Hawksbill Sea Turtles.

The same setup requirements apply when building your own aquarium, although a new enclosure will not necessarily have the prepared facilities and animal supplies provided during that scenario.

## How to Build an Aquarium in Planet Zoo 2

Start with the enclosure rather than buying the animals.

In the Nordic Fjords scenario, the aquarium barrier is already standing when you arrive. Dr. Brooks asks you to install the missing staff gate. In a newly built zoo, you need to create the aquarium enclosure yourself before adding that entrance.

The basic construction sequence is:

1. Open the construction browser and create an enclosure using **Aquarium Barriers**.
2. Complete the enclosed area and decide where staff should enter.
3. Select the barrier and install an **Aquarium Staff Gate**.
4. Connect the gate to the zoo's path network so staff can reach it.
5. Confirm that the aquarium fills with water.

The important distinction is the gate type. An ordinary Habitat Staff Gate is not the correct entrance for a filled aquarium.

The aquarium gate can also be positioned above ground level because staff enter the water using diving equipment. You do not have to force the gate onto the aquarium floor.

![An Aquarium Staff Gate installed in the enclosure barrier, allowing the aquarium to fill with water.](/images/planet-zoo-2/aquarium-staff-gate-water-fill.webp)

### Why is my aquarium not filling with water?

Check the gate before changing the filtration network.

One aquarium construction attempt leaves the finished enclosure completely empty despite the surrounding barriers being in place. The missing piece is not a water pump or a temperature setting: the wrong type of gate was installed.

Replacing it with the correct Aquarium Staff Gate causes the enclosure to fill.

This is worth checking first because filtration equipment does not replace the entrance needed to establish the water-filled aquarium.

Also inspect the gate's path connection. An aquarium may have water but still be inaccessible to the staff responsible for maintaining it.

## How to Connect Water Filters to an Aquarium

Once the aquarium contains water, open **Facilities** and place a Water Filter.

The filter is not automatically connected merely because it is nearby. You must assign it through the Filtration Network before it can maintain the aquarium's water conditions.

In Nordic Fjords, the existing filtration system is already at capacity. The new aquarium requires another filter rather than simply relying on the facilities already serving the other enclosures.

### Set up the filtration network

1. Place a Water Filter where it can serve the new aquarium.
2. Select the filter or the relevant water facility to open its Filtration Network controls.
3. Connect the aquarium to the intended network.
4. Set the network's salinity to the water type required by the animals.
5. Ensure the filter has an active power supply.

![The aquarium filtration network showing the water filter connection and water salinity controls.](/images/planet-zoo-2/aquarium-filtration-network.webp)

A connection by itself is not enough. In the Nordic Fjords sequence, the filtration system has to be connected and powered before the aquarium's water is properly maintained.

This distinction matters when you have multiple aquariums. A facility may already be operating elsewhere in the zoo without covering the new enclosure.

### Freshwater, brackish water, and saltwater

Filtration Networks can be configured for three water types:

- **Freshwater**
- **Brackish water**
- **Saltwater**

The salinity is a setting on the filtration system, not something determined simply by how the aquarium looks.

For the Golden Trevally and Hawksbill Sea Turtle setup in Nordic Fjords, the scenario specifically calls for **Saltwater**. Leaving the network on Freshwater is not the correct configuration for those animals.

A network operates with a selected salinity, so plan separate filtration arrangements when you need to maintain aquariums with different water types.

Before connecting a new aquarium to an existing network, check the water type already assigned to that network. A convenient connection is not useful when it supplies the wrong salinity.

## Why Is My Aquarium Filter Not Working?

An aquarium can have a valid filtration connection while the Water Filter itself is still unable to operate.

After the filtration network is connected in Nordic Fjords, the next requirement is to provide electricity. A Power Generator must cover the filtration facility before it can perform its job.

Check these three conditions in order:

- The aquarium is assigned to the intended Filtration Network.
- The Water Filter is covered by a working power source.
- The network uses the salinity required by its animals.

Correcting the first condition without the second still leaves the setup unfinished.

### Should you use a standard generator or renewable power?

The game offers conventional power facilities alongside options such as wind and solar generation.

Renewable power facilities have a higher initial construction cost, but they provide useful Sustainability benefits and avoid the running costs associated with conventional generators.

For an early aquarium, prioritize getting the filtration facility powered with the resources available to your zoo. You can choose the more sustainable setup when its higher initial expense fits your budget.

### Set the aquarium's water temperature

Water temperature is controlled separately from salinity. In Nordic Fjords, the Aquarium must reach **28°C** before the incoming animals can be introduced.

Select the Aquarium, open the **Environment** tab, and adjust the temperature to **28°C**. This is the scenario's required setting, not a universal temperature for every aquatic species.

![Nordic Fjords requires the Aquarium temperature to be set to 28°C through the Environment tab.](/images/planet-zoo-2/aquarium-water-temperature.webp)

Before stocking another Aquarium, check the temperature requirements of its intended species. Once the water is at the required temperature and the filtration network is connected, powered, and set to the correct salinity, the enclosure is ready for its animals.

## How to Add Fish and Sea Turtles to Your Aquarium

The first aquatic exhibit in Nordic Fjords is actually a **Moon Jelly Exhibit**. It is placed and stocked separately from the larger aquarium used later for Golden Trevally and Hawksbill Sea Turtles.

That distinction is useful when deciding what to build. Small exhibit animals use their own exhibit housing, while fully aquatic habitat animals need an appropriate aquarium enclosure.

### Add Golden Trevally first

After preparing the main aquarium, Nordic Fjords provides Golden Trevally for the enclosure.

Move the fish into the prepared aquarium through the animal management and delivery controls.

Fish of this type are handled in **shoals**. The group can be fed, treated, and moved together, rather than requiring each fish to be managed as an unrelated animal.

Allow the delivery process to finish before judging whether the aquarium has been stocked successfully.

### Add the Hawksbill Sea Turtles

The next part of the scenario introduces **Hawksbill Sea Turtles**, with two turtles provided for the aquarium.

Golden Trevally and Hawksbill Sea Turtles are compatible in this particular setup. The aquarium has also been prepared with the necessary conditions for them.

That does not mean every fish and sea turtle combination will work.

Before purchasing animals for a custom aquarium, inspect their compatibility information, required water conditions, and available habitat space. Animals that share the same water type are not automatically suitable companions.

The trade-off is especially important when expanding an existing aquarium. Adding another species may look more efficient than building a second enclosure, but only when the current aquarium can meet the needs of every resident.

## What to Check When Aquarium Animals Are Unhappy

Water quality is the first thing worth inspecting when the aquarium is newly built or its filtration network has recently changed.

Open the animal and enclosure information panels and check whether the conditions match the species requirements.

A useful inspection order is:

1. Confirm the filter remains powered and connected.
2. Compare the water's salinity and temperature against the animal's needs.
3. Check habitat suitability, available space, and compatibility with other animals.
4. Look for illness or injury if the physical environment is correct but the animal is still unwell.

Nordic Fjords provides a separate example involving a **Manatee**. The animal behaves unusually and requires veterinary attention rather than another round of aquarium construction.

A veterinarian is hired to examine the manatee, and the animal recovers after treatment.

The distinction is important. Adjusting a working filter will not substitute for veterinary care when an animal is already sick.

Likewise, a veterinarian cannot make an unsuitable water environment safe indefinitely. Both the enclosure conditions and the animal's health need attention.

## How to Change an Aquarium After It Fills with Water

Large aquariums often need adjustments after the first enclosure is complete. You might want a different barrier shape, larger viewing windows, or a revised staff entrance.

The automatic water fill can make this less convenient than working on an empty enclosure.

During a custom aquarium build, changing the barrier requires temporarily dealing with the water and gate rather than continuing as though the enclosure were empty.

Use the **Drain Water** option when you need access to the barrier layout. Removing the Aquarium Staff Gate is another way to stop the automatic fill while reworking the enclosure.

Finish the barrier changes before reinstalling the correct entrance and allowing the water to return.

It is easier to establish the intended footprint and viewing windows before stocking the aquarium. A later redesign also means checking the path connection, filtration assignment, and water conditions again.

For large decorative builds, complete the practical enclosure and service layout before spending heavily on surrounding scenery. A finished-looking aquarium is expensive to rebuild if its staff entrance or filtration facilities are poorly positioned.

## How to Know Your Aquarium Is Ready

Before ordering additional aquatic animals, make one final inspection of the enclosure.

The aquarium should contain water, have an accessible staff entrance, be connected to a powered filtration system, and maintain the correct salinity and temperature.

Then check the animals' compatibility and habitat requirements.

In Nordic Fjords, these steps lead directly to the successful introduction of Golden Trevally and Hawksbill Sea Turtles. The completed setup also shows why Aquarium Staff Gates, Filtration Networks, and animal requirements must be treated as parts of the same construction process.

Once the animals are settled, future expansion should begin with their needs rather than the available space around the tank. An aquarium can always be made more elaborate, but the water conditions have to remain suitable for everything living inside it.
