**FENtastic Opportunity**, the fourth Career chapter in Planet Zoo 2, begins on the expensive islands of Maluku and opens into five former Envalza properties. Four assignments take place in zoos, while Poland and Tanzania are Wildlife Reserves. Together, they test aquarium management, mixed-species habitats, breeding programs, ecosystem restoration, and conservation releases.

Unlike the earlier chapters, you do not have to finish a specific Silver medal to move between the last five locations. Once **Mola Money, Mola Problems** earns Bronze, Channel Islands, Singapore, Poland Reserve, Swiss Alps, and Tanzania Reserve become available. You can work on their Bronze objectives in whichever order best suits the animals and Release Groups already available in your FEN network.

The six assignments are:

- **Mola Money, Mola Problems — Maluku Islands**
- **Not Much To Sea — Channel Islands**
- **Dragon, Ball, Zoo — Singapore**
- **No Ifs, No Putts — Poland Reserve**
- **From A To Ski — Swiss Alps**
- **Take Pride — Tanzania Reserve**

Several Gold medals demand considerably more work than their Bronze objectives. It is often more useful to unlock all six assignments first, prepare animals across the FEN network, and return to the longer collection, breeding, and biodiversity goals once the necessary resources are available.

## How to Unlock Chapter 4 and All Six Locations

To enter **Maluku Islands**, you need two things:

- **Career Accreditation Tier 4:** 12 Bronze medals and 2 Silver medals.
- **Sweden Reserve Bronze:** complete the opening objectives of Howls It Going?

The medal total and Sweden requirement are separate checks. Reaching 12 Bronze medals without completing Sweden does not substitute for that specific scenario medal.

If Sweden itself remains locked, you must return to Sumatra Reserve for Silver after completing the required Chapter 3 rescue assignments. The [Chapter 3 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3) covers the Sumatra return trip, Sweden's investigation, its wildlife releases, and the biodiversity objective.

After Sweden Bronze, inspect your Accreditation in the Planet Hub. If you are still short of Tier 4, return to earlier locations with unfinished medals rather than continuing to improve Sweden without a specific requirement.

### What unlocks after Maluku Bronze?

The opening Maluku assignment leads to the remaining five locations:

- **Channel Islands:** establish a new zoo with terrestrial, avian, aquatic, and Exhibit species.
- **Singapore:** recover an abandoned stadium and develop high-appeal animals and mixed habitats.
- **Poland Reserve:** restore the former golf course and return European wildlife to its wetlands.
- **Swiss Alps:** raise Wild Boars, prepare breeding groups, and supply conservation animals.
- **Tanzania Reserve:** establish African ecosystems and release wildlife across a large reserve.

You do not need Maluku Silver or Gold to visit these locations.

**Swiss Alps before Poland** is a useful route when you lack Eurasian Beavers or Eurasian Bitterns. Swiss Alps asks you to prepare Breeding Partners Release Groups for both species, while Poland requires their release into a restored wetland. Preparing the groups first reduces the need to interrupt Poland's restoration work to look for suitable animals.

There is no need to follow that order if your FEN network already contains the required groups.

## Maluku Islands — Mola Money, Mola Problems: Aquariums, Shoals, and Ocean Sunfish

**Starting money: $15,000**

Maluku presents an unusual combination of expensive terrain modification and a relatively small starting budget. The zoo spans several islands, and extending construction across the site can consume money quickly. Yet the opening objectives are mainly about aquatic animals already in the zoo, research, and the welfare of a new Ocean Sunfish.

Avoid beginning with a large new aquarium or unnecessary land connections. The existing facilities give you somewhere to start, and improving their animals is more useful than building extra capacity before you know what is missing.

### Bronze: Six aquatic animals, six shoals, and 100% welfare

Maluku Bronze requires:

- At least **6 aquatic animals**, satisfying the required **100% welfare** condition.
- At least **6 shoals**.
- **Hawksbill Turtle Research Level 4**.
- At least **500 guests**.
- **$3,000 profit in one season**.
- Adopt and place **1 new Ocean Sunfish**.
- Raise **Ocean Sunfish Average Animal Welfare to 100%**.

Inspect the animals and aquariums you inherited before making changes. In a tank shared by different aquatic species, an adjustment that improves one animal's Environment score can reduce another's. The resident sharks and other animals do not necessarily need identical substrate proportions, so repeatedly painting the whole aquarium with one terrain type can prevent you from reaching the welfare target.

Make small adjustments and inspect the affected animals after each one. Keep **Hawksbill Turtle research progressing toward Level 4** while working on environmental requirements; research is a separate objective, and completing welfare does not complete it.

The decision to build another aquarium should come from incompatible animal needs, not simply from finding an empty area of the island.

![Ocean Sunfish environmental requirements showing the water-temperature problem in Maluku Islands.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-maluku-sunfish-temperature.webp)

When the Ocean Sunfish arrives, open its animal information and examine its Environment requirements. **Water temperature** can be one of the remaining reasons it has not reached full welfare. Adjust the aquarium toward the animal's acceptable range, then inspect the welfare of any other species sharing that enclosure.

There is no single temperature you should apply to every aquarium. The [aquarium construction and filtration guide](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) explains the Staff Gate, Filtration Network, salinity, temperature, and power controls if the enclosure itself is not functioning.

### Why doesn't buying more fish complete the shoal objective?

Maluku has two different collection counters. The aquatic animal requirement concerns animals, while **6 shoals** concerns groups.

Buying six fish does not necessarily create six qualifying shoals. Inspect the number of shoals the game recognizes after stocking an aquarium and distinguish individual animal counts from group counts.

The Ocean Sunfish requirement is similarly specific: you must **adopt and place a new Ocean Sunfish**. Raising the welfare of another aquatic species does not satisfy that adoption objective.

Once the animal and research work is complete, let the zoo operate and inspect the seasonal financial result. The required **$3,000 profit** is not the same as having $3,000 in cash. Avoid expensive construction while waiting for the seasonal profit and guest objectives to register.

### Silver: Ten aquatic habitat species and ten shoal species

Silver requires:

- **10 different Aquatic Habitat species**.
- **10 different shoal species**.
- **Ocean Sunfish Research Level 4**.

Here the important distinction changes from groups to **different species**. Several shoals of one fish may increase a shoal-group count, but they do not become several different shoal species.

Check the species already living in the zoo before buying anything. Use Animal Trading to expand the collection with distinct species, and keep compatible water conditions and available aquarium space in mind. If the animals' requirements cannot be maintained together, use separate aquariums rather than lowering welfare across the entire collection.

Ocean Sunfish research can continue while you expand. Do not stop the research work just because the species counters have reached their targets.

### Gold: Fifteen species and a Thriving Ocean Sunfish

Gold requires:

- **15 Aquatic Habitat species**.
- **3 different Thriving species**.
- **1 Thriving Ocean Sunfish**.

The Bronze requirement for 100% Ocean Sunfish welfare and the Gold requirement for a **Thriving Ocean Sunfish** are not interchangeable. Check the animal's current status rather than assuming one completed welfare counter guarantees the other.

By Gold, Maluku becomes a much larger animal-collection project. Its limited starting budget makes it sensible to return when research is complete and the zoo can support additional aquariums without putting the existing animals or seasonal finances under pressure.

## Channel Islands — Not Much To Sea: Species Types, Guest Happiness, and Protest-Free Seasons

**Starting money: $45,000**

Channel Islands begins with an empty coastal zoo. There is room for several types of animal accommodation, but the medal requirements demand more than a large collection. Animal welfare, guest happiness, inspection results, reputation, and uninterrupted seasons without protesters must all be maintained.

It is easier to establish a compact, functioning zoo first than to spread habitats and staff facilities across the site before the initial animals can be delivered and cared for.

### Bronze: Four different animal categories

The Bronze objectives are:

- **1 Terrestrial Habitat species**.
- **1 Avian Habitat species**.
- **1 Aquatic Habitat species**.
- **4 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **95% Average Animal Welfare**.
- **75% Guest Average Happiness Need** with at least **350 guests**.
- **1 season without protesters**.
- **4-Star Zoo Inspection Report**.
- **3-Star Zoo Reputation**.

Begin with connected staff facilities and working animal accommodation. The Trade Center, Keeper Hut, staff paths, and individual Habitat or Exhibit entrances must be accessible before the zoo can reliably receive and maintain its animals.

The distinction between Habitat and Exhibit species is essential here.

![Channel Islands objective requiring four different Terrestrial Exhibit species.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-channel-terrestrial-exhibits.webp)

A Red Panda in a normal Terrestrial Habitat does **not** count as one of the four Terrestrial Exhibit species. A fish in an aquarium does not count either. Use animals housed in **Terrestrial Exhibits**, such as appropriate reptiles and invertebrates, and check the objective counter after each distinct species is placed.

If an animal is not being delivered or fed, check its actual access point and the connected staff path. An Exhibit placed beside a path may still be inaccessible when that connection is incomplete. Hiring more keepers will not correct a broken route.

### How to finish one season without protesters

The protest requirement measures a period of time, not just the zoo's current condition.

You can meet the welfare target, reach 350 guests, and improve reputation while the **one-season protest-free counter** remains unfinished. More construction is not necessarily helpful at this point.

Inspect active complaints and animal welfare alerts, then allow the zoo to operate without making changes that destabilize animal care. If a new protest interrupts the required period, you may have to complete the full streak again.

The **Zoo Inspection Report** and **Zoo Reputation** are also separate conditions. Do not assume that high Guest Happiness automatically produces the required inspection rating or reputation.

### Silver: Seven Habitat species and two protest-free seasons

Silver raises the requirements to:

- **7 different Habitat species**.
- **8 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **80% Guest Average Happiness Need** with at least **600 guests**.
- **2 seasons without protesters**.
- **4-Star Zoo Reputation**.

The collection now demands both more Habitats and a larger range of Terrestrial Exhibits. Expand in manageable stages, ensuring each new animal is delivered, fed, and housed correctly before introducing the next group.

Guest facilities need to grow with attendance. Watch food, drinks, toilets, seating, cleanliness, and staff capacity as the zoo passes the earlier guest threshold.

If protest-free progress repeatedly fails, stop treating animal purchases as the next priority. The missing condition is stability over two seasons.

### Gold: Eleven species, 900 guests, and four seasons without protests

Gold requires:

- **11 different Habitat species** meeting the required **100% welfare** condition.
- **85% Guest Average Happiness Need** with at least **900 guests**.
- **4 seasons without protesters**.
- **4.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

This is where the collection and stability objectives begin working against each other. Every additional species introduces more possible welfare problems, while increased attendance puts more pressure on guest services.

The practical order is to complete the species collection and correct its welfare problems **before depending on the four-season protest-free streak**. As guest numbers rise, inspect the Happiness and Reputation panels for whichever categories have fallen behind. Once the targets are stable, avoid adding unnecessary animals or changing functioning habitats during the remaining seasons.

![Channel Islands Gold medal awarded after completing all Bronze, Silver, and Gold objectives.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-channel-gold-medal.webp)

If the only unfinished objective is the seasonal counter, additional expansion is more likely to create another complaint than help the medal. Hold the zoo steady and let the required time pass.

## Singapore — Dragon, Ball, Zoo: Komodo Appeal and the Seven-Species Habitat

**Starting money: $45,000**

Singapore is built around an abandoned stadium that must be cleared and converted into a functioning zoo. Its objectives combine debris removal, a large mixed-species Habitat, Komodo Dragon appeal, animal welfare, education, and eventually five-star reputation.

The shared-Habitat requirements deserve attention from the beginning. The game counts how many **different species occupy one Habitat**, not how many species live across the entire stadium.

### Bronze: Clear the stadium and prepare the first mixed Habitat

Bronze requires:

- Remove **50 pieces of debris**.
- House **3 different species in one Habitat**, meeting the required **90% welfare** condition.
- Keep **1 Great Hornbill** at **90% welfare**.
- Reach **350 guests**.
- Have **1 Komodo Dragon** with at least **2,600 Animal Appeal**.
- Have **1 Thriving Komodo Dragon**.
- Provide **5 different Komodo Dragon education topics**.

Clear the required debris before building heavily over the stadium. Removing it opens space for the first animal accommodation and makes the cleanup objective easier to finish without searching beneath newly placed construction.

For the shared Habitat, choose animals that can actually live together. Introduce the required three species and inspect the Habitat's animal list and each species' welfare. Three separate Habitats, each containing one animal species, will not count.

The Great Hornbill has its own welfare requirement and does not have to be included among those three shared species.

### Komodo Dragon Appeal: Why does 2,500 not count?

Singapore asks for **one Komodo Dragon with at least 2,600 Animal Appeal**. A dragon showing 2,500 can appear close enough to make the remaining requirement confusing, especially when its welfare is otherwise improving.

![Singapore Komodo Dragon objective requiring 2,600 Animal Appeal.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-singapore-komodo-appeal.webp)

Inspect the individual dragon's **Animal Appeal** value rather than its welfare percentage. The scenario also requires a Thriving Komodo Dragon, so monitor Appeal and Thriving separately.

Improve the dragon's living conditions where the animal information indicates an unmet requirement, but do not assume that perfect welfare alone changes an individual dragon's Appeal to the required number. Likewise, purchasing another dragon without checking its Appeal does not guarantee that the objective will advance.

The education requirement asks for **five different topics about Komodo Dragons**. Five copies of one educational topic do not satisfy it. Configure the boards or other relevant education facilities with distinct available topics and inspect their actual selections.

### Silver: Five species in one Habitat

Silver requires:

- **5 different species in one Habitat**.
- **3 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **4.5-Star Guest Education**.
- At least **1,000 guests**.

You can continue developing a compatible mixed Habitat rather than beginning a new collection from scratch, but the larger species count makes welfare and space harder to maintain.

If the shared-Habitat counter remains unchanged, open the specific Habitat and inspect its residents. An animal in a separate Habitat, Aquarium, or Terrestrial Exhibit does not contribute to that single-Habitat total.

Education needs attention across the visitor experience as well. Use education facilities where guests can encounter the relevant animals and check the Education rating rather than adding repeated signs without confirming whether they improve the missing category.

### Gold: Seven shared species, ten Thriving species, and five-star reputation

Gold requires:

- **7 different species in a single Habitat**.
- **10 different Thriving species**.
- Start a **Komodo Dragon Marketing Campaign**.
- Keep **2 Komodo Dragons**, each with at least **2,400 Animal Appeal**.
- Reach **5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

Seven species sharing one Habitat and ten Thriving species across the zoo are different objectives. A successful seven-species enclosure does not automatically complete the ten-species Thriving target, and keeping ten Thriving animals in separate Habitats does not satisfy the shared-Habitat requirement.

Check the relationship and welfare conditions of the animals sharing space, then use the rest of the zoo to develop additional Thriving species.

A particularly frustrating final obstacle is a zoo sitting just below five-star reputation despite its large animal collection. At that stage, inspect the rating breakdown before building another enclosure. **Education is worth checking closely**: incomplete topic coverage or insufficient education performance can leave reputation short even when guests appear satisfied.

Education Boards and Speakers covering useful, distinct information can help improve the weak category. However, keep checking the actual Reputation score rather than assuming that placing a fixed number of boards will always reach five stars.

The **Komodo Dragon Marketing Campaign** is an explicit Gold objective. Start the required campaign through the relevant marketing controls; extra Komodo Dragons and education facilities do not substitute for it.

## Poland Reserve — No Ifs, No Putts: Restore the Fen Wetland and Release Beavers and Bitterns

Poland's abandoned golf course must be restored into functioning ecosystems before its wildlife can return. The opening restoration work includes reflooding degraded land, establishing a Fen Wetland, and supplying animals that meet the reserve's ecological needs.

It helps to separate two questions from the start: **Is the ecosystem established?** And **does it support the Release Group you are trying to introduce?** Satisfying the first does not guarantee that the second is ready.

### Bronze: Fen Wetland, four Breeding Partners groups, and three-star Ecosystem Rating

Bronze requires:

- Establish **1 Fen Wetland Ecosystem**.
- Release **2 Eurasian Beaver Breeding Partners groups**.
- Release **2 Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners groups**.
- Reach **3-Star Ecosystem Rating**.

Start in the Ecosystems view and inspect the damaged wetland. The former golf-course terrain needs sufficient wet conditions and appropriate vegetation; a large designation alone will not make it a functioning Fen Wetland.

The important adjustments include **Waterlogged coverage** and **Semi-aquatic Plants**. These are separate requirements. Adding aquatic vegetation without meeting the required wet-ground conditions can leave the ecosystem Invalid.

Inspect the remaining categories after every major terrain or vegetation adjustment. Broad removal-brush strokes can also undo recently restored plants, so work on the categories that are actually deficient rather than repeatedly clearing and repainting the entire area.

### Why are the Eurasian Bitterns still blocked?

The animals can be ready for transport while Poland remains unable to accept the group.

A blocked Eurasian Bittern release can point to missing ecological roles, including **Wetland Engineers** and **Terrestrial Foragers**. The fact that the Fen Wetland exists is not enough if the animal's complete release requirements are still unsatisfied.

Eurasian Beavers are important in this wetland system, but releasing an animal into the wrong conditions is not a way to bypass an unmet ecosystem requirement.

Open the blocked Release Group and inspect its required ecosystems and roles. Determine which conditions are still missing before preparing or buying additional animals. Returning to terrain editing will not solve a missing animal role, just as acquiring another group will not fix an Invalid ecosystem.

**The Bronze objective counts groups, not individual animals.** Poland asks for two Eurasian Beaver Breeding Partners groups and two Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners groups. Releasing two individual Beavers does not automatically fulfill the requirement for two qualifying groups.

### Use Swiss Alps to prepare the missing animals

Swiss Alps provides a useful connection to Poland because its Bronze assignment involves preparing Beaver and Bittern Breeding Partners Release Groups.

Keep track of which animals are still available in the zoo, which are in Storage, and which already belong to a qualifying Release Group. If a group is blocked, inspect its readiness and destination requirements before finalising another one.

Do not use a different group type simply because the species name matches. The requested **Breeding Partners** configuration matters.

The [Wildlife Reserve guide](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) explains the animal Storage, Release Group, ecosystem, and research systems if the transfer controls themselves are preventing progress.

### Silver: Open Canopy Forest and European Bison

Silver requires:

- Establish **1 Open Canopy Forest Ecosystem**.
- Release **1 European Bison Mixed Group**.
- Reach **4-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

The Open Canopy Forest is a separate restoration objective from the Fen Wetland. Choose an appropriate area, then work through its own vegetation and terrain conditions rather than copying the wetland's requirements.

**Forest Floor coverage** and appropriate nesting or vegetation elements can be relevant during the forest restoration work. Check which elements the Ecosystem panel currently requires before placing them; the correct arrangement depends on the selected area and its existing conditions.

The European Bison requirement specifically asks for a **Mixed Group**. A solitary Bison or another group configuration does not replace it.

After the forest and wildlife objectives are satisfied, inspect the reserve's Biodiversity Rating. Improving one ecosystem does not necessarily resolve a weak reserve-wide research or environmental score.

### Gold: Five Flourishing Ecosystems

Gold requires:

- **5 Flourishing Ecosystems**.
- **5-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

**Flourishing is a higher condition than Established.** Five established regions do not automatically become five qualifying Flourishing ecosystems.

The additional work can involve supplying the ecological roles needed by individual environments, not merely increasing their area or adding more vegetation. For each ecosystem, inspect what prevents it from progressing beyond Established and match the missing conditions to suitable wildlife.

This makes animal preparation across FEN's zoo network important even after the initial Beaver and Bittern releases are complete.

Do not confuse the Gold requirement with Tanzania's later target. Poland specifically needs five **Flourishing** ecosystems, whereas Tanzania Gold asks for nine **Established** ecosystems alongside its other conservation goals.

## Swiss Alps — From A To Ski: Wild Boar Breeding, Release Groups, and Gold Reputation

**Starting money: $45,000**

Swiss Alps occupies a former ski resort. Its connected building areas and existing transport features make the setting different from a flat, empty zoo, but the main work revolves around **breeding animals for FEN's conservation network**.

The first medal begins with Wild Boars, then brings Eurasian Beavers and Bitterns into the program. Silver broadens the required Release Group types, while Gold adds groups suitable for three biomes and a much higher zoo reputation.

### Bronze: Breed three new female Wild Boars

Bronze requires:

- Have at least **4 Wild Boars** in the zoo.
- Breed **3 new Female Wild Boars**.
- Create and Finalise **1 Wild Boar Matriarchal Sounder Release Group**.
- Reach **2-Star Zoo Reputation**.
- Prepare **1 Eurasian Beaver Breeding Partners Release Group**.
- Prepare **1 Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners Release Group**.

Start with functional staff facilities and suitable animal accommodation. The existing layout can make deliveries less straightforward than they appear, so confirm that the Trade Center and each receiving Habitat have working path access before acquiring many animals.

#### The Trade Center has animals, but they never arrive

An animal can remain in Trade Storage while the game fails to complete its transfer into the assigned Habitat.

![Swiss Alps showing the disconnected Trade Center path that prevents animals being delivered.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-swiss-trade-center-path.webp)

Inspect the path immediately outside the Trade Center and trace the route to the Habitat Staff Gate. If that route is disconnected, increasing keeper numbers or purchasing another copy of the animal will not correct the delivery problem.

Repair the access connection, then check whether the pending transfer progresses.

#### Three new female offspring are not three adopted females

Wild Boar breeding has a separate condition from the minimum population requirement. The task asks for **three new female offspring**, not simply three additional females acquired through Animal Trading.

A healthy breeding population can produce male offspring without advancing the female counter. Continue managing the breeding group, monitor births, and avoid removing eligible animals prematurely while waiting for the required females.

Once the breeding goal is satisfied, prepare the correct **Matriarchal Sounder** Release Group. The game checks group composition; placing Wild Boars into an unrelated group type does not fulfill the assignment.

#### Finish the Beaver and Bittern preparations

The other part of Bronze requires qualifying Breeding Partners groups for **Eurasian Beavers** and **Eurasian Bitterns**.

Beavers need usable water space alongside their other Habitat conditions. A large enclosure can still produce a welfare problem if the swimming area is insufficient or the land and water requirements are badly balanced.

Bitterns need suitable avian accommodation, including their nesting and environmental needs. Treat these as separate animal-care tasks rather than trying to use the same Habitat layout for both species.

Once the required animals are available, examine their Release Group eligibility and prepare the correct Breeding Partners configurations. The goal is to complete the requested groups, not to release unrelated animals simply to reduce the population.

When the Wild Boar objectives, two Breeding Partners groups, and **2-Star Zoo Reputation** are satisfied, Swiss Alps earns Bronze.

### Silver: Three Breeding Partners and three Mixed Release Groups

Silver requires:

- **3 Breeding Partners Release Groups**.
- **3 Mixed Group Release Groups**.
- **3.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

This stage can take longer than the first medal because it expands the group requirements beyond the animals initially raised at the ski resort.

The useful approach is to inspect your existing animals before creating additional Habitats. Open Animal Trading and Storage, identify eligible animals, and check which group configurations they can form. Animals already available through the FEN network may shorten the preparation process.

The Release Groups interface distinguishes **Breeding Partners** from **Mixed Groups**. Changing the species does not change the required group type, and selecting the wrong group category can make an otherwise useful animal appear unsuitable.

#### Why can't I complete the last Mixed Group?

Work backward from the incomplete counter.

Open the relevant Release Group category and compare it with the animals in Storage. Some animals may still be in Quarantine, too young for the intended group, injured, or otherwise unavailable for the combination you selected. Do not assume that every animal displayed in Trading can immediately be used in every group.

A practical sequence is to finish one qualifying group, inspect the objective counter, and then choose the next eligible species or combination. Preparing several groups without checking their types can leave you with the right number of animals but the wrong number of qualifying Release Groups.

Animal Trading can also help when your existing breeding population cannot supply the missing group. Look for animals that meet the specific requirements instead of buying replacements based solely on species.

During the later breeding work, groups involving animals such as wolves and birds can become useful, provided their composition meets the requested category. The exact species is less important than satisfying the group's eligibility and allowing the objective to register.

Keep the zoo's existing animals healthy while working through Storage and Trading. Silver also requires **3.5-Star Zoo Reputation**, so a completed group counter is not the entire medal.

Once all three groups of each required type and the reputation target are satisfied, the Silver medal is awarded.

### Gold: Taiga, Grassland, Tropical, and 4.5-Star Zoo Reputation

Gold requires:

- **1 Taiga Biome Release Group**.
- **1 Grassland Biome Release Group**.
- **1 Tropical Biome Release Group**.
- **4.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

The first three objectives are about **biome-qualified Release Groups**. They do not ask you to build three corresponding habitats inside Swiss Alps.

Inspect the animals available through Storage and Animal Trading, then check their biome information and Release Group eligibility. A group that is accepted for one biome does not necessarily satisfy another biome's counter.

Do not select animals based on the visual appearance of their enclosure or their real-world habitat alone. Use the actual biome shown in the game's animal and group information.

The important result is that the required group is accepted under the correct biome category. This allows you to progress without constructing an entirely new zoo area for each biome.

### The final Gold obstacle: 4.5-Star Zoo Reputation

After completing the three biome groups, the remaining objective can be **Zoo Reputation**. That changes what is worth doing next: more Release Groups will not help if the only unfinished condition is the rating.

Inspect the Reputation panel rather than continuing to purchase animals without a specific purpose.

In one successful Swiss Alps progression, the zoo had reached the required group objectives but still needed a higher reputation. Improving guest services and education became the focus.

Food and drink needs were among the weaknesses that required attention. Adding suitable guest facilities where visitors could reach them helped address those needs. Education was another area that needed improvement, so additional species information and educational activities were introduced while the zoo continued operating.

The rating can remain just below the threshold even after visible progress. At that point, look for the remaining weak categories:

- **Guest needs:** if visitors lack food or drinks, provide facilities where they are actually walking.
- **Education:** inspect the rating and improve useful coverage near animals guests can see.
- **Animal welfare:** identify species below their required living conditions rather than improving animals that are already thriving.
- **Staff and Habitat operations:** check that feeding, cleaning, animal treatment, and access are functioning across the zoo.

The Wild Boars and Beavers can be a distraction here because an enclosure may look spacious while its welfare panel still reports a particular deficiency. Check the actual category before adding more decorative objects or rebuilding the whole Habitat.

Increasing species variety can contribute to zoo development, but every new animal also introduces welfare, maintenance, and access demands. It is safer to improve existing facilities first when you are already close to 4.5 stars.

Swiss Alps Gold was achieved after the biome requirements were complete and further zoo improvements raised reputation to the required level. **The final task was not another biome group; it was getting the operating zoo across the reputation threshold.**

## Tanzania Reserve — Take Pride: Five Ecosystems, Wildlife Releases, and Research Photos

Tanzania is the final large-scale Wildlife Reserve assignment of Chapter 4. It provides unlimited money, but its progress depends on building suitable ecosystems, returning African wildlife, improving biodiversity, and photographing particular released animals.

Unlike Swiss Alps, where the last medal depends heavily on breeding and zoo reputation, Tanzania requires extensive work across the reserve itself.

The first thing to establish is which ecosystems are already usable and which still fail their environmental requirements. Starting a new ecosystem from scratch is not always necessary when an existing designated area can be corrected.

### Bronze: Establish five ecosystems and release the Rhinoceros and Leopard

Bronze requires:

- Establish **5 Ecosystems**.
- Release **1 Black Rhinoceros**.
- Release **1 African Leopard**.
- Take a Research Photo of **1 African Leopard**.
- Take a Research Photo of **1 Black Rhinoceros**.

Begin by opening the Ecosystems view and inspecting the existing areas. Tanzania contains large stretches of dry land and several different ecosystem types, including Savannah and other grassland, forest, and wetland environments.

The objective counts ecosystems with **Established** status. Simply having five regions designated on the map does not complete it.

### Why is the Savannah marked Too Small?

One of the first visible problems in Tanzania is a Savannah ecosystem reporting **Too Small**.

![Tanzania Savannah ecosystem marked Too Small, with unmet Grass, Scrub Grass, Detritus, Tree Litter, Termite Mounds, and Acacia Trees requirements.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-tanzania-savannah-too-small.webp)

This is an area requirement, not necessarily a missing-tree problem. Before filling the region with additional plants, inspect its minimum area condition and adjust the ecosystem boundary until the size requirement is satisfied.

Then check its remaining terrain and vegetation categories. The Savannah may need further environmental work after the boundary is large enough; solving the size problem is only one part of establishing it.

If you modify an area that borders another ecosystem, inspect the neighbouring region afterward. Changing one boundary can affect the usable area of another, so expanding the Savannah without checking adjacent ecosystems may create a new problem elsewhere.

The initial work is therefore about correcting the current failing conditions, not assuming that the entire dry reserve needs to be rebuilt.

### Established is not the same as supplying every ecological role

While inspecting Tanzania's Savannah, additional ecological roles such as **Low Browsers** and **Scavengers** become relevant.

These are not interchangeable with the basic area requirement. An ecosystem may reach Established and still have further requirements to improve its ecological state.

For Bronze, concentrate on reaching the required **five Established Ecosystems** and providing suitable conditions for the named wildlife releases. When an ecosystem needs additional ecological roles for later improvement, inspect which animals can supply them rather than randomly introducing species.

The [Wildlife Reserve ecosystem and release guide](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) explains how to interpret ecosystem status, ecological requirements, and release eligibility.

### Release and photograph the Black Rhinoceros and African Leopard

Once the required ecosystems are available, prepare the Black Rhinoceros and African Leopard groups using the FEN animal network.

Check the destination requirements for each animal before attempting the release. The appropriate habitats and any additional conditions must be satisfied; an animal waiting in Storage is not proof that the reserve can accept it.

Bronze also contains two separate photography objectives. After releasing the Black Rhinoceros, locate it within the reserve and obtain the required Research Photo. Do the same for the African Leopard.

The important distinction is that **a successful release does not automatically satisfy the photograph objective**. Both tasks must register for each named species.

Do not spend time preparing further animals merely because the photography counter has not advanced. Check which objective remains unfinished: the animal's release, its location in the reserve, or the required Research Photo.

### Silver: Buffalo, Lion, Elephant, and three-star Biodiversity

Silver adds release and photography objectives for:

- **1 Cape Buffalo**.
- **1 Lion**.
- **1 African Savannah Elephant**.

You also need **3-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

Each of the three species has both a release objective and a Research Photo objective. They should therefore be treated as separate requirements rather than one combined animal-delivery task.

Prepare ecosystems that can support the intended wildlife, then check each Release Group's destination conditions. As the reserve develops, inspect the Biodiversity Rating rather than expecting the animal releases alone to raise it to three stars.

The rating can remain unfinished even after the named species are present. Environmental restoration and the reserve's research capabilities contribute to biodiversity progress, so examine the rating breakdown before deciding whether the next improvement should be another animal, an ecosystem change, or research infrastructure.

### Gold: Nine ecosystems, fifteen Release Groups, and five-star Biodiversity

Gold requires:

- Release **1 African Tigerfish**.
- Release **1 Blunthead Cichlids group**.
- Complete **15 Release Groups**.
- Release and photograph **1 White-Backed Vulture**.
- Release and photograph **1 Secretarybird**.
- Have at least **9 Established Ecosystems**.
- Reach **5-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

The Gold objectives combine three distinct kinds of work: developing the reserve, introducing more animal groups, and completing species-specific releases and research photographs.

The fish require appropriate aquatic environments. Prepare the water ecosystems and confirm the release conditions before treating the African Tigerfish and Blunthead Cichlids objectives as ordinary animal purchases.

The White-Backed Vulture and Secretarybird each have a release requirement and a photograph requirement. Introducing the birds does not finish their photography tasks.

The **15 Release Groups** target is cumulative and should be monitored independently from the number of ecosystems. Fifteen individual animals are not automatically fifteen groups, and establishing additional ecosystems does not increase the Release Group counter.

Likewise, the Gold requirement for **9 Established Ecosystems** is different from Poland's five Flourishing Ecosystems. Tanzania does not require all nine to reach Flourishing for that specific counter, although further ecosystem improvement may still help with Biodiversity Rating.

### What to work on when Biodiversity is the last objective

Once the named wildlife releases and ecosystem counts are complete, look at the reserve-wide Biodiversity Rating.

Check the **Ecosystem Rating** and **Research Rating** rather than continuing the same activity without examining what is missing.

If the environment is behind, inspect incomplete ecosystems, remaining restoration needs, and the wildlife required for suitable ecological roles. If research performance is weaker, improve the monitoring network using the facilities available to the reserve.

Tanzania's long-term progression is best handled by keeping these counters separate: ecosystems established, qualifying Release Groups completed, required animals released, Research Photos taken, and Biodiversity Rating improved. A high value in one category does not replace an unfinished objective in another.
