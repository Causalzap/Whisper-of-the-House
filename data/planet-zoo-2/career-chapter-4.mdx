<GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Complete Career Chapter 4">
Earn **Sweden Reserve Bronze** and reach **Career Accreditation Tier 4** to open **Maluku Islands**. Complete Maluku Bronze first to unlock the other five Chapter 4 locations. From there, choose a zoo or reserve and follow its remaining Bronze objectives before returning for Silver and Gold.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The last Career chapter, **FENtastic Opportunity**, begins with an expensive island aquarium and expands into five former Envalza properties. You will rebuild empty zoos, restore wetlands, establish breeding programs, and repopulate a large African Wildlife Reserve.

The important change from Chapter 3 is that you no longer need to complete a particular Silver medal to access the next location. **Maluku Islands Bronze opens all five remaining assignments**, giving you a choice about where to spend your animals, money, and time.

There are six scenarios:

- **Mola Money, Mola Problems — Maluku Islands**
- **Not Much To Sea — Channel Islands**
- **Dragon, Ball, Zoo — Singapore**
- **No Ifs, No Putts — Poland Reserve**
- **From A To Ski — Swiss Alps**
- **Take Pride — Tanzania Reserve**

Maluku Islands, Channel Islands, Singapore, and Swiss Alps are zoo assignments, while Poland and Tanzania are Wildlife Reserves. Swiss Alps also connects the two sides by preparing breeding groups for conservation releases.

## How to Unlock Chapter 4 and All Six Locations

**Maluku Islands** requires **Career Accreditation Tier 4**, which means earning **12 Bronze medals and 2 Silver medals**. You must also have the **Bronze medal from Sweden Reserve**.

If Maluku remains locked, inspect both conditions in the Planet Hub. A sufficient total medal count does not replace the specific Sweden Bronze requirement.

The [Chapter 3 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3) covers the Sumatra Silver return trip needed to reach Sweden, followed by Sweden's conservation objectives.

Once Maluku Bronze is complete, the following destinations become available:

- **Channel Islands:** Build a diverse zoo from an empty site.
- **Singapore:** Restore an abandoned stadium and manage high-appeal animals.
- **Poland Reserve:** Recover a damaged wetland and reintroduce European species.
- **Swiss Alps:** Breed animals and prepare specialist Release Groups.
- **Tanzania Reserve:** Establish ecosystems and return African wildlife.

There is no requirement to earn Maluku Silver or Gold before visiting them.

One useful route is to finish **Maluku Bronze**, then work on **Swiss Alps** before attempting all the releases in **Poland Reserve**. Swiss Alps asks you to prepare Eurasian Beaver and Eurasian Bittern breeding groups, while Poland needs those same species released into its recovering ecosystems.

You can choose another order if the necessary animals are already available elsewhere in your FEN network.

## Maluku Islands — Mola Money, Mola Problems: Fix the Aquariums First

**Starting money: $15,000**

Maluku is the gateway to the rest of Chapter 4, but its starting budget is considerably smaller than the other Chapter 4 zoos. The islands also have unusually expensive terrain modification, so connecting large areas of land can consume money that would be more useful for animal care.

The location already contains aquatic facilities and animals. Your first priority is to bring those animals up to the required welfare level rather than immediately building another aquarium.

### Bronze: Repair welfare and prepare the Ocean Sunfish

Maluku Bronze requires:

- At least **6 aquatic animals** in the zoo, with the required welfare at **100%**.
- At least **6 shoals**.
- **Hawksbill Turtle Research Level 4**.
- At least **500 guests**.
- **$3,000 profit over 1 season**.
- Adopt and place **1 new Ocean Sunfish**.
- Raise the Ocean Sunfish's average welfare to **100%**.

Start by checking the animals already present. Several welfare problems can be improved without creating an entirely new tank.

For animals that share an aquarium, inspect each species' terrain and environment requirements before changing the entire enclosure. During this assignment, adjusting the substrate to satisfy one animal can lower another animal's welfare. The existing sharks and other aquatic residents do not necessarily want the same proportions of rock, soil, or vegetation.

That means a seemingly helpful terrain adjustment can move the objective in the wrong direction.

Adjust the existing aquarium's substrate in small increments, checking the welfare of every species sharing the tank after each change. Keep Hawksbill Turtle research moving toward Level 4 while correcting the animals' conditions. A separate aquarium becomes useful when two species cannot satisfy their environmental requirements in the same enclosure.

![Ocean Sunfish environmental requirements showing the water-temperature problem in Maluku Islands.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-maluku-sunfish-temperature.webp)

For the Ocean Sunfish, select the animal and inspect its own Environment panel. Adjust the water temperature toward its acceptable range and check the resulting welfare. A single fixed temperature is not a solution for every species housed in the same aquarium.

The [aquarium building and filtration guide](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) covers the Staff Gate, water-filling, filtration, and power systems in more detail.

### Do not confuse shoals with individual aquatic animals

The Bronze requirement for **6 shoals** is separate from the requirement for aquatic animals.

Buying six individual fish does not necessarily give you six shoals. Check the actual shoal count and the number of groups placed in the zoo before spending more money.

You also need a **new Ocean Sunfish** for the specific adoption objective. Improving an existing species' welfare will not substitute for adopting and placing the required animal.

Once the animal and research objectives are complete, let the zoo operate long enough to reach the guest and seasonal profit targets. The $3,000 requirement is a profit target for one season, not simply a minimum cash balance.

### Silver: Ten species and ten shoal species

Maluku Silver raises the collection requirements to:

- **10 aquatic habitat species**.
- **10 shoal species**.
- **Ocean Sunfish Research Level 4**.

The important word here is **species**.

Several shoals of the same fish may help with an earlier group count, but they do not satisfy a requirement for ten different shoal species.

Use Animal Trading to identify distinct compatible species, and confirm which ones are already present before buying additional animals. In a shared aquarium, compatibility and welfare matter more than fitting the largest possible collection into one tank.

Keep Ocean Sunfish research running while expanding the collection. It is possible to finish placing the required species and still have the research objective outstanding.

### Gold: The Ocean Sunfish must actually thrive

The final targets are:

- **15 aquatic habitat species**.
- At least **3 different Thriving species**.
- At least **1 Thriving Ocean Sunfish**.

An Ocean Sunfish with 100% welfare is not automatically guaranteed to satisfy a separate Thriving requirement at that moment. Inspect the animal's current status and any remaining needs instead of relying only on the welfare percentage.

Maluku is worth revisiting when the zoo has a reliable income and the required research is complete. There is little benefit in delaying the five newly unlocked Career locations solely to collect another five aquatic species for Gold.

## Channel Islands — Not Much To Sea: Get the Right Species and Stop Protests

**Starting money: $45,000**

Channel Islands begins as an empty coastal zoo. The available space makes expansion tempting, but spreading facilities too far apart creates longer staff routes and leaves guests without food, drinks, education, or viewing opportunities.

The first serious obstacle is understanding exactly which types of animals count toward the different collection objectives.

### Bronze: Four kinds of animal accommodation

To earn Bronze, you need:

- **1 Terrestrial Habitat species**.
- **1 Avian Habitat species**.
- **1 Aquatic Habitat species**.
- **4 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **95% average animal welfare**.
- **75% Guest Average Happiness Need**, with at least **350 guests**.
- No protesters for **1 season**.
- A **4-Star Zoo Inspection Report**.
- **3-Star Zoo Reputation**.

Build a connected service area before filling the zoo with animals. Keep the Trade Center, Keeper Hut, staff access routes, and essential guest facilities working, then expand into the required animal categories.

The four Terrestrial Exhibit species are a particularly easy requirement to misunderstand.

![Channel Islands objective requiring four different Terrestrial Exhibit species.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-channel-terrestrial-exhibits.webp)

**Terrestrial Exhibit species are not the same as Terrestrial Habitat species.**

A Red Panda placed in a conventional habitat does not count as one of the four Terrestrial Exhibit species. Neither does a fish added to an aquarium.

Look for species housed in terrestrial Exhibits, such as the appropriate reptiles and invertebrates. Check the species count after each new Exhibit animal is placed rather than assuming every new animal advances the same objective.

The early zoo also presents a practical staff-access problem. An Exhibit or habitat may exist and have an assigned animal while its keeper cannot reach it because a nearby path is disconnected.

When an animal delivery or feeding task is not progressing, inspect the path from the staff facilities to the actual access point. Fixing that connection is more useful than hiring another keeper to wait at the same inaccessible destination.

### The one-season protest requirement

Once the animal and guest objectives are close to completion, stop making disruptive changes and focus on the conditions driving complaints.

The first stage requires **one complete season without protesters**. Completing the welfare and guest targets does not automatically satisfy the time-based requirement.

Watch the protest counter and the remaining seasonal progress. A new protest during the required period can interrupt the streak even when the rest of the zoo is performing well.

The 4-Star Inspection Report and 3-Star Reputation requirements also need separate attention. Check those panels directly rather than assuming that high guest happiness means both are already complete.

### Silver and Gold: Expansion becomes a stability test

Silver requires:

- **7 different Habitat species**.
- **8 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **80% Guest Average Happiness Need**, with at least **600 guests**.
- **2 seasons without protesters**.
- **4-Star Zoo Reputation**.

Gold raises the targets again:

- **11 different Habitat species**, meeting the required **100% welfare** condition.
- **85% Guest Average Happiness Need**, with at least **900 guests**.
- **4 seasons without protesters**.
- **4.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

The hardest part is keeping all these conditions stable at the same time.

Adding species increases the number of habitats, food requirements, veterinary issues, and possible welfare alerts. More guests then place greater pressure on shops, toilets, drinks, seating, and maintenance.

That is why simply expanding the animal collection can make the protest objective harder.

For the later medals, follow a more controlled rhythm:

1. Add a small group of new species and bring their welfare up to the required level.
2. Wait for guest facilities and staff coverage to catch up.
3. Check for active protesters and unresolved animal alerts.
4. Only begin relying on the seasonal streak after the zoo has stabilized.

A real setback here is reaching the end of a protest-free period and seeing the counter restart because another protest began. You may need to wait through the full requirement again.

![Channel Islands Gold medal awarded after completing all Bronze, Silver, and Gold objectives.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-channel-gold-medal.webp)

Once the animal collection, guest numbers, and reputation are already sufficient, there is no advantage in repeatedly expanding while waiting for the last seasonal counter. Hold the zoo steady and let the remaining seasons pass.

## Singapore — Dragon, Ball, Zoo: Clear the Stadium and Fix Komodo Dragon Appeal

**Starting money: $45,000**

Singapore takes place at an abandoned stadium. Its objectives mix site cleanup, shared habitats, animal appeal, and guest education.

The building footprint is important: you need several species living in a single habitat, not merely several separate habitats scattered around the stadium.

### Bronze: Cleanup, a shared habitat, and the Komodo Dragon

The Bronze objectives include:

- Remove **50 pieces of debris** from inside the stadium.
- House **3 different species in one Habitat**, with the required **90% welfare**.
- Keep at least **1 Great Hornbill**, with **90% welfare**.
- Reach **350 guests**.
- Have **1 Komodo Dragon** with at least **2,600 Animal Appeal**.
- Have at least **1 Thriving Komodo Dragon**.
- Set up education facilities with **5 different Komodo Dragon education topics**.

Start with the debris. Clearing the overgrown stadium gives you usable space and prevents the cleanup goal from being forgotten under new construction.

Next, establish a shared Habitat for three compatible species. The objective counts different species inside the same habitat. Three species living in three separate habitats will not satisfy it.

Inspect the habitat's species list after introducing each animal, and pay attention to terrain, space, social groups, and welfare. Compatibility on paper is not enough if one of the residents remains stressed or cannot reach the required welfare level.

The Great Hornbill can be managed as its own objective. Its welfare requirement does not mean it must be one of the three shared-habitat species.

### Why is Komodo Dragon Appeal stuck below 2,600?

This is one of Singapore's most confusing requirements.

The objective asks for **one Komodo Dragon with Animal Appeal of at least 2,600**. A player can find a dragon showing only **2,500 Appeal** and have difficulty understanding why the objective is not progressing.

![Singapore Komodo Dragon objective requiring 2,600 Animal Appeal.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-singapore-komodo-appeal.webp)

Check the Animal Appeal value for the individual dragon in the zoo. Do not mistake the welfare percentage for the Appeal value.

The scenario also asks for a Thriving Komodo Dragon. Improve the animal's habitat, feeding, enrichment, research, and environmental conditions while monitoring those two requirements separately.

A second dragon is not automatically the solution to the 2,600 Appeal requirement. Confirm the actual Appeal value before purchasing or moving more animals.

For the education objective, five copies of the same sign are not enough if they all teach the same topic.

Select **five different education topics about Komodo Dragons** using the available education facilities. Check the assigned topic on each facility rather than counting the number of boards or speakers you have placed.

### Silver: Five species in one Habitat

Silver requires:

- **5 different species living in one Habitat**.
- **3 different Terrestrial Exhibit species**.
- **4.5-Star Guest Education**.
- At least **1,000 guests**.

The shared-habitat objective increases from three species to five. Expanding an existing compatible mixed habitat can save construction costs, but it also makes animal welfare harder to maintain.

Do not confuse Habitat species with Terrestrial Exhibit species here. They are two distinct counters.

When the five-species objective fails to advance, open the specific habitat and check its animal list. A species kept in an aquarium, a separate habitat, or an Exhibit elsewhere in the zoo does not increase the number of species sharing that habitat.

Guest Education is another separate target. More education signs will not necessarily improve it if they repeat a narrow range of subjects or are disconnected from the animals guests are actually visiting.

Spread relevant education facilities across the zoo and inspect the Education panel for the measure that remains below target.

### Gold: Seven shared species, ten Thriving species, and five-star reputation

Gold requires:

- **7 different species in a single Habitat**.
- At least **10 different Thriving species**.
- Start a **Komodo Dragon Marketing Campaign**.
- Have **2 Komodo Dragons**, each meeting the required **2,400 Animal Appeal** condition.
- Reach **5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

The shared Habitat and Thriving requirements are related but not interchangeable. Seven animals of different species living together do not automatically give you ten different Thriving species across the zoo.

It is worth keeping the shared Habitat limited to compatible animals while making the wider zoo collection thrive in several separate enclosures.

For the five-star rating, inspect the specific Reputation categories holding the score back. A zoo can appear successful, with satisfied guests and healthy animals, while still sitting at 4.9 stars.

Education coverage is particularly worth checking. Having excellent average guest education does not guarantee that every relevant education measure is fully satisfied.

The Komodo Dragon Marketing Campaign is also an explicit objective. Start the campaign instead of assuming that placing more dragons or education signs will count as marketing.

## Poland Reserve — No Ifs, No Putts: Restore Fen Wetland Before Releasing Animals

The abandoned golf course in Poland must be turned back into functioning wetland habitat. Its first objective is much more specific than simply drawing a large ecosystem boundary.

You need to establish **one Fen Wetland Ecosystem** and prepare wildlife that can survive in it.

### Bronze: Fen Wetland and the missing ecological roles

The requirements are:

- Establish **1 Fen Wetland Ecosystem**.
- Release **2 Eurasian Beaver Breeding Partners**.
- Release **2 Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners**.
- Reach **3-Star Ecosystem Rating**.

Begin by selecting the Fen Wetland and inspecting its individual terrain and nature requirements.

The damaged golf-course land needs wet conditions and suitable vegetation. In particular, restoring the area can involve changing its **Waterlogged** coverage and adding **Semi-aquatic Plants**.

For this former golf course, adjust Waterlogged coverage and Semi-aquatic Plants separately. Large removal-brush strokes can undo vegetation you have already restored, so check the Fen Wetland requirements again after each change.

Once Fen Wetland is established, the animal releases introduce a different challenge.

A Eurasian Bittern group may remain blocked even when the animals are ready for transport.

The blockage can be caused by missing ecosystem roles such as **Wetland Engineers** and **Terrestrial Foragers**.

Check the blocked release's requirements to see which ecological roles are still missing. Eurasian Beavers are particularly relevant to restoring wetland systems, but the game still needs the appropriate animals and suitable release conditions before those roles count.

This is where returning to the FEN zoo network becomes useful.

**Swiss Alps** has objectives involving Eurasian Beaver and Eurasian Bittern breeding groups. You can prepare the required animals through your zoos rather than repeatedly trying to release a group into an unsuitable reserve.

The [Wildlife Reserve release and ecosystem guide](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) explains how to inspect Release Group readiness and the ecosystem requirements that can block a transfer.

Be precise about the medal targets: Poland requires **two Breeding Partners groups for each named species** at Bronze, not merely two individual animals.

### Silver: Open Canopy Forest and European Bison

Silver adds three objectives:

- Establish **1 Open Canopy Forest Ecosystem**.
- Release **1 European Bison Mixed Group**.
- Reach **4-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

The forest and biodiversity targets are separate from the Fen Wetland objective.

After establishing the wetland, choose an appropriate area for Open Canopy Forest and work through that ecosystem's own terrain and vegetation requirements.

The European Bison group must meet the **Mixed Group** release requirement. Do not substitute a solitary animal or a different group configuration merely because the species is correct.

Once the releases are complete, continue improving the reserve until the Biodiversity Rating reaches four stars.

### Gold: Five Flourishing Ecosystems

Gold requires:

- At least **5 Flourishing Ecosystems**.
- **5-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

Notice the distinction between **Established** and **Flourishing**.

Creating five ecosystem areas and reaching Established status is not sufficient for a target that explicitly asks for Flourishing ecosystems.

As the reserve develops, inspect each ecosystem individually and improve those that remain below the required status. Increasing the number of designated ecosystems alone does not guarantee five-star biodiversity.

The best reason to return here is when the necessary release groups are available and the reserve's ecosystem roles can be satisfied. Until then, continued terrain work may not solve the specific animal-release blockage.

## Swiss Alps — From A To Ski: Breed Female Wild Boars and Prepare Release Groups

**Starting money: $45,000**

Swiss Alps occupies a former ski destination divided into separate building areas. It is designed around breeding animals for the FEN conservation network, rather than simply building the largest zoo possible.

The opening Wild Boar goals are not completed by adopting any four boars and immediately shipping them away.

### Bronze: Three new female Wild Boars

The Bronze goals include:

- Have at least **4 Wild Boars** in the zoo.
- Breed **3 new Female Wild Boars**.
- Create and Finalise **1 Wild Boar Matriarchal Sounder Release Group**.
- Reach **2-Star Zoo Reputation**.
- Prepare **1 Eurasian Beaver Breeding Partners Release Group**.
- Prepare **1 Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners Release Group**.

Start by setting up the staff infrastructure and a suitable Wild Boar Habitat.

Because this map has separated building areas and existing routes, inspect the path network carefully before adopting a large number of animals. You need a usable connection between the Trade Center, staff services, and each Habitat access point.

A straightforward failure occurs when an animal has been purchased but never arrives at its enclosure.

![Swiss Alps showing the disconnected Trade Center path that prevents animals being delivered.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4-swiss-trade-center-path.webp)

The animal may be sitting in Trade Storage while staff cannot complete delivery. Check the path immediately outside the Trade Center and the route to the receiving Habitat before increasing staff numbers or buying a replacement animal.

### Breeding more boars is not the same as breeding three females

Once the Wild Boars have suitable terrain, shelter, social conditions, food, and veterinary care, allow the breeding group to reproduce.

The task specifically requires **three new female offspring**.

A run of male piglets can leave the objective unchanged even though the habitat is producing healthy babies. The animals are breeding successfully, but the required sex count has not been reached.

Continue managing the breeding population and monitor the newly born animals. Buying an adult female from Animal Trading is not the same as breeding a new female for this objective.

The release group also has to be the correct type: **Wild Boar Matriarchal Sounder**.

Inspect the Release Group composition before finalising it. A group containing Wild Boars is not enough if its configuration does not match the requested group type.

### Prepare Beavers and Bitterns without losing control of the zoo

The remaining Bronze work involves Eurasian Beaver and Eurasian Bittern Breeding Partners groups.

Beavers introduce their own Habitat needs, including suitable water space. A habitat that appears large from above may still be unsuitable because it lacks enough usable water or the required terrain.

Arrange their enclosure before buying or moving more animals. Then make sure the selected animals qualify for the requested release group.

The Bittern group has a different animal-care setup. Treat its requirements separately rather than assuming the Beaver enclosure or social structure can be copied.

These groups are particularly useful for returning animals to Wildlife Reserves such as Poland.

Do not finalise the wrong group type just to clear animals out of storage. Inspect the requested breeding composition and make sure the correct group has been accepted.

### Silver and Gold: The breeding program gets broader

Silver requires:

- **3 Breeding Partners Release Groups**.
- **3 Mixed Group Release Groups**.
- **3.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

Gold changes the focus to releasing animals suitable for three different biomes:

- **1 Taiga Biome Release Group**.
- **1 Grassland Biome Release Group**.
- **1 Tropical Biome Release Group**.
- **4.5-Star Zoo Reputation**.

This is a good reason to build the Swiss Alps zoo around manageable breeding populations rather than making every new species part of one large shared habitat.

The later medals depend on supplying the correct types of groups, not simply accumulating animals in storage. Zoo Reputation must also be maintained while the population and staff costs grow.

## Tanzania Reserve — Take Pride: Establish Five Ecosystems and Release African Wildlife

Tanzania is the final large-scale conservation assignment in Chapter 4. The reserve has unlimited starting money and asks you to establish ecosystems before repopulating them with African wildlife.

Tanzania combines ecosystem restoration with wildlife releases and research photography. Each animal must meet the reserve's release conditions, and releasing a species does not automatically complete its photography objective.

### Bronze: Five Ecosystems, Rhinoceros and Leopard

Bronze requires:

- Establish **5 Ecosystems**.
- Release **1 Black Rhinoceros**.
- Release **1 African Leopard**.
- Photograph **1 African Leopard**.
- Photograph **1 Black Rhinoceros**.

Begin in the Ecosystems view. Check the terrain and vegetation requirements for the ecosystems you intend to restore, then establish five suitable areas.

Tanzania can support grassland, savannah, lowland forest, and wetland environments. The important target at Bronze is **five Established Ecosystems**, not simply five areas marked on the map.

Prepare the Black Rhinoceros and African Leopard Release Groups once suitable habitats and ecosystem conditions are available.

Each species has two separate tasks: release it into the reserve and photograph it in the wild.

Do not treat a successful release as completing the photography objective. After the animals have been introduced, locate them within the reserve and take the required research photographs.

If a release remains blocked, inspect the actual destination conditions rather than sending additional animals of the same species.

### Silver: Three more species and 3-Star Biodiversity

Silver asks you to release and photograph:

- **1 Cape Buffalo**
- **1 Lion**
- **1 African Savannah Elephant**

You must also reach **3-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

The release and photograph requirements apply to each named species, so the number of tasks is larger than a simple three-animal release list suggests.

Work on suitable ecosystems for these species while preparing the animals. As you introduce more wildlife, monitor whether the existing ecosystem conditions can support the animals and whether the reserve's Biodiversity Rating is increasing.

The rating is an additional requirement; completing the individual releases does not necessarily award Silver immediately.

### Gold: Fifteen Release Groups and nine Ecosystems

Gold expands the work considerably:

- Release **1 African Tigerfish**.
- Release **1 group of Blunthead Cichlids**.
- Complete **15 Release Groups**.
- Release and photograph **1 White-Backed Vulture**.
- Release and photograph **1 Secretarybird**.
- Have at least **9 Established Ecosystems**.
- Reach **5-Star Biodiversity Rating**.

Plan the aquatic species alongside suitable water ecosystems instead of leaving both fish releases until every terrestrial ecosystem is finished.

The bird objectives also require both release and photography. Ensure that the environment is ready for those species before preparing the corresponding groups.

There are two different ecosystem numbers to remember:

- **Bronze:** 5 Established Ecosystems.
- **Gold:** 9 Established Ecosystems.

You do not need nine Flourishing ecosystems for the objective as written. That is different from Poland Reserve Gold, which explicitly requires five Flourishing ecosystems.

The 15 Release Groups are another cumulative target to track separately from the number of ecosystems and the Biodiversity Rating.

Tanzania is best approached as a series of ecosystem and release milestones. Complete the five Bronze ecosystems and the first two wildlife releases, then expand toward Silver and Gold while keeping track of the species-specific photographs.
