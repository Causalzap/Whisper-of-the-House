**Planet Zoo 2** lets you manage animal habitats, restore Wildlife Reserves, and build conservation networks through several different game modes. Your next objective depends on whether you are following FEN's Career assignments or developing your own Franchise.

Career contains **18 scenarios across four chapters**, with Bronze, Silver, and Gold medals awarded for different objectives. Earlier locations remain available when you need to return for another medal. Franchise has separate Accreditation requirements, tied to the reputation of your zoos and the biodiversity of your reserves.


<section id="game-modes" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Career, Franchise, or Sandbox: Which Mode Should You Play?

**Career** is the best starting point when you want to learn the game's systems through FEN's assignments. Each location gives you specific animal care, construction, research, or conservation objectives, with medals opening further opportunities.

**Franchise** lets you build your own zoos and Wildlife Reserves. You choose the animals, manage your finances, and raise Accreditation to gain access to more species. Start with the part of your Franchise that needs attention:

- **Starting a zoo or losing money?** Follow the [Franchise starting and money guide](/planet-zoo-2/franchise-guide) for staff facilities, animal transport, admission prices, and early expansion.
- **Animals locked in Trading?** Check the [Franchise Accreditation requirements](/planet-zoo-2/franchise-accreditation) to see which Zoo Reputation and Wildlife Reserve ratings unlock the next tier.
- **Too many offspring or low animal welfare?** Use the [breeding and overcrowding instructions](/planet-zoo-2/franchise-breeding) to manage Contraception, social groups, and surplus animals.

**Sandbox** is suited to building and experimenting without following Career's medal progression. Its settings offer a different experience from managing a progressing Franchise.

## All 4 Career Chapters and 18 Scenarios

Career introduces new management responsibilities gradually. You begin with straightforward animal care and then move into larger conservation networks, financial recovery, research, and environmental restoration.

### Chapter 1: Wild At Heart

**2 scenarios**

- **Earning Your Stripes — Great Rift Valley Reserve:** Photography, exploring the reserve, and your first wildlife release.
- **Spreading Your Wings — East Africa:** Terrestrial Habitats, Aviaries, animal welfare, and preparation for conservation releases.

Completing the first Bronze medals is not the same as finishing the chapter's later objectives. You can return after progressing elsewhere.

### Chapter 2: Paws on the Path

**5 scenarios**

- **All A-Fjord — Nordic Fjords:** Aquariums, aquatic animals, water quality, and veterinary treatment.
- **Otterly Lush — South East Wales:** Quarantine, habitat repairs, guest education, and Zoo Reputation.
- **Grizzly Details — New England:** Donations, shops, marketing, and repaying an outstanding loan.
- **Deer, Oh Deer — Scottish Highlands:** Research coverage, predator reintroduction, and ecosystem restoration.
- **A Penny Learned — South Africa:** Rescued animal treatment, disease research, and staff training.

Nordic Fjords leads into South East Wales. After earning Bronze in South East Wales, New England, Scottish Highlands, and South Africa become available as separate assignments.

You do not need to finish those three locations in a fixed order.

New England is a useful choice when you want to focus on zoo finances. Scottish Highlands introduces more demanding Wildlife Reserve objectives, while South Africa concentrates on animal rehabilitation.

The [Chapter 2 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-2) explains the objectives and route through all five scenarios.

### Chapter 3: Claws and Effect

**5 scenarios**

- **Mine Your Business — Sumatra Reserve**
- **Creature Comforts — Java**
- **Trot To Go — Rural France**
- **Parakeet Paradise — Mountain Forests**
- **Howls It Going? — Sweden Reserve**

Chapter 3 moves from restoring Sumatra's damaged landscape to managing animal welfare in Java, education and donations in Rural France, aviaries in Mountain Forests, and conservation work in Sweden.

Some later objectives depend on medals earned elsewhere. If Sweden remains locked, check your Sumatra progress before spending more time at the current location.

The [Chapter 3 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-3) follows all five scenarios, including the Sumatra restoration, Java's habitat restrictions, Rural France's education requirements, the Parakeet Aviaries, and Sweden's wildlife releases.

### Chapter 4: FENtastic Opportunity

- **No Ifs, No Putts — Poland Reserve**
- **From A To Ski — Swiss Alps**
- **Take Pride — Tanzania Reserve**


Chapter 4 opens at **Maluku Islands** after you earn **Sweden Reserve Bronze** and reach **Career Accreditation Tier 4**. Maluku Bronze then unlocks the other five locations, which can be approached in any order.

The [Chapter 4 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-4) covers all six scenarios, including their Bronze, Silver, and Gold objectives, aquarium welfare, zoo reputation, breeding requirements, and Wildlife Reserve restoration.

## Why Is My Next Career Scenario Still Locked?

A completed Bronze medal does not always unlock the next assignment immediately.

Career uses **Accreditation Tiers** based on medals earned across its zoos and Wildlife Reserves.

These are Career requirements. Franchise Mode uses a different progression system based on Zoo Reputation and Wildlife Reserve Biodiversity.

When a destination remains locked, open the Planet Hub and inspect its medal requirement. Then return to a location where you can earn the missing medal.

For example, the Nordic Fjords location screen can show **Progress Blocked** when you try to continue to South East Wales. The requirement is at least **5 Career medals**. Even after completing the Aquarium objectives, you may need to earn additional medals at earlier locations before South East Wales becomes accessible.

## Should You Finish Bronze, Silver, or Gold Before Moving On?

**Bronze is usually the right first priority.** It introduces the location, establishes its main facilities, and contributes toward opening other Career assignments.

Silver often continues the same location after you have gained access to additional animals, research, or conservation opportunities.

Gold can require much larger investments, including broader species collections, high welfare, advanced research, strong zoo finances, or highly developed Wildlife Reserves.

That creates a practical choice:

- **Move forward** when a new scenario is available and the remaining objectives require substantial expansion.
- **Return to an earlier location** when a missing Silver medal is holding back your next unlock.
- **Work toward Gold** when the relevant animals, facilities, and resources are available and the location is ready for longer-term development.

You do not need every Gold medal to reach the highest Career Accreditation tier.

For **Excellent Finish**, you must eventually earn Gold in every Career Scenario. That goal is separate from the medals needed to reach the highest Career Accreditation tier.

## Why Is My Aquarium Not Filling or Filtering Water?


An Aquarium needs an **Aquarium Staff Gate** to fill with water, while its **Filtration Network** must be connected and powered. If the tank stays empty or the Water Filter is not working, follow the [Aquarium construction and troubleshooting guide](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) to identify the problem.

</section>

<section id="wildlife-reserves" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Why Can't I Release Animals Into a Wildlife Reserve?

Check whether the selected species is eligible for the destination, the Release Group is ready, and every required Ecosystem is Established. Some animals also need suitable prey or nesting structures. The [Wildlife Reserves instructions](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) cover blocked releases, Research Outposts, and how to improve Biodiversity Rating.

</section>
