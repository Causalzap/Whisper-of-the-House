<GuideQuickAnswer title="Where Should You Start in Planet Zoo 2?">
Begin Career at **Great Rift Valley Reserve**, earn Bronze there and in **East Africa**, then return to Great Rift Valley for Silver. This unlocks **Nordic Fjords**, where you begin Chapter 2.

**Planet Zoo 2** takes you between working zoos and Wildlife Reserves, with new animals, facilities, and locations opening as your Career progresses. The first challenge is not building a profitable zoo from scratch. It is understanding when to stay at your current location, when to travel elsewhere, and which unfinished objectives can wait.

Career has **18 scenarios across four chapters**. Each location awards Bronze, Silver, and Gold medals. You can revisit earlier zoos and reserves, and some later objectives require animals or research developed elsewhere in the FEN network.

For a first playthrough, follow the available Bronze objectives, earn the Silver medals needed to open new locations, and return for Gold when the necessary systems are available.

<section id="start-career" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Where to Go First in Career Mode

Start with **Earning Your Stripes** at Great Rift Valley Reserve. After earning Bronze, travel to East Africa for **Spreading Your Wings**, then return with the Masai Giraffes to complete Great Rift Valley Silver.

This first journey gives you the medals needed for Career Tier 2. The [Great Rift Valley and East Africa walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-1) covers the animal photography, Aardvark and Shoebill tasks, and Giraffe release in the order you encounter them.

</section>

<section id="career-chapters" className="scroll-mt-24">

## All 4 Career Chapters and 18 Scenarios

Career introduces new management responsibilities gradually. You begin with straightforward animal care and then move into larger conservation networks, financial recovery, research, and environmental restoration.

### Chapter 1: Wild At Heart

**2 scenarios**

- **Earning Your Stripes — Great Rift Valley Reserve:** Photography, exploring the reserve, and your first wildlife release.
- **Spreading Your Wings — East Africa:** Terrestrial Habitats, Aviaries, animal welfare, and preparation for conservation releases.

The essential progression is Great Rift Valley → East Africa → Great Rift Valley again.

Completing the first Bronze medals is not the same as finishing the chapter's later objectives. You can return after progressing elsewhere.

### Chapter 2: Paws on the Path

**5 scenarios**

- **All A-Fjord — Nordic Fjords:** Aquariums, aquatic animals, water quality, and veterinary treatment.
- **Otterly Lush — South East Wales:** Quarantine, habitat repairs, guest education, and Zoo Reputation.
- **Grizzly Details — New England:** Donations, shops, marketing, and repaying an outstanding loan.
- **Deer, Oh Deer — Scottish Highlands:** Research coverage, predator reintroduction, and ecosystem restoration.
- **A Penny Learned — South Africa:** Rescued animal treatment, disease research, and staff training.

Nordic Fjords leads into South East Wales. After earning Bronze in South East Wales, New England, Scottish Highlands, and South Africa become available as separate assignments.

You do not need to finish those three locations in a fixed order.

New England is a useful choice when you want to focus on zoo finances. Scottish Highlands introduces more demanding Wildlife Reserve objectives, while South Africa concentrates on animal rehabilitation.

The [Chapter 2 walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-2) explains the objectives and route through all five scenarios.

### Chapter 3: Claws and Effect

**5 scenarios**

- **Mine Your Business — Sumatra Reserve**
- **Creature Comforts — Java**
- **Trot To Go — Rural France**
- **Parakeet Paradise — Mountain Forests**
- **Howls It Going? — Sweden Reserve**

Chapter 3 follows the consequences of an environmental disaster. FEN must restore damaged ecosystems, relocate affected wildlife, and investigate the damage.

Sumatra introduces the restoration of environments affected by mining and a landslide, including habitats needed by rescued animals.

This is where the skills learned in earlier Wildlife Reserves become increasingly important. Restoring an Ecosystem and releasing animals are different tasks, and a successful release does not automatically finish the reserve's biodiversity goals.

### Chapter 4: FENtastic Opportunity

**6 scenarios**

- **Mola Money, Mola Problems — Maluku Islands**
- **Not Much To Sea — Channel Islands**
- **Dragon, Ball, Zoo — Singapore**
- **No Ifs, No Putts — Poland Reserve**
- **From A To Ski — Swiss Alps**
- **Take Pride — Tanzania Reserve**

The final chapter expands FEN's work into land reclaimed from the Envalza corporation.

These locations bring together the animal care, financial management, and conservation systems introduced in the earlier chapters.

Individual scenarios still have their own medal requirements. Opening Chapter 4 does not mean every remaining location or Gold objective is immediately complete.

## Should You Finish Bronze, Silver, or Gold Before Moving On?

**Bronze is usually the right first priority.** It introduces the location, establishes its main facilities, and contributes toward opening other Career assignments.

Silver often continues the same location after you have gained access to additional animals, research, or conservation opportunities.

Gold can require much larger investments, including broader species collections, high welfare, advanced research, strong zoo finances, or highly developed Wildlife Reserves.

That creates a practical choice:

- **Move forward** when a new scenario is available and the remaining objectives require substantial expansion.
- **Return to an earlier location** when a missing Silver medal is holding back your next unlock.
- **Work toward Gold** when the relevant animals, facilities, and resources are available and the location is ready for longer-term development.

You do not need every Gold medal to reach the highest Career Accreditation tier.

However, players aiming for **Excellent Finish** must eventually earn Gold in every Career Scenario.

The [39 Achievements checklist](/planet-zoo-2/achievements) can help you keep track of that goal alongside the game's breeding, research, and conservation achievements.

## Aquarium Not Filling or Water Filter Not Working?

An Aquarium needs the correct Staff Gate to fill with water, and its Water Filter must be connected and powered. If the tank remains empty or its filtration is not operational, check the [Aquarium construction and troubleshooting steps](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) before purchasing more animals.

## Why Can't I Release Animals Into a Wildlife Reserve?

Check that the Release Group is ready and its required Ecosystems are Established. A group can be prepared for transport while the destination remains unsuitable. Follow the [Release Group and Ecosystem requirements](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) to identify the missing condition.

<section id="zoo-management" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Should You Fix First When Your Zoo Stops Progressing?

Not every slow-moving objective calls for a new habitat or another animal purchase.

South East Wales demonstrates how animal welfare, guest needs, education, and maintenance can hold back Zoo Reputation. New England shows how donations, shops, and animal marketing can turn a struggling zoo into a profitable one.

The best next action depends on the condition that is actually behind.

Check the relevant management panel before spending more money:

- For **animal welfare**, inspect the animals with outstanding habitat or care problems.
- For **guest needs**, look at the availability of food, drinks, seating, and other facilities along busy paths.
- For **education**, check that nearby education facilities have suitable topics and animals assigned.
- For **money problems**, examine operating income and costs before expanding the zoo.

Building a new habitat increases the number of animals and facilities that need maintenance. That is not always helpful when the original problem is an inaccessible staff area, poor guest coverage, or an outstanding loan.

</section>

<section id="career-or-franchise" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Career, Franchise, or Sandbox: Which Mode Should You Play?

**Career** is the best place to begin when you want the game's systems introduced through practical objectives. It takes you through zoos and reserves with specific problems to solve and grants medals as you progress.

**Franchise** focuses on developing your own connected network of zoos and Wildlife Reserves. Its Accreditation progression is separate from Career, so completing Career medals does not automatically increase your Franchise tier.

**Sandbox** is suited to building and experimenting without having to follow the Career scenario route.

You can move into Franchise or Sandbox without finishing every Career Gold medal. Career remains useful when you want to learn new facilities through structured assignments or complete the game's achievements.
