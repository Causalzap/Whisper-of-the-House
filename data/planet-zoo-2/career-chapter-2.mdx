<GuideQuickAnswer title="How to Progress Through Chapter 2">
Complete Nordic Fjords Bronze and check the **5 Career medals** requirement before travelling to South East Wales. After South East Wales Bronze, choose New England, Scottish Highlands, or South Africa.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

</section>

**Chapter 2: Paws on the Path** starts at Nordic Fjords and branches into three different assignments after South East Wales. Instead of following a single uninterrupted route, you begin managing several zoos and a Wildlife Reserve with different problems.

The first two locations teach Aquarium construction and guest management. After that, New England is struggling with debt, Scottish Highlands needs its deer population brought back into balance, and South Africa is caring for rescued animals.

The chapter contains five scenarios:

- **All A-Fjord** — Nordic Fjords
- **Otterly Lush** — South East Wales
- **Grizzly Details** — New England
- **Deer, Oh Deer** — Scottish Highlands
- **A Penny Learned** — South Africa

The last three become available after South East Wales Bronze. You can travel between available locations through the Planet Hub, so there is no need to finish every Silver and Gold objective before beginning another assignment.

<section id="nordic-fjords" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Nordic Fjords: Complete the Aquarium and Heal the Manatee

**All A-Fjord** begins with **$25,000** and introduces fully aquatic animals.

You enter an existing coastal zoo rather than an empty construction site. Your first task is to place a Moon Jelly Exhibit and adopt a Moon Jelly.

The next objective concerns the much larger Aquarium already under construction.

### Get the Nordic Fjords Aquarium Ready

The existing Aquarium is missing its Staff Gate. Install an **Aquarium Staff Gate** to fill the enclosure, then prepare the water for the incoming animals.

The scenario requires **Saltwater** and a temperature of **28°C**. You also need one Water Filter connected to the Aquarium and supplied with power.

The filtration task can remain incomplete even after selecting Saltwater. Check for the red **Water Filter Not Powered** warning before changing the water settings again. The [Aquarium Staff Gate and filtration steps](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) will help you resolve the connection or power issue.


### Introduce the Fish and Turtles

Once the water conditions are ready, the scenario moves to the incoming animals.

The required deliveries include:

- **2 Golden Trevally**
- **2 Hawksbill Turtles**

These animals have already passed their health checks through another FEN project, so they can go directly into the prepared Aquarium without waiting in Quarantine.

Transfer them into the Aquarium and wait for delivery to finish. Golden Trevally are handled in shoals, so their animal management differs from transporting an individual turtle.

The required animals are already supplied for this task. There is no need to purchase additional aquatic species.

### Why Won't the Manatee Objective Finish?

The last part of Nordic Fjords Bronze concerns a sick West Indian Manatee.

Hire **one Veterinarian** and allow the animal to receive treatment.

The objective does not finish merely because the Veterinarian has been hired. The Manatee must recover and be returned to its Aquarium.

Wait for both the treatment and the return movement to complete before assuming the task has stalled.

With the animal recovered, Nordic Fjords Bronze is complete.

### South East Wales Is Still Locked

After Nordic Fjords, the next destination can require **five Career medals** before you are allowed to travel there.

This is a progression restriction, not an Aquarium construction problem.

Return to the Planet Hub and check the medals available from earlier scenarios if South East Wales is not yet accessible.

You can continue earning medals in the locations you already have rather than rebuilding Nordic Fjords.

</section>

## South East Wales: Tree Kangaroo, Escaped Otters, and Zoo Reputation

**Otterly Lush** begins with **$30,000** and a zoo that needs improvements to animal care and guest facilities.

The first task is straightforward, but its animal-transfer sequence is worth following carefully.

### Quarantine the Goodfellow's Tree-Kangaroo

Locate the highlighted empty habitat and adopt **one Goodfellow's Tree-Kangaroo**.

The animal does not go straight into its new enclosure. Send it to **Quarantine** first.

While the Veterinarian checks the animal, prepare the habitat with:

- One **Melon Feeder**
- One **Ground Water Fountain - Small**

After quarantine has finished, move the Tree-Kangaroo into the prepared habitat.

Do not purchase a second Tree-Kangaroo simply because the zoo's animal count has not updated. The task requires one animal, and its delivery must finish.

### Place Donation Bins and Education Facilities

With the animal settled, improve the visitor area around its habitat.

Place **two Donation Bins** near locations where guests can observe the Tree-Kangaroo. Putting them beside useful viewing areas gives guests an opportunity to donate after seeing the animal.

The next task introduces Education Boards and a Speaker.

Place **two Habitat Education Boards** and configure both for Goodfellow's Tree-Kangaroo, assigning these subjects separately:

- **Nocturnal Behaviour**
- **Mammal Evolution**

Then place one **Speaker - 360**, select the same animal, and set the speaker content to **Rainforest Ecosystem Ambience**.

Selecting the correct animal without changing the education topic is not sufficient. The objective checks the assigned content.

A board beside the wrong habitat also gives guests less useful educational coverage, so keep these facilities close to the Tree-Kangaroo viewing area.

### Fix the Broken Asian Small-Clawed Otter Habitat

The next interruption is more urgent.

The **Asian Small-Clawed Otter Habitat** has a damaged barrier, putting its occupants at risk of escaping.

Open the habitat information panel, switch to **Animals**, and use **Box All Animals**.

This secures the otters while the damaged enclosure is repaired.

![The South East Wales Asian Small-Clawed Otter Habitat during the escape response, with the Animals controls used to box the animals.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-2-otter-habitat-repair.webp)

Call a Mechanic to repair the barrier.

Wait until the enclosure is secure before allowing the animals to return to normal activity. Releasing them while the barrier remains damaged does not resolve the original escape risk.

This is a different situation from an animal whose welfare is low. Improving food or enrichment will not fix a broken barrier.

### Improve Guest Facilities and Ticket Prices

After the Otter Habitat is secured, return to the visitor facilities.

The scenario asks you to provide a Drink Shop and **two Benches**.

Then open the zoo management controls and change admission prices to:

- **Adult Ticket: $10**
- **Child Ticket: $8**

These are the requested prices for this scenario. You do not need to keep raising them until guests complain or experiment with different price points to satisfy the objective.

The remaining maintenance tasks are to train **one Caretaker to 3 stars** and place **three Bins**.

The Caretaker improves the zoo's ability to keep up with visitor mess. Position the bins along the routes guests actually use, especially where food and drink facilities are concentrated.

### Reach the Bronze Reputation Targets

South East Wales eventually moves beyond individual construction tasks and asks for several zoo-wide conditions at the same time.

The Bronze targets are:

- **Zoo Reputation:** 3 stars
- **Average Animal Welfare:** 85%
- **Guest Average Thirst Need:** 65%
- **Guest Education:** 2.5 stars

These numbers matter because an improvement in one category does not automatically complete the others.

If Thirst is still below the target, inspect where guests are walking and whether they have convenient access to drinks.

If Education remains behind, check the topic assignments and coverage of the Education Boards and Speakers already placed.

For low Average Animal Welfare, identify the animals dragging down the average rather than purchasing more animals immediately.

Zoo Reputation reflects several management outcomes, so it is more useful to inspect the underlying ratings than to keep placing decorations without knowing which condition is still missing.

Once the Bronze objectives are complete, three more Chapter 2 assignments become accessible.

## Which Chapter 2 Location Should You Visit Next?

After **South East Wales Bronze**, the next three destinations open together.

They have different purposes:

- **New England — Grizzly Details:** restore a zoo's finances and pay its loan.
- **Scottish Highlands — Deer, Oh Deer:** establish research coverage, restore ecosystems, and reintroduce predators and prey.
- **South Africa — A Penny Learned:** rehabilitate rescued animals and improve the staff research programme.

There is no requirement to complete all three in a fixed order.

New England is useful for learning how donations, shops, guest numbers, and marketing contribute to cash flow. Scottish Highlands expands Wildlife Reserve management. South Africa concentrates on Quarantine, disease research, staff training, and animal welfare.

You can choose the assignment whose available systems are easiest for you to manage, then return to the others.

</section>

## New England: How to Recover From Debt and Pay Off the Loan

**Grizzly Details** is a financial recovery assignment.

The zoo starts with **-$2,000** and an outstanding **$10,000 Nest Eggs loan**.

The owners have already built much of the park. Your job is to make the existing operation profitable enough to repay the debt.

### Start With Donations

The opening objective asks for **at least ten Donation Bins**.

Place them near useful animal viewing areas rather than concentrating all ten beside the entrance.

The zoo already has animals that visitors can see, including Greater Flamingos and other established habitats. Visitors are more likely to donate when they have a reason to stop and watch the animals.

Your seasonal donation target is **$5,000**.

After placing the bins, give guests time to use them and watch the donation income.

This is a more useful opening investment than immediately constructing an expensive new habitat while the zoo is in debt.

### Add the Required Shops

The next stage requires three different kinds of visitor shop:

- One Food Shop
- One Drink Shop
- One Gift Shop

Pay attention to the exact shop category.

A **Souvenir Shop** is not necessarily recognized as the required **Gift Shop**. During the New England construction sequence, placing a souvenir facility does not complete the Gift Shop objective, so the correct type has to be selected instead.

The shop selection matters more than the building's appearance.

Keep the shops accessible from the guest path network and avoid spending large amounts on custom scenery before the business is profitable.

### Increase Guest Numbers With Flamingo Marketing

Once donation and shop income are improving, New England asks you to grow attendance.

The Bronze requirements include:

- **8 different Habitat species**
- **800 guests**
- A **Greater Flamingo Marketing Campaign**

The marketing task concerns an animal already present in the zoo.

Open **Management → Finance → Marketing** and select a Greater Flamingo for the campaign.

![The New England marketing controls used to start a campaign featuring a Greater Flamingo.](/images/planet-zoo-2/career-chapter-2-flamingo-marketing.webp)

There is no need to adopt an entirely new Flamingo just to start the campaign.

The campaign helps attract guests, who can then contribute to the zoo through ticket purchases, donations, and shop spending.

If the species total is short, inspect the animals already housed in the zoo before buying more. Adding another individual of a species you already own does not increase the number of different species.

### When Should You Repay the Loan?

The scenario also requires **$10,000 in Zoo Cash** and no outstanding loans.

The main trap is repaying the loan before the zoo has enough operating cash to keep its facilities running.

Get the donation bins working, provide the required shops, and start the Flamingo Marketing Campaign before using the loan repayment option.

In the completed New England sequence, the zoo builds up its funds after improving income and attendance. Repaying the loan then finishes the central recovery task.

Once the debt is cleared, **Grizzly Details earns Bronze**.

Further expansion objectives become available afterward, including more Habitat species, improved staff happiness, and greater shop profits.

## Scottish Highlands: Release the Lynx and Restore the Moorland

**Deer, Oh Deer** is a Wildlife Reserve scenario rather than a conventional zoo-building assignment.

The reserve has an excessive deer population after years of poor land management. The initial plan is to restore suitable ecosystems and reintroduce predators.

Your first task is to establish a research network.

### Build the Research Outpost and Three Platforms

Place one **Research Outpost**, followed by **three Observation Platforms**.

The platform coverage areas must not overlap.

Spread them through the reserve instead of building them next to each other. You are trying to establish research coverage while minimizing disturbance to wildlife.

With the initial network established, attention turns to the environment needed by the Eurasian Lynx.

### Prepare the Lynx Release Area

The Eurasian Lynx need suitable established ecosystems before they can be released. Begin by creating a Montane Forest with enough area for the animals to roam.

The release can remain blocked even after the forest has reached Established. In Scottish Highlands, **Short Grass** can fall below its requirement, making the ecosystem invalid again.

Check the current ecosystem status before preparing the two Lynx groups. Restore any missing coverage, and make sure both Montane Forest and Mixed Lowland Forest meet the release requirements. The [Ecosystem requirements and release troubleshooting steps](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) explain how to correct the environment.

### Release Both Eurasian Lynx Groups

The initial Lynx task involves two groups:

- **Eurasian Lynx Maternal Family Group**
- **Eurasian Lynx Solitary Male**

Both are supplied for the restoration project.

Check **Animal Storage → Gifts** rather than searching a zoo for lynx to purchase.

The release also depends on the required environments being suitable. The Lynx may require both Montane Forest and Mixed Lowland Forest conditions, so one newly established forest does not necessarily satisfy every part of the destination requirement.

The [Wildlife Reserves walkthrough](/planet-zoo-2/wildlife-reserves) explains how Release Groups, ecosystem requirements, and the research network operate.

### Complete the Moorland and Bring in Prey Species

The next phase concerns **Moorland** and additional wildlife.

Improve the Moorland Ecosystem until it is Established.

The remaining Bronze reintroduction work includes:

- One **Mountain Hare Mixed Group**
- One **Long-Eared Owl Mating Pair**

This sends you back into the wider FEN network.

The project identifies animals available through Alice's zoo in Wales, so revisit South East Wales to prepare the required groups when instructed.

Returning to the reserve with the animals ready is not enough if their destination ecosystems still fail the required conditions. Check the reserve before finalizing the releases.

The purpose of this sequence is ecological balance. Introducing the Lynx addresses the excessive deer population, while the other releases help rebuild a more diverse Wildlife Reserve.

</section>

## South Africa: Heal the Wild Dog and Rescue the Zebras

**A Penny Learned** begins with **$20,000** and focuses on animal rehabilitation.

Unlike New England, the main challenge is not earning enough money to pay off debt. The immediate work is to treat sick animals, complete research, and prepare staff to handle additional rescues.

### Treat the African Wild Dog

The first rescued animal is an **African Wild Dog**.

Send the animal to Quarantine and allow a Veterinarian to treat it.

While treatment is underway, begin research on **Canine Distemper Virus** and raise it to **Level 1**.

Once the Wild Dog has recovered, move it from Quarantine into the designated African Wild Dog Habitat.

Do not skip the waiting stage. Assigning the animal to Quarantine is only the beginning of the treatment sequence, and the next step depends on the animal recovering.

### Move and Treat the Gifted Plains Zebras

The next task concerns rescued **Plains Zebras**.

These are supplied for the rehabilitation project, so check the available gifted animals before buying replacements.

The Bronze goals require:

- At least **3 Plains Zebras** in the zoo.
- All sick Plains Zebras cured.
- Average welfare of the required Zebras at **80%**.
- **Plains Zebra Research Level 1**.
- **Herbivorous Food-based Enrichments Research Level 1**.

The order of these tasks matters for efficiency.

Bring in the animals and get the necessary treatment underway, but do not ignore their habitat while waiting. The final welfare objective still depends on their enclosure conditions.

Research and animal treatment can progress while you prepare suitable feeding, shelter, and enrichment facilities.

A Zebra recovering from illness may still need habitat improvements to reach the welfare target.

### Build Up the Veterinary Team

South Africa also introduces several staff development requirements.

For Bronze, the research and training work includes:

- Train **3 Veterinarians to 2 stars**.
- Complete **10 Research Levels**.
- Place **one Large Staff Room**.
- Set the **Learning Resources** perk on a Staff Room.

The Staff Room perk is a separate requirement from constructing the building.

After placing the room, open its management settings and apply Learning Resources. Merely having a Large Staff Room does not finish the perk objective.

This stage prepares the rehabilitation centre for more demanding animal care later in the scenario.

Once the animal treatment, welfare, research, and staff objectives are complete, South Africa Bronze is earned.

## Should You Finish Silver and Gold Before Leaving Chapter 2?

Chapter 2's Bronze work opens a much larger part of Career. Silver and Gold continue developing the same locations, but the requirements are considerably more demanding.


### Returning to Nordic Fjords for the later medals

Nordic Fjords becomes a much larger aquarium-management challenge when you return for its later medals. One of the collection objectives requires **6 different Aquatic Habitat species**. Adding more individuals of a species you already own will not increase this counter. Two Hammerhead Sharks, for example, contribute one species, not two.

Another objective requires **at least 10 Blacktip Reef Sharks**. Check the existing population and the species' social requirements before buying the remaining animals. Adding sharks until the counter is satisfied can leave the group overcrowded or socially unsuitable, even when the number of animals is correct.

The aquarium itself can become the next obstacle. A newly constructed tank may look large enough but still fail the animals' usable-space requirements. In particular, large sharks can need substantially more room than the first enclosure provides. Inspect the animal's Space information before changing its environment or adding enrichment. Enlarging the tank can solve a space shortage, but it may also leave the existing Filtration Network unable to handle the increased water volume.

Check the tank's water conditions and filtration capacity after expansion. If the filter cannot support the enlarged aquarium, correct the network before introducing more animals. The [Aquarium building and filtration guide](/planet-zoo-2/aquariums) covers the construction and connection controls.

There is also a financial cost to keeping a large shark collection. Before continuing to purchase species, check animal food costs, admission income, donations, and the facilities already operating.

Once the species requirements are complete, inspect the remaining zoo-wide objectives. Education and sustainability may still need attention even when the aquariums are functioning. Improve the specific rating that is behind rather than continuing to expand the animal collection. When the remaining conditions are satisfied, allow the zoo's status and medal progress to update.

### The other Chapter 2 Gold objectives

South East Wales expands into Aquarium construction, donation income, animal welfare, guest needs, and zoo inspections. New England's later medals demand more species, stronger finances, and improvements to the operating zoo. Scottish Highlands requires further wildlife releases and higher Biodiversity Ratings, while South Africa continues its animal rehabilitation, research, and staff development work.

These objectives become easier to manage once the earlier Bronze assignments are operational, but their completion does not require rebuilding every zoo from the beginning.

The important decision is whether the next medal will help you progress now or whether another available location would provide a more useful change of activity.

Some locations also have specific Career medal requirements before their later objectives become available. A locked Silver or Gold stage is not necessarily a failure of the current zoo.

For a first journey through Career, finish the available Bronze objectives, open the next assignments, and return to larger development goals as the network grows.

<section id="after-chapter-two" className="scroll-mt-24">

## What Happens After Chapter 2?

Continuing through the available Career scenarios increases your medal total and eventually opens the next stage of FEN's conservation work.

**Chapter 3: Claws and Effect** introduces a damaged reserve in Sumatra and follows the recovery of animals affected by the environmental disaster.

The work there includes identifying the cause of the damage, restoring habitats, and bringing rescued wildlife back into suitable environments.

Before moving on, make sure the Chapter 2 locations you intend to revisit remain operational. In particular, New England should no longer depend on its original loan, while South East Wales and South Africa should have their rescued animals safely housed.

The wider Career journey continues from those established facilities rather than requiring every location to be completely rebuilt each time you return.
