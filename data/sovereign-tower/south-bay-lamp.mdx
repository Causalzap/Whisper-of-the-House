<h2 id="south-bay-route">
  The South Bay Lamp route from Ligia to the county alliance
</h2>

South Bay starts like a normal county audience, but Ligia's real problem is
personal.

Her father, **Yannis, Count of South Bay**, is seriously ill.

The condition appears to run in the family, and Ligia believes the legendary
**Wish-Granting Lamp** may be able to save him.

For the best outcome I confirmed, I use this sequence:

1. agree to help Ligia;
2. start an investigation before the 3-cycle deadline expires;
3. use the Tower Library if Wit is easier for my roster than Charisma;
4. get the Djinn Island lead;
5. keep two Knights available for the expedition;
6. use a mount if I can reduce the three-duration trip;
7. complete Djinn Island;
8. continue when the Lamp is reported stolen;
9. follow the auction lead instead of repeating the island;
10. recover the Wish-Granting Lamp;
11. let Ligia use it to resolve Yannis's illness;
12. confirm the South Bay alliance;
13. continue through Ligia's follow-up if I want her on the Round Table.

There are two places where I nearly misread the route.

The first is the island result.

**The missing Lamp is progression, not failure.**

The second is what happens afterward.

Learning that the Lamp was stolen does not guarantee Yannis will survive. I
still need to follow the Lamp to its next location and actually recover it.

<h2 id="ligia-request">
  Start Ligia's Lamp investigation before the 3-cycle deadline
</h2>

Once I agree to help Ligia, I get several ways to investigate the Lamp:

- **Port Taverns**
- **Shellington Market**
- **Tower Library**

The investigation stage has a **3-cycle deadline**.

That is the number I care about.

I do not leave the route sitting behind no-deadline Gold contracts if my useful
Wit or Charisma Knights are currently available.

The routes also give me flexibility.

The Market and harbor-side options lean more toward **Charisma**.

The Tower Library gives me a **Wit** approach.

So I do not treat one investigation as universally mandatory.

I choose the route that fits the Round Table I actually have.

The island expedition establishes three things:

- the Lamp is real;
- the Djinn Island lead was correct;
- someone removed the artifact before I arrived.

That means I do **not**:

- repeat Djinn Island;
- send a stronger pair;
- buy extra equipment to improve the island result;
- reload because I expected the Lamp itself as the reward.

I wait for the next South Bay audience.

The story has moved the Lamp somewhere else.

But I also do not confuse this with saying:

> Yannis is now automatically safe.

He is not.

I still need to complete the next part of the chain.

<h2 id="if-yannis-dies">
  What happens if you do not save Yannis?
</h2>

I also saw a substantially worse South Bay timeline.

In that branch, Ligia returned while I still did **not** have the Lamp.

Yannis's condition had deteriorated badly.

Ligia made it clear that there was no normal cure available.

Later, Yannis died.

That does **not** permanently delete South Bay from the run.

Instead, Ligia inherits the county and becomes the new **Countess of South Bay**.

The problem is that her father's former vassals do not fully accept her rule.

That creates a new quest:

**South Bay political instabilities**

<h2 id="south-bay-alliance">
  South Bay can still become an ally after Yannis dies
</h2>

This is the biggest correction the alternate route adds.

I initially assumed losing Yannis meant losing South Bay.

That was not true.

After the political-instability assignment succeeded, the game confirmed that:

- order had been restored;
- the opposition had been silenced;
- Ligia officially ruled South Bay.

A later audience then had Ligia return to complete the formalities for the
county **rallying to the Sovereign's cause**.

So there are at least two confirmed alliance routes:

### Cleaner route

**recover Lamp → Yannis survives → Yannis confirms South Bay loyalty**

### Worse but recoverable route

**fail to save Yannis → Ligia becomes Countess → resolve South Bay political
instabilities → Ligia formalizes the alliance**

I still prefer saving Yannis.

The second route gives me extra political cleanup and loses the family outcome.

But I no longer restart purely because I think South Bay itself is permanently
lost.

It may not be.

<h2 id="south-bay-stuck">
  What I check when the South Bay Lamp route seems stuck
</h2>

### I accepted Ligia's request but the investigation disappeared

The first investigation stage has a **3-cycle deadline**.

I start one of the information routes before it expires.

### I do not have a strong Charisma Knight

Use the **Tower Library** if Wit fits the current roster better.

That is the route I confirmed.

### My Library Knight is damaged

Check the assignment before repairing.

My Library investigation showed **0 Damage Risk**.

### The Library succeeded but I still do not have the Lamp

That is expected.

The Library points the route toward **Djinn Island, Southbay**.

### How long does Djinn Island take?

My assignment started at **3 duration** and used **two Knights**.

A mount reduced the duration by one in my run.

### What does Djinn Island require?

I saw roughly:

- Moderate Wit;
- Low Strength;
- Low Agility;
- 1–2 Damage Risk;
- two Knight slots;
- 75 Gold.

### Djinn Island says the Lamp was stolen

Continue.

That is not a failed island assignment.

The theft is the lead that moves the Lamp to the auction stage.

### Should I repeat Djinn Island?

I did not.

Once the game told me the Lamp had been stolen, I followed the new lead.

### The Lamp is at the auction

Treat the auction as a new assignment.

Do not assume the same two Knights used for Djinn Island are still the best
choice.

### Is Critical Success required at the auction?

I received a Critical Success with no damage.

I have no evidence that Critical Success itself is mandatory.

The important result is obtaining the Wish-Granting Lamp.

### What happens if I still do not have the Lamp when Ligia returns?

That can lead into the bad family branch.

In the route I saw, Yannis eventually died and Ligia inherited South Bay.

### Is South Bay permanently lost if Yannis dies?

No, not in the route I confirmed.

The county can continue through **South Bay political instabilities**.

Completing that political route allowed Ligia to stabilize her rule and later
formalize South Bay joining my cause.

### What does South Bay political instabilities require?

My assignment showed:

- one Knight;
- 2–4 Damage Risk;
- Low Wit;
- Low Strength;
- Moderate Charisma.

### Can Ligia still join the Round Table if Yannis dies?

I have not confirmed that branch.

I only confirmed Ligia's recruitment after the route where the Wish-Granting
Lamp saved Yannis.

### South Bay is already allied but Ligia has not joined

If Yannis survived, continue through the next Ligia audience.

Her recruitment comes after the clean alliance confirmation.
