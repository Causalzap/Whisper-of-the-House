<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To unlock more heroes in **Dungeonbound**, first check which mode you are playing. In **The Climb**, select a locked hero to see its required Quest. Complete the objective, open **QUESTS**, and claim the finished Quest to make the hero available. In **Story / The Long Haul**, additional classes are obtained through character upgrades, while defeating the final King also makes him available as an ally. Common early Climb unlocks include Haru, Himiko, Jeiku, Snuffy, and Paladin.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

A completed **Quick Paws** Quest can be ready to claim while Snuffy is still locked on the character selection screen. The same applies to **Laying on Hands** and Paladin: meeting the healing requirement is not the final interaction.

Open **QUESTS**, inspect the reward, and select **CLAIM** when the objective is complete. Then return to character selection.

Story classes follow a different process. Their unlocks are purchased through character upgrades, while the King becomes available after the final Story encounter.

## How Do You Unlock a Hero in Dungeonbound?

For most Climb heroes, use this process:

1. Open **The Climb** and go to the hero selection screen.
2. Select the character you want to unlock.
3. Read the Quest name and the requirement shown on its locked card.
4. Play the mode or Act specified by that Quest.
5. Return to the selection screen and open **QUESTS**.
6. Claim the completed Quest reward.
7. Select the newly unlocked character for your next run.

### Does Every Completed Quest Unlock a Hero?

No. Check the reward shown next to each Quest before claiming it.

Some Quests unlock characters, such as Quick Paws for Snuffy and Deeper Still for Himiko. Others reward equipment, name tags, dialogue, or additional features.

The Roguelite Quest menu is separate from Steam achievements. Completing a Quest does not necessarily unlock a hero.

The game shows 64 Roguelite Quests in the Quest menu, while Steam lists 84 achievements. These are different tracking systems.

### Do Daily and Ranked Runs Count?

Use regular Climb runs for hero-unlock objectives unless the Quest explicitly names another mode.

Daily runs should not be relied on for Climb hero-unlock progress.

Quests requiring an Act boss should be completed in a normal Act Climb, where the appropriate boss is available.

Ranked and Void Climbs do not provide the same Act-boss encounters, so they are not substitutes when a Quest specifies defeating the Flame Knight, Makken, Atlas, or The King in a particular Act.

## Which Heroes Can You Use at the Start?

The Climb begins with **Joshua**, **Ashigaru**, and **Pumpkin Head** available in the starting roster.

These characters give you a way to start working on the earliest unlock objectives without first completing the entire Story campaign.

## Which Heroes Unlock Through Combat, Shops, and Cumulative Quests?

These heroes are tied to specific milestones that can be completed while playing Climb.

Several conditions in this table sound similar to other achievements, but the exact action matters.

### How Do You Unlock Haru?

Complete **First Blood** by killing **30 monsters in Climbs**.

Ordinary monster kills contribute to the requirement. PvP rival victories are not interchangeable with monster kills.

This is a useful objective to pursue early because you can advance it while learning the first Act.

If an attempt fails, continue working toward the cumulative total in your next Climb.

### How Do You Unlock Himiko?

Complete **Deeper Still** by reaching **Floor 5 in a Climb**.

You do not need to win the entire Act to reach this milestone.

Reach Floor 5, then return to QUESTS and claim Deeper Still to unlock Himiko.

If you cannot consistently reach Floor 5, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Climb Act 1 walkthrough</Link> for early-floor survival and equipment decisions.

### How Do You Unlock Jeiku?

Complete **First Cut** by applying **Bleed to 10 different enemies**.

The word *different* is important.

Applying Bleed multiple times to the same enemy does not turn that enemy into several separate targets.

Equip an item that reliably applies Bleed, then continue defeating new enemies until the requirement is complete.

You can work on this while progressing through normal Climb encounters.

### How Do You Unlock Snuffy?

Complete **Quick Paws** by reaching **20 Speed in a Climb**.

This is a stat threshold, not a requirement to win a particular Act.

Choose equipment that raises Speed and check whether your displayed stat reaches 20.

Speed gained during combat can contribute, so inspect the character's effective Speed rather than judging the build only by its starting values.

Once the Quest is complete, return to QUESTS to claim Snuffy.

### How Do You Unlock Valkyrie?

Complete **Road to Valhalla** by defeating **25 PvP rivals**.

These victories accumulate across Climb attempts.

You do not need to defeat 25 rivals in one run.

Road to Valhalla tracks PvP rival victories across Climb attempts. After reaching 25 wins, claim the completed Quest to unlock Valkyrie.

For rival survival and equipment decisions during Act 1, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Climb Act 1 guide</Link>.

### How Do You Unlock Windrunner?

Complete **Restless** by rerolling the shop **50 times**.

Rerolls count toward the unlock requirement as you play.

You can advance it through normal shopping decisions instead of spending all your Gold trying to finish the entire counter immediately.

Claim Restless from Quests once the milestone is met.

### How Do You Unlock Achlys?

Complete **Venom Collector** by purchasing **10 Poison weapons** from shops.

The requirement specifically names Poison weapons.

Finding a Poison item as loot is not the same as buying a qualifying weapon.

Check the item's tags and confirm that the purchase is actually contributing to the Quest.

### How Do You Unlock the Alchemist in Climb?

Complete **Apothecary's Tab** by purchasing **15 potions**.

The relevant action is buying potions from shops.

Potions obtained as drops or created through another effect should not be assumed to contribute to that purchase counter.

The Climb Alchemist is distinct from unlocking the Alchemist class through the Story upgrade tree.

### How Do You Unlock Paladin?

Complete **Laying on Hands** by healing **5,000 HP in Climbs**.

This is a cumulative healing requirement.

Use equipment that restores HP during actual fights and continue playing until the healing total is reached.

Do not confuse the amount healed with maximum HP, Block gained, or the number of healing items carried.

After unlocking Paladin, you can use the character to work toward **Holy Fire**, a separate Quest requiring a Floor 8 victory.

### How Do You Unlock the Thief?

Complete **Thick as Thieves** by defeating **25 Thieves**.

The required targets are enemy types counted by this Quest, not simply any PvP opponent.

Act 1 includes several relevant enemies, making its encounters useful for progressing toward the unlock.

If the counter is moving slowly, check whether you are actually defeating qualifying targets rather than general monsters.

### How Do You Unlock Zagreus and Midas?

These two heroes use death-related milestones:

- **Zagreus — Try, Try Again:** Die 25 times in Climbs.
- **Midas — Undying:** Die 100 times in Climbs.

The counters advance as you lose fights during Climb attempts.

Your first 25 deaths contribute toward the larger 100-death requirement.

There is no need to deliberately sacrifice a promising run just to finish these unlocks early. Normal failed attempts can continue advancing the totals over time.

## Which Dungeonbound Heroes Require Clearing Acts or Defeating Bosses?

Some heroes cannot be unlocked through ordinary shop actions or cumulative monster kills.

They require a particular Act, boss, or elite encounter.

<GuideTable
  columns={actUnlockColumns}
  rows={actUnlockRows}
  minWidth="1030px"
  stickyFirstColumn
/>

### How Do You Unlock Albus?

Complete **First Steps** by clearing **Act 1**.

Defeat the Floor 8 boss of The Forest.

This opens Act II: The Castle and satisfies the first Act-clear requirement.

After the Act 1 boss is defeated in a solo Climb, Albus joins during the post-boss sequence.

Continue through the result screen and check the hero roster. This unlock does not follow exactly the same presentation as every Quest-based character reward.

If you have not beaten Floor 8 yet, follow the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Climb Act 1 walkthrough</Link>.

### How Do You Unlock Black Knight?

Complete **Broken Oath** by defeating the **Flame Knight in Act 2**.

You need access to The Castle before attempting this condition.

Play a normal Act 2 Climb and defeat the named boss.

A victory in another Act does not substitute for defeating the Flame Knight.

There is also another Quest named Broken Oath for Fallen Paladin, but it has a different requirement.

### How Do You Unlock Hades and Makken?

Both are connected to **Act 3**.

- **Hades — Gates of the Underworld:** Beat Act 3.
- **Makken — Stake Through the Heart:** Defeat Makken in Act 3.

Makken is the Act 3 boss, so completing that encounter can satisfy both objectives.

After the victory, check for both completed Quests instead of assuming that claiming one automatically claims the other.

If you have not reached Act 3, finish the previous Acts first.

### How Do You Unlock Atlas?

Complete **Toppled Titan** by defeating **Atlas in Act 5**.

The requirement names Atlas specifically, so simply clearing an unrelated boss encounter does not satisfy it.

Atlas appears as a special Act 5 encounter rather than being interchangeable with The King.

Once you have reached Act 5, check the encounter name and prepare for the relevant fight.

### How Do You Unlock Sol Knight?

Complete the **Sol Knight** Quest by defeating **two Act 5 elite enemies**.

The kills can accumulate across eligible Climb attempts.

The important restriction is the Act and enemy type.

Ordinary monsters in earlier Acts do not satisfy a requirement for Act 5 elites.

Use normal Act 5 Climbs for this objective rather than relying on Ranked or Void encounters.

### How Do You Unlock Sansara?

Complete **Wyrm's Awakening** by beating **Act 5**.

This is an Act-clear objective.

Defeat the final Act 5 boss and finish the victory sequence.

Because The King is also an Act 5 objective, a successful final clear can advance more than one hero-related Quest.

### How Do You Unlock The King in Climb?

The main unlock is **Kingslayer**, which requires defeating **The King in Act 5**.

There is also an alternative associated with **Cheating Death**, which requires dealing **1,000,000 damage to Death in a single fight**.

These are different encounters.

Death is not the same boss as The King.

If you complete the relevant Quest, return to the hero selection screen and check whether The King is available.

Do not confuse this Climb unlock with the King joining as an ally after Joshua completes the persistent Story campaign.

## Which Heroes Require Another Hero First?

Some characters have a dependency: you must unlock and use a specific hero before their Quest can be completed.

<GuideTable
  columns={specialUnlockColumns}
  rows={specialUnlockRows}
  minWidth="1030px"
  stickyFirstColumn
/>

### How Do You Unlock Erebus?

Erebus requires **Primordial Night**.

You must first unlock Zagreus, then win a Climb with Zagreus by defeating Floor 8.

The dependency is:

1. Die 25 times in Climbs to complete Try, Try Again.
2. Claim Zagreus from Quests.
3. Select Zagreus for a Climb.
4. Beat Floor 8.
5. Claim Primordial Night to unlock Erebus.

A Floor 8 victory with a different character does not satisfy this requirement.

### How Do You Unlock The Butcher?

Complete **Carving Night** by winning a Climb with **Pumpkin Head**.

Pumpkin Head is part of the starting Climb roster, so you can work toward The Butcher without first unlocking a long chain of other characters.

Choose Pumpkin Head and defeat Floor 8.

After the Act clear, return to Quests and claim Carving Night.

Pumpkin Head's special encounters may differ from those faced by other heroes. Do not assume that every named Act boss will appear in the same way while using Pumpkin Head.

### How Do You Unlock Fallen Paladin?

Complete the separate **Broken Oath** objective associated with Fallen Paladin.

This version requires **cursing 10 items while playing Paladin**.

The process is:

1. Unlock Paladin through Laying on Hands.
2. Select Paladin for your Climb.
3. Use eligible curse opportunities to curse items.
4. Reach the required total of 10.
5. Claim the completed Quest.

The curses must be applied while using Paladin.

Do not confuse this objective with Black Knight's Broken Oath, which requires defeating the Flame Knight in Act 2.

The two Quests share a name but have different unlock conditions.

## How Do You Unlock Heroes in Dungeonbound Story Mode?

The persistent Story campaign uses character progression rather than the same hero Quest system as The Climb.

The core Story classes include Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Monk, Archer, Alchemist, and Paladin.

These classes have their own progression and permanent upgrades.

A character's equipment and accumulated upgrades do not simply transfer over as a complete build when you switch to another class.

### Story Class Unlock Order

<GuideTable
  columns={storyHeroColumns}
  rows={storyHeroRows}
  minWidth="1100px"
  stickyFirstColumn
/>

The class unlocks follow a progression through the character upgrade trees.

For example, unlocking Mage is connected to the Warrior's upgrades. Later classes are connected to upgrades belonging to the preceding class.

If a Story class remains unavailable, inspect the **Unlocks** section of the relevant character's upgrade tree.

### Do You Have to Switch Heroes After Unlocking a New Class?

No. Unlocking another Story class does not require you to stop playing Joshua.

Each class has its own progression. If your immediate goal is finishing the five-dungeon campaign, continue with the character you have already developed.

For the full Story progression route, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/walkthrough">Dungeonbound Story walkthrough</Link>.

### How Do You Unlock The King in Story Mode?

Defeat **The King** in **The Dungeon**, the fifth Story area.

After the final encounter, the game displays **A New Ally** and confirms that The King can fight beside you.

Return to character selection and choose The King from the available characters.

For the complete five-dungeon route and final boss preparation, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/walkthrough">Story walkthrough</Link>.

<section id="unlock-quest-vs-achievement" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Is Unlocking a Hero the Same as Winning With That Hero?

No. Unlocking a character and completing its hero-specific victory are separate objectives.

For example, **Laying on Hands** unlocks Paladin after 5,000 HP healed in Climbs. **Holy Fire** is a later challenge requiring a Floor 8 victory with Paladin.

Likewise, unlocking Zagreus does not automatically unlock Erebus. Erebus requires a completed Climb victory using Zagreus.

For all 84 Steam achievements, including hero-specific victories, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/achievements">Dungeonbound achievements checklist</Link>.

<section id="unlock-problems" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Dungeonbound Hero Unlocks Not Working?

### The Quest Is Complete, but the Hero Is Still Locked

Open **QUESTS** and look for a completed objective with an unclaimed reward.

Claim the reward, then return to the character selection screen.

If the Quest is already claimed, check whether it actually rewards a hero. Some Quests unlock items, name tags, or other bonuses instead.

### I Reached the Required Floor, but the Quest Did Not Complete

Check whether the requirement says **reach**, **beat**, or **win**.

Reaching Floor 8 is not necessarily the same as defeating its boss. A Quest that names a particular Act or boss also requires that encounter rather than an unrelated victory.

Use a regular Act Climb for Act-specific boss objectives.

### A Named Act Boss Never Appears

Check which hero you are using.

Pumpkin Head and The Butcher have different Halloween-themed encounters in their solo Climbs. If you need the Flame Knight or Makken, choose another hero whose Act contains the required boss.

### Two Quests Have the Same Name

The game has two different **Broken Oath** unlock requirements:

- **Black Knight:** Defeat the Flame Knight in Act 2.
- **Fallen Paladin:** Curse 10 items while playing Paladin.

Read the actual Quest requirement and reward before assuming the first completed Broken Oath applies to both heroes.

### I Defeated The King, but Cannot Select Him

Check whether you completed the **Story** encounter or the **Climb Act 5** objective.

In Story, The King joins as an ally after the final encounter.

In Climb, check the **Kingslayer** Quest reward, then return to the hero selection screen.
