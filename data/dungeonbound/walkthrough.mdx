<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To finish **Dungeonbound Story Mode**, clear five dungeons in order: **The Forest → The Castle → The Crypt → The Demon Realm → The Dungeon**. Each has eight floors. Start with Joshua, keep improving your equipment and permanent upgrades between attempts, defeat the Dragon in The Demon Realm, and finally defeat **The King** in The Dungeon. After the last battle, claim your completed quests and the King becomes available as an ally.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Joshua enters The Forest with a Short Sword and Wooden Buckler, but even these opening encounters can end his descent before he gains a level. A failed attempt does not erase the equipment and character growth earned in The Long Haul.

After a defeat, return to the character screen and spend any available resources on upgrades that help Joshua survive the next attempt. As his equipment and permanent stats improve, he can push beyond the enemies that stopped him earlier.

The campaign continues through The Forest, The Castle, The Crypt, The Demon Realm, and The Dungeon, ending with the fight against The King.

## How Do You Start Joshua's Story?

Choose the Incremental campaign and begin with **Joshua**.

The opening takes place after Joshua returns to find his village destroyed. He sets out to discover what happened, beginning in The Forest.

Joshua starts with basic equipment, including a short sword and a wooden shield.

Your first job is to keep both pieces equipped, complete the opening fights, and collect items that improve his ability to survive.

### Why Did the First Chest Turn Into a Mimic?

An early chest can reveal a **Mimic** instead of providing ordinary loot. Joshua may encounter it before earning enough levels or finding strong equipment to survive the fight.

If the Mimic defeats you, return to the character screen and begin another Forest descent. The equipment retained from earlier attempts remains available, so you do not have to rebuild Joshua from his original starting items.

When another chest or event appears, consider whether the possible reward is worth the risk while Joshua is still weak. A safer fight or a useful permanent upgrade may help more than gambling on an early encounter.

## The Forest Walkthrough: How Do You Beat the First Dungeon?

The Forest is where Joshua establishes his permanent progression.

The first few attempts may end quickly. That is not a sign that you need an entirely new build.

Early enemies can be difficult when Joshua has very little base damage, limited HP, and only a few equipment effects.

### What Should You Upgrade After Your First Death?

Return to the character screen and open **Upgrades**.

Three useful early upgrades are:

- **Sharpened Blade:** Increases base damage.
- **Reinforced Plate:** Adds Block.
- **Iron Constitution:** Increases maximum HP.

If fights take too long, start by improving damage. Joshua needs to defeat enemies before their repeated attacks overwhelm his defenses.

If he dies before his existing damage effects can help, invest in survivability.

Do not spend everything on one stat simply because its displayed number is the largest.

A better attack is useful only if Joshua survives long enough to use it.

### What Equipment Should You Keep?

Prioritize equipment that directly helps the next attempt.

A basic sword and shield remain useful while you are finding stronger replacements. Add healing or another defensive effect when an opportunity appears.

An early recovery item such as **Mender's Moss** can help sustain Joshua through repeated fights.

When evaluating healing equipment, check when its effect activates.

An item that restores HP after a delay is less useful if Joshua is already dying before that delay ends.

Likewise, a healing item stored in the bag will not help during combat.

### Should You Sell or Keep Early Drops?

Before leaving a loot screen, move anything you want to keep into the Equipment grid or your bag.

Items left behind may be sold when you continue.

There is an important distinction between a dropped item you have not claimed and a piece you already own.

Do not use a bulk sale action without checking which items it affects.

Early on, keep equipment that solves an immediate problem. Sell items that do not fit, cannot be equipped, and provide no useful future upgrade opportunity.

A spare item can still be worth holding if you expect to combine it with something important at a Forge.

### When Should You Use a Forge in The Forest?

During the early Forest attempts, combining defensive equipment helped Joshua keep useful protection while freeing space for additional items.

If you encounter a Fusion Forge, first check whether your current shield and recovery equipment are already keeping Joshua alive.

Only commit when the finished item preserves the effects you need and leaves the loadout in a better position for the next fight.

You do not need to use every Forge encounter. If both available results weaken your current setup, keep the original items.

For Fusion variants, inherited effects, and item shapes, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/fusion-guide">Dungeonbound Fusion Guide</Link>.

### How Do You Beat the Forest Boss?

The Forest's Floor 8 boss is **Hydra**. It uses physical attacks and can apply Poison, making the fight dangerous even when Joshua has a reasonable amount of Block.

Poison ignores Block, so improving HP recovery and dealing enough damage to shorten the fight are especially important.

One failed attempt reached the end with a stronger defensive setup but still lost after the boss dealt heavy damage and applied Poison.

The important lesson is to avoid treating Block as Joshua's only survival tool.

Before retrying:

1. Improve the damage dealt by your primary weapon.
2. Keep an equipped healing or recovery effect.
3. Check whether Poison or another status is dealing damage Joshua cannot recover from.
4. Improve the defensive equipment you plan to keep.
5. Return to Floor 8 after buying the permanent upgrades that address the failure.

A later attempt was successful after substantial improvements to Joshua's equipment, damage, and survivability.

You do not need to find the same drops. You need enough damage to defeat the boss while maintaining enough effective HP to survive its attacks.

### What Happens After Clearing The Forest?

Complete the Floor 8 battle and resolve the dungeon completion reward.

Return to the character screen.

**The Castle** is the next Story destination.

Before entering it, spend useful Gold and Skill Points on Joshua. There is no advantage in entering the next area with unspent upgrades if the current equipment still has an obvious weakness.

## The Castle Walkthrough: How Do You Clear the Second Dungeon?

The Castle begins with the equipment Joshua carried out of The Forest.

You do not need to build his inventory again.

The challenge is that tougher encounters expose weaknesses in a board that was previously strong enough to defeat the first dungeon.

### What Should You Improve Before Entering?

Open Joshua's permanent upgrades and decide whether damage or recovery is holding him back.

One available recovery option is **Battle Trance**, which provides a chance to heal through attacks.

This can help during longer encounters, but it should not replace an already functioning healing item until the combined loadout can survive without it.

Damage upgrades are also important.

If Joshua spends too long fighting ordinary enemies, increasing his damage output may save more HP than another small defensive boost.

### How Do You Unlock More Equipment Space?

The Castle route makes inventory space increasingly important.

If Joshua has useful items that cannot fit on the active grid, check the **Unlock row** control associated with Equipment.

In one progression route, the first additional Equipment row cost **800 Gold**, and the next displayed a price of **1,650 Gold**.

These purchases expand the active area where equipment can provide its effects.

Do not confuse them with storage upgrades.

**Bigger Bag** expands the lower Inventory, which is useful for keeping spare equipment. It does not automatically make more room for active gear.

If you purchased Bigger Bag but still cannot equip another weapon or armor piece, check the Equipment grid instead.

### What Should You Do With Spare Weapons?

The Castle begins to expose a practical equipment problem: Joshua may find a stronger weapon without having enough room to use it alongside his armor and recovery items.

If the new weapon forces you to remove an essential defensive piece, compare the damage gained with the protection lost. Keep the existing weapon when its current effects are still winning fights, and save the spare for a useful upgrade or combination.

A Forge can improve an established weapon without requiring both ingredients to remain equipped. However, the resulting item must still fit the board and retain the abilities you depend on.

### Why Might Joshua Still Fail in The Castle?

A failed descent can reveal that the character's equipment is strong defensively but too slow offensively.

Check whether you are repeatedly losing HP because enemies remain alive for too long.

If that is the problem, improve the main weapon rather than spending every reward on another layer of Block.

Also check whether newly acquired equipment has displaced an important recovery item.

If a new weapon requires more space than you have, do not dismantle your only useful shield just to equip it.

A successful Castle run can be completed by continuing to improve the same loadout rather than abandoning it after one defeat.

### What Happens After Clearing The Castle?

Continue through Floor 8 and complete the final encounter.

Return to the character screen and select the next Story destination.

**The Crypt** is now the next area.

You may also encounter a notification that a new ally, such as the **Mage**, is available.

Acknowledge the notification. You can keep using Joshua to complete the campaign rather than switching characters.

By this stage, Joshua should already have a developed weapon, improved protection, and at least one dependable way to recover HP.

The most useful changes now often come from making the existing loadout more efficient rather than replacing every item.

### Which Permanent Upgrades Help Here?

Review Joshua's available upgrades for effects that solve the problems you are encountering.

Useful choices can include:

- **Executioner:** Additional damage against weakened enemies.
- **Field Dressing:** Recovery when entering another floor.
- **Blood Scab:** A chance to recover HP after taking damage.

These upgrades help at different points in a fight.

Executioner contributes to finishing enemies. Recovery between floors helps Joshua continue through repeated encounters. Healing after being hit helps when an opponent deals sustained damage.

Choose based on the failure you are experiencing, not just the largest number in the upgrade list.

### Why Can't You Equip Another Weapon?

Two restrictions can apply.

You need enough cells on the Equipment grid, but Joshua must also have permission to equip the additional weapon.

The **Extra Arms** upgrade increases weapon capacity.

If the game says you have reached the weapon limit, expanding the Equipment grid alone will not fix that restriction.

If you have an available weapon slot but the piece physically does not fit, buying another weapon slot will not create enough empty cells.

Check which restriction is preventing the equip action before spending Gold or Skill Points.

### When Should You Dismantle Equipment in The Crypt?

During the Crypt progression, Joshua sacrificed spare equipment at a Dismantling Bench to gain permanent stats and free space for more useful items.

This worked because the sacrificed pieces were no longer essential to the active loadout.

Before confirming, compare the permanent stat gain with the item you would lose. Keep your main weapon, recovery effects, and important defensive equipment unless the replacement setup is already sufficient.

If a spare item no longer contributes to your build, dismantling it can provide lasting value without weakening the next fight.

For the differences between Dismantling, Fusion, and upgrading items, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/fusion-guide">Fusion Guide</Link>.

### What Happens After The Crypt?

Clear the Floor 8 encounter, collect the completion rewards, and return to the character screen.

The next Story destination is **The Demon Realm**.

If an ally notification appears during this part of the campaign, you can acknowledge it and continue using Joshua.

You do not need to move your progression to another hero before advancing.

## The Demon Realm Walkthrough: How Do You Beat the Dragon?

The Demon Realm is the fourth Story dungeon.

Its final boss is the **Dragon**, encountered on Floor 8.

You should enter with the weapon, defensive equipment, and recovery effects that carried Joshua through The Crypt.

At this stage, a good run is often improved by upgrading a few established pieces rather than replacing them with unrelated equipment.

### What Should You Upgrade Before the Dragon?

Check the main weapon first.

If Joshua already has an effective source of damage, improve that item and preserve its useful effects.

During the later Story progression, Joshua continued improving an established damage item through available Forge and upgrade encounters.

The important choice was to preserve the effects already contributing to damage while increasing the weapon's overall strength.

The exact weapon combination is not mandatory.

What matters is that the main damage source becomes strong enough to defeat tougher enemies while Joshua remains protected.

If a new item has better raw Damage but would remove an essential effect, compare the total loadout before switching.

### What Should You Do at a Runic Pedestal?

If you encounter a Runic Pedestal during this descent, position the rune so your intended weapon or defensive item receives its effect.

Confirm the recipient before continuing, especially if you recently rearranged the grid.

Avoid disrupting a working loadout for a small bonus.

For rune coverage, equipment positioning, and inherited effects, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/fusion-guide">Fusion Guide</Link>.

### Should You Accept Every Cursed Shrine?

No.

A Cursed Shrine may offer a powerful improvement at the cost of a drawback.

If the penalty weakens an important defensive effect or interferes with Joshua's primary weapon, skipping it can be better than accepting an upgrade that looks impressive by itself.

Check the current build before deciding.

Also remember that combining equipment does not automatically remove curses from its ingredients.

### How Do You Prepare for the Dragon on Floor 8?

Before the last battle, make sure Joshua has:

- A strong equipped weapon that deals damage consistently.
- Enough HP and Block to survive the opening attacks.
- A reliable healing or recovery effect.
- Useful equipment bonuses that remain active after recent upgrades.
- No essential item accidentally moved into storage.

Do not spend the final encounter rearranging the entire board around one unfamiliar pickup.

If the build has carried Joshua through the previous floors without major problems, preserve its core and improve the weakest remaining part.

### What Happens After Defeating the Dragon?

Defeat the Floor 8 enemy, collect the completion reward, and return to character selection.

**The Dungeon** becomes available as the final Story destination.

The Dragon's defeat is not the campaign ending.

You still need to complete the last descent and confront The King.

## The Dungeon Walkthrough: How Do You Reach The King?

The Dungeon is the fifth and final standard Story area.

The transition brings Joshua back toward familiar surroundings as the story approaches its conclusion.

Continue through the opening sequence, then begin the new dungeon with the developed equipment you have been using.

There is no need to switch characters.

### What Should You Keep Upgrading?

Focus on the pieces that already contribute most of your damage and survivability.

The final area provides additional opportunities to improve and combine equipment.

A strong weapon can gain further damage or status effects, while a defensive item can become more useful through additional Block, HP, or recovery.

Only replace an established item when the alternative improves the complete loadout.

A new Legendary item is not automatically a better choice if it removes an effect that has been keeping Joshua alive.

### Should You Keep Spare Equipment Before The King?

During the final descent, Joshua had enough developed equipment that some new drops were less valuable than the Gold they could provide.

Keep a spare item only when it offers a clear upgrade or can be used in a worthwhile combination.

If your active loadout is already effective, selling an unnecessary pickup may be better than rearranging several important pieces to fit it.

### What Should You Do Before Floor 8?

During the final Dungeon run, Joshua used a late upgrade opportunity to strengthen several equipped items.

His damage output had developed around Burn and other effects, so the additional upgrades increased the power of a build that was already working.

Before reaching Floor 8, another Equipment row was available, but the active loadout no longer needed the extra space.

Instead of spending Gold on a row that would not immediately improve the build, the player kept the existing equipment arrangement.

Use the same decision rule before The King:

- Upgrade equipment that already contributes meaningful damage or survival.
- Keep the existing layout when newly found items do not offer a clear improvement.
- Avoid spending Gold on extra equipment space unless you have a useful item ready to equip.

You do not need to make another major fusion simply because a Forge is available. The final goal is to preserve the strongest functioning version of Joshua's loadout.

### How Do You Reach The King?

Continue through The Dungeon until **Floor 8**.

Resolve the encounters and loot opportunities leading to the final battle.

The story then brings Joshua face to face with the ruler he once served.

This is the final boss of the five-dungeon route.

## How Do You Beat The King in Dungeonbound?

**The King** is the final boss of The Dungeon.

The King uses **Magic Damage** and can apply both **Burn and Poison**.

  <Image
    src="/images/dungeonbound/dungeonbound-story-king-final-boss.webp"

  />

    so Block alone cannot prevent every source of damage.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Burn damages Block and HP, while Poison ignores Block. Joshua therefore needs more than a large Block value to survive every threat.

### What Should You Check Before Starting the Fight?

Keep your strongest damage item equipped.

If you have spent several dungeons improving one weapon through upgrades and fusions, replacing it with an unfamiliar pickup immediately before The King is usually unnecessary.

Check your defenses as well.

A build with high HP and Block can still benefit from recovery, particularly against an opponent that applies Burn.

Make sure any healing or Life Steal effects you have been relying on remain active.

Finally, check equipment timing.

If your main item requires Mana or a timed trigger, confirm that it can actually activate. A powerful effect is not helpful if the build no longer supports its activation requirements.

### What If The King Defeats You?

Return to Joshua's character screen and improve the character before another attempt.

You do not need to clear all earlier dungeons again with a fresh character.

Use the failed fight to identify the immediate problem:

- If Joshua dies before dealing enough damage, improve survivability.
- If the fight lasts too long, increase the effectiveness of the main weapon.
- If Burn or another status is the main threat, preserve recovery and consider available protection.
- If an equipment change made the build weaker, restore the effects that previously worked.

The Long Haul allows Joshua to return stronger after setbacks, so another round of permanent upgrades may solve a fight that the current setup cannot win.

### How Do You Confirm the Story Ending?

After The King is defeated, continue through the ending sequence.

The story confirms that peace has been restored.

Collect the final reward and return to character selection.

The last battle also makes **The King** available as an ally.

## What Unlocks After Clearing All Five Dungeons?

Defeating The King completes the main five-dungeon Story route.

Before moving on, check the following:

### Claim Your Story Quests

Open **Quests** from the character screen and review the objectives you have completed.

Two relevant completion objectives are:

- **Dungeonbound:** Clear all five dungeons.
- **Into the Void:** Clear Act 5, The Dungeon.
