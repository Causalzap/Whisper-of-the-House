Dungeonbound has **84 Steam achievements**. Track them with the checklist below, then work through the five Story dungeons, Climb victories, equipment upgrades, combat milestones, and Co-op requirements. Several achievements require winning on **Floor 8 with a specific hero**, while others demand deeper floors, special bosses, or large amounts of damage to Death in a single fight. Use the achievement's exact requirement rather than assuming similar names share the same objective.

A Floor 8 Climb victory can complete **First Steps**. Win that climb with Paladin and it can also satisfy **Holy Fire**. Neither result is the same as **Dungeonbound**, which requires clearing the five persistent Story dungeons.

Other objectives have restrictions that are easy to miss. **Fallen** counts deaths to PvP players, **Carving Night** requires a Pumpkin Head victory, and **Death Defier** requires one billion damage to Death in a single fight. Co-op victories and gifted items have their own requirements.

Mark each completed objective in the checklist, then filter what remains by the character, mode, or action you still need.

## Which Story and Act Achievements Should You Complete First?

Start with the progression goals that also open more of the game.

### Dungeonbound — Clear All Five Dungeons

The **Dungeonbound** achievement requires clearing all five dungeons.

Complete **The Forest, The Castle, The Crypt, The Demon Realm, and The Dungeon** to satisfy the five-dungeon requirement.

After the final Story clear, the game displays a **Dungeonbound** quest completion notification. Check Quests to claim the reward.

For Joshua's progression, equipment upgrades, boss fights, and the complete area route, follow the <Link href="/dungeonbound/walkthrough">Dungeonbound Story walkthrough</Link>.

### Into the Void — Clear Act 5, The Dungeon

**Into the Void** requires completing Act 5, The Dungeon.

The final Story completion screen displays **Into the Void** alongside **Dungeonbound**. Both completion notifications direct you to claim their rewards from Quests.

If you have not finished the final encounter, follow the <Link href="/dungeonbound/walkthrough">Story walkthrough</Link> for The Dungeon and The King.

### First Steps — Beat Act 1

**First Steps** requires beating Act 1.

In **The Climb**, defeat Floor 8 to clear Act 1. The victory screen confirms **ACT CLEARED — The Forest** and opens Act II: The Castle.

For the successful run, equipment decisions, and Floor 8 preparation, follow the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Dungeonbound Climb Act 1 guide</Link>.

### Later Act and Boss Achievements

The list also includes several more specific objectives:

- **Broken Oath:** Defeat the Flame Knight in Act 2.
- **Gates of the Underworld:** Beat Act 3.
- **Stake Through the Heart:** Defeat Makken in Act 3.
- **Kingslayer:** Defeat the King in Act 5.
- **Wyrm's Awakening:** Beat Act 5.
- **Toppled Titan:** Defeat Atlas in Act 5.

These are not interchangeable.

Beating an Act and defeating a named boss are listed as separate requirements. Check which objective has actually unlocked after the fight.

If you are working toward a particular hero, inspect the related Quest as well. The achievement condition, quest reward, and character selection may involve separate interactions.

## How Do You Get the Hero-Specific Floor 8 Achievements?

Several achievements require an Act clear with a particular hero.

The requirement is not simply to unlock that character or play a run with them. You must win a climb by beating Floor 8 while using the named hero.

### Which Heroes Have Victory Achievements?

The achievement list includes these character victories:

- **Crusade:** Win with Joshua.
- **True Aim:** Win with the Windrunner.
- **Holy Fire:** Win with the Paladin.
- **Dragonborn:** Win with Sansara.
- **Eye of the Storm:** Win with Snuffy.
- **Blood Moon:** Win with Makken.
- **Thunderhead:** Win with the Valkyrie.
- **Knight's Oath:** Win with the Black Knight.
- **Long Winter:** Win with Hades.
- **Unbroken:** Win with Atlas.
- **Treasure Seeker:** Win with the Thief.
- **Carving Night:** Win with Pumpkin Head.

All twelve specify defeating Floor 8.

For **Holy Fire**, the Paladin must defeat Floor 8. The completed Quest can then be claimed from Quests. If you have not won with Paladin yet, the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Climb Act 1 walkthrough</Link> shows a successful Paladin run.

### Should You Finish Every Hero Victory Immediately?

You can complete the available hero victories as you progress instead of attempting all twelve at once.

If a required hero is still locked, check its Quest conditions before planning that Floor 8 run.

Track each completed hero victory separately. Unlocking a character and winning a climb with that character are different objectives.

## Which Climb, PvP, and Floor Achievements Take Multiple Runs?

Some achievements measure progress across your attempts rather than requiring one specific build.

### PvP Victory Milestones

Three achievements track beating rival players:

- **Challenger:** Beat 10 PvP players.
- **Duelist:** Beat 50 PvP players.
- **Undisputed:** Beat 100 PvP players.

Continue playing Climb encounters and defeating rivals.

Do not confuse these totals with winning entire Climbs. A run may include multiple PvP victories before it reaches its final encounter.

### Climb Victory Milestones

The list also includes:

- **Veteran:** Win three Climbs.
- **Veteran Climber:** Win five Climbs.

Both use beating Floor 8 as the victory requirement.

You can work toward these while completing the hero-specific Floor 8 achievements instead of repeating the same hero unnecessarily.

### How Do You Reach Floors 10, 12, and 15?

Three achievements reward deeper progress:

- **High Ground:** Reach Floor 10.
- **Deep Delver:** Reach Floor 12.
- **Abyss Walker:** Reach Floor 15.

A normal Act 1 victory happens on Floor 8, so stopping immediately after your first clear is not enough for these objectives.

When the game offers **Continue to Endless**, you can continue the current build beyond the initial clear.

Choose to continue when your equipment is still effective and you want to work toward deeper floors.

You do not need to restart merely because the first Act has ended.

### Do Deaths Count Toward Achievements?

Yes. Some achievements explicitly reward failed attempts:

- **Fallen:** Die to 25 PvP players.
- **Undying:** Die 100 times in climbs.
- **Persistence:** Die 10 times.

There is also **Veteran Delver**, which requires starting 25 runs.

These objectives are useful to track while playing normally. You do not need to deliberately throw away successful runs just to complete them early.

The important distinction is that Fallen specifically requires deaths to PvP players, while the other death achievements have differently worded conditions.

## How Do You Complete the Fusion, Upgrade, and Rune Achievements?

Several achievements reward using Dungeonbound's equipment systems repeatedly.

The challenge is not only to find a Forge. You must perform the specific action named in the achievement.

### Fusion Achievements

The list contains:

- **Alchemist:** Fuse five items at the Forge.
- **Master Melder:** Fuse 15 items at the Forge.
- **Apprentice Smith:** Fuse 20 items.
- **Forgemaster:** Fuse 50 items.

These achievements track the number of items fused. You do not need a particular Fusion result to satisfy the listed counts.

If you are unsure which equipment to combine, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/fusion-guide">Dungeonbound Fusion Guide</Link> before committing your items.

### Upgrade Achievements

Six achievements involve upgrading equipment or reaching specific item levels:

- **Apprentice Smith:** Upgrade items 10 times.
- **Master Smith:** Upgrade items 50 times.
- **Grandmaster Smith:** Upgrade items 100 times.
- **Refinement, Tempering, and Perfection:** Reach item Levels 5, 7, and 10 respectively.

The three Smith achievements count upgrade actions. Refinement, Tempering, and Perfection require an item to reach Levels 5, 7, and 10.

For item upgrade methods and equipment progression, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/fusion-guide">Fusion Guide</Link>.

### Runes and Curses

Other equipment goals include:

- **Runecarver:** Earn 20 runes.
- **Dark Bargainer:** Curse five items.
- **Hexbound:** Curse 15 items.

Track these as separate activities.

Finding a rune is not the same task as applying a curse, and neither should be confused with normal equipment upgrades.

If a cursed item would seriously weaken your current build, it may be better to work on curse milestones during a less important run.

</section>

## Which Shopping, Relic, and Collection Achievements Should You Track?

The shop-related achievements reward actions that are easy to overlook when you concentrate only on winning.

### Buying, Selling, and Rerolling

The requirements include:

- **Hoarder:** Buy 150 items from shops.
- **Merchant:** Sell 100 items.
- **Restless:** Reroll the shop 50 times.
- **High Roller:** Reroll shops 150 times.

These achievements track the listed shop actions across your progression. Check your remaining counts as you continue using shops.

For decisions about Gold, purchases, and rerolls during your first climb, see the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Climb Act 1 guide</Link>.

### Reliquarian and Blood Pact

**Reliquarian** requires claiming 15 relics.

**Blood Pact** requires both beating 10 vampiric enemies and collecting 10 vampiric items.

Blood Pact is a two-part condition. Completing only one side does not satisfy the full description.

Check the actual objective progress before assuming it is finished.

### Venom Collector and Haunted House

**Venom Collector** requires buying 10 Poison weapons.

**Haunted House** requires buying 13 Haunted items from shops.

The descriptions specify item types, not simply the number of purchases made in general.

When working on either achievement, inspect the item's type before buying it.

These purchases can also contribute to general shop totals, making them useful goals to combine where possible.

</section>

## How Do You Finish Combat and Enemy-Kill Achievements?

Dungeonbound has multiple combat milestones that can be advanced while completing Story, Climb, and later content.

### Status Effects and Damage

Some achievements count applying or dealing damage with statuses:

- **First Cut:** Apply Bleed to 10 different enemies.
- **Afflicter:** Inflict 100 status effects.
- **Plaguebringer:** Inflict 500 status effects.
- **Slow Death:** Deal 5,000 damage with status effects.

First Cut specifically counts different enemies. Applying repeated Bleed stacks to the same target should not be treated as progress against ten separate enemies.

For Slow Death, the damage needs to come from status effects, not merely from a weapon that happens to carry a status-related tag.

### Healing and Speed

Two other challenges have specific thresholds:

- **Laying on Hands:** Heal 5,000 HP in Climbs.
- **Quick Paws:** Reach 20 Speed in a Climb.

**Laying on Hands** counts HP healed during Climbs, while **Quick Paws** requires reaching 20 on the displayed Speed stat during a Climb.

A faster item trigger is not necessarily the same as a higher character Speed stat. Check the actual value before assuming the threshold has been reached.

### Enemy-Kill Milestones

General kills include:

- **Slayer:** Kill 100 enemies.
- **Butcher:** Kill 500 enemies.
- **Exterminator:** Kill 2,000 enemies.

Other achievements require particular enemies:

- **Van Helsing:** Kill seven vampires.
- **Elite Hunter:** Kill 25 elite enemies.
- **Demonbane:** Kill 50 demons.
- **Regicide:** Kill 10 bosses.
- **Beast Tamer:** Kill 100 beasts.
- **Warren Cleaner:** Kill 100 Earth enemies.
- **Dragonslayer:** Kill 25 dragons.
- **Gravecleaner:** Kill 100 undead.
- **Thick as Thieves:** Defeat 25 Thieves.

Climb-specific monster totals are separate:

- **Beast Hunter:** Kill 150 monsters in Climbs.
- **Exterminator:** Kill 400 monsters in Climbs.

Notice that Exterminator also appears as a general 2,000-enemy achievement. They have separate conditions and should be tracked individually.

If you are close to completing several kill milestones, choose content where the required enemies appear rather than trying to finish everything in one run.

</section>

## Which Dungeonbound Achievements Require Co-op?

There are seven achievements whose descriptions explicitly require Co-op play or giving items to teammates.

You cannot complete these by simply winning a solo Climb.

### Co-op Floor Progress

- **Shield of the Court:** Reach 10 new floors in Co-op Climbs.
- **Shoulder to Shoulder:** Reach 20 new floors in Co-op Climbs.

These objectives specifically say *new floors*. Follow their progress in the game rather than assuming repeatedly entering an already completed floor adds to the total.

### Give Items to Teammates

Three achievements require gifting equipment:

- **Sword of the Court:** Give five items to teammates.
- **Open Hand:** Give 10 items to teammates.
- **Through the Glass:** Give 30 items to teammates.

Coordinate with teammates so that useful equipment reaches the player who needs it.

You can track these actions while working on Co-op floor progress instead of treating every gifted item as a separate farming run.

### Win Co-op Climbs

- **War Band:** Win one Co-op Climb by beating Floor 8 as a team.
- **Bow of the Court:** Win two Co-op Climbs by beating Floor 8 as a team.

A solo Floor 8 victory does not meet the team requirement.

If you plan to complete all achievements, reserve time for Co-op even if most of your progress so far has been in Story and solo Climb.

</section>

<section id="death-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">

## How Much Damage Do You Need for Death's Door, Cheating Death, and Death Defier?

Three achievements require dealing large amounts of damage to **Death in a single fight**.

They have different thresholds:

- **Death's Door:** Deal **500,000 damage** to Death in one fight.
- **Cheating Death:** Deal **1,000,000 damage** to Death in one fight.
- **Death Defier:** Deal **1,000,000,000 damage** to Death in one fight.

Death Defier requires one billion damage against Death during a single encounter. Damage from separate fights cannot be combined, and defeating The King in Story Mode does not satisfy these Death-specific challenges.

</section>

## What's the Best Order for 100% Dungeonbound Achievements?

Completing all 84 achievements is easier to manage when you separate normal progression from specialized challenges.

Several achievements can advance during the same activity. A Paladin Floor 8 victory contributes to the required climb-win totals while also satisfying Holy Fire. Buying qualifying Poison weapons can advance both Venom Collector and the general shop-purchase counter.

After clearing Floor 8, choosing **Continue to Endless** lets you pursue Floors 10, 12, and 15 without immediately abandoning the successful build. Co-op achievements require a different approach: gifting items and winning as a team cannot be replaced by ordinary solo victories.

When only a few achievements remain, filter the unfinished entries and identify the precise restriction for each one. A named boss, a particular hero, a Co-op action, and a single-fight damage threshold require different preparations.

For the five-dungeon Story route, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/walkthrough">Story walkthrough</Link>. For a first Floor 8 victory, use the <Link href="/dungeonbound/act-1">Act 1 walkthrough</Link>.
