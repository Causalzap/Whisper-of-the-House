Pandora Blade is easy to miss because its hidden room is close to a required quest item. You can collect the Shrine Gate Key, leave the Crypt, and continue toward the first boss without ever opening the wall that contains this optional sword.

With only **5 base Power**, Pandora Blade is not an obvious early damage upgrade. Its main advantage is the Lunging Stab: when followed by a well-timed jump, the attack can carry you farther across a gap. The reason to pick it up early is its unusual Lunging Stab: with the right jump timing, the attack becomes another way to cross gaps and extend movement through the air.

The blade also has a much higher weapon-level cap than most early weapons. That creates a separate question: whether spending time leveling it is actually worthwhile.

<h2 id="pandora-location">
  Where Is the Pandora Blade in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The Pandora Blade is hidden inside the **Crypt on the Isle of Neasied**, in the same general area where you obtain the Endu Shrine Gate Key.

You can reach it before defeating the Shrine Guardian. You do not need the Holy Sword Endu, Wind Gems, or any late-game movement upgrades to collect it.

To reach the blade, enter the Crypt through the ranged-switch entrance and descend to the **Endu Shrine Gate Key chamber**. The hidden wall is in this area.

If you have not unlocked the Crypt or obtained the Dagger Belt, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/isle-of-neasied-walkthrough">Isle of Neasied walkthrough</Link> first.

The Pandora Blade is an optional pickup. Missing it does not block the Shrine Guardian fight, and you can return for it after progressing beyond the starting region.

<h2 id="hidden-wall">
  How Do You Open the Hidden Wall to Get Pandora Blade?
</h2>

Once you reach the **Endu Shrine Gate Key chamber**, do not immediately leave after collecting the key.

Look for the unusual wall section where the usual brazier is missing. Compare the wall fixtures around the Shrine Gate Key chamber to identify the concealed entrance.

To open the passage:

1. Approach the suspicious wall where the usual brazier is missing.
2. Face the wall at close range.
3. Use **Interact** (`E` with the default keyboard controls).
4. Enter the newly revealed chamber.
5. Collect the **Pandora Blade** from inside.

You do not need to break the wall with repeated sword attacks or throw daggers at it. The important action is interacting with the hidden entrance.

The chamber contains several skeletons arranged in unusual poses. This makes the secret room distinctive once you have opened it.

### Can You Return for Pandora Blade Later?

Yes. The blade is not a one-time reward tied to the Shrine Guardian encounter.

If you already have Endu and have left the opening region, return to the Crypt and revisit the Shrine Gate Key room. You can open the same hidden wall and collect Pandora Blade without starting a new game.

<h2 id="secret-jump">
  How Do You Perform Pandora Blade's Secret Dash Jump?
</h2>

Pandora Blade's special movement comes from its **Lunging Stab**, a forward-moving attack that carries the character a short distance.

The useful trick is to jump during the lunge's recovery, transferring the attack's forward momentum into the jump.

Practice on a safe stretch of floor before attempting a crossing with a long fall.

1. Equip **Pandora Blade** as your active weapon.
2. Face the direction you want to travel.
3. Use its **secondary Lunging Stab attack**. On default mouse controls, this is the right-click attack.
4. As the forward thrust begins to recover, press **Jump**.
5. If the timing is correct, the jump carries you farther forward than a normal standing jump.

The critical part is not simply pressing Attack and Jump together. You are trying to carry the forward movement from the lunge into the next action.

### Why Isn't the Dash Jump Working?

If you get an ordinary short jump, the timing may be too early or too late.

Try these adjustments:

- **Jump barely moves you forward:** Let the lunge begin before pressing Jump, rather than jumping before the attack starts.
- **You finish the attack without jumping farther:** Try pressing Jump closer to the lunge's recovery.
- **You reach the gap but fall below the platform:** Start from a higher position. Extra horizontal momentum does not guarantee extra vertical height.
- **You cannot repeat the result consistently:** Practice against a fixed landmark on level ground so you can compare the distance of each attempt.

Do not use a dangerous platform as your first practice location. The technique becomes much easier to judge when you can repeat the same movement without restarting after every missed jump.

You can perform the secret movement with a low-level Pandora Blade. **Weapon Level 100 is not required.**


<h2 id="midair-lunge">
  Can You Chain Pandora Blade's Dash While Airborne?
</h2>

Pandora Blade's **Lunging Stab** can extend a jump, but it does not let you repeatedly trigger unlimited forward lunges during the same airborne sequence.

For a longer crossing, carry the lunge's forward momentum into your jump, then use any available **Endu Wind Dashes** to cover the remaining distance. The two movements can work together without increasing your installed Wind Gem count.

Height remains a separate limit. If the destination is above your starting ledge, additional horizontal movement may still leave you below it. Begin from a higher platform rather than relying on repeated Pandora attacks to gain elevation.

For the Wind Gem pickups that increase your available air Dashes, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu instructions</Link>.

A **+300% bonus** means the weapon's original 5 Power receives an additional three times that amount:

**5 base Power + 15 bonus Power = 20 equivalent Power before other combat modifiers.**

That does not mean the inventory changes its base Power from 5 to 20. The displayed value remains **5 +300%**.

Actual damage against an enemy can still depend on damage resistance and other equipment or character effects.

### Does Pandora Blade Evolve at Level 100?

No. Reaching Level 100 does not trigger an evolution comparable to Holy Sword Endu becoming **Soul Bound Endu**.

The sword reaches MAX with its increased Power bonus, and its special movement technique remains available.

Do not spend additional time trying to push the weapon beyond the displayed MAX level in expectation of another transformation.

<h2 id="leveling">
  How Do You Level Up Pandora Blade Faster?
</h2>

Pandora Blade gains weapon experience through combat. Its Power bonus grows by approximately **3 percentage points per weapon level**, eventually reaching +300% at MAX.

Because the base Power starts at only 5, the early percentage increases are modest. Even doubling the original base contribution takes substantial leveling before the weapon begins to compete with stronger pickups.

If you want to level Pandora Blade, prioritize fights in which its Slash attacks are effective rather than attacking resistant enemies simply to keep the sword equipped.

A practical approach is:

1. Keep Pandora Blade available during ordinary exploration instead of immediately committing to a long farming session.
2. Use it against enemies you can safely damage with Slash attacks.
3. Choose an area near a Crystal Monument if you decide to repeat encounters.
4. Carry enough healing supplies that repeated fights do not consume more resources than the upgrades are worth.
5. Check the weapon's displayed level and Power bonus periodically to decide whether continuing is worthwhile.

### Does Training Band Help With Weapon Leveling?

**Training Band** is an equipment option worth examining when you are working on weapon progression.

Its description explains that it synchronizes the user with their main weapon, making that weapon quicker to use more effectively. The description does not provide a precise numerical weapon-experience bonus.

If you have Training Band, you can compare weapon-level progress with and without it. Do not assume a specific experience multiplier from the item description alone.

Similarly, avoid relying on an unconfirmed item combination or farming location as a guaranteed fastest route to Level 100.

The important decision is whether you need additional Pandora Blade damage at all. Its movement technique already works without investing in the full upgrade path.

### Is a Max-Level Pandora Blade Stronger Than Soul Bound Endu?

Pandora Blade's maximum **+300%** bonus gives its 5 base Power a much more competitive damage value.

However, the two weapons do not have identical attack properties.

**Soul Bound Endu** gains a holy energy arc after evolving, giving it an offensive option beyond ordinary close-range Slash attacks. Pandora Blade instead combines its normal attacks with a useful forward-lunge movement technique.

That means comparing only the Power numbers will not tell you which sword is more effective in every encounter.

Pandora Blade is particularly attractive if you enjoy experimenting with movement or want a sword to develop over a long playthrough. Endu remains important for Wind Dash and its own combat progression.

For most players, the better early decision is to **collect Pandora Blade, learn the secret jump, and postpone the Level 100 grind** until they have a reason to invest in its damage.
