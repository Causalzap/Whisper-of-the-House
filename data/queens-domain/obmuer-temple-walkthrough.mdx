<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Use **two Wind Dashes** to cross the broken western bridge, collect all **four Triangular Slates** in the flooded ruins, and place them at the sealed door to enter **Obmuer Temple**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="how-to-enter">
  How Do You Enter Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

Four **Triangular Slates** are required to open Obmuer Temple. All four are found in the flooded ruins outside the dungeon, not behind the temple's sealed entrance.

You also need **two Wind Dashes** to reach this area. After obtaining the Holy Sword Endu, collect another Wind Gem before attempting the broken bridge west of the opening region. Players who still have only one Dash should follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu upgrade route</Link> rather than repeatedly trying to cross a gap they cannot yet clear.

From the starting region, head toward the western broken bridge near the area where you encountered the centipede-like enemies. Cross with two Wind Dashes and activate the Crystal Monument on the far side.

Beyond that save point is a flooded ruin containing broken columns, skeletons, aggressive fish, and the four missing slates. The area has several ways to climb or drop between levels, so collect the slates systematically instead of fighting every enemy in the water.

<h2 id="triangular-slates">
  Where Are All Four Triangular Slates?
</h2>

The easiest landmarks are the circular pool containing small red fish, the broken columns, and the elevated section where the **Trident weapon** can be collected.

### First Slate — East of the Red Fish Pool

Find the circular area filled with small aggressive red fish. Move behind it, then take the eastern route rather than heading west.

The first Triangular Slate rests on a rock guarded by skeletons. Draw those enemies away if they are blocking the pickup, or collect the slate once you have enough room to interact safely.

The red fish can make the pool expensive to cross at low health. Staying on the surrounding stonework avoids unnecessary damage while searching for the item.

### Second Slate — Broken Columns to the West

From the first slate, turn west toward the collapsed columns.

Climb the broken wall to reach the elevated stonework, then follow it to the slate. This one is easy to miss from ground level because the route depends on climbing the ruins rather than simply walking along the water.

Look upward when the next objective seems to be blocked. The broken architecture provides a route to the pickup.

### Third Slate — Below the Trident Area

Find the main ruined area containing several skeletons and the **Trident weapon** pickup.

Collect the Trident while you are here. It is useful later for an optional temple puzzle, although you do not need to level it before entering the dungeon.

From that upper area, drop to the lower level and move toward the **western corner**. The third Triangular Slate is below the main path.

The critical distinction is elevation: searching the upper platform again will not reveal a slate that sits beneath it.

### Fourth Slate — Isolated Column in the Middle

The final slate is on a large broken platform or column in the middle of the ruins. Reach it with Wind Dash rather than trying to find a ground-level approach.

Several skeletons awaken near the item. You can grab the slate and leave instead of remaining on the narrow platform to clear the encounter. Fighting a group on limited footing creates more risk than the collectible requires.

Once you have **four Triangular Slates**, travel to the sealed temple door at the back of the ruins and use them to open the entrance.

There is no need to defeat every fish or skeleton outside. The slates, Trident pickup, and access to the entrance are the important results of this section.

<h2 id="first-trident-key">
  How Do You Get Through the First Rooms of Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

The first rooms of Obmuer Temple alternate between flooded passages, vertical drops, and doors opened with Trident-shaped Keys. Find the first key before pushing deeper into the dungeon.

1. Enter the temple and descend into the watery area near the entrance.
2. Search the lower section for the **first Trident Key**.
3. Use that key to open the route forward.
4. Continue until you reach the **second Crystal Monument** and save.
5. From this save point, examine the large pit and the openings in its walls rather than treating the pit as a dead end.

The first key is part of the required route. Keep track of where you insert it because the dungeon expects you to move keys between sockets as you unlock different passages.

I would avoid leaving a usable key behind simply because the first door is open. Later rooms contain more sockets, and returning for a key you could have collected earlier means navigating the same enemies again.

<h2 id="second-trident-key">
  Where Is the Second Trident Key?
</h2>

From the second save point, descend toward the large pit and **Wind Dash into the opening in the wall below**.

The passage leads toward an upper section where a couple of skeletons ambush you. The **second Trident Key** is found in this part of the dungeon.

Clear the nearby enemies before searching the ledge, especially if you arrive with limited health. The route involves a change in elevation, making it easy to overlook while examining the pit from the save point.

Before attempting the large pit crossing, collect the **third Wind Gem** from the passage reached through the lower wall opening; you need **three Wind Dashes** to cross. Follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Endu Wind Gem locations</Link> if you have not found it.

<h2 id="trident-key-puzzle">
  Why Can You Find Only Two Trident Keys in Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

Further into the temple, you will reach a **third save point** in a room containing multiple Trident-shaped keyholes.

The number of sockets makes this room look like a mandatory four-key puzzle. It is not.

**Only two Trident Keys are found inside Obmuer Temple.** They are sufficient to progress through the dungeon, provided you move them between the necessary locks rather than expecting a different permanent key for every socket.

Additional keyholes lead to optional loot that cannot all be claimed during your first visit.

### Where Do the Other Two Trident Keys Come From?

There are **four Trident Keys** involved in the larger puzzle:

1. **First Temple Key:** Found in the watery area near the entrance.
2. **Second Temple Key:** Found in the section reached through the wall opening below the large pit.
3. **Trident Weapon Key:** Obtained by leveling the Trident weapon collected outside the temple to its maximum level. Its loosely attached head eventually breaks off.
4. **Shop Key:** Purchased from Umbada's Trading Outpost after defeating the boss of the next main dungeon, the Tower of Knowledge.

The third key is especially confusing because the Trident's item description hints that its head is loosely attached, but you cannot simply remove the piece through the inventory at the start.

**Use the Trident in combat and level it to its maximum.** Trying to detach the head immediately, hit random objects with it, or find a nearby switch to remove it does not satisfy the progression condition.

The fourth key is even more clearly tied to later progress. It becomes purchasable only after the appropriate shop inventory upgrade following the Tower of Knowledge boss.

<h2 id="royal-guards">
  How Do You Open the Door to Dagoom the Sea King?
</h2>

Leave the Trident Key save-point room and enter the large cavern beyond it.

You must defeat **three Royal Guard sub-bosses** in this area. The door to the main boss does not open while any of the three remain undefeated.

Search the cavern methodically, using the available passages and changes in elevation to find each guard. After defeating one, check the rest of the cavern instead of returning to the Trident Key puzzle room.

The guards are individual encounters, so take advantage of the space to avoid fighting more enemies than necessary. Keep enough healing items for Dagoom rather than spending all your resources clearing ordinary enemies on the way.

Once all three Royal Guards are defeated, the previously closed door opens and you can proceed to the main boss.

If the door remains shut, the immediate task is to find a surviving Royal Guard. You do not need the two optional Trident Keys to trigger this door.

<h2 id="dagoom-boss">
  How Do You Beat Dagoom the Sea King?
</h2>

**Dagoom the Sea King** is the main boss of Obmuer Temple. The fight takes place around water and combines direct attacks with poison, powerful currents, and smaller fish enemies.

The most punishing part of the encounter is trying to maintain melee pressure after Dagoom covers the nearby water with damaging effects. Even when the boss appears vulnerable, approaching immediately can expose you to continuing poison damage.

### Keep a Ranged Attack Available

Bring a healthy supply of healing items and make sure your throwing weapons have usable charges before entering.

Throwing daggers and other ranged attacks let you damage Dagoom while avoiding prolonged exposure to the most dangerous parts of the arena. The **Azure Torch** can also inflict burning damage, so landing its effect when Dagoom is accessible gives you another way to deal damage without continuously staying beside the boss.

A practical approach is to:

1. Begin at a distance and watch which parts of the arena Dagoom's attacks affect.
2. Use throwing weapons while the boss is difficult to approach safely.
3. Apply the Azure Torch's burning effect when you have a clear opportunity.
4. Use Wind Dash to reposition when currents or poison threaten your current position.
5. Attack the smaller fish with Slash damage when they become an immediate obstacle.
6. Return to Dagoom once you have a safe opening, rather than chasing it through damaging water.

### How Do You Survive the Poison and Currents?

Dagoom can spread poison around the arena, and its water attacks can interfere with your movement. Some sequences also create strong currents that change which positions are safe.

Watch the water rather than focusing exclusively on Dagoom's body. A boss animation that appears to create a melee opening can still leave a damaging area around it.

Poison-duration reduction is useful, but it should not be treated as immunity to the arena's damage. Moving clear of the affected water is more reliable than waiting beside the boss for a resistance effect to solve the problem.

The smaller fish also make long melee exchanges harder. Remove the ones that interfere with your movement, then resume attacking Dagoom. Trying to clear every fish before damaging the boss may waste healing and ranged charges without improving your position.

### When Should You Heal?

Use healing items after creating distance from the boss and its dangerous water effects. Waiting until you are nearly defeated is risky because Dagoom can combine poison damage with displacement from currents.

I would use an herb before another poison wave leaves no room to recover. Save at the last available Crystal Monument before entering, so a failed attempt does not cost you additional unsaved exploration.

Keep a few recovery items available for the final portion of the fight, when overlapping attacks can make a previously safe position unusable.

<h2 id="dagoom-rewards">
  What Do You Get for Defeating Dagoom?
</h2>

Defeating Dagoom unlocks the **Treacherous Baleen** achievement and rewards the **Silver Duck Figurine**.

The Silver Duck can be taken to Umbada, where it unlocks additional merchandise. You can return to the shop after the fight instead of purchasing extra equipment beforehand solely to complete the temple.

Once Dagoom is defeated, leave the boss area and follow the newly accessible route out of Obmuer Temple.

<h2 id="after-temple">
  Where Do You Go After Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

Light the **Gargoyle statue** along the exit route with the Azure Torch, then use the nearby fast-travel connection to return toward the opening region. The <Link href="/queens-domain/progression-guide">Tower unlock route</Link> explains how to complete the bell sequence.
