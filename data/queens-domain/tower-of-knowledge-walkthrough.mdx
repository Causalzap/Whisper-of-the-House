<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Light all three island Gargoyle beacons with the **Azure Torch** to open the Tower of Knowledge. Inside, ignite **Gargoyles 3 and 7** on the numbered-statue balcony to unlock the next section.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="enter-tower">
  How Do You Unlock the Tower of Knowledge?
</h2>

The Tower of Knowledge opens after all **three island Gargoyle beacons** have been lit with the Azure Torch and the bell has rung three times. If the entrance is still sealed, complete the <Link href="/queens-domain/progression-guide">Gargoyle beacon unlock route</Link> before returning.

Inside, expect Book Bugs, animated Gargoyles, and enemies capable of inflicting status effects. Bring ranged weapons and enough healing supplies to reach the next Crystal Monument.

Leave the screaming faces and fleshy growths alone during your first pass. They require an item obtained after defeating the Tower's main boss.

<h2 id="numbered-gargoyles">
  Which Gargoyles Do You Light in the Tower of Knowledge Puzzle?
</h2>

**Light Gargoyle 3 and Gargoyle 7.**

The numbered Gargoyle puzzle appears on a balcony inside the Tower of Knowledge. Several statues surround the area, and three stone plaques provide clues about which pair should receive the Azure Torch's flame.

One plaque is partly concealed among rubble, making it easy to assume you have read every clue when you have actually missed one.

Approach the numbered statues, locate **3** and **7**, and use the Azure Torch to light them. The correct pair opens a nearby door back inside the Tower.

Avoid testing every statue unless you deliberately want another fight. Lighting an incorrect Gargoyle can trigger an attack and bring out groups of Book Bugs. That adds combat without helping you reach the Archives.

The three island beacons used to unseal the Tower are a different puzzle. Their bells are already dealt with before entering; the numbered Gargoyles are an internal door mechanism.

### Where Do You Go After the Gargoyle Door Opens?

Return from the balcony through the newly opened interior passage. Work through the rooms beyond it until you find the next Crystal Monument, then continue up the stairwell.

The climb leads toward an upper fast-travel point and Knowledge Keeper Wourha's study. Explore the nearby rooms before continuing upstairs.

The Tower's enemies can apply several disruptive status effects, so preserve your healing supplies when crossing rooms that contain multiple threats. Ranged attacks are particularly helpful against enemies positioned above or across narrow paths.

If the next door appears inaccessible, check that you have entered the passage unlocked by the balcony puzzle. The correct Gargoyle pair changes an interior door; it does not automatically open every locked room in the Tower.

<h2 id="storm-gem">
  Where Is the Storm Gem in the Tower of Knowledge?
</h2>

From the upper section reached after the Gargoyle puzzle, continue toward the **large staircase near Wourha's study**.

Climb it to reach the **Storm Gem**, a missing component of the Holy Sword Endu.

Collect the **Storm Gem** to unlock **Raging Tempest**. To use the sword's new offensive ability, check <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">how the Storm Gem changes Endu</Link>.

The area around this pickup creates one of the Tower's most deceptive dead ends. You can reach the upper floor and still find locked doors with no obvious way to continue. Those doors are not opened simply by collecting the Storm Gem.

The route forward is **below the Storm Gem pickup**, through a movement-based shortcut leading to a switch.

<h2 id="rotating-staircase">
  How Do You Activate the Rotating Staircase?
</h2>

From the Storm Gem area, look beneath the platform where you collected the gem. A switch can be reached by using Wind Dash to cross to the lower opening or ledge.

Reach the switch and activate it. This **rotates the staircase**, creating a route to another part of the upper Tower.

The useful distinction is that the switch changes the path rather than directly handing you a key. After activating it, return to the staircase and follow its new alignment.

From there:

1. Go up the repositioned stairs.
2. Find the elevator accessible along the new route.
3. Ride it down to the area containing **Budaen**.
4. Speak with Budaen to obtain the **Forbidden Archives Key**.

The switch and elevator are the missing connection between the Storm Gem area and the Archives. Searching the locked doors above the gem without repositioning the stairs will not advance the dungeon.

<h2 id="forbidden-archives-key">

Return toward the locked doors near the Storm Gem. The key opens the **Forbidden Archives** section, which contains the route to the Tower's main boss.

This is the point where the dungeon becomes substantially more demanding. You will find another Crystal Monument before progressing through a series of enemy encounters.

Save before taking on the Archives. If you have been using most of your healing items to deal with the Tower's earlier status effects, make a supply run rather than attempting the final sequence with no recovery options.

<h2 id="necronomicon">
  How Do You Beat the Necronomicon in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The **Necronomicon** is the main boss of the Tower of Knowledge, encountered beyond the Forbidden Archives.

Unlike the Shrine Guardian, this fight rewards distance and controlled ranged damage more than constant close-range attacks. The Necronomicon can attack from afar and repeatedly apply status effects, making direct approaches costly.

Its behavior also changes during the encounter. The book can collapse into a position where melee attacks become practical, while another form resembles a twisted goat-like creature.

### Use Throwing Weapons Until the Book Drops

Equip your available throwing weapons before entering the arena, with the **Azure Torch** ready for opportunities to inflict fire damage. Keeping multiple ranged options available makes it easier to pressure the Necronomicon without repeatedly approaching its status attacks.

During the fight:

1. Keep your distance while the Necronomicon launches status-inflicting attacks.
2. Damage the book with throwing weapons rather than continuously chasing it into melee range.
3. Watch for the book to fall or become accessible, then close in for a short melee exchange.
4. Withdraw before the boss resumes attacking from a distance.
5. When the goat-like form appears, prioritize avoiding its attacks until the book form returns.

I would not spend the entire encounter trying to force melee hits against a boss that can punish the approach with multiple status effects. Maintaining a safe throwing distance gives you more opportunities to heal and makes each opening easier to recognize.

### When Should You Heal During the Fight?

Create distance before using an Elixir, especially after the Necronomicon applies a status effect. Its ranged attacks can punish a recovery attempt made while standing directly in front of the boss.

A brief collapse creates a chance to strike, but the goat-like form changes the timing of the fight. Save your recovery items for moments when you can safely disengage rather than spending them while chasing the boss.

<h2 id="acid-vial">
  What Do You Get After Defeating the Necronomicon?
</h2>

Defeating the Necronomicon unlocks the **Hell Transcribed** achievement and gives you an **Acid Vial**.

The Acid Vial changes what your basic throwing daggers can destroy. You can now use them against the fleshy growths found throughout the Tower of Knowledge, including the screaming faces and obstructions that previously resisted your attacks.

<h2 id="mausoleum">
  How Do You Reach Deimpo Mausoleum After the Tower?
</h2>

Return toward **Budaen's area** after defeating the Necronomicon.

Budaen is now dead, and a nearby fleshy obstruction blocks the way forward. Use your Acid Vial-enhanced basic throwing daggers to destroy it.

Removing this growth opens a path toward **Deimpo Mausoleum**, the location connected to the sealed entrance seen near the soldiers' camp at the beginning of the game.

Follow the opened passage rather than returning to the upper Gargoyle puzzle. There is also a way to open the route associated with the original camp entrance, making the area accessible from the earlier part of the island.
