<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Find the **Dagger Belt** near the shipwreck, collect the **Endu Shrine Gate Key** from the beach Crypt, then unlock the shrine and defeat the **Shrine Guardian** to claim the Holy Sword Endu.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

<h2 id="where-to-start">
  Where Do You Go First on the Isle of Neasied?
</h2>

Your first destination in Queen's Domain is not the enormous sealed door near the camp. The soldiers explain that your father entered the **Deimpo Mausoleum** with his knights and never returned, but you cannot open that entrance yet.

The immediate route takes you through a burial Crypt, into the Shrine of Endu, and eventually to your first major movement ability. Trying to force the Mausoleum entrance or explore every distant ledge before obtaining Endu will only send you back along the same paths.

Begin at the campfire after the shipwreck.

1. Speak to the soldiers and accept the **Rusted Sword**. Equip it before leaving the encampment.
2. Break the wooden planks blocking the path out of camp. Some obstacles require repeated attacks rather than an interaction prompt.
3. Activate the nearby **Crystal Monument** and make a manual save.
4. Follow the early coastal paths toward the remains of your ship. Look for the **Dagger Belt** before committing to the Crypt.
5. Equip the throwing daggers and continue around the shoreline toward the burial site.

The opening enemies include Ratty Bugs, flying creatures, aggressive plants, and skeletons. Your Rusted Sword can handle weaker targets, but attacks made before the yellow power gauge has recovered deal considerably less damage. Let enemies commit to their attacks, move out of range, and return with a charged strike rather than repeatedly swinging at an empty gauge.

There is no reason to clear every enemy between the camp and the shipwreck. A few are positioned to punish players who stop in open ground, particularly before they have a ranged weapon.

<h2 id="dagger-belt">
  Where Is the Dagger Belt in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The **Dagger Belt** is found along the early shoreline near the shipwreck debris. Reach it by following the coastal exploration route from the starting camp, rather than pushing toward the sealed Mausoleum door.

Watch for an accessible raised position near the wreckage. The pickup can be easy to overlook while avoiding the nearby flying enemies, so check the edges of the path before moving farther inland.

The belt is especially valuable because its enchanted daggers return after being thrown. You are not spending a permanent dagger every time you attack. Instead, you work around the available charges while they replenish.

Along the beach, use the returning daggers to attack laser flowers from a distance and pull individual enemies away from groups. Once you have the belt, continue toward the Crypt rather than spending more healing items clearing the shoreline. Its entrance requires a ranged hit to activate the switch.

The belt is worth collecting immediately. Running past an enemy to secure it can be more useful than spending your early healing items clearing a difficult stretch with only the Rusted Sword.

<h2 id="crypt-entrance">
  How Do You Find and Open the Crypt?
</h2>

The Shrine of Endu is guarded by a locked gate. An injured knight near the shrine explains that the key is believed to be in a nearby burial site.

Follow the coastline around to the Crypt rather than searching the shrine walls for a hidden lever. The Crypt entrance has a switch that can be activated with a throwing weapon.

Equip the Dagger Belt, aim at the switch, and throw a dagger. Once the entrance opens, move inside and descend through the burial chambers.

You can fight through the enemies or bypass some of them. The more important choice is whether you have enough health to survive the return journey after taking the key. Clearing enemies along the narrow passages makes the route easier to retrace, but using every healing item before the Shrine Guardian is an unnecessary cost.

### Pick Up the Club Before Leaving

A **Club** can be collected inside the Crypt. It deals Impact damage, providing an important alternative to the Rusted Sword's Slash damage.

Take it even if its slower attack speed feels awkward. Skeleton enemies can resist or respond differently to weapon damage types, and the Club becomes particularly useful against the Shrine Guardian.

Equip the Club in a second weapon slot so you can change attacks without repeatedly opening the inventory. Keep the Rusted Sword available for enemies better handled with Slash damage.

<h2 id="shrine-key">
  Where Is the Endu Shrine Gate Key?
</h2>

Continue down the Crypt stairs until you reach the lower chamber containing the **Endu Shrine Gate Key**. The item is found near the bottom of the burial route, not behind the large sealed Mausoleum entrance at the starting camp.

Pick up the key and prepare for enemies as you retrace the route. Skeletons can become an immediate problem around this point, especially when your health or throwing charges are low.

There is an elevator route back toward the earlier part of the island. Use it instead of unnecessarily repeating the entire descent through the Crypt.

Before leaving, check one easily missed secret.

### Find the Hidden Wall and Pandora Blade

Near the key chamber, examine the walls and compare their torch fixtures. One wall lacks the brazier seen on the surrounding walls.

Approach that unusual section and use **Interact** — `E` with the default keyboard controls. The hidden passage opens through interaction, rather than by repeatedly attacking every wall.

Inside is the **Pandora Blade**, an optional weapon associated with a special technique. It is worth picking up while you are already in the Crypt, but it is not required to defeat the Shrine Guardian.

There is no need to spend time leveling the Pandora Blade before continuing. The Club already provides the damage type needed for the next major fight, and the Shrine Guardian is the more important immediate obstacle.

Once you have both the Shrine Key and the optional hidden weapon, return toward the first Crystal Monument. **Rest, replenish your health, and make a manual save before approaching the shrine.**

That last action matters. Dying without saving can return your inventory and exploration progress to the previous saved state, including pickups made during the Crypt trip. Resting and saving should not be treated as interchangeable actions.

<h2 id="shrine-guardian">
  How Do You Beat the Shrine Guardian in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

Return to the locked Shrine of Endu with the key from the Crypt. Open the gate and enter the arena to fight the **Shrine Guardian**, a heavily armored skeletal warrior carrying a large cleaver.

The Guardian is **weak to Impact damage**, which makes the Club you found in the Crypt a practical early weapon choice. The Rusted Sword can still deal damage, but repeatedly using Slash attacks at close range makes this encounter harder than necessary.

### Keep Moving Around the Cleaver

The Guardian's close-range attacks punish players who stay directly in front of it. Use the following rhythm:

1. Approach far enough to draw an attack, then move around the Guardian rather than trading blows.
2. Let the attack animation finish before stepping back into melee range.
3. Land a charged Club hit while the Guardian is recovering.
4. Retreat to restore your attack gauge, then repeat.
5. Throw daggers whenever there is room to attack without exposing yourself to a cleaver swing.

Do not attempt to fight it as though you already have Wind Dash. You receive that movement ability after the encounter, so your options here are ordinary movement, positioning, jumping, and the weapons collected earlier.

The arena's rocks can also interfere with your escape path. Keep the fight in open space instead of circling too tightly around the terrain. Getting caught on an obstacle during a cleaver attack can undo several successful exchanges.

### When Should You Use Healing Items?

Bring the **Dried Herbs** and **Elixirs** collected in the Crypt. After the Guardian commits to a cleaver swing, move clear of its reach and heal before it closes the distance again. Using an item directly in front of the boss can cost more health than it restores.

Spend an herb when another hit would end the attempt. There is little advantage in saving every consumable if doing so forces you to repeat the fight from your last manual save.

### What Happens When the Shrine Guardian Dies?

Once defeated, the Guardian's dialogue acknowledges its failure to protect the shrine. You can collect the **Stone Duck Figurine** and proceed to obtain the **Holy Sword Endu**.

Both pickups matter. One changes how you move through the island; the other provides access to a merchant.

<h2 id="umbada-shop">
  How Do You Unlock Umbada's Shop After the Shrine Guardian?
</h2>

Take the **Stone Duck Figurine** obtained from the Shrine Guardian to an activated Crystal Monument. Interact with the monument and use the newly available connection to reach **Umbada's Trading Outpost**.

Umbada buys unwanted treasures and sells equipment for Ancient Coins. You can visit immediately, but there is no need to farm coins for a new weapon before leaving the opening area; check the <Link href="/queens-domain">early equipment priorities</Link> before committing your starting currency.
