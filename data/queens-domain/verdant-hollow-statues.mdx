<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To open the sealed entrance in **Verdant Hollow**, remove all **eight Sipeha seals** using the **Mask of Second Sight** and the appropriate lenses. The seals are found in **Rotting Cavern, Deimpo Mausoleum, the Underground Prison, the Tower of Knowledge underground area, two locations beneath the Isle of Neasied, the Obmuer Temple underground area, and Record of Sin**. If a seal remains inaccessible, check your lens collection before searching the same room again.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The sealed entrance connected to the **Temple of Puwema** is easy to discover before you are ready to open it. Its circular emblem provides a clue, but the required statues are scattered through regions you may have visited hours earlier.

The important distinction is between **finding a statue** and **removing its magical seal**. A stone figure can be visible long before you have the lenses needed to reveal the relevant magic. If you have already found seven statues, the final one may be in a concealed underground route rather than another major dungeon.

<h2 id="verdant-hollow-gate">
  How Do You Open the Eight-Statue Gate in Verdant Hollow?
</h2>

The tall stone doorway in Verdant Hollow remains sealed until all eight Sipeha seals have been cleared. Look for the circular emblem above its arch.

Beyond this barrier lies the temple route associated with the purification altar needed for the **True Holy Sword Akunra**. Removing the seals opens the way forward; it does not automatically forge the sword.

Before searching for every statue, make sure you have obtained the **Mask of Second Sight**. The mask accepts lenses that reveal magic and runes associated with different deities.

**Sipeha's Lens** reveals Sipeha's magic when attached to the mask. However, collecting this lens alone does not guarantee that every underground route is accessible. At least one of the eight seals requires additional lens-related progression.

These statues are separate from the **three island Gargoyle beacons** used to unlock the Tower of Knowledge. Lighting the Gargoyles with the Azure Torch does not complete the eight-seal objective.

Pay particular attention to the **two underground statues on the starting island** and the **two locations beneath the Tower of Knowledge**. They are easy to miss if you assume there must be exactly one statue in each named region.

<h3 id="rotting-cavern">
  1. Rotting Cavern — The Corrupted-Water Chamber
</h3>

The Rotting Cavern seal lies within the ruined underground chambers reached through the corrupted-water passages. Enemies and hazardous water can inflict **Magic Rot**, making prolonged exploration dangerous.

Look for the seated statue with a circular opening in front of its body. If its magic remains concealed, check your Mask of Second Sight and lens collection. The **Jigeki's Lens** progression is particularly relevant to this part of the underground route.

<h3 id="deimpo-mausoleum">
  2. Deimpo Mausoleum — The Statue Across the Chasm
</h3>

This statue is mounted against a steep rock wall rather than placed in the center of a conventional room.

The important obstacle is the **wide chasm separating the approach from the statue's position**. Look for an elevated route that provides enough height to cross toward the opposite rock face.

Use your available Wind Dashes to bridge the gap, but check where you intend to land before jumping. The statue is set into the wall, so searching only the lower floor can leave you directly beneath the objective.

<h3 id="prison-second-floor">
  3. Underground Prison — Second-Floor Boss Room
</h3>

One seal is found in the **second-floor boss room of the underground prison complex**.

This is not the upper library area of the Tower of Knowledge. You need to explore the lower prison route and its connected chambers.

The statue is easy to identify once you enter the correct room: it sits against the back wall, with candles and ceremonial vessels arranged around it.

  4. Isle of Neasied — The Hidden Route Beneath the Teleporter
</h3>

The useful landmark is the **blue-lit Crystal Monument inside a small stone chamber**. This is a navigation point, not the Sipeha statue itself.

Investigate the passages beneath the teleporter's level. A concealed route in this underground network involves hidden walls and leads farther down through an elevator connection.

A particularly useful clue is a **Meal Ticket pickup near one of the hidden-wall sections**. If you have found the pickup but appear to be at a dead end, examine the nearby wall before leaving the cave.

Do not count the Crystal Monument as an activated seal. Continue through the hidden passages until you find the seated stone figure with the circular emblem.

<h2 id="required-lenses">
  What Lenses Do You Need to Reveal the Sipeha Seals?
</h2>

The **Mask of Second Sight** reveals magical markings when the corresponding lens is available. **Sipeha's Lens** allows the wearer to see Sipeha's magic and runes, as stated in its item description.

Finding the mask is only the first step. The collection also involves other lenses, including **Jigeki's Lens**, which is relevant to a later seal-access requirement.

If a statue is visible but its magical seal cannot be revealed or removed, check the lenses you have collected rather than repeatedly interacting with the stone figure.

For the more demanding underground routes, **Jigeki's Lens** may be the missing requirement. Its acquisition involves a separate series of key fragments and a temple puzzle. Having Sipeha's Lens does not mean every other lens-related obstacle has been resolved.

Equip the Mask of Second Sight and return to the unresolved statue after collecting the missing lens.

<h2 id="seven-of-eight">
  Why Is the Verdant Hollow Gate Still Locked at Seven of Eight?
</h2>

If the gate remains sealed, first compare the statues you have actually reached with the <Link href="#all-eight-locations">eight-location table</Link>. Finding a statue and removing its magical seal are not necessarily the same step.

Check these easily missed conditions:

1. **Starting island underground:** There are two seal locations. Search beyond the hidden walls near the teleporter-area passages, including the deeper elevator connection.
2. **Tower of Knowledge underground:** The prison boss room and the separate lower statue chamber are different locations. Clearing the Tower's Gargoyle puzzle does not remove either seal.
3. **Deimpo Mausoleum:** Reaching the floor beneath the chasm does not count as reaching the statue embedded in the opposite rock wall.
4. **Lens requirements:** If you have seen a statue but could not reveal or remove its magic, return with the Mask of Second Sight and the necessary lenses. Pay particular attention to Jigeki's Lens and the Rotting Cavern route.

Once all eight seals have been cleared, return to the sealed doorway in Verdant Hollow. The passage leads toward the temple area involved in the **True Holy Sword Akunra** objective.

For Endu's weapon upgrades and the distinction between Soul Bound Endu and Akunra, see the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu guide</Link>.
