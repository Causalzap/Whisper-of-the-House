<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Defeat the **Shrine Guardian** and collect the **Holy Sword Endu** inside the shrine. Use its Wind Dash to cross gaps, then collect the next **Wind Gem** near the elevated Crystal Monument to unlock a second air dash.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Endu has two different upgrade paths: **Wind Gems increase how many times you can dash through the air**, while weapon experience increases its combat damage. Check your available Dash charges before attempting a distant platform; leveling the sword alone will not extend your reach.

<h2 id="how-to-get-endu">
  Where Do You Get the Holy Sword Endu?
</h2>

Defeat the **Shrine Guardian** and collect Endu inside the shrine to unlock the **Flying Sword** achievement. If the shrine is still locked, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/isle-of-neasied-walkthrough">Crypt Key and Shrine Guardian route</Link> first.

Open your inventory after collecting the sword. Endu starts with **10 Power**, deals **Slash** damage, and already contains **one Wind Gem**. Its description also shows sockets for five Wind Gems and a separate Storm Gem.

You can begin using Wind Dash as soon as you acquire the sword. The other four Wind Gems are additional pickups found as you explore the island.

<h2 id="wind-dash">
  How Does Wind Dash Work, and Do You Need Endu Equipped?
</h2>

**You can use Wind Dash without keeping Endu equipped.** Face the platform you want to reach and activate the ability to thrust through the air. Each installed Wind Gem adds one Dash charge, displayed by the gem indicators in the upper-left HUD.

You can keep another melee weapon equipped for enemies resistant to Endu's Slash damage without losing this movement ability.

This gives you three useful early movement stages:

- **One Wind Gem:** Your initial Dash, useful for shorter gaps and elevated paths.
- **Two Wind Gems:** Two consecutive Dashes, enough to cross the broken western bridge leading toward Obmuer Temple.
- **Three Wind Gems:** Three consecutive Dashes, required for the large interior pit inside Obmuer Temple.

The additional charges are particularly useful when a platform is visible but slightly beyond your current reach. One Dash may carry you most of the way, but you cannot solve every crossing by repeatedly attempting the same jump with only one gem.

<h2 id="second-wind-gem">
  Where Is the Second Wind Gem in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The second Wind Gem is on an elevated platform reached from the **second Crystal Monument above the starting region**.

After obtaining Endu, return toward the first Crystal Monument. Go uphill and use your single Wind Dash to cross toward the higher route leading to another save point.

From that second monument:

1. Look for the **rocks between the pillars** near the elevated path.
2. Climb those rocks to gain height before attempting the gap.
3. Face the platform containing the Wind Gem.
4. Use Wind Dash to cross and collect it.

After collecting the gem, check your interface. You should now have **two blue Wind Dash indicators**, allowing two consecutive Dashes.

This is the first major traversal upgrade after getting Endu. With two Dashes, you can cross the broken western bridge that previously stopped your progress.

There is no need to farm weapon experience for this crossing. The second Wind Gem is the requirement, and obtaining it is what changes the available route.

<h2 id="third-wind-gem">
  Where Is the Third Wind Gem in Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

The third Wind Gem is inside **Obmuer Temple**. You must first open the temple entrance with four Triangular Slates; follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Obmuer Temple entrance route</Link> if you have not reached the dungeon yet.

Inside, progress to the **second Crystal Monument** beside the large pit. Look below the pit for an opening in the wall, then use Wind Dash to reach it.

Continue through the passage toward the elevated section where skeletons ambush you. The **third Wind Gem** is in this area. Collect it before returning to the large pit.

You now have **three consecutive Wind Dashes**, enough to clear the interior gap and continue through the temple. Raising Endu's weapon level will not replace the missing third Dash.

<h2 id="remaining-wind-gems">
  How Many Wind Gems Does Endu Need?
</h2>

Endu has room for **five Wind Gems in total**. You begin with one, and each additional gem increases your available Wind Dashes by one.

The first three can be collected during the opening route and Obmuer Temple progression. Two more remain to complete the sword's five-gem capacity.

Collecting all five unlocks the **Feel the Wind** achievement.

The early three-gem route is enough to handle the required crossings through Obmuer Temple. You do not need to collect all five before entering the Tower of Knowledge.

For later exploration, remember paths that end at distant platforms or elevated openings. Endu's movement upgrades make previously inaccessible routes worth revisiting, rather than permanent dead ends.

When deciding whether to return to an earlier location, use the <Link href="/queens-domain/progression-guide">area progression route</Link> to check which regions become accessible after your next movement upgrade.

<h2 id="weapon-levels">
  How Do You Level Up and Evolve the Holy Sword Endu?
</h2>

Wind Gems are not Endu's only upgrades. The sword also gains **weapon experience through combat**, improving its damage as you use it against enemies.

Successful hits increase the equipped weapon's experience. Early Endu levels add **3% Power per level**: at Level 3, the sword displays a **+6% bonus**. Reaching **Level 10** triggers its evolution into Soul Bound Endu and resets the displayed weapon level.

Endu deals Slash damage, making it useful against enemies vulnerable to that type. However, an enemy resistant to Slash can still be a poor target even when Endu has gained several levels. Switching to a suitable Impact or Pierce weapon is often more efficient than forcing every fight with your sword.


### What Changes When Endu Evolves at Level 10?

At **Level 10**, Holy Sword Endu evolves into **Soul Bound Endu**. Its new item description shows **13 Power**, **Average** attack speed, **Slash** damage, and a fresh **Level 1** progression bar.

The original Endu has 10 Power, so the evolved weapon gains **3 base Power**. Its attacks also gain a new property: a sufficiently forceful swing produces an **arc of holy energy**, giving the sword an offensive option beyond its normal melee reach.

The evolution is tied to weapon experience, not the number of Wind Gems installed. You can continue using Wind Dash without giving up another weapon that is better suited to an enemy's damage weaknesses.

### Is Endu Worth Using as Your Main Weapon?

Soul Bound Endu's holy energy arc makes continued use of the sword more attractive in combat, but Wind Dash remains available when another melee weapon is equipped.

For normal exploration, I would keep Endu available alongside weapons that cover different damage weaknesses. Use the sword when Slash is effective and switch when another damage type is a better match. That advances Endu through suitable encounters without turning resistant enemies into unnecessarily long fights.

The movement charges remain tied to Wind Gems, so a higher weapon level alone will not let you cross a gap that requires another Dash.

<h2 id="storm-gem">
  What Does the Storm Gem Do to Endu?
</h2>

Recover the **Storm Gem** from the upper Tower of Knowledge to unlock **Raging Tempest**. It occupies a separate socket on Endu and does not increase your Wind Dash count.

The <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge route</Link> explains how to reach the gem after solving the numbered Gargoyle puzzle.

Once installed, the Storm Gem gives Endu a charge-based storm attack. Watch the sword's new power meter when using this offensive ability.

This matters when deciding what to collect next. A player missing the fourth Wind Dash needs another Wind Gem, not the Storm Gem. Recovering the Storm Gem improves Endu's offensive capabilities but does not replace the movement upgrades required for distant platforms.

<h2 id="akunra">
  How Is the True Holy Sword Akunra Related to Endu?
</h2>

Endu's item description connects it to the legendary **True Holy Sword Akunra**, while the Storm Gem's description explains more of that connection.

Akunra was shattered, and its Holy Storm Gem was split. The half retaining storm power became part of Endu's history, while the other half, containing holy power, was forged into the sister sword **Yuno**.

The game also has an achievement called **Blade Bathed in Holy Light**, awarded for forging the True Holy Sword Akunra.

Forging Akunra is a separate objective from leveling Endu or recovering the Storm Gem. Reaching a higher weapon level does not automatically satisfy the **Blade Bathed in Holy Light** requirement.
