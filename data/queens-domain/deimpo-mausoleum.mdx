<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Enter **Deimpo Mausoleum** from the Tower of Knowledge after defeating the Necronomicon and clearing the fleshy passage with your acid-enhanced throwing daggers. Explore the moving-stone-pillar routes, collect **Puwema's Lens** and the **Mask of Second Sight**, then equip the mask to find and dispel **six glowing sigils** throughout the Mausoleum. Return to the sealed boss passage, defeat **Poenir and Duenir**, and speak with **King Gius Abraon** to collect **Unok's Remains**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Deimpo Mausoleum is where Queen's Domain changes from a straightforward dungeon route into a larger search puzzle. Finding the Mask of Second Sight does not immediately open the boss door. You must revisit the Mausoleum's connected passages, locate the green magical sigils, and remove all six before continuing.

The area also contains the fourth Wind Gem, several useful shortcuts, and an important meeting that explains where the next stage of the adventure leads.

<h2 id="enter-mausoleum">
  How Do You Enter Deimpo Mausoleum?
</h2>

The route into Deimpo Mausoleum begins in the **Tower of Knowledge**.

Complete the Tower's numbered Gargoyle puzzle, recover the Forbidden Archives Key from Budaen, and defeat the Necronomicon. The boss drops an **Acid Vial** that automatically enhances your basic throwing daggers.

Return to the fleshy obstruction near Budaen's section of the Tower and destroy it with your throwing daggers. This opens the passage toward Deimpo Mausoleum.

If you have not obtained the Acid Vial, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge walkthrough</Link> first.

Entering the Mausoleum unlocks **Unsealed Tomb**.

### What Should You Bring Before Entering?

You should already have **three Wind Dashes** from the first three Wind Gems. That is enough to begin exploring, although another gem becomes available inside the Mausoleum.

Before proceeding:

- Save at an accessible Crystal Monument.
- Carry healing supplies for repeated fights against skeletons, spiders, and other enemies.
- Keep throwing weapons equipped for enemies positioned across gaps or on elevated platforms.
- Keep a weapon that deals damage other than Slash available for enemies that resist your sword.
- Check side passages before assuming every locked gate is part of the main route.

Several Mausoleum passages connect back to earlier rooms. Opening their shortcuts makes revisiting the area much less expensive.

<h2 id="moving-pillars">
  How Do You Get Through the Moving Stone Pillars?
</h2>

The early Mausoleum contains vertically connected chambers, narrow ledges, and **moving stone pillars**. Some pillars rise and fall, while others move between platforms.

These are navigation mechanisms, not simply background decorations.

When you reach a chamber with multiple stone pillars:

1. Check which pillar moves close enough to your current ledge to board safely.
2. Ride it until it reaches the height needed for the next platform.
3. Look for a second pillar or an opening in the adjacent wall before jumping.
4. Use Wind Dash to correct the remaining horizontal distance.
5. Check the destination for a switch, an openable shortcut, or another passage before moving deeper.

A common mistake is to jump toward the farthest visible platform immediately. The route may require transferring between pillars rather than crossing the entire chamber in one jump.

Some mechanisms also change how the pillars move. If a route looks impossible, check nearby walls and reachable platforms for an activation point instead of repeatedly trying the same Dash.

### How Do You Avoid Repeating the Entire Area?

Activate the shortcuts you encounter, even when they appear to lead backward.

The Mausoleum loops through several familiar chambers at different elevations. A door or pillar that seems to return you to an earlier area may be the connection that makes later exploration manageable.

Save whenever you reach another Crystal Monument. The six-sigil objective eventually requires revisiting parts of the Mausoleum, so preserving easy access to those rooms is more useful than clearing every respawned enemy.

<h2 id="fourth-wind-gem">
  Where Is the Fourth Wind Gem in Deimpo Mausoleum?
</h2>

The **fourth Wind Gem** is found along the elevated moving-pillar route in Deimpo Mausoleum. Look above the lower chambers as you travel through the connected stone platforms.

Collecting it increases your available Wind Dashes from three to four, making distant ledges easier to reach. However, the fourth gem is not required to complete the six-sigil puzzle or open the boss passage.

For the pickup landmark and the remaining Wind Gem requirements, see the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu Wind Gem locations</Link>.

<h2 id="puwemas-lens">
  Where Do You Get Puwema's Lens?
</h2>

As you explore deeper into Deimpo Mausoleum, look for a **chapel-like chamber with a save point**.

Inside, speak with the figure who addresses you as Prince Beruim and explains that you must continue the task your father could not complete.

The conversation directs you toward Puwema's shrine and rewards you with **Puwema's Lens**.

Puwema's Lens is a **key item**, not an ordinary weapon or damage accessory.

Its description identifies Puwema as a goddess associated with nature and fertility and explains the lens's connection to her magical symbols.

### Why Can't You See the Hidden Sigils Yet?

Obtaining the lens alone does not mean you have finished the puzzle.

The lens is designed to work with the **Mask of Second Sight**. Continue searching the Mausoleum until you recover the mask, then equip it before returning to previously explored corridors.

Do not confuse the lens with the final puzzle reward. Its purpose is to make the next set of magical obstacles visible.

<h2 id="mask-second-sight">
  Where Do You Find the Mask of Second Sight, and How Does It Work?
</h2>

The **Mask of Second Sight** is another key pickup inside Deimpo Mausoleum.

Continue exploring the Mausoleum's branching chambers after obtaining Puwema's Lens. The mask is recovered during the deeper exploration sequence that also includes elevated ledges and dangerous side encounters.

After collecting it, open your equipment menu and **equip the mask**. Make sure Puwema's Lens is available for the mask's magical sight function.

The mask's description explains that it can reveal the magic of **four different gods**, provided the appropriate lenses are installed.

For the current Mausoleum objective, Puwema's Lens is the relevant one.

The mask is also used later to reveal Sipeha's magical seals outside the Mausoleum. One of the eight statues is found across a wide chasm within Deimpo; see the <Link href="/queens-domain/verdant-hollow-statues">eight Sipeha statue locations</Link> if you are returning for that objective.

### Does the Mask Reveal Every Hidden Wall?

No. The Mask of Second Sight is not a universal secret-wall detector.

It reveals particular magical marks associated with its installed lenses. Ordinary hidden walls and mechanical passages are separate discoveries.

If you are looking for the Mausoleum's main puzzle, concentrate on the **green glowing symbols** revealed by the mask rather than checking every stone wall for a hidden door.

<h2 id="six-sigils">
  How Do You Find and Dispel All Six Sigils?
</h2>

After equipping the Mask of Second Sight, return through the connected Mausoleum rooms and look for **six Puwema sigils**.

These appear as luminous green magical symbols. Their glow becomes the important visual clue once the mask is active.


### Where Should You Look for the Six Puwema Sigils?

With the **Mask of Second Sight** equipped and Puwema's Lens available, follow the floating green particles through Deimpo Mausoleum.

The six markings do not need to be cleared in a fixed order. These landmarks will help you identify the passages while exploring:

1. **Near the two blue flames:** Find the burial chamber with a large stone sarcophagus and two blue-flame braziers. Look for the green marking inside this room.

2. **Across the gap:** Pass through the stone archway, jump across to the opposite passage, and follow the path to its end.

3. **At the right-hand end of the passage:** Return to the earlier route, continue straight to the end, and check the right-hand side for the next marking.

4. **Beside the candlelit altar:** Look for another chamber containing a stone sarcophagus and an alcove illuminated by orange candles. The sigil appears near the end of the sarcophagus, in front of the candlelit area.

5. **Along the rocky passage:** Check the corridor with hanging purple growths and uneven stone flooring. Watch for green particles leading toward a marking among the rocks.

6. **Around the elevated blue-lit section:** Look for the elevated route overlooking a blue-glowing pedestal. Use the pedestal to orient yourself, then search the adjoining passages for the remaining green trail.

The last two landmarks are less distinctive than the earlier rooms. If you cannot immediately spot a sigil, follow its green particle trail rather than searching only the visible wall.

Once you have dispelled all six sigils, return to the sealed boss passage and check that all six indicators are illuminated.

If any indicator is still dark, keep the mask equipped and revisit the Mausoleum's connected rooms, including the lower chambers and moving-pillar routes.

### What If You Can Only Find Four or Five Sigils?

First, make sure you are wearing the Mask of Second Sight rather than merely carrying it in your inventory.

Then return to the **lower chambers and moving-pillar connections**. The area's vertical design makes it easy to revisit the same hallway while missing an entrance at another height.

Check both ends of opened shortcuts and the side passages that connect to Crystal Monuments. You can also follow the direction of the visible green trails when another mark appears.

Avoid wasting time fighting every enemy that has respawned. The immediate objective is to locate the remaining sigils, not to clear the entire Mausoleum again.

If the boss passage remains sealed, treat that as a sign to recheck your sigil progress before searching for an unrelated key.

<h2 id="boss-door">
  Where Do You Go After Dispersing the Six Sigils?
</h2>

After clearing all six markings, return toward the **sealed boss passage near Chef Gagi's section of Deimpo Mausoleum**.

Chef Gagi is the NPC who accepts Meal Tickets. His collection rewards are separate from this dungeon puzzle; finding him does not mean you must turn in a particular number of tickets to open the boss route.

The sigils are the important requirement.

Use the Mausoleum's Crystal Monuments, shortcuts, and available fast-travel items to shorten the return journey. You do not need to replay the entire dungeon in the same order as your first exploration.

Save and restore your health before entering the newly accessible encounter.

<h2 id="poenir-duenir">
  How Do You Beat Poenir and Duenir in Deimpo Mausoleum?
</h2>

The boss encounter beyond the sigil puzzle is **Poenir and Duenir, Malformed Demonic Aberrations**.

Defeating them unlocks **Demonic Servitude**.

This encounter is different from the earlier Necronomicon fight because you need to account for more than one enemy. An apparent opening against one target does not guarantee the other has stopped attacking.

### What Should You Use Against Poenir and Duenir?

The safest preparation is to bring weapons that let you maintain damage without standing directly underneath both enemies.

Useful options include:

- **Throwing weapons:** Keep pressure on targets that are difficult to approach.
- **A Slash weapon:** Useful when you can reach a vulnerable target and land a charged hit.
- **Poison attacks:** Can provide continuing damage while you reposition, when the target is susceptible.
- **Wind Dash:** Helps create distance and cross the arena when your current position becomes dangerous.
- **Healing items:** Preserve enough recovery for unexpected attacks from the second enemy.

Avoid spending all your throwable charges on an unreachable target when you may need them to maintain damage safely.

When an enemy descends or moves into melee range, use a short opening rather than staying close through its next attack.

### Why Does the Fight Sometimes Seem Unusually Easy?

Do not judge the encounter solely by the behavior of one visible enemy.

Both Poenir and Duenir belong to the encounter. If one remains outside normal fighting range or appears trapped against the arena geometry, that is not a dependable combat strategy.

For a normal attempt, keep track of both threats, maintain space with Wind Dash, and attack during clearly safe openings.

After the encounter ends, continue forward to meet **King Gius Abraon**.

<h2 id="king-gius">
  Where Do You Find King Gius Abraon?
</h2>

King Gius Abraon is encountered beyond the Poenir and Duenir boss sequence.

He recognizes **Prince Beruim** and explains what happened when he confronted **Queen Siraatsu**.

The King's own holy sword, **Unok**, was shattered in the encounter. He also identifies the Holy Sword Endu as Unok's sister sword and asks you to recover what remains of the broken weapon.

The item description confirms several important details:

- **Unok** was the holy sword wielded by King Gius Abraon.
- It shattered when the King struck Queen Siraatsu.
- The broken weapon contains **six holy gems** that have lost their former power.
- Unok and Endu are related weapons, but Unok's Remains is not another Wind Gem for Endu.

Keep Unok's Remains. It is an important story item connected to the later holy-weapon progression.

Do not assume that collecting these fragments immediately repairs Unok or forges a new sword. The Mausoleum provides the remains and the King's instructions, not a completed restoration.

<h2 id="ancient-world">
  Where Do You Go After Meeting King Gius Abraon?
</h2>

After recovering Unok's Remains, continue toward the **fissure and passage beyond the King's location**.

This route leads away from the traditional stone halls of Deimpo Mausoleum and toward the **Ancient World**.

You will begin encountering unfamiliar machinery, energy-powered equipment, and a different set of environmental obstacles.

The next region eventually introduces technological weapons and machinery that operate differently from the swords, torches, and Trident Keys used earlier.

Reaching the Ancient World unlocks **Genesis**.

For now, your important progress checks are:

1. All six Puwema sigils have been dispelled.
2. Poenir and Duenir have been defeated.
3. King Gius Abraon's conversation has been completed.
4. Unok's Remains is in your inventory.
5. The route beyond the Mausoleum is open.

Once those conditions are satisfied, you have completed the main Mausoleum progression and can continue exploring the next area.

<h2 id="stuck">
  What Should You Check If You're Stuck in Deimpo Mausoleum?
</h2>

### I found Puwema's Lens but cannot see the green symbols.

Find and equip the **Mask of Second Sight**. The lens is only one part of the setup. Carrying the lens without using the mask does not complete the puzzle.

### I have the mask, but the boss door will not open.

Check for all **six** glowing Puwema sigils. Return through the Mausoleum's branching rooms and follow the visible magical trails. Do not confuse the six-sigil requirement with collecting four lenses for the separate **Magic Eyes** achievement.

### I found the fourth Wind Gem but still cannot progress.

The Wind Gem gives you more movement capacity, not permission to skip the mask puzzle. Continue searching for Puwema's Lens, the Mask of Second Sight, and the six sigils.

### I found Chef Gagi but cannot reach the boss.

Chef Gagi's Meal Ticket quest is separate. The nearby sealed boss route depends on the Mausoleum's sigil puzzle, not on returning a fixed number of Meal Tickets.

### I defeated Poenir and Duenir but do not know what to do next.

Continue beyond their encounter and speak to **King Gius Abraon**. Recover **Unok's Remains** and follow the route toward the fissure leading into the Ancient World.
