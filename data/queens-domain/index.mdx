<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Collect the **Dagger Belt** near the shipwreck, retrieve the **Endu Shrine Gate Key** from the Crypt, and defeat the **Shrine Guardian** to claim the Holy Sword Endu.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Queen's Domain puts a sealed royal tomb near your starting camp, distant ruins across broken bridges, and plenty of paths that look reachable before you have the equipment to cross them. Finding a location does not necessarily mean you are ready to enter it.

The first major goal is the Holy Sword Endu. Its movement ability opens the island in stages, while keys, Gargoyle beacons, and equipment acquired in later dungeons determine which passages can be opened next.

You can explore beyond the main route, but there is a practical difference between taking a worthwhile detour and spending half an hour attacking a door that requires an item you have not found yet.

<h2 id="where-to-start">
  What Should You Do First in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The sealed entrance to Deimpo Mausoleum is visible near the starting camp, but you cannot enter it yet. Take the **Rusted Sword**, follow the coast to the shipwreck for the **Dagger Belt**, and retrieve the **Endu Shrine Gate Key** from the Crypt.

Before fighting the **Shrine Guardian**, equip the Club for Impact damage and save at a Crystal Monument. Defeat the Guardian to claim Endu. For the Crypt entrance switch, hidden passage, and boss attack windows, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/isle-of-neasied-walkthrough">opening island route</Link>.

<h2 id="movement">
  Why Can't You Reach Certain Platforms or Cross a Broken Bridge?
</h2>

Check Endu's available Wind Dashes before attempting a distant platform. The western broken bridge requires **two consecutive Dashes**, while the large pit inside Obmuer Temple requires **three**. Increasing the sword's damage will not extend your reach.

Collect the missing Wind Gems using the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu instructions</Link>. Wind Dash remains available even when another melee weapon is equipped.

<h2 id="tower-unlock">
  Why Is the Tower of Knowledge Still Locked?
</h2>

The Tower remains sealed until you ignite **three island Gargoyle beacons** with the Azure Torch. The first two are accessible during early exploration, but you cannot reach the third until after defeating Dagoom in Obmuer Temple.

If you have only heard two bells, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/progression-guide">third Gargoyle beacon route</Link> before returning to the Tower.

<h2 id="dungeons">
  What Are the First Major Dungeons in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

<h3>Obmuer Temple — The First Major Dungeon</h3>

After crossing the western bridge, you enter the flooded ruins surrounding Obmuer Temple. Opening the temple is a separate task from reaching the area.

The interior combines locked passages, underwater hazards, a movement upgrade, and several required encounters before its main boss, **Dagoom the Sea King**.

You do not need to obtain every optional Trident Key reward to finish the required route. Prioritize the dungeon's mandatory objectives, then return for equipment that requires later unlocks.

If the temple entrance is sealed or the route to Dagoom remains blocked, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Triangular Slate and Trident Key route</Link> to clear the required passages.

<h3>Tower of Knowledge — The Route to Deimpo Mausoleum</h3>

Enter the Tower once all three island bells have rung.

Its main obstacles include a numbered Gargoyle puzzle, a staircase mechanism, and the Forbidden Archives. You also encounter **Budaen**, who provides the key needed to continue toward the Tower's main boss.

Defeating **the Necronomicon** provides the Acid Vial, making previously resistant fleshy obstructions accessible. That ability is required for the route leading toward Deimpo Mausoleum.

If the next passage remains locked, consult the <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge walkthrough</Link> for the actual sequence of switches, rooms, and boss encounters.

<h2 id="combat">
  Why Are Your Attacks Doing So Little Damage?
</h2>

Queen's Domain rewards selecting the right attack more than repeatedly swinging the same weapon.

Your yellow attack-power gauge affects how hard an attack hits. Striking before it has recovered can produce substantially weaker damage, even when your weapon has a reasonable Power value.

Wait for an enemy to commit to an attack, move out of its reach, and return with a charged strike. Against fast or dangerous opponents, use throwing weapons to keep some distance instead of repeatedly entering melee range.

Different weapons also use different damage types:

- **Slash:** Used by swords such as the Holy Sword Endu.
- **Impact:** Available from weapons such as the Club.
- **Pierce:** Useful against enemies vulnerable to piercing attacks.

Enemy weaknesses and resistances affect how effectively these attacks perform. A sword animation that looks like a thrust does not automatically mean the weapon deals Pierce damage; check its actual item description.

The Bestiary helps identify enemy properties after you encounter them. A resistant enemy can make an otherwise strong weapon feel surprisingly ineffective.

### Should You Fight Every Enemy?

No. Running past a difficult group can be more useful than spending all your healing items before reaching the next Crystal Monument.

This matters most when you are retracing familiar ground or moving through a section that contains several enemies but no immediate required encounter.

However, do not charge blindly through an unfamiliar dungeon. Clearing a dangerous enemy near a narrow corridor or important pickup can make subsequent movement considerably safer.

<h2 id="coins-and-shop">
  What Should You Buy First With Ancient Coins?
</h2>

Defeating the Shrine Guardian also gives you the **Stone Duck Figurine**, which can be used at a Crystal Monument to access **Umbada's Trading Outpost**.

Ancient Coins become more useful once you have access to the merchant. You can spend them on combat equipment and upgrades rather than carrying them while struggling through the next dungeon.

A practical early purchase is additional **throwing-weapon charges**. Replenishing ranged attacks are helpful for separating enemies, maintaining pressure during fights, and hitting targets without standing in front of dangerous attacks.

Another option is the **Morning Star**, priced at **20 Ancient Coins**. Its Impact and Pierce damage complement Endu's Slash attacks, giving you alternatives against enemies resistant to the sword.

The decision depends on what is making your current encounters difficult:

- **Running out of ranged charges:** Improve throwing capacity before investing in another melee weapon.
- **Struggling against Slash-resistant enemies:** Consider the Morning Star for additional damage types.
- **Frequently affected by status conditions:** Look at protective equipment, including the Warding Amulet, rather than spending everything on damage.

Do not buy equipment solely because you have reached a merchant. Keep enough resources for healing and later purchases when the next dungeon may introduce different enemies.

Umbada also accepts additional duck figurines, which expand the shop's offerings as you progress.

<h2 id="saving">
  How Do Saving, Healing, and Status Effects Affect Progress?
</h2>

Crystal Monuments are particularly important before entering a dangerous region or attempting a major boss.

**Resting and making a manual save are not the same action.** Resting can restore your condition, but you should save separately to preserve recently acquired equipment, items, and exploration progress.

Make a manual save after collecting an important key or weapon. Otherwise, dying before your next save may force you to repeat a lengthy pickup route.

Healing herbs are worth using when they help you reach the next monument or survive a boss attempt. Preserving every common healing item is less valuable than keeping your progress moving.

Status-curing consumables deserve more caution. Later areas introduce effects that can interfere with throwing weapons, magic, or damage output, and the appropriate cures may be harder to replace than ordinary healing supplies.

When you are near a rest point, returning to recover can be more economical than spending rare consumables. Deeper inside a dungeon, keep enough recovery options to escape a dangerous encounter.
