<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Get **Endu** from the shrine. Collect the **Azure Torch** near the Tower of Knowledge and the **second Wind Gem** above the opening region. Then use two Wind Dashes to cross the western broken bridge.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The huge door near the starting camp leads toward Deimpo Mausoleum, but that is not your first destination. Queen's Domain lets you see locations long before you have the movement abilities or items needed to enter them.

Your early route begins with Endu, the Azure Torch, and a second Wind Gem. Light the first two Gargoyle beacons, cross the western bridge, and clear Obmuer Temple to reach the third. With all three bells rung, return to the Tower of Knowledge and open the path toward Deimpo Mausoleum.

<h2 id="after-shrine">
  Where Should You Go After the Shrine Guardian?
</h2>

After defeating the Shrine Guardian and claiming the Holy Sword Endu, **head east rather than trying to enter Deimpo Mausoleum**.

Endu starts with one Wind Gem, allowing a single Wind Dash. That is sufficient to reach the eastern bridge toward the Tower of Knowledge, where your next important item is waiting.

If you have not obtained Endu, complete the <Link href="/queens-domain/isle-of-neasied-walkthrough">Shrine Key and Shrine Guardian route</Link> before continuing.

### Get the Azure Torch Near the Tower of Knowledge

Cross the eastern bridge using Wind Dash and approach the Tower of Knowledge area.

Find the Gargoyle statue holding the **Azure Torch** and collect it. You need this torch to ignite the island's Gargoyle beacons and ring the bells that eventually open the Tower.

<h2 id="second-wind-gem">
  Where Do You Go to Unlock Two Wind Dashes?
</h2>

Return to the first Crystal Monument, climb the hillside, and use Wind Dash to reach the higher save point. The **second Wind Gem** is on an elevated platform nearby.

Collect it before attempting the western broken bridge. You need **two consecutive Wind Dashes** to reach the other side. For the climb between the pillars and the gem's exact pickup position, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">second Wind Gem route</Link>.

<h2 id="three-gargoyle-bells">
  Where Are All Three Gargoyle Beacons in Queen's Domain?
</h2>

The Tower of Knowledge opens after you ignite **three Gargoyle statues** using the Azure Torch. Each activation causes a bell to ring.

Two statues are accessible around the elevated opening region. The third is reached after clearing Obmuer Temple.

You can activate the first two before crossing the western bridge.

### First Gargoyle — Above the Pillars and Broken Stairway

Return to the pillars near the route you used to obtain the second Wind Gem.

Climb onto the rocks again, but this time look for a **small blue lantern** above the route. Use it as a landmark, then dash across toward the broken stairway.

Climb to the top, defeat the skeletons guarding the area, and find the Gargoyle statue.

### Second Gargoyle — North of the Upper Elevator

The second beacon is accessed from another elevated section above the first Crystal Monument.

Return toward the path you used to reach the higher save point. With two Wind Dashes available, you can now explore the upper area near that crossing.

Follow the elevated path until you find an **elevator connecting the upper level to the ground**.

From the elevator area, wrap around toward the **north**. You will find the second Gargoyle statue near a locked gate.

**Leave the nearby locked gate alone for now.** Lighting the second Gargoyle does not grant immediate access through every locked passage in the area.

You have completed the two beacons available before Obmuer Temple. The third is on the other side of the next main dungeon.

### Third Gargoyle — After Dagoom in Obmuer Temple

The final Gargoyle is found along the route **out of Obmuer Temple after defeating Dagoom the Sea King**.

Leave the dungeon after the boss fight, locate the statue, and ignite it with the Azure Torch.

A nearby fast-travel connection returns you toward the opening region, close to the second Gargoyle and its previously locked gate.

For the exact exit route and statue location, use the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Obmuer Temple walkthrough</Link>.

With all three bells rung, the Tower of Knowledge becomes accessible. You can now return there rather than searching for additional statues.

<h2 id="western-bridge">
  How Do You Cross the Western Broken Bridge?
</h2>

Once you have the **second Wind Gem**, head back toward the eastern section of the starting region where you encountered the centipede-like enemies.

From there, move toward the broken bridge leading west across a large gap.

You need **two consecutive Wind Dashes** to reach the other side. Attempting this crossing with just the Wind Gem that came installed in Endu will leave you short.

Land on the opposite side, then activate the **Crystal Monument** before exploring the flooded ruins.

The next area consists of flooded ruins with skeletons, aggressive fish, and broken stonework. This is the approach to Obmuer Temple.

Do not confuse reaching this region with opening the dungeon itself. The temple door has a separate requirement.

<h2 id="obmuer-temple">
  What Do You Need to Complete Before Leaving Obmuer Temple?
</h2>

Collect **four Triangular Slates** in the flooded ruins to open Obmuer Temple. Inside, your main objectives are to obtain the third Wind Gem, pass the required Trident Key locks, defeat **three Royal Guards**, and reach **Dagoom the Sea King**.

Only **two Trident Keys** are required for the main route. Do not delay your progress searching for the optional keys that become available through other activities.

For the slate locations, locked passages, Royal Guards, and Dagoom's fight, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Obmuer Temple walkthrough</Link>. After defeating Dagoom, continue along the exit route to light the final island Gargoyle.

<h2 id="tower-after-temple">
  Where Should You Go After Defeating Dagoom?
</h2>

Once the third Gargoyle has been lit, use the nearby fast-travel connection to return toward the opening region. Head back to the **Tower of Knowledge** and enter its now-accessible interior.

Inside, solve the numbered Gargoyle puzzle, recover the **Storm Gem**, and find **Budaen** to obtain the Forbidden Archives Key. Your main target is **the Necronomicon** beyond the Archives.

The Tower's numbered statues are separate from the three island beacons you have already activated. Follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge walkthrough</Link> for the correct statue numbers, rotating staircase, key route, and boss fight.

<h2 id="deimpo-mausoleum">
  How Do You Unlock Deimpo Mausoleum?
</h2>

After defeating **the Necronomicon**, return to the blockage near **Budaen** and clear it with the newly acquired **Acid Vial** ability. The passage leads toward **Deimpo Mausoleum**.

Follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower exit route</Link> for the exact obstruction and the shortcut connecting back toward the starting camp.

<h2 id="wrong-area">
  How Can You Tell If You Have Reached an Area Too Early?
</h2>

Queen's Domain allows you to find tougher regions well before the main progression leads there. A sudden jump in enemy difficulty can mean you have taken an unintended route rather than simply needing to fight better.

One useful indicator is the **Bestiary**.

Defeat an enemy in the unfamiliar area and check where its entry appears. Enemy entries are numbered, so a creature appearing far beyond the enemies you have already encountered, with many empty entries in between, is a sign that you may have reached a later region.

This is not an absolute rule. A newly accessible dungeon can still be harder than the previous one. However, it provides a practical check when you find yourself surrounded by enemies that seem disproportionately powerful.

Before committing to a difficult new route, ask three questions:

- **Do you have enough Wind Dashes?** A distant platform may require another Wind Gem rather than a different jump.
- **Is a specific key or item missing?** Locked passages and resistant obstacles often become accessible after another major encounter.
- **Does the Bestiary suggest much later enemies?** A large gap in entry numbers can indicate that you should explore another region first.

I would prioritize an unfamiliar path that has a clear unlock requirement over one that only contains enemies far stronger than anything encountered so far. Defeating ordinary enemies for experience is unlikely to compensate for a missing movement ability or key item.

<h2 id="when-to-backtrack">
  When Should You Return to Previously Explored Areas?
</h2>

Return to an earlier area when you gain a new way to reach something that was previously inaccessible. A locked door or distant platform does not always mean you missed a nearby switch.

Three signs make a return trip worthwhile:

- **More movement charges:** A previously unreachable platform may now be within Wind Dash range.
- **A new key or weapon upgrade:** A door or optional reward may become accessible without clearing the entire dungeon again.
- **A new obstacle-clearing ability:** Barriers that resisted ordinary attacks may now have a specific solution.

Prioritize returning for passages that lead to the next required area. Optional treasure can wait until you have the necessary items and enough healing supplies for another exploration trip.

Before retracing a long route, check whether a nearby Crystal Monument or newly opened shortcut gives you a faster way back.
