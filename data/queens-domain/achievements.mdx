## Which Locations and Bosses Have Achievements in Queen's Domain?

Queen's Domain has **10 location achievements and eight boss achievements**. They are different kinds of milestones: reaching a dungeon is not the same as defeating the enemy guarding its final route.

Three important early location milestones are:

- **Ocean Walk:** Reach Obmuer Temple.
- **Endless Pages:** Reach the Tower of Knowledge.
- **Unsealed Tomb:** Reach Deimpo Mausoleum.

These locations are connected by the island's main progression, but they do not unlock at the same time. If your next destination is still inaccessible, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/progression-guide">area order and three Gargoyle beacon route</Link> before returning.

### Dagoom and the Necronomicon

**Treacherous Baleen** unlocks after defeating Dagoom the Sea King. The fight is reached inside Obmuer Temple, beyond the Royal Guards. Use the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Temple route and Dagoom fight instructions</Link> if the boss passage is still blocked.

**Hell Transcribed** requires defeating the Necronomicon in the Tower's Forbidden Archives. Reaching the Tower only unlocks Endless Pages; you still need to solve its internal puzzle and obtain the key that opens the Archives.

After the Necronomicon falls, the Acid Vial also gives you a way to open passages that ordinary throwing weapons could not clear.

### Boss Achievements That Name Two Enemies

Pay particular attention to three later boss requirements:

- **Demonic Servitude:** Poenir and Duenir.
- **Fallen Angels:** Yuumed and Woyeki.
- **Wake From Dreams:** Usunha and Wujomiod.

Each achievement names two enemies. Keep the pair together when checking your completion progress rather than assuming defeating one named opponent has finished the entire requirement.

The checklist also includes **Sworn Protector**, **Beneath Rotten Waters**, and **Dread Silver**, each tied to its own named boss.

Reaching the later regions is a separate objective. Their achievement descriptions do not establish a mandatory order between every destination, so follow the areas and paths actually accessible from your current progression.

## Which Weapons, Accessories, and Items Must You Collect?

**Extensive Armaments** requires collecting every Main Weapon, while **Perfected Weaponry** requires upgrading every Main Weapon. Finding the last weapon does not finish both challenges.

Keep the other equipment collections distinct as well. **Versatile Volley** counts Throwing Weapon types, **Eclectic Accoutrements** requires every Accessory, and **Glimmering Treasures** requires every Treasure.

Some upgrades also affect exploration. For example, the Trident's progression is connected to an optional key in Obmuer Temple. Its exact requirement and use are covered in the <Link href="/queens-domain/obmuer-temple-walkthrough">Trident Key instructions</Link>.

The two four-item collections should also be kept separate: **Magic Eyes** requires four lenses for the Mask of Second Sight, while **Overflowing Energy** requires four Energy Cells. Finding four of one type does not advance the other objective.

### The Akunra Forging Requirement

**Blade Bathed in Holy Light** specifically requires forging the True Holy Sword Akunra.

Endu's weapon levels, Wind Gems, and Storm Gem affect different aspects of the sword. Do not treat ordinary weapon leveling as proof that the legendary forging objective has been completed.

The <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu instructions</Link> explain the distinctions between its damage progression, movement upgrades, and Storm Gem ability.

## What Do Feel the Wind and Raging Tempest Require?

**Feel the Wind** unlocks after collecting all **five Wind Gems**. Endu starts with one installed, and the three available during the early island and Obmuer Temple progression are not enough to complete the requirement.

Track the remaining gems separately from Endu's weapon levels. The <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu instructions</Link> cover the confirmed pickup routes and how additional gems affect movement.

**Raging Tempest** has a different requirement: recover the **Storm Gem** in the Tower of Knowledge.

The Storm Gem adds an offensive capability to Endu; it does not count as one of the five Wind Gems. A player who has recovered the Storm Gem may still have unfinished movement upgrades.

## Which NPC Quests Should You Track From the Beginning?

Several achievements require bringing collected objects back to a particular character or completing an interaction after making progress elsewhere.

These are worth recording when you first meet the NPC. Returning to a familiar location later is much easier when you know why you are going back.

Several NPC requests remain unfinished even after you have found the relevant item or met the character. Return to the correct NPC to complete delivery and reporting objectives, and check the achievement after the interaction.

For activities involving the grimoire, a mysterious ritual, or Ossi's experiment, keep the objective unfinished until its required event actually occurs. Finding the associated location or NPC alone does not establish completion.

The Petrified Garden has a similar distinction: **Memorial Sculpture** requires arriving there, while **Living Stone** requires waking it. Reaching the Garden alone does not satisfy the latter.

### Umbada — Duck Figurines and 1,000 Ancient Coins

Umbada's Trading Outpost has two independent achievements:

- **A Fine Collection:** Bring every duck figurine to Umbada.
- **Let's Make a Deal:** Spend **1,000 Ancient Coins** at the outpost.

A duck figurine found in a dungeon is not enough for the first objective until it has been delivered. Likewise, holding 1,000 Ancient Coins is not the same as spending them.

Use purchases that actually improve your inventory or combat preparation while building toward the spending requirement. There is no need to assume the final achievement requires spending the entire amount in one transaction; its description specifies a total spent.

When you encounter a duck figurine, make a note to return to Umbada after your next convenient travel connection.

Meal Tickets are collectible numbered items associated with Chef Gagi's food service. They can appear during normal exploration, including relatively early areas.

### Knowledge Keeper Wourha — How Do You Advance Her Research?

Speak with **Knowledge Keeper Wourha** inside the Tower of Knowledge.

Her research focuses on cataloguing the creatures found across the isle. She asks you to report the beasts you encounter, then return after making more discoveries to continue her work.

### Sparkling Clean — Return to the Tower After the Necronomicon

**Sparkling Clean** requires cleaning up the Tower of Knowledge. This is separate from reaching the Tower or defeating its boss.

After defeating the Necronomicon and obtaining the Acid Vial, return to the Tower's previously blocked areas. The <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge walkthrough</Link> explains how to remove its fleshy obstructions.

Opening the required exit is not proof that **Sparkling Clean** is complete. Check the achievement status after clearing additional affected areas rather than marking it finished when you first leave the Tower.

## Which Achievements Require Every Chest, Secret Door, and Travel Point?

Three achievements require island-wide exploration:

- **Well Travelled:** Activate every Crystal Monument and Wing Altar.
- **Concealed Passages:** Open every secret door.
- **Emptied Coffers:** Open every chest.

A fourth related objective, **Forgotten Rooms**, requires exploring every treasure vault.

These are separate goals. Opening a hidden door does not guarantee that you have opened every chest behind it, and activating a Crystal Monument does not replace an unvisited Wing Altar.

The easiest way to reduce later backtracking is to deal with accessible objectives while they are in front of you. Activate travel points, inspect unusual walls, and look through side rooms before leaving a dungeon.

One early example is the unusual wall in the starting Crypt. You can interact with it to reveal an optional passage; the exact location is in the <Link href="/queens-domain/isle-of-neasied-walkthrough">opening Crypt walkthrough</Link>.

Other passages may require a later key, another Wind Dash, or an obstacle-clearing item. When you cannot proceed, remember the area rather than spending healing supplies repeatedly attempting something your current equipment cannot open.

Keep a separate record of treasure vaults. **Forgotten Rooms** should not be marked complete solely because you have finished the secret-door or chest achievements.

## How Do You Track All Four Queen's Domain Endings?

There are **four ending achievements**, and each corresponds to a different ending number:

- **Ending 1:** Most Unfortunate
- **Ending 2:** A Kingdom Free From Darkness
- **Ending 3:** God Slayer
- **Ending 4:** Eat

Record each ending as you actually unlock it. Finishing one version of the story does not satisfy the other three ending achievements.

Before committing to a major late-game decision, check whether you can preserve a separate save. The achievement descriptions do not establish which decisions lead to each ending, whether a completed save can revisit the relevant choice, or whether all four endings are possible without replaying earlier content.

Keeping a save before a major irreversible-looking decision is a precaution, not a guarantee that every ending can be obtained from that point.

## What Should You Finish Before Attempting 100% Completion?

A useful way to finish the achievement list is to work in several passes instead of demanding every optional reward on the first visit to each region.

**First pass — follow the main route.** Collect Endu, complete the early movement requirements, reach Obmuer Temple, defeat Dagoom, and unlock the Tower of Knowledge. Track the location and boss achievements as they appear.

**Second pass — return with new abilities.** Once the Acid Vial and additional movement upgrades are available, revisit doors, ledges, and fleshy obstructions that previously blocked exploration. Activate any travel points you missed along the way.

**Third pass — complete collections and deliveries.** Review Main Weapons and their upgrades separately. Track all five Wind Gems, four Mask Lenses, four Energy Cells, Meal Tickets, duck figurines, and the other NPC requests by their individual requirements.

**Final pass — check exploration and endings.** Pay attention to every chest, secret door, treasure vault, Crystal Monument, and Wing Altar. Keep the four endings separate from normal boss completion.

The final achievement, **Queen's Domain**, is described as overcoming every challenge on the Isle of Neasied. Its description does not provide an independent pickup location or boss, so leave it as a final completion check while working through the remaining achievements.

Use the checklist to identify what is unfinished before beginning another long trip. A specific missing collection or travel point gives you a better next objective than exploring an already cleared dungeon without knowing what you still need.
