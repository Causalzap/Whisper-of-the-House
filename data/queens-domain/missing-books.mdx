<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Return **six specific books** to the numbered library shelves inside the **Tower of Knowledge**. The correct order is **The Great Work IV – Rubedo (I)**, **The History of Dragons (II)**, **Dams and Weirs (III)**, **Upim's Fairy Tales (IV)**, **The Old Ones Beyond (V)**, and **Koko's Sapphic Adventure (VI)**. Rubedo and Upim's Fairy Tales require trips outside the Tower, while The History of Dragons comes from **Knowledge Keeper Wourha** after enough Bestiary progress. Check every title before placing it; collecting unrelated books does not finish the puzzle.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The library notice lists six subjects, but not the titles you must return. Other collectible books can look like suitable matches without completing the task.

**The Great Work IV – Rubedo** and **Upim's Fairy Tales** are hidden outside the Tower, while **The History of Dragons** comes from Knowledge Keeper Wourha. Check the books already in your inventory before searching another part of the island.

<h2 id="library-quest">
  Where Do You Return the Six Missing Books?
</h2>

The return point is the **library inside the Tower of Knowledge**.

After reaching the library, examine the **Missing Books** notice. It lists six numbered subject sections and explains that the **Rare Artifact Storage** will remain closed until the missing books have been returned.

The six sections use Roman numerals, from **I to VI**. Each subject corresponds to one particular book.

You can begin investigating this puzzle while exploring the Tower. You do not need to wait until you have defeated the Necronomicon before reading the notice or collecting books.

If you have not reached the library, follow the <Link href="/queens-domain/tower-of-knowledge-walkthrough">Tower of Knowledge walkthrough</Link> for the entrance, rotating staircase, elevator, and Forbidden Archives route.

Once you find the notice, I would check the book titles already in your inventory before leaving to search elsewhere. This avoids revisiting an area for a book you picked up earlier without realizing it was needed.

<h2 id="book-locations">
  Where Do You Find All Six Missing Books?
</h2>

The most efficient approach is to collect the books accessible around the Tower first, speak to Wourha whenever you have made Bestiary progress, and save the two longer trips outside the Tower for when you are ready to explore those routes.

You can collect the books in a different order. The Roman numerals identify their destination shelves, not a mandatory pickup sequence.

<h3 id="rubedo">
  I — Where Is The Great Work IV – Rubedo?
</h3>

**Category:** Alchemic Recipes

**Location:** A cave below the Cemetery on the starting island.

This book is not in the Tower of Knowledge.

To find it:

1. Return to the starting island and travel toward the **Cemetery**.
2. Search the cliffside below the burial area rather than staying on the upper Cemetery path.
3. Look for a cave entrance with an **egg outside**.
4. Enter the cave and follow the stairs down toward the lower chamber.
5. Locate the central pedestal holding a throwable stone cube.
6. Look for the altar to the right of that central item. The book is on the altar.

The altar is the important final landmark. Finding the cave is only the first step; do not leave after examining the throwable item in the middle.

If the altar is out of reach from your current position, use the height of the stairs or central pedestal and your available aerial Dashes to cross the gap.

After collecting **The Great Work IV – Rubedo**, take it back to **Shelf I — Alchemic Recipes**.

<h3 id="history-dragons">
  II — How Do You Get The History of Dragons?
</h3>

**Category:** Fantastical Creatures

**Location:** Reward from Knowledge Keeper Wourha inside the Tower of Knowledge.


Unlike the other books hidden inside rooms and caves, **The History of Dragons** is given to you by **Knowledge Keeper Wourha** in the upper Tower of Knowledge.

Speak with her, report the creatures you have discovered, and return after making further Bestiary progress. Once she awards the book, place it on **Shelf II — Fantastical Creatures**.

If she acknowledges your discoveries but does not give you the book, see <Link href="#wourha-dragons">why Wourha's research reward is still locked</Link>.

<h3 id="dams-weirs">
  III — Where Is Dams and Weirs?
</h3>

**Category:** Construction Techniques

**Location:** Tower of Knowledge, beside the teleporter.

This is one of the easiest books to overlook because it sits low on a bookshelf rather than in an obvious chest.

Return to the **Tower of Knowledge teleporter** and examine the adjacent bookcase.

Focus on the **bottom shelf**. Move close enough to inspect the books at that height and collect **Dams and Weirs**.

If you have searched the Tower several times without finding it, there is little reason to repeat every upper-floor corridor first. Recheck the shelves beside the teleporter, including the lowest row.

Return **Dams and Weirs** to **Shelf III — Construction Techniques**.

<h3 id="upims-fairy-tales">
  IV — Where Is Upim's Fairy Tales?
</h3>

**Category:** Fairy Tales

**Location:** A hidden cave on the Obmuer Temple side of the island, near the raiders' shipwreck route.

This is the book most likely to require a separate exploration trip and careful movement.

The entrance lies around the water-covered region associated with **Obmuer Temple**. Look toward the side passages near the raiders' shipwreck rather than searching the Tower's library shelves.

To reach the book:

1. Travel toward the Obmuer Temple side of the island.
2. Find the route across the water and cliffs leading toward a hidden cave entrance.
3. Approach from the right-hand side and use **three aerial Dashes** to reach the opposite side where the entrance is accessible.
4. Continue through the dark cave, preparing for the enemies inside.
5. Follow the passage until you reach a chamber containing a **large opening in the floor** and a giant tree below.
6. Drop into the opening, but **use Wind Dash to reach one of the upper ledges** before falling too far.
7. Follow the ledge around until you find a skeleton and a food pickup. **Upim's Fairy Tales** is nearby.

The crucial step is **the first landing after dropping into the hole**.

Do not treat the shaft as a normal descent where you can safely fall to the bottom and work your way back up. The book route uses a ledge above the lowest level.

Before jumping, identify the landing platform and plan where to Dash. If you fall past the correct ledge, you may need to return through another route or use an available travel item to reset your approach.

If you do not yet have enough Wind Dash charges to reach the entrance comfortably, collect the earlier Wind Gems first. The <Link href="/queens-domain/endu-holy-sword">Holy Sword Endu guide</Link> explains where to get the second and third Wind Gems.

Once you have the book, return it to **Shelf IV — Fairy Tales**.

<h3 id="old-ones-beyond">
  V — Where Is The Old Ones Beyond?
</h3>

**Category:** Eldritch Horror

**Location:** Behind a hidden wall in the Tower of Knowledge, near an upper entrance to the library rooms.

This book is associated with **Archivist Inha**.

Search the upper part of the library area for a concealed passage above the entrance to one of the rooms. Inside, you encounter a skeleton.

Defeat the skeleton and inspect the area around its remains.

The important confirmation is **Bones of Archivist Inha**. The recovered book is **The Old Ones Beyond**, which describes strange places, beings, and rituals beyond ordinary understanding.

<h3 id="kokos-adventures">
  VI — Where Is Koko's Sapphic Adventure?
</h3>

**Category:** Queer Romance

**Location:** An upper room of the Tower of Knowledge, around the section where you meet Wourha.

This book is found in the Tower rather than in an outside cave.

Explore the upper rooms near Knowledge Keeper Wourha and inspect the books available in that part of the library.

You are looking for **Koko's Sapphic Adventure**, an illustrated book about the heroine Kotaro Cuko and her encounter with a vampire.

It is a collectible book, not a reward for completing Wourha's entire Bestiary research.

That distinction matters because another required book, **The History of Dragons**, does come from Wourha's research dialogue.

After finding Koko's Sapphic Adventure, place it in **Shelf VI — Queer Romance**.

<h2 id="wourha-dragons">
  Why Won't Wourha Give You The History of Dragons?
</h2>

If Wourha talks to you but does not hand over The History of Dragons, the most likely problem is that your Bestiary reporting has not reached the relevant milestone.

There are two useful stages to distinguish:

1. **Early report:** Wourha acknowledges your discoveries but says her research still needs more progress.
2. **Later report:** After you have encountered and catalogued more creatures, she gives you The History of Dragons and tells you to return it to its proper shelf.

You should not keep searching Wourha's room for a copy of the book when she has not yet awarded it.

Instead, return to exploration, encounter additional creatures, and speak with her again.

Wourha also encourages investigating caves beneath the island. Those side areas can help fill out the Bestiary while you are collecting other missing books.

Once you receive The History of Dragons, check your inventory before returning to the six shelves.

The item belongs under **Fantastical Creatures**. Receiving it does not mean every other library book has been found.

<h2 id="wrong-books">
  Which Books Do Not Belong in the Six-Shelf Puzzle?
</h2>

One of the most frustrating situations is having several collectible books and assuming the library puzzle should already be solvable.

There are other readable and collectible books throughout Queen's Domain.

Two books that can cause confusion are **The Ratty Bug Compendium** and **The Rotting God**.

Although their subjects may appear suitable for the library's categories, neither is one of the six books listed in the required collection.

If the shelves are not progressing, check the exact book titles in your inventory rather than repeatedly rearranging every book you own.

A missing required title cannot be replaced by another book from the same genre.

<h2 id="rare-artifact-storage">
  Why Is Rare Artifact Storage Still Locked?
</h2>

The library notice states that **Rare Artifact Storage** will reopen after all the missing books are returned.

If you have placed several books and the storage remains inaccessible, check the collection before searching for another key.

### Check 1: Do You Have the Correct Six Titles?

Use the table above and compare each title exactly.

Do not count unrelated books just because they fit one of the six genres.

If you are missing **The Great Work IV – Rubedo** or **Upim's Fairy Tales**, remember that both are outside the Tower.

If you are missing **The History of Dragons**, return to Wourha rather than searching another bookshelf.

### Check 2: Is Each Book in the Correct Numbered Section?

Compare every placed book with the <Link href="#book-order">six-shelf order table</Link> above.

Each book must match its numbered subject section. If a shelf contains the wrong title, correct that placement before checking the storage entrance again.

Do not treat an occupied shelf as proof that the required book has been returned.

### Check 3: Are You Mixing Up Different Book Puzzles?

The Tower's six-category library puzzle is separate from the book arrangement puzzle encountered later in the **Record of Sin**.

The library requires books about subjects such as alchemy, creatures, and romance. Record of Sin instead uses books associated with different deities.

Completing one puzzle does not mean you have already solved the other.

### Check 4: Have You Checked the Storage Again?

After returning all six books to the matching sections, revisit the restricted Rare Artifact Storage entrance and check whether it can now be opened.

There is no reason to repeat the Necronomicon boss fight merely because the library's collection is incomplete.

The Tower's main dungeon progression and its optional library collection are separate objectives.

<h2 id="late-fee-achievement">
  How Do You Unlock I'm Not Paying the Late Fee?
</h2>

**I'm Not Paying the Late Fee** requires returning all lost books to the Tower of Knowledge library.

Collecting the books is not enough. Place the six required titles in their matching subject sections and complete the library's return objective.

After returning the collection, check your achievement status and revisit Rare Artifact Storage to see whether its restriction has been lifted.

This achievement is separate from **A Lifetime's Work**, which requires completing Wourha's research, and **Sparkling Clean**, which involves cleaning the Tower.

For the other achievements and a completion tracker, use the <Link href="/queens-domain/achievements">Queen's Domain achievements checklist</Link>.
