**We Were Here Tomorrow** begins with two Explorers waking inside a Norček Industries facility after nearly five years beneath the Antarctic ice. Director Kathleen Bowe wants them to continue the work of a missing research team, but neither Explorer has all the information needed to operate the machinery alone.

The journey moves from rehabilitation and experimental equipment into Exotic Matter research, particle acceleration, laser systems, and rooms where gravity no longer behaves normally. Progress depends on recognizing which part of a machine your partner can operate, rather than trying every control visible on your own screen.

## We Were Here Tomorrow: Where Do You Go Next?

Your current room is the most useful clue when deciding where to continue. The laboratory introduces several similar-looking control panels, access cards, and power mechanisms, but they belong to different stages of the route.

Follow these landmarks to identify your position:

- **Wellness Check:** Leave the rehabilitation area, complete the cooperative console interactions, and progress through the opening machinery.
- **Shrinking Laboratory:** Use the size-changing equipment to access miniature circuitry and complete the laboratory work.
- **Floppy Disk and 3D Printer:** Pass the miniature computer barriers and obtain the Green Key Card needed to leave the earlier research area.
- **Prototype Testing:** Gain access to the Hook Gun and Goop Gun, then use their different functions to operate the connected mechanisms.
- **Exotic Matter Laboratory and Cargo Elevator:** Restore access through the elevator machinery and collect the required access cards.
- **Particle Accelerator and Magnetic Stabilizer:** Restore the relevant machinery, using both Explorers' tools and positions to activate the connected systems.
- **Laser Calibration:** Complete the crystal-based laser equipment and open the route beyond the laboratory.
- **Gravitonics:** Navigate the rotating rooms, inverted platforms, and colored passages until the next section becomes accessible.

These are progression landmarks, not interchangeable rooms. Finishing one machine does not automatically complete every device connected to the same corridor.

The two Green Key Card objectives are particularly easy to confuse. The earlier card comes from the miniature laboratory sequence, whereas the Cargo Elevator has another access-card requirement much later. Returning to the first shrinking machine will not resolve a missing card in the elevator area.

For the actual console inputs, resin recipe, circuit component combinations, Fuse retrieval, laser configuration, Six Rings symbols, and gravity-room route, follow the [We Were Here Tomorrow puzzle walkthrough](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough) from the landmark where you are currently stuck.

## Can You Play We Were Here Tomorrow Alone?

**No. We Were Here Tomorrow requires exactly two players in online co-op.** Both players need an internet connection and a working microphone.

Each Explorer receives a different perspective and a different tool. Even when both characters enter the same large room, the controls, accessible passages, or puzzle information may differ between them.

The game supports crossplay between PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, so partners do not have to use the same platform.

You can also start here without finishing the earlier We Were Here games. The stories are connected, but each game is designed as a standalone cooperative adventure.

### Decide who reads and who operates

The Explorer holding the relevant instructions is not necessarily the one who should press the buttons.

For the first few interactions, agree on a simple communication pattern:

1. Identify the current device and its visible markings.
2. Have the Explorer with the instructions read the complete sequence.
3. Let the Explorer at the controls repeat the sequence before activating it.

This becomes increasingly useful once both players begin using separate tools. A description such as "the upper button" is unreliable when one player is standing upside down or viewing the mechanism from another room.

Use colors, wire connections, distinctive objects, and the orientation of the actual device instead.

## What Should You Do When the Walkie-Talkie Cuts Out?

Some of the longer cooperative puzzles become difficult to coordinate if one Explorer hears only the beginning of the other's instructions.

Before retrying a failed sequence, stop interacting with the device and test communication in both directions. Have one player speak a short sequence of numbers while the other repeats it back, then reverse roles.

Check the configured talk controls and avoid speaking over each other. If the signal is still cutting out, check both players' voice settings and restore reliable communication before attempting another sequence.

For symbol puzzles, do not assume silence means your partner has completed the input. Wait for an explicit confirmation that the current symbol or control has registered.

This matters most when a wrong input resets the puzzle. A communication failure can look like a wrong solution even when both players have correctly understood the required symbols.

## Hook Gun or Goop Gun: Which Tool Should You Use?

Once the Explorers leave the miniature laboratory, their equipment changes how they navigate later rooms.

The two prototype weapons solve different kinds of obstacles:

- **Hook Gun:** Connects to compatible targets and can pull or grapple, making it useful for distant mechanisms and movement between otherwise inaccessible positions.
- **Goop Gun:** Produces solid cubes. These can provide platforms, operate pressure-based mechanisms, and add weight to another Explorer.

The distinction becomes important when a doorway is connected to several differently colored wires.

A reachable hook target is not necessarily a place to generate a cube, and a pressure plate cannot always be activated by standing on it normally.

The Goop Gun can also affect your partner directly. That interaction becomes essential when a mechanism requires more weight than one unmodified Explorer can provide.

Only one deployed cube may be available at a time. When a new cube cannot be created, recall the existing one before trying again.

These tools become more important as the facility progresses from ordinary door mechanisms to moving platforms and altered gravity.

## Which Achievements Should You Watch For?

We Were Here Tomorrow has **37 achievements**, including story milestones, optional prototype interactions, and collectibles.

Several can be recognized through normal progress. Others require doing something differently from the straightforward route, so it helps to decide whether you are pursuing optional objectives before activating a major piece of machinery.

### The Cargo Elevator has an alternative objective

**Many Ways** requires activating the Cargo Elevator **without using a Fuse**.

That is a different condition from the normal Fuse-assisted route. Using the Fuse to activate the elevator does not satisfy the achievement's requirement.

For a straightforward first run, the Fuse route allows the Explorers to continue without making the optional challenge a prerequisite for progression.

### Try the partner interactions after receiving the prototypes

Two achievements are tied directly to using your equipment on the other Explorer:

- **Goop Lookin'** — Goop your partner.
- **Now I'm Hooked** — Hook your partner.

Once both tools are available, these interactions are easy to recognize and do not require reaching the later research areas.

**You're Chatty for a Statue** has a more specific requirement: use the Goop Gun to lock your partner in place. Simply generating an ordinary cube elsewhere is not the same condition.

### Keep an eye out for the plushies

**Class 1 Hoarder** requires collecting all unique plushies.

The plushies are not part of the machinery requirements for completing the main laboratory tasks. Players aiming for achievements should examine accessible side areas rather than assuming every object in an optional room is irrelevant.

A plushie collection objective is separate from the main route's access cards, Fuses, and other puzzle equipment.

## Is Gravitonics the End of We Were Here Tomorrow?

**No. Reaching the rotating gravity rooms does not mean the adventure is finished.**

After the laboratory and Gravitonics stages, the larger journey continues through objectives associated with **R&D Headquarters**, the **Temporal Research Centre**, and eventually **Rift Zero**.

The later progression includes restoring power to research facilities and continuing the expedition beyond Norček's earlier experimental areas.

The main objective remains completing the work needed to deal with the unstable rift, not simply escaping the first laboratory.

If you have just finished the inverted rooms, continue through the passage opened by the final activated mechanism and follow the next set of separated-room objectives.

Do not mistake the transition into another research section for a completed ending.

The late-game achievement objectives also distinguish between securing Rift Zero and closing it. Those are separate completion conditions; completing an earlier laboratory milestone does not establish either result.
