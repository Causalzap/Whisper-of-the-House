**We Were Here Tomorrow** begins with two Explorers waking inside a Norček Industries facility after nearly five years beneath the Antarctic ice. Director Kathleen Bowe needs them to continue the work of a missing research team, but neither Explorer has all the information required to operate the machinery alone.

The journey starts with rehabilitation and miniature laboratory equipment, then moves through experimental weapons, particle acceleration, gravity-shifting rooms, and increasingly complex research facilities. Later puzzles introduce different time periods, plant cultivation, and recorded actions that affect subsequent attempts.

## We Were Here Tomorrow: Where Do You Go Next?

The easiest way to find your next objective is to identify the room or machinery in front of you. Several stages use similar access cards, electrical connections, and paired controls, but they belong to different parts of the facility.

The main route follows these landmarks:

- **Wellness Check and Opening Consoles:** Complete the rehabilitation sequence, match your partner's symbols to the controls, and transfer the container between rooms.
- **Shrinking Laboratory:** Use the size-changing chamber to reach miniature circuitry, match the components, and prepare the 3D printing resin.
- **Floppy Disk Doors and 3D Printer:** Align the rotating direction indicators, complete the printer's light grid, and retrieve the first Green Key Card.
- **Prototype Testing:** Obtain the Hook Gun and Goop Gun, then use their different functions to operate connected devices.
- **Cargo Elevator:** Retrieve the required access cards and restore the elevator machinery before entering the next research area.
- **Particle Accelerator and Magnetic Stabilizer:** Complete the symbol controls and use the prototype equipment to activate the connected systems.
- **Laser Calibration and Six Rings:** Select the correct crystals, establish the laser path, and match the ring symbols to open the next passage.
- **Gravitonics:** Navigate the rotating red floor, blue Beta passage, and inverted purple corridors.
- **R&D Headquarters:** Restore the numbered power network, open the connected electrical routes, and obtain the upgraded prototype equipment.
- **Temporal Research Centre:** Coordinate the different versions of the facility, operate the time-exchange devices, and restore access using the Fuse machinery.
- **Xenobotany:** Cultivate plants under the required conditions so their later growth creates new routes and interactions.
- **Time-Rewind Machinery:** Use actions established in earlier cycles to complete the later timed crossing.
- **Xenobotany Power Restoration:** Revisit the plant-related machinery and satisfy the connected electrical and environmental conditions.
- **Rift Zero:** Activate the final apparatus and decide how to handle the rift.

Two locations are particularly easy to confuse. The first **Green Key Card** comes from the miniature laboratory's printer sequence; the Cargo Elevator has a separate access-card requirement. Likewise, the Cargo Elevator Fuse and the later Temporal Research Centre Fuse arrangement are different puzzles.

If your current mechanism is not registering, use the [We Were Here Tomorrow walkthrough](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough) for the corresponding room. It covers the component combinations, resin recipe, laser crystal selections, R&D connections, Fuse arrangement, and later cooperative puzzles.

## Can You Play We Were Here Tomorrow Alone?

**No. We Were Here Tomorrow requires exactly two players in online co-op.** Both Explorers need an internet connection and a working microphone to communicate throughout the adventure.

The game supports crossplay between **PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**, so your partner does not need to be on the same platform.

You also do not need to finish the earlier We Were Here games before starting. Although the series shares a broader setting, this adventure can be played on its own.

### Decide who reads and who operates

Most of the early puzzles split information between the players. One Explorer might see a sequence of symbols, while the other can interact with the corresponding buttons.

Before entering a sequence:

1. **Identify the device.** Describe its color, markings, or connected cables.
2. **Read the complete instruction.** Agree on how to distinguish similar symbols.
3. **Repeat and confirm.** Have the operator repeat the sequence before activating it.

This becomes especially important in the rotating rooms, where "up" and "left" may mean different things depending on each Explorer's orientation.

Use the device's markings and recognizable surroundings instead of relying entirely on directions from your own viewpoint.

## What Should You Do When the Walkie-Talkie Cuts Out?

If your partner cannot hear a complete instruction, confirm that communication is working before resetting the puzzle.

Check the voice settings and talk controls on both sides. Have one Explorer read a short sequence of numbers while the other repeats it, then switch roles to confirm that both directions work.

When entering a long symbol sequence, let the operator confirm each successful input before the reader moves to the next one. This is more reliable than reading every instruction at once, especially when a wrong entry changes the symbols on the display.

If a mechanism resets unexpectedly, determine whether the previous input was rejected or whether your partner simply missed part of the instruction. The next attempt may require reading an entirely new combination.

## Hook Gun or Goop Gun: Which Tool Should You Use?

The Explorers receive different prototype weapons after the miniature laboratory. Each has a distinct role:

- **Hook Gun:** Connects to compatible targets for pulling, grappling, and interacting with objects beyond normal reach.
- **Goop Gun:** Produces solid cubes that can support movement, hold down pressure mechanisms, or add weight to another Explorer.

A door connected to multiple colored wires may require both tools. Follow the cable from each inactive indicator to determine which nearby mechanism still needs attention.

The Goop Gun also changes later in the adventure. **Before the R&D upgrade, you can deploy one cube at a time; afterward, you can keep two cubes active simultaneously.** This creates new possibilities for mechanisms that need more than one cube-controlled interaction.

A normal floor plate and a mechanism requiring a heavier Explorer are not solved in exactly the same way. The latter becomes important in the Magnetic Stabilizer.

## Which Achievements Should You Watch For?

**We Were Here Tomorrow has 37 achievements.** Most story milestones unlock as you progress through the laboratories, but several optional objectives require actions outside the normal puzzle route.

The ones worth keeping in mind are:

- **Cargo Elevator:** An optional achievement requires a different approach from the standard Fuse-assisted route.
- **Prototype weapons:** Some achievements involve using the Hook Gun or Goop Gun on your partner rather than on machinery.
- **Unique plushies:** Collectible toys appear in sleeping areas, offices, laboratories, and other places away from the main controls.
- **Rift Zero:** The two final choices lead to different completion achievements.

Use the [all 37 achievements checklist](/we-were-here-tomorrow/achievements) to track your progress, check individual unlock requirements, and identify which optional objectives remain unfinished.

You can finish the main adventure without completing every collectible or optional challenge.

## What Comes After Gravitonics?

**R&D Headquarters is the next major stage after Gravitonics.** Once the remaining gravity mechanism activates and the final passage opens, continue into the numbered power network.

The R&D machinery uses Greek-letter identifiers, terminal numbers, and interconnected power routes. You will need to restore the electrical connections before progressing toward the upgraded prototype equipment and the Temporal Research Centre.

If you have completed the blue-area mechanism but cannot find the final Gravitonics exit, return to the purple junction and check the remaining lever route rather than starting the entire gravity puzzle again.

The [Gravitonics and R&D walkthrough](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough) explains these connected objectives and the later route through Xenobotany, time rewind, and Rift Zero.
