**We Were Here Tomorrow** has **37 achievements** spanning the two-player story, prototype experiments, alternative puzzle interactions, collectibles, and the two Rift Zero outcomes. Most progression milestones are straightforward once the relevant machinery is running. The achievements to think about before leaving an area are the Cargo Elevator's alternative objective, the weapon interactions, and the plushies hidden away from the main controls.

## All 37 Achievements Checklist

Keep the ordinary story objectives separate from interactions you must deliberately perform. That way you can follow the main puzzle route without repeatedly stopping to check milestones that the story already leads you toward.

A practical completion plan is:

1. **During the first laboratories:** Perform a crouch slide, invite your co-op partner through the available friend-invite feature, and try the Hook Gun and Goop Gun interactions once unlocked.
2. **Before the Cargo Elevator:** Decide whether you want to attempt **Many Ways**. The normal Fuse route and the achievement's no-Fuse condition are different objectives.
3. **While exploring:** Inspect shelves, beds, desks, side rooms, and reachable ledges for unique plushies. Some can be carried and are not needed for the next puzzle.
4. **During R&D, TRC, and Xenobotany:** Continue the main route, checking the remaining special research objectives against the list.
5. **At Rift Zero:** Complete one ending, then use chapter selection to revisit the final choice for the other outcome.

## Which Achievements Unlock as You Progress?

The game's research areas provide the natural order for most story milestones. You do not need a separate detour for achievements such as **Corporate Exercise** (Wellness Check), **Micromanagement** (new access card), **'Morrow's Miracles** (first experimental prototype), **Onwards and Upwards** (Cargo Elevator), or **MAGNETS!** (Magnetic Stabilizer). They are tied to objectives on the main route.

The more important checkpoints later in the adventure are:

- **Any Colour You Like:** Reactivate the targeting array during the laser machinery sequence.
- **Spiraling / Relativity:** Enter Gravitonics and explore the impossible staircases.
- **Recon & Disclose / Turn It Off And On Again:** Reach the upper R&D area and restore its power network.
- **Double Time:** Obtain the upgraded Norček prototype in R&D.
- **A Matter of Time / Knowledge Transfer:** Enter the Temporal Research Centre and restore its power.
- **Forward to the Past:** Reach the TRC Construction Site.
- **Through the Looking Glass / Eternal Recurrence:** Continue beyond the Rift and encounter the time-loop machinery.

Some later milestones refer to specific equipment rather than simply entering a room. **Ensnared Potential**, for example, requires turning on the Particle Accelerator; merely reaching the Particle Accelerator Terminus is the separate **This is fine** milestone.

For the route through each machine, use the [We Were Here Tomorrow walkthrough](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough). The puzzle solutions, including the laser crystals, R&D terminal order, and TRC Fuse arrangement, belong to that continuous route rather than this achievement checklist.

## Optional Achievements to Handle Early

Two requirements do not depend on a difficult late-game puzzle:

- **Smooth Moves:** Perform a crouch slide. This is a movement action, not an achievement for simply crouching in place.
- **Referral Bonus:** Invite a friend to play We Were Here Tomorrow. Playing with an existing partner is not the same wording as sending an invitation, so use the game's available invite interaction when pursuing this achievement.

You can also handle the partner-targeting prototype interactions early, once both Explorers have acquired their weapons.

</section>

<section id="prototype-achievements" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Hook Gun and Goop Gun Achievements

These objectives involve a target that is easy to mistake for an ordinary mechanism:

- **Goop Lookin':** Hit your partner with the Goop Gun's effect.
- **Now I'm Hooked:** Use the Hook Gun on your partner.
- **You're Chatty for a Statue:** Use the Goop Gun to **lock your partner in place**. Producing a loose cube or hitting an unrelated pressure plate is not the specified interaction.
- **Danger! High Voltage:** Grapple to an **Energized surface** with the Hook Gun. An ordinary Hook Gun target does not satisfy the energized-surface requirement.

You do not need to carry a cube through the whole game to attempt the partner achievements. The relevant interactions become available after the prototypes are introduced; later upgrades expand cube capacity but do not replace the original weapons.

</section>

<section id="many-ways" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Many Ways — Activate the Cargo Elevator Without a Fuse

**Many Ways** has a specific condition: the Cargo Elevator must be activated **without using a Fuse**.

In the standard route, the Explorers lower the suspended Fuse, insert it into the machinery, and finish the elevator's remaining access-card requirements. That is a valid way to progress, but it does **not** fulfill this achievement's alternate condition.

If you are targeting Many Ways, do not insert the Fuse and then expect the normal elevator completion to count as a no-Fuse activation. The alternative operation must satisfy the achievement's wording. The ordinary [Cargo Elevator route](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough#cargo-elevator) is available for anyone who only wants to continue the story.

</section>

<section id="christmas-riot" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Christmas Riot — Find the Special Toy

**Christmas Riot** describes finding a highly sought-after toy, with the warning: **"Don't let your partner see it."**

This is not the same requirement as **Class 1 Hoarder**, which asks for *all unique plushies*. A toy that looks collectible should not automatically be assumed to trigger both achievements.

Inspect optional rooms and distinctive toys rather than concentrating exclusively on doors, cards, and machinery. The achievement's description identifies the target as a special toy, but it does not establish that every plushie is the required one.

</section>

## Special Research Achievements in the Later Areas

A handful of achievements have requirements more specific than reaching the next room:

- **All Grown Up:** Cultivate a **Battery Bulb**. This belongs to the plant-cultivation portion of the later journey.
- **Why So Molecular?:** Restore power to the **Theta Splice device**.
- **Essentia Emendare:** Finish the expedition team's project.
- **We Are Friends!:** Become acquainted with an alien lifeform.

These are different objectives. Completing one laboratory power network does not, by itself, establish that the other research conditions have been met. Match the stated requirement to the relevant machinery or story event and check for the unlock before crossing it off.

</section>

<section id="rift-zero-endings" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Dawn of Tomorrow and Tomorrow May Never Come — Both Endings

At the final Rift Zero apparatus, the Explorers choose between **Stabilize Rift** and **Close Rift**. These are alternative outcomes, not two switches that must both be activated in one run.

The associated achievements have different descriptions:

- **Dawn of Tomorrow:** Secure Rift Zero.
- **Tomorrow May Never Come:** Close Rift Zero.

After experiencing one result, chapter selection allows you to return to the final stage to attempt the other. Before selecting an ending, agree with your partner which outcome you still need.

The [Rift Zero walkthrough](/we-were-here-tomorrow/walkthrough#rift-zero) covers the final apparatus and access requirements. You do not need to repeat the earlier laser, battery network, and plant puzzles solely to choose a different ending from the available final chapter.

</section>

<section id="achievement-cleanup" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Before Finishing Your Achievement Run

Compare the unchecked items against what your Explorers can still reach. A missing story milestone usually means there is a research objective or mechanism left to activate. An unchecked prototype interaction is better handled while both players are together and have the appropriate weapons. A missing collectible requires another search of the environment rather than replaying a solved circuit puzzle.

The most important distinction is between **a completed story** and **37 completed achievements**. Finishing Rift Zero does not prove that you collected every unique plushie, fulfilled Many Ways, or performed every optional partner interaction. Use the checklist to track those remaining tasks separately.

</section>
