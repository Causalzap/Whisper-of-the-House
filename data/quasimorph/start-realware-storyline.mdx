The RealWare storyline does not begin with a RealWare marker on the System Map.

It begins with the AnCom data already aboard the Magnum after the opening
sequence. The first decision is who receives that information. When I want the
RealWare route, I give it to RealWare, wait for their reply and then complete an
AnCom Espionage mission that lets me hack a PCPU.

The route has two common points of confusion:

1. nothing may appear immediately after RealWare accepts the opening data;
2. the first Palace objective can appear before the campaign is ready to survive
   it.

This guide covers the opening trigger sequence through the Palace Asteroid EMP
Scorcher objective. It does not claim the complete RealWare storyline, every
later mission or a final ending route.

> Tested in Quasimorph 1.0. Contract locations, faction control, travel time and
> available AnCom Espionage jobs vary between campaigns. The trigger order is
> more reliable than any fixed mission location.

<h2 id="wait-for-contact">Wait for RealWare Instead of Searching the Map</h2>

After the confirmation, the storyline can appear inactive.

That is the first real player trap.

In my route, the follow-up did not appear immediately. Time had to pass before
RealWare sent the next instruction.

I use that gap to strengthen the campaign:

- complete nearby low-risk work;
- replace ammunition and medicine;
- recover injured operators;
- improve Monitoring, Scanner or Clone Rank;
- stay near systems that already contain useful contracts.

I do not fly across the solar system looking for a hidden RealWare marker.

The safest waiting plan is productive but reversible. I avoid spending the only
good operator or rare weapon merely to make time move.

<h2 id="read-hack-request">Read the PCPU Request Literally</h2>

RealWare's next important message explains that AnCom has made progress in
quasimorphic research.

The actionable request is specific:

- perform an **AnCom Espionage** mission;
- reach the mission PCPU;
- hack it using the data supplied by RealWare;
- allow RealWare to enter AnCom's closed network.

<h2 id="find-ancom-espionage">Find the Correct AnCom Espionage Contract</h2>

From the Magnum, I inspect available locations for a contract that clearly
combines:

- **AnCom**
- **Espionage**
- enough **Time remains**
- manageable Difficulty and Floors

I do not accept the first contract with AnCom somewhere on the card.

The tested route used an AnCom Espionage contract on Venus. By the time the
Magnum was ready, the contract had only about **two days remaining**.

### I take it when

- the contract explicitly matches the RealWare request;
- travel fits inside Time remains;
- the operator has a ranged weapon and compatible ammunition;
- the floor count fits the available food and medicine;
- failure will not remove every useful weapon and treatment item.

### I wait for another one when

- travel consumes nearly all of the remaining time;
- the operator is already injured;
- the mission requires the only irreplaceable loadout;
- the listed Difficulty is far beyond nearby normal work;
- success depends on medicine or ammunition found inside the station.

Monitoring can create another random mission in the current system, but I do not

<h2 id="hack-and-extract">Hack the PCPU and Protect the Successful Route</h2>

The station layout is generated, so there is no honest universal room-by-room
path.

My route is:

1. enter with enough food, medicine and ammunition for the listed Floors;
2. identify the elevator or objective direction before clearing every side room;
3. preserve ammunition for the return route;
4. reach the PCPU;
5. use the RealWare data to hack it;
6. finish the Espionage contract;
7. return to the Magnum instead of turning the successful hack into a full clear.

The PCPU is the reason I entered. Optional loot is secondary.

After the successful sequence, RealWare confirms that the hacked PCPU is
transmitting from AnCom's closed network.

<h2 id="do-not-rush-palace">Do Not Rush Palace Because the Marker Appeared</h2>

The Palace objective can appear before the campaign is ready for it.

In my tested route, the preparation discussion treated Covert Operations as a
major equipment and enemy-quality jump. The available screenshot confirms the
objective, but not every individual weapon tooltip, resistance or exact
Technology modifier.

The practical conclusion is still clear: I do not launch when success requires
perfect play.

### I go when

- a healthy operator can survive one bad ranged exchange;
- the main weapon has useful range against specialist enemies;
- compatible ammunition is not limited to one fragile stack;
- armour covers the damage types shown by the actual mission preview;
- medicine does not depend on finding an Auto-Doc inside the station;
- travel fits safely inside the contract window;
- failure will hurt without deleting the entire working roster and arsenal.

### I wait when

- the operator has very low Health;
- my only suitable weapon and ammunition would be lost together;
- armour has an obvious gap against the shown enemies;
- the mission needs every turn to go perfectly;
- one Clone Rank, Scanner or supply upgrade would create meaningful margin;
- failing after a long trip would require several ordinary contracts to rebuild.

A story marker is not an order to launch immediately.

It is a known future obligation. I use the time before Palace to remove the
failure that would end the run first.

<h2 id="route-boundary">What This Opening Route Confirms</h2>

The opening RealWare sequence is:

1. keep track of the AnCom PCPU/data;
2. give it to RealWare;
3. wait for the follow-up;
4. find an AnCom Espionage contract;
5. hack the mission PCPU;
6. complete the contract and return;
7. receive the Palace Asteroid EMP Scorcher objective;
8. prepare before attempting Covert Operations.

That solves the real early questions:

- How do I start the RealWare storyline?
- Why did no RealWare marker appear immediately?
- Which AnCom contract counts?
- What happens after the PCPU hack?
- Should I go to Palace immediately?

This page does not claim:

- every RealWare story mission;
- the exact EMP Scorcher location on a generated map;
- every Palace enemy and resistance;
- all choices after Covert Operations;
- the RealWare ending;
- a recovery route after giving the opening data elsewhere.

The correct stopping point is the last step I can route confidently.

Once the Palace mission has a complete, repeatable path, it should become its own
walkthrough instead of being guessed inside this starting guide.
