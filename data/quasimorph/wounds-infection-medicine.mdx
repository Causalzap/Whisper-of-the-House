Wounds in Quasimorph rarely kill me in one dramatic moment.

They kill the run because I treat them too late, stay in Run when I need the
inventory, trust medicine I have not found or keep exploring after the injury
has already changed the route.

I use one field rule:

> stabilize the next dangerous turn, then decide whether the mission is still
> worth the remaining map.

This guide focuses on that decision chain. It is not a medical-item
encyclopedia. It explains how I read the Health Monitor, leave the wrong movement
mode, choose between proper treatment and emergency stabilizers, use an Auto-Doc
without planning around an imaginary reset, and know when a wound has converted
a loot run into an extraction run.

> Tested in Quasimorph 1.0 on fresh campaigns. Wounds, infection rolls, room
> layouts, station events and Auto-Doc availability vary between missions. The
> values below come from the visible 1.0 UI used for this guide.

<h2 id="read-health-monitor">Read the Health Monitor Before Taking Another Detour</h2>

The red screen tint tells me that something is wrong. The Health Monitor tells
me what the run can still support.

One early operator showed:

- **100/130 HP**
- **Pain 7/12**
- **Fullness 1958/2200**
- **Regeneration +2 HP/turn**
- **Air 22%**
- **Infection 0**
- a Knee **Contusion** from blunt damage
- **4% infection** on the wound
- **2 Bandages** and **2 Splints** in the inventory

    not only the remaining HP.
  </figcaption>

I use the screen to answer five questions:

1. **Which body part is injured?**
2. **Is Health stable for the next exchange?**
3. **Is Pain reducing the margin I need to move or fight?**
4. **Does the wound already carry infection risk?**
5. **How many mandatory rooms and floors remain?**

The last question decides whether treatment is buying continuation or only a
safer retreat.

With the sample above, I may continue when the objective is two controlled rooms
away. I leave when the objective is already secured and the next two rooms are
only optional loot.

The wound has not ended the run. It has ended the luxury portion of the run.

<h2 id="leave-run">Leave Run Before You Try to Treat the Wound</h2>

The current 1.0 movement modes are **Stealth**, **Walk** and **Run**.

Their visible values are:

- Stealth: **1 AP**, **-1 kcal/AP**, inventory and actions available
- Walk: **2 AP**, **-2 kcal/AP**
- Run: **3 AP**, **-10 kcal/AP**, **75% Overall accuracy**, normal inventory and
  actions unavailable

That makes Run useful for a cleared corridor and dangerous as a recovery state.

My response after taking damage is:

1. finish or escape the immediate exchange;
2. leave Run;
3. choose Walk when the room is controlled;
4. choose Stealth when the next angle is uncertain;
5. open the inventory and Health Monitor;
6. treat the wound;
7. return to Run only when the route is known and the operator is stable.

I do not attempt to heal while remaining in Run. I also do not burn
**-10 kcal/AP** during a wounded retreat when Walk costs **-2 kcal/AP** and gives
me normal access to items.

That difference can decide whether the operator reaches the elevator with enough
Fullness to keep moving.

<h2 id="choose-treatment">Use Proper Treatment First and Emergency Stabilizers Second</h2>

Bandages and Splints are my planned answers.

Rags and Plastic are emergency answers.

I use the compatible clean treatment when it is available. I use an improvised
item when the wound is threatening the route and waiting for a better option is
more dangerous than the infection and durability cost shown by the tooltip.

### Rag

The Rag tooltip shows:

- wound stabilization for **8 turns**
- **15% infection chance**
- visible wound-healing values of **25**
- **Repairs +25 / -15 Max**

### I use Rag or Plastic when

- no compatible clean treatment remains;
- the wound is making the immediate route unsafe;
- the objective or elevator is close;
- the current floor is mostly controlled;
- eight stabilized turns are enough to reach a real solution.

### I turn back after using one when

- another mandatory floor remains;
- the station has no reliable follow-up medicine;
- Calories are already low;
- the wound carries infection risk and I have no answer aboard the operator;
- completing the next detour would require another temporary fix.

Emergency stabilization changes the mission.

The correct question is not “Did the item work?” It is “What can I safely finish
before the temporary window stops being enough?”

<h2 id="infection-risk">Treat Infection Risk as a Future Route Cost</h2>

The Health Monitor can show **Infection 0** while an individual wound still
carries an infection percentage.

That distinction matters.

In the knee example, the operator had no current Infection, but the Contusion
showed **4% infection**. Rag and Plastic introduce a much larger **15% infection
chance** while providing emergency stabilization.

I separate the decisions:

- **current Infection:** a status already affecting the operator;
- **infection risk:** a chance that the treatment or untreated wound creates the
  next problem.

I do not spend every Infection answer because a small percentage is visible. I
also do not ignore the percentage when the route requires many more turns.

I become more conservative when:

- an improvised stabilizer has been used;
- another floor remains;
- the station event removes medical supplies;
- the return path is long;
- the operator has no Sorbent, Antibiotic or other confirmed follow-up option;
- another wound would force a second risky treatment.

Some mission events explicitly state that the level contains no Bandages, First
Aid Kits or even toothpaste. When that warning appears after deployment, the
medicine already in the loadout becomes the entire reliable plan.

<h2 id="autodoc">Use an Auto-Doc Only After Checking Its Real State</h2>

An Auto-Doc is not automatically a full reset.

The verified machine showed:

- **60% charge**
- **HEAL 60 HP**
- **HEAL ALL WOUNDS** unavailable
- **CHARGE (5 BAT.)**

    was unavailable, while recharging required five standard batteries.
  </figcaption>

I do not infer from this screen why **Heal All Wounds** was unavailable. I only
use what the UI confirms.

My Auto-Doc route decision is:

### I use it when

- it is already on the controlled route;
- the current charge provides an action I need;
- the detour costs fewer turns than treating from the bag;
- the machine is close enough that the wound remains stable;
- I am carrying the five batteries required to recharge it and recharging is
  actually worth the inventory cost.

### I stop planning around it when

- the machine is several uncontrolled rooms away;
- I have not seen its charge or available actions;
- reaching it consumes the food or stabilization window needed for extraction;
- the operator can already retreat safely with carried medicine;
- the entire plan depends on Heal All Wounds being available.

An unseen Auto-Doc is not healing in reserve.

A visible machine with the wrong available action is not the solution I hoped
for.

<h2 id="recalculate-route">Recalculate the Mission After Treatment</h2>

Treatment does not restore the original plan automatically.

After stabilizing, I check:

1. Is the objective complete?
2. How far is the nearest known elevator or extraction point?
3. How many mandatory floors remain?
4. Can the main weapon cover the return ammunition?
5. Can the operator Walk the route without running out of Calories?
6. Is Quasimorphosis adding another timer?
7. Does the next room solve a named problem?

I continue when:

- the wound is controlled with proper treatment;
- the objective is still close and mandatory;
- the return route is known;
- Health, Pain, Calories and ammunition leave a real margin;
- the next room contains a specific target rather than generic loot.

I extract when:

- the objective is already secured;
- a production chip, mind chip or rare Magnum component is already protected;
- treatment is temporary;
- infection risk has no follow-up answer;
- a leg or knee injury makes every remaining room more expensive;
- the return depends on an unverified Auto-Doc;
- another floor would require a second emergency treatment;
- I am saying “one more room” without a named reason.

<h2 id="pack-before-launch">Pack Enough Medicine to Avoid Depending on the Floor</h2>

Most wound failures begin before landing.

My conservative early medical plan is:

- **2 Bandages**
- **2 Splints**
- one confirmed Infection answer when available
- enough food for the listed Floors and the return
- water or another useful liquid when the mission risk justifies it
- enough ammunition to finish without fighting badly to conserve rounds

I adjust the loadout upward when:

- the contract has several Floors;
- the station event removes medicine;
- the operator has low Health;
- the objective requires a return item;
- the mission is a story step I cannot easily replace;
- Quasimorphosis or long travel makes failure especially expensive.

I do not fill the entire backpack with medicine on every short contract.

The goal is not to carry every possible answer. It is to avoid a plan that only
works when the station gives me the exact treatment I forgot to bring.

<h2 id="field-rule">My Field Rule for Wounds and Infection</h2>

When a wound appears, I stop playing the route I planned several turns ago.

I finish the immediate danger, leave Run, read the body part and use the cleanest
compatible treatment available.

Then I decide what the treatment actually purchased:

- enough stability to finish the objective;
- enough stability to reach an Auto-Doc;
- or enough stability to extract.

If the answer depends on lucky medicine, an unseen machine or one more optional
room, the run has already told me what to do.

I turn back and bring home the smaller win.
