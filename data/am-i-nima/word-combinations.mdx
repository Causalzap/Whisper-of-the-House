### How to Find a Specific Word

Select **Result only** and enter the word you want to obtain.

For example, searching for Mom displays its alternative recipes, while searching for Pride helps you prepare the ingredients for CONSUMPTION.

If a required ingredient is missing, search for that word next. You can also find a word in the Collection Tracker and select its recipe count to see combinations that produce it.

### How to Find Combinations Using Your Current Words

1. Select **Mark starting words** if you are beginning a new game.
2. Mark any additional words you have already discovered.
3. Enable **Available recipes only** to find combinations using your marked ingredients.
4. Enable **Uncollected results only** to focus on missing discoveries.

After completing a combination in the game, mark the individual results you actually obtained.

Both ingredients must be marked for a recipe to appear as available. Make sure those ingredients are also accessible during your current day or loop.

## How Do You Combine Words in Am I Nima?

Click a discovered word to bring it into your brain. Drag two words together to combine their meanings.

When a combination succeeds, its result becomes available as another word or special discovery.

For the opening sequence:

1. Combine **Nima** and **Stranger** to obtain Mom.
2. Combine **Nima** and **Mom** to discover Love, Fear, and Family.
3. Continue combining the words that relate to your current situation.
4. Check the newly discovered results before moving on to the next conversation.

Creating a word and using a word in dialogue are separate actions. Combining words expands your vocabulary, whereas dragging a word into your head can generate a response to the current question.

If you are unsure how to continue the opening conversation, follow the <Link href="/am-i-nima">Am I Nima walkthrough</Link>.

### Why Can the Same Combination Have Multiple Results?

**Nima + Mom** can produce Love, Fear, and Family. Nima + Remnant has three possible results: Mind, Body, and Ghost.

Check your library after combining either pair. You may discover its results separately, so mark only the words you actually receive.

### Alternative Recipes for Missing Words

Missing Stranger? You can also obtain Mom through Nima + Isolation or Lonely + Love.

Choose the recipe whose ingredients are already available during your current loop. Having an alternative recipe is particularly useful when the other ingredients have not returned to your library yet.

### Can You Combine a Word With Itself?

Some combinations use the same word twice.

For example, **Water + Water** produces River.

A combination can also return a word you already know. Nima + Disgust produces Nima rather than a completely new concept.

Do not assume that every successful combination will increase your collection count.

### How Do You Check Previous Word Combinations?

Open **Word History** to review combinations you have already discovered.

### Which Word Combinations Should You Try First?

After discovering Mom and Love, combine **Love + Mom** to obtain Work.

If you have also discovered Lonely, you can expand your vocabulary through the following combinations:

1. Combine **Mom + Lonely** to obtain Dad.
2. Combine **Love + Dad** to obtain Water.
3. Combine **Water + Water** to obtain River.

Another useful branch starts with Lonely + Dad, which produces Grief. Combining Grief with Work then produces Science.

Prioritize missing results that give you new ingredients for further combinations. Skip a recipe if you already have its result and want to expand your collection.

### How Do You Find More Word Combinations?

When you discover a new word, consider whether it relates to:

- A person or family member.
- An object you recently examined.
- An emotion associated with a conversation.
- A place mentioned during a memory.
- Another concept with a similar or opposing meaning.

For example, Heart and Trophy lead to Pride, while Fossil and Science can lead to Present and Pride.

These are useful starting points for experimentation, but not every logically related pair has a valid recipe.

## Where Do You Find New Words in Am I Nima?

Word combinations are only one way to expand your vocabulary. Examining objects can introduce concepts that are difficult to discover through combinations alone.

Whenever you gain access to another room, inspect unfamiliar objects before returning to the next test.

Some objects provide more than one word. Examining a dying plant, for example, can introduce both Plant and Neglect.

### How Do You Get Fossil?

Inspect the paleobiology book on the upper shelf in the bedroom.

Fossil is useful beyond the initial object inspection because it participates in several known combinations, including a Memory recipe.

Once you also have Life, try combining the two.

### How Do You Get Trapped?

Inspect the aquarium on the bedroom shelf.

The description of the fish living inside an artificial environment introduces Trapped.

Keep this word available for future combinations rather than assuming its only purpose is to describe the aquarium.

### How Do You Get James Pond?

Combine **Fish + Dad** to obtain James Pond.

This word is particularly useful if you are investigating the locked cabinet in the bedroom.

For the cabinet password and the surrounding exploration steps, see the <Link href="/am-i-nima#locker-code">bedroom locker solution</Link>.

### Can Conversations Unlock New Words?

Yes. Certain dialogue responses and story events can introduce words.

Vancouver, for example, can become available during the drawing discussion when you consider the home described as a kingdom by the sea.

If you are missing a word with no known object location or recipe, continue exploring the available conversations and inspect your library after the discussion.

## Am I Nima Word List & Collection Tracker

The word library currently contains {amINimaWords.length} known ordinary word entries.

Open the **Collection Tracker** tab in the <Link href="#recipe-finder">word explorer</Link> and mark the words you have discovered. Use the Uncollected filter to identify missing entries.

Ordinary words, memories, Tomato Boy discoveries, and other character cards have separate collection categories.

Your marked discoveries are saved in this browser. You can return later to continue checking missing words without starting the list again.

The tracker does not read your game save, so update your collection manually as you discover new words.

### Does the Word Tracker Include Every Word?

No. The current list covers part of the game's vocabulary rather than every possible discovery.

A missing entry does not mean that the word cannot be obtained in the game.

Some words have known recipes, some come from object interactions, and others still have no known acquisition method in this list.

### Do You Keep Words Between Loops?

Words from the previous loop are remembered at the beginning of each day.

If you start another loop and cannot immediately find a previously discovered word, continue progressing through the relevant day.

See the <Link href="/am-i-nima#second-loop">next-loop guide</Link> for more information about restarting the story.

### Does 100% in the Tracker Mean Human Dictionary Is Complete?

No. The percentage measures how many entries you have marked in the tracker, not your full in-game vocabulary.

You can finish the available list while still missing words required for Human Dictionary. Check the <Link href="/am-i-nima/achievements#word-collection">collection achievement requirements</Link> and your Steam achievement progress.

## How Do You Unlock Memories in Am I Nima?

Certain word combinations trigger memories rather than simply producing another ordinary word.

These sequences reveal parts of Nima's past and can introduce special concepts that become relevant during later conversations.

The following Memory combinations are currently known.

<GuideTable
  columns={memoryCombinationColumns}
  rows={memoryCombinationRows}
/>

### How Do You Unlock OBSESSION?

Combine **Life + Fossil**.

OBSESSION reveals a memory involving Pearl visiting Nima during a period of intense heat and isolation.

If you are missing Fossil, inspect the paleobiology book on the bedroom shelf. Search your word library for Life before attempting the combination.

### How Do You Unlock CONSUMPTION?

Combine **Pride + Guilt**.

CONSUMPTION reveals a memory connected to swimming, competition, and Tiffany.

If Pride is missing, try either of these combinations:

- Heart + Trophy.
- Fossil + Science.

Use the recipe finder to check your remaining ingredients before attempting the Memory combination.

### Can You Combine Memories With Other Words?

Some memories can also serve as ingredients for further word combinations.

For example, combine **OBSESSION + God** to obtain Power.

This gives memories another use beyond revealing past events. Check the recipe finder for combinations involving a memory after you unlock it.

### Can You Use Memories in Conversations?

Some memories can generate additional responses during particular conversations.

If a memory does not produce an option for the current question, keep it for a later opportunity. Its usefulness depends on the conversation you are having.

Memories can become relevant to later story decisions, but obtaining a particular memory does not by itself guarantee a specific ending.

See the <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings">Am I Nima endings guide</Link> for choices that become available during later story branches.

## How Do You Get Tomato Boy Characters in Am I Nima?

Tomato Boy combinations introduce a separate group of character discoveries.

Instead of combining two ordinary concepts to expand your vocabulary, you can use Tomato Boy as an ingredient to create different characters.

### How Do You Unlock Tomato Boy?

Combine **Friend + Plush** to obtain Tomato Boy.

Once you have Tomato Boy, try combining it with other words from your library.

<GuideTable
  columns={tomatoCombinationColumns}
  rows={tomatoCombinationRows}
/>

Dad produces Tomato Pop, while Work produces Pear Boy.

These are useful starting combinations if you have just discovered Tomato Boy and want to begin collecting characters.

### How Do You Get Pomegranate Prince?

Pomegranate Prince is a character discovery obtained through exploration rather than a word combination.

1. Find the locked cabinet in the bedroom.
2. Enter **POND**.
3. Open the cabinet and inspect the Pomegranate Prince figurine.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/am-i-nima/am-i-nima-pomegranate-prince.webp"
  alt="Pomegranate Prince figurine and character card in Am I Nima"
  width={1920}
  height={1080}
  caption="Inspect the Pomegranate Prince figurine after opening the bedroom cabinet."

You can mark Pomegranate Prince under **Other character cards** in the Collection Tracker.

The cabinet also contains other items worth examining. Follow the <Link href="/am-i-nima#locker-code">bedroom locker solution</Link> if you have not unlocked it yet.

### How Do You Find More Tomato Boy Characters?

Keep Tomato Boy available when you discover new ordinary words.

Try combinations involving people, occupations, objects, or other concepts that could form a character.

Do not treat character discoveries as ordinary vocabulary progress. Tomato Boy character collection and ordinary word collection are separate goals.

The <Link href="/am-i-nima/achievements#tomato-kingdom">Tomato Kingdom achievement</Link> provides a longer-term reason to continue exploring these combinations.

The current character recipes are not a complete list of every Tomato Boy character.

## Why Are Your Word Combinations Not Working?

When a combination fails, check whether the problem is a missing ingredient, an invalid pair, or a word that cannot currently be used in dialogue.

### Why Does a Word Have No Recipe in the Finder?

Not every word has a known combination. Some discoveries come directly from inspecting objects or progressing through conversations.

Check the word's entry in the Collection Tracker for an acquisition method. If neither a recipe nor a location is listed, try exploring objects and conversations you have not completed.

A missing recipe in the finder does not mean that the word cannot be obtained in the game.

### What If a Word Does Not Produce the Dialogue You Need?

A successful combination does not automatically advance Mom's tests. Drag the resulting word into your head and check the response it generates.

If none of your available words addresses the question, identify your current test in the <Link href="/am-i-nima#first-loop-walkthrough">first-loop walkthrough</Link>.
