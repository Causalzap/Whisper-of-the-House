## How Do You Start Playing Am I Nima?

You begin in a basement with a limited understanding of your identity.

Mom expects you to answer her questions, but your vocabulary initially contains only a small number of words.

The first task is to use those words to create additional concepts and communicate with her.

### How Do You Answer Mom's Questions?

Combine Nima + Stranger to obtain Mom, then drag a relevant word into your head to generate a dialogue option.

Dragging words together creates new discoveries. Dragging a word into your head instead lets you respond to the current conversation.

Missing a word needed for the test? Search its ingredients in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#recipe-finder">word combination finder</Link>.

### What Do Green Words and Green Dialogue Options Mean?

A green-highlighted word can be used in conversation. This is different from the color of the dialogue button that appears after you drag the word into your head.

Green buttons often advance the current discussion, while white buttons may reveal additional questions, observations, or reactions. White answers can still have consequences and sometimes move the story forward.

Read the actual response before choosing it. A green button is not a guarantee that Mom will accept your answer or that you will reach the ending you want.

### How Do You Review Previous Words and Conversations?

The game provides both **Word History** and **Dialogue History**.

Use Word History when you have forgotten how a previous combination was made.

Use Dialogue History when you need to review an earlier conversation, a personal detail, or a clue that Mom mentioned.

This is especially useful when an object appears again during a later test and you cannot remember why it was important.

## Day 1 Walkthrough: Opening Questions and the Bedroom

### What Should You Do During the Congee Test?

Combine Hungry + Congee to obtain **Disgust** when both words become available.

Food-related responses can lead to tasting the congee and discussing Mom's cooking. Read her reaction and continue through the available dialogue until she moves on from the food test.

Disgust is a useful discovery during this conversation, not a required answer for every route.

### What Should You Inspect in the Bedroom?

When you gain access to the bedroom, investigate the objects around you before concentrating on the next test.

The bedroom contains several useful sources of personal information:

- Photographs and belongings connected to Nima's family.
- An aquarium and objects related to fish.
- Books connected to paleobiology and Mom's work.
- Drawings, personal items, and the locked cabinet.

These objects introduce subjects that may become important when Mom presents related items during later conversations.

The locked cabinet is also worth investigating. Its four-letter solution is covered in the <Link href="#locker-code">bedroom locker puzzle</Link> below.

</section>

<section id="day-2" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Day 2 Walkthrough: Bathroom Access and Object Tests

The next stage introduces additional room access and tests involving objects from Nima's life.

### How Do You Unlock the Bathroom?

If the bathroom door is locked, continue the available conversation with Mom.

During an early interaction, she notices that you have not cleaned yourself up. The conversation establishes that the bathroom was locked, and she subsequently unlocks it.

This is a story-controlled room-access event rather than a known password puzzle.

Return to the bathroom when it becomes accessible.

### How Do You Answer the Shark Plush Test?

Think of Dad when Mom presents the shark plush. The toy was a gift from him and is connected to memories of visiting the aquarium together.

A response associated with Dad can bring up those memories. Simply describing the shark or giving facts about the animal may not satisfy her.

### How Do You Answer the Fossil Test?

Try **River** when Mom presents the fossil. This association leads to the memory of finding it in the Kicking Horse River and giving it to her.

The fossil is connected to Mom's research, but the location and circumstances of its discovery are more relevant to this question than identifying the rock itself.

Search the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#recipe-finder">recipe finder</Link> if River is missing.

### How Do You Answer the Swimming Goggles Test?

If a general Swimming response disappoints Mom, try using **CONSUMPTION** instead. This memory recalls the swimming competition, the accident involving Nima, Tiffany, and Mom's reaction.

Combine Pride + Guilt if you have not recovered the memory. The <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#memory-combinations">Memory combinations guide</Link> can help with missing ingredients.

This is one possible approach to the goggles discussion, not a mandatory answer for every route.

</section>

<section id="day-3" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Day 3 Walkthrough: Drawings, Friends and Personal Memories

The drawing-related tests shift the conversation from identifying physical objects toward recalling Nima's life.

Mom asks about places and people connected to her past.

### How Do You Remember Vancouver?

During the drawing discussion, look for the reflection about a kingdom by the sea. The recollection describes a coastal home, frequent rain, and nearby rainforest.

Following this conversation can unlock **Vancouver**, giving you a more specific place-related word for the drawing test.

### What Should You Explore Before the Next Test?

Revisit rooms that have become accessible.

The bathroom is particularly worth investigating when its objects or surroundings appear to have changed.

An unusual painting can conceal an opening behind the wall. The hidden passage is explained in the <Link href="#bunker">hidden passage and bunker section</Link>.

Do not assume that completing a conversation means that every nearby object has already been explored.

</section>

<section id="day-4" className="scroll-mt-24">

## Day 4 Walkthrough: How Do You Complete the Boiler Room Test?

The Whiskers experiment presents a different kind of test.

Instead of simply recalling facts, you must decide how to respond to a situation involving another living creature.

### How Do You Enter the Boiler Room?

Mom instructs you to pick up Whiskers' cage and follow her.

She leads you into the Boiler Room, where the machinery produces intense heat and the door is locked behind you.

The test presents a conflict between obeying Mom and protecting Whiskers.

Examine the available objects before committing to a response.

### Can You Save Whiskers?

Yes. Saving Whiskers is one way to resolve the Boiler Room sequence.

Look for the small wall opening behind the boiler machinery. Freedom and Surgery are useful words during the rescue route.

Follow the <Link href="/am-i-nima/achievements#free-whiskers">Whiskers rescue steps</Link> for the corresponding dialogue choices and what to do when the initial release goes wrong.

Completing Mom's experiment and successfully rescuing Whiskers are not necessarily the same outcome.

### What Happens After the Boiler Room Test?

The story can return you to Mom's testing area after the room sequence concludes.

Continue the conversation and pay attention to what she says about the purpose of the experiment.

Your responses during this stage can reveal more about how she evaluates Nima's behavior.

When you regain access to the bedroom, investigate available interactions and prepare for the later identity-related conversations.

</section>

## Day 5 Walkthrough: Mirror Conversations and Identity Decisions

The later stages increasingly focus on whether you are Nima and how you intend to respond to Mom.

These conversations can introduce decisions that lead toward different story branches.

### What Should You Do During the Mirror Conversation?

If you want to gain Mom's trust, Pretend can reveal **I'm going to trick her.**

Other responses take the story in different directions. Check the wording before choosing, especially if you are trying to reach a particular ending.

### How Do You Answer the Final Identity Test?

When Mom asks what it means to be Nima, consider responses connected to her relationship with her daughter.

Family can reveal To love you., while later confrontations introduce different identity-related choices.

Match the actual question with the response you want in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings#ending-list">ending choices table</Link>.

### What Should You Do When Your First Ending Finishes?

If the opening basement scene appears again, continue with the <Link href="#second-loop">second-loop walkthrough</Link>.

If you want to investigate a different conclusion, use the <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings">endings guide</Link> to identify the story checkpoint you need to revisit.

## What Is the Locker Code in Am I Nima?

The bedroom locker code is **POND**.

Enter these four letters when interacting with the locked cabinet in the bedroom.

### Where Does the POND Password Come From?

The password is connected to **James Pond**, a name associated with the fish-related belongings in Nima's room.

Investigate the bedroom's photographs, drawings, and aquarium-related objects if you want to understand the clue before opening the cabinet.

You do not need to substitute Nima's own name for the password.

### How Do You Open the Bedroom Locker?

1. Explore the bedroom until you locate the locked cabinet.
2. Interact with its four-letter lock.
3. Enter **POND**.
4. Open the cabinet and inspect the belongings inside.

### What Is Inside the Locker?

The cabinet contains several personal items worth examining.

These include a diary, a photograph, and the Pomegranate Prince figurine.

The diary provides additional information about Nima's situation, while the figurine introduces another character discovery.

For the figurine and other character discoveries, see the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#tomato-boy-combinations">Tomato Boy and character combinations section</Link>.

### Why Is the POND Code Not Working?

Check that you are interacting with the locked bedroom cabinet.

POND is the known four-letter password for this particular container.

It is not an established solution for every locked object in the house, and it does not provide a known answer to the bunker keypad.

## How Do You Find the Bunker in Am I Nima?

Start by investigating the hidden areas behind the bathroom painting and beneath the basement.

You can discover a concealed opening, a metal floor hatch, and a heavy door with a numeric keypad.

Start by locating the hidden openings and the underground corridor. The final door into the secret lab remains unsolved.

### Where Is the Hidden Passage?

Investigate the bathroom when it becomes available.

Look for a painting that appears to have been disturbed.

During the relevant interaction, you can remove the painting from the wall. This reveals a dark opening behind it.

### How Do You Find the Keypad Door?

Further exploration of the hidden areas can lead to a metal floor hatch.

When you can access it:

1. Examine the hatch and its handle.
2. Lift the hatch.
3. Descend the metal ladder.
4. Investigate the corridor beneath the basement.
5. Examine the heavy metal door with the digital keypad.

The <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings#symbiosis-ending">Symbiosis ending</Link> includes the bunker, but opening the keypad door beforehand is not a confirmed requirement.

## What Happens When You Start the Second Loop in Am I Nima?

Returning to the opening basement scene after an ending can mark the beginning of another loop. Look for the déjà vu message and the tutorial explaining word recovery.

### Do Your Words Carry Over Between Loops?

Yes. The next-loop tutorial explains that you remember words from the previous loop at the start of every day.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/am-i-nima/am-i-nima-loop-word-carryover.webp"

  caption="Previously discovered words are remembered at the start of each day in another loop."

Words return at the start of each day, so a missing discovery at the beginning of the loop does not necessarily mean it has been lost. Continue to the relevant day and check your library again.

### Can You Skip Dialogue in the Second Loop?

Use SKIP when it is available to move past familiar dialogue. Do not assume every conversation can be skipped.

Read the available responses before skipping a scene where you intend to make a different decision.

### Should You Start a New Game or Continue the Loop?

Continue the available loop if you want to make use of previously discovered words. Before starting a new game, check whether the returning vocabulary gives you access to responses you missed during your first playthrough.

## Why Are You Stuck in Am I Nima?

When progress stops, identify whether the obstacle is caused by a missing word, the current conversation, an inaccessible room, or an unresolved puzzle.

These situations require different actions.

### What If You Have the Right Word but the Wrong Answer Appears?

The response generated by a word depends on the current scene.

A word associated with one ending or test may produce a completely different option somewhere else.

Check what Mom or the voice is currently asking.

If the response does not match the decision you are looking for, you may need to continue the story or investigate a different available interaction.

### Do You Need Every Word to Progress?

There is no established requirement to discover every word before completing your first loop.

You can concentrate on the concepts relevant to the current test and investigate the larger collection goals during later exploration.

The Human Dictionary achievement requires {amINimaCollectionTargets.humanDictionary} words, but this collection objective is separate from simply advancing the story.

If a missing word is preventing you from answering a question, search for that particular word rather than attempting to complete the entire collection first.
