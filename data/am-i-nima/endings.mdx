### Why Does Repression Produce Different Ending Choices?

Repression can generate three different responses:

- **Swallow your hate.** during the fish test.
- **I am Nima.** during the mirror identity scene.
- **It's me, Mom.** during the confrontation.

These responses are associated with Assimilation, Fissure, and Loyalty, respectively.

## How Do You Get the Loyalty Ending in Am I Nima?

Loyalty follows a route in which you try to satisfy Mom's expectations, insist that you are Nima, and eventually leave your fate in her hands.

The route has been reached during the first loop.

Three useful dialogue choices are associated with Pretend, Family, and Repression.

<GuideTable
  columns={loyaltyChoiceColumns}
  rows={loyaltyChoiceRows}
/>

These choices describe the known late-game route rather than a complete set of mandatory answers for every earlier test.

### Step 1: Decide to Trick Mom

During the mirror conversation, place **Pretend** into your head to reveal the response:

**I'm going to trick her.**

The plan is to make Mom believe that her tests are working. By gaining her trust, you hope to create an opportunity to escape.

Choosing this approach does not end the game. Continue to the next tests and conversations.

### Step 2: Answer the Identity Test

Mom eventually asks what it means to be Nima.

One available response is generated by placing **Family** into your head.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/am-i-nima/am-i-nima-loyalty-family-choice.webp"

The resulting choice is **To love you.**

This approach emphasizes Mom's importance in Nima's life.

However, this is not the only identity response associated with reaching Loyalty. The route has also been completed after choosing **Nima is happy.**

You do not need to treat Family as an established mandatory condition for this ending.

If you are missing Family, Nima + Mom is one of its known combinations. Other recipes and acquisition methods are available in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations">word combinations guide</Link>.

### Step 3: Continue to Insist That You Are Nima

The later confrontation becomes increasingly difficult as Mom questions whether you are really her daughter.

Place **Repression** into your head when the corresponding identity response becomes available.

<GuideImage
  src="/images/am-i-nima/am-i-nima-loyalty-repression-choice.webp"
  alt="Repression word revealing the It's me, Mom dialogue choice near the Loyalty ending"

  caption="Repression reveals the response It's me, Mom. during the confrontation."

Choose **It's me, Mom.** to continue presenting yourself as her daughter.

The conversation centers on returning to your former life and persuading Mom to accept you.

If you have not discovered Repression, search for it using the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#recipe-finder">word combination finder</Link>.

### Step 4: Continue Through the Final Confrontation

Mom remains unwilling to accept your identity despite your attempts to satisfy her.

The conversation eventually reaches the point where you tell Mom that if she does not trust you, she should kill you.

This line occurs within the final confrontation. It is not a separate known word combination or an additional established input requirement.

You leave your fate in her hands, and the confrontation ends with your death.

The story subsequently returns to the opening basement scene.

### Are All Loyalty Dialogue Options Required?

No fixed list of mandatory earlier answers has been established.

During the confrontation, you can also encounter responses involving apologies, fear, and your friends.

These options are not established requirements for Loyalty. Use the identity responses above to identify the relevant branch instead of treating every available dialogue option as a required step.

### What Happens After the Loyalty Ending?

The story returns to its opening scene and begins another loop.

Previously discovered words return at the start of each day, allowing you to explore alternative responses as you progress.

See the <Link href="/am-i-nima#second-loop">next-loop guide</Link> for how word recovery works.

Loyalty is also associated with an ending-related achievement. Use the <Link href="/am-i-nima/achievements#ending-achievements">ending achievement checklist</Link> to track your completed outcomes.

### Why Does Fear Give You a Different Answer?

Fear does not always produce the same dialogue option.

During other conversations, placing Fear into your head can instead reveal an expression of distress such as You're scaring me!

The response depends on the scene you have reached.

If KILL MOM! does not appear, possessing Fear alone is not enough. You need to reach the relevant mirror conversation.

The earlier decisions required to make this particular branch available are not yet fully known.

## How Do You Reach the Final Verdict in Am I Nima?

Judgement and Acceptance share the same late-game discussion about what happened to Nima.

Watch for CRUCIBLE during the later mirror conversation, followed by TRANSCENDENCE and Mom's explanation of her actions.

These scenes help you recognize the path toward the Final Verdict, although the choices required to enter it from an earlier loop remain unclear.

### What Does METEMPSYCHOSIS Do?

During the question **How are you going to explain yourself?**, placing METEMPSYCHOSIS into your head can reveal:

**You sacrificed Nima.**

This response leads into a discussion of Nima's identity and what happened when you first arrived.

METEMPSYCHOSIS is relevant to this late-game conversation, but the complete method for obtaining it and its necessity for reaching the Final Verdict are not yet established.

The route eventually reaches a more direct question:

**What is your verdict?**

At this stage, Judgement and Acceptance are available as separate words.

### What Is the Difference Between Judgement and Acceptance?

Both choices address how you respond to Mom after confronting what she has done.

They differ in whether you choose to leave her behind or forgive her.

### Is Acceptance the True Ending?

Acceptance resolves the final conversation through forgiveness, while Judgement resolves it by moving on.

Both are late-game outcomes reached after exploring Nima's identity.

There is not enough information to establish that Acceptance is the game's sole true ending or that one particular set of earlier decisions is mandatory for reaching it.

Choose between the two responses according to the outcome you want to explore.

## Why Are Some Ending Choices Missing?

Having a word in your library does not guarantee that its associated ending choice will appear.

Before attempting another route, distinguish between missing vocabulary and a story decision you have not reached.

### Do You Need Every Memory to Unlock the Endings?

Memories can introduce additional concepts and reveal responses during certain conversations.

However, there is no established requirement to collect every Memory before reaching every ending.

Some Memory combinations can be discovered before the second loop.

If you want to explore additional memories, start with the known combinations for OBSESSION and CONSUMPTION in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#memory-combinations">Memory combinations guide</Link>.

Do not assume that collecting either Memory automatically unlocks a particular ending.

### Can You Get Every Ending in One Loop?

Some of the major decisions lead to different conclusions.

At the Final Verdict, Move on. and Forgive her. represent separate branches rather than consecutive steps toward the same result.

You may need to revisit earlier story decisions to explore an alternative outcome.

The exact rules for returning to a previous decision, and whether every branch requires another full loop, are not yet established.

### Does a Green Choice Guarantee the Ending You Want?

No. A green option may advance the conversation without producing the ending you intended. Compare its exact wording with your intended branch before selecting it.

Check your unlocked ending achievements before attempting another branch. Use the <Link href="/am-i-nima/achievements#achievement-checklist">achievement tracker</Link> to mark completed outcomes.
