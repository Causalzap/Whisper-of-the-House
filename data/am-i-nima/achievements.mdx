## How Do You Unlock Nima 101 and Human Dictionary?

Nima 101 requires discovering {amINimaCollectionTargets.nima101} words.

Human Dictionary requires discovering all {amINimaCollectionTargets.humanDictionary} words.

These achievements involve expanding your vocabulary throughout the game rather than giving a specific answer during one of Mom's tests.

### Getting Your First 101 Words

Early discoveries provide ingredients for many additional combinations. Mark the words you possess in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#recipe-finder">recipe finder</Link>, then enable Available recipes only and Uncollected results only.

Work through the missing results before moving on to another set of ingredients.

### Finding the Last Missing Words for Human Dictionary

As you approach the larger collection goal, repeated discoveries become less useful. Search directly for missing results and check the object locations of words that have no known recipe.

Some vocabulary comes from story conversations, so a missing word may require more than another combination.

Remember that completing the <Link href="/am-i-nima/word-combinations#word-list">word collection tracker</Link> is not the same as completing Human Dictionary. Its percentage measures the entries listed in the tool, not your entire in-game vocabulary.

## How Do You Get the Free Whiskers Achievement?

Free Whiskers requires saving Whiskers during the Boiler Room experiment. Releasing him is not enough if the rat trap catches him afterward.

The rescue sequence uses two important words: **Freedom** and **Surgery**.

### Step 1: Find Whiskers and the Wall Hole

Find the small opening behind the boiler machinery while holding Whiskers. The gap is too small for you, but he can enter it.

Place **Freedom** into your head and select **Let Whiskers go.**

### Step 2: Retrieve Whiskers After the Trap Snaps

Whiskers does not immediately escape. When you hear the trap snap, return to the area behind the boiler and retrieve him. He is injured but still alive.

### Step 3: Use Surgery to Save Whiskers

When the voice suggests letting Whiskers die, place **Surgery** into your head.

This reveals the green dialogue option:

**You can save him.**

<GuideImage
  src="/images/am-i-nima/am-i-nima-whiskers-surgery-choice.webp"
  alt="Surgery word revealing the You can save him dialogue option during the Whiskers rescue"
  width={965}

  caption="Use Surgery to reveal the rescue response instead of abandoning Whiskers."
/>

Select this response and continue through the game's treatment sequence.

The acquisition methods for Freedom and Surgery are still unclear. Check your available words and continue exploring combinations if either is missing.

### Step 4: Release Whiskers

After the treatment sequence, the story describes you putting Whiskers down.

He moves toward the hole in the wall before Mom returns.

This is the important rescue outcome to look for: you have helped Whiskers escape instead of sacrificing him for your own escape.

The subsequent conversation with Mom addresses what happened and your willingness to help him.

Check whether Free Whiskers has unlocked before continuing to the next test.

### Does Releasing Whiskers Complete the Achievement?

Not necessarily. In the trap-and-rescue sequence, you still need to retrieve Whiskers and help him after the trap catches him.

Other rescue methods may exist, but check for the achievement unlock before leaving the experiment.

The <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings#symbiosis-ending">Symbiosis ending</Link> includes the bunker in its story sequence.

Whether entering it through that sequence unlocks the exploration achievement remains unclear. Check your Steam achievements after reaching the area.

## Which Achievements Require Different Endings?

There are {amINimaEndingAchievements.length} ending-related achievements.

Each requires reaching its corresponding ending, rather than simply discovering a particular word or revealing a dialogue option.

Different branches can become available during the fish test, mirror conversations, the confrontation with Mom, or the Final Verdict.

### Which Ending Achievement Should You Attempt Next?

Filter the achievement checklist by Endings and Incomplete to identify the outcomes you still need.

Compare those missing endings with the story checkpoints in the <Link href="/am-i-nima/endings#ending-list">ending choices table</Link>.

Choose a branch that differs from the outcome you have already completed. Pay attention to the required dialogue context rather than simply collecting its associated input word.

Earlier story decisions may still affect which options become available.

### Why Is an Ending Achievement Still Locked?

Seeing the relevant dialogue button does not mean that you have completed its associated ending.

Continue through the resulting story sequence and check which outcome you actually reached.

Some endings return you to the opening scene, while others lead into a different conclusion or the credits.

There is not yet a complete set of established starting conditions for every ending. If your story develops differently from the expected branch, review the scene and earlier choices before attempting the same response again.

### Can You Finish Every Achievement in One Playthrough?

A single story outcome cannot cover all the different ending achievements.

You will need to explore alternative branches, continue expanding your collections, and revisit decisions that produce different results.

The exact number of loops required to complete every achievement is not yet established.

Do not restart a successful collection route simply because you have reached an ending. Check which words return during the next loop and continue working toward missing discoveries.
