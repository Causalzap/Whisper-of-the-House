<h2 id="squad-leader-requirements">
  Squad Leader requires Getting Started and Squad Leader Training
</h2>

When I first hit **Requirements Not Met**, I assumed the game wanted a certain
Career Level or Role Level.

That was not the gate shown by the game.

For Squad Leader, the requirement message tells me to complete:

**Getting Started**

and:

**Squad Leader**

in Basic Training.

I therefore do not start grinding normal matches hoping the role will unlock
through XP.

I leave the server if necessary, return to **Basic Training**, and finish those
two Tutorials.

The order I use is simple:

**1. Complete Getting Started.**

**2. Complete Squad Leader Training.**

**3. Let the Tutorial reach its completion screen.**

**4. Return to the role selection and test Squad Leader again.**

I do not leave Squad Leader Training halfway through just because I already
understand Garrisons or Tunnels.

For unlock purposes, I want the Tutorial itself to finish.

<h2 id="commander-requirements">
  Commander requires three completed Tutorials
</h2>

Commander is where the prerequisite is easier to misread.

I originally expected:

**Commander Training → Commander unlocked.**

But the actual **Requirements Not Met** message lists three Tutorials:

**Getting Started**

**Commander**

and:

**Squad Leader.**

My Commander unlock route is therefore:

**Getting Started → Squad Leader → Commander.**

I do not skip Squad Leader just because Commander has its own dedicated training
session.

That is the most important correction on this page.

If I finish Commander first and still see **Requirements Not Met**, the first
thing I check is not whether Commander Training was long enough.

I check whether **Squad Leader Training is also complete**.

<h2 id="why-squad-leader-comes-first">
  I finish Squad Leader before taking Commander anyway
</h2>

The prerequisite also makes sense from the way the command structure works.

Squad Leaders are the layer between normal unit members and the Commander.

So even apart from the unlock requirement, I want to understand:

- how a squad is organized;
- how leadership communication works;
- how forward spawns affect the unit;
- and what Squad Leaders can realistically do when Commander asks for
  something.

I do not need to master every Squad Leader strategy before unlocking Commander.

I just want enough context that Commander orders are connected to something I
have already done myself.

The actual Commander match strategy belongs in the
<Link href="/hell-let-loose-vietnam/usa-commander-guide">
  {" "}USA Commander guide
</Link>
.

This page only handles getting the role available in the first place.

<h2 id="requirements-not-met">
  I distinguish a normal requirement from a registration problem
</h2>

There are two very different situations that can look similar.

### I have not completed every Tutorial listed by the game

That is a normal prerequisite.

For Squad Leader:

**Getting Started + Squad Leader.**

For Commander:

**Getting Started + Squad Leader + Commander.**

I finish the missing Tutorial.

### I already completed the required training

That is when I stop treating the problem as a normal unlock requirement.

There have been live cases where Commander behaved as though training had not
been completed even after the player had already completed it.

In one case, the player had previously been able to use Commander and later saw
the role treated as locked while Squad Leader was still available.

That is a different problem from simply forgetting Squad Leader Training.

<h2 id="completed-training-still-locked">
  If all required Tutorials are complete, I test before replaying everything
</h2>

If I know the requirements are complete but the role still does not behave
normally, I do not immediately spend another long session repeating every
Tutorial.

My order is:

**1. Confirm the exact prerequisites again.**

For Commander, that means all three:

**Getting Started + Squad Leader + Commander.**

**2. Return to the main menu.**

I want the game to reload the role state rather than repeatedly opening the same
role slot.

**3. Restart the game if the role still does not register.**

Then I enter a server and test the role again.

**4. Check Squad Leader separately.**

If Squad Leader works but Commander does not, that tells me the basic leadership
path is at least being recognized differently from Commander.

**5. Only then consider repeating the affected Tutorial.**

I use replaying training as a practical retry, not as proof that the original
requirement secretly changed.

At launch, players have encountered cases where the game claimed required
training was incomplete even after it had been completed.

So I separate:

**missing prerequisite**

from:

**completed prerequisite not being recognized.**

<h2 id="commander-training-alone">
  Repeating only Commander Training does not replace the three prerequisites
</h2>

This is the mistake I most want to avoid.

If Commander still says **Requirements Not Met**, repeatedly finishing Commander
Training does not change the requirement shown by the game.

The role still expects:

**Getting Started**

**Squad Leader**

**Commander.**

So before replaying Commander, I check the other two.

That prevents me from solving the same Tutorial twice while the actual missing
requirement is somewhere else.

<h2 id="training-menu-confusion">
  The Tutorials menu does not always make completion obvious at a glance
</h2>

One thing that made this more annoying for me was checking which Tutorial I had
already finished.

The Basic Training menu presents the different lessons, but I do not rely only
on remembering:

**I think I did that one already.**

I remember reaching a point where I entered Squad Leader Training again before
realizing I had already completed that lesson.

That is why I prefer two stronger checks:

**Did I actually reach Tutorial Complete?**

and:

**What does the role's Requirements Not Met message currently ask for?**

Those are more useful than guessing from how familiar the Tutorial looks.

<h2 id="squad-leader-unlocked">
  Once Squad Leader unlocks, I do not need to take it every match
</h2>

Unlocking Squad Leader and being ready to lead every public squad are two
different things.

I wanted the role available because there were matches where I needed to:

- create my own unit;
- keep my squad together;
- replace a missing forward spawn;
- or stop depending on another Squad Leader to solve the same problem.

But if I am still learning the map or simply want to play another role, I do
not force myself into leadership because I finally unlocked it.

The unlock gives me an option.

It does not create an obligation.

<h2 id="commander-unlocked">
  I take Commander after the role unlock and the battlefield systems make sense
</h2>

I use the same rule with Commander.

The moment the role becomes selectable is not necessarily the moment I want to
run the team.

Before I volunteer for Commander, I want to be comfortable reading:

- the Tactical Map;
- the team's spawn situation;
- where Squad Leaders are trying to move;
- and what the team is actually asking for.

If those systems still feel confusing, I keep learning them from another role
first.

When I am ready to lead the US side, I use the
<Link href="/hell-let-loose-vietnam/usa-commander-guide">
  {" "}USA Commander guide
</Link>
for the actual match decisions.

<h2 id="unlock-troubleshooting">
  What I check when Squad Leader or Commander is still unavailable
</h2>

I keep the final check short.

### Squad Leader says Requirements Not Met

I complete:

**Getting Started + Squad Leader.**

### Commander says Requirements Not Met

I complete:

**Getting Started + Squad Leader + Commander.**

### I finished Commander Training but Commander is still locked

I check **Getting Started and Squad Leader** before repeating Commander.

Commander Training alone is not the full requirement.

### I finished the required Tutorials but the game still says I did not

I return to the menu, restart, and test the role again.

If the same problem remains, I treat repeating the affected Tutorial as a
registration retry rather than assuming I misunderstood the prerequisite.

### Squad Leader works but Commander does not

I re-check the three Commander prerequisites individually.

That situation has occurred even when Squad Leader itself was selectable.

### Squad Leader Training is blocked by the Tunnel objective

I finish the specific E5 connection step through the
<Link href="/hell-let-loose-vietnam/tutorial-connect-tunnel-garrison">
  {" "}Connect Tunnel to Garrison guide
</Link>
instead of restarting the whole training session.

Once I separated the explicit Tutorial requirements from the occasional
registration problem, the role screen became much easier to diagnose.

**Requirements Not Met** first tells me what training is missing.

Only after I know every listed Tutorial is complete do I start treating it as
something else.
