There are **49 achievements in Hell Let Loose: Vietnam**, but I do not treat
them as 49 separate grinds.

A lot of them overlap naturally while I am already learning:

- both factions;
- different roles;
- maps and modes;
- boats and helicopters;
- building;
- Career progression;
- and normal combat.

The achievements I deliberately plan around are the ones with a **one-match
condition**, an unusual interaction, a hidden requirement, or a counter large
enough that I do not want to discover it at the very end.

My overall route is:

**learn the systems → let easy achievements unlock naturally → clear the four
hidden achievements → finish one-match conditions → overlap map and mode wins →
push different roles toward Level 5 → finish the largest counters last.**

<h2 id="best-100-percent-route">
  The 100% route I use
</h2>

I split the run into six stages.

### 1. Let the first matches clear the natural achievements

I start by playing both factions, learning the main systems, and finishing the
Tutorials I actually need.

Early achievements such as a first kill, faction wins, Career milestones, and
using a Tunnel can unlock while I am still learning the game.

There are also achievements for completing **4 different Tutorials** and
**8 different Tutorials**, so I let leadership and role training contribute to
those instead of treating Tutorial completion as a separate grind.

### 2. Target the unusual and hidden conditions

Once I understand Tunnels, boats, helicopters, Medic interactions, and building,
I start targeting achievements that are unlikely to happen accidentally.

### 3. Clear the one-match requirements

Anything that explicitly needs to happen **in one match** gets priority when the
right role and lobby are available.

I do not want to discover near the end that something I assumed was cumulative
actually needed one concentrated attempt.

### 4. Overlap maps and game modes

The six map achievements require **3 wins on each launch map**.

The four mode achievements require **20 wins in each mode**.

Every match already has both a map and a mode, so I track those together.

### 5. Spread Role Level 5 progress deliberately

The role achievements build from:

**1 role → 3 roles → 6 roles → 12 roles at Level 5.**

I use roles that also help with other achievement conditions whenever possible.

### 6. Finish large cumulative counters last

Only after the targeted achievements are mostly gone do I deliberately farm
things such as:

- 100 headshots;
- 100 Artillery kills;
- 100 Manpower Nodes;
- 100 Fuel Nodes;
- 100 Munitions Nodes;
- Career Level 50.

Those are the counters I do not want to grind twice.

<h2 id="hidden-achievements">
  The four hidden achievements
</h2>

Only four achievements are hidden, so I keep these visible in my checklist from
the beginning.

<h3 id="caved-in">
  Caved In — dismantle an enemy Tunnel
</h3>

**Caved In** requires me to **dismantle an enemy Tunnel**.

I target this while already fighting the NVA rather than creating a separate
match around it.

In one backline push, we found the enemy Tunnel and dismantled it instead of
chasing another infantry kill.

<h3 id="shipwrecked">
  Shipwrecked — kill an enemy player with a boat
</h3>

**Shipwrecked** is easier to justify when I combine it with the other boat
achievements.

The same useful boat session can work toward:

**Floater** — drive a boat for 3 minutes in one match.

**Gonna Need Another Boat** — destroy an enemy boat.

**Shipwrecked** — kill an enemy player with a boat.

I therefore look for a match where boats are already relevant around a river,
crossing, or active shoreline.

If I am only driving through empty water, I use the boat for transport and wait
for a better opportunity instead of forcing the kill.

<h3 id="death-from-below">
  Death From Below — the pilot is the target
</h3>

This is the hidden helicopter achievement I keep separate from **Eagle Has
Landed**.

**Death From Below** requires me to kill an **airborne helicopter pilot while
the pilot is still seated**.

That means I am not simply trying to destroy the aircraft.

I need the pilot.

I do not spend an entire quiet match staring at the sky if the enemy helicopter
crews are barely operating.

I keep the achievement unfinished in the checklist and take the attempt when an
airborne helicopter is already relevant to the fight.

<h3 id="eagle-has-landed">
  Eagle Has Landed — destroy an enemy helicopter
</h3>

**Eagle Has Landed** targets the aircraft instead:

**destroy an enemy helicopter.**

I keep the distinction simple:

**Death From Below → pilot**

**Eagle Has Landed → helicopter**

If I am still struggling with the aircraft systems themselves, I learn them
through the
<Link href="/hell-let-loose-vietnam/helicopter-controls">
  {" "}Helicopter Controls guide
</Link>
instead of mixing basic flight practice with achievement cleanup.

<h2 id="guardian-angel">
  Guardian Angel — drag one wounded ally 20 meters in one attempt
</h2>

**Guardian Angel** is one of the achievements I deliberately set up because the
condition is unusually specific:

**as Medic, drag a wounded ally 20 meters in one attempt.**

The important part for me is:

**one attempt.**

I do not drag somebody a short distance, stop, revive them, and assume several
small pulls will add together.

I look for a casualty where the ground behind me gives enough room to keep
dragging toward actual cover.

My preferred situation is:

**wounded teammate just outside cover → begin the pull → keep moving toward a
wall, trench, building, or dense vegetation → finish the long drag → revive.**

The interaction I used was:

**hold F to pull the wounded player.**

If I am specifically attempting Guardian Angel, I do not revive immediately.

I complete the drag first.

Then I turn the achievement attempt back into normal Medic play and revive the
teammate.

<h2 id="pilot-achievements">
  I combine the pilot achievements with useful Transport runs
</h2>

I stack the helicopter achievements instead of flying around HQ only for a
counter.

**Flight of the Valkyrie** requires me to pilot a helicopter for **3 minutes in
one match**.

**LZ Not Secure** requires me, as Pilot, to drop **30 friendly troops into enemy
territory**.

The second achievement is the longer job, so I let the three-minute pilot
condition happen during real Transport work.

My loop is:

**pick up troops → confirm the destination → approach the enemy-side LZ → get
the passengers out → leave the LZ → return for another useful pickup.**

I do not hover over the same landing zone after everybody has exited.

That only makes losing the helicopter more likely before I can make the next
drop.

Takeoff, landing, Collective, Helipad, Recon Flare, and Supply Helo controls
remain in the Helicopter guide.

<h2 id="one-match-achievements">
  The one-match achievements I do not leave until the end
</h2>

There are several conditions where I commit to the same role or vehicle long
enough to finish the requirement in one match.

### Bloodthirsty and Relentless

These require:

**5 kills without dying**

and:

**12 kills without dying.**

I do not start every life thinking about the 12-kill streak.

But if I am already four or five kills into a life, I stop taking the same
low-value risks I would take at the beginning.

### Floater

Drive a boat for **3 minutes in one match**.

I combine it with the other boat conditions whenever the match gives me a useful
water route.

### Flight of the Valkyrie

Pilot a helicopter for **3 minutes in one match**.

A normal Transport or logistics run can handle this without a separate farm.

### Can we fix it?

Build or upgrade **30 objects or structures in one match**.

This is the one where I commit to the building job early.

I do not wait until the final ten minutes and then discover I still need most of
the thirty actions.

I use the team's real defensive and resource needs to keep the building session
useful while I finish the counter.

<h2 id="map-win-achievements">
  I track the six 3-win maps together
</h2>

The six map achievements each require **3 wins**:

**Cam Ranh Port**

**Vạn Tường**

**Thanh Hòa Bridge**

**Đăk Tô Airfield**

**Quảng Ngãi**

**Huế Outskirts**

That is **18 winning results across the six map targets**.

Early in the run, I do not chase one map while almost everything else is still
unfinished.

Any useful win is progress.

Later, once only one or two map achievements remain, server rotation starts to
matter more.

That is when I stay in lobbies that are actually moving toward the maps I still
need rather than repeatedly completing maps whose achievement is already done.

<h2 id="game-mode-wins">
  I overlap the four 20-win mode achievements with map cleanup
</h2>

The long mode counters are:

**20 Conquest wins**

**20 Domination wins**

**20 Offensive wins**

**20 Warfare wins**

I do not treat those as four completely separate grinds.

A Warfare win on Huế Outskirts can move both:

**the Warfare total**

and:

**the Huế Outskirts map achievement.**

Early on, I play the modes I still need and avoid abandoning useful matches
because the map is not perfect.

Near the end, I use the checklist to find whichever mode is lagging behind and
start prioritizing that one.

<h2 id="role-level-five">
  I start the 12-role Level 5 grind early
</h2>

The role achievements are nested:

**Alfa One** — 1 Role to Level 5.

**Charlie Three** — 3 Roles to Level 5.

**Foxtrot Six** — 6 Roles to Level 5.

**Lima One Two** — 12 Roles to Level 5.

I do not wait until every other achievement is complete before diversifying
roles.

Instead, I overlap them with targeted conditions.

For example:

- Medic while working on Guardian Angel;
- Pilot while working on Flight of the Valkyrie and LZ Not Secure;
- building roles while working on construction and Node counters;
- combat roles while working through their normal progression.

The actual equipment, weight, slots, and unlock decisions belong in the
<Link href="/hell-let-loose-vietnam/progression-loadouts">
  {" "}Progression and Loadouts guide
</Link>
.

For achievements, my only concern is avoiding unnecessary XP on roles that have
already reached Level 5 while several others still need it.

<h2 id="career-levels">
  Career Level 50 stays in the background
</h2>

The Career milestones arrive at:

**Level 10**

**Level 20**

**Level 30**

**Level 40**

**Level 50**

I do not farm these separately.

By the time I am working through:

- twelve Level 5 roles;
- map wins;
- mode wins;
- construction;
- Nodes;
- Artillery;
- vehicle conditions;

Career XP has already had a lot of time to accumulate.

If Career Level 50 genuinely becomes my last remaining achievement, I keep
playing the roles and modes I still enjoy.

Until then, it is not a dedicated cleanup problem.

<h2 id="node-achievements">
  The three Node achievements mean 300 total builds
</h2>

There are separate cumulative achievements for:

**100 Manpower Nodes**

**100 Fuel Nodes**

**100 Munitions Nodes**

That is **300 Node builds** across the three resource types.

I start contributing to these early because waiting until 46 or 47 achievements
are already complete would turn them into a much more artificial grind.

I prefer building Nodes when the team actually needs the income.

That way the counter progresses while I am still contributing to the match.

If I play regularly with the same group, I rotate into the building job instead
of assuming somebody else will always handle it.

<h2 id="long-cleanup">
  The long counters I leave for the final cleanup
</h2>

Several requirements are straightforward but can take a long time.

### Marksman

Get **100 total headshots**.

I let normal infantry combat move this counter before deliberately chasing it.

### Demolition Man

Detonate **10 satchel charges that I placed**.

I make sure I am placing the satchel myself rather than assuming another
player's charge helps my counter.

### Is it a Bird?

Get **100 Artillery kills**.

This is large enough that I only start intentionally farming it once I know how
much remains.

### Don't Look Down

Destroy a vehicle with an **AT mine**.

I keep the appropriate mine loadout in mind when enemy vehicles are repeatedly
using predictable roads or crossings.

### F.U.B.A.R

Destroy a vehicle with an **Artillery Strike or Mortar**.

I keep this separate from **Is it a Bird?**

One is a specific vehicle-destruction condition.

The other is the much larger cumulative Artillery kill total.

<h2 id="missing-achievement">
  When one achievement is missing, I identify its category first
</h2>

Near the end, I stop playing random matches and diagnose the unfinished
category.

### Hidden achievement missing

I check:

**Caved In → Shipwrecked → Death From Below → Eagle Has Landed.**

Those are the four hidden achievements.

### Map achievement missing

I check which map still needs its third win.

### Mode achievement missing

I check Conquest, Domination, Offensive, and Warfare individually.

### Role progression missing

I count how many roles have actually reached **Level 5**.

The final milestone needs **12**.

### Node achievement missing

I separate the counters:

**Manpower → Fuel → Munitions.**

Building a lot of Nodes overall does not tell me which individual 100-build
achievement is unfinished.

### Guardian Angel missing

I deliberately create one continuous **20-meter drag** before reviving.

### LZ Not Secure missing

I stop doing empty helicopter repositioning and plan repeated useful troop drops
into enemy territory.

That is much faster than hoping the final achievement appears while I am playing
something unrelated.

<h2 id="final-cleanup-rule">
  My final 49/49 check
</h2>

Before I start farming random actions, I check four layers.

**Specific actions**

Hidden achievements, Guardian Angel, vehicle conditions, building tasks, and
other one-off requirements.

**Match results**

All six map achievements and all four mode achievements.

**Progression**

Twelve roles at Level 5 and Career Level 50.

**Long counters**

Headshots, Nodes, Artillery kills, satchel detonations, and any other cumulative
target still unchecked.

Once I know which layer is incomplete, the final grind becomes much more
specific.

I am no longer asking:

**What achievement am I somehow missing?**

I am asking:

**Which exact category still has unfinished work?**

The checklist gives me that answer.
