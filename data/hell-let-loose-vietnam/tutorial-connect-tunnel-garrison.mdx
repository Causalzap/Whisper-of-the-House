<h2 id="connect-tunnel-garrison">
  Open the Tactical Map and select the E5 Tunnel first
</h2>

This was the point in Squad Leader Training where I briefly stopped knowing what
the game wanted from me.

A few moments earlier I had been using the Field Pad to place structures in the
world.

Then the objective changed to:

**Connect a Tunnel to a Garrison.**

My first instinct was to keep walking around with the Field Pad looking for
another placement area.

That was the mistake.

This connection is made from the **Tactical Map**.

The Tunnel for this tutorial step has already been placed by Command in
**E5**, so I do not need to build anything else first.

I use this exact sequence:

**1. Open the Tactical Map.**

**2. Find the Tunnel icon in E5.**

**3. Select the Tunnel icon.**

**4. Select the Garrison I built during the previous step.**

The connection is created as soon as I select the correct two structures.

<h2 id="why-it-feels-stuck">
  The tutorial changes interfaces without much warning
</h2>

The reason this step confused me is that the actions immediately before it are
physical placement tasks.

I had already been:

- placing an Outpost;
- placing a Garrison;
- placing a Tunnel;
- and watching territory change.

So when the game suddenly asked for a connection, I expected another Field Pad
action.

Instead, the workflow changes:

**place the structures in the world → connect them on the Tactical Map.**

That is the one distinction I need to remember for this blocker.

If I am still holding the Field Pad and searching the jungle for another marker,
I am already on the wrong path.

<h2 id="use-e5-tunnel">
  Do not build another Tunnel — use the one in E5
</h2>

Another easy way to waste time is assuming the tutorial expects me to make a new
Tunnel specifically for the connection.

It does not.

Command has already placed the Tunnel used by this objective in **E5**.

My route through this part of training is therefore:

**build the requested Garrison → open Tactical Map → find E5 → select Tunnel →
select Garrison.**

I do not go back into the world looking for another Tunnel placement zone.

Adding another structure only makes the map more confusing when the objective is
already pointing me toward a specific one.

<h2 id="enter-garrison">
  When the connection works, the objective changes to Fast Travel
</h2>

The easiest way I know the connection succeeded is that the tutorial stops
asking me to link the structures.

The next step introduces fast travel and tells me to **enter the Garrison**.

<h2 id="dismantle-friendly-tunnel">
  The next step switches back to the Field Pad
</h2>

After the fast-travel section, the tutorial eventually asks me to:

**Dismantle Friendly Tunnel.**

This is another interface switch.

For the connection step I needed the Tactical Map.

For dismantling, I need to go back to the physical Tunnel and use the
**Field Pad**.

If I am still trying to select map icons at this point, I have stayed in the

<h2 id="connection-does-not-work">
  What I check if the objective still does not advance
</h2>

I use a short checklist rather than placing random structures.

### I am still using the Field Pad

I put it away and open the Tactical Map.

The connection is not created in the world.

### I cannot find the correct Tunnel

I look at **E5**.

The tutorial has already placed the Tunnel I need there.

### I built another Tunnel and nothing changed

I go back to the E5 Tunnel.

The extra Tunnel is not required for this objective.

### I selected the wrong icons

I reset and use:

**E5 Tunnel → the Garrison I just built.**

I do not try to connect two Garrison icons.

### The objective now says Fast Travel

The connection already worked.

I stop trying to rebuild it and enter the Garrison.

### The objective now says Dismantle Tunnel

I switch back to the Field Pad and move to the physical Tunnel.

The connection portion is already complete.

<h2 id="finish-squad-leader-training">
  After this blocker, I follow the objective rather than repeating the same action
</h2>

The main thing this sequence taught me was to watch **which interface the current
objective expects**.

For this section of Squad Leader Training:

**placing structures → Field Pad**

**connecting Tunnel to Garrison → Tactical Map**

**fast travel → enter the connected structure**

**dismantling → Field Pad again**

Once I stopped trying to solve every Tunnel objective with the same tool, the
rest of the lesson moved much faster.
