The **Servant of the Lake Coffee puzzle** is one of those puzzles where I spent
more time staring at the lock than I should have.

The coffee itself is easy.

The real problem is getting the **Coffee Beans**, because the three-digit code
does not come from the grinder or the kitchen.

I have to walk back through the house, look at the three Vanderbooms, notice
which direction each one is pointing, and match those directions to the
number clue.

The result is:

**Alexandra = 3, Aldous = 1, William = 5**

So the Coffee code is:

**315**

<h2 id="when-coffee-starts">
  When the Coffee puzzle starts
</h2>

**Preparing Coffee** is the first main task on the morning of Day 2.

Kate tells me the schedule before I start:

William needs waking up after a terrible night, Aldous wants to go hunting
later, Alexandra wants her bath in the evening, but first I need to make some
**strong coffee for the gentlemen**.

The problem is that I do not begin Day 2 with Coffee Beans.

I can find the grinder, but having the grinder does not help until I solve the
three-digit clue.

That is why I leave the coffee equipment alone for a moment and investigate the
house instead.

<h2 id="coffee-code-clue">
  Where I find the clue for the Coffee code
</h2>

The first thing that threw me off was the changed portraits.

Some of the familiar visual information from the previous day is missing or
different, so I initially kept checking the same places expecting a number to
appear directly.

What matters is the **direction each Vanderboom is pointing**.

I check them one at a time.

That is much safer than trying to remember all three directions while moving
between rooms.

<h2 id="alexandra-three">
  Alexandra gives me 3
</h2>

I start with **Alexandra**.

She is pointing **up**.

When I match that direction to the number clue, I get:

**Alexandra = 3**

I keep that as the first value.

<h2 id="aldous-one">
  Aldous gives me 1
</h2>

Next I check **Aldous**.

His pointing direction maps to:

**Aldous = 1**

Now I have:

**3 → 1**

At this point I know I am not supposed to enter the values in the order I
happened to discover the rooms.

I am building the three-number answer from the three Vanderboom clues.

<h2 id="william-five">
  William gives me 5
</h2>

The remaining clue comes from **William**.

His direction points me to:

**William = 5**

That completes the sequence:

**3 → 1 → 5**

The directions are not the answer themselves.

They are telling me **which number belongs to each person**.

<h2 id="coffee-code-315">

</h2>

I enter:

**315**

The lock accepts it and gives me the **Coffee Beans**.

If `315` is not working, the first thing I check is whether I am interacting
with the correct three-digit puzzle.

I do not reverse it to `513` just because William gave me the value 5.

The working combination is:

**315**

Now I return to the coffee grinder I found earlier.

I put the Coffee Beans into it and grind them.

That gives me:

**Ground Coffee**

The single puzzle reward is what I need to continue the normal morning route.

<h2 id="serve-aldous">
  Serve Aldous the Coffee first
</h2>

With the coffee prepared, I return to **Aldous**.

He asks me to pour his coffee.

I serve it.

He then reminds me not to be late for the hunt later that afternoon.

That line is also my confirmation that I have completed his part of Preparing
Coffee correctly.

<h2 id="bring-coffee-william">
  Then bring the Coffee to William
</h2>

I am not finished just because Aldous has had his cup.

The next instruction is to bring coffee to **William**, through the door to the
left.

That is where the Day 2 morning route shifts away from the Coffee puzzle and
into waking William.

I stop looking for another Coffee code at this point.

The normal progression is:

**direction clues → 315 → Coffee Beans → Ground Coffee → serve Aldous → bring
coffee to William.**

<h2 id="coffee-puzzle-stuck">
  What I check when Preparing Coffee is not progressing
</h2>

If I found the grinder but have **no Coffee Beans**, I am too early in the
ingredient chain.

I go back to the three Vanderboom directional clues.

If the portraits or figures seem useless, I stop searching them for written
digits and pay attention to **where they point**.

If I have three values but the lock rejects my answer, I use:

**Alexandra 3 → Aldous 1 → William 5**

which gives:

**315**

If I already have the Coffee Beans but nothing happens at the coffee setup, I
grind them first.

If Aldous has already accepted his coffee and the task still seems unfinished,
I follow the next instruction instead of making another pot:

**bring the coffee to William through the door to the left.**

That is the point where I leave the Coffee puzzle behind and continue Day 2.
