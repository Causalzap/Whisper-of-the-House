The **Waking up Aldous puzzle in Servant of the Lake** is the first Day 1 task
where I have several correct-looking interactions available at the same time,
but only one useful order for getting through the whole morning routine.

The note in Aldous's bedroom gives me the structure:

**open the curtains → wake him at 7:00 → shave → breakfast → exercise → dress
up**

It also warns me that he **doesn't like cold feet**.

The parts that actually stopped me were not the obvious instructions. They were
finding the Razor, dealing with the result of the shave, working out what was
missing from his porridge, and realizing the Toy Soldier is the exercise clue.

<h2 id="aldous-morning-routine">
  I use the morning routine note as the order of operations
</h2>

The most useful clue in the room is not a hidden code.

It is Aldous's routine itself.

It tells me to:

**open the curtains**

**wake him at 7:00**

**shave**

**serve breakfast**

**exercise**

**dress up**

There is also a warning:

**he doesn't like cold feet.**

I open the curtains first.

Then I deal with the clock.

The target time is:

**7:00**

If the clock cannot be set properly yet, I make sure the missing **Clock Hand**
has been added before trying to force the dial.

I put them on him during the morning routine and then concentrate on the shave.

<h2 id="get-razor">
  How I get the Razor
</h2>

Aldous wakes up and immediately tells me he needs a shave.

The Razor is not sitting openly beside the bed.

The box in the room is the important object.

I rearrange its movable blocks so that they line up with the corresponding
symbols.

I do not look for a numerical combination here.

It is a **matching / positioning puzzle**.

<h2 id="shave-earlobe">
  Shaving Aldous — yes, the earlobe is supposed to come off
</h2>

This was the first moment where I genuinely wondered whether I had failed the
task.

I use the Razor manually on Aldous's face.

During the shave, his **earlobe gets cut off**.

Aldous reacts badly and tells me I am not off to a good start, but this does not
mean I need to reload.

The sequence continues.

I pick up the **Earlobe**.

Then Aldous tells me:

**clean my ear.**

This produced another real blocker for me because I had already used the Razor
and expected the game to advance automatically.

It does not.

<h2 id="clean-aldous-ear">
  Use the Cloth to clean Aldous's ear
</h2>

The item I need here is the **Cloth**.

I use it on Aldous's injured ear.

If he keeps repeating that I need to clean his ear, I do not search for a new
medical item, another blade, or a way to reverse the shave.

I check my inventory for the Cloth.

That was the entire problem when I got stuck here.

The Earlobe remains in my inventory after the cleanup, and that is deliberate.

I do not try to put it back onto Aldous.

<h2 id="earlobe-crow">
  Give the earlobe to the crow
</h2>

The crow at the window looked like background flavor when I first opened the
curtains.

After the shave, it finally has a purpose.

I give the **Earlobe** to the crow.

That interaction advances the small item chain around the locked storage in
Aldous's room.

The important result is that I can now get the **Berries**.

This is the point where Aldous's strange morning routine becomes much easier to
read.

The chain is:

**Razor → Earlobe → crow → access to Berries**

rather than four unrelated interactions.

<h2 id="aldous-porridge">
  What is missing from Aldous's porridge — Berries
</h2>

I already made porridge before entering the room, so when Aldous says
**"Something is missing"**, my first instinct was to think I had cooked it
incorrectly.

I had not.

The missing ingredient is the **Berries**.

I take the Berries from the newly accessible locked storage and add them to the
porridge.

Then I bring the completed breakfast back to Aldous.

Aldous is still not impressed by the bowl on its own.

I continue the serving interaction until he tells me to **feed him**.

Once he has eaten enough, the routine moves to exercise.

<h2 id="exercise-sequence">
  Aldous exercise solution — Left, Right, Down, Right, Right, Down
</h2>

This is the other major hard stop in the room.

Aldous starts exercising, but simply clicking directions randomly does not
finish the sequence.

The clue is the **Toy Soldier** I picked up earlier.

Its movements match what Aldous expects me to reproduce.

The correct sequence is:

**Left → Right → Down → Right → Right → Down**

The moment this works, Aldous says the exercise helps him stay young.

That line is my confirmation that I am done with the movement puzzle.

I do not keep repeating the sequence.

<h2 id="dress-aldous">
  Dress Aldous to finish the task
</h2>

The final step is much more direct.

Aldous asks me to prepare his outfit.

I put the clothing pieces on him and finish dressing him.

Once he thanks me and starts talking about his brother and sister arriving, I
know **Waking up Aldous** is complete.

The next objective is no longer inside his bedroom.

I leave instead of continuing to click the clock, crow, box, or bed.

<h2 id="aldous-stuck">
  What I check when Waking up Aldous will not progress
</h2>

When Aldous is still asleep, I check the routine instead of giving him random
inventory items:

**curtains open → Clock Hand attached → alarm at 7:00.**

If he wakes but still needs a shave, I solve the symbol box and take the Razor.

If the shave goes wrong and his earlobe comes off, I **do not reload**.

I continue the sequence.

If Aldous keeps telling me to clean his ear, I use the **Cloth**.

If breakfast says **something is missing**, I stop remaking the base porridge.
The missing ingredient comes from the earlobe → crow → locked Berries chain.

If exercise never finishes, I stop guessing.

I copy the Toy Soldier exactly:

**Left → Right → Down → Right → Right → Down.**

And once Aldous is dressed and tells me his siblings will arrive shortly, I
leave the room.

That is the point where I consider his Day 1 morning routine finished.
