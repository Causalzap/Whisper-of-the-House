The **Servant of the Lake laundry puzzle** looks almost finished long before it
actually is.

That was what caught me.

I collected the dirty clothes, dealt with Alexandra's baby, washed everything,
and hung the laundry to dry. Then nothing happened.

The missing step is the clothesline itself.

Each piece of laundry has a different number of line markings. I have to arrange
all seven pieces in order from **0 lines to 6 lines**.

Once that order is correct, I get the **Bathroom Key** and can move directly
into Alexandra's next Day 2 task.

<h2 id="when-laundry-starts">
  When Doing the Washing starts on Day 2
</h2>

I reach **Doing the Washing** in the evening after the hunting sequence.

Once I come back inside, the game sends me to laundry duty rather than letting
me go straight to Alexandra's Bathroom.

That order matters because the Bathroom is still locked.

I cannot solve that by searching the Bathroom door for another interaction.

The Key comes from finishing the laundry properly.

  First I collect all the dirty clothes

I start by checking the laundry area carefully.

There is a loose **sock** and other dirty clothing around the room.

I pick up everything I can before trying to use the wash area.

This is exactly where I got stuck.

The clothes were washed.

They were hanging.

There was no obvious missing item.

So I started checking the individual pieces instead of walking around the house
again.

That is when the line markings finally made sense.

<h2 id="clothes-order">
  The laundry clothes order is 0 through 6 lines
</h2>

Each piece has a different number of line markings.

There are **seven positions** in the final sequence.

I arrange them from the piece with no line marking through the piece with six:

**0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6**

The important detail is that the first item is not another "1."

It is the piece with **zero lines**.

Then I place:

**1 line → 2 lines → 3 lines → 4 lines → 5 lines → 6 lines**

I do not sort by:

- clothing size;
- clothing type;
- color;
- who I think the item belongs to;
- or the order I washed the pieces.

Only the **number of line markings** matters.

Once the full seven-piece sequence is correct, the puzzle releases a **Key**.

<h2 id="laundry-not-working">
  What I check when the laundry puzzle is not finishing
</h2>

If I still have dirty items in my inventory, I go back through the laundry area
before touching the clothesline.

The loose sock is easy to overlook.

If the baby is still waiting to be handled, I finish the diaper interaction and
wash the dirty diaper with the laundry.

If I have a tub full of wet clothes, I take it to the drying area instead of
continuing to interact with the wash station.

The most important check comes after that.

**If every piece is already hanging and nothing happens, I do not leave the
area.**

The game is waiting for the clothesline order.

I inspect the markings and arrange the seven pieces:

**0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.**

That is the point where I know Doing the Washing is actually complete: the
Bathroom Key appears, and I can move on to Alexandra's bath.
