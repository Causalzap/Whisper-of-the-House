The **Servant of the Lake Alchemy puzzle** was the point where Day 3 stopped
feeling like a normal room puzzle and started feeling like I had been handed an
entire language to decode.

When I first enter Aldous's lab, almost everything is represented by symbols:
numbers, directions, ingredients, family members, Water, Heat, Oil, Blood, and
the instructions for the Elixir.

The mistake that cost me the most time was trying to understand every symbol
before doing anything.

I get through this much faster by solving only the symbols I need, using them to
open the **2451 cabinet**, then producing the ingredients in a fixed order.

<h2 id="entering-alchemy-lab">
  Where the Assisting in Alchemy puzzle starts
</h2>

This is a **Day 3 Morning** task.

After I finish looking for the cat, Aldous leaves a note that leads me toward
his hidden lab.

Once I enter, he explains what the room is actually for: he wants to create the
base version of the Elixir, and the first job is to decipher the recipe by
sorting the symbols on the board.

My first reaction was that there were too many symbols to keep straight.

What helped was separating them into jobs.

Some symbols represent **numbers**.

Some represent **Left, Middle, and Right**.

Others represent actual materials such as **Water, Oil, Acid, Blood, and
Juice**.

Once I stopped treating them as one giant cipher, the room became a sequence of
small recipes.

<h2 id="left-right-symbols">
  First I solve the Left and Right symbols
</h2>

One clue gives me a sequence I can use to distinguish the two directional
symbols:

**Left → Left → Right → Right → Left → Right**

I use that pattern to identify which symbol means Left and which means Right.

This matters later because the machine does not simply ask me for "Oil" or
"Acid."

It tells me how many times to operate controls associated with different
positions.

I also identify the **Middle** position when the family clues make it clear
that Aldous occupies the middle.

I do not move on until I can reliably tell:

**Left / Middle / Right**

from one another.

Mixing up those three controls is the easiest way to make the Oil and Acid
recipes look as though they are not working.

<h2 id="alchemy-2451-code">
  The Alchemy cabinet code is 2451
</h2>

The next real blocker is the four-digit cabinet.

Several number symbols have already been established, but I found it easy to
overthink the remaining ones because the dial only uses values from the small
set shown by the puzzle.

The completed code is:

**2451**

Opening this matters for more than just getting another object.

It confirms more of the number-symbol language I need for the recipes around
the lab.

At this point, I stop spending time trying to solve the entire board
theoretically.

I start making the actual ingredients.

<h2 id="get-egg">
  How I get the Egg
</h2>

One recipe asks for:

**5 Water**

I put **five drops of Water** into the indicated container.

That gives me the **Egg**.

The Egg is important enough that I keep it aside instead of immediately
experimenting with it.

The Elixir recipe explicitly tells me that the Egg goes into the final bottle
**first**.

That word matters.

If I begin adding Juice, Acid, Oil, or Blood before the Egg, I am solving the
right recipe in the wrong order.

<h2 id="make-oil">
  How I make Oil — 2 Left + 3 Middle
</h2>

Oil was the first ingredient where I realized the machine is really just asking
me to translate symbols into control inputs.

For Oil, I use:

**2 Left + 3 Middle**

I press the Left control twice, then the Middle control three times.

The reason I emphasize the controls is that I initially confused the result
with Water.

The safe check is not the color of the liquid.

It is whether I entered the correct recipe:

**Left, Left, Middle, Middle, Middle.**

Once I know that, I treat the output as Oil and move on.

<h2 id="make-acid">
  How I make Acid — 4 Right + 1 Left
</h2>

Acid uses a different input:

**4 Right + 1 Left**

I press Right four times, then Left once.

That produces **Acid**.

This is where Oil and Acid finally stop being interchangeable mystery liquids
for me.

I remember them as two compact formulas:

**Oil = 2 Left + 3 Middle**

**Acid = 4 Right + 1 Left**

I do not try to memorize their symbols separately once those formulas are
working.

<h2 id="get-blood">
  Where I get Blood — the bird is not the answer
</h2>

This was my biggest practical dead end in the whole room.

The board tells me that **Meat can be processed into Blood**.

There is a bird in the lab.

So naturally, I started looking at the bird as the obvious source of Meat.

That is not the route I need.

The useful clue instead combines **Acid with Aldous**.

I use the Acid on Aldous and get a **boil from his tongue**.

That gives me **Blood**.

This is the point where the rest of the ingredient chain starts moving again.

If I have Oil and Acid but cannot progress, Blood is the ingredient I check
next.

<h2 id="strange-fruit">
  How I get the Strange Fruit
</h2>

Now I use another recipe from the board:

**4 Water + 1 Blood**

I add four drops of Water and one drop of Blood.

The result is a **Strange Fruit**.

This solves another question that had me circling the room:

**Where does the fruit come from?**

I do not need to find a normal piece of fruit elsewhere in the house.

I make it in the lab.

<h2 id="make-juice">
  How I turn the Strange Fruit into Juice
</h2>

The board shows the next transformation:

**Fruit → process 4 times → Juice**

I put the Strange Fruit into the machine and operate it four times.

That produces **Juice**.

This is also why I do not try to finish the Elixir as soon as I find the Egg.

The final bottle needs several processed ingredients, and Juice depends on a
chain that itself depends on Blood:

**Acid → Aldous material → Blood → Strange Fruit → Juice**

Once I see that dependency, the order of the room makes much more sense.

<h2 id="final-elixir-recipe">
  The final Elixir recipe and correct order
</h2>

Before touching the final bottle, I make sure I have:

- the **Egg**;
- **Juice**;
- **Acid**;
- **Oil**;
- and enough **Blood** for three drops.

Then I follow the recipe exactly.

First:

**Egg**

Then:

**1 Juice → 1 Acid → 1 Oil → 3 Blood**

The complete order I use is therefore:

**Egg → Juice → Acid → Oil → Blood → Blood → Blood**

Once the mixture is accepted, Aldous takes the bottle and says the journey to
enlightenment starts here.

That completes the normal **Assisting in Alchemy** sequence.

<h2 id="alchemy-not-working">
  What I check when the Alchemy puzzle is not working
</h2>

I had several points where I thought the game was refusing a correct answer,
but each one came down to a different missing dependency.

**If I cannot tell Oil from Acid**, I stop judging the liquid visually.

I remake them from the controls:

**Oil = 2 Left + 3 Middle**

**Acid = 4 Right + 1 Left**

**If I cannot find Meat for Blood**, I stop trying to use the bird.

I make Acid, use the Acid clue involving Aldous, take the resulting material,
and process it four times.

**If I cannot find Fruit**, I make it.

The Strange Fruit comes from:

**4 Water + 1 Blood**

**If I think I have no Water**, I check what I already collected before
restarting recipes. This room made me search for an ingredient that was already
sitting in my inventory.

**If the final bottle refuses the recipe**, I empty my mental sequence and start
again with the Egg.

The correct order is:

**Egg first → 1 Juice → 1 Acid → 1 Oil → 3 Blood**

I do not add extra drops between those steps.
