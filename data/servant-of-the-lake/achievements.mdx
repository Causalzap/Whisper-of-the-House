There are **49 achievements in Servant of the Lake**, but I do not try to
collect all of them during my first trip through the Vanderboom estate.

That made the first run much cleaner.

I split the list into two groups:

- **24 main task achievements** that follow the three-day story;
- **25 optional achievements** tied to extra interactions, alternate actions,
  and hidden post-game tasks.

My preferred 100% route is:

**finish the three-day story → unlock chapter replay → revisit the missing
optional stars → finish the secret-location route.**

That is much faster than restarting large sections every time I realize I
missed a small interaction.

<h2 id="day-1-achievements">
  Day 1 achievements
</h2>

Day 1 contains **9 main task achievements** plus **9 optional achievements**.

The main ones come naturally from finishing the first day's schedule.

### Main Day 1 task achievements

- **The Arrival** — Morning, Day 1 — **92.9%**
- **Preparing Breakfast** — Morning, Day 1 — **91.7%**
- **Waking up Aldous** — Morning, Day 1 — **89.5%**
- **Preparing the Reception** — Afternoon, Day 1 — **86.5%**
- **Welcoming the Guests** — Afternoon, Day 1 — **79.2%**
- **Serving Tea** — Evening, Day 1 — **76.6%**
- **Opening the Reunion** — Evening, Day 1 — **71.6%**
- **The Night Watch** — Night, Day 1 — **65.7%**
- **Searching the Attic** — Night, Day 1 — **61.4%**

The completion-rate drop is useful.

The Arrival was already above 90% in the snapshot I checked, but Searching the
Attic was down to **61.4%**.

That tells me a substantial part of the achievement loss is simply players not
finishing the full three-day route yet, not necessarily missing hidden
interactions.

### Optional Day 1 achievements

The Day 1 cleanup list is:

- **Chase away the Crow** — Night — **56.1%**
- **Checking for Intruders** — Night — **44.9%**
- **Petting the Horse** — Morning — **31.7%**
- **Small Repairs** — Afternoon — **26.3%**
- **Exercising with Mr. Aldous** — Morning — **24.7%**
- **Ruining the Bouquet** — Afternoon — **13.1%**
- **Feeding the Cat** — Morning — **8.9%**
- **Soothing the Baby** — Evening — **8.5%**

I clean these up by task window rather than by achievement percentage.

For example, if I am missing **Petting the Horse**, I replay the Day 1 morning

Tea**.

I do not replay the Night Watch just because both achievements happen on the
same calendar day.

The Notebook task is the more useful locator.

<h3 id="perfect-hunt">
  A Perfect Hunt — the one hunting achievement I make sure not to miss
</h3>

**A Perfect Hunt** was at **17.3%** in the achievement snapshot.

During **Going Hunting**, the hunting list gives me five targets:

- deer;
- pheasant;
- rabbit;
- pigeon;
- boar.

The important instruction is:

**shoot them all.**

I do not stop as soon as I have enough meat for the required progression.

I keep checking the listed habitats and shoot every target before finishing the
hunt.

<h2 id="post-game-cleanup">
  Why I save most optional achievements for after The Departure
</h2>

This was the most important decision in my 100% run.

After **The Departure**, the Notebook changes from a simple task tracker into
the easiest cleanup tool in the game.

I can select old tasks again and revisit memories from the previous three days.

That means I no longer need to repeat:

**Day 1 → Day 2 → half of Day 3**

just because I missed one interaction during Preparing Dinner.

I replay the exact task that owns the missing optional star.

<h2 id="achievement-priority">
  The achievements I leave until last
</h2>

If I want the shortest cleanup session, I do not begin with the achievements
that already unlock naturally for most players.

I start with the bottom of the list.

The rarest achievements in the snapshot I checked were:

**Checking the Furniture — 3.6%**

**Finishing Touch — 3.6%**

**You've found him! — 4.2%**

**Seasoning the Soup — 4.4%**

**First Aid — 5.0%**

**Serving Mr. Aldous his Favorite Coffee — 5.2%**

**Serving Matcha Latte — 5.4%**

**Pest Control — 5.5%**

**Animal testing — 5.6%**

Those are the slots I inspect first when my normal story completion is already
done but my achievement count is still well short of 49.

My final rule is:

**finish the story once, use the Notebook for the 25 optional achievements,
then finish the secret-location route.**

That keeps achievement hunting separate from the first-playthrough puzzle flow
and saves me from replaying the Vanderboom weekend more times than necessary.
