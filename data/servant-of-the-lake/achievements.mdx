There are **49 achievements in Servant of the Lake**, but I do not try to
collect all 49 during the first three-day run.

The list breaks down cleanly:

- **24 story achievements** for completing the normal Notebook tasks;
- **24 hidden achievements**, with one hidden achievement attached to each
  normal task;
- **1 additional secret achievement**, **You've found him!**, tied to the
  post-game missing-brother route.

That changes how I approach 100%.

I finish **The Departure** first, unlock Notebook replay, then return directly
to the memories that still have a missing hidden star.

My route is:

**finish the story once → unlock Notebook replay → clear the 24 task-linked
hidden achievements → complete the secret-location route.**

<h2 id="story-achievements">
  The 24 story achievements
</h2>

I do not farm these separately.

They unlock from completing the normal Notebook route.

### Day 1 story achievements

**The Arrival → Preparing Breakfast → Waking up Aldous → Preparing the
Reception → Welcoming the Guests → Serving Tea → Opening the Reunion → The
Night Watch → Searching the Attic**

### Day 2 story achievements

**Preparing Coffee → Waking up William → Cleaning the Floors → Going Hunting →
Doing the Washing → Helping with Bathing → Night Time Duties → Inspecting the
Cellar**

### Day 3 story achievements

**Looking for the Cat → Assisting in Alchemy → Unforeseen Maintenance →
Preparing Dinner → Dressing up for Dinner → Serving Dinner → The Departure**

If one of these is still missing, I do not start searching for a secret object.

I replay or continue the corresponding main task first.

The hidden achievements are the ones that require an extra action beyond normal
progression.

<h2 id="day-1-hidden-achievements">
  Day 1 hidden achievements
</h2>

Day 1 has **9 task-linked hidden achievements**.

I clean them up in Notebook order.

### Petting the Horse

I do this during **The Arrival**.

I feed the horse the carrot, then keep interacting with and petting it before
leaving the front yard.

If I only feed it and immediately enter the estate, I can move the story
forward without registering the hidden interaction.

### Feeding the Cat

This belongs to **Preparing Breakfast**.

Rusty has an extra feeding interaction during the breakfast route.

I use the alternate food interaction on Rusty before finishing the task instead
of assuming the normal mouse interaction covers the hidden achievement.

Once the hidden star registers, I finish breakfast normally.

### Exercising with Mr. Aldous

I handle this while **Waking up Aldous**.

I complete the exercise interaction before dressing him and moving on.

The important thing is not to rush directly from breakfast into the final
outfit step while Aldous is still expecting the exercise sequence.

### Small Repairs

This belongs to **Preparing the Reception**.

The small painting in the servant area looks suspicious much earlier than it
becomes useful.

During the Reception replay, I keep interacting with it until it changes and
comes apart, then put the painting back together.

I do this before completing the rest of the reception setup.

### Ruining the Bouquet

I do this during **Welcoming the Guests**.

The goat is the extra interaction.

I let the goat interfere with the bouquet before I finish and deliver the
flowers normally.

I still complete the bouquet afterward.

The confirmation I use is that the finished flowers are accepted with the
goat-related reaction instead of behaving like the clean normal bouquet.

### Serving Matcha Latte

This is the Day 1 hidden achievement I find easiest to miss.

It belongs to **Serving Tea**.

Rusty can provide the extra ingredient route.

I interact with Rusty during the tea memory, find the **green powder**, then use
that powder while preparing Alexandra's drink before finishing the normal tea
and sugar sequence.

The drink receives the unusual taste reaction when I have done the hidden route
correctly.

<h3 id="serving-matcha-latte">
  Serving Matcha Latte: the order I use
</h3>

I do not prepare Alexandra's final normal tea first.

My order is:

1. enter the Serving Tea memory;
2. check Rusty before finishing Alexandra's drink;
3. collect the green powder from the extra cat interaction;
4. use it in the tea route;
5. finish the rest of Alexandra's required drink sequence.

If I advance to the Living Room before doing this, I use chapter replay instead
of trying to force the ingredient into a later task.

### Soothing the Baby

This belongs to **Opening the Reunion**.

I use the **Champagne** on baby James during the reunion sequence before
finishing the normal family scene.

It is a very small optional interaction, so I do it before the Notebook leaves
the reunion.

### Checking for Intruders

I handle this during **The Night Watch**.

The normal rule is:

- strange hallway state → ring the bell;
- normal hallway state → blow out a candle.

For the hidden achievement, I inspect the suspicious hallway changes rather
than rushing immediately to the bell or candle.

I check the round carefully, confirm the abnormal scene, then give the correct
response.

### Chase away the Crow

This belongs to **Searching the Attic**.

When the Crow appears during the Attic sequence, I interact with it before
finishing the larger constellation and Attic route.

I keep the Crow interaction inside this memory rather than assuming it belongs
to a later story event.

Once it leaves and the hidden star registers, I continue the normal Attic
puzzles.

<h2 id="day-2-hidden-achievements">
  Day 2 hidden achievements
</h2>

Day 2 has **8 hidden achievements**.

This is the day where I pay the most attention to extra interactions because
several of them sit directly beside mandatory progression.

### Serving Mr. Aldous his Favorite Coffee

This belongs to **Preparing Coffee**.

The hidden difference is the beans.

I **roast the coffee first**, then use the roasted coffee for Aldous's drink.

When it is correct, his reaction specifically recognizes the better flavor and
aroma.

I do this before taking the normal coffee route to William.

### Tickling Mr. William

I do this while **Waking up William**.

William's feet and toes are already part of the wake-up logic.

Before finishing the whole morning sequence, I use the extra tickling
interaction and keep following it until the achievement registers.

<h3 id="perfect-hunt">
  A Perfect Hunt: when I stop shooting
</h3>

I use the hunting list itself as the checklist.

I do not end the memory until I have accounted for:

**deer → pheasant → rabbit → pigeon → boar.**

Once every target has been handled and the achievement registers, I let the
normal route return to the estate.

### Close Inspections

This belongs to **Doing the Washing**.

I use the extra inspection interaction on the suspicious laundry-related item
before finishing the wash and leaving with the task reward.

I do not leave the memory as soon as the required washing sequence looks
complete.

If the hidden star is still empty, I inspect the objects involved in the wash
again before advancing.

### Cleaning the Windows

I do this during **Helping with Bathing**.

The important timing is after the Bathroom window has become **steamy**.

I do not try to force the achievement immediately after entering the room.

Once the glass is fogged, I return to it and complete the extra cleaning
interaction.

That is the point where the window-cleaning achievement can register.

### Garbage Disposal

This belongs to **Night Time Duties**.

There is an extra disposal interaction during the night route before I commit
to the Cellar.

I handle the throw-away / garbage interaction while the Night Time Duties
memory is still active, then continue with the three Time Pieces and Cellar
handoff.

If I have already entered the Cellar and the hidden star is still missing, I
replay Night Time Duties rather than searching underground for it.

### Pest Control

This belongs to **Inspecting the Cellar**.

I inspect the suspicious pest / hole interaction while I am still in the Cellar
memory and use the available tool interaction on it.

If my first tool does not complete it, I continue the optional interaction
instead of assuming the branch is finished.

I do this before resolving the rest of the Cellar route.

<h2 id="day-3-hidden-achievements">
  Day 3 hidden achievements
</h2>

Day 3 has **7 task-linked hidden achievements**, followed by the separate
**You've found him!** secret achievement.

Most of these are easy to miss because the final day moves quickly toward the
ending.

### Petting the Cat

This belongs to **Looking for the Cat**.

Once I find Rusty during the search, I stop and pet him before letting the task
finish.

I do not assume simply locating him is enough for the hidden achievement.

### Animal testing

This belongs to **Assisting in Alchemy**.

After making the base Elixir, I use the completed Elixir on **Harvey** during
the Alchemy memory.

I do this before turning the final Alchemy result into normal story progression.

The extra reaction confirms that I used the experimental route rather than
only completing Aldous's recipe.

### Checking the Furniture

This belongs to **Unforeseen Maintenance**.

During the Day 3 maintenance memory, I inspect the furniture before finishing
the normal repair task and interact with the suspicious hidden spot until the
concealed note appears.

I do this before completing the final required repairs.

<h3 id="checking-the-furniture">
  Checking the Furniture: why I do it first
</h3>

Maintenance already asks me to repair obvious furniture and portraits.

That makes it easy to click only the damaged objects and leave.

Instead, I inspect the furniture for the extra hidden interaction **before**
finishing the normal maintenance objective.

Once the concealed note appears, I know the hidden route has registered and I
can finish the normal repairs.

### Seasoning the Soup

This belongs to **Preparing Dinner**.

I catch the **fly**, keep the **dead fly**, and put it into the soup before the
normal dinner preparation is finalized.

I do not add every unusual ingredient to every dish.

This hidden achievement is specifically tied to the soup.

The soup can then continue through the normal tasting sequence.

### Finishing Touch

This belongs to **Dressing up for Dinner**.

The important item is the **fish pin**.

I obtain the pin through the evening route, then give the fish pin to
**William** instead of keeping it.

That extra handoff is the part I watch for before completing the rest of the
dinner preparation.

<h3 id="finishing-touch">
  Finishing Touch: do not keep the fish pin
</h3>

The fish pin looks like the kind of item I might need to preserve for another
puzzle.

For this achievement, I do the opposite.

Once I have it during the correct evening memory, I give it to William and wait
for that optional interaction to resolve before moving deeper into the dinner
route.

### Safety Test

This belongs to **Serving Dinner**.

During the evening setup, I test the suspicious prepared object before
advancing the meal.

I keep interacting with the object until the game accepts the extra safety
check instead of assuming the normal setup is enough.

The key is timing:

**do the safety test while the evening task still owns the object.**

Once the story has already moved into the later dinner sequence, I replay the
correct memory instead of trying to access it afterward.

### First Aid

This belongs to **The Departure**.

The final escape sequence is urgent, which makes this one particularly easy to
skip.

When I reach the injured William section, I stop before advancing the escape
and complete the extra treatment interaction.

Removing the obvious objects is not where I stop.

I keep treating him until the first-aid interaction completes and the hidden
achievement registers.

<h3 id="first-aid">
  First Aid: stop during The Departure
</h3>

The mistake I avoid is treating **The Departure** like one continuous escape
scene.

When William is injured, I temporarily stop following the exit route.

I finish his optional treatment first, then continue toward James and the final
escape.

This was the last additional task in my cleanup route before the game confirmed
that all additional tasks were complete.

<h2 id="post-game-replay">
  How Notebook replay changes achievement cleanup
</h2>

After **The Departure**, I continue rather than starting a new save.

The game tells me there are still tasks left and lets me select previous
memories directly through the Notebook.

That is the important 100% unlock.

Instead of asking:

**"Where in the entire Vanderboom estate did I miss something?"**

I can ask:

**"Which task still has an empty hidden achievement?"**

I then replay only that memory.

<h2 id="achievement-cleanup-rule">
  My final 49/49 cleanup rule
</h2>

I do not judge progress by how many hidden interactions I found naturally on
the first run.

I use three checks:

**Story complete?**  
The 24 normal task achievements should come from the Day 1–3 walkthrough.

Once all three are true, I am done with the Servant of the Lake achievement
route.
