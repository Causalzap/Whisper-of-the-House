Big Walk is a cooperative adventure built around navigation, physical puzzle rewards, shared equipment, and communication. For a clean first progression run, I use this route:

**Tutorial → Red Tower and Map Room → Green Tower and Chairlift → Yellow Tower and Tunnel → Blue Tower and Train → Black Tower and Wall → final black sphere → Big Goodbye → post-game cleanup → Big Game.**

This is not the only valid colored-tower order. I use Green before Yellow and Yellow before Blue because the active transport routes reduce backtracking. The island also contains more challenges than each receiver requires, and Head rewards are not locked to the color of the nearest tower.

<h2 id="progression">Use receivers, physical Heads, and finished keys as progression checks</h2>

Most major routes repeat the same structure:

1. complete practical challenges near the active route;
2. collect the physical Head rewards;
3. return the required number to the receiver;
4. assign the blank key to one carrier;
5. complete every cutter in that key chain;
6. use the finished key at the route's major system.

The published progression through Train activation uses:

- **4 tutorial Heads** for the drawbridge;
- **5 Red Heads** for the Map Room route;
- **5 Green Heads** for the Chairlift route;
- **5 Yellow Heads** for the Tunnel route;
- **5 Blue Heads** for the Train route.

A solved puzzle does not automatically increase the receiver count. I still need to recover the physical reward, name its carrier, return it, and insert it.

Heads are not color-locked. The colored regions are route anchors, not reward restrictions. When one nearby challenge is blocking the group, I use another completed Head rather than forcing the same puzzle for the current receiver.

<h2 id="opening-route">Finish the opening route before chasing island equipment</h2>

The tutorial has one progression objective: fill the four-slot beach receiver, finish the first key, and cross the drawbridge.

I return each Head when the route passes the receiver and avoid collecting optional island equipment before the bridge is open.

Use the [Tutorial and Drawbridge Walkthrough](/big-walk/tutorial-walkthrough) for the exact opening puzzles, cutter sequence, and stuck-key fixes.

<h2 id="red-tower-route">Use Red Tower to unlock the Map Room</h2>

After the drawbridge, I use the inactive Train Station as a regrouping landmark and the red beacon as the direction toward Red Tower.

The Red route ends when the completed Red key opens the physical Map Room. Reaching the upper red entrance with a partly shaped key is the main reason the door appears to accept the key without responding.

Use the [Red Tower and Map Room Guide](/big-walk/how-to-unlock-map) for the complete Red progression route.

<h2 id="map-room">Use the Map Room to choose the next route</h2>

The island model shows the relationships between towers, transport systems, completed challenges, and unfinished locations.

I use the Map Room to choose the next region, then carry only the navigation tool that helps that route:

- portable map for multi-region travel;
- Compass for a stable direction;
- Laser Pointer for marking a distant landmark.

I leave unused tools at a named station. A light or communication item is often more valuable than carrying every navigation tool into a puzzle.

<h2 id="green-route">Complete Green and activate the Chairlift</h2>

Green is the first transport route I complete after the Map Room.

The Hub-level checkpoint is simple: return the required rewards, finish the Green key, insert it at the Chairlift station beside Green Tower, and board a moving seat.

The [Green Tower and Chairlift Guide](/big-walk/green-tower-walkthrough) owns the reward cache, carrying plan, cutter chain, correct station, Chairlift activation, and Big Sit trigger.

<h2 id="yellow-route">Use the upper transport route to reach Yellow</h2>

After Green activates the Chairlift, I use the upper route to reduce the walk toward Yellow.

The Yellow route is complete when the finished key opens the underground Tunnel and the group crosses the threshold. Reaching the exterior door or last cutter is not the final checkpoint.

The [Yellow Tower and Tunnel Guide](/big-walk/yellow-tower-tunnel) owns the Yellow reward route, challenge methods, cutter chain, and Tunnel entry.

<h2 id="blue-route">Use the Tunnel or Map Room route to reach Blue</h2>

The Yellow Tunnel shortens travel toward Blue, but it does not complete the Blue receiver. I leave the underground route, recover high ground, and locate the Blue side.

The Blue route is complete when the finished key activates the Train loop and the group boards a moving carriage.

The [Blue Tower and Train Guide](/big-walk/blue-tower-train) owns the Blue reward route, puzzle roles, easy-to-miss cutter, Train activation, and Big Ride trigger.

<h2 id="late-game-endings">Continue from the Train to the Wall and both endings</h2>

After Blue and Train activation, I return to the Map Room and use the transport network to finish unresolved progression before entering the late route.

The late sequence is:

**Black Tower progression → pass beyond the Wall → enter the final black sphere → shut down the final mechanism → follow the route across the water → Big Goodbye.**

I keep the Black Tower and black sphere separate:

- the **Black Tower** is an earlier late-game progression structure;
- the **Wall** is a milestone the group must pass beyond;
- the **large black sphere** is the final main-route puzzle area;
- the **ending building** is the structure across the water where the group uses Wave Goodbye.

After Big Goodbye, I continue on the same save. Seven purple post-game puzzles appear, but every unfinished normal puzzle still counts toward complete progression.

The [Big Walk Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) owns the final sphere mechanism, Wave Goodbye entrance, purple-puzzle cleanup, final key, hidden room, and Big Game explanation.

<h2 id="multiplayer">Use fixed multiplayer roles during long routes</h2>

Big Walk supports groups of **2–12 players**, but larger groups need fewer leaders, not more.

Before a long route, I assign:

- one route leader;
- one Head and key carrier;
- one light carrier;
- one communication lead.

Extra players become puzzle runners and object carriers.

I use Flares at stable landmarks such as towers, stations, and puzzle entrances. I avoid them during blindfold, sound, and symbol communication, where extra noise makes instructions harder to follow.

After a disconnect, I check Lost and Found, receiver progress, the latest key location, Map Room flags, and the reward point before replaying a full challenge.

The [Beginner Guide](/big-walk/beginner-guide) owns the complete Host, role, Flare, reconnect, equipment, and session-checkpoint plan.

<h2 id="achievements">Use achievements as checkpoints, not route detours</h2>

Achievements work best as confirmation that the current progression step registered.

Examples include:

- **Big Walk** after crossing the opening drawbridge;
- **Big View** after entering the Map Room;
- **Big Sit** after boarding the moving Chairlift;
- **Big Tunnel** after entering the opened Tunnel;
- **Big Ride** after boarding the moving Train;
- **Big Goodbye** after the normal ending;
- **Big Game** after the complete hidden ending.

I do not leave an active receiver route to chase an unrelated equipment achievement. I finish the current progression step, then use the [All 12 Achievements Guide](/big-walk/achievements) as the cleanup checklist.

<h2 id="stuck">Big Walk progression fixes</h2>

### The key fits but the door or transport does nothing

The key is still blank or partly shaped. Return to the latest cutter and follow its arrow to the missing station.

### A solved puzzle did not increase the receiver count

Find the physical Head reward and insert it into the active receiver.

### We are forcing one puzzle because it is beside the tower

Leave it and use another completed Head. Rewards are not restricted to the nearest tower color.

### The Green route is complete, but the Chairlift is inactive

Confirm that the Green key finished every cutter and was inserted at the Chairlift station beside Green Tower, not the Train Station.

### The Yellow Tunnel is open, but Blue is still unfinished

The Tunnel is transportation. Exit toward Blue and complete the separate Blue receiver and key route.

### The Train is active, but we do not know what comes next

Return to the Map Room, finish unresolved progression, and move into the Black Tower and Wall route. The [Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) takes over once the final black sphere route begins.

### Big Goodbye unlocked, but Big Game did not

Continue on the same save. Complete the seven purple puzzles, every remaining normal puzzle, and the final-key route.

### We disconnected after finishing a puzzle

Check the completion flag and reward location before replaying the challenge. The puzzle may remain complete while its physical reward still needs to be recovered.

<h2 id="faq">Big Walk guide FAQ</h2>

### Is there one fixed colored-tower order?

No. This Hub uses Green → Yellow → Blue because the active Chairlift and Tunnel reduce backtracking.

### Are Heads locked to a tower color?

No. I can use any completed physical Head at the active receiver.

### Do I need to solve every puzzle near a tower?

No. Each receiver requires a fixed number of Heads while additional challenges remain available for later cleanup.

### Does the Map Room activate the Chairlift, Tunnel, or Train?

No. The Map Room provides route and completion information. Each transport system has its own receiver, key, and activation route.

### What should I do after activating the Train?

Return to the Map Room, finish unresolved progression, then continue through the Black Tower and Wall route toward the final black sphere.

### Does Big Goodbye completely finish the game?

No. Big Goodbye finishes the main route. Big Game requires the post-game puzzle cleanup, final key, and hidden completion room.

### Can the group continue without the original Host?

The Host owns the save and world progress. The same Host needs to start that save again.

### Can Big Walk be played solo?

The selectable world versions begin at two players, and the route is built around cooperative roles.
