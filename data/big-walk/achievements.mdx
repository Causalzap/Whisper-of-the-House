Big Walk has **12 achievements**. I track them in progression order rather than
rarity order: four early achievements, three transport and elevation goals, then
five late-game completion goals.

The first four can be cleaned up during the Red Tower and Map Room route. The
remaining eight belong to later tower, transport, ending, and completion
progression, so I keep them on one checklist instead of interrupting the first
island loop.

<h2 id="big-walk">
  Big Walk achievement: cross the opening drawbridge
</h2>

Big Walk triggers when the group crosses the first red drawbridge.

The route is:

1. return four Heads to the beach receiver;
2. take the blank key;
3. follow the cutter arrows until the key is fully shaped;
4. insert the finished key into the drawbridge mechanism;
5. wait for the bridge to extend;
6. cross to the far side.

The achievement is tied to the crossing, not simply inserting the key. I keep
moving until both players are on the main-island side.

A key that fits but does not open the bridge is usually unfinished. I return to
the latest cutter and follow its arrow rather than repeatedly inserting the same
partly shaped key.

<h2 id="big-pack">
  Big Pack achievement: wear the Backpack
</h2>

Big Pack requires a player to **wear a Backpack**. Carrying a Head, Walkie-talkie,
light, or Backpack-shaped object in the hands does not satisfy the trigger.

I pick up the first Backpack after the drawbridge and place it onto the
character's back. Once equipped, it becomes useful beyond the achievement:

- one Head or loose tool can be stored without occupying a hand;
- the carrier can still climb and point;
- a light or Walkie-talkie can remain available;
- and the group can move more progression objects per return trip.

I give the first Backpack to the player responsible for Heads and keys rather
than automatically assigning it to the Host.

I do not abandon an active route to search for several Backpacks. One equipped
Backpack, two working Walkie-talkies, and a known return point are enough for the
first Red and Green collection loops.

<h2 id="big-view">
  Big View achievement: enter the physical Map Room
</h2>

Big View triggers when the group reaches the physical Map Room after the Red
Tower route.

The short progression check is:

1. cross the tutorial drawbridge;
2. reach the five-slot Red Tower receiver;
3. return five Heads;
4. take and fully shape the second blank key;
5. open the upper red entrance;
6. enter the room containing the island model.

Picking up the portable map is not the same action. The portable map, Compass,
and Laser Pointers are equipment found after entering the room.

<h2 id="big-help">
  Big Help achievement: wear an item on the hip
</h2>

Big Help requires a player to wear something on the hip.

My clean trigger is:

1. pick up the hip belt;
2. equip the belt around the character's waist;
3. attach the folded portable map to the available side;
4. leave the item seated on the belt until the achievement appears.

Holding the belt in a hand is not enough. Holding the portable map against the
character is also not enough. The item needs to be attached to the equipped hip
slot.

The belt remains useful after the trigger because the map carrier can keep both
hands free while moving through an unfamiliar region. I keep it when:

- the portable map is needed for a longer route;
- the Backpack is already holding a Head;
- both hands are reserved for communication or puzzle objects;
- or another player needs to remove and inspect the map during travel.

I leave the belt and map at a named station when the next objective is a short
return to a visible tower. Carrying fewer optional items makes lost progression
objects easier to identify.

<h2 id="transport-achievements">
  Big Sit, Big Ride, and Big Climb achievement order
</h2>

These three achievements belong after the early Map Room and tower routes.

### Big Sit

**Requirement:** Ride the Chairlift.

I keep Big Sit on the checklist until the Chairlift route is active. Reaching an
inactive station or pressing its controls does not complete the requirement. Once
the system works, I board and remain on the Chairlift long enough for the ride to
begin.

### Big Ride

**Requirement:** Ride the Train.

The inactive Train Station is useful as a staging point early in the game, but
standing on the platform does not count. I finish the Train progression, board
one of the running trains, and wait until it begins moving.

### Big Climb

**Requirement:** Reach the highest point.

I chase Big Climb after transport and tower progression have opened higher routes.
That avoids spending an early session trying to scale terrain that is intended to
be reached from a later station or elevation path.

My transport cleanup order is:

1. open the Map Room;
2. complete the relevant tower receivers;
3. shape the awarded keys;
4. activate the transport route;
5. ride it before leaving the station;
6. use the new route to check elevation objectives.

<h2 id="late-achievements">
  Big Tunnel, Big Wall, Big Goodbye, Big Game, and Big Makeover
</h2>

I keep the final five achievements as separate checklist states because their
requirements describe different progression milestones.

### Big Tunnel

**Requirement:** Enter a big tunnel.

I mark this complete only after the character has physically entered the tunnel.
Finding a sign, exterior entrance, or nearby station is not the same trigger.

### Big Wall

**Requirement:** Go beyond the wall.

I keep this separate from reaching or viewing the wall. The checklist completes
after the group passes beyond it.

### Big Goodbye

**Requirement:** Finish the game.

This is the normal finish state. I do not merge it with Big Game because the game
uses a separate achievement for complete completion.

### Big Game

**Requirement:** Completely finish the game.

I track this after Big Goodbye and keep unfinished challenge sites, tower routes,
and completion objectives on a separate cleanup list.

### Big Makeover

**Requirement:** Get Shiny.

I treat Shiny as its own completion objective rather than assuming it will occur
automatically during an ending.

Before beginning the late completion route, I:

- check the Map Room for unfinished challenge locations;
- finish active tower and transport progression;
- return important objects to named stations;
- and keep Big Goodbye, Big Game, and Big Makeover as three separate boxes.

<h2 id="completion-checklist">
  My 12-achievement completion checklist
</h2>

I mark achievements in this order:

- [ ] Big Walk — crossed the opening drawbridge
- [ ] Big Pack — wore a Backpack
- [ ] Big View — entered the physical Map Room
- [ ] Big Help — wore an attached hip item
- [ ] Big Sit — rode the Chairlift
- [ ] Big Ride — rode the Train
- [ ] Big Climb — reached the highest point
- [ ] Big Tunnel — entered the big tunnel
- [ ] Big Wall — went beyond the wall
- [ ] Big Goodbye — finished the game
- [ ] Big Game — completely finished the game
- [ ] Big Makeover — became Shiny

The first four fit naturally into the early guide cluster. The remaining eight
stay visible without forcing the group to abandon its current tower route for an
unrelated achievement.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Big Walk achievement fixes
</h2>

### Big Walk did not unlock after the bridge opened

Cross all the way to the far side. Inserting the key and watching the bridge
extend is not the same as completing the crossing.

### Big Pack did not unlock when I carried a Head

Equip the Backpack on the character's back. The achievement is for wearing the
Backpack, not carrying an object.

### Big View did not unlock when I picked up the portable map

Enter the physical Map Room. The portable map is an item found inside and is not
the room-visit trigger by itself.

### Big Help did not unlock when I picked up the belt

Equip the belt, then attach a compatible item such as the folded portable map to
the hip slot.

### Big Sit or Big Ride did not unlock at the station

The transport must be active and the character must ride it. Reaching an inactive
station is not enough.

### Big Goodbye unlocked but Big Game did not

Keep the two achievements separate. Big Goodbye covers finishing the game; Big
Game covers complete completion and may still require unfinished objectives.

<h2 id="faq">
  Big Walk achievement FAQ
</h2>

### How many achievements are in Big Walk?

There are 12 achievements.

### Are hidden achievements automatically missable?

No. A hidden requirement is concealed in the platform list, but that alone does
not mean it can be permanently missed.

### Should I follow achievement rarity order?

No. I follow progression order. The early equipment achievements are easiest to
clean up around the Red Tower and Map Room, while transport and ending goals fit
later routes.

### Is Big Game the same as Big Goodbye?

No. Big Goodbye requires finishing the game. Big Game requires completely
finishing it.

### Does picking up the portable map unlock Big View or Big Help?

Entering the physical Map Room completes Big View. Attaching the portable map to
an equipped hip belt is the clean Big Help trigger.
