<h2 id="big-walk">Big Walk achievement: cross the opening drawbridge</h2>

**Big Walk** unlocks when I cross the first red drawbridge and reach the main-island side.

The trigger is the crossing itself. Finishing the tutorial receiver, shaping the key, inserting it, or watching the bridge extend is not enough if the character never moves to the far side.

My short check is:

1. return the four tutorial Heads;
2. finish the opening key;
3. insert it into the drawbridge mechanism;
4. wait for the bridge to extend;
5. cross completely to the opposite side.

The [Tutorial and Drawbridge Walkthrough](/big-walk/tutorial-walkthrough) owns the four Head solutions and the complete opening cutter route.

<h2 id="big-pack">Big Pack achievement: wear the Backpack</h2>

**Big Pack** requires the Backpack to be equipped on the character's back.

Holding a Backpack in both hands does not count. Carrying a Head, light, or another large object also does not satisfy the requirement.

I give the first Backpack to the player carrying Heads and keys. Once equipped, it remains useful because the carrier can store one progression object without giving up both hands.

<h2 id="big-view">Big View achievement: enter the physical Map Room</h2>

**Big View** unlocks when I enter the physical Map Room after the Red Tower route.

Picking up the portable map is not the same trigger. The portable map, Compass, and Laser Pointers are equipment found after entering the room.

The short progression check is:

1. complete the Red receiver;
2. finish the Red key;
3. open the upper red entrance;
4. walk into the room containing the island model.

The [Red Tower and Map Room Guide](/big-walk/how-to-unlock-map) owns the complete five-Head return, Red cutter chain, and Map Room unlock route.

<h2 id="big-help">Big Help achievement: wear an item on the hip</h2>

**Big Help** requires an item to be attached to an equipped hip belt.

My clean trigger is:

1. pick up the hip belt;
2. equip it around the character's waist;
3. fold the portable map;
4. attach the map to the available hip slot;
5. leave it attached until the achievement appears.

Holding the belt or map is not enough. The compatible item must be visibly seated on the equipped hip slot.

<h2 id="transport-achievements">Big Sit, Big Climb, Big Tunnel, and Big Ride</h2>

These four achievements sit on top of the Green, Yellow, and Blue progression routes. This page keeps only the final trigger for each one; the dedicated tower guides own the Head collection, puzzle selection, key cutters, and transport activation.

### Big Sit

**Requirement:** Ride the Chairlift.

I complete the Green route, insert the finished Green Key at the Chairlift station beside Green Tower, wait for the chairs to circulate, and board a moving seat.

Reaching the station or standing beside an inactive chair does not count. I remain seated until the achievement appears.

Use the [Green Tower and Chairlift Guide](/big-walk/green-tower-walkthrough) for the full Green progression route.

### Big Climb

**Requirement:** Reach the highest point.

The Chairlift provides access, but riding it is not the Big Climb trigger. I leave the upper Chairlift route and continue toward the island's highest terrain.

I do not wait on the chair for a second transport achievement. Big Climb appears only after the character reaches the highest-point trigger.

Until the complete summit route is documented, I use the upper Chairlift station as the starting landmark and avoid claiming one unverified final turn or ledge.

### Big Tunnel

**Requirement:** Enter a big tunnel.

I complete the Yellow route, insert the finished Yellow Key, wait for the Tunnel entrance to open, and walk fully through the doorway.

Finding the exterior entrance, reaching the last Yellow cutter, or standing at the threshold does not count.

Use the [Yellow Tower and Tunnel Guide](/big-walk/yellow-tower-tunnel) for the complete Yellow route.

### Big Ride

**Requirement:** Ride the Train.

I complete the Blue route, activate the Train loop, wait for a carriage to move, and board it.

Standing on the platform or entering a parked carriage does not count. In my run, Big Ride appeared after the group jumped onto the moving Train.

Use the [Blue Tower and Train Guide](/big-walk/blue-tower-train) for the complete Blue progression and activation route.

### Big Wall

**Requirement:** Go beyond the wall.

I do not mark Big Wall when the group only reaches, sees, or stands beside the Wall. The character must pass beyond it.

Big Wall is a progression milestone in the Black Tower and Wall route. It is not the same trigger as finishing the game.

The confirmed boundary is simple:

- finding the Wall is not enough;
- reaching a closed entrance is not enough;
- crossing to the far side is the achievement state.

### Big Goodbye

**Requirement:** Finish the game.

Big Goodbye is the first finish state. I complete the late main route, finish the final black sphere, reach the building across the water, and enter the normal ending sequence.

I do not reproduce that route here. The [Big Walk Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) covers the final sphere mechanism, Wave Goodbye entrance, and what happens after the first ending.

### Big Game

**Requirement:** Completely finish the game.

Big Game is the separate full-completion state after Big Goodbye. It requires the post-game puzzle cleanup, final key, hidden room, and complete ending sequence.

Big Goodbye unlocking does not mean Big Game should unlock at the same time. The [Big Walk Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) explains the seven purple puzzles, remaining normal puzzles, final-key check, and hidden completion room.

I do not assume Big Makeover or every platform achievement is required for Big Game. The confirmed full-ending route is based on puzzle completion and the final hidden-room sequence.

### Big Makeover

**Requirement:** Get Shiny.

I keep Big Makeover separate from both ending achievements. Reaching an ending, seeing a cosmetic area, or standing beside a Shiny interaction does not complete it automatically.

Until the full Shiny route is documented, I use only the confirmed trigger boundary: the character must complete the interaction that produces the Shiny state.

<h2 id="stuck">Big Walk achievement fixes</h2>

### Big Walk did not unlock after the bridge opened

Cross completely to the far side. Watching the bridge extend is not the achievement trigger.

### Big Pack did not unlock when I picked up a Backpack

Attach the Backpack to the character's back. Holding it by hand does not count.

### Big View did not unlock when I picked up the portable map

Enter the physical Map Room. The portable map is an item inside the room, not the room-visit trigger.

### Big Help did not unlock when I equipped the belt

Attach a compatible item, such as the folded portable map, to the equipped hip slot.

### Big Sit did not unlock at the Chairlift station

Confirm that the Chairlift is active, then board a moving seat. Reaching the station or standing beside an inactive chair does not count.

### Big Climb did not unlock during the Chairlift ride

Leave the upper Chairlift route and continue to the island's highest point. The trigger is elevation, not time spent riding.

### Big Tunnel did not unlock when the Yellow door opened

Walk fully through the entrance into the underground passage.

### Big Ride did not unlock at the Train Station

Wait for the Train to circulate and board a moving carriage. A parked carriage does not count.

### Big Wall did not unlock when we found the Wall

Pass beyond it. Seeing the Wall and crossing it are separate progression states.

### Big Goodbye unlocked but Big Game did not

Continue on the same save and complete the full post-game route. Use the [Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) instead of repeating the ending or waiting beside the final-key position.

### Big Makeover did not unlock when I found the cosmetic area

Complete the interaction that changes the character into the Shiny state. Reaching the area alone is not enough.

<h2 id="faq">Big Walk achievement FAQ</h2>

### How many achievements are in Big Walk?

There are 12 achievements.

### Should I follow achievement rarity order?

No. I follow progression order: early equipment and Map Room checks first, Green Chairlift next, then Yellow Tunnel, Blue Train, and the four late-game achievements.

### Are Big Goodbye and Big Game the same achievement?

No. Big Goodbye is the normal finish state. Big Game is the later full-completion ending.

### Does Big Game require every achievement?

The confirmed route is tied to complete puzzle progression, the final key, and the hidden ending room. I do not treat Big Makeover or every platform achievement as a requirement unless the game separately confirms it.

### Can I continue after Big Goodbye?

Yes. Continue on the same save to access the post-game puzzle cleanup and work toward Big Game.

### Does picking up the portable map unlock Big View or Big Help?

Neither trigger comes from simply picking it up. Entering the physical Map Room unlocks Big View. Attaching the folded map to an equipped hip belt is the clean Big Help trigger.
