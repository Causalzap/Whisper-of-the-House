Big Walk has **12 achievements**, but I do not treat them as 12 separate detours.

Most of them are checkpoints on routes I already need to travel. I use this page to answer three questions:

- what action actually triggers the achievement;
- when I should attempt it;
- what I check when the achievement does not appear.

I keep the long progression routes on their Owner Pages. This page does **not** repeat full Head collection routes, key-cutter chains, Black Tower progression, the Shiny tunnel route, seven Purple Puzzle solutions, or either complete ending.

The trigger column matters more than simply reaching the correct area.

Several achievements only register after I perform one final action:

- crossing instead of merely opening;
- wearing instead of holding;
- entering instead of standing outside;
- riding instead of reaching a station.

That is the pattern I check first when an achievement appears to be stuck.

<h2 id="big-walk">Big Walk: cross the opening drawbridge</h2>

**Big Walk** unlocks when I actually cross the opening drawbridge and reach the main-island side.

Opening the bridge is not the final trigger.

My progression check is:

1. return the four tutorial Heads;
2. finish the opening key;
3. use the key;
4. wait for the drawbridge to extend;
5. walk completely across.

If the bridge is already open and the achievement is missing, I cross to the far side before changing anything else.

The [Tutorial and Drawbridge Walkthrough](/big-walk/tutorial-walkthrough) owns the four opening puzzles, key cutters, and drawbridge troubleshooting.

<h2 id="big-pack">Big Pack: wear the Backpack</h2>

**Big Pack** is one of the easiest achievements to misunderstand because picking up the Backpack is not enough.

I need to **attach it to the character's back**.

I normally give the first Backpack to whoever is already carrying important progression objects.

That turns the achievement attempt into a useful equipment upgrade rather than a separate detour.

If Big Pack does not unlock, I check whether the Backpack is visibly attached instead of still being held.

<h2 id="big-view">Big View: enter the physical Map Room</h2>

**Big View** is tied to the **Map Room**, not simply obtaining the portable map.

My trigger sequence is:

1. complete Red progression;
2. finish the Red key;
3. open the Map Room;
4. walk into the room containing the large island model.

The portable map, Compass, and other navigation tools come afterward.

So if I picked up a map and Big View did not appear, I do not keep dropping and picking up the item.

I verify that I actually entered the physical Map Room.

The [Red Tower and Map Room Guide](/big-walk/how-to-unlock-map) owns the full five-Head route, Red key chain, and Map Room unlock.

<h2 id="big-help">Big Help: wear an item on the hip</h2>

For **Big Help**, I need more than the hip belt itself.

The reliable setup I use is:

1. equip the hip belt;
2. fold the portable map;
3. place the folded map into the available hip slot;
4. leave it attached until the achievement registers.

<h2 id="transport-achievements">Big Sit, Big Climb, Big Tunnel, and Big Ride</h2>

These four achievements overlap the Green, Yellow, and Blue progression routes.

I therefore keep only their final trigger here.

The actual Head routes, puzzle choices, keys, and transport activation remain on the tower Owner Pages.

### Big Sit

**Requirement: ride the Chairlift.**

I complete Green progression first.

Once the Chairlift is active, I wait for a seat to circulate and board it.

Reaching the station is not enough.

Standing beside an inactive chair is not enough.

I want the character physically riding the functioning Chairlift.

Use the [Green Tower and Chairlift Guide](/big-walk/green-tower-walkthrough) if the transport system itself is still locked.

### Big Climb

**Requirement: reach the highest point.**

Big Climb is separate from Big Sit.

The Chairlift helps me reach the upper route, but simply remaining on the chair does not satisfy the highest-point trigger.

I leave the upper Chairlift route and continue toward the highest terrain.

I do not claim a single exact final ledge here because I do not yet have a sufficiently reliable standalone summit route documented.

The useful troubleshooting boundary is:

**Chairlift ride = Big Sit.  
Highest-point trigger = Big Climb.**

If Big Sit unlocked but Big Climb did not, I keep climbing instead of repeatedly riding the Chairlift.

### Big Tunnel

**Requirement: enter the opened Tunnel.**

After completing Yellow progression, I walk completely through the opened entrance.

I do not expect Big Tunnel to trigger merely from:

- reaching the outside entrance;
- finishing the Yellow key;
- seeing the door open;
- standing at the threshold.

I cross inside.

Use the [Yellow Tower and Tunnel Guide](/big-walk/yellow-tower-tunnel) if the underground route is not open yet.

### Big Ride

**Requirement: ride the Train.**

I complete Blue progression, activate the Train network, wait for a carriage to circulate, and board it.

I do not count:

- reaching the Train Station;
- seeing the Train;
- standing on the platform;

as the final achievement state.

In my run, the useful checkpoint was boarding the moving Train.

Use the [Blue Tower and Train Guide](/big-walk/blue-tower-train) if the Train itself is still inactive.

<h2 id="big-wall">Big Wall: pass beyond the Wall</h2>

**Big Wall** belongs to the Black progression route, but the achievement trigger itself is simple:

**pass beyond the Wall.**

I do not mark it complete when I merely:

- find the Wall;
- see the large gate;
- arrive with the Black Key;
- stand beside the closed entrance.

The character needs to reach the far side.

The complete late-game route is now owned by the [Black Tower and Black Key Guide](/big-walk/black-tower).

That guide covers:

**Black Tower → vision → current requirement → Black Key → Wall.**

This page only owns the Big Wall trigger.

<h2 id="big-goodbye">Big Goodbye: finish the normal ending</h2>

**Big Goodbye** is the normal campaign-complete achievement.

It comes after the Black Tower and Wall route, followed by the final Black Sphere sequence.

I do not reproduce those ending mechanics here.

Once the Wall is open, I switch to the [Big Walk Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide).

That page owns:

- Black Sphere progression;
- the final mechanism;
- the route across the water;
- Wave Goodbye;
- the ending building;
- the Big Goodbye completion sequence.

For achievement troubleshooting, the important distinction is that **opening the Wall is Big Wall progression, not Big Goodbye**.

Big Goodbye appears only after completing the later ending sequence.

<h2 id="big-game">Big Game: completely finish the game</h2>

**Big Game** is not supposed to unlock together with Big Goodbye.

After the normal ending, I continue the **same save**.

Full completion then requires additional cleanup before the final-key and hidden-ending route can finish.

I intentionally keep those requirements out of this page because they now belong to two separate Owner Pages:

- [All 7 Purple Puzzles](/big-walk/purple-puzzles) owns the seven new post-game challenge solutions.
- [Big Walk Endings Guide](/big-walk/ending-guide) owns full-completion checks, older unfinished puzzles, the final key, hidden room, and Big Game ending.

My achievement-level rule is simple:

**Big Goodbye = normal completion.  
Big Game = full completion.**

If Big Goodbye already unlocked but Big Game did not, I do not replay the normal ending.

I continue cleanup.

<h2 id="big-makeover">Big Makeover: get Shiny</h2>

**Big Makeover** is its own optional late-game route.

It is not part of the normal tower-to-Big-Goodbye sequence.

The achievement requires me to **Get Shiny**, which means actually completing the hidden Shiny interaction rather than merely reaching the underground area.

I do not duplicate that underground route here anymore.

The [How to Get Shiny Guide](/big-walk/how-to-get-shiny) owns:

- the Yellow progression prerequisite;
- the Blue-side Tunnel entrance;
- green-pipe landmark;
- mixed-color and green tunnel route;
- multicolor bridge;
- red zigzag stairs;
- hidden Shiny location;
- final Big Makeover interaction.

If I have reached the hidden location but Big Makeover is still missing, I finish the Shiny interaction before leaving.

<h2 id="achievement-route-decisions">When I chase achievements and when I ignore them</h2>

I divide the achievements into three groups.

### Natural progression achievements

I let these happen during the normal route:

- Big Walk;
- Big View;
- Big Sit;
- Big Tunnel;
- Big Ride;
- Big Wall;
- Big Goodbye.

I do not leave the current route to farm them because the game naturally sends me through their trigger points.

### Quick equipment achievements

I complete these whenever the required item is already in front of me:

- Big Pack;
- Big Help.

If the Backpack or hip equipment is currently being used for progression, I trigger the achievement then instead of making a separate return trip later.

### Cleanup achievements

I leave these until their route is convenient:

- Big Climb;
- Big Game;
- Big Makeover.

For Big Climb, I wait until I already have access to the high Green route.

For Big Makeover, I wait until Yellow progression has opened the underground network.

For Big Game, I finish the normal campaign first and then perform complete cleanup.

That order keeps achievement hunting from breaking the progression flow.

<h2 id="stuck">Big Walk achievement fixes</h2>

### Big Walk did not unlock when the drawbridge opened

Cross completely to the other side.

Opening the drawbridge and crossing it are separate states.

### Big Pack did not unlock when I picked up a Backpack

Attach it to the character's back.

Holding it is not wearing it.

### Big View did not unlock when I picked up the portable map

Enter the physical Map Room.

The portable map is not the Big View trigger.

### Big Help did not unlock after equipping the hip belt

Attach a compatible item to the belt.

I use the folded portable map as my clean test.

### Big Sit did not unlock at Green Tower

Make sure the Chairlift is active and board a moving seat.

Reaching the station alone is not enough.

### Big Climb did not unlock during the Chairlift ride

Leave the upper transport route and continue toward the island's highest point.

Big Climb is an elevation trigger, not a Chairlift trigger.

### Big Tunnel did not unlock after the Yellow door opened

Walk fully inside the Tunnel.

### Big Ride did not unlock at the Train Station

Board the functioning Train rather than waiting on the platform.

### Big Wall did not unlock when I found the gate

Finish the [Black Tower route](/big-walk/black-tower) and pass beyond the Wall.

### Big Goodbye unlocked but Big Game did not

That is expected.

Continue the same save and move into [Purple Puzzle cleanup](/big-walk/purple-puzzles) and the [Big Game completion route](/big-walk/ending-guide).

### Big Makeover did not unlock when I reached the Shiny area

Finding the location is not the final trigger.

Complete the Shiny interaction described in the [Shiny Guide](/big-walk/how-to-get-shiny).

<h2 id="faq">Big Walk achievement FAQ</h2>

### How many achievements are in Big Walk?

There are **12 achievements**.

### Should I follow achievement rarity when deciding what to do next?

No.

I follow progression order and treat rarity as unrelated to the best route through the island.

Optional equipment or hidden interactions can be rarer than later progression achievements.

### Are Big Goodbye and Big Game the same completion state?

No.

Big Goodbye finishes the normal campaign. Big Game is the later full-completion achievement.

### Does Big Game require Big Makeover?

I do not treat Big Makeover as part of the confirmed Big Game route.

Big Game progression is handled through full puzzle completion, the final-key check, and the hidden ending route. Big Makeover remains a separate Shiny achievement.

### Can I still work on achievements after Big Goodbye?

Yes.

I continue on the same save for Purple Puzzles, unfinished normal challenges, Big Game cleanup, and optional achievements I skipped earlier.

### Is Big View triggered by picking up the portable map?

No.

Big View comes from entering the physical Map Room. The portable map can then be used for Big Help by attaching it to an equipped hip belt.
