Big Walk has **12 achievements**. I track them in progression order rather than
rarity order: four early achievements, four tower and transport achievements,
then four late-game completion goals.

The first four fit naturally into the tutorial, Red Tower, and Map Room route.
Big Sit and Big Climb grow out of Green progression, Big Tunnel belongs to
Yellow, and Big Ride belongs to Blue. I keep the final four as separate trigger
checks until the complete Black Tower, ending, and Shiny routes are available.

<h2 id="big-walk">
  Big Walk achievement: cross the opening drawbridge
</h2>

Big Walk triggers when the group crosses the first red drawbridge.

The route is:

1. return four Heads to the beach receiver;
2. take the blank key;
3. follow the cutter arrows until the key is fully shaped;
4. insert the finished key into the drawbridge mechanism;
5. wait for the bridge to extend;
6. cross to the far side.

The achievement is tied to the crossing, not simply inserting the key. I keep
moving until both players are on the main-island side.

A key that fits but does not open the bridge is usually unfinished. I return to
the latest cutter and follow its arrow rather than repeatedly inserting the same
partly shaped key.

<h2 id="big-pack">
  Big Pack achievement: wear the Backpack
</h2>

Big Pack requires a player to **wear a Backpack**. Carrying a Head, Walkie-talkie,
light, or Backpack-shaped object in the hands does not satisfy the trigger.

I pick up the first Backpack after the drawbridge and place it onto the
character's back. Once equipped, it becomes useful beyond the achievement:

- one Head or loose tool can be stored without occupying a hand;
- the carrier can still climb and point;
- a light or Walkie-talkie can remain available;
- and the group can move more progression objects per return trip.

I give the first Backpack to the player responsible for Heads and keys rather
than automatically assigning it to the Host.

I do not abandon an active route to search for several Backpacks. One equipped
Backpack, two working Walkie-talkies, and a known return point are enough for the
first Red and Green collection loops.

<h2 id="big-view">
  Big View achievement: enter the physical Map Room
</h2>

Big View triggers when the group reaches the physical Map Room after the Red
Tower route.

The short progression check is:

1. cross the tutorial drawbridge;
2. reach the five-slot Red Tower receiver;
3. return five Heads;
4. take and fully shape the second blank key;
5. open the upper red entrance;
6. enter the room containing the island model.

and Laser Pointers are equipment found after entering the room.

<h2 id="big-help">
  Big Help achievement: wear an item on the hip
</h2>

Big Help requires a player to wear something on the hip.

My clean trigger is:

1. pick up the hip belt;
2. equip the belt around the character's waist;
3. attach the folded portable map to the available side;
4. leave the item seated on the belt until the achievement appears.

Holding the belt in a hand is not enough. Holding the portable map against the
character is also not enough. The item needs to be attached to the equipped hip
slot.

The belt remains useful after the trigger because the map carrier can keep both
hands free while moving through an unfamiliar region. I keep it when:

- the portable map is needed for a longer route;
- the Backpack is already holding a Head;
- both hands are reserved for communication or puzzle objects;
- or another player needs to remove and inspect the map during travel.

I leave the belt and map at a named station when the next objective is a short
return to a visible tower. Carrying fewer optional items makes lost progression
objects easier to identify.

<h2 id="transport-achievements">
  Big Sit, Big Climb, Big Tunnel, and Big Ride achievement order
</h2>

These four achievements follow the Green, Yellow, and Blue progression routes.

### Big Sit

**Requirement:** Ride the Chairlift.

The Green receiver does not activate the Chairlift directly. My trigger route is:

1. return five Heads to the Green receiver;
2. collect the blank Green Key;
3. pass it through all five cutter stations;
4. insert the finished key at the Chairlift station beside Green Tower;
5. wait for the chairs to begin circulating;
6. board a moving seat.

Reaching the station or standing beside an inactive chair does not count. I remain
on the moving seat until Big Sit appears.

  Green Tower and Chairlift Guide

covers the five-Head cache route, all five cutters, the correct station, and the

### Big Climb

**Requirement:** Reach the island's highest point.

The Chairlift is access, not the Big Climb trigger itself. I activate it through
the Green route, ride toward the upper section, leave the Chairlift, and continue
toward the island's highest visible terrain.

I do not wait on the seat for a second transport achievement. Big Climb appears
only after the character reaches the highest-point trigger.

Until I have a complete summit route, I use the upper Chairlift station as the
starting landmark and avoid promising one exact turn or drop-off point.

### Big Tunnel

**Requirement:** Enter a big tunnel.

My trigger route is:

1. return five Heads to the Yellow receiver;
2. collect the blank Yellow Key;
3. pass it through all five cutter stations;
4. insert the finished key at the Yellow Tunnel door;
5. wait for the entrance to open;
6. walk fully through the doorway.

Finding the exterior entrance, reaching the final cutter, or standing beside the
closed door does not complete Big Tunnel. I cross the threshold and continue into
the underground passage before checking the achievement.

### Big Ride

**Requirement:** Ride the Train.

The inactive Train Station is useful as a staging point early in the game, but
standing on the platform or inside a parked carriage does not count.

My trigger route is:

1. return five Heads to the Blue receiver;
2. collect the blank Blue Key;
3. pass it through all five cutter stations;
4. use the finished key at the Train lock;
5. wait until the Train begins circulating;
6. board a moving carriage.

In my run, Big Ride appeared immediately after the group jumped onto the moving
Train. I stayed aboard until the achievement notification appeared instead of
jumping off at the first landmark.

<h2 id="late-achievements">
  Big Wall, Big Goodbye, Big Game, and Big Makeover
</h2>

  I use the four sections below as separate trigger checks. They prevent the
  Wall, first ending, complete ending, and Shiny goals from being merged into one
  vague objective, but they are not substitutes for complete Black Tower,
  ending, and Shiny route walkthroughs.
</div>

### Big Wall

**Requirement:** Go beyond the wall.

I do not mark Big Wall when the group only reaches or looks at the Wall. The
character must pass beyond it.

This belongs to the later Black Tower progression. Until the complete route is
documented, I treat these as the confirmed boundaries:

- seeing the Wall is not enough;
- reaching a closed entrance is not enough;
- crossing to the far side is the achievement state.

I keep the Wall route separate from Big Goodbye because passing it is a
progression milestone, not automatically the end of the game.

### Big Goodbye

**Requirement:** Finish the game.

I track Big Goodbye as the first finish state. It is not the same box as Big Game.

I do not use this short requirement as permission to guess the complete route.
The achievement belongs after the late Black Tower and Wall progression, and I
mark it only when the game reaches its finish trigger.

If the group can continue afterward, I leave Big Game unchecked and return to the
remaining late objectives.

### Big Game

**Requirement:** Completely finish the game.

Big Game is the full-completion state after Big Goodbye. I keep the two separate
because reaching an ending does not prove every later completion requirement has
been satisfied.

My current checklist is deliberately conservative:

- Big Goodbye has already unlocked;
- the remaining late progression has been completed;
- no required end-state objective is still open;
- and the game has produced the separate complete-finish trigger.

I do not claim that every achievement, every visible puzzle, or Shiny is required
unless the final route confirms it.

### Big Makeover

**Requirement:** Get Shiny.

I treat Shiny as an independent cooperative objective, not as an automatic reward
for finishing the game.

Until the full route is available, I use three boundaries:

- reaching an ending does not complete Big Makeover by itself;
- seeing a cosmetic or color-changing area is not enough;
- the character must complete the interaction that produces the Shiny state.

I keep this box separate from Big Game so I do not assume the two triggers are
identical.

Before beginning late-game cleanup, I:

- check the Map Room for unfinished challenge locations;
- finish the active tower and transport progression;
- return important objects to named stations;
- and keep Big Wall, Big Goodbye, Big Game, and Big Makeover as four separate
  checklist states.

<h2 id="completion-checklist">
  My 12-achievement completion checklist
</h2>

I mark achievements in this order:

- [ ] Big Walk — crossed the opening drawbridge
- [ ] Big Pack — wore a Backpack
- [ ] Big View — entered the physical Map Room
- [ ] Big Help — wore an attached hip item
- [ ] Big Sit — rode the activated Green Chairlift
- [ ] Big Climb — used the upper Chairlift route and reached the highest point
- [ ] Big Tunnel — entered the Tunnel opened through the Yellow route
- [ ] Big Ride — boarded the moving Train after Blue activation
- [ ] Big Wall — went beyond the Wall
- [ ] Big Goodbye — reached the first finish state
- [ ] Big Game — reached the separate complete-finish state
- [ ] Big Makeover — became Shiny

The first four fit naturally into the early guide cluster. The next four align
with Green, Yellow, and Blue progression. The final four remain separate late-game
checks without forcing the group to abandon its current tower route.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Big Walk achievement fixes
</h2>

### Big Walk did not unlock after the bridge opened

Cross all the way to the far side. Inserting the key and watching the bridge
extend is not the same as completing the crossing.

### Big Pack did not unlock when I carried a Head

Equip the Backpack on the character's back. The achievement is for wearing the
Backpack, not carrying an object.

### Big View did not unlock when I picked up the portable map

Enter the physical Map Room. The portable map is an item found inside and is not
the room-visit trigger by itself.

### Big Help did not unlock when I picked up the belt

Equip the belt, then attach a compatible item such as the folded portable map to
the hip slot.

### Big Sit did not unlock at the Chairlift station

Confirm that the Green Key completed all five cuts and activated the Chairlift.
Then board a moving seat. Reaching the station or standing beside an inactive
chair does not count.

### Big Climb did not unlock during the Chairlift ride

Leave the upper Chairlift route and continue toward the island's highest point.
The achievement is tied to elevation, not to remaining seated.

### Big Tunnel did not unlock when the Yellow door opened

Walk fully through the doorway into the underground passage. Opening the entrance
or standing at the threshold is not enough.

### Big Ride did not unlock at the Train Station

Confirm that the Blue Key completed all five cuts and activated the Train loop.
Then wait for a carriage to move and board it. Reaching the platform, touching a
parked carriage, or standing inside the station does not trigger Big Ride.

### Big Wall did not unlock when we found the Wall

Pass beyond it. Reaching or viewing the Wall is a different state from crossing
to the far side.

### Big Goodbye unlocked but Big Game did not

Keep the two achievements separate. Big Goodbye is the first finish state; Big
Game is the later complete-finish state and can still require unfinished
progression.

<h2 id="faq">
  Big Walk achievement FAQ
</h2>

### How many achievements are in Big Walk?

There are 12 achievements.

### Are hidden achievements automatically missable?

No. A hidden requirement is concealed in the platform list, but that alone does
not mean it can be permanently missed.

### Should I follow achievement rarity order?

No. I follow progression order. I clean up the early equipment goals first, then
use Green for Chairlift achievements, Yellow for Big Tunnel, Blue for Big Ride,
and the late route for the final four checks.

### Is Big Game the same as Big Goodbye?

No. Big Goodbye requires finishing the game. Big Game requires completely
finishing it.

### Does picking up the portable map unlock Big View or Big Help?

Entering the physical Map Room completes Big View. Attaching the portable map to
an equipped hip belt is the clean Big Help trigger.
