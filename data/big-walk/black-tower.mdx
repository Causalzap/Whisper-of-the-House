The **Black Tower in Big Walk** is where the late-game route starts to feel different from the Red, Green, Yellow, and Blue progression.

The tower itself is not the final area. Its job is to introduce the Black route, show the strange vision sequence, and eventually give you the **Black Key** used at the huge Wall / gate leading toward the large black sphere.

That distinction matters. I originally treated the Black Tower, the Wall, and the black sphere like one destination, which makes the late-game instructions much harder to follow than they need to be.

  </ol>

My stopping rule for this page is simple: **the Black Tower route is complete when the Black Key has opened the Wall**. Everything beyond that belongs to the final sphere and ending route.

<h2 id="where-is-black-tower">Where to find the Black Tower</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

I look for the Black Tower around the **central part of the island**, rather than treating it like another colored tower sitting at the edge of its own region.

The easiest way I first recognized it was from the **Chairlift**.

From higher ground, the dark monolith stands out much more clearly than it does while walking through the trees and terrain below.

In my route, the tower sat toward the central / eastern direction from where we were travelling, with the Blue side and the Yellow tunnel area giving useful orientation nearby.

I do not obsess over compass-perfect directions here.

Once I can see the monolith, I switch from map navigation to direct visual navigation.

### When I go to the Black Tower

I treat the Black Tower as **late main progression**.

By this point I already want good access to the island through the earlier colored tower routes, because the Black Tower can send me back out to collect more unfinished puzzle rewards.

If I still have an obvious colored tower route unfinished, I would rather finish that first than walk repeatedly between the Black Tower and distant parts of the map.

<h2 id="trigger-black-tower">Trigger the Black Tower together</h2>

At the tower, I do not immediately run through the room independently.

There is a cooperative activation where the players need to **operate the trigger together**.

In my two-player run, we solved this by simply counting down:

**3 → 2 → 1 → press.**

If nothing seems to happen, I do not start searching the tower for another hidden button.

I regroup everyone at the trigger and try the synchronized input again.

This is much faster than splitting up around the structure.

<h2 id="black-tower-vision">What happens during the Black Tower vision</h2>

After the activation, one player may suddenly lose normal control and enter a strange **vision / hypnotized state**.

That happened to one of us while the other player remained outside the same experience.

The vision included imagery of:

- the dark monolith;
- a large black ball / sphere;
- a red figure;
- other distorted characters or shapes.

The important practical point is that **different players do not necessarily experience the moment in exactly the same way**.

If my teammate says they are stuck in a cutscene while I can still move, I wait.

I do not assume their game has frozen, and I do not immediately leave the tower carrying progression objects away from them.

The sequence finishes and returns the affected player to normal control.

### The vision is a progression clue, not another puzzle

I do not try to decode every image as a separate combination.

The useful information is directional: the Black Tower is connecting this part of the game with the **Wall and the large black sphere beyond it**.

That becomes much clearer once the Black Key appears.

<h2 id="black-tower-completion-requirement">Check what the Black Tower still needs</h2>

After the first activation, I check the Black Tower's current completion requirement.

This is where I would avoid copying a fixed number from somebody else's run.

In one two-player run, the tower was asking for **six completion pieces total**. We already had two available, which left **four more** to find.

I treat that number as the state of that particular session, not a universal rule for every group.

The better rule is:

**trust the tower's live requirement.**

If it still needs more, I leave the Black Tower and target unfinished normal puzzles instead of walking circles around the monolith.

For individual puzzle solutions, I use the [Big Walk Puzzle Solutions guide](/big-walk/puzzles).

### When I turn around instead of searching near the tower

If the tower says I still need several completion pieces, I leave.

There is no reason to repeatedly search the Black Tower interior for hidden rewards.

The missing progress is elsewhere on the island.

That was an important time saver for me: the Black Tower is a **progress checkpoint**, not the place where all of its required pieces are earned.

<h2 id="how-to-get-black-key">How to get the Black Key</h2>

Once the Black Tower's current requirement is satisfied, I return to the monolith and check the upper tower area again.

At that point, the tower progression opens access to the **Black Key**.

I do **not** casually throw the Black Key down slopes or keep passing it between players while travelling.

Unlike a normal puzzle attempt, losing track of the key creates a long recovery walk.

My group gives it to one player and keeps that player in sight.

<h2 id="where-black-key-goes">Where does the Black Key go?</h2>

The **Black Key goes to the Wall / huge gate**.

It does not go into the Black Tower again, and it does not get inserted directly into the large black sphere.

The gate is a huge structure in the Wall leading toward the sphere area.

In one route, we used the **green train line** to cut part of the trip. In another, we approached using the **gondola**.

I do not unlock either transport system specifically for this step if I already have a direct route. They are conveniences, not new Black Tower objectives.

The useful visual landmarks are:

- the enormous Wall;
- the large green gate;
- the higher section of the Wall;
- a large finger-shaped rock visible on one approach;
- the black sphere beyond the gate.

The interaction point is at the gate.

<h2 id="use-black-key-wall">Use the Black Key at the Wall</h2>

As I approach the correct part of the Wall, the atmosphere changes noticeably.

Communication begins to distort and nearby players can become difficult or impossible to hear normally.

I treat that as confirmation that I am entering the correct late-game transition area rather than assuming the Walkie-Talkie has simply failed.

When I reach the key point, I bring the Black Key directly to the Wall interaction.

In my run, once the key was clearly aligned with the correct location, we committed to the insertion instead of continuing to search for another socket.

The progression boundary is:

**Black Tower → Black Key → Wall opens → Black Sphere → Ending.**

Keeping those as separate landmarks avoids the common mistake of calling every late-game structure the “Black Tower.”

<h2 id="black-tower-stuck">Why the Black Tower route is not progressing</h2>

### Nothing happened when we activated the tower

I regroup the players and synchronize the trigger again.

A simple spoken countdown is more reliable than having everyone press whenever they arrive.

### My teammate is stuck or cannot move

I wait for the vision sequence.

One player can enter the strange cutscene-like state while another remains mobile, so different perspectives do not automatically mean the progression failed.

### The Black Key is not ready

I check the tower's current completion requirement.

If it still wants more puzzle rewards, I leave and finish unresolved island puzzles instead of searching the monolith itself.

### I have the Black Key but cannot use it

I make sure I am at the **Wall gate**, not at the black sphere.

The key belongs to the huge gate separating the island from the sphere route.

### We cannot hear one another near the Wall

I keep moving toward the key interaction.

Voice distortion is part of the transition around this section. I do not turn around solely because communication suddenly becomes unreliable.

<h2 id="black-tower-faq">Big Walk Black Tower FAQ</h2>

### Does everyone see the same Black Tower vision?

Not necessarily. In my run, one player entered the vision while the other could still move and observe the tower normally.

### Should I carry the Black Key straight to the black sphere?

No. I use it on the Wall / large gate first. The sphere is on the other side of that progression step.

### Does opening the Wall immediately end the game?

No. Opening the Wall gives access to the next late-game area. The large black sphere still contains the final main-route sequence before Big Goodbye.

<h2 id="black-tower-takeaway">Treat the Tower, Wall, and Sphere as three separate steps</h2>

The cleanest way I remember the late-game route is:

**Black Tower → Vision → tower requirement → Black Key → Wall → black sphere.**

The biggest mistakes are trying to decode the vision as another standalone puzzle, searching the Black Tower for completion pieces that are actually elsewhere on the island, and carrying the Black Key directly toward the sphere without first opening the Wall.

Once the Wall opens, I stop using this guide and move to the final sphere and ending route.
