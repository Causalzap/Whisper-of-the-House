*Major ending spoilers follow.*

Big Walk has two completion states that are easy to confuse.

**Big Goodbye** is the normal ending. I reach it after the main tower route, Black Tower progression, the Wall, and the final Black Sphere sequence.

**Big Game** is the full-completion ending. I reach it later on the same save, after clearing the post-game Purple Puzzles, finishing anything I skipped earlier, collecting the final key, and entering the hidden completion room.

<h2 id="how-to-get-the-big-goodbye-ending">How to get the Big Goodbye ending</h2>

<AdUnit placement="article_after_first_h2" />

I start this guide's main route **after the Wall has opened**.

If I still have the Black Key, am searching for its slot, or have not passed beyond the Wall yet, I am not at the ending sequence.

The [Black Tower and Black Key Guide](/big-walk/black-tower) owns:

- the Black Tower location;
- synchronized activation;
- vision sequence;
- Black Tower completion requirement;
- Black Key;
- Wall / gate interaction.

Once the Wall opens, I leave that route behind and continue toward the **large Black Sphere**.

The late-game landmarks are easier to follow when I keep them separate:

**Black Tower → Wall → Black Sphere → ending building.**

<h2 id="black-sphere-ending-route">Complete the final Black Sphere route</h2>

The Black Sphere is the enclosed final main-route area.

This is not the Black Tower again.

By the time I enter the sphere, the Black Tower and Black Key progression should already be finished.

Inside, I continue through the cooperative rooms until I reach the dark red final chamber with the suspended orb-like mechanism.

### Press the final button beside the hanging mechanism

Near the end of the sphere, I reach a dark red room containing the suspended mechanism and a nearby button.

This is the final progression check inside the sphere.

**Wave Goodbye and opening the entrance are two separate steps.**

If I waved but the ending did not continue, I return to the entrance button before retracing the route.

<h2 id="how-to-get-the-big-game-ending">How to get the Big Game ending</h2>

I begin the full-completion route only after Big Goodbye.

My order is:

1. continue the same save;
2. complete all seven Purple Puzzles;
3. return to the Map Room;
4. identify any normal puzzles still unfinished;
5. complete those remaining challenges;
6. recover any completion objects that were left behind;
7. deliver every required object;
8. return to the final-key position near the beginning;
9. collect the final key when it rises;
10. use it to open the hidden completion area;
11. finish the final reading;
12. walk through the white opening.

The important decision is **when to stop checking the final-key location**.

If the key is not there, I do not repeatedly reload or stand beside the spawn point.

I assume the save still has an incomplete puzzle or delivery somewhere else.

At this stage, losing the final key creates a much longer recovery problem than carrying it carefully.

<h2 id="why-the-final-key-is-not-appearing">Why the final key is not appearing</h2>

When the final key is missing, I do not treat the spawn point itself as broken first.

I assume the save has not passed its full-completion check.

### One or more Purple Puzzles are unfinished

I recount all seven rather than stopping after the easiest or most visible ones.

If I am unsure which Purple challenge is still incomplete, I use the [Purple Puzzles Guide](/big-walk/purple-puzzles) rather than re-solving them from memory.

### A normal puzzle was skipped before Big Goodbye

The post-game Purple set does not erase unfinished main-island puzzles.

I return to the Map Room and inspect the full island again.

### A challenge was started but not actually completed

Reaching the location or partially operating the mechanism is not enough.

I look for the actual completion state or reward.

### A physical completion object was left behind

This is one of the easiest full-completion requirements to overlook.

I check:

1. the solved challenge area;
2. the usual temporary cache;
3. player Backpacks;
4. tower or completion rooms;
5. any place where the group dropped objects before using transport.

I do not assume every reward moves automatically.

### I am checking the final-key position too early

Big Goodbye alone does not generate the key.

Even finishing the seven Purple Puzzles is not enough if older progression remains incomplete.

My final-key checklist is:

1. Big Goodbye complete;
2. all seven Purple Puzzles complete;
3. no unresolved normal challenges;
4. every required completion object delivered;
5. final-key position checked again.

<h2 id="big-walk-ending-explained">What the Big Game ending means</h2>

Big Walk does not end full completion with a boss, villain reveal, or large lore twist.

The final room returns to what the game has been asking the group to do the entire time:

- find one another after getting separated;
- explain things without perfect communication;
- hand objects between different roles;
- trust someone working on a part of the puzzle I cannot see;
- turn mistakes into shared stories.

The final reading makes that theme explicit.

The island and its challenges were built for the players, but the memorable part of the walk comes from what the group did together inside that space.

That is why the final sequence asks for reflection rather than presenting another difficult puzzle.

My interpretation of the two endings is:

**Big Goodbye ends the journey through the island.**

**Big Game asks me to think about who made that journey memorable.**

That is an interpretation of the ending presentation, not a separate hidden lore statement from the game.

<h2 id="ending-faq">Big Walk endings FAQ</h2>

### Do I need 100% completion for Big Goodbye?

No.

Big Goodbye finishes the normal main route. Full completion is required for Big Game.

### Can I continue after Big Goodbye?

Yes.

I continue the same save for the seven Purple Puzzles, unfinished normal challenges, final key, and Big Game route.

### Is Big Game a different branching choice?

No.

I reach Big Goodbye first and then continue toward Big Game on the same save.

### What is the building across the water called?

I have not found a clear in-game proper name for it.

I call it the **ending building** or **building across the water** instead of inventing an official name.

### Can I keep playing after Big Game?

Yes.

The final completion screen still provides a **Continue** option.

<h2 id="final-takeaway">Stop at Big Goodbye or continue to Big Game</h2>

I use one stopping rule.

If the group only wants to finish the main journey:

**Black Sphere → final mechanism → building across the water → Wave Goodbye → Big Goodbye.**

If the group wants full completion:

**Big Goodbye → seven Purple Puzzles → remaining normal puzzles → completion deliveries → final key → hidden room → white exit → Big Game.**

I do not mix those two goals while playing.

Finishing the normal route first keeps the ending clean.

Then I decide whether the group still wants to return to the island and clear everything.
