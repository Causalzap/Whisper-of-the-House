<h2 id="what-you-need-before-getting-shiny">What you need before getting Shiny</h2>


There is one important progression requirement:

**complete the Yellow Tower / lighthouse route first.**

That completion opens the underground network connecting the different colored parts of the island.

I keep the actual Yellow puzzle, Head requirement, key route, and tunnel unlock on the [Yellow Tower and Tunnel guide](/big-walk/yellow-tower-tunnel). Once that route is finished, I stop thinking about Yellow progression and use the tunnel network only as transport to Shiny.

I do **not** need to repeat the Yellow Tower.

### Bring a light, but do not overprepare

The tunnel becomes dark very quickly.

I do not cross the island looking for special equipment before starting, because there is a usable light source around the entrance route.

What matters is taking it with me.

Without a light, the first colored sections are still navigable, but identifying the green pipe, drops, side passages, and lower tunnel becomes much more annoying than it needs to be.

<h2 id="best-entrance-for-shiny">Use the Blue tunnel entrance</h2>

There are multiple colored sections in the underground system, but I do not try to enter from whichever tower happens to be closest.

For this route I use the **Blue Tower / lighthouse entrance**.

From the Blue side, I go through the tunnel doors and immediately prepare for the darkness.

The first useful landmark is the **green pipe**.

My route from here is:

**Blue entrance → green pipe → right → drop → right.**

I do not keep pushing straight past the green-pipe section.

That is the first place where an otherwise simple route starts branching enough to waste time.

<h2 id="mixed-color-tunnel-route">Go through the mixed-color tunnels</h2>

After the drop and right turn, I enter an area where several tunnel colors begin mixing together.

This is a useful confirmation point.

If everything still looks purely Blue and I have been walking for a while, I assume I missed the earlier turn and backtrack rather than continuing deeper.

Inside the mixed-color section, I take the route that leads **left**.

That eventually brings me into the **green tunnel section**.

The colored tunnel walls are more useful to me than trying to remember distance. Underground, several stretches look similar, so I navigate by transition:

**Blue → mixed colors → Green.**

That sequence tells me far more than “walk for another minute.”

<h2 id="green-tunnels-shiny-route">Do not take the dangerous turn in the green tunnels</h2>

The green section contains the route mistake I am most careful about.

There is a direction that looks usable but can send me toward a fall.

I do **not** take that drop-off route.

I keep going straight.

This is one of the places where I stop experimenting once I know the correct path. Falling here does not teach me anything useful; it only forces another recovery.

My next checkpoint is a bridge using **multiple tunnel colors**.

4. the green tunnels;

<h2 id="where-to-find-shiny">
  Where to find Shiny in Big Walk
</h2>

The lower tunnel after the red stairs leads into the hidden Shiny location.

This is not a surface paint station and it is not attached directly to one of the major towers.

That is why I found the achievement much easier once I stopped searching around visible buildings and treated it as an **underground secret route**.



I interact with the Shiny setup here and gain access to the previously unavailable color options.

I then use those colors on the characters instead of immediately leaving the room.

That final use of the Shiny colors is the part I treat as the actual **Big Makeover completion check**.

For the full achievement list and the other unlock conditions, I use the [Big Walk Achievements guide](/big-walk/achievements). This page owns only the Shiny route and Big Makeover completion process.

<h2 id="shiny-route-not-working">Why the Shiny route is not working</h2>

When I cannot reach Shiny, I troubleshoot the route by progression checkpoint instead of wandering through random tunnel branches.

### The underground route is not available

I check Yellow progression first.

If the Yellow Tower / lighthouse route is unfinished, I stop. The correct fix is completing that progression, not finding another hidden entrance.

### I keep getting lost immediately after entering

I return to the **Blue entrance** and restart from the green pipe.

My first four moves are:

**green pipe → right → drop → right.**

If I cannot reproduce those cleanly, continuing deeper usually makes navigation worse.

### I reached green tunnels but cannot find the bridge

I check whether I took the wrong branch in the green section.

I keep straight through the safe route rather than dropping into the bad path.

The multicolor bridge is the landmark that confirms I am back on track.

### I crossed the bridge but cannot find Shiny

I look for the **red zigzag stairs** and go down.

The secret location is reached from the lower tunnel. Staying on the upper level is the mistake.

### I found the Shiny area but Big Makeover did not finish

I do not leave immediately after discovering the room.

I use the newly available Shiny colors on the characters first, then check the achievement state.

<h2 id="big-walk-shiny-faq">Big Walk Shiny FAQ</h2>

### Do I need to carry a light from somewhere else on the island?

I would not make a special trip for one. A light source is available around the tunnel entrance route. I take it before entering the darker sections.

### Is the Blue entrance mandatory?

It is the route I use because it is the cleanest path to the correct tunnel branch. The underground system connects multiple colored sections, but approaching from another side creates more navigation than this achievement needs.

### Should I keep exploring the tunnels after finding Shiny?

Only if I am deliberately looking for other underground secrets. For Big Makeover, once I reach Shiny and use the new color options, I consider this route finished rather than turning the achievement run into a full tunnel sweep.

<h2 id="shiny-route-takeaway">Use six landmarks instead of memorizing the entire tunnel system</h2>

I do not try to memorize every underground junction.

I only remember these six checkpoints:

**Blue entrance → green pipe → mixed colors → green tunnels → multicolor bridge → red zigzag stairs.**

Then I drop to the lower tunnel and follow it into Shiny.

That turns a confusing underground network into a short sequence of visual checks.

The biggest time saves are finishing Yellow progression before searching, carrying the available light, refusing the bad drop in the green tunnel section, and remembering that the last move at the red stairs is **down**.
