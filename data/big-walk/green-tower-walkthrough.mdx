The Green Tower route does not end when the blank Green Key appears. I collect
five Heads around the Green region, use the Train Station as a safe cache, return
all five to the receiver, shape the Green Key through five cutters, insert the
finished key at the Chairlift station beside Green Tower, and board a moving
chair to unlock **Big Sit**.

The Green route has two stations that are easy to confuse. I use the inactive
**Train Station** only as a Head cache. The **Chairlift station near Green Tower**
is the destination for the finished Green Key.

<h2 id="use-train-station">
  Use the inactive Train Station as the Green Head cache
</h2>

The Train Station is the most useful staging point in the Green region even
before the Blue route activates the Train.

I use one visible part of the platform for:

- completed Heads;
- the portable map after the return path is learned;
- spare tools that do not help the next challenge;
- and equipment that would occupy a reward carrier's hands.

I keep Heads together and put navigation equipment somewhere else. That prevents
a radio, panel, or loose puzzle object from being mistaken for the fifth reward
during the final recount.

The Train Station is only a cache in this walkthrough. I do not search for a Train
key or press inactive controls while completing Green. The transport unlocked by
the Green Key is the separate Chairlift station near Green Tower.

<h2 id="choose-five-heads">
  Choose five practical Head rewards around the Green route
</h2>

The receiver needs five physical Heads, but it does not demand one fixed list of
Green-colored challenges. Heads are not locked to the color of the nearest tower.

My route used rewards already staged near the Train Station plus longer challenges
such as Green Path, Counting and Symbol, and Blindfold. I replace one of those
when another nearby completed Head gives the current group a cleaner return.

I leave a puzzle and choose another when:

- the required communication tool is missing;
- darkness removes the landmark needed for the return;
- the challenge would force the group to carry existing Heads inside;
- the current player-count version adds a role the group cannot fill;
- or several minutes have passed without an agreed method.

<h2 id="protect-three-heads">
  Protect three Heads before the longer communication challenges
</h2>

Three Heads is my Green consolidation point.

As soon as the group has three physical rewards, I stop leaving them beside
different challenge buildings. I move them to the Train Station before starting
the two longer split-role challenges.

I use one short rule here:

> Three Heads go back to the station before I start another long Green puzzle.

The reason is practical: the final challenges can occupy both players, block
normal communication, remove vision, or require every free hand. The full
challenge-specific explanation stays in the puzzle guide rather than being
repeated here.

Before leaving the station again, I take only the equipment needed for the next
room. The map can remain at the cache when the route is already familiar.

<h2 id="finish-last-two">
  Finish the last two challenges without carrying the first three Heads
</h2>

The last two rewards are where the Green route usually slows down.

For Counting, both sides agree on the number language before the separating door
closes. For Blindfold, I leave Heads, navigation tools, and spare equipment
outside before anyone puts on the large face.

I assign the new reward immediately after completion:

- one player carries the Head;
- one player keeps the light or communication tool;
- and nobody replaces the Head with optional equipment on the way back.

When the fifth physical reward is ready, I stop exploring. A visible unfinished
challenge can wait until the active Green progression is complete.

<h2 id="carry-four-heads">
  Move four Heads in one two-player load
</h2>

A two-player group with one Backpack can move four Heads at once:

- one Head inside the Backpack;
- one Head in the Backpack wearer's hands;
- two Heads carried by the second player.

Both players are full. I leave the portable map, spare radio, and every optional
tool behind.

The destination for this load is the Train Station, where the fifth cached Head
is waiting. I keep both players together because one fall or wrong turn can send
several progression objects onto the same slope.

<h2 id="recover-fifth-head">
  Recover the fifth Head and recount the physical rewards
</h2>

At the Train Station, I place the arriving Heads beside the cached reward and
recount all five.

My final check is:

1. one Head is secured in the Backpack;
2. four loose Heads are visible;
3. no tool or puzzle object is being counted as a reward;
4. both players know the direct Green receiver route;
5. no side challenge will be started before submission.

<h2 id="return-to-green">
  Leave the cache together and go directly to the Green receiver
</h2>

The final Head return is not another exploration loop.

Both players leave the Train Station together, follow the known route to Green,
and ignore unrelated structures. At the receiver, I insert the Heads one at a
time and watch every slot activate.

When the fifth slot appears empty, I check the ground and Backpack before walking
back toward a puzzle. A completed challenge does not submit its reward
automatically.

<h2 id="get-green-key">
  Insert five Heads and assign the blank Green Key
</h2>

Completing the receiver produces the blank Green Key.

At this point, the Green Head collection is complete, but Green progression is
not. I assign one player as the key carrier and keep that role unchanged until the
Chairlift moves.

The second player becomes the light and arrow reader. Extra players stay behind
the key carrier so the important object does not disappear inside the group.

<h2 id="green-key-cutters">
  Shape the Green Key through all five cutters
</h2>

The Green key needs five complete cuts.

After every cutter, I wait for the cutting action to finish and read the arrow on
top before removing the key. The arrow points toward the next station, but the
next cutter can sit above, below, or behind the obvious line of travel.

My role split is:

- one permanent Green key carrier;
- one light carrier and arrow reader;
- one route leader when the group is larger than two;
- everyone else follows behind.

I stop after every fall and confirm the key is still held. I do not throw or kick
it between players, and I do not assume a key that fits into the next machine has
already received the previous cut.

When the group loses the route, I return to the latest completed cutter rather
than wandering toward a transport station with a partly shaped key.

<h2 id="find-chairlift-station">
  Take the finished Green Key to the Chairlift beside Green Tower
</h2>

After the fifth cut, I return toward Green Tower and use the nearby cable-car line
as the destination landmark.

This is the point where the two stations matter:

- **Train Station:** earlier Head cache; not the Green key destination.
- **Chairlift station:** beside Green Tower; accepts the finished Green Key.

I do not take the completed key toward the Blue rail system or the Yellow Tunnel.
The Green key belongs at the Chairlift controls next to Green Tower.

The key should now be visibly shaped rather than blank. When it still looks
unfinished or the station does not respond, I return to the last cutter before
pressing every control around the platform.

<h2 id="activate-chairlift">
  Insert the finished key and wait for the Chairlift to move
</h2>

I insert the completed Green Key at the Chairlift station and remain on the
platform until the chairs begin circulating.

The receiver animation, fifth cutter, and key insertion are three separate
progression moments. I do not leave after the fifth cutter assuming the lift has
activated automatically.

Once the chairs are moving, I let one pass when the group is still scattered. A
moving chair is easier to board cleanly when every player agrees on the same seat
and direction.

<h2 id="unlock-big-sit">
  Board a moving Chairlift seat to unlock Big Sit
</h2>

**Big Sit** triggers from riding the activated Chairlift.

Reaching the station is not enough. Standing beside an inactive chair is not
enough. My complete trigger sequence is:

1. submit five Green Heads;
2. finish all five Green key cuts;
3. insert the key at the Chairlift station;
4. wait for the chairs to move;
5. board a moving seat.

I remain seated until the achievement notification appears.

<h2 id="big-climb-route">
  Use the upper Chairlift route as the starting point for Big Climb
</h2>

The Chairlift also removes the first part of the trip toward the island's highest
point.

Big Sit and Big Climb are different triggers:

- Big Sit comes from riding the Chairlift;
- Big Climb comes from reaching the highest point after leaving the lift route.

I use the upper station as the staging point, then continue west toward the higher
terrain. I do not expect Big Climb to appear while remaining seated.

This page keeps the summit section short because the Green progression objective
is activating the Chairlift. The achievement guide owns the final highest-point
check and any route-specific corrections.

<h2 id="session-checkpoint">
  Stop only after the Chairlift is moving
</h2>

The blank Green Key is no longer my preferred Green checkpoint.

A cleaner stopping point is:

- all five Heads accepted;
- all five cuts completed;
- the finished key inserted;
- Chairlift chairs circulating;
- and Big Sit confirmed when the group needs the achievement.

This leaves the next session with an active transport route rather than a loose
key whose remaining cutter progress must be reconstructed.

<h2 id="stuck">
  Green Tower, Green Key, and Chairlift fixes
</h2>

### We completed five puzzles, but one Green slot is empty

Find the physical Head at the latest challenge, Train Station cache, or Backpack.
Puzzle completion and receiver submission are separate actions.

### We have five Heads but cannot carry all of them

Move four in one two-player load and leave the fifth at the Train Station cache.

### The Green Key appeared, but the Chairlift controls do nothing

The key is blank. Pass it through all five cutters before returning to the
Chairlift station.

### The finished key does not activate the station

Confirm that this is the Chairlift station immediately beside Green Tower, not
the inactive Train Station used as the earlier Head cache.

### The arrow points away from the next cutter

Search the nearby high and low ground before traveling farther. The arrow gives a
direction but not the next cutter's elevation.

### Big Sit did not unlock

Wait for the chairs to circulate and board a moving seat. Reaching the station or
standing beside an inactive chair does not count.

### Big Climb did not unlock while riding

Leave the upper Chairlift route and continue toward the island's highest point.
Big Climb is an elevation trigger, not a second riding trigger.

<h2 id="faq">
  Big Walk Green Tower FAQ
</h2>

### How many Heads does the Green receiver need?

It needs five physical Heads.

### Where does the Green Key go?

After all five cuts, the Green Key goes into the Chairlift station beside Green
Tower.

### Is the Train Station the Green key destination?

No. I use the inactive Train Station as a Head cache. The finished Green Key
activates the separate Chairlift station.

### What achievement does the Green route unlock?

Boarding the moving Chairlift unlocks Big Sit.

### When is the Green route fully complete?

I treat it as complete when the Chairlift is moving, not when the blank Green Key
first appears.
