A bottle of rare whiskey, three workstation recipes, and a
long-distance bow kill can all make the difference between
finishing Permafrost's achievements and spending hours
repeating activities that no longer help.

Most gathering, crafting, repair, and travel milestones
accumulate during normal progression. The achievements
that deserve attention earlier are the ones tied to
specific items, difficulty settings, or equipment restrictions.

<h2
  id="station-specialist"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Unlock Station Specialist Without Building All Three Benches
</h2>

**Station Specialist** requires learning these three recipes:

- Tailoring Bench
- Disassembly Bench
- Weapons Bench

The achievement checks the recipes you have learned, not
whether all three workstations have been constructed at your
base.

Skinwalker's objectives at Hunter's Glade introduce the
Tailoring Bench, while Anna's work at House Camp leads to
the Disassembly Bench.

For the Weapons Bench recipe, reach Logan's Camp and follow
the assignment involving the Rat Queen. Speak with her
about Logan and accept the request concerning the creatures
threatening her people.

The **Parking Zoo** objective then directs you to collect
the Weapons Bench recipe. Learning it can complete Station
Specialist when the other two recipes are already unlocked.

  Connoisseur's Bargain and S.O.S.: Two Quick Item Achievements
</h2>

### Connoisseur's Bargain — Sell Rare Whiskey

A regular drink is not enough for this achievement. You need
a bottle of **rare whiskey**, and it must be sold to a trader.

Take the bottle to a settlement trader, open the trading
interface, and place the whiskey among the items you are
selling. Confirm the transaction instead of leaving the
trade screen with the item still in your inventory.

This achievement is triggered by the completed sale, not by
finding the bottle or drinking it.

Keep any rare whiskey you encounter until you have made
the transaction. Ordinary valuables can be sold whenever
money is needed, but consuming the whiskey would remove
the item required for this particular goal.

### S.O.S. — Fire a Flare Gun

**S.O.S.** requires firing a Flare Gun once.

Find a Flare Gun and its ammunition, equip the weapon,
load it, and fire a shot. The achievement does not require
hitting an enemy or completing a special signaling objective.

A successful Flare Gun shot is enough.

Check that the gun is actually loaded before attempting
this. Switching to an empty Flare Gun will not satisfy
the requirement.


<h2
  id="archer"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Archer Achievement: Kill an Animal From Over 55 Yards
</h2>

**Archer** requires killing an animal with a bow from
**more than 55 yards away**. The finishing shot must
be made beyond that distance.

A successful attempt involved shooting a Moose across
an open snowy clearing. The achievement unlocked
immediately after the animal was killed.

For another attempt:

- Find an animal with a clear, unobstructed sightline.
- Carry extra arrows so a missed shot does not end the attempt.
- Keep the animal at a substantial distance until the final hit.
- Move farther away before trying again if Archer does not unlock.

Do not let the animal draw you into a dangerous Cold Zone
while trying to maintain distance. Recover warmth and
attempt another shot from safer terrain if necessary.

<h2
  id="moonrock-and-exploration"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Mineralogist, Explorer, and Cartographer
</h2>

### Mineralogist — Collect Moonrock

**Mineralogist** unlocks when you collect Moonrock,
without requiring a Moonrock Ingot.

Moonrock can be found around meteor impacts and later
exploration areas near bunkers. Bring a pickaxe capable
of harvesting the deposit; the starting Stone Pickaxe
cannot do it.

Once the Moonrock is collected, you can leave the ore
unprocessed. For mining tools and metal processing,
see the <Link href="/permafrost/mining-guide">Permafrost mining guide</Link>.

### Explorer — Walk 31 Miles

**Explorer** requires 31 miles of travel on foot,
approximately 50 kilometers. The achievement can unlock
during ordinary journeys between settlements and quest
locations, so there is no need to prioritize it over
unfinished exploration objectives.

### Cartographer — Discover Three Regions

**Cartographer** requires discovering all three regions:

- The Woods
- House Camp
- The Urban District

The achievement checks these named discoveries, not
whether you have searched every building within them.

<h2
  id="skills-and-workstations"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Jack of All Trades and Master of One: Which Skills Should You Level?
</h2>

**Jack of All Trades** requires five different skills
at Level 10 or higher. **Master of One** requires a
single skill at Level 20.

Running is a practical choice for Master of One. The
achievement has unlocked when Running reached Level 20
during travel through later quest areas, without
requiring a particular combat build.

Jack of All Trades rewards broader progression.
One successful unlock occurred while crafting Hybrid
clothing at a Tailoring Bench, but the clothing itself
was not the requirement. The character had reached
Level 10 across five separate skills.

Check which skills are already closest to Level 10.
Running, Axes, Building, Crafting, and whichever
gathering skill you use most frequently can all
contribute toward the five-skill goal.

The <Link href="/permafrost/classes-skills">Permafrost classes and skills guide</Link>
covers how these skills develop through gameplay.


<h2
  id="survival-achievements"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Cold Resistance, Party Time, and Unlucky Duo
</h2>

### Cold Resistance — Spend 300 Seconds in a Cold Zone

This achievement requires more than **300 seconds**
inside a Cold Zone.

Five minutes of exposure is long enough for Body
Warmth to become dangerous without adequate equipment.

Wear your strongest cold-resistant clothing,
carry a working Makeshift Heater, and enter
with recovered Body Warmth.

Choose a Cold Zone near an accessible return route.
Move back to safety before Health becomes dangerously
low instead of committing to the entire duration
with inadequate protection.

The <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">Cold Zone survival guide</Link>
explains how equipment temperature ratings and
portable heaters affect warmth loss.

The achievement does not require a particular
Cold Zone or a specified temperature in its
listed condition.

### Party Time — Get Wasted

Party Time is a hidden achievement requiring
the **Wasted** state.

Alcohol can cause this status. Drinking until
the character becomes Wasted has triggered
the achievement.

The exact number of drinks required is not
established, so watch your status effects
rather than relying on a fixed bottle count.

This achievement can be attempted near a
safe resting location instead of during
a dangerous journey.

### Unlucky Duo — Parasites and Drunk Together

Unlucky Duo requires two effects at the same time:

- **Parasites**
- **Drunk**

Parasites can develop after eating unsafe food.
Once Parasites is active, alcohol can produce
the intoxication needed for the second condition.

Check your active effects after drinking. Both
**Parasites** and **Drunk** must be present at
the same time. If either effect disappears before
they overlap, the stated achievement condition
has not been met.

Different drinks may affect your status, so check
the actual effects rather than assuming that
drinking alcohol will always complete the combination.

<h2
  id="hidden-achievements"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Memory Collector: How to Find 10 Recordings
</h2>

**Memory Collector** requires collecting 10 recordings.

Recordings can be found while exploring bunkers, radio
installations, and other locations encountered during
the main story.

The Solar Facility contains recordings around its
radio area. Check the surrounding rooms and upper
sections while working toward the communication objective.

Collect each recording when you encounter it, especially
in locations that require a long return journey.

<h2
  id="story-challenges"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Hell Runner and The Hell Has Frozen: Final Story Requirements
</h2>

**The End** requires completing the main storyline.
Reaching Logan's Camp or preparing equipment for
White Hell does not finish the story.

### Hell Runner — Finish White Hell Without Lunar Armor

**Hell Runner** requires completing White Hell
without equipping Lunar Armor.

The restriction concerns what your character wears,
not whether Lunar Armor has been crafted or stored
in your inventory.

Before entering White Hell, check every equipped
clothing slot. Leave Lunar Armor unequipped and
prepare another suitable outfit for the cold.

A functioning Makeshift Heater and warming supplies
can help with exposure, but they do not remove
the armor restriction.

### The Hell Has Frozen — Complete the Story at Minimum Temperature

**The Hell Has Frozen** requires completing the
main story with **Temperature Around Player**
set to its minimum value.

For a deliberate attempt:

1. Create a world using Custom difficulty settings.
2. Set **Temperature Around Player** to the minimum.
3. Keep that setting throughout the storyline.
4. Complete the main story under those conditions.

Choose the required modifier before beginning the
run instead of relying on a difficulty change
immediately before the final objective.

The two achievements have different requirements:
Hell Runner restricts equipment during White Hell,
while The Hell Has Frozen concerns the temperature
modifier used for the story completion.

<h2
  id="cumulative-achievements"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Finish the Long Achievement Counters
</h2>

Several cumulative achievements can advance through
the same activities. Completing them alongside
normal progression saves resources and unnecessary
return trips.

- **Building and gathering:** Construct the structures
  needed at New Home and later settlements while
  collecting Wood for fuel and crafting. These
  activities contribute to Architect and Lumberjack.
- **Looting and dismantling:** Search containers during
  exploration and process unwanted salvage at the
  Disassembly Bench. This advances Scavenger and Recycler.
- **Crafting and maintenance:** Produce useful equipment,
  ammunition, and supplies while repairing frequently
  used tools. Crafting Enthusiast and Handyman benefit
  from these routine tasks.

Keep materials required for active objectives instead
of turning every resource into disposable items just
to increase a counter.

**Survivor** rewards completing a full day without
dying. Finish that objective before deliberately
attempting First Steps or Leap of Fail if you prefer
to avoid interrupting a death-free day.

The remaining kill, projectile, and cash milestones
can be checked in the achievement tracker as you
progress.

<h2
  id="combat-cleanup"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Killing Spree: How to Kill 5 Enemies in 15 Seconds
</h2>

**Killing Spree** requires defeating five enemies
within **15 seconds**.

The challenge depends on having enough enemies
within reach at the same time. Fighting isolated
targets one after another wastes most of the
available window.

Look for a group of at least five enemies and
prepare your weapon before engaging. Bring enough
ammunition to complete the sequence without
needing to stop and resupply.

Enemies that scatter or retreat can make the
challenge harder. Choose an encounter where
targets remain close enough to attack in
quick succession.

<h2
  id="completion-order"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  What Should You Finish First for 42 / 42?
</h2>

### Before Starting the Story

Decide whether to attempt The Hell Has Frozen on
your first run. Set Temperature Around Player to
minimum before starting if that achievement is
part of your plan.

### During Normal Progression

Collect recordings as you travel, learn the
workstation recipes when quests make them available,
and develop skills through gathering and crafting.

Keep a bottle of rare whiskey until you have
sold it to a trader for Connoisseur's Bargain.
Use other alcohol for Party Time rather than
consuming the rare whiskey first.

### Before White Hell and Final Cleanup

Check Hell Runner's restriction before equipping
Lunar Armor for the White Hell objective.

After completing the main story, review the tracker
for unfinished skill levels, cumulative counters,
specific weapon challenges, and discoveries.

Avoid spending scarce ammunition and crafting
materials on optional counters while your active
objectives still require those resources.
