  <GuideQuickAnswer title="Where Do You Go Next in Permafrost?">
    Complete Robyn's tasks at Horizon, heal and feed the dog in Bone Cave,
    then build New Home's radio. Follow Icarus's transmission to Hunter's
    Glade, repair the lookout, and speak to Skinwalker before preparing
    for House Camp.
  </GuideQuickAnswer>

The journey from Horizon to Logan's Camp takes you through several
different kinds of objectives. Early on, you are collecting basic
supplies and building a shelter. At Hunter's Glade, progression depends
on repairing damaged structures. House Camp introduces a longer
renovation assignment, followed by another radio transmission and
a journey through dangerous cold near the Solars.

The main route is:

**Starting Area → Horizon → Bone Cave → New Home → Hunter's Glade → House Camp → Tower of Finn → Solar Fields**

Each location has a clear objective that moves the story forward.
Reaching a marker is not always enough: the dog needs treatment,
New Home needs a working radio, Skinwalker's lookout must be repaired,
and Anna's assignments require specific construction and interaction
steps.

Optional hunting contracts, recordings, and trading opportunities can
appear alongside these objectives. You can complete them while
traveling, but they do not all need to be cleared before the next
main quest begins.


<h2
  id="opening-to-horizon"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  A New Beginning: Build a Campfire and Reach Shelter
</h2>

After the opening collapse, Anders contacts you over the radio.
The first objective is to survive the immediate cold and gather
enough materials to make basic equipment.

Collect the Wood and Stone scattered around the starting area.
The opening Campfire objective requires **3 Wood and 3 Stone**.

Place the Campfire using the Building Tool, add fuel, and warm
yourself beside it. The quest advances after you complete the
warming interaction.

Before leaving the area, follow Anders's instructions to prepare
basic tools and a weapon. You will use a Stone Shiv and Stone Axe
during the opening journey, while the Rope blueprint allows you
to prepare a Crude Bow.

Keep some Wood rather than spending everything on construction.
You may need another fire before reaching Horizon.

### Feel the Heat: Follow the Cave to Horizon

The next objective directs you through a cave toward the first
settlement.

The sequence is straightforward:

1. Enter the marked cave.
2. Follow the passage to its exit.
3. Continue toward Horizon.
4. Find the settlement leader.

You will encounter mineral deposits along the way, including
boulders that cannot be broken with the starting pickaxe.
They are not required for reaching Horizon.

Stay on the route marked by the compass and continue into
the settlement.

### Horizon's Kitchen: Cook Meat for Robyn

Find **Robyn** inside Horizon and speak to her about Icarus.

She explains that Icarus helped establish the settlement and
later left to work on communication between survivor communities.

Before sending you onward, Robyn asks you to prepare food.

Cook **one piece of Meat** at a Campfire or the available cooking
equipment, collect the finished food, and speak to Robyn again.

Returning to Robyn is important. Cooking the Meat satisfies
the first objective, but **Horizon’s Kitchen** also requires
the follow-up conversation.

After that, Robyn directs you toward Bone Cave.

Horizon has beds, traders, and equipment facilities worth
using before departure. Repair damaged tools and carry
at least one Bandage.

There is no requirement to purchase an expensive weapon
or outfit just to continue through Bone Cave.


<h2
  id="dog-days-bone-cave"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dog Days: Heal and Feed the Dog in Bone Cave
</h2>

Robyn sends you into Bone Cave to find a new companion.

Before entering, prepare two specific items:

- **1 Bandage** to treat the injured dog.
- **1 Raw Meat** to feed it afterward.

Any suitable Bandage can complete the treatment objective.
A Makeshift Bandage can be crafted from **2 Cloth**.

The Meat must be the raw item. A cooked Dish does not replace
it in the feeding interaction.

You can obtain Raw Meat by hunting deer outside Horizon.
After killing the animal, equip a suitable Shiv or knife
to process the carcass and collect the Meat.

### Find the Dog Inside Bone Cave

Enter Bone Cave and follow the passage deeper underground.

The injured dog waits in a chamber near a dead survivor,
with a flare helping illuminate the area.

Approach the dog and complete the interactions in order:

1. Select **Heal** and use the Bandage.
2. Interact again when the prompt changes to **Feed**.
3. Give the dog one piece of Raw Meat.

The second interaction completes Dog Days and unlocks
your companion.

If the feeding interaction is unavailable, check whether
the healing step has been completed. If the dog still needs
treatment, simply carrying Meat will not finish the quest.

The companion can follow you, carry supplies, warn of
danger, and search for interesting items.

You can access its storage by interacting with the dog's
pack. This is useful for transporting additional loot,
although items needed for immediate crafting should remain
in your character's inventory.

### Leave Bone Cave

A dangerous bear occupies part of the cave.

Keep a weapon ready when moving through its area.
The animal can become aggressive when approached,
including when it initially appears injured.

After rescuing the dog, follow the objective toward
the cave exit.

Leaving Bone Cave takes you into the Woods and begins
the construction objectives associated with **New Home**.


<h2
  id="new-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  New Home: Build the Required Wooden Shelter
</h2>

Outside Bone Cave, the game directs you to establish
a home using the materials available in the Woods.

Open the Building menu and unlock the required
wooden structures.

The initial shelter objectives are:

- **2 Wooden Foundations**
- **5 Wooden Walls**
- **1 Wooden Doorway**
- **2 Wooden Floor/Roof pieces**
- **1 Wooden Door**

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-new-home-building-objectives.webp"
    alt="Permafrost New Home objective list showing the required wooden foundations walls doorway roof pieces and door"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Complete the specified wooden construction
    objectives before building additional rooms.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Start with the two foundations on level ground. Place the
doorway and walls, add the required roof or floor pieces,
then install the door.

A compact structure is sufficient. You do not need
several rooms or a second floor to complete this stage.

Once the wooden shell is finished, the remaining
objectives introduce basic furniture and equipment.

### Build and Claim the Bed

Place the requested Bed and a Small Wooden Chest.

After constructing the Bed, interact with it and
complete the **Claim** action.

This distinction matters because a Bed can already
exist inside your shelter while the quest is still
waiting for it to be claimed.

If resting is unavailable because the room is too
cold, restore a nearby heat source before attempting
to sleep.

For Campfire interactions, indoor heating, and
equipment needed in colder regions, see <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">how to stay warm in Permafrost</Link>.

### Build the Workbench and Prepare Flint Tools

The next objectives introduce the Workbench and
upgraded tools.

Build a Workbench, then craft the requested Flint
equipment:

- Flint Pickaxe
- Flint Axe
- Flint Shiv

The Flint Pickaxe is especially important because
the starting Stone Pickaxe cannot mine Copper.

You can obtain Flint from Bone Cave, which you
have already passed through.

For Copper, upgrade to a Flint Pickaxe before heading
toward the nearby ravine. The damaged road bridge near
New Home can be repaired with Wood and Rope to make
the crossing easier.

The <Link href="/permafrost/mining-guide">Permafrost mining guide</Link>
covers the bridge repairs, Copper and Coal locations,
and the materials needed to turn ore into Wire.

Finish the tool objectives before moving on to
the furnace and communication equipment.


<h2
  id="new-home-radio"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  New Home: Build the Stone Furnace and Radio Station Bench
</h2>

The next part of New Home requires metal processing.

Unlock and construct the **Stone Furnace**, then
use it to produce the Copper Ingots needed for Wire.

The quest can also offer an optional objective to
search a Cold Zone for supplies. That detour is
not necessary if you have already obtained the
materials through mining and crafting.

Once the required Wire is available, return to
your shelter.

### Build the Radio Station Bench

The Radio Station Bench requires:

- **3 Wire**
- **10 Wood**

  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-radio-station-bench-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Radio Station Bench construction requirements showing three Wire and ten Wood"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The New Home radio requires 3 Wire and
    10 Wood before it can be constructed.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Check that the finished Wire is in your backpack.
Raw Copper and Copper Ingots are not substitutes
for the Wire required by this structure.

The building preview must be valid before
you can place the bench. Make enough room inside
the shelter, select the Radio Station Bench
from Utilities, and construct it.

### Listen to Icarus's Message

Approach the completed radio and select
the interaction to listen.

Allow the transmission to finish.

The message from Icarus directs you toward
**Hunter's Glade**.

When New Home completes, the next main
quest begins:

**The Outpost — Reach Hunter's Glade.**

You can now leave the shelter and follow
the marked route toward the lookout.


<h2
  id="the-outpost"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Outpost: How to Reach Hunter's Glade
</h2>

The road to Hunter's Glade is a longer journey
than the trip through Bone Cave.

You travel from the Woods toward the **Bunker
Checkpoint**, then continue along the road
toward the tall lookout tower.

Before leaving New Home, bring a working weapon,
food, a Bandage, and some Wood for the
repairs waiting at your destination.

### Follow the Road Past Bunker Checkpoint

The Bunker Checkpoint is a concrete facility
beside the road, near a damaged vehicle and
defensive obstacles.

You can search it for supplies, but you do
not need to clear every room to continue
the main quest.

The ruined checkpoint contains useful
containers and equipment, including a
Military Crate associated with improved
Flint equipment.

Be careful about following the stairs into
the exposed higher area behind the checkpoint.
That route enters dangerous cold and is
not required for reaching Hunter's Glade.

Continue along the main road past the
damaged vehicles and wooden watchtowers.

Eventually, the objective turns away from
the road toward a trail leading up to
the steel lookout.

Reaching Hunter's Glade completes
**The Outpost** and starts **High Frequency**.


<h2
  id="high-frequency"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  High Frequency: Repair All 13 Parts of the Lookout
</h2>

At Hunter's Glade, climb the steel lookout
to reach the cabin and upper walkway.

Find **Skinwalker** near the top and speak
to him about the radio equipment.

He explains that the lookout was damaged
and needs repairs before its communication
equipment can be used again.

The objective changes to renovating
the tower.

### Find the Damaged Structures

Equip the Building Tool and inspect
the damaged sections of the cabin
and surrounding walkway.

Repairable pieces appear as blue
construction outlines.

The renovation objective requires
**13 repairs**.

These include damaged walls, windows,
and equipment such as the Bed and
Radio Station Bench.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-hunters-glade-radio-repair.webp"
    alt="Permafrost High Frequency lookout showing a damaged Radio Station Bench that must be renovated"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Use the Building Tool on the blue repair
    outlines until the tower renovation reaches 13/13.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Approach each blue repair outline with the Building
Tool equipped and check its material requirements.
The lookout contains damaged walls, windows, and
equipment, including the Bed and Radio Station Bench.

Wood can be gathered from trees around Hunter's Glade.
If a repair also requires Scrap, check the supplies
collected along the road, particularly damaged vehicles
and containers near Bunker Checkpoint.

Repair each highlighted component rather than placing
a new structure over it. Check the cabin interior and
upper walkway for unfinished sections, then continue
until the renovation counter reaches **13/13**.

### Listen to the Repaired Radio

Once all repairs are complete, the journal
switches to listening to Icarus.

Interact with the restored Radio Station
Bench and let the transmission finish.

Icarus speaks about City Mall and the
possibility of safety for survivors.
The message also points toward the
conflict involving House Camp.

**High Frequency completes after the
transmission.**

The next main quest is **The Way Home**,
which begins with another conversation
with Skinwalker.

### Do You Need to Return Robyn's Recording?

Robyn's recording is a separate assignment. Collect it
from the lookout cabin if you have the task, then return
it to Horizon when convenient.

The main journey continues after you repair the lookout,
listen to Icarus, and speak with Skinwalker again. You do
not need to finish Robyn's recording handoff or Skinwalker's
hunting contracts before starting **The Way Home**.


<h2
  id="the-way-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Way Home: Claim Skinwalker's Reward and Make Tribal Clothes
</h2>

After the radio transmission, speak
to Skinwalker again.

He directs you toward House Camp
and provides access to a reward
box at the lookout.

Open the **Quest Reward** box before
leaving.

It contains a Utility Backpack
and unlocks the Tailoring Bench
recipe.

The Utility Backpack has **25 inventory
slots**, providing more carrying capacity
than the smaller backpack used earlier.

Claiming this reward is a distinct
objective. Speaking to Skinwalker
alone does not finish the equipment
preparation required by The Way Home.

### Build the Tailoring Bench

Return to a suitable construction area
and build the Tailoring Bench.

Its recipe requires:

- **15 Wood**
- **3 Copper Ingots**

  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-tailoring-bench-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Tailoring Bench construction menu requiring fifteen Wood and three Copper Ingots"
    width={1600}

  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Build the Tailoring Bench with 15 Wood
    and 3 Copper Ingots.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The material requirement specifies **Copper Ingots**,
not raw Copper.

If you used your previous ingots to craft Wire
for the Radio Station Bench, process another
batch before attempting construction.

Once the Tailoring Bench is placed,
continue with the clothing objectives
shown in your journal.

### Craft the Tribal Clothing Pieces

The Way Home includes preparation for
traveling farther south.

Unlock the **Tribal Clothes Set**
in the blueprint tree and use the
Tailoring Bench to make the required
pieces.

The clothing objectives include:

- Tribal Boots
- Tribal Cape
- Tribal Gloves
- Tribal Jacket
- Tribal Pants

A Tanning Rack is needed for
processing hide materials used
by the clothing recipes.

Check the active crafting requirements
as you work through the set. Depending
on the piece, you may need materials
such as Treated Hide and Cloth.

Creating the Tailoring Bench is only
one part of this sequence. If the quest
continues showing a Tribal clothing
objective, finish the specified item
before traveling onward.

After the equipment objectives,
follow the marker toward
**House Camp's Outskirts**.

### Reach the Church at House Camp

Take the southern route toward House Camp
and locate the church inside the settlement.

This is where you meet **Anna**,
who is investigating Icarus and
trying to keep the remaining
settlement defenses working.

Speak with her to continue the
main quest sequence.

<h2
  id="house-camp"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  House Camp Flipper: Find Metal Junk and Craft Scrap Tools
</h2>

Speak to Anna inside the church to begin restoring
House Camp's defenses. The first obstacle is obtaining
usable metal materials, since the damaged walls require
Scrap and Wire rather than ordinary Wood.

Search the damaged vehicles, containers, and abandoned
buildings around the settlement for Metal Junk. Follow
the journal's dismantling objective until you have
processed the required amount.

The next equipment objectives introduce three tools:

- **Scrap Axe**
- **Scrap Pickaxe**
- **Scrap Shiv**

Unlock the required recipes and craft the tools at the
appropriate station. Check the journal after each craft
instead of assuming that finding enough Metal Junk
automatically completes the preparation stage.

Once the equipment objectives are satisfied, move to
the damaged defensive sections marked around House Camp.


<h2
  id="house-camp-walls"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  House Camp Flipper: Repair the Four Damaged Defenses
</h2>

The House Camp renovation objective
marks four defensive sections:

- **Near Gate D**
- **Facing the Solar Facility**
- **Near Gate B**
- **Facing the Technocrats Colony**

Approach the damaged structures
with your Building Tool equipped.

The highlighted Armored Wall
interaction shows which materials
are required for the selected
section.

One Armored Wall requires
**10 Scrap and 1 Wire**.

Check the required materials at each
wall rather than assuming every section
uses exactly the same quantity.

After finishing a repair, consult the
journal and move to the next marked
location.

The task is not complete merely
because the first wall looks repaired.

### Where to Find More Scrap

House Camp contains ruined vehicles,
abandoned houses, electrical equipment,
and other salvageable objects.

Damaged cars are useful places
to search for metal materials.

The residential area also contains
**Electric Boxes**, which may hold
additional components.

An Electric Box may display **Knife failed to Unlock**
when an opening attempt fails. Check the equipped tool
and its condition, but do not assume that upgrading the
Shiv will guarantee success.

If the box remains inaccessible, continue collecting
metal materials from damaged vehicles and other
salvageable objects instead of spending the entire
trip on one container.

Make sure you are collecting the correct material.
**Plastic Scrap cannot replace Scrap** in an Armored
Wall repair.

### Return to Anna After the Walls Are Finished

Once the marked defensive
sections are repaired,
return to Anna inside the
church.

She acknowledges the work
and provides the next reward,
including **5 Frozen Pizzas**.

Collect the available reward
before continuing with
the construction objectives.

The next major requirement
is a Stove.

  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Build the Stove and Complete the Cooking Objective
</h2>

The Stove is unlocked during
the House Camp repair sequence.

Open Utilities in the Building
menu and select Stove.

Its construction requirement
is **30 Scrap**.

  <Image

Because the walls have already
consumed metal, you may need
another round of salvaging.

Return to nearby vehicles and
buildings, gather appropriate
materials, and dismantle what
you can use.

Once the Stove is constructed,
complete its cooking objective.

The Stove combines a primary
ingredient with an enhancer.
The quest asks you to cook
a Dish using two ingredients.

Check the combination and
produce a valid Dish rather
than repeatedly selecting
an incompatible ingredient slot.

The cooking system can produce
different results, including
Healthy Dish and Warming Dish.

For the actual warmth benefits
of food and how to use them
during cold crossings, see <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">Cold Resistance, heaters, and warming food</Link>.

After completing the cooking
objective, return to Anna
for the next assignment.

Anna has another conversation
available after the House Camp

Speak to her and follow

The next objective directs
you to obtain the key
to the church tower.

The reward interaction provides:

- **1 Key from Anna**
- **20 Bucks**

The Dismantle Bench recipe

### Repair the Access and Reach the Roof

Use the key to access the
upper part of the church.

Follow the stairway and
repair damaged access where
the objective requires it.

Continue toward the communication
equipment above the main
church interior.

At the radio, listen to
Icarus's transmission.

The message raises doubts
about City Mall and the
survivors associated with it.

After listening, return
to Anna and report what
you heard.

She discusses Icarus's
possible connection to
the Moon Cult and directs
you toward another
communication site.

<h2
  id="next-radio-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Tower of Finn: Reach the Top of the Radio Tower
</h2>

Anna explains that another
improvised radio tower
stands near the Solars.

The installation was used
for longer-distance communication,
but the surrounding area
has become dangerously cold.

A scavenger named **Finn**
was sent to work on the tower.

Follow the objective toward
his location.

### Prepare for the Cold Zone

Before leaving House Camp, inspect the Hybrid clothing
recipes unlocked during Anna's objectives. Equip pieces
that improve your Cold Resistance rather than replacing
your entire outfit without comparing the attributes.

Hunter characters who still have their Wolf Head Hat
should compare it with any newly crafted headwear
before switching.

Repair your Makeshift Heater and carry suitable food
and healing supplies. The journey to Finn's tower
passes through extreme cold, and reaching the top
does not immediately remove the danger.

For clothing comparisons, heater repairs, and warming
consumables, see <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">how to survive Cold Zones in Permafrost</Link>.

Once you arrive, climb
the structure.

The objective reads:

**Reach the tower's top and find Finn.**

ask him about the failed repair. He explains what
happened and mentions **Logan**, who is associated

You can leave the tower without searching for another
interaction.


<h2
  id="solar-fields"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Solar Fields: Find Logan's Camp
</h2>

After speaking with Finn, leave the radio tower and follow
the new objective toward Solar Fields. The surrounding
terrain remains cold, so keep your heating equipment
working until you reach the scavenger settlement.

Find **Logan** at the camp and speak to him about the
people operating around the Solars. The settlement has
facilities for resting, trading, and preparing equipment
before continuing your journey.

### Should You Pay Logan 900 Bucks?

Logan offers membership in his scavenger group for
**900 Bucks**, but joining is separate from ordinary
conversation and trading.

You can speak with him, inspect his goods, and decline
membership without paying the fee. Keep the money if
you still need repairs, supplies, or better equipment.

Once you have finished the conversation, check the
Journal for the next active objective involving the
remaining communication network and the search
for Icarus.
