<section id="quick-answer" className="scroll-mt-24">
  <GuideQuickAnswer title="Where Do You Go Next in Permafrost?">
    Follow the active main quest from Horizon through Bone Cave,
    New Home, Hunter's Glade, and House Camp. After meeting
    Logan, help the Rat Queen, visit the doctor, restore the
    Solar Facility radio, and reach Jeremiah at Crow's Nest.
    Prepare the required armor to advance to Last Stand.
  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>


The first major obstacle in Permafrost is rarely the distance
between settlements. Most of the time, you reach the correct
location and discover that something still needs to be repaired,
crafted, collected, or reported to an NPC before the next
objective appears.

At New Home, the radio cannot be built until you have processed
Copper into Wire. Hunter's Glade requires all 13 lookout repairs,
not just the damaged radio. House Camp adds several construction
and equipment objectives before Anna lets you continue.

Later, Logan's assignments introduce a Weapons Bench, ammunition
crafting, and a trip to the Rat Queen. The journey then leads
through the Solar Facility to Crow's Nest, where Jeremiah needs
supplies recovered from a Moon Cultist outpost before helping
you prepare for White Hell.

The main journey follows this progression:

**Starting Area → Horizon → Bone Cave → New Home → Hunter's
Glade → House Camp → Tower of Finn → New Town / Logan's Camp
→ Solar Facility → Urban District → Crow's Nest → White Hell
Preparations**

Use the current Journal objective to determine which interaction
is still missing. Several settlements have optional work,
traders, and exploration opportunities that can be completed
without interrupting the main storyline.


<h2
  id="opening-to-horizon"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  A New Beginning: Build a Campfire and Reach Horizon
</h2>

After the opening collapse, Anders contacts you over the radio.
Your immediate priority is to recover enough warmth to continue
moving.

Collect the Wood and Stone scattered around the starting area.
The first Campfire objective requires **3 Wood and 3 Stone**.

Open the Building menu, place the Campfire, add fuel, and warm
yourself beside it. Wait for the warming interaction to satisfy
the objective before leaving.

Anders also introduces the first tools and basic crafting
recipes. Prepare the requested Stone equipment and unlock
the Rope blueprint so you can work toward a Crude Bow.

Keep some Wood available after completing the Campfire.
The journey to the first settlement involves more cold
exposure, and spending everything on unnecessary structures
leaves you with fewer options if you need another fire.

### Feel the Heat — Find Horizon

The next objective sends you through a cave toward Horizon.

Enter the marked passage and follow it to the opposite exit.
There are mineral deposits along the route, but some cannot
be mined with your starting equipment. You do not need to
collect every deposit before reaching the settlement.

Continue toward the marked destination and find Robyn.

### Horizon's Kitchen — Cook Meat for Robyn

Robyn is one of the first important NPCs you meet. Speak
with her about Icarus and the settlement's communication
network.

Before sending you farther into the wilderness, she asks
you to prepare food.

Cook **one piece of Meat** using a Campfire or available
cooking equipment, collect the finished food, and return
to Robyn.

Cooking the food alone does not complete the entire exchange.
You still need to speak with Robyn and finish the conversation
before the next destination becomes available.

Once she directs you toward Bone Cave, check your supplies
before departing. Horizon has facilities for resting,
trading, and maintaining equipment.

A Bandage is especially useful for the next quest.
You do not need to purchase an expensive weapon or
new armor set just to reach Bone Cave.


<h2
  id="dog-days-bone-cave"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dog Days: Heal and Feed the Dog in Bone Cave
</h2>

Bone Cave contains an injured dog that becomes your companion
after completing two interactions.

Bring these items before entering:

- **1 Bandage** to treat the dog.
- **1 Raw Meat** to feed it.

A Makeshift Bandage can be crafted using **2 Cloth**.

The feeding objective requires Raw Meat rather than a cooked
Dish. You can obtain it by hunting an animal such as a Deer
outside Horizon, then processing the carcass with a suitable
Shiv or knife.

### Where to Find the Dog

Follow the passage deeper into Bone Cave until you reach
a chamber containing an injured dog near a dead survivor.
A flare provides some light around the area.

Approach the dog and complete the interactions in this order:

1. Select **Heal** and use the Bandage.
2. Interact with the dog again after the prompt changes.
3. Select **Feed** and give it **1 Raw Meat**.

  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-bone-cave-dog-location.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Bone Cave after completing the Dog Days rescue objective"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Heal the injured dog first, then feed it Raw Meat
    to complete Dog Days.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

When the Feed option is unavailable, check whether the
healing interaction has finished. Carrying both items
does not bypass the order required by the quest.

After the dog joins you, its pack provides extra carrying
space for supplies. Keep materials needed for immediate
crafting in your own inventory so you can use them without
repeatedly transferring items.

### Leave Bone Cave

A dangerous Bear occupies part of the cave. It can become
aggressive when approached, even if it initially appears
injured.

Keep your weapon ready and avoid getting trapped in a
narrow passage while dealing with it.

Once the dog is rescued, follow the exit objective out
of Bone Cave.

The next area is **the Woods**, where the New Home
construction sequence begins.


<h2
  id="new-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  New Home: Complete the Wooden Shelter Objectives
</h2>

New Home introduces a series of building requirements.
Constructing a large base immediately is unnecessary;
the quest advances through a specific set of wooden
structures before requesting furniture and equipment.

The initial requirements are:

- **2 Wooden Foundations**
- **5 Wooden Walls**
- **1 Wooden Doorway**
- **2 Wooden Floor/Roof pieces**
- **1 Wooden Door**

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-new-home-building-objectives.webp"
    alt="Permafrost New Home journal listing the required wooden foundations, walls, doorway, roof pieces and door"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Complete the required wooden structures before
    spending materials on additional rooms.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Choose reasonably level ground and place the foundations
first. Add the doorway and walls, then finish the floor
or roof pieces and install the door.

A compact shelter is enough for this stage.

Save additional construction plans until the required
structure is finished, especially if Wood is becoming
difficult to replace during poor weather.

### Build and Claim the Bed

The next objectives introduce a **Bed** and a
**Small Wooden Chest**.

After constructing the Bed, interact with it and
select **Claim**.

This is a separate action from placing the structure.
A finished Bed can be sitting inside your shelter
while the Journal still waits for it to be claimed.

If the sleeping interaction reports that the room
is too cold, restore a nearby heat source before
attempting to rest.

For heating equipment and temperature protection,
see <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">how to stay warm in Permafrost</Link>.

### Build the Workbench and Flint Tools

Continue with the Workbench objective, then craft
the requested Flint tools:

- Flint Pickaxe
- Flint Axe
- Flint Shiv

The Flint Pickaxe matters because the starting
Stone Pickaxe cannot harvest Copper.

Flint deposits are available in Bone Cave, which
you passed through earlier.

If you still need Flint, return with your mining
equipment and collect enough for the required tools
before continuing.

Copper becomes important during the next construction
stage. The damaged bridge near New Home can make
crossing the ravine easier once repaired, but the
bridge itself is not a substitute for upgrading
the pickaxe.

The <Link href="/permafrost/mining-guide">Permafrost mining guide</Link>
covers Copper, Coal, pickaxe requirements, and the
materials used to produce Wire.


<h2
  id="new-home-radio"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  New Home: Make Wire and Build the Radio Station Bench
</h2>

The later New Home objectives move from wooden construction
to metal processing.

Unlock and build the **Stone Furnace**, then process
Copper into the materials needed for Wire.

An optional Cold Zone supply objective may appear while
you work through this stage. You can obtain the necessary
materials through ordinary gathering and crafting rather
than relying on that detour.

### Radio Station Bench Requirements

The Radio Station Bench requires:

- **3 Wire**
- **10 Wood**

  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-radio-station-bench-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Radio Station Bench recipe requiring 3 Wire and 10 Wood"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The Radio Station Bench requires 3 Wire and 10 Wood.
    Copper Ingots cannot replace finished Wire.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Place the finished Wire in your character's inventory
before opening the Building menu.

Raw Copper and Copper Ingots are not interchangeable
with Wire for this objective.

Select the Radio Station Bench from Utilities and
find a valid construction position inside the shelter.
Move storage or furniture if the placement preview
is blocked.

Once the bench is constructed, approach it and
listen to Icarus's transmission.

Allow the message to finish before leaving.

The radio directs you toward **Hunter's Glade**,
and the next main quest becomes:

**The Outpost — Reach Hunter's Glade.**


<h2
  id="the-outpost"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Outpost: Follow the Road to Hunter's Glade
</h2>

Hunter's Glade is farther from New Home than the
earlier journey through Bone Cave.

Take food, a Bandage, a working weapon, and some
Wood for the repairs waiting at the destination.

Travel from the Woods toward the **Bunker Checkpoint**,
then continue along the road leading to the lookout.

### Passing Bunker Checkpoint

The checkpoint is a ruined concrete facility near
damaged vehicles and defensive obstacles.

You can search its containers for supplies, but
clearing the entire building is not required for
the main quest.

A Military Crate in the area may provide useful
equipment, including improved Flint gear.

Watch the route you take behind the checkpoint.
The exposed upper area leads into dangerous cold
and is not necessary for reaching Hunter's Glade.

Remain on the main road, passing damaged vehicles
and wooden watchtowers until the objective directs
you toward the steel lookout.

When you reach Hunter's Glade, **The Outpost**
completes and **High Frequency** begins.


<h2
  id="high-frequency"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  High Frequency: Repair the 13 Damaged Lookout Sections
</h2>

Climb the steel lookout at Hunter's Glade and
find **Skinwalker** near the upper cabin.

Speak with him about the radio equipment.

The tower cannot provide the next transmission
until its damaged structures have been repaired.

### How to Reach 13/13 Repairs

Equip the Building Tool and inspect the lookout's
cabin and upper walkway.

Damaged components appear as blue construction
outlines. These include walls, windows, and
equipment such as the Bed and Radio Station Bench.

The renovation objective requires **13 repairs**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-hunters-glade-radio-repair.webp"
    alt="Permafrost High Frequency lookout with a damaged Radio Station Bench highlighted for renovation"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Use the Building Tool on the blue repair outlines
    until the renovation counter reaches 13/13.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Approach each outline and inspect its material
requirements before repairing it.

Wood can be collected from trees around Hunter's
Glade. For repairs requiring Scrap, use supplies
gathered along the road or return to salvageable
vehicles and containers near Bunker Checkpoint.

There are two common reasons the counter remains
incomplete:

- A damaged component is still hidden inside the
  cabin or along the upper walkway.
- A replacement structure was placed nearby instead
  of repairing the original highlighted component.

Walk around both the interior and exterior of the
lookout before assuming the objective is finished.

### Restore the Radio and Listen to Icarus

After completing **13/13 repairs**, use the
restored Radio Station Bench.

Listen to the transmission from Icarus. It discusses
City Mall and the difficulties facing the surviving
communities.

When the message ends, speak with Skinwalker again.

This completes High Frequency and begins
**The Way Home**.

### Do You Need to Return Robyn's Recording First?

Robyn's recording is a separate assignment.

You can collect it from the lookout cabin and
return it to Horizon when convenient. The main
story continues after the radio transmission
and the follow-up conversation with Skinwalker.

Skinwalker's hunting contracts also do not need
to be completed before starting The Way Home.


<h2
  id="the-way-home"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  The Way Home: Claim the Reward and Craft Tribal Clothing
</h2>

Skinwalker directs you toward House Camp after
the restored radio transmission.

Before leaving Hunter's Glade, collect the reward
from the **Quest Reward** box.

The reward includes a **Utility Backpack** and
access to the **Tailoring Bench** recipe.

The Utility Backpack provides **25 inventory slots**,
making it useful for carrying materials needed
during the next stretch of the journey.

Talking to Skinwalker is not the same as claiming
the reward. Open the reward box before setting off.

### Build the Tailoring Bench

Return to a valid construction area and open
the Building menu.

The Tailoring Bench requires:

- **15 Wood**
- **3 Copper Ingots**

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-tailoring-bench-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Tailoring Bench recipe showing 15 Wood and 3 Copper Ingots"
    width={300}
    height={100}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Prepare 15 Wood and 3 Copper Ingots
    before constructing the Tailoring Bench.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The recipe requires processed Copper Ingots rather
than raw ore.

If the earlier radio construction consumed your
available Copper materials, return to the furnace
and produce the additional ingots first.

### Complete the Tribal Clothes Set

The Way Home includes clothing objectives before
you continue toward House Camp.

Unlock the **Tribal Clothes Set** in the blueprint
tree and craft the required equipment at the
Tailoring Bench.

The set includes:

- Tribal Boots
- Tribal Cape
- Tribal Gloves
- Tribal Jacket
- Tribal Pants

The **Tanning Rack** is needed to process hide
materials used by these recipes. Check each item
for its required Treated Hide, Cloth, and other
ingredients.

Building the Tailoring Bench alone does not satisfy
the clothing objectives. Continue crafting until
the Journal updates.

Once the equipment preparation is complete,
follow the objective toward **House Camp's Outskirts**.

### Meet Anna at the Church

Travel toward House Camp and locate its church.

Speak with **Anna**, who is investigating Icarus
while helping the settlement maintain its defenses.

Her assignments introduce the next series of
construction and communication objectives.


<h2
  id="house-camp"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  House Camp Flipper: Get Metal Junk and Craft Scrap Tools
</h2>

Anna's first major assignment concerns the
settlement's damaged defenses.

Wooden construction materials are not enough for
the Armored Walls. You need Scrap and Wire, so
the first step is to prepare usable metal.

Search the abandoned buildings, damaged vehicles,
and containers around House Camp for **Metal Junk**.

Follow the dismantling objectives in the Journal
until the required materials have been processed.

You will also need to unlock and craft three
Scrap tools:

- Scrap Axe
- Scrap Pickaxe
- Scrap Shiv

Use the appropriate crafting station for each
piece and check the Journal after crafting.

Collecting Metal Junk does not automatically finish
the tool objectives. The requested equipment must
actually be crafted.

After the preparation objectives are satisfied,
move to the damaged defenses marked around House Camp.


<h2
  id="house-camp-walls"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  House Camp Flipper: Repair All Four Defensive Sections
</h2>

House Camp has **four marked defensive sections**.

The Journal identifies them as:

- Near Gate D
- Facing the Solar Facility
- Near Gate B
- Facing the Technocrats Colony

Approach the marked structures with the
Building Tool equipped.

The damaged Armored Walls display the resources
needed for each repair. One selected wall requires
**10 Scrap and 1 Wire**.

  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-house-camp-wall-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost House Camp Flipper quest showing four damaged defensive sections and an Armored Wall requiring Scrap and Wire"

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Repair each of the four marked defensive sections.
    The selected Armored Wall requires 10 Scrap and 1 Wire.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Check the material requirement at every repair
location instead of assuming all four sections
have the same cost.

After repairing one wall, inspect the Journal
and travel to the next marked defense.

The assignment is not complete simply because
the wall nearest the church looks repaired.

### Where to Find More Scrap

House Camp contains several useful sources of
salvage, including ruined vehicles, abandoned
houses, and electrical equipment.

Cars are worth checking for metal materials.
Residential areas also contain **Electric Boxes**,
which can hold additional supplies.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-house-camp-electrical-box.webp"

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">

An Electric Box may show **Knife failed to Unlock**
when an opening attempt is unsuccessful.

Check your equipped knife and its condition.
An improved tool may help with harder objects,
but the message alone does not establish that
a particular upgrade will guarantee success.

If the box remains inaccessible, collect Scrap
from damaged vehicles and other salvageable objects
instead of spending the entire trip on one container.

Also watch the material names in your inventory:
**Plastic Scrap cannot replace Scrap** in an
Armored Wall repair.

### Report Back to Anna

Once all four defensive sections are repaired,
return to Anna inside the church.

She acknowledges the completed work and provides
the next reward, including **5 Frozen Pizzas**.

Collect the available reward before continuing.

The next important construction objective
is the Stove.


<h2
  id="house-camp-stove"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  House Camp: Build the Stove and Cook a Two-Ingredient Dish
</h2>

After completing the defensive repairs, unlock
the Stove from the Building menu.

The Stove requires **30 Scrap**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-stove-requirements.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Stove construction recipe requiring 30 Scrap"
    width={300}
    height={100}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    The Stove requires 30 Scrap, so keep gathering
    metal after completing the wall repairs.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

The wall objectives may have consumed much of
your Scrap supply. Return to nearby buildings,
vehicles, and salvageable equipment if you cannot
afford the Stove yet.

Once it has been constructed, complete the
cooking objective.

The quest asks for a Dish made from **two ingredients**.

Use a primary ingredient and a compatible enhancer
in the Stove's cooking interface. Check the available
combination rather than repeatedly placing items
into an incompatible slot.

The cooking system can produce different dishes,
including Healthy Dish and Warming Dish. The effects
of these foods become more useful during later
Cold Zone travel; see <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">Cold Resistance and warming food</Link>
for those mechanics.

After cooking the required Dish, return to Anna
to continue the settlement's main quest.


<h2
  id="church-radio"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Breaking the Radio Silence: Get Anna's Church Tower Key
</h2>

Anna has another conversation available after
the construction and cooking objectives.

Speak with her about the church's communication
equipment.

The next objective is to obtain the key
to the church tower.

The reward interaction provides:

- **1 Key from Anna**
- **20 Bucks**

The Disassembly Bench recipe also becomes
available around this stage of progression.

    Collect Key from Anna before attempting

  </figcaption>
</figure>

When the Journal still says **Get the key to the
Church's tower**, return to Anna and check her
available dialogue.

The objective changes to **Reach the Church's Roof**
after you collect the key.

### Reach the Church Roof and Use the Radio

Use the key to access the upper section of the
church.

Follow the stairs and repair any damaged access
required by the active objective.

Continue upward until you reach the communication
equipment.

Interact with the radio and listen to Icarus.

The transmission raises concerns about City Mall
and the people associated with it.

Return to Anna after listening.

She discusses what you heard, including Icarus's
possible connection to the Moon Cult, and sends
you toward another communication installation.


<h2
  id="next-radio-tower"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Tower of Finn: Reach the Top and Find Finn
</h2>

The next destination is an improvised radio tower
near the Solars.

Anna explains that a scavenger named **Finn**
was sent to work on the installation.

Follow the marked route toward the tower.

The surrounding area is much colder than the
earlier roads around House Camp. Check your
equipment before departure:

- Wear clothing with enough Cold Resistance
  for the route.
- Carry a working Makeshift Heater.
- Bring food, healing supplies, and a weapon.

Equipment temperature ratings, Heater repairs,
and the effects of warming food are explained
in <Link href="/permafrost/staying-warm">how to survive Cold Zones</Link>.

### Find Finn at the Upper Level

Climb the damaged radio tower.

The Journal objective reads:

**Reach the tower's top and find Finn.**

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-solar-radio-tower-finn.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Tower of Finn objective showing the upper level of the damaged radio tower"
    width={1600}

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Climb to the upper section of the radio tower
    and speak with Finn.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Find Finn at the top and ask him about the
failed communication repair.

He discusses what happened and points you toward
**Logan**, who is associated with the scavengers
operating around the Solars.

After the conversation, check the updated Journal.

The next destination is Logan's camp.

Do not remain at Finn's tower searching for another
radio interaction after the quest has already
directed you onward.


<h2
  id="solar-fields"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Find Logan's Warehouse in New Town
</h2>

Leave Finn's tower and follow the objective toward
the settlement associated with Logan.

The Journal identifies the destination as
**Logan's Camp**, with a warehouse objective
in **New Town**.

The route takes you through the larger area
around the Solars. Keep your heating equipment
available until you reach the settlement.

Find Logan and ask about access to the nearest
radio tower.

### Should You Pay Logan 900 Bucks?

Logan offers membership in the scavenger group
for **900 Bucks**.

That payment is not the same interaction as
asking him about the radio tower.

You can speak with Logan, inspect his available
goods, and begin his assignment without paying
the membership fee first.

The membership offers benefits such as a
scavenger discount, but spending 900 Bucks
immediately may leave you short of cash for
equipment and supplies.

If you are still preparing ammunition or need
materials for the next objectives, keeping the
money is reasonable.

### Start Logan's First Assignment

When you ask Logan how to gain access to the
radio tower, he asks you to help his group.

The first assignment sends you to the
**Rat Queen at Solar's Crossing**.

Set this objective as active and travel toward
her settlement.


<h2
  id="parking-zoo"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Parking Zoo: Rat Queen, Weapons Bench, and Animal Targets
</h2>

At Solar's Crossing, find the settlement occupied
by the Rat Queen.

The area is associated with **Parking Zoo**.

Speak with her about Logan and the scavengers.

She is concerned about dangerous creatures
threatening her people and asks you to deal
with them before agreeing to Logan's request.

This assignment has several stages. Speaking
to the Rat Queen does not immediately complete
Logan's favor.

### Collect the Weapons Bench Recipe

Follow the new objective to obtain the
**Weapons Bench recipe**.

The reward interaction also provides weapon-related
materials, including Weapon Parts and Sulfur.

After learning the recipe, the Journal directs
you to build a Weapons Bench.

This is an actual construction objective,
separate from the act of learning the recipe.

Return to a suitable building area with
the necessary materials.

### Build the Weapons Bench — 10 Iron Ingots

The Weapons Bench requires **10 Iron Ingots**.

Prepare the ingots before placing the structure.
If your earlier furnaces are still processing Iron,
collect the finished output and make another batch
until you reach the required amount.

You may also need more free space inside your
existing shelter, since this workstation occupies
additional room.

After construction, interact with the Weapons
Bench and follow the new crafting objectives.

### Craft a Weapon and 30 Rounds of Ammunition

The assignment next requires crafting a weapon
and ammunition.

The ammunition objective calls for
**30 rounds**.

You can choose an ammunition type that matches
a weapon available to you. Avoid crafting rounds
for a firearm you do not own or intend to use.

Gunpowder becomes relevant during this stage.
Its crafting materials include Coal, Sulfur,
and Scrap.

For one available ammunition recipe,
**1 Gunpowder and 1 Scrap produce 10 rounds**.

That means three successful batches of that
recipe can satisfy the 30-round objective.

Check the selected recipe's actual output
before crafting more than required.

Some higher-tier ammunition types need
materials such as Quality Metal, which may
not yet be available.

Using a simpler ammunition recipe is enough
when it satisfies the current Journal objective.

### Kill the Creatures Threatening Parking Zoo

After the equipment preparation, the Rat Queen's
assignment moves into combat.

The displayed hunting objectives include:

- **3 Wolves**
- **3 Boars**
- **1 Bear**, marked as optional

Follow the active Journal markers to locate
the required animals.

The Bear is an additional optional target,
so do not assume it must be killed to finish
the main assignment.

Carry ammunition and maintain your equipment
before starting the marked encounters.

The combat areas can be far apart, and returning
home after every fight wastes time.

When the required hunting objectives are finished,
return to the Rat Queen.

Speak with her again so she can respond to
your completed work.

### Report Back to Logan

After the Rat Queen accepts the outcome,
return to Logan's Camp.

Tell Logan that the arrangement with Parking Zoo
has been settled.

The conversation about access to the radio
tower can now continue.

If Logan still appears to be waiting for the
first favor, confirm that you completed the
Rat Queen's follow-up conversation rather
than stopping immediately after the animal kills.

Logan's next assignment involves a doctor
working at a clinic.


<h2
  id="doctor-clinic"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Logan's Doctor Assignment: Visit the Clinic and Return
</h2>

After the Parking Zoo arrangement, Logan sends
you to a doctor at a clinic.

Follow the marked objective to the medical
facility and speak with the doctor about
Logan's request.

The doctor discusses the difficulties of
treating people in the frozen settlements
and asks for help collecting supplies.

### Collect the Requested Supplies

Check the Journal after accepting the assignment.

The task involves collecting food supplies
from marked locations.

Search the required containers and recover
the items requested by the doctor.

Some supplies may already have been collected
during earlier exploration. Check your current
objective count before traveling to every
location again.

The doctor also warns about the dangers of
unsafe meat, including parasites and illness.

Treat the requested food as quest materials.
There is no reason to consume it while
trying to complete the delivery.

The surrounding area includes valuable mineral
deposits, but the doctor's assignment is not
a requirement to mine everything nearby.

Once the needed supplies have been collected,
return to the clinic.

### Speak to the Doctor, Then Logan

Hand over the requested items and complete
the doctor's conversation.

Afterward, return to Logan's Camp.

Speak with Logan again to report the finished
assignment.

This handoff is important because the next
communication objective becomes available
through the continuing conversation with him.

The route now turns toward the **Solar Facility**.


<h2
  id="solar-facility"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Solar Facility: Reach the Upper Radio and Hear the Transmission
</h2>

The Solar Facility is a large industrial
building with multiple levels.

After Logan's assignments, the next objective
directs you toward its radio installation.

The important instruction is to find a route
**up to the radio tower**.

### How to Reach the Radio

Enter the facility and work your way upward
through the accessible passages and
upper sections.

The lower area contains rooms and supplies
worth searching, but the main objective
is above them.

Continue climbing until the Journal changes
to the radio interaction.

You do not need to renovate the entire
facility before reaching the upper radio
in this sequence.

### Listen to the Transmission

Interact with the radio at the upper level.

Listen to the available transmission before
leaving the installation.

The message provides more information about
Icarus and the Technocrats.

Additional recordings can also be found in
the facility. Some contain information about
people affected by the catastrophe and the
groups operating in the region.

Collect nearby recordings when convenient,
but do not confuse collecting an optional
recording with completing the active
radio objective.

After listening to the required transmission,
the Journal directs you back to **Anna
at House Camp**.

### Return to Anna

Travel back to the church at House Camp
and speak with Anna.

She responds to what you discovered and
directs you toward the next major settlement.

Your new destination is:

**Crow's Nest in the Urban District.**


<h2
  id="crows-nest"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Urban District: Find Jeremiah at Crow's Nest
</h2>

The Urban District introduces a larger
built-up area with more hostile encounters
and abandoned structures.

Follow Anna's objective toward Crow's Nest.

Before entering the district, repair the
equipment you have been using and bring
enough ammunition for fights around the
settlement.

### Find Crow's Nest

Continue into the Urban District until
you reach Crow's Nest.

The area has scavenger facilities,
including a place to rest, and damaged
structures that can be repaired.

You do not need to complete every
renovation before speaking with Jeremiah.

Find **Jeremiah** and ask about the
equipment needed to recover Moonrock
and continue the search for Icarus.

He has work that must be completed before
providing further assistance.

### Jeremiah's Missing Supplies

Jeremiah explains that a nearby
Moon Cultist group has taken supplies.

He needs the shipment recovered.

The objective sends you to an outpost
**south of Crow's Nest**.

The directions include two important
landmarks:

- Scaffolding
- A frozen billboard

Use these to identify the correct
outpost rather than searching every
building in the Urban District.

The stolen supplies include useful
survival materials such as food,
medicine, and ammunition.


<h2
  id="jeremiah-supplies"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Jeremiah's Supplies: Find the Container Near the Frozen Billboard
</h2>

Leave Crow's Nest and travel south toward
the Moon Cultist outpost.

Look for the **frozen billboard** and
nearby **scaffolding** mentioned by Jeremiah.

The location contains hostile enemies,
so approach with a ready weapon.

### Recover the Supplies Container

Once you reach the correct outpost,
search the structures and accessible
areas around the objective location.

The item you need is a
**Supplies Container**.

Make some room in your inventory
before picking it up.

Ordinary loot can fill your backpack
while the required shipment is still
waiting to be collected.

After taking the Supplies Container,
the objective changes to:

**Return supplies to Jeremiah.**

### Return to Crow's Nest

Travel back to Jeremiah at Crow's Nest.

He acknowledges the completed work,
allowing the conversation about
Lunar equipment to continue.

### Ask Jeremiah About Lunar Armor

After the supply handoff, speak with
Jeremiah again about the armor needed
for the journey ahead.

This part of the progression introduces
advanced equipment recipes and tools
suitable for harvesting Moonrock.

Depending on the recipes already learned,
some rewards may appear as known
blueprints rather than entirely new
crafting options.

Check the Journal and your blueprint
tree after the conversation.

If a further objective involving
Bunker information or equipment
blueprints is still active, complete
that requirement before assuming
the armor preparation is finished.

The next important stage is
**Dressed for White Hell**.


<h2
  id="dressed-for-white-hell"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Dressed for White Hell: Prepare the Required Armor
</h2>

This part of the main story requires
equipment capable of handling a much
colder environment.

The objective introduces advanced
armor components associated with
Lunar and Permafrost equipment.

Use the active Journal to identify
the required items rather than
crafting an entire unrelated
armor set.

The preparation includes five
clothing slots:

- Boots
- Cloak
- Gloves
- Jacket
- Pants

### Prepare Quality Metal and Moonrock Materials

The later armor recipes require
advanced materials, including
**Quality Metal** and processed
**Moonrock**.

Ordinary Copper and Scrap equipment
are no longer sufficient for these
crafting requirements.

The **Professional Smelter** becomes
important here because it can
process the advanced materials
used by the armor recipes.

If you already built basic furnaces,
check whether you have enough
space for the new smelter before
starting production.

Prepare the needed Iron, Scrap,
Moonrock, and fuel according to
the recipes currently available
in your game.

Higher-tier pickaxes are required
for harvesting Moonrock deposits.
The <Link href="/permafrost/mining-guide">Permafrost mining guide</Link>
covers the relevant tool tiers
and mineral processing steps.

### Craft the Five Required Pieces

Return to a suitable crafting area
with the processed materials.

Open the armor recipes and produce
the clothing pieces requested by
Dressed for White Hell.

Work through the Boots, Cloak,
Gloves, Jacket, and Pants,
checking the Journal after each
successful craft.

Some recipes have similar names
but different material requirements.
Use the exact quest item names
and completion counter rather
than assuming a visually similar
piece will satisfy the objective.

Do not use materials reserved for
the required clothing to craft
optional tools unless they are
needed to obtain more ore.

Once the last required item is
crafted, **Dressed for White Hell**
completes.

The next main objective becomes:

**Go to the Last Stand.**

### Before Equipping Lunar Armor

There is also a separate achievement
for completing White Hell without
equipping Lunar Armor.

If you intend to complete
**Hell Runner**, review the armor
restriction before changing your
equipped clothing.

The <Link href="/permafrost/achievements">Permafrost achievements checklist</Link>
includes Hell Runner and the
minimum-temperature story challenge.

Completing the crafting objective
and completing White Hell without
Lunar Armor are different goals.


<h2
  id="last-stand"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Go to the Last Stand: What Comes After Dressed for White Hell?
</h2>

After finishing the required
armor preparation, open the Journal.

The next objective directs
you toward **Last Stand**.

Before departing, collect the
finished equipment and check
your inventory for ammunition,
healing supplies, food, and
any materials needed to
maintain your tools.

This is also a sensible point
to repair damaged equipment
and store materials that are
no longer needed for the
current journey.

Follow the new objective
marker toward Last Stand.

The **Go to the Last Stand**
objective marks the transition
from equipment preparation
into the next part of the story.
