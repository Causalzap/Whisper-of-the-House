Permafrost gives you more to collect, craft, and explore than you
need for any single objective. The difficult decisions come when
several needs compete for the same supplies: Wood can become
construction materials, furnace fuel, or emergency heat, while
Copper Ingots can be turned into Wire or saved for equipment
you will need farther south.

The best next move depends on what is stopping you. A missing
crafting component calls for different preparation than a
dangerous Cold Zone, an unfinished conversation, or a piece
of damaged equipment.

Your journey initially takes you from Horizon through Bone Cave
to New Home. After restoring communication with Icarus, the route
continues toward Hunter's Glade, House Camp, and the Solar Fields.
Each stage changes which materials and upgrades are worth
prioritizing.


<h2
  id="starting-class"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Which Starting Class Should You Choose?
</h2>

Choose your Background and Specialization before creating the
world. For a first run, **Hunter + Engineer** is a useful default:
Hunter provides a Wolf Head Hat and Improvised Coffee, while
Engineer starts with bonuses to Running, Axes, and Toughness.

That combination supports early travel, Wood gathering, and
survival. Hunter + Tracker is more attractive for frequent
hunting, while Smuggler + Scavenger favors looting and stealth.

Your choice does not prevent other skills from improving later.
For the starting equipment, all four Specializations, and the
differences between the combinations, see <Link href="/permafrost/classes-skills">Permafrost's starting classes and skills</Link>.


<h2
  id="first-steps"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Much Should You Prepare Before Reaching Horizon?
</h2>

Anders guides you through the opening survival objectives after
the collapse. The priority is establishing a way to recover
warmth, gathering enough materials for basic equipment, and
reaching the first settlement with supplies remaining.

Three things are worth completing before leaving the starting
area:

- **Get a Campfire working.** Recover Body Warmth instead of
  beginning a journey while already cold.
- **Prepare usable tools and a weapon.** The opening objectives
  introduce gathering equipment and a Crude Bow.
- **Keep some emergency supplies.** Spare Wood, food, and
  a working weapon are more useful on the road than a
  backpack filled with optional loot.

Once these needs are covered, head toward Horizon.

You can return to the starting area later. Locked containers,
unmineable deposits, and distant structures do not all need
to be explored with your first set of tools.

Horizon also provides access to resting facilities and traders.
Reaching those services is generally more useful than using
your remaining daylight to search every container near the
wreckage.

### Should You Buy Equipment at the First Settlement?

Inspect what you already have before spending money.

A replacement weapon is worth considering when your current
one is badly worn or struggles against the enemies on your
route. Clothing is more valuable when the cold is becoming
the main reason you cannot travel safely.

There is little reason to buy several expensive upgrades
before knowing what your next objective requires.

Early construction introduces new material demands, while
later destinations make cold protection more important.
Keeping money available allows you to respond to whichever
problem appears first.


<h2
  id="progress-blockers"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Where Are You Stuck in Permafrost?
</h2>

The Journal tells you where to go, but the objective marker
does not always reveal why progress has stopped. Before
repeating a journey, identify whether the game is waiting
for an interaction, a completed construction task, or
equipment you have not prepared.

### Still at Horizon or Bone Cave?

Robyn's early assignment requires completing her request
and speaking with her afterward. If the next destination
does not appear, return to her rather than searching
outside the settlement.

Bone Cave introduces your dog. The rescue requires two
different interactions, beginning with treatment. Prepare
the required supplies before entering so you do not have
to travel back to Horizon halfway through.

Once the companion joins you, continue through the cave
toward the Woods.

### Stuck Building New Home?

Check the individual construction objectives instead of
judging the shelter by its appearance.

A finished-looking wooden structure can still be missing
a required piece or an interaction such as claiming the
Bed.

The more demanding obstacle comes when the game asks
you to establish radio communication. The Radio Station
Bench requires **Wire**, not simply Copper collected
from a deposit.

Work out which part of the material chain is missing:

- **Cannot break Copper Boulders:** Your mining tool
  needs an upgrade.
- **Have ore but no usable metal:** You need to process
  the material.
- **Have metal but cannot build the radio:** Check the
  finished component required by the recipe.

The damaged road bridge near New Home can also be
repaired to make travel toward the nearby ravine easier.

For the bridge, mineral locations, pickaxe requirements,
and Copper processing, follow the <Link href="/permafrost/mining-guide">Permafrost mining instructions</Link>.

Once the radio is built, listen to Icarus's transmission
before leaving New Home.

### Reached Hunter's Glade but Cannot Leave?

Hunter's Glade introduces a renovation objective at
Skinwalker's lookout.

Restoring only the radio is not necessarily enough.
Check the remaining damaged structures and complete
the repair counter before listening to the transmission.

Afterward, speak with Skinwalker again and collect
the available reward. That interaction leads into
the preparation required for the journey toward
House Camp.

Robyn's recording assignment and Skinwalker's hunting
contracts can remain unfinished while you continue
the main journey.

### Running Out of Scrap at House Camp?

The material priorities change when you reach Anna.

Instead of spending most of your time chopping trees,
begin searching damaged vehicles, abandoned buildings,
and salvageable objects for metal materials.

House Camp's repair objectives depend on **Scrap and
Wire**. Carrying large amounts of unrelated materials
will not complete the damaged defenses.

Follow the equipment and dismantling objectives before
returning to the marked walls. Once the renovations are
finished, speak with Anna to continue the church tasks.
