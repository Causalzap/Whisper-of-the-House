Your Health can still be full when the cold becomes dangerous.
Once Body Warmth reaches zero, continued exposure starts
damaging Health. A short trip across the Woods may be
manageable with basic equipment, while the exposed ground
around Bunker Checkpoint or Finn's radio tower can drain
warmth before you reach the next sheltered location.

The key is reading the temperature against your protection.
For example, equipment rated for approximately **−29°C**
can handle an area around **−25°C**, but entering a
**−33°C Cold Zone** puts you below that equipment's
protection range. The Body Warmth meter starts falling
even though you have not changed clothing.

Campfires, heated shelter, suitable clothes, portable
heaters, and consumables solve different parts of that
problem. Recover your warmth before leaving safety,
then decide how much protection the next crossing needs.


<h2
  id="body-warmth"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Body Warmth vs. Cold Resistance: Why Are You Losing Health?
</h2>

The snowflake meter in the lower-left corner represents
**Body Warmth**. It is separate from Health, Hunger,
Energy, and Stamina.

Body Warmth falls when your current protection cannot
handle the surrounding conditions. Once it is exhausted,
your character begins losing Health from exposure.
Remaining in the cold can also cause Hypothermia.

Three indicators matter during exploration:

- **Body Warmth:** The snowflake meter shows how much
  warmth remains before exposure starts damaging Health.
- **Temperature:** The display near the compass shows
  the current conditions. Cold Zones can be significantly
  colder than the surrounding area.
- **Status effects:** Check the active positive and
  negative effects when using heaters, food, drinks,
  or when Hypothermia appears.

### What Does Your Equipment's Temperature Rating Mean?

The equipment screen gives you a way to compare your
current protection with the environment.

One early Cold Zone encounter shows:

- Outdoor temperature: approximately **−25°C**
- Equipment protection: approximately **−29°C**
- Temperature after entering the Cold Zone:
  approximately **−33°C**

At −25°C, that equipment provides enough protection
for Body Warmth to recover. At −33°C, warmth begins
draining instead.

The exact temperature changes with location and
conditions, so a number that works around New Home
cannot be treated as a universal requirement.

Check the actual meter after crossing into colder
terrain. It tells you whether your equipment is
handling the environment or merely slowing down
the loss of warmth.

### What Happens When Body Warmth Reaches Zero?

Health begins falling while the dangerous exposure
continues. Taking damage from an animal or enemy
during this period makes the situation worse.

Leaving the Cold Zone can allow Body Warmth to recover
when the surrounding temperature is within your
equipment's protection range. A heater or nearby
heat source can speed up that recovery.

Healing Health without improving your warmth leaves
the original danger unresolved.


<h2
  id="campfire-warm-hands"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Restore Body Warmth With a Campfire
</h2>

A lit Campfire is one of the most dependable ways
to warm yourself before traveling.

Open the Building menu and place a Campfire on
suitable ground. Add Wood to its fuel inventory
and turn it on.

Standing near a lit fire provides warmth, but
the Campfire also has a direct interaction that
is easy to miss.

### Use the Warm Hands Interaction

Approach the burning Campfire and open its radial
interaction menu.

Select **Warm Hands**.

  <Image

    alt="Permafrost Campfire radial menu showing the Warm Hands interaction beside a burning fire"
    width={1600}

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Select Warm Hands at a lit Campfire to recover
    Body Warmth before leaving shelter.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Wait until you have recovered enough warmth for
the next trip. Lighting the fire and immediately
walking away is a poor substitute for actually
warming up.

A long crossing is easier when you start with
a full meter rather than relying on emergency
supplies after most of your warmth is gone.

Keep Wood available for fuel. Running out while
you are recovering can leave you with an
unusable heat source at exactly the wrong time.

### Why Can't You Place a Campfire?

Two different construction problems can prevent
placement.

**Obstructed** means the selected spot is unsuitable
for the structure. Try flatter ground or move away
from objects blocking the placement.

**No-Build Zone** means ordinary construction is
restricted in that area. Moving the Campfire a
small distance within the same restricted zone
will not solve it.

This becomes important near abandoned facilities
and hostile Cold Zones. You may have enough Wood
and Stone to build a fire but still be unable
to place one where you need it.

Establish a warming point outside the restricted
area before entering, and use portable heating
equipment for the exposed section.


<h2
  id="heated-shelter"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Can't You Sleep When Your Shelter Is Too Cold?
</h2>

Building a Bed and successfully sleeping in it
are separate problems.

At New Home, the Bed can be constructed and claimed
while the surrounding temperature is still too
low for resting.

A completed roof, walls, and door provide shelter,
but they do not guarantee that Body Warmth is
recovering.

Place a Campfire near the resting area where
construction is permitted, supply it with Wood,
and turn it on. Use Warm Hands when necessary,
then allow your character to recover before
attempting the Bed again.

When resting remains unavailable, check whether:

- The Campfire still has fuel and is burning.
- You are close enough to benefit from its warmth.
- Body Warmth is recovering instead of draining.
- Another source of damage is interrupting rest.

Wall Torches can illuminate the room, but a lit
interior is not proof that the Bed's temperature
requirement has been satisfied.

Use the warmth meter rather than the appearance
of the shelter to decide when to try sleeping.

A heated settlement such as Horizon is another
option when your base is unfinished and you
cannot keep the room warm with the resources
currently available.


<h2
  id="makeshift-heater"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Makeshift Heater: How It Works, Repairs, and When to Buy Another
</h2>

The **Makeshift Heater** provides portable protection
while you are away from Campfires and heated buildings.

It is especially valuable on roads, exposed platforms,
and Cold Zone routes where construction is restricted.

Unlike warm clothing, a Makeshift Heater has limited
durability. Its benefits disappear when the heater
is no longer functional.

### How Much Does a Makeshift Heater Help?

A working heater can substantially change the speed
at which your warmth meter moves.

The demonstrated heater effect provides:

- **100% faster Body Warmth recovery**
- **50% slower Body Warmth loss**

These are changes to warmth gain and loss rates,
not a guarantee that the surrounding temperature
has become safe.

Inside a severe Cold Zone, Body Warmth can continue
falling with a heater equipped, although more slowly.
Outside that zone, the same heater can make recovery
noticeably faster.

This makes the heater useful for short excursions,
but its durability can be consumed rapidly if you
leave it running through every harmless stretch
of road.

Equip the heater in the appropriate equipment slot,
then watch your active effects and warmth meter
to confirm that it is helping.

### Where Can You Get a Makeshift Heater?

Makeshift Heaters can be found while looting
survival supplies. Skinwalker at Hunter's Glade
also offers heaters through trading.

Obtaining one before the longer Cold Zone journeys
gives you more flexibility than relying entirely
on campfires.

You do not need to purchase several immediately.
The condition of your current heater and the
distance to your next safe location determine
whether another one is worth the money.

### How to Repair a Makeshift Heater

A worn Makeshift Heater can be restored at a
Repair Bench.

The repair requirement shown for the earlier
heater includes:

- **1 Cloth**
- **1 Plastic**

Open the Repair interface, select the heater,
and inspect the ingredient icons. Bring the
materials required by the recipe currently
displayed at your bench.

Repair it before setting out rather than
waiting until Body Warmth is already dropping
inside a Cold Zone.

Plastic is particularly useful to keep in
reserve because it is needed for heater
maintenance as well as other equipment.

### Repair or Buy a Spare?

For short trips around New Home or Horizon,
repairing your current heater is usually
the more economical choice.

A spare becomes more valuable when:

- The route contains a long exposed Cold Zone.
- The destination is far from a heated settlement.
- Your remaining heater durability may not cover
  the return journey.
- You cannot rely on placing a Campfire along
  the way.

The Tower of Finn journey is a good example:
reaching the tower does not automatically
restore your warmth, and you still need enough
protection to leave the area.

Before buying another heater, check whether
you can repair the one you already have.
Two damaged heaters offer little advantage
over one functioning unit.


<h2
  id="cold-resistance-drinks"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Improvised Coffee vs. Improvised Tea: Cold Resistance and Recovery
</h2>

Both **Improvised Coffee** and **Improvised Tea**
provide **45 Cold Res.**, but their other
benefits are different.

### Improvised Coffee

- **45 Cold Res.**
- **45 Energy**
- **10 Hunger**

Hunter can begin with Improvised Coffee.

It is useful when Energy is low and you still
need to travel through dangerous weather.
The drink addresses fatigue while providing
additional protection against the cold.

### Improvised Tea

- **45 Cold Res.**
- **45 Health**
- **10 Hunger**

Survivor can begin with Improvised Tea.

Tea is more useful when you have already
taken damage and want Health recovery
alongside the cold-protection benefit.

The two drinks provide the same listed
Cold Resistance, so your current Health
and Energy should determine which one
offers better value.

### Should You Drink Before Entering a Cold Zone?

Save your warming drink for a section where
the extra protection matters.

Ordinary travel near a heated base may not
justify using your last Coffee or Tea.
An exposed crossing with no nearby shelter
is a better opportunity.

Check the status effect after drinking.
Consumable protection does not permanently
upgrade your outfit, and it should not be
assumed to remain active for an unlimited
journey.

Alcohol also needs separate attention.
Do not assume every alcoholic drink provides
warmth: Moonshine has been shown with a
negative Cold Resistance effect.

Read the actual item effects before
consuming a drink in severe cold.

during a long trip:

The combination is valuable when you expect
cold exposure and your Hunger is already low.

A Warming Dish can provide more listed
Cold Resistance than Improvised Coffee
or Tea, while also addressing Hunger.

However, it is not a substitute for
adequate clothing or a functioning
heater when the environment is colder
than your equipment can tolerate.

### How Does Cooking a Warming Dish Work?

The Stove has separate **Primary** and
**Enhancer** ingredient slots.

The combination determines the resulting
Dish. A Healthy Dish and a Warming Dish
have different benefits, so check the
selected ingredients and the resulting
item before cooking.

Some ingredients cannot be placed in
a particular slot.

### Why Does the Stove Say Invalid Slot?

The message:

**Invalid slot for this kind of item**

means the selected ingredient is not
accepted in that position.

Check whether you are placing the
ingredient in Primary or Enhancer,
then try a compatible combination.

Simply adding Meat and another item
does not guarantee a Warming Dish.

The Stove becomes available during
Anna's work at House Camp. The
<Link href="/permafrost/main-quests-walkthrough">Permafrost main quest walkthrough</Link>
covers the construction requirement
and the conversation that follows it.

For later Cold Zone travel, carry the
completed Dish until both the Hunger
recovery and added cold protection
will be useful.


<h2
  id="clothing"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Choose Warmer Clothing Without Wasting Materials
</h2>

Clothing is your primary protection
during ordinary travel because it
does not require consuming another
item every time you leave shelter.

The equipment screen displays
separate attributes for:

- **Armor:** Protection relevant
  to incoming attacks.
- **Cold Res.:** Protection relevant
  to cold exposure.

An item with higher Armor is not
necessarily warmer.

Likewise, an outfit unlocked later
in the game is not automatically
better in every equipment slot.

As Moon Cult, Tribal, and Hybrid
equipment become available, compare
individual pieces rather than
replacing your entire outfit
without checking their stats.

### Should You Craft the Full Tribal or Hybrid Set?

Craft the clothing required by
your active quest, but assess
optional replacements separately.

The Way Home requires specific
Tribal clothing, and later crafting
introduces Hybrid equipment.

Those tasks do not mean every
new piece is better than your
current gear.

For a cold crossing, compare the
Cold Res. values in the equipment
screen. When hostile enemies are
also a concern, check Armor
before trading away protection.

Treated Hide, Cloth, and other
materials can also be needed
for repairs and additional
crafting. Keep strong existing
equipment when replacing it
would cost resources without
improving the attribute you need.

### How Do You Know Your Outfit Is Warm Enough?

Compare the equipment's temperature
protection with the conditions
at your destination.

A set that keeps Body Warmth stable
in the Woods may still lose warmth
quickly in a Cold Zone.

After entering colder terrain,
watch the snowflake meter.

Stable or recovering warmth is
a better indication of sufficient
protection than the name or
appearance of your outfit.

Hunter can start with a Wolf Head Hat.

obtain one by completing
**Skinwalker's wolf hunting challenge**
at Hunter's Glade.

hunting task, and complete the
required wolf objective.

Return to him and claim the reward.

The hunting challenge is separate
from the main lookout repair task.

You do not need to restart as
Hunter solely to get the hat.

The comparison values above apply
to the displayed equipment setup.
Check your own equipment screen
when deciding whether another
piece provides better protection.


<h2
  id="cold-zones"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Enter Cold Zones and Get Back Out Safely
</h2>

Cold Zones are most dangerous when
they separate you from reliable
heat sources.

The air can become much colder
after crossing the zone boundary,
and the available construction
space may be restricted.

Recovering Body Warmth before entry
gives you a reserve, but you also
need enough protection for the
return journey.

### Bunker Checkpoint — Bone-Chilling Cold

The exposed upper area around
Bunker Checkpoint can show
**BONE-CHILLING COLD**.

One encounter displays a
feels-like temperature of
**−42°F**.

That is an example of the conditions
at one location, not a universal
temperature threshold for every
character and outfit.

The checkpoint also has elevated
stairs and platforms, increasing
the danger of taking fall damage
while already losing Health.

Some nearby ground restricts
construction. Do not assume a
Campfire can be placed halfway
through the crossing.

When you only need to continue
toward Hunter's Glade, the
main road avoids unnecessary
exploration through the
coldest exposed sections.

### Tower of Finn — Prepare for the Return Trip

The journey toward Finn's tower
is a more demanding crossing
than the early travel around
New Home.

Before leaving House Camp,
make three checks:

1. **Clothing:** Compare the
   Cold Resistance of your
   equipped pieces and keep
   the warmer combination.
2. **Heater:** Repair the
   Makeshift Heater and consider
   a spare if durability is low.
3. **Supplies:** Carry a warming
   drink or Dish, plus healing
   supplies for unexpected damage.

Reaching the upper radio tower
does not immediately remove
the danger of the cold.

You still need enough Body
Warmth and heater durability
to descend and leave the area.

After speaking with Finn,
follow the updated objective
rather than remaining in the
exposed location unnecessarily.

For the destination and
subsequent main quests, use
the <Link href="/permafrost/main-quests-walkthrough">main quest walkthrough</Link>.

### How Long Should You Stay Inside a Cold Zone?

Short visits are safer when your
current equipment barely meets
the environmental requirements.

Establish a warming point outside
the zone if construction is allowed,
then enter with repaired equipment
and a full warmth meter.

Collect the item or complete the
interaction you came for, and
head back while you still have
enough warmth to handle a delay.

If you are working toward the
Cold Resistance achievement,
its time requirement is listed
in the <Link href="/permafrost/achievements">Permafrost achievements checklist</Link>.
Surviving the exposure still
depends on your equipment
and available heat.

A Cold Zone does not become
safe simply because your
current Health is high.
Watch the rate of warmth loss
throughout the crossing.


<h2
  id="cold-warning-troubleshooting"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Still Freezing With Warm Clothes? Check These Four Things
</h2>

A stronger jacket does not
guarantee that Body Warmth
will remain stable.

The cause usually becomes
clear after checking the
temperature, equipment,
and active status effects.

**1. Your clothing is not rated for the current temperature.**

Compare the protection shown
in the equipment screen with
the conditions outside.
Equipment suitable for
−25°C can still struggle
after entering a zone
around −33°C.

**2. The Makeshift Heater is damaged or exhausted.**

Inspect its remaining durability
and confirm that it is equipped.
A broken heater cannot provide
the protection expected from
a functioning one.

**3. Your consumable's protection has ended.**

Coffee, Tea, and Warming Dishes
provide additional benefits,
but those effects should not
be treated as permanent.

Check the status display
before relying on them for
the return trip.

**4. The weather or time of day has changed.**

Storms and nightfall can
make familiar routes colder.
A journey that was manageable
earlier may drain warmth
more quickly on the way back.

If Body Warmth is already
near zero, stop the exposure
before trying to solve
another problem.

Return to a heated location,
use Warm Hands, repair the
heater, and adjust clothing
or supplies before attempting
the crossing again.

Restoring Health while your
warmth continues falling
only delays the same danger.
