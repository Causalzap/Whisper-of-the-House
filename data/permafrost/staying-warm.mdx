<section id="quick-answer" className="scroll-mt-24">
  <GuideQuickAnswer title="How Do You Stop Freezing in Permafrost?">
    Use **Warm Hands** at a lit Campfire to recover Body Warmth.
    Equip clothing with better Cold Resistance and check your
    Makeshift Heater before leaving shelter. Carry a warming
    drink or meal when traveling through a Cold Zone.
  </GuideQuickAnswer>
</section>


A full Health bar can disappear quickly once your Body Warmth runs out.
This becomes particularly dangerous around Bunker Checkpoint and
during the journey toward Finn's radio tower, where exposed terrain
and severe temperatures leave little time to recover.

The equipment that keeps you comfortable around New Home may not
be enough for those locations. A better jacket can slow warmth loss,
but it cannot replace a heated shelter when you are already freezing.
A Makeshift Heater allows you to travel farther, although its
durability decreases during use. Food and drinks can provide
additional Cold Resistance when you need to cross an exposed area.

The useful distinction is between **getting warm again** and
**staying warm long enough to reach your destination**. Check which
problem you are facing before consuming supplies or continuing into
colder terrain.


<h2
  id="body-warmth"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How Body Warmth and Cold Resistance Work
</h2>

The snowflake icon in the lower-left status display represents
**Body Warmth**. It is separate from Health, Hunger, Energy,
and Stamina.

As the temperature falls, your warmth can drain faster than
your equipment can protect you. Continued exposure eventually
damages Health, making an otherwise manageable fight or
short journey much more dangerous.

Watch three things while traveling:

- **Body Warmth:** The snowflake meter shows how much warmth
  remains before prolonged cold becomes dangerous.
- **Temperature and weather:** The display near the compass
  shows current conditions. Storms and nightfall can make
  the same route more dangerous.
- **Cold warnings:** Messages such as `BONE-CHILLING COLD`
  and `COLD ZONE` indicate hazardous exposure.

Cold Resistance helps you tolerate lower temperatures, but
the number shown on your equipment is not a guarantee that
every Cold Zone is safe.

Your available protection also depends on the clothing
you have equipped, whether your portable heater is still
working, and any temporary benefits from food or drinks.

A useful warning sign is **Body Warmth continuing to fall
rapidly despite your current protection**. At that point,
turning around before Health starts dropping gives you more
room to deal with wildlife, bad weather, or an unexpected
detour.


<h2
  id="campfire-warm-hands"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Recover Body Warmth With a Campfire
</h2>

A Campfire provides a reliable way to recover warmth around
your base or anywhere construction is permitted.

Open the Building menu, select Campfire, and place it on
suitable ground. Add Wood to the fuel inventory and turn
the fire on.

Standing near the fire helps, but the Campfire also has
a direct warming interaction.

### Use Warm Hands

Approach the lit Campfire and hold the interaction key
to open its radial menu. Select **Warm Hands**.

<figure className="my-8">
  <Image
    src="/images/permafrost/permafrost-campfire-warm-hands.webp"
    alt="Permafrost Campfire radial menu showing the Warm Hands interaction beside a lit fire"
    width={1600}
    height={900}
    className="rounded-xl"
  />

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">
    Select Warm Hands at a lit Campfire to recover Body Warmth
    before heading back into the snow.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Allow your warmth to recover before leaving. Lighting a fire
and immediately walking away may not provide enough time
to recover from prolonged exposure.

The Campfire consumes Wood while operating. When you finish,
turn it off and recover unused fuel if you need that Wood
for your next trip.

### Why Won't the Campfire Place?

Construction needs suitable terrain. An obstructed placement
may become valid after moving onto flatter ground, but a
**No-Build Zone** prevents ordinary construction in that area.

Use the Building Tool's placement feedback to distinguish
between the two situations.

This matters most when crossing remote areas. Finding a
sheltered-looking corner does not mean you will be allowed
to build a fire there. Keep portable heating equipment
available when the route leads through restricted terrain.


<h2
  id="heated-shelter"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Is Your Shelter Too Cold to Sleep?
</h2>

At New Home, constructing a Bed does not guarantee you can
immediately use it.

The game can prevent resting when the surrounding area is
too cold. Rest may also be interrupted if your character
is taking damage.

Use a lit Campfire near the resting area and give your
Body Warmth time to recover. Keep Wood available so the
fire does not go out while you are preparing to sleep.

A completed roof, walls, and door provide shelter, but
the room may still need a working heat source.

Wall Torches are useful for lighting an interior. Do not
depend on one as your only protection when the Bed reports
that it is too cold to rest. Use the Body Warmth meter
to judge whether your character is actually recovering.

When the Bed remains unavailable, check the following:

- The nearby Campfire is lit and contains fuel.
- Your character is close enough to the heat source.
- Body Warmth is recovering rather than continuing to fall.
- Health is not being reduced by another ongoing danger.

If your shelter is unfinished and supplies are running
low, returning to an established settlement such as
Horizon may be safer than attempting to rest in an
unheated room.

You only need to solve the temperature problem once
before sleeping. Repeatedly interacting with the Bed
without changing the conditions will not help.


<h2
  id="makeshift-heater"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Makeshift Heater: Where to Get One and How to Repair It
</h2>

A **Makeshift Heater** provides portable cold protection
without requiring a nearby Campfire.

It is useful for open roads, abandoned buildings, and
areas where the Building Tool cannot place a new heat
source.

Makeshift Heaters can be found among supplies during
exploration. **Skinwalker at Hunter's Glade** also offers
them through trading.

The important limitation is that a heater wears out
during use. Having one in your inventory is not the
same as having a functioning heater ready for another
journey.

Equip the heater for use and inspect its remaining
condition before entering an exposed area.

### Repair a Makeshift Heater With Cloth and Plastic

The repair requirement is:

- **1 Cloth**
- **1 Plastic**

Open the Repair interface, select the damaged Makeshift
Heater, and check that both materials are available.
Use the Repair interaction to restore its condition.

  <figcaption className="mt-3 text-sm text-slate-400">

Keep a small reserve of these materials instead of using
all your Cloth and Plastic on equipment that can wait.

The heater can wear out during an extended cold crossing.
Its durability should be checked before you leave, not
after Body Warmth has already begun falling.

### Should You Buy a Spare Makeshift Heater?

A spare becomes worthwhile when the destination is far
from reliable shelter and your current heater may not
last the journey.

For ordinary trips around Horizon and New Home, repairing
the unit you already own may be enough. You have more
opportunities to return to shelter and replenish supplies.

The journey toward Finn's tower creates a different
problem: the exposed route can remain dangerously cold
even after you reach the objective.

Before spending money on another heater, check whether
the existing one can be repaired. Buy or carry an extra
when you cannot rely on a single unit lasting through
both the outward and return journey.

There is no advantage in carrying several unusable heaters.
Their remaining condition determines whether they can
actually protect you.


<h2
  id="cold-resistance-drinks"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Improvised Coffee vs. Improvised Tea: Which Should You Use?
</h2>

Improvised Coffee and Improvised Tea both provide
**45 Cold Res.**, but they restore different additional
needs.

- **Improvised Coffee:** 45 Cold Res., 45 Energy, and
  10 Hunger. Hunter begins with this drink.
- **Improvised Tea:** 45 Cold Res., 45 Health, and
  10 Hunger. Survivor begins with this drink.

Coffee is the better choice when Energy is low and you
still need to travel. Tea is more valuable when you
have already taken damage and need both Health and
protection against the cold.

Both drinks can be saved for a colder section rather
than consumed as soon as they enter your inventory.

Their Cold Resistance is a consumable benefit. It does
not permanently improve the clothing you have equipped,
so continue using suitable winter equipment even after
drinking one.

### When Should You Use a Cold-Resistance Drink?

Save it for a route where the extra protection matters.

For example, ordinary gathering around a heated base
does not justify consuming your last warming drink.
A journey through an exposed Cold Zone may.

Before drinking, consider whether your Energy or Health
also needs recovery. Using the drink when both benefits
are valuable makes better use of a limited supply.

The exact duration of the effect matters during longer
journeys. Check the active status effect after consuming
the drink rather than assuming its protection will remain
throughout the entire trip.

>
  Warming Dish: Cold Resistance and Hunger Recovery
</h2>

A **Warming Dish** provides two substantial effects:

- **50 Cold Res.**
- **200 Hunger**

That combination makes it useful before an extended
journey through cold terrain, particularly when your
Hunger is already low.

The Stove uses a **Primary** ingredient and an **Enhancer**.
The selected combination determines which Dish is produced.

A Healthy Dish and a Warming Dish serve different purposes.
Choose the result that addresses your immediate need
instead of consuming valuable ingredients without
checking the output.

### Why Does the Stove Say Invalid Slot?

The message **Invalid slot for this kind of item** means
the selected ingredient cannot be placed in that slot.

Check which ingredient is assigned as Primary and which
one is being used as Enhancer. Some combinations work
while others are rejected.

Do not assume that every piece of Meat or every drink
can be combined to make a Warming Dish. Inspect the
result before committing your ingredients.

The Stove is introduced during Anna's objectives at
House Camp. The <Link href="/permafrost/main-quests-walkthrough">Permafrost main quest walkthrough</Link>
covers the construction objective and the route to that
settlement.

Carry a prepared Warming Dish for the next dangerous
crossing. Eating it inside a warm settlement provides
less value when you already have full Hunger and
do not need additional Cold Resistance.


<h2
  id="clothing"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Which Clothes Have Better Cold Resistance?
</h2>

Clothing provides protection during normal exploration
without requiring a consumable every time you leave shelter.

The equipment comparison displays both **Armor** and
**Cold Res.** These attributes answer different questions:
Armor matters when taking attacks, while Cold Resistance
matters when the environment is damaging your warmth.

A piece with higher Armor is not necessarily warmer.
Likewise, a newly unlocked outfit is not automatically
better in every equipment slot.

This becomes important as you progress from starting
clothing into Moon Cult, Tribal, and Hybrid equipment.

### Should You Replace Moon Cult or Tribal Clothing?

Check individual equipment pieces before crafting or
equipping an entire set.

Moon Cult equipment can be obtained during exploration,
while Tribal pieces become available through clothing
crafting and progression.

The Way Home includes crafting Tribal clothing, but
finishing that objective does not require you to assume
every new piece is better than what you already own.

Inspect each item's actual Armor and Cold Res. before
replacing your current equipment.

Treated Hide and Cloth are also used for other crafting
and repairs, so there is little reason to consume
additional materials on an optional replacement that
does not improve the attribute you need.

When moving through ordinary hostile territory, Armor
may be important. When the next destination is an extreme
Cold Zone, prioritize the pieces that improve cold
protection without leaving you unnecessarily vulnerable.

  Wolf Head Hat vs. Hybrid Hat: Which Is Warmer?
</h2>

The Wolf Head Hat is a useful example of why an entire
outfit should not be replaced without comparing its
individual parts.

In the equipment comparison, the displayed Cold Res.
values are:

- **Wolf Head Hat:** 95 Cold Res., 3 Armor.
- **Hybrid Hat:** 89.6 Cold Res., 3 Armor.

That is a difference of **5.4 Cold Res.** with no
difference in the displayed Armor value.

For that equipment setup, keeping the Wolf Head Hat is
the stronger choice when cold protection is the priority.
The Hybrid Hat costs more materials to obtain without

These are the displayed comparison values. Check your
own equipment screen when evaluating other gear and
skill combinations.

### How to Get the Wolf Head Hat

**Hunter** can begin with the Wolf Head Hat as part
of its starting equipment.

Players who begin with Survivor or Smuggler can also
obtain a Wolf Head Hat through **Skinwalker's wolf
hunting challenge** at Hunter's Glade.

Speak to Skinwalker at the lookout, accept the wolf
contract, and complete the required hunting objective.
Return to him to claim the reward.

That makes the hat relevant beyond the Hunter background.
You do not need to restart your character solely to
obtain one.

Skinwalker's hunting challenge is separate from the
main radio repair objective. Complete it when you
need the reward rather than delaying urgent
construction or survival tasks.

### Do You Need the Full Hybrid Outfit?

The other Hybrid pieces may still improve your equipment.
Compare the jacket, gloves, trousers, boots, and headwear
separately against the clothing already equipped.

Replacing one weaker piece at a time avoids wasting
materials and allows you to keep a particularly useful
item such as the Wolf Head Hat.

When preparing for a Cold Zone, examine the resulting
Cold Res. after changing each slot. A full matching
outfit is not automatically the best combination
for surviving low temperatures.


<h2
  id="cold-zones"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  How to Cross Cold Zones Without Freezing
</h2>

Cold Zones create the greatest risk when your character
is far from an existing heat source.

The temperature can become severe enough that ordinary
winter clothing provides very little time to explore.
Body Warmth may start falling rapidly shortly after
entering the area, leaving Health exposed once the
remaining warmth is exhausted.

These locations should be treated differently from
ordinary roads where you can stop, gather Wood, and
build a fire whenever needed.

### Bunker Checkpoint: Extreme Cold and No-Build Zones

The elevated area around Bunker Checkpoint can become
dangerously cold.

One exposed section shows a **feels-like temperature
of -42°F** alongside the `BONE-CHILLING COLD` warning.

Some terrain around dangerous locations also restricts
building. A **No-Build Zone** prevents placing an emergency
Campfire, even when you have the materials in your backpack.

Before climbing toward an exposed structure, recover Body
Warmth in a safer area and make sure your portable heater
is functioning.

The checkpoint also contains elevated platforms and stairs.
Falling while already losing health to cold can turn an
otherwise survivable mistake into a fatal one.

If you only need to pass the checkpoint on the way to
Hunter's Glade, continue along the road rather than
entering every optional cold area around the building.

### Tower of Finn: What Should You Prepare?

The journey from House Camp toward Finn's radio tower
includes severe exposure. This is where combining
different kinds of protection becomes worthwhile.

Before leaving House Camp:

1. **Compare your equipped clothing.** Use pieces that
   provide stronger Cold Resistance, keeping warmer
   individual items even when other parts of the outfit
   have been upgraded.
2. **Check the Makeshift Heater.** Repair it before
   departure and bring another usable heater when
   your journey may outlast one unit.
3. **Carry warmth and recovery supplies.** A warming
   drink or Warming Dish provides additional protection,
   while healing supplies help recover from damage
   sustained during the crossing.

The destination is the upper section of a damaged
radio tower, but the cold does not become harmless
as soon as you reach the building.

Complete the conversation with Finn, check the updated
objective, and continue toward Solar Fields without
unnecessary detours through exposed terrain.

For the route to Finn and the progression after speaking
with him, follow the <Link href="/permafrost/main-quests-walkthrough">main quest walkthrough</Link>.

### When Should You Retreat From a Cold Zone?

Watch how your warmth responds during the first part
of the crossing.

A rapidly declining meter despite your current protection
is a reason to turn back before Health begins falling.
The return journey may be colder if the weather changes,
and a damaged heater may not provide the same protection
you had when you entered.

Recover at a heated settlement, repair equipment, or
change clothing before making another attempt.

The goal is to keep enough protection available to leave
the Cold Zone after collecting an item or completing
an interaction. Reaching the objective with almost no
warmth remaining can leave you unable to return safely.


<h2
  id="cold-warning-troubleshooting"
  className="scroll-mt-24"
>
  Why Are You Still Freezing With Warm Clothes?
</h2>

When Body Warmth continues dropping after upgrading your
clothing, check the actual conditions instead of assuming
that the new equipment is not working.

**Your clothing may be insufficient for the temperature.**
Cold Resistance that works around New Home may not be
enough inside a severe Cold Zone. Compare your equipment
and retreat if the meter continues falling too quickly.

**Your portable heater may be worn out.** Inspect its
condition and confirm that a usable heater is equipped.
An item that has exhausted its durability cannot be
treated as dependable protection.

**A temporary food or drink benefit may have ended.**
Check your active effects rather than assuming the
protection from a previously consumed item is still
available.

**The weather may have changed.** Storms and nightfall
can make the return journey colder than the trip out.
Wait somewhere heated when conditions have become
too dangerous for your remaining supplies.

Once the cause is clear, recover Body Warmth before
continuing. Healing damage without addressing the
cold leaves you exposed to the same danger again.
