<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To unlock Fast Travel in Underkeep, speak to **Khelebar** in Village of Underkeep and accept his request to investigate **four Obelisks** near Underkeep Tower. Interact with each monolith to copy its runes, then return to Khelebar with all four records. Completing **Wards of the West** rewards the **Teleportation Stone**, which lets you use the ancient Waystone network.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Finding a Waystone does not immediately let you travel. If you have already spoken to Khelebar but are still missing one or more rune records, use the four locations below to identify what you skipped.

The overgrown passage and the fallen stone beneath the collapsed glade are easy to overlook. Another Obelisk lies along the Orc Encampment route, where enemy resistance may stop you before you reach the monolith.

<h2 id="start-fast-travel">
  How Do You Start the Fast Travel Quest in Underkeep?
</h2>

First, reach **Village of Underkeep**.

If you are still traveling through the starting forest and underground passages, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/early-game-walkthrough">early-game walkthrough</Link> until you have completed Garric's request and entered the village.

Once inside, find **Khelebar**, the local alchemist.

Ask him about the ancient stone portals.

He explains that the structures are part of an old travel network. The stones no longer function normally, but an appropriate artifact can awaken them.

Ask how to obtain that artifact.

Khelebar directs you toward **four Obelisks** scattered around the wilderness near Underkeep Tower. He wants you to visit each one and copy the runes carved into its surface.

Accept his request before heading out.

### Do You Need to Enter Underkeep Tower?

No. The Fast Travel investigation takes place around the tower and its surrounding paths.

The tower's large sealed entrance is a separate obstacle. You do not need to solve its main gate to begin looking for the four Obelisks.

Start by exploring **Roots of Underkeep**, the wilderness surrounding the tower.


<h2 id="obelisk-locations">
  Where Can You Find the Obelisks for Fast Travel?
</h2>

Khelebar asks for four sets of rune records.

The Obelisks are not all equally easy to spot. Some stand openly beside the forest paths, while another has fallen into an underground chamber.

The four Obelisks are found along different routes around Underkeep Tower, including an overgrown passage, the underground chamber beneath the collapsed glade, and the Orc Encampment. Interact with each stone to copy its runes before returning to Khelebar.

Approach each Obelisk and interact with its runes. Your party copies the symbols onto parchment for Khelebar, even if the stone is damaged.

Make sure the interaction updates **Wards of the West** before moving on. Simply finding a monolith is not enough.

<h2 id="first-obelisk">
  Where Can You Find an Obelisk Near Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

Begin in the **Roots of Underkeep** area.

Explore the paths surrounding the tower and look for a tall stone monolith marked with ancient runes.

One of these stones can be found during the early exploration of the tower-side wilderness, near the route where you can also encounter an old druid.

Approach the stone and interact with it.

The inscription explains that the monolith is old and weathered, but the runes remain visible enough to copy.

Once the interaction finishes, continue searching for the remaining stones.

### Should You Speak to the Druid?

Yes, especially if you intend to investigate the fallen Obelisk.

The old druid explains that the standing stones help maintain the protective seal around Underkeep.

He is concerned about a monolith that has sunk beneath the earth in the **northwestern reaches** of the area.

The druid asks you to investigate that fallen stone and report what happened.

This gives you another reason to follow the same exploration route, but it is distinct from Khelebar's request to collect all four rune records.

You can work on both objectives during the same visit.

<h2 id="hidden-obelisk">
  Where Is the Hidden Obelisk Behind the Bushes?
</h2>

One of the easiest stones to miss is reached through an **overgrown passage**.

While exploring Roots of Underkeep, check side passages that appear to end at thick bushes. One overgrown section hides a narrow route leading toward an Obelisk.

### How Do You Reach It?

1. Return to the wilderness paths around Underkeep Tower.
2. Check branches that appear to end at thick bushes.
3. Approach the vegetation and try moving through it.
4. Follow the accessible passage beyond the brush.
5. Continue toward the monolith and interact with its runes.

<h2 id="fallen-obelisk">
  How Do You Reach the Fallen Obelisk in Underkeep?
</h2>

The **Fallen Obelisk** is one of the more involved locations.

The druid describes a stone that has sunk into the earth in the northwestern reaches of Underkeep.

Following the surrounding paths eventually brings you to a quiet glade containing a collapsed section of ground.

At the center is a deep pit.

You can see the fallen monolith below, but the drop is too deep to jump safely and the walls cannot simply be climbed.

The solution is to find another way underground.

### Find the Underground Route

Search the nearby paths for an entrance into the connected cavern system.

The underground area contains damaged stonework, blocked passages, and signs of large beetles.

As you explore, you discover evidence that the insects have been digging through the earth around the fallen stone.

Prepare for combat before entering the deeper chambers.

The beetles can attack while you are trying to navigate the tunnels, so clear immediate threats before examining puzzles or narrow passages.

Some rocks and mineral deposits in the area are not part of the direct route to the Obelisk. If a wall or deposit cannot be cleared with your current tools, check the other available passages.

<h2 id="beetle-tunnels">
  How Do You Copy the Runes From the Broken Obelisk?
</h2>

Continue through the beetle-infested passages toward the chamber beneath the collapsed glade.

After clearing the creatures blocking the way, you can reach the fallen stone.

The monolith is damaged and partly buried.

Unlike the standing Obelisks, much of its inscription has been destroyed. However, enough of the runes remain for your party to record the surviving symbols.

Interact with the fallen stone and copy the visible markings.

This is the important result for Khelebar's investigation.

### Should You Return to the Druid?

If you accepted the druid's request, return to him after inspecting the fallen Obelisk.

Tell him what you discovered beneath the ground.

The conversation explains that beetles were responsible for undermining the stone, damaging one part of the old protective system.

Completing this conversation advances the druid's separate objective and provides a protective reward.

However, that reward is not the **Teleportation Stone** used for Fast Travel.

For Fast Travel, you still need to finish recording all four Obelisks and return to Khelebar.

<h2 id="orc-encampment">
  Where Is the Obelisk at the Orc Encampment?
</h2>

The **Orc Encampment** is another route worth investigating if you are still missing an Obelisk.

The approach is guarded by heavily armored orcs and crossbow-wielding enemies near the wooden palisades.

These enemies can be difficult to defeat without sufficient healing and combat resources.

### What Should You Do at the Encampment?

If the guards prevent you from reaching the monolith, leave this route for later and record the other Obelisk runes first. You can return after improving your equipment.

The Orc Encampment route leads to another rune-covered monolith. After dealing with the enemies blocking your exploration, continue through the accessible area until you find the standing stone.

Interact with it to copy the runes onto parchment. This is one of the four records required for Wards of the West.

<h2 id="four-rune-records">
  How Do You Know When You Have All Four Obelisk Runes?
</h2>

A monolith only counts after you complete the interaction that copies its markings onto parchment. Check whether **Wards of the West** updates before leaving the location.

If you reached a stone but never recorded its runes, return to it. Khelebar cannot finish the investigation with an incomplete set of records.

<h2 id="teleportation-stone">
  How Do You Get the Teleportation Stone?
</h2>

After collecting all four rune records, return to **Khelebar in Village of Underkeep**.

Speak to him again about the Obelisks.

The investigation concludes through **Wards of the West**, and you receive the **Teleportation Stone**.

This is the artifact Khelebar described when you first asked about the ancient portals.

The Teleportation Stone is what allows you to begin using the Waystone network.

If Khelebar does not offer the completion dialogue, review your remaining Obelisk progress rather than assuming you must defeat a major dungeon boss.

The four rune records are the relevant requirement.

<h2 id="use-waystones">
  How Do You Use Waystones After Unlocking Fast Travel?
</h2>

Once you have the Teleportation Stone, approach an ancient Waystone.

These are the large stone travel structures found in locations around Underkeep, including the village.

Interact with the Waystone after obtaining the artifact.

The formerly inactive travel system becomes available, allowing travel between accessible Waystone locations.

This is particularly useful when returning to regions you have already explored.

For example, traveling between the village and the western cemetery area becomes much more convenient than repeatedly walking through the forest.

### Does Unlocking Fast Travel Open Every Locked Door?

No. The Teleportation Stone activates Waystones; it cannot open the tower's sealed entrance or replace dungeon keys. If Underkeep Tower remains locked, check the <Link href="/underkeep">four Key Fragments required for its entrance</Link>.

<h2 id="fast-travel-not-working">
  Why Is Fast Travel Still Locked in Underkeep?
</h2>

There are several reasons the Waystone network may still be unavailable.

### You Have Not Spoken to Khelebar

Find Khelebar in Village of Underkeep and ask about the stone portals.

His investigation gives you the objective of recording the four Obelisk runes.

### You Have Only Recorded Three Obelisks

Four rune records are required.

Return to the tower-side wilderness and check the hidden passages, fallen Obelisk route, and unexplored approaches around the Orc Encampment.

### You Found the Fallen Obelisk but Did Not Record Its Runes

Reaching the underground chamber is not the same as completing the rune interaction.

Approach the damaged stone and copy the surviving symbols.

### You Finished the Druid's Request but Have No Teleportation Stone

The druid's investigation of the fallen Obelisk and Khelebar's four-rune task are different objectives.

Return to Khelebar once all four rune records are complete.

### You Have the Teleportation Stone but Are Still Walking Everywhere

Return to a Waystone and interact with it.

The artifact makes the ancient travel network usable. It is not a substitute for approaching the travel structure itself.

<h2 id="after-unlocking">
  What Should You Do After Unlocking Fast Travel?
</h2>

With Fast Travel available, returning to distant regions becomes easier.

You can revisit places where you left an unopened chest, an unfinished quest, or a passage that required an item you did not have earlier.

The Waystone network is especially useful when working between Village of Underkeep, the cemetery, and the deeper dungeon regions.
