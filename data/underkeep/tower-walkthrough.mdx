<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To finish **Underkeep Tower**, insert all **four Key Fragments** into the entrance seal. Inside, complete the floor-switch trial without reusing an activated circle and uncover the **Shadow Beast** by opening the curtains to let in light. Work through the Mimic rooms, Gallery statues, and colored rune puzzle to reach the **Architect**. His encounter can inflict **Stun, Burn, and Mental Fog**. After defeating him, return to the **ground floor** and take the newly opened route beneath the tower. Confront **Torin** beneath the tower, survive the enemies emerging from the red portal, and attack him when he comes within reach. After Torin is pulled into the portal, **enter it to seal the gate from the other side**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Underkeep Tower begins where the four-fragment seal finally gives way, but the sealed entrance is only the first obstacle. A spectral wizard challenges your party with a series of trials, and the route through the Gallery leads to a confrontation that still does not finish the story. Beyond the wizard lies a deeper passage and the person who helped you obtain one of the fragments in the first place.

The Tower entrance requires **four Key Fragments**. The three Golden Keys from the Mausoleum and the eight Blue Key Spheres from the Soulforge open different mechanisms and cannot replace them.

<h2 id="four-key-fragments">
  How Do You Open Underkeep Tower With Four Key Fragments?
</h2>

Approach the sealed tower entrance with all four **Key Fragments** in your inventory. Interact with its circular locking mechanism and insert each fragment when prompted.

The gate reacts to the first three pieces but remains closed. The first fragment settles into an empty socket, the second causes a vibration, and the third briefly illuminates the seal's runes. **The fourth fragment completes the seal and opens the tower.** If the door still reports that it is sealed, you have not finished collecting the required fragments.

Torin offers one Key Fragment during earlier exploration, but you need all four before the Tower seal will open. If you are still working through the regional objectives or need to revisit Torin, return to the <Link href="/underkeep">Underkeep progression guide</Link>.

Once you have all four fragments, insert them into the seal to enter the Tower.

<h2 id="first-tower-trial">
  How Do You Get Past the First Tower Switch Puzzle?
</h2>

A pale apparition greets you shortly after entering. It resembles a wizard, but the figure is only a magical echo. He tells you to endure the tower's trials before you can speak with him in person.

The early rooms contain floor circles, switches, and a reset mechanism. Opening one passage does not necessarily complete the whole trial. The important restriction is **not to cross an activated circle a second time**.

Follow a route that reaches each required circle without retracing your steps over one you have already used. If the mechanism rejects your attempt, find the reset switch and start the sequence again. Once the correct route is completed, the next passage becomes accessible.

Do not confuse the reset control with the switch that opens an individual hallway. One changes the current state of the puzzle; the other lets you restart after an incorrect crossing.

<h2 id="shadow-beast">
  How Do You Defeat the Shadow Beast in Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

One of the earliest trial chambers shuts its doors behind you and becomes almost completely dark. A creature moves somewhere in the gloom, but attacking immediately does not solve the encounter.

Search the chamber for the **curtains that block the light**. Pull them aside until the room brightens. When the final curtain opens, light drives back the unnatural darkness and the **Shadow Beast** becomes vulnerable.

Attack after the light returns, not while you are still searching for the creature in darkness. Once it has been defeated, continue through the opened route and into the tower's next section.

<h2 id="tower-level-two">
  How Do You Get Through Tower Level Two and the Mimic Rooms?
</h2>

Tower Level Two contains a trap that is easy to mistake for an ordinary reward. Interacting with a chest can abruptly move you into another room, where you encounter enemies disguised as treasure containers. The **Mimics** can inflict **Stun** when they hit, and some encounters place more than one in your path.

Clear the enemies before trying to retrace the corridor. When an apparently isolated chamber seems to have no working switch, inspect the **torches** rather than only the doors or floor. Lighting the available torches opens the next encounter in this route.

A nearby accessible area can also provide an opportunity to Rest. If a chamber will not let you Rest, check whether another enemy is still active or whether you can return to the safer adjoining section.

The Mimic encounters and their chests are separate from the Gallery arrangements farther ahead. Do not spend time trying to solve the statue puzzle before you have opened the passage leading to it.

<h2 id="tower-statues">
  How Do You Move the Tower Statues and Open the Gallery?
</h2>

The next set of rooms introduces switches marked by a clue that **only the outer ones should awaken**. Operating the relevant outer switches changes access to the nearby statue area, though it may not immediately open the door you expected.

You then reach movable statues and two reset controls. The nearby group of **three reference statues** gives the clue to the required placement. Arrange the movable statues to reproduce that pattern **shifted one position**, rather than guessing independently at every floor tile.

If you push one into the wrong place, use a reset control instead of trapping yourself with an unusable arrangement. The successful configuration advances the route toward the **Gallery**.

<h2 id="tower-gallery">
  How Do You Solve the Underkeep Tower Gallery Puzzle?
</h2>

The Gallery contains paintings, movable figures, and clues that can be misleading when read one by one. A painting of wolves in a forest and other scenes may suggest placing a figure directly in front of whatever it depicts, but this alone does not produce the solution.

Look at the **larger reference composition** and compare how its figures are arranged. Move the Gallery's pieces to match the displayed arrangement instead of trying every possible pairing between a statue and a painting. The side controls allow you to reset an incorrect configuration.

Once the figures match the reference, the mechanism responds and the way forward opens. This takes you beyond the Gallery toward the last set of colored runes.

<h2 id="tower-rune-colors">
  What Is the Colored Rune Puzzle Solution in Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

A later chamber shows rune symbols that can be switched on or off in different colors. The solution is **not** to illuminate every symbol. Match the four positions and colors shown by the reference pattern:

- **Top:** Blue **Z-shaped** symbol.
- **Left:** Red **T-shaped** symbol with a small projection.
- **Right:** Red **cross** symbol.
- **Bottom:** Blue **combined or irregular T/Z-shaped** symbol.

Leave the other color variants dark. If the gate does not respond, revisit the reference before flipping more switches: the T-shaped glyph and the cross are both red, while the upper Z and lower irregular glyph are blue.

Setting the four symbols correctly opens the door. Examine the rewards along this route before continuing; the tower also contains items such as a **Feather of Mercy** and a **Medal of Baldderin**.

<h2 id="architect-encounter">
  What Happens When You Meet the Architect?
</h2>

The path beyond the trials warns that you are nearing the end of your journey and that **there will be no turning back**. Use the available recovery and revival facilities before entering the next chamber. You will not be able to rely on an easy return to the village between the last two major fights.

A wizard hangs above an altar in a room covered with ancient runes. Unlike the apparition at the tower entrance, he recognizes actions your party took earlier. If you **killed one of the guardians**, he sees that act and announces that there will be **no negotiation**.

<h2 id="architect-boss">
  How Do You Beat the Architect in Underkeep?
</h2>

The **Architect** can inflict several dangerous effects during the fight, including **Stun**, **Burn**, and **Mental Fog**. These conditions are more disruptive than the raw damage alone: Stun may prevent the character you need from acting, and Mental Fog can interfere with access to abilities.

Bring status-removal items and enough recovery supplies for a prolonged encounter. Remove disabling conditions from the characters whose next actions matter most, and spend your high-damage abilities when those characters can actually act. Do not rely on one healer staying available through the entire battle.

### Should You Throw Torin's Tincture at the Architect?

If you choose to throw **Torin's tincture** at the wizard before combat, it strikes an invisible barrier and has no effect. The fight can be won through normal combat, so do not depend on the tincture to remove his protection or defeat him instantly.

### What Do You Get After Defeating the Architect?

During his final dialogue, the Architect recognizes **Soulcleaver, Axe of the Deep Forge**, if you brought it from the Soulforge. The tower's runes react, opening a passage **beneath the ground floor**.

The Architect is not the final enemy. Follow the newly opened route to continue toward the red portal.

<h2 id="tower-depths">
  Where Do You Go After Defeating the Architect?
</h2>

After the Architect dies, the next objective points to a newly opened passage **below Underkeep Tower's ground floor**. Look for the descent rather than searching for another exit behind the altar.

The passage leads into a vast underground hall with tall pillars and a **red portal**. Torin, who previously offered you a Key Fragment, waits beside it.

<h2 id="torin-reveal">
  Who Is Torin and Why Does He Open the Final Portal?
</h2>

Torin admits that he has spent centuries drawing adventurers toward the ruins with promises of treasure and forgotten power. Your party's victories weakened the protections he wanted removed.

He identifies the **Source beneath the graveyard**, the **marsh creature at Grimfin**, and the **dragon** as parts of the network protecting the tower. He also describes his efforts to overcome the remaining guardian through the blood-drinkers he created. Even if the marsh creature and dragon survived, Torin claims the bonds protecting the tower have been weakened.

His goal is not to destroy the world outright. He wants to use the portal and the power beyond it to **rule**. When you refuse to let him proceed, his form changes and enemies emerge from the crimson light.

<h2 id="torin-boss">
  How Do You Defeat Torin in the Final Boss Fight?
</h2>

The final confrontation begins with hostile creatures coming from the portal. Torin may remain out of your immediate attack range while those enemies occupy the party. Control the space between the **pillars** so your team is not surrounded, but leave a route that allows you to reach Torin when he comes into range.

Prioritize survival through the opening pressure rather than exhausting every high-value recovery item on the first wave. When Torin becomes reachable, turn your damage toward him while keeping the characters who can heal or revive others alive.

Torin has considerable Health, and the fight can consume the supplies left over from the Architect. If you cannot survive both encounters, reload before committing to the final part of the tower and bring more healing and protection against disabling effects.

At the end of the battle, Torin's wounds begin to close, but he is staggered. **Something beyond the red portal seizes him and pulls him through.** Your party defeats him in the encounter; he is not simply left as an ordinary corpse with a final key to collect.

<h2 id="underkeep-ending">
  What Happens at the End of Underkeep?
</h2>

After Torin is dragged away, the portal remains open. The wizard's lingering presence makes the final task clear: **the gate must be sealed from within**.

Your party steps through the red light. A strange world appears on the other side, and the gateway closes behind the characters. The ending returns to the village, which survives to see another dawn, unaware of what the party sacrificed beyond the tower.

<h2 id="tower-stuck">
  Why Are You Stuck in Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

<GuideTable columns={towerTrialColumns} rows={towerTrialRows} />

If the **Architect is dead but the story has not finished**, the newly opened route is below the tower's ground floor, not beyond the altar. The final enemy is in the underground portal chamber.

If the **last fight is overwhelming**, remember that defeating the Architect does not replenish your potions or remove the danger ahead. Prepare before passing the no-return warning, and keep any revival items you found in the tower available for the final stretch.
