<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To reach **Village of Underkeep**, follow the starting road into the forest, explore the underground passages through **Hollow Grave Tunnels**, and work your way back toward the surface. Use the **Silver Key** on the locked gate leading to the next forest section. Continue past **Jacob's Cottage**, then speak to **Garric** on the village approach. Clear the burrowing enemies south of his position and return to him to open the way into the village.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Underkeep does not give you one straight corridor from the opening area to its first village. Forest paths connect to underground passages, some doors cannot be opened from the side you first encounter, and several keys belong to optional chests rather than the route forward.

That makes it easy to spend time searching the wrong part of the map. The early journey is easier to follow when you separate the path toward the village from the items and side encounters that can be revisited later.

The route below follows a progression through the starting forest, Hollow Grave Tunnels, the warehouse area, and the village approach. Some side rooms and loot pickups can be handled in a different order. The important checkpoints are reaching the underground passages, finding the exit that uses the Silver Key, and completing Garric's request.

<h2 id="starting-road">
  Where Do You Go After Starting Underkeep?
</h2>

After creating your party, you begin with the quest **Whispers of the Underkeep**. A letter has summoned your adventurers to a distant village, and your first objective is to make your way there.

Follow the road toward the forest and begin exploring the connected paths. The early area contains a Revival Statue, enemies, gravestones, and passages that initially appear inaccessible.

Do not assume that every visible section of the map can be reached from the same side. Some routes loop through lower passages and return to the surface elsewhere.

Follow the accessible forest paths toward the underground entrances. Check the map at junctions, collect supplies along your route, and leave optional locked chests for a return visit. Some passages reconnect from the other side, so a blocked doorway does not necessarily mean you have missed the way forward.

There is a wounded monk in a small graveyard along the early exploration route. His dialogue offers different responses, and a hostile outcome leads to an undead encounter.

This is not a reason to treat the graveyard fight as a required step for reaching the village. If you choose to engage, prepare for the undead enemy and recover before moving deeper into the forest.

The early forest also contains optional items and environmental interactions. You can return to unfinished locations after finding the relevant key or tool.

<h2 id="merchant-cart">
  How Do You Help the Traveler Attacked by Rats?
</h2>

Continue exploring the forest until you encounter a traveler near a weathered cart. He is under attack by oversized rats.

The encounter begins with the traveler attempting to defend himself, and you can intervene in the fight.

Deal with the rats before speaking to him again. Once the encounter is resolved, the traveler thanks you and hands over a **small iron key** for his supply chest.

This key is connected to the traveler's belongings; it is not the Silver Key used later to open the forest exit.

After the fight, speak to the traveler and check the nearby supplies. Then continue exploring the forest for the route into the underground passages.

### Do You Need the Traveler's Key to Reach the Village?

No. The key opens the traveler's supply chest beside his cart; it does not unlock a passage on the route to Village of Underkeep.

If you leave the chest unopened, you can continue toward Hollow Grave Tunnels without returning for it.

<h2 id="hollow-grave-tunnels">
  How Do You Get Through Hollow Grave Tunnels?
</h2>

As you explore beyond the forest paths, you can descend into **Hollow Grave Tunnels**.

The area changes from outdoor exploration to enclosed underground rooms containing undead enemies, equipment, supplies, and multiple routes between levels.

The important decision is to continue searching for connected passages rather than treating every locked door as the only possible way forward.

Some doors and gates are encountered before you have access to their keys or their opposite side.

### Find the Lower Passages

Explore the available tunnel sections and inspect rooms for stairs, drops, and other transitions.

An underground route leads through areas containing undead enemies and additional loot. Before entering a deeper section, make sure your party has enough recovery supplies for several encounters.

The undead can be dangerous when several approach together. Ranged attacks can help before they reach the frontline, but they may be less affected by some status-based strategies than living enemies.

If you encounter a gate you cannot open, check nearby corridors for another route. The tunnels contain connections that become accessible only after exploring from a different direction.

### What If a Rockslide Blocks Your Path?

One tunnel passage is blocked by fallen rocks.

Trying to dig through with your bare hands does not clear the obstruction. The game indicates that some kind of tool may be needed.

**Do not treat this blocked passage as proof that your current route has ended.**

You do not need to clear this rockslide to continue toward the village. Mark its location and take the other available passages. You can return to the obstruction after finding a suitable tool.

<h2 id="underground-gate">
  How Do You Open the Gate That Requires a Thrown Object?
</h2>

In the underground sections, you encounter a mechanism that can be activated from a distance.

The game allows you to **throw a held item by releasing it over the center of the first-person view**. This matters at the gate mechanism because the target is not directly beside the party.

To operate it:

1. Locate the distant target associated with the closed gate.
2. Select an item you can throw.
3. Aim toward the target in the first-person view.
4. Release the item so it strikes the mechanism.
5. Check whether the gate rises before continuing through.

### Where Do You Go After the Gate Opens?

Continue through the accessible underground passages and look for the routes that reconnect with the surface.

There are additional obstacles, including gates and spider webs, along the way. Webs can be cut through, while other barriers may require you to approach from the opposite side.

You can also encounter areas where the party drops to a lower level and must find stairs to climb back out.

Before dropping into an unfamiliar section, check your map and make sure the party is prepared for the enemies below.

The route eventually leads toward the warehouse and surface areas. When you reach another closed gate, remember that some of these passages are shortcuts or connections between sections rather than entrances that must be opened immediately.

<h2 id="warehouse-orcs">
  How Do You Get Through the Warehouse and Orc Chieftain?
</h2>

The warehouse section contains orcs, locked containers, and passages leading between underground rooms and the surface.

The enemies here can be more demanding than the rats encountered earlier. Recover before approaching an unfamiliar group if your party is already low on Health or combat resources.

### Find the Warehouse Key

While clearing the orc-controlled area, you can obtain the **Warehouse Key**.

Use the Warehouse Key on the matching locked container in this area. It is separate from the Silver Key used to open the forest passage later in the journey.

If you encounter more than one locked chest, do not assume that every chest accepts the Warehouse Key. The area contains separate key requirements.

### Defeat the Orc Chieftain

Further along this warehouse branch, you encounter an **Orc Chieftain**.

Defeat the Orc Chieftain and collect the **Chieftain Key**. It opens the Chieftain's treasure chest; you still need the separate Silver Key to leave through the later underground gate.

You may also find other named keys while exploring the tunnels. Keep them until you identify their matching locks, and do not assume that every key belongs to the same path.

<h2 id="silver-key">
  Where Do You Use the Silver Key?
</h2>

After clearing the warehouse encounters, continue exploring until you reach a grassy area bordered by a stone wall. Open the chest here and check the loot for the **Silver Key**. Its item description reads "Probably opens a door."

The Silver Key is not used for the Chieftain's treasure chest. Return to the lower underground passages and find the locked gate with the large keyhole beside it. This is the gate you encountered earlier but could not open.

If you are backtracking through the tunnels, use the map to identify passages you have not fully explored. The route may require returning to an earlier level instead of looking for another exit in the warehouse room.

At the correct gate, using the Silver Key removes it from your inventory and opens the way forward.

Beyond the gate, the path leads upward and returns to the outdoors in a different part of the forest.

This is an important progress marker: **you have moved beyond the earlier underground route and reached the next forest section.**

### What If You Still Cannot Find the Gate?

Check these conditions:

- You have obtained the Silver Key rather than only the Warehouse Key or Chieftain Key.
- You are looking at the lower-area gate, not one of the optional treasure containers.
- You have returned to the relevant underground connection instead of searching only the surface warehouse area.
- You have checked other passages on the map rather than repeatedly trying the same locked door.

Do not spend a Lockpick on an unrelated optional chest just because you are temporarily unable to find the Silver Key's gate.

<h2 id="jacobs-cottage">
  What Should You Do at Jacob's Cottage?
</h2>

After leaving the underground area, continue through the forest until you reach a small clearing with a weathered cottage.

The cottage contains environmental clues and a letter that introduces **Jacob** and points you toward a person in the village.

Search the cottage and inspect the available objects.

A letter marked with the words **"If I am gone"** provides a warning about unusual activity beneath the area and directs you to seek **Khelebar** in the village.

Take the letter before leaving.

It is useful because it gives you a specific person to look for once you arrive at Underkeep, rather than leaving you to speak to every NPC without a clear lead.

The cottage also contains objects connected to potion preparation and the surrounding story. You can examine these while you are there, but do not assume that every object has to be solved or used before you can leave.

After exploring the cottage, continue along the forest trail.

<h2 id="garric">
  How Do You Get Past Garric and Enter Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Before reaching the village, you encounter a guard named **Garric** on a muddy forest road.

Garric explains that he is supposed to watch the approach, but he has a problem with burrowing creatures south of his position.

He offers to let you pass if you clear them out.

This is the remaining objective to complete before entering the village on this route.

### Clear the Burrowing Enemies

From Garric's position, explore the area to the south and locate the burrowing enemies.

These enemies can be awkward to fight because they move underground and may not be attackable while burrowed.

Watch for them to emerge before committing attacks that require a visible target.

Keep enough recovery resources available for several encounters. If an enemy emerges close to the party, it may be better to finish that immediate threat before attempting to reposition.

Continue clearing the creatures until the objective is satisfied.

### Return to Garric

Once the area has been cleared, return to Garric and speak to him again.

He acknowledges that the job is complete and allows you to pass toward the village.

You will know this stage is complete when Garric lets you through and the scene transitions from the forest approach to the rainy village.

If Garric still refuses to let you pass, search the southern area for remaining enemies rather than continuing to hunt for another warehouse key.

<h2 id="village">
  What Should You Do After Entering Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Once Garric allows you through, the journey from the starting forest is complete. You can now speak to the villagers, replenish supplies, and decide which region to explore next.
