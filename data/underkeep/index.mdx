<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start Underkeep by creating a **four-character party** and following **Whispers of the Underkeep** through the starting forest and underground passages. Use the Silver Key to reach the later forest route, then complete **Garric's request** to enter Village of Underkeep. In the village, speak to Mayor Aldren and Khelebar. From there, explore the surrounding regions, unlock Fast Travel by recording four Obelisks, and investigate the Cemetery and Mausoleum. The **Mausoleum Source Gate requires three Golden Keys**, while Underkeep Tower has a separate seal requiring **four Key Fragments**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Underkeep is a first-person dungeon crawler built around a four-character party, grid-based exploration, and turn-based combat. You spend much of your time moving between forest paths, underground passages, villages, and dangerous ruins.

The challenge is not only defeating enemies. You also need to recognize when a locked door belongs to your current objective, when you should return with another item, and when an unexplored route leads toward a different quest.

The opening journey gives you a clear destination: Village of Underkeep. Once you arrive, the world opens into several connected investigations. You can pursue them in different orders, but some locations have their own keys, combat encounters, or puzzle requirements.

<h2 id="starting-underkeep">
  What Should You Do First in Underkeep?
</h2>

Start by creating a four-character party. Underkeep uses two front-row positions and two back-row positions, so choose characters who can survive direct attacks alongside characters who provide ranged damage, healing, or support.

Before entering the first underground passages, check that each character has usable equipment and an active skill assigned.

You do not need a single fixed party composition to finish the opening journey. Choose a balanced group that can survive repeated encounters without exhausting its recovery supplies too quickly.

For class abilities, racial traits, equipment restrictions, and party combinations, see the <Link href="/underkeep/races-classes">Underkeep races and classes guide</Link>.

If you are unfamiliar with movement, combat turns, potion use, or Rest, the <Link href="/underkeep/beginner-guide">beginner guide</Link> explains those systems.

<h2 id="reach-village">
  How Do You Reach Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Follow **Whispers of the Underkeep** through the starting forest and the connected underground passages.

Your journey takes you through Hollow Grave Tunnels and the Orc Warehouse area before returning to the forest route leading toward the village.

The **Silver Key** is an important checkpoint because it opens a passage needed to continue toward the later forest section.

Near the village entrance, **Garric** stops your party and asks you to clear the burrowing enemies south of his position.

Defeat those enemies, then return to Garric and speak to him again to gain entry to **Village of Underkeep**.

If you cannot find the Silver Key, are blocked inside the tunnels, or cannot finish Garric's request, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/early-game-walkthrough">Underkeep early-game walkthrough</Link> for the complete route.

<h2 id="village-first-steps">
  What Should You Do First in Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Village of Underkeep becomes an important place to return between expeditions.

After spending time in the forest and tunnels, you may be short on Food, healing items, or money. You will also have access to several NPCs who provide directions toward other regions.

Three conversations are particularly useful.

### Speak to Mayor Aldren

Mayor Aldren can tell you about a missing expedition.

The adventurers were sent toward **Northern Carolyn**, but they did not return as expected.

The mayor directs you toward the western side of that region to look for the expedition's entrance.

This gives you a concrete exploration goal outside the village.

### Speak to Khelebar

Khelebar is the village alchemist.

Ask him about Underkeep Tower, the ancient Obelisks, and the stone portals scattered around the region.

He explains that the Waystone network requires a special artifact before it can be used.

To obtain that artifact, he asks you to copy the runes from four ancient Obelisks around the tower.

Completing this investigation rewards the Teleportation Stone.

### Visit the Tavern and Traders

Before leaving town, check your supplies and equipment.

Food is especially useful if you expect several difficult fights or plan to spend a long time underground.

You can also sell unwanted equipment and purchase supplies or upgrades that better suit your party.

Keep some Gold available for important interactions outside the village. Not every valuable quest item comes from defeating a monster or opening a chest.

<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  Where Should You Explore After the Village?
</h2>

Your next destination depends on what you want to accomplish. To make repeated journeys less tedious, take Khelebar's request and <Link href="/underkeep/fast-travel">record the four Obelisks</Link>. Mayor Aldren's missing expedition leads toward Northern Carolyn, while the search for Jacob takes you south to Underkeep Cemetery and the <Link href="/underkeep/mausoleum-walkthrough">Mausoleum</Link>.

Fangroot Forest offers another option if you would rather explore caves and side encounters first. These investigations do not all have to be completed in one fixed order, so you can return to the village and change destinations when an enemy, locked passage, or missing item stops your progress.

<h2 id="northern-expedition">
  Where Do You Find the Missing Northern Expedition?
</h2>

Mayor Aldren sends you toward **Northern Carolyn** after an expedition fails to return. His clue points to an entrance on the western side of the region, so concentrate your search on those approaches rather than wandering through every available passage.

If a western path appears blocked, examine the surrounding connections before abandoning the search. You can leave the investigation unfinished and resume it after exploring another region.

<h2 id="dwarven-camp">
  How Do You Learn Mining and Clear Hard Cave-ins in Underkeep?
</h2>

At the **Dwarven Camp**, find **Brenna Ironsong** and ask her about dwarven craft.

Brenna offers two skills that make exploring caves and underground passages easier.

- **Clear Hard Cave-ins — 30 Gold:** Learn how to break through hard rock obstructions. Brenna also gives you a heavy Pickaxe.
- **Mine Gold Veins — 40 Gold:** Learn how to extract gold from mineral deposits. Brenna provides a special dwarven Chisel for the job.

<h2 id="underkeep-tower">
  How Do You Open the Sealed Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

Underkeep Tower is connected to an ancient protective seal.

However, the tower's main entrance is not opened by the Teleportation Stone, and it does not use the three Golden Keys from the Mausoleum.

The tower has a separate requirement involving **four Key Fragments**.

You must collect the fragments associated with the tower's seal before the entrance can be opened.

### Where Can You Get a Tower Key Fragment?

One documented opportunity comes from **Torin**, a roaming adventurer encountered near Underkeep Tower.

Torin has found part of an ancient key and offers to sell it.


He asks for **300 Gold**.

### Why Is the Tower Gate Still Sealed?

Placing one fragment is not enough.

The gate requires **four Key Fragments**, each contributing to the larger seal.

If you have inserted only one or two fragments, continue exploring other objectives and searching for the remaining pieces.

Do not confuse the tower's four-fragment requirement with the Mausoleum Source Gate.

They are different locations with different keys.

<h2 id="mausoleum-progress">
  How Do You Progress Through the Mausoleum?
</h2>

Follow the investigation into **Jacob's disappearance** toward Underkeep Cemetery and the connected Mausoleum.

Inside, you can rescue Jacob, investigate the Blood Order, and uncover the route leading to the Source.

Opening the **Source Gate** requires three Golden Keys obtained from the High Clerics. However, entering the gate is not the end of **Heart of the Source**.

Beyond the gate, you must defeat the Source Guardians and decide what happens to the Source.

Destroying the Source allows you to report the result to Khelebar in Village of Underkeep. The separate seal at Underkeep Tower remains another objective.

For the three High Clerics, Ixarion, Jacob's rescue, fire puzzles, and both Source choices, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/mausoleum-walkthrough">Underkeep Mausoleum walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="fangroot-forest">
  What Can You Find in Fangroot Forest?
</h2>

Fangroot Forest contains additional exploration routes and side encounters.

The surrounding caves include a **Rat Nest** and a **Spider Cave**, each with its own dangerous enemies.

The Rat Nest leads to a Rat Queen encounter, while the Spider Cave contains the spider boss Arachneira.

These are opportunities to explore beyond the immediate objectives associated with Village of Underkeep.

Before entering either cave, restore your party and make sure you have enough recovery supplies for the enemies inside.

### Should You Return the Mushroomlings to Their Kin?

Another optional quest involves **Barlo** and five missing Mushroomlings.

You can collect the Mushroomlings and decide what to do with them.

One available choice is to return them to the Mushroom Kin in the forest rather than hand them back to Barlo.

Returning them to their kin triggers an encounter that grants **Mushroom Bond**.

However, the related Barlo quest can subsequently be marked as failed because you chose to return the Mushroomlings to their kin instead of letting Barlo keep them.

This is an important distinction if you are trying to complete every quest without a failure entry.

The two outcomes are not treated as identical by the quest log.

Decide which result you want before committing to the return dialogue.

<h2 id="what-next">
  What Should You Do After Heart of the Source?
</h2>

Once you have dealt with the Source, report the outcome to Khelebar and review the objectives still open in your quest log. The Source Gate and Underkeep Tower are separate obstacles: completing Heart of the Source does not supply the four Key Fragments needed for the tower entrance.

You can continue searching for those fragments, pursue the missing expedition in Northern Carolyn, or visit the Dwarven Camp to learn how to clear hard cave-ins and mine gold. Fangroot Forest and its surrounding caves also remain available if you have unfinished encounters there.

Choose a destination according to your remaining quest objectives and the equipment or tools you have acquired. A passage that stopped you earlier may now be worth revisiting.
