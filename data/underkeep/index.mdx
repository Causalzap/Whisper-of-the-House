<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Start Underkeep with a **four-character party**, follow **Whispers of the Underkeep** through the forest and tunnels, find the **Silver Key**, and complete **Garric's request** to enter Village of Underkeep. Speak to Mayor Aldren and Khelebar to begin exploring the surrounding regions. You can unlock Fast Travel by recording four Obelisks, investigate the Mausoleum and its three Golden Keys, and complete the Dwarven Expedition leading to the **Soulforge**. To enter **Underkeep Tower**, collect four separate **Key Fragments**. Inside the tower, overcome the trials, defeat the **Architect**, then confront **Torin** beneath the tower and enter the red portal to seal the gate.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Underkeep combines first-person grid exploration with turn-based combat for a four-character party. Your first destination is Village of Underkeep, reached by following the forest and underground route. After arriving, you can investigate the Cemetery, travel toward the Dwarven Expedition, explore surrounding forests, or begin restoring the Waystone network.

The larger story leads toward Underkeep Tower, but its entrance requires four Key Fragments rather than the Golden Keys found in the Mausoleum or the Blue Key Spheres used in the Soulforge. If a route is blocked, check which quest or item belongs to that obstacle before leaving to search another area.

<h2 id="starting-underkeep">
  What Should You Do First in Underkeep?
</h2>

Start by creating a four-character party. Underkeep uses two front-row positions and two back-row positions, so choose characters who can survive direct attacks alongside characters who provide ranged damage, healing, or support.

Before entering the first underground passages, check that each character has usable equipment and an active skill assigned.

You do not need a single fixed party composition to finish the opening journey. Choose a balanced group that can survive repeated encounters without exhausting its recovery supplies too quickly.

For class abilities, racial traits, equipment restrictions, and party combinations, see the <Link href="/underkeep/races-classes">Underkeep races and classes guide</Link>.

If you are unfamiliar with movement, combat turns, potion use, or Rest, the <Link href="/underkeep/beginner-guide">beginner guide</Link> explains those systems.

<h2 id="reach-village">
  How Do You Reach Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Follow **Whispers of the Underkeep** through the starting forest and the connected underground passages.

Your journey takes you through Hollow Grave Tunnels and the Orc Warehouse area before returning to the forest route leading toward the village.

The **Silver Key** is an important checkpoint because it opens a passage needed to continue toward the later forest section.

Near the village entrance, **Garric** stops your party and asks you to clear the burrowing enemies south of his position.

Defeat those enemies, then return to Garric and speak to him again to gain entry to **Village of Underkeep**.

If you cannot find the Silver Key, are blocked inside the tunnels, or cannot finish Garric's request, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/early-game-walkthrough">Underkeep early-game walkthrough</Link> for the complete route.

<h2 id="village-first-steps">
  What Should You Do First in Village of Underkeep?
</h2>

Village of Underkeep is where you can replenish supplies and collect leads for exploring the surrounding regions. Speak to Mayor Aldren and Khelebar before setting out again, then visit the traders if your party needs Food, healing items, or equipment.

### Speak to Mayor Aldren

Mayor Aldren tells you about an expedition that has not returned from **Northern Carolyn**. His information points toward an entrance on the western side of that region. Take the assignment if you want to investigate the missing adventurers.

### Speak to Khelebar

Khelebar, the village alchemist, explains the ancient Obelisks and the inactive Waystone network. He asks you to copy rune inscriptions from four Obelisks and bring the records back to him.

Completing **Wards of the West** rewards the **Teleportation Stone**, allowing you to use the Waystones for travel between discovered locations.

### Visit the Tavern and Traders

Before heading into a long dungeon, check your healing supplies and Food. Sell equipment your party no longer needs and keep some Gold available for purchases or quest interactions outside the village.

<h2 id="where-to-go-next">
  Where Should You Explore After the Village?
</h2>

Your next destination depends on what you want to accomplish. To make repeated journeys less tedious, take Khelebar's request and <Link href="/underkeep/fast-travel">record the four Obelisks</Link>. Mayor Aldren's missing expedition leads toward Northern Carolyn, while the search for Jacob takes you south to Underkeep Cemetery and the <Link href="/underkeep/mausoleum-walkthrough">Mausoleum</Link>.

Fangroot Forest offers another option if you would rather explore caves and side encounters first. These investigations do not all have to be completed in one fixed order, so you can return to the village and change destinations when an enemy, locked passage, or missing item stops your progress.

<h2 id="northern-expedition">
  Where Do You Find the Missing Northern Expedition?
</h2>

Mayor Aldren sends you toward **Northern Carolyn** after an expedition fails to return. His clue points to an entrance on the western side of the region, so concentrate your search on those approaches rather than wandering through every available passage.

If a western path appears blocked, examine the surrounding connections before abandoning the search. You can leave the investigation unfinished and resume it after exploring another region.

<h2 id="dwarven-camp">
  How Do You Learn Mining and Clear Hard Cave-ins in Underkeep?
</h2>

At the **Dwarven Camp**, find **Brenna Ironsong** and ask her about dwarven craft.

Brenna offers two skills that make exploring caves and underground passages easier.

- **Clear Hard Cave-ins — 30 Gold:** Learn how to break through hard rock obstructions. Brenna also gives you a heavy Pickaxe.
- **Mine Gold Veins — 40 Gold:** Learn how to extract gold from mineral deposits. Brenna provides a special dwarven Chisel for the job.

These skills are useful when exploring areas containing blocked cave passages or valuable mineral deposits.

If you previously encountered an obstruction that could not be cleared, return after receiving the appropriate training and tool.

Brenna's two training options are separate purchases. Clearing Hard Cave-ins is useful when you return to blocked passages, while mining training lets you collect Gold from mineral deposits.

The Dwarven Expedition also leads into the **Soulforge**. After resolving Captain Korr's dragon expedition, you can enter the connected ruins, collect eight **Blue Key Spheres** from East Garden and West Garden, and reach the ancient forge. <h2 id="underkeep-tower">
  How Do You Open the Sealed Underkeep Tower?
</h2>

Underkeep Tower is sealed by a mechanism requiring **four Key Fragments**. You can insert the fragments as you find them, but the gate will remain closed until the fourth is placed. The Teleportation Stone and the Mausoleum's Golden Keys cannot open this entrance.

### Where Can You Get a Tower Key Fragment?

One documented opportunity comes from **Torin**, a roaming adventurer encountered near Underkeep Tower.

Torin has found part of an ancient key and offers to sell it.


He asks for **300 Gold**.

Using a Key Fragment at the tower entrance causes the ancient seal to respond. If the gate remains locked, you still need to obtain and place the remaining fragments.

### What Happens After You Insert All Four Key Fragments?

The first three fragments activate parts of the ancient mechanism without opening the entrance. The **fourth Key Fragment** completes the seal and opens Underkeep Tower.

Inside, a magical wizard's apparition introduces a series of trials. You must get through the floor-switch puzzle, expose the Shadow Beast to light, and solve the Gallery's statue and colored-rune mechanisms before confronting the **Architect**.

Defeating the Architect opens a passage beneath the tower's ground floor. Follow it to meet **Torin** beside the red portal. The final confrontation ends when Torin is pulled into the portal, after which your party must enter the gate and seal it from within.

For the tower puzzles, Architect fight, Torin, and the ending, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/tower-walkthrough">Underkeep Tower walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="mausoleum-progress">
  How Do You Progress Through the Mausoleum?
</h2>

Follow the investigation into **Jacob's disappearance** toward Underkeep Cemetery and the connected Mausoleum.

Inside, you can rescue Jacob, investigate the Blood Order, and uncover the route leading to the Source.

Opening the **Source Gate** requires three Golden Keys obtained from the High Clerics. However, entering the gate is not the end of **Heart of the Source**.

Beyond the gate, you must defeat the Source Guardians and decide what happens to the Source.

Destroying the Source allows you to report the result to Khelebar in Village of Underkeep. The separate seal at Underkeep Tower remains another objective.

For the three High Clerics, Ixarion, Jacob's rescue, fire puzzles, and both Source choices, follow the <Link href="/underkeep/mausoleum-walkthrough">Underkeep Mausoleum walkthrough</Link>.

<h2 id="fangroot-forest">
  What Can You Find in Fangroot Forest?
</h2>

Fangroot Forest contains several side encounters beyond the village's main objectives. The **Rat Nest** leads to the **Rat Queen**, while the **Spider Cave** contains **Arachneira**. Both are separate exploration challenges you can return to when your party is prepared.

### Should You Return the Mushroomlings to Their Kin?

Barlo's quest involves finding **five missing Mushroomlings**. After collecting them, you can return them to Barlo or choose to bring them back to the Mushroom Kin.

Returning the Mushroomlings to their kin triggers an encounter that grants **Mushroom Bond**. However, Barlo's related quest can then be marked as failed because you did not return them to him.

Check which outcome you want before committing to the final dialogue. Gaining Mushroom Bond and completing Barlo's quest are not treated as the same result.

<h2 id="what-next">
  What Should You Do After Heart of the Source?
</h2>

After resolving the Source's fate, return to Khelebar in Village of Underkeep and report the result. The Source's three Golden Keys do not unlock Underkeep Tower, so check how many of the tower's four Key Fragments you have already collected.

If you still have unfinished regional objectives, you can continue exploring Northern Carolyn, Fangroot Forest, or the dwarven ruins. Completing the <Link href="/underkeep/soulforge-walkthrough">Soulforge expedition</Link> provides Soulcleaver, which the Architect can recognize during the later confrontation.

Once all four Key Fragments are ready, open Underkeep Tower and continue through its trials. The Architect is not the final enemy: after defeating him, find the underground passage leading to Torin and the red portal. The <Link href="/underkeep/tower-walkthrough">Tower walkthrough</Link> covers the final battles and ending.
