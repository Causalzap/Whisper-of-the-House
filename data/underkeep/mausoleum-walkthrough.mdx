<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
Defeat **Maltherion, Zerethic, and Thalric** to collect the three Golden Keys, then use them to open the **Source Gate** in the deeper Mausoleum. Beyond the gate, defeat the **Source Guardians** and choose **End it** to destroy the Source. Choosing **Let it live** leaves Heart of the Source marked as failed. You can also rescue Jacob and bind Ixarion while exploring the dungeon.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

The Mausoleum is one of the larger dungeon areas connected to Underkeep Cemetery. Its passages lead through undead encounters, a summoning altar, prison cells, puzzles, and three powerful enemies carrying the keys to the Source Gate.

Finding a locked door here does not necessarily mean you are missing a key from somewhere outside the dungeon. Several routes connect to different chambers, and some objectives require you to return to a location after discovering something elsewhere.

The clearest way to approach the area is to keep track of three separate goals: find Jacob, investigate the summoning altar, and collect the Golden Keys needed for the deeper gate.

<h2 id="mausoleum-location">
  How Do You Reach the Mausoleum in Underkeep?
</h2>

Start in **Village of Underkeep** and investigate the disappearance of Jacob.

Speak to **Khelebar**, the village alchemist, about Jacob. Khelebar explains that Jacob was investigating hooded figures associated with the Blood Order near **Underkeep Cemetery**, south of the village.

The conversation provides another useful lead: Jacob had been searching the area because he believed something dangerous was happening beneath the graves.

Head toward the cemetery and explore its paths, tombs, and connected passages. The area contains undead enemies, unusual gravestones, and entrances that may not be obvious from the main route.

Look for the passage identified as **Mausoleum Entrance**.

Once inside, follow the available corridors and check side rooms instead of assuming that every locked door requires an item from the village.

### What Should You Bring Before Entering?

The three High Clerics pose different threats. **Maltherion** can Freeze party members, **Zerethic** inflicts Burn, and **Thalric** can cause Paralysis.

Bring healing supplies and any items you have that can remove those conditions. The Mausoleum also contains optional locked chests, so carrying a Lockpick can be useful if you intend to explore its side rooms.

<h2 id="summoning-book">
  What Should You Do With the Summoning Book?
</h2>

Near the entrance, you can interact with a **summoning book at an altar**. This advances the quest **Probably a Bad Idea**.

The important detail is that encountering the book does not immediately give you everything needed to finish the summoning.

Continue exploring the Mausoleum and search the rooms containing undead enemies and treasure chests.

One chest contains a **Scroll of Binding** that identifies a demon named **Ixarion**.

If you find the scroll after moving deeper into the dungeon, you do not need to abandon the rest of the area. Remember where the altar is and return when you are prepared.

<h2 id="skeleton-king">
  How Do You Defeat the Skeleton King?
</h2>

The Mausoleum's earlier chambers include a skull-themed locked area and an encounter with the **Skeleton King**.

This fight deserves more preparation than the ordinary skeleton encounters leading up to it.

Before approaching the chamber, recover your party and make sure important healing abilities are available.

### Skeleton King Fight

Enter the Skeleton King encounter with your party healed and your main combat abilities ready.

Keep your frontline alive while your damage dealers focus on the King. Reserve an emergency healing or resurrection item for a character who falls during the fight.

**Check fallen party members before delivering the final blow.** A champion who remains dead when the boss is defeated may miss the experience from that victory.

If someone falls near the end of combat, consider reviving them before finishing the King rather than losing the experience reward.

### What Does the Skeleton King Drop?

Defeating the Skeleton King provides the **Kingbreaker Blade**.

Collect it before leaving the chamber, and check its equipment requirements before deciding who should use it.

This weapon is a separate reward from the three Golden Keys. Defeating the Skeleton King does not, by itself, provide the key needed to open the Source Gate.

Search nearby rooms for any remaining loot and unfinished passages before moving on.

<h2 id="ixarion">
  How Do You Summon Ixarion in the Mausoleum?
</h2>

After finding the **Scroll of Binding**, return to the summoning altar associated with **Probably a Bad Idea**.

The scroll supplies the name **Ixarion**, which is used to initiate the encounter with **Ixarion the Bloodbound Herald**.

Do not treat this as an ordinary dialogue interaction. Be prepared for combat when completing the summoning.

### Fight Ixarion

Restore your party before activating the altar.

Use your available attacks and healing abilities to survive the encounter. Avoid committing every limited-use resource before you understand the threat.

Once Ixarion has been defeated, the encounter concludes with the demon being **bound**.

This is the important completion result for the summoning objective.

After binding Ixarion, the demon can assist your party in combat inside the Mausoleum, attacking enemies automatically at the end of your turns.

Ixarion is not a permanent fifth party member. Its binding is tied to the Mausoleum, so do not rely on keeping its assistance after leaving the dungeon.

If you have obtained the Scroll of Binding but cannot finish the summoning immediately, return to the book altar and check the available interaction again.

<h2 id="golden-keys">
  Where Are the Three Golden Keys in Underkeep?
</h2>

The deeper Mausoleum contains a large gate secured by three Golden Keys.

Each key is obtained by defeating a named High Cleric.

**You need all three keys for the Source Gate.** Completing only one or two of these boss encounters is not enough to open it.

The following sections explain how to identify the relevant encounters and what to do after each victory.

<h2 id="maltherion">
  How Do You Get the Golden Key of Maltherion?
</h2>

**Maltherion the Sleepless Watcher** is one of the High Clerics encountered in the Mausoleum.

The fight occurs after progressing through the summoning-related section of the dungeon.

Before engaging Maltherion, check whether your party is still carrying injuries or depleted combat resources from earlier encounters.

### Maltherion Fight

Maltherion uses freezing attacks that can disrupt your party during the fight. Bring a way to remove Freeze before entering his chamber.

Clear Freeze from affected characters when it prevents them from acting effectively, then resume your attacks. Keep healing available for the damage sustained while your party is disabled.

After defeating Maltherion, collect the **Golden Key of Maltherion** and inspect the **Staff of Ice Crystals**.

### What Should You Do After Maltherion?

Search the back room and adjoining areas before moving on.

A chest in this section contains the **Belt of Bull**.

The adjacent passages lead toward further cells and dungeon encounters. You do not need to return to the Source Gate immediately: one Golden Key cannot open it alone.

Continue exploring until you locate the remaining High Cleric routes.

<h2 id="brazier-puzzle">
  How Do You Get Past the Fire Puzzles in the Mausoleum?
</h2>

The Mausoleum contains several fire-related obstacles. They do not all work the same way.

### The False Flame Riddle

One inscription begins:

> One flame lies, the path it bars, its warmth is false like painted scars.

The clue distinguishes a false flame from a true flame that must remain lit.

Do not assume that lighting or extinguishing every brazier will solve this riddle. Examine the nearby flames and their interactions before changing the mechanism.

### Cross the Hall Without Touching the Flames

Another passage warns:

> Let no flame burn your soul.

To pass this section, reach the door across the hall **without touching the fire lamps** along the way.

Avoid contact with the flames as you move toward the opposite doorway. The game confirms that the puzzle is solved when you successfully cross the area.

### Reveal the Hidden Switch

Farther into the Mausoleum, a fire conceals a switch near a doorway.

Extinguish the obstructing flame and examine the area behind it. Once the switch is visible, use it to continue through the passage.

The green-flamed braziers in Thalric's chamber are part of his boss encounter, not the earlier corridor puzzles. Handle them when you reach Thalric.

<h2 id="jacob-rescue">
  Where Do You Find and Rescue Jacob?
</h2>

Jacob's disappearance is one of the reasons you were directed toward Underkeep Cemetery.

His investigation took him into the Blood Order's activities and eventually into the dungeon beneath the cemetery.

You can find evidence of his movements before reaching him, including a letter explaining that he intended to infiltrate a gathering connected to the Order.

The letter advances **Shadows of the South** and directs you to continue searching below the cemetery.

### Find Jacob in the Cells

Continue exploring the Mausoleum's prison area.

Jacob is found **chained in the dungeon**. Interact with him to resolve his captivity and continue the related conversation.

Jacob's rescue is an important story checkpoint, but it is not a substitute for obtaining the three Golden Keys.

After freeing him, continue exploring the remaining Mausoleum passages.

<h2 id="zerethic">
  How Do You Get the Golden Key of Zerethic?
</h2>

**Zerethic the Heart Eater** is one of the three High Clerics guarding the route to the Source Gate.

  </figcaption>

Before entering this encounter, confirm that your party has recovered from previous fights.

The dungeon contains several difficult enemies in relatively close succession, so running out of recovery supplies can become a bigger problem than a single boss attack.

### Zerethic Fight

Prepare Burn-removal potions before entering Zerethic's chamber. The door closes behind you, and the boss can attack with fire while your party is still approaching.

Zerethic also regenerates Health during combat. Minor damage-over-time effects alone may not be enough to overcome that recovery.

Remove Burn when necessary, close the distance, and concentrate your available damage to defeat him before regeneration prolongs the fight.

After winning, collect the **Golden Key of Zerethic** and the **Staff of Fireball**.

<h2 id="forgotten-cells">
  How Do You Get Through the Cells of the Forgotten?
</h2>

The deeper prison area contains the **Cells of the Forgotten**, where a **Blood Golem** appears among the hostile encounters.

The Blood Golem is separate from the three High Clerics, so defeating it does not provide one of the Golden Keys. After the encounter, search the nearby cells and passages before continuing toward Thalric's chamber.

<h2 id="thalric">
  How Do You Get the Golden Key of Thalric?
</h2>

**Thalric the Undying Flame** is another named High Cleric in the deeper Mausoleum.

Defeating Thalric rewards the **Golden Key of Thalric**, one of the three keys required for the Source Gate.

Prepare for the fight by restoring injured characters and checking your available healing and recovery items.

### Thalric Fight

Thalric is protected by a ritual involving the four green-flamed braziers in his chamber.

You cannot damage him normally while that protection remains active. Deal with the brazier mechanisms first, then attack Thalric once he becomes vulnerable.

Thalric can also inflict Paralysis, so keep recovery options available if your party becomes unable to move or act.

After defeating him, collect the **Golden Key of Thalric** and inspect the **Staff of the Archmage**.

- **Golden Key of Maltherion**
- **Golden Key of Zerethic**
- **Golden Key of Thalric**

If one is missing, return to the corresponding High Cleric's chamber and check whether you collected the key after the fight.

Once all three keys are in your inventory, return to the large skull-shaped gate in the deeper Mausoleum.


<h2 id="source-gate">
  How Do You Open the Source Gate With Three Golden Keys?
</h2>

Return to the **large triple-locked gate** in the deeper Mausoleum.

    Find the skull-shaped gate with three golden locks. The keys from Maltherion, Thalric, and Zerethic are required to proceed.
  </figcaption>

This is the gate connected to the Source. It requires the three Golden Keys from Maltherion, Thalric, and Zerethic.

### Open the Three Locks

1. Return to the large skull-shaped gate in the deeper Mausoleum.
2. Make sure you have the **Golden Key of Maltherion**, **Golden Key of Zerethic**, and **Golden Key of Thalric**.
3. Interact with the gate and use the three Golden Keys to clear its locks.
4. Once the gate opens, enter the passage leading into the **Source**.

### What If the Source Gate Will Not Open?

First, check your inventory for all three named Golden Keys.

A key from another region, an ordinary chest, or the earlier Skeleton King encounter is not a substitute.

If you have defeated only two High Clerics, return to the unexplored branch containing the remaining encounter.

If all three High Clerics have been defeated, check whether their key rewards were actually collected.

The gate requires the named Golden Keys rather than a Lockpick or a generic dungeon key.

<h2 id="after-source-gate">
  What Happens After You Enter the Source in Underkeep?
</h2>

After opening the three-key gate, continue into the deeper chamber containing the **Source**.

Save your game before approaching the center of the room. This area contains another major encounter, followed by a decision that affects the outcome of **Heart of the Source**.

### How Do You Defeat the Source Guardians?

Inside the chamber, a mass of twisted roots and flesh rises from the crimson floor. As you approach, several **Source Guardians** emerge to defend it.

The encounter prevents you from simply walking past the enemies and interacting with the Source.

Focus on defeating the Guardians while keeping your party alive. They approach from different positions, so watch which characters are being threatened before ending each combat round.

Keep enough healing available for the later stages of the encounter.

Once the Guardians have been defeated, the Source becomes accessible.

### Should You End the Source or Let It Live?

After the Guardians fall, the Source reveals its suffering and presents two choices:

- **End it.**
- **Let it live.**

**Choose "End it"** to destroy the Source. The chamber shakes as the twisted roots collapse into the crimson floor, ending the suffering at its center.

**Choose "Let it live"** to spare the Source. The Source remains intact, but **Heart of the Source** is marked as failed.

If you want to complete the objective by destroying the Source, choose **End it** before leaving the chamber.

### What Should You Do After Destroying the Source?

Return to **Village of Underkeep** and speak to **Khelebar**.

Tell him that the Source has been destroyed. He explains that the corruption did not arise on its own and points toward **Underkeep Tower** as an important next lead.

If you have already rescued Jacob, you can also return to him near his cottage.

Jacob thanks you for rescuing him and offers **Jacob's Longbow**. After you confirm that the Source has been dealt with, he also gives you a family amulet.

These rewards are separate from the three Golden Keys used to enter the Source chamber.

Opening the Source Gate and resolving Heart of the Source do not automatically remove the separate seal at Underkeep Tower.
