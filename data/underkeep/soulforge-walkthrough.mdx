<GuideQuickAnswer title="Quick Answer">
To reach the **Soulforge in Underkeep**, join Captain Korr's expedition in **Duskhollow Woods**. Solve the five-symbol puzzle in the order **Flame → Crown → Eye → Tree → Serpent**, then find the hidden switch in the looping corridor. To spare the dragon, persuade Korr to destroy the supporting pillars instead. Report the outcome to **Durok** to enter the ruins, collect all **eight Blue Key Spheres** — four from East Garden and four from West Garden — and open the eight-socket door. Trap the Giant in the Incinerator, reach the ancient anvil, and return to Durok to receive **Soulcleaver, Axe of the Deep Forge**.
</GuideQuickAnswer>

Captain Korr plans to destroy the dragon beneath the mountain, but Durok warns that killing it could endanger the ancient Soulforge. If you want the dragon to survive, challenge Korr's plan during the expedition and convince him to target the chamber's supporting pillars.

Beyond the dragon's lair, the Soulforge ruins contain two gardens, an eight-socket gate, and a furnace chamber. The gate requires **eight Blue Key Spheres**. A separate **Garden Key** opens the entrance to West Garden, while ordinary iron keys are used farther below. None of these items replaces the four Key Fragments needed at Underkeep Tower.

<h2 id="dwarven-expedition">
  How Do You Find the Dwarven Expedition and Start the Dragon Quest?
</h2>

Follow Mayor Aldren's lead toward the missing expedition and search the southern part of **Duskhollow Woods**. The middle of the region is Dragon Territory, where fire strikes the ground. Avoid tiles already burning and wait for the flames ahead to die down before crossing. You can reach the dwarven camp without standing in the fire.

A guard challenges you at the wooden encampment. Tell him you are looking for the expedition and identify **Durok**, the dwarven priest, if you learned his name in the village. Paying the guard **10 Gold** is another way to gain entry if you cannot answer his question.

Inside, speak to both **Captain Korr** and **Durok** before leaving on the expedition. Korr describes a plan to bring down the dragon's den with explosives. Durok asks you to avoid killing the creature because its fate may be tied to the Soulforge. Agree to help Korr, then tell him you are ready to depart when you have finished preparing.

Brenna Ironsong's Hard Cave-in training provides a **Pickaxe**. Keep one available, since an offering puzzle deeper inside the Soulforge requires it. For Brenna's training options and costs, see the <Link href="/underkeep">Underkeep progression guide</Link>.

<h2 id="sanctum-symbols">
  What Is the Five-Symbol Order in the Sanctum of the Last Flame?
</h2>

Korr leads you into an old mine passage, but the direct route is blocked. Follow the alternative passage until you reach a hall with five rune-marked floor platforms and a sealed stone door.

The inscription gives relative positions for the Flame, Crown, Eye, Tree, and Serpent. **Step on the five platforms in this order:**

1. **Flame**
2. **Crown**
3. **Eye**
4. **Tree** (the leaf symbol)
5. **Serpent**

The door opens after the complete sequence. If it does not, look at the symbol on each platform rather than choosing the nearest one. The order comes from the inscription's instruction to begin with the lowest symbol and finish with the highest.

### Why Does the Corridor Keep Sending You Back?

Past the symbol room, the passage appears to loop. Walking forward returns you to a familiar cracked pillar, even though you have not turned around. Korr points out that a hidden switch may be controlling the illusion.

Backtrack slightly within the repeating passage and inspect the **right-hand wall** for a small, rock-shaped switch. Press it, then walk forward again. This removes the loop and opens the approach to the dragon's den.

<h2 id="spare-dragon">
  How Do You Spare the Dragon During Korr's Assault?
</h2>

Korr stops at several points to prepare explosive charges. When he discusses killing the dragon, choose the responses arguing that the creature should be spared.

Maintain that position through his objections. Near the final door, insist that the group bring down the chamber's supporting pillars without killing the dragon.

Korr eventually agrees to attack the **pillars** rather than kill the dragon. Enter the lair with the dwarves, survive the encounter, and allow the group to bring down the supporting pillars. Fire and Burn are major threats during this battle, so keep healing and a way to handle Burn available.

When the last supports fall, the chamber begins collapsing and the dragon **escapes alive**. Search the accessible loot after the battle, including **Moonsteel Ore**, then return to the dwarven camp.

Speak to **Durok** and report that the dragon survived. He allows you into the ruins beneath the camp and gives you **Durok's Promise**, a mace, in recognition of the decision. You can also bring Moonsteel Ore to **Brenna**, who offers a choice of dwarven equipment. After these conversations, take the newly available passage down into the Soulforge ruins.

<h2 id="soulforge-entry">
  How Do You Get Past the Colored Seals and Find the Eight-Key Door?
</h2>

The first underground rooms contain **Acid Oozes**. These split into smaller creatures when defeated, so leave room to deal with the additional enemies before moving through narrow passages.

### Seal of Yellow and Seal of Purple

One chamber contains colored flames and two seals. Match the flame combinations to the inscriptions:

- **Seal of Yellow:** activate **Red + Green**.
- **Seal of Purple:** activate **Red + Blue**.

If you have opened the chest but still cannot advance, return to the other corridor instead of repeatedly adjusting the flames.

The main passage leads to a massive door with **eight circular sockets**. You cannot open it with a Garden Key, an iron key, or the Key Fragments from Underkeep Tower. All eight matching blue keys come from the nearby garden challenges.

<h2 id="eight-garden-keys">
  Where Are All Eight Soulforge Keys?
</h2>

The eight-socket door requires **eight Blue Key Spheres**: four from East Garden and four from West Garden. Each garden contains four separate challenges, and you can complete them in different orders.

Collect every sphere before returning to the gate. The ordinary Garden Key used to enter West Garden does not count toward these eight sockets.

<h2 id="east-garden">
  How Do You Collect the Four Keys in East Garden?
</h2>

East Garden lies beyond the first part of the Soulforge ruins. The local enemies include Fangspires, and stone gargoyles near some challenges offer hints in exchange for **10 Gold**. The hint payment is optional; it is not the price of the key.

### Northwest: Three Offerings

Find the wall with three recesses and read the descriptions of the required offerings. Match the objects to their meanings:

- **Bread** goes with the food made by fire and human hands.
- **Moonpetal** goes with the offering associated with the moon and life without sunlight.
- **Potion of Minor Healing** goes with the small vial of red medicine.

Place the three items in their matching recesses and collect the key that appears on the nearby altar. Ordinary Ham, stew, or other food does not substitute for **Bread**. If your party has eaten your last Bread while Resting, buy another before returning to the puzzle.

### Southwest: Three Fountains

Explore the fountain area and deal with its enemies. This challenge does not require a complicated sequence of lever pulls: find the altar and take its key once you can reach it safely.

### Northeast: Test of Courage

Approach the altar in the statue-filled area to trigger the fight. The statues guarding it are hostile. Defeat them, then return to the altar for the key. Walking up to the reward before clearing the encounter is not enough.

### Southeast: Frog King and Mushroom Creatures

Continue to the mushroom-filled section and find the **Frog King**. The creature attempts to influence your party. Refusing its demand can turn the surrounding mushroom creatures against you as well, creating a fight from multiple directions.

After resolving the Frog King encounter, check the nearby reward location and collect the **Blue Key Sphere**. If the surrounding mushroom creatures become hostile, deal with them before returning to the reward.

<h2 id="west-garden-access">
  How Do You Get the Garden Key and Reach West Garden?
</h2>

The route from East Garden to West Garden passes back through the **Outer Halls**. One locked entrance requires an ordinary **Garden Key**, which is separate from the eight blue keys.

Look for a room with **four braziers and a seasons inscription**. The clue describes spring growth, summer sun, autumn leaves, and winter frost. Light the braziers in the sequence **Green → Red → Orange → Blue** to receive the **Garden Key**.

Use that key on the corresponding West Garden entrance. A separate **Blind Corridor** also connects toward this area, but stepping off its intended route sends you back to the entrance. The Garden Key route avoids having to guess your way through that corridor.

There is also an optional room with a skeleton and four floor plates. Activate the mechanism to release the skeleton, then lead it across the plates to open the nearby chest route. This is not one of the eight garden-key challenges.

### Northwest: Five Statues and Pressure Plates

This room has **five movable statues** and five target plates. A tree obstructs the most obvious push toward the central plate, and moving a statue carelessly can block another one.

Work from the **sides** rather than pushing every statue straight ahead. Move an outer statue far enough to create maneuvering space, then push the next statue into the center lane before finishing the remaining plates. The game confirms success when the last statue reaches its plate, after which you can collect the key.

### Northeast: The Hidden Statue

The clue warns that not every concealed threat is alive. Search **behind the vegetation** for a statue that looks like part of the scenery. Attack it **from a distance** to expose the reward, then take the key from the altar. Searching only for a floor switch will not finish this challenge.

### Southeast: Copy the Statue Pattern From East Garden

This puzzle asks you to arrange seven statues according to the corresponding corner of the **other garden**. Return to East Garden and inspect that arrangement before moving the West Garden statues.

Arrange the seven statues in West Garden as a **mirror image** of the corresponding arrangement in East Garden. Compare the positions carefully before moving the final statue. Once the mirrored pattern is correct, the pedestal activates and produces a Blue Key Sphere.

### Southwest: Five Weighted Pressure Plates

Five plates sit beyond gaps in the floor, beyond easy walking distance. **Throw objects** onto the plates instead of trying to cross first. The room's clue emphasizes the **weight** of the objects rather than their value.

Use light disposable items for distant plates and adjust the weight used on the others when a plate does not respond. Bones, mushrooms, and a Pickaxe are examples of items tested during the route; the important check is whether the individual plate activates. When all five have been satisfied, the gaps close and the pedestal produces the key.

Once you have four Blue Key Spheres from each garden, return to the eight-socket door. Insert all eight spheres to open the passage into the **Soulforge Depths**.

<h2 id="soulforge-depths">
  How Do You Solve the Puzzles Beyond the Eight-Key Door?
</h2>

Opening the garden gate does not take you straight to the ancient anvil. The depths contain another series of barriers, including item offerings, hidden buttons, locked chests, and a torch-clock puzzle.

### Which Four Items Open the Offering Door?

In the room with four item clues, place one object matching each inscription:

- **Rock** — the object born of the mountains.
- **Apple** — the orchard's sweet prize.
- **Pickaxe** — a tool made to break stone.
- **Shovel** — a tool made to carry earth.

Use **four different objects**; the Shovel and Pickaxe are not interchangeable. If the Apple is missing, check whether it was consumed when your party Rested. The merchant at the dwarven camp can provide more supplies. The completed offering puzzle opens another passage, though not necessarily the large locked door immediately beside it.

### Where Are the Hidden Buttons and Iron Keys?

In one apparently empty room, inspect the **walls** rather than the floor. There are **four small buttons**; activating them exposes a path to an **iron key**.

Another iron key is kept in a locked chest. Use the nearby lever to access its room and **throw an item onto the pressure plate** to open the chest. Use these iron keys on the matching doors farther into the depths.

### What Does the 2:35 Torch Puzzle Mean?

The clue **2:35** refers to the position of the hands on a clock. Examine the pillars arranged around the room and treat their torch positions as clock markers.

Light the torch markers at the **2 o'clock** and **7 o'clock** positions: the hour hand indicates 2, while 35 minutes places the minute hand on 7. Do not light every torch. A correct arrangement triggers the mechanism and makes the next key accessible.

### How Do You Cross the Five Ditches?

Farther ahead, a row of five covered pits can suddenly lose one of its grates. Crossing them is not a simple matter of placing one permanent bridge. If you fall, use the return portal below to get back to the start of the crossing and try again.

Once across, deal with the skeleton interfering with the pressure plates. Ranged attacks are helpful when gaps keep you from reaching it directly. The passage opens after the encounter is resolved.

A separate **Test of Dexterity** offers optional treasure: throw a light object far enough to land on the distant plate, then use the unlocked route and the key it provides to reach the nearby chest. You do not need to complete that optional chest to reach the Soulforge.

<h2 id="giant-furnace">
  How Do You Kill the Giant With the Incinerator?
</h2>

The giant in the lower ruins is an **environmental encounter**, not a normal battle to win by trading attacks. Attempting to stand your ground and attack it produces **no damage** and can quickly end the attempt.

When it charges down the corridor, **press the nearby button to slam a heavy door shut** and escape into the adjoining passages. The goal is to make the giant follow you into the **incinerator chamber** without letting it reach your party.

1. Open the route into the chamber and draw the giant inside.
2. Retreat through an exit that lets you operate the doors from outside.
3. **Close every door around the furnace chamber** to trap the giant.
4. Find the **master lever** in the adjoining control room.
5. Pull the lever only after all chamber doors are secured.

If the lever produces a **safety-lock warning** instead of fire, at least one chamber door is still open. Return to the door controls and close it before trying again; repeatedly pulling the lever will not bypass the interlock.

With the giant trapped and the doors shut, the machinery ignites, flames fill the chamber, and the giant is destroyed. Return toward the route where it first appeared and continue into the vast cavern beyond.

<h2 id="soul-cleaver">
  How Do You Get Soulcleaver, Axe of the Deep Forge?
</h2>

At the end of the ruins, you discover the enormous ancient **Soulforge anvil**. The encounter returns your party to **Durok**, who recognizes the anvil and undertakes the work he promised.

Durok rewards you with **Soulcleaver, Axe of the Deep Forge**, a two-handed axe that scales with Strength and requires **8 Strength** to equip.

The weapon deals **10–20 base damage** and adds **10–20 Void damage**. Its active skill, **Void Pulse**, pushes the target one step backward and has a **10-turn cooldown**.

<h2 id="soulforge-stuck">
  Why Are You Still Stuck in the Soulforge?
</h2>

**The dragon is gone, but the Soulforge entrance is still blocked.** Return to Durok at the dwarven camp and finish the conversation about the dragon's fate. Reaching the lair alone does not grant access to the ruins.

**The eight-socket door still refuses to open.** Check the four East Garden rewards and the four West Garden rewards individually. The Garden Key used to reach West Garden and the iron keys found in deeper rooms do not count toward these eight sockets.

**The West Garden entrance is locked.** Return to the Outer Halls and solve the seasons brazier puzzle for the Garden Key. The Blind Corridor is a separate route, not another garden-key source.

**The colored seals worked, but nothing opened toward the gardens.** The Yellow and Purple seals reveal a treasure route. Follow the other passage to reach the eight-key gate and East Garden.

**The furnace lever will not activate.** The safety mechanism requires **every chamber door to be closed**. Check the doors before returning to the master lever.
